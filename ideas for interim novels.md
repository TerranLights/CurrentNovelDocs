# Interim Novel Ideas

A sketched-up rundown of ideas for possible novels between now and when the CRPG eventually releases

## Ideas

#### Placeholder

Belgrano-Byrd Mission

#### Basic Premise

An account of the mission beginning from Palmer City, Rothera, and Belgrano. And the team, the crew of robots who made the expedition to find the location of Byrd and establish it as a settlement, which later became a city.

- - -

#### Placeholder

The Long Night Convoy

#### Basic Premise

In three months of polar darkness during the Long Night War (2812), a robot leads refugees from Janbogo down the highway to Concordia.

- - -

#### Placeholder

The Cradle Goes Dark

#### Basic Premise

When Amundsen Tower falls, Mountain Pass loses the power its robot-making chambers depend on. A technician fights to keep the last one running.

- - -

#### Placeholder

The Evacuees

#### Basic Premise

The robots who escaped up the Tower build stations in orbit and on the moons of Mars, then lead the first landing on Mars itself.

- - -

#### Placeholder

Ji-Eun Kim

#### Basic Premise

A robot nanotech engineer in Unified Korea fights her way to the top of her field just as anti-robot hatred peaks in the 2550s. Her success counts for nothing under the Falkland Treaty's blanket exile.

*Possible, with adjustments still to come.*

- - -

#### Placeholder

The Boneyard Times

#### Basic Premise

Belgrano survives the Long Night War, then slowly falls to ruin, while its last robots refuse to stand down.
