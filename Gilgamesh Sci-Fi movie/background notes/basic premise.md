This is a sci-fi reinterpretation and retelling of the Epic of Gilgamesh, due to be an audio/visual movie (and not a novel, audio drama, etc), its visuals AI-rendered, and roles voiced by human voice actors. It's set shortly before and leading up to the events of the Global Great War of 2083, just off the coast of Hawaii at Underwater Research Union: Kappa (or "U.R.U.K." i.e., Uruk). This is where the first ever sentient robot was created, a form of En.Ki.Du. 

Gilgamesh is reimagined as Gilbert Gamesh, who is the CEO and possibly owner of United Robotics, whose initials stand for UR, which is Ur, one of the cities of ancient Sumeria.

Enkidu's name, in the form of a "back-ronym", retroactively stands for "Entelechian Kinetic Duumvir".
- Entelechy: A school-of-thought as theorized by Aristotle --> a conception of being fully and completely actualized; the state of something that is fully realized, actuality as opposed to potentiality
- Kinetic: (self-)moving/-motion (as En.Ki.Du. is a robot)
- Duumvir: Part of an "assembly of two" (cf. "Triumirate" --> "Triumvir")

In every scene, somewhere in the scene, there is a reference to either ancient Sumeria, ancient Sumerian culture, or to the original Epic of Gilgamesh itself.

The various gods of the Sumerian pantheon are reimagined as scientists and engineers.
