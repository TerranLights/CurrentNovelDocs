## Rules for this folder

- **Compression regroups; it never condenses.** A merged stanza holds every source cell in full and word for word. Each cell is labeled with its source position, as in `(4.2)`, and the cells are joined with `//`. Only the grouping into stanzas and Story Circle sections changes. This way the full detail is there when the lines are written.
- **Each stanza is one full Dan Harmon Story Circle across 16 lines of 12 syllables.** [YOU][NEED],[GO] / [SEARCH][FIND][TAKE] / [RETURN][CHANGE] can split the 16 lines any way the stanza needs.
- Anything new is marked **NEW** with its date. Open slots use `{{currently undetermined: …}}`.
- Chapters not listed below are still identical to 00.
