# Compression proposals (for the author's decision)

Status: **ALL APPROVED AND APPLIED 2026-11-14** in `01_adjusted_chapter_layout/` (cells regrouped, text kept verbatim). This file stays as the record of the reasoning. Once the author decides, the chosen version goes into `01_adjusted_chapter_layout/`. Chapter numbers are the layout numbers (for Ch 1–13 they are the same as the source numbers).

**Notation:** `4.2` means stanza 4, cell 2. The cells are 1 = [YOU][NEED],[GO], 2 = After [SEARCH],[FIND], but [TAKE], 3 = Upon [RETURN],[CHANGE].

**Two constraints on every new stanza:**
1. **Space:** 16 lines × 12 syllables = 192 syllables, about 130 words. Only the total is fixed. The three circle sections can split the 16 lines however the stanza needs (author, 2026-11-14: e.g. 3 / 9 / 4 or 4 / 7 / 5). A heavy section, such as a dialogue exchange or a setting description, can borrow lines from a light one, such as a gesture or a transit beat.
2. **Dan Harmon's Story Circle:** each stanza is a full circle. It needs a status quo and a want, then a crossing (You · Need · Go). It needs an adaptation and a price paid (Search · Find · Take). It ends returned, with something shifted (Return · Change). So cells are grouped by the circle step they serve, not just by order. Each new stanza has a **Take** line and a **Change** line saying what fills those two steps.
