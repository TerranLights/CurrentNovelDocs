### P4. Her flaw and her two needs (updated 2026-10-21)
**AUTHOR (2026-10-21):** the point is that **Valerie is a random person, and part of her flaw is that her randomness and over-exuberance for new experiences and activities harm other people**: not necessarily physical harm or damage, but her randomness and Type 7 behavior **cause problems for others**.
