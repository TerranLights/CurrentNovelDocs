# Snag 1: "We can go, or survive, but not both" (show, don't tell)

Sources: the transcribed stanza tables (Ch 1-22 proofread by the author; Ch 23-38 not yet), `character arcs/` (Valerie working copy, Craig.md, Martin.md), the three character bios, and the author's appended notes. The sample rows below use the outline's four columns and abbreviations, so they can be dropped straight into a stanza table.

## 1. The snag in one paragraph

Ch 18 stz 7-9 (author-proofread): at a travel agency the rep notices V's eyes and "says there are tariffs"; "They ask how much; rep shows [them?]; C is wide-eyed while V tilt-nods her head" (stz 7). Leaving, "V has enough saved up; C: that $$ belongs to her; besides, what to do when arriving in Vegas", and then the two lines "V: 'so we can pay the tariffs'; C: 'or we can survive'" (stz 8). Then "C says they have no choice but to explore their options here (in S.R.)" (stz 9). The summary cell already names the dilemma ("can pay tariffs or [service], not both"), and then the characters say it to each other. It falls flat because two people who have lived together for eight months, looking at the same number, are announcing a conclusion they have both already reached. The scene ends on a thesis, not on an act, and the reader is told the cost instead of watching someone pay it. The author's note on Ch 18 adds that stz 7-12 are no longer strictly necessary in their current form (the record exec is cut), but the book still needs some way, somewhere, to show that robots are charged a tariff to cross a border.

## 2. What the solution has to do

- **Show the robot tariff clearly, once, somewhere** (author's Ch 18 note). It does not have to be in Ch 18.
- **Keep what stz 8 says but drop its lines:** she has enough saved to pay her own way; the brothers won't take her money; nothing is waiting for them in Vegas.
- **Keep it Valerie's choice and Valerie's money.** The money runs in a chain: currencies kept "to stay hopeful about traveling" (bio; Ch 2 stz 12) → enough for her own tariff (Ch 18 stz 8) → offered and refused (Ch 21 stz 11) → all of it into store credit for Martin (Ch 25 stz 15-16 and the appended rows, "all of it?!"). **The money she could have traveled on becomes her gift.**
- **Keep "both"** (AUTHOR, confirmed): when she loses the house she wants Vegas (Ch 11 stz 5-6; Iman, Ch 13) *and* the Jazz cats' tour (Ch 2 stz 8; gone by Ch 8 stz 3). Wanting both is her Type 7 flaw (Social "Missing out": half-commits, pencils in backup plans).
- **Two facts already in the files make the impossibility concrete, at no cost:**
  - The tour is domestic: "tour through Cascadia" (Ch 2 stz 8); the original arc says N. Cal. and Oregon, and Oregon is wholly Cascadian (`worldbuilding/nations/New North America - Territory Definitions (read from map).md`). She could have gone with an ordinary ticket and no tariff. **The trip that was free is the one she let go.**
  - Las Vegas is in the Colorado Republic (same file: Nevada). **The Colorado dollars she already keeps (Ch 2 stz 12) are Vegas money.**
- **Respect Craig's arc** (Craig.md, FIND): Craig finds out about the tariff himself during the Road of Trials; Martin suggests leaving V with Ric while the brothers try Vegas; Craig refuses; "Martin has a passport; Craig doesn't." The current stz 7-9 never shows that Craig himself can't board without a passport. Any version that keeps the scene should show it.
- **Shared knowledge stays unspoken.** Craig already knows her low-siligel signs (author's note on Ch 18 stz 4) and her money habits; the brothers know each other's papers. Nobody explains these things.
- **Don't take the moral decision from Ch 29** (AUTHOR). The Ch 18 moment is pressure, Ch 25 is the sacrifice, and Ch 29 is where she depends on others.
- **Keep the mechanics small:** paperwork, a counter, a crate, a tag. Where a rule is needed, the options below fit the PROPOSED regime in `worldbuilding/nations/Cascadia - Proposed Extrapolations (author decides).md` (robots cross as freight under a declared value and tariff, travel inactive, owner bonded, no passports). It is not canon until the author confirms it. Valerie's serial, "EG7-KV42", comes from the author's own `structure/main story/Save the Cat.md` (Ric's line in the Dark Night note), an earlier outline, so it also needs the author's confirmation.
- **Bonus, not required:** Ch 2 stz 10 (the flyer, "possibly may require adjustment") and Ch 18 stz 10-12 (the record-label flyer, now orphaned) are free to repurpose.
