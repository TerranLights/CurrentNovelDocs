## 0. The shape of the unlearned state

### What is withheld until Ch 38 stz 1
These are the signs of the lesson. None of them should appear in Ch 28-37:
1. **Stillness that nobody is holding in place** (not Craig's hands, not Ric's hug) **and that is not a drained battery.** Ch 7 stz 10 and Ch 31 stz 7-10 show what her low-energy stillness looks like: slow, labored, falling. In Ch 38 her timer has given her a full charge, so her stillness there can only be chosen.
2. **Attention resting on one ordinary thing** without fear, a deadline or an audience.
3. **A small new thing tried without frenzy** (and failed, and adapted).
4. **Not checking**: the timer, her own playing, the room, the plan.
5. **Her fingers landing on a beat** (item 6).
6. (PROPOSED) **Her eye-glow pulsing at a regular interval** (the vocabulary is from Ch 23 stz 10: "blinking at regular & irregular intervals").

So in Ch 28-37: no rain-focus beat, no music built from a single sound, no slow eating, no chosen sameness, and no calm watching of the fire, the tree or the rain.

### Clamp and scatter
After the shock her Seven energy has two settings, and neither one is calm:
- **Clamp:** fear holds her to the known. The same songs, the long way round, hands behind her back, checking and correcting. This is her stress direction (-1: "perfectionistic, critical"), with the 6-wing's need for safety (Hornevian variant: "Demands security").
- **Scatter:** the old restlessness, now turned onto safe ground. Four jobs at once, lists, every face in the room, tapping, the next thing. This is the core Seven and her Social subtype ("scatter her energy and resources; to half-commit... 'pencils-in' backup plans").

The opening image (Ch 1, "a bit all-over-the-place") was scatter with novelty. Act 3 is clamp and scatter without novelty. Ch 38 is a third thing, her integration (+5: "focused, fascinated by life"). Each item below is tagged clamp or scatter, so the author can keep the mix from going static.

### The three-question test (for any post-shock beat)
1. **Is she doing something she has not done before?** If yes, cut it or make it something old.
2. **Does it cost someone else time, plans, money or mood?** If yes, move it before Ch 27-28 stz 14 or give it to someone else.
3. **Is she settled on one thing by her own choice, with nobody holding her still?** If yes, it belongs to Ch 38.
