# Valerie/Craig interruption conflicts — batch 2

Generated after a full read-through of the confirmed chapter outlines (1–38), so these are tied to specific established chapters/beats rather than generic hobby scenarios. Same six criteria as the original list (see `plans - conflict - Claude suggestions.md`): unsolicited, well-meant, in-character for both, a real interruption, in-character for Craig, real objective clash.

Two categories below: **Act 1 entries** are written to fold into existing thin/vague stanzas (Act 1 has zero open stanza budget), not to add new stanzas. **Road of Trials / Bad Guys Close In entries** are full new-scene candidates, since both sections have real open space.

## Act 1 (fold into existing stanzas — no new stanza budget)

### A. The Repair Bay Recital (Ch.1)
**Craig's plan:** While Ric finishes on Valerie's arm, Craig's quietly returning Ric's tools to their exact spots on the pegboard — respecting the established "chaotic mess, but Ric knows where everything is" detail.
**Valerie's plan:** The second her arm control comes back online (stz.9), she grabs every tool off the wall to "test" her new dexterity by juggling/inspecting them.
**Conflict:** Ric's memorized chaos gets scrambled right as Craig finishes restoring it — sweet, zero-stakes, matches the tender opening tone.
**Placement:** stz.8–9, currently thin.

### B. The Ticket Kept, The Ride Missed (Ch.2 — light touch, one added clause, not a new stanza)
Craig's already pocketing the cop's fine to deal with later (stz.1). Give him a reason to be mentally earmarking their remaining cash for it specifically — then when Valerie hands him the pre-war record she blew their savings on (stz.12, already written), "babe, you shouldn't have" carries real stakes instead of just surprise.
**Placement:** stz.1 setup + stz.12 payoff, both already written.

### C. Kitchen Chemistry (Ch.3 — fleshing out an already-vague beat)
Stz.2 already has Valerie "doing something with the food" while unsupervised — currently unspecified. Give Craig a reason the meal mattered (rationed grocery money, or something saved for Martin after a rough day), and let Valerie's "improvement" be a specific, well-meant kitchen disaster.
**Placement:** stz.2, already a stanza slot, just needs content.

### D. The Wrong Kind of Quiet (Ch.4)
**Craig's plan:** While Valerie's out shopping, he's finally in a groove re-cataloguing his vinyl by era.
**Valerie's plan:** Mid-spree, she has him summoned to the store because she "needs his opinion" — then makes the decision herself before he even arrives.
**Conflict:** Broken flow state, for nothing.
**Placement:** stz.13's vague "C is sitting, reading over tea" beat.

### F. Keysight's Other Wing (Ch.10)

### G. The Ric Stash (Ch.16)

Ties into her core wound (can't ask for help directly, decides unilaterally instead) — same register as the existing list's #17 Ricardo's Emergency Appointment.

Stz.9 already has Valerie wanting to keep old school supplies and Craig telling her to let it go. Worth expanding into a fuller scene given they're about to need to carry everything they own. **No other character involved — just Craig and Valerie.**
