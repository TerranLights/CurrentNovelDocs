## A. Her friends and the raids (asked 2026-10-20)
1. **How many robots were deported with her**, and are they named or numbered? *(No recommendation; the author's naming tradition.)*
2. **How did they die on the station?** One defining raid or a series over time? *Recommended: one defining raid, with later raids as echoes*, because it gives the revenge a single event and lets the tear replay one night.
3. **How were her friends and family on Earth killed, and by whom?** The same system that caught her (the Garrison), or something else? *No recommendation.* It decides what she hates beyond the scavengers.
4. **Does she see her Earth friends in the flashes?** Presumably not, since the flashes are of the station; but they could appear in her own mind, if the author wants psychological horror from her. *No recommendation.*
5. **Do the flashes include her deported friends alive?** If so, they torment her too (fits the "a wound that is also a place" premise in the synthesis). *Recommended: yes, sparingly.*

## B. The opening and the eye-gouging (asked 2026-10-20)

8. **What exactly does the new opening show?** The remnants of old death, the robot skeletons, her walking; and what hints at the tear?

11. **Does the tear reset anything?** *Recommended: no.* **Does she pay a price for using it?** *Recommended: yes.*
