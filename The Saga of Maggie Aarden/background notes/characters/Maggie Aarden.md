Subvariant: [[Self-Preservation]] :: Self-Pres Five — "Isolation and Hoarding" [SOURCED]
	The Self-Pres Five builds deliberate boundaries as shelter, "wanting everything necessary contained safely
	inside them so there's little reason to venture out." Her stated Goal below is that description almost
	word-for-word. Least communicative of the three subtypes; difficulty expressing anger directly —
	displeasure surfaces as withdrawal and silence rather than confrontation. [ENNEAGRAM]

+8: Integration → Eight. Reclaims physical presence and instinctual energy; takes on leadership and major
	challenges directly, and others correctly sense she isn't acting out of self-interest — which draws
	real support toward what she's building. [ENNEAGRAM]
	** This is the load-bearing irony of the entire saga: her healthy integration is exactly what makes
	her valuable enough for Upper Earth to target. A less-whole Maggie would have been useless to them. **

-7: Disintegration → Seven. Under sustained stress, reacts against her own isolation by scattering into
	restless, impulsive activity that fails to satisfy — agitated, mentally accelerated, diffuse. [ENNEAGRAM]

Core Desire:
To be capable and competent; to be self-sufficient and not dependent on anyone for what she needs. [ENNEAGRAM]

Core Fear:
Being useless, helpless, or incapable — and beneath that, engulfment: being taken over, used up, or absorbed
by others' demands until nothing of her own is left. [ENNEAGRAM]

Core Weakness:
Avarice — but the Five's version: hoarding time, energy, privacy, and information rather than material wealth.
Fives are among the least materialistic types; what she guards is the reserve of self. [ENNEAGRAM]

Wounding Message:
"Nothing will be provided for you, because everyone can see you don't need anything." [PROPOSED]
	Her formative privation (author-confirmed 2026-09-18 as a combination of her activation context and her
	first employment): she came online at full adult competency into a world that had legal personhood for
	robots but no practice of actually nurturing one — no robot childhood, no upbringing, no equivalent of
	parenting. Nobody withheld care from her maliciously; they simply had no concept that a being who
	arrived able to do everything might still need something. The industrial yard then welded the lesson on:
	valued entirely for output, left alone precisely as long as she performed.
	** Structural resonance worth keeping: her private wound is the era's public wound at personal scale.
	The Grand Timeline's own throughline for this period is that legal personhood "never translated into
	being treated as though it were real." That is also, exactly, what happened to her individually. **
