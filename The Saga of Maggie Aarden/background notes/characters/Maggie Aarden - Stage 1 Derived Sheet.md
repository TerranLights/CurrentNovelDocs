**Note on "Undercurrent" itself:** this is not an Enneagram-textbook concept — it's the author's own original
addition to the framework (established 2026-08-11, documented at
`TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Enneagram_Undercurrents.md` and, in full, at
`InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Enneagram/Undercurrents.md`), so it wouldn't show up anywhere in the mined Enneagram
book extractions — checked both and confirmed absent, as expected. An Undercurrent is a real second Enneagram
type/wing/instinct profile sitting underneath the main type (not a blend, not a Wing — unrestricted by
adjacency, can be any of the other eight types), optional, and not obligatory to identify. The working baseline
elsewhere in the project is ~80% Type / ~20% Undercurrent, explicitly a placeholder rather than a locked
mechanic; Maggie's 85/15 split is simply this specific character's own version of that same working ratio, not
a deviation needing resolution.

This undercurrent is also load-bearing for the Ghost proposal below (A4) — per the framework's own note that
"occasional conflict is a feature, not a bug," it's the mechanism that explains why a fundamentally
withdrawal-oriented 5w6 doesn't just refuse the leadership role duty keeps forcing on her.

### A4. Existing Biographical and Historical Material

Extensively **SOURCED** already: industrial-yard technical work → Steampunk art as a private outlet → forced
into "third option" resettlement leadership as anti-robot sentiment rises → War of Upper Earth escalates her

**Ghost/psychological wound — proposed via Enneagram-Rooted-Contradiction (STRONG INFERENCE, not yet
canon):** the existing character sheet has this genuinely blank (Core Fear, Wounding Message all empty), so
this is a real gap worth actually filling rather than leaving blocked, since the seed and existing material give
enough signal to derive one per the methodology's own primary technique (deriving inner conflict from the
Enneagram type's own structural tension, never inventing independently of it):

A dominant 5w6's core fear clusters around being incompetent, overwhelmed, or unable to protect her own
reserves (5), compounded by the 6-wing's anxiety about safety and reliable support structures. Her established
behavior already shows this precisely: "she would rather be left alone with her expertise and her inner world
than be responsible for anyone else" (existing character file). **The proposed wounding mechanism**: her
Wounding Message is something like *"the moment you're needed, whatever you built for yourself gets taken from
you"* — every time she's shown competence (industrial-yard mastery, then resettlement organizing, then wartime
credibility), the world responds not by leaving her alone with it, but by escalating what it demands of her
next. Her withdrawal instinct isn't general misanthropy — it's a learned prediction that *visible competence
gets punished with more responsibility, never with being left alone.* This tracks the established arc precisely:
industrial job → resettlement leadership → full wartime leadership → treaty signatory, each step imposed
*because* she proved capable at the step before.

**Where the 1w9 undercurrent does real work**: a pure 5w6 has a coherent motive to refuse escalating demands —
but Maggie doesn't refuse, she just resents having to comply (per the existing self-contradiction note). The
1-undercurrent's suppressed sense of "how things *ought* to be run" is the plausible mechanism for why she
can't actually walk away once the stakes are collective, not personal — a quiet, uncomfortable sense of moral
obligation overriding the 5w6's self-protective instinct, exactly often enough to keep dragging her forward
against her own preference. **This is a proposal to test against future drafting, not settled canon** — flag it
explicitly as such if it goes into the Stage 3 character-data pass.

**Weakness/Flaw — likewise proposed, not yet canon:** something like *"she treats being needed as identical to
being used up, so she under-asks for support and over-extends herself alone rather than risk finding out she's
right"* — directly consistent with the existing "Behavior under stress" note and the "wants nothing more than to
be left alone... becomes, against her will, the single most consequential robot in this era" self-contradiction
already on file.
