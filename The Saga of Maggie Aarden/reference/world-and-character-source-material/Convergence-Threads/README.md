## The four Finale-section threads (First Interwar Period)

The parent timeline repo explicitly flags these four as **"ready to be woven into a connected narrative"** for
the War of Upper Earth's Finale beat (~June 2468 – June 21, 2564) — not yet resolved into one story anywhere,
including in the source repo itself:

- **Maggie Aarden** — see `../Maggie-Aarden-Character/`.
- **Ji-Eun Kim** — see `Ji-Eun_Kim.md` (full character file copied here, 65 lines). Unified Korea, robot
  nanotechnician, rose to the top of her field despite "systematic discrimination, professional exclusion, and
  social hostility" during the worst years of pre-war anti-robot sentiment. Named directly in the Falkland
  Treaty material as a robot exiled "because she is a robot," not individually named in the Treaty text itself.
- **Trisha Miller** — see `Trisha_Miller.md` (full character file copied here, 69 lines). Midwestland radio DJ
  robot; her own history records "human-robot tensions during her Midwestland years," opposition specifically
  for being a robot, alongside a loyal fan base.
- **The Sinian Federation** — confirmed as one of the Upper Earth nations that persecuted robots and
  participated in the War of Upper Earth ("The Sinian Federation was among the Upper Earth nations that
  persecuted robots and participated in the War of Upper Earth," source: InnerTepeniaGDD's
  `Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Zhongshan.md`). **Participation/persecution is established; causation of the war
  itself is explicitly not** — no single nation caused the War of Upper Earth, which is framed as a global
  human-vs-robot war (plus humans who sided with robots) with multiple combatant nations and no single origin
  point.

**Explicitly unresolved even in the parent repo:** how these four threads connect to each other, and to
whatever specific event constitutes the War's own Break into Three (~June 2468) and actual outbreak
(~2563–2564). This is genuinely open territory — exactly the gap this novel's own planning can fill.

## The theme this era shares with the Long Night War (Second Interwar Period)

Maggie's coerced signature on the Falkland Treaty (2564) is repeatedly called **"Tepenia's single deepest
historical wound"** in the parent repo, explicitly tied to the *same* underlying theme as the Long Night War's
own inciting incident 248 years later (an Upper Earth diplomat denying a Palmer City gynoid — `TBN [SE-031]`,
working title "Akina," not developer-confirmed — personhood and trying to assault her; she kills him in self-defense) — both framed as **"a robot denied full
agency or personhood, with lasting consequences for everyone around her."** Two instances of the same wound,
two and a half centuries apart, bookending the entire Tepenian era. Worth keeping in mind as a structural
rhyme across the saga's own eventual ending and the wider universe's later history, even though the Long Night
War itself sits chronologically after this saga's own scope.
