**Weakness (fundamental flaw):** Believes connection to her is inherently dangerous to the people she loves — that closeness is the vulnerability an enemy exploits, not something worth the risk anymore. Not cowardice; guilt reshaped into an absolute rule she has never tested against the possibility that she's wrong.
**Strengths & Skills (what they're good at):** Peerless nanotech engineering — among Tepenia's best, arguably the best. A systemic thinker who designs for the worst-case actor rather than the average one (her concealment work assumes an adversary willing to torture allies for information, not merely search for her). Capable of engineering detection countermeasures precise enough to stay hidden from friendly, sympathetic observers, not only hostile ones.
**Goal (broad-scale desire):** To remain permanently, absolutely undetectable — not primarily to protect herself, but to ensure she can never again be the reason someone she loves becomes a target.
**Values & Passions (what/who they care about most):** Her craft, practiced for its own sake as much as for who it protects. The people she's lost, held to even now, in absentia. An unspoken loyalty to the Eyes of Gold that her own concealment now prevents her from acting on.
**Internal Conflict (competing desires or psychological contradiction):** The isolation she engineered to prevent further loss is also the exact mechanism preventing her from ever repairing the loss already caused — Calethina's not knowing, the Eyes' inability to reach her. She is, in effect, still protecting people from a threat that may no longer be active, at a cost she has never had to justify to anyone but herself.

## Backstory Summary
- Brief Backstory Summary (abbreviated): TBD
Ji-Eun Kim is a master nanotech engineer — one of the most technically accomplished practitioners of nanotechnology in Concordia, and possibly in the broader Tepenian Federation. She was built and trained in Unified Korea during a period when anti-robot sentiment was at an all-time high, and fought her way to the top of her field in that environment. By the late 25th century she had become recognized as one of the greatest nanotechnicians on the planet — a global reputation, not merely a Tepenian one.

Following the Falkland Treaty (June 21, 2564), she was exiled to Antarctica along with the broader robot population — sent, not chosen. Her first landing was at Sejong, on King George Island. She later relocated to Zhongshan, whose cultural insularity and serious technical character suited her, before eventually moving to Concordia, drawn by a city large enough and busy enough that a person who did not want to be noticed could simply not be noticed.

She continued her nanotech research after arriving in Concordia, establishing a testing facility (now in ruins) and engineering the Eyes of Gold's gold ocular prosthetics — the surgical implants that serve as that faction's rite of passage. She was a friend to the Eyes of Gold without ever being a member.

She is alive. Her testing facility is in ruins, but she herself disappeared before or during the Long Night War — not as a casualty, but as a choice, using the same nanotech discipline that defined her career to make herself permanently undetectable. The concealment was originally built as a defensive gift for someone she cared about, who did not survive to receive it; it was also the first thing she ever built purely for her own sake, after a career spent building things for others. The reversal is no longer simple — she designed the concealment to be permanent, and undoing it now is genuinely difficult and dangerous.

## Role in Main Story / World
- Story Function (why this character matters to the plot mechanically): A figure defined by absence rather than presence — her value to any storyline that touches her is the question of whether/how she could ever be found, and what it would cost her (and everyone still grieving her) if she were.
- Connection to larger narrative: A brilliant engineer who chose deliberate, costly disappearance over being found — her story is not one of rescue, but of whether she wants to be found at all, and what that costs everyone involved. She is known to some through her connection to the Eyes of Gold, and her fate is tied to the questions surrounding her.
- Growth/Change Arc (how they evolve over the course of the story): Would need to move from "I keep people safe by remaining unreachable" toward something closer to "hiding didn't prevent the loss, it just made sure everyone who already lost me keeps losing me, for a threat that may not even still be active" — genuinely open whether she ever completes that arc, or whether the story only raises the possibility.
- Relationship Dynamics (beyond simple alliance/rivalry — include contradictory feelings like jealousy, resentment, or hidden admiration): Calethina doesn't know she's alive — not through any failure of Calethina's own network senses, but because Calethina's own distributed server network was damaged in the same catastrophe (Amundsen Tower's destruction) that caused the Planetary Split Brain, and her city-wide signal projection only holds intermittently even within Concordia. The Eyes of Gold can't reach her either — not from lack of trying, but because her concealment was deliberately built with no whitelist: any exception, even for people she trusts completely, is exactly the kind of vulnerability that already got someone she loved killed under Upper Earth interrogation.
