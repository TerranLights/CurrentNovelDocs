# World & Character Source Material — Consolidated 2026-09-17

Everything findable across the shared universe's repos (TepenianUniverseTimeline, InnerTepeniaGDD,
SouthernLights) about Maggie Aarden, the Falkland Treaty, and the War of Upper Earth, pulled into one place so
planning this saga doesn't require jumping between four repos. See the top-level `The Saga of Maggie Aarden`
memory in this project for the survey session that produced this.

**Scope change this consolidation was done for:** originally planned as 4 books following Maggie's own
Maiden→Hero→Queen→King archetypal arc. The real scope turned out to be much bigger — this is really a
**historical account of the ~200 years leading up to the War of Upper Earth and the Falkland Treaty**, with
100+ characters from 30+ countries whose storylines converge at the Council Meeting in the Falkland Islands
where Maggie is forced by Upper Earth government representatives to sign the Falkland Treaty. **New working
plan: 8 books, each split into 2 Parts.** The series-level
`structure/Dev-Road-Map/02-Blocking-Decisions.md` file still describes the old 4-book decision as resolved —
that needs a rewrite to reflect this, not yet done as of this consolidation.

## What's here

- **`Maggie-Aarden-Character/`** — her full character file from the parent Timeline repo (README, Timeline,
  Conflict_Experiences, Relationships, Loyalties, Formative_History) plus InnerTepeniaGDD's companion-sheet
  version (extra narrative color layered on top, game-mechanic content not carried over). **Relationships.md
  has been corrected in this copy**: two husbands (first — shy, grows confident, dies of old age, matches this
  novel's own original Book 1 companion sketch; second — proud/boisterous, grows humble, dies in the War of
  Upper Earth, matches the original Book 2 sketch) plus a separate robot friend (unnamed). This supersedes the
  novel's older "two boyfriends" framing and the Timeline repo's own unresolved husband-personality gap —
  propagate back to the Timeline repo when convenient.
- **`Convergence-Threads/`** — the parent timeline repo's own four pre-identified "ready to be woven into a
  connected narrative" threads for this exact era (Maggie, Ji-Eun Kim, Trisha Miller, the Sinian Federation),
  plus the full character files for Ji-Eun Kim and Trisha Miller. Explicitly unresolved even at the source: how
  these four connect to each other and to the war's actual outbreak — open territory for this saga's own
  planning.
- **`SouthernLights-Findings/`** — cross-project synthesis from the sibling TV series repo, which already did
  real research into this era: real-world treaty models (Antarctic Treaty 1959 as the likely direct structural
  ancestor, Lausanne 1923 for compulsory population transfer, Hittite-Egypt for non-punishment/refugee
  provisions), the "Great Corruption" (a live, unsolved mystery — Janbogo's pre-Treaty archives were
  deliberately wiped in a specific order in the hours before signing, implying the Treaty was negotiated under
  false pretenses), and a wider "Writers' Room Hooks" survey of load-bearing dramatic material across the whole
  shared universe, several items of which sit in or near this era.

## Universe-Wide Material — Held in `worldbuilding/`

These govern every book in the line, not this saga alone, so they live in the repo-wide `worldbuilding/`
folder and are maintained as one shared source:

- **`worldbuilding/history/Falkland-Treaty/`** — the full drafted treaty text, its structural scaffold
  (genre/register reasoning), the real-world-treaties influence log, and the condensed article-by-article
  quick reference. The direct textual source for the 30+ converging nations.
- **`worldbuilding/history/World-History-Excerpt.md`** — an excerpt (not a full copy) of the parent repo's
  master `World_History_Reference.md`, scoped to this era. That source is actively evolving and covers the
  whole shared universe — check it live for anything beyond this snapshot.
- **`worldbuilding/nations/World-Nations-Roster.md`** — every nation confirmed or documented so far, with the
  running tally against the 30+ target.
- **`worldbuilding/nations/New North America - Territory Definitions (read from map).md`** — which real-world
  territory each post-war nation actually covers.

## Known open gaps (worth flagging for planning, not yet resolved anywhere)

- Names for both husbands and the robot friend.
- The order/overlap of the two marriages relative to her Falkland leadership and the signing itself.
- The exact composition of the Assembly's negotiating delegation.
- What "the evacuation" actually was and what was suppressed about it.
- How the four convergence threads (Maggie, Ji-Eun Kim, Trisha Miller, Sinian Federation) actually connect to
  each other and to the war's outbreak — the single biggest open structural question for this saga.
- The Great Corruption's actual content — what was erased from Janbogo's archives and why (per SouthernLights'
  findings, nobody has written the answer anywhere yet — genuinely first-mover territory for this saga if
  wanted).
