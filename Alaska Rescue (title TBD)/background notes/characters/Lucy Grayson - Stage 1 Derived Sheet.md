### A2. Enneagram Assignment

**Type 5w6, Social subtype (80%), with a 3w4 Self-Preservation undercurrent (20%). SOURCED** from the seed.

**What the Social Five specifically means here.** Per the project's own Type 5 material, the Social Five
("The Specialist" / "Totem") channels Avarice into knowledge itself — expertise becomes both the identity
marker and the connection point at once, standing in for the nourishment ordinary relationships would
otherwise provide. And the detail that matters most for her: **despite outward appearances of indifference to
status, this subtype genuinely wants recognition and prestige within whatever field it has claimed as its
own.** [ENNEAGRAM]

That lands exactly on her stated Goal — *"to secure a setup where she's appreciated (and not simply used) for
her work and her skills."* The Social Five's hidden hunger **is** recognition-within-expertise, which is the
thing she says she wants. And her stated opening Problem is that hunger's precise inverse: a boss who extracts
12–16 hour days of her expertise while valuing none of it. Her starting situation is engineered, whether
deliberately or not, to be the exact maximum-pain configuration for her type — total use, zero recognition.

**General Type 5 mechanics carrying into her:** core desire to be capable and self-sufficient; core fear of
being useless or depleted; the defense mechanism of **Isolation** (thinking about something without
experiencing the feeling attached to it). Her stated personality reads as textbook — "cerebral," "doesn't talk
much without reason," "can come off as cold and standoffish when she actually just doesn't feel any need to
engage" is the type's characteristic *resignation* about its own isolation, treating it as simply how life is
rather than as a wound. [ENNEAGRAM]

**What the 3w4 Self-Pres undercurrent adds (20%).** The SP Three ("Workaholism"/"Security") is the Three
countertype — it denies rather than displays the vanity driving it, working hard specifically to build
self-sufficiency and competence at handling life without leaning on anyone, while carrying real private
anxiety about how much that costs. Naranjo's phrase for it is **"vanity for having no vanity"** — wanting to
be seen as successful while actively not wanting to be caught wanting that. Downtime reads as incapacity. The
3w4 wing ("The Professional") roots self-esteem in the work itself rather than in charm, and at average health
makes nearly every project feel like it puts the whole of one's self-worth on the line. [ENNEAGRAM]

**The two profiles interlock into her single defining problem.** Her stated Weakness — *"a tendency to refuse
outside help when she really should probably accept"* — is **doubly locked**, one lock from each profile:
the Five believes contact depletes rather than replenishes, and the SP Three's entire project is competence
without leaning on anyone. She has two structurally independent reasons to refuse help, which is presumably
why it rises to the level of a fundamental flaw rather than a quirk. Breaking it requires defeating both, not
one. [PROPOSED, built on ENNEAGRAM]

**And the central bind worth building her arc around:** she wants appreciation badly (Social Five), and she
wants the work itself to be recognized as excellent (3w4) — but the Social Five is structurally withdrawn and
the SP Three specifically cannot be caught wanting recognition. **She has a real, driving hunger she is
constitutionally forbidden from voicing.** She can only wait to be noticed, which is exactly what the railyard
will never do. [PROPOSED]

**Integration → Eight** (self-confident, decisive). **Disintegration → Seven** (scattered, impulsive,
stimulation-seeking). Both verified against `Enneagram_Dynamics.md`. [ENNEAGRAM]

### A4. Existing Biographical and Historical Material

**SOURCED:** built to work as a full-time railyard engine mechanic in Whitehorse, at one period working nearly
all day; overworked and underappreciated under a human boss who doesn't value her; her established Inciting
Event is Human #1 arriving in Whitehorse from Vancouver.

**Ghost / formative privation — PROPOSED.** Type 5 psychology is rooted in early privation: nothing reliably
provided, so the response is to need very little and hold tight to whatever meager resources exist. For a
robot built pre-personhood into a single function, that translates cleanly without needing invention: **she
was provided exactly what her function required and nothing beyond it, and what resources she did have — time,
energy, attention — were consumed by the job at a rate she never got to set.** The Social Five's particular
flavor sharpens it further: expertise is her only channel to the world, because it is literally the reason she
was made and the only basis on which anyone interacts with her at all. Her Wounding Message would be something
in the register of *"you are what you can do, and nothing else about you is wanted."* Which is also, notably,
a plain factual description of her legal status in this era, not a distortion she invented.
