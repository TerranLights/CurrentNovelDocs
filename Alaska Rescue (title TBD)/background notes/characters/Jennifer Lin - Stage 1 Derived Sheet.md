### A2. Enneagram Assignment

**Type 9w1, Social subtype (65%), with a 4w5 Social undercurrent (35%). SOURCED** from the seed.

**First, a convergence worth noting.** The project's own Type 9 README names the 9w1 wing **"The Dreamer"**
(idealistic, reserved, quietly principled). Her existing character file — written before this seed, without
reference to any Enneagram assignment — independently lists her archetype as *"Dreamer type."* The seed and the
prior material arrived at the same word by separate routes, which is about as strong a confirmation of a type
assignment as this methodology can produce. [ENNEAGRAM + SOURCED]

The 9w1 wing also accounts directly for the seed's *"consistently looks for and tries to find the goodness and
positive aspects of things"* — that is the Nine's characteristic positive-outlook stance sharpened by the One
wing's idealism. [ENNEAGRAM]

**Her stargazing is the Type 9 Inner Sanctum, made literal.** The source material describes Nines as, despite
appearing the most sociable of types, among the most *withdrawn* — not physically, but by maintaining an
**Inner Sanctum**: a private mental space no one can intrude on, populated by idealized things rather than the
real people and real problems of the actual world. Retreating there produces genuine calm in a crisis; but the
more a Nine inhabits it, the more they drift into hazy daydreaming, increasingly absentminded and disconnected
from meaningful action.

Her Personal Vision is that passage rendered as a scene: alone on a rock at night with a cup of coolant,
tracking constellations and meteors, perfectly fine with the solitude. **Her Inner Sanctum is not metaphorical
— she physically goes and sits in it.** Which gives the arc a ready-made cost structure: the same habit that is
her genuine joy and her one real source of self is also, mechanically, the thing pulling her away from action.
Both readings are true at once and neither cancels the other. [ENNEAGRAM → PROPOSED]

**Her stated weakness has a mechanism, and it is worse than it looks.** The Nine's primary defense is
**dissociation** (sometimes called narcotization) — a *dimming of awareness* rather than a dramatic split,
buffering the Nine from feelings, needs, and wants they'd rather register. The source notes the advantage is
real in a genuinely threatening or traumatic moment.

Her seed weakness is *"terrified of bears; great difficulty facing danger."* Put those together: **her type's
automatic response to mortal threat is to dim her own awareness of it.** On a route through central BC where
bears are the established hazard, that is the single most dangerous possible reaction, and the cruel part is
that it will *feel* like it's working — the source says so explicitly. She won't panic. She'll go quiet and
absent, and register the danger less, right when registering it is survival. Her flaw isn't cowardice; it's a
defense that is functionally load-bearing everywhere else in her life and catastrophic in exactly this one
situation. [ENNEAGRAM → PROPOSED]

**Her Goal restates the Nine's core wound as an ambition.** The type's defining injury is that its own
priorities go unregistered — attention runs outward by default, toward what others want, and turns inward only
with real deliberate effort; asked what they actually want, a Nine characteristically struggles to say. Her
stated Goal is *"to find a place in life where she can apply her love and passion for Astronomy in a way that's
meaningful."* Stripped down, that is: **I want my own want to count for something.** For a Nine that is not a
modest goal — it is the growth edge itself, stated as a destination. And her legal status makes it doubly
steep: she is property with an assigned function, so her having a want of her own is not merely psychologically
hard but structurally unrecognized. [ENNEAGRAM → PROPOSED]

**FLAG — the one real tension in the inputs, which I am not resolving on my own.** The Social Nine is the
type's **countertype**: markedly *more* outgoing, energetic, and expressive than the other two Nine subtypes,
working hard to earn membership in the group, giving generously, hiding pain. The seed's personality summary
says *"shy, highly timid, terribly lacking any sort of confidence."* Those do not obviously match.

My reading — offered as a reading, for confirmation or correction:

- The **4w5 Social undercurrent is doing the surface work**, and at 35% it is substantial enough to. Social
  Four is **"The Outsider" / "Shame"** — a chronic sense of inferiority where guilt attaches to almost any
  personal want, and where at lower health fear of rejection produces near-total social withdrawal. The 4w5
  wing is **"The Bohemian"** — more introverted and socially withdrawn than 4w3, dwelling more exclusively in
  imagination. That is her presented surface almost line for line: timid, unconfident, dreamy, wistful,
  content alone.
- The **Social Nine's outgoingness is therefore not her current state but her available motion** — the
  overcompensation she has not performed yet. Which makes it arc fuel rather than a contradiction: the
  direction she can travel, not a description she fails to match at page one.
- And the two subtypes share an identical engine, which is what makes the pairing coherent rather than
  arbitrary. Social Nine's drive to participate *"stems from an underlying sense of not quite belonging."*
  Social Four is *"the most socially active and engaged of the three, wanting real involvement in the social
  world while feeling persistently unsure how to actually belong in it."* Both are built on wanting to belong
  and not knowing how. Her main type and her undercurrent are not two different people; they're one problem
  with two different volumes.
- One further note the story can use directly: Social Nine's "sense of not quite belonging" and Social Four's
  outsiderhood are, for a pre-personhood robot, **not distortions at all.** She is in literal fact not a member
  of the society she lives in. Her psychology and her legal status point at the same fact, which means her
  growth cannot consist of realizing she was wrong about it.
- Also worth flagging for later, as Social Four material the arc can draw on: a real, mostly-hidden
  competitiveness runs underneath the visible shame — wanting recognition and to be first, even while the
  surface presentation stays modest and self-deprecating. Combined with the Social Nine's habit of hiding pain
  and rarely burdening others with what the accommodation costs, she has a genuine appetite she is
  well-practiced at never showing. [ENNEAGRAM → PROPOSED]

**Integration → Three** (self-developing, energetic, able to act on her own behalf). **Disintegration → Six**
(anxious, worried, suspicious). Both verified against `Enneagram_Dynamics.md`. The stress direction is
especially playable here: a shy doll on a dangerous overland route sliding into Six-ish anxiety is a very
natural pressure response, and it sits right next to the dissociation problem above without duplicating it —
one dims awareness, the other floods it. [ENNEAGRAM]

### A4. Existing Biographical and Historical Material

**SOURCED:** built for meteorology and radio engineering in Vancouver; long-developed habit of sitting alone at
night looking at the stars.

**The two interlock better than they need to, and it's worth recording why.** HF radio propagation improves at
night — the ionospheric D-layer dissipates without solar radiation, so long-range signal work is a night
occupation. Her job put her outdoors and awake in darkness, professionally, on a regular schedule. The
stargazing didn't have to be stolen time or a sentimental indulgence; **it grew in the hours her work already
placed her in.** Her Inner Sanctum and her assigned function occupy the same shift. Full skill-by-skill
breakdown lives in `Jennifer Lin - Built Purpose (Meteorology and Radio Engineering).md`.

**Ghost / formative wound — PROPOSED, and partly already settled.** The author has previously chosen a
combination of proposals #1 and #2 for her Ghost origin. Consistent with the Enneagram layer above, the shape
that fits is a formative experience of **her own preferences being structurally irrelevant** — not cruelty, but
the ordinary condition of being commissioned equipment: competent, relied upon, and never once asked what she'd
prefer. A Nine's characteristic conclusion from that is not resentment but *acquiescence* — the belief that her
own wants don't rise to the level of things worth raising. Wounding Message in the register of *"what you want
does not enter into it."* Which, again, is a plain description of her legal standing rather than a distortion
she invented.
