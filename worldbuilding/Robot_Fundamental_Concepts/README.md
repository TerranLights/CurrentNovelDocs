# Robot Fundamental Concepts

**The question (author, 2026-10-30):** the Tepenian universe is fundamentally robot-oriented, so what are the **fundamental robot concepts** of things? For example:
- What is the robot concept of **justice**?
- What is the robot concept of **crime**, of what it means for something to be a crime?
- What are the robot concepts of **trust** and **care**?
- What are the robot concepts of **honor** and **duty**?

**The starting frame: the twelve meta-categories of the zodiac.**
- The twelve signs of the zodiac are treated as twelve ready-made **meta-categories of human understanding**: areas of human psychology, behavior and life.
- For each meta-category, the work asks: **what is the fundamental robot understanding of, and approach to, this area?**
- This is **not** a robot version of the zodiac, and **not** robot astrology. The signs are only the starting set of categories; their astrological meanings are researched as human understanding, not adopted as belief.
- **The twelve may not be the whole list.** The author expects there may be other meta-categories of human understanding, psychology and behavior. Candidates are collected in `03 - Beyond the twelve/`.
- **Why the zodiac:** Concordia, the centerpiece city of the Inner Tepenia GDD, has twelve districts based on the twelve signs, plus a thirteenth, neutral district (the Hub) that has no sign.
- **Concordia comes after.** The fundamental concepts are a universal floor, and each district is one population's variation on it. Eventually Concordia's twelve districts will be a microcosm of the robot universals and of what robot culture means. The concepts are worked out here first; Concordia is then designed around the results.

**Expected scale:** many months of research, synthesis and development (author). Nothing here is quick, and nothing here is settled until the author says so.

## Layout
| Folder or file | What goes there |
|---|---|
| `PROGRESS TRACKER.md` | Status of every meta-category and concept, decisions made, and open questions for the author. **Read first when resuming.** |
| `00 - Foundations/` | The existing material this work builds on: Brown's *Human Universals*, the Robot Universals, the zodiac research already done for Concordia, and related canon. Pointers, not copies. |
| `01 - The twelve meta-categories/` | One folder per meta-category, each split into a human-understanding layer (research) and a robot-understanding layer (development). |
| `02 - Concepts/` | Specific concepts the author names (justice, crime, trust, care, honor, duty and more), one file each. A concept can draw on several meta-categories. |
| `03 - Beyond the twelve/` | Candidate meta-categories that don't fit inside any of the twelve. |

## How the work runs
1. **Human understanding first** (research; any hour): what established human sources say about each area.
   - Sources include the astrological meaning of the sign and its house, and how human cultures and disciplines understand that area of life.
2. **Robot understanding second** (derivation; only in the 05:00-14:59 window, with the author): the fundamental robot understanding and approach, built from the human layer and the Robot Universals. Every proposal is marked PROPOSED until the author decides.
3. **Concepts** are worked once the meta-categories they draw on have enough material.

**No fixed order.** Work can start in any meta-category. Sources are open-ended: anything and everything related to a topic relevant to a meta-category. They are added wherever they fit, as they are found.

**Standing rules:**
- **The zodiac stays out of the fiction.** Everything zodiac-derived is design input, never lore. This is the same rule the GDD's zodiac substrate binds itself with.
- **The Universal Design Laws apply,** for example No National Stereotypes (index: `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Universal Design Laws/README.md`).
- **This repo is public.** Source books stay out of git. Research files paraphrase and cite, with short quotes only.
- **Resumable research.** Every working file carries a `STATUS:` line. Agent briefs and resume prompts go in a `_dispatch/` folder here when research agents are used.
- **Staging.** Like everything in `worldbuilding/`, this is a working space; nothing moves to the Timeline repo until it is settled and the author asks.
