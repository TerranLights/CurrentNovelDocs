## Decisions (author)
- **2026-10-30:**
  - The twelve zodiac signs are the starting set of meta-categories of human understanding.
  - This is not robot astrology and not a robot zodiac.
  - Other meta-categories may exist beyond the twelve.
  - The work lives in `worldbuilding/Robot_Fundamental_Concepts/`.
  - **Concordia comes after.** The fundamental concepts are a universal floor, and each Concordia district is one population's variation on it ("fundamentally correct"). Eventually Concordia's twelve districts will be a microcosm of the robot universals and of what robot culture means. So the concepts are worked out here first, and Concordia is designed around the results afterward.
  - **No fixed order.** Work can start anywhere. Resources are added wherever they fit, as they are found, in any order.
  - **Sources are open-ended:** anything and everything related to a topic relevant to a meta-category.
  - **The zodiac arrangement stays exactly as it is** for this research. New classifications come later, when the material is applied to settings, locations and categories in general.
  - **The signs are research classifiers for now.** Zodiac names as folder titles are fine at this stage. Inner Tepenia will likely be the only work that uses the zodiac as its basis of reference; other works will use other series of symbols and metaphors.
  - **Later stage, once everything is collected:** sort the lowest-level topics into general categories, then tag each topic with the symbols it can be associated with across several symbol systems (for example Scorpio in the zodiac, Saturn in the planetary symbols, Electromagnetism in the Robot Elementals, the Octahedron in the Platonic solids). Not now.
    - A low-level topic can be anything: for example hope, joy, faith, fear, anger, justice, duty, honor (author).
    - The raw material for this is each notes file's "Topics in this area" list.
    - Symbol systems already on file: Derivation Mechanisms `Data_Reference/Symbolism/` (`Planetary_Symbols`, `Zodiac`, `Robot-Elementals`). The Platonic solids research is in the GDD's `to-be-integrated/` (`platonic-solids-symbols.md`, `-mathematics.md`, `-chemistry.md`, `-forces-and-occurrence.md`).
  - **No South Asian works as sources.** The No Subcontinentals law covers research sources too.
  - **Body-part correspondences are out** of this project (which organs a sign rules, and the illnesses tied to them). They can't serve as meta-categories of understanding. The GDD's `Concordia_Body_Symbology.md` was only reference symbolism for telling the districts apart. The body as an area of life or behavior stays.
  - **The Hub (neutral 13th district):** the author is inclined to keep it out. There is a possible argument for tying it to **neutrality** as a phenomenon: not getting involved, not taking sides. That candidate is recorded in `03 - Beyond the twelve/Neutrality (candidate).md`.

## Gaps the notes found (for later sources)
- **The houses.** Every sign's house (its areas of life) is barely developed in the GDD documentation. Clare Martin's *Mapping the Psyche* Vol. 2 covers the houses and is the obvious next extraction.
- **Collective scale.** The astrology material describes individuals and pairs, almost never groups, institutions, law or economy. Mundane astrology is a lead (not yet checked); sources outside astrology matter more here.
- **The named concepts.** Justice and trust are well covered (Libra, Scorpio, Pisces, Gemini); honor, crime and duty are thin outside Capricorn and Leo.
- **Neutrality.** Sources outside astrology: the ethics of impartiality, bystander and conflict-avoidance psychology, mediation.
- **Contradictions inside the GDD files.** Each notes file lists them under Gaps (for example, constellation sizes and some correspondences).
