## Borderline
- `advantage`, `motivation`: listed among the tenth house's domains but never developed into an idea of human life.
- `consent`: only through the receptive mode's general failure of silence read as consent; folded into `silence`.
- `projection`: the general failure rule for every opposition, not specific to this sign.
- `creativity`, `empathy`, `selfishness`, `spontaneity`: named only in the Capricorn-Aquarius cusp list, with no Capricorn-specific angle.
- `inhibition`, `meanness`: from Snodgrass's blunt register, which he himself partly disowns as overgeneralized.
- `elegance`: understated elegance is a taste, arguably a style rather than a concept.
- `brevity`: the sign's writers are sparing with words; a prose style rather than an idea of life.
- `war`: the military exemplars imply soldiering, but the material never treats it as a topic.
- `doom`: the omega symbol of doom is astrological symbolism; `decay` carries the idea.
- `goodness`, `peace`: the goat's meanings in other cultures, correspondences rather than this area's concepts.
- `capture`: the labor of capturing rather than killing, a myth detail with no developed point.
- `luck`: appears only in a star name and lucky numbers.
- `magic`, `creation`: attributes of a mythic counterpart god, not claims about the area; the felt sense is under `enchantment`.
- `truth-seeking`: digging up what really happened is shared with Scorpio and belongs more to that sign.
- `art`, `craftsmanship`: one decan makes time for art and the earned comfort of craft; too thin to stand alone.
- `nagging`, `ridicule`, `misunderstanding`: small dislikes; ridicule and being misread are folded into `humiliation`.
