## Borderline

- **spirituality:** the first decan lives through adventure or spiritual exploration, a single phrase; otherwise religion appears only as something refused.
- **martyrdom:** implied by Joan of Arc, burned and still a symbol, but the notes never name it as the sign's concern.
- **pilgrimage:** Chaucer's pilgrimage is only timed by the Sun in Aries; a setting, not the sign's idea.
- **fertility:** one reading of the glyph, closer to a correspondence than a concept of the area.
- **harvest:** the spring festival seeks the sign's favor for the harvest; left under `renewal` and `ritual`.
- **jealousy, brooding, silent treatment:** how the Aries-Cancer pairing sours; arguably Cancer's register more than Aries' own (jealousy is also Leo's failure, not Aries').
- **humor:** blunt humor shared with Sagittarius, a single mention.
- **worry:** water's worry about the future, which fire disrespects; the concept belongs to the other side of the pairing.
- **argument:** fire leaves rather than argues when air kills its idea with a fact; one mention.
- **balance, projection:** mechanics of the Aries-Libra axis, carried mostly by the Libra end.
- **goodwill:** Sagittarius' wish for everyone's good fortune, offered as what widens Aries' aim; not the sign's own trait.
- **tradition:** named only as absent (no inherited wisdom to consult); kept under `optimism`.
- **legacy:** Capricorn's kind of ambition, named only for contrast.
- **money:** appears only through generosity and entitlement, so folded into those.
- **composure:** the adult raising the Aries child must keep calm; a parenting note, not the sign's trait.
- **intellect:** the third decan is called intellectual, one word in a weak source.
- **excess:** the GDD's rule that vices are virtues pushed too far is a pattern, not a concept.
- **cowardice, first impression, volunteering:** real ideas, but the notes never treat them apart from `caution`, `appearance` and `initiative`, so they were merged there.
- **grief, poverty, endurance, mourning, slow loss, self-image, self-presentation:** named only in §12 as gaps, so not shown to belong to the sign.
