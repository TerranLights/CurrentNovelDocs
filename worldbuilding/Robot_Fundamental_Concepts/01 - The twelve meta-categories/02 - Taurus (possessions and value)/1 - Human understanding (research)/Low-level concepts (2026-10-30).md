## Borderline

- **integration:** one of Snodgrass's key words for the sign, given without any explanation of what it means here.
- **germination:** Rudhyar's plant-cycle stage for the sign; a phase of growth rather than an idea of human life.
- **talent:** Venus's faculty is said to cover an individual's talents, but the notes never develop it beyond craft.
- **communication:** the second decan is very communicative; one line in a weak source, kept under `discretion`.
- **accommodation:** Taurus accommodates Leo until it suddenly stops; a single passage, folded into `stubbornness` and `power struggle`.
- **drama:** a dislike of drama shared with Virgo; one mention.
- **grounding:** it keeps Pisces grounded; arguably `stability` seen from outside.
- **imagination / inner life:** what Pisces gives Taurus because it cannot produce them itself; named as absent.
- **tidiness:** a tidy workspace, one detail, kept under `method`.
- **shopping:** Tierney's joke about claiming a thing the moment it goes on sale; a gag rather than a stated concern.
- **perseverance, devotion, overeating, hedonism, constancy, intrusion:** real ideas, but the notes give them only alongside `determination`, `loyalty`, `appetite`, `self-indulgence`, `nourishment` and `territory`, so they were merged there.
- **self-reliance:** used here for what the notes call self-sufficiency, so that it matches the Aries file's name for the same idea.
- **debt, trade, earning a living, economy, poverty:** named only in §12 as missing from the material.
