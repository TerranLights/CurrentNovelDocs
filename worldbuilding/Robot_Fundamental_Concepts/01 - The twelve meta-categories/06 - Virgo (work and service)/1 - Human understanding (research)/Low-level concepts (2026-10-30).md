## Borderline
- `wisdom`: one decan is linked to a herdsman figure for using wisdom, and one reading of the glyph weaves feeling together with wisdom. Too thin to stand alone.
- `pet keeping`: small domestic pets and a housecat are assigned to the sign. These are correspondences rather than a human idea, though keeping pets is a practice.
- `death at work`: one star is named for a grape-gatherer killed at his labor, and §10 lists it as a topic. It rests on that single star name.
- `virginity`: the sources insist the maiden means self-contained, not the biological term. Filed as `self-possession`.
- `fertility`: one source names the Babylonian figure as a goddess of harvest and fertility. Covered by `harvest`.
- `theory`: earth has no patience for endless discussion of theory. This is an element trait mentioned once.
- `relevance`: it rates information by whether it changes an outcome. Folded into `analysis` and `discernment`.
- `merging and separating`: one of the axis's themes, named in a list and never developed.
- `escape`: the longing to escape duty and drudgery. Folded into `drudgery`.
- `fashion`: the sources split between good with fashion and unfashionable. Too contested and thin.
- `music`: one form is musical, named in passing.
- `youthfulness`: mutable lightness reads as youth. This describes the modality more than the area.
- `evil`: one decan is linked to a hero who defeats evil, a single constellation link.
- `crime`: the notes say nothing here concerns crime. The nearest points are `violence`, `censorship`, `stinginess` and `manipulation`.
- `intelligentsia` and `business leadership`: Mercury's governance column names them as labels only.
