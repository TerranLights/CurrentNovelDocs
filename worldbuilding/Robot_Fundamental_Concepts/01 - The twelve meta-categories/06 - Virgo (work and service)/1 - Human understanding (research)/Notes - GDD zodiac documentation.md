## 1. Areas of life
Astrology, as the documentation reports it, gives Virgo and its natural house (the sixth) these domains:
- **The sixth house:** everyday activities such as errands and chores; colleagues; routines; health; the people one deals with daily (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §12; ⚠️ book not named). Martin links six, Virgo's number and house, to bees and their six-sided honeycomb, an image of industrious, productive work (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §12, from Martin).
- **Service and work.** Snodgrass's key phrase for Virgo is *I serve* (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, from Snodgrass). The Full Attributes file calls it the zodiac's craftsperson: precision in the service of usefulness, happiest improving something real (Full Attributes, Virgo).
- **Analysis and discernment.** Silva's key phrase is *I analyze*; Tierney renames the sign the Analyzer; the mode is assimilation (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, from Silva, Tierney and Snodgrass). Silva's decan keywords are *analyze*, *efficient* and *discriminate* (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §2, from Silva).
- **Record keeping and writing.** Martin's Mercury in Virgo is the scribe, Thoth, record keeper of the gods (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, from Martin).
- **Medicine, healing and health.** Thoth taught people writing and medicine; Virgo's glyph shares an old medical symbol with Scorpio's (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1 and §9, from Martin). Health is a sixth-house matter (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §12).
- **The body's upkeep:** diet, hygiene, checkups (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §12, from Silva, Snodgrass and Martin).
- **Order, cleanliness and the home.** Snodgrass's topography assigns Virgo to *home* (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §12, from Snodgrass); Silva describes Virgo's need for a neat, decluttered home (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Silva).
- **Craft and skilled, meticulous work** (Full Attributes, Virgo; Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass).
- **Refinement and purification.** Martin's Virgo is also the alchemist, purifying and perfecting base material rather than making new things (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, from Martin).
- **Prevention and regulation** (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1–2, from Tierney).
- **Harvest and nourishment** (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass and Silva).
- **Self-possession.** The maiden as one who belongs to no one (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10, from Snodgrass, Martin and Tierney).
- **Employment relations:** the Virgo employee, boss and colleague (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, §6, §7, from Silva's professional chapter).
- **Small domestic animals.** Silva gives small, domesticated pets; Snodgrass's image is a housecat (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §12).
- **Mercury's governance column:** intelligentsia and business leaders (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Appendix IV, from Snodgrass).

## 2. Core meaning
- **Archetype: the Maiden,** whom Tierney prefers to call the Analyzer (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, from Tierney; Full Attributes, Virgo). The substrate's summary of the archetype: service performed as an act of judgment, the tension between *I serve* and *I analyze* (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1).
- **Driving motive: preventing failure at the smallest scale.** Tierney: Virgo knows that a big, complex machine can grind to a halt if nobody notices one loose screw, so it looks for the small things that could go wrong well before a crisis (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, from Tierney). The substrate reads the motive as dread of preventable failure, not a love of tidiness.
- **Never finished improving.** Silva closes his Virgo part on the sign's motto: "My best can surely be better" (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, from Silva).
- **Scribe, not messenger.** Mercury rules both Gemini and Virgo, but Martin says Gemini's Mercury is the messenger of the gods while Virgo's is the scribe, Thoth, who taught writing and medicine. She describes a ritual pleasure in record keeping: neat columns of figures, making order out of chaos, sorting wheat from chaff (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, from Martin).
- **The alchemist.** Purify, refine and perfect what already exists (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, from Martin).
- **A reaction to Leo.** Tierney: much of Virgo reads as a reaction against Leo's self-promotion; Virgo sees Leo selling parts of its soul for fame and chooses to go unseen (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1; `05_Leo.md` §8, from Tierney). His joke: after five signs of pure self-expression, the cosmos hired Virgo as an outside efficiency expert to review productivity (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, from Tierney).
- **Core needs** (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §5):
  - **To be useful, and to see that the usefulness registered.** Silva: Virgo likes appreciation shown, and wants the people it helps to show it is not wasting its time; Virgo Moons are itching to be useful (from Silva).
  - **To be signed off.** Silva: at work Virgo often will not rest until feedback says it did well (from Silva).
  - **Autonomy within the job.** Silva: a decent team player who prefers its own space to do what it does best, and wishes someone would simply grant that autonomy (from Silva).
  - **Order as a requirement.** Silva: Virgo is not stubborn about order for nothing; it truly needs order to function and feels off without it (from Silva).
  - **Control over who enters its home and for how long,** since home is where it allows itself to be vulnerable (from Silva).
  - **Rules that keep a profession's reputation clean** (from Silva).
  - **To mean something,** to know its existence contributes to bettering the whole (⚠️ book not named). Martin's client Penny took six months off and was miserable; a Virgo without useful practical work is an unhappy Virgo (from Martin).
  - **Not to be owned** by anybody or anything (from Tierney).

## 3. The area at its best
The Full Attributes positives: analytical skill and precision; diligence and follow-through few can match; practicality grounded in what helps; a sincere, often self-effacing wish to be useful; reliability built on attention to detail; modesty that needs no credit to keep working (Full Attributes, Virgo; Quick Reference, Virgo).
- **Prevention.** Why wait for things to spin out of control? Virgo takes preventive measures to avoid bigger trouble later (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2; ⚠️ book not named, probably Tierney).
- **Regulation (the dimmer switch).** Tierney's image: life cannot run at full brightness all the time, so Virgo builds a dimmer to tone things down and save energy, regulating the body's systems so none wears out from overstimulation (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2; `A_Elements.md` §3, from Tierney).
- **Self-reliance.** Virgo would rather fix things itself than depend on hired help, distrusts others' claimed expertise, and teaches itself what it needs (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2; ⚠️ book not named, probably Tierney). Silva: one of the few employees who thrive with or without supervision (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, from Silva).
- **Composure in crisis.** Silva: in confusion, panic or a heated argument, the Virgo is the most poised person in the room, and the friend who produces a contingency plan just when it is needed (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, from Silva).
- **Mediation and advice.** Not keen on leading but able to light the path; an excellent mediator; the person friends and family consult before big decisions, because it is good at finding weak links in their plans (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, from Silva; `B_Modalities.md` §4, from Silva).
- **Criticism that spares no rank, including its own.** Silva: Virgo will point out flaws in a system even when it caused them, owns its mistakes, and does not exempt the boss, because it values perfection over the sanctity of titles (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, from Silva).
- **Discretion about people.** Silva on the Virgo boss: criticism is usually given in private to protect the person's dignity and standing at work (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, from Silva).
- **Practical, active care.** Silva: a Virgo friend offers a shoulder to cry on and then helps you back on your feet, reminding you why you are capable, even if it has to drag you out of your slump (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, from Silva).
- **Trusted with responsibility.** People load Virgo with responsibility and trust it to handle whatever comes; Silva's Scorpio-side view: the Virgo is the friend for a reality check or an unvarnished truth (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §4, from Silva).
- **Reliability as a friend.** Silva: promise to help at an event and Virgo arrives half an hour early with everything that might be needed (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §9, from Silva).
- **Earth's general gifts:** perseverance, endurance, realism, delivery, turning intention into an object, and care for the body's needs for food, rest and exercise. Tierney calls earth the custodial element that straightens up the world when it gets out of control (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §3, from Tierney and Martin).
- **Mutable gifts:** noticing before anyone else that an adjustment is needed, going around obstacles, mediating between forces that will not move (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §4, from Tierney and Martin).
- **Hidden warmth.** Silva: people wrongly assume Virgo is cold, not knowing how deeply it feels; under a distant, critical first impression is a surprising modesty and a quiet charm (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §4, from Silva).
- **The integrated form.** Silva's Virgo Rising chapter, Mercury in Pisces: a poetic imagination that balances technique and attention to detail, the technician and the artist at once. The substrate calls it the corpus's nearest description of a healed Virgo (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §11, from Silva).

## 4. The area gone wrong
The GDD calls Virgo's shadow material unusually harsh and specific (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3). The Full Attributes negatives: perfectionism that makes good enough feel like failure; over-criticism turned outward and just as often inward; anxiety and worry spiraling over details nobody else noticed; nitpicking; rigidity about how things should be done; real difficulty relaxing or accepting imperfection (Full Attributes, Virgo). Its pattern note gives Virgo as the example of a strength pushed too far: precision becomes paralysis (Full Attributes, Worth Your Attention; Quick Reference, pattern note).

**Judging others.**
- **An incompetent world.** Tierney: Virgo assumes it is dealing with an incompetent world, does not trust that others know what they are doing, and tests that assumption with a list of questions (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Tierney). Silva: people are taken aback by Virgo's thousand questions, and Virgo knows it (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **The censor.** Tierney casts Virgo as a censor with a watchdog attitude, trying to suppress what it finds objectionable, which can be a great deal (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Tierney). Silva's Virgo Moon disapproves by default (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Criticizing without being criticized.** Silva: Virgo avoids situations where others could criticize it, unless it is the rare kind that can take criticism. At worst the Virgo boss is a fussy micromanager who judges without sympathy, points out every flaw and becomes insatiable (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Criticism that provokes violence.** Silva lists it as a plain challenge: Virgo cannot help interfering, criticizing and seeming unemotional, and this can make others angry and even violent (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Contempt for the frail.** Snodgrass: robust health reduces Virgo's sympathy for invalids, so the Virgo nurse or physical therapist tends to be tight-lipped and disapproving of frail patients and malingerers (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Snodgrass).
- **Contamination anxiety.** Tierney: uneasy around people who cough or sneeze (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Tierney). Silva: hiding a mess is no help; Virgo knows it is there (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Contempt for the unmeasurable.** Silva: Virgo lives by facts, looks down on psychics and crystal balls, and dislikes judgments based on emotion (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8, from Silva).
- **Condescension.** Tierney: Virgo believes the other signs have mishandled life, though it thinks Gemini at least could be retrained (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8, from Tierney).

**Judging itself.**
- **Perfectionism that ruins the work.** Silva: Virgo will not allow itself to fall short of the perfect image in its head, and will ruin a whole project because it was not done perfectly; overthinking can make it see problems that are not there (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Standards nobody set.** Silva's Virgo girl exhausts herself meeting other people's standards while neglecting her own joy, and some of those standards are only her assumptions about what is expected, not what anyone asked (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Self-blame that does not fade.** Silva: Virgo looks back on a problem from years ago, cringes, and blames itself (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Never finished.** Martin: until we can see the whole picture, everything we achieve feels spoiled or imperfect, and we end up consumed by duty and drudgery and longing to escape (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Martin).
- **Discipline that suffocates.** Silva's Virgo Moon lives in total discipline, like someone who keeps the top shirt button done up even when it is uncomfortable because it looks right (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Self-sacrifice into depression.** From Silva's Virgo–Capricorn material: Virgo can make too many sacrifices and become depressed (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Inferiority.** Silva on Virgo children: they may become so isolated that they feel unlike others, which can lead to an inferiority complex (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).

**Anger, grudges and denial.**
- **Peeved, never angry.** Virgo types claim they are never really angry, only peeved. A mistake can be forgiven; the same mistake made again and again cannot (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3; ⚠️ book not named, probably Tierney).
- **The list.** Silva's *Sun and Moon Signs*: people know not to cross Virgo or they go on its list of people who have wronged it. The *Ultimate Guide*: Virgo may accept the advice to forgive and forget, but takes a red pen to the forgetting, especially if hurt repeatedly (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva). The substrate links this to the scribe: grudges kept in writing.
- **Denial.** Silva: Virgo avoids its true emotions and prefers denial, insisting it is not angry while the feelings stay hidden inside (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Understanding turned into manipulation.** Silva: in a badly raised Virgo, the strong wish to understand things can turn into an ugly need to manipulate (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).

**Control and compulsion.**
- **The polarization trap.** Martin: if the Virgo/Pisces axis tips to the Virgo end, the person needs strong control of daily routine to keep chaos away; but if Pisces qualities are not integrated, they surface from the unconscious, and the person feels more and more overwhelmed, draws in more disruption and loses control (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Martin). The GDD ranks this as the most important shadow mechanism in its Virgo file.
- **Compulsion.** Silva: cleaning the same place twice a day; a Virgo Moon whose mind cannot rest, as if an inner narrator never stops talking (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Truth over friendship.** Silva: Virgo will not mind losing friends along the way if it stays committed to the truth (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Blowing up small things.** Calling a small snake an anaconda; turning molehills into mountains by overcomplicating them (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Closed to advice, good or bad.** Silva: once Virgo has pictured what it wants, it shuts its ears, even to good opinions, and falls back on what it knows works rather than trying something new (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Workaholism and neglect of the self.** Silva: most Virgos are workaholics, believing in service first and leisure later, and few will take a break unprompted (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva; ⚠️ Silva states a figure of five in seven with no source). Martin's Uranus–Pluto-in-Virgo generation is obsessed with health yet starves or abuses the body despite shelves of diet books and cupboards of supplements (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Martin; ⚠️ a generational placement).
- **Two Virgos.** Emerson: a power struggle in which each picks apart what the other does and neither can loosen up. Silva: two Virgos feed each other's insecurities instead of encouraging each other, and both play it too safe (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Emerson and Silva).

**Snodgrass's hostile traditional portrait** (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Snodgrass). The GDD treats this as a hostile outside view, not its own verdict: shrewdness that looks out for itself first; holding back its own money while spending a partner's; marriages that founder on rigid demands; and the most rigid types spending old age like hermits, celibate in a bare room with only a housecat. Snodgrass even opens his book with a mocking rhyme about Virgo loving its own disease.

**Structural shadows.**
- **Jupiter in detriment:** the big picture, faith and expansion are weakest here (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10). Silva's Virgo Rising with Mercury in Virgo shows it: chasing the tiniest detail, lost in facts, unable to see the overall meaning or direction (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §11, from Silva).
- **Venus in fall:** pleasure and beauty for their own sake are at their lowest (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10, from Silva).
- **Earth's failures:** calcifying into cynicism and a sense that the world has let it down (Martin); settling for a secure life without happiness or appreciation (Silva); keeping chaos at bay by making and enforcing rules, custodianship one step from policing (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §3, from Martin and Silva; ⚠️ the rule-enforcement line names no book).
- **Mutable failures:** scattering energy, wavering, and believing one has a right to change one's mind whatever was promised (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §4, from Tierney).
- **Snodgrass's pathology table:** psychological symptoms hypercriticism, self-absorption and hypochondria (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Virgo, Appendix V).

## 5. Behavior under pressure and over time
**At work.**
- Will not pass off anything incomplete as finished; gets everyone to slow down and go over the job with a magnifying glass; Emerson adds triple-checking (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Silva and Emerson).
- Values consistency and a defined purpose over variety (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6). The substrate's reading: the mutable streak wants new problems inside a fixed routine, not a changing routine.
- Silva's working day: arrives half an hour before most; works slowly because of the care it takes; criticizes upward regardless of rank; gives negative findings in private; does not stop. It admits to being a little shy, which it can overcome by speaking up (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Silva).
- Silva's advice for newcomers working under Virgos: hold back criticism for about three months, until you understand the place and its power dynamics (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §7, from Silva).
- Silva's code for working for a Virgo boss: bring facts and figures, since every word is weighed; follow procedure; back each suggestion with evidence; keep yourself and your workspace clean; dress plainly; keep sentiment out of the work unless the work needs it; do not gossip; own any damage at once and fix it (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §7, from Silva).
- Silva contradicts himself on teamwork: one chapter calls the mutable Virgo the best team player you could have; the child chapter names teamwork as its blind spot; the professional chapter says it is a decent team player who prefers its own space (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §15, from Silva). The substrate's guess, flagged as inference: Virgo works well in a defined role and badly under shared authorship.
- Concentration is not to be interrupted. Silva: if your Virgo friend is deeply focused, that is exactly the time not to bother them with something trivial (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §7, from Silva).

**At home.**
- Silva: nothing matters more than a neat, tidy home; Virgo declutters often, to the puzzlement of friends who see nothing to clear. Space and light come first; the style is minimal but not bare, with beautiful things that are also useful (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Silva).
- The public and private selves differ. Silva: out in the world Virgo may seem too straitlaced, but at home it lets loose and might break into a silly song and dance over a good pizza (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §4, from Silva).
- Two accounts of Virgo's private mess. Martin's Virgo-rising sketch drives a small white car slowly and by the rules, with chaos in the trunk and glove box. Silva says even a Virgo's mess has an order only the Virgo understands, so leave it alone (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §4 and §15, from Martin and Silva).

**Socially.**
- Watches: the one at the party with a drink, enjoying watching others dance (Silva). Martin: would often rather wash the glasses than join the noise (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Silva and Martin; `A_Elements.md` §6, from Martin).
- Avoids small talk; opens up briefly with a drink; always leaves before the party ends (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Silva).
- Silva: Virgos wish friends would stop dragging them to random parties, yet they are superb at planning parties and events (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Silva).
- Blends in by choice. Tierney's advice to the sign: do not try to stand out or lead; blend in, disappear at times, and nobody bothers you (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §4, from Tierney). Martin: it can spot an error, or a chip in a cup, at fifty yards. Silva's Virgo Rising notices external and internal stimuli faster than any other rising sign (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §4, from Martin and Silva).
- Snodgrass's outward portrait: neat, unassuming, unfashionable, buying clothes to last a lifetime; a grave, sober expression; smiles that look put on for form's sake; a cold, brief handshake; a shrill voice but witty, well-chosen words (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §4, from Snodgrass).

**Put on the spot.**
- Silva: putting a Virgo on the spot is a sure way to anger or embarrass it. The mutable Virgo will cope, then tell you afterward it was not okay. Yelling only makes it close up for good (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Silva).

**Under stress.**
- Martin's bus-stop scene: a Virgo stuck waiting feels its blood pressure rise, takes stress pills from one pocket of the organizer and digestion pills from another, and phones the office to rearrange the day while keeping the panic out of its voice (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Martin). The panic is real; hiding it is the reflex.
- Silva: worry makes Virgo tense, tension makes it ill, and illness confirms the worry (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Silva).

**Health habits.**
- Hypochondria is named repeatedly as Virgo's typical false alarm; Silva also notes the opposite habit of turning up for checkups nobody scheduled. Snodgrass's eating note: picky and dyspeptic (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §12; `G_Correspondences.md`, Virgo, Appendix V).
- As a mutable sign it avoids conflict, except as mediator or go-between, and under stress tends to slip away or take the go-between role (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §4, from Martin).

**In crisis and after loss.**
- Calmest in the room during the crisis (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, from Silva).
- Afterward, work as an anesthetic. Snodgrass: the literary Virgo echoes Orpheus's frenzy for work while recovering from loss or failed love; on the rebound it seeks numbing work, often at the cost of its health (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §6, from Snodgrass).

**In childhood (Silva on raising Virgo children).** (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §7, from Silva.)
- Children of this sign volunteer for chores, especially when praised, and the habit lasts into adulthood if they were encouraged when young.
- They never jump into a strange situation; they move ahead only with a familiar adult's guidance, checking constantly for approval. The adult's job is to supply praise, confidence and explanations.
- Do not point out their mistakes: they are self-critical and see their faults first, and having them pointed out only demoralizes them.
- Correct by asking (what if you tried it this way?) rather than telling.
- They need intense concentration and may throw a fit if someone barges into their space with suggestions.
- Do not suggest their need for order is wrong; encourage it, and teach separately that others are less orderly and that their messes can be tolerated within reason.
- Show them as much of the world as possible through books, travel and documentaries, to use the sign's adaptability.
- Teamwork is a blind spot that may go unnoticed until team sports; start them in groups built around their interests.
- A Virgo child drifts away from parents who it feels misunderstand it.
- The two failures of a bad upbringing are the inferiority complex and understanding turned into manipulation (section 4).
- Eating: never force-feed a Virgo child, since they tend to eat exactly what they need (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §12, from Silva).

**Over time and in maturing.**
- Martin's correctives are the planets in detriment and fall: Jupiter lets Virgo keep the whole wood in view as well as the trees; Venus gives permission to live simply and enjoy small details and rituals for their own sake without anxiety (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10, from Martin). She says the missing planets balance an otherwise all-work-and-no-play approach (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §6, from Martin).
- On the axis, the task is to integrate Pisces rather than tighten control (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Martin).
- Silva on handling criticism: a rare kind of Virgo can take criticism and admit it does not always get things right (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- Snodgrass's old age: the most rigid live like hermits with a housecat; Virgos also get on well with the elderly (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3 and §13, from Snodgrass).
- Mutable signs keep a lightness that reads as youth; they are light on their feet rather than plodding (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §4, from Tierney).
- Earth signs need to watch their workaholic streak, since overwork drains the body (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10; ⚠️ book not named, probably Tierney).

## 6. Myth, history and cultural associations
- **Maiden, not virgin.** Snodgrass: *Virgo* is Latin for maiden or unmarried woman, often mistranslated as the biological term. Martin: the constellation is the original great goddess before she split into the many faces of the feminine, and *virgin* here means self-contained, needing no one else to be complete. Tierney: the label means staying true to one's essence and keeping honor and virtue intact rather than being corrupted by others' baser energies; remaining self-possessed; not wanting to be owned by anyone or anything (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10, from Snodgrass, Martin and Tierney). What it says about the area: service rendered by someone who still belongs to herself.
- **The figure with empty hands.** Snodgrass: Virgo is the only zodiac sign shown entirely as a plain, unadorned human figure; Aquarius at least pours from a jar, but Virgo "has no assigned task." Ptolemy later put a sheaf of wheat in her hand, and Silva also gives her the sheaf (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10 and §15, from Snodgrass and Silva). The sources disagree on whether the worker holds anything.
- **Ishtar's descent.** Snodgrass: the Babylonians and Assyrians saw Virgo as Ishtar, a love goddess who, like Isis, went through the seven gates of the underworld to seek her husband Tammuz. While she was below, the earth lay in thick night; when she came back with him, light and warmth returned (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass). Silva's *Sun and Moon Signs* instead names the Babylonian figure as Shala, goddess of harvest and fertility (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §15, from Silva). What the myth says about the area: unseen labor below that the world above depends on.
- **Astraea and the departure of justice.** Snodgrass ties Virgo to Astraea, goddess of justice, and the myth of the ages of man: after the golden and silver ages ended, she took justice away from the earth and left humanity to its violence. The poet Aratus makes her Dike, daughter of Zeus and Themis. As Astraea or Fortuna she is "the decisive judge who doles out penalties and rewards without regard to prestige, wealth, or power" (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass). What it says about the area: justice as impartial judgment, and withdrawal as the answer to a corrupted world.
- **Harvest.** Demeter and the Roman Ceres, goddesses of grain; Ptolemy's sheaf of wheat; Spica, the alpha star, also called Arista, the grain harvest (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass). Silva: Virgo plants its skills and knowledge and harvests the results, a nourisher (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Silva). Other names: Bethulah (Hebrew), Adarah (Arabic), Parthenos (Greek).
- **A replaced identity.** In the Babylonian list of 419 B.C. this slot was Spica, later changed to Virgo (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Appendix I, from Snodgrass).
- **The sky's largest and faintest.** Snodgrass's data ranks Virgo the dimmest of the twelve and by far the largest in area (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Appendix II). The Virgo file calls it the second-largest constellation (in the whole sky, ⚠️ presumably), made of a cluster of hundreds of galaxies, with eleven Messier objects and a region called the Realm of the Nebulae, home of the Sombrero Galaxy. Snodgrass: at its center is the strongest radio signal in the sky, thought to come from gas streaming from a huge black hole, and near gamma Virginis is the brightest quasar, an object that lent support to the Big Bang (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass). The substrate's reading: the constellation of small, unseen labor holds the sky's loudest signal.
- **Ampelus's star.** Vindemiatrix was once Vindemiator, named for the grape-gatherer Ampelus, whom Dionysus set among the stars after the boy fell and broke his neck (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass). A star named for a laborer killed at work. The other named stars mark parts of the figure: Kaphir (left forearm), Khambalia (left foot), Zaniah (southern wing), Porrima (a double star), and Zavijah at the base of the head, which helped confirm one of Einstein's theories during the solar eclipse of September 21, 1922 (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass).
- **The sphinx.** Snodgrass: the Egyptian zodiac at Denderah began at Virgo and ended at Leo, and the union of maiden and lion produced the sphinx, a word meaning closely united. In the medieval sequel the maiden easily calms the lion, overpowering it by the strength of her virtue: purity civilizing brutality (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass).
- **Heraldry and trademarks.** Bodies stressing fair trade, justice and impartiality show the maiden holding scales, combining Virgo with Libra; banks and finance houses wishing to look even-handed and trustworthy put her, scales in hand, atop a shield. The maiden also decorates perfume and jewelry and the trademarks of face soap, hand cream and shampoo (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass).
- **Traditional occupations.** Snodgrass: useful work in libraries, newsrooms, print shops, garages, surgical wards and pharmacies; fine artisans who put up with meticulous chores (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass). Silva's modern list: accountant, bookkeeper, auditor, coder, statistician; nutritionist, dietitian, massage therapist, acupuncturist, holistic healer, yoga instructor; housekeeper, home stager, professional organizer, personal or executive assistant; writer; and service fields such as charity, health, banking and finance, entertainment (writing, directing, continuity), education and hospitality (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Silva). The substrate groups these as record trades, body trades and order trades.
- **Monastic service.** Martin v3: in the Age of Pisces, Pisces is balanced by its opposite Virgo, which describes monastic life as spiritual devotion (Pisces) plus practical service (Virgo), and the parable of the loaves (Virgo) and fishes (Pisces) (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Martin). Christian iconography gave Virgo wings, merging her with Mercury, and later remade her as the Virgin Mary with the Christ Child (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13).
- **The equinox.** The fall equinox falls in Virgo (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §12).

## 7. Structure
**Element: earth.** Earth checks reality through the five senses and builds what lasts. Martin: earth's truth is objective yet personal, coming from the senses, so if earth dislikes the smell, taste or color of something, that settles it (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §1, from Martin). Tierney calls earth the custodial element, practical know-how about what can be done with the resources at hand (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §3, from Tierney). Strengths: perseverance, endurance, realism, delivery, care of the body. Blind spot: taking the tangible layer for the only real one, and no patience for endless discussion of theory. Shadow: cynicism, settling for security over happiness, and rule-making that edges into policing. Needs: unwelcome disruption from air, and permission from water to feel (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §3, from Tierney, Martin and Silva).
- Within earth, Virgo is earth made workable: Tierney's images are potter's clay, solid but malleable (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §3) and sand, which shifts and reshapes under pressure rather than resisting (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10, from Tierney). Its job is to regulate (the dimmer switch, section 3).
- Earth's pairings that touch Virgo (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §6; `06_Virgo.md` §10): with water it binds deeply and easily (Silva), though water can open emotions earth is not ready to handle; with air it shares a dry, delicate, drama-averse humor but seems rigid and slow to air, and the conflicts become procedural; with fire, earth is the party pooper that meters fire's energy, and Martin pictures the Virgos washing up while the fire signs hold the floor; two earth signs move at one pace and form a closed, shared reality.

**Modality: mutable.** Mutable signs close a season and break down what has hardened so it can be renewed; they absorb change and pass it on altered (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §1 and §4, from Tierney). They notice first that an adjustment is needed, go around obstacles, and mediate between the forces that start and the forces that hold (Martin). Emerson calls them the finishers (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §4). Failures: scattering, wavering and reneging (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §4, from Tierney). Silva on mutable earth: takes the path of least resistance, is not keen on leading but can light the way (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §4; `06_Virgo.md` §10, from Silva). Silva's image is a chameleon adapting to its setting (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10, from Silva). Tierney counts Virgo among the two mutable signs that run on facts and figures that can be measured and tested (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §4, from Tierney). ⚠️ The sources disagree on whether mutable or fixed signs are the finishers (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §8).

**Polarity: receptive (also called negative, yin, or traditionally feminine).** Inward-turned and responsive; Silva: oriented inward, with deeper insight into its own inner world (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10, from Silva). Receptive is not passive or weak (Substrate `C_Polarity.md` §3, from Silva and Martin).

**Opposite sign: Pisces. The axis: order and chaos, service and devotion.**
- Martin's list of the axis's themes: order and chaos, control and trust, form and content, the practical and the imaginal, realities and dreams, service and devotion, sickness and health, accepting and judging, merging and separating (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8, from Martin).
- She frames it with the grail legend: Pisces asks whom the grail serves, and Virgo answers, the one who serves the grail (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8, from Martin).
- Silva's blunter version: Pisces is self-delusion and dreams, Virgo self-advancement and function (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8, from Silva).
- Tierney: Pisces is not selective about what it lets in; that lack of discrimination gets it into trouble but makes it the most tolerant sign, since discriminating would mean weeding something out (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8, from Tierney). Martin reads Virgo's function as deciding what to absorb and what to expel (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8, from Martin). The same function, set opposite: filtering against admitting everything.
- Attraction runs alongside the antagonism. Martin: people with Virgo rising often admire Piscean types, or the vulnerable and sick, whom they can care for practically. Silva: they are drawn to people who need help, or perhaps the reverse; earth and water bind deeply; the Virgo Moon is oddly drawn to Pisces despite disliking illogical minds; the main obstacle is Virgo's dislike of mess, and nothing is messier than emotion (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8, from Martin and Silva).
- Polarized, the axis produces Martin's trap: control at the Virgo end summons the disorder it fears (section 4).
- The two signs trade dignities: Mercury is exalted in Virgo and in fall in Pisces; Venus is exalted in Pisces and in fall in Virgo (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §5). ⚠️ Mercury's exaltation in Virgo and fall in Pisces are not stated anywhere in the corpus; the GDD derives them from the standard rules (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §7).

**Ruling planet: Mercury (domicile and, by derivation, exaltation).** As a faculty: analysis, record, transmission and verification; objectivity; truth as partial and multiple; and the humor of holding more than one frame at once (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §3, from Martin). In Virgo it is the scribe, not the messenger (section 2). Mercury rules one active sign (Gemini) and one receptive (Virgo), the same faculty in two postures (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §1, from Martin). Virgo is the only sign whose ruler is also its exalted planet, so one faculty is at maximum with no second (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §5).
- **Detriment: Jupiter.** Expansion, faith and the big picture are weakest here; Martin's corrective is that Jupiter keeps the wood in view as well as the trees (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10; `F_Rulerships.md` §3, from Martin).
- **Fall: Venus.** Pleasure and beauty for their own sake are at their lowest; Silva states it in his Virgo–Capricorn material. Martin's corrective: Venus permits living simply and enjoying small details and rituals for their own sake (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10, from Silva and Martin).
- Under the modern scheme Neptune (Pisces's modern ruler) is in detriment in Virgo, forced by the rules rather than stated in the corpus (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §2).
- Snodgrass's planet table gives Mercury the governance of intelligentsia and business leaders (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Appendix IV).

**Squares: Gemini and Sagittarius** (both mutable, so friction stays soft and nothing gets decided; Substrate `D_Aspect_Geometry.md` §2 and §4).
- **Gemini:** same ruler and modality, different element. Tierney: Gemini finds all information stimulating, suffers overload, and is noncommittal, all talk and options with no action; Virgo rates information by relevance and cuts the superfluous. Silva: Virgo is not like Gemini, whose attention flits from one unfinished task to the next. Tierney adds Virgo's condescension: Gemini, at least, could be reeducated. Emerson: too complicated to last, as Virgo's analysis overwhelms Gemini's free spirit. Silva's earth-and-air note softens it: they share a dry humor, delicate sensibilities and distaste for drama, while air finds Virgo rigid and over-measured (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8, from Tierney, Silva and Emerson). The area in conflict: information for its own sake against information that changes an outcome.
- **Sagittarius:** Jupiter, Sagittarius's ruler, is in detriment in Virgo. Tierney: Sagittarius lacks Virgo's discrimination and needs to attend to small but critical details. Emerson: they can barely be friends; Virgo cannot stand Sagittarius's irresponsible, ever-changing ways, and Sagittarius flees at the first criticism. Silva: to a Sagittarian child the Virgo parent is stifling; as friends they share a love of knowledge, but Virgo cannot see why Sagittarius will not settle down, while Sagittarius is fascinated by Virgo's analysis of everything. The truce: Virgo lets the archer stretch its experience, and the archer accepts Virgo's advice to get organized. Silva's diagnosis: Virgo's dislike of adventure contradicts its thirst for knowledge, so it will not go on the mountain hike but will encourage a friend who does (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8, from Tierney, Emerson and Silva). The area in conflict: detail and routine against expansion and freedom.

**Trines: Taurus and Capricorn** (same element; Substrate `D_Aspect_Geometry.md` §4).
- **Taurus:** Tierney: the Bull teaches Virgo that a job well done takes time; Virgo keeps Taurean comfort from sliding into indulgence and laziness. Silva: Taurus can anchor Virgo. Emerson: beware falling into a rut together (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §9, from Tierney, Silva and Emerson).
- **Capricorn:** both perfectionists, likely met through work, sharing respect for family traditions and routines (Silva). Tierney: Capricorn is the earth sign willing to take calculated risks, which Taurus and Virgo struggle to do. Silva: Virgo wants to move forward, Capricorn to move up, so they rarely agree on the destination; Capricorn goes to extremes for its goals and cannot understand Virgo's lack of competitive drive; Capricorn decides when a debate is over, while Virgo picks the next topic. Emerson: beware suffocating each other (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §9, from Silva, Tierney and Emerson).

**Sextiles: Cancer and Scorpio.**
- **Cancer:** Tierney: Virgo shares Cancer's urge to nourish life carefully, but Cancer's emotion gets in the way of hard practical decisions. Silva: Virgo gives Cancer a rest from nurturing everyone. Emerson: Virgo will not constantly criticize Cancer (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §9, from Tierney, Silva and Emerson).
- **Scorpio:** the corpus's strongest Virgo affinity. Martin: both glyphs are built from the same *m*, an ancient medical symbol; both signs are linked with medicine and healing, the closed loop of Virgo and the scorpion's sting being female and male versions of one sign. Tierney: Scorpio's perfectionism was borrowed from Virgo. Emerson: Virgo praises Scorpio's successes more than anyone's. Silva: Scorpio's intensity inspires the shy Virgo (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §9, from Martin, Tierney, Emerson and Silva).

**Quincunxes: Aries and Aquarius.** Martin, on Aries and Virgo: Aries wants to act, Virgo wants to get it right; they neither confront nor support each other and are linked awkwardly (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §9; `D_Aspect_Geometry.md` §3, from Martin). Emerson on Aquarius: workable with great effort, since Virgo wants to tell Aquarius what to do and limit its freedom; Silva's one point of contact is a shared interest in unorthodox healing (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §9, from Emerson and Silva).

**Semi-sextiles (neighbors): Leo and Libra.**
- **Leo:** Virgo as the sign that cleans up after Leo's party and reacts against its self-promotion (Tierney); Leo needs ego-stroking and Virgo is no stroker (Emerson); Silva instead sees a mature, balanced pair (Substrate `05_Leo.md` §8). The sphinx joins them (section 6).
- **Libra:** Silva: Virgo enjoys Libra's intelligence but cannot understand its avoidance of discomfort, since Virgo prefers to tackle problems head-on before they grow; Libra dislikes unasked-for advice and being scolded for impulsive spending. Mercury in Libra over a Virgo Ascendant gives tactful criticism and a reputation as arbitrator or mediator (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §9, from Silva).

## 8. Decans
Emerson gives all three; Silva adds keywords, constellations and fuller descriptions for Virgo, one of only four signs with a Silva decan treatment (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §2; `06_Virgo.md` §11). The scheme blends the sign with itself, then with the other two earth signs.
1. **Virgo / Mercury. Keyword: *analyze*.** Emerson: fussy, wants things done in order; talkative and prone to bragging; orderly at work but rather bohemian at home. Silva: warm and witty but admits few to its inner circle; its insights are taken as prophecy when they are only close observation; Silva links it to the constellation Crater, Apollo's cup, for emotional generosity (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §2–3, from Emerson and Silva). Sub-theme: observation and order.
2. **Capricorn / Saturn. Keyword: *efficient*.** Emerson: materialistic in the make-ends-meet sense rather than luxury; security comes first; good with organization and money; tactful, responsible, a great administrator; sometimes narrow-minded. Silva: seen as focused and persistent while really adaptable; plans ahead to avoid mistakes; the perfectionist who suffers when falling short of its own expectations; linked to Hercules, who defeats evil (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §2–3, from Emerson and Silva). Sub-theme: administration, money and security.
3. **Taurus / Venus. Keyword: *discriminate*.** Emerson: artistic with forms, colors and shapes; musical; quiet, reserved and steady; learns slowly; dislikes small talk; good with fashion. Silva: approval is its main motivator; a connoisseur of expression with a perfect use of color; known for diplomacy and sensitivity; unhappy living under limits; linked to Boötes, the herdsman, for using wisdom (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §2–3, from Emerson and Silva). Sub-theme: craft, beauty and the wish for approval.

Silva's two cusp zones (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §11, from Silva):
- **Leo side:** reliable, generous and gracious; creative and well dressed; witty and optimistic; often in charge of events for its legendary organizing; impatient with slower minds; at its best socially; warm and loyal in love.
- **Libra side:** perceptive and intelligent, in love with ideas; outgoing and able to reach people psychologically; most valuable socially at work; a lover of beauty, prone to collecting rare and beautiful things.

⚠️ The two sources give different decan dates (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §5).

## 11. Where the author's named concepts appear
- **Justice:** strongly present through myth. Astraea, goddess of justice, judges without regard to prestige, wealth or power, and withdraws justice from a corrupted earth (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass). The maiden with scales stands for fair trade, justice and impartiality in heraldry (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass). In behavior, Silva's Virgo criticizes the boss and itself alike, putting standards above titles (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, from Silva), and Martin's axis includes accepting against judging (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8).
- **Crime:** nothing on crime as such. The nearest points: Astraea leaving humanity to its violence once justice is gone (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13); Tierney's censor and watchdog suppressing what it finds objectionable (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3); the list of people who have wronged it and the refusal to forgive repeated mistakes (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva); Snodgrass's Virgo holding back its own money while spending a partner's (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3); and understanding turned to manipulation (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Trust:** Virgo distrusts others' competence and expertise and tests them with questions (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2–3, from Tierney). Others trust Virgo with heavy responsibility and for unvarnished truth (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §4, from Silva). Martin's axis pairs control with trust (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §8). Heraldry uses the maiden with scales to signal trustworthiness in finance (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13). Loyalty depends on feeling understood (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §7, from Silva).
- **Care:** care in a practical, corrective register: dragging a friend out of a slump, caring for the sick, giving Cancer a rest, the healing professions and the medical glyph (sections 3, 7, 6). Its shadow is Snodgrass's disapproving nurse (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3). Self-care is the weak spot: workaholism and neglect of the body (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3).
- **Honor:** Tierney's reading of the maiden as keeping honor and virtue intact and staying self-possessed (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10, from Tierney); owning one's mistakes (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, from Silva); criticizing in private to protect others' dignity and reputation (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §2, from Silva); and the maiden's virtue taming the lion (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Snodgrass).
- **Duty:** central. Service as the key phrase; the sixth house of chores and routine; Martin's warning of being consumed by duty and drudgery (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §1, §3, §12). Silva's service first, leisure later, and the responsibility others load onto Virgo (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §3–4). Duty as monastic practical service (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13, from Martin). Against duty-as-ownership stands Tierney's Virgo, who serves but will not be owned (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10), and Astraea, who withdraws when the world no longer deserves her service (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §13).

## 12. Gaps
- **The sixth house is thin.** One unattributed line lists its domains (errands, chores, colleagues, routines, health, daily contacts), plus Martin's number-six note. Employment as such, servants and employees, and small animals get no house-level treatment.
- **Silva has no dark-side or decan chapter for Virgo,** unlike some signs; the shadow material is spread across other chapters. Parts of his Virgo material are lost: friendship entries for Aries through Leo and for Aquarius and Pisces, the end of the career list, and all but the first paragraph of the sexual-compatibility chapter (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §15; `00_Method_and_Sources.md`).
- **Decans rest mainly on Emerson,** with Silva's added layers; the GDD notes Emerson's Virgo block is internally consistent but still single-source (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §11 and §15).
- **The Aquarius relationship is thin** (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §15).
- **Disagreements the GDD records and does not resolve** (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §15):
  - *Teamwork:* best team player, blind spot, or decent but solitary (all Silva).
  - *The private space:* Martin's chaos in the trunk against Silva's immaculate home and hidden order.
  - *The Babylonian figure:* Ishtar (Snodgrass) or Shala (Silva).
  - *What the figure holds:* nothing (Snodgrass) or a sheaf of wheat (Ptolemy, Silva).
  - *Leo as neighbor:* friction (Tierney, Emerson) or balance (Silva) (Substrate `05_Leo.md` §8).
  - *Colors and stones:* three color schemes; sapphire against jade, neither secure.
- **The GDD is inconsistent on the squares.** The Virgo file says no book treats Virgo–Gemini or Virgo–Sagittarius as squares and that both are constructed from compatibility entries and rulership logic (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §15), while the aspect file cites Silva stating that Virgo and Sagittarius are 90 degrees apart, a square (Substrate `D_Aspect_Geometry.md` §4).
- **Two images of mutable earth.** Potter's clay (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §3) and sand (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §10), both apparently Tierney.
- **Two derived dignities.** Mercury's exaltation in Virgo and fall in Pisces are not attested in the corpus (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §7).
- **No Persephone.** Demeter and Ceres appear once in passing; the descent myth supplied is Ishtar's (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §15).
- **Unverified figures and dated views.** Silva's five-in-seven workaholism figure has no source. Snodgrass's portrait is hostile in a register no other source shares, and a gendered aside in Silva's Moon chapter is left out by the GDD (Substrate `06_Virgo.md` §15).
- **Several points carry no book name** (the sixth-house line, prevention and self-reliance, peevishness, wanting to mean something, the earth workaholism line). Marked ⚠️ above.
- **Excluded by the GDD's own rules:** Silva's and Snodgrass's lists of Virgo countries and cities are left out of these notes, since the GDD bars sign-to-place mappings as national stereotyping (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Appendix III).
- **Full Attributes names no sources,** so its Virgo points cannot be traced further.
