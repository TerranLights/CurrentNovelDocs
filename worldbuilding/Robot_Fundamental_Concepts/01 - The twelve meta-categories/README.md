# The twelve meta-categories

One folder per meta-category, in the zodiac's natural order. **The labels are provisional working labels**, condensed from each sign's traditional areas of life (its natural house). The first research task in each folder is to establish the full range of meanings, and the labels change if that research says so.

| # | Sign | Provisional label | Traditional areas of life | Concordia district folder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aries | self and identity | the self, the body, appearance, beginnings, initiative | `05_Aries` |
| 2 | Taurus | possessions and value | possessions, money, resources, values, self-worth | `02_Taurus` |
| 3 | Gemini | communication and learning | communication, siblings and neighbors, short journeys, early learning | `09_Gemini` |
| 4 | Cancer | home and family | home, family, parents, roots, nurture | `01_Cancer` |
| 5 | Leo | creativity and self-expression | creativity, play, pleasure, romance, children | `03_Leo` |
| 6 | Virgo | work and service | work, service, daily routine, health | `12_Virgo` |
| 7 | Libra | partnership and justice | partnership, marriage, contracts, open enemies, balance, justice | `08_Libra` |
| 8 | Scorpio | death and transformation | death and rebirth, sex, shared resources, inheritance, secrets | `04_Scorpio` |
| 9 | Sagittarius | belief and philosophy | religion, philosophy, law, higher learning, long journeys | `11_Sagittarius` |
| 10 | Capricorn | authority and duty | career, status, reputation, authority, responsibility | `06_Capricorn` |
| 11 | Aquarius | community and ideals | friends, groups, hopes, humanitarian ideals | `07_Aquarius` |
| 12 | Pisces | the hidden and transcendence | the unconscious, confinement and institutions, sacrifice, compassion, transcendence | `10_Pisces` |

The district folders are in `InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City/Districts/District_Megasheets/`, where the districts are numbered in their own order. The thirteenth district, the Hub (`13_Hub`), has no sign and stays outside the twelve; its possible tie to neutrality is a candidate in `../03 - Beyond the twelve/`.

**Each folder holds:**
- `README.md`: the meta-category, its status and its starting points;
- `1 - Human understanding (research)/`;
- `2 - Robot understanding (development)/`.
