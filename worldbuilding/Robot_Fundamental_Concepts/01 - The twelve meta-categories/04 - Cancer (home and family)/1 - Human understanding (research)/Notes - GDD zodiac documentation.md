## 1. Areas of life
Astrology, as the documentation reports it, gives Cancer and its natural house (the fourth) these domains:
- **Home and family,** with family broadly defined. The Full Attributes file calls Cancer the zodiac's emotional foundation: home, family, and the instinct to protect the vulnerable (Full Attributes, Cancer).
- **Belonging.** Martin uses the single word *belonging* as the fourth house's title (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §5, from Martin). Her fourth-house language covers the quality of the nurturing a person received and how safe they feel about belonging (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §3, from Martin).
- **Roots.** Martin, citing Rudhyar's cycle of phases, places Cancer at the phase of putting down roots, with *deciding* as the phase's keyword (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10 and §15, from Martin v3). Martin v3 also gives the waxing square a Cancer theme: putting down roots to support further personal growth (Substrate `D_Aspect_Geometry.md` §8, from Martin).
- **Nurture and nourishment.** Feeding others and being fed. Martin reads the sign as giving nourishment out and taking it in, and says well-being lasts as long as that ebb and flow stays in balance (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §12, from Martin).
- **Mothering and the parent-child bond.** Martin calls the Cancer/Capricorn pair the parent-child axis (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Martin). The glyph is read as the closeness of mother and child (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §12, from Martin).
- **Shelter and protection of the vulnerable.** Tierney describes the sign as moved to protect everything in life that is vulnerable and needs shelter (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §1, from Tierney).
- **The emotional life and moods.** Ruled by the Moon, the sign is described as phase-driven and cyclical (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10; Full Attributes, Cancer).
- **The past: memory, history, ancestry.** Records, albums, keepsakes, continuity and honoring ancestors (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2). With Jupiter exalted, Martin says belonging reaches past the clan to community, society, country, ancestors and history (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10; `F_Rulerships.md` §6, from Martin).
- **Country and patriotism.** *Patriotic* is in Snodgrass's Cancer trait list (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §4, from Snodgrass).
- **Gestation, birth, baptism and rebirth,** and psychologically sympathy and the awakening of the conscious mind (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass).
- **Hospitality and hosting** (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2, from Silva).
- **Care work.** The decans name therapists, psychologists, detectives and nurses (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §2, from Emerson).
- **The Moon's governance column.** Snodgrass's planet table gives the Moon *workers and public figures* (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md` Appendix IV).
- ⚠️ The documentation never lays out the fourth house as a full list of domains. The house appears only through Martin's keyword, her nurturing/belonging language and the axis with the tenth house. The list above is pieced together from sign material.

## 2. Core meaning
- **Archetype: the Crab,** a soft body inside a hard shell built because what is underneath is so soft (Full Attributes, Cancer). Arabic and Syriac names for the constellation mean *the Holder* (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass).
- **Driving motive:** Tierney presents Cancer as the first sign in the sequence to feel both love and sadness for the human condition, and as driven to protect whatever is vulnerable and needs shelter. Crucially, it does not wait to be asked: once it picks up distress, it moves in to mother, even into other people's emotional space (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §1, from Tierney).
- **The substrate's reading of the motive:** shelter as an obligation the sign assigns itself, and not plain altruism. It turns other people's need into its own security, on the logic that someone who is needed cannot be left (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §1).
- **Martin's dignity set as a summary:** the Moon rules (care governs), Jupiter is exalted (belonging expands past the clan), Saturn is in detriment (limits and authority work badly here), Mars is in fall (self-assertion works badly here) (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §1, from Martin).
- **Core need:** to be *needed*, which the substrate treats as the one form of love that cannot be taken back. Emerson's south-node line: you feel best when needed and often unsafe in public (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §5, from Emerson). ⚠️ This is a node placement in Cancer, not the Sun sign itself.
- **Beneath that:** belonging (Martin's house keyword), and under belonging the twin core fears that Martin names for the axis, **engulfment and abandonment** (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §5 and §8, from Martin).
- **A need for acknowledgment.** Emerson's note on Ceres in Cancer: the challenge is to expect less praise for a good deed. The substrate reads this as Cancer needing its care acknowledged and feeling unacknowledged care as betrayal (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §5, from Emerson). ⚠️ Ceres placement, not the Sun sign.

## 3. The area at its best
- **Nurturing instinct and empathy.** Notices who is hurting before they say anything; deep emotional intuition (Full Attributes, Cancer; Quick Reference, Cancer).
- **Absorptive perception.** Tierney: always alert to others' unspoken feelings; reads posture and face before speech (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2, from Tierney). Water in general is said to perceive hidden motives and to offer support no matter what the other person has done, without explaining the feeling away (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §5, from Martin and Tierney).
- **Loyalty that fights.** Snodgrass: fights a friend's enemies as if they were its own. Martin on Mars in Cancer: the fierce aggression of a mother protecting her young, with nothing weak about it (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2, from Snodgrass and Martin).
- **Tenacity and resourcefulness** once committed to protecting someone, especially where safety or care is at stake (Full Attributes, Cancer).
- **Mediation and hosting.** Silva: good at choosing among options already on the table, at brokering compromises where both sides win, and at hosting (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2, from Silva).
- **Indirect competence and patience.** Martin: like a crab, it approaches goals quietly and sideways, waits for the tide or until others lose interest, then gets what it wants to everyone's surprise (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2; `B_Modalities.md` §2, from Martin).
- **Memory and custodianship.** A strong memory; records, albums, continuity, honoring ancestors (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2).
- **Staying power at work.** Silva: Cancer types stay with an employer past the point of readiness to leave, feeling they owe something (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2, from Silva).
- **Strength in crisis.** Martin, on the receptive signs generally: they seem frail but draw on a deep inner reserve and grow very strong in a crisis (Substrate `C_Polarity.md` §3, from Martin).
- **Generosity and mentorship** under the Jupiter exaltation (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10, from Martin).
- **Not compartmentalizing.** Emerson: Cancer does not split home from work, and so burns out less than expected (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6, from Emerson).
- **Myth:** the crab honored for tenacity and courage in the face of certain death (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass); see §6.

## 4. The area gone wrong
The substrate's framing: Cancer's vices are all **deformations of protection**, which makes them hard to name from inside (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3). The Full Attributes file makes the same general point for every sign: the negative traits are the positive ones pushed past their useful range, and it gives Cancer's protective instinct turning into a wall nobody can get past as its example (Full Attributes, Worth Your Attention; Quick Reference, pattern note).
- **Keeping loved ones dependent (the substrate calls it the *smother-lock*).** Tierney describes the mothering side as unconsciously refusing to let loved ones grow up and become independent, because someone who no longer needs parenting might leave. He adds that Cancer is not objective enough to see how oppressive this is (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3, from Tierney).
- **Help as trespass.** Help arrives unasked and does not stop when asked. The substrate adds that help from outside is felt as a criticism of Cancer's own care (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3 and §1, from Tierney; the second half is the substrate's reading).
- **Feeding as emotional blackmail.** Martin's phrase: "if you don't eat my food it means you don't love me" (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3, from Martin). Hospitality that cannot be declined without cost.
- **Guilt and manipulation.** Guilt-tripping, often unconscious rather than calculated (Full Attributes, Cancer). Silva: manipulation is not a habit but appears when the person feels extremely vulnerable (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3, from Silva).
- **Passive aggression.** Silva: months of silence, then a sink of dishes sets it off; snide notes, sly messages, things said months ago brought back. Emerson also notes passive aggression and reluctance to say what is wrong (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3, from Silva and Emerson).
- **Bottled rage.** Tierney: afraid of its own anger, expecting isolation as the punishment for venting, it holds anger until it erupts blindly and destructively, then wants to treat the outburst as a bad dream. There is no gradual escalation (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3, from Tierney).
- **Victim posture and self-pity.** Everyone else becomes at fault; overblown emotional drama that pulls others in; drowning in its own sorrow more than any other sign (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3). ⚠️ The GDD gives no book for this passage.
- **Selective nostalgia.** Remembers only the good parts of the past (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3; ⚠️ book not named); a tendency to live in the past (Full Attributes, Cancer).
- **Grudges.** Snodgrass: when blocked, it broods over a private list of hurts and betrayals. The substrate adds that it forgives aloud and never forgets (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3, from Snodgrass). Real difficulty letting old hurts go (Full Attributes, Cancer). Water in general stores grievance instead of releasing it, and stored hurt festers (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §5, from Tierney).
- **Safe relationships instead of intimacy.** Tierney: once badly hurt, Cancer may keep only relationships that cannot wound it, giving deeply through something like volunteer work while avoiding real closeness. Charity replaces intimacy (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3, from Tierney).
- **Cold shutdown.** After repeated hurt it can seem coldly distant and switch its feelings off, like the cold-blooded creature that symbolizes it (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3; ⚠️ book not named). Martin's tidal image: when the tide goes out, you knock on the shell and nobody is home (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §5, from Martin).
- **Defensiveness.** The shell walls out people who meant no harm (Full Attributes, Cancer).
- **Moodiness and oversensitivity.** Moods that change a room's temperature without warning; reacting to slights real or imagined (Full Attributes, Cancer).
- **Clinginess and fear of being left** (Full Attributes, Cancer).
- **Jealousy and possessiveness,** strongest in the first decan, which demands intense loyalty (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §2, from Emerson).
- **Insulation.** Tierney describes the guarded type physically putting on weight and water as a buffer; Martin's case of emotional starvation expressed as obesity (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §3, from Tierney and Martin).
- **Expecting others to read its needs.** Silva: Cancer types are always signaling what they need and leave it to others to pick up on it, which Silva calls unfair, and often it is (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §4, from Silva). Related rule: Cancer expects to hear only what it can handle emotionally (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §5; ⚠️ book not named).
- **Unable to stand alone or set limits.** With Saturn in detriment, boundaries, refusal and formal authority work poorly; the missing strength is standing on one's own feet (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10, from Martin).
- **Unable to fight for itself.** With Mars in fall, aggression only comes out on behalf of a dependent (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §1 and §10, from Martin).
- **Neediness under polarization.** Martin: when the Cancer/Capricorn axis polarizes, the Cancer end grows more and more needy (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Martin).
- **Taking in too much.** Absorbs the surroundings without filters (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10, from Tierney). Water's general failure is poor self-preservation, getting swept into others' turmoil or becoming the turmoil (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §5, from Tierney).
- **Duplicity, as antiquity read it.** The crab's sideways walk suggested deceit; Snodgrass calls Cancer the most nebulous of archetypes (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §4, from Snodgrass).
- **Good at family, poor at friendship.** Snodgrass's verdict on the type: "excellent parents, but intolerant or inaccessible friends" (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §4, from Snodgrass).
- **Snodgrass's pathology table** lists Cancer's psychological symptoms as torpor, suspicion, vengefulness and overeating, and its eating habits as favoring watery foods, with a tendency to gain weight (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Cancer, Appendix V). Snodgrass himself calls this kind of table reductive (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Appendix V note).
- **Withdrawal past self-protection.** The receptive mode's general failures: isolation, holding what should be released, stagnation, and silence that others take as consent (Substrate `C_Polarity.md` §3, from Martin and Jung).

## 5. Behavior under pressure and over time
**At work.**
- Prefers commitment over mobility; wants independence but dislikes working alone; needs colleagues who are competent and think differently (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6, from Silva).
- Silva: makes the right choice when it comes down to a few options, but goes wrong when it must find, research and narrow the options itself (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6, from Silva).
- When disillusioned, becomes the chronic complainer with little idea how to fix things (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6, from Silva).
- Careers tend to arrive sideways, through adjacency and convenience rather than application (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §7, from Silva).

**Socially.**
- Silva calls Cancer a walking contradiction: seems introverted, yet hosts (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §4, from Silva).
- Small, regular gatherings on its own ground; consistency over novelty. At someone else's party it sits off to the side with two friends and leaves early. It mixes friend groups that others keep apart, so it seems to know everyone. It turns down invitations and expects to keep getting them (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6, from Silva).
- Friendships start sideways, through a relative's partner's friend or a mutual acquaintance, never by direct approach (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §7, from Silva).
- Silva describes a weekly gathering where regulars come whether invited or not, and a host who sometimes skips the invitations to see who shows up anyway (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §7, from Silva).
- Silva: with a Cancer man you do not stay a friend; you become family for as long as you stay (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §7, from Silva). See Gaps on Silva's gender split.
- A blank, unmoved face drives Cancer to distraction; it needs a visible reaction of any kind. It also spots anyone performing coolness at once (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §4; ⚠️ book not named).

**Under stress.**
- Withdraws, goes quiet, and molts. Silva's crab-biology gloss: molting crabs hide in a dug-out cave, or fake death so predators leave them alone (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6, from Silva).
- Tierney: when angry it throws itself into heavy housework (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6, from Tierney).
- Silva's counseling example: if she is knitting, leave her be; if she is doing chores she hates, that is the time to worry (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6, from Silva).

**In conflict and crisis.**
- Tierney contrasts cardinal water's heavy, rushing drench with the other two water signs: it surges outward fast and hits its surroundings at once (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6, from Tierney). It is the most demonstrative water sign and cannot hide what it is going through for long (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §5, from Tierney).
- It will fight to the death for a dependent, but cannot hold the line for long; after that comes either the eruption or the cold shutdown (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6).
- As a cardinal sign it shares the group's impatience with unresolved situations and deadlock, and the reflex to attack or defend when provoked (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §2, from Tierney).

**When secure.**
- Mediates, hosts, brokers win-win outcomes, and steps in with its claws only when someone is plainly unruly or unfair (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Silva).
- Trust: Martin says it does not trust unless it is completely safe, but finds the past and memories safe to trust (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §14 hook 11, from Martin). Silva instead says its loyalty produces easy mutual trust (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §15). See Gaps.

**In childhood (Silva on raising Cancer children).**
- Children of this sign tend to become the labels they are given: call them *bad*, *a bully* or *stubborn* and they lean into it even though it hurts them (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §7, from Silva).
- Discipline works only at a distance; the child learns to turn a scolding into a hug. The need for privacy spikes around ages 5 to 9 and again in adolescence, when a searched room feels like betrayal and even a casual question about their day can set off alarms (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §7, from Silva).
- Left alone, it settles into one or two close bonds and stops there; someone has to push its horizon open (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §7; ⚠️ book not named, probably Silva).

**Over time and in maturing.**
- Tierney: Cancer takes in its surroundings without protective filters and only builds a hard shell after absorbing too much negativity. The substrate's gloss: the shell is scar tissue, not skin; the hardness is learned and dated, not inborn (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10, from Tierney).
- Martin's approach treats detriment and fall as the developmental task: for Cancer, integrating Saturn (the strength to stand on its own feet) and Mars (asserting itself) (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §4; `04_Cancer.md` §10, from Martin).
- Tierney: Cancer sees itself as timid and shy, which he says is not so; its cardinality makes it assertive and quick to act (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10, from Tierney). Snodgrass's trait list says the reverse (bashful, elusive, secretive). See Gaps.
- Martin's polarization law describes a runaway loop over time: the more the Cancer end needs, the more the Capricorn end withholds, which raises the need further (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Martin).
- Silva notes winter-onset low moods (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §12, from Silva).

## 6. Myth, history and cultural associations
- **The crab that attacked Heracles.** In the Greek myth Hera sends a crab to nip Heracles' heel while he fights the Lernaean hydra, and the crab is trampled. Hera places it in the sky as a sign of tenacity and courage in the face of certain death. Snodgrass adds that its constellation was set far from Hercules', at a safe distance from the strongman (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass). What it says about the area: honor for a doomed act of protection on someone else's behalf.
- **Names.** Arabic *Alsartan* and Syriac *Sartano*, both meaning *the Holder*; the Tibetans saw a frog, the Egyptians a scarab; a prehistoric Indian eight-part cosmology has *nand*, the crab (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass). The Holder names the sign's grip on what it keeps.
- **The gate.** The constellation is linked with the gate through which humanity enters the world; the Romans called it *janua inferni*, the gate of hell, opening from the Milky Way to earth. A Buddhist reading sees an ominous sleep of death, and the Inca shared the foreboding (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass). Birth and death share one threshold here.
- **Molting as rebirth.** Because the crab sheds its shell, Christian apologists used it for repentance and being born again (John 3), and in the apostolic scheme Cancer answers to John the Divine. The sign governs gestation, baptism and rebirth, and psychologically sympathy and the awakening of the conscious mind (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass).
- **Duplicity and the sideways walk.** Aristophanes (421 B.C.), quoted by Snodgrass: "You cannot teach a crab to walk straight." In antiquity the crab's sideways movement suggested duplicity (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §4, from Snodgrass). A late-5th-century B.C. coin from Acragas in Sicily pairs the monster Scylla with a crab, read either as the sea or as a hint at the city's name for devious dealing (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass).
- **The name of a disease.** *Carcinus* gives the word carcinogen; Hippocrates first applied the crab image to the tumor. Snodgrass says the sign shares with tumors the grasping reach that chokes off an organ's life, and that this makes Cancer one of the least popular sun signs in art, literature and language (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass). The substrate's reading: the shadow of protective holding, a thing that grows by consuming what it holds.
- **Iconography.** The crab; a she-crab holding a ball of fertilized eggs in one protective claw; the Egyptian scarab; and an arm raising a sword, the last two read as emblems of struggle. The medieval *Book of Hours* draws it as a plump ring topped by three stars, with eight star-tipped legs (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §12, from Snodgrass). The one huge claw a real crab thrusts at whoever comes uninvited is, in Tierney's reading, a display rather than an attack, theater over a soft inside (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §4, from Tierney).
- **The glyph.** Martin reads it as circular, inward and protective, an image of mother and child held close (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §12, from Martin).
- **Stars.** The alpha star *Acubens*, the claw, a white-and-red double star; *Praesepe*, a cluster long used to predict rain because water vapor dims it; zeta Cancri, a revolving group of five; the Chinese grouping of two of its stars as *Kwei*, the spectre; the Hindu name *Pushya*, the flower, for its delta star (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §12, from Snodgrass).
- **A replaced identity.** In the Babylonian list of 419 B.C. this slot was *Praesepe*, later changed to Cancer (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Appendix I, from Snodgrass).
- **A faint, small constellation.** Snodgrass's data ranks Cancer second-dimmest of the twelve and gives it the fewest stars, 23 (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Appendix II, from Snodgrass).
- **Hearth against public square.** Silva names the Cancer/Capricorn axis after the Greek *Agora* and *Hestia*, the public square against the hearth (Substrate `C_Polarity.md` §6, from Silva). ⚠️ The GDD notes the idea is named but not developed.
- **A historical exemplar.** Snodgrass's example is Nelson Rockefeller, who built large imposing buildings and then withdrew, crab-fashion, into culture and philanthropy, putting facades and money in place of more personal pleasures (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass).
- **Family and catastrophe.** Martin notes that Pluto entered Cancer as World War I broke out, a time when old securities were destroyed and families across Europe were decimated (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Martin). ⚠️ This is a generational planet placement, not the Sun sign.
- **The anti-family counter-position.** Martin's Uranus-in-Cancer generation does not believe in family, belonging or biological ties and panics at any kind of nest; its task is to build new tribes and clans on shared ideals rather than shared genes, while still needing belonging at an ordinary human level (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Martin). ⚠️ Generational placement, not the Sun sign.

## 7. Structure
**Element: water.** Water absorbs: it reads what was not said and slips past boundaries it was not invited through. Its strengths are reading hidden motives, unconditional support and endurance under emotional load; its blind spot is its own edges; its two failures are being swept into others' turmoil and storing hurt until it festers. It needs a container, a filter borrowed from the other elements, and real solitude (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §5, from Tierney and Martin). Martin frames water's truth as collective and inward, found in shared emotional undercurrents, opposite air's truth, which lies outward in systems (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §1, from Martin). Within water, Cancer is the demonstrative one that cannot hide its state for long, and it is tidal (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §5, from Tierney and Martin). Tierney: it soaks up its surroundings without filters and grows a shell only after taking in too much (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10, from Tierney). ⚠️ Snodgrass dissents, calling the water signs persuaders and fun-lovers; the GDD follows Martin and Tierney (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §9).
- Water's pairings that touch Cancer: water and earth fit naturally, earth giving the container and water the inner life (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §6, from Silva). Fire and water start hot and deplete each other; Silva notes Cancers often jump into relationships with fire signs because the early feelings are too intense to refuse, and Tierney has fire unable to respect the Crab's worry about an uncertain future (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §6, from Silva and Tierney). Two water signs understand each other without words but deadlock in sulking standoffs (Substrate `A_Elements.md` §6, from Silva).

**Modality: cardinal.** Cardinal signs open a season and start change (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §1–2). Tierney calls Cancer the cardinal sign that seems out of place among the assertive ones; Martin's correction is that cardinal water reaches its goals quietly and waits for the tide. The GDD's summary: cardinal means initiating, not loud (Substrate `B_Modalities.md` §2, from Tierney and Martin). The substrate reads Cancer as a sign that acts first while sincerely describing itself as only defending (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10). Tierney also contrasts the two pulls: cardinality reaches out for a support system, the Moon goes inward to build a secure home base, and Cancer does both at once (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10, from Tierney).

**Polarity: receptive (also called negative, yin, or traditionally feminine).** Inward-moving, holding, absorbing, magnetic rather than projecting (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10; `C_Polarity.md` §3). The sources insist receptive does not mean passive or weak (Substrate `C_Polarity.md` §3, from Silva and Martin). The GDD notes polarity adds little beyond element (Substrate `C_Polarity.md` §1).

**Opposite sign: Capricorn. The axis: parent and child, home and public standing.**
- Martin calls it the parent-child axis: there is no child without a parent and no parent without a child. It sets the Moon against Saturn and the fourth house against the tenth: the need to be held against the need to stand alone (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Martin).
- Its whole subject is what is enough and what is not enough, and it names the twin core fears of engulfment and abandonment (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Martin).
- Polarized, the Cancer end grows needy and the Capricorn end withholding and withdrawn, each feeding the other (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Martin).
- Each end supplies what the other refuses to develop. Saturn, Capricorn's ruler, is in detriment in Cancer, so Cancer cannot generate the limit-setting Capricorn specializes in (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Tierney and Martin). The two signs also trade dignities: Jupiter is exalted in Cancer and in fall in Capricorn; Mars is exalted in Capricorn and in fall in Cancer (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §5).
- Both Silva books say Cancer and Capricorn pair very well once responsibilities are divided; the opposition is either a working partnership or a runaway loop, never neutral (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §9, from Silva).
- Silva's Greek names for the two ends: *Hestia* (hearth) and *Agora* (public square) (Substrate `C_Polarity.md` §6, from Silva).
- General rule for oppositions: each side tends to see its own disowned half in the other and condemn it there (Substrate `D_Aspect_Geometry.md` §2, from Martin).

**Ruling planet: the Moon (domicile).** As a faculty: care, belonging and the emotional container; the capacity to house and feed people and make a place feel like home (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §3, from Martin). The Moon makes behavior cyclical rather than steady and reflective rather than generative; Silva says it stands for reaping the benefits of work already done (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10, from Silva). The substrate defines Moon-power as the willingness to protect life by making things feel safe (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10; ⚠️ book not named).
- **Exalted: Jupiter.** Belonging reaches past the clan to community, society, country, ancestors and history; also mentorship and open-handed generosity (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10; `F_Rulerships.md` §6, from Martin).
- **Detriment: Saturn.** Boundaries, refusal and formal authority misfire; the missing strength is standing on one's own feet (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10, from Martin). Martin stresses that a planet in detriment still works, but against the grain (Substrate `F_Rulerships.md` §4, from Martin).
- **Fall: Mars.** Self-assertion is the weakest faculty; aggression comes out only on behalf of a dependent, never for oneself (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §1 and §10, from Martin). Martin's image of Mars in Cancer, a mother defending her young, shows the same faculty at full force when it is for someone else (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2, from Martin).

**Squares: Aries and Libra** (both cardinal, so the friction is fast and wants resolution; Substrate `D_Aspect_Geometry.md` §2 and §4).
- Shared cause, per Tierney: Cancer invests heavily in a calm, peaceful home where relationships reflect clear agreements, so it grates against combative Aries and argumentative Libra (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Tierney; corroborated in `D_Aspect_Geometry.md` §4).
- **Aries:** Tierney has Aries unable to respect a sign that uses its weakness and insecurity to draw sympathy. Emerson: Cancer finds Aries self-centered and ends up insecure, while Aries is worn down by the insecurity. Silva: Aries is the least compatible sign with any water sign, Cancer most of all (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Tierney, Emerson and Silva). The area in conflict: shown vulnerability against self-reliance.
- **Libra:** two ideas of fairness. Cancer's is loyalty-weighted and felt (Silva: it brokers win-win outcomes easily and steps in with its claws when someone is plainly unfair); Libra's is procedural and balanced (the substrate's reading). Silva: both seem kind and fair-minded, yet they do not mix, because Cancers sniff out fakeness and Libras cannot handle emotional ups and downs. Emerson: both shut down and hide their feelings, so nothing reaches the table (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Silva and Emerson).

**Trines: Scorpio and Pisces** (same element; Substrate `D_Aspect_Geometry.md` §4).
- **Scorpio:** magnetism and fights that do not end the bond (Silva); it holds on condition that Cancer gives up control (Emerson) (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §9).
- **Pisces:** a bond so deep it feels psychic, but both dissolve into each other; Silva warns about mothering tendencies, and Emerson says neither will carry the financial load (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §9, from Silva and Emerson).

**Sextiles: Taurus and Virgo.**
- **Taurus:** Taurus grounds and provides, Cancer gives emotional support; good friends all round (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §9, from Silva).
- **Virgo:** Silva's *Sun and Moon Signs* says Virgo's own nurturing gives Cancer a rare rest, since Cancer does all the nurturing with most other signs: the nurse gets nursed. Friction: Virgo is privately reserved and resists emotional demands (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §9, from Silva).

**Quincunxes: Sagittarius and Aquarius,** which the corpus treats as Cancer's true worst matches. Aquarius under-communicates and puts logic over feelings and fairness, and cannot keep Cancer reassured; Sagittarius over-communicates, is too self-interested to give attention when needed, and cannot promise steady material and emotional support (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Silva and Emerson).

**Semi-sextiles: Gemini and Leo** (neighbors that share nothing structural; Substrate `D_Aspect_Geometry.md` §4). The documentation says nothing specific about Cancer's relations with them.

## 8. Decans
Emerson is the only decan source for Cancer; the scheme blends the sign with itself, then with the other two water signs (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §1–2; `04_Cancer.md` §11).
1. **Cancer / Moon (first ten degrees): openly warm and openly possessive.** Very authentic and emotionally charged; compassion in everything; demands intense loyalty from lovers and friends; prone to jealousy (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §2, from Emerson).
2. **Scorpio / Pluto (second ten degrees): reserved on the surface, deep underneath.** Very deep feelings behind a face that can seem emotionless; possessive but very giving; suited to therapy, psychology or detective work (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §2, from Emerson). Sub-theme: perceptive, professional care.
3. **Pisces / Neptune (last ten degrees): the most sensitive.** Possibly the most sensitive in the zodiac; helps others tirelessly; natural nurses, though moody; feelings that change fast without a logical reason; warned against being taken advantage of; might end up meditating in a monastery (Substrate `E_Decans.md` §2; `04_Cancer.md` §11, from Emerson). Sub-theme: self-sacrificing care and its exploitability.

## 11. Where the author's named concepts appear
- **Justice:** Cancer's sense of fairness is loyalty-weighted and felt rather than procedural. Silva has it brokering win-win outcomes and stepping in only when someone is plainly unruly or unfair; the square with Libra sets this against Libra's balanced, procedural fairness, with each side sure it is the fair one (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Silva; setting felt fairness against procedural fairness is the substrate's reading). Tierney links Cancer's friction with Aries and Libra to its wish for a home where relationships reflect clear agreements (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Tierney).
- **Crime:** nothing in this material on crime as such. The nearest points are antiquity's reading of the crab's sideways walk as duplicity, the Acragas coin as a hint at devious dealing, and Snodgrass's vengefulness as a Cancer symptom (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §4 and §13; `G_Correspondences.md`, Appendix V, from Snodgrass).
- **Trust:** Martin says Cancer does not trust unless it is completely safe, but trusts the past and memories; Silva says its loyalty breeds easy mutual trust (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §14 hook 11 and §15). It quickly detects fakeness and performance (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §4 and §8, from Silva). Aquarius cannot keep it reassured (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §8, from Emerson).
- **Care:** the center of this sign. Nurture, shelter, feeding, the Moon as the faculty of care, and the decans' nurses and therapists (sections 1–3, 8). The documentation is richest on care's failure modes: smothering, intrusion, feeding as blackmail, guilt, unwanted help, the need for care to be acknowledged, and care given to strangers in place of intimacy (section 4). Virgo is the sign that cares for the carer (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §9, from Silva).
- **Honor:** Hera honors the crab with a place in the sky for tenacity and courage in the face of certain death (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §13, from Snodgrass). Honoring ancestors belongs to the area (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2). Nothing on honor as a personal code.
- **Duty:** shelter as an obligation the sign assigns itself (the substrate's reading, §1); staying with an employer out of a sense of owing (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §2, from Silva); fighting to the death for a dependent (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §6). Duty here is to family and dependents rather than to rules or office; Saturn, the planet of law and limits, is in detriment (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §10).

## 12. Gaps
- **The fourth house is thin.** The documentation gives Martin's keyword (belonging) and her nurturing language, but no full list of the house's domains. ⚠️ Topics that wider astrology often files under the fourth house, such as property and one parent in particular, are not discussed (this is outside knowledge, to check against a house source).
- **Nothing at collective scale.** The sources describe individuals and pairs. The substrate's Cancer file lists what the corpus is silent on: architecture, clothing and textiles, music, funeral practice, governance, law, economy, and strangers or refugees at scale (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §15; `00_Method_and_Sources.md`).
- **Decans rest on Emerson alone,** a few sentences each, with no second source (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §15; `E_Decans.md` §5).
- **Several shadow points carry no book name** in the GDD (victim posture, selective nostalgia, cold shutdown, the need for a visible reaction). Marked ⚠️ above.
- **Some key lines are not about the Sun sign.** Emerson's south-node and Ceres-in-Cancer lines, and Martin's Pluto-in-Cancer and Uranus-in-Cancer generations, describe planet placements. They are used above as color on the area, flagged ⚠️.
- **Disagreements the GDD records and does not resolve** (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §15):
  - *Concealment or openness:* Silva's *Sun and Moon Signs* says Cancers tell partners plainly what is bothering them; Emerson and Silva's *Ultimate Guide* say the reverse. The GDD treats the openness claim as an outlier.
  - *Timidity:* Tierney says the shyness is a false self-image and Cancer is assertive; Snodgrass's trait list (bashful, elusive, secretive) says it is shy.
  - *Trust:* Silva says easy mutual trust; Martin says trust only when completely safe.
  - *Color:* Snodgrass's violet and ice blue against Emerson's blue, white and sea green.
  - *Herb:* Emerson's sage against the Moon herbs.
- **Silva's male/female split.** Both Silva books organize Cancer behavior by gender (different molting, friendship and loyalty patterns). No other source supports it; the GDD treats it as one author's scheme (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §15). The male-Cancer friendship line in section 5 comes from that scheme.
- **Source quality.** Tierney is an OCR'd scan; the GDD reconstructed his lines conservatively. Emerson's Cancer data block wrongly prints the element as Air (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, data quality log). Martin v3 gave almost nothing sign-specific (Substrate `04_Cancer.md` §15).
- **Full Attributes names no sources.** Its Cancer entry is a compiled summary with no books cited, so its points cannot be traced further.
- **Excluded by the GDD:** Snodgrass's Appendix III maps Cancer to countries and cities; the GDD bars it as national stereotyping and these notes leave it out (Substrate `G_Correspondences.md`, Appendix III).
