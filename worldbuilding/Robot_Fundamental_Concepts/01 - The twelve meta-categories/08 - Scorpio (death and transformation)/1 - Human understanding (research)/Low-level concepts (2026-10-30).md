## Borderline

- **loneliness:** "feels alone" and "buried loneliness." Folded into `isolation` to match the Libra file; a merge may split them.
- **loss / sorrow:** folded into `grief`.
- **repression:** folded into `suppression` and `self-discipline`; the notes use both words for the same containment.
- **contrition / repentance:** folded into `remorse`.
- **cunning / strategy:** folded into `scheming`.
- **sensuality:** folded into `sexuality`.
- **transmutation / inspiration:** the notes' own word is transmutation. Named `sublimation` as the common English term for turning raw drive into a higher aim; a merge may prefer `transmutation`.
- **ownership / possessions:** the Taurus axis (personal against shared possessions, the resources-and-ownership axis). Covered by `legacy`, `money` and `retention`; a merge may want `ownership` here too.
- **funeral rites / festival of the dead:** folded into `mourning`, `burial` and `death`.
- **underworld / hell:** Persephone's descent; the hells Sagittarius believes in. Mythic places more than concepts.
- **detection, research, surgery, auditing:** occupations folded into `investigation`, `policing` and `medicine`.
- **delegation / obedience:** a delegated task treated as done; dislikes taking orders. Folded into `authority`.
- **wit:** folded into `humor`.
- **self-education / routine work:** the revolution cusp is self-taught and unsuited to a nine-to-five job. Too thin to list.
- **coercion:** compulsion does not work on the child. A one-line parenting note.
- **sociopathy / autism:** named only as outsiders' misreadings of the type, not as concepts the area claims.
- **fraud / inhumanity:** how Scorpio reads Aquarius's easy detachment. Folded into `attachment`.
- **territoriality / self-interest:** filters prescribed for water from other elements, not Scorpio's own traits.
- **irritability:** Snodgrass's "nettlesomeness" symptom. Left out as an unclear translation; `dominance` and `destructiveness` from the same list are in the table.
- **calm, protection:** functions of stones. Correspondence-derived.
- **sainthood:** Valjean's earned sainthood; the concept taken is `remembrance`.
