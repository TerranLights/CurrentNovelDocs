## Borderline
- `shame`: named in a §10 heading next to humiliation but never developed. Folded into `humiliation`.
- `truth`: fire's truth is said to be subjective and to need no proof. This describes the element more than this area of life.
- `intuition`: appears only in one source's list of fire's qualities.
- `brutality` and `purity`: the medieval pairing of lion and maiden shows purity civilizing brutality. It is a single art motif; the idea of power tamed is filed under `gentleness`.
- `fertility`: the Nile flood is welcomed though it is destructive. The point is a blessing that comes with damage rather than fertility as a human concern.
- `emptiness`: the lion stands for emptiness in one tradition, mentioned only in passing.
- `stewardship`: named only as what the sign does not prefer (hidden stewardship against splendor).
- `incorruptibility` and `refinement`: alchemical gold as incorruptible and the lion as raw gold awaiting refinement. They are correspondence lore.
- `strategy`: in one fable the lion beats three bulls by separating them. That is one tactic, not a theme of the area.
- `inner child`: folded into `playfulness`; it could stand alone if other files use it.
- `music`: music tames the lion. Covered by `gentleness` and `art`.
- `evangelism`: listed with high-church religion as a domain. Covered by `splendor` and `faith`.
- `elation` and `depression`: one cusp swings between them overnight. This is too narrow to stand alone.
- `false hope`: it refuses commitments rather than raise false hopes. Folded into `commitment` and `honesty`.
- `innocence`: the untaught heart that thaws the frozen land in the Fisher King framing. It is a single image.
- `crime`: the notes say nothing here concerns crime. The nearest points are `favoritism`, `laziness` and `intimidation`.
- `self-erasure`: the counter-generation turns down advancement on principle and so erases itself. Folded into `equality`.
