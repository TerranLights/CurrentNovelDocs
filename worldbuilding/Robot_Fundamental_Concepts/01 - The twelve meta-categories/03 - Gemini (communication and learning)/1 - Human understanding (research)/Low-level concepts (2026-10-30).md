## Borderline

- **law, diplomacy:** lawyer and ambassador appear only in Silva's occupation list, and the judiciary belongs to Jupiter, Gemini's weak planet; kept under `justice`, `translation` and `negotiation`.
- **twins:** the sign's figure rather than an idea of human life; its content is under `siblings`, `duality` and `taking turns`.
- **resilience:** mutable resilience reads as youthfulness; kept under `youthfulness` and `cheerfulness`.
- **imitation:** treated only as part of storytelling; merged into `storytelling` (and kept apart from `mimicry`, which is taking on another's energy).
- **gesture:** talking with the hands; one line, closer to a physical habit than a concept.
- **poetry:** Dante's thanks to the twin stars for his gift; a single testimony.
- **compassion:** said of people born near the Gemini side of Cancer, so arguably Cancer's.
- **validation:** what Pisces wants and Gemini does not give; Pisces' concept more than Gemini's.
- **seduction:** Emerson finds Gemini and Sagittarius both out to seduce and conquer; one line, in the Sagittarius pairing.
- **idealism:** the failing of the Gemini-Aquarius pair, too much idealism and too little planning; arguably Aquarius'.
- **imagination, synthesis:** what the sign stands for in heraldry and trademarks; a correspondence rather than the area itself.
- **civility:** Martin calls air the most civilized element; a single word.
- **immortality:** Polydeuces sharing his immortality with his mortal brother; the concept carried is `taking turns`.
- **housework:** it loses interest in domestic chores; kept under `boredom` and `home`.
- **organization:** what Libra supplies to Gemini, named only as absent.
- **beauty:** the second decan seeks friendship and beauty; Venus' contribution through the decan, not Gemini's own.
- **kinship:** first contact with Sagittarius feels like kinship; one line.
- **literacy as education, language learning, writing as a craft, ethics of communication, rumor at scale:** named in §12 as gaps (only literacy itself appears, in air's political note, and is in the table).
