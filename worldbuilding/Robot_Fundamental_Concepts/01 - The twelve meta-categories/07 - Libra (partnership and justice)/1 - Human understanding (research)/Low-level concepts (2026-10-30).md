## Borderline

- **diligence:** the notes say Libra counts its delay as diligence. Folded into `deliberation` because diligence itself is not this sign's theme.
- **equity:** the scale as a symbol of equity. Folded into `fairness`, since the notes never tell equity apart from fairness or equality.
- **status / standing:** the core need is called standing. Folded into `being needed`; a later merge may want `status` as its own concept.
- **recognition / admiration / validation:** folded into `approval`. Another sign's file may list these separately.
- **loneliness:** the child cannot bear being alone. Folded into `isolation`, the word the air material uses.
- **outrage / outburst / comeuppance / karma:** folded into `indignation`, `rage` and `retribution`.
- **ethics:** named once (Watergate reading). Folded into `disillusionment` and `honor`.
- **patience:** mentioned only to say a patient, soft-spoken sign can still initiate. Too passing to list.
- **protocol, hospitality, complaint:** from the list of suitable jobs (protocol, VIP arrangements, complaints desks). Read as occupations, not concepts.
- **order:** orderly queues and orderly classrooms. Covered by `civility`; unsure whether order alone is a concept of this area.
- **symmetry:** a recurring aesthetic (clean symmetrical lines, bilateral symmetry). Mostly a correspondence (shapes), so left out.
- **skepticism:** the child questions the Santa Claus story. A one-off illustration of analysis, not a theme.
- **decline / culmination:** the peak where decline starts, from the cycle image. Reads as astrological mechanics more than a life concept.
- **golden age:** Astraea leaves after the golden and silver ages. The concept taken is `lawlessness`; the golden age itself may belong elsewhere.
- **satire / mockery:** the Petronius platter that mocks each sign. Incidental to Libra.
- **double bind:** the result of projecting one's own force onto partners. Folded into `projection`.
- **complacency / inertia:** a general property of trines and of air grand trines. Folded into `detachment` and `learning`.
- **authority:** the Sun's faculty of authority that doesn't depend on others' opinion, which Libra lacks. Folded into `identity`.
- **scarcity, promise, rivalry:** named only as gaps (no material on scarcity; contracts and promise-keeping, open enemies and rivalry to be filled). Clearly in the area but undocumented here.
- **tolerance, stamina, luck, dominance, inspiration, nightmares:** functions attached to stones and colors. Correspondence-derived, so left out.
