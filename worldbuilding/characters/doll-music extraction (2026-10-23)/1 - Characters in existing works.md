**Lyrics only (song):**

**Cross-check:**
- Files consulted: `TL/Garcelle [G.A.R. - Cell E]/` (canonical); the older copies at `GDD/Off-World_or_MIA/Garcelle [G.A.R. - Cell E]/` and `NR/reference/Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Off-World_or_MIA/Garcelle [G.A.R. - Cell E]/`; and the film notes, `NR/Garcelle Sci-Fi Horror (movie, title TBD)/Author Statements (2026-10-19).md`. The film notes do not mention this song.
- **Agrees:**
  - Her name comes from G.A.R., Cell E (TL README).
  - A guard tortured her with electric shocks to her eyes (TL `Personal_Background/Conflict_Experiences.md`).
  - She was forced off Earth.
  - She hates humans: "the only good human is a dead human" (TL `Personal_Background/Loyalties.md`).
  - Her friends were killed: the film notes name robot friends and family killed on Earth, plus fellow deportees.
  - Salvagers come to her station and die. The GDD `Personal_Background/Timeline.md` says she "Lives aboard a small Von Braun Wheel ... some humans will come and try to 'salvage' from her ship, and she puts them through near-literal Hell."
- **Conflict 1, the war's name:** the prompt equates "the War For Upper Earth" with "The Long Night War". Canon treats these as two separate wars. The War of Upper Earth ends with the 2564 Falkland Treaty, and TL `Maggie Aarden/Personal_Background/Timeline.md` says it "precedes the Long Night War chronologically". The film notes date the Long Night War to 2812.
- **Conflict 2, timing (lyrics):** TL says she survived the Long Night War, refused evacuation, and was caught "long after the Long Night War". The lyrics tie her capture to the war itself. The prompt's "after" fits canon.
- **Difference, eye damage:** TL gives heterochromia, corneas burned black, and memory loss severe enough that she lost her original name. The song's partial blindness to some visible wavelengths, with **infrared and ultraviolet vision**, is not in any character file. The two do not contradict each other.
- **Conflict 3, location:** the song says Sun-Earth L3. The film notes give the author's immediate idea as "one of the Earth-Moon Lagrange points", with the Moon visible in early shots. The GDD note says "a small Von Braun Wheel", and the author has confirmed that the wheel stays.
- **Research note on L3 ⚠️M:** Sun-Earth L3 lies on the far side of the Sun from Earth, while the point "behind the Earth" as seen from the Sun is Sun-Earth L2. So the chorus line "where sunlight dies behind the Earth" and the prompt's "L3" do not describe the same point.
- Canon's "Division 14" does not appear in the song. Her origin is "a Tepenian city (name TBD)" in TL and "Earth" in the song; these do not contradict.
