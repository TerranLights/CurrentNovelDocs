**What it is not (yet):** the extraction ran outside the 05:00-14:59 window, so **nothing was derived**. Each file ends its character sections with **questions for the derivation pass**, to be worked in the window and only with the author's decisions.

**How to read the facts:**
- **(song)** marks anything that comes only from lyrics. The author has said song content can be older or looser than current canon (confirmed for Feray Yazıcı and Estefanía Velásquez).
- **Prompts outrank lyrics**, since the lyrics were often generated from them.
- **"ChatGPT" drafts** (for example Imelda's background file, *the body elektrik*'s topic files) are generated text, not the author's statements.
- **Carryover**: song slots stamped from a template sometimes describe a different character; those are listed, never attributed.
