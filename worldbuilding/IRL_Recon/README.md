# IRL reconnaissance

The Lead Writer/Designer/Developer will be doing real-world research, reconnaissance, investigation, and data-collection. This will involve real-world travel, talking to people and understanding their lives, hardships, struggles, hopes, goals, and cultures.

## Folder Purpose

This folder will house a collection of information learned as a result of real-world travel, research, and investigation. It may contain results in forms including, but not limited to:
- writs
- audio recordings
- video recordings
- interviews
- book scans
- music
- etc.

## Planning

This will also house a currently yet-to-be-determined basis of planning for international travel and research, which may take a variety of forms.
