# [Nation Name] — National Character Profile

Template, 2026-09-19. Copy this file per post-war nation/region and fill it in — one copy per country, same
convention as this repo's other `z-template` files.

**Two layers, from two different sources:**

- **The cultural layer** comes from `../extractions/American_Nations_Extraction.md` — Part Two (Woodard's
  theory/method) and Part Four (the synthesized checklist for applying it to continents the book doesn't
  cover). Those sections are marked with their checklist point in parentheses.
- **The material layer** — *Material Position: Surplus, Deficit, and Dependency* — is an addition, not from
  Woodard. Fill it from `material-base/[Nation] - Real-World Material Base.md` and its supplements rather
  than from imagination. Woodard explains what a nation *believes*; the material files explain what it *has*.
  A nation needs both before it can plausibly trade, bargain, or be coerced.

**How to fill this in:** give as much or as little as you actually have, the same way the character-intake
vision statement works — an incomplete sheet still seeds real downstream work. The one-line summary below is
the single highest-leverage field; everything else can stay blank longer than that one.

**Where the two layers disagree, don't resolve it.** A nation whose cultural affinities point one way and
whose material dependencies point another is not a mistake in the profile — it is the most useful thing the
profile can tell you, and it is where treaties, resentments and wars come from.
