## The Decision

**Author, 2026-09-23:**

> In the post-war pre-Federation era, combine Qinghai with the Northwest. Then, once the countries begin
> connecting and unifying, it becomes part of the Sinian Federation.

**Qinghai has been drawn cross-hatched and unassigned on every map so far, pending research. That research is
done and the question is closed.** Qinghai now has a home in both eras.

| Era | Qinghai belongs to |
|---|---|
| **Early** — the broken century | **The Northwest (13B)**, alongside Shaanxi, Gansu and Ningxia |
| **Late** — after unification | **The Sinian Federation**, like the rest of the Northwest |

## The Change, Per Map

### `asia-east-early-era.png` — the broken century

**Add Qinghai to The Northwest (13B).** That state becomes **Shaanxi + Gansu + Ningxia + Qinghai**, drawn as
one contiguous territory in its existing deep cyan `#2E8CA8`.

⚠️ **Gansu and Ningxia keep their hatching; Qinghai does not.** The hatch marks the six subdivisions whose
Federation membership is still undecided — Yunnan, Guizhou, Guangxi, Gansu, Hainan and Ningxia. **Qinghai was
never one of those six**, and it is now settled, so it draws **solid**. Within one state you will therefore
have hatched Gansu and Ningxia beside solid Shaanxi and solid Qinghai. That is correct.

### `asia-east-nations.png`, `asia-east-post-court.png` — the late era

**Qinghai becomes ordinary Sinian Federation territory.** Remove the cross-hatch and the "Qinghai —
unassigned" label; fill it solid in the Federation cyan `#3FA9C9` like any other Federation province.

**Remove the "Qinghai / Amdo — unassigned" entry from the legend entirely.**

### `asia-east-sinian-federation.png` — the Federation's internal map

**Qinghai joins the Mandarin member**, which already holds Gansu and Ningxia. Fill it in Mandarin's cyan
`#4FC3D9` and remove the grey "Qinghai" annotation.

## Tibet Shrinks — and This Is the Point

**Tibet is now the Tibet Autonomous Region and nothing else.** Its `domain_territory` was "Qinghai, if the
author takes it"; the author has not taken it. Tibet's borders on the map **do not change** — they were always
TAR-only — but the possibility of extension is now closed.

**Why it went this way, in one line:** adding Qinghai to Tibet would have cut Tibet from **86.0% Tibetan to
45.6%** — a forty-point collapse costing it the majority that justifies its existence. Adding Qinghai to the
Northwest costs that state **2.96 points** of Han share. The same province is an existential problem for one
polity and a rounding error for the other.

## What the Northwest Becomes

Worth knowing, because it changes what that state *is*:

| | Shaanxi + Gansu + Ningxia | **+ Qinghai** |
|---|---|---|
| Population | 71,752,485 | **77,676,442** |
| Han | 92.77% | **89.81%** |
| Hui | 5.56% | **6.35%** |
| Tibetan | 0.70% | **2.25%** |

**It now holds 4,935,833 Hui — roughly 43% of every Hui in China, in a single polity.** Combined with the
Ma clique precedent, in which Ma Qi and Ma Bufang governed exactly Gansu, Ningxia *and* Qinghai from 1919 to
1928, this makes the Northwest **the Hui homeland** rather than merely a state that contains the Hui pale.

It also picks up **the Yellow River's source at Bayan Har** and **Qarhan, China's largest potash source**.

No label changes are needed on the map for any of that — it is context for why the shape is right.

## Do Not

- **Do not cross-hatch Qinghai on any map, ever again.** The question is closed.
- **Do not hatch Qinghai as one of the undecided six.** It was never one of them; that set is Yunnan,
  Guizhou, Guangxi, Gansu, Hainan and Ningxia, and it is unchanged.
- **Do not extend Tibet.** It stays TAR-only.
- **Do not put a date on any map.**
- **Do not change anything else** — borders, palette, projection, legend structure, captions and city sets all
  stay as they are.
