# World Nations & Regions Roster — Post-2083 World

Running log, started 2026-09-18, building toward the "30+ countries" scope for the saga's convergence cast.
Logged as the author works through each region — not a finished list, expect ongoing additions. Distinct from
`Falkland-Treaty/`'s Assembly-of-Nations signatory list (a narrower, later-era subset of whichever of these
nations actually joined the United Assembly of Nations) — this file is the broader world-geography roster this
saga's cast gets drawn from, whether or not a given nation ends up an Assembly member.

**Source key:** **[MAP]** — `InnerTepeniaGDD/Reference/Images/Maps/North America with tentative labels.jpg`.
**[ASSEMBLY]** — the drafted Falkland Treaty's confirmed signatory list. **[CHAR]** — inferred from an existing
character's own file. **[AUTHOR 2026-09-18]** — stated directly by the author this session, not yet documented
anywhere else in the shared universe.

## North America — fully mapped (one open thread)

**See `worldbuilding/extractions/American_Nations_to_New_North_America_Cross-Reference.md`** (repo
root level) for how these 12 regions line up against Colin Woodard's *American Nations* county-level regional
cultures — the analytical basis for this map — including several real state-line-vs-county-level discrepancies
worth a look (Cascadia and the CSA each collapse multiple Woodard regions into one; a striking independent

All 12 from the map, confirmed **[MAP]**:

  Territory: Alaska minus the Panhandle, plus Yukon, Northwest Territories and Nunavut. The real-world capital,
  Juneau, is Cascadian in this setting, so the choice was open; Whitehorse was selected over the larger
  candidates for reasons recorded in `material-base/Alaska Republic - Oil, Gas, Agriculture and Energy
  (supplement).md` under "The capital problem."
- **The People's Democratic Republic of Cascadia** — also **[ASSEMBLY]**. Earlier in its history it was simply
  the **Republic of Cascadia**, and became the People's Democratic Republic at some later point; the date and
  circumstances of that transition are not yet worked out. Worth noting this is one of two nations on the list
  carrying the "People's Democratic Republic" formula (alongside New England) — whether that reflects a shared
  political current is open.
- **Colorado** (Colorado Republic) — also **[ASSEMBLY]**.
- **Sonora** (Republic of Sonora) — also **[ASSEMBLY]**.
- **The Prairie Federation** — also **[ASSEMBLY]**.
- **The Midwest Republic** (colloq. "Midwestland") — also **[ASSEMBLY]**; Maggie's own place of origin.
- **New England** (People's Democratic Republic of New England) — also **[ASSEMBLY]**.
- **Appalachia** (Appalachia Republic) — also **[ASSEMBLY]**.
- **The CSA** — also **[ASSEMBLY]** (as "the CSA," tentative naming per that file); covers the full Deep South
  (Texas through Florida), not just Texas — Mallory Dufay's file describes her as "from the CSA (Texas)."
  **[CHAR]**
- **Quebec** — previously only a tentative map label, now confirmed as its own labeled region.
- **Greenland** — same as Quebec, previously tentative, now confirmed.
- **DMZ** — a small demilitarized zone around the Great Lakes/Ontario border area. **Genuinely new, unexplained
  find** — not on the Assembly list, not mentioned in any character file surveyed so far. What conflict or
  settlement produced it is completely open.

**Open:** Mexico and Central America are shown on the map but left entirely uncolored/unlabeled — no successor
nation(s) assigned there yet. Author has not yet worked out the rest of Latin America either **[AUTHOR
2026-09-18]**.

### East Asia — ✅ COMPLETE, migrated to canon 2026-09-24

Fully named and rendered: the **Sinian Federation** (Han core, federation of linguistic nations — Mandarin,
Wu, Yue, Min, Hakka, Xiang, Gan, Jin), **Zhongyuan** and **Qin** (the two other named Han successor states),
**North Korea** and **South Korea**, plus separate polities for **Manchuria, East Turkestan/Xinjiang, Tibet
and Inner Mongolia** (none of which ever join the Federation) — every nation also has a researched
native-language name (震旦联邦 for the Sinian Federation, 한국 for unified Korea, etc.). A six-map linear
sequence (A→F) carries the whole history from Mongolia's unification through the 2318 Jeju-do tribunal, with
real in-universe years and epoch summaries overlaid on every map. Final maps live at
`Doll-Fi/media/Reference/TepenianUniverseTimeline/Worldspace/Locations/Earth/Upper_Earth/Asia_East/`; the full
working package (CSVs, renderer, every decision's provenance) is at `y-files/Map Files/Asia (East)/
08 follow-up - all visuals fully updated/instruction set/`. See STATUS tracker §3 and §4.1 for every settled
decision (the southern-border question, Qinghai/Qin, Jeju-do's three-power condominium, etc.) — all resolved,
nothing left open here.

### Southeast Asia — ✅ COMPLETE, both mainland and maritime, 2026-09-26

Unlike East Asia, this map makes almost no partition decisions — Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia,
Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Brunei, Singapore and Timor-Leste all keep their real-world names and
(mostly) real-world borders. **The one exception**: Thailand's Deep South (Pattani, Yala, Narathiwat) becomes
its own independent nation after the 2083 war — ⚠️ **still needs its final name** (Patani is the obvious
candidate, not yet asserted; this is the region's one open Stage 5 item). Indonesia is internally divided into
8 polities (island-group blocs, Aceh and West Papua carved out as their own full blocks). Both the mainland
map (plus a mandala/sphere overlay version) and the maritime map are built and rendered as two official pairs
each; see STATUS tracker §4.3-§4.4 for full detail.

### West Asia — IN PROGRESS, all three sub-regions through Stage 3, two through Stage 4

Covers Kazakhstan/the other "-stans" (Central Asia), Turkey/the Middle East/Afghanistan, and — added
2026-09-28 — Georgia/Azerbaijan/Armenia (the South Caucasus). All three have real, sourced Stage 1 (frameworks)
and Stage 2 (material) surveys and Stage 3 map-data packages. **Stage 4 (rendering) is done for Central Asia
and the Middle East** as Woodard-style culture/ethnicity maps (culture is the primary drawn layer, not
political borders) — see `y-files/Map Files/Asia (West)/`, currently mid-way through an active region-by-region
"divisions" review (which pieces stay as-is vs. get redrawn for an eventual nations layer). South Caucasus is
folded into the Middle East/composite maps rather than rendered standalone. No nation names assigned yet
anywhere in West Asia — still Stage 4/in-progress-divisions, well before Stage 5. Full detail: STATUS tracker
§4.8, which is being kept current turn-by-turn for this region specifically.

**Open:** South Asia and Egypt/North Africa remain out of scope by explicit author decision (deferred to
future regions, not part of West Asia). Africa beyond that remains entirely undocumented.

## Oceania

**✅ COMPLETE, 2026-09-26** — updated 2026-09-28; this section previously described Australia/NZ/the Pacific
as entirely undocumented, which stopped being true once this region was chosen (over Russia) as the priority
after Latin America/East Asia. Two companion maps, 23 nations total, built and WebUI-confirmed across three
rounds (a framing fix and a flat-color polish pass). Full detail: STATUS tracker §4.6.

- **The Sovereign Republic of Hawaii** — **[ASSEMBLY]**; a post-2083 successor nation. U.R.U.K. (the
  Underwater Research Union: Kappa), where the first sentient robot En.Ki.Du was created in 2083, sits in what
  later became Hawaiian territory, but **the facility itself predates the war and was under United States
  dominion at the time [AUTHOR 2026-09-18 correction]** — the Sovereign Republic of Hawaii didn't exist yet
  when U.R.U.K. was built or when En.Ki.Du was created there. Also home to Doris Morikawa's Hall of Archives, a
  "politically and economically neutral zone" for pre-war knowledge preservation. **A dedicated National
  Character Profile exists**: `Sovereign Republic of Hawaii - National Character Profile.md` — Hawaii is
  severely food/fuel import-dependent (85-90% food imported, 80%+ fuel imported), in sharp tension with its
  "neutral haven" identity. Hawaii's own North America map inset (corner-box, Pacific Ocean) is also done.
- **Australia and New Zealand** — both stay intact, not fractured, per real research supporting the author's
  own initial hypotheses: Australia's 87-95% coastal population concentration and NZ's own considered 1901
  non-federation choice both hold up, though Australia's real 1933 Western Australia secession vote (66% for,
  blocked only by the Constitution) is flagged as genuine historical secessionism the "won't fracture" case
  has to argue past, not one the record settles automatically.
- **The rest of the Pacific** — 20+ further nations/territories across the two rendered maps; not
  individually re-listed here, see the map-data package and STATUS tracker §4.6 for the full roster.

## South America

**✅ DONE — fully divided, rendered, and author-confirmed.** Updated 2026-09-28; this section was stale since
2026-09-23 and previously described the region as "no nations named yet," which is no longer accurate. Covers
everything south of Sonora (all Mexico below the northern tier, Central America, the Caribbean, and all of
South America including the Guianas) — the entire map from the US/Mexico border to Cape Horn, plus every
Caribbean island, now belongs to a specific, named nation. Full detail, region-by-region reasoning, and every
settled decision live in `worldbuilding/STATUS - Cross-Session Progress Tracker.md` (§3, the Latin America
block) — not duplicated here.

- **`../extractions/Latin America - Regional Frameworks and Colonial Origins (survey).md`** — the analytical
  basis. There is no direct Woodard equivalent, but **Darcy Ribeiro's *As Américas e a Civilização* (1970)**
  makes the same founding-population move 41 years earlier, and his *O Povo Brasileiro* (1995) is effectively
  *American Nations* for Brazil.
- **`material-base/Latin America - Material and Economic Base (survey).md`** — what the territory physically
  holds, with economic-complexity data and a post-collapse assessment.
- **Rendered maps**: `y-files/Map Files/Latin America/02 reorganization/08 follow-up/map-7-nations.png` (full
  map) and `map-mexico-central-america-caribbean.png` (dedicated crop), both author-confirmed 2026-09-25 —
  "everything checks out."

**Every nation is either kept at its real-world name or has a final, settled invented one** — no more open
naming questions of the "Deep South Thailand" or "West Papua boundary" kind anywhere on this map:

- **Amazonia** (new, trans-national, capital Manaus) — carved from pieces of Brazil, Peru, Colombia, Bolivia
  and Venezuela on a road-isolation/connectivity logic, with one deliberate exception (Venezuela's Bolívar).
- **La República de Yucatán** (new) — Chiapas, Tabasco, Campeche, Yucatán, Quintana Roo (Mexico) + Petén
  (Guatemala), echoing the real 1841–48 Republic of Yucatán.
- **Saint Martin** (new) — reunifies the whole island plus Saint Barthélemy; official bilingual nation
  (French/Dutch), a character note beyond the border itself.
- **Araucanía-Patagonia Autonomous Zone [APAZ]** / **La Zona Autónoma de Patagonia y Araucanía [ZAPA]** (new,
  named 2026-09-28) — the demilitarized joint condominium of Argentina and Chile covering Wallmapu/Mapuche
  territory in the north and geographically isolated Patagonia in the south.
- **Everything else** — Argentina, Chile, Uruguay, Paraguay, Brazil, Venezuela, Guyana/Suriname/French Guiana
  (a trade union, not merged), Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, Colombia, and every Central American and Caribbean
  nation — keeps its real-world name, at real-world or lightly-adjusted borders per the STATUS tracker's own
  settled-decisions record.

**Still open, not blocking**: "Virgin Islands" and "French Antilles"/"Aruba-Curaçao" remain working names for
three smaller Caribbean groupings, not yet finalized.

**Still structurally significant**, since the saga's own environ notes (`../../background notes/environ notes.md`)
place a setting shift through South America en route to the Falkland Islands.
