# Eastern Ukraine and Crimea — Decision Log and Open Items (working)

**Opened 2026-09-30.** The running record of what the author has decided about the eastern-Ukraine territory and Crimea, what is only assumed, and what is deferred.
The evidence and the options are in `Eastern Ukraine - Candidate Border Lines (working).md`; the map is `eastern-ukraine-proposed-line-v2.png`; the unit-level record is
`map-data/designations.csv`. **Status marks:** ✅ settled by the author, 🟡 a working assumption not yet confirmed, ⏳ deferred to a later session, ⚠️ open.

## 1. Settled by the author
- ✅ **The territory (2026-09-30).** Luhansk, Donetsk, Kharkiv, Dnipropetrovsk and Zaporizhzhia oblasts **in full**; Kherson Oblast **only east of the Dnieper**
  (about 75% of the oblast by area). The villages the author named (Shulhivka, Prydniprianske, Natalyne, Sydorenkove, Kolontaiv, Kaplunivka, Korbyni Ivany,
  Tymofiivka) only trace the border; each lies within 10 km of an existing oblast border, so the border is the oblast border everywhere except along the Dnieper
  through Kherson Oblast.
- ✅ **Crimea goes to Donska-Kubania (2026-09-30).** Donska-Kubania is the Russia project's nation (Krasnodar, Rostov, Stavropol, Adygea, Kalmykia, Volgograd,
  Astrakhan; capital Rostov-on-Don). The Kerch Strait joins Crimea to Krasnodar Krai.
- ✅ **Crimea is an Autonomous Zone inside Donska-Kubania (2026-09-30).** The author's reason: during the Ukrainian period Crimea was an autonomous unit through
  almost the whole time (the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, ⚠️ from 1991; earlier a Crimean ASSR inside the Russian SFSR, 1921 to 1945, ⚠️ recalled), so the
  designation continues what it already was. It also carries the Crimean Tatar question (about 12% in 2001) and the distance from Rostov (about 700 km by road).
