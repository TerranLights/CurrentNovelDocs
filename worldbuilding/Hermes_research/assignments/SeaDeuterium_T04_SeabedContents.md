## What to find

**Part A: deuterium ratios.** Find ★ measured deuterium-to-hydrogen values, written as δD in per mil (‰) or as a D/H ratio (state which), for:
- ordinary seawater (the standard, VSMOW)
- surface seawater in the Southern Ocean (by region, if given)
- the pore water in seafloor sediments
- hydrothermal vent fluids
- clay minerals and serpentinites from the seafloor or oceanic crust
- methane gas hydrates (the water part and the methane part, as separate entries)
- mid-ocean-ridge basalt (MORB) and mantle water

**Part B: lithium.** Find:
- ★ the lithium concentration in seawater (in mg per liter)
- ★ lithium in seafloor sediments, brines and hydrothermal fluids
- the methods that have been tried to take lithium from seawater (adsorbents, membranes, electrochemical). For each: the amount recovered, the energy used and who tested it.

**Part C: uranium.** Find:
- ★ uranium concentration in seawater
- the adsorbent recovery trials: grams of uranium per kilogram of adsorbent, the soak time, the site and who tested it
- uranium in seafloor phosphates and sediments

**Part D: helium-3.** Find:
- helium isotope ratios (³He/⁴He, in R/Ra) in mid-ocean-ridge vent fluids and plumes
- any published estimate of how much helium-3 these carry (an amount or concentration)

**Part E: seabed deposits in the Southern Ocean.** Find which of these have been reported south of 50°S, with locations: polymetallic nodules, ferromanganese crusts, seafloor massive sulfides, phosphorites.

**Search words to try:** `deuterium hydrogen isotope ratio serpentinite delta D`, `delta D seawater VSMOW Southern Ocean surface`, `delta D pore water marine sediment`, `delta D gas hydrate water methane`, `lithium seawater adsorbent recovery kg`, `uranium seawater adsorbent grams per kilogram trial`, `helium-3 hydrothermal plume mid-ocean ridge`, `Southern Ocean polymetallic nodules distribution`.
