## What to find

Find known hydrothermal vent fields and geothermal sites in or near the Southern Ocean. Include:
- the East Scotia Ridge vent fields
- the Bransfield Strait vents
- any vent fields on the Southwest Indian Ridge, the Pacific-Antarctic Ridge, or near the Kerguelen or Balleny Islands
- geothermal sites on land or islands in the region (for example Deception Island and Mount Erebus)

**For each site, find:**
- its name and its latitude and longitude
- its depth below the sea surface
- the temperature of the fluid or the ground
- the year it was found and the survey ship or team
- ★ the vent fluid flow rate or heat output, where published
- its distance from the nearest land

**Search words to try:** `East Scotia Ridge hydrothermal vent temperature depth`, `Bransfield Strait hydrothermal vents`, `Southern Ocean hydrothermal vent fields list InterRidge database`, `Deception Island geothermal temperature`, `Mount Erebus lava lake heat output`.
