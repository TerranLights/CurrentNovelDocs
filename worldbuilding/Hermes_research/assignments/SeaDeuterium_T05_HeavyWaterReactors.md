## What to find

Use Canadian, European, American, East Asian and other non-South-Asian sources (see Avoid, below).

**Find:**
- ★ how much heavy water a reactor of this type holds, per megawatt of electric power (tonnes of D₂O per MWe)
- ★ how much tritium these reactors make per reactor per year, and how much of it is recovered (kg or grams)
- the world total of tritium held by stores and by recovery plants, and where
- how a tritium removal plant works (one paragraph, copied, with the source)
- the fuel used (natural uranium or other) and the tonnes of fuel loaded per year per reactor
- the number of heavy-water power reactors running in the world, by country, and their combined power. List South Asian ones only by count in `Open threads`.
- the largest heavy-water stocks, and how much heavy water is held in total
- how heavy-water reactors use the heavy water: as moderator, as coolant, or both
- ★ the price of reactor-grade heavy water (US dollars per kilogram, with the year)

**Search words to try:** `CANDU heavy water inventory tonnes per MWe`, `CANDU tritium production per reactor per year grams`, `Darlington tritium removal facility kilograms tritium`, `heavy water price per kg reactor grade`, `tritium inventory world stockpile kg 2025`, `pressurized heavy water reactors operating worldwide IAEA PRIS`.
