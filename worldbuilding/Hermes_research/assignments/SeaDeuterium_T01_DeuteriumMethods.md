## What to find

For each of these five methods, find the items below:
1. Girdler-sulfide (also called GS or dual-temperature water-hydrogen sulfide exchange)
2. Ammonia-hydrogen exchange
3. Combined electrolysis and catalytic exchange (CECE)
4. Water distillation
5. Any other method a primary source names for heavy-water production (for example laser or membrane methods), one entry each

**Find for each method:**
- ★ energy used per kilogram of deuterium, or per kilogram of heavy water (D₂O). State which.
- ★ how much seawater or feed water is processed per kilogram of deuterium or of heavy water
- the share of the deuterium recovered from the feed (recovery fraction)
- the working temperatures and pressures
- the chemicals used and their hazards (for example hydrogen sulfide, ammonia, hydrogen)
- the size of a known plant (output per year), where one exists
- whether any plant runs now, or ran in the past (name, country, years). Skip South Asian plants (see Avoid, below).
- how many stages or towers it needs

**Search words to try:** `Girdler sulfide process energy consumption per kg heavy water`, `heavy water production methods review`, `deuterium from seawater extraction energy`, `CECE process heavy water production`, `ammonia hydrogen exchange deuterium`, `IAEA technical reports heavy water reactors status`.

**Prefer:** IAEA reports, national laboratory reports, handbooks of nuclear chemical engineering, review papers.
