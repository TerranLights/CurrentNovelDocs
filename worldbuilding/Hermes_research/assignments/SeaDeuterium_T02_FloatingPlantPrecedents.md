## What to find

Find the following for each of these: floating liquefied natural gas plants (FLNG), floating production, storage and offloading vessels (FPSO), ocean thermal energy conversion (OTEC) platforms (built or tested), floating desalination plants or barges, and seawater uranium or lithium recovery trials at sea.

**For each named example (at least 2 for each type), find:**
- the name, the owner, the year it began operating
- its mass or displacement
- its power supply and the power it uses
- its crew (people on board)
- the largest waves or wind it was designed for (the "design sea state")
- whether it has worked in cold water or ice (and where)
- its main hazards
- the mooring method (anchored, turret, dynamic positioning)

**Search words to try:** `FLNG crew size displacement design sea state`, `FPSO crew complement design wave height`, `OTEC platform pilot plant power output`, `floating desalination barge capacity`, `seawater uranium extraction at sea trial adsorbent`, `FPSO ice class Arctic`.
