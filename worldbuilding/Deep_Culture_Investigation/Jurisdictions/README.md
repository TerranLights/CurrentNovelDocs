# Jurisdictions

**Material for the law research of Capricorn and Libra**, the only places DCI researches law (author, 2026-11-10). One folder per pre-war state, province or country (or tribal nation with its own courts), holding its legal profile, `01 - Legal.md` (DCI Stage 1): part A, the legal system; part B, the legal orientation by domain. How each culture views law is researched in Capricorn and Libra, per unit. Each profile is written once and reused by every unit and target that includes the jurisdiction.
