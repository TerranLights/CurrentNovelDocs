# Deep Culture Investigation (DCI): results

How the people of each culture represented in the Tepenian Federation understand and approach each concept, using the twelve zodiac signs as categories and their concepts as subcategories. The signs are research classifiers only.

**The method** (runbook, templates, concept lists, source registry, regional frameworks) is in DerivationMechanisms `Environment/DCI [Deep Culture Investigation]/`. Start with its `RUNBOOK.md`.

**Location:** these results are staged here for now. Once the methodology is sorted, they move to the Derivation Mechanisms repo (author, 2026-11-02).

| Folder or file | What goes there |
|---|---|
| `MASTER TRACKER.md` | Every target and unit, with its status. **Read first when resuming.** |
| `Units/<unit>/` | The unit library: each regional culture, ethnic identity or Indigenous people, researched once and reused by every target that contains it (Stages 1-4) |
| `Jurisdictions/<jurisdiction>/` | Material for the law research of Capricorn and Libra, the only places DCI researches law: each pre-war state, province or country (or tribal nation with its own courts), with its legal profile, written once and reused |
| `Targets/<target>/` | Each successor nation: its scope, progress tracker, composites and divergence (Stages 0, 5 and 6) |
| `Data/` | Values extracted from datasets, with citations. Raw datasets stay out of git. |
| `_dispatch/` | The briefs actually sent to research agents, saved before launch |

**Pilot:** Cascadia, one sign at a time from Aries (author, 2026-11-02).

**Rules:** this repo is public, so quotations are short (a sentence or less) and cited, and no copyrighted books, raw datasets or third-party images are added. Research runs at any hour; Stage 6 (divergence) is derivation, 05:00-14:59, with the author.
