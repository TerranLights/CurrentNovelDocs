# Which nations are represented in the Federation's cities (draft, by subnet and city)

**Purpose:** to make it easier to prioritize the DCI research. For every chartered city of the Tepenian Federation it lists the nations that live there (the census's tiers), and then rolls the same data up by DCI target.

**Source:** the Inner Tepenia GDD's `Official_Population_Census.md` (`games/Inner Tepenia/InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/`), Section A (National Origin Composition by City), with the city populations from its Section B (Census I, the pre-orbital peak). Section A already writes every origin in its **latest-period form**, so the names are the DCI targets themselves; no mapping was needed. **The copy of the census under CurrentNovelDocs `reference/` is out of date** (older, pre-war country names, 712 lines against the GDD's 892); this draft uses the GDD file.

**Tiers (the census's own):** **Primary** = dominant national community, shapes the city's main language and culture; **Significant** = distinct, visible communities; **Notable** = present, background texture. A **(founding wave)** tag means the nation is named as the city's founder in the Founding Register.

**Not covered here:** the Orbital Era (Census II), Concordia City's districts beyond its one row, robots (Kunlun and Dome Fuji are robots-only places: their mix is the heritage of the robots living there), and the lost cities of Section D.
