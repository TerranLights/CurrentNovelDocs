**What to look for** (starting points, not limits; anything marked "check" is from general knowledge and must be confirmed or dropped):
- Barman, *The West Beyond the West*; Harris, *The Resettlement of British Columbia*; the Canadian Encyclopedia and the *Encyclopedia of British Columbia*.
- **Political culture:** there is no Elazar for Canada; the nearest equivalents are Wiseman, *In Search of Canadian Political Culture* (2007), and the fragment theory of Hartz and Horowitz. BC's polarized party history (Social Credit against the NDP) belongs here.

- **Formative events:** the Fraser rush; Confederation (1871) and the railway promise; the Chinese head tax (1885-1923) and the 1923 exclusion act; the 1907 Vancouver riot; the Japanese Canadian internment and dispossession (1942); Expo 86; the residential schools (the Northwest Coast unit covers them in detail; note here how settler society came to terms with them).
