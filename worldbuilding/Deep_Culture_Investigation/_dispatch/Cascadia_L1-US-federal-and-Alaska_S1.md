**What to look for** (starting points, not limits; anything marked "check" is from general knowledge and must be confirmed or dropped):
- **United States (federal):** English common law, received colony by colony; precedent and the federal courts. **All of Cascadia's U.S. states and Alaska sit in the Ninth Circuit,** so its precedent is a regional legal layer of its own: say what that means. Constitutional review by the ordinary courts (*Marbury v. Madison*); civil and criminal juries; what is federal and what is left to the states (criminal law, family and property law are mainly state law).
- **Federal law on Native nations** belongs here as the frame for the tribal profiles: the Marshall trilogy, plenary power, *Oliphant* (1978), Public Law 280 (1953), the Indian Civil Rights Act (1968), ICWA (1978) and *Haaland v. Brackeen* (2023), IGRA (1988), the VAWA tribal jurisdiction of 2013 and its 2022 expansion. Summarize; the tribal profiles (job L6) go into detail.
- **Part B, federal:** federal taxes and welfare; the Controlled Substances Act; federal gun law and *Heller* (2008) and *Bruen* (2022); the First Amendment; the federal death penalty; surveillance law (FISA, the Patriot Act) and emergency powers. Measures: Caughey and Warshaw (national trends), the Correlates of State Policy Project.
- **Alaska:** the 1956 constitution (statehood 1959); the unified court system; merit selection of judges with retention elections (check); boroughs instead of counties; the constitutional right to privacy and *Ravin v. State* (1975); the Permanent Fund dividend; the optional community-property act (1998, check); Public Law 280 (mandatory since 1958, except Metlakatla, check); ANCSA (1971) and *Alaska v. Native Village of Venetie* (1998); the state's 2022 recognition of tribes (check); constitutional carry (2003, check); marijuana (2014); ranked-choice voting (2020) and the attempt to repeal it (check). Write it for the **whole state**; the Panhandle (Juneau, the capital) is one area of variation against Anchorage, the Mat-Su and the rural Bush. This profile also serves the Alaska target (decision 11).
