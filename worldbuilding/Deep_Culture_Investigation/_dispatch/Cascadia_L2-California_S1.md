**What to look for** (starting points, not limits; anything marked "check" is from general knowledge and must be confirmed or dropped):
- **Part A:** the Spanish and Mexican civil-law layer and how California replaced it with common law in 1850, while keeping **community property** from it (check the statute and the 1849 constitution); the Field Code and the codes of 1872, a common-law state with codes; how judges are chosen (appointment and retention elections for the appellate courts; elected superior-court judges; check); the initiative, referendum and recall (1911) as a source of law; the California Supreme Court's constitutional review.
- **Part B:** taxes and Proposition 13 (1978); regulation, labor and welfare; CEQA, rent control and the state's zoning reforms (for example SB 9, 2021); business law; drugs, cannabis (2016), gambling, marriage and reproduction; gun law (the RAND database); speech and religion; policing and sentencing (Propositions 47 in 2014 and 36 in 2024, the death-penalty moratorium of 2019, check), incarceration; privacy (the CCPA) and government reach. Measures: Caughey and Warshaw; Cato's *Freedom in the 50 States* (tagged practitioner); the Fraser Institute's EFNA (practitioner); RAND; DPIC; BJS.
- **Variation inside the jurisdiction:** counties whose sheriffs, prosecutors and ordinances differ from Sacramento's (for example the recall of San Francisco's district attorney in 2022 and the 2024 defeat of Los Angeles's, check), and which units those counties belong to: California holds three settler units (Left Coast, Far West, El Norte), so map the variation onto them using `Data/Unit_Territories.md`.
