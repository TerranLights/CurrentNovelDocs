**Rules:**

- **Native, Indigenous or Aboriginal, never "Indian"** (author, 2026-11-10, out of respect for the Indigenous peoples and nations of this land). In your own words say Native, Indigenous or Aboriginal; in Canada also First Nations, Inuit or Métis; in Mexico, Indigenous or *pueblos indígenas*. Keep as published only what isn't yours to rename: published titles and names (the *Handbook of North American Indians*), a nation's own name, quotations, and census category names (write: Native, the census category "American Indian and Alaska Native"). - **No South Asian works as sources.** A modern study that would be set aside only because of an author's name goes in `held_for_review_<name>.md` beside your output, not into the text.
