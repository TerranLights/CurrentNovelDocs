**Founders first:** the Jesuit mission province (Loreto 1697, the oldest European founding in Cascadia, then 17 missions) and the soldiers' and settlers' descendants who became the ranchers of the sierras; then the Franciscans and Dominicans, mining (El Triunfo; the French copper works at Santa Rosalía), La Paz, territorial status until statehood (1974), mainland migration and the Los Cabos tourist economy. The Guaycura, Pericú and southern Cochimí, who did not survive the mission period, are part of the founding history, not a living unit.
