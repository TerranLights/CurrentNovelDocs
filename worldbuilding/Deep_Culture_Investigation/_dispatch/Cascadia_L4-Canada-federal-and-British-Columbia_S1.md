- **Canada, Part B:** the Canada Health Act and federal transfers; firearms law (the 2020 prohibition and the 2022-2023 handgun freeze, check); cannabis (2018); MAID (2016); abortion with no criminal law since *Morgentaler* (1988); hate-speech law; the end of the death penalty (1976); the Emergencies Act's use in 2022 and the Federal Court's 2024 ruling on it (check); surveillance and national-security law.
- **British Columbia, Part A:** the reception of English law (the English Law Act's 1858 date, check); Westminster government; the BC courts and how judges are appointed; family property under the Family Law Act (2013); DRIPA (2019), the first provincial UNDRIP statute; the Provincial Court's Indigenous Courts (check).
- **British Columbia, Part B:** the carbon tax (2008) and the end of its consumer charge in 2025 (check); the speculation and vacancy tax and the 2023 housing reforms; rent and tenancy law; the drug-decriminalization pilot (2023) and its later changes (check its status in 2026); gun and policing policy within federal limits; government reach.
- **Variation inside British Columbia:** the coast against the interior (use `Data/Unit_Territories.md`), municipal politics (Vancouver against the interior cities), and RCMP against municipal policing.
