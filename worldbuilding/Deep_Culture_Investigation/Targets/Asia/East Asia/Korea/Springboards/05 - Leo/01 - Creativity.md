# Korea: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja for craft and classical terms); Jeju-eo; northern usage for the North. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Heritage-listed master craftspeople against commercial studios and designers. Seoul's pop-culture and webtoon makers against regional craft towns. Jeju makers working in island materials. Northern state studios against market-era private making. Home hobbyists and school art students. Marriage migrants who bring their own crafts. Ritual specialists who make ritual objects. Older and younger generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on industry and participation; mixed on how people themselves define creativity; thin for the North, mostly state sources. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for craft terms; English for scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ritual objects and some shamanic crafts are not for outsiders. Northern art is state-ordered; read it as such. Ownership and copying disputes that turn on statute go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search RISS and KCI for 창의성 and 창작 with 문화.
