# Korea: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja for classical terms); Jeju-eo; northern usage for the North. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Churchgoers, temple members and nonreligious givers. Rural villages with labor exchange and apartment-block neighbors. Chaebol-family foundations against small donors. Older givers and younger givers who may choose different causes. Gentry-descended households and others with local giving legends. Jeju neighbors and kin networks. Marriage migrants who send money abroad. Seoul against provincial towns. Northern households and defectors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on donation and volunteering; mixed on private, informal giving and favors; thin and state-framed for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for gift and ritual terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Gift-money and favor norms can touch on bribery cases, which belong to Libra and Capricorn. Northern "voluntary" giving may be compelled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
