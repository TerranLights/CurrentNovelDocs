*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja for formal and historical terms); Jeju-eo; northern usage for the North. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Students in schools with heavy bullying against others. Junior and senior workers, and employees against customers and owners. Conscripts and their officers. Service staff and delivery workers. Women and men in how insult is felt. Teens and adults online. Celebrities and trainees under public pile-ons. Jeju islanders and descendants of labeled families. Marriage migrants and migrant workers facing slurs. Descendants of colonial-era and wartime victims. Northern residents and defectors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on bullying, work and online talk; mixed on how people name private shame; thin and secondhand for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; slang and slurs shift quickly, so date every term.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Minors and suicide-linked cases: do not name victims. Wartime and colonial humiliation is politically charged. Defamation and insult statutes go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
