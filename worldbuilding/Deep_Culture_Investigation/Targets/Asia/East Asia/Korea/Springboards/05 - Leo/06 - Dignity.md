# Korea: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja for classical and formal terms); Jeju-eo; northern usage for the North. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Older adults in poverty against those with pensions and family help. Contract and subcontract workers against salaried staff. Hospital and care-home residents. Families who claim old gentry descent against newer urban households. Survivors and bereaved of Gwangju, the war and the ferry. Marriage migrants and migrant workers. Jeju islanders. Women and men, in how bearing is judged. Christian, Buddhist and nonreligious views of the dying. Northern residents and defectors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on elders, work and welfare; mixed on how ordinary people define dignity; thin and one-sided for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for classical texts; English for rights scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Survivor and bereaved testimonies call for care. End-of-life and human-rights statutes go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
