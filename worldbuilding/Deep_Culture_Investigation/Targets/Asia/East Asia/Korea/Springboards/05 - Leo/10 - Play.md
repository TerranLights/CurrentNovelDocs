# Korea: Play (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jeju-eo; northern usage for the North. **Concept in one line:** activity done for its own sake, set apart from ordinary life by rules or make-believe: games, sport as play, pretending, joking, toying.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Children with after-school classes against those with free afternoons. Village children and apartment-block children. Boys and girls. Gamers and PC-room regulars against occasional players. Older adults at park card tables against younger adults at game cafes. Jeju islanders. Children of marriage migrants. Performers and festival audiences. Northern children in organized programs and those outside them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on games, time use and folk games; mixed on unscheduled play; thin for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; regional dialect words for games vary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Gambling and game-addiction material involves minors and private lives. Gambling regulation goes to Libra and Capricorn. Northern organized play is state-managed.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
