# Korea: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja for classical terms and titles); Jeju-eo; northern usage for the North. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Business-family heads against salaried managers and start-up founders. Military officers and conscripts. Village heads and neighborhood-association leaders against city apartment-block officers. Student presidents and class monitors. Women leaders in politics and business. Older bosses and younger staff who use words like 꼰대. Jeju fishing-village leaders. Political leaders in different regions. Northern party cadres and local officials.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on business, politics and surveys; mixed on informal and household leadership; thin and one-sided for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for classical texts and titles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Presidential and party figures are politically charged; read across parties. The northern leader cult is state-controlled material.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
