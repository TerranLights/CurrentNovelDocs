# Korea: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja are needed for classical and heritage records); Jeju-eo; northern usage for the North. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Houses with a clan seat and genealogy against households without. Families of independence fighters, veterans and the war dead. Families of the accused and wrongly branded, such as Jeju's. Men and women, where family honor has fallen unevenly. Confucian-ritual households, Christians, Buddhists and the nonreligious. Military and police against civilians. Business owners and salaried staff. Seoul against provincial towns. Northern families by class background and defectors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the classical period and the state's honors; mixed on how ordinary people use honor now; thin and state-shaped for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja and some classical Chinese; English for scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Honor tied to women's chastity and to the war dead is painful; handle with care. Defamation and military-code statutes go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
