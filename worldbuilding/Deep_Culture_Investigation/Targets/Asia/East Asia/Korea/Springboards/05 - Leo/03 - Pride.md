# Korea: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja for older and formal terms); Jeju-eo; northern usage for the North. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Korean | 자부심 (jabusim) | pride in one's worth or work | The common "proud" word |
| Korean | 자존심 (jajonsim) | self-respect, wounded pride | Compare with 자존감 (self-esteem) |
| Korean | 긍지 (geungji) | dignified pride | Formal register |
| Korean | 뿌듯하다 (ppeutdeutada) | pleasantly full, proud | Felt-pride word |
| Korean | 잘난 척 / 으스대다 (jallan cheok / eusedaeda) | showing off / swaggering | Pride's blamed side |
| Korean | 교만 / 거만 (gyoman / geoman) | arrogance | Moral writing |
| Korean | 가문의 영광 (gamun-ui yeonggwang) | "glory of the house" | Family pride phrase |
| Korean | 민족적 자긍심 (minjokjeok jagungsim) | ethnic pride | Survey and school usage |
| Korean | 국뽕 (gukppong) | slang for excessive national pride | Mocking word *check* |
| Korean | 애향심 (aehyangsim) | love of one's home region | Local pride |
| Korean | 우리식 (urisik) | "our style", a northern slogan word | State usage *check* |

English keywords: Korean pride, national pride, self-esteem, family honor, regional pride, hubris, achievement.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Yeongnam and Honam regions against Seoul and the capital area. Jeju islanders and their island pride. Graduates of elite schools and those without them. Business-family and worker households. Older generations who lived through rapid growth and younger ones raised with K-culture. Gwangju and Daegu local memory. Migrants and returnee adoptees. Churchgoers and nonreligious people. Northern residents and defectors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on national pride in surveys; thin on private, personal pride; North only through state sources and defector accounts. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English for the survey codebooks.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Pride tied to the colonial period, the war and the division is charged; read for variation, not one voice. Textbook disputes are political.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull the Korea tables from the ISSP national identity data.

3. Search BIGKinds for 국뽕 by year.
4. Open the unification survey question lists for identity items.
5. Read the table of contents of Shin Gi-wook's book for sources.
