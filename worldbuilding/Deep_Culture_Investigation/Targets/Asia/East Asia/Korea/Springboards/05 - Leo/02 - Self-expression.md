## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Teens and young adults against older generations. Seoul's youth districts against provincial towns. Idol trainees and fan communities against ordinary students. Women and men, whose expected restraint may differ. Workplace and school settings against home and friends. Jeju residents and outsiders who moved there. Sexual and gender minorities. Christian and nonreligious communities. Northern residents and defectors speaking from outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South in surveys and online talk; mixed on private feeling; thin and state-controlled for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English loanwords and slang change fast, so check dates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Gender and sexuality expression and political speech are touchy; the laws on speech go to Libra and Capricorn. Northern dress and speech rules come through defector accounts.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
