# Korea: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jeju-eo; northern usage for the North. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Idol trainees, streamers and other seeking-attention occupations against workers behind the scenes. Young adults living alone and elders living alone. Employees close to a boss against those unnoticed. Women and men facing looks standards. Marriage migrants and migrant workers. People with disabilities. Jeju islanders against mainland arrivals. Seoul against small towns. Defectors, who were watched in one setting and now are visible in another.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on looks, media and loneliness; mixed on felt invisibility; thin and state-shaped for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; online slang changes fast.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cosmetic surgery and lonely death involve private people. Northern watching comes through defector accounts and is sensitive. Privacy and surveillance statutes go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
