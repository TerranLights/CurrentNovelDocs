*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); hanja for village-school and academy records; Jeju-eo for local oral teaching. **Concept in one line:** how people come to know and to do: in the family, by watching and pitching in, through play, in early school, at work and across a life (higher education belongs to a later sign).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Urban apartment families and rural villages. Different class and income groups. Women's and men's routes. Craft lineages (potters, lacquer workers) and Jeju haenyeo diver communities. Households of North Korean origin. Marriage-migrant mothers raising children. Christian mission-school heritage against temple and Confucian academy traditions. Cohorts that grew up before and after near-universal schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on schooling and tutoring; mixed for informal learning in families and crafts; thin for the North, mostly state curriculum material and defector memoirs. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for old schools; English for comparative scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Student stress and suicide data are heavy and need care. Craft secrets may be guarded by lineage holders. Higher education is a later sign; school law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
