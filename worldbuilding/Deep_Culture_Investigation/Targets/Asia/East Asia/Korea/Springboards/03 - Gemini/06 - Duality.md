# Korea: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul and hanja); some English for adoptee and diaspora scholarship. **Concept in one line (proposed concept):** how a culture lives with two-ness: two identities, languages, loyalties or views held at once; the world divided into pairs; and how people showing two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
North Korean origin residents against southern-born, since each holds division differently. Overseas adoptees returning, and returnee families. Christian, Buddhist, shamanic and nonreligious people for overlapping practices. Mixed-heritage and marriage-migrant households. Jeju residents with island and mainland ties. Speakers of different dialects. Office workers with work and private personas. Generations that learned hanja and those who did not.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed. Rich for division and adoption; thin on ordinary two-ness in daily life; this concept is proposed, so definitions will need author choices. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for pair concepts; English for adoptee studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Division, defection and adoption are painful subjects; informants and relatives may be at risk. Do not generalize "han" or similar labels into a trait; treat them as contested terms.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
