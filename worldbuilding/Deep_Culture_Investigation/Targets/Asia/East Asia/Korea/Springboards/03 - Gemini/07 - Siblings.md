# Korea: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja for genealogies and household records). **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how a culture understands the sibling bond: who counts, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Eldest sons, later sons and daughters, since terms and duties may differ. Only children and large-family cohorts. Lineage heads (종가) against ordinary households. Families with a northern origin and relatives across the line. Jeju households with island kin networks. Rural against urban sibling care of elders. Adoptees and their siblings. Households with half- or step-siblings. Religious groups that use "brother" and "sister" for members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for household statistics and genealogy in the South; mixed for emotional and day-to-day sibling life; thin for the North beyond defector and separated-family testimony. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for genealogies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Separated-family testimony and adoption searches are painful. Inheritance statutes go to Libra, or Capricorn for their spirit.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
