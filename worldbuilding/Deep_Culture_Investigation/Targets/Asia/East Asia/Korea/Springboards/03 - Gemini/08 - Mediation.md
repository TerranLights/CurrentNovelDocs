# Korea: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja for Buddhist and Joseon texts). **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between: brokers, interpreters, intercessors.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Villagers with a headman and clan council against apartment residents with management offices. Families who use clergy, elders or shamans as go-betweens. Buddhist, Christian and nonreligious families. Matchmaking-industry clients and families who arrange introductions informally. Migrant and marriage-migrant households that depend on interpreters. Northern and southern liaison staff. Workplaces with and without mediating seniors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed. Rich for formal mediation services in the South; thin for informal go-betweens, which are often undocumented; very thin for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for Wonhyo and Joseon records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Court and labor mediation rules belong to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit); do not research them here. Family-conflict and neighbor-dispute cases carry privacy limits. Shamanic practice may be private.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
