# Korea: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); hanja for clan and association records. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Regional communities (Gyeongsang, Jeolla, Chungcheong, Gangwon, Jeju) where regional ties have their own histories. Alumni circles of different schools. Clan lineages against nonclan households. Urban single-person households and rural elders. Church, temple and club members. Salaried workers, the self-employed and farmers. North Korean origin households, whose ties were broken by division. Marriage-migrant and foreign-worker networks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich on surveys, loneliness and associations in the South; mixed on informal ties and mutual aid; thin for the North, where reports are state-filtered or come from defectors. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for clan records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Regional-tie research touches discrimination and political divides. Solitary-death and shut-in data are painful. Defectors' contacts left behind put relatives at risk.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull KGSS items on trust and club membership.

3. Search RISS for 학연 지연 혈연 and 정 심리.
4. Look for hometown and alumni association directories at the National Library.
