## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Schoolchildren by school type (ordinary, science-focused, alternative) and students in tutoring against those in play-based programs. Women and men of different ages on what counts as nosy. Scientists, engineers and hobbyists against office workers. Rural villagers and apartment neighbors on asking about others. Christian groups and Buddhist temples on questioning teachings. Northern residents under restricted information, and defectors who describe new questions in the South. Migrant families answering questions about origins.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed. Plenty of Korean pedagogy and media on 호기심, but little ethnography of everyday asking and not-asking. Thin for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for Joseon texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Questions about age, marriage, salary and origins touch privacy and discrimination; handle polls with care. Do not assume a single attitude: compare settings, not "the Korean way."
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
