## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Older and younger people by main news source (television, portals, video platforms, messaging groups). Rural and urban residents. Readers of papers from different political leanings. Jeju residents against mainlanders for local news. North Korean elite and ordinary households, and defectors describing both. Migrant workers using home-language media. Religious networks (church bulletins, temple groups) as channels.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South (surveys, archives, press databases); mixed for informal channels; thin and politically filtered for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for pre-1945 press; English for monitoring services.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Northern access to foreign media is dangerous for the people reporting it; protect sources. Privacy and disclosure statutes go to Libra, or Capricorn for their spirit.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
