# Korea: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul), including internet slang; the northern standard for North Korean sources. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Schoolchildren, university students and office workers, who use different platforms. Women and men, with care not to assume who gossips. Villagers and apartment dwellers. Fans and celebrities. Investors and finance workers for market rumors. Church and temple congregations. Jeju and mainland communities with closer local ties. Defectors, whose accounts span North and South.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich on online comments, rumors and tabloids in the South; thin on face-to-face gossip, which rarely leaves records; very thin on the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean, including fast-changing slang; some English for platform studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cases involving deaths linked to online rumor are painful and may name living people; use aggregate reports. Defamation and cyber-insult statutes go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
