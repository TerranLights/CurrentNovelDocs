## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Activist generations (labor, student, candlelight) against older officials; religious believers (Protestant, Catholic, Buddhist, Jehovah's Witnesses); lineage families that keep scholar traditions; conservative and progressive political camps; women and men in how firmness is praised; military conscripts and officers; recent arrivals from the north, whose convictions were formed under a different system; Jeju-do with its 1948 memory; the north's own cadre culture.
