*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Readers of differing newspaper lines (conservative and progressive); heavy users of video platforms against print readers; older and younger generations; families of those killed in 1948 on Jeju-do and in 1980 in Gwangju, whose own accounts come first; religious groups with their own teaching of truth; scientists and clergy as authorities; recent arrivals from the north, who can speak to what each side's media says of the other; northern state media consumers as described by defectors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south (surveys, press, inquiries); mixed for Jeju-do; the north is known through state media and contested testimony. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; the commission reports are mostly Korean, with English summaries for a few.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inquiries into killings, and individual defector accounts of the north, are contested in part; weigh each source's stake; no law research here, which goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
