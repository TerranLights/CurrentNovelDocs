# Korea: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul). **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
By generation (those who built postwar industry, the democratization generation, the youngest adults); by religion (Protestant, Buddhist, unaffiliated); by class and by education track; by region (Seoul area, the southeast, Honam, Jeju-do, with its own work songs and dialect); women and men; recent arrivals from the north, whose accounts of purpose are to be asked, not assumed; workers in the north's state system, as reachable through defector accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south (surveys, bestseller lists, drama); mixed for Jeju-do; thin for the north, where only exile testimony reaches outsiders. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Korean. Some Pew and OECD material exists in English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** despair, suicide and loneliness are heavy topics; treat survey numbers with care and add no inference. Defector testimony may be shaped by interviewers.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. KOSIS: search 삶의 만족도 and 사회조사, across the survey years.
2. KOSSDA catalog: 삶의 의미 and 행복.
3. Pew 2021 meaning-in-life study: read the South Korea chapter's question wording.
4. Bestseller lists (Kyobo, Yes24) for 힐링 and 인생, by year.
5. RISS: 삶의 의미 척도 for validated scales in Korean.
