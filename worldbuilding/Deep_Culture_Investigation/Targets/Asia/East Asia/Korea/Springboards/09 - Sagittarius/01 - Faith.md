# Korea: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul). **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Protestants (large-church, small-church, anti-church "Canaan" believers), Catholics, Buddhists, Won Buddhists, Cheondoists, shamanic clients, the unaffiliated; people with several practices at once; Jeju-do, with its own village-god shrines; the north's state faith and its registered congregations; recent arrivals from the north who joined southern churches; by generation and by city or village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south; mixed for Jeju-do; thin and mediated for the north. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; church and temple sources are mostly Korean-only.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** north-side practice is known through defectors and monitors; shamans' rites and client lives are private; abuse and money scandals attach to some congregations.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
