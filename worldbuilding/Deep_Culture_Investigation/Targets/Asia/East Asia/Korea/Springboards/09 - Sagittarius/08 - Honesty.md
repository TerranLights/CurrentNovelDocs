## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Officials and private-sector workers; students under exam pressure against older workers; small merchants in traditional markets against large corporations; religious groups with explicit teachings on lying; regional speech cultures (the capital area, Gyeongsang, Jeolla, Chungcheong, Jeju-do), where candor norms may differ and are to be checked; men and women; senior and junior ranks in the same workplace; recent arrivals from the north, whose workplaces ran on other rules; northern officials and traders as described by defectors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south (integrity surveys, press, education texts); mixed for Jeju-do; thin for the north, with the market economy there reported only through defectors. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; classical Chinese for the older texts, with modern editions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases name living people and carry defamation risk; tax and customs honesty are law topics and go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
