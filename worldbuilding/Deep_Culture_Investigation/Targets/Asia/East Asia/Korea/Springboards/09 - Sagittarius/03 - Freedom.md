# Korea: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul). **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; freedom as a belief and a political ideal, including collective freedom such as independence and liberation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Independence-lineage families against exile and migrant families; the 1960 and 1980 student generations against the youngest adults; conservative and progressive political camps; workers and labor unions; women's and sexual-minority groups; religious groups (Protestant, Catholic, Buddhist) on conscience; Honam, Gyeongsang and the capital area, whose political memories differ and are to be checked; Jeju-do and its 1948 memory; the north's own meaning of independence; recent arrivals from the north.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south (archives, polls, press); mixed for Jeju-do; thin and state-shaped for the north. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; some Asian Barometer and Freedom House material is in English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1948 and 1980 events are politically charged topics, so source slant needs checking; the constitutional and statutory side of freedom belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
