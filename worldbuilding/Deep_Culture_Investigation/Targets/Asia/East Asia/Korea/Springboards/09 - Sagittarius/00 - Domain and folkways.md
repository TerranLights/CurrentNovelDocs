## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Protestants (megachurch and small-church), Catholics, Buddhists (temple and lay), Won Buddhists, Cheondoists, shamanic practitioners and clients, the unaffiliated; Confucian lineage families and their ritual halls; Jeju-do, which keeps its own shamanic tradition; the north's state ideology and its limited registered congregations; recent arrivals from the north; urban against rural; generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south (surveys, census, scholarship); mixed for Jeju-do; thin and mediated for the north.
- **Languages needed:** Korean essential; English covers older scholarship only. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** north-side religious life is reported through defectors, officials and outside monitors, each with a stake; living sects draw lawsuits and slurs; shamans' ritual knowledge may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
