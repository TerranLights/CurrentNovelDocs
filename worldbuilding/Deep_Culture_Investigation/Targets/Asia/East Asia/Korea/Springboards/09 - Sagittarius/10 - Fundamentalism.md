# Korea: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul). **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular and political certainties is contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Conservative, mainline and progressive Protestants; Catholics; Buddhist orders; Confucian lineage and academy circles; members and ex-members of new religious movements; the young adults who left churches; northern state-ideology followers as reported; recent arrivals from the north who joined southern churches; Jeju-do, with its own religious mix; urban megachurch and rural village congregations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south in press and denominational papers; mixed for Jeju-do; thin for the north. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; some Joseon texts need classical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "heresy" and "cult" labels are fought over, so record who labels whom; living leaders and survivors are involved, with defamation and abuse risk; some Netflix and television sources are one-sided by design; no group is to be judged here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
