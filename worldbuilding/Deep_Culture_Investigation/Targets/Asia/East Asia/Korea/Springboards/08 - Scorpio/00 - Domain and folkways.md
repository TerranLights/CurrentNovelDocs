## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South against North:** separate regimes and separate rites of the dead and of power.
- **Jeju-do:** its own shamanic and burial traditions and memory of 1948.
- **Regional South (Honam, Yeongnam, Gangwon, Seoul region):** different rite and kin practice, to check.
- **Religion:** Protestant, Catholic, Buddhist, Confucian-ritual and shamanic households.
- **Generation and city against village:** funeral halls and cremation against village rites.
- **Lineage (munjung) households against others.**
- **Defectors and migrants in the lands:** other funeral and secrecy habits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (statistics, ethnography, media); thin and state-filtered for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Hanja for older texts; Jeju language for local sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and lonely-death data; sexual-violence data; shamans' restricted knowledge; testimony of massacre survivors and defectors.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
