*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (elections, finance, scholarship, film); thin and filtered for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Hanja; English for much scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** named officials in scandal coverage; defector testimony. Constitutional and criminal law go to Libra or Capricorn; power as authority is also Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
