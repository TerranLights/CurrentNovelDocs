# Korea: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul and romanization). **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, a family, a community or a country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Survivors and families of named disasters** (Daegu, Seoul, Pohang, Jeju-do ferry and typhoon coasts).
- **War-refugee families from the North and their descendants.**
- **Workers by security of employment:** regular against irregular.
- **Fishing and farming households against urban salaried ones.**
- **Generations:** 1997 job-loss cohort against the youth of the 2010s.
- **People from the North:** the 1990s famine memory.
- **South and North:** public response against state-controlled information.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (official records, press, film); mixed for private crises (kept inside families); thin for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English for UN and scholarly sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disaster survivors and bereaved families; suicide after crisis; defector testimony. Disaster and safety regulation goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
