*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Exam-track students and their parents against vocational-track students; idol trainees; master craftspeople (장인) against assembly workers; chaebol staff against small shops; women facing appearance standards against men facing job-spec standards; older workers trained under strict masters; Jeju and rural communities with a looser "good enough" attitude (*check*); the North's performers and workers under state standards.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South on school stress, appearance and corporate quality; thin for the North beyond spectacles; sparse on everyday household standards.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; some psychology work is in English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** studies touch on suicide and eating disorders; idol-trainee cases involve named people; handle with care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Korean lands.
