# Korea: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia (pre-war lands: South Korea, North Korea, Jeju-do). **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul), with hanja for older texts. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose: a person's contribution and being needed, and the practical value of things and knowledge.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Elders in farm villages against urban retirees; elders who work against those supported by family; women in unpaid housework against paid workers; disabled people and their families; job-seeking youth; Jeju women divers, who work into old age (*check*); the North, where each person's use is assigned by the state; temple and church communities with their own roles for older members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South on aging, disability and attitudes; mixed on the nineteenth-century idea of utility; thin on the North.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for premodern texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elder poverty and suicide statistics need careful reading; goryeojang is a story, and its use as history is disputed (*check*); disability testimony needs consent.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Korean lands.
