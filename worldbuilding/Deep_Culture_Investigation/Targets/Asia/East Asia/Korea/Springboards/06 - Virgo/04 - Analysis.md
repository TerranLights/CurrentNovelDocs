## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Science and engineering students against humanities students; the capital's Gangnam tutoring districts against provincial schools; elite university graduates against vocational-school graduates; old scholar-official (yangban) heritage against commoner skills; Go and esports players; Jeju and rural elders who reckon by weather and tide (*check*); North Korean scientists and technicians against ordinary workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on schooling, test results and scholarly history in the South; thin for the North and for everyday reasoning outside school.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanmun for the annals and Silhak texts (modern translations exist, *check*).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** North Korean science sources are access-controlled and propaganda-shaped; exam pressure studies touch on student suicide.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Korean lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the curriculum body's site for 비판적 사고 and 논리 in the national curriculum.
2. Search the Annals database for 격물 and 실사구시 to see premodern use.
3. Find Choi's and Nisbett's papers and read their Korean sample sections and bibliographies.
4. Search BIGKinds for 이세돌 알파고 and read how reasoning was framed.
5. Search for 입시 분석 and 학원 on a Korean portal.
