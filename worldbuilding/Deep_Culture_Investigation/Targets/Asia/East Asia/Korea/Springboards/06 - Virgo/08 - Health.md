## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Users of Korean medicine against users of biomedicine, and those who combine them; Seoul against the provinces (district-level survey data); Jeju's women divers and their occupational health (*check*); elders in farm villages against city elders; temple-food communities; Protestant and Catholic communities with healing prayer; shaman-using households; Korean Chinese and other migrant patients; the North's clinics and hospitals against its folk remedies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (surveys, insurance data, medical history); mixed on folk healing; thin and hard to verify for the North.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja and hanmun for classics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide, mental illness and shaman rites are sensitive; some insurance data is access-controlled; defector health testimony needs source protection.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Korean lands.
