# Korea: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia (pre-war lands: South Korea, North Korea, Jeju-do). **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul), with hanja for older and Buddhist texts. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what does not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Older against younger readers of news; Buddhist, Protestant, Catholic and nonreligious communities, each with its own sense of judging; urban office workers, who talk of nunchi, against farm and fishing villages; collectors and dealers against ordinary buyers; online communities that police "fake" claims; the North's sorted social classes against its resettled people; Jeju, which has its own folk judging practices (*check*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on media trust, scams and consumer complaints; mixed on taste and everyday judging of people; thin and state-shaped on the North.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja and Buddhist vocabulary for doctrine.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** songbun testimony is sensitive; face reading and fortune practices are held differently by believers and critics.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Korean lands.
