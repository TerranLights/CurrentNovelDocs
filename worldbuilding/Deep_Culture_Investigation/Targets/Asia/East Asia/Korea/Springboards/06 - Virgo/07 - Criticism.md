## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Seniors and juniors in the same office; parents and children; teachers and students; officials and the press; the old yangban against commoner satirists; regional mask-dance traditions (Andong's Hahoe in the southeast; Bongsan in the North); online generations, with their own comment cultures; Protestant churches, where the pastor's word is rarely challenged (*check*); Jeju's folk songs (*check*); the North's neighborhood and workplace units.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in the South for press, online comments and satire; premodern remonstrance well documented; the North is thin and state-framed.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanmun for court records (translations exist, *check*).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** online-abuse cases involve named victims and suicides; North Korean testimony needs source safety. Defamation and censorship as law belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Korean lands.
