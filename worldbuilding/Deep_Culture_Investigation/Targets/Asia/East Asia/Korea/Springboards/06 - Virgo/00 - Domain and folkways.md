*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Capital region against Yeongnam, Honam, Chungcheong and Gangwon (regional work and diet ways); Jeju, with its women divers (해녀) and its own labor-exchange custom; the North's regions (Pyongyang, the northeast, the northwest); chaebol staff against small firms and irregular workers; farm and fishing villages against cities; temple communities; Protestant and Catholic congregations; Korean Chinese (조선족) workers; migrants from other countries working in factories and fields; northern-born resettlers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (surveys, press, scholarship); thin and filtered for the North; Jeju is a mixed case.
- **Languages needed:** Korean (hangul), with some hanmun for premodern texts. Many scholarly works are Korean-only.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** North Korean material is state-made and some is access-controlled; defector testimony needs care for source safety. Past status groups (노비, 백정) are painful topics.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Korean lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search KOSIS for 생활시간조사 and 경제활동인구조사; note which tables exist.
2. Search the Encyclopedia of Korean Culture for 두레, 품앗이 and 장인.
3. Search RISS or KCI for 노동 윤리 and 직장 문화.
4. Look up Koo and Janelli in a library catalog and read their bibliographies.
5. Search the National Folk Museum's archive for 농기구 and 세시풍속.
