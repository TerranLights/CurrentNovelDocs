# Korea: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia (pre-war lands: South Korea, North Korea, Jeju-do). **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul). **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place it holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Chaebol office staff, small-firm workers and irregular workers; platform and day workers; farm and fishing households; Jeju divers (해녀) and their cooperatives; the heavy-industry belt of the southeast against the Seoul region; the North's mine, farm and factory districts; student job-seekers; older workers kept on past retirement; Korean Chinese (조선족) workers; resettled northerners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (surveys, press, fiction, labor history); thin and state-shaped for the North; Jeju mixed.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; some key labor-history texts exist in English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** North Korean material is propaganda-made; defector accounts need care for source safety; industrial-death cases involve living families.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Korean lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search KOSIS for 경제활동인구조사 and note the hours and irregular-work tables.
2. Search BIGKinds for 과로사 and 열정페이 to date when each term appeared.
3. Find Koo and Song in a library catalog and read their bibliographies.
4. Search RISS for 노동관 and 직업윤리 to find attitude studies.
5. Search an archive for 천리마운동 to find how the North frames work.
