# Korea: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia (pre-war lands: South Korea, North Korea, Jeju-do). **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul), with hanja for early-modern texts. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, work, home, land, community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Saemaul-era farm villages against industrial cities; the southeast and the capital region against the southwest in development stories; yangban-descended families against commoner-descended ones in older uplift ideals; Protestant, Catholic and Buddhist communities with different frames of striving; test-prep families against families outside that race; young adults who use spoon-class talk against those who do not; Jeju's mandarin and tourism boom (*check*); the North's campaign-driven workplaces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South on development history, self-help publishing and mobility attitudes; thin and state-framed for the North.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for early-modern reform texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** development-era campaigns have disputed legacies; "improving" peoples links to colonial-era uplift rhetoric and should be read critically; North Korean sources are propaganda-made.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Korean lands.
