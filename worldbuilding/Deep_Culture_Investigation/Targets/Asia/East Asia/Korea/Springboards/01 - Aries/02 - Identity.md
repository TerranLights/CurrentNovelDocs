## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Hometown regions (Seoul area, Gyeongsang, Jeolla, Chungcheong, Gangwon, Pyongan, Hamgyong, Hwanghae); Jeju-do islanders; defectors and their children; families formed by marriage across borders; descendants of people who returned from Manchuria, Japan or Central Asia (a group to confirm); religious communities; veterans' and war-orphan generations; clan seats; the three generations since the divide.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (surveys, scholarship); thin for the North and tilted to state sources; mixed for Jeju. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Jejueo; some Chinese and Russian for the diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** defector identity and safety; contested origin myths; regional-feeling politics.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
