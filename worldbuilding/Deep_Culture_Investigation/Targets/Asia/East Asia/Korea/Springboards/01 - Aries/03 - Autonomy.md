## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Age cohorts (people raised before industrialization, after the 1997 crisis, the youngest); women and men; urban singles against rural families; eldest sons against others; haenyeo villages and other Jeju villages; Protestant churches with strong leaders against Buddhist temples; the North's collective farms against the South's cities; defector households after resettlement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for living arrangements and youth panels in the South; thin for the North; Jeju mixed. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; some Jejueo for local material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** North Korean sources are state-made; family-conflict material is personal; medical-choice questions belong partly to law, which is outside this sign.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Legal self-determination: point to Libra and Capricorn.
