## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Veterans of the 1950s war and later conscripts; women (divers, nurses, protesters) against men; protesters, rescuers and soldiers as separate kinds; civilians who intervene against those in uniform; haenyeo of Jeju (sea danger); defectors who crossed borders; North Korean soldiers against farm and mine workers; clans or villages with distinct war memories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South's war memory, commemoration and award records; thin for the North beyond official hero narratives; mixed for Jeju. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; classical Chinese for some old texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime-violence memory (including Jeju) calls for careful handling; defector testimony can endanger kin; military material may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Look up 용기, 배짱 and 의인 in the standard dictionary and compare senses.
2. Read excerpts of the war diary and one veteran memoir.
3. List the award categories of the veterans ministry and the LG awards.
4. Search RISS for "내부고발 한국 조직 문화".
5. Search Hanawon and NKDB for published escape accounts.
