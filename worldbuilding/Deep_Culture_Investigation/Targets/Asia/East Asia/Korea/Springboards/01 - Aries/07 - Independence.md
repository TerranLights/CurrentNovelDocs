## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Elders living alone and elders living with children; farm households and city wage earners; people receiving public aid; those who left cities for farming; married women against those who live alone by choice; Jeju natives and in-migrants; the North's households under rationing and self-reliance campaigns; defector households settling in the South; cooperatives' members against non-members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elders, welfare and rural moves in the South; thin and slanted for the North; Jeju mixed. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** poverty and aid recipients; North Korean official doctrine is state-made; elder isolation material is delicate.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
