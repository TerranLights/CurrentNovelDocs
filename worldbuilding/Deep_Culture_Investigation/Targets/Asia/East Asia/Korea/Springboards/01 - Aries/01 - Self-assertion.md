## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Generations (war and industrialization cohorts, democratization-era, the youngest); women and men; workers in large firms against small shops; civil servants against private staff; the South's regions (Gyeongsang, Jeolla, Seoul area); Jeju residents with their own neighbor ways; the North's party-linked staff against farmers and soldiers; religious groups (Protestant church members, Buddhists).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for South Korean surveys and press; thin for the North; Jeju mixed. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; some Jejueo.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the North's political expression is monitored; defector testimony needs care; avoid treating the aviation claims as settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Law of complaint or petition: point to Libra and Capricorn.
