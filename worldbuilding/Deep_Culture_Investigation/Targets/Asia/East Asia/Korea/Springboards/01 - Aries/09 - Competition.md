## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Children in the Seoul metropolitan area against provincial towns; students in elite against vocational schools; first-time exam takers and repeaters; women and men in hiring; school alumni networks against non-members; Jeju students and clubs; regional sports-club supporters; the North's mobilized work teams against students in elite schools; defector students entering southern schools.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for education spending, exams and sports in the South; thin for the North beyond staged spectacle. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English for the scholarly literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exam-pressure and youth-distress material; do not generalize exam-prep culture to every family; North Korean sources are state-made.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
