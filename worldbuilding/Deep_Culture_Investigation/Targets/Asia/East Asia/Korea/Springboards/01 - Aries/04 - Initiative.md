## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Founders in the capital area against provincial towns; large-firm employees against small-shop owners; women and men; students and recent graduates; church and temple volunteers; 1970s village leaders against present neighborhood groups; Jeju residents (tourism and farming starters); the North's mobilized brigades against private market traders (a group to confirm); defector entrepreneurs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South's business and volunteering statistics; thin for the North; Jeju is mixed. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official campaign language may stand in for what people did; North Korean market activity is poorly documented and risky for sources.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
