*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean, Jejueo; English. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Women and men; elders who carry war and displacement against the youngest; wage workers in hierarchical firms against self-employed; the South's regions; Jeju survivors' families and others; protesters, drivers and online commenters as separate settings; Protestant, Buddhist and shamanic ways of handling grievance; the North's public sessions against private talk; defectors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for clinical and crime statistics and for folk forms in the South; thin for private anger in the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English for the psychiatric literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and suicide-adjacent material; shamanic rites (permission); survivors' testimony; do not read the clinical term as a trait of a whole people.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Law of violence: point to Libra and Capricorn.
