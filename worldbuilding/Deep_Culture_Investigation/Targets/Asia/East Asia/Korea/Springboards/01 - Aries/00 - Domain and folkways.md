## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
The two halves split by the old division (South and North), Jeju-do (own language and divers), the Seoul region, Gyeongsang, Jeolla, Chungcheong, Gangwon, Pyongan and Hamgyong lineages; elders of the war generations against recent exiles; urban against rural; clan lineages with different seats; women divers against other Jeju residents; religious communities (Buddhist, Protestant, Catholic, shamanic practitioners, Cheondogyo).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South's surveys and genealogy; mixed for Jeju; thin and filtered for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean (hangul and some hanja); Jejueo for Jeju.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic ritual knowledge; North Korean sources are state-produced; defector testimony needs care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Look up 정체성, 자아 and 이름 in the national encyclopedia and the dictionary.
2. Search KOSIS and KOSSDA for name and self-description tables.
3. Search RISS for "자아 개념" and "한국인의 자아".
4. Browse the National Folk Museum's life-cycle rites.
5. Search the Unification portal for North Korean identity and naming.
