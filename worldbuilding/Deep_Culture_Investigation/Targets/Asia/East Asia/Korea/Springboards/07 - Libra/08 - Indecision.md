## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Students facing exams and majors; job seekers; couples at proposal age.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; slang usage is easy to trace, real decision behavior is thinly measured.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Hanja for classical records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** end-of-life cases are private; shaman clients may not speak openly.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
