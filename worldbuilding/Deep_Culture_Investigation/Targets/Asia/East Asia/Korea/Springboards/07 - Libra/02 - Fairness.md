## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Job seekers against those already inside firms (regular and irregular workers).
- Men and women, on conscription and hiring fairness.
- Regional capital-area and provincial residents, on admissions and jobs.
- Jeju fishing and farming communities, for turn-taking customs.
- Northern lands: ration-era equality language and market-era practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south (surveys, press, forums); thin for the north.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Hanja for Joseon records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gender and generational grievance threads are heated; forum posts need anonymizing.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

4. Search 더치페이 and 한턱 in etiquette columns and forums.
5. Search Jeju studies for 수눌음 and catch-sharing rules.
