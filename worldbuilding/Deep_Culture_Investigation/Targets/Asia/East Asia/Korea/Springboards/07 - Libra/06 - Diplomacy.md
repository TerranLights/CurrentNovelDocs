# Korea: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (south, north, Jeju); Hanja for Joseon records. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Diplomats, business negotiators, labor negotiators and everyday speakers.
- Older and younger generations, on indirect speech and honorifics.
- In-law households across holidays.
- Jeju, as a self-declared peace island.
- Northern lands: state diplomacy and its separate vocabulary.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south and for state diplomacy; everyday tact is thinly measured and mostly in essays.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Hanja for Joseon; English for external guides.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** northern documents are state-shaped; some inter-Korean records are unreleased.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
