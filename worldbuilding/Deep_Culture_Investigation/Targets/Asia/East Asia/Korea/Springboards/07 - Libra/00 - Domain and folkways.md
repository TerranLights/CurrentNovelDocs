## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- South Korean provinces and the capital region, for marriage statistics.
- Jeju-do: its own speech, neighbor-labor words and women's diving cooperatives.
- Northern lands: own family law, neighborhood units and vocabulary.
- Older and younger cohorts; urban and rural; Christian, Buddhist, Confucian-lineage and nonreligious households.
- Migrant-marriage families and exiles from the north.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south (statistics, surveys, courts); thin and filtered for the north, mostly defector testimony and outside reports.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Hanja helps for old texts; Jeju speech for local material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** defector testimony is risky to the speakers; reconciliation records involve living victims.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
