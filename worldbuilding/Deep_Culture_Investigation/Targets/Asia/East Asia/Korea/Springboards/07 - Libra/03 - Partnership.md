*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Single-person households, cohabiting couples and married couples.
- Cross-border marriage families and their children.
- Provincial and capital-region couples; Jeju households.
- Older and younger cohorts, the latter with the word for giving up courtship and marriage (삼포세대 sampo sedae *check*).
- Northern lands: party and neighborhood-unit framing of partnership.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the south (panels, census, media); thin for the north.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Jeju speech for local rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** same-sex partnership is contested; migrant-marriage families are a protected-privacy group.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull Statistics Korea household-type tables with 1인 가구, 동거.
2. Search 여성가족패널 documentation for couple-level items.
3. Search 생활동반자법 and 사실혼 판례 in the statute and court databases.
4. Search 한미동맹 with 여론조사 on the Asan Institute site.
5. Find the Jeju volume of the folklore survey in a university library.
