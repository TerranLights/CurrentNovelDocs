*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Women and men, daughters-in-law and in-laws, on endurance at home.
- Workers in large firms and small shops; students and teachers.
- Victims' families who settled and those who did not.
- Pacifist and religious objectors; Jeju villagers split over the naval base.
- Northern lands: meeting-based discipline and exile accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; clinical and survey data for the south, anecdote for silence; thin for the north.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Jeju speech for local testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic and workplace abuse cases; massacre survivors; defector safety.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
