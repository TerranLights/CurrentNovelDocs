- Office workers, platform and shift workers, farmers and fishers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hours, surveys and trend writing in the south; thin for the north.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Hanja for medical classics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** overwork deaths and suicides are in the data; treat with care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
