## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Men who served and those exempted, and women whose roles differ. Parents of schoolchildren in cities against villages. Residents of Jeju with island memories of 4.3, and fishing communities. Bereaved families of the Sewol and Itaewon disasters. Elders living alone and the services reaching them. Defector families who knew different guard systems. Migrant workers and marriage migrants outside local safety networks. Christian, Buddhist and folk-ritual households as to protective rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on safety and crime surveys; mixed on beliefs and rites; thin and state-filtered for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled. Law on child or elder protection belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for folk sources; English for outside scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Disaster and massacre memory is contested and painful to the bereaved; abuse casework is restricted; military sources can be classified.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
