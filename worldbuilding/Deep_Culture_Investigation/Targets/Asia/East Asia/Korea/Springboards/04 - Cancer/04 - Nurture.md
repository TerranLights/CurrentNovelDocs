## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Mothers, fathers and grandparents as the main carers. Rural households and apartment-complex families. Dual-earner and single-parent homes. Households sending children to private tutoring against those who do not. Jeju families and island fishing households. Northern families, including defector parents raising children in the South. Marriage-migrant mothers. Religious families (Christian, Buddhist) and the nonreligious.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on surveys and policy; mixed on warmth and feeding practices; thin for the North and mostly state or defector sources. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled. Education and schooling pursuits belong to Gemini and Sagittarius; discipline as obedience to Capricorn.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; classical Chinese for older advice texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Child-protection and abuse files are restricted; interviews with minors need ethical care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
