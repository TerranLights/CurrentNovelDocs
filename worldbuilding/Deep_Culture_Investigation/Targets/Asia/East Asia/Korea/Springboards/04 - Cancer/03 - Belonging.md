*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Natives of each region (Seoul, Gyeongsang, Jeolla, Chungcheong, Gangwon, Jeju) as to hometown ties. Alumni-network members against people outside such ties. Northern defectors. Korean Chinese and Koryo-saram returnees. Marriage migrants and their children. Overseas adoptees returning. Migrant workers. Religious congregations against the nonreligious. Rural villagers against apartment dwellers, and young people who withdraw from society.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South in surveys; mixed on the felt side; thin and state-filtered for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; some Chinese and Russian for returnee groups; English for outside scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Defectors' and adoptees' interviews carry personal risk; "who is Korean" is contested; the word 혼혈 is often hurtful and should be quoted only as a term under study.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
