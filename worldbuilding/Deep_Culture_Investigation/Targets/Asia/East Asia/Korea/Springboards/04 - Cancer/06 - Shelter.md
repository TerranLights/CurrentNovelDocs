*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jeju-eo; northern usage; English for refugee and asylum sources. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
People sleeping rough in Seoul stations against residents of jjokbang and gosiwon. Elders living alone in public rental. Youth who ran away from home. Women and children in violence shelters. Northern defectors in resettlement. Refugee claimants, including those who arrived on Jeju. Marriage migrants and migrant workers in company housing. Disaster evacuees. Jeju island households with their own hospitality customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South in counts and policy; mixed on felt experience; thin for the North, where housing is assigned by the state and sources are outside accounts. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English for refugee and policy scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Homeless and violence-shelter residents are vulnerable; shelter addresses are often withheld; refugee files are restricted. Housing and refugee law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
