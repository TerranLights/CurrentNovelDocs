# Korea: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja for medical and older texts); Jeju-eo; northern usage. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Daughters, daughters-in-law, sons and spouses as the main carers. Paid carers, many of them migrant or older women. Rural elders and urban elders living alone. Families who use care homes against those who do not. Jeju households and island clinics. Northern households and defector families caring for kin on both sides of the border. People with disabilities and their families. Christian and Buddhist volunteer carers. Young carers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South on elders, insurance and time use; mixed on felt care and on men's roles; thin for the North, mostly defector accounts. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled. The duty of children toward parents (filial duty) belongs to Capricorn.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for older medical texts; English for outside care-ethics scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Reports of caregiver exhaustion and tragedy are painful; health records are restricted; defector care ties cross a closed border.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
