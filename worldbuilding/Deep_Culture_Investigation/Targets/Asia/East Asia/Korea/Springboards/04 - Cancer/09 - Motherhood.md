# Korea: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul; hanja for older texts); Jeju-eo; northern usage. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Stay-at-home mothers, working mothers and mothers with interrupted careers. Daughters-in-law and mothers-in-law. Rural mothers and apartment-complex mothers. Jeju women divers who are mothers. Single, divorced and unwed mothers. Adoptive, foster and stepmothers; mothers who gave children up for adoption. Marriage-migrant mothers. Northern mothers and defector mothers. Christian, Buddhist and shaman-practicing households.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South in statistics, fiction and film; mixed on mothers' own voices; thin and state-shaped for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled. Care ethics and child-rearing belong to the Care and Nurture sheets.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for older texts; English for outside feminist scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Births, abortion and adoption are private and contested; many debates around motherhood are political; do not name private mothers.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
