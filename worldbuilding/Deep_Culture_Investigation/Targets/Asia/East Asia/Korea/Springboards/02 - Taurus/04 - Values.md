*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); some classical Chinese (hanja) terms. **Concept in one line:** what a person or culture holds worth having, doing or being, how those things are ranked, and how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Generational cohorts, since value surveys split by age. Protestant, Catholic, Buddhist and unaffiliated households. Capital-area against Yeongnam, Honam and Jeju. Women and men. Scholar-lineage (yangban-descended) clan villages against newer settlements. Workers in chaebol firms against small-business owners. Northern households under the state's moral instruction, and defectors. Migrant and multicultural families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South (many surveys, archives); mixed for older eras; thin for the North, mostly official texts and defector accounts. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja reading for older ethical texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Values politics (generation, gender, region) is heated in the press; state-promoted slogans are not the same as held values. Name variation, not one voice.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
