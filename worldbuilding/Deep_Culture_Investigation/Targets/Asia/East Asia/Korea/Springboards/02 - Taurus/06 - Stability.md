## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Lineage-village residents against apartment-block dwellers. Capital-area movers against provincial stayers. Jeju natives against mainland settlers. Elders aging in place against young renters. Chaebol and public-sector staff against contract workers. Small-shop and farm families passing work on. Northern households under movement controls, and displaced southern-origin and northern-origin families. Migrant workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South (migration, tenure, vital statistics); thin on felt meanings of stability; thin for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; some hanja for genealogy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Separated-family and 4.3 displacement stories are painful. Genealogy records can touch status-based stigma; handle respectfully.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Download KOSTAT migration tables by province.
2. Check the Hundred-Year Shops roster by region.
3. Search the Academy encyclopedia for 종가 and 집성촌.
4. Search RISS for 평생직장 and 토박이.
