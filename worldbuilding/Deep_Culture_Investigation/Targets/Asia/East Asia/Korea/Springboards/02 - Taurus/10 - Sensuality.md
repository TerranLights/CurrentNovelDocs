## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Region by cooking and sound tradition (Jeolla, Gyeongsang, Gyeonggi, Jeju). Urban spa-goers against rural households that heat and bathe at home. Women and men, young and old, on skincare, drinking and bathing habits. Buddhist temple food and Christian abstention groups. Northern households, where leisure is shaped differently, and defectors' later changes. Migrant and multicultural households with other body customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for food and consumer trends (South); thinner on private pleasure and on touch norms; thin for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Jeju-eo for island sound terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nudity in bathhouses and alcohol culture involve consent and workplace-pressure topics; keep clear of sexual material, which is Scorpio's. Do not reduce taste or festive spirit (흥, 신명) to one national trait.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
