## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Elders who lived through the war and its displacement, against later generations. Households in small apartments (Seoul region) against rural homes with sheds and courtyards. Jeju islanders and haenyeo (women divers), whose tools are personal gear. Northern lands, where what a household may keep is a different question. Christian, Buddhist and Confucian-ritual households, who treat the dead's things differently. Single-person households, a growing group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed. Strong on secondhand trade and decluttering; thin on how people feel about things; North thin and defector-sourced. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Jeju-eo for island folk sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Victims' belongings from Jeju 4.3 and war losses are grief material; handle with care. No law: ownership claims go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
