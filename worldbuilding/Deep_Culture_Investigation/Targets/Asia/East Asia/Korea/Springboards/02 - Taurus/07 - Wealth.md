## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Capital-area (especially southern Seoul) property holders against provincial households. Chaebol-owning families against salaried and self-employed. Old clan-lineage houses against newly rich. Jeju: tourism and land holders against divers and farmers. Northern state-office holders against market traders (donju), and defectors. Elders asset-rich but income-poor. Religious congregations with distinct teaching on money. Migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South (surveys, tax and private-bank reports); thin for the North (estimates, defector reports). The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for older records; English for the scholarly work cited.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Chaebol scandals are litigated and political; wealth of named living people needs care. Do not lead with one rich-class image. No law: inheritance and tax law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
