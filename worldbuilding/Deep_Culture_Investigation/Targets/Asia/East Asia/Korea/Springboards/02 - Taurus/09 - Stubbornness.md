## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Generational cohorts on apology and giving way. Regional groups (Gyeongsang, Jeolla, Chungcheong, Jeju), long stereotyped in press talk, so test rather than assume. Labor-union members against managers. Village holdouts against developers or the state. Scholar-lineage descendants. Women and men, who are described differently when firm. Northern lands, where public stubbornness is dangerous and sources are second-hand. Protestant, Catholic and Buddhist communities with martyr or steadfastness teaching.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed: strong on protest and labor records; thin on private habits; thin for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; classical Chinese for chronicle entries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Protest and holdout episodes are politically charged; keep sides labeled as theirs. Regional stereotypes are a trap: name variation only. No law.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
