*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jeju-eo for island speech. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Capital-area credential-seekers against provincial and rural residents. Women and men, given the gender-role debates. Youth cohorts, tagged in the press as giving up on milestones (N-po generation). Elders in poverty. Jeju haenyeo. Northern households sorted by background, and defectors afterward. Descendants of 4.3 victims, who carried stigma. Migrant workers and Korean-Chinese residents. Christian, Buddhist and unaffiliated believers on where worth comes from.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for surveys and public debate (South); thin and political for the North. The exact lands of the nation are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Jeju-eo for folk material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Suicide, shame and stigma material needs care. Do not treat 한 or 눈치 as the nation's one voice.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Look up 자존감 and 자존심 side by side in the dictionary.
2. Search RISS for 자존감 척도 and 체면 연구.
3. Pull the Welfare Panel's status-perception items.
4. Search BIGKinds for 학벌 across decades.
