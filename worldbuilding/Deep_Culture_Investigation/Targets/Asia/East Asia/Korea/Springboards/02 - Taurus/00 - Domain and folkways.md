# Korea: Domain and folkways (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul), with Jeju-eo (Jeju speech) for the island. **Domain in one line:** possessions, money, resources, values and self-worth: what is worth having, and how is it held? Folkways: food, dress, wealth, building.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Southern urban (Seoul capital area) against provincial and rural lands, since housing and wealth data split by region. Jeju islanders, with their own speech and labor customs. Northern lands, where state rationing and market economies differ from the South's. Defector and exile communities, for what was carried. Gyeongsang, Jeolla and Chungcheong food regions. Generations (elders, mid-life, youth), where surveys split by age. Christian, Buddhist and unaffiliated households, where tithing or temple giving shows up. Migrant and Korean-Chinese residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South (national surveys, academic output); mixed for Jeju; thin and filtered for the North, mostly defector-sourced. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean essential; Jeju-eo for island sources; English for foreign scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** North Korean sources carry political bias on all sides; defector testimony needs care about identification. No law research here: point to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Encyclopedia of Korean Culture for 계, 전세 and 부조.
2. Pull the KGSS and WVS Korean value items on money and work.
3. Search RISS for 소비문화, 재산 상속 and 장마당 theses.
4. Browse National Folk Museum collections for food and dress.
5. Compare KOSTAT household finance tables by province.
