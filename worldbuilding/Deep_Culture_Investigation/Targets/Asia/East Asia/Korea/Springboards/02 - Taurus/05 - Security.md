## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Elderly renters and homeowners, since old-age income differs sharply. Salaried and temporary workers. Civil servants and chaebol staff against self-employed shopkeepers. Rural farm and Jeju fishing households. Households with and without children's support. Women in later life. Northern households under rationing, and defectors. Migrant workers. Protestant and Buddhist congregations that run aid.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for the South (pension, insurance, panel surveys); mixed for informal mutual aid; thin and externally sourced for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanja for historical granary records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Elderly poverty and suicide data are heavy; the word 안보 pulls toward military security, which is a different topic. No law: social-insurance statutes go to Libra, or Capricorn for their spirit.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
