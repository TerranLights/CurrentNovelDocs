## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Partners by gender and age cohort, since survey items split that way. Mothers and fathers of school-age children, in highly competitive education zones against elsewhere. Mother-in-law and daughter-in-law relations, a long-noted pairing to check. Village or fishing-ground insiders against newcomers (Jeju and coasts). Workplaces with long hierarchies. Northern households, where community watching is reported differently. Christian congregations with teaching on marriage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed: good official data on dating and domestic violence (South); weaker on milder possessive habits; thin for the North. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Jeju-eo for kin terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Partner-violence data and stalking cases are heavy; keep case details out. Avoid reading 정 or family closeness as possessiveness without evidence. No law: stalking and custody law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
