## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Jeju island: its own language, shrine system and shaman tradition, and its 1948 memory.
- Seoul metropolitan area against provincial towns and fishing villages: help-seeking and ritual use may differ.
- Religious communities: Protestant, Catholic, Buddhist, Won Buddhist, Cheondogyo, and the unaffiliated.
- Hereditary and charismatic shamans (southern and northern traditions): practice differs by region.
- Northern-origin and defector-origin households, whose experience of institutions is separate.
- Generations: older rural against young urban cohorts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, mental health and shamanism in the south; thin and indirect for the northern lands.
- **Languages needed:** Korean essential; Jejueo for island sources; English summaries exist but cover less.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in welfare institutions; suicide data; ritual knowledge that practitioners guard; political imprisonment. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled, so northern material is separated from southern.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
