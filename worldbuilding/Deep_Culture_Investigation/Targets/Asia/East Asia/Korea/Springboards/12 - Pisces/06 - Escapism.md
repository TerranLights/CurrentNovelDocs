# Korea: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul). **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Students and exam-prep youth, office workers, retired people, and farm households.
- Seoul-region residents against provincial towns, where leisure options differ.
- Women and men, for drinking, games and fan activity.
- Jeju islanders against incoming migrants.
- Young recluses and their families.
- Northern-origin arrivals, whose escape routes and media differ.
- Those in casino and coal-region towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on health, media and leisure surveys; mixed on informal withdrawal; thin on the north, where sources are indirect.
- **Languages needed:** Korean essential; many terms are loanwords or slang that dictionaries lag.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** addiction, gambling debt, suicide risk in withdrawn youth; minors in survey data. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; start with the southern core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
