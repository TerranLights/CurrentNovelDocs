*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- The unaffiliated, who are a large and varied group in the survey categories.
- Protestants, Catholics, Buddhists, Won Buddhists, Cheondogyo members, Daesoon followers.
- Shaman clients and practitioners, against institutional believers.
- Jeju island, with its own pantheon and mountain, against the mainland.
- Practitioners of body-mind cultivation (gi, Sundo-style schools) against sitting-meditation groups.
- Generations: elders at temples and shrines against young seekers on apps and trails.
- Northern-origin arrivals and the north's officially secular order.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on affiliation and the major religions; mixed on spirituality outside churches; thin on the north.
- **Languages needed:** Korean essential; Classical Chinese terms for older texts; Jejueo for island speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** new religious movements are contested and legally and publicly disputed; ritual knowledge held by practitioners; coverage of the north is indirect. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; start with the southern core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
