## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Shamans with an initiation story against those from hereditary lines.
- Craft masters, cooks, divers and farmers: expert intuition that accumulates with years.
- Doctors of Western medicine against doctors of Korean medicine.
- Clients of divination against non-users, where intuition stands for something else.
- Catholic, Protestant and Buddhist communities, each with its own account of inner guidance.
- Women, men and generations, as sayings about a "sense" differ.
- Jeju sea-working communities against mainland farm villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; scholarly work on shamanic gifts and cognition is deep, while general beliefs in hunches are thinly surveyed.
- **Languages needed:** Korean essential; Jejueo for island craft knowledge.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiation accounts are personal and a practitioner's private knowledge may be guarded; physiognomy in hiring has fairness issues. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; start with the southern core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
