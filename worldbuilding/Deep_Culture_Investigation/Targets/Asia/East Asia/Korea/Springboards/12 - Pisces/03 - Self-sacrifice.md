*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for island sources. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Mothers, eldest sons and eldest daughters: expectations differ by role.
- Jeju women divers and their families, against inland farm households.
- Christian, Buddhist and Confucian-lineage families: each frames giving up the self differently.
- Soldiers, firefighters and police against office workers.
- Generations: those who lived the postwar decades, the 1980s activists, and today's young adults.
- Northern-origin households, and a separate northern official idiom to be checked.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on care surveys and commemoration; mixed on private meaning, found in memoir, film and fiction.
- **Languages needed:** Korean essential, with Sino-Korean terms; Jejueo for island oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** self-immolation and suicide; wartime and state-commemoration disputes; accounts of overwork deaths. Northern sources are indirect. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; start with the southern core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
