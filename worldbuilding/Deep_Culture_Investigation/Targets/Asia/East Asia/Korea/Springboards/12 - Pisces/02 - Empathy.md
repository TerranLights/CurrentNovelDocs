## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Service workers and customers: emotional labor sets a separate norm.
- Doctors, nurses, teachers and counselors as trained empaths.
- Men and women, and generations, where the public arguments over empathy have been sharpest.
- Regional speech communities: Jeju, the southeast and the northwest differ in speech style and may differ here.
- Northern-origin arrivals and migrants, who receive and test host empathy.
- Christian, Buddhist and Confucian-leaning families, each with a different source of the ideal.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in psychology and workplace surveys; thin on lived, informal empathy; mixed on translated scales, since the English word may not map onto 공감.
- **Languages needed:** Korean essential, including the Sino-Korean idioms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gender-conflict debates are polarized; workplace studies may name firms. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; start with the southern core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
