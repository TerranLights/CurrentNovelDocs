# Korea: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for the island. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Apartment-complex residents against detached-house and rural households.
- Jeju island households, whose house-entrance and wall customs are their own.
- Daughters-in-law, mothers-in-law and sons: the in-law line is argued about.
- Workers by firm size and sector, for after-hours boundaries.
- Generations, as speech-level and privacy habits shift.
- Residents of the border counties (Paju, Cheorwon, Ganghwa) against the interior.
- Northern-origin arrivals, who cross a very different line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on housing, workplace and border topics; mixed on touch and personal space; thin on private family lines, which turn up in memoir, advice columns and television.
- **Languages needed:** Korean essential; Jejueo for island customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** border and military sites are access-controlled; domestic-violence and harassment material; northern material is indirect. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; start with the southern core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
