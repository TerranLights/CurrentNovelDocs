# Korea: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for the island. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Border counties and Seoul, which face the northern threat differently.
- Coastal and Jeju sea communities against inland towns, for typhoon and sea dread.
- Shaman clients and church-goers: different ways of naming and warding danger.
- Children, parents and elders, for bogey figures against disaster worry.
- Students and workers, for exam, job and status anxiety.
- Northern-origin arrivals, whose fears relate to surveillance and flight.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on polls, film and folk tales; mixed on private worry; thin on the north.
- **Languages needed:** Korean essential; Jejueo for island tales; Classical Chinese for old ghost literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disaster victims and bereaved families; mental-health data; military-alert material. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; start with the southern core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
