## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Urban youth subcultures (Hongdae, Daehangno, university towns) against rural conformity.
- Women and men, and the young against their elders, given the recent gender-based movements.
- LGBTQ communities in Seoul, Busan and elsewhere; church-based critics and supporters.
- Religious minorities, including new religions and shamanic practitioners.
- North: people labeled deviant by the state; South: people labeled outsiders by neighbors.
- Jeju, where outsiders and the branded have a particular history.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for recent urban subcultures and the press; thin for older rural life; state-shaped for the North. Exact lands of the nation are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English for festival and movement coverage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sexual-minority safety; "pseudo-religion" labels used against groups; defector testimony. Questions of law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
