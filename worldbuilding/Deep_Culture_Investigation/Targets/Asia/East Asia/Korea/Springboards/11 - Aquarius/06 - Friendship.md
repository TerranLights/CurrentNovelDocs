# Korea: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for the island. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Men's and women's friendships; school-year groups and mixed groups.
- Rural and urban; Jeju, Busan and Seoul peer cultures.
- Generations: those who drank with workmates, and those who socialize online.
- Same-age and across-age friendships.
- North and South: comrade language against friend language.
- Migrants, defectors and the mixed-heritage young, whose friend circles may be thin or special.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South in surveys and fiction; thin and filtered for the North. Exact lands of the nation are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English for comparative scales.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** loneliness and suicide data; informing on friends in the North; school bullying.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
