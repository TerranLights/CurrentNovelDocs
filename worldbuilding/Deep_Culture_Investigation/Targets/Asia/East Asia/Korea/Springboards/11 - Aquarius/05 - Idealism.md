## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Classical Confucian ideals, Donghak and Cheondogyo hopes, Christian ideals, Buddhist ideals, and secular and socialist ideals as separate strands.
- The independence generation, the 1980s movement, and the present young.
- South and North: voluntary ideals against state-taught ideals.
- Jeju, with its own paradise legends.
- Urban professionals and returnees to the countryside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literary and movement idealism; mixed for everyday attitudes; the North's materials are state texts. Exact lands of the nation are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanmun for classical works; English for secondary studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** new-religious-movement communities and their critics; "sellout" accusations in politics; the North's ideal as propaganda.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
