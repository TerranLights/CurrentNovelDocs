*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for the island. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (place, faith, trade, origin, interest, cause), who recognize one another as members and owe one another something.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Farming and fishing villages, small towns, Seoul-area apartment blocks, and old inner-city districts.
- Jeju villages, with their own island labor customs.
- Northern collective farms, factories and neighborhood units.
- Generations: elders in village halls, young single-person households.
- Newcomers: defectors, migrant workers, marriage migrants, ethnic Korean returnees (Joseonjok) *check*.
- Christian congregations and Buddhist temple communities as separate community types.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South in surveys and media; mixed for the North, where sources are state texts or defector testimony. Exact lands of the nation are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English for ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 4.3 villages burned and emptied; surveillance by neighbors in the North; defector safety.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
