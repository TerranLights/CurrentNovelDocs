## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Generations: those who saw rapid growth, the 1980s protest generation, and the present young.
- Class and school-name groups, since belief in mobility may differ.
- Christian, Buddhist, shamanic and unaffiliated hope practices.
- Separated families and their descendants; people from Jeju and Gwangju with memorial hopes.
- Defectors in the South and people still in the North.
- Migrants and their children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (surveys, media, rites); thin for the North and mostly retrospective. Exact lands of the nation are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English for the international polls.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grief of disaster families; separated families, many now very old; defector safety.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
