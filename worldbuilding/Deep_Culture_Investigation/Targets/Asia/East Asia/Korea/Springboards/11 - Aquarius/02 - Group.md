## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Regional groupings (Honam, Yeongnam, Chungcheong, Gangwon, Seoul area, Jeju): regionalism in politics and hiring is a likely axis.
- Clan-based and non-clan families.
- Generations: conscripted men and older workers, versus young people with online groups.
- Men's and women's groups, including military service and its absence.
- North against South: party-assigned groups against voluntary ones.
- Religious communities: Protestant megachurch cells, Buddhist temples, Catholic parishes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (scales, surveys, fandom, workplace studies); thin and state-shaped for the North. Exact lands of the nation are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; English for the scale studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective blame by family (the social side only; legal questions belong to Libra and Capricorn); school bullying; regional prejudice; defector safety.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
