## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Reform from above (state campaigns) against reform from below (peasant, student, labor, civic movements).
- Regions: Honam and Gwangju, Jeju, Busan, Seoul; the North.
- Christian, Cheondogyo, Buddhist and secular reformers.
- Generations: the 1980s student movement, the candlelight generation, today's young activists.
- Women's, labor, farmers' and environmental movements as separate units.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (archives, press, civic groups); the North is thin and written through the state's view. Exact lands of the nation are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; hanmun and older hangul for pre-1945 papers; English for secondary works.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reform camps and forced re-education carry painful testimony; Gwangju and 4.3 are contested; political slogans differ by camp. Reform of laws belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
