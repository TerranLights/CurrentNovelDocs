# Korea: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for island sources. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

English keywords: hierarchy, seniority, yangban, caste-like stigma, address terms, school ranking, class background, inherited advantage.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of old elite and commoner lines:** whether descent still counts, and where.
- **Graduates of the top universities and others.**
- **Men and women** inside workplace and household rank.
- **Jeju-do and its address and kin networks;** mainland regions and their rivalries.
- **Large-firm and small-firm staff;** regular and contract workers.
- **Northern class-background groups,** if the North's lands are carried in.
- **Migrant workers and marriage migrants** as a newer lower tier or outside it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in the South; the North's system is documented chiefly from outside and from defectors; historic records are rich but partly in classical Chinese.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; classical Chinese for archives, in translation first.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent stigma, class-background labels and slurs are sensitive; describe without applying them to living groups. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
