# Korea: Domain and folkways, authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul), with some Jeju-language (Jejueo) material. **Concept in one line:** the domain of authority and duty: who commands, who obeys, and what is owed; plus career, status, reputation, age ways, time ways, rank ways and order ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The pre-division southern and northern halves**, now one nation: separate formal authority systems, but compare, do not assume, how far they differ in daily practice.
- **Jeju-do:** its own language and a distinct local history of power and island life; compare against the mainland.
- **Provinces and the Seoul region:** regional loyalties and migration histories.
- **Generations:** those raised under authoritarian rule, under democracy, and after the 1997 crisis.
- **Women and men; religious groups:** Confucian-descended households, Protestants, Buddhists, Catholics, the unaffiliated.
- **Migrant and returnee communities,** including Koreans from China and from the former Soviet lands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the South (surveys, press, scholarship); thin and mediated for the North, where defector accounts and official texts are the main routes. Jeju is mixed.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; Jejueo for local oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** North Korean sources are state products or defector testimony, to be tagged by origin. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the dictionary entries for 권위, 의무, 도리 and 체면; note how each is defined and cross-referenced.
2. Search KOSIS and KGSS for occupation, hours worked and class-identity items.
3. Search the Encyclopedia of Korean Culture for 어른, 연장자, 신분 and 시간.
4. Search RISS for "한국인 권위주의" and "서열 문화" to list the standard Korean-language studies.
5. Run 갑질 and 꼰대 through BIGKinds to date when each term entered the press.
