# Korea: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for island sources. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Office-holders and ordinary citizens:** two different standards of answering may exist.
- **Founders' families and employees** in large firms.
- **Jeju-do,** with its own memory of state violence and its remedies.
- **Older and younger generations:** their images of "growing up" and who pays for what may differ.
- **Women carrying household management** against men as nominal heads.
- **North Korean public life,** where group review is the main mechanism; compare cautiously.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in the South for institutional accountability and public apology; thin for everyday household responsibility, which needs ethnography or time-use sources. Mixed on the North.
- **Languages needed:** Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** colonial, wartime and state-violence responsibility are contested; record disagreements, do not settle them. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
