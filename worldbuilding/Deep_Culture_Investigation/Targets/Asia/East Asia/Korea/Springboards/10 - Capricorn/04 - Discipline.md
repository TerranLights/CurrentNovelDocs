# Korea: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for island sources. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others or by oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Home, school, army, firm and temple or church:** each sets a different standard; compare by setting first.
- **Students on the exam track** and those in vocational or arts schools.
- **Jeju-do:** the divers' lifetime training and the cooperative's rules.
- **Generations:** those who were physically punished at school and those raised after it was restricted.
- **Women and men,** given the men's conscription years.
- **Protestant, Buddhist and Catholic practitioners:** differing spiritual disciplines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for schooling and the military; mixed for family discipline, which is mostly in surveys and memoir; thin on North Korean everyday discipline beyond official and defector sources.
- **Languages needed:** Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse and suicide in barracks, sports and schools are painful subjects; use institutional reports and handle accounts with care. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
