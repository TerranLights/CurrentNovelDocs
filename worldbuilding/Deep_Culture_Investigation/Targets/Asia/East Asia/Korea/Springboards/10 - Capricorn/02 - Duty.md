# Korea: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for island sources. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eldest sons, other sons and daughters:** the older ritual system assigned duties unevenly; compare how families now describe it.
- **Jeju-do:** its diving cooperatives and neighbor exchange as a separate model.
- **Rural villages and big-city apartment households.**
- **Christian, Buddhist and unaffiliated families,** who differ over ancestor rites.
- **Men who served and men exempted;** women's relation to service.
- **The North's organized group life** against the South's family-centered duties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in the South (surveys, ethnography, drama); mixed on Jeju; the North mostly official or defector sources.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; classical Chinese for the oldest texts, in translation first.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** military service and its exemptions are politically charged; tag by source. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
