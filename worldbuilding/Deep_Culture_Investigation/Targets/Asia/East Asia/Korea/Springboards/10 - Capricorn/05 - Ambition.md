# Korea: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for island sources. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is, in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Exam-track students and those outside it:** vocational, arts and sport pathways.
- **Seoul-area and other regions,** including Jeju-do and its diaspora.
- **Women and men,** given career breaks at marriage and childbirth.
- **Founder families and salaried employees.**
- **Generations:** the postwar builders, the 1990s cohort, the post-1997 cohort.
- **Migrants from the North** and their children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in the South: panels, surveys, slang, fiction; thin for the North beyond official campaigns and defector accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mobility debates are politically charged; slang terms are partisan or insulting at times, so describe them without using them as labels for people. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Look up 야망, 포부, 욕심 and 출세; compare tone in the example sentences.
2. Search the education-expenditure survey and KLIPS for tables by income and region.
3. Search news archives for 흙수저, N포 세대 and 갓생 to date each term.
4. Search for the Saemaul and Chollima campaigns side by side.
