# Korea: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Korea, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Korean (hangul); Jejueo for island sources. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Test-track graduates, vocational graduates and the self-taught.**
- **Regular and contract workers;** civil servants and private-sector staff.
- **Men and women** in hiring and promotion.
- **Seoul-area and other regions,** including Jeju-do, in access to elite schools.
- **Generations** that took a single exam as the road to rise and those facing today's mixed routes.
- **Founder families and salaried managers.**
- **The North's merit and background mix,** if the North's lands are carried in; sources are official and defector.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in the South for polls and scandal coverage; historical exam data is rich; the North thin.
- **Languages needed:** Korean; classical Chinese for exam records, in translation first.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** admissions scandals involve named living people; use published reporting only and do not repeat accusations. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Look up 능력주의, 공정 and 실력 in the dictionary and in RISS titles.
2. Search KEDI for admissions-fairness surveys.
3. Search the Munkwa database for sample records by region and year.
4. Search news archives for 낙하산 and 블라인드 채용 to track the argument.
