## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha** ger districts versus rural soums; **Oirat** groups (Dörvöd, Torguud, Zakhchin, Bayad, Myangad, Olnod): separate religious histories.
- **Buryat** (Dornod, Selenge, Khentii), **Darkhad** and **Dukha (Tsaatan)** of Khövsgöl: best-documented shamanic practice.
- **Kazakh** of Bayan-Ölgii and **Khoton** (Uvs): Islamic frame, own healers.
- **Altai Uriankhai, Dariganga, Barga, Ööld:** small groups for Stage 0.
- Lamas, shamans, Tengerist revivalists, Christian converts, secular urbanites; female shamans (удган); city versus steppe for sleep and care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on shamanism and Buddhism in English; mixed on mental health and confinement; thin on sleep and the unconscious. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (header carries China too); written for the Mongolian core lands.
