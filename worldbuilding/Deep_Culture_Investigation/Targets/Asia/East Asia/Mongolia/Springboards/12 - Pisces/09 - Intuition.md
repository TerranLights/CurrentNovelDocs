# Mongolia: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Khalkha Mongolian (Cyrillic; classical Mongol script for manuscripts); Kazakh (Cyrillic) for Bayan-Ölgii; Buryat, Oirat, Tuvan and Dukha forms; English for the agency and ethnographic literature. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders against urban professionals:** different kinds of expertise (weather, animals against data and tests).
- **Lamas, shamans, almanac readers and traditional doctors** as separate guides.
- **Kazakh eagle hunters and diviners of Bayan-Ölgii;** **Buryat, Darkhad, Dukha and Oirat communities** (to be tested).
- **Women and men; elders and the young;** **doctors, nurses and traditional healers.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on weather, dzud and divination; thin on "gut feeling" in daily life and work. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (the header carries China as well as Mongolia); this sheet is written for the Mongolian core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian Cyrillic; classical script for manuscripts; Kazakh; English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic and divinatory knowledge may be restricted; almanac use is studied here as a practice, never as a source; a researcher must not present a people's intuition as a fixed trait.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
