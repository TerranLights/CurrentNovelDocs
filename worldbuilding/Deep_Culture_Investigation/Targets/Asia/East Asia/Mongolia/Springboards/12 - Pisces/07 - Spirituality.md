*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Khalkha Mongolian (Cyrillic; classical Mongol script for scriptural and chronicle sources); Kazakh (Cyrillic) for Bayan-Ölgii; Buryat, Tuvan and Oirat forms; English and French for ethnography; Chinese for pilgrimage and Qing-era sources. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion (the experience and the practice; institutional faith belongs to Sagittarius).
