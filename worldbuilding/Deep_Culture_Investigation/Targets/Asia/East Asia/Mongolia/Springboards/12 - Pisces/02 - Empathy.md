# Mongolia: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Khalkha Mongolian (Cyrillic), with Kazakh (Cyrillic), Buryat and Oirat forms; classical Mongol script for old dictionaries and moral texts; some psychology literature is in English or Russian. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ulaanbaatar professionals against herding households:** different training, different habits of speaking about feeling.
- **Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat, Dukha and Darkhad:** local speech and child-rearing may differ (to be verified).
- **Kazakh of Bayan-Ölgii:** their own language of sympathy.
- **Generations:** socialist-schooled elders against the post-1990 cohort; **gender;** **doctors and patients;** **lamas, shamans and secular counselors** as three kinds of listener.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Little published on Mongolian empathy as such; most material will be adjacent (psychology theses, curricula, helpline reports, media). The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (the header carries China as well as Mongolia); this sheet is written for the Mongolian core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian Cyrillic; some English; Kazakh for Bayan-Ölgii; Russian for older psychology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** helpline records involve children and are likely only in aggregate; testimony of persecution involves living families.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
