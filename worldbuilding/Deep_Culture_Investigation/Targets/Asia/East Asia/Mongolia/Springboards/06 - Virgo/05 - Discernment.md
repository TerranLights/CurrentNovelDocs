# Mongolia: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Tuvan; Russian for socialist-era material. **Concept in one line:** telling things apart and judging well: true from false, good from bad, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't, and what to say and what to keep quiet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Lamas and their lay clients, shamanic communities and secular urban households: different authorities for judgment.
- Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan and Dukha communities: different tricksters and tests.
- Herders (judging animals, weather and neighbors) against ger-district migrants and apartment-block households.
- Generations: those raised under party censorship, those schooled in the 1990s, and the online young.
- Traders and brokers, miners and their middlemen; elders and the young.
- Women and men as advisers and as targets of fraud (a research question).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Epics, tales and shaman ethnography are rich; everyday judgment of people and truth is thin and mostly in Mongolian.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian Cyrillic; Kazakh for Bayan-Olgii; Russian for older archives. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the political repression of the 1930s and informer histories; shamanic and monastic knowledge; victims of fraud. Do not publish personal names.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (a category only).
