# Mongolia: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for calligraphy and older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects; Tibetan for ritual manuals; Russian for the planned-economy period. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it; where the culture puts the line between exactness and "good enough".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Monastic specialists (Tsam, thangka, liturgy) against lay craftspeople and musicians.
- Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh, Dukha and Tuvan makers: different crafts, different finish.
- Herders (practical rightness of the job) against urban schoolchildren (exam and grade pressure).
- Socialist-era factory and cooperative workers against private-business owners (cashmere, exports).
- Women and men in craft and in school: who is held to exacting standards in which field.
- Generations: Soviet-schooled elders, the 1990s cohort, and online youth.
