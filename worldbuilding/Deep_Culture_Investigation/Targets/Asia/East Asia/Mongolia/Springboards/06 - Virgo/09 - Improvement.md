# Mongolia: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script and Tibetan for pre-modern and Buddhist texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Tuvan; Russian for the socialist period. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, work, home, land, community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed; what the culture treats as progress and who decides.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Herding households (who may hold land improvements at arm's length) against ger-district residents and apartment owners.
- Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan and Dukha communities: separate answers to state and donor "improvement".
- Monastic and lay Buddhist learners against secular self-help readers and shamanic communities.
- Soviet-era literacy and "culture" campaign cohorts, the 1990s generation, and the online young.
- Returnees and diaspora, miners and mining-town residents, civil servants.
- Women and men, and first-generation university students.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Official development and campaign records are open; personal self-improvement habits and minority views are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian Cyrillic; Russian for socialist-era campaigns; Tibetan and classical Mongol for older texts; Kazakh for Bayan-Olgii. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "backwardness" campaigns and their effects on lamas, herders and minorities; ger-district displacement; community consent for minority projects.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (a category only).
