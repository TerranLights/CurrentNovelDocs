# Mongolia: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic, with traditional script for old texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Tuvan; Russian for the socialist period. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Khalkha herders in the steppe and Gobi: work shaped by the herd and the season.
- Hangai and Khovsgol forest-steppe households compared with Gobi camel herders.
- Dukha (Tsaatan) reindeer herders and Tuvan groups: different animals, taiga work, trade.
- Bayan-Olgii Kazakhs: eagle hunting, felt and embroidery craft, market trade.
- Buryats of the northeast, Dariganga, Uzemchin and Oirat groups: farming, craft and herding mixes.
- Ulaanbaatar wage workers, ger-district migrants, mine and construction workers, and former collective-farm workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: herding and the socialist period are well studied; the urban service sector and the informal economy are thinner; minority groups thin.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian Cyrillic; Russian for older work; Kazakh for Bayan-Olgii. Traditional script only for old texts. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** illegal or informal mining accounts can identify workers; keep them anonymous.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (a category only).

4. Read the proverb section in a Mongolian folklore collection and collect lines under "ажил" and "залхуу".
5. Look up the Sant Maral Foundation's list of past surveys for a work or income module.
