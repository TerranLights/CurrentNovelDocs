# Mongolia: Domain and folkways, work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional Mongol script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects (Dörvöd, Torguud, Bayad, Zakhchin, Uriankhai), Tuvan (Dukha, Khovd Tuvans), Russian for socialist-era sources. **Concept in one line:** what is good work and a well-kept life: work, service, daily routine, health, the kept house and body.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Khalkha herders (Central, Hangai, Gobi) against Ulaanbaatar households: setting changes work and routine.
- Gobi camel herders (scarce water) against Hangai and Khövsgöl (rain, forest).
- Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii: own crafts, eagle hunting, Muslim practice, a separate labor calendar.
- Buryats (Dornod, Selenge), Dukha reindeer herders, Tuvans, Darkhad, Oirat groups, Dariganga, Üzemchin: different animals and crafts.
- Ex-negdel workers and their children, mine workers, service workers, rural-to-urban migrants.
- Women, men and children: the division of tasks within a herding household.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Herding and the socialist era are well documented in English and Russian; urban service work and cleanliness are thin; minority sources thinner still.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic) first; Russian for older work; Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan; traditional Mongol script. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic healing knowledge is often held by lineages; ask before collecting or publishing. Mining accounts can name illegal workers.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (a category only).

## 5. First queries
1. Search 1212.mn for "хөдөлмөр эрхлэлт" and the latest labor force tables; note which categories split herders from others.
2. Search eHRAF for Khalkha, Buryat, Kazakh and Tuvan documents under OCM categories 46, 51, 74 and 75.
3. Read Humphrey and Sneath on the herding household, collect their task terms, then check each in a Mongolian dictionary.
4. Query "хөдөлмөрийн баатар" in the national library catalog for honor lists and biographies.
5. Search "Манба дацан" and "Монгол уламжлалт анагаах ухаан" for the school and its publications.
