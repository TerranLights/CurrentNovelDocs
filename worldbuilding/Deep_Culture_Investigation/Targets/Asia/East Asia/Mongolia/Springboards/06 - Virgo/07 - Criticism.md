# Mongolia: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh (Bayan-Olgii), Buryat, Oirat dialects; Russian for socialist-era material. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, in feedback, reproach, satire, complaint, dissent and self-criticism; who may criticize whom, and where.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Khalkha urban professionals against herders: different settings for public and private criticism.
- Bayan-Olgii Kazakhs: the verse contest and the elder's reproach.
- Buryats, Oirat groups, Dukha: their own forms of correction and teasing.
- Monastic communities and lay Buddhists; shamanic communities.
- Generations: Soviet-era party members, the 1990 cohort, online youth.
- Journalists, officials and ordinary citizens; women and men as critics and as targets.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Press and protest records are fairly open; everyday family and workplace criticism is thin and needs fieldwork or oral sources.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian Cyrillic; Russian for party records; Kazakh for Bayan-Olgii; traditional script for older printed texts. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** purge and denunciation histories and living journalists' safety are sensitive; names of complainants stay out. Defamation and speech law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (a category only).
