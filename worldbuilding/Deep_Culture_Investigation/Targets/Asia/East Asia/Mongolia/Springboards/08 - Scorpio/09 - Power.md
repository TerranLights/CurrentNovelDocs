# Mongolia: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Khalkha Mongol (Cyrillic and traditional script), Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh, Russian, Mandarin for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it and how it is used; legitimate authority is Capricorn's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat and Dariganga groups: separate aristocratic and clan histories.
- Descendants of banner princes and of the Golden Lineage against commoner families.
- Herders against Ulaanbaatar elites and mining-sector figures.
- Aimag, soum and bag officials against the capital.
- Lamas, shamans and party figures as three kinds of authority.
- Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii and other small groups seeking a share of office.
- Women and men, and older and younger generations, on who may command.
- Inner Mongolian Mongols (if the lands include them): power within a different state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on rulers, parties and elections; mixed on money and hidden influence; thin on household and informal power.
- **Languages needed:** Khalkha Mongol; Classical Mongolian for old texts; Russian for Soviet-era sources; Mandarin for Inner Mongolia; Kazakh for Bayan-Ölgii.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current officials and business families (defamation risk); closed party files; the legitimacy of authority and law belongs to Capricorn, not here; the nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
