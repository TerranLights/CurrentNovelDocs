# Mongolia: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Khalkha Mongol (Cyrillic and traditional script), Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan, Mandarin for Inner Mongolia, Russian for Soviet-era sources. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so; self-control belongs to Capricorn and freedom from control to Aries.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Mobile herders against settled city dwellers: how control of time, space and money differs.
- Khalkha, Buryat and Oirat households on authority inside the home.
- Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii: a Muslim household order and a falconry tradition.
- Dukha reindeer herders and Tuvans under state park rules.
- Women and men, older and younger generations, on decision-making.
- Buddhist, shamanic and secular households on fate and effort.
- Mining-district herders against ger-district residents of Ulaanbaatar.
- Inner Mongolian Mongols (if the lands include them): grazing bans and resettlement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on pastoral management and the socialist period; mixed on household authority; thin on psychological control and private coercion.
- **Languages needed:** Khalkha Mongol; Russian and Mandarin for archives and policy; Kazakh, Buryat and Tuvan for minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence reports; closed party files; ceremonies for weather and spirits kept by their holders; Inner Mongolian resettlement is politically charged; the nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
