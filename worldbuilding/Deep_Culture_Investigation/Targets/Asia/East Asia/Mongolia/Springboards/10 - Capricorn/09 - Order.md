# Mongolia: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan; Chinese for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herders in different ecological zones (steppe, Gobi, mountain, taiga); ger-district residents against apartment dwellers in Ulaanbaatar; Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan and Dukha communities; monastic against lay; the old Party generation against younger city residents; Inner Mongolian Mongols under stability policy against Mongols elsewhere; men and women as they describe household order.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; ethnography is strong for household and herding order; public-order statistics are limited and need definition checks; minority sources thin.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Kazakh; Chinese; Tuvan for Dukha sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** taboo lists and ritual practices can hold restricted knowledge; riot and security sources are politically charged; Inner Mongolia material is sensitive. The nation's exact post-war lands remain to be settled; the rest is written for core Mongol lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
