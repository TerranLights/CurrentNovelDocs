*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herder children in dormitory schools against city children; monastic against lay; racehorse-training and wrestling circles; conscripts and officers; Kazakh community (Islamic practice, Kazakh-language school); Buryats; Dukha reindeer herders; Mongols in Inner Mongolia; young men's online self-improvement audiences against their elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; wrestling, schooling and religion are well covered in Mongolian; home discipline is mainly in surveys and ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Japanese, Kazakh and Chinese for the cases above.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child jockeys, hazing and alcohol are sensitive; Inner Mongolia sources are politically divided; monastic practice may hold restricted teachings. The nation's exact post-war lands remain to be settled; the rest is written for core Mongol lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
