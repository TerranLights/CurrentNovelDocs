# Mongolia: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan; Chinese for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Families claiming noble or Chinggisid descent against families of commoner origin; former Party families; monastic lineages; herders against city dwellers and ger-district residents; the capital against the aimags; Khalkha against Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan and Dukha communities; Inner Mongolian Mongols compared with Han neighbors; men and women in each setting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the pre-modern and Qing ranks; thin for current attitudes to rank; minority ranking systems are mostly oral.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Classical Mongolian script for archives; Kazakh; Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent claims and purge-era lists; ethnic ranking in Inner Mongolia; monastic teachings. The nation's exact post-war lands remain to be settled; the rest is written for core Mongol lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
