# Mongolia: Domain and folkways, authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional Mongol script for older and Inner Mongolian texts), with Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh and Tuvan for the minorities; Chinese for sources on the Mongol lands in China. **Concept in one line:** who commands, who obeys, and what is owed (career, status, reputation, age, time, rank, order and power).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Khalkha herders against Ulaanbaatar's urban and ger-district residents; the Oirat peoples (Dörvöd, Torguud, Zakhchin, Khoton and others); Buryats of the northeast; Darkhad and Tuvan or Dukha reindeer herders of the north; Kazakhs of the far west; Barga, Khorchin, Chakhar, Ordos and other Mongols of Inner Mongolia; Hui and Han settlers; monastic against lay; the older party generation against post-1990 cohorts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the empire and the twentieth century; mixed for contemporary opinion; thin on minority-language views and for Inner Mongolia in Mongolian.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Chinese for Inner Mongolia, Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** purges and repression memory; politics in Inner Mongolia; restricted ritual and shamanic knowledge. The nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled, since its header lists China and Mongolia as pre-war lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
