# Mongolia: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat, Tuvan; Chinese for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herding households with and without close neighbors; Ulaanbaatar apartment and ger-district families; migrants working abroad and the households they support; monastic communities; Buryats; Kazakhs; Oirat groups; Dukha and Darkhad herders; Mongols of Inner Mongolia against Han neighbors there; soldiers and ex-soldiers against civilians.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; family and herding duties lean on ethnography; migration and remittance data are fair; minority-language sources thin.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Kazakh; Chinese for Inner Mongolia.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some rites at ovoo sites and mountain worship may carry restricted knowledge; sources on wartime and purge memory are politically charged. The nation's exact post-war lands remain to be settled; the rest is written for core Mongol lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
