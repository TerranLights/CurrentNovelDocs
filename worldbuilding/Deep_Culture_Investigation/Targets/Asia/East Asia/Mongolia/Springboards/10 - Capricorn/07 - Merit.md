# Mongolia: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan; Chinese for Inner Mongolia; Persian and Chinese for the thirteenth-century sources. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herders against city workers; Ulaanbaatar graduates against aimag graduates; families with Party or business ties against those without; men and women; Khalkha, Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan and Dukha communities; monastic against lay; Inner Mongolian Mongols under quota and language rules; students who studied abroad against those who stayed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; honors and exam data are plentiful; people's actual beliefs about fairness are mostly in surveys and interviews; minority views thin.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Persian or English translation for Rashid al-Din; Kazakh; Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and patronage claims are politically charged; keep each accusation tied to its source. The nation's exact post-war lands remain to be settled; the rest is written for core Mongol lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
