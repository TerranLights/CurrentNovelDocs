# Mongolia: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Tuvan, Buryat; Chinese for Inner Mongolia; Korean and Japanese for labor-migrant and student sources. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herder households (rich in stock and poor) against the ger-district poor and city professionals; mining-region households; labor migrants and returnees; students abroad; women and men; Kazakhs; Tuvans; Buryats; Inner Mongolian Mongols in the two school tracks; the old Party elite and its children against new entrepreneurs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; enrollment, migration and livestock figures are good; the inner meaning of ambition is in literature, religion and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Korean or Japanese for migrants; Chinese; Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrant labor abuse and debt; party patronage; political education choices in Inner Mongolia. The nation's exact post-war lands remain to be settled; the rest is written for core Mongol lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
