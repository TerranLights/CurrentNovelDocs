# Mongolia: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat; Chinese for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herding households of the western aimags and the Gobi, where risk is high; Ulaanbaatar renters and apartment owners; mining-area communities against distant city residents; families of the repressed; monks and laypeople; men and women as described in each; Kazakhs, Buryats, Dukha and Darkhad; Inner Mongolian Mongols compared with Mongols in the Mongolian state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; policy, press and audit data are plentiful; deep accounts of what "being answerable" means are in ethnography and religious teaching and need Mongolian reading.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Kazakh and Buryat for minority comparison; Chinese for Inner Mongolia.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** purge and Cultural Revolution memory; politics of mining; shamanic and ritual knowledge held by specialists. The nation's exact post-war lands remain to be settled; the rest is written for core Mongol lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
