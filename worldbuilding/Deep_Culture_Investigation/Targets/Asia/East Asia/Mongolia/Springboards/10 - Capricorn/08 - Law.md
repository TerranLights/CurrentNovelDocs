# Mongolia: Law, the spirit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat, Tuvan; Chinese for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether to obey, use, bend or avoid it. This sheet takes the spirit of law only; the letter of the law belongs to Libra.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herders with customary pasture ties against ger-district and apartment dwellers; Ulaanbaatar against aimag centers; the elderly who remember socialist legality and the post-1990 generation; Khalkha, Oirat, Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan and Dukha communities; monastic against lay; Mongols in Inner Mongolia against Mongols in the Mongolian state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for old codes and surveys of trust; mixed for everyday practice; minority customary law mostly oral.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script); Kazakh; Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption allegations, the Inner Mongolia material and the memory of 1930s trials are sensitive. Law here means spirit only; the law's letter and statutes are Libra's. The nation's exact post-war lands remain to be settled; the rest is written for core Mongol lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
