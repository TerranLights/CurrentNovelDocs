## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Ulaanbaatar readers with Facebook-centered news against herders who rely on radio, phone and word of mouth.
- Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii, with Kazakh-language channels.
- Buddhist clergy, almanac readers and seers as holders of restricted knowledge.
- Buryats and Tuvans, with different media reach.
- Mongolian speakers in the south, under a different information system.
- Different generations and education levels on trust.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for outlets and polls; mixed for rural information habits; thin on informal channels.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian Cyrillic; Kazakh; Russian for older records; Chinese for the south.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monastic and diviner knowledge; surveillance and censorship in the southern lands. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet follows the core lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
