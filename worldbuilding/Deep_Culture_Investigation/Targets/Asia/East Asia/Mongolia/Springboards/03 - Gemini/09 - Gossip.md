## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Small herding communities against Ulaanbaatar ger districts and apartment blocks.
- Soum centers and aimag towns, where everyone knows each other.
- Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii, with their own gossip words and norms.
- Buddhist, shamanic and secular circles.
- Men's and women's talk spaces (barbers and salons; herders' gatherings).
- Young Facebook users against older speakers.
- Mongolian speakers in the south, with different limits on speech.
