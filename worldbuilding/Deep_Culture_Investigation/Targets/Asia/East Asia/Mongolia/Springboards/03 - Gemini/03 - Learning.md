*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Herder families against Ulaanbaatar ger-district and apartment families.
- Gobi, Khangai and western herding zones, where distances to school differ.
- Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii, in Kazakh-medium schools.
- Dukha, Darkhad and other Tuvan-speaking groups of Khövsgöl.
- Buddhist monastic learners against secular pupils.
- Mongolian speakers in the south, where Chinese carries part of the schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official education and donor reports; mixed for family teaching; thin in English for home learning in minority groups.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian Cyrillic; traditional script; Tibetan for monastic sources; Kazakh; Chinese for the south.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monastic and ritual learning; schooling language policy in the southern lands is contested. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet follows the core lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
