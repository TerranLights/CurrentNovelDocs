*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Khalkha, Buryat, Oirat and Darkhad groups on religion and identity.
- Buddhist lay and monastic communities against shamanic practitioners.
- Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii, living between two homelands.
- Mixed families (Russian-Mongolian, Chinese-Mongolian, Korean-Mongolian).
- Urban Ulaanbaatar against rural herders on cosmopolitan outlook.
- Mongolian speakers in the south, living between Mongol and Han worlds.
