*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Herding camp neighbors against city apartment and ger-district dwellers.
- Newcomers from the countryside, linked by home district, against long-settled city families.
- Khalkha lineage and clan practice against Buryat, Oirat and Darkhad groups.
- Kazakh families and their cross-border kin.
- Buddhist clergy and lay supporters; shamanic kin groups.
- Migrant workers abroad and the households that receive from them.
- Mongolian speakers in the south, with networks that cross into Han society.
