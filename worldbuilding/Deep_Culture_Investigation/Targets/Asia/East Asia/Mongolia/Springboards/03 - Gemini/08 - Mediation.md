## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Herding communities (elders and bag heads) against Ulaanbaatar residents using professionals or the city's local offices.
- Buddhist and shamanic communities, where intercession with the spirit world is part of settling quarrels.
- Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii, with elders and *biys*.
- Mining-region herders, with outside mediators.
- Buryat, Dörvöd and Darkhad groups, with distinct elder roles.
- Mongolian speakers in the south, with state mediation committees.
