# Mongolia: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan, Oirat dialects, Chinese for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men**, by region and age; **herders against city workers**; **ger-district against apartment residents**.
- **Descendants of old noble lines** against commoner families (if any claim survives); **elders** against youth.
- **Minorities:** Kazakhs, Tuvan Dukha, Buryats, Khoton, Oirat groups (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Myangad); Chinese-descended residents.
- **Inner Mongolia**: Mongols, Han, Hui, Daur and Ewenki, under a different state classification.
- **People with disabilities; LGBTQ people; mining-area and border-town communities.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich for statistics and gender; thin for rank today and for minorities' own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Kazakh and Tuvan; Chinese; Russian for older work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** discrimination against minorities, LGBTQ people and Chinese-descended residents; Inner Mongolia's politics around language and schooling; any claim about noble descent. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the core Mongolian lands plus Inner Mongolia.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
