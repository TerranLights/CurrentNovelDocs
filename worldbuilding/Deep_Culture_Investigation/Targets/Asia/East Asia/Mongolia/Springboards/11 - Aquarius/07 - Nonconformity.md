# Mongolia: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan, Oirat dialects, Chinese for Inner Mongolia, Russian for Soviet-era press. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or nature; and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban youth** against **herders**; **men** against **women**; **older generation** against **post-1990 generation**.
- **Shamans (böö, udgan)** and **Buddhist lamas** against **Christian and other new congregations**.
- **Rock, metal and hip-hop scenes** and **tattoo and skate circles**; **LGBTQ people**.
- **Darkhad and Buryat** shamanic regions; **Tuvan Dukha**; **Kazakhs** of Bayan-Ölgii and Muslim **Khoton**.
- **Inner Mongolia**: Mongol activists, students and musicians; Han, Hui, Daur and Ewenki neighbors.
