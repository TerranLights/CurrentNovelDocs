# Mongolia: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan, Oirat dialects, Chinese for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men's and women's friendships**; **herding camps** against **soum centers** and **Ulaanbaatar**.
- **Class and school cohorts**: Soviet-era generation, 1990s youth, present youth.
- **Darkhad**, **Buryat** and **Tuvan Dukha** of the north; **Oirat** peoples of the west; **Kazakhs** of Bayan-Ölgii and Muslim **Khoton**.
- **Monastic and lay Buddhist** circles; **Christian congregations**; **hip-hop and rock scenes**.
- **Inner Mongolia**: Chahar, Ordos, Khorchin, Barga, Daur, Ewenki, with Han and Hui neighbors; **Mongolians abroad**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed: strong on epic and sworn brotherhood, thin on present-day ordinary friendship; little survey data; urban youth better covered than herders.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian, Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan, Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drinking and its harms; state "friendship" talk that serves politics (Soviet, Chinese); sexuality and chosen family in a conservative setting. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the core Mongolian lands plus Inner Mongolia.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
