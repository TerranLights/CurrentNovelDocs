# Mongolia: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Tuvan, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Chinese for Inner Mongolia, Russian for Soviet-era sources. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha intellectuals and poets** against **Oirat** and **Buryat** literary traditions.
- **Buddhist clergy**, **lay revival groups** and **shamanic practitioners**.
- **Soviet-era generation** against the **post-1990 generation**; **youth activists** against **elders**.
- **Herders protecting rivers** against **mining-region residents** and **city professionals**.
- **Kazakhs** and **Tuvan Dukha** with their own ideals of honor and customs.
- **Inner Mongolia**: Mongol writers and students, Han migrants, Hui, Daur and Ewenki; **Mongolians abroad**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on literature and 20th-century ideology, thin for everyday moral idealism, thin for minority voices.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Russian; Kazakh and Tuvan; Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** purges that killed idealists; activists jailed or intimidated; Inner Mongolia's politics; some ritual and religious teachings. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the core Mongolian lands plus Inner Mongolia.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
