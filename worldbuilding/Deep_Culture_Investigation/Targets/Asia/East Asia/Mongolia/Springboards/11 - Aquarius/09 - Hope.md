# Mongolia: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Oirat dialects, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan, Chinese for Inner Mongolia, Korean for migrants (*check*). **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders** whose hope tracks weather and livestock, against **city and ger-district** residents and **mining-region** workers.
- **Youth** against **elders**; **women** against **men**; **migrants** and **families left behind**.
- **Buddhist laity**, **shamanic practitioners**, **Christian converts**.
- **Oirat** west, **Darkhad** and **Buryat** north, **Tuvan Dukha**, **Kazakhs** and **Khoton**.
- **Inner Mongolia**: pastoral banners, farming zones and cities; Han, Hui, Daur and Ewenki.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in oral blessing and religion, thinner in surveys; migration and mining hope are well covered in English.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Chinese; Korean for migrant sources; Kazakh and Tuvan; Russian for older material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and despair; shamanic and Buddhist ritual texts; Inner Mongolia's politics and language disputes; political promises tied to elections. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the core Mongolian lands plus Inner Mongolia.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
