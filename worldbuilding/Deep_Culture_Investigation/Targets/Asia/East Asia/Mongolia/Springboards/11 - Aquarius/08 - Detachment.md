# Mongolia: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Tuvan, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Chinese for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance, whether emotional, personal or social, from people, one's own feelings or outcomes; which forms are asked about as valued (calm, objectivity, impartiality) and which as condemned (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders in far-spread camps** against **soum-center and Ulaanbaatar** residents.
- **Men and women**; **elders and youth**; **monks and lay people**; **shamanic** and **Christian** communities.
- **Darkhad, Buryat and Tuvan Dukha** of the north; **Oirat** west; **Kazakhs** and **Khoton**.
- **Professionals** whose work demands detachment: doctors, journalists, officials.
- **Inner Mongolia**: Mongol herders, resettled households, urban Mongols; Han and Hui neighbors; Daur and Ewenki.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed; anecdotal in outsider accounts; some survey proxies; little direct research.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Chinese for Inner Mongolia; Russian for older studies; Kazakh and Tuvan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and mental health; alcohol; political repression and fear of being visible; Buddhist and shamanic esoteric teaching. Outsider impressions of "reserve" must be treated as claims to test, not findings. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the core Mongolian lands plus Inner Mongolia.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
