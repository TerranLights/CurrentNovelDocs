# Mongolia: Domain and folkways, community and ideals (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional vertical script for Inner Mongolia and older texts), plus Oirat dialects, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan and Chinese for the minorities and the southern lands. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups, and what do they hope for (friends, groups, hopes, humanitarian ideals).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha herders against Ulaanbaatar ger-district and apartment residents:** different neighbors, different gathering places.
- **Western Oirat peoples** (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Myangad, Uriankhai) and the Jangar tradition.
- **Darkhad and Khövsgöl**, **Buryat** of the northeast, **Tuvan (Dukha reindeer herders)**: shamanic and borderland communities.
- **Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii** and Muslim **Khoton**: another language, faith and diaspora tie.
- **Inner Mongolia**: Chahar, Ordos, Khorchin, Barga, Daur, Ewenki, plus Han and Hui neighbors; a separate institutional history.
- **Buddhist monastic communities** against lay herders; **urban youth** against elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich: strong English ethnography, thin primary sources for minority peoples; Mongolian-language material is large and little indexed in English.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Chinese for Inner Mongolia, Russian for Soviet-era works; Kazakh, Tuvan and Buryat for the minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** purge-era records and Inner Mongolia's political campaigns and language-policy disputes; shamanic and clan ritual knowledge (some is restricted); advocacy sources on Inner Mongolia need cross-checking. The exact lands of the post-war nation are still to be settled; the rest is written for the core Mongolian lands plus Inner Mongolia.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
