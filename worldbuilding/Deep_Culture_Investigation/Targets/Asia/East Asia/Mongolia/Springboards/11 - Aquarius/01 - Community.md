# Mongolia: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for Inner Mongolia), Oirat dialects, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (place, faith, trade, origin, interest, cause), who recognize one another as members and owe one another something, and the felt quality of that bond.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mongolian | олон нийтийн, нийгэмлэг (olon niitiin, niigemleg) | of the public; community, society | Modern abstract words; find where the word "community" is a loan |
| Mongolian | айл, хот айл (ail, khot ail) | household or family; camp of households | Basic unit that "community" is built from |
| Mongolian | хөрш, хөршлөх, айл саахалт (khörsh, khörshlökh, ail saakhalt) | neighbor; to neighbor | Camping near one another; shared work and visits |
| Mongolian | нутаг, нутгийн хүн, нутгийн холбоо (nutag, nutgiin khün, nutgiin kholboo) | homeland, countryman, hometown union | Community by place of origin, strong in the city |
| Mongolian | багийн иргэдийн хурал, сум, баг, хороо (bagiin irgediin khural, sum, bag, khoroo) | bag citizens' meeting; local units | Smallest administrative community; khoroo in the city |
| Mongolian | гэр хороолол, хашаа (ger khoroolol, khashaa) | ger district, fenced plot | Urban edge settlement |
| Mongolian | хүрээ, дацан, хийд (khüree, datsan, khiid) | monastic camp-town, college, monastery | Faith community, pre-socialist and revived |
| Mongolian | овоо тахих, овооны найр (ovoo takhikh, ovoony nair) | to worship the cairn; the cairn feast | Local rite that gathers a district |
| Mongolian | зуд, туслах (zud, tuslakh) | winter disaster; to help | Community in disaster |
| Buryat / Kazakh / Tuvan / Chinese | *check* | words for neighbor, community, village | 社区, 嘎查, 屯 for Inner Mongolia *check* |

English keywords: Mongolian herder community, camp group, ger district, bag, hometown association, ovoo, dzud, Buddhist monastery community, Inner Mongolia village.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Camp-based herders** across the Khangai, Gobi and eastern steppe regions, set against **soum centers** and **Ulaanbaatar** (apartment blocks against ger districts).
- **Buddhist monastic communities** and their lay patrons, against **shamanic Darkhad and Buryat** groups.
- **Western Oirat groups** (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Myangad); **Tuvan Dukha**; **Kazakhs and Khoton** in Bayan-Ölgii and Khovd.
- **Inner Mongolia**: pastoral banners, farming zones, and cities with Han majorities; **Daur**, **Ewenki**, **Hui** neighbors.
- **Overseas Mongolians** (Korea, Czech lands, United States *check*) as communities made abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong English ethnography on herders, thin on ger districts' own voices, thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Russian for older works; Chinese for Inner Mongolia; Kazakh and Tuvan for those peoples.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some ritual knowledge and local clan matters are held within communities; Inner Mongolia's politics. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the core Mongolian lands plus Inner Mongolia.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Mongolian dictionary and NSO glossary for нийгэмлэг, олон нийт and хөрш to see which words carry "community".
2. Pull the ADB and UN-Habitat ger-area studies and note how they describe neighbor networks and local units (bag, khoroo).
3. Search the 2009 to 2010 dzud reports for "relatives", "neighbors" and herder help, by aimag.
4. Search Facebook and news for "нутгийн холбоо" plus a province, and list the groups and what they do.
5. Compare one Khalkha, one Oirat and one Inner Mongolian ethnography for what each calls the basic local group.
