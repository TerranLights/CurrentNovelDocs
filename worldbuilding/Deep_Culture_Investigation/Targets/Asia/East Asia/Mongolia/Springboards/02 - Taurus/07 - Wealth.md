# Mongolia: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Chinese (Inner Mongolia), Russian for older sources. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it. (The left-right axis, government spending, is read under Ownership.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large-herd** against **small-herd** households, and **ex-herders** now in the city.
- **Ulaanbaatar business** and **political** elites against **mining-town** workers.
- **Informal miners** and **cashmere** goat herders.
- **Kazakhs** of Bayan-Ölgii with feast-based display.
- **Dukha** and **Darkhad**: reindeer as wealth.
- **Oirat** and **Buryat** communities with their own jewelry and feast customs.
- **Monastic** wealth historically.
- **Women** as holders of jewelry and money.
- **Inner Mongolian** Mongols and Ordos, if included.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed to rich: strong on income, herds and mining; thin on private fortunes and on unrecorded inheritance. The exact lands the nation carries are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian, Kazakh, Russian, Chinese for Qing-era and Inner Mongolian sources, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Corruption claims and named fortunes carry legal risk; check by public record. Law and tax belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
