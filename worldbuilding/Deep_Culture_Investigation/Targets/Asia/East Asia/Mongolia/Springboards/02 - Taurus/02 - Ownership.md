# Mongolia: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Chinese and Russian for outside sources. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on. (What the statutes say belongs to Libra or Capricorn; this sheet looks at customs, claims and talk.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha** pastoralists against **Oirat** and **Buryat** groups: different camp and pasture customs.
- **Kazakhs** of Bayan-Ölgii with their own clan marks.
- **Dukha** and **Darkhad** of Khövsgöl: reindeer and spirit-mastered land.
- **Ulaanbaatar plot-holders**, apartment owners and **tenants**.
- **Mining-area** residents (Gobi, Erdenet).
- **Monasteries** and lay temples as holders.
- **Inner Mongolian** herders under grassland contracting, if included.
- **Women** as herders and heirs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed. Rich on pastoral tenure and mining governance; thin on informal inheritance and on small minorities. The exact lands the nation carries are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian, Russian (older studies), Kazakh, Chinese, Manchu for Qing records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** The Chinggis name, sacred mountains and Inner Mongolian land are politically or religiously charged. Law belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Look up өмч, эзэмших and эзэн in Mongolian dictionaries; note whether owning and holding are separated.
2. Search Fernández-Giménez and Mearns for "pasture tenure Mongolia" and list the terms they use for rights.
3. Search NSO for housing tenure and livestock-by-owner tables.
4. Search the *Secret History* index for хувь and "subjects".
5. Search Mongolian news for "Чингис хаан нэр" and trademark disputes.
