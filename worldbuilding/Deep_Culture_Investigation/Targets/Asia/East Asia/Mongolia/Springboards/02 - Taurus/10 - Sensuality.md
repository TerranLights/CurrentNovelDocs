## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha** herders against **Ulaanbaatar** city dwellers: foods, baths and music differ.
- **Oirat** groups with distinct song and dance traditions (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud).
- **Buryat** of the northeast with their own music and foods.
- **Kazakhs** of Bayan-Ölgii: qymyz, dombyra, a different feast table.
- **Dukha** and **Darkhad** of the north.
- **Buddhist** monastic and **lay** households; **Muslim** Kazakh households.
- **Men and women**, **youth and elders**.
- **Inner Mongolian** Mongols, if included.
