# Mongolia: Stubbornness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Chinese (Inner Mongolia), Russian and English for press. **Concept in one line:** holding to a position, habit or course against persuasion, evidence or pressure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders** and **youth** in herding and city households.
- **Khalkha** with **Oirat** (Zunghar-era history to check) and **Buryat** communities.
- **Kazakhs** of Bayan-Ölgii: a minority holding to language and faith.
- **Dukha** and **Darkhad**: small groups under pressure.
- **Mining-affected herders** against company and state.
- **Ger-district** plot holders facing redevelopment.
- **Political parties** and parliamentarians in deadlock.
- **Inner Mongolian** Mongols and schools, if included.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed. Rich on political and protest history; thin on everyday habits and on private "stubborn" character; read the label with care, because the same act may be called principle or obstruction depending on the speaker. The exact lands the nation carries are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian, Kazakh, Russian, Chinese, English; classical Mongolian for chronicles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** The 1930s repressions and Inner Mongolian protests are sensitive; ask carefully. Law and dispute-settlement as law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
