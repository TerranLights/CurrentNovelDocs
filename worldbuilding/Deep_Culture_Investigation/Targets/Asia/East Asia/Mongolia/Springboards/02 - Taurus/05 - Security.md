# Mongolia: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Chinese (Inner Mongolia), Russian and English for reports. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders in zud-prone Khangai and Gobi** against steppe herders of the east: different risks and reserves.
- **Western Oirat** and **Altai Kazakh** herders with winter-pasture and migration choices.
- **Dukha** and **Darkhad** of the north: reindeer and distance.
- **Ger-district** households and **apartment** households of Ulaanbaatar.
- **Mining-town** workers and **informal miners**.
- **Pensioners** and the **young** unemployed.
- **Monastic communities** as a traditional shelter.
- **Inner Mongolian** herders under grazing bans, if included.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich on weather disaster, insurance and welfare programs; mixed on informal sharing; thin on private fear and on small minorities. The exact lands the nation carries are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian, Kazakh, Russian, Chinese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Crime, domestic violence and Inner Mongolian relocation are sensitive. Law and policing as law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

1. Search NAMEM and the IFRC for dzud reports and list each type by name.
