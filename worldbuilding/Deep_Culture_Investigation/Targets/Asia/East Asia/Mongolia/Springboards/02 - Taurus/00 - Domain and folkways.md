# Mongolia: The domain: possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional upright script for older texts), Buryat, Oirat dialects, Kazakh and Tuvan (Cyrillic), Russian and Chinese for outside sources. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how is it held (possessions, money, resources, values, self-worth), plus the food ways, dress ways and wealth ways of the place.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha** of the central and eastern steppe and the **Khangai** against **Gobi** herders: different herd mixes and risks.
- **Oirat groups** in the west (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Olot, Myangad, Uriankhai).
- **Buryat** of the northeast; **Dariganga** and **Khamnigan** in the east.
- **Kazakhs** of Bayan-Ölgii and Khovd: a separate language and religion, with their own gift and feast words.
- **Dukha (Tsaatan)** reindeer herders and **Darkhad** of Khövsgöl.
- **Ulaanbaatar ger-district settlers** against apartment dwellers, and **mining-area** residents (Gobi, Erdenet): urban and extractive wealth.
- **Inner Mongolian groups** (Chahar, Ordos, Khorchin, Barga, Daur, Evenki) if the lands include them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for herding economy and the 13th-century and Qing-era past; mixed for urban consumption; thin in print for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Russian (Soviet-era studies), Kazakh, Buryat, Chinese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sacred-mountain and shamanic knowledge may be restricted; ask how the peoples share it. The Chinggis cult and Inner Mongolia are politically charged. The exact lands the nation carries are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands. Law belongs to Libra or Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
