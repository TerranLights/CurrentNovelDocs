# Mongolia: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Chinese (Inner Mongolia), Russian. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders** in crowded pasture districts against herders in sparse ones.
- **Urban couples** in Ulaanbaatar against **rural** couples.
- **Khalkha**, **Oirat** and **Buryat** with different marriage customs.
- **Kazakhs** of Bayan-Ölgii with an Islamic and clan frame.
- **Dukha** and **Darkhad**: sharing in small groups.
- **Women** and **men** as survivors or accused; **in-laws**.
- **Mining-area** communities guarding local jobs and land.
- **Inner Mongolian** Mongols, if included.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed. Good survey data on partner violence; thin on everyday possessiveness over objects and on small minorities; folklore is rich. The exact lands the nation carries are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian, Kazakh, Russian, Chinese, English; Manchu for Qing archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Partner violence data and survivor accounts need care and consent. Evil-eye and spirit beliefs may be private. Law on domestic violence belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
