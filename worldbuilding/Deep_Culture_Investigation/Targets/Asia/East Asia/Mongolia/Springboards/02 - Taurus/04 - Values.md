# Mongolia: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Chinese, Russian. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, how those things are ranked, and how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha** herders against **Ulaanbaatar** professionals: differing rankings of work, kin and religion.
- **Buddhist laity**, **Tengerist and shamanic** followers, **Christian** converts and **Muslim** Kazakhs.
- **Oirat** groups with their own epic and memory of an old state.
- **Buryat** and **Dariganga** communities.
- **Dukha** and **Darkhad** of the north.
- **Generations:** socialist-era elders against post-1990 youth.
- **Mining-area residents** and herders who lost land or water.
- **Inner Mongolian** Mongols, if included.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Mixed. Rich on stated and national values (policy, schools, public rites); thinner on private moral reasoning; surveys may not separate regional or minority samples. The exact lands the nation carries are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian, Kazakh, Buryat, Russian, Chinese; classical Mongolian for older texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nationalism and talk of foreigners can be charged; religious and shamanic teaching may be private or restricted. Law and justice belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
