# Mongolia: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional script for old texts), Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan; Russian, Korean and Chinese for migration and older travelers' accounts; English. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage, migration (including seasonal rounds as the herding peoples describe them) and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Khalkha herders of the Khangai and Gobi against Altai Kazakhs and Tuvans, Dukha reindeer herders in Khövsgöl and Buryats of the northeast; Oirat western groups; urban Ulaanbaatar residents and recent ger-district migrants; monastics and pilgrims; labor migrants and returnees; students abroad; tour-camp owners and guides; the educated elite and its travel. Whether the lands of Inner Mongolia come into the target is open.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on pastoral mobility and historical travel accounts; mixed on modern tourism and migration; thin on how ordinary people evaluate the traveler or the foreign. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Korean and Russian for migration; Kazakh and Buryat for regional work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some mountain sites have access customs; migration data may be incomplete; disaster years carry loss.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
