# Mongolia: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional script for old texts), Buryat, Oirat, Kazakh, Tuvan; Russian; English. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word, including honesty toward the state.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herding households and city residents; market traders, officials, business owners and professionals; Khalkha, Buryat, Oirat, Kazakh and Tuvan communities, each with its own trickster tales and proverbs; monks, shamans and teachers as moral voices; men and women; mining-sector workers and contractors; older and younger people; migrants working abroad. Whether the lands of Inner Mongolia come into the target is open.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on corruption surveys and folklore; thin on everyday honesty and white lies. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; English for the indexes; Kazakh and Buryat for minority proverbs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption allegations are politically live and some cases are contested; the survey results record perception as well as experience; no law research here (Libra, Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
