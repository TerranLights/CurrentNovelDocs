# Mongolia: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional script for old texts), Buryat, Oirat, Kazakh, Tuvan; Russian; English. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content (not the legal sense of "conviction").

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Democrats of 1990 and their later opponents; monks and former monks; herders affected by mines and activists against mining; urban protesters; Buryat intellectuals and their descendants; Kazakh and Tuvan community leaders; shamanic practitioners guarding lineage rules; converts to newer faiths; party members and independents; men and women, and older and younger activists. Whether the lands of Inner Mongolia come into the target is open.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on 1990 and on the Mongol-era chronicle in print; thinner on ordinary conviction and on how it is judged socially. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script); Russian; English; Kazakh and Buryat for minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the repressions of the 1930s; political violence and protest cases; the unresolved 1998 killing of a democrat; some activists face pressure. This sheet does not treat the legal sense of "conviction"; that belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
