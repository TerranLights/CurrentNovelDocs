# Mongolia: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional script for old texts), Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan; Chinese and Russian for older outside accounts; English. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true about God, the world, people and right and wrong (religious or secular), and what warrant people ask for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Khalkha herders and Ulaanbaatar residents; Buryat, Oirat, Darkhad, Dukha, Tuvan, Uriankhai and Kazakh communities; Muslim Khoton; Buddhist monastics and lay people; shamanic practitioners; Christian converts and members of newer faiths; people who say they hold no religion; those schooled under the socialist system and later generations; older and younger believers within one family. Whether the lands of Inner Mongolia come into the target is open.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on folk and shamanic belief in ethnography; thin on private belief and on skeptics. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan; French for Delaplace; Russian for older work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic and lineage knowledge; taboo subjects around death and spirits; advocacy databases may exaggerate numbers; beliefs may be hidden from outsiders.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
