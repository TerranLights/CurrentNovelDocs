# Mongolia: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional script for pre-1941 documents), Buryat, Oirat dialects, Kazakh, Tuvan; Russian and Chinese for older records; English. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or the state, and freedom as a belief and political ideal, including collective freedom (independence, sovereignty).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herders and city dwellers; Khalkha, Buryat, Oirat, Kazakh and Tuvan communities and their different histories of the 1930s; those who lived through 1990 and those born after; journalists and publishers; former monks and families of the repressed; people in mining districts; women; labor migrants abroad; Chinese-, Korean- and Russian-heritage residents. Whether the lands of Inner Mongolia come into the target is open.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on the 1911, 1921 and 1990 turning points in English and Mongolian; thinner on ordinary people's own words for freedom. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Russian, Chinese for Qing-era files; Kazakh and Buryat for minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the political killings and repressions of the 1930s; the unsolved 1998 murder of a leading democrat; press-freedom disputes are live; law research is only for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
