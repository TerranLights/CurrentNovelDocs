# Mongolia: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional script for old texts), Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan; English, Russian. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what cannot be seen or proven (gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Lay Khalkha Buddhists and monastics; Oirat communities with their own Buddhist lineage; Buryat Buddhists, shamanic practitioners and Christians; Tuvan and Dukha shamanic communities; Kazakh Muslims of Bayan-Ölgii; Khoton Muslims; urban Christians and the religiously unaffiliated; people raised in the socialist school system against their grandparents; Gobi herders against Ulaanbaatar apartment dwellers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Buddhism and shamanism, mixed on Islam and Christianity, thin on private doubt. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian essential; Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan for regional work; Russian for older scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lineage ritual knowledge; the memory of lamas killed; some people are wary of being asked about belief.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
