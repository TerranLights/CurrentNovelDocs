# Mongolia: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic), plus Buryat, Oirat, Kazakh and Tuvan for regional work; English; Russian for older scholarship. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it, and where people say that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herders of the Khangai, Gobi and Altai against Ulaanbaatar's apartment and ger-district residents; Khalkha, Buryat, Oirat, Kazakh and Tuvan communities; monks, former monks and lay Buddhists; people schooled in the socialist period against the post-1990 generation; men and women on questions of duty; mining-town workers (Oyu Tolgoi and others) against herders; the educated returning from study abroad; Mongolian labor migrants in South Korea and elsewhere, as a diaspora; urban Christian converts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on herding ethnography and on poetry; thin on direct survey items about "meaning". The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic), English, Russian; Kazakh, Buryat and Tuvan for minority voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and alcohol data; personal accounts of loss; ritual knowledge held within lineages.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
