# Mongolia: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Oirat, Buryat, Chinese for Inner Mongolia and Xinjiang; Persian and Russian for older outside accounts. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost: physical and moral courage, endurance, and the courage to leave.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oirat (Dörvöd, Torguud, Zakhchin) with their epic tradition, against Khalkha:** the epic's home.
- **Buryat:** the Geser tradition and border memory.
- **Kazakhs and Dukha:** hunting and climate-defined endurance.
- **Wrestlers and archers, horse riders:** the three games' own codes.
- **Herders and miners:** dangerous work in different settings.
- **Women, elders, youth:** who is called brave and for what.
- **Veterans and peacekeepers:** state-sponsored valor.
- **Inner Mongolian and Xinjiang Mongols:** the same words under another state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in epic and chronicle; mixed on modern moral courage; thin on private fear.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (both scripts), Kazakh, Chinese, Russian; Persian for outside chronicles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** accounts of political repression; epic singers' lineage knowledge; the exact lands of the target are still to be settled (the header carries Mongolia and China), so this sheet is written for the Mongolian core with Mongolic pointers from the Chinese side.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search "зориг" and "эр зориг" in the Mongolian proverb collections and note who is the subject.
2. Find the UNESCO files for "Mongolian traditional art of wrestling" and read the community statements.
3. Search "Жангар туульс" for a Mongolian-language text and note the hero's tests.
4. Search "1990 оны өлсгөлөн" with "Сүхбаатарын талбай" for first-person accounts.
5. Search "зуд аврах" at the National Emergency Management Agency for named rescue stories.
