# Mongolia: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat, Chinese for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed: righteous anger, temper, grudges, collective anger.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat, Darkhad:** differing spirit beliefs and norms of speech.
- **Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii:** honor-linked anger in Kazakh terms.
- **Herders and city residents:** quarrels in the ger district against the pasture camp.
- **Men and women, elders and youth:** who may show anger and where.
- **Drinkers and non-drinkers:** the alcohol connection.
- **Lamas and shamans:** religious frames for anger and its cure.
- **Inner Mongolian Mongols:** anger under another state and press.
- **Migrants abroad:** anger in foreign workplaces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich in folklore and medicine, thinner on measured emotion; strong health and violence reports.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (both scripts), Kazakh, Chinese, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic-violence testimony; protest and nationalist material; advocacy sources need cross-checks; the exact lands of the target are still to be settled (the header carries Mongolia and China), so this sheet is written for the Mongolian core with Mongolic pointers from the Chinese side.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
