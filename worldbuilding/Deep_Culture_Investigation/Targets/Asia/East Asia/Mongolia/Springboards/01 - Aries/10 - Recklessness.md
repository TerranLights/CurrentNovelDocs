*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men, drivers, drinkers:** the usual focus of risk statistics.
- **Herders (Gobi, steppe, mountain, taiga):** risk as a pasture and herd choice, differently shaped by land.
- **Kazakh hunters and Dukha reindeer people:** a risk-managing culture with its own vocabulary.
- **Ulaanbaatar and rural provinces:** traffic and nightlife against pasture work.
- **Miners and construction workers:** occupational risk.
- **Women and men; elders and youth:** who is blamed.
- **Lamas and monastery communities:** religious cautions.
- **Inner Mongolian and Xinjiang Oirat Mongols:** the same words under another regulation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on health and road statistics; mixed on finance narratives; thin on everyday attitudes.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (both scripts), Chinese, Kazakh, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drinking and violence topics; financial scandals involving named people; marmot-hunting stories can stigmatize groups; the exact lands of the target are still to be settled (the header carries Mongolia and China), so this sheet is written for the Mongolian core with Mongolic pointers from the Chinese side.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
