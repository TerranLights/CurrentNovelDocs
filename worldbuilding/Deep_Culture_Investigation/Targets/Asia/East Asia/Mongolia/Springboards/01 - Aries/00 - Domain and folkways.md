# Mongolia: Domain and folkways (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional vertical script in Inner Mongolia and in revival use), Oirat dialects, Buryat, Kazakh, Tuvan; Chinese for the Inner Mongolian side. **Concept in one line:** what a self is here and how it asserts itself: personhood, the body, appearance, beginnings, and the ways of naming.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha** (the majority), **Oirat** peoples (Dörvöd, Torguud, Bayad, Zakhchin, Olnuud): separate dialects, dress and clan names.
- **Buryat**, **Dariganga**, **Khotgoid** and **Darkhad** (northern shamanic region): different naming and spirit worlds.
- **Kazakh** (Bayan-Ölgii) and **Tuvan** and **Dukha** (Khövsgöl): Turkic-language people with their own self-terms.
- **Inner Mongolian** groups (Chahar, Khorchin, Ordos, Barga, Daur, Evenki): a Chinese-state frame for the same questions.
- **Rural herders, city residents (Ulaanbaatar ger districts), diasporas** (South Korea, Czechia, the United States): the self at home and away.
- **Lamas and ex-lamas**, **shamans**, and **Muslim Kazakhs**: religious naming and renaming practices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in anthropology and historical texts; thin for naming statistics and online self-presentation; mixed for survey items.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script); Chinese for Inner Mongolia; Russian for older scholarship; Kazakh, Buryat, Tuvan for minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic knowledge and spirit names held by lineages; Inner Mongolian ethnic and language policy topics; the exact lands of the target are still to be settled (the header carries Mongolia and China), so this sheet is written for the Mongolian core with pointers for the Mongolic and other peoples of the Chinese side.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
