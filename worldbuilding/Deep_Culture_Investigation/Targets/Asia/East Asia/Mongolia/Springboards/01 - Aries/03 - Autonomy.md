# Mongolia: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Oirat, Buryat, Chinese for Inner Mongolia; Russian for older scholarship. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled, from personal life choices to the self-rule of households, herding groups and regions.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western and northern pastoral herders against Gobi herders:** pasture decisions differ by land.
- **Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat, Dariganga:** differing family and herding group forms.
- **Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii:** a province with its own majority and its own governance habits.
- **Dukha and Tuvan reindeer people:** small groups with distinct rules on movement.
- **Ger-district households and apartment dwellers:** autonomy over housing and daily life.
- **Women, elders, youth:** differing say in household matters.
- **Inner Mongolian groups:** autonomy under a Chinese regional frame.
- **Lamas and monastery communities, shamans:** religious authority over personal choices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on pastoral decision-making and collectivization; mixed on family choices; thin on personal bodily autonomy.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (both scripts), Kazakh, Chinese, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Inner Mongolian political autonomy history and current policy are sensitive; domestic violence testimony; the exact lands of the target are still to be settled (the header carries Mongolia and China), so this sheet is written for the Mongolian core with Mongolic pointers from the Chinese side.
