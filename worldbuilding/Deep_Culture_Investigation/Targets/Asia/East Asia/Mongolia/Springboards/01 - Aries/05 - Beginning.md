# Mongolia: Beginning (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Buryat, Kazakh, Oirat, Tuvan; Chinese for Inner Mongolia. **Concept in one line:** how a culture treats starting: origins, firsts, new years and seasons, birth, founding, and starting over after failure or migration.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha, Buryat, Oirat, Dukha, Tuvan, Kazakh:** different new years, origin stories and first-rites.
- **Herders against city residents:** seasonal camp moves against calendar-year starts.
- **Lamas, shamans, Muslims, Christians:** whose calendar and whose rites.
- **Migrants to Ulaanbaatar and abroad:** the fresh start by displacement.
- **Inner Mongolian Mongols:** founding cults under Chinese state sponsorship.
- **Elders and youth:** what is kept of the year-start rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in chronicles and festival description; mixed for migration narratives; thin for private first-rites.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (both scripts), Chinese, Kazakh, Buryat, Russian for older ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic and monastic ritual knowledge; the politics of founding dates and Chinggis Khan's cult; the exact lands of the target are still to be settled (the header carries Mongolia and China), so this sheet is written for the Mongolian core with Mongolic pointers from the Chinese side.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
