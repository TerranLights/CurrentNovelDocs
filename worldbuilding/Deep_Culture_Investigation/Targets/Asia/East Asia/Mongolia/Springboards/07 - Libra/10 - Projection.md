# Mongolia: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Chinese for Inner Mongolian lands; Buryat, Oirat forms *check*. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others what one disowns in oneself, and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Khalkha majority and Oirat, Buryat, Kazakh and Tuvan minorities; Han and other Chinese residents; urban Ulaanbaatar against rural; ger-district residents; Buddhist, shamanist and Muslim communities; descendants of purge victims; nationalists and their critics; Inner Mongolian Mongols and Han.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political enemy images and history; mixed for private attitudes; thin for minority views of the majority. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Mongolian core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Chinese; Kazakh; Russian for Soviet sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anti-neighbor hatred and violent groups need careful handling; accusations of separatism are state-driven and endanger informants; spirit beliefs may be private.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
