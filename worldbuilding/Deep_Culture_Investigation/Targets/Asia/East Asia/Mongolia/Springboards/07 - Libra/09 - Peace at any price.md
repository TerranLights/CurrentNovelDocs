## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Women and men within households; herders against Ulaanbaatar workers; families of purge victims; Buddhists against shamanist and Muslim communities; Kazakhs and other minorities in relation to the Khalkha majority; Dukha and Darkhad; mining-region residents; Inner Mongolian Mongols under state stability policy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political events and domestic-violence law; mixed for private silence, which surveys undercount; thin for minority views. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Mongolian core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Kazakh; Chinese; Russian for older work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse survivors' accounts need care; politically charged cases (2008, 2022, Inner Mongolia) have partisan sources; interviewing is not advised without ethical review.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
