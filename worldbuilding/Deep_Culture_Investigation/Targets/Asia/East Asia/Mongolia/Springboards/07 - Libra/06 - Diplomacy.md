*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Chinese, Russian for older diplomatic records; Buryat, Oirat *check*. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between peoples and states, and in daily social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Foreign-service professionals against herding households; Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat and Darkhad practices of welcome; Kazakhs (Muslim customs of mediation); Buddhist monastic envoys; business elites; Inner Mongolian Mongols mediating with Chinese authorities; borderland traders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state diplomacy and the Mongol Empire; mixed for daily tact, which is best seen in ethnography and memoir; thin for minority practices. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Mongolian core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Russian; Chinese; Persian or Latin for imperial-era sources, usually in translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current diplomatic pressure from neighbors is politically charged; some archives are closed.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
