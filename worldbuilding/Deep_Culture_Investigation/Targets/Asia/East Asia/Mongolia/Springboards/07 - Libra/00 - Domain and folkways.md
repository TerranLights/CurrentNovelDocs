*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Khalkha majority; Oirat groups (Dörvöd, Bayad, Torguud, Zakhchin, Uriankhai, Myangad); Buryats; Darkhad and Dukha (Tsaatan) reindeer people; Dariganga; Tuvans; Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii (Muslim, own customary law); Inner Mongolian groups (Chakhar, Khorchin, Ordos, Barga, Daur, Evenki); Han and Hui settlers; herders against Ulaanbaatar ger-district and apartment households; Buddhist, shamanist, Muslim and Christian families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the codes, statistics and ethnography; mixed for oral bargains and sworn friendship; thin in English for Oirat and Tuvan voices. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Mongolian core with Inner Mongolia flagged.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic, plus the traditional script for older codes); Kazakh; Chinese; Buryat and Oirat vocabulary from their dialect dictionaries, *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Inner Mongolian law and language policy are politically sensitive, and advocacy sources there need critical reading; shamanic and spirit-bargain knowledge is restricted to practitioners; domestic-violence data is underreported.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
