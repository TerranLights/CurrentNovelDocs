## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herders against urban households; Gobi, steppe, Altai and Khövsgöl herders (different seasonal rounds); Kazakh and Tuvan migrations; Dukha reindeer people; mining-region communities; Buddhist medical practitioners against biomedicine and shamanic healers; Inner Mongolian herders under grazing bans.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ecology, policy and traditional medicine; mixed for balance as a lived value; thin for minority terms. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Mongolian core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Tibetan terms in transliteration; Chinese; Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred mountains and healers' knowledge may be restricted; mining politics are contested.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
