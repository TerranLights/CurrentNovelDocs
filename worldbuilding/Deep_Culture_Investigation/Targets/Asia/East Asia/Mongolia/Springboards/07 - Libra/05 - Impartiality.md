# Mongolia: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic), Kazakh, Chinese for Inner Mongolian lands; Buryat, Oirat forms *check*. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Ulaanbaatar bureaucrats against rural soum officials; aimag-origin networks (those from the same home district); Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat and Darkhad kin groups; Kazakh clans in Bayan-Ölgii; party-affiliated and independent media; Buddhist monastic offices; Inner Mongolian Mongol and Han officials; judges, referees and journalists as professional groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes and corruption surveys; mixed for everyday favoritism, which is partly self-reported; thin for minority offices. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Mongolian core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Kazakh; Chinese; Russian for older work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** named corruption cases are partisan and may be defamatory; Inner Mongolian court material is restricted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
