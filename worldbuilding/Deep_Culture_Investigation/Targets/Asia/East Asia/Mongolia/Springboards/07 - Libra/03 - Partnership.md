# Mongolia: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Buryat and Oirat *check*, Chinese for Inner Mongolian lands. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between parties who share aims, risks and returns as more or less equals: love and marriage, work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Khalkha and Oirat couples; Buryat families with their ancestral household; Darkhad and Dukha herders (reindeer partnerships); Kazakhs (matchmaking alliance, Islam); Tuvans; urban Ulaanbaatar couples against rural ones; herding cooperatives against private households; Mongol-Han and Mongol-Kazakh mixed couples; Inner Mongolian couples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, law and history of alliance; mixed for ordinary couples' views; thin for Tuvan and Dukha partnership. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Mongolian core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Kazakh; Chinese; Russian for Soviet-era work; Buryat and Oirat as *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic spirit-partnerships are practitioners' knowledge; household violence and money disputes are underreported.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
