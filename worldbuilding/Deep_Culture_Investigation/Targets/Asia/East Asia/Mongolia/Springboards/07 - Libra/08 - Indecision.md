# Mongolia: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic), Kazakh, Chinese for Inner Mongolian lands; Buryat, Oirat forms *check*. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Herders deciding moves together against urban households; Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat and Darkhad practices of consulting spirits and elders; Buddhist families against shamanist, Muslim and Christian ones; Kazakh elders' councils; parliamentarians and party leaders; Inner Mongolian herders facing state migration programs; youth in Ulaanbaatar.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political delay and formal assemblies; mixed for personal indecision; thin for minority groups. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Mongolian core.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Kazakh; Chinese; Tibetan terms in transliteration for divination.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination and shamanic consultation are practitioners' knowledge and may be private; political blame for stalled deals is partisan.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
