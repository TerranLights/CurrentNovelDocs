*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic), Kazakh, Chinese for Inner Mongolian lands; Buryat, Oirat, Tuvan forms *check*. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 4. Flags

- **Languages needed:** Mongolian; Kazakh; Chinese; Russian for Soviet-era records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** offerings to spirits and mountains can be restricted; informal lending can involve debt shame.
