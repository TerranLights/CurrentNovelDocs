*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Tuvan; Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, crafts, problem-solving and everyday life; individual originality against faithful variation within a tradition; who owns what is made.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha rural makers against Ulaanbaatar artists:** workshop craft against gallery and studio work.
- **Oirat groups (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Altai Uriankhai):** distinct epic and song traditions; worth separating from Khalkha practice.
- **Buryats, Dariganga, Darkhad:** each has its own craft and ornament repertoire.
- **Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii:** felt, embroidery and eagle-hunter regalia as a separate craft world.
- **Dukha and Tuvans:** small-scale craft, tourist art, reindeer-based materials.
- **Socialist-era trained artists, post-1990 independents, online creators.**
- **Inner Mongolia, if carried:** Chakhar, Ordos, Khorchin, Barga, Daur, Ewenki, Oroqen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for epic, music and Buddhist art; mixed for modern art and everyday invention; thin for minority crafts outside Kazakh. The exact lands are still to be settled; written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Russian for older Soviet-era studies, Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line, English for the anthropology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred images and ritual objects; shamanic costume and its making; designs tied to clans or families.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
