# Mongolia: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Tuvan; Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action; against restraint, reserve and the mask.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ulaanbaatar and provincial centers against rural herders:** city anonymity and social media against small-group talk.
- **Women and men:** possibly different rules for emotion, speech and dress.
- **Generations:** elders, the socialist-era trained, and the post-1990 young.
- **Khalkha, Oirat groups (Dörvöd, Bayad, Torguud, Zakhchin, Altai Uriankhai), Buryats, Dariganga:** each has its own dress and song idiom.
- **Kazakhs (Bayan-Ölgii, Khovd):** sung verbal contests, Islamic modesty norms; separate from Mongol groups.
- **Dukha, Tuvans and Darkhad:** small peoples whose display is often shaped by tourism.
- **Monastics, lay Buddhists, shamanic practitioners and Christian converts:** different rules of voice.
- **Sexual and religious minorities.**
- **Inner Mongolia, if carried:** Chakhar, Ordos, Khorchin, Barga, Daur, Ewenki, Oroqen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for dress, song and protest history; mixed for private feeling and candor; thin for everyday emotion, which comes mostly from ethnography. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Russian for older sources, Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line, English for anthropology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic voice and spirit possession; gender and sexual minorities; protest and political speech, including on the Chinese side.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Mongolian dictionary: look up "илэрхийлэх", "шулуун", "ичгүүр" and "хэл ам"; note each sense and the example phrases.

4. Library: Buyandelger, *Tragic Spirits*, and a deel-by-group costume monograph; list the groups they cover.
5. Look up the latest Asian Barometer wave for Mongolia and note the items on speaking out and trust.
