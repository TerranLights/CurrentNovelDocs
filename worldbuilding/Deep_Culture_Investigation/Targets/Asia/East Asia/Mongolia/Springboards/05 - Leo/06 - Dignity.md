# Mongolia: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Tuvan; Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned. (Law is not researched here; see Libra and Capricorn.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders against city dwellers:** respect given to rural work against the status of an office job.
- **Ger-district residents against apartment owners in Ulaanbaatar.**
- **Elders and the young;** men and women; children.
- **Informal and mining workers, civil servants, herders.**
- **Khalkha, Oirat groups (Dörvöd, Bayad, Torguud, Zakhchin), Buryats, Dariganga:** possible differences in manners of respect.
- **Kazakhs, Tuvans, Dukha:** minority idioms of worth and respect.
- **People with disabilities; the very poor; survivors of repression and their families.**
- **Monastics and lay Buddhists.**
- **Inner Mongolia, if carried:** Chakhar, Ordos, Khorchin, Barga, Daur, Ewenki, Oroqen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for survey and agency material; mixed for ethnography; thin for what people say about dignity itself in Mongolian, so start with the words. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Russian for older sources, Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line, English for agency reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and child discipline; repression and its victims; mining and migrant labor; funeral practices; do not treat an advocacy or agency report as neutral.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. No law research here: see Libra and Capricorn.

## 5. First queries
1. Dictionary and corpus search: "нэр төр", "хүн чанар", "доромжлох", "басамжлах"; note whether dignity is distinct from honor.

3. Search "нинжа уурхайчид" (artisanal miners) with "хөдөлмөр" (labor) and read agency reports for the words used.
4. Library catalog: "Mongolian taboos customs цээр" and pick one handbook to read for what may never be done.
5. Search "Хэлмэгдэгсдийн дурсгалын музей" (Memorial Museum for the Victims of Repression) for oral-history holdings.
