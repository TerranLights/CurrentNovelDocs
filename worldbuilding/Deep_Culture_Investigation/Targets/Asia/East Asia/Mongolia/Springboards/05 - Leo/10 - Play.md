## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herder children against Ulaanbaatar children:** animal bones and horses as toys against apartments, streets and screens.
- **Ger-district children against apartment dwellers:** play space and safety.
- **Khalkha, Oirat groups (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Altai Uriankhai), Buryats, Dariganga:** regional games, riddles and festival play.
- **Kazakhs:** their own games and their link to Kazakhstan.
- **Tuvans, Dukha:** northern play and horse- or reindeer-based games.
- **Adults and elders:** table games, cards and outings against youth gaming.
- **Girls and boys:** differences in games and toys.
- **Inner Mongolia, if carried:** Chakhar, Ordos, Khorchin, Barga, Daur, Ewenki, Oroqen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for shagai, chess and folklore; mixed for children's everyday play and digital play; thin for adult play and minority games outside the Kazakh. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Russian for older folklore, Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line, English for esports and agency material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shagai used for fortune-telling is religious knowledge; gambling uses of play are outside this sheet; children's data needs care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
