# Mongolia: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Tuvan; Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended; honoring others. (No law research here.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women:** who is held to which standard of word, courage and reputation.
- **Khalkha, Oirat groups (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Altai Uriankhai), Buryats, Dariganga:** each with its own epic and oath tradition.
- **Herders and urban professionals:** word-of-mouth honor in small camps against institutional reputation.
- **Soldiers and veterans, wrestlers and sports figures.**
- **Kazakhs:** a Muslim minority with its own honor terms and codes.
- **Tuvans, Dukha:** small groups; honor norms barely recorded.
- **Buddhist clergy and shamanic practitioners:** vows and ritual honor.
- **Elders and the young.**
- **Inner Mongolia, if carried:** Chakhar, Ordos, Khorchin, Barga, Daur, Ewenki, Oroqen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for pre-modern oaths and loyalty, and for military and state ceremony; mixed for everyday honor; thin for what Mongolians say about honor itself. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Russian for socialist-era sources, Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line, English for scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ritual and sacred sites; nationalist claims of honor; violence and vengeance; women's reputation; do not treat a medieval or epic code as today's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
