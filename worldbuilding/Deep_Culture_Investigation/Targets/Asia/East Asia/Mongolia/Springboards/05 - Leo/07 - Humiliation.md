# Mongolia: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Tuvan; Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone; collective humiliation of peoples; humbling chosen.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha, Oirat groups (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Altai Uriankhai), Buryats, Dariganga:** possible differences in mockery and shame.
- **Kazakhs:** a Muslim minority with its own shame idiom (ұят).
- **Dukha, Tuvans, small groups:** shaming and teasing norms barely recorded.
- **Herders and city dwellers:** teasing in close camps against anonymity in Ulaanbaatar.
- **Women and men; children; conscripts and prisoners.**
- **Survivors of the 1930s repression and their families.**
- **Monastics and lay Buddhists:** discipline and humbling chosen.
- **Inner Mongolia, if carried:** Chakhar, Ordos, Khorchin, Barga, Daur, Ewenki, Oroqen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for collective and historical humiliation; mixed for school and online shaming; thin for everyday ridicule and rural norms. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Russian for older sources, Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line, English for agency reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse of women and children; hazing and detention; repression survivors; persecution in Inner Mongolia; the line between humor and cruelty; shame around suicide and mental illness.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Mongolian dictionary: compare "доромжлох", "гутаах", "егөөдөх" and "дээрэлхэх"; read the example sentences.
2. Search "наадам уралдаан сүүлчийн морь" (last horse in the Naadam race) and the wrestling rules on losing to see whether defeat has a rite.
3. WHO GSHS and UNICEF Mongolia: find the bullying tables and the latest year.
4. Library: Rossabi, *Modern Mongolia*; list the chapters on dependence and the purges.
5. Search Mongolian news for "олон нийтийн сүлжээгээр гутаах" (to disgrace through social media) and read recent cases.
