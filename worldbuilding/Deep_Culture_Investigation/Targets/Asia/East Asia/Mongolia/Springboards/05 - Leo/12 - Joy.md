# Mongolia: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic; traditional script for older texts), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat dialects, Tuvan; Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it; joy beside grief.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders and Ulaanbaatar residents:** feast-centered joy in camps against urban leisure and screens.
- **Khalkha, Oirat groups (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Altai Uriankhai), Buryats, Dariganga:** each with its own festival calendar and song idiom.
- **Kazakhs:** Nauryz, dombra and a Muslim reading of joy.
- **Tuvans and Dukha:** their New Year and hunting-herding joys.
- **Buddhist laity, clergy and shamanic practitioners:** spiritual joy, and how each side marks a festival.
- **Children and elders; men and women.**
- **People in poverty and in ger districts;** the unemployed and the well-off.
- **Inner Mongolia, if carried:** Chakhar, Ordos, Khorchin, Barga, Daur, Ewenki, Oroqen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for festivals and songs; mixed for measured happiness; thin for how people describe joy itself, and for the minorities except Kazakhs. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core Mongolian lands.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Russian for older sources, Chinese for the Inner Mongolia line, English for survey reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** funerals and mourning; sacred festivals and shamanic ritual; alcohol and its harms at feasts; do not treat a tourism image of the "happy nomad" as data.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Dictionary search: "баяр", "жаргал", "аз жаргал", "найр"; note which are joined and which are separate.

4. Search "зуслан" (summer camp) with "амралт" (rest) for urban leisure accounts.
5. Search "найрын дуу" (feast songs) and "хуримын дуу" (wedding songs) for songbooks.
