# Mongolia: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Chinese, Russian. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families against ger-district and apartment families:** distance, work and migration shape who leaves.
- **Mothers who raise children alone against fathers who raise them:** who is judged.
- **Children of migrant workers abroad against children of internal migrants.**
- **Elderly people in rural soums against those in Ulaanbaatar.**
- **Khalkha, Oirat and Buryat families;** Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii: different customs for taking in orphans.
- **Mining-region households, and emptied soums after the collectives.**
- **Inner Mongolia families,** with Chinese state child-welfare and left-behind-children programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for statistics and media coverage; mixed for experience in people's own words; thin for ritual and for small peoples. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Mongolian core and Inner Mongolia.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic, traditional script), Chinese, Kazakh, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Accounts of abandoned children and of abuse; identifying details of minors; blame attached to single mothers and migrants. Note variation, avoid stereotypes about drinking or family breakdown.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Adoption and custody law belongs to Libra.
