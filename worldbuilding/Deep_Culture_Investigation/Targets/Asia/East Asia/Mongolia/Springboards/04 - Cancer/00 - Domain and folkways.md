# Mongolia: Domain and folkways, home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Khalkha, Cyrillic; traditional vertical script for Inner Mongolia), Oirat and Buryat varieties, Kazakh (Bayan-Ölgii), Chinese (Inner Mongolia and other carried lands), Russian (Soviet-era scholarship). **Concept in one line:** what is home, and who is family? Home, family, parents, roots and nurture, plus the building, family and child-rearing folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha herding households against Ulaanbaatar ger-district and apartment households:** the same word "home" under very different roofs.
- **Western Mongols (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Oirat groups):** their own dialects, epic tradition and house customs.
- **Buryat in the north-east,** and Daur, Barga and Evenki-related groups in the Hulunbuir lands.
- **Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii,** with a separate language, house form and kin system.
- **Dukha and Darkhad of Khövsgöl:** reindeer and forest herders with a distinct dwelling.
- **Inner Mongolia:** Chahar, Ordos, Khorchin and Tümed Mongols living among Han majorities; Mongol households in mixed towns.
- **Gobi herders against steppe and mountain herders,** and religious difference (Buddhist, shamanist, Muslim, Christian households).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for household statistics and Mongolian-language ethnography; mixed for Inner Mongolia and smaller peoples; likely thin for Tuvan, Dukha and Oirat kin terms in open sources. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (the tracker header lists China and Mongolia as pre-war lands); this sheet is written for the Mongolian core and Inner Mongolia.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic), traditional Mongolian script for Inner Mongolia, Chinese; Russian for older ethnography; Kazakh for Bayan-Ölgii.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Shamanic and household ritual knowledge, family lineage records, and ancestor-spirit practices may be restricted by their keepers; check consent in published ethnography. Political sensitivity around Inner Mongolian language and schooling.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Law on the family and housing: see Libra and Capricorn.
