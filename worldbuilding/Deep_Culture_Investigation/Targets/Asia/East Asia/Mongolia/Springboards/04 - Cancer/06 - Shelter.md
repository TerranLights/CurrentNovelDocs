# Mongolia: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Chinese, Russian. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mobile herders against Ulaanbaatar ger-district residents:** the same cold, with very different shelter.
- **Street children and unhoused adults in the capital against rural poor.**
- **Dzud-hit herding households:** the Gobi, the west and the central steppe in different years.
- **Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii:** a Muslim hospitality tradition and winter-camp life.
- **Buddhist monasteries and lay households:** who offers lodging.
- **Inner Mongolia pastoral households against resettled herders and urban Mongols.**
- **Women and children leaving violent homes,** and migrant workers returning from abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for disaster and urban-housing policy; thin for homeless adults and for what people themselves call hospitality. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Mongolian core and Inner Mongolia.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic, traditional script), Chinese, Kazakh, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Reports on abused women and children; street-child accounts (consent and privacy); Chinese relief and resettlement policy.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Law on housing and asylum is outside this sign.

## 5. First queries
1. Search Mongolian news and the NSO for "орон гэргүй иргэд" (homeless citizens) and "хоргодох байр" (shelter).
2. Search "хоолойн хүүхэд" or "гудамжны хүүхэд Улаанбаатар" together with Christina Noble Children's Foundation.
3. Search FAO and World Bank for "Mongolia dzud livestock shelters" and read one evaluation.
4. Search a library catalog for the Mongol yam or "өртөө" (relay-station) system and Marco Polo's account of it.
5. Search "зочин хүлээн авах ёс" (customs of receiving guests) and "内蒙古 棚圈 建设" to open the hospitality and Inner Mongolia strands.
