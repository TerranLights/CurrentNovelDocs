# Mongolia: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Buryat, Oirat, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khalkha, Oirat (Dörvöd, Bayad, Zakhchin, Torguud, Üzemchin), Buryat:** different clan-name traditions and kin vocabulary.
- **Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii:** seven-ancestor tradition and their own marriage routes.
- **Darkhad and Dukha of Khövsgöl:** small groups with their own kin accounts.
- **Herding households against urban apartment and ger-district households:** distance from kin and how it is kept.
- **Inner Mongolia Mongols, Daur and Evenki, against Han and other carried peoples:** intermarriage and mixed families.
- **Religious difference:** Buddhist, shamanist, Muslim and Christian families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich on statistics and classic ethnography; mixed on how people in their own words define family; thin for smaller peoples. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Mongolian core and Inner Mongolia.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic), traditional script, Chinese; Kazakh, Buryat for minority sources; Russian for older studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ancestor-spirit practices and family genealogies are private; state surname policies in Inner Mongolia are sensitive.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Law on marriage and inheritance belongs to Libra and Scorpio.
