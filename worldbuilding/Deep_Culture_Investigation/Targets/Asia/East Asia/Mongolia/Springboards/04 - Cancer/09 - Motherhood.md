# Mongolia: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Buryat, Chinese, Russian. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it, including the mother as a figure of the culture (mother of the nation, Mother Earth, the mother tongue).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural herding mothers against Ulaanbaatar mothers:** birth setting, help from kin, and work.
- **Single mothers, mothers abroad as migrant workers, and mothers of many children.**
- **Khalkha, Oirat, Buryat and Kazakh mothers:** different birth customs and mother figures.
- **Dukha and Darkhad women of the north,** and Gobi women.
- **Buddhist, shamanist, Muslim and Christian mothers.**
- **Inner Mongolia Mongol mothers,** Daur and Evenki mothers, and Han and Hui mothers living among them.
- **Generations:** the socialist-era mother of many children against younger mothers after 1990.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for health and statistics; mixed for ideals and cult; thin for mothers' own accounts in open sources. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Mongolian core and Inner Mongolia.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic, traditional script), Chinese, Kazakh, Russian; Buryat *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Birth and pregnancy beliefs may be private; maternal-health outcomes; the Inner Mongolia orphans narrative and language policy are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
