## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders against urban residents:** wolves and dzud against crime and fire in ger districts.
- **Women, children, the elderly and the disabled:** who is described as needing protection, and by whom.
- **Buddhist, shamanist, Muslim and Christian households:** different protective objects and prayers.
- **Khalkha, Oirat groups and Buryat:** variation in blessings and spirits.
- **Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii and Dukha and Darkhad of the north:** minority customs of guarding.
- **Inner Mongolia Mongols, Daur and Evenki:** ovoo rites and herder safety under another administration.
- **Border-region residents and military families.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for disaster and child-protection reports; mixed on ritual protection; thin on how families describe it themselves. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Mongolian core and Inner Mongolia.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic, traditional script), Chinese, Kazakh, Russian; Buryat *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Domestic violence and child abuse reports; shamanic and household ritual knowledge; sacred-site access.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Law on protection (child protection, domestic violence) belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
