# Mongolia: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Buryat, Chinese, Russian. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mobile herding households against fixed-address urban households:** the same word for home, different practice.
- **Ger-district residents against apartment residents in Ulaanbaatar:** class and migration history.
- **Provincial towns (aimag centers) against the capital and the countryside.**
- **Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii, Dukha and Darkhad of Khövsgöl, and Buryat of the north-east:** different dwellings and house terms.
- **Gobi herders against steppe and forest-steppe herders:** pasture limits shape the camp cycle.
- **Inner Mongolia:** pastoral, semi-farming and urban Mongols, and resettled herders.
- **Han and other households in carried Chinese lands:** a different house tradition under the same post-war roof.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich on urban housing and ger heritage; mixed on what home means to residents in their own words; thin for small peoples. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Mongolian core and Inner Mongolia.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic), traditional script, Chinese; Kazakh and Buryat for minority material; Russian for older sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ritual rules about the hearth and the honored place may be treated as private by the keepers; resettlement and language politics in Inner Mongolia are sensitive.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Law on land and housing is outside this sign (Libra, Capricorn).

## 5. First queries
1. Download the UNESCO file on the Mongol ger and list its bearer communities and the customs it names.
2. In the NSO tables search "өрхийн орон сууцны төрөл" (household dwelling type) by aimag and district.
3. Search a library catalog for "Mongols from Country to City" and "Mongolian pastoral mobility seasonal camps".
4. Search "гэр хорооллын дахин төлөвлөлт" (ger-district redevelopment) and read two city or donor reports.
5. Search "киіз үй Баян-Өлгий" and "内蒙古 牧民 定居 住房" to open the Kazakh and Chinese strands.
