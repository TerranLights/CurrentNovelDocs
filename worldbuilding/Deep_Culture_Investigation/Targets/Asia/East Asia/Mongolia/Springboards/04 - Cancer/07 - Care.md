# Mongolia: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Mongolia, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mongolian (Cyrillic and traditional script), Kazakh, Chinese, Russian. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding households against Ulaanbaatar apartment and ger-district households:** distance from clinics and kin.
- **Women and men:** who bears care, and who is expected to.
- **Khalkha, Oirat and Buryat families,** and their healers.
- **Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii;** Dukha and Darkhad of Khövsgöl: separate healing and elder-care customs.
- **Buddhist, shamanist, Muslim and Christian households.**
- **Inner Mongolia:** Mongols, Daur and Evenki against Han and Hui neighbors, and rural against city eldercare.
- **Migrants' families,** with grandparents raising children left behind.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Rich for health-system and welfare policy; mixed for unpaid care; thin for how people describe care in their own words. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Mongolian core and Inner Mongolia.
- **Languages needed:** Mongolian (Cyrillic, traditional script), Chinese, Kazakh, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Patient privacy; shamanic healing knowledge; elder abuse and institutional neglect reports.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
