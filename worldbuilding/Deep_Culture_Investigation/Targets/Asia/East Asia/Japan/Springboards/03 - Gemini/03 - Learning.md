# Japan: Learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan languages; Portuguese and Korean for minority schools. **Concept in one line:** how people come to know and to do: in the family, by watching and pitching in, through play, in early school, at work and across a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban exam-track families against rural and island families:** juku use and distance to schools.
- **Family background:** check the studies of class and achievement.
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuans:** their own language-teaching efforts and school histories.
- **Zainichi Koreans (ethnic schools)**, **Nikkei Brazilians and other newcomers**, **night-school learners.**
- **Craft and arts lineages (iemoto)** against school learners; **girls and boys.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school measures, family spending and school ethnography; mixed for apprenticeship and informal learning; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan teaching.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; Portuguese and Korean for ethnic schools; Ainu and Ryukyuan for language classes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** school bullying and suicide data; Okinawan history of dialect punishment in schools; non-enrollment of foreign children.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
