# Japan: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Ainu; Ryukyuan languages; Korean for Zainichi sources. **Concept in one line (proposed):** how the culture lives with two-ness: two identities, languages or loyalties at once; the world divided into pairs; and how it treats people who show two faces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Okinawans (and Amami, Miyako, Yaeyama):** Uchinānchu and Yamatunchu; the kingdom's two-power past.
- **Ainu** and **wajin:** double identity and naming.
- **Zainichi Koreans and Chinese:** two names, ethnic schools, naturalization.
- **Mixed-heritage people (hāfu, daburu)**; **Nikkei Brazilian and Peruvian returnees.**
- **Hidden-Christian communities** (Nagasaki, Gotō); **religious households** with kami shelf and Buddhist altar.
- **Returnee children** and **international-school families.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Meiji-era pair concepts and on minority literature; mixed for surveys of identity; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan voices in English.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for kanbun and Ryukyu texts); Korean and Portuguese for minority sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Zainichi naming and discrimination; Ainu and Ryukyuan identity politics; hidden-Christian community privacy; the terms hāfu and daburu are contested.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
