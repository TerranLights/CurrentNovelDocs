# Japan: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kansai, Tokyo, Tohoku, Kyushu:** dialect and styles of talk; Kansai's comic traditions against Tokyo's.
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuans:** own languages and ritual speech; histories of enforced standard speech.
- **Deaf Japanese:** a language community with its own norms.
- **Workplace against home against school**; **generations** (digital natives).
- **Zainichi Koreans and newcomers** (Nikkei Brazilians, Filipinos, Chinese): speaking across languages, children as interpreters.
- **Urban and rural:** neighbors and village assemblies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in scholarship and corpora; check Japanese-language critiques of the English-language models; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan speech.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical Japanese for Heian and Edo texts); Ainu and Ryukyuan for primary material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu ritual speech belongs to its keepers; helpline material touches suicide; Okinawan history of enforced standard speech.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
