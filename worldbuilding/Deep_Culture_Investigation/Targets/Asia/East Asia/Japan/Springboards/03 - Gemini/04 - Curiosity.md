# Japan: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Ainu; Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, find out and explore, and how far the culture encourages, channels, limits or punishes it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuans:** the studied as well as the studying; island and Hokkaido scholarship.
- **Kansai, Tokyo, Tohoku:** differences in how much asking of strangers is welcome (to check).
- **Children against adults against elders:** curiosity programs, lifelong-learning groups.
- **Hobby communities (otaku, nature clubs, railway fans)** against general audiences.
- **Rural and urban:** neighbors' questions and the village's limits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science attitudes and reading; mixed for everyday prying and nosiness; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan views of being studied.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for Heian and Edo texts); Ainu and Ryukyuan for primary material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu remains and research ethics; Ainu knowledge held by its keepers; blood-type and personal questions touch workplace harassment (law goes to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
