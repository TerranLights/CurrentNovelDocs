# Japan: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan languages; Korean for Zainichi sources. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban singles and the old living alone** against **village and family households.**
- **Regions with strong origin associations:** Okinawa (moai, monchū), Tohoku, Kyushu.
- **Ainu** (Hokkaido and Tokyo), **Zainichi Koreans**, **Nikkei Brazilians**, other newcomers.
- **Salaried workers** against **self-employed and farm families.**
- **Disaster-affected areas** (Kobe 1995, Tohoku 2011) as natural experiments in ties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loneliness, support and marriage surveys; mixed for networks and cronyism (more critique than measurement); thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan tie structures.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; Korean and Portuguese for minority associations; Ainu and Ryukyuan terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** kodokushi and hikikomori involve suicide risk and family shame; Ainu and Ryukyu trade history carries colonial weight; fraud and cronyism claims (amakudari) require care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
