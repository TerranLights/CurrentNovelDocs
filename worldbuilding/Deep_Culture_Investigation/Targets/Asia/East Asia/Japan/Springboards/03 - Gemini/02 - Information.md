# Japan: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan languages; Korean and Portuguese for the minority press. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how the culture gathers, passes on, trusts, withholds and uses it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu:** data and knowledge ownership questions; own radio.
- **Ryukyuans:** a separate press and a separate war-records archive.
- **Tohoku, Kobe and Kumamoto disaster areas:** how information worked in crisis.
- **Zainichi Koreans, Nikkei Brazilians and other newcomers:** media in their own languages and information gaps.
- **Urban and rural**; **older and younger** readers (newspaper against phone).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, media and surveys; mixed for rumor and local knowledge; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan information practice.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; Korean and Portuguese for minority press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Kantō earthquake rumors and violence against Koreans; Ainu remains and research ethics; the law of access to information and state secrecy goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
