# Japan: Domain and folkways (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia (the archipelago: Honshu, Kyushu, Shikoku, Hokkaido, the Ryukyu chain and the smaller islands). **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; the Ryukyuan languages; Japanese Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** the domain of communication and learning: how people talk, learn and connect close to home (speech, schooling, neighbors, short journeys).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Regions by dialect:** Tohoku, Kanto, Kansai, Chūgoku-Shikoku, Kyushu; Hokkaido settler speech.
- **Ainu** (Hokkaido and urban Ainu): own language and teaching bodies.
- **Ryukyuans** (Okinawa, Amami, Miyako, Yaeyama, Yonaguni): several languages; standard-language enforcement.
- **Deaf Japanese**; **Zainichi Koreans** (Korean ethnic schools, 朝鮮学校); **newcomers** (Brazilian Nikkei, Filipino, Chinese, Vietnamese): schooling and language support.
- **Urban against rural**; **generations**; **Hachijō** and other island speech.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for standard-language use, schooling, time use and media; mixed for neighbors; thin and fragile for Ainu and Ryukyuan speech. The nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core islands.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; Ainu and Ryukyuan for primary material; English for pragmatics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Okinawan dialect-punishment history (方言札, *hōgen fuda*); Ainu cultural property and consent; Zainichi school politics.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
