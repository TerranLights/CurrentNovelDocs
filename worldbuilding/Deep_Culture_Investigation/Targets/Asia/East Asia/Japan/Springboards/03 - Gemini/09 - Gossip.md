# Japan: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Ainu; Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, with rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small villages and islands against big cities:** reach of talk and ostracism.
- **Kansai, Tokyo, Tohoku:** styles of teasing and gossip (to check).
- **Women's neighborhood networks (mothers, elders)** against **men's workplace and drinking networks.**
- **Teenagers (LINE, school boards)** against **older adults.**
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuan** communities: their sayings and sanctions.
- **Zainichi and newcomer communities:** rumor in close-knit groups and rumor about them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the gossip press, online pile-ons and school surveys; mixed for everyday gossip (rarely recorded); thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan talk-sanction customs.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for waka and Edo satire); Ainu and Ryukyuan for primary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** defamation and privacy law go to Libra and Capricorn, not here; stigma of illness and of atomic-bomb survivors; rumor violence in 1923 (see Information); names of private persons in pile-ons.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
