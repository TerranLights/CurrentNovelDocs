# Japan: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Ainu; Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between who stands between parties.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu:** *charanke* and elders against modern organizations.
- **Okinawa and Amami:** yuta, noro, Kume village past; U.S. base conflict as a mediation setting.
- **Villages, small towns and big cities:** headmen and chōnaikai against anonymous districts.
- **Firms against households against schools.**
- **Zainichi and newcomer communities:** who stands between them and city hall.
- **Religious communities:** temple, shrine, and new-religion counselors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corporate and school ethnography and Edo village records; mixed for neighbor and household mediation; thin for Ainu and Ryukyu settlement customs in English.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for old village papers); Ainu and Ryukyuan; Portuguese and Korean for minority go-betweens.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu oratory and ritual belong to its keepers; family-court and court-annexed conciliation (調停) go to Libra and Capricorn, not here; organized-crime sources need caution; medium practice is private.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
