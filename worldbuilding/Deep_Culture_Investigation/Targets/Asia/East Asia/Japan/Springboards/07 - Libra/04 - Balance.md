# Japan: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others, and the steadiness that results: in a life, a body, a household, a government and the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Okinawa and the Ryukyu chain (diet, longevity, islands); Hokkaido and Ainu communities; the Noto and other *satoyama* regions; coastal fishing villages; Tokyo and Osaka office workers against farm households; Tohoku after the 2011 disaster; Minamata and other polluted regions (imbalance); Ogasawara (an island reserve); generations of workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on hours, leave and diet; mixed on ecological and aesthetic balance (much is essayistic); thin on Indigenous accounts outside community sources. "Everything in moderation" must be tested, not assumed.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese and some Chinese for Edo and earlier texts; Ainu and Ryukyuan for Indigenous terms. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the core islands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu ritual detail; karoshi cases involve named families; contested science on longevity claims.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
