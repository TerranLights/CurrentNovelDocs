# Japan: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (classical for early sources); Ainu; Ryukyuan languages; Korean (Zainichi sources). **Concept in one line:** seeing in others what one disowns in oneself, and how a culture places its unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other": scapegoating, enemy images, misfortune blamed on others or on spirits, and the opposite practices of owning one's faults.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Zainichi Koreans and Chinese; Burakumin; Ainu; Okinawans; foreign workers and technical interns; evacuees from Fukushima; people accused of illness during pandemics; families named in folk-possession traditions of Shikoku and Chugoku (to be approached through local historians); Tokyo and Osaka residents and their mutual images; prefecture-by-prefecture self- and other-images; religious minorities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on propaganda, rumor-harm and hate-speech surveys; thin on private projection, which is nearly unmeasurable; thin on Ainu and Ryukyuan accounts outside community and outsider-critique sources. "Japan sees itself as unique" and "blames the outside" are claims to test only.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for tales and temple records; Ainu, Uchinaaguchi and Korean for community terms. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the core islands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Burakumin and Zainichi identity; named families in possession folklore; massacre history and denial debates; hate-group material (cite, do not amplify); Ainu ritual detail.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
