# Japan: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Hereditary-politics districts against open seats; prefectures with past hiring or bid-rigging scandals; Okinawa (base-related funds and island politics); Hokkaido and Ainu communities; Kansai against Kanto politics; small towns against large cities; the public service against private firms; the foreign residents excluded or admitted to public posts (a question of nationality clauses); communities affected by hiring bias against outcaste lineages (see flags).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on audits, disclosures and polls; mixed on lived favoritism, which people rarely describe on record; thin for Indigenous leadership beyond community sources. Terms like *sontaku* and *jōkyū kokumin* are net and press words; track date and meaning.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for medieval and Edo texts. Ainu and Ryukyuan for Indigenous terms. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the core islands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Burakumin lineage and the family register (past misuse in hiring checks): use community and rights-organization sources only; names of officials in open scandal cases.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Law here takes the letter only (ethics acts, recusal rules); the spirit is Capricorn's.
