# Japan: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: couples, co-parents, business and work partners, ritual kin, and alliances between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Tohoku and other rural *ie* households against metropolitan couples; Kansai commerce against Kanto; Okinawa and the Sakishima islands; Ainu communities in Hokkaido; international couples and migrant spouses; Nikkei returnees; same-sex couples in municipalities with certificates; farming and fishing families against company-town families; one-person households and the never-married.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on couples, firms and cooperatives; thin on Indigenous partnership outside outsider records. Idioms such as *teishu kanpaku* and *kakaa denka* must be tested against data, not used as findings.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese, classical for Heian and Edo; Ainu and Ryukyuan for Indigenous terms; English for treaty and business literature. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the core islands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu and Ryukyuan ritual knowledge; domestic-violence cases; organized-crime fictive-kin sources need careful handling.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
