# Japan: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Japanese | 割り勘 (*warikan*) | splitting a bill evenly | everyday sharing |

| Japanese | 取り分, 分け前 (*toribun, wakemae*) | one's share | dividing food, catch, estates |
| Japanese | 年功序列, 成果主義 (*nenkō joretsu, seika shugi*) | seniority order; pay by result | fairness by time against by merit |
| Japanese | 格差, 勝ち組・負け組 (*kakusa, kachigumi makegumi*) | disparity; winners and losers | the 2000s inequality debate |
| Japanese | 八百長 (*yaochō*) | a fixed match | rigging in sport |
| Japanese | じゃんけん, あみだくじ (*janken, amidakuji*) | rock-paper-scissors; ladder lottery | choosing without favor |

English keywords: fairness, equal pay, seniority wage, cutting in line, commons, match-fixing, welfare of non-regular workers, school duty rotation, inheritance division.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Regular against non-regular workers and women against men at work; Kansai merchants against Kanto; Hokkaido settler regions; Okinawa and the Sakishima islands; Ainu communities; farming and fishing villages with commons; younger against older cohorts ("kakusa" generation); foreign residents and foreign technical interns; Tohoku after 2011 (aid shares).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on pay, inequality and opinion; mixed on everyday practice; thin on Indigenous sharing outside community sources.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for medieval and Edo texts; Ainu and Ryukyuan for Indigenous terms. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the core islands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu and Ryukyuan community knowledge; discrimination complaints that name individuals.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Equal-pay and admissions law is the letter only (Libra); its spirit goes to Capricorn.
