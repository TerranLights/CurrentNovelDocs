# Japan: The domain, partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia (the main islands, Hokkaido, the Ryukyu chain, the Ogasawara islands). **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu (*Ainu itak*); Ryukyuan languages (Uchinaaguchi and others). **Concept in one line:** what is a fair bargain and a good partnership: marriage and gender ways, bonds between equals, promises and contracts, open enemies, the settling of disputes, civility, and (Libra only) the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Hokkaido (Ainu Mosir, settler regions); Tohoku, Kanto, Chubu, Kansai, Chugoku-Shikoku, Kyushu (regional images such as "Kyushu men" and Gunma's *kakaa denka* are claims to test); the Ryukyu chain, Okinawa and the Amami and Sakishima islands; the Ogasawara islands; Ainu; Ryukyuan peoples; Zainichi Korean and Chinese communities; Brazilian and Peruvian Nikkei and other migrant-spouse communities; Burakumin (see flags); urban against rural; fishing and farming villages against company towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, law and the media; mixed for Ainu and Ryukyuan custom (much is written by outsiders); thin for private bargains. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core islands.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical Japanese for Edo and earlier sources); Ainu and Ryukyuan for Indigenous terms; English for the legal summaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Burakumin identity and family-register records have a history of misuse, so approach through community-published work; some Ainu and Ryukyuan ritual knowledge may be restricted; names of private persons in divorce and court records.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
