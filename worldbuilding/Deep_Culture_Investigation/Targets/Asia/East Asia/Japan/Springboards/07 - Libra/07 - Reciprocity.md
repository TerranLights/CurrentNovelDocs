# Japan: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan languages; Korean (for Zainichi community terms). **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them (revenge itself is Scorpio's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Rural villages with *chōnaikai* and farm exchange against city apartments; Okinawa and the Ryukyu chain (*moai*); Ainu communities in Hokkaido; Tohoku disaster-affected towns; Kansai and Kanto gift-return customs (compare through etiquette sources); Zainichi Korean, Chinese and Brazilian Nikkei communities; religious communities (shrine and temple supporters' groups); older against younger gift-givers; companies with seasonal gift lists.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on gift sales, postal and donation figures; mixed on household-level accounts; thin on Indigenous reciprocity outside community sources. The Benedict-era view of "on" is a famous outsider framework to read critically, not a finding.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for tales and medieval texts; Ainu, Uchinaaguchi and Korean for community terms. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the core islands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu ritual; family funeral records; religious-group donation practices.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Gift-tax and Furusato Nōzei rules are letter-of-law questions for the Libra domain sheet, not here.
