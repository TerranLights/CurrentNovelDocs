# Japan: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost (physical, moral, social, endurance, admitting fault, leaving).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former warrior-class descendants** against **commoner** traditions (firefighters, divers, farmers, merchants).
- **Okinawa** (war memory, karate) against **mainland** (*tokkō* memory); **Hokkaido** workers and settlers.
- **Ainu hunters** and **Ryukyuan** martial and sailing traditions.
- **Women and men**; **older cohorts** shaped by war and **younger cohorts**.
- **Zainichi and foreign residents** who acted in public crises.
- **Public servants** (fire, coast guard, Self-Defense Forces) against **volunteers**; **company employees** who spoke out.
- **Sports clubs** with *konjō* training against clubs that reject it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Japanese for war memory, sports and disaster work; mixed for minorities; "bushidō" is a heavily constructed topic, so separate period texts from later uses.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for war tales and treatises; Ainu and Okinawan for primary material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war memory disputes (Yasukuni, Okinawan mass deaths); school corporal-punishment cases involving minors; suicide in sources; Ainu sacred hunting knowledge.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the sheet is written for the core lands.
