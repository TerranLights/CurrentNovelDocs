# Japan: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages; Korean (Zainichi); Portuguese (Nikkei returnees). **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu** (Hokkaido and urban Honshu), **Ryukyuans** (Okinawa Island, Amami, Miyako, Yaeyama, Yonaguni): own languages and labels.
- **Zainichi Koreans and Chinese** (separate Mindan-, Chongryon-linked and unaffiliated people; newcomers against old residents).
- **Nikkei returnees** (Brazil, Peru) and recent immigrants (Vietnam, Philippines, Indonesia).
- **Mixed-parentage people (*hāfu*)**, who differ by parent and by region.
- **Hokkaido settler descendants**, **Tōhoku**, **Kansai**, **Kyushu**, **Shikoku**: hometown and dialect identities.
- **Burakumin descendants** (see flags), **hereditary house families** (temples, shrines, performance houses), **farm families**, **salaried workers**.
- **Generational cohorts** and **religious bodies** (Shinto parishes, Buddhist sects, new religions, Christians, Muslims).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Japanese for the majority; mixed for Ainu and Ryukyuans; the state has long been cautious about asking ethnicity, so use surveys and minority-run sources.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; Ainu, Okinawan and Korean for primary sources; classical Japanese for Edo texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** burakumin identity and registry exposure (study it only as the people and their groups present it); Ainu remains and sacred knowledge; claims to Ainu or Ryukyuan identity are contested in public.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the sheet is written for the core lands.
