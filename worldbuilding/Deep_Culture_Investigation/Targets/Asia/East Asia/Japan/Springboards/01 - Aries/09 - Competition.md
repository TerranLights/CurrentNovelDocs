# Japan: Competition (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** striving against others for a prize, a rank or a result (sport, school, work, status, rivalry between places, fair play, winning and losing).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elite and non-elite schools; Tokyo and regional students;** private-school and public-school families; first-time **rōnin** and repeat exam-takers.
- **Kansai and Kantō** (team and city rivalries), **Hokkaido, Tōhoku, Kyushu, Shikoku:** regional clubs and local derbies.
- **Okinawa** with its own contest forms; **Ainu** and other minority traditions.
- **Large firms, small firms and the self-employed;** **sales** and **research** staff.
- **Women and men** in admissions, sports and work.
- **Zainichi** and **foreign-born** athletes and students.
- **Fans** (baseball, soccer, sumo, idols) against **players**; **village festival teams.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Japanese on school, sport and work; mixed for informal envy and for minority contests; the "group harmony" claim sits against heavy ranking, so gather evidence on both.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for old contests; Okinawan and Ainu for primary material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** admissions scandals; school sports abuse by coaches (minors); cheating and match-fixing allegations; suicide linked to exam stress; discrimination in admissions.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the sheet is written for the core lands.
