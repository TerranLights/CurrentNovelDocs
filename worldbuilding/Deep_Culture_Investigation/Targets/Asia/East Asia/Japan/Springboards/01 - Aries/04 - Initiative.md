# Japan: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked (at work, in business, civic life, politics and the home).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hokkaido** (pioneer story) against **Tōhoku**, **Kansai** (merchant tradition) and **Okinawa** (*moai*; high business-start rates and low incomes are claims to check).
- **Ainu** language-revival and cultural projects; **Ryukyuan** community self-help.
- **Large firms, small firms, family businesses, public service.**
- **Women and men; young and old;** the *shūkatsu* class against irregular workers.
- **Zainichi and burakumin** self-employment histories, **new immigrants** starting shops.
- **Volunteers and nonprofits** in disaster areas against ordinary neighborhoods.
- **Rural villages with their own work-sharing customs.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Japanese on firms, schools, volunteers and business statistics; thin on informal acts; popular claims that Japanese workers wait to be told conflict with the quality-circle record, so collect both.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for Edo texts; Okinawan and Ainu for minority material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** burakumin occupations and registry questions; politically charged histories of Hokkaido colonization; disaster-victim privacy.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the sheet is written for the core lands.
