# Japan: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed (righteous anger, temper, rage, grudges, collective anger, anger management).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Okinawa** (protest history; the Koza Riot) against **mainland** protest traditions.
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuan** accounts of offended spirits and of grievance against the state.
- **Zainichi Koreans** and other minorities as targets of displaced anger, and as speakers.
- **Women and men**, and **older and younger cohorts** (the "snapping" panic about youth, and later about elders).
- **Workers** in firms with harassment problems, **customers** and **service staff**.
- **School sports clubs** and **delinquent youth groups**.
- **Urban commuters** against **rural drivers**; **Kansai** and **Kantō** claims of different temper (check, do not assume).
- **Shrines and festivals** that appease angry spirits, by region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Japanese on idioms, folklore, stage works and workplace harassment; mixed for emotion surveys (self-report limits); thin for private anger in families beyond official surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for goryō and Noh texts; Okinawan and Ainu for primary material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and child abuse data; violence against minorities in 1923; suicide and bullying contexts; spirit-appeasement rites held by shrine and temple communities.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the sheet is written for the core lands.
