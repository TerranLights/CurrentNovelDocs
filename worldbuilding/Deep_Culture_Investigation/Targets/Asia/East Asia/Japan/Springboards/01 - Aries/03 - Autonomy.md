# Japan: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled (personal, bodily, moral, at work, in youth, and the collective autonomy of towns and peoples).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuans** on collective self-determination, with different histories.
- **Okinawa Island** against the **outer Ryukyu islands**, and Okinawans in Osaka against those at home.
- **Women and men; first and second sons** (the household-heir line); **unmarried adults** against married ones.
- **Rural villages against big cities**; **large firms, small firms, public service, the self-employed**.
- **Disabled people and elders in care**; **young people in school or outside it**.
- **Zainichi Koreans** and **foreign residents** on who decides their status and names.
- **Religious communities** with their own authority (temple families, new religions).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Japanese on family, school and marriage; mixed for collective autonomy of minorities; thin in English for local associations.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for medieval and Edo items; Ainu and Okinawan for primary material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse and coercion cases within families; medical cases; Ainu sacred knowledge; politically charged base and independence debates in Okinawa; no law research here (point to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the sheet is written for the core lands.
