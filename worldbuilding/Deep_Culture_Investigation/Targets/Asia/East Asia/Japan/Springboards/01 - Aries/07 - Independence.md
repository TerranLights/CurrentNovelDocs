# Japan: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others: self-reliance and self-sufficiency (economic, practical, emotional, from government, in the household, regional and Indigenous food sovereignty).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders living alone, with children, or in care**, in cities and in depopulating villages.
- **Young adults at home** against those who left; **women and men**; **irregular workers** against salaried staff.
- **Farm households** against **urban households**; **Kansai, Kantō, Tōhoku, Kyushu, Shikoku** (check regional differences in household form).
- **Ainu** (salmon and forest rights) and **Ryukyuans** (diaspora remittances, base economy, independence groups).
- **Hokkaido settler** and **island** communities; **fishing and mountain** villages.
- **Zainichi and foreign-born residents** who lack some public support.
- **Welfare recipients** and **those who refuse welfare**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Japanese for households, aging, food and disasters; mixed for minorities and informal self-provisioning; politically loaded for welfare and independence debates.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for Buddhist and Edo texts; Ainu and Okinawan for primary material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** welfare and lonely-death cases; suicide in sources; independence debates in Okinawa and Ainu fishing rights (litigation: do not research the law here, point to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the sheet is written for the core lands.
