# Japan: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages; Korean (Zainichi sources). **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Japanese | 自己主張 (jiko shuchō), 自己表現 (jiko hyōgen) | self-assertion; self-expression | the plain research terms; also 意見を言う (iken o iu), "to state an opinion" |
| Japanese | 断る (kotowaru), 言い返す (iikaesu), 反論 (hanron) | to refuse; to talk back; to rebut | where, to whom and how refusal is done |
| Japanese | 遠慮 (enryo), 控えめ (hikaeme), 謙遜 (kenson) | restraint; reserve; humility | the poles against which assertion is measured |
| Japanese | 空気を読む (kūki o yomu), KY, 忖度 (sontaku) | to read the air; the abbreviation for one who cannot; anticipating a superior's wish | the social pressure around speaking up |
| Japanese | 出る杭は打たれる (deru kui wa utareru) | proverb on the stake that sticks up | a proverb to date and trace, and to set against counter-proverbs |
| Japanese | わがまま (wagamama), 図々しい (zūzūshii), 自己中 (jikochū) | willful; pushy; self-centered | words of censure for assertion |
| Japanese | アサーション (asāshon), クレーム (kurēmu), カスハラ (kasuhara) | assertiveness training; complaint; customer harassment | modern loanwords and abbreviations |
| Japanese | 諫言 (kangen), 直訴 (jikiso), 目安箱 (meyasubako) | remonstrance to a superior; direct appeal; suggestion box | pre-modern forms of speaking up |
| Ainu | チャランケ (charanke) | formal debate or negotiation to settle a dispute (*check*) | confirm the form and meaning with Ainu sources |
| Okinawan | ウチナーグチ (Uchinaaguchi) proverbs, ゆいまーる (yuimaaru) | local language and proverbs; mutual help | *check* for proverbs on speaking up or keeping quiet |

English keywords: Japanese assertiveness, nail that sticks out, enryo, reading the air, direct appeal, complaint culture, customer harassment, protest, speaking up.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kansai against Kantō and Tōhoku:** popular claims about talkativeness and bargaining; test them with data, not repeat them.
- **Okinawa:** a long history of protest politics and its own proverbs.
- **Ainu:** formal debate (*charanke*) and a modern political movement.
- **Zainichi Koreans:** the campaigns against fingerprinting and for equal treatment; speaking up as a minority.
- **Women and men; age cohorts:** the *ūman ribu* generation, the bubble generation, and youth now.
- **Workers in different settings:** big firms, small firms, public-sector workers, service staff dealing with customers.
- **Rural villages against cities:** meeting customs differ; compare village councils with city assemblies.
- **Burakumin movement:** a long organized campaign (see flags).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Japanese for schools, labor and consumer complaints; mixed for minorities; the "Japanese are indirect" claim is an old stereotype, so collect variation by setting and speaker.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for pre-modern items); Ainu and Okinawan for minority material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bullying cases involving minors; harassment cases; burakumin organizing; no law research here (point to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet covers the core lands.
