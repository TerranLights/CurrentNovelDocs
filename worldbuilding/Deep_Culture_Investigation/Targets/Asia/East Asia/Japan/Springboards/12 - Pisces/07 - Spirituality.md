# Japan: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Okinawan; Ainu; Korean for Zainichi sources; Portuguese for Nikkei Brazilian communities. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shrine-parish rural communities against urban non-parishioners:** inherited ties against chosen visits.
- **Okinawa and the Ryukyus:** women-led household and village prayer with its own deities.
- **Ainu of Hokkaido:** ceremonies of kamuy, now in revival.
- **Hidden-religion regions:** Nagasaki and Amakusa Christians; Kagoshima and Hitoyoshi *kakure nenbutsu*.
- **Mountain-ascetic and pilgrimage districts:** Dewa Sanzan, Kumano, Shikoku.
- **New-religion members against temple-affiliated families:** modern movement against inherited parish.
- **Young adults:** goshuin and power-spot visitors against older shrine-goers.
- **Zainichi Korean, Nikkei Brazilian, Muslim and other immigrant communities:** the sacred brought in.
- **Tōhoku after 2011:** disaster-time practice and rinshō shūkyōshi.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, official affiliation counts, and temple and shrine research; mixed for what people mean when they say "spiritual"; thin for Ryukyuan and Ainu details published by the communities themselves.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for Kojiki, Hōnen); Okinawan and Ainu for minority sources; Portuguese and Korean for diaspora groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu and Ryukyuan ceremonies and sacred sites have closed parts; new-religion controversies (Aum, Unification Church) need care; hidden-Christian descendants' privacy.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
