# Japan: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Okinawan and other Ryukyuan; Ainu; Hachijō (*check*). **Concept in one line:** the coming apart of a fixed form: the loss of the self's edges in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural depopulated areas against large cities:** the dissolution of villages and parishes against crowded anonymity.
- **Okinawa, Amami, Miyako, Yaeyama and Hachijō:** island languages and cultures in revival or decline.
- **Ainu in Hokkaido and elsewhere:** assimilation history and today's language work.
- **Festival towns** (Tokushima, Aomori, Kishiwada, Kyoto): different forms of group ecstasy.
- **Mountain-ascetic and Zen communities:** doctrinal training in the loss of self.
- **New-religion and fandom communities:** devotion that blurs the individual.
- **Elderly in care and their families:** the self coming apart as lived by households.
- **Zainichi Koreans and Nikkei:** assimilation and naturalization pressures.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for divorce, depopulation and dementia statistics, festival ethnography and Zen philosophy; mixed for ecstatic and trance experience as people describe it; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan language shift outside specialist work.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for Dōgen and Noh); Okinawan, Ainu and Hachijō for minority material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aum Shinrikyō victims and survivors; Unification Church and other cult-recovery accounts; dementia care families; Ainu and Ryukyuan restricted ritual knowledge.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
