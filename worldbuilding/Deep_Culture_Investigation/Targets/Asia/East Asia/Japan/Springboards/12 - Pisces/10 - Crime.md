# Japan: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Okinawan; Ainu; Korean for Zainichi sources. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. (Law as written is researched under Libra and Capricorn; this sheet takes wrongdoing and the wrongdoer.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tokyo, Osaka and Fukuoka:** big-city policing and distinct yakuza groups (Kantō, Kansai, Kyushu).
- **Okinawa:** the base-related offense category and its own history of local crime groups.
- **Rural districts with *chūzaisho* police and watch groups:** low-anonymity enforcement against city anonymity.
- **Burakumin, Zainichi Koreans and foreign residents:** groups against which crime beliefs have been aimed and who have answered them.
- **Elderly victims of special fraud against young recruits to "dark" jobs:** victims and perpetrators of a new form.
- **Ainu communities:** their own accounts of wrong and repair (query first).
- **Families of offenders and of victims:** two kinds of shaming.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official statistics, victim surveys and crime fiction and film; mixed for public attitudes about wrongdoing, which sit inside general polls; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan community accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for the Engishiki and Edo plays); Okinawan and Ainu; Korean for Zainichi sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** victims and offenders' families; burakumin and Zainichi links to organized crime are politically sensitive and often stereotyped; juvenile offender anonymity; base-related incidents involve survivors; ethnic crime beliefs are to be recorded as beliefs and tested.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
