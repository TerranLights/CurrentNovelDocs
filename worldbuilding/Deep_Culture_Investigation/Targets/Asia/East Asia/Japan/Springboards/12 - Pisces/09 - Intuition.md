# Japan: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Okinawan; Ainu. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tōhoku and mountain communities:** *matagi* hunters and sky-reading farmers.
- **Okinawa, Amami and the island chain:** *yuta* and *sādaka* people whose intuition is a calling.
- **Ainu of Hokkaido:** spiritual specialists and their own omen words.
- **Kyoto, Nara and old court towns:** the heritage of Onmyōdō and shrine divination.
- **Urban office workers against craftspeople:** *kan* as trade skill against *kūki o yomu* as social skill.
- **Women, men and teenagers:** different habits with fortune-telling, blood types and *kokkuri-san*.
- **Nurses, firefighters and shōgi professionals:** expert intuition by profession.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for trade, craft and business knowledge, fortune-telling markets and popular blood-type lore; mixed for everyday trust in hunches, rarely asked about directly; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan material outside specialist books.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for court diaries and Onmyōdō sources); Okinawan and Ainu terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *yuta* and Ainu spiritual roles include restricted knowledge; blood-type personality talk can shade into prejudice and has been challenged by researchers; spiritual-sales scams involve victims.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
