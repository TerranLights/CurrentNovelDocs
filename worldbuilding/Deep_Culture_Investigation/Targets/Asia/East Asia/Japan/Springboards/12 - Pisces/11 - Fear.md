# Japan: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Okinawan; Ainu; Korean for Zainichi sources. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety are its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sanriku and Pacific coast against inland areas:** tsunami memory and drills against low risk.
- **Tōkai, Kantō and Shikoku Nankai coasts:** the megaquake forecast zones.
- **Fukushima and its evacuees:** nuclear fear, rumor harm and return.
- **Hiroshima and Nagasaki:** radiation fear carried through survivors and their children.
- **Akita (Namahage) and other regions with child-fright figures:** local rites of frightening.
- **Okinawa and Amami:** typhoons, base accidents and island-specific dangers.
- **Hokkaido and Tōhoku bear country, with Ainu communities:** fear of and relation to the bear (heard in Ainu voices first).
- **Age groups:** elders' old-age and loneliness worries against youth's social fear.
- **Korean and other minority residents:** the object of rumor-driven fear, and its memory.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disaster-preparedness, anxiety statistics, horror film and folklore; mixed for everyday talk of fear, since polls ask about "worry" more than "fear"; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan voices in print.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for the Edo plays and picture scrolls); Okinawan and Ainu; Korean for Zainichi memory of 1923.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Fukushima evacuees and hibakusha; the 1923 massacre of Koreans is a painful, contested memory; patients' privacy in clinical studies; Ainu bear-rite knowledge.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
