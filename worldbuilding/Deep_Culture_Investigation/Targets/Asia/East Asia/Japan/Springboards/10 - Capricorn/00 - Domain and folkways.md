# Japan: The domain, authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia (the archipelago: Honshu, Shikoku, Kyushu, Hokkaido, the Ryukyu chain, the Ogasawara islands). **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; the Ryukyuan languages (Uchinaaguchi and its sister languages). **Concept in one line:** who commands, who obeys, and what is owed (authority, rank, age, time, reputation, career, status, power).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu** of Hokkaido: own elder roles and a distinct pre-colonial order.
- **Ryukyuan peoples** of Okinawa, Amami, Miyako and Yaeyama: a former kingdom with its own rank, kin groups and priestess offices.
- **Regional cultures:** Kansai merchant towns against Kanto samurai Edo; Tohoku; Kyushu (Satsuma's youth age-grade schooling); the Aizu and Chōshū domains.
- **Burakumin** and other descendants of Edo outcaste groups, **Zainichi Koreans** and Chinese, **Nikkei returnees**, the **Ogasawara** settlers' descendants: who sits outside the ladders.
- **Generations and sexes:** shūkatsu-era graduates, women in "general" and "career" tracks, freelancers and non-regular workers against core employees.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the mainland (surveys, archives, scholarship in two languages); thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan voices outside published works.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese, with classical Japanese and kambun for pre-modern texts; Ainu and Uchinaaguchi for primary work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** burakumin lineage is a privacy issue (never trace individuals); Ainu and Ryukyuan ritual knowledge belongs to its holders; imperial-institution material is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
