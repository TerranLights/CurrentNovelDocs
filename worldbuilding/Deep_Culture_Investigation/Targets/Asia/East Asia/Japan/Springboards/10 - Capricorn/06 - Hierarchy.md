# Japan: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Uchinaaguchi and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuan/Okinawan** peoples: ranked by the center, and with internal ranks of their own (the Ryukyu gentry).
- **Burakumin** and descendants of other outcaste groups: lowest-ranked in the old order; contested "today".
- **Zainichi Koreans** and Chinese, **Nikkeijin**, and **technical interns:** citizens against non-citizens.
- **Old families and the former peerage** against **new money:** Tokyo, Kansai, the regions.
- **Regular against non-regular workers;** men against women in corporate tracks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for class survey data, language and corporate rank; mixed on minority rank because of privacy; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan own-terms.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for Edo and earlier); Ainu and Uchinaaguchi for minority work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** burakumin lineage and place information is a protected privacy matter (never map hamlets or trace families); colorism and ethnic slurs are to be described, never used; imperial matters are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
