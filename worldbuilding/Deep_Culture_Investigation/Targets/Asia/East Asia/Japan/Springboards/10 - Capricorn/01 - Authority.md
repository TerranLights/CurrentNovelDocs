# Japan: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Uchinaaguchi and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu** communities (Hokkaido, with Kanto-area Ainu): own elders and leadership forms.
- **Ryukyuan** peoples (Okinawa, Miyako, Yaeyama, Amami): a separate kingdom and priestess order.
- **Domains of old** (Satsuma, Chōshū, Tosa, Aizu, Mito): different local authority traditions and rebellions.
- **Generations:** Showa-born managers, the "lost generation" and the youngest workers.
- **Institutions:** police, the Self-Defense Forces, schools, temples and shrines, new religions, yakuza-linked groups.
- **Outsiders to the ladder:** burakumin descendants, Zainichi Koreans and Chinese, Nikkei returnees.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the mainland, with heavy survey and historical coverage; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan voices outside published works.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical and kambun for Edo and earlier); Ainu and Uchinaaguchi for primary work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the emperor system, wartime responsibility, and abuse by people in authority; burakumin lineage; Ainu and Ryukyuan ritual knowledge held by its keepers.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
