# Japan: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Uchinaaguchi and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuan/Okinawan** populations: different ladders, discrimination and overseas routes.
- **Regions:** Kansai merchants, Tokyo salaried elite, Aichi industry, Hokkaido pioneers, Tohoku leavers.
- **Men and women:** career tracks and the 2018 admissions case.
- **Cohorts:** bubble-era, "lost generation," *satori* and *yutori* generations.
- **Zainichi Koreans** and other minorities in business and entertainment (the "ethnic entrepreneur" question).
- **Elite universities** against **local colleges;** **core** against **non-regular** workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on schooling, hiring and mobility; mixed on how ambition is felt and spoken of; thin on Ainu and Ryukyuan own-word concepts.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for Edo-era prose.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** discrimination in hiring and school admissions; suicide and overwork; minority ancestry (never trace individuals).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
