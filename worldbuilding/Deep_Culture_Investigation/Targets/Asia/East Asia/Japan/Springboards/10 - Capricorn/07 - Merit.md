# Japan: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Uchinaaguchi and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuan/Okinawan** residents: different routes to schooling and posts, and the effects of discrimination.
- **Elite university graduates** (Tokyo, Kyoto) against **other graduates:** the *gakubatsu* effect.
- **Core workers, non-regular workers and freelancers:** who is judged on what.
- **Hereditary political families** against first-generation candidates.
- **Women and men** in the "career" track.
- **Regional cultures:** Kansai's merchant outlook against the Kanto bureaucracy; local patronage in rural prefectures.
- **Zainichi and foreign-resident workers:** credential recognition.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on exams, hiring and pay systems; mixed on beliefs about merit; thin on Ainu and Ryukyuan own-words.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for Edo documents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** discrimination in admissions and hiring; scandals involving named individuals; student suicide linked to exams.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
