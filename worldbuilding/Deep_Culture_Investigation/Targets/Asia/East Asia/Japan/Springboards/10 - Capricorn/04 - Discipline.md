# Japan: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Uchinaaguchi and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others or by oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuan** peoples: own raising and training traditions.
- **Craft, temple and arts lineages** against **corporate and school settings:** a master's discipline against institutional discipline.
- **Sports clubs:** baseball (Koshien), judo, kendo, brass bands; each with distinct discipline.
- **Public against private** schools; **elite academies** and **ordinary** schools.
- **Generations:** postwar parents against the cohort raised under the post-2000 bans.
- **Women and men,** and **regional cultures** (Kyushu "strict" image to test).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on school, sport and work practice and on classical craft texts; mixed on private home practice; thin on Ainu and Ryukyuan own-word discipline.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for Dōgen, Zeami and Musashi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse and deaths in school sport and clubs; discipline of minors; religious training secrets of lineages.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
