# Japan: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Okinawan and Ainu terms *check*. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden (privacy is one facet).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ryukyuan communities (Okinawa, Yaeyama, Miyako):** utaki and festivals closed to outsiders.
- **Ainu communities:** protocols for what may be recorded and shared.
- **Hidden Christian and hidden-nenbutsu descendants** (Nagasaki, Gotō, Amakusa, Satsuma, Hitoyoshi).
- **Art-school lineages (iemoto) and craft families:** transmitted secrets.
- **Villagers and city dwellers:** how much neighbors are expected to know.
- **Sexual minorities,** by city and generation.
- **Company workers, bureaucrats and journalists:** information held and released.
- **Zainichi Koreans:** names, family history and what is kept from outsiders or children.

## 4. Flags
