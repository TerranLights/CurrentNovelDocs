# Japan: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Okinawan and Ainu terms *check*. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how the culture judges who holds it, how it is held and how it is used (legitimate authority sits with Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Okinawa and Hokkaido (Ainu):** peripheries in a long contest with the center.
- **Kansai (Osaka, Kyoto), Tokyo and Kyushu:** different local political machines and business families.
- **Dynastic political families against newcomers and reformist parties.**
- **Bureaucrats, party members and business federation (Keidanren) leaders.**
- **Unions and resident movements** against corporate and state bodies.
- **Women and men:** power in households, workplaces and politics.
- **Zainichi Koreans, Burakumin and other minorities:** voice and exclusion.
- **Rural and urban districts,** where seat apportionment and local bosses differ.
