# Japan: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu and Ryukyuan communities:** schools and officials purged languages and customs.
- **Zainichi Koreans:** scapegoated after 1923; later discrimination.
- **Buraku communities:** historically placed on the "unclean" side of a purity line; handle with care.
- **Hansen's disease survivors and Minamata patients:** bodies and places treated as polluted.
- **Shinto, Buddhist, Shugendō and new-religion practitioners:** each has its own purging rites.
- **Urban salaried workers against villagers and fishing communities:** scandal and apology versus ostracism.
- **Generations:** those raised on danshari and sauna culture against those raised on old year-end rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pollution histories, Shinto rites, scandal coverage and decluttering; mixed for ostracism and bullying in practice; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan accounts in English. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** modern Japanese; classical Japanese for norito and Meiji edicts; Korean for Zainichi accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Buraku lists and identification; Hansen's disease and Minamata families; the 1923 massacre (disputed in politics); eating disorders. Eugenics statutes and legal expulsion belong with Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
