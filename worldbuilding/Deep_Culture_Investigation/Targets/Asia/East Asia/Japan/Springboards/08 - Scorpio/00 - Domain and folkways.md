# Japan: Death and transformation, the domain and its folkways (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia (post-war nation; the tracker records no change to its pre-war lands, so the sheet is written for the core islands and the Ryukyu chain, and the exact lands are still to be settled). **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (standard, plus Okinawan/Ryukyuan and Ainu terms, marked *check*); Korean for the Zainichi community. **Concept in one line:** how the culture faces death, power and what is hidden: death ways, sex ways, shared resources, inheritance and secrets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu (Hokkaido, and communities in Tokyo and the Tohoku):** their own words for the soul and the other world.
- **Ryukyuan peoples (Okinawa, Amami, Miyako, Yaeyama):** separate tombs, bone-washing, women's ritual roles.
- **Zainichi Koreans and other Korean-heritage communities:** their own rites, grave and ancestral practice.
- **Hidden Christians of Nagasaki, Gotō and Amakusa:** how the dead are prayed for.
- **Tohoku, Kansai, Kyushu and Hokkaido settlers:** regional funeral, inheritance and sex customs.
- **Urban against rural:** Tokyo and Osaka against depopulating villages (grave closure, solitary death).
- **Nikkei returnees, Chinese and other migrants** (check), and **Buraku communities**, historically tied to handling death and defilement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for funerals, ancestors, vital statistics and sex surveys; mixed for inheritance practice and money in marriage; thin for secret societies and for how secrecy works in practice. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** modern Japanese; classical Japanese and kanbun for pre-modern texts; Ainu and Ryukyuan *check*; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Buraku lists and histories (privacy and discrimination); Ainu and Ryukyuan restricted ritual knowledge; hibakusha testimony; bereaved families and suicide. Inheritance law and legal secrecy rules go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
