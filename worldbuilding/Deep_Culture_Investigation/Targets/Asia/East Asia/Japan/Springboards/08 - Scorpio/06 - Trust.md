# Japan: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Okinawan, Ainu and Korean terms *check*. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down (generalized against particular trust; trust in institutions and in deals; loyalty, and repair after a breach).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Prefectures and rural against urban** (social-capital scores differ across prefectures).
- **Okinawa (moai), Kansai merchant cities (Osaka, Ōmi), Tokyo:** different commercial and neighbor ties.
- **Ainu and Ryukyuan communities:** trust in the state and in outsiders.
- **Zainichi Koreans:** kye, credit unions, in-group reliance and distrust.
- **Older and younger generations:** scam targets against online natives.
- **Fukushima evacuees and Tohoku fishing and farming communities:** trust in agencies and in the market.
- **Company employees against self-employed and farm households:** where one's circle ends.
