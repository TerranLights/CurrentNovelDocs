## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu and Ryukyuan communities:** control of land, language and livelihood by the state.
- **Company workers, shift workers and freelancers:** workplace monitoring and overwork.
- **Wives and husbands, daughters-in-law and mothers-in-law:** household authority, by generation.
- **Schoolchildren and teachers:** rules, clubs, exams.
- **Rural villagers and urban apartment residents:** neighborhood association pressure.
- **Disaster regions** (Tohoku coast, Kobe) against inland towns: flood and quake control.
- **Followers of new religions or high-control groups** against ordinary parish members.
- **Zainichi Koreans** and other minorities: pressure to conform or hide.

## 4. Flags
