## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sanriku and Fukushima coast, Kobe, Kumamoto, the Noto peninsula:** different disasters and different recoveries.
- **Okinawa and Yaeyama:** typhoons, the 1771 tsunami and war memory.
- **Hokkaido and Ainu communities:** remoteness, blackout and the cold.
- **Cities against depopulating villages:** who can evacuate and who is left.
- **Older people, children, disabled people and foreign residents:** unequal exposure.
- **Contract and migrant workers (Nikkei Brazilians) against permanent employees.**
- **Hibakusha and families of the war dead.**
- **Generations:** those who lived through the bubble crash against those who grew up after it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, with huge archives and surveys; rich for economic crisis; mixed for family crises and mental-health breakdown; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan voices. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** modern Japanese; classical Japanese for chronicles; Okinawan and Ainu *check*; easy Japanese and Portuguese for migrant sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bereaved families; suicide; evacuees and children; Fukushima health claims. Emergency powers and liability rules are law (Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
