# Japan: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Ainu; Ryukyuan languages (Okinawan and others; *check*). **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gender:** men's friendships (workmates, drinking circles, club and hobby groups) against women's (mama-tomo, school friends, neighborhood circles); each as a question to test.
- **Life stages:** schoolchildren (classroom friend groups), students (circles and clubs), office workers (同期), parents (mama-tomo), retirees (hobby and neighborhood clubs), elders alone.
- **Places:** cities (Tokyo and Osaka) against farming and fishing villages; Okinawa's moai circles against mainland company-based ties; Hokkaido's settlers and Ainu neighbors.
- **Peoples:** Ainu, Ryukyuan peoples, Zainichi Koreans (friendship within and across community lines), Nikkei and migrant workers.
- **Alone by choice or by chance:** people who describe themselves as bocchi, hikikomori, and those in solitude programs.
- **Sworn and organized ties:** yakuza oath brotherhoods (gikyōdai, as reported), school alumni networks, bar regulars at neighborhood snack bars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys on support and contact, and for literature; mixed for men's and elders' friendships; thin for minority friendship customs.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for the earliest texts); Ainu; Ryukyuan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** minors and ijime; people living alone or isolated (privacy); organized-crime sworn-brotherhood material comes mainly from police and journalism, so read as outside accounts.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
