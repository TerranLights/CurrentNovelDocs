# Japan: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan languages (*check*); Korean for Zainichi groups. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen, as a unit; how a culture weighs the group against the person, draws "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Workplace types:** large-firm enterprise unions against small shops, public servants, farmers' co-ops, and freelancers or contract workers outside any group.
- **School and club cultures:** sports and club teams (bukatsu), cram schools, universities, against dropouts and non-attenders.
- **Regions:** Kansai merchant culture against Kantō and Tōhoku; Hokkaido, Okinawa and the Amami islands each with their own kin and age groups.
- **Peoples:** Ainu groups, Ryukyuan kin groups, buraku communities, Zainichi Koreans, newer migrants; each with its own boundary of "us".
- **Margin groups:** organized crime, bōsōzoku and gang crews, cult followers, online communities, supporters' groups.
- **Generations:** postwar workers, bubble-era entrants, the lost generation, young adults who join few groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, labor, school and corporate studies; mixed for gangs and cults (journalism over data); thin for minority groups in English.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ijime cases involving minors, ex-member testimony from cults, yakuza material (legal and safety issues), buraku identity records. Read "groupism" claims critically; the literature itself disputes them.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
