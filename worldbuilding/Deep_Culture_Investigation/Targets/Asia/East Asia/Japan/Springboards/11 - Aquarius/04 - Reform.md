# Japan: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Ryukyuan languages (*check*); Korean for Zainichi sources. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Reformers by place:** Tokyo and Osaka urban movements, pollution-hit areas (Minamata, Yokkaichi, Toyama), farming villages, depopulated prefectures.
- **Reformers by sector:** public servants, teachers, doctors, farmers' co-ops, women's and consumer groups, labor unions, students, religious groups.
- **Peoples as targets and authors:** Ainu, Ryukyuan peoples, buraku communities, Zainichi Koreans, migrant workers.
- **Generations:** postwar reformers, the 1960s generation, those after 2011, young online activists.
- **Resisters:** local opposition to reforms such as municipal mergers, new airports and dams, and supporters of the old ways.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for postwar movements and official programs; mixed for pre-modern reform (documents survive but scattered); thin for minority-led reform in English.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (including classical Japanese for Edo texts); Ainu; Ryukyuan; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** victims of pollution disease and their families (testimony needs care); ex-offenders and juvenile records; political violence of the 1960s-70s (Rengō Sekigun and others). The legal side of reform (statutes, litigation) is for Libra; what law is for is for Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. NDL Digital Collections: search 享保の改革, 寛政の改革 and 報徳仕法 to pull the primary-source collections.
2. Search the Minamata Disease Archives and 水俣学研究センター sites for their finding aids.
3. CiNii: search 市民運動 戦後 and 住民運動 公害 to get a core list of movement studies.
4. Search 大原社会問題研究所 and 憲政資料室 finding aids for 労働運動 and 自由民権運動 holdings.
5. Search Okinawa Prefectural Archives for 復帰運動 and 島ぐるみ闘争 to find documents and photographs.
