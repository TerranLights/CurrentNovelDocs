# Japan: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (including honorific speech); Ainu; Ryukyuan languages (*check*); Korean for Zainichi sources. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men:** by region, firm size and generation; full-time against non-regular work.
- **Regular and non-regular workers:** contract, dispatch and part-time workers against lifetime-employment staff.
- **Regions:** Tokyo and Osaka against depopulating prefectures; Okinawa against the mainland; Hokkaido's Ainu and settler lands.
- **Peoples:** Ainu, Ryukyuan peoples, buraku communities, Zainichi Koreans and Chinese, Nikkei workers, newer migrants.
- **Households:** single-parent families, elders living alone, families sending children to cram schools.
- **Generations:** the middle-class postwar cohort against the "lost generation" and the young.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on income, education and gender; mixed on minorities and non-citizen residents; contested over what the numbers mean, so the dispute itself is a source.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (honorific and older registers); Ainu; Ryukyuan; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** buraku area lists and family registers; discriminatory terms (record and cite as terms, never reuse); minors in poverty data. The legal side of equality (discrimination law, equal treatment as written) is for Libra; rank and seniority as an order is for Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
