# Japan: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Ainu; Ryukyuan languages (*check*). **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eras:** Edo scholar-hermits, Meiji idealists, Taishō and Shōwa utopians, postwar democrats, today's young.
- **Settings:** farming and village communes against city volunteers, corporate "mission" firms and the NPO sector.
- **Faiths:** Pure Land and Nichiren followers, Christian communities, Zen and monastic traditions, new religions.
- **Peoples:** Ainu, Ryukyuan peoples, Zainichi communities, each with their own ideals of right living.
- **Regions:** Okinawa's peace tradition; Hokkaido's settlers and communes; Kansai and Tōhoku.
- **Attitudes:** those who admire idealists against those who call them naive (ishiki takai kei).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and religion; good surveys for youth; thin for ordinary people's private ideals; communes are described by founders more than by others.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for pre-modern texts); Ainu; Ryukyuan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu sacred knowledge; cult histories (Aum and others) fall under group and extremes, not idealism proper; wartime idealism in service of the state (a contested and sensitive subject, handle with care).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
