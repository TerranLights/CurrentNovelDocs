# Japan: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Ainu; Ryukyuan languages (*check*); Korean for Zainichi sources. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature, and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Youth styles:** Harajuku and Shibuya fashion scenes, yanki and bōsōzoku, otaku and dōjin communities, visual-kei and indie music scenes.
- **Withdrawal:** school non-attenders, hikikomori, and those in free schools and support groups.
- **Religious dissent:** hidden Christians and hidden Pure Land groups (historic), new religions, Okinawan yuta, and non-religious believers.
- **Gender and sexuality:** Shinjuku Nichōme community, Takarazuka traditions, transgender and nonbinary people across cities and countryside.
- **Regions:** Tokyo and Osaka scenes against smaller towns; Okinawa's own rock and folk revival.
- **Peoples:** Ainu, Ryukyuan peoples, Zainichi Koreans, and others whose "difference" is assigned from outside.
- **Eras:** Edo kabukimono, Meiji dissenters, wartime non-converts, postwar avant-garde, 1990s onward.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for arts, subcultures and surveys; mixed for religious dissent (records from hostile authorities); thin for quiet or private nonconformity.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical and slang); Ainu; Ryukyuan; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** minors; hikikomori families; outing risk for sexual minorities; Okinawan yuta and Ainu ritual knowledge are restricted to practitioners; wartime political prisoners' records. The legal side (conscientious objection, public-order law) is for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
