# Japan: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (kanji and kana; classical Japanese for Heian and Edo texts); Ainu; Okinawan (*check*). **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it. Law on partner violence, custody and fishing rights is not scouted here (see Libra and Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Women, men and couples across age: youth dating against long marriages. Parents and children: urban middle-class parents against farm households. In-laws: rural eldest-son households against new nuclear households. Ainu communities (territory customs). Ryukyuans (Okinawa, Amami, Yaeyama): inheritance and the ancestral tablet. Hunting, fishing and farm communities with guarded grounds. Fans and idol followers. Office organizations and ministries. Zainichi Koreans and other foreign-born couples.
