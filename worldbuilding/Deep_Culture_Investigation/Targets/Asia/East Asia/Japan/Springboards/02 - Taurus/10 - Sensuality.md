# Japan: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (kanji and kana; classical Japanese for Heian and Edo texts); Ainu; Okinawan (*check*). **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound, sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure. Sexuality itself belongs to Scorpio and is not scouted here.
