# Japan: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Prefectures: Tokyo and Aichi cores against Tohoku, Shikoku and Kyushu, with Okinawa's income position. Old merchant families (Kyoto, Osaka, Omi) against newer founders. Farm households (JA) and fisher households (JF). Ainu communities and their trading history. Ryukyuans, with base-land income earners as one group. Zainichi Koreans, including the pachinko and yakiniku trades. Single-parent and elderly households. Temple and shrine households.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on income, wealth surveys and business history; mixed on minority wealth; thin on how the very rich live.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical Japanese and kanbun for Edo and earlier records); Ainu for trade terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** private fortunes and family records; Buraku and Zainichi trades (use communities' own accounts); the pachinko trade's reputation.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
