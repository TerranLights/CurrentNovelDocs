# Japan: Stubbornness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (kanji and kana; classical Japanese for older texts); Ainu; Okinawan (*check*). **Concept in one line:** holding to a position, habit or course against persuasion, evidence or pressure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Farmers, fishers and miners in long local disputes (Chiba, Kumamoto, Fukuoka, Tochigi). Okinawan residents in base towns (Henoko, Futenma). Ainu communities (Hokkaido; Tokyo). Hidden-Christian and Catholic communities (Nagasaki, Goto, Amakusa). Craftspeople and small-restaurant owners. Salaried workers and managers in consensus decisions. Older men in family settings against younger adults. Unions and companies. Zainichi Koreans and other foreign-born residents in long civic campaigns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on labor, protest and apology; mixed on everyday stubbornness; thin on minority words for it.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical Japanese for Nichiren and older proverbs); Ainu and Okinawan for local terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pollution victims' testimony; Ainu remains and war accounts as the communities tell them; contested pro- and anti-base positions in Okinawa.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
