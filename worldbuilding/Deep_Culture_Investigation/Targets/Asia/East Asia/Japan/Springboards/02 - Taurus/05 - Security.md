# Japan: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (kanji and kana; classical Japanese for pre-modern records); Ainu; Okinawan and Ryukyuan (*check*). **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Tohoku coast and Sanriku fishing towns, Nankai-trough coasts (Shikoku, Kii, Tokai), and Tokyo and Osaka cores: hazard cultures differ. Okinawa and the Ryukyu islands: typhoons, bases, child poverty. Ainu communities of Hokkaido. Regular employees, non-regular workers, small-business owners, farmers and fishers (JA and JF coverage). Older adults living alone; single-parent households. Technical-intern workers, Zainichi Koreans and other foreign residents. Day laborers and people living rough.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on disasters, insurance, labor and pensions; mixed on crime perception; thin on informal mutual aid.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical Japanese for Edo granary and famine records); Ainu and Okinawan for local lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 2011 disaster testimony, including loss of children at school; base-related incidents; homelessness and solitary death; nuclear accident politics.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
