# Japan: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (kanji and kana; classical Japanese for early texts); Ainu; Okinawan and Ryukyuan (*check*); Portuguese for Nikkei returnees. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Hokkaido settler towns against old Honshu villages; Tohoku, Kansai and Kyushu regions; depopulating rural areas against metropolitan cores. Ainu (Hokkaido; Tokyo). Ryukyuans (Okinawa, Amami, Miyako, Yaeyama). Old merchant houses (Kyoto, Osaka) and family workshops. Company employees on transfer against local small-business owners. Temple parishes and shrine communities. Zainichi Koreans of the second to fourth generation; Nikkei returnees; disaster-displaced households.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on statistics, firms and rural decline; mixed on household routine; thin on minority continuity outside Ainu and Okinawan heritage records.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical Japanese for old texts); Ainu and Okinawan; Portuguese for Nikkei material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** displaced communities after 2011; war-time rupture in Okinawa; Ainu ceremonies are the communities' to describe.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
