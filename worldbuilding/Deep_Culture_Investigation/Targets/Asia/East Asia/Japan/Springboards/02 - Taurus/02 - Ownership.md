# Japan: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (kanji and kana; classical Japanese for old records); Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages (*check*). **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on. Legal doctrine of ownership is not scouted here: it belongs to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Ainu (Hokkaido, Sakhalin-descended, Tokyo): ancestral grounds. Ryukyuans (Okinawa main island, Amami, Miyako, Yaeyama): clan and village land, bases. Farm villages with iriai commons (Tohoku, Chubu, Kyushu highlands) against cities. Old merchant houses (Kyoto, Osaka) against Tokyo-area owners. Hokkaido settler descendants. Buraku communities. Zainichi Koreans and Chinese residents (who may own). Condominium dwellers against renters.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on tenure, household and land-reform history; mixed on Ainu and Ryukyuan holding; thin on informal norms.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for early land records); Ainu and Okinawan for tenure terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu land loss and remains; Buraku identification (use the communities' own research bodies); base-land owners' privacy.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search 入会 McKean and 入会地 現在 to see how commons are described.
2. Search 所有者不明土地 研究会 報告書 and read the opening definitions of "owner".
3. Search 軍用地主 沖縄 and 一坪反戦地主 to separate three Okinawan stances toward the same land.
4. Search アイヌ イオル再生 and 共有財産 for what Ainu bodies say they hold.
