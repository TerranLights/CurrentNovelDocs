# Japan: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (kanji and kana; classical Japanese and kanbun for pre-modern texts); Ainu; Okinawan and Ryukyuan (*check*). **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, how those things are ranked, and how worth is assigned to things. Law is not scouted here (see Libra and Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Ainu (Hokkaido; Tokyo). Ryukyuans (Okinawa, Amami, Miyako, Yaeyama). Regions: Tohoku, Kanto, Kansai, Kyushu, Hokkaido settler towns. Generations: postwar, bubble-era, job-ice-age, current. Religious communities: Shinto parish, Buddhist sects, new religions, hidden Christians. Zainichi Koreans and other foreign-born residents. Firm employees against the self-employed and farmers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on surveys, texts and school curricula; mixed on minority values; thin where only national-character writing exists.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese, with classical Japanese and kanbun for Edo and earlier sources; Ainu and Okinawan for oral material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu teaching as the communities share it; religious minorities' own accounts; avoid imperial-era and Yasukuni material without context.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the ISM 義理人情 item and its answers by year, then the NHK 「日本人の意識」 parallel.
2. Search 社会意識に関する世論調査 社会貢献 and note the exact question wording.
3. Open 十七条憲法 and 御教条 side by side for first-listed virtues.
4. Search 生活定点 博報堂 価値観 for the latest findings.
