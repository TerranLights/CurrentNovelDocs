# Japan: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard"). Formal authority and hierarchy are Capricorn's; this sheet takes guiding and being followed.*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan (*check*); Korean for Zainichi sources. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command.

## 4. Flags
