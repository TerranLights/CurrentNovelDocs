# Japan: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard"). Punishment law is Libra's and Capricorn's.*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan (*check*); Korean for Zainichi sources. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others (shaming, ridicule, degradation, collective humiliation), and lowering chosen as humility or penance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Schoolchildren by level** (elementary to high school) and **school clubs.**
- **Company types:** large firms with demotion rooms against small firms.
- **Villages and old neighborhoods** against **cities** in ostracism.
- **Women and men:** household humiliation (yome-ibiri) and workplace humiliation.
- **Performers and idols:** public penance and the shaved-head apology.
- **Okinawa and Ryukyuan islands:** dialect-tag history and memorial days.
- **Ainu, burakumin, Zainichi Koreans, other minorities:** humiliation by name, school and marriage.
- **Online communities** against offline groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for bullying and workplace harassment surveys, for broadcasting and apology coverage; mixed for rural ostracism and household humiliation; thin on minorities' humiliation vocabularies.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; Ainu, Okinawan, Korean for those units.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child suicides and victims; identification of burakumin and Zainichi people (never compile lists); victims of harassment (names withheld); stigmatized places.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
