# Japan: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages (*check*); Korean (Zainichi sources). **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action; and the restraint, masks and silences that go with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men, by generation:** pronoun choice, school rules and dress norms.
- **Youth subcultures:** gyaru, Lolita, yankī, otaku, cosplayers.
- **Okinawa** (festive display, coming-of-age ceremonies) against **Honshu** cities (*check* how far the contrast holds).
- **Kansai** (a frank, comic style is often claimed) against **Tokyo** and **Tōhoku** (*check*).
- **Ainu** and **Ryukyuan** speakers: how self is expressed in the minority language against Japanese.
- **Zainichi Koreans, Nikkei Brazilians, other migrants:** naming and speaking out as minority.
- **LGBTQ communities** and Pride organizers (Tokyo Rainbow Pride *check*), and **online anonymous users.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on theory and essays by Japanese authors; mixed on actual behavior (surveys, little ethnography in English); thin on minority voices.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; Ainu and Okinawan for those units; Korean for Zainichi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tattoo (irezumi) stigma and its links; identities of Zainichi and burakumin; minors' online posts; private anonymous accounts.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
