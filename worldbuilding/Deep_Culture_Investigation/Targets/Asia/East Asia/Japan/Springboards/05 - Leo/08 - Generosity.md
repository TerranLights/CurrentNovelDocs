# Japan: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard"). Tax and gift law is Libra's.*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan (*check*); Korean and Portuguese for resident communities. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise; hospitality, gifts and their obligations, giving that makes standing.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages with surviving mutual-aid groups** against **city apartment blocks.**
- **Okinawa** (moai, yuimāru) against **mainland** regions.
- **Shikoku pilgrimage towns** against other hospitality traditions.
- **Temple and shrine communities** (offerings) against **secular volunteers.**
- **Disaster regions** (Kobe 1995, Tōhoku 2011) and their volunteers.
- **Women and men,** **elders and youth** in who gives and who receives.
- **Ainu, Zainichi Koreans, Nikkei Brazilians:** feast sharing, kye circles, remittances.
- **Firms** (company gifts, seasonal gifts) against **households.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on organized giving and gift calendars; mixed on informal giving (people may not count it); thin in English on minority giving traditions.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; Ainu, Okinawan, Korean, Portuguese for those units.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu ritual details and who may describe them; donors' names; disaster victims.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
