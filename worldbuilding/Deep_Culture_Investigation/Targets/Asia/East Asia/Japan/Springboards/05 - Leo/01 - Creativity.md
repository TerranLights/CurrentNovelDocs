# Japan: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages (*check*). **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, crafts, problem-solving and everyday life; and who owns what is made.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iemoto lineages and guilds** (tea, flower arrangement, dance, Noh) against **independent and avant-garde artists.**
- **Craft towns by region:** Kyoto, Kanazawa, Arita and Karatsu (Kyushu), Mashiko, Tōhoku crafts, Okinawa.
- **Ainu makers** of Hokkaido, whose designs belong to families and regions (*check*).
- **Ryukyuan islands:** Okinawa Main Island, Amami, Miyako, Yaeyama have distinct textiles and ceramics.
- **Manga, anime, game and dōjin creators** against **traditional craftspeople.**
- **Zainichi Korean and Nikkei artists,** and **women and men** (who is called gifted).
- **Salaried inventors** inside firms (kaizen) against solitary makers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official honors, school curricula and art history; mixed for everyday improvising (kufu), which sits in folk and business writing; thin in English on Ainu and Ryukyuan makers' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (modern and a little classical); Ainu and Okinawan for those sub-units.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu family and regional designs and ritual objects (who may show or use them); anonymity of craft workers; lineage secrets (hiden) in iemoto schools. Ownership law belongs to Libra; ownership of the intangible is Taurus.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
