# Japan: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard"). Revenge and feud are Scorpio's; duty and reputation as rank are Capricorn's.*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and some classical); Ainu; Okinawan (*check*); Korean for Zainichi sources. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended; honoring others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Warrior-family descendants** (samurai lineages) against **merchant and artisan houses.**
- **Old firms** against **new firms;** **family businesses** and **the salaried.**
- **Sumo, judo, kendo and karate** communities.
- **Women and men,** and how family name and chastity were tied in older teaching.
- **Okinawa and the Ryukyu islands** (own martial and court traditions).
- **Ainu** families with heirloom treasures and feast duties.
- **Zainichi Koreans** and others using real names.
- **Veterans' families** and **military-related groups** (sensitive).
- **Outlaw groups,** treated as a separate case.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical texts and film; mixed on current honor (survey items are few; reputation language lives in business and sport); thin in English for minority honor vocabularies.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese, including some classical (kobun); Ainu, Okinawan, Korean for those units.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war-dead and yasukuni-linked debates; military codes and their uses; Ainu heirlooms and ritual detail; yakuza accounts (never treat as typical); suicide.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
