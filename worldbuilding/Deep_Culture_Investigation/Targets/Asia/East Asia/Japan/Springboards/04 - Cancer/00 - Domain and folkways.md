# Japan: The domain, home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu (*check*); the Ryukyuan languages (Okinawan and others, *check*); Korean (for the Zainichi communities); Portuguese and Chinese for newer communities. **Concept in one line:** what is home, and who is family? (household forms, multigenerational living, fathering, roots, family memory, hospitality, the home's walls, meals, emotional climate, house-building ways, family ways, child-rearing ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Region:** Hokkaido (settler households and Ainu), Tohoku, Kanto, Chubu (snow country), Kansai, the western mainland, Shikoku, Kyushu, Okinawa and the Amami islands: house types and ancestor practice differ.
- **Ainu and Ryukyuan peoples:** own languages, kin forms and house traditions.
- **Urban and rural households,** and public-housing (danchi) families against farm and merchant families.
- **Zainichi Korean and Chinese communities, Nikkei returnees from South America, newer migrant households:** other family forms and languages.
- **Burakumin communities and long-standing outcaste lineages** (handle with care).
- **Religious households** (Buddhist temple parishioners, Shinto, Christian, new religions) where practice shapes the home altar and ancestor rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census, surveys and scholarship; the ie model has an enormous literature. Thin in English for Ainu, Ryukyuan and Zainichi households.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (including classical for pre-modern texts); Ainu and Okinawan for the minorities; Korean for Zainichi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu and Ryukyuan knowledge is the peoples' to share; burakumin identification is sensitive (never trace lineages for them); family registers are private. Law belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. In e-Stat, open the latest census tables on 世帯の家族類型 (household family type) and 三世代世帯, and read how "family household" is defined.
2. Search CiNii (the Japanese scholarly index) for 家 家族 社会学 and for 二世帯住宅, and pull the NFRJ question list from the SSJ Data Archive.
3. Search the Aozora Bunko for 先祖の話 and 家郷の訓 and read the sections on family and home place.
4. Search Upopoy's site and the Okinawa Prefectural Museum for chise and for Okinawan house types (赤瓦の家).
5. Search NHK Archives (NHKアーカイブス) for 家族 ホームドラマ to see which programs set the public picture of the family.
