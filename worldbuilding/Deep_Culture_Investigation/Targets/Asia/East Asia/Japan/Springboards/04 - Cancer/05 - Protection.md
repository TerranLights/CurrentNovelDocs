*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hazard region:** earthquake, tsunami and typhoon coasts (Tohoku, the Pacific coast, Okinawa), snow country, volcanic areas, cities and villages.
- **Urban and rural:** police-box and apartment neighborhoods against villages with volunteer corps.
- **Gender, age and kin roles:** who protects whom within the family.
- **Ainu and Ryukyuan peoples,** with their own protective beliefs.
- **Zainichi Korean, Chinese and other migrant communities** as both protected and targeted.
- **Children, the old, the disabled and those without kin.**
- **Religious groups:** shrine, temple and new-religion protection practices.
- **Self-Defense Forces families** and local fire and rescue volunteers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, child welfare and crime figures; mixed for what people say they owe each other; thin in English for Ainu and Ryukyuan practice.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for the Man'yōshū); Okinawan, Ainu and Korean for the minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grief from the 2011 disaster; abuse cases; the 1923 massacre; hate incidents against minorities; Ainu ritual knowledge. Policing law belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
