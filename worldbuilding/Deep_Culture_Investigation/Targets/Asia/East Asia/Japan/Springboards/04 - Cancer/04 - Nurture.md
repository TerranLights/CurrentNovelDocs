# Japan: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Ainu and Okinawan (*check*); Korean and Portuguese for Zainichi and Nikkei families. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban and rural:** large-city nursery waitlist families against village households with grandparents.
- **Region:** Hokkaido, Tohoku, Kansai, Kyushu and Okinawa for sleep, feeding and festival customs (check).
- **Mothers, fathers, grandparents, paid carers and nursery staff** as separate nurturers.
- **Single-parent and low-income households,** and children's cafeteria users.
- **Ainu and Ryukyuan families** raising children in or beside the language.
- **Zainichi Korean, Nikkei returnee and other migrant families** with ethnic schools.
- **Religious households** (Buddhist, Shinto, Christian, new religions) and their teaching of children.
- **Burakumin families** (sensitive).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, advice literature and media; mixed for actual practice; thin in English for Ainu and Ryukyuan child-rearing. Avoid reading "amae" or "indulgence" as one national trait.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for Ekiken); Ainu, Okinawan, Korean, Portuguese for the minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child abuse and neglect cases; punishment of children for speaking minority languages in schools; Ainu children's ceremonies belong to Ainu families. School-attendance law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
