# Japan: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Ainu and Okinawan (*check*); Korean for Zainichi mothers; Portuguese, Tagalog and Chinese for migrant mothers. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Region:** Tohoku, Kanto, Kansai, Kyushu, Okinawa, and Hokkaido settler areas: birth-visit and family-help customs.
- **Mothers by status:** full-time homemakers, part-time and full-time workers, single mothers, young mothers, mothers over forty.
- **Mothers and their in-laws:** the mother-in-law relation inside the household.
- **Ainu and Ryukyuan mothers,** and women's rites (some sensitive).
- **Zainichi Korean mothers (オモニ);** Filipino, Brazilian, Chinese and other migrant mothers raising children in Japan.
- **Burakumin families** (sensitive).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for statistics, scholarship and media; mixed for individual experience; thin in English for Ainu, Ryukyuan and migrant mothers.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for the Heian diary); Ainu, Okinawan, Korean, Tagalog, Portuguese for the minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abortion and miscarriage grief (mizuko rites); postpartum depression and infanticide cases; Ainu women's rites and tattoos belong to Ainu women to share; mothers' accounts of abuse. Law on parental authority belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
