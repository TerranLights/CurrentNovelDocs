# Japan: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese (modern and classical); Okinawan and Ainu (*check*); Korean; Indonesian, Filipino and Vietnamese for migrant care workers. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Region and settlement:** depopulating rural areas, snow country, cities, new towns.
- **Households:** living with elders, alone, spouse-only; sons and daughters, daughters-in-law, grandchildren; men as caregivers.
- **Okinawa and the Amami islands,** with their own mutual-aid and elder customs.
- **Ainu and Zainichi Korean elders,** with community-run care and language needs.
- **Migrant care workers and their employers.**
- **People with disabilities and chronic illness;** children as carers.
- **Religious care:** temple and church hospices, new-religion welfare work.
- **Burakumin communities** (sensitive).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for statistics and policy writing; mixed on family feeling; thin in English for Ainu and Ryukyuan care. Insurance and social-security law is not covered here; send it to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for Edo sources); Okinawan, Ainu and Korean; Indonesian, Filipino or Vietnamese for migrant carers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse and neglect cases; dementia and end-of-life decisions; carers' suicides; interviews with young carers need care. Law belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
