# Japan: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu and Okinawan (*check*); Korean for Zainichi communities. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Climate and region:** snow-country Hokkaido and the Sea of Japan coast, the Pacific coast, the southwestern islands; typhoon and quake zones.
- **Urban, suburban, rural and depopulating villages:** rental apartment, danchi, detached house and abandoned house.
- **Ainu and Ryukyuan peoples** and their own home concepts.
- **Disaster-displaced people** from Tohoku, Kumamoto and Noto (*check* region list), and nuclear evacuees.
- **Zainichi Korean, Chinese, Nikkei and other migrant households** for housing access.
- **Homeless and net-cafe dwellers:** the homeless poor in cities, for the margin of home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing statistics and architecture; mixed for feeling and meaning, which must be read from essays, fiction and interviews. Thin in English for Ainu and Ryukyuan homes.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (classical for the Hōjōki); Ainu and Okinawan for the minorities; Korean for Zainichi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Fukushima evacuees' testimony and bereavement; Ainu sacred hearth and house-rite knowledge; the burakumin question in housing. Property and tenancy law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. In e-Stat, pull 住宅・土地統計調査 tables for 持ち家比率 (homeownership rate) and 空き家率 (vacancy rate) by prefecture.
2. Search Aozora Bunko for 方丈記 and 陰翳礼讃, and mark every passage on the house itself.
3. Search CiNii for 住宅双六 and マイホーム 戦後 to find the scholarship on the my-home dream.
4. Search the Reconstruction Agency and Kobe's disaster memorial for 仮設住宅 生活 聞き取り (temporary housing life, interviews).
5. Search NHK Archives for 住まい (housing) and 我が家 programs for the media picture of home.
