# Japan: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu and Ryukyuan for the island peoples. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Retired salaried men against retired farm and fishing elders, whose work does not end at a set age.
- Women as household and care workers against firm employees.
- Tōhoku, Hokkaido and urban households (thrift and repair traditions differ).
- Okinawa, with its own elder and mutual-aid ideas and tourism image.
- Ainu communities, whose land and role language was reshaped by settlement.
- Disabled people, sanatorium residents and their families.
- Withdrawn young adults (hikikomori) and ice-age-generation workers.
- Nikkei, Zainichi and migrant workers as "useful labor" in public talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, literature and media; thin for private feelings of being a burden.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for the old tales; Ainu and Ryukyuan *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eugenic sterilization and leprosy-isolation history; elder suicide and lonely death; "productivity" disputes about minorities; Ainu land history.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
