# Japan: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu and Ryukyuan (Okinawan, Yaeyama) for the island peoples. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Farm, fishing and forestry households (兼業農家) against urban wage earners.
- Large-firm regular employees against nonregular workers, and women against men.
- Osaka merchant and Kantō white-collar cultures.
- The Ainu of Hokkaido, whose work came under contract-fishery and settler pressure.
- Okinawa and the Ryukyus, with their own work and mutual-aid forms.
- Burakumin trades, day laborers (寄せ場), and migrant and Nikkei workers.
- Generations: the postwar cohort, the ice-age cohort, and the youngest.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, history and literature; mixed for Ainu and Ryukyuan voices (often written by outsiders).
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for Edo texts; Ainu and Ryukyuan *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu forced labor and community consent over research; burakumin; migrant-worker abuse; overwork deaths of named persons.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

4. National Diet Library digital collection: 近世蝦夷人物誌 and 女工哀史 for the voice of the working margins.
5. Ainu and Okinawan dictionary look-ups for the work words before any claim about them.
