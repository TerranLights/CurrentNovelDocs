# Japan: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu and Ryukyuan for the island peoples. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Superiors, equals and subordinates in firms, schools and clubs (rank governs who may criticize).
- Okinawa and the Ryukyus against the main islands, as to state criticism and base issues.
- Ainu communities, their own correction customs, and their criticism of outsiders and researchers.
- Kansai (comic retort, manzai) against Kantō.
- Women and men on harassment and on being told off.
- Generations: the postwar protest cohorts, salaried cohorts, and the online youth.
- Zainichi Koreans and other minorities, who criticize from outside the majority.
- Journalists in press clubs against weekly-magazine and freelance writers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, press and school ethnography; mixed for private family criticism; thin for Ainu and Ryukyuan norms.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for Edo and earlier forms; Ainu and Ryukyuan *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1972 United Red Army "sōkatsu" killings, where self-criticism became lethal; corporal punishment in schools and sports; workplace abuse and suicide; online harassment of named people; claims of a "national silence" must be tested, not assumed (name variation, not one voice).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
