# Japan: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu and Ryukyuan for the island peoples. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Artisans and small-factory owners against large-firm engineers and office workers.
- Craft regions: Kyōto, Tsubame-Sanjō, Ōta, and the Okinawan and Ainu craft communities.
- Women and men, on appearance, housework and school standards.
- Students in exam-track schools against those in vocational schools, and young adults who withdrew.
- Ritual specialists (Ise priests, noh and kabuki families, tea schools) against ordinary worshipers.
- Tōhoku and Hokkaido makers against Kansai and Kantō ones.
- Foreign residents and workers meeting Japanese standards at work.
