# Japan: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu and Ryukyuan for the island peoples. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Large-city department stores, small ryokan and family shops, and rural neighborhood associations.
- Shikoku pilgrimage towns (osettai) against other regions.
- Samurai-descended, merchant and farm households, whose service forms differ historically.
- Women who work as maids, housekeepers and proprietresses; men in public service and the Self-Defense Forces.
- Okinawa (noro and yuta traditions, base towns) and Hokkaido Ainu communities.
- Temples and shrines (priests, parishioners 氏子) and newer volunteer and NPO circles.
- Foreign care and domestic workers and technical interns (*check*), against Japanese-born service workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, public-service and commercial service; mixed for domestic service (often fiction and oral history).
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for Zen, bushi and merchant texts; Ainu and Ryukyuan *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime labor service and the military past; Ainu and Ryukyuan ritual knowledge held by communities; abuse reports from care and technical-intern workers; the shrine and priestess traditions of Okinawa.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
