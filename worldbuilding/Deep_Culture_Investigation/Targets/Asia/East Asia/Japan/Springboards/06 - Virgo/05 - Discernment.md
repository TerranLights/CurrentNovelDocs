# Japan: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu and Ryukyuan for the island peoples. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Urban Tokyo and Kansai taste cultures (iki and yabo; the Osaka merchant's eye) against rural judging of land, weather and animals.
- Elders, who judge by experience, against younger people who judge by reviews and apps.
- Women and men in the marriage market, and families who use go-betweens against those who do not.
- Tōhoku itako and kamisama, Okinawan yuta and noro, and temple Zen communities: three different judging traditions.
- Ainu communities (omens, kamuy) and new religions' members.
- Scam victims, especially older people, and the agencies that serve them.
- Foreign residents, who are often targeted by or excluded from reading "the air".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts, consumer and fraud statistics; mixed for folk shamanic judging; thin for private moral judging.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical Japanese for Heian, medieval and Edo texts; Ainu and Ryukyuan *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aum Shinrikyo survivors and victims; new-religion donation disputes; yuta and itako knowledge and clients' privacy; whether prejudice has been presented as judgment (to be checked, including for burakumin and in the 興信所 trade); scam victims' shame.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
