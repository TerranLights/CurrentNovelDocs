# Japan: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu and Ryukyuan for the island peoples. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Large-firm employees (kaizen, training) against self-employed and farm households.
- Meiji-born, postwar, bubble, ice-age and youngest cohorts, whose views of effort and mobility differ.
- Hokkaido settlers and the Ainu; main-island Japanese and Okinawans (the standard-language campaign).
- Regions with revitalization drives (Ōita, Shimane, Tōhoku coast) against depopulating ones.
- Women and men on lessons, certificates and appearance work.
- Urban redevelopment neighborhoods and the people displaced.
- Zainichi Koreans and other minorities, whose names and schooling were "improved" or resisted.
- New-religion and business-ethics groups against secular readers of self-help.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Meiji texts, management, adult learning and regional policy; mixed for private self-help; the Ainu and Ryukyuan side needs community voices.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; some classical and Meiji-era Japanese; Ainu and Ryukyuan *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced assimilation of Ainu, Ryukyuans and colonial subjects; eugenic "improvement" and its survivors; displacement from redevelopment; training that crosses into harassment.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
