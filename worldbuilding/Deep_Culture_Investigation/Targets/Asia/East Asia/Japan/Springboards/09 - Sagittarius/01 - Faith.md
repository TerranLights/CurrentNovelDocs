# Japan: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan and other Ryukyuan languages. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu**: faith expressed in kamuy relations, not shrine visits; compare Hokkaido's Ainu communities (Biratori, Shiraoi, Kushiro).
- **Ryukyuan**: ancestor and shrine-priestess systems; compare Okinawa main island, Miyako, Yaeyama and Amami.
- **Hidden Christians and Catholics** of Nagasaki, Goto and Ikitsuki; **Protestant** minorities; **Zainichi Korean** churches.
- **Members of new religions** against the unaffiliated and the household-temple parish.
- **Disaster regions** (Tohoku, Kobe): faith after loss.
- **Generations**, including the post-2022 "religious second generation".
- **Urban and rural**: grave and household-altar practice differs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on survey and text; mixed regionally; Ainu and Ryukyuan material often in the communities' own recordings and local archives. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core islands and Ryukyu/Okinawa.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (modern and classical); Ainu and Okinawan useful; Portuguese and Latin for Kirishitan documents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aum Shinrikyō and Unification Church harms; Ainu and Ryukyuan sacred knowledge; interviews with disaster survivors; religion-and-state law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
