*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Prefectures and regions**: Tokyo/Kansai against rural prefectures in university access; **Hokkaido** and **Tohoku** (group-employment history).
- **Okinawans**: language policy and the teachers' movement; **Ainu**: language revival and the history of separate schooling.
- **Zainichi Korean, Brazilian and other foreign-background students**; **Burakumin** descendants *check*: gaps and special programs.
- **Public, private and religious schools**; **women's and men's colleges**; **juku-attending** and **non-attending** children.
- **Students who refuse school** (futōkō) and **adult returners** (night schools, open university).
- **Craft apprentices** and **iemoto students** against degree seekers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official statistics, exams, history and school institutions; rich in pre-modern sources; mixed for minority-language and community schooling; thin outside print for Ainu and Ryukyuan own-voice. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core islands and Ryukyu/Okinawa.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (modern and classical); Korean and Portuguese for community schools; Ainu and Okinawan helpful.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children's records and bullying cases; discrimination in schooling (Burakumin, Zainichi, foreign children); the Ainu schools' historical names and the harm tied to them; school-policy law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).

## 5. First queries
1. Download MEXT 学校基本調査 and 大学進学率 by prefecture, and the SSM data summary for educational returns.

3. Search "寺子屋" and "藩校" with a region name to find a local school and its teaching.
4. Search the Ainu Culture Foundation and the Okinawa Prefecture Shimakutuba pages for language programs and their history of school punishment.
5. Search "夜間中学" and "朝鮮学校 歴史" to map schooling outside the standard path.
