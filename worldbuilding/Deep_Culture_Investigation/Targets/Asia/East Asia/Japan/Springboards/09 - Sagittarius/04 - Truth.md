# Japan: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan; Ryukyuan-language sources partly in classical Chinese and Japanese. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it (scripture, elders, scientists, the press, one's own experience) and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on news trust, history disputes and classical texts; mixed on rural belief and oral authority; thin for fully own-voice Ryukyuan and Ainu material outside print collections. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core islands and Ryukyu/Okinawa.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (modern, classical bungo and kanbun for pre-modern); Ainu and Okinawan helpful.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disputed wartime histories (Nanjing, comfort women, forced labor) and the 1923 massacre must be handled through documents from every side; Aum Shinrikyō material; Ainu and Ryukyuan restricted tales; names of Fukushima evacuees.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
