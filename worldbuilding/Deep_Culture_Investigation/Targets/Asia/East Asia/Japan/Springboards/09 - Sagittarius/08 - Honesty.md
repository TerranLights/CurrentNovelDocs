## 3. Who to compare inside the nation

- **Rural stand-keepers and town dwellers** (honor-box practice differs).

- **Foreign workers and Zainichi residents**: experiences of fraud, brokers and unequal dealing.
- **Generations** taught under different moral curricula (prewar, postwar, post-2018).
