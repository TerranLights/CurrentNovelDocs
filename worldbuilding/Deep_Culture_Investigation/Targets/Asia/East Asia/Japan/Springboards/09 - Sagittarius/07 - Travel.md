# Japan: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan; Portuguese and Spanish for emigrant communities abroad. **Concept in one line:** going far from home (long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage, migration) and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Okinawans and Okinawan diaspora** against **mainland emigrants**.
- **Ainu**: a past of trade routes and forced relocation.
- **Hokkaido** and **Tohoku** rural departures against **Tokyo-Osaka** arrivals.
- **Pilgrim regions** (Shikoku, Kumano, Ise, Kōya) and their hosts against mass-tourism cities.
- **Nikkei returnees**, **foreign workers**, **Zainichi** residents.
- **Generations**: group-employment migrants of the 1950s-70s, bubble-era tourists, today's young.
- **Women** and **solo travelers** against families and company groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official tourism, border and migration statistics; rich in pre-modern diaries, guides and prints; mixed on regional breakdown; thin in accessible form for Ainu and Ryukyuan own-voice travel material. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core islands and Ryukyu/Okinawa.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese; classical for diaries; Portuguese and Spanish for emigrant sources; Ainu and Okinawan helpful.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced relocations and wartime emigration (Manchuria, repatriation) and their memory; names of foreign workers; border and visa matters belong to Libra's letter-of-law sign, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
