# Japan: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or the state, and freedom as a belief and political ideal. Law as such is researched only in Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Okinawans**: the case for collective self-determination, with bases and reversion as the setting.
- **Ainu**: Indigenous claims distinct from Okinawan ones.
- **Zainichi Koreans, Burakumin descendants** *check*, **Nikkei returnees** and other residents: freedom as equal standing.
- **Women** and **sexual minorities**: freedoms claimed in family and work.
- **Hokkaido** and **Tohoku**: frontier and rural views against the capital's.
- **Generations**: the 1960s protest cohort, the 2011 and 2015 cohort, and the pandemic-era young.
- **Former patients and survivors** (Hansen's disease; Siberia; Hibakusha): freedom lost and recovered.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Meiji debate, social-movement history, press and surveys; mixed for rural and minority views; own-voice Ainu and Ryukyuan collective-freedom writing exists but is scattered. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core islands and Ryukyu/Okinawa.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (modern and Meiji-era bungo); Ainu and Okinawan helpful; Korean for Zainichi materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hansen's disease patients' names and testimony (follow museum guidance); comfort-women, forced-labor and war-memory controversies; Okinawan base politics; outside indexes should be read as external ratings, not as the culture's own view. The legal text of any freedom belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
