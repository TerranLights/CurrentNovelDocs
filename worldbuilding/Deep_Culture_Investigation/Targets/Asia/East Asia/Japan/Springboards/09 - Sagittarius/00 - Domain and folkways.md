# Japan: Domain and folkways, belief and philosophy (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Japan, region East Asia (the main islands of Hokkaido, Honshu, Shikoku and Kyushu, with the Ryukyu/Okinawa chain). **Language(s) for searching:** Japanese; Ainu; Okinawan (Uchinaaguchi) and the other Ryukyuan languages; English for the scholarship. **Concept in one line:** the domain question, what is true and where is meaning found: religious landscape, religion in public life, secularity, philosophy and worldviews, the foreign and far away, wisdom and who holds it, providence and luck, religious ways, freedom ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ainu** (Hokkaido; also Sakhalin and Kuril histories): a separate religious and philosophical tradition in its own language.
- **Ryukyuan peoples** (Okinawa, Amami, Miyako, Yaeyama): a separate priestly and shrine system and its own relation to the mainland.
- **Hidden Christian (Kakure Kirishitan) and Catholic communities** (Nagasaki, Goto, Ikitsuki, Sotome): a distinct inheritance from the 1500s.
- **Regions of the main islands**: Tohoku (Osorezan, Dewa Sanzan mountain practice), Kansai (Kyoto, Nara, Kumano, old capital temples and shrines), Hokuriku and other strongholds of one Buddhist lineage, Shikoku (the pilgrimage), Kyushu, Hokkaido settler areas.
- **Zainichi Korean** communities, **Nikkei returnees** from South America, other long-resident foreign communities including Muslims and Christians from abroad: religion as migration.
- **Burakumin** descendants: temple records and discrimination in religious life *check* (sensitive).
- **The new religions' members** against **no-affiliation** respondents: two ways of living outside the old parish system.
- **Generations and the urban/rural split** (Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya against depopulating rural areas).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official and survey data (yearbook, repeated national surveys, ISSP); rich in pre-modern and modern text; mixed on regional breakdowns; thinner on Ryukyuan and Ainu own-voice material in accessible form. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core islands and Ryukyu/Okinawa.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese (modern and classical bungo); Ainu and Okinawan helpful; Portuguese and Latin only for early missionary documents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ainu and Ryukyuan sacred knowledge and the handling of remains (follow the communities' own published guidance); Yasukuni and State Shinto as politically live subjects; Aum Shinrikyō (1995) and the Unification Church controversy (2022 onward) as recent harm and trauma; Burakumin lineage and temple discrimination. Religion-and-state law is not researched here: it belongs to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
