# Hakka (客家, Hak-kâ): sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, springboard tracker

**Read this first when resuming work on this sub-unit.**

- **What this is:** a tenth sub-unit of the **Sinian Federation** (#5 in the master tracker), added to the roster 2026-11-17 at the author's request. The other nine are the Han member states; the Hakka (about 44 million) are a Han people with their own language and culture who live across several of those states and form a majority in none, so without their own unit they would be split across the Yue, Min and Gan sheets. It is a springboard sub-unit, not a separate target; the Sinian Federation's DCI stages stay in `../../PROGRESS TRACKER.md`.
- **Non-territorial:** this sub-unit has no lands of its own. Write each sheet for **the Hakka people and their communities**, not for a territory: the Hakka language (客家話 / 客家话) and its dialects, Hakka customs, lineages and migration history, and how Hakka culture differs from the Cantonese, Hokkien, Gan and other neighbors it lives among. Mention the federation as a whole only briefly.
- **Where the communities are (working list, for the author to confirm):** the Hakka heartland where Guangdong, Fujian and Jiangxi meet (Meizhou in Guangdong, western Fujian around Longyan and the Yongding tulou, southern Jiangxi around Ganzhou), plus communities in Guangxi, Sichuan, Hunan, Hainan and Hong Kong's New Territories. Taiwan's Hakka belong to the Taiwan target, and the overseas diaspora lies outside the federation; mention either only for comparison.
- **Overlap:** Hakka material will also turn up in the Yue, Min and Gan sheets. This unit is where the Hakka are the subject, not a minority in someone else's state.
