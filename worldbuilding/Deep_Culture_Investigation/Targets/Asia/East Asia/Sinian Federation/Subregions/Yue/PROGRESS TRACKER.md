- **Territory:** the provinces listed above (27 units in nine member states across the federation), to be confirmed by the author in Stage 0. Write each sheet for **this member state's lands and peoples** (its dominant Han regional culture, its other Han and non-Han peoples, its languages and dialects), and mention the federation as a whole only briefly.
