## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars** of Kazan's academic and business milieu against rural Tatars; **Kryashens** and **Mishars** for different school routes.
- **Bashkirs** in Ufa's administration and universities against villages where folk contests still confer standing.
- **Chuvash**, **Mari**, **Udmurts**, **Erzya**, **Moksha**: education as the classic way up; compare titular-republic quotas with members living elsewhere.
- **Russians**: Ural engineering and defense towns, where technical merit and seniority mix.
- **Kazakhs** of Orenburg; **migrants** whose foreign diplomas may not count.
- **Women** in each group, for how earned standing is judged by gender.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for education, prizes and Soviet promotion; mixed for present beliefs about merit against connections; thin on how Finnic villages honor effort.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (Arabic script for *Asar*); Bashkir; Chuvash, Udmurt, Mari, Erzya, Moksha for local prizes and press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** quotas by nationality and claims of favoritism along ethnic lines are charged topics; exam-fraud claims touch named schools and officials.
- **Held for review:** none.
