## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars** of Kazan's old quarter and new districts, and rural Tatars, for household and neighborhood order.
- **Bashkirs** of the mountain and Trans-Ural districts, with a different settlement history from the river lowlands.
- **Chuvash**, **Mari**, **Udmurts**: traditional-faith villages where sacred groves and seasonal rites order the year, against Orthodox and urban members.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha**, for village layout and festival order.
- **Russians**: Ural factory towns, Soviet planned cities (Togliatti, Naberezhnye Chelny), closed cities, **Orenburg Cossack** settlements.
- **Old Believers**, whose strict household rules are a distinct order.
- **Migrant neighborhoods** in Kazan and Yekaterinburg, often the target of order campaigns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for planned cities, crime statistics and Kazan's urban history; mixed for household order; thin for Finnic cosmology outside regional institute series.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Chuvash (Ashmarin is in Chuvash and Russian); Tatar and Bashkir; Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha for ritual and festival terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred groves and prayer rites are shared only as communities allow; protest coverage is politically sensitive; crime data can stigmatize groups and needs care.
- **Held for review:** none.
