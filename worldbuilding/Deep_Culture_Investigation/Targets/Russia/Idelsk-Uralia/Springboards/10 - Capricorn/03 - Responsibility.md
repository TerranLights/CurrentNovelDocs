## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Volga Muslim Tatars and Bashkirs**: theological views of decree and agency, and the gap between scholars and village practice.
- **Russians** of former commune villages against **Ural factory and closed-city populations** (Ozersk, Snezhinsk), where state secrecy shaped who answers.
- **Bashkir and Tatar villages on the Techa**, whose experience of harm and blame differs from the cities.
- **Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt** village communities, where collective prayer and offerings may carry shared answerability.
- **Erzya and Moksha**, with their own folk pedagogy literature.
- **Kryashens** and **Old Believers**, whose confession practices differ from both Muslim and mainstream Orthodox neighbors.
- **Labor migrants** in Kazan and Yekaterinburg, often blamed in public debate.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters and official blame; mixed for theology (needs specialists); thin for everyday answerability in Finnic villages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (Arabic script and Ottoman-influenced literary language for 19th-century theology); Arabic for some scholarly texts; Bashkir, Chuvash, Udmurt, Mari, Erzya, Moksha for folklore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** closed-city and nuclear topics; Memorial's archives after its 2021-2022 liquidation; the school shooting involves victims' families, handled with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
