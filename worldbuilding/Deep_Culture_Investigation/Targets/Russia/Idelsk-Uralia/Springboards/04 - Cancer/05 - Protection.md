## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar and Bashkir Muslims**, whose household protection runs through Quranic verses and prayers.
- **Kryashens**, Orthodox but Tatar-speaking, with their own protective customs.
- **Mari keeping the traditional faith**, and **Udmurts** with lineage guardians.
- **Chuvash**, with Orthodox and older layers side by side.
- **Erzya and Moksha**, with protection stitched into dress.
- **Russian villagers, Old Believers and Orenburg Cossacks.**
- **Factory-town families of the Urals**, whose men worked in heavy and defense industry.
- **Techa River villages**, a separate case.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for charms and amulets; mixed for present-day ideas of who protects whom.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir, including Arabic script on amulets; Chuvash, Mari and Mordvin for charms and prayers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** traditional-faith prayers and sacred groves; healers may not wish to be named; the Techa and Kyshtym record and nuclear sites; military topics; violence in the home is sensitive and some crisis-center NGOs work under legal pressure. Child-protection law and guardianship go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
