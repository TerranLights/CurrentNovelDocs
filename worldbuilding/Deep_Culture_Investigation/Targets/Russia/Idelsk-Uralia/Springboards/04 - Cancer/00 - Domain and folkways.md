## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Volga Tatars**, split into Kazan Tatars, Mishars and Kryashens (Orthodox Tatars), whose house and family customs differ by faith and dialect.
- **Bashkirs**, with steppe-south, mountain and northwestern groups, from a semi-nomadic past.
- **Chuvash**, upper (Viryal) and lower (Anatri) groups, plus Chuvash villages in Tatarstan and Bashkortostan.
- **Mari**: Meadow, Hill and Eastern Mari, including those keeping the traditional faith.
- **Udmurts**, northern and southern, with the Besermyan as a separate group.
- **Erzya and Moksha**, two Mordvin peoples, often studied together but not one.
- **Komi-Permyaks**, in the Perm north.
- **Russians**: old Volga villages, Ural factory towns, Old Believer communities, Orenburg Cossacks.
- **Smaller groups**: Nagaibaks, Teptyars, Kazakhs of the Orenburg border, Volga German descendants, Jewish families of Kazan and Yekaterinburg.
- **City and countryside**: Kazan, Ufa, Yekaterinburg, Samara and the factory new towns against the villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich. Soviet and post-Soviet ethnography of the Volga-Ural peoples is large, and the census is detailed. Thinner for the Besermyan, Nagaibaks and Komi-Permyaks.
- **Lands:** Idelsk-Uralia's exact lands are still to be settled; the tracker header carries "Russia". These sheets are written for the Volga-Ural core (Tatarstan, Bashkortostan, Chuvashia, Mari El, Udmurtia, Mordovia and the Russian-majority oblasts of the middle Volga and the Urals).
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Tatar and Bashkir (Cyrillic, plus Arabic script before 1927 and Latin *Yañalif* 1927-1939); Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya and Moksha for folklore and local press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** census ethnic categories are contested (Kryashen and Tatar; Tatar and Bashkir in northwestern Bashkortostan); the Mari traditional faith's prayer sites; mosque registers name living families.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull the 2021 census household-composition tables for Tatarstan, Bashkortostan and Chuvashia: search Rosstat for "Всероссийская перепись населения 2021 домохозяйства".
2. Find the *Народы и культуры* volumes for each people in a library catalog and list their family and dwelling chapters.
3. Search eLibrary.ru for "семейный быт" with each people's name (татар, башкир, чуваш, марийцев, удмуртов, мордвы).
4. Ask the National Archive of Tatarstan for its guide to *ревизские сказки* and *метрические книги*.
5. Query D-PLACE for each people's name and note what is coded and by whom.
