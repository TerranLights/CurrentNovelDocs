# Idelsk-Uralia: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya and Moksha. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatars** against **Kryashens**, whose Orthodox faith brings godparent kin (*кум*) into a Tatar-speaking family.
- **Mishars** of the western districts.
- **Bashkir clans** (*ырыу*), whose lineage system differs from Tatar family memory.
- **Udmurt *воршуд* groups** and the **Besermyan**.
- **Mari** who keep the traditional faith against Orthodox Mari.
- **Chuvash** in Chuvashia against Chuvash villages in Tatarstan and Bashkortostan.
- **Erzya and Moksha** separately.
- **Russian merchant and factory families** of the Urals; **Old Believer** families; **Orenburg Cossack** families.
- **Mixed families** in the big cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for kin terms, wedding rites and genealogies; mixed for present-day family life by ethnic group.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir (including Arabic script for older *шәҗәрә*); Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya and Moksha for kin terms and laments.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genealogies name living families; clan and ethnic belonging overlap with contested census categories. Marriage law and family law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Locate Kuzeev's *Башкирские шежере* and list the clans it covers.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "термины родства" with "татарском", "башкирском", "чувашском", "удмуртском" and "марийском".
3. Search the KFU library catalog for "шәҗәрә" manuscripts.
4. Download RLMS household rosters and filter to Tatarstan, Bashkortostan and Chuvashia respondents.
5. Search "межнациональные браки Татарстан" in eLibrary.ru.
