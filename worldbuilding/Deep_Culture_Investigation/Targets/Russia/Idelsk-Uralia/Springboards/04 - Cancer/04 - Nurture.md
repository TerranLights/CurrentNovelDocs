# Idelsk-Uralia: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Udmurt; Mari; Erzya. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar Muslim families** against **Kryashen** families, whose religious nurture differs.
- **Bashkir** families of the south and the northeast.
- **Chuvash** families, the home ground of ethnopedagogy as a field.
- **Mari** and **Udmurt** families, where the question of raising children in the native tongue is pressing.
- **Erzya and Moksha** households, kept apart since their child-rearing words differ.
- **Russian** families of old villages, Ural factory towns, Old Believer communities.
- **Grandparent-raised children** in villages whose parents work away.
- **Urban middle-class parents** in Kazan, Ufa, Yekaterinburg and Samara.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk pedagogy and lullabies; mixed for present-day parenting by ethnic group. Soviet ethnopedagogy can idealize; read it as one voice.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash and Udmurt for lullabies and children's press; Arabic-script Tatar for older moral literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** language-of-schooling debates in the republics; Islamic education is closely watched by the state. School discipline as rule goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
