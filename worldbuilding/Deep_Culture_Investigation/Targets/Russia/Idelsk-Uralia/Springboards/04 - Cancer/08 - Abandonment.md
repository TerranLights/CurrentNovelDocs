*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars and Bashkirs**, whose orphan lore runs through Islamic duty to the *ятим*.
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya and Moksha** villages, where emigration to cities is heavy.
- **Russian villages** of the Volga and Urals given up as "unpromising".
- **Mining and single-industry towns** of the Urals.
- **Shift-work families** in Bashkir and Tatar districts.
- **People raised in children's homes**, and families who adopt or foster.
- **Elders left in emptied villages.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for orphanhood history and folklore; mixed for present-day desertion, which is often private.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Old Tatar and Arabic script for Qol Ghali and older *бәет*; Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari and Mordvin for laments.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children's records are confidential; adoption by foreigners is a political subject; memoirs of children's homes can describe abuse. Child-support enforcement and the law of adoption go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
