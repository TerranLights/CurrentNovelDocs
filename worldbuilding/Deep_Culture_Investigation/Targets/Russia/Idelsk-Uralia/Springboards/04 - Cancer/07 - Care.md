# Idelsk-Uralia: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar and Bashkir families**, with Islamic duties to parents and zakat-based charity.
- **Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt villages**, where healer and help-bee customs may survive.
- **Erzya and Moksha** villages of Mordovia and the scattered Mordvin settlements beyond it.
- **Russian Orthodox** families and sisterhoods; **Old Believer** communities that keep care inside the faith.
- **Big-city families** in Kazan, Ufa, Yekaterinburg, Samara and Chelyabinsk, who buy care or use care homes.
- **Villages emptied of the young**, where elders are cared for by neighbors or no one.
- **Central Asian women** working as paid carers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for folk healing and charity history; thinner for present-day family care by ethnic group.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic-script Tatar for manuscripts; Tatar and Bashkir for broadcast and press; Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt for healer studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers and traditional-faith practitioners may not wish to be named; conditions in care homes and psychoneurological institutions are a sensitive subject; Islamic charities are watched by the state. The law of guardianship goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "народная медицина татар", "чувашская народная медицина" and "удмуртская народная медицина".
2. Ask the KFU manuscript department for its list of Tatar medical manuscripts.
3. Pull Rosstat tables on residents of social-service institutions for the Volga and Ural districts.
4. Search "система долговременного ухода Татарстан" for program reports.
5. Search "помочи" with "удмурты", "чуваши", "татары" for help-bee studies.
