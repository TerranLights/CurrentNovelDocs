# Idelsk-Uralia: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya and Moksha; Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar village households** against **Kazan's urban Tatars**, whose house forms and courtyards differ.
- **Bashkirs** of the southern steppe (later settling) against the mountain and forest zone.
- **Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt** villages, each with summer houses and outbuildings of their own.
- **Komi-Permyaks**, in the northern forest.
- **Russians**: old Volga villages, Ural factory settlements, Old Believer homesteads, Orenburg Cossack *станицы*.
- **Factory new towns** (Togliatti, Naberezhnye Chelny, Magnitogorsk) where almost no one grew up in a house.
- **People displaced by the reservoirs** and their descendants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for house forms and statistics; mixed for home as feeling; thin for the Komi-Permyak and Besermyan home.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir for local-history and village books; Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt and Mordvin for house-spirit and building rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Mari and Udmurt traditional faiths' household rites; displacement histories. Violence in the home belongs elsewhere (Protection); any law of housing goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
