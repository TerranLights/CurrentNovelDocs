## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar Muslim scholars and imams** against **secular Tatar intellectuals** against rural believers.
- **Bashkirs** against **Tatars** where their histories compete (the northwest, Ufa).
- **Chuvash** heritage claims to the Bulgar past, against Tatar ones.
- **Udmurt, Mari, Erzya, Moksha, Komi-Permyak** knowledge holders (priests, elders) against academy scholars of the same people.
- **Ural engineering and science cities** (Yekaterinburg, Chelyabinsk, the closed nuclear towns) against farming districts.
- **Old Believers**, with their own textual authorities.
- Generations: Soviet-schooled against post-1991; television watchers against internet users.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on historiography disputes and Muslim intellectual history; media-trust data are national; folk evidence on oaths and lies is scattered in old ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Arabic for scholarly treatises; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya; German for Kemper and older studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** media in Russia work under strong pressure; several outlets are closed or labeled, so "what is true" is itself politically charged. History of origins is an identity fight between peoples: never take one side's account as neutral. Repression archives may be partly closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
