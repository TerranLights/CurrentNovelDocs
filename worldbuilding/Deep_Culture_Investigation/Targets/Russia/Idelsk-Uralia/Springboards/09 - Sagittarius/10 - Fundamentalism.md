# Idelsk-Uralia: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Arabic (for doctrinal texts). **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar and Bashkir Muslims**: Hanafi institutional Islam, Salafi-leaning circles, Sufi-linked villages, secular "ethnic Muslims".
- **Converts** to Islam among Russians, Chuvash and Mari, as a distinct group.
- **Orthodox** parishes against radical lay movements and convents in conflict with bishops.
- **Old Believers**, each accord with its own strictness.
- **Protestants** (Pentecostals, Baptists) and **Jehovah's Witnesses**, often labeled by others.
- **Mari, Udmurt, Erzya** native-faith purists against blended village practice.
- Young urban believers against the Soviet-born.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Historical debates are rich; present-day study is shaped by security framing, so many sources are official or partisan.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (incl. Arabic script for pre-1917 press); Bashkir; Arabic; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya; Turkish for the lyceum story.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** high. Several Muslim groups, Jehovah's Witnesses and some Orthodox radicals are banned or prosecuted; "Wahhabi" and "sect" are used as accusations; members and leavers may face danger. Protect informants, never reproduce labels as findings, and route all legal matters to Libra and Capricorn. Violent cases are rare edges, not the faith.
- **Held for review:** none.
