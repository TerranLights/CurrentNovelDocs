# Idelsk-Uralia: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meadow, Hill and Eastern Mari**: three relations to the old beliefs.
- **Udmurts** (northern Orthodox-influenced, southern near Tatars) and **Besermyan**.
- **Chuvash**: Upper, Lower and the unbaptized villages.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha**, separately; **Komi-Permyaks** of the Kama forests.
- **Tatars**: city and village; Kryashens with their own mixed lore; Mishars.
- **Bashkirs**, with revivalists (Tengrist) against Muslim and secular majorities.
- **Russian** Ural mining towns, Old Believers, Volga villages; New Age seekers around Arkaim.
- Young urban people against grandparents in villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on old ethnography (much of it imperial or foreign and needing critical reading); mixed on current folk belief; national polls exist, regional data thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya; Moksha; Komi-Permyak; German and Finnish for the Finno-Ugric Society editions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "pagan" is an outsiders' word some communities reject; use their own names. Some spirit lore and grove knowledge is kept by priests; use what is published. Revivalist movements are watched by authorities, which shapes what members say.
- **Held for review:** none.
