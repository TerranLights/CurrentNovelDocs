# Idelsk-Uralia: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha, Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar and Bashkir Muslims**: villages with old Sufi lineages against new city congregations; generations raised under atheism against those taught after 1990.
- **Kryashens** and **Nagaibaks**: Orthodox Turkic faith, held apart from both Tatar Islam and Russian Orthodoxy.
- **Russian Orthodox** believers against **Old Believers** (the Urals' priestless chapel accord and its rivals).
- **Chuvash**: Orthodox majority, the unbaptized villages, and Muslim Chuvash where they exist *check*.
- **Mari**: Orthodox, "dual-faith" and traditionalist; the Eastern Mari outside Mari El.
- **Udmurts**, **Erzya** (Raskeń Ozks prayer revival), **Moksha**, **Komi-Permyaks**.
- **Protestants**: Baptists, Pentecostals, Lutherans among Germans; **Jews**; **Catholics**.
- Converts across lines (Tatars to Orthodoxy or Protestantism, Russians to Islam); women and men as carriers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Tatar Islam and Ural Orthodoxy; mixed for Chuvash and Bashkir; thin for Udmurt, Mari, Erzya, Moksha and Komi-Permyak practice, mostly in academy journals and foreign studies.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (with Arabic script for pre-1928 print); Bashkir; Chuvash; Meadow and Hill Mari; Udmurt; Erzya; Moksha; Arabic for prayer texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grove prayers and some kuala rites are not for outsiders; follow what the communities publish. Rivalry between Muslim administrations is partisan; read each side's own outlet. State designations of some groups belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "мөнәҗәт" and "мунаджат" and for "кумалтыш" to find the folklore collections.

4. Search "уполномоченный по делам религий" with "Татарская АССР", "Марийская АССР", "Удмуртская АССР" in the archive guides.
