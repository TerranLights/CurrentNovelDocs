# Idelsk-Uralia: Domain and folkways (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari (Meadow and Hill); Udmurt; Erzya and Moksha; Komi-Permyak; Kazakh in the Orenburg steppe. **Concept in one line:** the domain of belief and philosophy: what is true, and where is meaning found, with religious ways and freedom ways as its folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazan Tatars**, urban and rural, apart from **Mishar Tatars** (different dialect, settlement history) and **Kryashens** (baptized Tatars) and **Nagaibaks** of the southern Urals (Orthodox Cossack origin).
- **Bashkirs**: mountain-forest, steppe, Trans-Ural; the Tatar-speaking northwest.
- **Chuvash**: Upper (Viryal) and Lower (Anatri); the unbaptized Chuvash villages of Tatarstan, Bashkortostan and Samara.
- **Mari**: Meadow, Hill and Eastern (in Bashkortostan and the Urals), whose relation to the old faith differs.
- **Udmurts**, northern and southern, and the **Besermyan**.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha** as two peoples, not one "Mordvin" voice; **Komi-Permyaks**.
- **Russians**: Volga villages, Ural factory towns, Old Believers, Orenburg Cossacks.
- **Orenburg Kazakhs**; Volga Germans; Jewish communities of the big cities; newer Central Asian and Caucasus migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for Tatar Islam and Orthodoxy; mixed for Bashkir and Chuvash; thin and scattered for the Finno-Ugric peoples, much of it in foreign scholarship. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Middle Volga, Kama and Ural core.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar in Cyrillic, Latin (1927-1939) and Arabic script; Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya; Moksha; Komi-Permyak; Kazakh; Arabic and Persian for manuscripts; German, Finnish, Hungarian and Estonian for older ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some religious groups and outlets carry state designations since the 2010s and 2020s; their legal side goes to Libra and Capricorn. Mari and Udmurt priests' grove rites include knowledge not meant for outsiders: use what communities publish. Language-politics material is heated; read several sides.
- **Held for review:** none.
