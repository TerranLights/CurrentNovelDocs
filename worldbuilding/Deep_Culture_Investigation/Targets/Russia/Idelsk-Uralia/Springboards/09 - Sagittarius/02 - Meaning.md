# Idelsk-Uralia: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazan and Ufa** professionals against **village** Tatars and Bashkirs; Bashkir steppe and Trans-Ural herding heritage against forest farming.
- **Ural factory and mining towns** (Russian, Tatar and Bashkir workers) against university cities.
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha, Komi-Permyak** villages against their members in cities, where language and rite may thin out.
- **Old Believers** and **Muslims of old Sufi villages**, where meaning may be framed by faith.
- **Orenburg Cossacks** and **Nagaibaks**: meaning in service.
- **Young people leaving for Moscow** or abroad; **labor migrants** from Central Asia.
- Men and women, separately; veterans of Afghanistan and Chechnya.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Literature and epics are rich; survey data exist nationally but regional cuts are uneven; psychology studies are many but small.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Erzya; Udmurt; Komi-Permyak; German and Hungarian for some older epic scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide is a sensitive topic and some mortality tables may be harder to reach since the early 2020s *check*; war losses and mobilization from the republics are raw and political; handle with care and several sources. The Idegäy ban is a live symbol in Tatar debate.
- **Held for review:** none.

2. Search "Урал-батыр" and "Идегәй" in the Ufa and Kazan institutes' catalogs for annotated editions and studies.
3. Pull Rosstat deaths by external causes for each republic and oblast and set them beside 2021 census nationality shares.
4. Search Tatar news sites for "яшәү мәгънәсе" to find interviews and columns.
5. Search "моногород" with "Свердловская область" and "социологическое исследование".
