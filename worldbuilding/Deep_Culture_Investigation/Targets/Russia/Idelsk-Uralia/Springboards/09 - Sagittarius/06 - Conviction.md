## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers** (Ural priestless and priestly) against mainstream Orthodox.
- **Kryashens** against Tatar Muslims, over centuries of pressure from both sides.
- **Protestants** of the Soviet underground (Baptists, Pentecostals) and **Jehovah's Witnesses**, as they tell it.
- **Tatar and Bashkir national activists** against loyal officials and the indifferent.
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha** language and faith activists, and village elders.
- **Ural industrial towns** (environmental activists, workers) against university-city liberals and the Orthodox lay movements.
- Soviet-trained generations against those formed after 1991.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Historical conviction (Old Believers, Kryashens, Soviet believers) is well archived; present-day conviction is hard to research because speaking out carries risk.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts; Tatar (incl. Arabic script for petitions); Bashkir; Chuvash; Udmurt; Mari.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** since 2022, statements of conscience about the war carry legal danger; Jehovah's Witnesses, some Muslim groups and some national organizations are banned, and prosecutions of Mari and Bashkir activists are reported *check*; route their legal side to Libra and Capricorn, and protect any informant. Archives of security services are largely closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
