*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazan Tatar** merchant heritage against **rural Tatars** and **Mishars**.
- **Bashkirs** of honey and herding districts against city Bashkirs.
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha, Komi-Permyak** village communities, where reputation is close, against their urban kin.
- **Russian** Ural factory towns, Old Believer business families, Volga market towns.
- **Muslim** and **Orthodox** teaching on honesty, and the secular Soviet "conscience" tradition.
- **Market traders** (including Central Asian and Caucasus sellers) and their customers, as each side tells it.
- Students and teachers in exam season.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in proverbs, tales and ethics books; survey data on corruption exist but vary in method; everyday honesty (white lies, trade) is thin and needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (incl. Old Tatar and Arabic script); Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya; Komi-Permyak; Chagatai-Kipchak forms for the 14th-century text.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption is politically loaded and some monitoring groups have been labeled; the legal side goes to Libra and Capricorn. Never let a proverb or a joke stand for a people: name the variation.
- **Held for review:** none.
