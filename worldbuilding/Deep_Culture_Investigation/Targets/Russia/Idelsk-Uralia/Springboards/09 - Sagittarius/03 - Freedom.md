# Idelsk-Uralia: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bashkirs**: steppe and Trans-Ural (land as freedom) against northwestern farming districts.
- **Tatars** of Kazan, of the Kama towns and of the Ural cities; Mishars; Kryashens.
- **Russian** Ural factory towns (bonded-labor memory) against Volga peasant villages (serf memory) against **Orenburg Cossacks**.
- **Old Believers**, priestless and priestly.
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha, Komi-Permyak**: memory of the 1930s purges and of language rights.
- **Orenburg Kazakhs**; **Volga Germans** (deportation of 1941); **Jews**; **labor migrants**, whose movement is limited.
- Women against men; the Soviet-born against the post-1991 born.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on rebellions and national movements; mixed on everyday freedom; surveys are national with few regional cuts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (incl. Arabic script before 1928 and émigré Turkish-Tatar print); Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya; Turkish and German for émigré archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national-movement, protest and sovereignty topics are politically live; several organizations and outlets have been banned or labeled since 2014, and talk of independence carries real risk for speakers. Treat interviews with care. Prison and repression files may be closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
