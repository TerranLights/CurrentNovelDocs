## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazan, Ufa and Yekaterinburg** professionals against **small towns** and **villages**.
- **Tatar** families who choose Tatar-language schools against those who choose Russian; **Mishars**; **Kryashens**.
- **Bashkirs** and Bashkortostan's Tatar-speaking northwest, where language-of-school questions are contested.
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha, Komi-Permyak**: language in school, and the teacher as community figure.
- **Russians** of closed science towns and engineering cities against farm districts.
- **Muslim** and **Orthodox** religious-education graduates; **Old Believer** home learning.
- **Labor migrants'** children; first-generation students.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. The madrasa and missionary-school history is well studied; official statistics are good; the lived standing of the educated is mixed and needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar in three scripts; Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya; Moksha; Komi-Permyak; Arabic and Persian for madrasa texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** language-of-schooling policy is a heated identity issue; never treat one side's numbers as neutral. Script changes carry political meaning. Some foreign-funded education programs have been closed; note dates.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search library catalogs for "Асар" with "Фәхреддин" and for "медресе Мухаммадия история".

4. Search eLibrary.ru for "национальная гимназия" with "удмуртский", "марийский", "эрзянский".
5. Download the HSE admissions-monitoring tables for the region's universities.
