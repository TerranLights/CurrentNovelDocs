# Idelsk-Uralia: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Udmurt. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is researched only under Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); this sheet points there for any legal question.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazan and Naberezhnye Chelny youth:** the street-gang code and those who stayed out.
- **Ural industrial cities:** 1990s organized crime and its memory.
- **Muslim Tatars and Bashkirs:** sin, shame and the mosque community's response to a wrongdoer.
- **Udmurts, Mari and Chuvash:** the Multan memory and older community sanctions.
- **Russian villages and towns:** village punishment, tolerated pilfering, Cossack patrols.
- **Roma and Central Asian migrants:** groups often blamed; compare blame with data.
- **Former prisoners and their families:** the wrongdoer's way back.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1990s organized crime, media and literature; mixed for victimization and moral-judgment data by region; thin for village sanctions after 1930.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Udmurt, Chuvash and Mari for older community sanctions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blaming peoples (Udmurts in the 1890s; Roma and migrants now) is a stereotype to record and test, never adopt; living gang figures and their families; extremism prosecutions of religious groups are a law matter for Capricorn and Libra, so do not research them here.
- **Held for review:** none.
