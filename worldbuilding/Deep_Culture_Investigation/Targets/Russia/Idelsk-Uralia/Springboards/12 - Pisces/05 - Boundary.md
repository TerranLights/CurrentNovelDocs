## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bashkirs:** clan land lines (ырыу) and later settler disputes; mountain and steppe groups differ.
- **Kazan Tatars, Mishars, Kryashens:** the halal-haram and Muslim-Orthodox lines inside one people.
- **Mari and Udmurt traditional believers:** fenced groves, family shrines, and what outsiders may not see.
- **Chuvash, Erzya and Moksha:** village and household thresholds in forest-steppe settings.
- **Orenburg Cossacks and Kazakhs:** the old frontier from both sides.
- **Ural city Russians:** communal-flat memory against today's private flats.
- **Old Believers:** strict lines on food, dishes and marriage with outsiders.
- **Central Asian migrants:** the newest "чужие".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical land and frontier lines; mixed for household privacy (ethnography and forums); thin for personal-space measures by region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir (Arabic-script genealogies); Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Bashkir land history and the Tatar-Bashkir boundary are live political issues; grove rules may be closed to outsiders; Old Believer communities limit contact.
- **Held for review:** none.
