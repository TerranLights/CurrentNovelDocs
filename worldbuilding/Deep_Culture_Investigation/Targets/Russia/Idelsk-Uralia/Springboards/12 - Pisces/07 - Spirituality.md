*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion; here the experience, the practice and the sacred outside the church (institutions are Sagittarius's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars:** traditional Hanafi practice, Sufi-minded devotion, and reformist or Salafi-leaning youth.
- **Kryashens and Nagaibaks:** Orthodox spirituality in Tatar.
- **Bashkirs:** saints' graves, Tengrist revival and Islam side by side.
- **Chuvash:** Orthodox majority and unbaptized villages in Tatarstan, Bashkortostan and Samara.
- **Mari:** Chimarij grove faith, Kugu Sorta, and Orthodox Mari; eastern Mari separately.
- **Udmurts and Besermyans; Erzya and Moksha; Komi-Permyaks:** revivals of different strength.
- **Russians:** Orthodox churchgoers, Old Believers, Rodnovers, Protestants and New Age seekers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Islam and Orthodoxy; mixed for native faiths (mostly community and press sources); survey data by republic exists but is uneven.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya; Arabic-script Tatar and Church Slavonic for older texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grove prayers and some rites are closed, so use what communities publish; some Muslim and Christian minority groups have faced state pressure, so treat their sources with care for the people named; Tengrism and Rodnoverie can carry nationalist politics.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
