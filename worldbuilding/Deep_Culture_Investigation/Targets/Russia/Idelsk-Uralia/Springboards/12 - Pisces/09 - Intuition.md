*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar and Bashkir Muslims:** dream reading and fal practices against clerical objections.
- **Mari, Udmurt and Chuvash traditional believers:** the seer's role inside the community.
- **Orthodox Russians and Kryashens:** discernment taught by priests and elders; Christmastide fortune-telling.
- **Old Believers:** reliance on scripture over feeling.
- **Bashkir beekeepers and herders; Volga fishers; Ural hunters:** expert feel for land and weather.
- **Urban professionals in Kazan, Ufa and Yekaterinburg:** gut against data at work.
- **Women and men; elders and the young:** who is granted the gift.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and popular belief; mixed for expert intuition; thin for measured intuitive thinking by region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic-script Tatar for manuscripts; Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt for folk practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** seers' work may be private or tied to closed rites; the psychic trade includes fraud, so treat it as a trade to study; the Muslim clergy's stance on fortune-reading is a live debate.
- **Held for review:** none.
