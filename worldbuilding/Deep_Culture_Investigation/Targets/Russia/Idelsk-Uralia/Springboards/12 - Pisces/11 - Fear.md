*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars and Bashkirs:** albastı, ubır and şüräle; fear of God in sermons; social fear of gossip.
- **Kryashens and Nagaibaks:** shared spirits, Orthodox frame.
- **Chuvash, Mari and Udmurts:** night demons, forest spirits, and fear of offending a feared spirit.
- **Erzya and Moksha; Komi-Permyaks:** separate spirit lore.
- **Ural and Volga Russians:** domovoy and leshy, terror memory, the fear of the 1990s.
- **Old Believers:** end-times fear.
- **Closed-city and industrial-town residents:** fear of radiation and pollution.
- **Labor migrants and Muslims:** fear of police, raids and labels.
- **Children:** the bogeyman figures used on them in each language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and disasters; mixed for anxiety data by region; thin for children's fears outside folklore.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt and Erzya.
