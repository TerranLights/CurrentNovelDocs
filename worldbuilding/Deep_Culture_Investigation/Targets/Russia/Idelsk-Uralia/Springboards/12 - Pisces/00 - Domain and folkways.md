## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazan Tatars, Mishars, Kryashens, Nagaibaks:** one language, Muslim and Orthodox histories.
- **Bashkirs:** mountain-forest, steppe and Trans-Ural groups.
- **Chuvash:** upper and lower, and unbaptized villages outside Chuvashia.
- **Mari:** meadow, hill and eastern Mari, who kept the grove religion most.
- **Udmurts and Besermyans; Erzya and Moksha; Komi-Permyaks:** separate soul-terms, seers, shrines.
- **Russians:** Ural Old Believers, Orenburg Cossacks, factory towns, old Volga cities.
- **Orenburg Kazakhs; Volga German remnants; Jewish communities:** small, distinct traditions.
- **Cities against villages:** Kazan, Ufa, Yekaterinburg, Samara, Perm.

## 4. Flags
- **Lands:** the tracker header lists only "Russia"; the name points to the Volga-Ural lands, so this sign's sheets are written for that core. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Data:** rich for Tatar, Bashkir and Russian sources; mixed for Finno-Ugric peoples (good folklore, thin modern data); statistics only at republic or oblast level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the six republic languages plus Komi-Permyak; Arabic-script Tatar; Finnish, Hungarian, Estonian and German for early ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grove ceremonies and priests' prayers may be closed to outsiders, so use what communities publish; Gulag and closed-city archives may be restricted; some rights archives and outlets have been shut or blocked; suicide data need care.
- **Held for review:** none.
