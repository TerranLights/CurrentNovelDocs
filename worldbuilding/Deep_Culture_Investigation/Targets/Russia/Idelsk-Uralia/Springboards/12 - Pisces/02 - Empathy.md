# Idelsk-Uralia: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazan Tatars and Mishars against Kryashens:** empathy across the old Muslim-Orthodox line inside one people.
- **Bashkirs of the steppe and of the Trans-Urals:** separate lament and bäyet traditions.
- **Mari, Udmurts, Chuvash, Erzya and Moksha:** lament cultures with different occasions and voices.
- **Ural industrial Russians:** the "hard" factory-town ideal against the new-drama view of the same towns.
- **Men and women; old and young:** laments were mostly women's genres; test where men's empathy shows.
- **Central Asian labor migrants:** the group whose feelings locals are least asked to share.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and literature are rich; direct measurement exists in Russian psychology but is scattered in theses and small samples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir for bäyets; Mari, Udmurt, Chuvash, Erzya and Moksha for laments.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** funeral laments may be performed only at real deaths, so recordings and permissions matter; interethnic surveys are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
