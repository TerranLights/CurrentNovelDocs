# Idelsk-Uralia: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Udmurt, Mari. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatars and Bashkirs:** abstinent ideals, actual drinking, and rest at koumiss resorts.
- **Udmurts, Mari, Chuvash, Erzya and Moksha:** ritual home brew against the vodka economy.
- **Old Believers:** abstinence, and flight from the world as faith.
- **Ural industrial towns:** shift work, the garage, the garden plot, the binge.
- **Yekaterinburg, Kazan and Ufa youth:** gaming, music and clubs against village leisure.
- **Rotational workers and their families:** escape during and after the shift.
- **Women and men:** different escapes and different stigma.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for alcohol and drug statistics and for literature; mixed for leisure and gaming by region; thin for gambling outside national data.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Udmurt, Chuvash and Mari for ritual drink; Church Slavonic and Old Believer texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drinking is often pinned on particular peoples as a stereotype, so compare data, never assume; addiction registries are confidential; coercive rehab touches abuse claims.
- **Held for review:** none.
