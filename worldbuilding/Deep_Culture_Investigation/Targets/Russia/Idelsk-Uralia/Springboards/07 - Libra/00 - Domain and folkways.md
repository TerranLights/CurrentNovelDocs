## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars, separately:** Kazan Tatars; Mishars; Kryashens; Nagaibaks of Chelyabinsk; Teptyars; faith and history differ.
- **Bashkirs:** mountain, steppe, Trans-Ural and northwestern groups.
- **Chuvash:** upper (*viryal*) and lower (*anatri*); unbaptized Chuvash villages in Tatarstan and Bashkortostan.
- **Mari:** Meadow, Hill and Eastern; traditional-faith and Orthodox Mari.
- **Udmurts and Besermyan**; **Erzya and Moksha**; **Komi-Permyaks**; **Northern Mansi** of northern Sverdlovsk.
- **Russians:** Ural Old Believers; Orenburg and Ural (Yaik) Cossack descendants; factory towns; closed nuclear towns of Chelyabinsk province.
- **Orenburg Kazakhs; Ukrainians of Bashkortostan; Jewish communities of Kazan and Yekaterinburg; Central Asian and South Caucasus migrants** in the big cities; urban against rural throughout.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Russian and Tatar; mixed in Bashkir and Chuvash; thin for the Besermyan, Nagaibaks, Mansi and Komi-Permyaks. The tracker header lists all of Russia as pre-war lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the core lands, the Volga-Ural republics and the Ural provinces.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (Cyrillic, Latin and Arabic script); Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya and Moksha; Arabic for religious law.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mari and Udmurt grove rites belong to their communities; the Bashkir accession to Muscovy and Tatarstan's sovereignty are contested; some religious books are on Russia's banned list and some pollsters carry "foreign agent" labels.
- **Held for review:** none.
