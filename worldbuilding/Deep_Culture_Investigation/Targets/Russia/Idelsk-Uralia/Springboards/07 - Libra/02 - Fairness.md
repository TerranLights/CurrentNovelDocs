# Idelsk-Uralia: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya and Moksha; Arabic for inheritance manuals. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatar and Bashkir families** (Islamic shares) against **Orthodox Russian, Kryashen and Chuvash families** (code and commune customs) in dividing estates.
- **Former commune villages** of the Volga against **Ural factory settlements**, where wages and company housing set the measure.
- **Mari and Udmurt villages** with collective work customs.
- **Erzya and Moksha** in rural Mordovia.
- **Monotowns** (Magnitogorsk, Nizhny Tagil, Karabash) against **Kazan and Yekaterinburg** professionals.
- **Labor migrants**, paid and housed apart; **Soviet-born against post-Soviet generations** on leveling and merit; **women and men** on household shares.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial statistics and federal law; mixed for current attitudes, since most surveys report the country or the federal district, not the republics.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Chuvash for Ashmarin; Arabic and Old Tatar for madrasa texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** how daughters' shares are handled under Islamic and customary division is a live family matter; ethnic pay gaps touch migrants who may not speak freely.
- **Held for review:** none.
