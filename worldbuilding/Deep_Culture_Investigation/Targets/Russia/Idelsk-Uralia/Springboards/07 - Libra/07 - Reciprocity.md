# Idelsk-Uralia: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya and Moksha; Arabic for alms and endowments. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar villages** (Sabantuy gifting, *ömä*, memorial *mäcles*) against **Mishars and Kryashens**.
- **Bashkirs:** steppe and mountain hospitality, *ömä*.
- **Chuvash:** *nime* and the *yupa* memorial; baptized and unbaptized villages.
- **Mari and Udmurts:** grove offerings and *veme*.
- **Erzya and Moksha**; **Komi-Permyaks**.
- **Russians:** Volga *pomochi*; Ural factory mutual aid; Old Believer communal help.
- **Labor migrants** and the families who receive their remittances.
- **Urban against rural**; **women** as keepers of the gift accounts in many households.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in village ethnography; mixed for present-day practice; thin for the smaller peoples' own published accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt for rite terms; Arabic for waqf and alms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grove offerings belong to their communities and need consent; Islamic charities are watched by security services, so data may be partial.
- **Held for review:** none.
