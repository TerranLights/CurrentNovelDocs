# Idelsk-Uralia: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt; Arabic for the qualifications of judges and witnesses. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural districts** where officials, teachers and police are kin, against **Kazan, Ufa, Yekaterinburg and Chelyabinsk**.
- **Bashkir clan networks** and **Tatar village kin**, compared without assuming either dominates.
- **Mari and Udmurt activists** who have complained of unequal treatment by regional administrations.
- **Chuvash and Mordovian** republican administrations.
- **Company towns** where one employer stands in for the state.
- **Labor migrants** facing document checks; **Old Believers and Cossack descendants** with their own arbiters; **Orenburg Kazakhs** near the border.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on national favoritism research and law; mixed for regional measures; thin for the smaller peoples' own sense of fair officials.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir for kin terms and local press; Arabic for madrasa law.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Golos and Transparency International bodies carry "foreign agent" or "undesirable" labels in Russia; corruption cases involve living people; avoid casting any people as clannish.
- **Held for review:** none.
