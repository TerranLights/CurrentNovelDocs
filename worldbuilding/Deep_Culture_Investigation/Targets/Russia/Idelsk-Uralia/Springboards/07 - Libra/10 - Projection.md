## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars and Russians:** the "yoke" image and its answers.
- **Tatars and Bashkirs:** mutual claims over people and land in northwestern Bashkortostan.
- **Udmurts, Mari and Chuvash:** once suspected by outsiders of dark rites; their own beliefs about harmful neighbors.
- **Volga Germans and Mennonites; Jewish communities of Kazan and Yekaterinburg**: groups once labeled enemies.
- **Central Asian and Caucasus migrants:** today's blamed outsiders.
- **Roma communities** in the region's cities.
- **Muslims under "extremist" suspicion** against Muslims who accept the official line.
- **Urban against village**, where witch and evil-eye beliefs may differ.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on enemy images and folk belief; mixed on present-day blame; most opinion data are national or district-wide.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar, Bashkir and Chuvash for folk terms; Udmurt for charms; German for deportee and Mennonite records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic disputes and "foreign agent" labels are live and can harm the people named; xenophobia data must not be read as the voice of any whole people; charms may be private.
- **Held for review:** none.
