# Idelsk-Uralia: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya and Moksha. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women in joint households** (daughters-in-law) in Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari and Russian villages.
- **Muslims** (*sabr*) against **Orthodox** (*smirenie*) against **traditional-faith Mari and Udmurts**.
- **Old Believers**: withdrawal.
- **Company-town residents** where one plant is the employer.
- **Finno-Ugric minorities**: when to speak up as a small people.
- **Labor migrants**: silence for fear of losing papers.
- **Younger urban women and men** against their parents' generation.
- **Erzya and Moksha**; **Kryashens and Nagaibaks**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in law and literature; mixed for present-day practice; most surveys are national or district-wide.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir for proverbs and family terms; Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt for village ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and abuse are traumatic topics; several Russian anti-violence groups and pollsters carry "foreign agent" labels; conscription and protest after 2022 are dangerous subjects for people inside Russia.
- **Held for review:** none.
