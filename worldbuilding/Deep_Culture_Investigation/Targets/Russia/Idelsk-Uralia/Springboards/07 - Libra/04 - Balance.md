# Idelsk-Uralia: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatars and Bashkirs** (fasting and the middle way) against **Orthodox Russians, Kryashens and Chuvash** (church fasts).
- **Traditional-faith Mari and Udmurts:** keeping the land's spirits in balance.
- **Trans-Ural and mountain Bashkirs:** seasonal pasture and steppe medicine.
- **Old Believers:** strict fasts and measured living.
- **Ural factory and shift-work towns** against **Kazan and Yekaterinburg** office workers.
- **Bilingual families** balancing Tatar or Bashkir and Russian; **Erzya, Moksha and Komi-Permyaks**.
- **Villages near Mayak and other polluted sites.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on federalism and religious policy; mixed on folk medicine; thin for work-life data below the national level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir for healing texts and pasture terms; Chuvash and Mari for bath rites; Arabic for *wasaṭiyya*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "moderate" against "extremist" Islam is a security label in Russia and colors every source; the language-law change is contested; healers' charms may be private.
- **Held for review:** none.
