# Idelsk-Uralia: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt; Arabic for religious guidance in choosing. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatars and Bashkirs** (*istikhāra*, elders' advice) against **Orthodox Russians, Kryashens and Chuvash** (priests, elders, fortune-telling at holidays).
- **Traditional-faith Mari, Udmurts and Chuvash** who still consult diviners.
- **Old Believers:** council decisions.
- **Young people in villages** against their parents on marriage, study and leaving.
- **Kazan and Yekaterinburg professionals** against **company-town workers**.
- **Erzya and Moksha**; **Orenburg Kazakhs**; **migrants** deciding whether to settle or return.
- **Men and women**, to test the stereotype that one sex dithers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; folk and religious material is rich, measured data is national only; little on indecision as such, so look through choices (marriage, migration, voting).
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Arabic for *istikhāra* texts; Chuvash and Mari for diviners.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** decisions to emigrate or avoid conscription after 2022 are dangerous to discuss and should be handled with care; diviners may not want to be named.
- **Held for review:** none.
