## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russians in Ural cities vs. Volga villages:** to test how Soviet leveling and market-era gaps are remembered in each.
- **Tatars and Bashkirs:** equality of peoples and of languages argued between and within them.
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha:** smaller peoples weighing equal schooling and standing; Erzya and Moksha argued separately.
- **Women in Muslim, Orthodox and traditional-faith families,** read in each group's own sources.
- **Cossack descendants, Kryashens and Nagaibaks:** groups whose status was defined by service or faith.
- **Labor migrants and Roma:** groups likely to be left outside "equal".
- **Mansi:** status as a small Indigenous people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet nationality policy and income statistics; mixed for attitudes; thin for smaller peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (including Arabic-script before 1927) and Bashkir; Chuvash for Yakovlev material.
