## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Volga Tatars,** split into Kazan Tatars, Mishars and Kryashens (Orthodox Tatars), plus Nagaibaks in Chelyabinsk: to test whether mosque parish, church parish and Cossack service shape different communities.
- **Bashkirs,** by clan and by mountain against steppe zones: whether genealogy still frames belonging.
- **Chuvash,** including those living in Tatarstan, Bashkortostan and Samara: Orthodox and traditional-faith currents to keep apart.
- **Mari** (Meadow, Hill and Eastern), **Udmurts and Besermyans**, **Erzya and Moksha**, **Komi-Permyaks**: separate village worlds; traditional-faith groups among Mari and Udmurts to treat on their own.
- **Mansi** of northern Sverdlovsk: small Indigenous communities.
- **Russians,** separated into Ural factory towns, Old Believers, Orenburg and Ural Cossacks, Volga villages and the million-cities (Kazan, Ufa, Samara, Yekaterinburg, Chelyabinsk, Perm).
- **Orenburg Kazakhs; Volga Germans' heirs; Jewish communities of Kazan and Samara; Central Asian and Caucasian migrants.**
