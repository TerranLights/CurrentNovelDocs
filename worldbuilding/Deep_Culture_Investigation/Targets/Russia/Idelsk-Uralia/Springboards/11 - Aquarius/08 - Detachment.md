*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar and Bashkir Muslims:** Sufi renunciation, *ädäp* restraint, and secular urban styles.
- **Orthodox Russians and Chuvash:** monastic and folk-Orthodox views of withdrawal.
- **Old Believers:** separation from "the world" as a rule of life.
- **Mari and Udmurt villages:** restraint norms in their own folklore, set apart from outsiders' labels.
- **Ural industrial men and veterans:** composure, numbing and their costs.
- **Older people living alone** in shrinking villages and monotowns.
- **Young city people** drawn to online Stoicism or to ironic distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Russian-language psychology and film; mixed for Muslim devotional material (much in old script); thin for Finno-Ugric emotional norms.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar in Arabic script (pre-1927) for Sufi texts; Bashkir; Chuvash and Udmurt.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sufi groups face official suspicion; veterans' trauma; electoral data is politically charged. Impartiality in courts goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
