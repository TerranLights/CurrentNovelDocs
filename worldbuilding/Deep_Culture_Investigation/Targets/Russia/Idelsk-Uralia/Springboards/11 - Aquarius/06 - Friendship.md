## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian city men in the Ural industrial belt:** test whether garages, fishing trips and the banya are where men's friendships form, and for whom.
- **Tatar and Bashkir villages:** guest-friendship and youth gatherings; urban Tatars in Kazan and Chelny.
- **Bashkir and Kazakh herders of the Orenburg steppe:** formal partner bonds.
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Mordvin villages:** friendship among age-mates, as folklore records it.
- **Students in Kazan, Yekaterinburg, Perm, Samara:** cross-ethnic friendship in dormitories.
- **Labor migrants:** friendship inside and across groups.
- **Veterans:** friendship in war against friendship in peace.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Russian sociology and folklore; mixed for minority languages; thin for present rural friendship and for Mansi and Komi-Permyaks.
