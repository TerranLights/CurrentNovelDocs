# Idelsk-Uralia: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars and Bashkirs:** national-poet ideals (Tukay, Jalil; Salavat, Karim) and religious ideals (pious, reformist).
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha:** founders of written cultures, each in its own frame.
- **Komsomol-generation Russians and their children** in KAMAZ, Togliatti and Ural plant towns.
- **Ural intelligentsia** (Yekaterinburg, Perm) and its literary disillusion.
- **Mari and Udmurt traditional-faith communities:** ideals of right living tied to groves and ancestors.
- **Old Believers:** purity of faith as a lived ideal.
- **Present volunteers and environmental defenders** (Bashkir shikhans; Ural forests).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and Soviet memory; mixed for present values; thin for traditional-faith ideals in print.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash; Mari and Udmurt for literary founders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1930s purges and present-day activism; Mari priestly teachings may be partly closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
