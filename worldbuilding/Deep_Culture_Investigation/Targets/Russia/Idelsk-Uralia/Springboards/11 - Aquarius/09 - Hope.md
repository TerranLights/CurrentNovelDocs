# Idelsk-Uralia: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar and Bashkir Muslims:** how far hope is phrased through God's will; pilgrimage to Bolgar.
- **Orthodox Russians, Chuvash and Kryashens:** saints and icons; spring processions.
- **Mari, Udmurt and Erzya traditional-faith villages:** calendar prayers for the year.
- **Ural monotown families:** hope in the plant, or in leaving.
- **Big-city young people** (Kazan, Yekaterinburg, Samara): careers, emigration, the future.
- **Villages flooded or emptied by Soviet projects and the 1957 Kyshtym accident:** hope after loss.
- **Labor migrants:** hope sent home as money.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and official statistics; mixed for present hopes; thin for small peoples' modern attitudes.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Bashkir, Tatar; Erzya; Mari, Udmurt, Chuvash for prayer texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data; families of the missing; traditional prayers may be closed to outsiders; hopes about the present war are politically dangerous to voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
