# Idelsk-Uralia: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected (or not).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazan Tatars and village Tatars:** mahalla and home-district ties against city networks.
- **Mishars and Kryashens:** separate kin and church networks; Kryashen festival Pitrau as a reunion.
- **Bashkirs:** clan ties (strong in the southeast to check) against Ufa professional networks; the Bashkir-Tatar northwest.
- **Chuvash:** village-of-origin ties among Chuvash outside Chuvashia.
- **Mari and Udmurts:** kin groups and village prayers that gather relatives; eastern Mari far from Mari El.
- **Erzya and Moksha:** scattered villages across several oblasts; ties kept across borders of regions.
- **Ural Russians:** factory and mono-town ties (Magnitogorsk, Nizhny Tagil), Old Believer kin-and-faith networks, Cossack stanitsa bonds.
- **Kazakhs of Orenburg** with kin across the border; **Volga Germans** with kin in Germany; **migrants** with kin in Central Asia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on blat and Soviet cities in general; mixed on ethnic diaspora bodies (official sources dominate); thin on how ties actually work in small-language villages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari and Udmurt for kin terms and festivals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan and genealogy data can be family property; diaspora congresses are political; favors that shade into corruption touch law (Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.
