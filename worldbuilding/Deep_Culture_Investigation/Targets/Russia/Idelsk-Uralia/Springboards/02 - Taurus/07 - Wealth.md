## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of Tatar merchant families** and the **new Kazan business elite**, against **Tatar villages of the Zakamye**.
- **Bashkir pastoralists** of the Trans-Urals and southern mountains, for whom herds count as wealth.
- **Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt farming villages**, where grain, cattle and the house may be the measure.
- **Ural industrial and Old Believer merchant lines**, and the **gold prospectors** of the Miass, Uchaly and Baymak fields.
- **Oil towns** (Almetyevsk, Leninogorsk, Neftekamsk) with company-linked prosperity.
- **Orenburg Cossacks** and **Kazakh** steppe households.
- **Families of those labeled kulaks**, and how they tell the loss.
- **Central Asian traders and migrant workers**, sending wealth home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history and Ural industry; rich statistics; mixed for folklore on riches outside Tatar and Bashkir; thin for Komi-Permyak and Moksha.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (Arabic script for merchant-era press such as *Вакыт*); Bashkir; Chuvash; Udmurt; Mari; Komi-Permyak; Arabic for travel accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the fortunes of present-day regional elites and the privatization of oil companies are politically sensitive; 1990s organized crime touches living people; dekulakization is family trauma. Taxation law is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
