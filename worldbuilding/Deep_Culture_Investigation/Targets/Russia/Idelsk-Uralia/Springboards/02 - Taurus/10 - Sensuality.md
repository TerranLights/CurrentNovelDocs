## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatars and Bashkirs** who abstain from alcohol, against those who do not; tea culture across both.
- **Kryashens**, whose feast foods and drinks may differ from Muslim neighbors'.
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya and Moksha** villages, with home brews tied to feasts and rites.
- **Bashkir and Kazakh** kumis-drinking pastoral areas.
- **Russian Ural cities**, banya and dacha culture.
- **Old Believers**, who may hold to stricter rules on drink, tobacco and food.
- **Komi-Permyak** forest villages, bathhouse and berry culture.
- **Urban young people** of Kazan, Yekaterinburg and Perm with café and club scenes.
