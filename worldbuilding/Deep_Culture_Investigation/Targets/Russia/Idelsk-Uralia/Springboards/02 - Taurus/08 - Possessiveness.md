# Idelsk-Uralia: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Udmurt; Mari; Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatar and Bashkir families**, and **Kryashens**, on guarding daughters and honor; city and village apart.
- **Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt** villages, each with its own wedding and lament traditions.
- **Erzya and Moksha** families, separately.
- **Russian urban couples** of Yekaterinburg, Perm and Samara, across generations.
- **Old Believer communities**, guarding their faith and its objects from outsiders.
- **Komi-Permyak and Ural forest hunters**, guarding territories.
- **Young men's groups** (fan crews, district gangs), guarding turf.
- **Central Asian migrant families**, with their own norms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; survey work on jealousy and control is limited and politically sensitive; strong folklore record; thin for current Finno-Ugric family life.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya, Moksha; Komi-Permyak.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and control are underreported; some support NGOs face legal pressure; bride abduction must not be presented as a living norm without evidence. Domestic-violence law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
