*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural pensioners** in Mari, Udmurt, Chuvash and Mordvin villages, against **urban pensioners**.
- **Workers of single-industry towns** (Magnitogorsk, Nizhny Tagil, Naberezhnye Chelny, Tolyatti), and **oil-town employees** with company welfare.
- **Techa river and East Ural communities**, including Tatar and Bashkir villages.
- **Kazakh households** of the Orenburg steppe and **Orenburg Cossack** villages.
- **Old Believer communities**, known for self-reliance to check.
- **Central Asian migrant workers**, whose security runs through remittances and the household back home.
- **Kazan and Ufa youth** across generations of street memory.
- **Bashkir pastoral villages** of the Trans-Urals, exposed to drought.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and the 1990s; rich archival material on famines; mixed for village risk-sharing customs; thin in English for Finno-Ugric communities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Udmurt; Mari; Erzya; English for relief archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mayak and the Techa villages involve health harm and contested compensation; the famine of 1921-22 and later Soviet hunger are sensitive; organized crime of the 1990s touches living people. National security and policing are outside this concept.
- **Held for review:** none.
