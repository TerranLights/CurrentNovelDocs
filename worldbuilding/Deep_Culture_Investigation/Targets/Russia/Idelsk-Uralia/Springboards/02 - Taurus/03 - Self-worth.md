# Idelsk-Uralia: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt; Erzya; Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men of the single-industry Ural and Kama towns** after layoffs, against **professionals of Kazan, Ufa and Yekaterinburg**.
- **Village elders** whose worth rests on age and respect, against the urban young.
- **Udmurt, Mari, Erzya and Moksha speakers**, for whom language and worth are closely tied; separate village and city.
- **Kryashens and Nagaibaks**, whose recognition as distinct groups is itself a question of standing (sensitive).
- **Komi-Permyaks**, between stigma and reclaimed pride.
- **Bashkirs and Tatars**, each on its own terms, not set against each other.
- **Women**, whose standing may rest on household, work or faith differently by community.
- **Central Asian labor migrants**, whose worth as workers and as people is argued over in the press.
- **Veterans** of Afghanistan, Chechnya and later wars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; psychology studies exist in Russian but tend to use student samples; rich literary and Soviet-labor sources; thin for Finno-Ugric minorities outside activist writing.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (Arabic script for Muhammadyar and Jadid texts); Bashkir; Chuvash; Udmurt; Mari; Erzya; Komi-Permyak.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data and Razin's death need care; ethnic recognition disputes and language policy are charged; avoid ranking peoples' pride against each other.
- **Held for review:** none.
