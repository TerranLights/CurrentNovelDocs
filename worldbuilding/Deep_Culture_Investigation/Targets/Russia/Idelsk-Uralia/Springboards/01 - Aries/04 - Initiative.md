## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar merchant lineages** and their descendants against **Tatar villagers** with a work-bee tradition.
- **Bashkirs** of the pastoral south and east; **Teptyar** farming villages.
- **Chuvash** seasonal migrant workers (отходники) and village craftsmen.
- **Udmurt, Mari, Erzya, Moksha and Komi-Permyak** villages, where communal help words survive in different forms.
- **Old Believer** merchant and craft families of the Urals.
- **Factory-town Russians** (Uralmash, Chelyabinsk, Magnitogorsk) against **Kazan and Yekaterinburg** founders.
- **Women entrepreneurs** in markets and trades; **Central Asian** migrants running stalls and small firms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business statistics and merchant history; mixed for village reciprocity; thin for what "acting unasked" means at home and at work by ethnic group.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar in Arabic script for merchant and jadid-era papers; Chuvash, Udmurt, Mari dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** civic initiative outside approved channels can carry risk; volunteer and NGO data reflect official preferences. Rules on registering firms or NGOs go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
