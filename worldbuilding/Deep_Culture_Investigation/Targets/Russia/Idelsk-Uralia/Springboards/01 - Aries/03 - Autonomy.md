# Idelsk-Uralia: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha, Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled, for persons and for peoples and communities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Tatar and Bashkir households** with strong elder authority against **Kazan and Ufa** young professionals.
- **Kryashen and Orthodox Chuvash** against **Muslim** neighbors on marriage choice.
- **Mari and Udmurt villages**, including the eastern Mari of Bashkortostan, where old household forms lasted.
- **Erzya and Moksha** separately; **Komi-Permyaks**.
- **Monotown workers** (steel, auto, defense) against **Yekaterinburg** service and tech workers.
- **Old Believer** communities, priestless and priested.
- **Orenburg Kazakhs** and Cossack descendants.
- **Migrant workers** from Central Asia, whose choices are framed by employers and kin abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good survey panels at national level; rich histories of collective self-rule; thin data on household decisions split by ethnic group.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir, including the 1917-20 press in Arabic script; Turkish for Validi's later writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** republican sovereignty and the 1917-20 autonomies touch current politics; bride abduction and forced marriage need care with informants. Any law on autonomy or self-government goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
