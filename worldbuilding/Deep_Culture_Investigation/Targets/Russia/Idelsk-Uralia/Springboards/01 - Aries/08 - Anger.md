## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatars and Bashkirs**, whose moral teaching on anger draws on Islamic ethics, against **Orthodox** neighbors.
- **Mari, Udmurt and Chuvash** communities where older spirit-based channels for grievance were recorded.
- **Erzya and Moksha**; **Komi-Permyaks**.
- **Kazan and Naberezhnye Chelny youth** of the gang era against today's young people.
- **Bashkir rural districts** active in land and environmental protests.
- **Ural industrial towns** with high recorded violence against university cities.
- **Men and women**, and how each is allowed to show anger.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good violence and mortality statistics; good linguistic work on Russian; thin for emotion norms in the Finno-Ugric languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir (Arabic-script religious texts); Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya-Moksha.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest participants face prosecution; some domestic-violence NGOs carry restrictive legal labels; gang history touches living people. Never identify informants. Assault and violence law goes to Libra; revenge to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
