# Idelsk-Uralia: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Udmurt, Mari, Erzya, Moksha, Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others; self-reliance and self-sufficiency. (Collective self-rule of peoples sits mostly under Autonomy.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bashkir pastoral and forest communities** (Burzyan, the trans-Urals) against **Ufa** salaried households.
- **Tatar villages** with vakhta earners against **Kazan** dual-earner households.
- **Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt villages** with heavy household-plot reliance; **eastern Mari** of Bashkortostan.
- **Erzya and Moksha** rural districts; **Komi-Permyak** forest settlements.
- **Ural monotown pensioners**, whose income rests on state transfers, against **Ural prospecting** and small-workshop families.
- **Orenburg steppe** farmers, Kazakh and Russian.
- **Disabled people**, elders living alone, migrant laborers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household-plot and living-condition statistics; mixed for ethnography; thin for attitudes to asking for help, split by people.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir for village studies; the Finno-Ugric languages for proverbs and local terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** poverty, dependence on transfers and disability carry stigma; interview with care. Benefit rules and dependent status in tax law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
