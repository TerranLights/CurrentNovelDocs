## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatars and Bashkirs** (Bolgar, Nauruz) against **Kryashen** and **Orthodox Chuvash** (church calendar).
- **Followers of the old Mari, Udmurt, Chuvash and Erzya faiths**, whose year begins differently.
- **Bashkir trans-Ural communities** where the Ural-batyr story is local landscape.
- **Ural factory-city Russians**, whose city births are works dates.
- **Komi-Permyaks** of the forest north.
- **Descendants of deportees and special settlers** in the Urals, whose family histories begin with a forced move.
- **Newcomers**: Central Asian migrants, Russians from the north and east.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for founding stories and calendar rites; mixed for birth customs; thin for personal starting-over by group.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir epic language; Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt and Erzya-Moksha for folklore; German and Finnish for older collections.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** founding dates and conversion history are contested between peoples; deportation memory is politically sensitive and some archives and museums have closed or changed. Some new-year rites of the old faiths may be closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
