# Idelsk-Uralia: Recklessness (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha, Komi-Permyak. **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mortality, traffic and rescue statistics; mixed for the meanings people give to risk; thin for most minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir for sermons and folklore; Udmurt, Mari and Chuvash for spirit tales; English for the epidemiology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol and drug data invite stereotypes about particular peoples; report variation, not character. Drug policy and fraud prosecution touch law, which goes to Libra and Capricorn. The Dyatlov case draws conspiracy content; use primary records.
- **Held for review:** none.
