## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Village wrestling cultures**: Tatar, Bashkir and Chuvash festival wrestling against Mari and Udmurt contests at their own festivals.
- **Kazan, Ufa, Yekaterinburg, Chelyabinsk**: the main urban rivalries.
- **Monotown industrial workers**, raised in plant-ranking culture, against private-sector workers.
- **Elite-school families** in the capitals against **rural schools**.
- **Erzya and Moksha**; **Komi-Permyaks**; **Kryashens** and **Nagaibaks** at their own festivals.
- **Orenburg steppe** Kazakhs and Cossacks with horse contests.
- **Girls and boys**: who is pushed into which contests.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport and exam statistics; mixed for factory history; thin for envy and rivalry norms by people.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; German for Fuchs's circle; the Finno-Ugric languages for games and folk medicine.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exam-integrity data and regional rivalry can be politically touchy; evil-eye practice may be private. Competition and antitrust law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
