# Idelsk-Uralia: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Erzya, Moksha, Udmurt, Komi-Permyak; Kazakh on the Orenburg edge. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazan Tatars and Mishar** against **Kryashen** villages: different conduct literatures (adab against Orthodox catechism).
- **Bashkirs** of the trans-Ural steppe, where verbal duels and horse culture persist, against urban Ufa.
- **Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt villages**, where shame words do a lot of social work, against their own urban, Russian-speaking generations.
- **Erzya and Moksha**, kept apart.
- **Ural industrial Russians** (Yekaterinburg, Chelyabinsk, Magnitogorsk, Nizhny Tagil) against **Volga river-town Russians**; **Orenburg and Ural Cossack** descendants.
- **Old Believer** families; **Kazakhs** of Orenburg (aitys tradition).
- **Women and men**, and **elders and the young**, in each group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich archive of petitions and Soviet letters; thin direct measurement of assertiveness by ethnic group.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir (Arabic script for pre-1927 conduct books); the Finno-Ugric languages for proverbs and village talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** public protest, labor action and ethnic activism are policed subjects; protect anyone interviewed. Legal channels of complaint belong to Libra; here only the custom of complaining.
- **Held for review:** none.
