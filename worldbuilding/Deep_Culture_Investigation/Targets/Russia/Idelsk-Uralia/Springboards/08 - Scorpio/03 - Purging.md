*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatars and Bashkirs**: ritual purity, the mosque and the village healer.
- **Kryashens**: Orthodox rites over a Turkic folk base.
- **Udmurts, Mari and Chuvash** with and without baptism: bathhouse and healer traditions.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha** apart.
- **Old Believers** of the Urals and the Kama: the strictest purity boundaries in the region.
- **Russian factory towns** living with pollution (Karabash, the Techa villages, Chapaevsk).
- **Communities hit hardest by Soviet purges**: Tatar and Bashkir clergy, Finno-Ugric teachers and writers, deported kulak families.
- **Officials and business elites** in each republic.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet purges and pollution; mixed on folk cleansing; thin on present-day purges in the press because of censorship.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir (Arabic script for madrasa texts); Chuvash, Udmurt, Mari, Erzya.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** repression archives are partly closed again; Memorial was liquidated and its sites may be blocked; healers' methods may be private; corruption cases are political.
- **Held for review:** none.
