# Idelsk-Uralia: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar and Bashkir Muslims**: Sufi discretion; the sin of backbiting; private and public piety.
- **Kryashens**: a community that kept its own ways under pressure from two sides.
- **Mari** and **Udmurt** old-faith congregations: restricted rites.
- **Chuvash** villages with and without baptism.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha**; **Komi-Permyaks**.
- **Old Believers** of the Urals and Kama.
- **Closed-city residents** (Ozyorsk, Snezhinsk, Lesnoy, Novouralsk) and the Techa villages.
- **Families of the repressed**, who kept silent about relatives.
- **LGBT people** in the large cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on state secrecy and religious history; mixed on family secrets; thin on present-day privacy views by region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir (Arabic script for manuscripts); German for Kemper; Mari, Udmurt, Chuvash, Erzya.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grove and kuala rites are restricted by their keepers and are not to be retold; closed cities need permits; some Muslim groups and LGBT "propaganda" are banned in Russia, so informants may face risk; nuclear and biological files remain classified.
- **Held for review:** none.
