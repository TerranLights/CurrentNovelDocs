## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatar** and **Chuvash** villages of the middle Volga: famine memory and rain rites.
- **Bashkir** steppe and mountain districts: drought, herds and the 1921 famine.
- **Udmurts**, **Mari** and **Komi-Permyaks**: language loss and rural decline felt as crisis.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha** apart.
- **Russian** factory cities (Chelyabinsk, Magnitogorsk, Nizhny Tagil, Togliatti): war mobilization and the 1990s collapse.
- **Kazan** and **Yekaterinburg** youth.
- **People with HIV** and drug users in the Ural cities.
- **Families of disaster victims**.
- **Orenburg** border districts: drought and steppe fires.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on famines, war mobilization and recent disasters; mixed on folk responses; thin on personal crises by region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Chuvash, Udmurt, Mari, Erzya; English for relief archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the war in Ukraine and the 2022 mobilization are restricted topics; school shootings (Kazan, 2021) need care; HIV and suicide carry stigma; Razin's death is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
