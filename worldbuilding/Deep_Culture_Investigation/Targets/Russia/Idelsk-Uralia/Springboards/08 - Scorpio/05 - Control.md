*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatar and Bashkir families**, urban and rural: fate, family elders, men's and women's spheres.
- **Russian Orthodox and Old Believer households** in the Urals.
- **Unbaptized Chuvash and Mari** villages: fate powers and household ritual.
- **Udmurts** and **Komi-Permyaks**.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha** apart.
- **Factory and single-industry towns** (Magnitogorsk, Nizhny Tagil, Togliatti, Naberezhnye Chelny): control by the plant.
- **Closed nuclear cities**: control by the state.
- **Villagers resettled by reservoirs**.
- **Labor migrants** from Central Asia in the cities, under heavy control.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on industrial and state control in history; mixed on family control; thin on coercive control in couples by region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir (Arabic script for older Sufi texts); Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance is a political subject; closed cities limit outsider access; family violence is under-reported and NGOs working on it face pressure; some regional values work may be paywalled.
- **Held for review:** none.
