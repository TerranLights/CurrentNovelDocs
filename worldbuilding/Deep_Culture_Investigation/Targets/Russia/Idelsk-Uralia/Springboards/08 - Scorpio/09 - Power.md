# Idelsk-Uralia: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how the culture judges who holds it and how it is used.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars** in Kazan, in the oil towns of the southeast, and in the countryside.
- **Bashkirs**: lineage elders, republic elites and protest movements.
- **Russians** in the republics against those in the Ural oblasts.
- **Chuvash** and **Mari**: republic elites against village elders and old-faith priests (*kart*).
- **Udmurts**: the defense-industry city of Izhevsk against villages.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha**: unequal weight inside Mordovia.
- **Komi-Permyaks**: a former autonomous district merged into Perm krai.
- **Factory and oil company towns**; **Cossack** communities of Orenburg.
- **Muslim clergy** of rival muftiates.

## 4. Flags
