# Idelsk-Uralia: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars** in Tatarstan against Tatars elsewhere in the belt.
- **Bashkirs** of the southeast against the northwest: trust in kin, clan and republic leaders.
- **Russians** in the republics against Russians in Russian-majority oblasts.
- **Chuvash**, **Mari**, **Udmurts**: village trust and trust in outsiders.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha** apart.
- **Kryashens**, **Nagaibaks**.
- **Old Believers**: trust kept inside the faith.
- **Muslim congregations** loyal to different muftiates.
- **Central Asian and Caucasian migrants** in Kazan, Yekaterinburg and Samara.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on survey trust at national and federal-district level; mixed at republic level; thin for Finno-Ugric villages outside ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** election analysts and observer groups are labeled or harassed; interethnic surveys are politically watched. Trust in police and courts is the spirit of the law and belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
