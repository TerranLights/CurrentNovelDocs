## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bashkirs**: the uprising tradition and Salawat as an avenger.
- **Tatars**: 1552 memory, and the Muslim teaching on forgiveness.
- **Kryashens** and **Nagaibaks**: Orthodox forgiveness rites in Turkic villages.
- **Chuvash**: literature of revenge by a wronged woman.
- **Mari** and **Udmurts**: curse rites and grove justice.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha** apart.
- **Russian factory workers** of Izhevsk, Votkinsk and the Urals: civil war reprisals.
- **Ural and Orenburg Cossacks**.
- **Kazan, Naberezhnye Chelny and Yekaterinburg youth and gang milieus**.
- **Families of victims** of 1990s violence.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on historical risings and reprisals; mixed on folklore of curses; thin on everyday revenge apart from forums and crime reporting.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grievance over 1552 and the Bashkir risings is political; curse rites may be private; gang histories involve living people. Private vengeance set against the state's punishment is the spirit of the law and belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
