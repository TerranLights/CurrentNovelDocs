*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazan Tatars** against **Kryashens** and **Mishars**: who counts as having left the people.
- **Bashkirs**: land-rights memory and old grievances against Russian settlers and the state.
- **Mari** and **Chuvash**: conquest-era alliances remembered as loyalty or treachery.
- **Erzya** and **Moksha**: their own medieval split.
- **Udmurts** and **Komi-Permyaks**.
- **Ural Cossacks** and **Old Believers**: loyalty to the tsar against loyalty to the old faith.
- **Kazan and Naberezhnye Chelny youth** of the 1980s and 1990s.
- **Families of informers and of their victims** in the factory cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on historical treason and Soviet informing; mixed on infidelity; thin on present-day personal betrayal outside forums and TV.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Mari, Chuvash, Erzya, Moksha, Udmurt.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** WWII collaboration is a legal and political minefield; security-service archives are mostly closed; the Kryashen question is a live ethnic quarrel; polygyny is a sensitive topic for Muslim families.
- **Held for review:** none.
