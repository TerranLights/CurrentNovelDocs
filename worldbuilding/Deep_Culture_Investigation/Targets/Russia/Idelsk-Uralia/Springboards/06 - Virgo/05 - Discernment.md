## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Tatars and Bashkirs under Sufi or mosque guidance; Orthodox Russians and Kryashens under confessors; traditional-faith Mari and Udmurts:** three settings for spiritual discernment.
- **Merchant families (Tatar, Russian Old Believer) against farm villages:** commercial shrewdness against rural trust.
- **Ural stone and metal craftsmen:** a trained eye.
- **Bashkir and Kazakh herders:** judging animals and pasture.
- **Erzya and Moksha villagers; Chuvash upper and lower groups:** a thinner record to check.
- **Older people targeted by phone scams; young city dwellers online.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich folklore and Sufi history; thin present-day regional data on scams and media judgment.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (Old Tatar and Arabic script for Kul Gali and Sufi texts); Bashkir; Chuvash; Mari and Udmurt.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic attitude research is politically delicate; Sufi and Islamic-revival topics draw state attention; trust-in-media polling is constrained.
- **Held for review:** none.
