## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ural craft towns (Kasli, Zlatoust, Nizhny Tagil, Kungur):** inherited craft standards.
- **Old Believers against mainstream Orthodox:** exactness of rite.
- **Muslim reciters and madrasa students:** exactness of sacred text.
- **Tatar, Chuvash, Mari, Erzya and Moksha women embroiderers:** separate pattern traditions.
- **Defense and car plants (Izhevsk, Tolyatti, Naberezhnye Chelny):** industrial quality regimes.
- **Arts schools and elite lyceums in Kazan, Ufa, Perm and Yekaterinburg:** performance pressure on the young.
- **Bashkir and Udmurt villages:** a thinner record to check.
