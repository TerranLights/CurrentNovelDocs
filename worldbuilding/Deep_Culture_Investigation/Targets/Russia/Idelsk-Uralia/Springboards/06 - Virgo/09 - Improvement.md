## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jadid-heir Tatar and Bashkir urban families against conservative rural Muslims.**
- **Kryashens, Chuvash, Mari and Udmurts:** targets of missionary and Soviet "improvement", and their own enlighteners.
- **Bashkir pastoral districts:** land "improved" away from them.
- **Villages moved for reservoirs on the Volga and Kama.**
- **Oil and car towns:** progress built in one generation.
- **Russians of old Ural factory towns:** decline and renewal after 1991.
- **Kazakhs of the Orenburg steppe; Erzya and Moksha villages:** thinner records to check.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Jadid, missionary and Soviet history; mixed for present self-improvement; thin for regional mobility statistics.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar (Arabic script before 1928); Bashkir; Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt for enlighteners' writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** imposed betterment (missions, collectivization, repressions of the 1930s) must be told in the affected peoples' voices; urban-demolition disputes; land history is politically live.
- **Held for review:** none.
