## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **University and science cities (Kazan, Yekaterinburg, Ufa, Perm) against villages.**
- **Closed cities of the Urals (Snezhinsk, Ozyorsk, Novouralsk):** engineering cultures under secrecy.
- **Muslim scholars (Tatar and Bashkir) against secular schooling:** reason inside and outside religion.
- **Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt communities that keep their own diviners and healers.**
- **Old Believers:** textual scrutiny of books as a religious practice.
- **Oil and car industries' engineers (Almetyevsk, Tolyatti, Naberezhnye Chelny).**
- **Kazakhs and Bashkirs of the steppe:** herders' reckoning of animals and pasture.
