*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars and Bashkirs, Muslim and Kryashen:** folk cure, prayer and the mosque.
- **Chuvash, Mari and Udmurt traditional healers' clients:** sacred groves and healers still consulted.
- **Erzya and Moksha:** health in greeting and custom; a thinner record.
- **Russians of Ural industrial towns:** pollution, alcohol and work injury.
- **Villages on the Techa River:** Tatar and Bashkir communities under radiation.
- **Bashkir and Kazakh herders:** kumys and steppe foods.
- **Old Believers:** their rules on food, drink and doctors.
- **Large cities (Kazan, Yekaterinburg, Ufa):** HIV, drug use and modern wellness.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and radiation research; mixed for folk medicine (many Russian-language ethnographies); thin for present healer practice.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir (Arabic script for old remedy books); Chuvash; Mari; Udmurt.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' and sacred-grove knowledge to be shared only as communities choose; radiation victims' records; HIV and addiction; closed-city data. Medical-aid-in-dying and drug law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
