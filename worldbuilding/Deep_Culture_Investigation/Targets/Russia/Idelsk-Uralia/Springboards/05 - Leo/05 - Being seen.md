# Idelsk-Uralia: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Idelsk-Uralia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Tatar; Bashkir; Chuvash; Udmurt; Mari. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars** (Kazan, Mishar, Kryashen) and **Bashkirs:** evil-eye and modesty customs; women's public visibility in religious and secular families.
- **Udmurts, Besermyan, Mari** (meadow, hill, eastern), **Erzya, Moksha, Komi-Permyaks:** small peoples' visibility in media and census; village dying-off.
- **Chuvash:** visibility as a large but quiet people; Christian and unbaptized villages.
- **Russians** of monotowns and dying villages against Yekaterinburg, Perm and Samara residents.
- **Elders living alone; migrants from Central Asia** working in the cities: two groups often unseen.
- **Young people online** against their parents' "don't stick out" upbringing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Evil-eye and wedding ethnography is rich; media-representation data for small peoples is thin and must be built from program archives; isolation data exists only at regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Chuvash, Udmurt, Mari, Erzya for local media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance and protest visibility carry risk; folk-healing practitioners may not wish to be named; migrants' legal status must never be recorded. Rules on data and cameras go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
