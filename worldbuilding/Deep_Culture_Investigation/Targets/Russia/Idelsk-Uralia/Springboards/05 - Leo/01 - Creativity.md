## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ural factory towns** (Zlatoust, Kasli, Nizhny Tagil, Kungur stonecutters): craft dynasties shaped by the plant.
- **Russian Old Believers** of the Urals and Kama: copying, icon painting, a strong ethic of fidelity to models.
- **Kazan Tatars, Mishar and Kryashen:** ornament, leather mosaic, gold embroidery; Kryashen dress differs.
- **Bashkirs:** felt, leather, wood and kurai-making; mountain and steppe districts.
- **Chuvash** (upper and lower), **Mari** (meadow, hill, eastern), **Udmurts** (northern, southern, Besermyan): embroidery and weaving traditions compared.
- **Erzya and Moksha;** **Komi-Permyaks** (Kudymkar woodwork).
- **Orenburg region:** down-shawl knitters as a women's craft community; Orenburg Kazakhs.
- **Big cities** (Yekaterinburg, Perm, Kazan, Ufa, Samara): art schools, rock and theater scenes, IT startups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Ural crafts and Russian-language art history; good for Tatar and Chuvash ornament; thin and mostly Soviet-era for Udmurt, Mari, Mordvin and Komi-Permyak creativity.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar, Bashkir and Chuvash for primary sources; Udmurt, Mari, Erzya and Moksha dictionaries for terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1930s repression of national writers and artists; some contemporary art projects drew political pressure; sacred carvings and traditional-religion objects may carry restrictions set by the communities. Copyright and craft law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
