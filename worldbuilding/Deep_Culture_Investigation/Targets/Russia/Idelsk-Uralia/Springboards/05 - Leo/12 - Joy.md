## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars** (Kazan, Mishar, Kryashen): Muslim and Kryashen festive calendars; accordion culture.
- **Bashkirs:** spring and summer gatherings; kurai and dance.
- **Chuvash, Mari, Udmurts, Erzya, Moksha:** winter mumming, round dances, traditional-religion feasts.
- **Russians:** Orthodox and Old Believer views of merriment; Ural city comedy and music scenes; Orenburg Cossack songs.
- **Muslim, Orthodox and secular** households: joy in worship against joy in leisure.
- **Generations:** Soviet holiday culture against today's festivals and online joy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and music; mixed for measured happiness, which is national or by federal district; everyday joy needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Bashkir; Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya for festival names and songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** traditional-religion feasts may be closed; whether to celebrate in wartime is contested; alcohol at festivals is a delicate topic in Muslim and sober communities. Holiday law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
