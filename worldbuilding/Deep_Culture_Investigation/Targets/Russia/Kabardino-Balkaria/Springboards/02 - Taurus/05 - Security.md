# Kabardino-Balkaria: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Balkar families of the upper gorges: mudflow and avalanche risk on top of the memory of deportation.
- Kabardian plain villages: a security built on household plots, orchards and kin networks.
- Nalchik wage earners and pensioners: security bought through salaries, savings and state pensions.
- Young men who work away (Moscow, the north): remittances as household insurance.
- Russian and Cossack towns of the north: industrial job loss after 1991.
- Practicing Muslim families who felt watched after 2005, against those who did not: security from the state, or from it.
- Meskhetian Turk families, refugees from Fergana in 1989: insecurity of status and papers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for finance and disaster statistics; mixed for lived fear, which needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for nearly all data and reports; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for charms, rites and testimony; Kazakh or Kyrgyz archive guides for the special-settler records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2005 attack, detentions and counterterrorism are dangerous to discuss for local people; do not name private persons. Deportation memory is painful. Crime and policing as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
