## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Balkar villages of the Elbrus and Chereksky districts: pasture rights against neighboring Kabardian villages and against resort plans.
- Kabardian villages of the Zolsky and Baksan districts: the plains side of the same pasture quarrels, often over the same slopes.
- Families with princely or noble descent against former peasant families: whose land was taken in 1918-1930 and who later asked for it back.
- Cossack stanitsas of the Prokhladny and Maysky districts: land granted for service under the empire.
- Nalchik flat owners against village plot holders: owning a flat bought after privatization against owning the family yard.
- Ossetian villages and the Mountain Jews of Nalchik: small communities whose property was held under different rules.
- Women as heirs: whether daughters and widows hold land in their own names across all these groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and for the current dispute; thin for how families divide land among heirs today.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for archives and land papers; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for village statements, elders and the press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the land question between Kabardian and Balkar villages has led to protests and is politically charged; quote each side as itself. Land law, contracts and court rulings go to Libra; what law is for goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
