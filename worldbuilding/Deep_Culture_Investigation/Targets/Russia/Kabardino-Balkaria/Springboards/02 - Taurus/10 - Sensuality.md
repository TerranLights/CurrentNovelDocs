## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kabardian plain villages: the dance gathering, millet beer and the feast table.
- Balkar gorges: dairy foods, mountain springs and felt, with seasonal pleasures tied to the high pastures.
- Devout and secular families: music, dance and drink allowed in one house and limited in the next.
- Nalchik's young people: cafés, clubs and online food culture against village feasts.
- Russians and Cossacks of the north: Orthodox feast foods and drink customs.
- Ossetian and Mountain Jewish families: separate festive foods and music.
- Spa visitors from other Russian regions: an outside clientele that shapes the resort's offer.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dance, food and resorts; thin for private comfort and touch, which needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for songs, food words and poetry; Russian for resort and festival material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol and music at weddings divide religious opinion. Sexual pleasure belongs to Scorpio. Russian Romantic texts on Circassians exoticize; read them as outsiders' views.
- **Held for review:** none.
