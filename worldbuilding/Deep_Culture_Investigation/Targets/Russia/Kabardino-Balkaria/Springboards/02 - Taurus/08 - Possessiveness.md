# Kabardino-Balkaria: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Ossetian for the Ossetian minority; Turkish for diaspora sources. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kabardian and Balkar families in villages against those in Nalchik: how strongly a husband's family claims a bride's time and work.
- Younger women with degrees and jobs: the point where a family's claim meets a woman's own plans.
- Religious and secular households: whether jealousy is framed by faith, by custom or by law.
- Russian and Cossack families of the north: different courtship rules and different talk of jealousy.
- Hunters and shepherds of the Balkar gorges: guarding of hunting grounds and flocks.
- Circassian diaspora and repatriates: guarding a people's marriages against outsiders.
- Meskhetian Turk and Mountain Jewish families: small communities that may guard marriage choices closely, to be tested not assumed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Domestic control is under-recorded in Russia; folklore and ethnography carry most of the trail.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for songs and online talk; Turkish for KAFFED.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bride abduction and domestic violence involve real victims; never name private persons. Divorce and custody law go to Libra; family authority as the spirit of law goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
