# Kabardino-Balkaria: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Young Kabardian and Balkar men in Nalchik: worth through sport, cars and work in Moscow, against worth through conduct at home.
- Women of the two main peoples: modesty and family standing against education and careers; separate the village from the city.
- Elders who lived through deportation and return: worth framed by survival and by a people's restored name.
- Families claiming princely, noble or uzden descent against those without: whether birth still counts.
- Russians and Cossacks of the northern districts: standing tied to Cossack revival or to Soviet work honors.
- Circassian repatriates from Syria: worth rebuilt in a homeland where they arrive as newcomers.
- Meskhetian Turks and Mountain Jews: small communities whose standing is measured both inside the republic and against kin abroad (Turkey, the U.S., Israel).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in ethics, epic and poetry; thin in measured self-esteem by group; the RLMS sample may not cover the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for songs, codes and the epic; Russian for psychology and statistics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data and family shame are private; deportation memory is a living wound. Some praise and shaming songs name real families.
- **Held for review:** none.
