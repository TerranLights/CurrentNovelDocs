# Kabardino-Balkaria: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Practicing Muslims, observers of the old code, and secular Soviet-trained families: three rankings that may live in one household.
- Kabardians against Balkars: two codes of custom with shared and separate items.
- The generation raised before 1991 against those raised after the religious revival.
- Nalchik professionals against village farmers and shepherds.
- Orthodox Russians and Cossacks of the north, and the Ossetian minority: Christian rankings beside Muslim ones.
- Mountain Jews, Meskhetian Turks and Koreans of the republic *check*: small communities with their own lists of what comes first.
- Diaspora Circassians in Turkey and Jordan, and repatriates in Nalchik: the code kept abroad against the code at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Codes, proverbs and sermons are rich; surveys with KBR-level samples are scarce.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar and Russian; Arabic for some religious texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the quarrel between Islam and the old-code revival, and between official and unofficial Islam, is political; security services have treated some believers as suspects. Name positions, not people, unless they speak publicly. Law and custom as rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
