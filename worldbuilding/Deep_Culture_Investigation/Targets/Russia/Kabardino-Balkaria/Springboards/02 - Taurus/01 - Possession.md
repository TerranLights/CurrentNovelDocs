# Kabardino-Balkaria: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; also Ossetian, Judeo-Tat and Meskhetian Turkish for the smaller communities. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Balkar families with deportation memory: whether objects that survived exile and return are kept apart from ordinary goods.
- Kabardian households with princely or noble lineage claims: whether old weapons and saddles are kept as proof of descent.
- Terek Cossack families of Ekaterinogradskaya and Kotlyarevskaya *check*: icons, uniforms and Cossack arms as heirlooms of a different kind.
- Mountain Jews of Nalchik: household goods tied to the synagogue and to emigration to Israel.
- Meskhetian Turks who came after 1989: what a family brought from Uzbekistan.
- Young urban Nalchik against village elders: phones, cars and branded goods against chests and old arms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Museum, grammar and folklore sources should be rich; household-level studies of what people keep today are likely thin.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for grammar, tales and testimony; Russian for archives; Ottoman Turkish for refugee records of the 1860s.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation and exodus testimony is painful family memory; ask, do not extract. Weapons held at home touch police and legal questions; leave those to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the possession chapters in Colarusso (1992) and the 1976 Balkar grammar, side by side.
2. Ask the National Museum of KBR for accession lists of arms and silver ornaments given by families.
3. Search library catalogs for «Бугай депортация балкарцев документы».
4. Search the KBIGI folklore holdings for «хьэпшып», «тыгъэ» and «аманат».
