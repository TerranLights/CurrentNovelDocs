## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men with cars in Nalchik and the larger towns:** driving is the most visible arena where daring and danger may meet.
- **Mountain guides and Balkar residents of the Elbrus district against visiting climbers:** locals may judge risk on the peak differently from guests.
- **Wedding hosts and guests in Kabardian and Balkar villages:** gunfire and motorcades at a celebration, and who stops them.
- **Families of devout Muslims against secular families:** drink, betting and fate may be judged by different rules.
- **Older generations against the young:** whether youthful risk is forgiven as a phase.
- **Russian and Cossack towns of the north:** a different drink and driving culture to compare.
- **Repatriates from Syria:** risk may be weighed after war in another way.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; road, mortality and rescue data good; drug and gambling data patchy; ethnography of drink fair.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for folk tales and drink customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and arrests; the stereotype of the "wild" mountaineer, which must not shape findings; victims of accidents and their families. Bans on gunfire and driving rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
