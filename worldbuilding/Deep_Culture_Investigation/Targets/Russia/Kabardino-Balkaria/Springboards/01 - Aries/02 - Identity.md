## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardians who call themselves "адыгэ" first against those who lead with "Kabardian":** the pan-Circassian and the republic label may divide by age and politics.
- **Balkars of each gorge:** test whether gorge and house come before the name "Balkar".
- **Balkars and the neighboring Karachays:** the shared language and the separate republics give two candidate identities.
- **Terek Cossacks against other Russians:** an estate identity revived inside a wider Russian one.
- **Returnees from Syria, Turkey, Jordan and Kosovo *check*:** a homeland identity built abroad meets the actual homeland.
- **Mixed Kabardian-Balkar, Kabardian-Russian families:** how children choose a label.
- **Muslims, Orthodox Christians and Khabze followers:** religion as a rival anchor for "who we are".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; identity is a crowded topic in republic scholarship and the press, but much of it argues a side.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Turkish, Arabic and English for diaspora work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** origin claims (Alan heritage among them) are contested between peoples; genocide recognition; land and border disputes between Kabardian and Balkar villages; federal policy on native-language teaching. Keep pointers to every side.
- **Held for review:** none.
