## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian horse-breeding families and villages near Malka:** racing and breeding may carry lineage rivalry.
- **Balkar villages of the Elbrus and Chegem districts:** mountain skills and old games may be the arena.
- **Young men in wrestling and combat-sport schools across both peoples:** the main modern contest for status.
- **Girls and women:** check which contests (school, music, dance, sport) are open to them.
- **School pupils in Nalchik and in villages:** exam and olympiad competition, and fairness disputes.
- **Russian towns of the north:** team sports and school rivalry as a contrast.
- **Families staging weddings:** spending as rivalry, with possible pushback from religious and civic voices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich; sport and exam data good; folk games and envy beliefs well covered in older ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for game names and sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exam-fraud claims; athletes linked to violence or politics; interethnic rivalry at games. Keep to published coverage.
- **Held for review:** none.
