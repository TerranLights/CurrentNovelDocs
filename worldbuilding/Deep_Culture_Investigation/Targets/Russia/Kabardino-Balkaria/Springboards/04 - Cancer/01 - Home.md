# Kabardino-Balkaria: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Ossetian locally. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Balkars of the upper gorges** (Upper Balkaria in Chereksky district, Upper Chegem, Bezengi): stone houses and towers left after 1944, against the houses built on return.
- **Balkars in foothill and plains villages** and in Nalchik: home as a place rebuilt away from the ancestral gorge.
- **Herding families** with summer pasture huts: home split across seasons.
- **Kabardian villages** of the Baksan, Chegem and Urvan valleys (courtyard homesteads to examine), against **Terek-plain villages** with a different settlement history.
- **Nalchik flat dwellers**, Kabardian, Balkar and Russian alike: home without a yard.
- **Terek Cossack stanitsas** and Russian towns (Prokhladny, Maysky): a separate house tradition.
- **Ossetian villages** (*check* locations) and **Circassian repatriates** from Syria, rehoused in the 2010s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: housing statistics and material culture are well documented in Russian; the feeling of home is mostly in poetry, song and press, in the native languages.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Ottoman Turkish or German for the early travelers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation and expulsion memory; contested village lands and pastures; some news sources may be restricted inside Russia. Property and inheritance law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
