# Kabardino-Balkaria: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian villages**: lodging of guests and travelers in the homestead, and how it changed with hotels and guest houses.
- **Balkar families of the gorges**: shelter on the trail and at pastures, and the memory of being sheltered, or not, in exile.
- **Elbrus and Bezengi communities**: shelter as a trade (huts, guest houses) beside shelter as duty.
- **Nalchik**: the city's homeless, its institutions, and newcomers renting corners of flats.
- **Russian and Cossack towns**: Orthodox charity and Soviet-style social institutions.
- **Families who hosted war refugees** (from Abkhazia, South Ossetia, Ukraine) and the refugees themselves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin on homelessness and crisis shelters; richer on hospitality customs (ethnography) and on deportation memory (memoirs); refugee records scattered in the press.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Kazakh or Kyrgyz archives for the exile years.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation memory; refugee and evacuee topics tied to present wars; NGOs that may be designated or closed; domestic violence. Asylum and migration law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
