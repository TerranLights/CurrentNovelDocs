# Kabardino-Balkaria: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian mothers living with the husband's family**: the young mother's place beside her husband's mother.
- **Balkar mothers** of the generation born in exile, and their daughters: motherhood marked by deportation memory.
- **Mothers in Nalchik** with paid careers, against village mothers.
- **Russian and Cossack mothers** of the plains towns: Orthodox and Soviet ideals of the mother.
- **Mothers of many children**, often with state honors, against mothers of one or two.
- **Mothers of sons in the army or in security cases**: motherhood as a public voice.
- **Repatriate mothers** from Syria: raising children in a new homeland and language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: demographic data rich; mothers' own voices thin outside memoirs and social media; much official writing speaks for mothers rather than by them.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reproductive choices and abortion; mothers of young men killed or detained in security operations; pressure on women about childbearing. Reproductive and family law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
