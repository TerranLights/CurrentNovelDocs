## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian village families**: how brothers, fathers and the surname group are expected to guard kin, and how that is debated.
- **Balkar communities of avalanche- and mudflow-prone gorges** (Elbrus and Chereksky districts): protection from the mountain itself.
- **Herding families**: guarding flocks at the summer pastures.
- **Russian and Cossack stanitsas**: a separate tradition of armed self-defense and the Cossack patrols of recent decades (*check*).
- **Women** in each community, as protected, as protectors of children, and as critics of protection that confines.
- **Devout Muslim families** under police attention: who they turn to for protection.
- **Nalchik apartment dwellers**: reliance on the state, police and neighbors over kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: emergency and protected-area data are open; customary protection is in ethnography and song; the security-operations strand is in NGO reports that may be blocked or incomplete.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counterterrorism operations, disappearances and the 2005 Nalchik attack; honor-based violence; weapons; informants' safety. Policing and criminal law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
