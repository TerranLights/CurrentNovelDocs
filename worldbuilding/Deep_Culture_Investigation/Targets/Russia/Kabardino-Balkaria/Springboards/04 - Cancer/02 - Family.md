# Kabardino-Balkaria: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Ossetian locally. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian surname groups**, including families of former princely and noble lines and those of commoner descent: whether lineage still sorts kin and marriage.
- **Balkar lineages by gorge society**: kin ties tested by exile in Central Asia and by return.
- **Ossetian lineages** (*check* villages), with their own surname-group tradition.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks**: kin reckoned on both sides, a contrast in how far "family" reaches.
- **Mixed families in Nalchik** (Kabardian-Balkar, Kabardian-Russian): which side's kin customs prevail.
- **Families with kin in Turkey, Jordan, Syria** or Central Asia: the family as a transnational web.
- **Devout and secular households** within each people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for kin terms and 19th-20th century ethnography in Russian; thin on how kin ties work today outside press and social media; thin in English.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Turkish and Arabic for diaspora kin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** feuds and honor disputes; bride abduction; pressures on marriage choice; same-sex families are legally restricted topics in Russia, so sources and informants need care. Family and marriage law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
