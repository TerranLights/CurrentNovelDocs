# Kabardino-Balkaria: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; some Arabic religious terms. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian households where the youngest son or his wife lives with parents** (*check* how common): the expected carer by custom against practice.
- **Balkar families split between upland villages and Nalchik**: care across distance.
- **Russian and Cossack families**, who may rely more on state homes and church help: a contrast to test, not assume.
- **Families of disabled children**: care as a long-term job, often by mothers.
- **Labor-migrant households**, with men working in other regions of Russia: who cares at home while they are away.
- **Rural and urban elders**: access to clinics and to paid help.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for unpaid care in the republic (national surveys may not sample it well); richer in folk-medicine ethnography and in disability statistics; social media gives today's practice.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; some Arabic religious vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disability stigma; medical privacy in crowdfunding posts; religious healing as a contested topic. Rules on guardianship and inheritance go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
