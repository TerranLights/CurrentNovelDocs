*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khabze revivalists:** the sacred sought in pre-Islamic Circassian practice.
- **Balkar Tengrists:** a parallel Turkic revival with its own sky deity.
- **Village Hanafi Muslims of the older generation:** rites at home, graves and mawlid.
- **Stricter young Muslims:** devotion framed against both custom and the official muftiate.
- **Women's devotional circles:** practice apart from the men's mosque.
- **Highland hunters and shepherds:** survivals of mountain and game rites.
- **Orthodox Russians and Cossacks; Mountain Jews:** minority sacred spaces and calendars.
- **Soviet-raised secular townspeople:** spirituality without religion, or none.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on folk-religious survivals and on Islam as security topic; thin on inner experience as people tell it.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic; Ottoman Turkish for some manuscripts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Islamic practice is securitized and Russia keeps a list of banned "extremist" materials; revival groups may keep rites private; respect closed practice. Doctrine and institutions go to Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
