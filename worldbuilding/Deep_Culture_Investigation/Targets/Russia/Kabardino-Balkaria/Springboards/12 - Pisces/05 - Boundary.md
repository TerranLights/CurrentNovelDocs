## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian plain households behind courtyard walls against Balkar highland tower villages:** two built answers to privacy.
- **Nalchik apartment blocks:** shared walls and stairwells where village rules on visiting may not hold.
- **Upper-valley villages inside the border zone:** life under permits and patrols.
- **Balkar villages in land and boundary disputes:** where the group line is a land line.
- **Terek Cossack stanitsas:** an old frontier identity on the plain.
- **Devout and secular Muslim families:** gender and purity lines drawn differently.
- **Meskhetian Turks and Ossetians:** small groups and the lines others draw around them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for kinship avoidance and frontier history; thin for personal space and privacy measures.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land disputes are live and divisive; Caucasian Knot is labeled a "foreign agent" in Russia; border-zone rules restrict access. Property and boundary law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
