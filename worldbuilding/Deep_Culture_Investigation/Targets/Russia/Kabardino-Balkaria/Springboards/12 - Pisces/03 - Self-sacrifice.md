# Kabardino-Balkaria: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for Islamic terms. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of the old princely and noble families against those of peasant lines:** whether the duty to die in battle was ranked by estate in their own family histories.
- **Balkar families with front-line soldiers in 1944:** sacrifice for a state that deported their kin.
- **Terek Cossack stanitsas** (Prokhladny district): an inherited military-service identity.
- **Kabardian plain villages against highland Balkar villages:** memorials and their wording, village by village.
- **Women caregivers and widows:** sacrifices kept within the household and rarely put on plaques.
- **Families of labor migrants:** the absent earner, as wives, parents and children speak of him.
- **Meskhetian Turks and Mountain Jews:** their own wartime and displacement losses, often left out of republic memorials.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war memory and heroic song; thin for family and caregiving sacrifice; current war casualty data are contested.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyrdom language was used by the insurgency and is policed; the current war is a censored subject; the deportation and hunger strikes touch live disputes. Law on protest belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
