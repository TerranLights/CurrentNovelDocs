# Kabardino-Balkaria: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for Islamic terms. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and the ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrnyauz and the upper Baksan valley:** a town shaped by mudflows and mine closure.
- **Nalchik residents who lived through 2005:** fear of attack and fear of the sweep that followed.
- **Villages placed under counterterrorism regimes:** fear of checkpoints and searches in daily life.
- **Balkar elders:** a deportation night remembered as the founding terror.
- **Children and parents in villages and in town:** which fear figures and dangers each uses in raising children.
- **Russians and Cossacks in Prokhladny and Maysky:** minority fears of outflow and of being outnumbered.
- **Syrian repatriates:** war fear and the fears of a new country.
- **Women and men:** the social fear of shame and talk, measured, not assumed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and disaster records are rich; surveys of fear for the republic alone are rare.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic; Ottoman Turkish and Crimean Tatar sources for early raids.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fear of security forces is a dangerous topic to research; outlets such as Kavkaz.Realii carry legal labels in Russia; deportation memory is painful. Courage belongs to Aries; no law here.
- **Held for review:** none.
