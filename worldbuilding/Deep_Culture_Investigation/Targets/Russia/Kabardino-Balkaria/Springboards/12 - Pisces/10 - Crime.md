# Kabardino-Balkaria: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar. **Concept in one line:** what counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how those who commit it are seen. No law research here: law is Libra's (the letter) and Capricorn's (the spirit).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland Balkar villages:** memories of feud and the shame that follows a killer's kin.
- **Plains Kabardian villages:** horse-theft lore and how old prowess is now judged.
- **Nalchik youth:** street codes, informing and fighting in a city setting.
- **Families of insurgents and families of those harmed by security forces:** two opposite sides on whose acts were crimes.
- **Women from families where abduction happened:** the act judged from the bride's side.
- **Students and workers in Moscow:** life under the "Caucasian" label.
- **Meskhetian Turks and Russians in the stanitsas:** minority views of local wrongs and of each other.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Official statistics and rights reports exist; studies of how people judge wrongdoers are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** insurgency, counterterrorism and rights abuses are dangerous topics; Memorial was dissolved by court order in 2021-2022; ethnic crime labels are stereotypes. Law, courts and customary settlement belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
