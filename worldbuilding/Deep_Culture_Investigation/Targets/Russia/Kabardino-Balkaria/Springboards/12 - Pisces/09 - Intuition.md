## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland Balkar herders and hunters:** reading weather, animals and terrain by long experience.
- **Plains horse breeders and traders:** the trained eye for animals, with money riding on it.
- **Older village women:** dream reading and foresight, as families describe their roles.
- **Hazard scientists and rescuers:** expert hunches inside a data-driven service.
- **Devout Muslims:** guidance prayer welcomed and fortune-telling refused.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** Slavic omens and Orthodox discernment.
- **Urban youth:** psychic TV, online readings and "vibes".
- **Repatriates:** Arabic-language dream lore set beside local custom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Ethnography of divination and weather lore exists; studies of everyday intuition and expert judgment are scarce.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fortune-telling is condemned by official Islam, so clients may hide it; divination records are read as belief, never as evidence.
- **Held for review:** none.
