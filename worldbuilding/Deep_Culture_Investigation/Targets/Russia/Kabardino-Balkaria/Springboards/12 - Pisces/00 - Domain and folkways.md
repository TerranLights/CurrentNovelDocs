# Kabardino-Balkaria: The hidden and transcendence (Pisces domain and folkways): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Kabardian (Kabardino-Circassian, Cyrillic script); Karachay-Balkar (Cyrillic script); Arabic for Islamic terms. **Concept in one line:** the domain of suffering, the unseen, confinement, transcendence and "magic ways": how people meet pain, hidden forces and the wish to escape.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardians of the plain villages** (Baksan, Zolsky, Chegem, Urvan, Terek, Leskensky districts) against **Nalchik's Kabardians**: village rite and urban practice.
- **Balkars by the old societies** (Malkar/Cherek, Bezengi, Kholam, Chegem, Baksan/Urusbiev): separate valleys with their own legends and shrines.
- **Balkars by deportation generation**: those born in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan, the returnees of 1957, and those born after.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks** (Prokhladny, Maysky districts): Orthodox and Slavic folk belief.
- **Smaller groups**: Ossetians, Meskhetian Turks, the remaining Mountain Jews (Juhuro) of Nalchik, Koreans, Armenians.
- **Circassian repatriates** from Syria, Turkey, Jordan and Kosovo, and **pious young Muslims** against the Khabze-revival milieu.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and pre-1917 ethnography are rich; surveys at republic level are thin and mostly Russia-wide; clinical data sit in Russian state reports. The nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the republic's core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for religious terms; Italian, Ottoman Turkish and German for early travelers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the insurgency years and counterterrorism operations; the 1944 deportation and the 1860s expulsion; disputes between official Islam, Salafi currents and Khabze revivalists; Kabardian-Balkar land tensions. Order ways (custom and mediation) belong to Capricorn; no law research here.
- **Held for review:** none.
