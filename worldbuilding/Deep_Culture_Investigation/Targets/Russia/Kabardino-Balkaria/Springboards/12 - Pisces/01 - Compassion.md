# Kabardino-Balkaria: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for Islamic almsgiving terms. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian plain villages against Nalchik:** collections for a bereaved family in a village, against crowdfunding among strangers in the city.
- **Highland Balkar villages against the post-1957 lowland Balkar settlements** (Khasanya, Belaya Rechka *check*): whether mutual aid survived resettlement.
- **Balkar elders who lived the deportation:** compassion recalled from exile, as givers and as receivers.
- **Circassian repatriates from Syria, Kosovo and Turkey:** the help they got from hosts, as each side tells it.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks:** parish charity and Soviet-era welfare habits, against Muslim almsgiving.
- **Meskhetian Turks:** a community displaced twice, on aid given and withheld.
- **The Mountain Jewish community of Nalchik:** communal self-help in a shrinking group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and religious teaching are rich; measured data on giving exist only at federal-district level; social-ministry reports are open but official.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for Islamic terms; German for Klaproth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Islamic charities have faced extremism-financing cases in the region, so name none without care; Syrian repatriation is politically charged. Mercy toward offenders in courts belongs to Libra and Capricorn; customary reconciliation to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
