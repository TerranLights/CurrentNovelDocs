## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elbrus district Balkars who host tourists:** people living inside the escape trade, not consuming it.
- **Nalchik youth against village youth:** clubs, screens and drugs in town against weddings and dance gatherings in the village.
- **Devout young Muslims who abstain against older Soviet-raised drinkers:** two generations' answers to drink.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks:** the dacha, the bathhouse and vodka as their own forms of release.
- **Labor migrants and students in Moscow:** escape outward, and what they return to.
- **Soviet-era climbers and spa workers:** memories of the mountains and resorts as sanctioned refuge.
- **Repatriates from Syria:** a war left behind, and the leisure of a new life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Addiction and tourism statistics exist; studies of everyday leisure and fantasy are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use is criminalized and stigmatized; alcohol is a fault line between religious and secular families; ask with care. Drug law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
