# Kabardino-Balkaria: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Ottoman Turkish and Crimean Tatar for early envoys; Turkish and Arabic for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Kabardian princely houses and Balkar noble families:** each had its own history of envoys, pledges and alliances.
- **Terek Cossack descendants:** neighbors whose frontier ties mixed trade, raids and truces.
- **Muslim and Orthodox leaders:** formal dialogue between faiths.
- **Ethnic associations of both titular peoples:** how each speaks to the republic and to Moscow.
- **Diaspora bodies in Turkey, Jordan and Syria:** negotiators for return, abroad.
- **Women as go-betweens:** query the reported custom of a woman's headscarf stopping a fight (*check*).
- **Young people at Nalchik dance evenings:** everyday diplomacy among peers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 16th-19th c. envoys and treaties (Russian archives); thin for present-day village and family diplomacy.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for speech genres; Ottoman Turkish for Porte records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1990s congresses, the Abkhaz war volunteers and land quarrels are live; official framings of Kabarda's ties to Russia are contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
