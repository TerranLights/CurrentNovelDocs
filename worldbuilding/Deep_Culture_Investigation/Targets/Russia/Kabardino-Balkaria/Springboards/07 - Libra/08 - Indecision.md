# Kabardino-Balkaria: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for religious terms. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families where elders settle a match and couples who decide alone:** test who is expected to choose.
- **Village and Nalchik youth:** choices about study, work and moving away.
- **Men and women:** examine the stereotype that one sex wavers more; do not assume it.
- **Devout Muslims who seek istikhara and secular families:** whether the choice is handed to prayer.
- **Balkar and Kabardian communities in moments of collective choice** (1917-1921; the 1990s congresses): how each group reached or failed to reach a common line.
- **Russian and Cossack families:** their own habits of consulting kin or priest.
- **Repatriates:** staying or leaving under uncertainty.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for indecision as such; it must be traced through marriage, migration, religion and politics.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for words, sayings and divination terms; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bride abduction can be framed as forcing a decision; treat with care. Divination may be frowned on by religious authorities; describe sources, not people.
- **Held for review:** none.
