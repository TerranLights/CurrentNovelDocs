## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of the old Kabardian estates** (princely, noble and peasant lines): payments for harm were once scaled by rank.
- **Balkar families by gorge** (Cherek, Chegem, Baksan, Bezengi, Holam): each society kept its own elders' judgments.
- **Balkars born in exile against those born after 1957:** the deportation is a justice question with different weight by generation.
- **Practicing Muslims under the muftiate and those in independent congregations:** they may differ on who has the right to judge.
- **Terek Cossack stanitsas** in the north: their community courts and memory of their own repression.
- **Meskhetian Turks**, also deported in 1944: a second community's claim to redress.
- **Syrian Circassian repatriates:** justice framed through return to the homeland.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial and Soviet records and for historical justice; thin and guarded for present feud and reconciliation practice.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for folklore and sayings; Arabic terms for Islamic courts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anti-extremism trials, disappearances, the 2005 attack, the deportation and the 1860s war are live; feud cases involve living families. Some monitoring groups have been closed or labeled; use archived copies.
- **Held for review:** none.
