# Kabardino-Balkaria: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Plain and mountain:** orchards and grain against high pastures; different seasons to balance.
- **Elbrus and Chegem gorge households:** tourism income weighed against grazing and protected land.
- **Balkar and Kabardian publics on representation:** each may judge the republic's balance of posts differently.
- **Russian-majority northern districts:** their weight in a republic named for two other peoples.
- **Families of seasonal labor migrants:** time away from home against earnings.
- **Congregations of different religious leanings:** each claims the middle ground.
- **Nalchik and the villages:** pace of life and rest.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for political power-sharing and mountain science; thin for folk ideas of bodily balance.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for calendar rites and healing terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic quotas are informal and touchy; "moderate" and "radical" are charged labels with legal consequences; quote the parties, never settle the question.
- **Held for review:** none.
