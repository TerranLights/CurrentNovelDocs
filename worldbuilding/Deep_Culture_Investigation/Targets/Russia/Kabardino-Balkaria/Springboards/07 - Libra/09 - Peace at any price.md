# Kabardino-Balkaria: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for religious terms. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Daughters-in-law in multigenerational households:** test whether household rules of restraint fall on them first.
- **Young men under security pressure after 2005:** whether keeping quiet served to shield themselves and their families.
- **Balkar elders who lived through exile:** the years when the deportation was not spoken of in public, and how that ended.
- **Circassian activists and those who prefer quiet:** whether the 1864 memory should be pressed.
- **Small-business owners:** paying off threats rather than reporting.
- **Russian residents who leave rather than complain:** exit instead of voice.
- **Repatriates from Syria:** gratitude to hosts as a reason to stay quiet (to test).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin and hard to reach: most evidence is in NGO reports, court records and testimony; little survey data.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for laments and household terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence in the home, honor-based violence, extortion and abuses in security operations involve living people at risk. Use published, anonymized material only; never contact named victims. Some NGOs carry "foreign agent" labels.
- **Held for review:** none.
