# Kabardino-Balkaria: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large, well-placed lineages and small or newly arrived families:** the reach of kin networks differs.
- **Kabardian and Balkar villages** on fair treatment by republic offices: each may see bias in a different direction.
- **Russian-majority northern districts:** whether they see republic posts as open to them.
- **Meskhetian Turks, Koreans and other small communities** (*check*): treated by officials without lineage ties.
- **Repatriates from Syria:** dealing with offices without local kin.
- **Men and women** before courts and officials: equal hearing as a separate question.
- **Speakers of Kabardian or Balkar only** (mostly older villagers): equal treatment in a Russian-language system.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich formal records (courts, audits, procurement); thin, anecdotal evidence on favoritism itself.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for kin terms and village sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims of clan rule and corruption name living officials; some monitors and outlets are labeled "foreign agents" or "undesirable," and citing them may carry risk for local contacts. Keep private persons out.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the AKAK index for "Кабарда суд посредники" and "почётные судьи".
2. Search the Judicial Qualification Collegium of KBR's site for disciplinary decisions.
3. Search Декларатор for KBR ministries and list declared relatives.
4. Search zakupki.gov.ru for KBR contracts above a set threshold, then the winners' founders.
5. Search for "клановость Кабардино-Балкария" in scholarly databases (eLibrary, CyberLeninka).
