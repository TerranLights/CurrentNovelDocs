## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kabardian and Balkar historians, whose accounts of land and origins can disagree; read each in its own language.
- Balkars of the Baksan and Chegem valleys, who live beside the observatory and the institute's stations, against Balkars of Nalchik.
- Older people who learned the truth of the world from Soviet schools and the "Znanie" lectures, against young people who learn it from Telegram and imams online.
- Orthodox Russians and Cossacks, with their own versions of Terek history.
- Diaspora Circassians, whose account of the 1860s rests on different archives and on family memory.
- Mountain Jews and Meskhetian Turks, whose own histories of the republic are rarely in its official volumes *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. History and folklore are rich; trust and misinformation are barely studied at republic level and will need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Ossetian and Turkish for the history disputes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** history disputes between Kabardians and Balkars touch land; accounts of counterterrorism operations are contested and dangerous to collect; genealogies may be private. Courts and testimony as written law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
