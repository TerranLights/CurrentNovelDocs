*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Men and women of the Soviet generation, for whom work and progress were the official answer, against their children who came of age in the 1990s.
- Young villagers who leave for work in Moscow or the oil towns, against those who stay with the land and the herd.
- Balkar families for whom the return from Central Asia gives a whole life its story, against Balkar families from the valleys that later moved to Nalchik.
- Young people who place their purpose in practiced Islam, against those who place it in sport, career or the national movement.
- Cossack families of the Prokhladny and Maysky districts, whose revival offers a purpose of service.
- Circassian returnees, who arrived with a purpose (homecoming) that the long-settled population does not share.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to thin. Folklore and literature are rich; survey data on meaning for this republic are thin or absent and may need to be inferred from national samples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; French for Dumézil.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and despair are under-reported in a Muslim-majority republic; laments for the 1864 expulsion and the 1944 deportation carry grief that is still personal for families. Death ways in detail belong to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
