## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Village Kabardians bound by kin and reputation, against Nalchik residents who deal mostly with strangers: shame works differently in each.
- Balkar highland villages with dense lineage ties, against Balkar families scattered by the deportation and return.
- Traders of the bazaars, many from different peoples, whose dealings cross ethnic lines.
- Officials, and the residents who deal with them over permits and services: where the everyday words for bribes and favors are tested.
- Teachers and students around the exam system.
- Russians and Cossacks of the northern districts, with their own words and norms for fair dealing.
- Diaspora returnees, who compare the republic's dealings with those of Syria, Turkey or Jordan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folk and ethnographic sources are rich; present-day honesty is studied mainly through corruption and exam data, which are national-frame and political.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; some Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and electoral fraud are risky topics for those interviewed; old writing on horse-raiding and "highland deceit" spreads stereotypes and must not be read as findings. Crime is Pisces; trust and betrayal are Scorpio; the law on bribery goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
