# Kabardino-Balkaria: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Turkish and Arabic for the diaspora; Ottoman Turkish for the older record. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Descendants of the princely and noble houses, against descendants of the freed dependent estates: freedom may carry a different story in each family.
- Balkars of the Cherek and Chegem valleys, whose 1990s movement spoke of self-rule, against Balkars who settled in Nalchik.
- Kabardian activists of the national movement, against Kabardians who keep away from politics.
- Terek Cossacks, whose own history of free service and later deportation (the 1919-1920 de-Cossackization) gives the word a separate past.
- Mountain Jews and Meskhetian Turks, groups with their own experience of flight and resettlement *check*.
- Returnees from Syria, who fled a war and came to a homeland governed very differently from the one their families left.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Historical and memory sources are rich in Russian; current attitudes are hard to survey and activists' writing is often online and partisan.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Turkish; Ottoman Turkish for the archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current political freedom, protest and the national movements are sensitive for anyone interviewed; Circassian and Balkar claims over the same lands can collide. Laws on rehabilitation, religion and speech go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit); autonomy and independence as personal traits are Aries.
- **Held for review:** none.
