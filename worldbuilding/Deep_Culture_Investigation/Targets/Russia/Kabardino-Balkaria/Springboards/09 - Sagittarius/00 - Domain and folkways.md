# Kabardino-Balkaria: Domain and folkways (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Kabardian (Kabardino-Circassian, адыгэбзэ); Karachay-Balkar (къарачай-малкъар тил); Arabic for Islamic texts; Turkish and Arabic for the Circassian diaspora; Ossetian for a small minority. **Concept in one line:** the domain of belief and philosophy: what is true, and where meaning is found (religion, philosophy, higher learning, long journeys), with Fischer's religious ways and freedom ways as folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Lowland Kabardians of the Baksan, Urvan, Chegem and Zolsky districts, against the Lesser Kabarda villages of the Tersky district: two histories of conversion and land.
- Balkar mountain societies (Cherek, Chegem, Baksan-Elbrus, Khulam-Bezengi): each valley with its own memory of the old faith.
- Balkars who were born in exile in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan, against those born after the 1957 return.
- Russians of Nalchik, Terek Cossacks of the Prokhladny and Maysky districts, and Ossetian villages *check*: the Orthodox and the Soviet-secular sides of the domain.
- Mountain Jews of Nalchik (much of the community emigrated), Meskhetian Turks, Armenians and Koreans *check*.
- Circassian returnees from Syria, Turkey and Jordan: a second Islam and a second idea of homeland arriving in the republic.

## 4. Flags
- **Lands:** the tracker carries "Russia" as the pre-war land, which is broad; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. These sheets are written for the core lands of the Kabardino-Balkarian Republic.
- **Data:** rich in Russian; mixed in Kabardian and Balkar; thin in English except on the diaspora and on armed Islamism.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, some Arabic and Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** currents tied to the 2005 Nalchik attack and its prosecutions; the 1864 expulsion and 1944 deportation as live memory; Kabardian-Balkar disputes over land and history. Customary law (*адат*) and religious law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
