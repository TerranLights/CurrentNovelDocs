# Kabardino-Balkaria: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for Islamic devotional texts; Juhuri and Hebrew for the Mountain Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Village Kabardians whose faith centers on the mosque and the home rite, against Nalchik Kabardians raised in Soviet secular families who returned to practice after 1990.
- Older Balkars who held to prayer through the 1944-1957 exile, against their grandchildren.
- Orthodox Russians and Terek Cossacks of Prokhladny and Maysky: a church faith tied to the Cossack revival.
- Khabze revivalists, a small but vocal circle with a separate idea of what Circassian faith means.
- Mountain Jews of Nalchik and their diaspora in Israel and Moscow.
- Circassian returnees from Syria and Jordan, who arrive with a different schooling in Islam.
- Protestant converts among Kabardians and Russians *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on institutions and post-Soviet revival in Russian; thin on private faith, on doubt and on unbelief; the Khabze revival is mostly online.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions on a person's practice can be read as screening for "nontraditional" Islam; the line between faith and the officially suspect is the subject of Fundamentalism (concept 10). Religious law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the KBR Ministry of Justice list of religious organizations and sort it by faith and district.
2. Search the Russian State Library catalog for "ислам Кабардино-Балкария диссертация" and "религиозность кабардинцев".
3. Search the Central State Archive of the KBR guide for the fond of the Commissioner for Religious Affairs.
4. Search *Адыгэ псалъэ* for "Ӏимани" and *Заман* for "ийман" to see how each paper writes about faith.
5. Look for the Khabze revival groups' own pages with "Тхьэшхуэ" and "адыгэ хабзэ дин".
