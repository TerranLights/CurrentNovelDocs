# Kabardino-Balkaria: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Arabic for the movements' own vocabulary; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for village and family speech; English for the security and political-science literature. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular and political certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Young urban Muslims in Nalchik who studied in independent communities, against village elders who keep the older Hanafi practice with its customary rites.
- Imams of the official muftiate, against imams once removed or closed down in the 2000s *check*.
- Graduates of study abroad in Arab countries, against graduates of the local Islamic institute.
- Families of men killed, imprisoned or missing after counterinsurgency operations, whose own view of strict Islam may differ from the men's.
- Khabze revivalists and secular Kabardian intellectuals, who reject both currents for different reasons.
- Balkar villages and Kabardian villages, each with its own history of mosques and imams after 1990 (measured, not assumed).
- Orthodox Russians and Cossacks, with their own strict traditionalists *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on security and politics, in Russian and English; thin and partisan on adherents' own views; survey data rarely isolate the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Arabic, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** high. Contact with Salafi communities or with families of the prosecuted can bring danger to them and to the researcher; banned groups and texts must be handled through scholarship and archives, not by seeking them out. Violence is the rare edge, and strict practice must not be conflated with it. Extremism law and the lists of banned groups and texts go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
