# Kabardino-Balkaria: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for recitation terms. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian village and Nalchik families**, on how strictly conduct and the home are held to standard.
- **Balkar families** from each gorge, on craft and conduct standards.
- **Women** (embroidery, felt, the kept house, the bride's presentation) against **men** (horsemanship, sport, public speech).
- **Pupils in elite Nalchik schools**, against village schools.
- **Pious households**, where exact prayer and recitation are a standard, against secular ones.
- **Russian and Terek Cossack communities**, with their own church and craft norms.
- **Professional performers** in the state ensembles, against amateurs at village gatherings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on craft and dance; thin on psychological measures for the republic; online wedding display is easy to sample.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for craft and ethics terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental illness carries stigma; claims of exam fraud; religious disputes over display. Avoid any one-voice picture of a "strict" people; vary by family and setting.
- **Held for review:** none.
