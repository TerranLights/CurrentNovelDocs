# Kabardino-Balkaria: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian farm villages** on the Baksan, Chegem and Malka: grain and horse work, with old estate differences that may still echo.
- **Balkar herding gorges** (Cherek, Chegem, Baksan valleys): seasonal herd work, then tourism near Elbrus.
- **Terek Cossack stanitsas and Russian towns** (Prokhladny, Maysky): a separate farm and service tradition.
- **Nalchik's professional and public-sector workers**, against village and informal work.
- **Mining Tyrnyauz**, a mixed Balkar and Russian work town.
- **Women's paid and unpaid work**, compared across all groups.
- **Circassian repatriates from Syria**, entering the local job market.
- **Labor migrants and rotation workers** abroad in Russia, and those who stay.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; regional labor statistics exist, while attitude surveys on work are likely national only; ethnography and folklore are rich in Russian.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for songs, proverbs and the native press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced labor in deportation; estate history touches descendants of named families; informal work and tax status. Labor law goes to Libra and Capricorn. The Soviet charge of *тунеядство* is a legal matter and goes there too.
- **Held for review:** none.
