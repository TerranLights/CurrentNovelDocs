# Kabardino-Balkaria: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what does not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian elders and the young**, on whose judgment of people and custom carries weight.
- **Balkar highland families**, where marriage within or outside the gorge may be judged by different marks.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks**, with their own church and community advisers.
- **Pious and secular households** in each group, on whom they ask: an imam, a parent, an elder or no one.
- **Women and men** as judges of a match, of goods and of guests.
- **Circassian repatriates from Syria**, judged by local standards and judging them in turn.
- **Ossetian, Meskhetian Turk and Juhuro neighbors**, in mixed towns and villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in ethnography of marriage, horses and dance; mixed for present-day practice; thin for measured judgment beyond fraud figures.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for tales, wedding terms and online debate.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic tension; religious rulings; old lineage rankings that may still shape matches. The elders' council as a court goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
