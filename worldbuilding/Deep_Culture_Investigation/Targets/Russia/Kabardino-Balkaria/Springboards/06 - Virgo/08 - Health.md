# Kabardino-Balkaria: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for religious healing terms. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian lowland villages and Nalchik**, on use of clinics, healers and spas.
- **Balkar highland villages**, farther from hospitals, with their own healers and remedies.
- **Elbrus-district communities** living beside mountain tourism and its injuries.
- **Pious households** who seek religious healing, against secular ones.
- **Russian and Terek Cossack communities**, with church and village healing.
- **Elders and the young**, on talk of mental health and on trust in doctors.
- **Women**, as birth-givers and nurses of the sick at home, and **men**, on whether they see doctors.
- **Repatriates from Syria**, including trauma and war injuries.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on spas and federal statistics; mixed on folk medicine; thin on mental health.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for healers' terms and remedies; Arabic for religious healing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental illness, addiction and HIV stigma; religious healing that may be contested by officials or imams; exile mortality; patients' privacy. Healers' knowledge may be private; ask before recording.
- **Held for review:** none.
