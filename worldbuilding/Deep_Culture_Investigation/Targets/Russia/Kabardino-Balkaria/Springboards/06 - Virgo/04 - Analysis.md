- **Nalchik's academic and engineering community** (KBSU, research institutes), against villages.
- **Kabardian and Balkar school districts**, compared on contests and exam results, and the reasons offered.
- **Elbrus-district Balkar villages** near the science stations.

- **Women in science and teaching**, against men, in each group.
- **The Juhuro community of Nalchik**, with its own religious study and schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on science institutions; mixed on everyday reasoning; exam data exist but are contested.
- **Languages needed:** Russian above all; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for riddles and folk knowledge; Arabic for religious reasoning.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exam fraud claims; religious disagreement, including currents the state calls extremist; divination may be stigmatized by present-day religious teachers.
- **Held for review:** none.
