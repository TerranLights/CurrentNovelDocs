*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian and Balkar families**, on who may correct whom: elders, parents, in-laws, siblings.
- **Village and Nalchik settings**, on public against private reproach.
- **Religious voices of different currents** and the **keepers of custom**, who criticize each other.
- **Human-rights and civic activists**, against state media voices.
- **Young online comedians**, against older audiences.
- **Russian and Terek Cossack communities**, with their own press and church voices.
- **Circassian repatriates and the diaspora**, who criticize the homeland from outside and are criticized for it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on folklore satire and Soviet records; thin and risky for present-day political criticism.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for songs, sketches and native press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current Russian laws on "discrediting" state bodies and on extremism make political criticism dangerous for sources; protect people named online; interethnic land disputes; religious currents under prosecution. Defamation and censorship as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru and the КБИГИ journal for "джэгуакIуэ" and "джегуако".
2. Search folk-song catalogs for "сатирические песни" with "адыги" and "балкарцы".
3. Ask the republic state archive for the Soviet regional party committee fonds and their meeting minutes.
4. Search YouTube for Kabardian-language sketches ("адыгэ сценка", "кабардинский юмор").
5. Read the Human Rights Center's annual reports for the republic.
