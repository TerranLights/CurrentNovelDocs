# Kabardino-Balkaria: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for Islamic legal texts. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, and whether to obey, use, bend or avoid it. *Capricorn takes the spirit of the law only; the letter of the statutes goes to Libra.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian village communities**: whether custom or the state's court is the first resort in a family dispute.
- **Balkar highland communities**: their own customary bodies, recorded separately, may still shape settlements.
- **Practicing Muslims of different orientations**: Islamic law as consulted through the official board or through independent teachers.
- **Russians and Cossack-descended residents**: state law and Cossack society rules, with little customary overlap, to test.
- **Lawyers, judges and police officers from the republic**: what they think law is for, against what residents expect of them.
- **Families of men caught up in counterterrorist cases**: law as experienced at its hardest.
- **Young people and students**: legal attitudes as surveyed by local researchers.
- **Repatriates from Syria and Jordan**: they bring other legal cultures and may use the courts differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the imperial period and legal anthropology; court statistics exist; attitude surveys are scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for customary terms; Arabic for Islamic legal texts and older court records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counterterrorism, burial disputes and torture claims; feud cases involve living families; never name parties from press reports.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library and eLibrary catalogs for "Бабич правовой плюрализм".
2. Find *Северный Кавказ в составе Российской империи* (2007) and list chapters on courts and custom.
3. Ask the republic's archive for the Nalchik verbal-court fonds and their finding aids.
4. Read the HUDOC record for *Sabanchiyeva and Others v. Russia* and list related cases from the republic.
5. Search press archives for "примирение" with "старейшины" and "Кабардино-Балкария".
