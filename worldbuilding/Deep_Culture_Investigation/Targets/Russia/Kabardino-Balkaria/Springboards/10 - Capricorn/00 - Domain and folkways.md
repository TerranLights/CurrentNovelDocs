# Kabardino-Balkaria: The domain: authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Kabardian (East Circassian, Cyrillic); Karachay-Balkar (Turkic, Cyrillic); for smaller communities, Ossetian and Juhuri (Judeo-Tat). **Concept in one line:** who commands, who obeys, and what is owed, across career, status, reputation, authority and responsibility, and the folkways of age, time, rank, order and power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardians of the plain districts** (Baksan, Zolsky, Urvan, Terek, Leskensky, lowland Chegem): heirs of the princely society, with village differences.
- **Balkars of the five former highland societies** (Malkar, Khulam, Bezengi, Chegem, Urusbiy): each had its own leading families.
- **Balkars by deportation generation** (born in Central Asia, 1944-1957, or after the return): exile may have reshaped who is obeyed.
- **Russians and the Terek Cossack villages** of Prokhladny and Maysky districts: a separate service tradition.
- **Nalchik's mixed urban population**: where office, career and origin meet.
- **Smaller groups**: Ossetians, Meskhetian Turks, the Mountain Jewish community (much of it emigrated *check*), Ukrainians, Armenians, Koreans.
- **Circassian returnees** from Syria, Turkey and Jordan, and the diasporas (Uzunyayla Kabardians in Turkey; Balkars in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich Russian-language ethnography and 19th-century records; the cross-cultural indexes (power distance, tightness, long-term orientation) exist only for Russia as a whole.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic and Ottoman Turkish for pre-1920s manuscripts; Ossetian and Juhuri for smaller communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1864 exile and the 1944 deportation; the insurgency years; Kabardian-Balkar disputes over land and office; descent from former dependent estates.
- **Lands:** the tracker gives the pre-war lands only as "Russia", so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; these sheets are written for the former republic's core lands.
- **Held for review:** none.
