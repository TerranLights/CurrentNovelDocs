# Kabardino-Balkaria: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eldest and youngest sons in Kabardian and Balkar households**: whether the obligation to stay with the parents and keep the house falls on one son more than the others.
- **Women of both peoples**: duties as daughter-in-law and as mourner, to describe from women's own accounts.
- **Balkar families who returned in 1957 and those who stayed in Central Asia**: duty to the ancestral gorges was answered differently.
- **Terek Cossack villages**: service to the state as an inherited estate duty, revived by present Cossack societies.
- **Muslim families of different practice**: religious obligations (prayer, fasting, alms) weighed against customary ones.
- **Labor migrants and their home villages**: duties kept at a distance and how villages judge them.
- **Conscripts and contract soldiers**: duty to the state set beside duty to the family.
- **Smaller groups** (Ossetians, Meskhetian Turks, Armenians): their own guest and kin duties, for contrast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore, poetry and ethnography; measured data on duty is thin and federal.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for songs and poems; Arabic script for older Balkar and Kabardian religious texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** military service and recent call-ups; deportation memory; women's household duties; some mourning rites may be private to families.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library catalog for "Канжальская битва" with "фольклор" and for Kabardian historical-song collections.
2. Find Kuliev's and Mechiev's collected works and list the poems on homeland, kin and faith.
3. Search for "Книга памяти Кабардино-Балкарской Республики" and note the village volumes.
4. Search library catalogs for "Дикая дивизия Кабардинский полк" histories and published letters.
5. Search for "куначество" with "кабардинцы" in ethnographic journals.
