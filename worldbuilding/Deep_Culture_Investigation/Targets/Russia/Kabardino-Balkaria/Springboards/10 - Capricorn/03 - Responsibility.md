## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian lineages (лъэпкъ) in old villages and in Nalchik**: whether a family still feels answerable for what one member does, and whether the city weakens that.
- **Balkar lineages (тукъум) of the gorges**: separate because their customary settlements were recorded apart from Kabarda's.
- **Families of men killed or jailed in the insurgency years**: they have faced the state's collective liability directly.
- **Officials and managers**: accountability in office as they describe it, against how villagers describe it.
- **Women heads of household** (widows, wives of labor migrants): who answers for the household when the man is away.
- **Russian and Cossack-descended townspeople** of Prokhladny and Maysky: a more individual model of liability may hold, to test.
- **Highland villagers of the Elbrus district**: responsibility for land, waste and tourists on the mountain.
- **Teenagers**: when they become answerable at home, at school and before the law.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for customary law and imperial records, thin for measured attitudes.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for kin terms and oral accounts; 19th-century Russian bureaucratic language for amanat records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relatives of insurgents and the 2013 law; forced apologies; disaster victims; interviews need care and anonymity.
