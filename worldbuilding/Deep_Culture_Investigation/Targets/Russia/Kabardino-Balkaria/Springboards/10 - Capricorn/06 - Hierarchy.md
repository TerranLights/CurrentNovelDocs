## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of former princely and noble families and of former dependents, among Kabardians**: whether the old line still shapes marriage, prestige and speech.
- **Balkar highland families of the five former societies**: the leading families and their dependents differed gorge by gorge.
- **Kabardians, Balkars and Russians as peoples**: a ranking among groups in office and land.
- **Men and women**, and **elder and younger siblings**: the household's own ranking.
- **Officials and the public**: the modern state's ladder, felt from above and below.
- **Terek Cossack-descended villages**: Cossack rank and estate as a separate past hierarchy.
- **Repatriates from the diaspora**: often outside both old and new ladders, a test of who counts.
- **Small minorities** (Meskhetian Turks, Armenians, Koreans): where they are placed by others, in their own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 19th century and for ethnography; thin for current measured status.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for estate terms and genealogies; 19th-century administrative Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent from former dependent estates; ethnic shares of office; gender; avoid naming living families' ancestry.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search digitized AKAK volumes for "кабардинские князья" and "балкарские таубии".

4. Find *Карачаевцы. Балкарцы* (2014) and list its chapters on social organization.
