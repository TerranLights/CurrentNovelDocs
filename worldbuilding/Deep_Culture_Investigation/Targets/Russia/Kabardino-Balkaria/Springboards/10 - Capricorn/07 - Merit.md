## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Graduates of Kabardino-Balkarian State University and graduates of Moscow universities**: whether a degree from outside counts for more at home.
- **Athletes and coaches**: sport as the one field where merit is publicly measured, to test against others.
- **Teachers and doctors in villages**: whether professional merit or local ties decide their posts.
- **Balkar families of the deportation generation**: merit denied by status, as they recall it.
- **Kabardian and Balkar officials**: whether people explain their rise by merit or by lineage.
- **Russian and Cossack-descended residents**: how they read the ethnic balance in hiring.
- **Young women in professions**: merit as seen by and of women, in their own accounts.
- **Syrian and Turkish Circassian returnees**: credentials earned abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; honors and exam data are open, clientelism data is mostly qualitative and contested.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for songs and local talk; Arabic for returnees' papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption claims about named people; exam-fraud allegations; ethnic hiring.
- **Held for review:** none.

4. Search for "Герои Социалистического Труда Кабардино-Балкарии" lists.
