# Kabardino-Balkaria: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Turkish for the diaspora's own word for the expulsions. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian lowland villages moved in the 19th century** against those left in place: whether a village's own removal is remembered.
- **Balkars of Chegem, Baksan and Cherek** against **mixed Balkar-Kabardian and Balkar-Russian households**: how the 1944 order treated them, and who counted as part of the removed people (*check*).
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks**: memory of the 1920 removals of Terek stanitsas elsewhere on the Terek (*check*).
- **Muslim currents**: the official Spiritual Board's congregations against younger groups labeled "Wahhabi"; who calls whom impure is the question to research.
- **Folk healers' clients** in villages against urban patients of clinics.
- **Ossetians and Meskhetian Turks**: the latter with their own expulsion history.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet purges and deportation documents; mixed for folk cleansing; thin and dangerous for 2000s religious purges.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Turkish for the diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the security services' archives are closed; "extremist" lists make some materials illegal to hold or share in Russia; informants on religious purges may face risk; never reproduce banned texts.
- **Held for review:** none.
