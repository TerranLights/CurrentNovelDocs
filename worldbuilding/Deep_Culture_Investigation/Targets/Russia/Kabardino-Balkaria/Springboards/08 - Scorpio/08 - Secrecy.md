# Kabardino-Balkaria: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Turkish for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how what may, must or must not be kept hidden is judged; privacy is one facet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian and Balkar women** in households with in-law avoidance against those without: whether the speech rules survive, and where.
- **Kabardian families of different estates' descent**: whether origins are spoken of openly or kept quiet is the question.
- **Adyghe Khabze faith communities** against Muslim congregations: what each keeps private and why.
- **Families of men in the 2000s underground**: secrecy imposed by fear, not custom.
- **Russians and Cossacks**: the secrecy of Soviet life in the northern districts, and Cossack family memory of the 1920s.
- **Diaspora returnees from Turkey and Syria**: habits of reticence learned elsewhere.
- **People living with HIV or mental illness**: secrecy against neighbors and kin, across all peoples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for avoidance speech and folklore; mixed for genealogy; thin and closed for state secrets and the underground.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Turkish for the diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some knowledge belongs to families and faith groups and should be recorded only as they allow; never reproduce clandestine materials; health secrets and lineage origins can harm people if published; keep informants anonymous.
- **Held for review:** none.
