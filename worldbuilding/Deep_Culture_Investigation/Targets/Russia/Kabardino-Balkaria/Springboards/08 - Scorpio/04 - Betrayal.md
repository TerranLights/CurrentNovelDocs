# Kabardino-Balkaria: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Balkars and Kabardians** on 1944: one people deported, the other not; how each remembers the neighbors' conduct is the question (handle with care).
- **Kabardian families of princely and of common descent**: the 1557 and Caucasian War debates may divide by lineage memory.
- **Families of insurgents and families of police officers** in the same villages: each may name the other's sons as traitors.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks**: Civil War memory of Reds and Whites and of neighbors who turned.
- **Diaspora Circassians in Turkey, Jordan and Syria** against homeland Kabardians: who left and who stayed is itself read as loyalty or betrayal by some.
- **Ossetian and Meskhetian Turkish communities**: their own wartime and resettlement accusations.
- **Women and men**: infidelity and second marriages are judged by different standards, a matter to test, not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical and folk betrayal; mixed for wartime collaboration; thin and private for infidelity.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian, Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective-treason charges still wound; accusations of collaboration can inflame ethnic tension between the two titular peoples; informer networks are a live danger; marital infidelity is a source of shame and of violence; keep informants anonymous.
- **Held for review:** none.
