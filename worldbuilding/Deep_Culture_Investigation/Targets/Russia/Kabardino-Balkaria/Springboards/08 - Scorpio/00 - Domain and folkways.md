## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardians of the plains and foothills** (Baksan, Zolsky, Chegem, Urvan, Terek districts): separate by district, near Nalchik against remote villages.
- **Balkars of the five old mountain societies** (Malkar/Cherek, Khulam, Bezengi, Chegem, Urusbi/Baksan): separate by gorge; the deportation and return reshaped each differently.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks** (Prokhladny, Maysky): Orthodox funeral and inheritance ways, Cossack memory of the 1920s.
- **Ossetians** in their villages, **Meskhetian Turks**, **Mountain Jews** of Nalchik (largely emigrated), Koreans, Armenians, Georgians, Ukrainians: each a separate set of death ways.
- **Returnees**: Syrian Circassians resettled after 2012; Balkars born in Central Asian exile.
- **Diasporas**: Circassians in Turkey (Uzunyayla), Jordan, Syria and Israel; Balkars in Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Turkey.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for funeral and wedding ethnography of both titular peoples; mixed for inheritance practice; thin and hard for sexuality, household money and the hidden.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Turkish and Arabic for the diaspora; Ossetian for minor units.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1864 expulsions and the 1944 deportation are living grief; the 2005 Nalchik attack and later counterinsurgency deaths are politically dangerous to research and to name sources; sexual diversity is legally restricted in Russia, so informants may be at risk. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (the tracker lists only "Russia"); these Scorpio sheets are written for the core lands of the present republic.
- **Held for review:** none.
