## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and young men at Kabardian and Balkar feasts**: seating and toasts show who is acknowledged first.
- **Women**: whose work at home and in the fields was named on honor boards, and whose was not.
- **Families of former princely and noble lineages against commoner families**: inherited against earned standing.
- **Balkars of the Chegem, Baksan, Khulam-Bezengi and Cherek gorges**: recognition of the deportation as a community matter, valley by valley.
- **Tyrnyauz and Prokhladny industrial workers**: the Soviet honor system in mixed towns.
- **Terek Cossacks**: recognition sought for Cossack service and history.
- **Meskhetian Turks and Mountain Jews**: recognition as communities in the republic's lists of peoples.
- **Circassian returnees**: recognition as compatriots, and of the 1864 expulsion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors and folklore; thin for everyday credit at work.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for songs and the press; Georgian and Turkish for recognition debates abroad.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genocide recognition and the deportation are political; rehabilitation acts are law, so their wording belongs to Libra and their spirit to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
