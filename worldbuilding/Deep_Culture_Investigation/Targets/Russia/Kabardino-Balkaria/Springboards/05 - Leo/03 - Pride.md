# Kabardino-Balkaria: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian lineages of former noble and of commoner descent**: pride in a name may differ with the estate a family remembers.
- **Balkar tukhums of each gorge**: valley pride, and pride in having returned from exile.
- **Kabardians in Nalchik against those in Baksan or Tersky villages**: urban and village pride may attach to different things.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks**: Cossack pride in service and in the stanitsa, against republic symbols.
- **Mountain Jews of Nalchik**: pride in a long presence and in an emigrated community abroad.
- **Meskhetian Turks**: pride in an identity without a recognized home region.
- **Diaspora returnees**: pride shaped by life in Turkey, Syria or Jordan.
- **Athletes' families**: pride in a champion as a family and village matter.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for symbols, movements and folklore; thin for measured pride at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for proverbs and movement texts; Turkish for diaspora material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rival historical narratives (the 1557 jubilees, the Caucasian War, the deportation); Kabardian-Balkar tensions over land and districts; movement leaders under official pressure. Treat each group's pride claims as their own words, not as findings.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Look up пагэ, гушхуэн, оьхтем and махтанчакъ in the two dictionaries, with example phrases.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "адыгские пословицы" AND "гордость" and for "нарт сёзле".
3. Search *Кабардино-Балкарская правда* archives for "Навеки с Россией" and for "день возрождения балкарского народа".
4. Find Sufian Zhemukhov's articles on Circassians and Sochi in Google Scholar.
5. Collect the honor lists of the KBR wrestling and judo federations.
