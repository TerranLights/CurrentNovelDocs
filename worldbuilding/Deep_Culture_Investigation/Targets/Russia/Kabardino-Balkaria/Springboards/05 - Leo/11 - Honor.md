*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Turkish for Meskhetian and diaspora sources. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian men and women**: honor may rest on courage for one and on reputation for the other; to be tested, not assumed.
- **Descendants of noble and of commoner families**: the old code was tied to rank.
- **Balkar families of each gorge**: namys and feud memory may differ by valley.
- **Young men in towns and old men in villages**: honor talk in sport and street against honor talk at the table.
- **Veterans and their families**: honor in service, and the deportation that cut Balkar soldiers off.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks**: service honor and Orthodox honor.
- **Meskhetian Turks**: family honor in a Turkish idiom.
- **Diaspora returnees**: honor codes kept in Turkey, Syria and Jordan, brought back.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for custom and history; thin and sensitive for current practice.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for the code's terms and songs; Turkish for Meskhetian and diaspora material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence against women in the name of honor; blood feuds; the deportation and soldiers' service. Feud and revenge belong to Scorpio; customary law to Libra (the letter) and Capricorn (the spirit). Never name private families.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Look up напэ, напэншэ, намыс and намыссыз in the two dictionaries, with all example phrases.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "Андемиркан" and for "уоркъ хабзэ".
3. Search for "аталычество кабардинцев" and read the earliest studies first.
4. Find a history of the Kabardian Horse Regiment in the National Library of the KBR catalog.
5. Check the 2018 Legal Initiative report for the regions it covers.
