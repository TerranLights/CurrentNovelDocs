# Kabardino-Balkaria: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; German for early travelers. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders in Kabardian villages and in Balkar gorge villages**: whether care at home or in an institution is seen as dignified.
- **Descendants of former bondsmen and of noble lineages**: memory of unequal worth may linger in marriage choices.
- **Deportation survivors and their children**: dignity kept or lost in exile is a living topic.
- **Disabled people in towns and villages**: access and respect differ with distance from Nalchik.
- **Observant Muslims and secular families**: religious and Soviet humanist grounds for human worth.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks**: Orthodox and Soviet frames of dignity.
- **Meskhetian Turks and Mountain Jews**: minorities' experience of respect in public offices.
- **Men who work away in Moscow or the north**: dignity at work far from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; ethics and ritual studies are rich, while measured treatment is thin and politically shaded.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for ethical terms; German and French for early travelers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** torture and detention reports; the deportation; the history of bondage. Rights and courts belong to Libra (the letter) and Capricorn (the spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
