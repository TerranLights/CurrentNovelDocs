# Kabardino-Balkaria: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian; Turkish for Meskhetian sources. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men in Kabardian and Balkar villages**: whether an insult must be answered by a fight, a family talk or nothing.
- **Young women**: reputation and online exposure as the main channel of shaming.
- **Balkar communities near disputed pastures and Kabardian riders on commemorations**: group insult around history and land.
- **Conscripts and their families**: army hazing as a rite many men pass through.
- **Meskhetian Turks**: twice removed, now a small minority in Nalchik and the districts.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks**: Cossack memory of de-Cossackization as a collective lowering.
- **Mountain Jews**: memories of hostility and of emigration.
- **Repatriates from Syria**: arriving as refugees and the standing that comes with it.
