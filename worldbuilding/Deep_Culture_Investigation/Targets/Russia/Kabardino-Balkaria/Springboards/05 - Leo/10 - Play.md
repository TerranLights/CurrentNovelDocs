# Kabardino-Balkaria: Play (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian. **Concept in one line:** activity done for its own sake, set apart from ordinary life by rules or make-believe: games, sport as play, pretending, joking, toying.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Boys and girls**: games, toys and permitted rough play may split by sex early.
- **Children in Balkar summer pastures and in lowland villages**: play shaped by herding and terrain.
- **Nalchik apartment children and village children**: courtyard play against open land.
- **Young men**: wrestling, dance circles and online games as adult play.
- **Elderly men**: backgammon, dominoes and talk in public places.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks**: Cossack games and holiday play.
- **Mountain Jews and Meskhetian Turks**: family games and holiday play of small communities.
- **Observant families**: religious limits on music, dance and games of chance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk games, thin for current play time and digital play at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for game names, rhymes and riddles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** street dancing by young men from the North Caucasus is a flash point in other Russian cities; gambling is restricted by Russian law and by religious teaching. Competition itself belongs to Aries.
- **Held for review:** none.
