## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families who keep the noble code** in memory against those for whom it was an imposed standard.
- **Soviet internationalists**, now elderly, whose ideal was the friendship of peoples.
- **Circassian unity activists** against Balkar activists with their own territorial ideal.
- **Devout believers** whose ideal is a godly community, and the small armed minority who pursued it by force.
- **Women**: the ideal of the *гуащэ* (lady) and how it is held or refused *check*.
- **Diaspora and returnees**: the ideal homeland against the real one.
- **Russians and Cossacks**, with their own ideals of the frontier and the state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and literature; thin in measured data on ideals for the republic itself.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for epics, proverbs and codes; Russian for scholarship; Turkish for diaspora writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pan-Circassian and Balkar territorial visions are politically charged; anniversaries of the "union with Russia" are contested; militant ideology must be read only through scholarship, never as a voice for believers in general.
- **Held for review:** none.
