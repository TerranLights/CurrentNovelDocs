## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women**: whose tears, anger or tenderness may be seen, and where.
- **Elders and the young**: restraint before seniors as a daily discipline.
- **Daughters-in-law and fathers of small children**, the two roles most bound by avoidance.
- **Families following reformist religious teaching** against those keeping older lament customs.
- **High-pasture shepherds** and their solitude.
- **Mountain guides and rescuers** of the Elbrus district.
- **Russians and Cossacks**, whose display rules may differ from both titular peoples.
- **Nalchik professionals** (doctors, teachers) trained in professional distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for avoidance and mourning customs in ethnography; thin for measured emotional styles or loneliness in the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for laments and restraint vocabulary; Russian for scholarship; Arabic terms for religious teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental illness and suicide carry strong stigma; mourning practices are a point of dispute between religious currents. Impartiality belongs to Libra; letting go and compassion to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** none.
