## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of princely and noble houses** (Kabardian *пщы* and *уэркъ*, Balkar *таубий*) against descendants of commoners and dependents: whether rank is still recalled at marriage.
- **Kabardians, Balkars and Russians** as peoples: each one's view of whether the republic treats them alike.
- **Women in Nalchik** against women in high villages: schooling, work and voice in family councils.
- **Mountain districts** (Chereksky, Elbrus) against the richer plains and the capital.
- **Meskhetian Turks, Ossetians and other small groups**, who hold no share in the titular balance.
- **Repatriates**, often counted as newcomers despite shared descent.
- **Soviet-schooled elders** against the post-1991 generation on leveling and private wealth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for estate history and Soviet ideology; mixed for present attitudes; thin for statistics broken down by people.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for songs, sayings and local press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent from dependents can still be a family matter; ethnic shares of office and land are politically charged; gender questions touch on religion and family honor. Justice and fairness belong to Libra; hierarchy to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
