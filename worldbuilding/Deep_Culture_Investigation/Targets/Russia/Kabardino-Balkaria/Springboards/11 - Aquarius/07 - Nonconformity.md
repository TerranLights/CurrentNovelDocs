# Kabardino-Balkaria: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far that departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Abrek memory** in Kabardian against Balkar villages: who was sung as a hero.
- **Young pious believers** against their parents' generation.
- **Women** who refuse an arranged match, stay unmarried or work far from home.
- **Nalchik's informal scenes** (musicians, artists, students) against village youth.
- **Russian and Cossack youth**, whose norms differ in dress and drink.
- **Religious converts** in any direction (to Orthodoxy, Protestantism, Islam, the old Khabze faith).
- **Returnees** marked as different by manners.
- **Sexual and gender minorities**, mostly hidden.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for abrek songs and festival clowns; thin and scattered for present-day nonconformity, much of it online.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar for songs and online talk; Russian for scholarship and press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sexual and gender minorities face legal and family danger; do not seek or publish identifying details. Religious nonconformity was policed as extremism in the 2000s, so sources are colored by security concerns. Freedom as such belongs to Sagittarius; crime to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** none.
