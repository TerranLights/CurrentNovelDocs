*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kabardino-Balkaria, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for religious intercession. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between who stands between parties. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardian and Balkar courtship customs**: whether the envoy is a kinsman, an elder or a friend, and how far each people relies on him.
- **Village quarrels and Nalchik quarrels**: elders and imams in the villages against police, officials or no one in the city.
- **Balkar villages near Nalchik**: who has been named as go-between in their land disputes, and who trusted whom.
- **Terek Cossacks and other Russian residents**: atamans and priests as their own third parties.
- **Women in each people**: their roles in making peace inside the family, and whether these are public.
- **Repatriate families**: children carrying their parents' words to offices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; much ethnography on courtship and feud reconciliation, little on present-day informal mediation; press archives cover public standoffs.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blood feud and its settlement; land disputes between the two titular peoples; security operations and protests, where go-betweens may be at risk.
- **Held for review:** none.
