*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kabardians and Balkars**: two different language families give two kin-term systems; whether the duties attached to them differ too.
- **Former Kabardian princely and Balkar taubiy lineages against commoner families**: whether seniority among brothers once carried more weight in ranked houses.
- **Large rural families in the valleys and smaller families in Nalchik and Prokhladny**: what work older children take on in each.
- **Russian and Cossack families**: their own sibling terms and inheritance customs.
- **Families of seasonal and long-term workers in Moscow and the north**: whether elder or younger brothers leave, and who stays with the parents.
- **Families split by the 1944 exile**: siblings raised apart.
- **Repatriate families**: brothers and sisters spread across Syria, Turkey, Jordan and KBR.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on kinship terms and folklore; demographic data good; thin on sibling relations as lived today.
- **Languages needed:** Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions on brothers and sisters' reputation may touch on honor-related violence; inheritance quarrels (legal side to Libra); exile separations.
- **Held for review:** none.
