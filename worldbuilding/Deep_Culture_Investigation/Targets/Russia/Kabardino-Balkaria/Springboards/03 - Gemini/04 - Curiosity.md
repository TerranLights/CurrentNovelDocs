## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages near the Baksan observatory and in Terskol and Elbrus**: whether daily contact with scientists and climbers changes what children ask about.
- **Balkar families of the Chegem and Cherek gorges**: how they view outsiders studying the crypts and towers in their gorges.
- **Kabardian families with strong lineage memory**: how far questions about a person's лъэпкъ are taken as interest or as prying.
- **Russian and Cossack settlements in the plains**: which questions count as rude or as friendly there.
- **Teenagers and university students in Nalchik**: online curiosity and its limits within families.
- **Observant Muslims and secular families**: what may be asked about faith.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for the concept itself; rich side routes through folklore, science outreach and mountain history.
