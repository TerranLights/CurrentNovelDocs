## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elbrus district and upper gorge villages (Tyrnyauz, Elbrus, Bezengi)**: whether signal gaps, hazard warnings and weather knowledge set them apart from the plains.
- **Kabardian and Balkar readerships**: each has its own paper; whether trust and topics part ways.
- **Russian-speaking Prokhladny and Maysky**: whether federal media outweigh republic outlets there.
- **Older people on television against young people on Telegram and Instagram**: whether the main source changes by age.
- **Observant Muslims**: religious sites and preachers online as a separate information stream, and how authorities treat it.
- **Diaspora-linked families** (Syria, Turkey, Jordan): news brought in from abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; national surveys exist but rarely break out KBR; press archives are good; online and rumor data must be gathered fresh.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first, then Kabardian and Karachay-Balkar; Turkish for diaspora media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some independent and foreign-funded outlets carry Russian legal labels, so access and citation need care; the 2005 attack and later security operations; deportation records with personal names.
- **Held for review:** none.
