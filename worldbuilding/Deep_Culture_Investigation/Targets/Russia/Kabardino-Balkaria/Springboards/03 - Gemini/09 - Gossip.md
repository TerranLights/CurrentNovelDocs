## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small mountain villages and Nalchik**: whether everyone knowing everyone changes what may be done without being talked about.
- **Women and men in each people**: who is said to gossip, who is talked about more, and whose reputation suffers most; a claim to test, not adopt.
- **Young people online and elders offline**: whether anonymous groups replace or feed the village's talk.
- **Observant Muslims and secular families**: religious teaching on backbiting as a brake, or not.
- **Russian and Cossack stanitsas**: their own places and words for talk.
- **Repatriate families**: newcomers as the subject of talk, and how they learn its rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin on gossip itself; rich side routes through folklore, shame and honor studies, and online groups.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Kabardian, Karachay-Balkar; Arabic terms for religious teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shaming of women and its possible links to honor-related harm; anonymous groups that name private people (do not copy names); rumors around security operations.
- **Held for review:** none.
