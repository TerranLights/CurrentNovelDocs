*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iron and Digor speakers:** language loss and literary absorption press on each form differently.
- **City youth in Vladikavkaz and village youth:** the main line of language shift; check, do not assume its direction.
- **Highland gorges emptied by resettlement, and the plains villages they moved to:** a whole society's form taken apart and rebuilt.
- **Terek Cossacks:** a host ended and partly revived; their own account first.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** deportation in 1944 and return; how their own communities came apart and re-formed.
- **Greeks, Germans and other small city communities of Vladikavkaz and Mozdok:** groups that thinned through emigration or blending.
- **Uatsdin believers and urban secular Ossetians:** two different senses of merging with land and people.
