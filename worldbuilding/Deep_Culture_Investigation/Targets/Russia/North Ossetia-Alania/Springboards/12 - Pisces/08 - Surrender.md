# North Ossetia-Alania: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor); Ingush; Arabic religious terms as used locally. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death), with release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox, Muslim and traditional-faith Ossetians:** смирение, tawakkul and the feast-offering frame give different words for yielding.
- **Ingush followers of Kunta-Haji's wird and others:** a teaching of non-resistance beside other Ingush views; their own accounts.
- **Highland and plains families:** rites of release for the dead differ by gorge and by distance from the old graves.
- **Beslan families:** acceptance and refusal side by side.
- **Karmadon and Gizeldon valley communities:** loss without bodies.
- **Terek Cossacks:** memory of defeat and of the ending of the host.
- **Older and younger generations:** sayings of fate against newer talk of control and self-help; test, do not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mourning ritual and historical commemoration; thin for measured attitudes and end-of-life care.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor) for sayings and rites; Ingush and Arabic terms for Muslim teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan and Karmadon families; Sufi teaching in the region; the accession history is political and contested; Soviet repression memories in living families.
- **Held for review:** none.
