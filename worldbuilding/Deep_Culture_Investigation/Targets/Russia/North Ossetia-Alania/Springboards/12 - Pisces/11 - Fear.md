# North Ossetia-Alania: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor); Ingush; Kabardian. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beslan and the Pravoberezhny district:** a town shaped by one event; its fears may differ from the rest of the republic.
- **Gorge villages under mudflow and glacier risk (Genaldon, Tsey, Digor):** fear of the land itself.
- **Prigorodny district, Ossetian and Ingush villages:** fear of the neighbor after 1992, on both sides.
- **Mozdok district:** a garrison town that lived through attacks; Cossack, Kabardian and Kumyk neighbors.
- **Shepherds and city dwellers:** wolves and weather against crime and terror.
- **Women and men:** who may show fear and who must hide it; test, do not assume.
- **Refugees from South Ossetia and inner Georgia:** fear carried from war.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; clinical studies of Beslan and hazard science are good; regional opinion data are thin; folklore is rich.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor); Ingush and Kabardian for minority lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan survivors, especially children named in studies; the 1992 conflict; terror attacks; hazard zones where families still live. Never reproduce case details.
- **Held for review:** none.
