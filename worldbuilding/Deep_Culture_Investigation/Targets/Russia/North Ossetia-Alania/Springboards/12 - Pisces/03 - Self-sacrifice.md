*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland and plains villages:** obelisk rolls and family stories differ with resettlement; compare by gorge.
- **Mothers, fathers, sons and daughters:** who is expected to give up what, inside the household; test, do not assume.
- **Orthodox, Muslim and traditional-faith Ossetians:** martyrdom, sacrifice at Kurban Bayram and offering at the dzuar are three distinct frames.
- **Digoria:** a gorge with its own feast calendar and a Muslim strand; separate records.
- **Terek Cossacks of Mozdok and the stanitsas:** their own memory of service, and of the losses of the Civil War years.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** their own memorials and terms, kept apart from Ossetian ones.
- **Beslan families:** honors given against their own accounts of loss.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war memory and honors; mixed for ritual offering; thin for household self-denial and caregiving.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor) for songs, inscriptions and feast speech; Ingush and Kabardian for minority memorials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan, the 1992 conflict and recent wars; animal sacrifice at shrines may be filmed only with consent; living families of the named dead.
- **Held for review:** none.
