# North Ossetia-Alania: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor); Ingush. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is researched only in Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); this sheet takes the idea of wrongdoing and of the wrongdoer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland lineages and plains villages:** feud memory and shame customs may differ with distance from the old towers.
- **Iron and Digor areas:** separate tale and custom records; Digoria's princely past shaped who could wrong whom.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** their own feud, shame and reconciliation terms; never framed by the other side alone.
- **Muslim and Christian Ossetians:** sin, shame and repentance in two religious frames.
- **Young men in sport and street groups of Vladikavkaz:** the outlaw's prestige against family disgrace; test, do not assume.
- **Terek Cossacks:** their own codes and memories of raids.
- **Families of the repressed and of terror victims:** two kinds of victim memory.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and feud ethnography; mixed for statistics; thin for measured moral judgments in the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor); Ingush.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** terrorism, the 1992 conflict and feuds involve living families; never name suspects or victims from news. Bride abduction stories may identify women. Customary and state law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
