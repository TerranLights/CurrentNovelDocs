*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men:** whose hunches are trusted (the wise elder woman of the household; the hunter) is a question to test.
- **Hunters, herders and farmers of the gorges:** knowledge of land and animals differs by gorge and trade.
- **Traditional-faith, Orthodox and Muslim Ossetians:** feast signs, saints' guidance and istikhara are three frames for the same act.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** Islamic and older Vainakh ways of reading signs.
- **Terek Cossacks:** omen lore with its own roots.
- **Vladikavkaz professionals and the city's paid psychics:** the expert's eye against the paid seer.
- **Elders and the young:** omens kept, mocked or revived online.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and ethnography; thin in measured studies of intuition.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor), including hunters' vocabulary; Ingush; Arabic terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some divination and hunting lore is held within families or trades; record only what is shared. Psychic services: study the trade, not the claims.
- **Held for review:** none.
