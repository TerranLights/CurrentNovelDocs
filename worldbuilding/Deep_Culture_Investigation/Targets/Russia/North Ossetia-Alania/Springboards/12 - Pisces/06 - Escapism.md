# North Ossetia-Alania: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women; young and old:** who drinks, games or leaves, and who is shamed for it; test, do not assume.
- **Orthodox, Muslim and traditional-faith Ossetians:** different lines between sacred drink and forbidden drink.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** Muslim norms on alcohol and their own forms of escape and leisure.
- **Vladikavkaz and the highland and plains villages:** city nightlife and cafés against village feasts and gorge retreats.
- **Mozdok district:** a garrison town with Cossack, Kabardian and Kumyk neighbors; its own patterns.
- **Ossetians in Turkey and Syria:** descendants of those who left in the 19th century.
- **Refugees and returnees from South Ossetia:** escape from war, and what it led to.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; official health and death data exist but undercount; feast ethnography is rich; gambling and drug data are thin and mostly in court or news reports.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor) for toasts, songs and abrek lore; Ingush; Turkish for the emigrant communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** addiction cases and court records name people; do not reproduce names. The illegal alcohol trade overlaps with crime (sheet 10). Muslim and Christian views on drink differ sharply; give both.
- **Held for review:** none.
