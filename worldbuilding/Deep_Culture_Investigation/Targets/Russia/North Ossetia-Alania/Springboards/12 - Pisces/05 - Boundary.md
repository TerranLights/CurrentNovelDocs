# North Ossetia-Alania: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor); Ingush; Georgian for the southern frontier. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brides, wives and mothers-in-law; men of the ныхас:** the household and village lines fall on each differently; test, do not assume.
- **Iron and Digor families:** in-law customs and house plans may differ by gorge.
- **Muslim Ossetians:** added purity lines in food and dress.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** clan lines and a contested district line; their own accounts first.
- **Villages on the Georgian Military Road:** life beside a closed or open border.
- **Refugees from South Ossetia and inner Georgia:** people who crossed the line and live on its other side.
- **Terek Cossack stanitsas:** a settlement form built as a frontier, with its own lines toward neighbors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household ethnography and frontier history; thin for measured personal space and privacy.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor); Ingush; Georgian for the southern crossing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Prigorodny dispute and the north-south Ossetian question are live; give every side. Shrines closed to some visitors: do not enter or photograph without leave.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for «обычай избегания осетин» and «невестка осетинская семья».
2. Find the National Museum's catalog entries for «рæхыс» or «надочажная цепь».
3. Read the 1996 Human Rights Watch report beside an Ossetian and an Ingush account of the district.
4. Search news archives for «Верхний Ларс очередь» to map how the crossing is lived.
5. Search «ныхас осетинский обычай» for studies of the village assembly place.
