*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Public-sector households** in Vladikavkaz against **private and informal earners.**
- **Mountain villagers** exposed to mudflows and cut-off roads against **plains farmers** with land and stores.
- **Pensioners** and **young people** looking for work in Moscow: a fixed state income against work far from home.
- **Beslan families** and **school communities** after 2004: physical safety at the center of daily life.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny district** and **Ossetian refugees** from Georgia and South Ossetia: displacement as the deepest loss of security.
- **Mozdok district:** Terek Cossacks, Kumyks and Kabardians near the military base.
- **Armenian and Georgian families of Vladikavkaz:** church and community support.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official income and employment statistics; mixed on felt security; thin on private coping in Ossetian.
- **Languages needed:** Russian above all; Ossetian for kin practice; Ingush for displaced families' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan, the 2010 Vladikavkaz market bombing, Prigorodny district, and security-service matters. Treat victims' groups with care and avoid one voice. Policing and criminal law go to Libra or Capricorn; national defense is not pointed to here.
- **Held for review:** none.
