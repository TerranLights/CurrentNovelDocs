*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Christian, Uatsdin and Muslim Ossetians:** three religious rankings of goods, sometimes in one family.
- **Elders and the young;** village councils (ныхас) against city youth online.
- **Iron and Digor communities;** Kurtatin and Tagaur against Digoria, for differing custom.
- **Vladikavkaz intelligentsia** against **rural districts:** reformers against keepers of custom.
- **Kabardians and Kumyks of Mozdok district:** Muslim codes of their own.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny district:** a separate code of conduct.
- **Terek Cossacks:** Orthodox and military service values.
- **Armenian and Georgian parishes of Vladikavkaz:** church-taught rankings outside the Ossetian frame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: some survey data on values in the North Caucasus, rich normative ethnography; weak on the gap between stated and lived values.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian; Kabardian and Ingush for their codes; Greek or a translation for Nicholas Mystikos.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious rivalry between Orthodox and traditional-faith groups; contested monuments; never describe one "Ossetian mentality". Customary law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
