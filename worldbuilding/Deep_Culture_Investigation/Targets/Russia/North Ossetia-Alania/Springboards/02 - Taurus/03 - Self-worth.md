# North Ossetia-Alania: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kabardian, Kumyk, Armenian and Georgian for minority communities. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is held to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and juniors:** worth assigned by age at the feast against worth earned by deeds.
- **Men and women:** the kerchief custom, the bride's position, mothers of the war dead.
- **Descendants of former noble families** (Tagaur aldars, Digor badeliata) against commoner lineages: inherited against earned standing.
- **Iron and Digor speakers;** Christian, Muslim and Uatsdin Ossetians: worth from God, from custom or from lineage, weighted differently.
- **Vladikavkaz professionals** and **labor migrants returning from Moscow:** money and education as new bases of esteem.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny district, Kabardians and Kumyks of Mozdok district, Terek Cossacks:** each judges standing by its own code and service record.
- **Refugees from South Ossetia and inner Georgia;** Beslan families after 2004: worth after loss and displacement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: much ethnography of honor and etiquette, a little psychology; very little first-person writing on low self-worth or private shame.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian; Ingush and Kabardian for their codes; French for Dumézil.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** feud, Beslan grief and interethnic standing after 1992. Never present one "Ossetian honor" as the voice of all; name variation. Feud law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
