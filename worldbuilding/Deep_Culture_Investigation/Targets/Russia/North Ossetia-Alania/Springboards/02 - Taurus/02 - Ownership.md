## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain lineages of the Tagaur, Kurtatin and Alagir societies:** towers and meadows held by kin.
- **Former estates** (Tagaur aldars, Digor badeliata) and their former dependents: unequal claims to land.
- **Plains villages founded by resettlement:** land given, not inherited.
- **Terek Cossacks of Mozdok district:** host land, allotments, the losses of the 1920s.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny district** and **Ossetian residents there:** rival histories of the same houses.
- **Kumyk and Kabardian villages of Mozdok district:** their own descent and land histories.
- **Vladikavkaz owners, renters and refugee households** from South Ossetia and inner Georgia: titled, rented and provisional claims to a home.
- **Women:** dowry and household property, as ethnography records it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on historical landholding and current registry statistics; contested and partisan on Prigorodny district.
- **Languages needed:** Russian above all; Ossetian for legends and terms; Ingush for Ingush accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Prigorodny district and the 1944 deportation remain live; use both communities' own sources and never one voice. Land law, customary law (Kovalevsky's studies of mountain custom) and courts go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
