*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women;** **older and younger generations,** on whether jealousy reads as love or as control.
- **Mothers-in-law and daughters-in-law** in extended households: holding on to a son and to household authority.
- **Iron and Digor speakers:** the language-status question.
- **Christian, Muslim and Uatsdin families:** different guarding of women and daughters.
- **Mountain villages** against **Vladikavkaz:** watchfulness of a small community against urban anonymity.
- **Kabardians and Kumyks of Mozdok district;** **Terek Cossacks:** separate customs of guarding wives, daughters and land.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny district:** own customs of courtship.
- **Villages on contested pastures** near the Kabardino-Balkarian line: turf guarded against neighbors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: much ethnography on wedding custom and envy beliefs; thin and uneven data on control in couples; no regional violence survey known.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian; Kabardian, Kumyk and Ingush for minority customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence, bride abduction and the public policing of women; name no private individuals and never present one community's practice as all. Criminal law and family law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
