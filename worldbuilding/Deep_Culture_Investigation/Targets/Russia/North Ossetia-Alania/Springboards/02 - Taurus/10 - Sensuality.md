## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain households** (cheese, meat, beer) against **plains farmers** (maize, vegetables, fruit).
- **Christian, Uatsdin and Muslim Ossetians:** drink, fasting and shrine abstentions.
- **Women and men:** who cooks, who dances, who may sing in public.
- **Vladikavkaz** restaurant and café life against **village feasts:** pleasure bought against pleasure hosted.
- **Digor and Iron:** song and dance repertories.
- **Kabardians and Kumyks of Mozdok district** (dance gatherings, wedding music) and **Terek Cossacks** (bath, song, table).
- **Georgians and Armenians of Vladikavkaz:** their own feast tables.
- **The old and the sick** at sanatoria: rest and cure; **the young** online: food and dance as shared images.
