## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of noble families** and **commoner lineages:** old wealth against earned.
- **Mountain herders** against **plains grain and maize farmers:** wealth counted in head of stock or in harvests.
- **Vladikavkaz traders and professionals;** the 1990s new rich: salaried comfort against sudden fortune.
- **Armenian merchant families** of Vladikavkaz and Mozdok: trade wealth over generations.
- **Terek Cossacks:** dekulakization and later recovery.
- **Kumyks and Kabardians of Mozdok district;** **Ingush of Prigorodny district:** alms, bride-wealth and display under their own customs.
- **Christian, Muslim and traditional-faith Ossetians:** charity, alms and feast spending.
- **Diaspora businesspeople in Moscow:** money made elsewhere and shown at home in houses and feasts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good regional statistics and imperial almanacs; thin and often sensational on how fortunes were made since 1991.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian; Arabic or translations for medieval geographers; Armenian for merchant records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** crime and the alcohol trade; named living people in rich lists; dekulakization memory. Report only what sources state, and keep to named sources. Taxation law goes to Libra.
