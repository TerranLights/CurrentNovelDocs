# North Ossetia-Alania: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kumyk, Kabardian and Armenian for minority communities. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain villages of Kurtatin and Alagir gorges** against **plains villages**: crypts and towers as family-held places against lowland houses and yards.
- **Digor households** (Digora, Chikola), including **Digor Muslims**: a separate line of funeral and gift practice.
- **Vladikavkaz apartment dwellers:** Soviet and post-Soviet belongings, the secondhand trade.
- **Terek Cossack stanitsas of Mozdok district:** chests, icons, dowry.
- **Kumyks and Kabardians of Mozdok district:** Muslim practice around the dead person's things.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny district:** displacement and return after 1992.
- **Armenians and Georgians of Vladikavkaz:** church-centered heirlooms and gifts.
- **Refugees from South Ossetia and inner Georgia:** households rebuilt from what was carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on funeral and shrine objects in ethnography and archaeology; thin on everyday belongings, clutter, lending and collecting today.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian (Iron and Digor); Ingush and Kumyk for minority practice; Armenian for parish records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** property lost in Prigorodny district in 1992; personal effects of Beslan victims, kept in memorial settings; shrine objects some keepers do not show. Theft and property rights as legal questions go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
