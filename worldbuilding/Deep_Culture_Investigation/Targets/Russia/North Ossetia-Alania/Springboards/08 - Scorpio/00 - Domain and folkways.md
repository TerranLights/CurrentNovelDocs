## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iron and Digor Ossetians:** separate dialects, literatures and gorges; Digoria has Christian and Muslim families.
- **The old mountain societies (Tagaur, Kurtatin, Alagir, Digor):** distinct nobilities, crypt types and memorial calendars.
- **Mountain villages and the plains:** 20th-century resettlement left the ancestral graves in the gorges.
- **Vladikavkaz:** Armenian, Georgian and Russian cemeteries beside Ossetian ones.
- **Mozdok district:** Terek Cossacks, Kabardians, Kumyks and Armenians, each with its own rites.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district:** a Muslim community whose memory of 1944 and 1992 differs from the Ossetian record.
- **Ossetians from South Ossetia and Georgia:** refugee families with their own customs and losses.
- **Orthodox, Muslim and traditional-faith Ossetians (Уацдин *check*):** three ritual frames.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for funerals, memorial feasts, the epic and archaeology; mixed for inheritance and shared resources; thin and sensitive for sex ways. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands of the present republic.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kumyk, Kabardian, Armenian and Georgian for minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan (2004) and the Ossetian-Ingush conflict (1992) are open wounds; some shrines (дзуар) bar outsiders or women; sex and family honor are private topics. The Alan heritage is claimed by several peoples. Customary law on vengeance and inheritance belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
