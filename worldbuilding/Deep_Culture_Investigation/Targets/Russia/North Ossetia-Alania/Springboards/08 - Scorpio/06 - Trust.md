# North Ossetia-Alania: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush, Armenian and Georgian for minorities. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Patrilineal kin, neighbors and strangers:** the radius of trust, which may differ by village and by city.
- **Ossetians and Ingush of the Prigorodny district:** trust across a line of recent violence.
- **Newcomers from South Ossetia and Georgia:** how incomers earn trust in a settled community.
- **Vladikavkaz's mixed population:** Russians, Armenians and Georgians beside Ossetians in shared trade and neighborhoods.
- **Mozdok's Cossacks, Kabardians and Kumyks:** their own credit and mutual-aid customs.
- **Christian and Muslim Ossetians:** trust through intermarriage, or the limits of it.
- **Young people online:** reputations built on social networks rather than in the village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for kin mutual aid and proverbs; mixed for survey trust (national samples are small for one republic); thin for trust between ethnic groups as people feel it.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Ingush, Armenian, Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic trust after 1992 is political; asking about blat and informal credit touches corruption. Trust in police and courts is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
