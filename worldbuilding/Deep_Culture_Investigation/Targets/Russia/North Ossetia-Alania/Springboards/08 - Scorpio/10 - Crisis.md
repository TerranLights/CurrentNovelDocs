## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beslan:** a town defined by one crisis, against the rest of the republic.
- **Refugees from South Ossetia and Georgia:** waves in 1991-1992 and 2008, each with its own losses.
- **Mozdok district:** Cossacks, Kabardians and Kumyks living beside a military base and a war.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district:** displacement in 1992 and the long return.
- **Mountain villages:** mudflows, avalanches and closed roads as recurring local crises.
- **Vladikavkaz traders and shoppers:** the market as a place struck more than once.
- **Families of men of draft age in 2022:** a crisis met by leaving or staying, as a question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medieval catastrophe, war and terrorism; mixed for natural hazards and refugees; thin for personal crises and how families get through hard times.
