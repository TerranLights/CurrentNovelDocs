# North Ossetia-Alania: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kabardian and Armenian for minorities; Arabic loan terms in Muslim use. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and beliefs that the dead return.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox Ossetians:** church reopening after Soviet closure and adult baptism as a return.
- **Muslim Ossetians:** a return to Islamic practice; how kin and neighbors receive it is the question.
- **Followers of the traditional faith:** a rebirth that reaches back past Christianity.
- **Digor speakers:** a minority within the minority, reviving a separate literary norm.
- **Beslan:** a town rebuilding its schools and its daily life after 2004.
- **Families who returned to the mountain gorges:** rebirth tied to ancestral land.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district:** returns to villages after 1992, with their own idea of what was restored.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medieval conversion and identity politics; mixed for language revival; thin for addiction recovery and personal conversion.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Greek, Arabic and Georgian for the medieval sources; Ingush.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Alan heritage is contested with neighboring peoples; conversion between Christianity, Islam and the traditional faith can split families; some minority faiths face legal limits in Russia.
- **Held for review:** none.
