*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beslan families:** a town marked by the deaths of children; mourning there has its own calendar and politics.
- **Muslim Ossetian lineages of Digoria and Tagauria:** burial within a day and Islamic prayer beside Ossetian mourning forms.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district:** dead and missing from 1992, mourned in another language and faith.
- **Terek Cossack stanitsas of Mozdok district:** Orthodox Russian memorial days such as Radonitsa.
- **Families from South Ossetia:** graves left across a border, which changes how the dead are visited.
- **Mountain and plains Ossetians:** the old crypts in the gorges against village cemeteries on the plain.
- **Vladikavkaz Armenians and Georgians:** their own churches, cemeteries and requiem customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore, myth and war memory; mixed for grief as people feel it now; thin for suicide and end-of-life care.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kumyk, Armenian, Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan's bereaved; the 1992 dead and missing on both sides; suicide; terrorism deaths. Approach survivor groups only with consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
