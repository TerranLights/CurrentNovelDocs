*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Kabardian for Mozdok; Greek and Georgian for medieval sources. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it, how it is held and how it is used.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of noble and commoner lineages:** whether old estate lines still matter in marriage, office or pride.
- **Digoria and the Iron gorges:** different noble orders and different outside overlords.
- **Kabardians of Mozdok district:** their own prince and noble ranks, and their present standing in the republic.
- **Russian officials and the military in Vladikavkaz and Mozdok:** federal power on the ground.
- **The post-Soviet business elite:** money as a route to influence.
- **Women:** where they hold power in the household, lineage and office, as a question.
- **Muslim and Orthodox leadership:** clergy as brokers between the state and believers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medieval and estate history and the epic; mixed for political power today; thin for hidden power and patronage, which are seldom written down.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Kabardian; Greek and Georgian for medieval sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** naming clans or businessmen as power brokers invites libel and danger; federal security matters are closed. Authority and legitimate rule are Capricorn's; corruption as law is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
