## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and juniors:** seniority as a working system of control, worth testing across generations.
- **Brides, daughters-in-law and unmarried women:** restraints on movement and speech, which may vary by family and by village.
- **Kabardians of Mozdok district:** a different code, Adyghe xabze, governing conduct.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district:** the ezdel code and their dealings with the republic's police, two kinds of control to keep apart.
- **Mountain villages and Vladikavkaz:** the village's eyes against the city's anonymity.
- **Muslim and Christian households:** religious teaching on headship and on drink.
- **Residents under counter-terrorist regimes:** control felt from the state, not from kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for feast hierarchy and seniority; mixed for state control; thin for coercive control in couples, which is underreported.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Kabardian and Ingush for minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and family control of women are private and risky to ask about; security regimes make some questions unsafe for respondents. Self-control as discipline is Capricorn's; freedom from control is Aries's.
- **Held for review:** none.
