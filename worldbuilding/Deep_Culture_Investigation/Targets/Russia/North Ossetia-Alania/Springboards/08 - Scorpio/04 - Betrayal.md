*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of the Tagaur aldars and their former dependents:** old estate grievances may color who calls whom a traitor.
- **Ossetian and Ingush neighbors of the Prigorodny district:** each side's memory of 1992 holds its own charge of betrayal.
- **Mixed Ossetian-Georgian families:** loyalties split by the 1990s and 2008 wars.
- **Beslan families:** betrayal framed against the state rather than a person.
- **Terek Cossacks of Mozdok district:** a civil-war memory of divided stanitsas.
- **Women and men:** the standards for infidelity may differ by gender, as a question to test, not assume.
- **Canonical and Old Calendarist Orthodox:** faithfulness and betrayal in a church split.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in the epic and folk song; mixed for history and conflict; thin for infidelity and informing in daily life.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Georgian and Ingush; Greek for Lucian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charges of betrayal in the 1992 conflict and around Beslan are live and political; infidelity touches family honor and is rarely spoken of openly.
- **Held for review:** none.
