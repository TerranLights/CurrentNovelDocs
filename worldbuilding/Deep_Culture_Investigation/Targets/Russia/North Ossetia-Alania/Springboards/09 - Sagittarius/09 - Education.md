## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vladikavkaz and the districts**: university access and the brain drain from mountain and plain villages.
- **Families of the old clergy and teacher class** and those with no such past.
- **Women and men**: women's entry into higher learning and present-day gaps, as data shows.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny, Kumyks and Kabardians of Mozdok district**: language of schooling and access to universities.
- **Armenians, Georgians and Greeks in Vladikavkaz**: community schools and diaspora ties.
- **Mining-town and agrarian families**: technical degrees against the humanities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; institutional histories and statistics are plentiful in Russian; the Ossetian-language side is thinner.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian; German for Sjögren; Georgian for the early script.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** declining Ossetian-language teaching is a live political question; exam-integrity data invites stereotypes (see sheet 08).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary for "Ардонская духовная семинария" and list histories and alumni studies.
2. Pull the 2021 census education tables for the republic from Rosstat.
3. Search the national library for "осетинский алфавит история" and "Шёгрен осетинская грамматика".
4. Read the SOGU and Gorsky GAU official histories and note their founding aims.
5. Search for "отток выпускников Северная Осетия" in regional press and sociology journals.
