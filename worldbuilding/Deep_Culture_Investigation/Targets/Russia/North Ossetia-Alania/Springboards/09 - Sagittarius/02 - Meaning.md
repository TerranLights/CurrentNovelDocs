# North Ossetia-Alania: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain villages and Vladikavkaz**: whether meaning is tied to the ancestral village and its tower, or to city careers.
- **Mining and factory towns** (Sadon, Mizur, Vladikavkaz's industrial districts): work as purpose and its loss.
- **Beslan and the Pravoberezhny district**: a town where shared grief shapes public life.
- **Older and younger generations**: Soviet-era callings against post-Soviet uncertainty and outmigration.
- **Men and women**: roles named at table and in laments differ; check whose meaning gets spoken.
- **Ingush, Kumyk and Cossack communities**: their own heroic songs and sayings.
- **Diaspora and returnees**: émigrés in Europe and Turkey, workers in Moscow, and those who came back.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in epic, literature and memorial culture; thin in direct survey data on meaning.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian for laments, toasts and proverbs; Russian for nearly all scholarship and statistics; Ingush for илли.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan survivors and bereaved families; suicide data; laments may be private family property, recorded only with consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
