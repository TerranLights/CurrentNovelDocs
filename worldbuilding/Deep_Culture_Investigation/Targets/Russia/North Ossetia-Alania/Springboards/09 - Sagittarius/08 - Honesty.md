## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain-descended and plain-born Ossetians**: whether village reputation still guards a man's word in the same way.
- **Traders at Vladikavkaz and Mozdok markets** (Ossetian, Armenian, Azerbaijani, Kumyk): fair dealing by trading community.
- **Ingush and Kabardian neighbors**: their own codes of honor and courtesy.
- **Soviet-trained officials and post-Soviet business**: differing expectations of the state.
- **Students and teachers**: exam honesty seen from both sides.
- **Women and men**: whether honesty norms are asked of each in the same way.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed; ethical vocabulary and folklore are well covered; measured honesty is thin and often framed as corruption data, which needs care.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian for moral terms and satire; Ingush and Kabardian for neighboring codes; Greek for Lucian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional corruption and exam data invite stereotypes; corruption and court cases belong to Libra or Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
