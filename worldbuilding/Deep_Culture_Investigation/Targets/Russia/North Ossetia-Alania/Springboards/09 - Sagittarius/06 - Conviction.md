## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Converts and the born-in**: Orthodox, Muslim, Protestant and traditional-faith converts against families of long standing.
- **Beslan activists and other bereaved**: different ways of holding to a cause after 2004.
- **Veterans and volunteers, and those who refuse service**: conviction about war in both directions.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny**: a long-held stand on return.
- **Mozdok's Cossack associations**: a revived Cossack identity held as a principle.
- **Vladikavkaz students and young professionals**: online stands, ecological causes.
- **Elders keeping custom against change**: firmness about tradition.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed; conviction is seldom measured; case material (protest, conversion, refusal) is the main route.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian for custom debates; Arabic for al-Masʿudi in the original.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the war since 2022 and mobilization; prosecution of Jehovah's Witnesses; Beslan; some groups are dissolved or labeled; protect individuals' names.
- **Held for review:** none.
