*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ossetians who join church, traditional-faith or both**: whether belief and belonging line up, or not.
- **Muslim Ossetians of Digoria and the Tagaur villages**: belief in Islam alongside Ossetian feasts.
- **Ingush, Kumyks and Kabardians**: Muslim teaching in different languages and schools.
- **Mozdok's Cossacks and Russians**: Orthodox and folk belief from a Russian frontier lineage.
- **Armenians, Georgians and Greeks**: Christian belief outside the Russian Orthodox frame.
- **Soviet-educated scientists and engineers**: secular belief and its warrant.
- **Children and teenagers**: what they are taught to believe at home, at school, at the shrine.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; old ethnography and folklore are rich; measured data on belief in the republic is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian, German for 18th-19th c. travelers, Greek for Byzantine letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** quarrels between the church and the traditional-faith movement; banned groups under Russian law; folk beliefs can be private, especially about the dead.
- **Held for review:** none.
