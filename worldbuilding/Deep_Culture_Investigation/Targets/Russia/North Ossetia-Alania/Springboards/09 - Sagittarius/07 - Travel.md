*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kabardian, Kumyk, Georgian, Armenian; Turkish for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler (short journeys stay in Gemini).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain-born families and plain villages**: those whose ancestors made the descent, and those long settled below.
- **Mozdok's Cossacks and Kumyks**: a steppe and river frontier with its own roads.
- **People of the Darial and Upper Lars area**: life beside the main crossing.
- **Refugees from South Ossetia and Georgia**: arrival as exile.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny**: deportation (1944), return and displacement (1992).
- **Labor migrants and students in Moscow and abroad**: leaving and coming back.
- **Turkish and other diaspora Ossetians** who visit: return from far away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for road history and literature; mixed for current mobility.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian for songs and hospitality terms; Latin, French and Georgian for older travel sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Upper Lars exodus of 2022; refugees and deportation memory; the border zone near Georgia restricts some travel and research.
- **Held for review:** none.
