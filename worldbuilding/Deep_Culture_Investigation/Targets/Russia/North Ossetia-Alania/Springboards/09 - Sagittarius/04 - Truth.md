## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ossetian and Ingush communities**: rival historical accounts of Prigorodny and of Alan descent.
- **Beslan families and officials**: different answers on what happened in 2004.
- **Ossetian-language and Russian-language readers**: do they meet the same news?
- **Elders and the young**: oral authority against online sources.
- **Orthodox, traditional-faith and Muslim communities**: whose revealed truth, and whose reading of the Alan past.
- **Georgians, Armenians and Greeks of Vladikavkaz**: their own histories of the city.
- **Mozdok district**: a Cossack and Kumyk telling of the frontier past.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; contested history is richly documented on all sides; data on media trust at republic level is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian; Ingush and Georgian for rival accounts; Old Georgian for the chronicles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan, Prigorodny (1992) and the 2008 war are raw; some outside media are labeled or blocked in Russia; avoid taking a side in the Alan dispute.
- **Held for review:** none.
