## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **By sex and age:** compare risk data across groups rather than assume who takes risks.
- **Mountain-road drivers and city drivers:** different everyday exposures.
- **Miners and herders of the gorges:** risky trades with their own lore.
- **Christian, Muslim and traditional-faith families:** different teachings on drink and on tempting fate.
- **Vladikavkaz students:** survey-reachable youth.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny; Terek Cossacks and Kabardians of Mozdok:** their own daring traditions and sanctions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics (roads, mortality); mixed for folklore of rashness; thin for attitudes.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian, Ingush, Kabardian, French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Karmadon and mining deaths concern victims' families; gunfire and drug cases may name individuals; avoid casting any group as reckless.
- **Held for review:** none.
