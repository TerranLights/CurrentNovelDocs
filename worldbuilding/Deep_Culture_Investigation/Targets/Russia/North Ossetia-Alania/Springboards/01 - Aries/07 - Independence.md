## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain shepherd families against plain farmers and city wage earners:** self-provisioning differs sharply.
- **Former noble, free commoner and dependent lineages:** inherited memories of standing alone or serving.
- **Elders living alone against multigenerational houses:** household form shapes what living on one's own means, so separate the two in any survey.
- **Labor migrants and their home households:** dependence across distance.
- **Refugee families since 1990:** reliance on kin and state while re-establishing.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** *маршо* in a minority setting.
- **Terek Cossacks:** a frontier self-provisioning tradition.
- **Kabardian and Kumyk (*check*) villages of Mozdok district.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for economic history and statistics; mixed for attitudes to asking for help.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian, Ingush; Persian and Latin via translations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national independence or unification with South Ossetia is politically sensitive in Russia; feudal-estate history touches living lineages' standing.
- **Held for review:** none.
