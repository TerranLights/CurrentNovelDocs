*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women:** who may show anger openly, and who voices it through lament.
- **Elders and young men:** who is expected to calm a quarrel, and who to answer it.
- **Ossetians and Ingush of Prigorodny:** collective grievance from 1992 on both sides.
- **Beslan families:** anger at the state and at those blamed for 2004.
- **Vladikavkaz residents near the zinc plant:** environmental anger.
- **Refugee families from Georgia and South Ossetia:** grievances carried from 1990 to 1992 and 2008.
- **Christian, Muslim and traditional-faith Ossetians:** different teachings on wrath and forgiveness.
- **Terek Cossacks and Kabardians of Mozdok district:** separate grievances over land and the frontier past.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for feud and conflict history; mixed for everyday anger; thin for surveys of emotional display.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for rights reports and press; Ossetian for laments and emotion words; Ingush for the Prigorodny side; Kabardian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1992 conflict and Beslan; victims and named families must not be re-exposed; the legal side of feud custom belongs to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
