## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iron and Digor speakers:** two literary standards, separate dialect identities; Digoria (Digorsky and Irafsky districts) also has Muslim as well as Christian villages.
- **The former gorge societies (Tagaur, Kurtatin, Alagir, Digor):** older social orders with their own nobilities and sanctuaries.
- **Mountain villages against plain villages settled in the 19th and 20th centuries:** resettlement may have changed lineage ties.
- **Vladikavkaz:** an old multiethnic city; urban selves may differ from village ones.
- **Mozdok district:** Terek Cossacks, Kabardians, Kumyks (*check*), Armenians and Mozdok Ossetians, a separate frontier history.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny district:** own language and kin system, and the 1992 conflict.
- **Migrants from South Ossetia and Georgia since the 1990s:** a displaced layer.
- **Faith groups:** Orthodox, the traditional Ossetian faith, Muslims; personhood rites differ.
- **Diasporas:** Ossetians in Turkey and Syria (19th-century emigrants), and in Moscow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore, ethnography and identity politics; mixed for self-construal surveys; thin for body image at republic level.
- **Lands:** the tracker header carries only "Russia" as pre-war lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; these sheets are written for the republic's core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Ingush and Kabardian for minority districts; French for Gazdanov and the Nart scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1992 Ossetian-Ingush conflict over Prigorodny; Beslan (2004); "Alan" heritage claims are contested with neighbors; sanctuary rites may be closed to outsiders or to women.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search *Известия СОИГСИ* for "этническая идентичность осетин".
2. Pull the 2010 and 2021 census nationality and language tables for North Ossetia-Alania from Rosstat.
3. In a library catalog, find Kaloev's *Осетины* and Shnirelman's *Быть аланами*; read the chapters on family, dress and the "Alan" name.
4. Read *Tales of the Narts* for its hero portraits, then search Abaev's dictionary for *хи*, *ном*, *мыггаг*.
5. Search local news for "ЗАГС Северной Осетии популярные имена" to get recent name lists.
