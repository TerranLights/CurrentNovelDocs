## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women:** wrestling and horse contests are largely male; check women's sport, dance and school contests.
- **Lineages:** rivalry in feasts and funerals runs between families, not only persons.
- **Digor and Iron gorges:** teasing rivalry and separate games.
- **Vladikavkaz against the districts:** sport schools and exam competition concentrate in the city.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** *ях* and their own wrestling tradition.
- **Terek Cossacks and Kabardians of Mozdok district:** riding contests.
- **Ossetians of South Ossetian origin:** North-South rivalry in sport and the arts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport results and folklore of contests; mixed for attitudes to envy and fairness.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for sport and education data; Ossetian for games and dance; Ingush; Kabardian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic jokes and fan rivalries can slide into prejudice; doping and match-fixing claims name living athletes.
- **Held for review:** none.
