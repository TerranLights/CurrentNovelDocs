# North Ossetia-Alania: Beginning (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Armenian and Kabardian for community calendars; French and Greek for older sources. **Concept in one line:** how a culture treats starting: origins, firsts, new starts, and starting over after failure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Christian and traditional-faith households:** different openings of the year and of a house.
- **Muslim Ossetians and Ingush:** another calendar of beginnings.
- **Mountain-origin and plain-founded villages:** which founding story a village tells.
- **Vladikavkaz Russians, Armenians and Georgians:** the city's founding told from other sides.
- **Mozdok Cossacks and Kabardians:** a frontier founding story of their own.
- **Refugees and resettled families since 1990:** starting over within living memory.
- **Brides entering new households:** a recurring personal beginning.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for calendar and life-cycle ethnography; mixed for modern "starting over" (press and statistics only).
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian, French (Dumézil), Greek (Byzantine letters, via translation), Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** founding stories of Vladikavkaz and Prigorodny overlap disputed claims; sanctuary rites may admit only men or members.
- **Held for review:** none.
