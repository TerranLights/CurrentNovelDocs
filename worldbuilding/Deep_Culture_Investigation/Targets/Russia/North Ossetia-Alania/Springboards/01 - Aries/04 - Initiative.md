## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men and young women:** expected forms of initiative differ (the guest's table, the work party, public life).
- **Juniors in a household:** acting unasked near elders may read as zeal or as presumption.
- **Vladikavkaz small business against village farming families:** who starts a venture, and with whose permission and capital, is worth separating by setting.
- **Mountain gorges (Digoria, Kurtatin, Tsey):** tourism is the main new venture.
- **Beslan and Pravoberezhny district:** a distinct industrial and civic history.
- **Armenian, Georgian and Russian families of Vladikavkaz:** older trading and craft niches.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** *белхи* and their own business networks.
- **Mozdok district:** Cossack, Kabardian and Kumyk (*check*) farming communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business statistics and civic grants; mixed for workplace initiative; ethnography of *зиу* likely solid but scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian, Ingush.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1990s alcohol trade involves crime and named people; Beslan material concerns victims.
- **Held for review:** none.
