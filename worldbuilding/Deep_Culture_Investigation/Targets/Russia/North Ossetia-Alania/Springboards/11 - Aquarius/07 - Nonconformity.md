## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Highland villages and Vladikavkaz: a small community sees every departure; the city lets more pass unseen.
- Orthodox, Muslim and Uatsdin Ossetians: each can read the others as departing from the norm.
- Converts to Protestant churches and to other faiths.
- Young Muslims in Digoria and among the Ingush of Prigorodny: piety read as difference.
- Russians, Cossacks, Molokan descendants and Armenians: minority norms of their own.
- Women who live outside the expected roles (unmarried, childless, working far from home).
- Youth scenes in Vladikavkaz and the diaspora in Moscow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on epic and folk misfits; thin and risky on present-day dissent, religious minorities and sexual minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Iron, Digor; Ingush and Kabardian for Muslim minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** LGBT people face legal and social danger in Russia: never identify individuals. Banned faiths and protesters face prosecution. Do not call any faith a "sect" in the sheet's own voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
