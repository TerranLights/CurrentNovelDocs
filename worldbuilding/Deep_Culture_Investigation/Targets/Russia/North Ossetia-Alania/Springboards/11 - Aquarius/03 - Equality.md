*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Tagauria, where aldars held most land, against Kurtatin and Alagir, where the uazdan order differed: the estate map shaped what "equal" meant.
- Digoria, with its badeliata and Muslim and Christian houses.
- Women and men; older and younger: seniority and sex as the daily axes of rank.
- Ingush of Prigorodny, whose claims of unequal standing need their own sources.
- Russians and Terek Cossacks of the stanitsas, with their land history against Ossetian land shortage.
- Armenian and Georgian townspeople of Vladikavkaz and Mozdok.
- Rural and urban: Vladikavkaz against hill districts on income and services.
- South Ossetian newcomers and their standing as residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the estate system and the land question; mixed on gender; thin on present attitudes to equality at republic level.
