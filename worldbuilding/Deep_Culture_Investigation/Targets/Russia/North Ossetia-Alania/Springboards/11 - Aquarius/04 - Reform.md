## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Iron and Digor speakers: script and standard reforms affected each differently.
- Orthodox Ossetians, Muslim Ossetians and Uatsdin followers: three paths of religious reform.
- Village elders and urban youth: the feast-reform debates set seniority against cost.
- Women: education and marriage reforms were done to and by them.
- Vladikavkaz residents near Electrozinc against hill districts: different reform agendas.
- Russians and Cossacks of the stanitsas: reforms of Cossack status and their revival after 1991.
- Ingush of Prigorodny: reforms of status and return after 1992.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on enlighteners, schools and the church in Russian; mixed on Soviet reform locally; present-day reform from below is reported mostly in independent media.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Iron, Digor; Greek and Georgian for medieval church history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest coverage may be blocked or risky for sources; legal reform as written goes to Libra, the spirit of law to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
