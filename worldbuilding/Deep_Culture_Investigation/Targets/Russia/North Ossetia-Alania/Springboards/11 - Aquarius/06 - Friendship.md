# North Ossetia-Alania: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Iron and Digor Ossetian, Russian; Kabardian and Ingush; Greek for the classical sources. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Men and women: separate social worlds make men's and women's friendships worth studying apart.
- Highland and lowland villages, against Vladikavkaz, where friends come from school and work rather than kin.
- Muslim and Christian Ossetians: whether friendship crosses faith freely.
- Ossetians and Ingush in Prigorodny: friendships before and after 1992.
- Ossetians, Kabardians and Cossacks in Mozdok district: old kunak ties.
- Armenian and Georgian townspeople: friendship across the city's minorities.
- Wrestling and army comrades: friendship formed in shared ordeal.
- South Ossetian newcomers and North Ossetians: friendship across the range.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on kunak and fosterage in older ethnography; thin on present-day friendship, which is likely to need fieldwork and polls.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Iron, Digor; Kabardian, Ingush; Greek and French for the steppe comparisons.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Scythian-Alan-Ossetian link is a scholarly theory and a national claim: hold it apart from evidence. Cross-conflict friendships may expose people; anonymize.
- **Held for review:** none.
