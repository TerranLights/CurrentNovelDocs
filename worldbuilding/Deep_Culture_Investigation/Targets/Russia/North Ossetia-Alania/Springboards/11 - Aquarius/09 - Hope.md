# North Ossetia-Alania: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Iron and Digor Ossetian, Russian; Ingush; Avestan and Old Iranian scholarship for the oldest words. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Elders and the young: blessings at the table against hopes acted on by leaving for Moscow.
- Highland villages and Vladikavkaz: hopes for the land and the herd against hopes for work and schooling.
- Orthodox, Muslim and Uatsdin Ossetians: hope in God, the saints and the dzuars, each in its own terms.
- Beslan families: hope after catastrophe.
- Ingush of Prigorodny: the hope of return.
- South Ossetian newcomers: hopes tied to reunification.
- Russians and Cossacks of the stanitsas; Armenians of Vladikavkaz: hopes of minorities in the republic.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in ritual and folklore of blessing; mixed on measured optimism, which is mostly national; political hopes are well covered in the press but partisan.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Iron, Digor, Ingush; Iranian philology for farn.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan grief; the return of the Ingush and reunification are opposed hopes in a live dispute: give each in its own sources. Some shrine rites limit who may attend.
- **Held for review:** none.
