# North Ossetia-Alania: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Highland villages and Vladikavkaz professionals: epic and honor ideals against Soviet and civic ideals.
- Orthodox, Muslim and Uatsdin Ossetians: different ideals of the righteous person.
- Generations: Soviet-raised elders, the 1990s generation, those born after 2004.
- Digor writers and readers: their own heroic and moral literature.
- Russians and Cossacks: Cossack revival ideals after 1991.
- Ingush of Prigorodny: ideals of return and home, in their own sources.
- Émigrés and their descendants in France, Turkey and Syria.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in epic scholarship and literature; thin on measured idealism at republic level; survey data may stop at the national level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Iron, Digor; French for émigré contexts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war volunteering and heroic memory are politically charged; avoid treating honor codes as one voice for a people.
- **Held for review:** none.
