## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Tagaur, Kurtatin, Alagir and Digor: the estate order and its groups differed by gorge, so "the group" did not mean the same unit everywhere.
- Muslim and Christian Ossetians: whether faith forms a separate "we" inside one people.
- Ingush of Prigorodny: a group defined from outside by deportation and conflict; their own sources.
- Terek Cossacks of the stanitsas: an estate-born group with its own circle and atamans.
- Kabardians and Kumyks of Mozdok district: lineage forms of their own.
- Urban youth of Vladikavkaz: sports teams and friend groups alongside lineage.
- Women: how they belong to the husband's and to the father's lineage.
- Ossetian volunteers in recent wars: a group forged by service; handle with care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on lineage and estates in Russian ethnography; mixed on present-day group life; polling on ethnic "we" exists but is uneven.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Iron, Digor, Ingush, Kabardian, Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 deportation, the 1992 conflict and the feud: present each side from its own sources. Collective responsibility in custom touches law: the legal side belongs to Libra and Capricorn. Volunteer units are a live political issue.
- **Held for review:** none.
