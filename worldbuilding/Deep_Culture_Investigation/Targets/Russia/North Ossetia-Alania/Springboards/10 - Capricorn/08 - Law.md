# North Ossetia-Alania: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kabardian and Kumyk for minorities. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it. Capricorn takes the spirit of the law; the letter is Libra's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Christian and Muslim Ossetians:** whether religious rules shape family disputes differently in the Muslim villages.
- **Prigorodny Ingush:** their own arbiters and their uneasy ties to the republic's courts.
- **Terek Cossacks:** a remembered tradition of self-judgment in the stanitsa.
- **Mozdok Kabardians and Kumyks:** a separate customary and religious law tradition.
- **Market traders and informal workers in Vladikavkaz:** daily dealings with inspectors and permits.
- **South Ossetian incomers:** papers, citizenship and the law as a gatekeeper.
- **Highland villages:** courts and police are far away, so other ways of settling may hold.
- **Lawyers, police and officials:** how insiders say the law is meant to work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial and Soviet law and custom; mixed for trust surveys (mostly Russia-wide); thin for current elder hearings.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Ingush, Kabardian, Kumyk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** feuds and settlements between named families; criminal underworld codes; police violence claims; human-rights work under present restrictions. The correctional-control figure sits with Authority; the wording of statutes belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
