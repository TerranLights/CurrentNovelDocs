## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Boys in wrestling and combat-sport halls, girls in dance ensembles:** two separate training paths to test, not assume.
- **Suvorov cadets and Cossack cadet classes:** state and Cossack versions of drilled youth.
- **Orthodox, native-faith and Muslim Ossetians:** each keeps different fasts and prayer hours.
- **Mozdok Kabardians:** an etiquette of restraint with its own literature.
- **Prigorodny Ingush:** a separate code of refined conduct taught at home.
- **Parents raised in the 1980s and 1990s, and parents now:** whether gentler child-rearing talk in Russian media has reached village homes.
- **Highland herding families:** boys trained through stock work, to set beside city schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, dance and military schooling; mixed for child-rearing ethnography; thin for local data on punishment at home.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor); Kabardian; Ingush.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse of children; hazing in sport and in military schools; personal health habits. Prison discipline belongs under Law and Order.
- **Held for review:** none.
