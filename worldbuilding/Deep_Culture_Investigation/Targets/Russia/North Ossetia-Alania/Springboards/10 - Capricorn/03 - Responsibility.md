## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineages and lone urban households:** where the surname group still steps in for a member, and where a person answers alone.
- **Ossetian and Ingush neighbors in Prigorodny:** they hold opposing accounts of who answers for 1992.
- **Orthodox and Muslim Ossetians:** confession and repentance follow different rites.
- **Beslan families and the authorities:** a standing dispute over blame.
- **Residents near the old Elektrozinc site:** corporate answerability is lived, not abstract, there.
- **Highland villages of the Genaldon and Karmadon valleys:** disaster risk and who must warn.
- **Young men aged 14 to 25:** the years when the law and the family begin to hold them answerable.
- **Widows and women heads of household after the wars:** they took on loads the household had placed elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for customary law and inquiries into disasters; mixed for surveys; thin for figures on the household load in the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian, German (Haxthausen), Ingush, Kabardian, Kumyk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan; Prigorodny; feuds between named families (no private families named in research notes); Memorial's archive now that the body is dissolved. Juvenile and alimony law is read here only for its spirit.
- **Held for review:** none.
