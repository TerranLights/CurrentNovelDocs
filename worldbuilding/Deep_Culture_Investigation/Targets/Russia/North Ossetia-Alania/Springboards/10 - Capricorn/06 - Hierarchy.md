## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of noble lineages and of commoner surnames:** whether old rank still weighs in marriage and public life.
- **Gorges with and without a noble estate:** Tagauria and Digoria against Alagir and Kurtatin, where the old order may have differed.
- **Men and women at the feast and in the house:** who sits, who serves, who speaks.
- **The eldest and the youngest at table:** precedence by age inside one sex.
- **Mozdok Kabardians:** princes, nobles and commoners in a separate system.
- **Terek Cossacks:** a former estate with its own ranks.
- **Prigorodny Ingush:** whether clans are ranked among themselves, as Ingush describe it.
- **Russian and other non-titular residents:** where they stand in the republic's offices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 18th- and 19th-century estates; mixed for the feast and family; thin for today's rank and class in survey form.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian (Iron and Digor), German (Güldenstädt), Kabardian, Ingush.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descendants' claims to noble or lowly origins; past bondage; ethnic ordering after the 1992 conflict; lineage knowledge, as families share it.
- **Held for review:** none.
