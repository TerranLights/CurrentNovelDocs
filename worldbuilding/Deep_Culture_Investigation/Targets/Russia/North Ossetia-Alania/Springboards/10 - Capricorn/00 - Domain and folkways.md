# North Ossetia-Alania: The domain, authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (the Iron literary standard and Digor), Russian; for minorities Ingush, Kabardian, Kumyk, Armenian, Georgian. **Concept in one line:** who commands, who obeys and what is owed, across career, status, reputation, authority and responsibility, with the folkways of age, time, rank, order and power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iron and Digor speakers:** separate literary norms and separate historic noble estates (aldars in Tagauria, badeliata in Digoria).
- **The gorge societies (Tagaur, Kurtatin, Alagir, Digor, Tual of the Nar-Mamison basin):** some had a noble estate, some did not, so rank and authority may split by gorge.
- **Orthodox, Muslim and Uatsdin (native-faith) Ossetians:** different sacred grounds for command and duty.
- **Migrants and refugees from South Ossetia (1991-1992, 2008):** came out of another state's experience of power and war.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny district:** their own code and kin structure, and the 1992 conflict over the land.
- **Mozdok district:** Terek Cossack stanitsas, Kabardians, Kumyks and Armenians, each with its own historic offices.
- **Vladikavkaz against the villages:** a multiethnic city (Russians, Armenians, Georgians, Greeks) beside village life.
- **Generations and diaspora:** Soviet-raised against post-1991 adults; Ossetians in Moscow and descendants of 19th-century emigrants in Turkey.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ethnography, folklore and imperial records; mixed for surveys (mostly Russia-wide); thin for Digor and minority material.
- **Lands:** the tracker carries the pre-war lands as "Russia", which is broad; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. The sheets in this sign are written for the core lands, the present Republic of North Ossetia-Alania.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor); French and German for Dumézil, Klaproth and other travelers; Georgian for medieval chronicles and Tbilisi archives; Ingush and Kabardian for minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1992 Prigorodny conflict; Beslan (2004); the Ossetian-Georgian wars; the contested claim to the Alan name; shrine rites and lineage knowledge, to be used as each community shares them.
- **Held for review:** none.
