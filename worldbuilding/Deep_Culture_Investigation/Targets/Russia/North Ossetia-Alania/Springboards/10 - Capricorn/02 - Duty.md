# North Ossetia-Alania: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sons by birth order:** which son stays with the parents and carries the house, to research rather than assume.
- **Daughters-in-law:** the duties a bride takes on in her husband's household, and how these are changing.
- **Muslim Ossetian villages:** religious duties (prayer, alms, the fast) beside the duties shared with Christian neighbors.
- **Terek Cossack families:** service to the state as a family obligation over generations.
- **Prigorodny Ingush:** communal work and kin duties inside their own lineages.
- **South Ossetian households in the north:** duties to kin and graves left across the range.
- **Vladikavkaz salaried families against village households:** who can afford the feasts and memorials that kin expect.
- **Ossetians in Moscow and other Russian cities:** the pull to return for funerals and memorials.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ritual duties and war memory; mixed for civic duty; thin for figures on family care inside the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Ingush, Kabardian, Armenian; classical Greek for Lucian and Herodotus (translations exist).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war deaths since 2022 and mobilization; family grief; shrine offerings, as each community shares them. Legal duties (conscription law) are read here only for their spirit.
- **Held for review:** none.
