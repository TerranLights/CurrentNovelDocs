*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland and plain Ossetian villages:** the father's and eldest's word may weigh differently where settlement came late from the gorges.
- **Orthodox, Muslim and Uatsdin Ossetians:** each turns to a different holder of sacred authority (priest, imam, shrine keeper).
- **Prigorodny Ingush:** after 1992, the republic's police and courts are contested authority for them.
- **Terek Cossacks of Mozdok district:** an elected ataman inside a state-registered host.
- **Mozdok Kabardians:** the presiding elder and the memory of princely lines.
- **South Ossetian incomers:** they lived under a different government and its militias.
- **Beslan families:** a community in open dispute with the security services' account.
- **Young urban adults against village elders:** challenges to elders' say may run on this line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medieval and imperial sources and ethnography; mixed for polls; thin for Digor- and minority-specific data on obedience.
