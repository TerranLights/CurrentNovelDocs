## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Those who leave for Moscow and St. Petersburg, and those who stay:** ambition may be measured by leaving or by building at home.
- **Uniformed careers and private business:** police, army and state jobs against trade and enterprise.
- **Young women:** whether education and work away from home meet family support or pushback.
- **Highland Digoria (Iraf district) and the plain:** emptying villages against the city's jobs.
- **Mining towns such as Mizur and Sadon:** the closure of old industry and what young people aim for there.
- **Vladikavkaz Armenian families:** a long urban presence with its own networks; check their routes rather than assume them.
- **Prigorodny Ingush:** whether access to public jobs and office differs for them.
- **South Ossetian incomers:** people rebuilding a life with little.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for biographies of officers, officials and artists; mixed for migration and education data; thin for surveys of aspirations inside the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Armenian, Georgian, Kabardian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption in hiring and admissions (point to Merit, record claims as claims); envy and the evil eye, as communities share them.
- **Held for review:** none.
