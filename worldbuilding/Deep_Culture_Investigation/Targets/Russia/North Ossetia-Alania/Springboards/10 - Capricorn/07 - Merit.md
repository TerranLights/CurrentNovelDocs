## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families descended from unequal-birth lines and from nobles:** the Chermen legend makes merit against birth a live local story.
- **War heroes' families:** a forebear's deeds as standing passed down, not earned.
- **Young professionals in state and private posts:** open contest against hiring through kin, to test.
- **Women teachers, doctors and scientists:** how their merit is counted beside men's.
- **Terek Cossacks:** standing by service record.
- **Mozdok Kabardians:** a valor term of their own.
- **Prigorodny Ingush:** whether they see hiring and admissions as fair to them.
- **Highland and plain school pupils:** access to tutors and olympiad training differs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for honors, heroes and epic; mixed for hiring records; thin for local surveys on fairness.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Kabardian, Armenian, Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption claims against named people (record as claims, name no private persons); unequal-birth descent, as families share it.
- **Held for review:** none.
