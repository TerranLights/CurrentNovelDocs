# North Ossetia-Alania: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Ingush; Turkish for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineages and surnames:** large family-name groups against small or broken ones.
- **Highland-origin families on the plains:** ties to villages left behind.
- **Families split between North and South Ossetia:** a road and a border in the middle.
- **Women and men:** a married woman's ties to her own family against her husband's lineage.
- **Digor:** separate lineage networks and Muslim and Christian lines.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** clan networks of their own, and broken neighbor ties after 1992.
- **Terek Cossacks, Kabardians, Kumyks of Mozdok district:** different kin and brotherhood forms.
- **Armenians and Georgians of the towns:** church and community networks.
- **Diaspora in Turkey and Moscow:** ties at a distance.
- **Older people living alone:** loneliness in emptying villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on kinship and ritual ties in ethnography; thin on measured loneliness or networks at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian; Turkish for the diaspora; Ingush for Ingush networks.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims of descent from Scythians and Alans are politically loaded; list them as claims. Informal networks touch corruption; keep to how ties work, and send law to Libra and Capricorn. Ingush-Ossetian ties after 1992 are sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
