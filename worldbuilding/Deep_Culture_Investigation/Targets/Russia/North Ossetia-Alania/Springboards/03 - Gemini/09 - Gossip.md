# North Ossetia-Alania: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Armenian and Georgian for town communities. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men:** the spring and the house against the nykhas; whether gossip is named differently for each.
- **Small highland villages and Vladikavkaz:** close watching against city anonymity.
- **Old families and newcomers:** talk about lineage and origins.
- **Digor and Iron communities:** separate networks of talk.
- **Christian and Muslim Ossetian families:** religious teaching on loose tongues.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** rumor across the ethnic line.
- **Terek Cossacks, Kumyks and Kabardians of Mozdok district:** stanitsa and village talk.
- **Armenians and Georgians of the towns:** tight communities with their own talk.
- **Young people online:** anonymous pages against face-to-face talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: honor-and-shame ethnography and folklore rich; current gossip mostly in social media, unstudied.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian; Armenian and Georgian for small communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gossip pages name real people (quote nothing, name no one); honor talk can endanger women; rumors from 1992 and Beslan can still harm. Slander as a legal matter goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
