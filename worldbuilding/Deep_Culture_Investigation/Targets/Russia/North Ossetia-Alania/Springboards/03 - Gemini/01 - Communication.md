# North Ossetia-Alania: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kabardian, Kumyk, Armenian for minorities. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and what counts as good communication and what it is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gender:** women and men may hold different speaking rights at the nykhas, the table and funerals; compare, do not assume.
- **Generations:** older villagers and young Vladikavkaz speakers who mix Russian and Ossetian in one sentence.
- **Digor and Iron:** different forms of greetings and toasts.
- **Christian and Muslim Ossetians** (Muslim families in Digoria and parts of Tagauria): table speech and prayer formulas may differ.
- **Ingush villages of Prigorodny district:** their own decorum (эздел) and language.
- **Kabardians and Kumyks of Mozdok district:** neighboring codes of manners.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks:** Russian-only households, and their own feast speech.
- **Armenians of Vladikavkaz and Mozdok:** a long-settled trading community, likely multilingual.
- **Deaf Ossetians:** communication outside speech.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ritual and table speech, folklore and philology; thin for everyday conversation and recorded talk.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron and Digor); Kabardian and Ingush for minority etiquette.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prayers at sanctuaries and some feast prayers belong to the Ossetian folk faith, and keepers may limit what is recorded; lament is tied to grief and to the 2004 Beslan dead. Avoidance customs touch family privacy.
- **Held for review:** none.
