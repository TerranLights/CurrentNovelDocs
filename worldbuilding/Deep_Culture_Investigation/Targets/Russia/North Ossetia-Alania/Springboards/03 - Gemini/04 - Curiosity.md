# North Ossetia-Alania: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Armenian, Georgian, Ingush for minorities. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Children and elders:** whether children may question adults, or learn by watching first.
- **Girls and boys:** what each may ask about, and of whom.
- **Highland villages with ancestral tombs** (Dargavs, Fiagdon, Kurtatin gorge): curiosity aimed at them by tourists.
- **Vladikavkaz students and engineers:** mining and technical schools.
- **Digor and Muslim Ossetian families:** different religious teaching on questions.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** asking across a sensitive boundary.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks of Mozdok district:** their own local-history circles.
- **Armenians and Georgians:** community curiosity about origins.
- **Mountain guides and climbers:** curiosity as profession.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: folklore and archaeology rich; little direct work on curiosity as such.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian; French and German for Dumézil, Chantre and Virchow.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ancestral tombs and human remains (Dargavs, Koban finds held abroad); questions about family origins can touch the Ingush-Ossetian conflict; sanctuary sites of the folk faith may be closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
