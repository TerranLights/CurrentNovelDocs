## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Christian, Muslim and traditional-religion Ossetians:** different festivals, and different rules on drink and dancing.
- **Women musicians and male toast-givers:** two roles in making a feast glad.
- **Elders and the young:** feast and round dance against clubs, concerts and online music.
- **Beslan families:** joy bounded by grief.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Mozdok, Armenians of Vladikavkaz:** their own festive calendars.
- **Highland villages and Vladikavkaz:** shrine festivals against city holidays.
- **Diaspora in Turkey:** weddings and festivals as the main shared joy far from the homeland.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and songs; mixed for present-day celebration; thin for measured happiness in the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Kabardian, Kumyk, Armenian, Georgian; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan commemorations; alcohol harm; mental illness. Rites around lightning or luck may be private; ask before recording.
- **Held for review:** none.
