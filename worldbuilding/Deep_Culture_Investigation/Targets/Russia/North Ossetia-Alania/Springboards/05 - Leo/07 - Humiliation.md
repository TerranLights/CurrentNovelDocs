*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men in peer groups:** where teasing ends and an insult demands an answer.
- **Young women:** shaming tied to reputation and marriage; check how families answer it.
- **Conscripts and veterans:** humiliation in the army and how it is told back home.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny and Ossetian neighbors:** two memories of the same events, to keep apart.
- **Georgians of Vladikavkaz after 2008 and South Ossetian refugees:** humiliation on both sides of one war.
- **Schoolchildren in Vladikavkaz and in villages:** bullying settings differ.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Mozdok:** their own shaming customs and language.
