## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former noble lineages of Tagaur and Digoria and the gorges without nobles (Alagir, Kurtatin):** inherited against chosen leaders.
- **Village elders and Vladikavkaz officials:** seniority against office.
- **Women:** epic counselors, Beslan's mothers' leaders and women officials; informal leading at home.
- **Muslim and Christian communities:** imams and priests as leaders, and how followers choose between them.
- **Terek Cossacks:** the ataman and the circle.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny and Armenians of Vladikavkaz:** minority community heads.
- **Young men in sport and business:** new routes to standing outside age rank.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical and epic leadership and for formal office; mixed for informal leading; thin for measured leadership preferences in the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Georgian; Kabardian, Ingush, Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan and the 1992 conflict; criminal "authorities" sometimes act as informal leaders, a topic for care, not for interviews without safeguards. The formal right to command belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
