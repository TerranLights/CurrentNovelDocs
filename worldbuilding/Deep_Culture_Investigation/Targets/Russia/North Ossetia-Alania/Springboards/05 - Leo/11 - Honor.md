# North Ossetia-Alania: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Latin for antiquity; Ingush, Kabardian, Kumyk, Armenian, Georgian for minorities; Turkish for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women:** whether honor rests on different conduct for each, a pattern to test, not assume.
- **Elders and young men:** honor through restraint and counsel against honor through answering challenges.
- **Christian, Muslim and traditional-religion Ossetians:** different sacred anchors for oaths and honor.
- **Military and sport families:** honor through service and medals.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny and Kabardians of Mozdok:** distinct honor terms and customs.
- **Terek Cossacks and Russians:** imperial and Cossack honor traditions.
- **Diaspora in Turkey:** honor language shaped by Turkish *namus* and *şeref*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ethnography and military history; mixed for present-day youth; thin for measured honor ideology.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Latin (or translation); Ingush, Kabardian, Kumyk, Armenian, Georgian; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence justified by honor; women's reputation; men's shrine rites that women do not join. Blood revenge belongs to Scorpio; customary law to Libra and Capricorn. Keep "warrior people" claims as outsiders' claims only.
- **Held for review:** none.
