# North Ossetia-Alania: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kabardian, Armenian, Georgian for minorities. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders kept at home and elders in state homes:** where dignity in old age is said to be kept or lost.
- **Christian, Muslim and traditional-religion families:** different rules on the body of the dead.
- **Displaced Ossetians from Georgia and displaced Ingush of Prigorodny:** two groups in long temporary housing, to study separately.
- **Mining and smelter towns (Sadon, Mizur, Vladikavkaz's industrial districts):** dignity of labor and its decline.
- **Disabled people in highland villages and in Vladikavkaz:** access differs sharply.
- **Women in extended households:** bodily and social dignity within the family order.
- **Central Asian migrants and Russian residents of Mozdok:** the treatment of outsiders and of a local minority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for etiquette and funerals, thin for measured respect and for the treatment of the old and disabled.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Ingush, Kabardian, Armenian, Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** human remains in crypts; Beslan survivors; displaced Ingush; domestic violence. Law on rights belongs to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); here only lived treatment.
- **Held for review:** none.
