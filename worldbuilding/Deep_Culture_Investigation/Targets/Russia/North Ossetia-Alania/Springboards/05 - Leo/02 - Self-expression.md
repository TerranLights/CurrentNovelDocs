# North Ossetia-Alania: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kabardian, Kumyk for minorities; Turkish for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young brides and elder women:** etiquette may weigh hardest on the newly married; check how it eases with age and status.
- **Young men of Vladikavkaz and village young men:** urban scenes give other outlets (music, sport, online) than the village *ныхас*.
- **Muslim and Christian Ossetians, especially in Digoria:** different rules for women's dress and public appearance.
- **Russian and Cossack residents of Mozdok and Vladikavkaz:** a Russian-language public sphere with its own norms of frankness.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district:** expression shaped by the *эздел* and by the memory of 1992.
- **Refugees from South Ossetia and Georgia:** identity statements made under displacement.
- **Diaspora in Turkey:** expression in Turkish public life, Ossetian at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for etiquette and avoidance customs; mixed for present-day youth culture; thin for measured emotional expression.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Ingush, Kabardian, Kumyk; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political expression in present-day Russia carries legal risk for informants; law on speech belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here. Beslan grief and the 1992 conflict are raw; seek consent. Some Western platforms are blocked in Russia, so archives may be partial.
- **Held for review:** none.
