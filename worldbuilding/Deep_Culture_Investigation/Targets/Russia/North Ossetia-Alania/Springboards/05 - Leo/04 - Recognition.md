## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and young men at the table:** the toast order places them differently; check how a young man earns a named toast.
- **Women who cook and serve and the men who are toasted:** whose work is thanked aloud, and whose is not.
- **Soviet-era laureates and post-Soviet achievers:** labor honors against sport, business and stage titles.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** recognition here is tied to the wrongs of 1944 and 1992.
- **Terek Cossacks:** their own campaign to be recognized as a people after 1991.
- **Refugees from South Ossetia and Georgia:** recognition of status and loss.
- **Digor and Iron cultural figures:** whether honors lists balance the two.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors and commemoration; mixed for the feast's informal credit (described often, rarely studied closely); thin for measured workplace recognition.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Ingush, Georgian, Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan and the 1992 conflict; rehabilitation claims are disputed between peoples; law on rehabilitation belongs to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit). Some memorial practices are family-private.
- **Held for review:** none.
