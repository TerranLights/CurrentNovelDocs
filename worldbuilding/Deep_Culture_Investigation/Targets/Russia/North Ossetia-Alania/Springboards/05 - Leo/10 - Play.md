## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Village children and Vladikavkaz courtyard children:** open land and shared yards give different play.
- **Boys and girls:** riding, shooting and wrestling against domestic and dance play; check where girls now join sport.
- **Adult men and elders:** table games in courtyards and parks, and when play is thought unfit for an elder.
- **Muslim families of Digoria:** limits on gambling, music and mixed games.
- **Cossack stanitsas of Mozdok:** their own games and fairs.
- **Kumyk, Kabardian and Ingush children:** game names and rules to record apart.
- **Online gamers:** a new play world across ethnic lines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and epic contests; mixed for present-day children's play; thin for measured play time in the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Kumyk, Kabardian, Ingush, Armenian, Georgian; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling is a crime outside set zones, so informants may be at risk; celebratory gunfire can be dangerous and illegal, so treat with care. Law on gambling belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
