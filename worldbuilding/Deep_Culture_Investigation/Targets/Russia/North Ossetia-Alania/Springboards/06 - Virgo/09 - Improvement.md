# North Ossetia-Alania: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ossetian (Iron, Digor); Armenian and Georgian for minorities, with Ingush and Kumyk where sources allow. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Resettled plains villages and the gorges left behind:** two sides of the same betterment story.
- **Christian-educated and Muslim Ossetians:** seminary schooling against mosque schooling and the old Digor nobility.
- **The Soviet-educated generation and today's young:** what "getting ahead" means in each.
- **Ossetian-language activists and Russian-speaking city families:** different ideas of improving the people's future.
- **Terek Cossack villages of Mozdok:** farming development on the steppe.
- **Ingush families:** deportation in 1944 and return shape any story of improvement (see flags).
- **South Ossetian migrants and the Turkish diaspora:** improvement sought by leaving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the enlighteners, schooling and Soviet projects; mixed for present-day development; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Iron and Digor Ossetian; Greek and Church Slavonic for early church sources; Ingush for minority accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** missionary and Soviet "improvement" must be read critically; the Ingush deportation and the dam and resort quarrels are charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
