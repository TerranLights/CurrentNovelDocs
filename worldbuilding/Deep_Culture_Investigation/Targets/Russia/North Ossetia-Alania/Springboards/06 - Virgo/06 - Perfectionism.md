## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dancers and wrestlers:** youth disciplines where precision is drilled for years.
- **Women craftworkers and housekeepers:** standards for needlework, pies and a feast-ready house fall mainly on women.
- **Elders who lead feasts and shrine rites:** whether the words and order must be exact.
- **Musicians and conservatory-trained families of Vladikavkaz:** a classical standard of exactness.
- **Factory and mining engineers:** precision as an industrial rule.
- **Digor and Iron villages:** local dance and pie customs may differ.
- **Armenian jewelers and builders of the capital:** a separate craft standard to test.
- **Kumyk, Kabardian and Cossack villages of Mozdok:** their own dance and craft traditions.
