## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders, men and women:** whose counsel is sought in matchmaking and quarrels may differ by sex as well as age.
- **Christian Ossetians:** discernment taught through confession and church practice.
- **Muslim Digor and Ingush:** discernment taught through mosque teaching and its texts.
- **Uatsdin communities:** reading signs and shrine customs as their own ways of judging.
- **City and village:** urban savvy about scams against village trust in known families.
- **Older people:** the usual target of phone fraud.
- **Mozdok's Kumyks, Kabardians and Cossacks:** their own horse and trading knowledge.
- **Armenian and Georgian families:** separate matchmaking and trade customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for matchmaking and folk sign-reading in older ethnography; mixed for modern taste and fraud; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Iron and Digor Ossetian; Ingush, Kumyk, Armenian, Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some sign-reading practices may be private to families or shrine communities; the 2020 protest is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
