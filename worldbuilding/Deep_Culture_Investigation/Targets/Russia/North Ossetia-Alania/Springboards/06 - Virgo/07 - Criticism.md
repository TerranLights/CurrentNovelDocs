## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and the young:** who may correct whom at the feast and in the street, and whether the young may answer back.
- **Men and women:** censure of women's conduct against men's; women's voice in public criticism.
- **Writers, journalists and comedians:** professional critics with their own risks.
- **Officials and citizens:** criticism upward through complaints, protest and social media.
- **Digor and Iron Ossetians:** two literary traditions, each with its own satire.
- **Ingush and Ossetian communities:** criticism between them is tied to the 1992 conflict.
- **Russians, Armenians and Georgians of the capital:** their own press and community voices.
- **Muslim and Christian congregations:** sermons that rebuke and the limits on criticizing clergy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and satire; mixed for the press; thin for private criticism in families.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Iron and Digor Ossetian; Ingush for minority media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of authorities carries risk; inter-ethnic criticism needs care; shaming touches family honor.
- **Held for review:** none.
