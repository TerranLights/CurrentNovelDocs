## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Engineering and mining families of Vladikavkaz, Sadon and Mizur:** technical reckoning as a family trade.
- **Mountain shepherds and old farmers:** judgment from long experience, not from numbers.
- **University staff and students of Vladikavkaz:** formal analysis taught in Russian.
- **Digor and Iron Ossetian speakers:** how each counts and reasons in its own speech.
- **Orthodox and Muslim religious teachers:** each has a tradition of reasoned argument over texts.
- **Russians and Armenians of the capital:** long ties to the technical and trade professions.
- **Ingush and Kumyk villages:** separate schooling and exam records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exam and statistical data; mixed for scholarship on reasoning styles; thin for folk reckoning outside ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian above all; Iron and Digor Ossetian for number words and riddles; Ingush and Kumyk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hazard analysis after Karmadon touches grief; exam data may be disputed.
- **Held for review:** none.
