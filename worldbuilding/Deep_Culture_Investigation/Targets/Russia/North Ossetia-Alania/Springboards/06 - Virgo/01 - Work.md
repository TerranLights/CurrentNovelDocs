## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain villages of the gorges:** herding, haymaking and the move to winter pastures shape a different working year from the plains.
- **Plains farming villages** (Pravoberezhny, Kirov, Ardon districts): grain, maize and orchards; the collective farm's legacy.
- **Digor Ossetians:** a separate literary language with its own proverb and tale record of work.
- **Vladikavkaz and Beslan workers:** factory, rail and service jobs, and the shift from industry to trade after 1991.
- **Mozdok district:** Terek Cossacks, Russians, Kumyks and Kabardians, each with a farming tradition to separate.
- **Armenians and Georgians of the capital:** long-standing trades and commerce.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district:** a different household economy (see flags).
- **Men against women; labor migrants against those who stay.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the traditional economy and the Soviet era; mixed for current work, where informal jobs are hard to see.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Iron and Digor Ossetian; Ingush, Kumyk and Armenian for minority villages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Prigorodny district and the 1992 conflict; the Soviet "parasitism" charge is a law topic and belongs to Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
