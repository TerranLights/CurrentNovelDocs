## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iron and Digor Ossetians:** different local mother figures (Zadalesk Nana in Digoria).
- **Uatsdin, Christian and Muslim Ossetians:** Mady Mairæm, the Mother of God, or Islamic teaching on mothers.
- **Village and Vladikavkaz mothers:** life in the husband's family house against a separate flat.
- **Mothers of Beslan and mothers of men killed in 1992 and 2008:** the bereaved mother as public figure.
- **South Ossetian mothers resettled in the north:** mothering after displacement.
- **Ingush mothers of Prigorodny:** their own customs and losses.
- **Terek Cossack and Russian mothers; Kumyk and Kabardian mothers of Mozdok district:** different household roles.
- **Armenian and Georgian mothers of Vladikavkaz:** church, language and kin networks of their own may give motherhood customs worth recording apart.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on the mother as a symbol (epic, legend, memorials); mixed on present-day practice; good on birth statistics.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Iron and Digor Ossetian for legend, verse and online talk; Ingush and Kumyk for the minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bereaved mothers of Beslan, of 1992 and of 2008; reproductive questions (abortion, Aries) and the split of household work (Libra) belong to other signs. Some women's shrines may admit women only.
- **Held for review:** none.
