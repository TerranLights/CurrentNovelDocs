# North Ossetia-Alania: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush, Georgian, Chechen for displaced groups. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Refugees from South Ossetia and Georgia proper** (Trialeti, Borjomi and other Ossetian communities that left in the 1990s): sheltered, then settled.
- **Ingush displaced from Prigorodny** and Ossetians displaced by the same conflict: shelter on both sides of a contested line.
- **Mozdok district:** people fleeing the Chechen wars; Cossack and Kumyk villages that received them.
- **Highland families and herders:** shelter from the mountains themselves (avalanches, mudflows, glacier falls).
- **Vladikavkaz's homeless and those in state institutions:** urban shelter.
- **Orthodox, Muslim and traditional-faith Ossetians:** the faith communities that give lodging.
- **Armenian and Georgian communities:** their own networks for newcomers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on refugees and displacement (international and Russian reports); thin on street homelessness and everyday hospitality to strangers.
- **Languages needed:** Russian above all; Ossetian; Georgian, Ingush and Chechen for displaced groups' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** displacement after 1992, the Chechen wars and 2008 is politically charged; give each displaced group's own account. Lermontov and Pushkin carry imperial-era stereotypes.
- **Held for review:** none.

5. Read the "Бэла" chapter and the Darial pages of Pushkin's journey, noting the dwellings described.
