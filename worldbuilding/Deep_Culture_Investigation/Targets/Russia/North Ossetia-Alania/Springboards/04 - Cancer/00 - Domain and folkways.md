# North Ossetia-Alania: The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron and Digor varieties, Cyrillic script), Russian; for minorities, Ingush, Kumyk, Kabardian, Armenian. **Concept in one line:** the domain question, what is home and who is family, across household forms, kin, building ways, family ways and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iron and Digor Ossetians:** separate dialects and dictionaries, partly separate customs.
- **The old highland communities** (Tagaur, Kurtat, Alagir, Digor; Tual around Zaramag): each gorge kept its own house ways.
- **Highland against lowland plain villages** (Ardon, Digora, Beslan, Chikola): the move down reshaped households.
- **Vladikavkaz:** apartments against village courtyards.
- **Christian, Muslim and traditional-faith (Uatsdin) Ossetians:** home rites may differ.
- **South Ossetians resettled in the north:** families rebuilt after displacement.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district:** a separate kin system and a contested history of homes.
- **Terek Cossacks and Russians** around Mozdok; **Kumyks and Kabardians** of Mozdok district.
- **Armenians, Georgians and Greeks of Vladikavkaz:** long-settled urban minorities.
- **Diaspora:** Ossetians in Moscow and in Turkey (descendants of 19th-century emigrants).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for Ossetian ethnography and folklore; mixed for statistics by people; thin for the smaller minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian above all; Iron and Digor Ossetian for folklore and press; Ingush, Kumyk, Kabardian, Armenian for minorities.
- **Lands:** the tracker gives the pre-war lands only as "Russia"; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. These sheets are written for the republic's core lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 Ingush deportation and the 1992 Prigorodny conflict; the 2004 Beslan school siege; South Ossetian refugees. Some sanctuaries (*дзуар*) keep rules on who may enter.
- **Held for review:** none.
