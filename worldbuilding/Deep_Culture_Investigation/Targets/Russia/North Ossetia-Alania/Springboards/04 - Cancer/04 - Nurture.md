# North Ossetia-Alania: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kumyk, Armenian for minorities. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland and lowland Ossetian families:** herding and farm chores gave children different work.
- **Iron and Digor speakers:** different lullaby and tale traditions to collect.
- **Ossetian-speaking and Russian-speaking homes in Vladikavkaz:** the language a child is raised in.
- **Christian, Muslim and Uatsdin families:** which religious teaching shapes a child.
- **Grandparent-raised children:** where parents work in Moscow or abroad.
- **Ingush families of Prigorodny:** their own upbringing ideals and schools.
- **Terek Cossack families:** cadet classes and a military upbringing.
- **Armenians, Georgians, Kumyks:** minority schools and homes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich in the ethnopedagogy genre, which is plentiful but often idealized; thin on measured practice at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ossetian; Ingush; Kumyk; Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Beslan siege (2004) took the lives of many children and changed schooling and child protection; approach with care. Discipline and punishment of children belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "этнопедагогика осетин" and sort by citations.
2. Search for "полилингвальная модель образования Северная Осетия" and the republic's education ministry pages.
3. Find a collection of Ossetian lullabies and children's folklore in the national library's catalog.
4. Check Rosstat's time-use and diet surveys for republic-level tables.
5. Search for "ингушская народная педагогика" and "казачьи классы Моздок" for the two main minorities.
