# North Ossetia-Alania: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush for Prigorodny; Kabardian for Mozdok district. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men inside extended households:** whether one side is expected to give way more, as each describes it.
- **Juniors and elders:** silence toward elders as respect or as cost.
- **Ossetian and Ingush residents of the mixed villages:** what each gives up for coexistence.
- **Beslan families who took different paths after the inquiries:** different answers to the same choice.
- **Muslim and Christian Ossetians, and the small pacifist religious minorities:** religious grounds for yielding or refusing.
- **Business owners in Vladikavkaz and the districts:** paying for peace.
- **South Ossetian arrivals:** peace after war across the range.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; ethnography on reconciliation and NGO reports on violence at home exist; survey data on conflict avoidance in the republic is likely thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian, Russian; Ingush and Kabardian; Latin or a translation for Rubruck.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence in families and named victims; the Prigorodny villages; Beslan survivors; prosecutions of religious minorities; criminal extortion.
- **Held for review:** none.
