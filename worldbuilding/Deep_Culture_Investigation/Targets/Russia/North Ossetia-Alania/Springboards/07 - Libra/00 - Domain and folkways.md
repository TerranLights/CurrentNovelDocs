## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iron and Digor Ossetians:** different histories of nobility and dependents (the Digor *badeliatæ*, the Tagaur aldars) behind marriage and bargain rules *check*.
- **The historical societies by gorge** (Tagaur, Kurtatin, Alagir, Digor, and the Zaka gorge's Tual people): customary rules were recorded gorge by gorge.
- **Christian, traditional-faith (Uatsdin) and Muslim Ossetians:** oaths and marriage rites may follow different authorities.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny district and Vladikavkaz:** a separate legal-customary tradition, and the side of a land dispute.
- **Terek Cossacks and other Russians of Mozdok district:** the stanitsa assembly (*круг*) as a separate model of collective decision.
- **Mozdok Kabardians, Kumyks, Armenians, Georgians and Greeks:** their own marriage and mediation customs.
- **Arrivals from South Ossetia and inner Georgia** after the 1990s and 2008 wars.
- **The diaspora in Turkey and Moscow:** custom carried away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 19th-century custom and the Nart epic; mixed for present practice; thin for republic-level surveys. **Pre-war lands:** the tracker carries "Russia" as the pre-war lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the sign's sheets are written for the republic's core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian (both dialects), Russian; Georgian; French; Ingush and Kabardian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1992 Ossetian-Ingush conflict and the Prigorodny land question; the Beslan school siege (2004) and its trials; the Georgian-Ossetian wars; traditional-faith shrines, where some rites are for members only.
