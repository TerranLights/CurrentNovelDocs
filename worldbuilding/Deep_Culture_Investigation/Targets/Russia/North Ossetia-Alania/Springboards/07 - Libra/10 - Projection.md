*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ossetians and Ingush of Prigorodny district:** each side's image of the other, as each describes it.
- **Iron and Digor Ossetians:** the jokes and images each holds of the other.
- **South Ossetian arrivals:** images of Georgians formed in war.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Mozdok district:** images of mountain peoples, Chechens in particular.
- **Muslim Ossetians:** whether they are cast as outsiders inside their own people, as they report it.
- **Traditional-faith adherents and Orthodox clergy:** each side's charges against the other.
- **Armenians, Georgians and other small Vladikavkaz communities:** how they are seen, and how they see the majority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; studies of interethnic images from the 1990s exist; evil-eye and sorcery beliefs are in ethnography; current measures for the republic are likely thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian, Russian; Ingush; Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Ossetian-Ingush conflict and the 1944 deportation; Beslan and collective blame; recorded jokes and slurs about neighbors, which must be quoted only as data; named persons accused of sorcery.
- **Held for review:** none.
