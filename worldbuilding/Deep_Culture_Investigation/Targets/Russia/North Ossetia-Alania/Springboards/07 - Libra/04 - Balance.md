## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain gorges (Kurtatin, Tsei, Digoria, Dargavs) and the plain:** different balances of herding, farming and tourism.
- **Ossetian-dominant families and Russian-dominant families:** two ways of balancing languages.
- **Iron and Digor speakers:** whether the two dialects hold different places against Russian.
- **Orthodox, traditional-faith and Muslim Ossetians:** how each speaks of the others.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Mozdok district, and the Kabardians and Kumyks there:** a district with its own ethnic balance.
- **Ingush residents of Prigorodny:** balance of representation as they see it.
- **Workers in Vladikavkaz industry and in agriculture:** time-use and rest.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; folk calendar and pantheon material is likely rich; balance of powers in law is well documented; republic-level time-use and well-being data is likely thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian (both dialects), Russian; Georgian and Armenian for those communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic balance in public posts touches the Ingush question; traditional-faith rites; the Karmadon disaster's victims.
- **Held for review:** none.
