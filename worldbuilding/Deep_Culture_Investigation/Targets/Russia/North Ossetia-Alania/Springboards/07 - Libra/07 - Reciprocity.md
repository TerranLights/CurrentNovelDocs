## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain villages and Vladikavkaz apartment blocks:** whether exchange of work and food carries over into city life.
- **Iron and Digor families:** their funeral and wedding contributions, as each community records them.
- **Muslim Ossetians, Kabardians and Kumyks:** alms (*садака*) and Islamic giving beside customary exchange.
- **Orthodox and traditional-faith Ossetians:** offerings and vows to different holy figures.
- **Ingush of Prigorodny:** *белхи* and their own obligations of help.
- **Terek Cossacks and Russians of Mozdok district:** their own forms of labor help.
- **Migrant workers in Moscow and the diaspora in Turkey:** obligations kept at a distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for ethnographic gift and funeral exchange; mixed for favors in modern economic life; thin for survey measures of reciprocity in the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian, Russian; Ingush and Kabardian; Greek or translations for Herodotus and Lucian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** offerings at traditional-faith shrines; donations after Beslan touch grief and disputes over aid; favors in public office overlap with corruption cases.
- **Held for review:** none.
