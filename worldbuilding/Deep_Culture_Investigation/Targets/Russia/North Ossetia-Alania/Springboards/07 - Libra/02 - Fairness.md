# North Ossetia-Alania: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush and Kabardian for minority communities. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and juniors (*хистæр* and *кæстæр*):** whether shares at the table and in the family follow age, and how younger people see that order.
- **Families with land in the mountain gorges and on the plains:** two histories of allotment.
- **Christian or traditional-faith and Muslim Ossetians:** possibly different inheritance shares.
- **Women and men in inheritance and household work.**
- **Mozdok's Cossacks, Kabardians and Kumyks:** their own customs of sharing land and food.
- **Vladikavkaz residents near the smelter, and the rest of the city.**
- **Athletes and sports families:** fairness as a contest question, judged by referees.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; ethnographic material on feast portions and inheritance is likely rich; survey data on perceptions of fairness for the republic alone is likely thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian, Russian; Ingush and Kabardian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inheritance quarrels within named families; exam-cheating claims tied to a region; sacrificial portions at traditional-faith shrines.
- **Held for review:** none.
