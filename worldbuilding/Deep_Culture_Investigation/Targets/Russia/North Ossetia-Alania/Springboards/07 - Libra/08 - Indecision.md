## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young people in Vladikavkaz and those in mountain and plains villages:** how much of a life choice is the person's.
- **Families with a living lineage council and those without one:** who has the last word.
- **Muslim and Christian Ossetians:** handing a choice to God or to a religious adviser, and how each phrases it.
- **Traditional-faith adherents:** the place of signs and diviners in choosing.
- **Ingush families of Prigorodny:** decisions tied to the Prigorodny land question.
- **South Ossetian arrivals:** a decision to stay in the north or return south.
- **Georgian, Armenian and Russian families in Vladikavkaz:** their own norms of who decides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely thin; writing on indecision as such in the republic is probably scarce; start with decision-making customs, divination and the deferred political questions, then the language's dictionaries.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian, Russian; Georgian; Latin or a translation for Ammianus.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Prigorodny land question and the union of the two Ossetias are live political issues; divination customs connected with the traditional faith.
- **Held for review:** none.
