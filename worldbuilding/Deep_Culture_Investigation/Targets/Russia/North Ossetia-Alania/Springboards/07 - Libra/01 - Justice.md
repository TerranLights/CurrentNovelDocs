# North Ossetia-Alania: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** North Ossetia-Alania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ossetian (Iron, Digor), Russian; Ingush, Kabardian and Kumyk for minority communities. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered in a society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beslan families and the town of Beslan:** a community whose idea of what is owed was shaped by one event and its inquiries.
- **Ingush and Ossetian residents of Prigorodny district:** the two sides of a land and expulsion dispute; redress means different things to each.
- **Mountain villages against Vladikavkaz:** feud and reconciliation customs against urban courts.
- **Muslim Ossetians (mostly Digor) and Christian or traditional-faith Ossetians:** oath, shrine and religious-court traditions may differ.
- **Terek Cossacks of Mozdok district:** a separate lineage of stanitsa justice.
- **Kumyks and Kabardians of Mozdok district:** their own customary and Islamic legal heritage.
- **South Ossetian refugees and families:** justice claims tied to the 1990s and 2008 wars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial-era records and for Beslan; mixed for present-day feud practice; thin for regional surveys of trust in courts.
- **Languages needed:** Ossetian, Russian; Ingush for the Prigorodny side; English and French for European Court materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Beslan victims and their grief; the 1992 conflict, where every account is contested; shrine oaths tied to traditional-faith practice; named living persons in feuds.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search HUDOC for "Tagayeva" and read the facts section for the list of inquiries named.
2. Search the Russian State Library for "Терские ведомости" holdings and their court columns.
3. Search sudrf.ru for the Supreme Court of the Republic of North Ossetia-Alania's published criminal judgments.
4. Search "примирение кровников Осетия" in Russian scholarly databases (eLibrary, CyberLeninka).
5. Search the Nart epic's Russian edition for the vengeance for Soslan.
