## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa kin and godparent networks against Rostov and Krasnodar city dwellers.**
- **Adyghe families:** guest-friendship, fosterage and clan ties, by sub-group.
- **Armenians (Don and Hamshen), Pontic Greeks, Meskhetian Turks:** trust within and across groups.
- **Labor migrants from Central Asia and the Caucasus:** trust given and withheld by hosts.
- **Cossack societies against non-Cossack neighbors.**
- **Generations:** those who lost savings in the 1990s and those who did not.
