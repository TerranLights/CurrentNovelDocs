## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Adyghe:** feud and blood-price customs, by sub-group and between Adygea and the coastal Shapsugs.
- **Don and Kuban Cossacks:** frontier honor and the civil-war reprisals; stanitsa feuds over land.
- **Descendants of *inogorodnie* and Red partisans:** the other side of the reprisals.
- **Armenians, Pontic Greeks, Meskhetian Turks:** own norms of honor and redress, as questions.
- **Migrants from the eastern North Caucasus:** feud customs brought into the region, studied without stereotyping.
- **Rostov's criminal subculture** against ordinary town life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history and folklore; mixed on present-day feud; thin on measured vengefulness at regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Adyghe, Ukrainian, Armenian, Greek, Turkish.
