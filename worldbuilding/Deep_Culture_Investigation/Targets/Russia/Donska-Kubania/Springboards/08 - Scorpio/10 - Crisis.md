## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm stanitsas:** drought, hail and dust as the crises that matter.
- **Coastal towns** (Novorossiysk, Tuapse, Sochi, Anapa): floods and wind.
- **Coal towns** (Shakhty, Gukovo, Novoshakhtinsk): economic collapse.
- **Rostov and Krasnodar:** financial crises and urban hardship.
- **Families of refugee origin:** Hamshen Armenians, Pontic Greeks, Meskhetian Turks.
- **Ukrainian-descended Kuban families:** famine memory.
- **Adyghe auls:** relocation and loss of land.
- **Recently displaced newcomers** after recent wars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historic famines, war and the 1990s; mixed for natural hazards; thin for open data on personal crises.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish; Italian and Latin for Tana sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine naming; refugee status of recent arrivals; labor protests; suicide data.
- **Held for review:** none.
