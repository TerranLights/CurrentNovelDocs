# Donska-Kubania: The domain: death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia; core lands taken here as the Don basin (Rostov Oblast) and the Kuban (Krasnodar Krai with Adygea). **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Kuban *balachka* (Ukrainian-based speech); Adyghe; Armenian; Greek (Pontic); Turkish (Meskhetian); Kalmyk for the historical Don steppe. **Concept in one line:** how the culture faces death, power and what is hidden: death ways, sex ways, shared resources, inheritance and secrets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don Cossacks:** upper Don against lower Don (Cherkassk/Novocherkassk); Old Believer families; stanitsa against city.
- **Kuban Cossacks:** Black Sea (Ukrainian-descended) against Line (Russian-descended) stanitsas; different dialects and rites.
- **Non-Cossack settlers (*inogorodnie*) and their descendants:** a different place in land and power.
- **Adyghe:** Temirgoy, Bzhedug, Abadzekh remnants and the coastal Shapsug; the Circassian diaspora abroad.
- **Armenians:** Don Armenians of Nakhichevan-on-Don (now Rostov) against Hamshen Armenians of the Sochi-Tuapse coast.
- **Pontic Greeks** (Gelendzhik, Anapa, Krymsk) and **Meskhetian Turks** (Krymsk area; much emigration).
- **Rostov's Jewish community; Koreans; Roma; Germans** (pre-1941); **Don Kalmyks** (Salsk steppe, deported 1943).
- **Coal towns of eastern Rostov Oblast** (Shakhty, Gukovo, Novoshakhtinsk); **resort coast** (Sochi, Anapa); **Rostov and Krasnodar** as cities.
- **Diasporas:** the 1920 Cossack emigration; the Nekrasov Cossacks' return from Turkey.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Cossack folklore and Soviet-era vital statistics; mixed for the minorities; thin for open survey data at regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Ukrainian for Kuban sources; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1864 Circassian expulsion and its naming; de-Cossackization (1919); the 1932-33 famine; the 1943 deportation of Kalmyks; some pollsters and the Memorial society carry state labels or were closed. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Don and Kuban core.
- **Held for review:** none.
