*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack households against *inogorodnie* and urban families:** household command and its limits.
- **Adyghe families:** etiquette of restraint and avoidance, by sub-group (Temirgoy, Bzhedug, Shapsug).
- **Armenian, Greek and Meskhetian Turkish families:** elders' and husbands' authority, as questions.
- **Old Believers and Molokans:** community control of members.
- **Villages under agroholdings against farm-owning stanitsas.**
- **People moved by reservoirs:** Don stanitsas and Kuban auls.
- **Generations:** late-Soviet parents and post-Soviet children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on water projects and historical household norms; mixed on parenting and couples; thin on regional measures of felt control.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Adyghe, Ukrainian, Armenian, Greek, Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence; Cossack patrols' powers; forced labor on the canal. Laws on family violence and curfews belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
