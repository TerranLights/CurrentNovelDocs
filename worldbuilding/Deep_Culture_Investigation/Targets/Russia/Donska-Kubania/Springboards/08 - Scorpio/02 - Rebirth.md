*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Registered Cossack societies against unregistered and émigré-linked ones:** rival revival stories.
- **Descendants of Cossacks against descendants of *inogorodnie*:** whose past is being reborn.
- **Orthodox returnees, Old Believers, Molokans, Baptists and Pentecostals:** sudden against gradual change.
- **Adyghe of Adygea, coastal Shapsugs and repatriates** from Kosovo, Syria and Turkey.
- **Hamshen and Don Armenians; Pontic Greeks; Meskhetian Turks** (many emigrated, a fresh start elsewhere).
- **Coal-town families** after the mines closed; **Sochi residents** before and after 2014.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the Cossack revival and church rebuilding; mixed on recovery; thin on measured conversion at regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** banned religious groups and their converts; claims about who are "real" Cossacks; the 1940s deportations of Greeks and others; addiction as stigma.
- **Held for review:** none.
