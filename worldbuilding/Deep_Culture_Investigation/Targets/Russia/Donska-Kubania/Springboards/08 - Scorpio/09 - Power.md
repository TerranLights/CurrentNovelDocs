## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don against Kuban Cossacks:** assembly tradition against appointed atamans; registered against free societies.
- **Adyghe:** descendants of princely and noble families against commoners; clan networks in Adygea's politics.
- **Armenians, Greeks:** business and trade power in Rostov and the coast.
- **Agroholding villages against independent farmers.**
- **Port, resort and defense towns** (Novorossiysk, Sochi, Taganrog) against farm districts.
- **Coal towns:** power lost when the mines closed.
- **Meskhetian Turks and labor migrants:** the powerless end, as a question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Cossack history and regional politics; mixed on present elites (much from investigative press); thin on surveys cut by region.
