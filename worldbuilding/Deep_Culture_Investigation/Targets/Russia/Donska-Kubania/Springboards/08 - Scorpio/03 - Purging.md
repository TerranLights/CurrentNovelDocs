## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox Cossacks against Old Believers and Molokans:** very different lines between clean and unclean.
- **Descendants of the purged (Cossack families) and of the purgers (Red Army, party):** two memories of one event.
- **Adyghe and the Circassian diaspora:** expulsion as a people's defining event.
- **Meskhetian Turks, Pontic Greeks, Germans, Kalmyks:** expelled peoples and returnees.
- **Rostov's Jews:** Holocaust and later campaigns against "cosmopolitans".
- **Armenian Apostolic families:** their own fasts and confession.
- **Industrial and mining towns** (Shakhty) against farm stanitsas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet purges and deportations; mixed on folk healing; thin on present-day measures of disgust or purity.
