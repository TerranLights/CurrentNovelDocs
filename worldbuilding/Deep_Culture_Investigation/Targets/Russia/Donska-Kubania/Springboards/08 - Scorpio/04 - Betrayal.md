## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don against Kuban Cossacks:** different histories of rebellion, flight and service to the tsar.
- **Old Believers and Nekrasov descendants:** loyalty to faith over state.
- **Families of Whites, Reds and wartime collaborators:** opposed memories of who betrayed whom.
- **Adyghe:** princes' alliances during the 19th-century war, and the diaspora's view of them.
- **Armenians, Greeks, Meskhetian Turks:** promises made to settlers and to the deported.
- **Prison and street subculture of Rostov** against farm stanitsas.
- **Men and women:** the double standard on infidelity, as a question to test.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history and folklore; mixed on present attitudes; thin on regional measures of infidelity.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish; German and English for Lienz sources.

- **Held for review:** none.
