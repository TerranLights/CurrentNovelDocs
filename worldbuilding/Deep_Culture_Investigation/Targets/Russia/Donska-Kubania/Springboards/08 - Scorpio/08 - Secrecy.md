## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack families against *inogorodnie* descendants:** which pasts were hidden.
- **Old Believers and Molokans:** secrecy of faith under pressure.
- **Adyghe:** secret registers, restricted lore, the diaspora's open record against the homeland's caution.
- **Germans, Jews, Pontic Greeks:** origins hidden after deportation or persecution.
- **Armenians and Meskhetian Turks:** family privacy norms, as questions.
- **Defense-industry towns** (Taganrog) against farming stanitsas.
- **Small stanitsas where nothing stays secret** against anonymous Rostov and Krasnodar.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet-era secrecy and folklore; mixed on present privacy attitudes; thin and risky on hidden sexual and religious identities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Adyghe, Ukrainian, Armenian, Greek, Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** security-service archives are largely closed; LGBT and banned-faith secrecy carries legal risk for informants; Adyghe restricted lore to be handled as its keepers allow.
- **Held for review:** none.
