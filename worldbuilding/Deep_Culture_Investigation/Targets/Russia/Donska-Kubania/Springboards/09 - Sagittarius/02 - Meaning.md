# Donska-Kubania: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and Kuban balachka; Adyghe; Armenian; Meskhetian Turkish. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Grain farmers of the Kuban plain against Rostov and Krasnodar office workers:** meaning from land and harvest against meaning from career.
- **Miners' towns of the eastern Don:** a lost industrial purpose.
- **Black Sea resort workers (Sochi, Anapa, Gelendzhik):** seasonal work and its sense of life.
- **Cossack revival members against non-Cossack neighbors:** heritage as a source of purpose.
- **Adyghe and Shapsug communities:** *адыгагъэ* as a frame, and repatriates rebuilding life.
- **Don and Hamshen Armenians; Pontic Greeks; Kalmyks of the Salsk steppe:** meaning bound to a people's survival.
- **Generations:** late Soviet, 1990s youth and post-2000 youth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in literature and song, thin in regional survey items on meaning itself.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe and Armenian for the epic and Nalbandian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** veterans of recent wars and their families; suicide data; Soviet-era propaganda read as evidence.
- **Held for review:** none.
