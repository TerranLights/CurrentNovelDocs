## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don Cossacks against Kuban (former Black Sea) Cossacks:** Russian *воля* against a Zaporozhian *вольниця*.
- **Cossacks against *иногородние*:** freedoms held by one estate and not the other.
- **Old Believers (Nekrasovites, Don groups):** freedom of conscience through flight.
- **Adyghe, Shapsugs and returning diaspora:** freedom as a people's memory of independence.
- **Meskhetian Turks, Hamshen Armenians, Pontic Greeks:** freedom of residence and of movement.
- **Novocherkassk, Rostov, the coal towns:** workers' protest and its suppression.
- **Black Sea resort visitors against locals:** the freedom of the holiday and the cost to residents.
