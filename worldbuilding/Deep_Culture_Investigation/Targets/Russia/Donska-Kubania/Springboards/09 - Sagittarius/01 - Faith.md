## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don Cossack stanitsas against Kuban stanitsas:** different founding churches and different Soviet experiences.
- **Rostov and Krasnodar against Taganrog, Novorossiysk and Armavir:** city size and port life against attendance.
- **Old Believers (Nekrasovites, Don priestless groups):** faith kept apart from the main church.
- **Stundist, Baptist and Pentecostal converts:** faith as chosen rather than inherited.
- **Adyghe and Shapsug Muslims, and Adyghe Khabze followers:** two sources of faith in one people.
- **Don Armenians, Hamshen Armenians (some historically Muslim), Pontic Greeks, Yazidis, Meskhetian Turks:** each a faith carried by migration.
- **Generations:** those raised in Soviet unbelief against those baptized after 1988.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good archival and folklore depth, thin regional survey numbers.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Church Slavonic, Adyghe, Armenian, Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** registration and pressure on minority faiths; the Hamshen Muslim and Christian divide; Soviet repression of clergy.
- **Held for review:** none.
