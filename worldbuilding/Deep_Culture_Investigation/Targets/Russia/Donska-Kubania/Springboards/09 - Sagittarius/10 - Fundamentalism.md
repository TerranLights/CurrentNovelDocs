## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers (priestly and priestless) against the Moscow Patriarchate's parishes:** two claims to the true rite.
- **Adyghe and Shapsug Muslims:** official "traditional Islam", Sufi heritage and Salafi currents, along with repatriates from the Middle East and Kosovo, each in its own words.
- **Orthodox zealot groups and Cossack societies:** integralism inside the majority.
- **Pentecostal and unregistered Protestant congregations:** separation as a minority.
- **Rostov and Krasnodar against the stanitsas and mountain villages:** where strict groups gather.
- **Generations:** converts after 1991 against those born into a tradition.
- **Leavers:** across all of the above.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin and skewed: official sources label rather than describe, and members are wary of researchers.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Church Slavonic; Arabic and Adyghe for Islamic sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prosecutions under extremism laws; stigmatizing labels ("Wahhabi", "sect"); never treat a faith or people as fundamentalist; members' and leavers' safety. Legal aspects belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
