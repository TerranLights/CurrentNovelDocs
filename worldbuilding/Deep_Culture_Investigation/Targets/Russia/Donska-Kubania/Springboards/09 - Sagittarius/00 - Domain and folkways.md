## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don Cossacks against Kuban Cossacks:** Russian Don roots against Zaporozhian-descended Black Sea Cossacks with Ukrainian speech.
- **Cossacks against *иногородние* and later urban migrants:** belonging and faith split along estate lines.
- **Rostov, Krasnodar and Sochi cities against stanitsas:** urban secularity against village parish life, to test, not assume.
- **Adyghe (including Shapsugs on the coast):** Sunni Islam alongside Khabze; repatriates from Kosovo and the Middle East.
- **Armenians:** Don (Nor Nakhichevan) and Hamshen (Sochi and Tuapse districts) as separate lineages.
- **Pontic Greeks, Meskhetian Turks, Kalmyks, Yazidis, Germans, Jews:** each its own faith and migration story.
- **Old Believers and Spiritual Christians:** Nekrasovites returned from Turkey in 1962; Molokans.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich church and Cossack history; thin, survey-only data on present belief.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Adyghe, Armenian, Ukrainian; Pontic Greek, Kalmyk and Turkish for the smaller groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Circassian exile of the 1860s; decossackization (1919 onward); Meskhetian Turk emigration from Krasnodar; registration rules for minority faiths. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Don and Kuban core with Adygea and the Black Sea coast.
- **Held for review:** none.

1. Pull the Sreda *Arena* tables for Rostov Oblast, Krasnodar Krai and Adygea.
