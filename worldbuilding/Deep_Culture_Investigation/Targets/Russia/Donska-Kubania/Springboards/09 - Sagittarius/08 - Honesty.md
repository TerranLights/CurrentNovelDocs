## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack stanitsas against regional capitals:** keeping one's word in a small community against anonymous city dealing.
- **Resort economy against agrarian interior:** honesty toward visitors against toward neighbors.
- **Old merchant communities (Armenian, Greek) against Soviet-era workplaces:** different codes of fair dealing, without assuming either.
- **Adyghe, Shapsugs and repatriates:** honor and candor inside the customary ethic.
- **Meskhetian Turks, Kurds, Yazidis and other newer communities:** trust across group lines.
- **Generations:** those who lived through *blat* and those raised with online reviews.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; corruption surveys exist but are dated; market and folk material needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic stereotypes about traders and cheats are common in everyday talk and must never be taken as findings; corruption claims about named people.
- **Held for review:** none.
