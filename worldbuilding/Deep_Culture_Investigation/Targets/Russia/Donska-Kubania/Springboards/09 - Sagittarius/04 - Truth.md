## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don against Kuban:** different literary and historical quarrels over their own past.
- **Cossack revival organizations against historians:** origins, heroes and victims told differently.
- **Television audiences against internet users:** age and place shape whose word is believed.
- **Adyghe and Shapsug communities:** oral memory against imperial records.
- **Armenians, Pontic Greeks, Meskhetian Turks, Kalmyks:** histories of deportation or genocide, each contested in its own way.
- **Rural stanitsas against regional capitals:** local silence against urban press.
- **Old Believers and Orthodox:** a dispute over the true church.

## 4. Flags
