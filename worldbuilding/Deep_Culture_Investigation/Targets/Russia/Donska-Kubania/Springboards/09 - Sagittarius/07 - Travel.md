## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coast towns (Sochi, Anapa, Gelendzhik, Tuapse) against the inland steppe:** hosts of travelers against senders of migrants.
- **Rostov and Krasnodar:** destinations for newcomers from the North, the Caucasus and Central Asia.
- **Cossack families with emigrant branches abroad:** exile and return.
- **Adyghe and Shapsugs, diaspora and repatriates:** a diaspora far larger than the home community, by most accounts *check*, and its returns.
- **Hamshen and Don Armenians; Pontic Greeks:** arrivals from Anatolia and departures to Greece and Armenia.
- **Kalmyks:** nomadic heritage and the 1943 deportation.
- **Roma communities of the Don and Kuban:** mobility seen from inside and outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; tourism and migration well recorded, older travel writing plentiful.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe, Arabic and Turkish for diaspora sources; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportations of Kalmyks, Greeks and others; Circassian exile; migrant workers' status; how locals speak of newcomers.
- **Held for review:** none.
