## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rostov against Krasnodar:** two university cities with different founding histories.
- **Novocherkassk and Taganrog:** an old capital and a port town, each with its own institutes.
- **Stanitsas and farm districts:** the agricultural college against the city university.
- **Adyghe and Shapsug families:** native-language schooling and diaspora returnees' children.
- **Armenians, Pontic Greeks, Jews, Germans:** community schools past and present.
- **Cossack revival families:** cadet schooling as a chosen path.
- **First-generation students from the coal towns and the steppe:** who goes to university and who goes to work.
