## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don stanitsas against Kuban stanitsas:** Russian and Ukrainian-rooted folk belief.
- **Coast (Gelendzhik, Tuapse, Sochi):** dolmen and New Age believers against long-settled residents.
- **Adyghe and Shapsugs:** Islam, the customary ethic and pre-Islamic belief in one people.
- **Pontic Greeks, Hamshen Armenians, Don Armenians:** Black Sea and Anatolian folk beliefs.
- **Kalmyks of the Salsk steppe:** Buddhist belief.
- **Molokans and other Spiritual Christians, Old Believers:** dissent within Christianity.
- **Soviet-educated elders against young people raised after 1991:** disbelief taught against belief rediscovered.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; folklore archives rich, belief surveys mostly national.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Adyghe, Greek, Kalmyk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pressure on new religious movements; healers' charm texts some families keep private.
- **Held for review:** none.
