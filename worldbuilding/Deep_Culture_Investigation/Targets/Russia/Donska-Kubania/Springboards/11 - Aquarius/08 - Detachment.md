# Donska-Kubania: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (incl. Church Slavonic ascetic terms), Ukrainian, Kalmyk, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; valued as calm, objectivity or equanimity, condemned as coldness, indifference or withdrawal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox monastics and lay ascetics** against **Kalmyk Buddhists**; Old Believers' separate withdrawal from the world.
- **Adyghe** and **Cossack** families, compared on composure and display at funerals and feasts; neither assumed.
- **Men and women**, especially in grief and in emotional reserve.
- **City dwellers** (Rostov, Krasnodar) against **stanitsas**, on reserve with strangers.
- **Herders, fishermen and seasonal workers** whose work is solitary.
- **Elderly people living alone** in emptied villages and mining towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for religious and folk sources; mixed for present-day reserve and apathy; thin for Kalmyk Buddhist life on the Don.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Church Slavonic; Kalmyk; Adyghe; Ukrainian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political apathy is a charged topic and turnout figures are disputed; the Imyaslavie dispute is still sensitive in church circles. Impartiality goes to Libra; letting go goes partly to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** none.
