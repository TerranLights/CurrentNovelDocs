# Donska-Kubania: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with Don and Kuban dialect forms), Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Kalmyk, Turkish, Kurmanji. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Cossack stanitsas** against **settler villages** of former *inogorodnye*, which grew up outside the Cossack land community.
- **Cities** (Rostov-on-Don, Krasnodar, Novorossiysk) against **resort towns** of the coast (Sochi, Anapa, Gelendzhik), where seasonal residents shift who counts as a member.
- **Adyghe auls** in Adygea and the **Shapsug** coast; **Hamshen Armenian** hill villages near Sochi and Tuapse; **Don Armenian** Myasnikovsky district.
- **Greek** villages near Gelendzhik and Krymsk; **Meskhetian Turk** and **Kurdish** villages in Krasnodar Krai.
- **Mining towns** of the Russian Donbas (Shakhty, Gukovo, Krasny Sulin), whose communities formed around pits now closed.
- **Faith communities:** Orthodox parishes, Old Believers, Armenian Apostolic parishes, Muslim communities in Adygea, Kalmyk Buddhists, Rostov's Jews, Protestant congregations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the stanitsa as a historical community; mixed for present-day neighboring (few regional surveys); thin for small minority villages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish, Kalmyk for villages' own sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Kushchevskaya case and local corruption; deportation memory; the Olympic resettlement; any study of faiths now restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
