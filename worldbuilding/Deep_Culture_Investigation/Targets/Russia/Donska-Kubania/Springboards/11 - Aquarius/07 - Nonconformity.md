## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers**, **Nekrasovites**, **Molokans** and other dissenting faiths against Orthodox parishes.
- **Rostov's urban youth scenes** against stanitsa youth; Krasnodar's student milieu.
- **Registered Cossack patrols** as norm-enforcers, against the groups they police.
- **Adyghe** families, where custom and modern life meet; returned diaspora Adyghe, who bring other norms.
- **Religious minorities** treated as deviant (Jehovah's Witnesses, some Protestant groups).
- **Sexual and gender minorities**, mostly unseen in public sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical rebels and dissenting faiths; mixed for youth scenes; thin and risky for sexual minorities and banned faiths.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, including argot; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** banned religious groups; LGBT topics, now legally restricted, so sources may endanger people (use only published material); criminal subculture; police and Cossack-patrol violence.
- **Held for review:** none.
