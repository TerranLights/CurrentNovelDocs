# Donska-Kubania: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (incl. Don and Kuban usage), Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish, Kurmanji, Romani. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossacks** against **non-Cossack** settlers (former *inogorodnye*), and the rich and poor inside each.
- **Adyghe** society's old estates against its present, and Adyghe against Russian neighbors in Adygea.
- **Women** in stanitsa, aul and city; generations (Soviet-born against post-Soviet).
- **Minorities** with distinct status histories: Meskhetian Turks, Kurds and Yazidis, Roma, Jews of Rostov, Koryo-saram, Armenians (Don and Hamshen).
- **Labor migrants** from Central Asia and the Caucasus in Rostov and Krasnodar.
- **Resort economy** (Sochi, Gelendzhik) against farm districts and the depressed mining towns.
