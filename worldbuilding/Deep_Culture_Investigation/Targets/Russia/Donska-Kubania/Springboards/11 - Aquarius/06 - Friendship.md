# Donska-Kubania: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (Don and Kuban usage), Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Kalmyk, Turkish, Kurmanji. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women**, and generations (Soviet schoolmates against post-Soviet youth).
- **Cossack** comradeship words and rites against **Adyghe** guest-friendship and fosterage.
- **Don Armenians** (where *barekam* can name a friend or a relative); **Greeks**, with wedding-sponsor ties.
- **Meskhetian Turks**, **Kurds and Yazidis** and **Don Kalmyks**: friendship across and within small communities.
- **Veterans** of the Afghan and Chechen wars and of later conflicts.
- **Students and migrants** in Rostov and Krasnodar, newcomers making friends in a large city.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and ethnography; mixed for present-day friendship (national polls, few regional ones); thin for minority languages' own friendship terms.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Ukrainian (for Kuban rites).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recent war veterans; friendship networks that slide into favoritism and patronage (*блат*) belong in part to Capricorn and Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
