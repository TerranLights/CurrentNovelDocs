# Donska-Kubania: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian and the Don and Kuban dialects, Ukrainian (for Kuban's Black Sea material), Adyghe, Kalmyk, Armenian, Greek, Kurmanji. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; how the culture weighs the group against the person, draws the line between "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Registered Cossacks** (state-service societies) against **public Cossack** organizations and people of Cossack descent who join neither.
- **Don** against **Kuban** hosts, and inside Kuban the **Black Sea** and **Line** traditions.
- **Adyghe lineages** and sub-ethnic groups (Temirgoy, Bzhedug, Abadzekh, Shapsug) against the Adyghe as one people.
- **Deported and returned peoples:** Don Kalmyks, Pontic Greeks, Germans; **Meskhetian Turks**, many of whom left for the United States.
- **Workers' towns** (Novocherkassk, Taganrog, Shakhty) against agrarian districts.
- **Faith groups** with strong membership lines: Old Believers, Molokans, Armenian Apostolic parishes, Yazidis, Protestant congregations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Cossack hosts and on group repression; mixed on present-day group behavior; regional survey data are sparse.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian for Kuban's early sources; Adyghe, Kalmyk, Greek, Turkish, Kurmanji for minority memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective punishment and deportations; the 1962 shooting; registered Cossacks' role in policing; football violence; minority relations in Krasnodar Krai.
- **Held for review:** none.
