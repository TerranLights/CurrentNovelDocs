## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical reforms; mixed for present-day reform attitudes, where open debate is limited; thin for minority reform movements apart from the Adyghe.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1932-1933 famine and the end of Ukrainization; current protest and environmental activism (arrests, "foreign agent" labels); land disputes tied to crime. Legal reforms go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
