## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Soviet-formed generations** against those raised after 1991, on ideals and their loss.
- **Cossack revival** milieus (idealized past) against urban professionals in Rostov and Krasnodar.
- **Don Armenians**, with their own intellectual tradition from Nor Nakhichevan.
- **Adyghe** communities, whose ethical ideal has its own name and literature; returned diaspora Adyghe.
- **Religious idealists:** Orthodox parish activists, Old Believers, Molokans, Protestants, Muslims of Adygea, Kalmyk Buddhists.
- **Coastal incomers** (eco-settlers, downshifters, retirees from the north) against long-settled coastal villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet ideals and their literature; mixed for present-day idealism; thin for minority ideals outside the Adyghe and Armenians.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Armenian (classical and modern); Adyghe; Ukrainian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Krasnov's collaboration; forced labor on Soviet projects; some new religious and eco-movements face legal pressure; present-day "patriotic" idealism is state-promoted, so separate official and lived ideals.
- **Held for review:** none.
