# Donska-Kubania: Community and ideals, the domain and social ways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; the Don and Kuban dialects (Kuban *balachka*, Ukrainian-based); Adyghe; Armenian (Don and Hamshen varieties); Greek (Pontic); Kalmyk; Meskhetian Turkish; Kurmanji. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups, and what they hope for: associations, social ways (settlement, migration, hospitality, neighboring, gathering places), humanitarian ideals, progress and the future, upheaval, causes and tolerance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don Cossacks:** Upper Don against Lower Don and Cherkassk.
- **Kuban Cossacks:** the Black Sea Cossacks (Zaporozhian roots, *balachka*) against the Line Cossacks (Don and Russian roots).
- **Inogorodnye** and later Russian and Ukrainian settlers, who were outside the Cossack estate.
- **Adyghe peoples:** Temirgoy, Bzhedug, Abadzekh and others in Adygea; the **Shapsugs** of the Black Sea coast (Lazarevsky district); returnees from the diaspora (Kosovo, Syria).
- **Don Armenians** (Nor Nakhichevan, Chaltyr, Myasnikovsky district) and **Hamshen Armenians** of the Sochi-Tuapse coast.
- **Greeks** of the Anapa-Gelendzhik-Krymsk area; **Don (Buzava) Kalmyks** of the Sal steppe; **Nogais** (historic Kuban presence).
- **Meskhetian Turks**, **Kurds and Yazidis**, Assyrians, Koryo-saram, Roma, Rostov's Jews, German and Czech colonists; **Old Believers** and returned **Nekrasovites**.
- Cities (Rostov-on-Don, Krasnodar, Sochi, Novorossiysk, Taganrog) against stanitsas and khutors; the Russian Donbas mining towns (Shakhty).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Cossack history, folklore and Russian-language surveys; mixed for Adyghe, thin for smaller minorities. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled: this sign's sheets are written for the core Don and Kuban lands (Rostov Oblast, Krasnodar Krai, Adygea and the Black Sea coast).
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Kalmyk, Turkish, Kurmanji; Ukrainian for Kuban sources before 1932.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** decossackization, the 1932-1933 famine, deportations (Kalmyks, Greeks, Germans), the Caucasian War and the 1864 expulsion; present-day limits on NGOs ("foreign agent" lists) and some faiths; political pressure on survey answers.
- **Held for review:** none.
