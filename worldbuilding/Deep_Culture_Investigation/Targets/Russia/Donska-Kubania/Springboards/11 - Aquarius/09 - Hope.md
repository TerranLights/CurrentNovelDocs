# Donska-Kubania: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian, Adyghe, Kalmyk, Armenian, Greek, Turkish, Kurmanji. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young people** in Rostov and Krasnodar against those in villages and mining towns.
- **Cossack descendants**, for whom revival carried hopes, against neighbors who did not share them.
- **Adyghe** residents against **repatriates** from Turkey, Syria and Kosovo.
- **Don Kalmyks**, **Pontic Greeks** and **Meskhetian Turks**, each with a history of exile and return or departure.
- **Faith communities:** Orthodox, Old Believers, Armenian Apostolic, Muslim, Buddhist, Protestant and Jewish forms of hope.
- **Resort-coast** workers against farm districts; families of servicemen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and literature; mixed for measured expectations (national polls, central-bank data); thin for minority-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian; Kalmyk; Adyghe; Greek; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime losses and missing servicemen; repatriates' status; emigration as a political topic. Religious hope as faith goes partly to Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
