# Donska-Kubania: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Meskhetian Turkish; Kurmanji; Estonian and Pontic Greek for two small coastal communities. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hereditary Cossacks**, **revival Cossacks** and **non-Cossack** locals of the same stanitsa: three claims to one place.
- **Adyghe** at home against **repatriates** from Turkey, Syria and Kosovo: does birth abroad change one's place?
- **Newcomers from northern Russia and Siberia** in Krasnodar and Anapa against old residents.
- **Meskhetian Turks**, **Yezidis** and **Kurds**: communities whose standing was disputed by local officials.
- **Don Armenians** and **Hamshen Armenians**; **Pontic Greeks**; **Estonians** of the Sochi hills; **Roma**; **Koreans**.
- **Families displaced from the Donbas** into Rostov Oblast since 2014.
- **Students** from other regions in Rostov and Krasnodar universities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Cossack identity and Circassian diaspora debates; mixed for newcomers (mostly online and journalistic); thin and testimony-based for Meskhetians and Yezidis.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Turkish; Kurmanji; Armenian; Estonian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cossack status and patrol roles are politically charged. Memorial was dissolved in 2021-2022, so its archive may be hard to reach and sensitive to cite inside Russia. Circassian remembrance is contested by officials. Residence and citizenship rules belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
