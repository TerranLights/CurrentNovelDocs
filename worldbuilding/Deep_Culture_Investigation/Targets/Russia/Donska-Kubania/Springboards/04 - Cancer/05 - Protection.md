## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack revival bodies** that patrol, against **non-Cossack residents** who are patrolled: protection seen from both sides.
- **Adyghe**: guest-friendship and family honor as protection; compare villages of Adygea with the **Shapsug** coast.
- **Black Sea resort towns** (Sochi, Anapa, Gelendzhik) facing floods and mudflows, against **steppe districts** of the Don facing fires and drought.
- **Meskhetian Turks**, **Yezidis** and **Kurds**, who have at times sought protection from local officials rather than received it.
- **Armenian** and **Greek** coast villages; **Old Believers**; **Roma** settlements.
- **Women's** and **men's** views of who should protect whom, inside each group.
- **Families displaced from the Donbas** and border districts shelled since 2022.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military history and disaster reporting; mixed for household safety (one national survey, little regional depth); thin for minority self-protection.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Church Slavonic; Adyghe; Turkish; Kurmanji.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cossack patrols, the Kushchevskaya case and ecological activism touch state power and may expose sources; cite published material only. Border-district shelling and war displacement are live. Spoken charms may be held as private by their keepers. Policing and courts belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
