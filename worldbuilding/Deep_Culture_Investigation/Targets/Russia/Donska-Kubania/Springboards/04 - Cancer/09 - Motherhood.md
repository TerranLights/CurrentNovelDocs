*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with Don and Kuban forms); Church Slavonic for spiritual verses; Adyghe; Armenian; Pontic Greek; Turkish; Kurmanji. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it; and the mother as a figure of the culture (the mother of the nation, the Virgin Mother, Mother Earth).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack** and **non-Cossack** Russian families of the Don and Kuban: whether the revival's ideal of the mother differs from neighbors'.
- **Adyghe**: the daughter-in-law's standing and the mother's authority in villages, Maykop and the **Shapsug** coast.
- **Muslim Meskhetian Turk** and **Kurdish** families; **Yezidis**.
- **Don Armenians**, **Hamshen Armenians**, **Pontic Greeks**: mothers in diaspora households.
- **Old Believer** and **Molokan** mothers; **Roma** mothers; **Koreans**.
- **City professionals** in Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar against **rural** mothers; age at first birth is a question.
- **Mothers of soldiers**, a group to separate since 2014.
