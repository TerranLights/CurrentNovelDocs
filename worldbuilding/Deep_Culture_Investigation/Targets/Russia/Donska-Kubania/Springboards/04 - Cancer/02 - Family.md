# Donska-Kubania: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (Don and Kuban dialects included); Adyghe; Armenian; Pontic Greek; Meskhetian Turkish; Kurmanji. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Don** and **lower Don** stanitsas: different settlement histories, a question for family size and kin reach.
- **Black Sea (Zaporozhian-origin) Cossack** villages of the western Kuban against **Line Cossack** villages of the eastern Kuban.
- **Non-Cossack villagers** and **city families** of Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar.
- **Adyghe** (Temirgoi, Abadzekh, Bzhedug subgroups, to be checked) and the **Shapsugs**: lineage and avoidance customs may differ by subgroup.
- **Meskhetian Turks**, **Yezidis** and **Kurds**: large kin groups and ritual kinship.
- **Don Armenians** of Nor Nakhijevan, **Hamshen Armenians**, **Pontic Greeks**.
- **Old Believer** and **Molokan** families: kin bound to faith.
- **Koreans** of Rostov and Krasnodar; **Roma**; **Don Kalmyks**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Cossack archives and national surveys; mixed for Adyghe sources; thin and often only oral for Yezidi, Meskhetian and Hamshen families.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Turkish; Kurmanji; Pontic Greek; Church Slavonic for older registers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Yezidi caste and marriage rules are the community's own matter; ask through its organizations. Families torn by de-Cossackization, famine and deportation. Revival groups' "family values" campaigns are advocacy, not a description. Inheritance belongs to Scorpio; any legal side to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
