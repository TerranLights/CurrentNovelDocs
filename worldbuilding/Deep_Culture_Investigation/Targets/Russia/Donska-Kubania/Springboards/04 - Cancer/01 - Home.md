# Donska-Kubania: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with Don and Kuban dialect forms); Adyghe; Armenian; Pontic Greek; the Turkish of the Meskhetian community; Kurmanji. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa house-and-yard owners** against **city flat-dwellers** of Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar: house and flat as two meanings of home.
- **Newcomers to Krasnodar and the Black Sea coast** from northern Russia and Siberia: home chosen late in life against home inherited.
- **Adyghe of Adygea**, the **Shapsugs** near Sochi, and **repatriates** from Kosovo, Syria and Turkey: house against ancestral homeland.
- **Meskhetian Turks**, **Pontic Greeks**, **Hamshen Armenians** and **Yezidis**: peoples whose remembered home lies outside the nation's lands.
- **Families displaced into Rostov Oblast** from the Donbas since 2014: homes left recently and nearby.
- **Resort workers and seasonal renters** in Sochi, Anapa and Gelendzhik: home let out to strangers each summer.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing statistics and Cossack house types; mixed for city-life debates; thin for the smaller peoples' own writing about home.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe for returnee and *хэку* debates; Armenian; Turkish and Kurmanji for the smallest communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** homes lost in the 1864 Circassian exile, the 1930s stanitsa deportations and the 1940s deportations; displaced families from Ukraine. Residence registration (*прописка*) is bureaucratic, but any legal side belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
