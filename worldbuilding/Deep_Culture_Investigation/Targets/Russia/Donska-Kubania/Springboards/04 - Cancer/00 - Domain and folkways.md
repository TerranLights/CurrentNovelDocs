# Donska-Kubania: Domain and folkways, home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, including the Don and Kuban dialects (the Kuban *balachka*); Adyghe; Armenian (the Nor Nakhijevan and Hamshen varieties); Pontic Greek; Ukrainian for older Kuban sources. **Concept in one line:** what is home, and who is family: household forms, roots, family memory, hospitality, building ways, family ways and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don Cossack stanitsas** of the upper and lower Don against **Kuban stanitsas**: Hosts of different origins, house types and dialects.
- **Non-Cossack Russian and Ukrainian peasants** (*иногородние*) in the same villages: settled later, with a different standing.
- **Adyghe** of Adygea, the **Shapsugs** of the Black Sea coast, and returnees from Turkey, Syria and Kosovo: guest-house, fosterage and avoidance customs to separate by group and generation.
- **Armenians**: the Nor Nakhijevan community of Rostov against the **Hamshen Armenians** of the Sochi-Tuapse coast; two histories of arrival.
- **Pontic Greeks** of the Kuban and the Black Sea coast; **Meskhetian Turks** of Krymsk and Apsheronsk districts; **Yezidis and Kurds**; **Estonian villages** near Sochi; **Koreans**; **Roma**; **Don Kalmyks** of the Salsk steppe.
- **Old Believers**, Molokans and returned Nekrasov Cossacks: religious households to set apart.
- **Cities** (Rostov-on-Don, Krasnodar, Sochi, Taganrog, Novorossiysk) against **mining towns** (Shakhty, Novoshakhtinsk) and farming stanitsas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Cossacks and Rostov-Krasnodar census tables; mixed for Adyghe sources outside Russian; thin for Hamshen, Yezidi, Meskhetian and Estonian village households.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; some Ukrainian; Pontic Greek and Turkish for the smaller groups.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet set is written for the Don and Kuban core (Rostov Oblast, Krasnodar Krai, Adygea).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1864 Circassian exile, the 1919 de-Cossackization, the 1932-1933 famine and stanitsa deportations, the 1943-1944 deportations (Kalmyks, Karachay neighbors, Meskhetians, Germans). Whether Cossacks are a people or an estate is disputed; treat both views as sources. The 2022-present war brings displaced families into Rostov; handle with care. Adyghe *хабзэ* is etiquette here; any legal side goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
