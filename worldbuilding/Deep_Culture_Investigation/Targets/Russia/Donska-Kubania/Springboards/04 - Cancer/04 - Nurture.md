# Donska-Kubania: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with Kuban and Don dialect forms); Adyghe; Armenian; Turkish; Kurmanji; Pontic Greek. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack revival families** against other **stanitsa families**: whether the revival changes how sons and daughters are raised.
- **Adyghe** families of the plain and the hills; **Shapsugs**; **repatriate** families raised in Turkish or Arabic settings.
- **Muslim** families (Adyghe, Meskhetian Turks, Kurds) against **Orthodox** and **Old Believer** ones.
- **Yezidi** families; **Don Armenians**; **Hamshen Armenians**; **Pontic Greeks**.
- **Rostov and Krasnodar** professional families against **mining-town** families of the eastern Donbas fringe (Shakhty, Gukovo).
- **Grandparent-raised children** whose parents work in the north or abroad, a question to test.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and Soviet-era school sources; mixed for present parenting (polls and forums); thin for the smaller peoples' child-raising, except in community memory.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Turkish; Kurmanji.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children in war-displaced families from Ukraine, and the transfer of such children, are a live political issue: handle as testimony, with care. Military-patriotic schooling is charged. Discipline and its rules belong to Capricorn; any law to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
