# Donska-Kubania: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian and Kuban *balachka*, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don and Kuban Cossack** assembly traditions (open criticism in circle) against Soviet and present workplace norms.
- **Adyghe** families, where criticism of elders may be bound by custom, against urban Adyghe youth online.
- **Industrial workers** with the Novocherkassk memory against farm and resort workers.
- **Armenian communities** of Sochi and Nakhichevan-on-Don; their humor traditions.
- **Old Believers and Protestants**, and **Meskhetian Turks**: check how criticism aimed at minorities in the 1990s-2000s was voiced and answered.
- **Journalists, activists and ecologists** against the general public.
- **Generations:** Soviet meeting culture against online anonymity.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in archives for Soviet criticism; current data shaped by censorship and self-censorship.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Adyghe, Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of the state and the war carries legal risk now; foreign-agent labels on several outlets and NGOs; protect sources and do not expose living critics. Laws on speech: Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
