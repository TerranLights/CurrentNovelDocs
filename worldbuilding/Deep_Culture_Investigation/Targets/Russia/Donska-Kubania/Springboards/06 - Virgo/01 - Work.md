# Donska-Kubania: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Kuban *balachka* and Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian, Meskhetian Turkish, Kalmyk. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack householders against *inogorodnie* and *batraki*:** who worked their own land and who hired out.
- **Eastern Donbass miners** against Rostov, Taganrog and Novocherkassk factory workers.
- **Resort and port service workers** of Sochi, Anapa, Gelendzhik and Novorossiysk: seasonal against year-round work.
- **Adyghe aul farmers** against urban Maikop professionals.
- **Hamshen Armenians** (hill farming, tea, tobacco) against **Don Armenians** (an urban trade and craft history to check).
- **Meskhetian Turks** in Krymsk district vegetable farming, and the families resettled in the U.S. in the mid-2000s.
- **Kalmyk herders** of the Salsk steppe and Pontic Greek villages: steppe and hill economies outside the grain belt.
- **Central Asian and Caucasian labor migrants** in construction and markets.
- **Women's paid and unpaid work** in stanitsa and city; men on *vakhta* rotation far from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet industrial and kolkhoz labor; mixed for the post-Soviet informal economy; thin in Adyghe and minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Turkish for the Meskhetian community abroad.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dekulakization and collectivization; the 1962 Novocherkassk events (see Criticism); exploitation of migrants. Labor law and "parasitism" statutes go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
