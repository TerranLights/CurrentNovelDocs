# Donska-Kubania: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with Don and Kuban speech), Kuban *balachka*, Adyghe; Armenian, Greek, Turkish, Kalmyk for minorities. **Concept in one line:** what good work and a well-kept life are: the place of work in a life, service, the daily and yearly round, the kept house, craft, rest and health.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper against Lower Don Cossacks**, plus the Khopyor and Medveditsa districts: different settlement and farm histories.
- **Black Sea against Linear Kuban Cossacks:** Zaporozhian-descended, *balachka*-speaking against Don- and Volga-descended, Russian-speaking.
- **Inogorodnie** (non-Cossack settlers) and their descendants: landless labor against Cossack service.
- **Adyghe** by subgroup (Temirgoy, Bzhedug, Abadzekh), and the **Black Sea Shapsugs** of the Sochi coast.
- **Don Armenians** of Nakhichevan-on-Don (Myasnikovsky district) against **Hamshen Armenians** of the Sochi and Tuapse hills.
- **Pontic Greeks** (Gelendzhik, Krymsk, Anapa areas); **Meskhetian Turks** (Krymsk district); **Don Kalmyks** of the Salsk steppe, historically part of the Don host.
- **Old Believers**, including the returned **Nekrasov Cossacks**; Estonian and Czech villages of the Black Sea coast; Kurds and Yazidis; Roma; the Jewish community of Rostov.
- **Work settings:** eastern Donbass mining towns (Shakhty, Gukovo); ports (Novorossiysk, Taganrog); resorts (Sochi, Anapa); grain farming; the two big cities.
- **Diasporas:** the Cossack emigration (France, Serbia, the U.S.); the Circassian diaspora (Turkey, Jordan, Syria); Kosovo Adyghe repatriates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Russian for the Cossack hosts and Soviet labor; mixed for the Adyghe; thin for smaller minorities. Soviet ethnography and film stage work as an ideal: read as claims.
- **Lands:** the tracker header carries only "Russia" as pre-war lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet and the rest of the Virgo set are written for the core Don and Kuban lands (Rostov Oblast, Krasnodar Krai, Adygea).
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Adyghe, Ukrainian, Armenian and Greek for depth; Turkish for the Meskhetian and Circassian diasporas.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** decossackization, the 1932-33 famine, the 1864 Circassian expulsion, wartime deportations; current censorship and the foreign-agent status of some pollsters. Cossack host statutes and labor law: Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
