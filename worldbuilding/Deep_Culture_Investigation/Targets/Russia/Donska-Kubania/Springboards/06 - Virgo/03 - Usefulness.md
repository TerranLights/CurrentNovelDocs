*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa pensioners** (plots, livestock, grandchildren) against **city pensioners** in Rostov and Krasnodar.
- **Cossack elders** in revived councils against non-Cossack elders.
- **Adyghe elders** (*nakhyzh*) and their public roles, against urban Adyghe youth.
- **Men in garage and repair cultures** against office workers; **women** as household providers and as grandmothers.
- **Disabled workers** and their organizations; veterans of recent wars returning injured.
- **Mining towns** after pit closures (Shakhty, Gukovo, Krasny Sulin): a whole community's usefulness questioned.
- **Don Armenians** and their enlightenment tradition; **Pontic Greeks**; **Meskhetian Turks**; **Kalmyks** of the steppe.
- **Labor migrants** valued only as hands; **young people** who leave for Moscow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; national surveys exist, regional cuts need checking; Adyghe and minority sources thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Adyghe, Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** end-of-life, disability and suicide among older men; wounded veterans. Pension and disability law: Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
