# Donska-Kubania: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian and Kuban *balachka*, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Displaced villagers** of the Tsimlyansk and Krasnodar reservoirs against those who gained irrigated land.
- **Kuban delta fishers** against rice farmers.
- **Adyghe** communities: imposed improvement in Soviet times, self-directed renewal now; **repatriates** from Kosovo and Syria against long-settled Adyghe.
- **Cossack** revivalists, who seek improvement by restoring old forms, against modernizers.
- **Agronomists and breeders** as local heroes against ordinary farm workers.
- **City newcomers** in fast-growing Krasnodar against older residents; **Sochi residents** moved for Olympic building.
- **Meskhetian Turks** who left and who stayed; **Hamshen Armenians** and **Pontic Greeks**, whose ties to emigrant kin are worth checking.
- **Young people** who leave for Moscow for betterment against those who stay.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state projects and crop science; mixed for personal self-improvement; thin for minority voices on imposed "improvement".
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Adyghe, Armenian, Turkish; Arabic for Syrian Circassian repatriates' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** displacement for dams and Olympic building; the 1860s settlement of Circassian lands framed as "taming"; repatriates' legal status. Property and migration law: Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
