# Donska-Kubania: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian and Kuban *balachka*, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Adyghe master craftswomen and craftsmen** (embroidery, silver, saddles) against mass-produced "souvenir" work.
- **Black Sea and Linear Kuban Cossack** textile traditions; **Don** stanitsa crafts.
- **Old Believers** (exact rite) against mainstream Orthodox and secular neighbors.
- **Factory and precision-industry workers** (Rostov, Taganrog aircraft and helicopter plants) against farm work run on season and weather.
- **High-pressure school tracks** (city lyceums, medal hunters) against rural schools.
- **Armenian and Greek** craft and baking traditions; **Hamshen** tobacco and tea growers' standards of leaf.
- **Women** and the perfect home and appearance; **men** and the perfect vehicle, weapon or horse.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crafts and Soviet quality campaigns; thin for psychological perfectionism outside student samples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Adyghe, Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eating disorders, anxiety and suicide among high achievers; handle with care. Craft knowledge held by families may not be shared freely.
- **Held for review:** none.
