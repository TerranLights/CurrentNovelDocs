# Donska-Kubania: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian and Kuban *balachka*, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox parishioners** (with confession and elders) against **Old Believers** and **Protestant** congregations, each with its own way of testing spirits.
- **Adyghe** families, judging by *adygage* and kin standing, against urban Maikop youth.
- **Don and Kuban winegrowers and horse breeders:** two expert taste cultures.
- **Market traders** of several origins (Armenian, Azerbaijani, Meskhetian, Central Asian, Russian) against their buyers.
- **Elderly people** (to check whether they are the main fraud targets) against young urban users.
- **Kuban "old Ekaterinodar" families** against newcomers and northern retirees on the coast.
- **Hamshen and Pontic Greek** villages, where matchmaking and godparent choice may run on other lines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; church and folk sources rich, measured data on judgment thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Church Slavonic for older texts, Adyghe, Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "fake news" laws and state fact-checking (point any law to Libra or Capricorn); prejudice against Caucasian and Central Asian traders presented as savvy.
