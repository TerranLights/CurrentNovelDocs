# Donska-Kubania: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Adyghe, Kuban *balachka*, Armenian, Greek, Turkish. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Registered Cossack societies** against unregistered ones and against non-Cossack neighbors: who claims service as a birthright.
- **Adyghe**, by subgroup and by former estate (princely, *uerk*, free, dependent): service once ran along those lines.
- **Orthodox, Muslim and Armenian Apostolic** charity and volunteering; Protestant and Old Believer congregations.
- **Resort workers** of Sochi, Anapa and Gelendzhik, often seasonal and from elsewhere, against the guests they serve.
- **Military families** in garrison towns (Novocherkassk, Maikop, Rostov) against civilian households.
- **Domestic workers**, past (merchant households of Rostov and Nakhichevan-on-Don) and present (migrant nannies and cleaners).
- **Pontic Greek and Hamshen** village communities, with their own feast-serving roles.
- **Kalmyk and Buddhist** service to temple and elders, where Kalmyks live in the nation's lands.
- **Émigré Cossacks**, for whom service to the old host continued abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Cossack service history; mixed for volunteering; thin for domestic service and in Adyghe-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Adyghe, Armenian; German and English for the 1945 handover accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current conscription and mobilization; wartime collaboration and the Lienz handover; the Circassian dependent estates (unfree service). Conscription law and the legal status of Cossack societies: Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
