# Donska-Kubania: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian and Kuban *balachka*, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish, Kalmyk. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa households** with *babki* and charm healers against city clinics; Don and Kuban each separately.
- **Black Sea Cossack** healing lore (Ukrainian forms) against **Linear and Don** forms.
- **Adyghe** families: the *chapsh* heritage, Muslim teaching on illness, and modern medicine.
- **Hamshen Armenians**, **Pontic Greeks** and **Meskhetian Turks**, each with home remedies and evil-eye beliefs to check.
- **Kalmyks** and Tibetan-tradition healers, where Kalmyk communities fall within the lands.
- **The Ubykh legacy** of the Sochi coast, whose language is gone: what is remembered of their healing places only through others.
- **Old Believers**, whose stance toward state medicine needs checking, not assuming.
- **Miners**, **farm workers** exposed to chemicals, and **resort staff** against resort guests.
- **Women** as family nurses; **men** and doctor avoidance, as a question to test.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state health statistics and resort medicine; mixed for folk healing; thin in Adyghe.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian; Kalmyk if Kalmyk lands are included.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' charms are often kept secret and passed to chosen heirs; ask, do not extract. HIV, drug use, abortion, alcohol and mental illness need care. Health law and insurance rules: Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
