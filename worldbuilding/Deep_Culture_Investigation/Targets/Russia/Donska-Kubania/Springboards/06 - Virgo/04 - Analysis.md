## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Engineers and academics** of Rostov, Taganrog and Novocherkassk against **farmers and agronomists** of the Kuban grain belt.
- **Cossack practical wit** (*smekalka*) as a self-image, against school-taught analysis; test both, assume neither.
- **Adyghe intelligentsia** in Maikop against aul elders whose authority rests on experience.
- **Don Armenians**, whose merchant history is worth checking for reckoning habits; **Pontic Greeks**; **Meskhetian Turks**.
- **Men and women** in STEM study and work.
- **Orthodox, Muslim and secular** views of reason and science.
- **Generations:** Soviet technical intelligentsia against post-Soviet managers and coders.
- **The resort coast** (service economy) against the industrial Don.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institutions and exam statistics; thin for folk reasoning and for minority-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** contested census categories (Cossack as an ethnicity; Adyghe and Circassian naming); current limits on publishing some statistics.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Hermitage collection site and eLibrary.ru for «Тмутараканский камень» and note the measurement it records.
2. Search the Russian State Library for «Кубанский сборник» and list the statistical issues digitized.
3. Download FIPI's regional ЕГЭ reports for Krasnodar Krai and Rostov Oblast in mathematics.
4. Search eLibrary.ru for «Шпильрейн Ростов» for local memory work on her.
5. Pull city counts from rating.chgk.info for the region's towns.
