## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack descendants**, whose tradition of electing their own judges and atamans may shape trust in appointed ones.
- **Adyghe communities**, with elders, imams and state courts as competing arbiters.
- **Nakhichevan-on-Don Armenians**, a community with its own historical court; and the **Hamshen** of the coast.
- **Meskhetian Turks, Kurds and Yazidis**, who may see officials as partial against newcomers.
- **Rostov-on-Don's business and university milieu** against small district towns where "everyone knows the judge".
- **Agro-business districts of Kuban**, where land and courts meet.

## 4. Flags
