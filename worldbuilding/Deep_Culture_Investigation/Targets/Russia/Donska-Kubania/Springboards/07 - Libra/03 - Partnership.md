# Donska-Kubania: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and balachka; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black Sea (Zaporozhian-descended) stanitsas against Line stanitsas** of Kuban: different wedding rites and words for spouses.
- **Upper Don against Lower Don** families; and Don Old Believers, who may keep stricter rules on marriage within the faith.
- **Adyghe and Shapsug families**, where alliance between kin groups weighs heavily in a match.
- **Nakhichevan-on-Don Armenians and Hamshen Armenians** of the Sochi coast: two distinct Armenian communities.
- **Pontic Greeks, Meskhetian Turks, Kurds and Yazidis**: endogamy and mixed marriage to compare.
- **Cities against stanitsas**: Rostov and Krasnodar students and migrants against farm families.
- **Work crews**: Azov fishermen, combine-harvester teams, port workers at Novorossiysk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wedding folklore and national statistics; mixed for business partnership; thin for minorities' marriage practice in Russian-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish; Ukrainian for balachka texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bride abduction in some Caucasian practice; underage marriage; domestic violence (partly decriminalized in 2017); same-sex couples face legal restrictions in Russia, so data and voices are hard to reach safely.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "свадебный обряд кубанских казаков" and "донская казачья свадьба".
2. Pull the 2021 census marital-status tables for the three subjects of the federation.
3. Search "аталычество" and "адыгская свадьба" in the АРИГИ and Adyghe State University catalogs.
4. Search the Family Code text for "брачный договор" and "совместная собственность супругов".
5. Ask the Museum of Russian-Armenian Friendship whether it holds Nakhichevan family papers or marriage records.
