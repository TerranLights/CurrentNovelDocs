## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don against Kuban** political memory: the Don's Host assembly and the Kuban Rada balanced power differently.
- **Adygea's two main communities**, Adyghe and Russian-speaking, and how each is represented.
- **Wine country of the Black Sea coast** (Anapa, Abrau-Durso, Gelendzhik) against grain stanitsas, for moderation and drink.
- **Fishing settlements of the Azov coast and the Kuban delta** (Yeysk, Primorsko-Akhtarsk, Temryuk), where the balance of the waters is a livelihood.
- **Old Believers and devout Orthodox** against secular townspeople, for the fasting year.
- **Sunni Adyghe and Meskhetian Turks**, whose fasting month brings its own rhythm.
- **Armenian and Greek** communities with their own church calendars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good for ecology, law and time-use statistics; thinner for folk ideas of bodily balance and for minority sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** representation rules by ethnicity in Adygea are politically delicate; environmental reporting on the Azov may be limited.
- **Held for review:** none.
