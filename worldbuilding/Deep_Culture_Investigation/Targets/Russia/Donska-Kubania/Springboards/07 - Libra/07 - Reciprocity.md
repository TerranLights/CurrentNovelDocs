# Donska-Kubania: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian and its Don and Kuban dialects; Ukrainian and balachka; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black Sea stanitsas** (balachka, Ukrainian-rooted caroling and work-bees) against **Line stanitsas** with Russian customs.
- **Don stanitsas of the upper and lower river**, and Old Believer families, for whom funeral and feast obligations may differ.
- **Adyghe villages of Adygea** and **Shapsug villages near Tuapse and Sochi**, for hospitality and the guest's return.
- **Nakhichevan-on-Don Armenians** and **Hamshen Armenians**: two forms of the shared feast.
- **Pontic Greeks**, **Kurds and Yazidis**, and **Meskhetian Turks**, each with village feasts and kin networks.
- **City households** in Rostov and Krasnodar against farm households, where help with harvest and building may still be swapped.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Russian-language folklore and for survey data on household transfers; mixed for Adyghe customs; thin for Greek, Kurdish and Yazidi sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian (including dialect dictionaries); Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish; Kurmanji.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** favor exchange shades into bribery (keep it apart from law, which is in this sign's section 0); Yazidi religious practice is private.
- **Held for review:** none.
