# Donska-Kubania: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and balachka; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself, and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack descendants and non-Cossack settlers**: each group's picture of the other.
- **Kuban's Ukrainian-rooted stanitsas** and Russian-rooted ones, as borderland identities shift.
- **Adyghe and Shapsugs** against Slavic neighbors: images held on both sides.
- **Meskhetian Turks, Armenians, Kurds, Yazidis and Chechens** in Rostov and Krasnodar: check how press talk of "migrants" groups or separates them.
- **The Jewish community of Rostov-on-Don** and its memory of 1942.
- **Border districts of Rostov Oblast** facing the Donbas against inland Kuban and the Black Sea resorts.
