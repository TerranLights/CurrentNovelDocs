# Donska-Kubania: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and balachka; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Don stanitsas**, the setting of the Melekhov story and of Civil War switching of sides, against the **lower Don**.
- **Black Sea against Line** factions in Kuban political memory.
- **Revived Cossack societies** (decision by assembly) against municipal bodies (decision by office).
- **Adyghe councils and families**; the **Shapsug** communities of the coast.
- **Armenian, Greek and Meskhetian Turkish families**, where parents and elders may share or take a young person's choices.
- **Young people in Rostov and Krasnodar universities** against farm families, on choosing careers and partners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Civil War's divided loyalties and for literary criticism; mixed for psychology data from the south; thin for minority languages' vocabulary of decision.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Civil War loyalties still divide families; current choices about war, mobilization and emigration are sensitive to ask about.
- **Held for review:** none.
