# Donska-Kubania: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and balachka; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack descendants**, split by family memories of 1918, 1933 and 1942: when giving way was judged survival and when betrayal.
- **Adyghe families**, with their own rites for ending feuds.
- **Pontic Greeks, Kalmyks and Meskhetian Turks**, all deported at some point: what silence meant for each.
- **Evangelical, Baptist and other small faiths**, for principled nonviolence against simple compliance.
- **Women in stanitsas and in cities**, for endurance at home; **migrant workers**, for whom complaint can cost residence.
- **Port and resort towns**, where protection payments may be part of business.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: law and court data are rich; voices of those who kept quiet are thin by nature and need oral history.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Greek; Turkish; Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence; wartime speech prosecutions; collaborator memorials; OVD-Info and ANNA Center have been labeled "foreign agents"; interviewees may be at risk.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the 2017 amendment to article 116 and the related Administrative Code article 6.1.1.

3. Pull settlement counts from the Judicial Department's civil-case reports for the three regions.
4. Search the OVD-Info database for cases in Rostov Oblast, Krasnodar Krai and Adygea.
5. Search eLibrary.ru for "депортация греков 1949 Кубань устная история".
