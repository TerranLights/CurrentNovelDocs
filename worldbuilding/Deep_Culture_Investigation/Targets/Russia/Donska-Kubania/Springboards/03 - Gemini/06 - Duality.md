# Donska-Kubania: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian (with Hamshen), Greek and Turkish (for Turkish-speaking Greeks and Meskhetians). **Concept in one line:** how a culture lives with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how it treats people who show two faces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kuban families of Black Sea Cossack origin**, with Ukrainian roots and Russian schooling, against **Line Cossack** families of Don and Russian origin.
- **Upper Don and lower Don**, and **Red and White** family memories inside the same stanitsa.
- **Adyghe** communities: customary code beside Islam; Adygea's villages against the Shapsug coast.
- **Christian Hamshen Armenians** against **Muslim Hemshin**; **Don Armenians** in a city once their own.
- **Pontic Greeks** against **Turkish-speaking Greeks**; **Meskhetian Turks** between Georgia, Russia and Turkey.
- **Nekrasovite descendants** and other Old Believers; **Cossack revivalists** against those who call themselves simply Russian.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history and identity scholarship; mixed for present-day attitudes; thin for the smaller groups.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish.
