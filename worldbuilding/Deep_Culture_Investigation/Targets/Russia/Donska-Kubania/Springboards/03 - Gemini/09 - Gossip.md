# Donska-Kubania: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian-based Kuban speech, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish (Meskhetian). **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small stanitsas and khutors**, where everyone knows everyone, against **Rostov and Krasnodar** neighborhoods.
- **Adyghe auls**, where shame and family name may weigh heavily; the **Shapsug** coast villages apart.
- **Armenian, Greek and Meskhetian Turkish** communities, small and close, where reputation may follow families across countries.
- **Resort towns** (Sochi, Anapa), with seasonal strangers and celebrity talk.
- **Women's and men's** gossip settings, measured, not assumed; **young online** against **older bench-side** talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and Soviet-era rumor records; mixed for current life; online pages are plentiful but unsystematic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Kushchevskaya and other crime cases involve real victims; famine-era rumor ties to repression; gossip pages name private people, so cite pages and patterns, never individuals. Slander and insult law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for «слухи» with «Кубань», «Дон», «Северный Кавказ»; sort by period.

3. Search the «Фольклор и постфольклор» site for «слухи» and «современные легенды».
4. Search news archives for «Крымск 2012 слухи» and list official replies.
5. Search folklore catalogs for «частушки Кубань» and «джэгуако» and note collectors and districts.
