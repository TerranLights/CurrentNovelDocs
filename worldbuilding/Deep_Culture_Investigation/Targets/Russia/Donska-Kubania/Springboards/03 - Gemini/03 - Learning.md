## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack families** with revived cadet and heritage schooling against **non-Cossack** families in the same stanitsas.
- **Adyghe village schools** teaching Adyghe against **Maykop city schools**; the **Shapsugs** of the Sochi coast, where Adyghe schooling is scarcer, *check*.
- **Don Armenians** (Rostov, Myasnikovsky district) and **Hamshen Armenians** (Sochi, Tuapse): church, Sunday and home schooling.
- **Pontic Greeks**, **Koreans**, **Meskhetian Turks**: heritage-language classes and their limits.
- **Farm, wine and fishing districts** against **university cities** (Rostov, Krasnodar, Novocherkassk, Taganrog).
- **Old Believers** and other religious minorities with their own ways of teaching.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial and Soviet school history; mixed for current regional data; thin for minority heritage schooling.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the end of Ukrainian-language schooling in the Kuban in the early 1930s; minority-language school hours as a political question; militarized youth programs. Schooling as an institution is Sagittarius's education; keep this sheet on learning in family, play and work.
- **Held for review:** none.
