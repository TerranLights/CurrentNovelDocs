# Donska-Kubania: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Adyghe (and Turkish and Arabic for the Circassian diaspora), Armenian, Greek, Korean, Turkish (Meskhetian). **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected (or not).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack families** inside registered hosts against those outside them.
- **Adyghe lineages** in Adygea's villages, and **Circassian repatriates** from Kosovo and Syria finding their place.
- **Don Armenians** of Rostov and the Myasnikovsky district against **Hamshen Armenians** on the coast.
- **Pontic Greeks** with kin in Greece; **Meskhetian Turks** with kin in the United States and Turkey; **Koreans** with kin in Central Asia.
- **Central Asian and Caucasian labor migrants** in farm and building work.
- **Elderly people in emptied khutors** against families in growing suburbs of Krasnodar and Rostov.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on blat as a national theme; mixed for southern networks; thin on loneliness measured below the national level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Turkish and Arabic for diaspora Circassian media; Armenian; Greek; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patronage inside Cossack hosts and local government touches corruption, which goes to Libra or Capricorn as law; diaspora ties can draw official suspicion; family splits from deportation and emigration are painful histories.
- **Held for review:** none.
