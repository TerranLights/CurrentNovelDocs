*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek, Turkish (Meskhetian), Kalmyk; English for 19th-century British travelers in Circassia. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and, more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Adyghe villages**, where elders and lineage heads may stand between families; the **Shapsugs** apart.
- **Cossack stanitsas**, with the assembly and the ataman, against **non-Cossack** neighbors who used other channels.
- **Don Armenians** and **Hamshen Armenians**, with church and community leaders as brokers.
- **Meskhetian Turks**, **Kalmyks**, **Pontic Greeks**: small communities that may rely on internal go-betweens.
- **Mining towns** of the eastern Don, where labor brokers matter.
- **Cities** with professional mediators against **villages** with kin and clergy.
