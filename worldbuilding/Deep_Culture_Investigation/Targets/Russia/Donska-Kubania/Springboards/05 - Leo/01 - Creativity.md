*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black Sea Cossacks of the Kuban** (embroidery and *rushnyk* lineage from the Dnieper) against **Line Cossacks** and the **Don** (different craft inheritance).
- **Adyghe makers,** separating Adygea's auls from the coastal **Shapsugs**: gold embroidery, mats and metalwork, and who may hold a family design.
- **Don Armenians** (Saryan's Nakhichevan) and **Hamshen Armenians** on the coast; **Pontic Greeks** (instrument making, the *lyra*, *check*).
- **Kalmyks** of the Salsk steppe: felt, embroidery and Buddhist arts.
- **Rostov and Krasnodar art scenes** against stanitsa crafts; the resort crafts trade along the Sochi and Anapa coast.
- **Industrial towns** (Taganrog, Novocherkassk, Rostov) as engineering cultures.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Cossack and Adyghe craft in museums and ethnography; mixed for contemporary art; thin on everyday improvising, which needs oral and online sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian for older Kuban craft vocabulary; Adyghe; Armenian; Kalmyk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1998 prosecution of Avdey Ter-Oganyan and later clashes between activists and art shows; Circassian heritage held in museums abroad after 1864; claims to Cossack symbols by rival organizations.
- **Held for review:** none.
