# Donska-Kubania: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Adyghe, Armenian, Kalmyk; Turkish for Meskhetian sources. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack descendants** in revived hosts against those outside them: different routes to rank and honor.
- **Shapsugs** against other **Adyghe:** distinct official status.
- **Kalmyks** (rehabilitated after deportation) and **Meskhetian Turks** (registration denied for years): two histories of recognition given and withheld.
- **Don and Hamshen Armenians, Pontic Greeks, Ukrainians, Kurds and Yazidis, Roma.**
- **Women's unsung work** in stanitsa farms and households, against men's honored service.
- **Seasonal and migrant workers** on Kuban farms and Black Sea resorts: work often uncredited.
- **Professions:** miners, grain farmers, teachers and doctors, each with their own titles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors and military awards; mixed for everyday praise; thin for withheld recognition, which lives in NGO, émigré and oral sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Kalmyk; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1864 expulsion and genocide-recognition claims; the deportations and their rehabilitation; Meskhetian statelessness; NGOs that documented these were labeled or closed before the war.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search *Память народа* by birthplace for Krasnodar Krai and Rostov Oblast; sample the citation wording.
2. Download the regional honors statutes of Krasnodar Krai and Rostov Oblast and their published recipient lists.
3. On eLibrary.ru, search «шапсуги коренной малочисленный народ признание» and «турки-месхетинцы Краснодарский край регистрация».
4. Locate published texts of the 1570 letter and the 1792 Black Sea Host charter.
