# Donska-Kubania: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian (Kuban), Adyghe, Armenian, Turkish (Meskhetian), Kalmyk. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack men under host discipline** against men outside the hosts.
- **Brides and young women** in Cossack and Adyghe families: shaming tied to reputation, *check* how each group framed it.
- **Conscripts and their families** across all groups.
- **Stanitsas blacklisted in 1932-1933,** and **Kalmyks, Meskhetian Turks, Adyghe and Shapsugs:** collective humiliations differ in kind and in memory.
- **Roma, labor migrants, Kurds and Yazidis:** targets of hostile coverage.
- **Prisoners and former prisoners:** the criminal underworld's caste of the degraded, *check* sources.
- **Schoolchildren, by town size and ethnicity.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical collective humiliation and ethnography; mixed for bullying and hazing; thin for everyday family shaming.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Turkish; Kalmyk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1932-1933 famine and its framing; deportations; army hazing records are closed; prison degradation involves sexual violence and needs care; NGO sources carry foreign-agent labels; punishment as a legal matter belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries

3. Read the chronicle entry on Rededya, then search АРИГИ catalogs for Adyghe tellings of the duel.
4. Pull Soldiers' Mothers reports naming units in the Southern Military District.
5. Search press archives for «Кущёвская позор станицы» to collect residents' own words.
