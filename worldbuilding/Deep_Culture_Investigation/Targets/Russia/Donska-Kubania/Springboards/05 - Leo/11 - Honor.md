# Donska-Kubania: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (including criminal argot), Adyghe, Kalmyk, Armenian, Turkish (Meskhetian), Kurmanji. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women** in every group: test, not assume, whether honor rests on courage for one and propriety for the other.
- **Cossack descendants** in revived hosts against those outside them.
- **Adyghe and Shapsugs:** the *khabze* vocabulary of honor.
- **Meskhetian Turks, Kurds and Yazidis:** *namus*.
- **Don and Hamshen Armenians, Pontic Greeks, Kalmyks** (*Jangar*), **Ukrainians.**
- **Military families and veterans;** the **criminal subculture;** urban youth and fight clubs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for epic, Cossack and Caucasus ethnography; mixed for contemporary practice; thin and sensitive for family honor and violence.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, including argot; Adyghe; Kalmyk; Armenian; Turkish; Kurmanji.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor-related violence and bride abduction must be researched in survivors' words; Lienz and collaboration are bitter topics; wartime commemoration is politicized; defamation as law belongs to Libra, duty to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
