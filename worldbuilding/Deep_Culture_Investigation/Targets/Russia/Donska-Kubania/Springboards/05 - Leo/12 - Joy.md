*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox Cossack descendants, Old Believers and evangelicals:** three Christian registers of joy.
- **Adyghe and Meskhetian Muslims;** **Kalmyk Buddhists;** **Armenian Apostolic** and **Greek Orthodox** coastal communities.
- **Men and women:** who may show delight openly, in dance and at the table.
- **Young and old:** concerts, beaches and clubs against church feasts and family tables.
- **City, stanitsa and resort coast:** the coast sells joy to visitors; locals may keep different holidays.
- **Mining towns and farm villages** in hard years.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and folklore; mixed for surveys (national, some district cuts); thin for private and quiet joy.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Kalmyk; Armenian; Pontic Greek; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** public festivity was curtailed during the fighting from 2022 (cancelled festivals and fireworks, mourning periods), so recent calendars are atypical, *check*; depression data are scarce; alcohol at celebrations is a health topic, not a measure of joy.
- **Held for review:** none.
