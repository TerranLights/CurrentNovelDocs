# Donska-Kubania: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Adyghe, Armenian; Yiddish and Hebrew for older Jewish community records; Kalmyk. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Industrial and mining workers** (Novocherkassk, Shakhty, Gukovo) against **Kuban grain farmers and farmhands.**
- **The old in stanitsas and *хутора*** against those in city care homes.
- **Disabled people** in Rostov and Krasnodar against the resort coast built for 2014.
- **Adyghe and Shapsugs** (their own ethical code), **Kalmyks** (deportation memory), **Meskhetian Turks**, **Rostov's Jewish community**, **Don and Hamshen Armenians**, **Roma**, **labor migrants**.
- **Orthodox, Old Believer, Muslim, Buddhist, Armenian Apostolic and Protestant** communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, church teaching and Adyghe ethics; mixed in surveys; thin and fragmented for institutional care and migrant life.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Kalmyk; Yiddish or Hebrew for older community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Novocherkassk 1962 documents were long secret; Holocaust memory disputes; reports on care homes and detention; NGO sources may carry foreign-agent labels. "Honor and dignity" as a legal claim belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. On eLibrary.ru, search «адыгская этика цІыхугъэ» and «достоинство в народной культуре кубанских казаков».
2. Read the 2008 Orthodox document on dignity, then search Don and Kuban diocesan sites for sermons citing it.
3. Pull the last five annual reports of the Krasnodar Krai and Rostov Oblast human-rights commissioners.
4. Search the Russian State Library for «Новочеркасск 1962 документы».
