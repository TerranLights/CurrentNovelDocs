## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Rich folklore on omens; little measured data on intuition in the region; much popular material is sensational.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian (Classical for dream books); Greek; Turkish; Arabic for Islamic terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** psychic and healer trades can exploit clients; keep private consultations anonymous. Gut suspicion of ethnic outsiders shades into prejudice: study it as such.
- **Held for review:** none.
