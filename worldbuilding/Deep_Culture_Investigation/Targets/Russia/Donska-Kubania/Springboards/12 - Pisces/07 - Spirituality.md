# Donska-Kubania: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian and Church Slavonic; Kuban *balachka* and Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian (including Hamshen); Greek and Pontic Greek; Turkish; Kurmanji for Yazidi and Kurdish communities. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion (experience and practice; institutions are Sagittarius's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Churched Orthodox** against **believers "at heart"** and the secular.
- **Old Believers; Molokans; Baptists and Pentecostals.**
- **Adyghe Muslims** against **Хабзэ revivalists**; returnees from Syria with their own Islamic practice.
- **Don Armenians** (Apostolic) against **Hamshen Armenians** of the coast.
- **Pontic Greeks; Meskhetian Turks; Yazidis; Kurds; Jews of Rostov.**
- **Kalmyk Buddhists** if Kalmyk lands are included.
- **Cossack revival groups,** for whom Orthodoxy marks identity.
- **Resort-coast newcomers** in yoga and retreat culture.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Orthodox devotion is well recorded; Adyghe, Hamshen and Yazidi practice mostly in small ethnographies; surveys rarely split below the region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Church Slavonic; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish; Kurmanji; Italian and English for early travelers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Yazidi rites and some Adyghe grove rites may be closed: research only what communities publish. "Spiritual" language is used by the state; separate it from people's own usage. Several religious groups face bans; their legal status is Libra's or Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Interiano's text in an English or Russian edition (search "Interiano Zychi Circassi").
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "адыгское язычество Хабзэ возрождение" and "священные рощи адыги".
3. Search diocesan websites for "святые источники Краснодарского края" and "Донская митрополия святые источники".
4. Search for "амшенские армяне матах" and "понтийские греки Краснодарский край Панагия".
5. Pull the Pew 2017 Russia figures on prayer and belief as the national baseline.
