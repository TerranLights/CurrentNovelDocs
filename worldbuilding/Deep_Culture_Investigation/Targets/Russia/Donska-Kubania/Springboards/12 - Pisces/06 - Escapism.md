# Donska-Kubania: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Kuban *balachka* and Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal resort towns** (Sochi, Anapa, Gelendzhik), whose living is others' escape, against **inland stanitsas**.
- **Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar** nightlife against farm districts.
- **Mining towns** of the Eastern Donbass, with their own drinking and drug history.
- **Adyghe and Meskhetian Turk** Muslim households (how far religious abstinence holds is a question to check) against Orthodox and secular drinking customs; **Armenian** and **Pontic Greek** feasts.
- **Newcomers from the North** who moved south to escape against long-settled families.
- **Men and women; young and old;** Old Believers and Protestants who forbid drink.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tourism and alcohol statistics; thin for gaming, reading and daydreaming at regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, including slang for drinking and gambling; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and rehab abuses involve living people; gambling and drug rules go to Libra or Capricorn. The Bulavin rising is told differently by Cossack, Soviet and current official histories.
- **Held for review:** none.
