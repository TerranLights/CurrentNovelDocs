# Donska-Kubania: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Kuban *balachka* and Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek and Pontic Greek; Turkish. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher; letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don and Kuban Cossack descendants,** whose memory centers on 1920, Lienz and de-Cossackization.
- **Adyghe:** whether 1864 is told as defeat, expulsion or surrender varies; Muslim and traditional ideas of fate.
- **Pontic Greeks** and **Armenians:** fate in laments and in genocide and deportation memory.
- **Meskhetian Turks:** those who stayed and those who left.
- **Novocherkassk** families and the industrial towns.
- **Orthodox, Old Believers, Protestants, Muslims, secular:** whose resignation is a virtue.
- **Men and women; elders and the young:** "never give up" against "God's will".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on war and political memory; thin on everyday letting go, quitting and end-of-life choices at regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek and Pontic Greek; Turkish; German and English for Lienz sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surrender and capture in current wars are dangerous topics; 1864, Lienz and Novocherkassk are politically charged. End-of-life rules belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
