# Donska-Kubania: The domain, the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Kuban *balachka* (Ukrainian-based) and Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian (incl. Hamshen); Pontic Greek; Meskhetian Turkish; Kalmyk if its lands fall inside. **Concept in one line:** how the culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape; its magic ways and, as runner-up, its order ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper and Lower Don Cossacks** (*верховые*, *низовые*): different histories and speech.
- **Black Sea and Linear Kuban Cossacks:** Zaporozhian, Ukrainian-speaking origin against Russian-speaking Don and Volga origin.
- **Non-Cossack settlers** (*иногородние*) and recent migrants.
- **Adyghe subgroups:** Temirgoy, Bzhedug, Abadzekh, coastal Shapsugs; returnees from Kosovo and Syria.
- **Armenians:** Don Armenians (Nakhichevan-on-Don, Chaltyr) and coastal Hamshen Armenians.
- **Pontic Greeks** (Gelendzhik, Krymsk, Anapa); **Meskhetian Turks**; **Kurds and Yazidis**; **Roma**; **Koreans**; **Jews** of Rostov and Taganrog.
- **Old Believers, Molokans**; **Muslim, Orthodox and Armenian Apostolic** households.
- **Kalmyks** (Don *Buzava*, Salsk steppe) if included.
- **Cities against stanitsas and auls;** resort coast against grain steppe.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for Cossack song and pre-1917 church and ethnographic records; thinner for Adyghe, Armenian, Greek and Turkish communities; belief surveys rarely split below the region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian throughout; Ukrainian and *balachka* for Kuban; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek and Pontic Greek; Turkish.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet writes for the core lands (the Don, Kuban, Adygea, the Black Sea coast).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** memory of the 1864 Circassian expulsion, the 1919 de-Cossackization, the 1932 to 1933 famine and the stanitsa "blacklists", the deportations of Kalmyks, Greeks, Germans and Meskhetian Turks, and Novocherkassk 1962 is contested; read the communities' own accounts first. Witchcraft talk can name living neighbors: keep it anonymous. Some pollsters carry legal labels.
- **Held for review:** none.
