# Donska-Kubania: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Kuban *balachka* and Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian (including Hamshen); Greek; Turkish. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa households** behind fences and shared streets against **city apartment** dwellers.
- **Don and Kuban Cossack descendants** with a frontier memory against **non-Cossack** settlers.
- **Adyghe families:** in-law avoidance, the guest house, and men's and women's spaces, each family varying.
- **Hamshen and Don Armenians; Pontic Greeks; Meskhetian Turks; Kurds and Yazidis; Roma:** group lines kept from inside and drawn from outside.
- **Old Believers** and other dissenting Christians.
- **Border towns:** those near the Ukrainian border in Rostov Oblast, Adler at the Abkhaz line, and Taman.
- **Labor migrants** in dormitories and shared rooms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich historical frontier material; thin modern data on personal space and privacy at regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, with older survey and archival hands; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian, including Hamshen speech; Greek; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the pre-war line with Ukraine was a war front; treat crossers' accounts with care. Ethnic exclusion in Krasnodar Krai is politically charged; Memorial was dissolved by court order, so its archives may be hard to reach. Property and border law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
