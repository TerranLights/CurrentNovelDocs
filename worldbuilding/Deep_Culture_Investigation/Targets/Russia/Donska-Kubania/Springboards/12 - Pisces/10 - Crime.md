# Donska-Kubania: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Kuban *balachka* and Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. **Law:** not here; this sheet takes the idea of wrongdoing and the wrongdoer, and points to Libra (the letter of the law) and Capricorn (legal culture) for law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don and Kuban Cossack descendants:** outlaw-rebel heroes against Cossack self-policing ideals.
- **Rostov-on-Don** with its criminal-world nickname against farm stanitsas.
- **Fishing towns** on the Azov coast (poaching) and the **resort coast** (seasonal theft, fraud, corruption around the 2014 Olympics).
- **Adyghe:** feud and reconciliation customs; the *абрек* image.
- **Roma, Meskhetian Turks, labor migrants:** groups often blamed in local talk; record the stereotypes, never adopt them.
- **Armenians, Pontic Greeks:** their own words and fears.
- **Men and women; generations** shaped by the 1990s against those after.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in press and official data; thin on everyday moral judgment by region. Official statistics reflect recording practice.
- **Languages needed:** Russian (including prison argot); Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** victims of Chikatilo and Kushchevskaya have living kin; avoid names beyond the public record. Ethnic crime stereotypes may appear in regional media: treat them as data about prejudice. Wartime "crimes" and protest charges are politically charged. Law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
