## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don stanitsas and Rostov:** village mutual help against urban charity and NGOs.
- **Black Sea and Linear Kuban Cossacks:** Ukrainian-rooted and Russian-rooted church and song traditions of pity.
- **Adyghe auls:** *хабзэ* duties to guest, orphan and elder; Muslim almsgiving; returnees from Syria and Kosovo as both givers and receivers.
- **Armenians:** Don Armenian church charity; Hamshen coastal villages.
- **Pontic Greeks** and their church brotherhoods.
- **Meskhetian Turks:** a community long refused registration in Krasnodar Krai, many resettled abroad in the 2000s: compassion they received and withheld.
- **Protestant and Orthodox** social work compared; **Old Believers**.
- **Refugees and displaced people** from neighboring war zones in Rostov Oblast since 2014.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good pre-1917 church and municipal records; modern giving surveys are national, with few southern breaks; Adyghe and Armenian material mostly in community publications.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian for Kuban song; Adyghe; Armenian; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** aid to displaced people and soldiers is politically charged; the Meskhetian Turks' treatment in Krasnodar Krai is contested; NGO legal status belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
