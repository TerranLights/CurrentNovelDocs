# Donska-Kubania: The domain: possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia; written for the core Don and Kuban lands (Rostov Oblast, Krasnodar Krai with the Republic of Adygea inside it, the Azov shore and the Black Sea coast from Taman to Sochi). **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and the Kuban *balachka*; Adyghe; Armenian (Don and Hamshen varieties); Greek (Pontic); Kalmyk for the Don Buzava. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how it is held: possessions, money, resources, values and self-worth, with the food, dress and wealth folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don Cossacks** against the **inogorodnie** (non-Cossack settlers, long without land shares): land and status split.
- **Kuban Black Sea Cossacks** (balachka speakers) against **Line Cossacks** of the eastern Kuban: different founding stocks and household ways.
- **Adyghe** of Adygea (Temirgoy, Bzhedug, Abadzekh) and the **Shapsugs** of the Sochi coast: hospitality and gift customs.
- **Don Armenians** of Nor Nakhichevan (trading tradition) and **Hamshen Armenians** of the coast (tea, tobacco and orchards).
- **Pontic Greeks**, **Meskhetian Turks**, **Koreans** of Rostov Oblast (seasonal farming), **Old Believers** and **Nekrasovite** returnees, **Molokans**, **Don Kalmyks** (herding past, deportation).
- **Coal towns** of the eastern Donbass part of Rostov Oblast (Shakhty, Gukovo); **Azov fishers** (Yeysk, Taganrog); **resort coast** (Sochi, Anapa, Gelendzhik); **rice growers** of the Kuban delta; **big cities** (Rostov-on-Don, Krasnodar).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official statistics, Cossack land history and Kuban food; mixed for Adyghe and Armenian household economy; thin for Kalmyks, Koreans and Meskhetian Turks.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the core Don and Kuban lands named above.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Adyghe; Ukrainian and balachka; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dekulakization and the 1932-33 Kuban famine, including the "black boards" stanitsas; de-Cossackization and confiscation (1919-20); the Circassian expulsion and loss of land; 1990s privatization of collective-farm land and land seizures ("raiding"). All contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
