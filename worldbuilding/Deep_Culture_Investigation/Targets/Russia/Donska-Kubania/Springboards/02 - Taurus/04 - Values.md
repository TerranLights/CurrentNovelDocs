## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox majority**, **Old Believers** and **Molokans**, **Armenian Apostolic** families, **Adyghe Muslims**, **Meskhetian Turks**, **evangelical Protestants** (Baptists and Pentecostals have long roots in the south *check*): different value rankings by faith.
- **Kuban Black Sea** (Ukrainian-rooted) against **Line Cossack** and **Don** traditions.
- **Adyghe** urban Maikop against village Adygea; **Shapsugs** on the coast.
- **Don Armenians** (urban, mercantile) against **Hamshen Armenians** (rural, coastal).
- **Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar** young professionals against stanitsa elders.
- **Resort coast** (Sochi, Anapa, Gelendzhik), where outsiders, tourists and money press on local values.
- **Coal towns** of eastern Rostov Oblast; **Koryo-saram** and **Pontic Greeks** as smaller groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official and church rhetoric; mixed for survey data at regional level; thin for minority voices.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Ukrainian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "traditional values" is political; environmental NGOs face restrictions; the stated values of the state are not the people's values. Record variation, not one voice. Law-related values go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
