## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack descendants** against **inogorodnie descendants**: the old line between holders and renters.
- **Collective-farm pensioners** who sold their land shares, **family farmers** (фермеры) and **agroholding** managers.
- **Adyghe** of Adygea (land inside a separate republic) and **Shapsugs** of the coast (land lost, little regained).
- **Hamshen Armenians** and **Pontic Greeks** around Sochi and Anapa: orchard and tobacco plots; resort land pressure.
- **Koryo-saram** (leased fields), **Meskhetian Turks** (blocked registration), **Kalmyk** families with herding past.
- **Coast homeowners** in Sochi, Anapa, Gelendzhik, where land value soared; **coal-town** owners in eastern Rostov Oblast where it fell.
- **Old Believers**, **Molokans** and the returned **Nekrasovites**: communities whose ownership rules differ.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land statistics and press; mixed for opinion by region; thin for minority land histories.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Korean (minor); Ukrainian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Kushchevskaya killings; violent seizure of land; collectivization and Circassian expulsion; Meskhetian Turk exclusion. Real victims' names stay out of a public repo.
- **Law:** property statutes, land codes and court rulings go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
