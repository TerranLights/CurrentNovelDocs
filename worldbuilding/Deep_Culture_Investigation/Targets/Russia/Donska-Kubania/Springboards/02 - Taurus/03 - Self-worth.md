*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women**: worth tied to providing, service and drink for men; to household, children and appearance for women: test, do not assume.
- **Cossack revival members** (rank, uniform, oath) against non-Cossack neighbors.
- **Adyghe noble-descended families** against commoner families; Shapsugs on the coast.
- **Coal-town families** of eastern Rostov Oblast after the closures, against agrarian Kuban.
- **Don Armenians** (old merchant standing in Nor Nakhichevan) and **Hamshen Armenians**.
- **Pontic Greeks**, **Koryo-saram**, **Meskhetian Turks**, **Kuban Roma**: worth under suspicion or exclusion.
- **Veterans** of Afghanistan, Chechnya and later wars; **pensioners**; **young city workers** in Rostov and Krasnodar.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on honor cultures and labor history, thin on self-esteem surveys at regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Romani (minor).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide; the 1962 shootings; ethnic stigma; current war veterans. Do not frame any people as one "honor culture".
- **Held for review:** none.
