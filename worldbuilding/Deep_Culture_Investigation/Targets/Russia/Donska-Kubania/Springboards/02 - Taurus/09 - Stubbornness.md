## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Adyghe** of Adygea and **Shapsugs** of the coast: memory of the long refusal; feud and reconciliation customs.
- **Old Believers**, **Nekrasovite** returnees, **Molokans**: steadfastness in faith.
- **Kuban Black Sea** and **Line** Cossack descendants; **Don Cossacks** of the upper and lower Don.
- **Farmers** facing land loss against **townspeople**; **coal-town** families after closures.
- **Don Armenians**, **Hamshen Armenians**, **Pontic Greeks**, **Meskhetian Turks**: words such as *inat* shared across groups.
- **Elders** against **the young**; **men** and **women** (who gets called stubborn and who determined).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich history of resistance and folklore; thin direct survey data on stubbornness.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Circassian war and its naming; the 1932-33 famine; current protests carry legal risk for participants. Ethnic jokes about stubborn peoples circulate in the region: record them only as a phenomenon, never as a frame. Protest and rehabilitation law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
