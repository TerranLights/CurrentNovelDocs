## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old merchant peoples**: Don Armenians, Taganrog and coastal Greeks.
- **Cossack families** with dispossessed kulak memory against families of collective-farm chairmen and officials.
- **Adyghe** families of princely or noble descent against commoner families; wealth in horses and hospitality.
- **Agro-tycoons and resort owners** against **farm workers** and seasonal staff.
- **Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar** elites against **eastern Donbass coal towns** and poor hamlets.
- **Hamshen Armenians**, **Meskhetian Turks**, **Koryo-saram**: wealth through family trade and farming against exclusion.
- **Orthodox**, **Old Believer** and **evangelical** teachings on riches; **Muslim** Adyghe and zakat practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and merchant history; mixed for elite wealth (opaque); thin for minority wealth customs.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dekulakization memory; criticism of officials' wealth (legal risk for sources in Russia); organized-crime fortunes of the 1990s. No living private individuals in a public repo beyond public figures.
- **Held for review:** none.
