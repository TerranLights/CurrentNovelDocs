## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa** tables and home wine against **city** cafés and bars in Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar.
- **Don fish cookery** (Azov, lower Don) against **Kuban grain-and-pork** cooking.
- **Adyghe** (cheese, sauces, dance gatherings; Muslim restraint on alcohol for some) and **Shapsugs**.
- **Hamshen Armenians** (tea, orchards) and **Don Armenians** (urban Armenian cuisine).
- **Pontic Greeks**, **Koryo-saram**, **Meskhetian Turks**: dishes and dances of their own.
- **Orthodox fasters**, **Old Believers**, **Baptists and Molokans** (abstaining), against the secular majority.
- **Resort coast** (Sochi, Anapa, Gelendzhik: spa, sea and holiday pleasure) against inland farm districts.
- **Generations**: Soviet sanatorium culture against new wellness and café culture.
