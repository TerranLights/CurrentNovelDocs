## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Cossack families** of the stanitsas against **newcomers** from the north and from Siberia now settling in Krasnodar and Anapa.
- **The Don** (out-migration, aging hamlets) against **the Kuban** (in-migration, growth).
- **Adyghe** villages with long continuity of lineages against **Shapsug** coastal villages remade by resorts.
- **Don Armenians** (in Nor Nakhichevan since 1779) and **Pontic Greeks** (many left for Greece in the 1990s).
- **Nekrasovites** and **Old Believers**, **Molokans**: deliberate continuity.
- **Koryo-saram** seasonal farmers who move each year; **migrant workers** from Central Asia on the coast and in construction.
- **Coal towns** whose institutions closed; **military towns** and the base economy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration and vital statistics; mixed for opinion; thin for lived accounts outside literature.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Kalmyk (historical).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anti-migrant feeling ("понаехали") must be recorded as variation, not adopted; the political use of "stability". Avoid treating the old as the "real" population.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Rosstat: net internal migration for Krasnodar Krai, Rostov Oblast and Adygea, 2000-2023.
2. Levada: search "стабильность или перемены опрос" and check whether regional or settlement-type splits exist.
3. Search the *Донской временник* site for "старожилы", "юбилей хутора", "родословная".
4. Library catalog: "Тумилевич некрасовцы" and "легенды о дольменах адыги".
5. Press search: "снос исторической застройки Ростов Нахичевань" and "Краснодар понаехали".
