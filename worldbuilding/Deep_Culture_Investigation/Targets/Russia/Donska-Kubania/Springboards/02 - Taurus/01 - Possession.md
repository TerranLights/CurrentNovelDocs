# Donska-Kubania: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and the Kuban balachka; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa households** against **city flats** in Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar: sheds, cellars and yards against small apartments.
- **Don Cossacks** whose families lost goods in 1919-20 and 1930-33, against families who came later.
- **Kuban Black Sea Cossacks** (embroidered towels and chests of Ukrainian type) against **Line Cossacks**.
- **Adyghe** families of Adygea and the **Shapsugs** of the coast: gift and hospitality obligations around objects.
- **Hamshen Armenians**, **Don Armenians**, **Pontic Greeks**: heirlooms carried from Anatolia and Crimea.
- **Deported and displaced groups**: Don Kalmyks, Kuban Germans, Greeks, Meskhetian Turks (arrived in Krasnodar Krai from 1989), Donbass arrivals in Rostov Oblast after 2014.
- **Old Believers and Molokans**: plain living and rules on goods.
- **Generations**: Soviet-era keepers ("it may come in useful") against young renters and resellers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and Soviet ethnography; mixed for surveys (often federal-district level only); thin for minority oral history outside a few projects.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Ukrainian; Armenian; Greek; Kalmyk for testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** confiscation files name real families; deportation testimony is personal and still painful; keep living people's names out of a public repo.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Rosstat: find the latest Comprehensive Survey of Living Conditions and pull durable-goods tables for Krasnodar, Rostov and Adygea.
2. Library catalog: "Кубанские станицы 1967"; read the household chapters for inventories of chests and dowry goods.
3. Search "синдром Плюшкина Краснодар" and "Ростов" in regional news for local framing of hoarding.
4. Search "отчуждаемая неотчуждаемая принадлежность адыгейский язык" for the grammar of having.
5. Search for oral-history collections: "депортация калмыков воспоминания вещи", "депортация греков Кубань воспоминания".
