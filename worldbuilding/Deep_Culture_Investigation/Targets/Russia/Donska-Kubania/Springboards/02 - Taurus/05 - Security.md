## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa households** (gardens, cellars, livestock) against **city renters** in Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar.
- **State-paid workers** (teachers, doctors, officials) against **private and seasonal workers** on the resort coast.
- **Farm owners** exposed to hail, drought and prices; **Azov fishers**; **coal-town families** after closures.
- **Border districts** of Rostov Oblast exposed to war since 2014, against the interior Kuban.
- **Adyghe** and **Shapsugs**: kin networks; **Armenians**: community aid; **Pontic Greeks**: emigration to Greece as an exit.
- **Meskhetian Turks**, **Koryo-saram**, **Kuban Roma**: security with weak legal status.
- **Pensioners** against the young.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for savings and opinion polling; mixed for disasters; thin for minority strategies.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the famine and its naming; ongoing war effects on Rostov and Krasnodar (attacks, mobilization, displacement), which change fast and are subject to censorship; Cossack patrols and ethnic profiling. National security and policing law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
