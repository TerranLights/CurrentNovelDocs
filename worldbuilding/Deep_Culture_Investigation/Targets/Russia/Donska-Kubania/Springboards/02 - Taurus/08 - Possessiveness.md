## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men** in Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar against **stanitsa** couples; **generations** (whether jealousy still reads as love).
- **Adyghe** families (in-law customs, marriage forms) and **Shapsugs**.
- **Don Armenians**, **Hamshen Armenians**, **Meskhetian Turks**: family honor framings, tested not assumed.
- **Cossack revival** members against non-registered "Cossacks" and non-Cossacks: guarding a name.
- **Coastal locals** against **holidaymakers and newcomers**; **anglers** on the Don and Azov.
- **Pontic Greeks**, **Koryo-saram**, **Kuban Roma**: family and community boundaries.
- **Orthodox**, **Old Believer**, **evangelical** and **Muslim** teaching on jealousy and covetousness.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: much press and online talk; little regional survey data; NGO data uneven since restrictions on foreign-funded groups.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Turkish; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence in the home and its legal status (law goes to Libra or Capricorn); bride abduction; "honor" framings that can stereotype minorities. Name variation inside every group.
- **Held for review:** none.
