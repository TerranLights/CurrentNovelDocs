# Donska-Kubania: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and *balachka*; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Planned Cossack towns** (Novocherkassk) against **merchant and port towns** (Taganrog, Novorossiysk) and fast-growing **Krasnodar**.
- **Stanitsas** against **cities**; the **resort coast** with its seasonal crowds against farm districts.
- **Adyghe** villages, with their own etiquette of gathering, and **Shapsug** villages on the coast.
- **Armenian** and **Greek** villages on the Black Sea coast; **Meskhetian Turk** and **Kurdish** settlements; **Roma** communities; **Old Believer** households with strict domestic rules.
- **People who lived through the 1990s** as adults against younger residents, for what *беспредел* and order mean.
- **Mining towns** after the pit closures against richer suburbs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Civil War and planning history; mixed for perceived safety (national polls); thin for minority customs of order today.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Ukrainian for Black Sea Host papers; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Civil War's terror on both sides; protest policing and wartime restrictions; Sochi-era evictions; any portrayal of minority districts as disorderly must be recorded as others' claim. Police and court law as written is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
