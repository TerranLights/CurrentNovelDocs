# Donska-Kubania: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and *balachka*; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack families** with a remembered service tradition against **non-Cossack** neighbors (descendants of *inogorodnie*, Soviet-era migrants).
- **Adyghe** families, where duties to kin and guests run along their own lines, and the **Shapsugs** of the coast with ties to a diaspora that a person may also owe.
- **Armenians** (Nor Nakhichevan and Hamshen): church and kin duties; **Pontic Greeks** after the 1940s deportations and the 1990s emigration to Greece.
- **Meskhetian Turks**, many of whom left for the United States in the 2000s: duty to kin across a split family.
- **Old Believers and Molokans:** religious refusal of some state duties in the past.
- **Generations:** those who did Soviet service against today's conscripts and contract soldiers; **women** as carers against men as servers.
- **Rostov's working districts** against **resort Sochi** and farm stanitsas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Cossack and Soviet layers; mixed for present-day family duty (survey items are mostly national); thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian for Black Sea Host papers; Adyghe for laments and proverbs; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** military service and draft avoidance since 2022 are legally risky to ask about; memorial culture is state-managed; mobilized men's families are a vulnerable group. Military law itself is Libra's; here only what duty is for.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Presidential Library (prlib.ru) for "Жалованная грамота Черноморскому войску 1792".
2. Search the Russian State Library catalog for "проводы на службу кубанские казаки песни" and "Бигдай".
3. Download the RusGGS documentation and find the filial-obligation items and the regional sample list.
4. Search *Молот* and *Кубанские новости* archives for "проводы в армию" and "уход за пожилыми родителями".
5. Search the National Library of Adygea for "гъыбзэ" and "адыгские плачи".
