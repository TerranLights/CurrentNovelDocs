# Donska-Kubania: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and *balachka*; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack descendants** with officer-family memories against **descendants of the *inogorodnie*** who had no land share.
- **Merchant minorities:** **Armenians** (Nakhichevan, and Hamshen growers near Sochi), **Greeks** (Taganrog, Anapa), and **Old Believer** merchant lines.
- **Adyghe** families, where personal success meets kin obligations and the ideal of *адыгагъэ*; **Shapsugs** on the resort coast.
- **Meskhetian Turks** and **Koreans** as farming communities; Kalmyk descendants on the Don.
- **Krasnodar and Rostov** against **mining towns** of the eastern Donbas fringe (Shakhty, Gukovo), where the pits closed.
- **Men against women** in what success is expected to look like; **young people** leaving for Moscow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant and Soviet labor history; mixed for attitudes (national polls); thin for minority aspirations in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian for Kuban rural sources; Armenian; Greek; Adyghe.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dekulakization and famine; the 1990s rise of fortunes, with violent cases; the war as a path to pay and status for some men from these regions; envy and leveling must be recorded as variation, never as a national trait.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
