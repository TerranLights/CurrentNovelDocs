# Donska-Kubania: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and *balachka*; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack descendants against *inogorodnie* descendants:** the region's own old line of rank, still remembered in some stanitsas.
- **Adyghe princely and noble families** (Temirgoi, Bzhedug) against commoner lineages, and the **Shapsug and Abadzekh** tradition that set princes aside.
- **Armenians** of Nor Nakhichevan (old merchant elite) and **Hamshen** Armenians; **Pontic Greeks**; **Meskhetian Turks**; **Kurds and Yazidis**; **Roma** communities; **Central Asian migrant workers**, whose place on local ladders is a question to research.
- **Kalmyk descendants** of the Don; **Nogais**; **Old Believers**.
- **Krasnodar and Rostov elites** against **stanitsa farmers** and **coal-town** families; the **Sochi** resort economy, with owners and seasonal staff.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-1917 estate statistics and Soviet Party records; mixed for present attitudes; thin for minorities' own ranking terms.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Ukrainian for older Kuban records; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic ranking (migrants, Roma, Meskhetian Turks) must be recorded as others' prejudice, never adopted; coastal elite estates and the palace near Gelendzhik are tied to sources now banned as "extremist"; slavery and dependent status in old Adyghe society need care and the Adyghe scholars' own framing.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library and the Kuban Scientific Library for digitized "Кубанский сборник" volumes and their estate tables.
2. Search "Дубровин Черкесы" and "социальная структура адыгов" in the National Library of Adygea.
3. Pull Rosstat's regional Gini and decile tables for Rostov Oblast, Krasnodar Krai and Adygea.
4. Check the RLMS-HSE questionnaire for the ladder items and the regional sample list.
5. Ask the Tanais museum-reserve for its published inscription catalog.
