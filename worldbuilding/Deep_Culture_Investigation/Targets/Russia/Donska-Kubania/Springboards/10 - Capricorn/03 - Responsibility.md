# Donska-Kubania: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and *balachka*; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa Cossacks** with a tradition of collective answerability against **city dwellers** in Rostov and Krasnodar.
- **Descendants of famine-era blacklisted stanitsas** against settlers brought in afterward: different memories of who answered for what.
- **Adyghe lineages**, where kin may answer for a member, and **Shapsugs**, whose coastal lands are where building and pollution disputes are sharpest.
- **Armenians** of Nor Nakhichevan and Hamshen; **Pontic Greeks** and **Kalmyk descendants**, both deported peoples with their own stake in state responsibility.
- **Meskhetian Turks**, with a long registration dispute with krai officials *check*: whom they hold answerable.
- **Men against women** in who carries the household's load; **young adults** after the 1990s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on historical cases and Soviet scapegoating; mixed on attitude measures; thin on how minorities speak of responsibility now.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian for Kuban famine-era and Black Sea Host papers; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine naming and numbers are contested; Circassian genocide recognition is politically charged; Environmental Watch and other critics carry "foreign agent" labels; the Chikatilo investigation, where an innocent man was executed, is a painful local case of failed answerability. The age of criminal responsibility as written is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
