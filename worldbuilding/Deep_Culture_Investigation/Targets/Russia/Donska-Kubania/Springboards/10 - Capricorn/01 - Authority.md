# Donska-Kubania: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and the Kuban *balachka*; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elected against appointed authority:** stanitsa atamans (elected in older practice) against the crown's nakaznoi atamans and, today, registered atamans confirmed from above.
- **Adyghe princely lineages** (Temirgoi, Bzhedug) against the **Abadzekh and Shapsug** tradition of assemblies: two inherited models of who may command.
- **Old Believer and Nekrasovite descendants:** a community defined by refusing a church and a state.
- **Armenian communities**, where the church and the old merchant families carried weight; **Pontic Greeks**; **Meskhetian Turks**, whose dealings with krai officials are their own story to trace; Kalmyk descendants on the Don.
- **Rostov and Krasnodar** against small stanitsas, where the head of the settlement, the Cossack ataman and the priest may share or contest authority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history; mixed for present attitudes (most polls are national); thin for Adyghe and smaller minorities' own statements.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian for Black Sea Host records; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of officials and of the war carries legal risk now, so survey answers on authority may be guarded; Cossack patrols and minorities; the legacy of the 1990s criminal "authorities". The permission share is scored from the law in force with Libra's method; here only the spirit is asked.
