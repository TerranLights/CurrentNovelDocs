# Donska-Kubania: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and *balachka*; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it. Capricorn takes the spirit of the law only; the letter is Libra's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa Cossacks**, with memories of a law of their own, against **city dwellers** who meet the law mainly as courts and traffic police.
- **Adyghe** communities, where custom, Islamic teaching and state law overlap; the **Shapsugs** of the coast, whose land and building disputes test the law's reach.
- **Armenians, Pontic Greeks, Kurds and Yazidis**, each with their own ways of settling disputes inside the community.
- **Meskhetian Turks** and **Roma**, whose meetings with police and registration rules are a separate line of evidence; **Central Asian migrants** under migration law.
- **Rostov's** old criminal-world lore against everyday city life; **Sochi's** resort and building economy.
- **Old Believers and Molokans**, whose ancestors stood against state law on conscience.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for imperial-era customary courts and for notorious cases; thin for surveys of legal consciousness in these regions.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe and Arabic-script older Adyghe records; Ukrainian for older Kuban papers; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political trials at the Southern District Military Court in Rostov; "foreign agent" and "undesirable" labels on many legal-rights groups; Kushchevskaya victims' families; ethnic profiling must be recorded as reported experience, never assumed. Statutes and court figures are Libra's; the correctional-control share sits with Authority.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
