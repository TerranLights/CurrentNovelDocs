# Donska-Kubania: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and *balachka*; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others (parents, teachers, armies, employers, churches, the state) or by oneself (self-discipline, self-control, willpower).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack families and Cossack-class pupils** against **non-Cossack** neighbors in the same stanitsas.
- **Adyghe** families of noble descent, where fosterage and etiquette are remembered, against commoner lineages; **Shapsugs** of the coast.
- **Nekrasovite returnees and other Old Believers;** **Molokans;** **Muslim** Adyghe keeping Ramadan; **Armenian Apostolic** fasts.
- **Pontic Greeks; Meskhetian Turks; Koreans; Ukrainians;** Kalmyk descendants on the Don.
- **Factory and mining towns** (Rostselmash Rostov, Shakhty, Novoshakhtinsk) against **farm stanitsas** and the **resort coast**, whose work rhythms differ.
- **Generations:** Soviet-schooled parents against children raised online, where self-discipline talk aimed at young men is common.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military and Cossack training, mixed for parenting attitudes (national polls), thin for minorities' upbringing in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe for upbringing terms; Ukrainian for older Kuban sources; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corporal punishment of children and the 2017 change in the domestic-battery rules; hazing (*дедовщина*) in conscript units; the *нагайка* used by Cossack patrols on protesters; prison conditions; "patriotic upbringing" programs tied to the war. The written rules on punishment are Libra's.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
