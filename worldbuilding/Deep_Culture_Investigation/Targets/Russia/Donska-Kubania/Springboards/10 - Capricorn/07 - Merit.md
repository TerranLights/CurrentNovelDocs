# Donska-Kubania: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and *balachka*; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack families** with decorated ancestors against neighbors with no such record.
- **Adyghe** families, where renown and lineage may both count, and where some posts in Adygea are seen through ethnic balance.
- **Armenians**, **Pontic Greeks** and **Koreans**, whose paths through study and trade are a separate line to trace; **Meskhetian Turks** and **Roma**, for whom documents and access may be the first question.
- **City school-leavers** against **stanitsa** pupils in olympiads and admissions.
- **Veterans and their families** against others, as war service becomes a claimed merit.
- **Public-sector** against **private-sector** workers, in how much connections are said to count.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors and contest results; mixed for attitudes (national surveys); thin for how minorities judge merit.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe for bardic tradition; Ukrainian for older Kuban sources; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption reporting is risky for journalists; war service as merit is politically loaded; ethnic balance in Adygea's posts is a sensitive subject; record merit talk as varied, never as one view.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library for "Донские дела Русская историческая библиотека" and "дуван казаки".
2. Pull the regional stage results of the All-Russian School Olympiad for Krasnodar Krai, Rostov Oblast and Adygea, latest three years.
3. Check the ISSP data archive for Russia's Social Inequality rounds and the "getting ahead" items.
4. Search the regional honors decrees for "заслуженный работник Кубани" and "почётный гражданин Ростова".
5. Search "джегуако" and "адыгские певцы-сказители" in the National Library of Adygea catalog.
