## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack stanitsa communities** with revived work customs against **urban entrepreneurs** in Rostov-on-Don, Krasnodar and Sochi.
- **Armenian** businesses of Rostov and the coast; **Greek** families with trade histories.
- **Adyghe** communities in Adygea and the Shapsug coast: communal help customs and new businesses.
- **Former Soviet factory towns** (Taganrog, Novocherkassk, Shakhty and the Donbass mining towns of eastern Rostov) where large employers shrank.
- **Farmers and agro-holding staff** across the Kuban and Don grain belt.
- **Resort-economy workers** on the Black Sea coast: seasonal, small-scale business starts.
- **Women** and **men**: whose initiative is praised, and where.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business statistics and founders' stories; mixed for volunteering; thin for everyday workplace initiative, where only national studies may exist.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Armenian and Greek for community histories; Adyghe for mutual-help customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grassroots volunteering and NGOs face official registration pressures; some volunteer leaders may not wish to be named. Business takeovers (*рейдерство*) are a known risk topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
