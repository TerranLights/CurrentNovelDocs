## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Don Cossacks by district** (upper Don against lower Don) and the **descendants of non-Cossack settlers**.
- **Kuban Black Sea Cossacks**, **Line Cossacks** and **Kuban residents who identify as Ukrainian**: one region, several answers.
- **Adyghe sub-groups** (Temirgoy, Bzhedug, Abadzekh, Shapsug) and **Syrian Circassian returnees**.
- **Don Armenians** and **Hamshen Armenians** (some Christian, with a separate Muslim Hemshin history to check).
- **Pontic Greeks**; **Don Kalmyks**; **Koreans** of Rostov Oblast; **Germans** of the former Kuban colonies; **Assyrians** in the Kuban (*check*).
- **Recent arrivals**: Central Asian and South Caucasian migrants; people displaced from neighboring war zones.
- **City people** (Rostov-on-Don, Krasnodar, Sochi) against **stanitsa** people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Cossack, Adyghe and Armenian identity; mixed on Greek and Ukrainian identity; thin on Kalmyks, Assyrians and Koreans here.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ukrainian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ukrainian identity in the Kuban is politically charged; the Circassian genocide label is contested between governments; the "Cossack" census category and official Cossack registers are disputed. Name variation, never one voice, for every group.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
