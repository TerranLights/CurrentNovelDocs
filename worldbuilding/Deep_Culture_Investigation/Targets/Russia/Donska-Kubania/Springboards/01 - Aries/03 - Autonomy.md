*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Registered Cossack societies** (tied to the state) against **unregistered Cossack groups**: two meanings of self-rule.
- **Adyghe families** in Adygea and **Shapsug** villages on the coast: personal choice inside the household and collective claims to land and administration.
- **Old Believer and Nekrasovite descendants**: autonomy of conscience.
- **Don Armenian** and **Hamshen Armenian** families; **Pontic Greek** families: who decides marriages and careers.
- **Small farmers** against **agro-holding employees** in the Kuban and Don steppe.
- **Eco-settlers and back-to-the-land newcomers** against long-settled stanitsa people.
- **Young adults** in Rostov-on-Don and Krasnodar (university cities) against those in rural districts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on collective and historical autonomy; mixed on family decision-making (mostly North Caucasus or all-Russia studies); thin on regionally broken-down survey data.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Ukrainian for Kuban Rada sources; Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional autonomy and separatism are legally sensitive topics in Russia; contacts and interviewees need protection. Questions about what the law allows belong to Libra; obedience and legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
