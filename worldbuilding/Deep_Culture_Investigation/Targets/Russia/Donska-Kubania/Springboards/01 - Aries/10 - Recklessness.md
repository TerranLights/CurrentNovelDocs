# Donska-Kubania: Recklessness (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian and the Kuban balachka; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men** in stanitsas, mining towns and cities: test whether they dominate the risk data here.
- **Azov and Black Sea fishermen**; **miners** in eastern Rostov: risk as work.
- **Cossack revival youth** against **urban youth**: different models of daring.
- **Adyghe** communities: norms of restraint against bravado, by generation.
- **Armenian** and **Greek** families: attitudes to gambling and speculation.
- **Holiday visitors** to Sochi, Anapa and Gelendzhik against **locals**: who takes the sea and mountain risks.
- **Women**: where their risk-taking is noticed or ignored.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in official injury, crash and rescue statistics; mixed in folklore; thin in local attitude surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and illegal mining involve criminal exposure; interview leads need anonymity. Regional rankings on drinking can feed stereotypes; report them as data with caveats. Rules and penalties belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Download crash data for Krasnodar Krai, Rostov Oblast and Adygea from stat.gibdd.ru, 2015 to now, by cause.

3. Library search: "Слово о полку Игореве" commentary on Igor's campaign and its judgment of rashness.
4. Search "копанки Ростовская область" and "Гуково копанки" in press archives.
