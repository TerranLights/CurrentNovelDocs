# Donska-Kubania: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with Don and Kuban dialect forms), Kuban balachka and Ukrainian, Adyghe, Armenian, Greek. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stanitsa Cossack families** where circle custom is revived against **urban Rostov and Krasnodar** professionals: assembly voice against office voice.
- **Adyghe** households in Adygea and the **Shapsugs** near Tuapse and Sochi: elder-junior rules may vary by community and generation.
- **Don Armenians** of Rostov's old Nor Nakhichevan against **Hamshen Armenians** on the coast: trade versus farming histories of bargaining and claim-making.
- **Pontic Greeks** around Gelendzhik, Anapa and Krymsk.
- **Women and men** in each group: whose speaking up is praised and whose is called brazen.
- **Migrant workers** from Central Asia and the Caucasus in Rostov and Kuban cities: limits on claim-making.
- **Meskhetian Turks** who stayed in the Kuban after the 2000s emigration: claim-making by a pressured minority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on collective and historical assertion (petitions, circles, strikes); thin on everyday interpersonal assertion, where most data will be all-Russia linguistics to be tested locally.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, with dialect dictionaries; Adyghe; Armenian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest participation can expose people to legal risk; anonymize any interview leads. Novocherkassk 1962 archives were long closed; some files may still be restricted. Rights and procedure questions belong to Libra, the spirit of obedience to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries

3. Search Rospotrebnadzor Krasnodar and Rostov sites for "обращения граждан" annual reports.
