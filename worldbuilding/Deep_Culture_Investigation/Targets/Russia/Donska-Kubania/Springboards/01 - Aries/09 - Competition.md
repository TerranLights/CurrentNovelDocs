## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rostov-on-Don against Krasnodar** (the "capital of the South" rivalry); **Taganrog, Novocherkassk and Sochi** as rivals in their own right.
- **Cossack** stanitsas reviving contests against **urban sports clubs**.
- **Adyghe** communities: horsemanship, dance and wrestling traditions.
- **Armenian** and **Greek** families: board games, educational striving (from data, not stereotype), and envy beliefs.
- **Don Kalmyk** descendants, if wrestling and horse traditions persist.
- **Boys and girls**: which contests each is steered into.
- **Agro-districts** of the Kuban and Don: harvest rivalry then and now.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, school results and Soviet labor contests; mixed for folk contests; thin for attitudes to competition itself at the regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek; Ukrainian for older Kuban sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** doping cases involving regional athletes; exam-cheating scandals; school rankings that may single out minority schools.
- **Held for review:** none.
