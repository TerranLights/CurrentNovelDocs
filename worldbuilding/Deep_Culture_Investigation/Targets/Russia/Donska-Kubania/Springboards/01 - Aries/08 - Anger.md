# Donska-Kubania: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Donska-Kubania, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with southern dialect words); Ukrainian and the Kuban balachka; Adyghe; Armenian; Greek. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women** in each group: whose anger is excused and whose is shamed.
- **Adyghe** families in Adygea and the coast: feud memory against reconciliation customs; Muslim and secular households.
- **Cossack** stanitsas and **Rostov and Krasnodar** city dwellers.
- **Armenian** and **Greek** families: religious and family norms for temper.
- **Chechens, Dargins and other Caucasus communities** in eastern Rostov and the Kuban: honor and grievance under outside stereotypes (handle with care).
- **Football supporters**, **young men in mining towns** and **migrant workers**: anger in public spaces and toward them.
- **Veterans** of Afghanistan, Chechnya and the current war.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on collective anger in history and on folklore; mixed on everyday display; thin on regional survey data on emotion.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Adyghe; Armenian; Ukrainian for Kuban folklore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic stereotypes of "hot-tempered" Caucasus peoples must be named as stereotypes, not used. Domestic violence data are patchy and politically sensitive. Feud and revenge questions touch law: what courts did belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
