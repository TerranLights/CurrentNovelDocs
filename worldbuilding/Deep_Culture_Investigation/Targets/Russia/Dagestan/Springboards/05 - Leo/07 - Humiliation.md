## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women:** a man's lowering tied to his family's women and to answered insults; a woman's tied to reputation.
- **Avars and Dargins:** the best-documented highland feud and song traditions.
- **Andi-Tsez peoples:** small villages where ridicule may weigh more because everyone knows everyone.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** lowland and steppe peoples with other words for shame and other mocking genres.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls:** compare southern shaming customs.
- **Laks:** a people that also lost land in the resettlements.
- **Chechen-Akkins:** deportation as remembered humiliation.
- **Mountain Jews:** humiliation as a religious minority in Soviet years.
- **Pious and secular young people:** different triggers for shame, from alcohol to an uncovered head.
- **Migrants and conscripts:** lowering met outside the republic.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in press for recent cases; mixed in ethnography; thin for private and family humiliation.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar and Lezgin for curses and songs; Arabic for religious writing on humility.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** apology videos, shaming pages, abduction and police abuse involve real victims; feud cases can reopen conflicts; name no private person beyond the published record.
- **Held for review:** none.
