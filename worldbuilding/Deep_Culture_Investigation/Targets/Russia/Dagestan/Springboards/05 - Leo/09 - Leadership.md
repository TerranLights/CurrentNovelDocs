## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars:** the nutsal tradition and Shamil's Imamate; the main source for the highland model.
- **Andi-Tsez peoples:** free societies outside the khanates; leading without a ruler.
- **Dargins and Kaitag:** a large people with a former utsmi line.
- **Kumyks:** the shamkhal and princely families of the plain.
- **Nogais:** steppe nobility and epic leaders.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Rutuls, Tsakhurs:** southern free societies and khanates tied to Azerbaijan.
- **Laks:** the Kazikumukh khanate and an educated urban class.
- **Mountain Jews:** community leadership by rabbis and elders.
- **Chechen-Akkins:** teip and elder traditions shared with Chechnya.
- **Sufi, Salafi and secular currents:** three models of the good leader.
