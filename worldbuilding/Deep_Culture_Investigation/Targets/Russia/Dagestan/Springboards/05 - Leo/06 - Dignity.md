# Dagestan: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars and Dargins of the highlands:** where village seating and address customs for elders are best described.
- **Andi-Tsez peoples:** small villages where an elder's standing may work differently from Avar towns.
- **Kumyks:** lowland hosts to resettled highlanders; compare how each side speaks of respect.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans:** a southern pattern with Azerbaijani-border ties.
- **Laks:** a long history of work away from home.
- **Nogais:** a minority with a land grievance.
- **Mountain Jews and Russians:** smaller communities shaped by emigration.
- **Chechen-Akkins:** memory of deportation as an indignity.
- **Women, widows and the divorced:** dignity tied to family position.
- **Herders, migrants and city professionals:** dignity at work differs by trade.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in religious and ethnographic writing, thin in direct measures.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for adab literature; Avar and Lezgin for proverbs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** torture and abduction reports; the Memorial archive's legal status in Russia; disability and family privacy.
- **Held for review:** none.
