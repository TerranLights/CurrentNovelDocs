*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men:** the largest split on visibility; compare by age, marital state and town.
- **Avars and Andi-Tsez highlanders:** close village watching against Makhachkala's anonymity.
- **Dargins and Kubachi:** a craft village used to buyers and tourists looking in.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** lowland and steppe settlement patterns and other rules for public space.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans:** southern towns near the border, with kin in Azerbaijan.
- **Laks:** many live in cities and in Russia proper.
- **Derbent's Mountain Jews and Azerbaijanis:** a mixed city where faith is visible in dress and buildings.
- **Chechen-Akkins and Russians:** different headscarf and street norms.
- **Pious and secular youth:** beards and headscarves as signs that draw notice from neighbors and the police.
- **Migrants in Moscow:** being seen as "кавказцы" (Caucasians) and not as any one people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in press for weddings and display; mixed for the evil eye; thin and guarded for women's private views.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for amulets; Avar and Kumyk for terms; Polish for Kaliszewska.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** women's images; the preventive register and police profiling; some amulet and healing knowledge is kept within families.
