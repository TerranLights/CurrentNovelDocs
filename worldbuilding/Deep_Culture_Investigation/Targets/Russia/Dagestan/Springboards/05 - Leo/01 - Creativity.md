## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kubachi:** a separate speech community within the Dargins, worth its own unit because inherited craft defines it.
- **Laks of Balkhar and Tsovkra:** a women's craft beside a performers' village.
- **Avars of Untsukul and Gotsatl:** woodwork and metalwork villages shaped by Soviet combines.
- **Andi-Tsez peoples:** felt-making and weaving that rarely reach the museum label.
- **Tabasarans, Lezgins and Derbent's Azerbaijanis:** carpets, where village patterns may be tied to a place more than a person.
- **Kaitag:** embroidery that was collected abroad but is little studied at home.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** plain and steppe crafts and Turkic verse forms, a different idea of the maker.
- **Russians of Kizlyar:** winemaking and brandy as a craft with its own pride of skill.
- **Women and men:** pottery, weaving and knitting against metal and wood; whose making is called art.
- **Religious views:** families wary of images against those who keep figural crafts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the famous craft villages; mixed for painting and music; thin for everyday improvising.
