## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars, Dargins, Kumyks, Lezgins, Laks:** the five largest peoples; compare their shares of honors to see whether credit tracks size or power.
- **Andi-Tsez peoples, Archi and Kubachi:** recognition as distinct peoples is itself at stake.
- **Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs:** smaller southern peoples with their own press and writers.
- **Nogais:** distance from Makhachkala and a steppe economy.
- **Chechen-Akkins:** recognition of a historical wrong.
- **Mountain Jews and Russians:** smaller, often emigrating communities; who remembers their honored people.
- **Women and men:** honors in sport and war against honors for motherhood and teaching.
- **Religious scholars against secular officials:** two ladders of credit that may not meet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors and Soviet records; mixed for religious titles; thin for everyday credit and thanks.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for ijāzas; the national languages for obituaries and local press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Aukh dispute; the insurgency and its victims; ethnic shares of honors can inflame rivalry.
- **Held for review:** none.
