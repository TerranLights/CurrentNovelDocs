## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars and Andi-Tsez peoples:** the highland guest code is best described for them; check whether small villages host differently.
- **Dargins and Kubachi:** wealth from craft, and its duties.
- **Kumyks:** lowland hosts whose land fed resettled highlanders; giving between them.
- **Nogais:** steppe hospitality and herding wealth.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans:** southern giving with Azerbaijani-border kin.
- **Laks:** a trading people with long-standing wealth abroad.
- **Mountain Jews of Derbent:** community charity in a different faith tradition.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Kizlyar:** Orthodox giving and church charity.
- **Sufi-leaning and Salafi-leaning communities:** different views of mawlid feasts and of where alms should go.
- **Migrants and the village:** money sent home as gift, duty or display.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hospitality and religious giving; mixed for figures; thin for private gifts within kin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for waqf deeds; Avar, Kumyk and Lezgin for guest terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charity can be read as linked to militancy by the authorities; the giving of powerful men touches politics.
- **Held for review:** none.
