# Dagestan: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak, Dargin, Juhuri; Arabic; English and German for outside scholarship. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars and Dargins:** the most studied feud and honor customs of the highlands.
- **Andi-Tsez peoples:** small, closed villages where honor and gossip may work differently.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** Turkic terms for honor and a former princely order.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs:** southern honor customs; compare with kin over the Azerbaijani border.
- **Laks:** a heroine in their epic; check how women's honor is told.
- **Chechen-Akkins:** honor codes shared with Chechnya.
- **Mountain Jews and Russians:** other faiths and other family codes.
- **Men and women, old and young:** honor carried differently, and changing.
- **City and village:** whether Makhachkala loosens or hardens the code.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in ethnography and rights reporting; mixed for change over time; thin for women's own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the national languages for songs and terms; Arabic; English and German.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor killings, feuds and the control of women involve living people; customary and religious law go to Capricorn and Libra, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
