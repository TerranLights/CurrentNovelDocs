## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kumyks**: the most fully ranked estate order of the plain.
- **Avars of the former khanate and of the free societies**: noble rule against societies that kept fewer ranks.
- **Dargins of Kaitag against the Akusha union**: a ruling house against a confederation.
- **Laks**: a khan's court at Kumukh, then an urban professional rank.
- **Tabasarans and Lezgins**: southern beks and their dependents.
- **Rutuls and Tsakhurs**: small polities tied to the Tsakhur sultanate.
- **Nogais**: steppe mirzas and commoners.
- **Descendants of dependents and slaves, in any people**: whether old rank still marks them (handle with care).
- **Sayyid and shaykh families**: religious rank by descent.
- **Mountain Jews and Shia Azerbaijanis of Derbent**: their own community ranks.
- **Women and younger sons**: lower places in the order of the house, to test people by people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on 19th-century estates; mixed on present-day rank; thin on how rank is felt.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Arabic, Kumyk, Avar, Dargin, Lezgin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent from dependents or slaves (stigma; never name families); the ethnic ranking of posts is politically charged; women's status is contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
