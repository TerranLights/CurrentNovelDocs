# Dagestan: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avar and Dargin wrestling villages**: merit by open contest, with families behind it.
- **Lezgins**: a folk-poet tradition that prized earned standing.
- **Laks**: professional and scholarly careers in Makhachkala and Moscow.
- **Kumyks**: old noble standing set against newer earned rank.
- **Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**: smaller peoples' reach to posts and honors.
- **Nogais**: merit and honors on the republic's edge.
- **Chechen-Akkins**: whether their candidates for posts are judged on merit or by people.
- **Students of Islam and hafiz families**: religious merit outside state credentials.
- **Mountain Jews**: success in trade, the arts and emigration.
- **Women**: whose merit is honored (mothers, poets, athletes, scholars).
- **City professionals against village families**: credentials against connections.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on honors and sport; thin and politically charged on bought posts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Arabic, Avar, Lezgin, Lak, Dargin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption claims (name cases only from court records); counterterror victims; ethnic shares of posts.
- **Held for review:** none.
