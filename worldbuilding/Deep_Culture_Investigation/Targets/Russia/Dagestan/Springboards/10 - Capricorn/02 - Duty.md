# Dagestan: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak, Tabasaran and the other peoples' languages; Arabic for religious duty; Judeo-Tat for the Mountain Jews. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland Avar and Dargin villages**: communal duties may still bind through the village assembly.
- **Resettled lowland villages (Kizilyurt, Khasavyurt, Babayurt districts)**: duties carried down from the mountains or loosened by the move.
- **Kumyks**: old estate duties of dependents to lords, and their memory.
- **Laks and Dargins working in Russia's north and in Moscow**: duty to parents and village kept from afar.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans**: duties to kin across the Azerbaijan border.
- **Nogais**: steppe herding duties and their own funeral customs.
- **Chechen-Akkins**: duties to return and rebuild after deportation.
- **Shia Azerbaijanis of Derbent**: mourning duties of Muharram, unlike Sunni practice.
- **Mountain Jews and Tats**: Jewish religious and community duty.
- **Cossacks and Russians**: Orthodox and state-service duty.
- **Women**: duties as daughters-in-law and caregivers, apart from men's public duties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in religious texts and ethnography of rites; thin in surveys of filial and civic duty.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Arabic, Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin; Azerbaijani for Derbent; Judeo-Tat or Hebrew for Juhuro sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conscription and the 2022 mobilization; duty claimed by armed groups (jihad as duty) must be read as their claim, with care for informants; law of duties goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
