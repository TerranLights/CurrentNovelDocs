# Dagestan: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avar highland villages**: kin groups whose answerability for a member may still be live in disputes.
- **Dargin villages of Akusha and Kubachi**: craft and village self-government, where a master answers for his apprentices.
- **Kumyks of the plain**: estate memory of lords answering for dependents.
- **Laks**: families answering for migrants' conduct far from home.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**: border villages where kin answer across two states.
- **Nogais**: herders answerable for flocks on steppe pastures.
- **Chechen-Akkins**: disputes over returned land and who answers for its loss.
- **Families of men killed or jailed as insurgents, in every people**: the state's collective blame from below.
- **Makhachkala**: anonymous city life, against village answerability.
- **Mountain Jews, Derbent Azerbaijanis, Cossacks**: community and parish answerability outside the Sunni frame.
- **Men and women**: whether a man must answer for the household's fortunes and a woman for its name, as each group puts it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in 19th-century custom records and human-rights reporting; thin in surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Arabic, Avar, Kumyk, Lezgin, Nogai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** families of insurgents and counterterror victims; corruption cases that are politically charged; criminal liability as written goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
