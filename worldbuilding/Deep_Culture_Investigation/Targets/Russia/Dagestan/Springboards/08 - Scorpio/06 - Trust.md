## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars, Dargins and Laks of the highlands**: trust may run along village and lineage (*тухум*) lines more than ethnic ones.
- **Andi, Tsez and Archi villages**: very small communities where everyone is known.
- **Kumyks of the plain and Nogais of the steppe**: older ties with Russian traders and settlers.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans and other southern peoples**: links across the border to Azerbaijan.
- **Azerbaijanis and Mountain Jews of Derbent**: a mixed city's long neighborly trust and its strains.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Kizlyar**: a minority's trust in republican institutions.
- **Chechen-Akkins of Khasavyurt**: trust after deportation and land disputes.
- **Sufi murids, Salafi-leaning Muslims and secular townspeople**: each trusts different authorities.
- **Labor migrants and the diaspora in Moscow and Turkey**: trust built far from the village.
- **Young urban women and men**: dating, work and banking with people outside the family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hospitality ethnography and 19th-century history; mixed for surveys (few regional series); thin for informal credit.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk and Lezgin for proverbs and verse; Azerbaijani and Judeo-Tat for Derbent.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** election fraud is a risky subject locally; Golos and some media carry "foreign agent" labels; interfaith tension after 2023; trust in police and courts is the spirit of the law, under Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
