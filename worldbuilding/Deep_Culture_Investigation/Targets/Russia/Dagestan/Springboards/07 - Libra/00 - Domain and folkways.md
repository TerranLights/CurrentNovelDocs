*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak, Tabasaran, Chechen, Nogai, Azerbaijani, Rutul, Agul, Tsakhur, Juhuri; Arabic as the old legal language. **Concept in one line:** what a fair bargain and a good partnership are: bonds between equals, promises, open enemies, the settling of disputes, civility, marriage and gender ways, and the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars**, for the contrast between the old khanate and the free societies.
- **Andi-Tsez peoples** of Tsumada, Botlikh and Tsunta, counted as Avars, for their own marriage customs.
- **Dargins**, with **Kaitag** and **Kubachi**, for the Akusha-Dargo union and the old utsmi principality.
- **Kumyks**, for shamkhal rule and plain-land disputes with resettled highlanders.
- **Lezgins**, split by the Azerbaijan border, for the sikhil and cross-border kin.
- **Laks**, for the Kazikumukh khanate, labor migration and the Novolak resettlement.
- **Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**, for customs often folded into larger neighbors.
- **Nogais**, for a pastoral steppe frame.
- **Akkin Chechens**, for the 1944 deportation and contested return.
- **Azerbaijanis of Derbent**, for a largely Shia legal tradition.
- **Mountain Jews**, for their own marriage and arbitration customs.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks** of Kizlyar, for Orthodox family ways.
- **Sufi-affiliated and Salafi villages**, for opposed views of what sharia requires.
- **Makhachkala, migrants in Western Siberia and Moscow, and emigrant descendants in Turkey**, for how bonds hold outside the village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for customary law, marriage and feud; mixed for current practice.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled (the tracker carries "Russia" as pre-war land); these sheets write for the Republic of Dagestan's core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for legal manuscripts; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk and Lezgian for folklore; smaller languages through their institutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blood feud, honor killings and female genital cutting are documented by rights groups and risky to name locally; counterinsurgency; Salafi-Sufi tension; some NGOs labeled or closed. Tsarist and Soviet ethnography is a colonial record.
- **Held for review:** none.
