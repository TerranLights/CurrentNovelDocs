*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak, Nogai, Azerbaijani, Juhuri; Arabic for religious and polemical texts. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain Jews**, often targets when blame looks for an outsider.
- **Salafi communities**, labeled "Wahhabis" and blamed for violence, and who in turn charge Sufis with idolatry.
- **Sufi brotherhoods**, accused and accusing in the same quarrel.
- **Avars and Dargins**, the largest peoples, for how smaller peoples picture them.
- **Kumyks and Nogais**, for how plains peoples speak of highland newcomers.
- **Laks and Akkin Chechens of Novolak**, each blaming the other in the land dispute.
- **Lezgins**, for images of Azerbaijanis across the border and of Avars at home.
- **Derbent's Azerbaijanis**, a Shia minority sometimes cast as foreign.
- **Kizlyar Russians**, both carriers and targets of ethnic images.
- **Dagestanis in Moscow and Western Siberia**, who meet Russian majority stereotypes daily.
- **Women healers and ruqya clients**, for gendered explanations of misfortune.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good literary and stereotype research; folk belief well recorded in older ethnography; current scapegoating documented mainly in press and monitoring.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for polemics and healing texts; Kumyk and Nogai for spirit lore; Juhuri for Mountain Jewish testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** antisemitic violence, extremist labels and terrorist attacks are dangerous to research; SOVA is labeled by the state; neighbor jokes can wound, and are recorded as data, not repeated as claims.
- **Held for review:** none.
