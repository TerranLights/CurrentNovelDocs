# Dagestan: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak, Nogai, Azerbaijani, Tabasaran; Arabic for inheritance law. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland Avar and Dargin villages**, since shares of terrace land, water and herding duty ran by household turn.
- **Kumyk plain villages**, where resettled highlanders hold land the Kumyks call theirs, a fairness dispute in itself.
- **Nogai steppe herders**, because pasture shares meet newcomers' flocks there.
- **Laks**, whose craft and trade migration put fairness in wages and partnerships first.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans of the south**, for orchard and carpet-making shares among households.
- **Rutuls and Tsakhurs**, with high pastures disputed across districts and the Azerbaijan border.
- **Andi-Tsez communities**, for small-village rules of rotation and lot.
- **Derbent's Azerbaijanis and Mountain Jews**, for bazaar haggling and fixed-price habits.
- **Kizlyar Russians**, where state equal shares may weigh more than religious shares.
- **Women heirs**, for whether they claim state or religious shares.
- **Young exam-takers and their families**, as the generation facing national rules.
- **Wrestlers and fight fans**, for a sport-centered sense of the fair call.
- **Migrant crews in the oil towns**, where shares are settled far from village elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong historical work on land and estates, fair-division customs scattered in ethnography, little survey data on fairness attitudes for the republic itself.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for faraʾid; Kumyk and Nogai for steppe pasture terms; Avar and Dargin for highland rotas.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land disputes have turned violent; women's inheritance is a live family conflict; exam-integrity reporting can shame named schools.
- **Held for review:** none.
