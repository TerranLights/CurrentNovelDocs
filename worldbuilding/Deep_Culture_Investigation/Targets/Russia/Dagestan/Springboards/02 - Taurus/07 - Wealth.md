## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Makhachkala elites** and suburban new houses against **highland villages**; district towns (Buynaksk, Khasavyurt, Izberbash).
- **Laks** and **Kubachi Dargins**: trade and craft wealth, including Lak business in Moscow.
- **Avars** and **Dargins** with large herds and kutans; **Nogai** herders of the steppe.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans** of the south; **Derbent** Azerbaijani and **Mountain Jewish** merchant families, past and present.
- **Kumyks** of the plain; **Russians and Cossacks** of the wine and brandy country; **Chechen-Akkins**.
- Women's wealth (gold, dowry) against men's (houses, cars, herds); migrants earning in the north against those at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** official income data exist by region; display and giving are in press and social media; much wealth is informal and unrecorded.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kumyk and Lezgian for poetry; Persian and Azerbaijani for Derbent; Arabic for Sufi texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption, the sale of posts, poaching and organized crime; naming private people's wealth; leave claims to the sources.
- **Law:** tax law, anti-corruption law and the legal side of mahr go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Rosstat: «коэффициент Джини по субъектам» and «уровень бедности Республика Дагестан», last ten years.
2. Find al-Alkadari's «Асари-Дагестан» in Russian translation; list passages on revenues and gifts.
3. Library catalogs: «Кавказский календарь» volumes with Dagestan oblast statistics, 1880-1910.
4. Download one year of declarations for the republic's government and a highland district.
5. Search press for «расходы на свадьбу Дагестан муфтият» to find calls to limit wedding display.
