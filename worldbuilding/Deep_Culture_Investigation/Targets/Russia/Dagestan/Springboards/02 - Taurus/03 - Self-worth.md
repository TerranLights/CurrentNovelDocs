*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars, Dargins, Laks**: highland villages with strong lineage memory; check whether free descent still weighs in marriage.
- **Small highland peoples** (Andi, Tsez, Archi, Bezhta, Hunzib): the worth of very small peoples beside large ones.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Rutuls, Aguls, Tsakhurs**: southern honor vocabulary shared with Azerbaijani.
- **Kumyks** and **Nogais**: Turkic honor terms; former nobility and commoner lines on the plain.
- **Mountain Jews**, **Russians and Cossacks**, **Azerbaijanis** of Derbent.
- Women against men; elders against youth; Sufi against Salafi-leaning youth (worth through piety); migrants against those at home; Makhachkala's middle class against villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in epic, poetry and religious teaching; thin and scattered for measured self-esteem; much of the press is anecdotal.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Lezgian, Dargwa and Kumyk for epics and poetry; Arabic for Sufi texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor violence, forced apology videos and descent from former dependents are painful and political; protect informants; no single voice for a people.
- **Law:** blood feud and honor crimes as legal matters go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
