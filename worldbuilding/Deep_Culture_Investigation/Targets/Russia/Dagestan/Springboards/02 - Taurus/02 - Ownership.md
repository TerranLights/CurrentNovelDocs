# Dagestan: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Kumyk, Avar, Dargwa, Lezgian, Lak, Nogai; Azerbaijani; Arabic for endowment and imamate records. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland jamaats** (Avar, Dargin, Lak) against their **resettled lowland villages** and kutans.
- **Kumyks** (Tarki, Buynaksk, Khasavyurt plain) and **Nogais** (Nogai district): lowland peoples on land claimed by others.
- **Laks** of Novolak and **Chechen-Akkins**: one stretch of land, two claims since 1944.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**: southern villages, Samur pastures, a border that cut lands.
- **Azerbaijanis** and **Mountain Jews** of Derbent: urban houses and vineyards; property left on emigration.
- **Russians and Cossacks** of Kizlyar and Tarumovka; **Makhachkala** newcomers in self-built suburbs.
- Women against men in inheritance; mosque and waqf property against private.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-Soviet and Soviet tenure; contested and partisan for the post-Soviet land question; official registry data likely incomplete.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kumyk and Nogai for lowland claims; Avar, Dargwa, Lak for highland ones; Arabic for imamate and waqf records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land disputes have turned violent; ethnic movements' material is partisan; give each side its own voice.
- **Law:** land codes, the republic's land legislation, adat and sharia on property go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
