## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland Avar, Dargin and Andi-Tsez villages** against **Kumyk lowland farmers**: stores and herds against irrigated fields.
- **Nogai steppe herders**: security hinging on pasture and water.
- **Laks and Kubachi craftsmen**: long habits of earning away from home.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**: kin across the Azerbaijan border spread risk.
- **Akkin Chechens** and **resettled Laks** of Novolak: the same land lost or regained from opposite sides.
- **Derbent Azerbaijanis, Mountain Jews, Kizlyar Russians and Cossacks**: safety shaped by emigration and attacks.
- **Makhachkala public-sector families** against **informal traders** and **oil-town migrants**: state wage against remittances as a floor.
- **Caspian fishers, women heading households, families of the detained**: the thinnest protection.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; statistics at republic level; informal saving, mutual aid and fear of violence mainly in press, rights reports and oral sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak for village and folklore sources; Arabic for manuscripts; Azerbaijani and Juhuri for Derbent.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counterinsurgency, disappearances, extremism cases, the 2024 attacks, resettlement land disputes; Memorial was liquidated and some outlets carry state labels, affecting reach and risk; protect informants.
- **Law:** pension, counterterrorism and Islamic-finance law, and adat as custom-law, go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
