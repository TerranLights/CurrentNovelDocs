# Dagestan: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargwa, Lezgian, Lak, Kumyk, Tabasaran, Nogai; Azerbaijani and Juhuri in Derbent; Arabic for religious texts. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kubachi** Dargins (metalwork, wall displays) against other **Dargin** and **Avar** highland villages.
- **Andi, Tsez and Archi** speakers: small highland communities whose household terms may be undocumented in the main literature.
- **Laks** of Kumukh against the resettled Laks of Novolak; **Chechen-Akkins** of the Aukh lands.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Rutuls, Tsakhurs, Aguls**: carpets and copper as household goods in the south.
- **Kumyks** of the plain and **Nogais** of the steppe: Turkic household vocabulary; Nogai herding and portable goods.
- **Azerbaijanis** and **Mountain Jews** of Derbent; **Russians and Cossacks** of Kizlyar.
- Makhachkala apartment households against village houses; women's possessions (dowry, jewelry) against men's (weapons, tools, herds).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good museum and ethnographic records of valued objects; thin on everyday contemporary possessions, lending and loss.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Dargwa, Lezgian, Lak and Kumyk for folklore and terms; Arabic for religious categories; Juhuri for Mountain Jewish sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation and resettlement claims are live disputes; women's property within families; weapons as heirlooms.
- **Law:** found property, deposits and loans in Islamic and Russian law, and old seizure customs, go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
