## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars** and **Laks** of Andalal and Gunib: the memory of resistance in the villages that carry it.
- **Dargins**, **Kumyks** and **Nogais**: whose histories include accommodation with empire as well as refusal.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Rutuls, Tsakhurs, Aguls**: southern memory of Persian and Russian rule.
- **Chechen-Akkins**: deportation and return as a story of refusal to give up land.
- **Grebensky Cossacks and Old Believers**; **Mountain Jews**; **Azerbaijanis** of Derbent.
- Sufi, Salafi and secular currents (each called stubborn by the others); women protesters; truckers and market traders; generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history, epic and press; no measured data on stubbornness in the republic; "the unbending mountaineer" is a stereotype to test, never a finding.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar and Lak for the Andalal tradition; Kumyk and Lezgian for humor and proverbs; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest against mobilization and the state carries legal risk for those involved; feud cases are private; resistance history is political.
- **Law:** feud settlement as law, and the law on protest, go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
