# Dagestan: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargwa, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak, Nogai, Tabasaran; Azerbaijani (Derbent, including its Shia community); Arabic and Ajami-script local languages for religious and didactic texts. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sufi-affiliated** communities (several brotherhoods, with differing sheikhs) against **Salafi-leaning** communities and secular, Soviet-educated families.
- **Avars, Dargins, Laks, Kumyks, Lezgins, Tabasarans**: each with its own proverbs and folk pedagogy.
- **Nogais** of the steppe; **Rutuls, Tsakhurs, Aguls** and the **Andi-Tsez** peoples, rarely surveyed.
- **Shia Azerbaijanis** of Derbent and the Shia Lezgin village of Miskindzha *check*; **Mountain Jews**; **Orthodox Russians and Cossacks**.
- Elders against youth; women against men; Makhachkala against villages; migrants against those at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in religious texts, proverbs and pedagogy; thin for representative values surveys; national surveys rarely report Dagestan alone.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic and Ajami; Avar, Kumyk, Lak, Lezgian and Dargwa for proverbs and verse; Azerbaijani for Derbent.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sufi-Salafi disputes are tied to violence and policing; state "traditional values" are political; no voice speaks for all Dagestanis.
- **Law:** the ranking of adat against sharia as law, and the decree's legal force, go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
