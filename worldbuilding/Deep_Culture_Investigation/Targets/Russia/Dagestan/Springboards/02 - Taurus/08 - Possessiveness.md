# Dagestan: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargwa, Lak, Lezgian, Kumyk, Tabasaran, Nogai; Chechen for the Akkin districts; Arabic for religious terms. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars, Dargins, Laks**: highland villages with differing courtship and marriage customs.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**: southern families, some with kin in Azerbaijan.
- **Kumyks** and **Nogais**: Turkic courtship terms; steppe and plain customs.
- **Chechen-Akkins**: abduction and family custom shared with Chechnya, to check.
- **Mountain Jews** and **Azerbaijanis** of Derbent; **Russians and Cossacks** of the north: courtship and family customs that may differ from the Muslim majority's, to check.
- Makhachkala against villages; Sufi, Salafi and secular families; generations (parents against children on phones and dating); mothers-in-law against daughters-in-law.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for measured jealousy or control; rich but one-sided testimony in rights media; old ethnography on courtship and kin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar for classical lyric; Avar, Lak and Kumyk for songs; Chechen for the Akkin districts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence against women, child custody, abduction and online shaming; some outlets and projects are harassed or restricted; protect people who speak; name variation, never one voice.
- **Law:** divorce, custody and abduction as law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
