## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avar, Dargin, Lak** highland cooking (flour, dried meat, dairy) against **Kumyk** and **Nogai** plain and steppe cooking.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs** of the south: fruit, nuts and herbs; ties to Azerbaijani taste.
- **Derbent's** Azerbaijani and **Mountain Jewish** tables; teahouses.
- **Russians and Cossacks** of the north: wine, song, a different sensory calendar.
- Caspian coast fishing towns; **Chechen-Akkins**.
- Sufi and Salafi teaching on music and dance against secular families; women's and men's spaces of pleasure; generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in food and music culture; thin on measured pleasure or time use; much of the online food writing is commercial.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Lezgian, Kumyk and Lak for song texts; Juhuri for Mountain Jewish sources; Arabic for religious views of music and pleasure.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol, music and dance divide religious currents; mixed-gender dancing and women's bodies in public are delicate; sexuality is Scorpio's.
- **Law:** rules on alcohol sales and public conduct as law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
