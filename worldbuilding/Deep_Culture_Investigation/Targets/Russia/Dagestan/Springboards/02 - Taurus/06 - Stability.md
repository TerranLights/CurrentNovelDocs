# Dagestan: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargwa, Lak, Lezgian, Kumyk, Tabasaran, Nogai; Arabic for genealogies and Sufi lineages. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland villages that stayed** against **resettled lowland villages** and their mother villages.
- **Avars, Dargins, Laks** (Kumukh, Tsovkra, Khosrekh), **Andi-Tsez** peoples: different resettlement histories.
- **Kumyks** of old plain villages (Tarki, Endirei, Aksai *check*) living beside new settlers.
- **Lezgins, Rutuls, Tsakhurs, Aguls** of the Samur and its side valleys; **Tabasarans**.
- **Nogais** of the steppe: pastoral rhythm and settlement.
- **Derbent** old families (Azerbaijani, Lezgin, Mountain Jewish); **Russians and Cossacks** of the north; **Chechen-Akkins** returned from deportation.
- Makhachkala newcomers of the last thirty years against long-settled city families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** village histories and genealogies are plentiful but local and uneven; resettlement is well studied; censuses give movement only by district.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for genealogies; Avar, Lak, Dargwa, Kumyk and Lezgian for village books and festivals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genealogies may encode status by descent; resettlement and the Aukh lands are live disputes; family documents are private.
- **Law:** the rules on ethnic representation and on land for resettlers go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
