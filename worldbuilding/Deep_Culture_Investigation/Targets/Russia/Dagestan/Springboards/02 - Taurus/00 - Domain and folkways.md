# Dagestan: Possessions and value, domain and folkways (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Avar, Dargwa, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak, Tabasaran, Nogai, Azerbaijani, Chechen (Akkin), Rutul, Agul, Tsakhur, Juhuri (Judeo-Tat); Arabic for religious and manuscript sources. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how is it held? (possessions, money, resources, values, self-worth; with the folkways of food, dress and wealth).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars**, and apart from them the small **Andi and Tsez (Dido) peoples** of the western highlands.
- **Dargins**, with **Kubachi** (silversmiths) and **Kaitag** (embroidery) kept apart as craft communities.
- **Laks**: long seasonal migrant craftsmen and traders; urban diaspora against the Kumukh villages.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs** of the south: carpet weaving; ties across the Azerbaijani border.
- **Kumyks** of the coastal plain and **Nogais** of the northern steppe: Turkic speakers; plains farming and herding.
- **Azerbaijanis** of Derbent; **Mountain Jews** (Juhuro), a small remnant with a large diaspora.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks** of Kizlyar and Tarumovka; **Chechen-Akkins** of Khasavyurt and Novolak.
- Highland villages against lowland **resettled villages** and **Makhachkala**; Sufi-affiliated against Salafi-leaning communities; labor migrants in western Siberia and Moscow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich Soviet ethnography of material culture (mostly 19th to mid-20th century); measured data only at republic level, nothing by people; lively journalism.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Avar, Dargwa, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak for folklore and press; Arabic and Ajami for manuscripts; Azerbaijani and Persian for Derbent.
- **Lands:** the tracker header carries the pre-war lands only as "Russia"; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. These sheets are written for the Republic of Dagestan as the core lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious divisions, counterinsurgency, land conflicts, corruption reporting; consent matters for family wealth and debt.
- **Law:** property, inheritance, adat and sharia rulings on goods go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
