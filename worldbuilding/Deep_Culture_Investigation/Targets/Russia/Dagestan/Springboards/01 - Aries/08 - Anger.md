# Dagestan: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women**: what anger each may show, and before whom.
- **Elders and youth**, and the elders and imams who act as cooling agents.
- **Avar, Dargin and Lak** highland villages, where feud histories may be best recorded, against **lowland and city** life.
- **Kumyks and Nogais**, whose Turkic feud terms and customs may differ.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aghuls and Rutuls** in the south; **Azerbaijanis** of Derbent, whose Shia mourning rites are worth reading for grief and anger.
- **Chechen-Akkins**, sharing Chechen feud and reconciliation customs.
- **Russians and Cossacks**, and **Mountain Jews**, as smaller groups with their own norms.
- **Sufi, Salafi and secular** milieus, which may teach different ideas of righteous anger.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; feud and reconciliation are well documented; everyday anger and emotion norms are thin and must come from linguistics, sermons, psychology and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for ethics texts; Avar, Lezgin, Lak, Kumyk for emotion words, curses and songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the airport riot and antisemitism; insurgent violence; feuds still active; domestic violence and honor killings; the "hot-blooded" stereotype, which sources must not reproduce. Feud law and its courts belong to Libra; customary justice and its spirit to Capricorn; revenge as such to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
