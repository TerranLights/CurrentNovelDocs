## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and young men**: the main axis to test, inside each people.
- **Women and men**, and women of different ages and piety; widows and the unmarried.
- **Avar and Dargin highland villages**, where the godekan is strongest; **Lak** villages and the Lak urban class in Makhachkala.
- **Kumyk** and **Nogai** lowland and steppe communities, with different social histories (old princely ranks among Kumyks).
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans and Azerbaijanis** of the south and Derbent, with their verse-dueling and market traditions.
- **Chechen-Akkins**, where Chechen ideas of standing apply.
- **Mountain Jews** and **Russians** of Kizlyar and Makhachkala.
- **Makhachkala youth** online, against village norms; **migrants in Moscow** asserting themselves as a minority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; ethnography and folklore are rich in Russian, but direct study of everyday assertion is thin. Field observation and online material will matter.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Lezgin, Kumyk, Dargin for proverbs and verse; Azerbaijani for meykhana.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** journalists and activists in Dagestan have faced violence; protest (for example the 2022 mobilization protests) is a risky topic for informants. Women's speaking up touches family control and honor violence. Formal complaints to courts belong to Libra; customary sanctions and legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
