*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young women and young men**, separately; the gap between them is the first thing to measure.
- **Sufi followers, Salafi circles and the secular or lightly practicing**: three different ideas of whose will guides a life.
- **Highland villages** with strong lineage control, **lowland resettled villages**, and **Makhachkala, Kaspiysk and Derbent**.
- **Kumyks and Nogais**, whose family forms may differ from highland ones; **Nogai steppe households** with herding work.
- **Russians and Cossacks** of Kizlyar, and **Mountain Jews**, with different marriage customs.
- **Kubachi, Gotsatl and Untsukul** craft villages, where trade gave men a living outside farming.
- **Labor migrants in the oil towns of western Siberia and in Moscow**, where distance changes who decides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; marriage and family are well studied, but direct work on "autonomy" as a value is thin. Much current material is journalism.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic terms for the religious debate; Avar, Lak and Lezgin for songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** female genital cutting, forced marriage, honor-related violence, and women's flight from families are dangerous topics for informants and subjects; the Kadar episode ties into insurgency history. Family law, Sharia arbitration and marriage registration belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
