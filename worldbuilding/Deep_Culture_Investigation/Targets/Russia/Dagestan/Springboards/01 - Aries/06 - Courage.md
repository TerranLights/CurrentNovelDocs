## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars** of the western highlands (Shamil's and Hadji Murat's homeland) against **Avars** of lowland towns.
- **Laks**, with the Partu Patima tradition and old Gazikumukh memories; **Dargins** of Akusha and Kaitag.
- **Kumyks and Nogais**, whose Turkic-language epics (Koroglu, Edige) offer other hero models.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans** of the south, with the Sharvili cycle.
- **Chechen-Akkins**, whose courage words come from Chechen.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks**, with their own frontier and war memory.
- **Mountain Jews**, with their World War II and frontier memories.
- **Women**: endurance, widowhood, and armed women in songs.
- **Police, soldiers and rescuers** against **civilians, clergy and journalists** (physical against moral courage).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heroic folklore, the Caucasian War and war memory; mixed for moral and social courage, which sits in journalism.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for chronicles; Avar, Lak, Kumyk, Lezgin for songs; Chechen for the Akkin villages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the words for martyr and holy war are used by armed groups and by the state's opponents, so informants and sources may be guarded; insurgency and counterinsurgency deaths remain raw. Shamil and Hadji Murat are claimed by several peoples. Avoid the stock "fearless highlander" image. Courts and punishment for cowardice or desertion belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
