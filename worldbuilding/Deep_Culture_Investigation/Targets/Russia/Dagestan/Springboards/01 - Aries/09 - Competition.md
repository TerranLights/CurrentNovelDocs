## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avar highland districts** (Tsumada, Khunzakh, Gumbet), often named as wrestling heartlands (*check*), against other peoples' areas.
- **Khasavyurt**, a multiethnic wrestling center with Chechen-Akkin, Kumyk and Avar clubs.
- **Kumyk and Nogai** lowland and steppe communities, with horse and Turkic-language contest traditions.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Rutuls, Aghuls and Tsakhurs** of the south, with their own festival games.
- **Laks and Dargins**, including Kubachi craftsmen's rivalry in skill.
- **Girls and women**, whose contests may lie in song, craft, study and new women's sport.
- **Urban Makhachkala** families' school and status competition, against villages.
- **Russians, Cossacks and Mountain Jews**, with other sports and school traditions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport and schooling; mixed for envy and status rivalry, which need ethnography and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak for folk games and songs; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exam-cheating claims stigmatize the whole republic in federal media; ethnic competition for posts is politically delicate; sport ties to organized crime and to armed groups appear in some reporting. Avoid treating sport success as a trait of a people. Election rules and contracts belong to Libra.
