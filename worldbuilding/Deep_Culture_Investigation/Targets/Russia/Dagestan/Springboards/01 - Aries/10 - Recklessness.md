## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men** against everyone else; then **young men by district and people**.
- **Laks of Tsovkra and the Kulinsky district**, for the rope-walking tradition.
- **Lezgins** of the Shalbuzdag area and the south, with pilgrimage risk.
- **Coastal communities** of Makhachkala, Kaspiysk and Derbent (sea, poaching, drowning) against **high-mountain villages** (roads, cliffs, avalanches).
- **Terek Cossacks and Nogais**, with horse-riding and steppe traditions of daring.
- **Chechen-Akkins and Kumyks** of the Khasavyurt plain, with their own car and wedding cultures.
- **Devout and secular youth**, who may judge gambling, drink and stunts differently.
- **Migrants in Moscow**, whose stunts draw federal attention.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; road and death statistics are good; meanings of daring need folklore, video and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Lak and Lezgin for rope-walking and pilgrimage traditions; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** car-stunt and wedding-gunfire stories feed anti-Caucasus prejudice in Russian media; drug use and poaching are criminal matters; informants may fear police. Avoid treating daring as a trait of a people. Penalties and policing belong to Libra; the spirit of the rules to Capricorn.
