# Dagestan: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Lezgin, Avar, Kumyk, Dargin, Lak, Azerbaijani, Chechen, Juhuri; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lezgins,** for a line of protest poetry from Sayid of Kochkhyur to the Soviet ashugs.
- **Avars and Dargins,** for satire in verse and drama in large highland languages.
- **Kumyks,** for an early national theater and Turkic genres of teasing.
- **Laks,** to check for a separate comic and satirical tradition rather than assume one.
- **Mountain Jews,** whose emigrant press can criticize home from abroad.
- **Akkin Chechens,** whose apology and honor norms come partly from Chechnya.
- **Journalists and bloggers against officials,** because criticizing upward carries real risk.
- **Mothers-in-law and daughters-in-law,** because criticism inside the house runs downward by age.
- **Sufi and Salafi-leaning believers,** for criticism of each other's practice.
- **Young comedians against elders,** because age gives a license to criticize that youth lacks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, journalism and the cost of criticism; thin for everyday feedback at work and at home.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Lezgin, Avar, Kumyk, Dargin, Lak; Arabic; Azerbaijani; Juhuri and Hebrew for emigrant sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** journalists have been killed and prosecuted; criticism of religion and officials can lead to forced apologies or worse; some sources carry Russian "foreign agent" or similar designations. Protect informants. Defamation and speech law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
