## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Graduates of madrasas and of Islamic institutes against Soviet- and Russian-trained engineers and scientists,** because they learned two traditions of reasoning.
- **Avars and Dargins of the old learning villages,** for the highland Arabic scholarly network.
- **Laks around Kumukh,** an old center of learning with its own scholars.
- **Lezgins,** for a southern tradition of learned writing and for schooling shared with Azerbaijan.
- **Kumyks,** because Temir-Khan-Shura's reformist printers argued for new subjects.
- **Mountain Jews,** for Hebrew religious study and a Soviet professional class.
- **Russian-speaking Makhachkala families,** whose children study in Russian-only schools.
- **Village and city schools,** because exam and olympiad results may split by place.
- **Women and men,** for access to technical higher education.
- **Sufi and Salafi-leaning believers,** because they reason from a master's guidance or from the texts directly.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. The Arabic learned tradition is well catalogued; current reasoning and thinking-style data by people are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Arabic first; then Avar, Dargin, Lak, Lezgin, Kumyk; Azerbaijani.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exam-integrity controversies in the region are documented in the press: handle as a research question, never as a label. Religious reasoning is contested between communities. Reasoning about law (jurisprudence, inheritance) goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
