## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kumyks, Azerbaijanis and Lezgins,** for wisdom-tale and poetry genres shared with the wider Turkic and Persianate world.
- **Avars and Dargins of endogamous highland villages,** because a person may be judged first by his *tukhum*.
- **Kubachi masters,** for connoisseurship inside one craft and its defense against fakes.
- **Tabasaran and Lezgin weavers,** for how a carpet's makers judge its quality.
- **Nogai and highland herders,** for judging livestock and pasture.
- **Mountain Jews,** for matchmaking in a small community scattered by emigration.
- **Kizlyar Russians and Derbent vineyard workers,** for the tasting and grading of wine and cognac.
- **Sufi and Salafi-leaning believers,** because they judge religious authority by different tests.
- **Young people on Telegram and Instagram against elders,** because the sources of trusted information differ by age.
- **Akkin Chechens,** for a separate family-reputation and elders' counsel tradition.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and kinship ethnography are rich; data on scams, rumor and taste are thin and mostly federal.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic; Kumyk, Lezgin, Avar, Dargin; Azerbaijani; Chechen.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2023 airport events were an antisemitic riot with criminal cases: handle with care for the Jewish community and without blaming a people. Judging a match by lineage can shade into prejudice between peoples and estates. "False sheikh" accusations are part of a live and sometimes violent dispute. Judging by courts and elders' mediation goes to Libra or Capricorn.
