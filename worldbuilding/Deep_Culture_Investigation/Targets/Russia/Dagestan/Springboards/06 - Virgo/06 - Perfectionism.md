# Dagestan: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kubachi (Dargin) and Untsukul and Gotsatl (Avar) masters,** because the exactness of a craft is the village's reputation.
- **Laks of Tsovkra and Balkhar,** for performance and pottery standards with a different stake (a fall, a cracked pot).
- **Avars, Dargins and the mixed town of Khasavyurt,** for wrestling's drilled perfection.
- **Kumyks and Lezgins,** for stage dance and lowland craft traditions.
- **Tabasaran and Lezgin weavers,** for exact counted patterns in carpets.
- **Mountain Jews of Derbent,** for exact religious observance in a small community.
- **Kizlyar Russians,** for whom the standards may run through Russian school and church.
- **Religious students (hafizes) against secular high achievers,** because flawless recitation and flawless marks are different ideals.
- **Brides and daughters-in-law,** because household perfection is asked of women in particular.
- **Young people online,** where weddings and bodies are displayed against a perfect image.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on crafts and sport; thin on perfectionism as a psychological trait and on its costs.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic; Dargin (Kubachi dialect), Avar, Lak; Azerbaijani.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental-health topics (anxiety, eating disorders, suicide) carry stigma and are hard to study; exam-score lists have a contested integrity history; sport fame mixes with politics. Some masters' techniques are family knowledge.
- **Held for review:** none.
