# Dagestan: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars,** with the Andi and Tsez peoples whose kin terms may differ from standard Avar.
- **Dargins, with Kubachi and Kaitag;** and **Laks.**
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Agul, Rutul and Tsakhur:** small southern languages with their own terms.
- **Kumyks and Nogais,** with Turkic kin terms and steppe household patterns.
- **Akkin Chechens,** with Chechen clan (teip) structures.
- **Azerbaijanis and Mountain Jews of Derbent,** and **Russians of Kizlyar,** for non-Caucasian-language family models.
- **Highland villages and Makhachkala:** large and smaller families.
- **Religious milieus,** on brothers' duties toward sisters.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Soviet family ethnography and kin-term data are good; adult sibling relations, care of parents and rivalry are thinly studied.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; each national language for kin terms and songs; Hebrew and Juhuri for Mountain Jews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** brothers' control over sisters and honor violence; cousin marriage and inherited disease; inheritance disputes (law side: Libra).
- **Held for review:** none.
