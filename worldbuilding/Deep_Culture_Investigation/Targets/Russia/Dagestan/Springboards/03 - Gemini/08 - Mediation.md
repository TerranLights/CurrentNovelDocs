# Dagestan: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Lak, Kumyk, Lezgian, Chechen, Azerbaijani, Juhuri; Arabic for religious terms and old documents. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and, more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors). (Courts, codes and the letter of customary or Islamic law go to Libra; their spirit to Capricorn.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland jamaats,** where elders and imams settle quarrels, against **Makhachkala,** where police, fixers and lawyers step in.
- **Avars, Dargins and Laks,** with their own customs of reconciliation.
- **Kumyks,** in land quarrels with resettled highlanders on the plain.
- **Laks of Novolak and Akkin Chechens,** at the center of the Aukh land question.
- **Lezgins and Azerbaijanis** of the south, and border quarrels with Azerbaijan.
- **Sufi-linked and Salafi-leaning communities,** who differ on intercession.
- **Mountain Jews and Russians of Kizlyar,** and the interfaith go-betweens.
- **Women and men:** who may step between fighting men, and who sends for the matchmaker.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Imperial records and feud ethnography are rich; present-day mediation practice is reported mostly in the press.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for old documents and religious debate; German for Kemper; Azerbaijani for the south.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blood feud and its settlement; land disputes between peoples; Sufi–Salafi disputes, where the wrong question can endanger a source; bribery and fixers. Russia's federal mediation law and the courts go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
