## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highlanders with a second home on the plain** against **villagers who never moved.**
- **Avars, Dargins, Laks and Lezgins** in how they combine ethnic and Dagestani belonging; small peoples (Tsez, Andi, Archi) counted within larger ones.
- **Kumyks,** whose plain lands are shared with resettled highlanders.
- **Akkin Chechens and Laks of Novolak,** tied to two homes after deportation and resettlement.
- **Mountain Jews and Tats,** and **Russians and Cossacks of Kizlyar** with their own double belongings.
- **Sufi-linked and Salafi-leaning communities,** and secular Soviet-trained families.
- **Bilingual city youth** against monolingual elders.
- **Diasporas:** Lezgins and Avars of Azerbaijan, living across a border from kin.
