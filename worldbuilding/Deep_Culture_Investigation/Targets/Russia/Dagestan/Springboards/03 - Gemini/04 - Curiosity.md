# Dagestan: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland villages and the city:** questions asked of a stranger in a small jamaat against anonymity in Makhachkala.
- **Avars, Dargins and Laks,** each with its own tale collections.
- **Kumyks** of the plain and **Azerbaijanis of Derbent,** long exposed to traders and travelers.
- **The southern peoples** (Lezgins, Tabasarans, Agul, Rutul, Tsakhur) along the old roads to the south.
- **Nogais** of the steppe, with a herding life.
- **Religious milieus,** since teaching on proper and improper questions differs between Sufi, Salafi-leaning and secular families.
- **Girls and boys,** in what each may explore and ask.
- **Mountain Jews and Russians** of Derbent and Kizlyar.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to thin. Travelers and folktales are rich; measured curiosity and data on hobbies are scarce.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for al-Masʿudi and sermons; Ottoman Turkish or translations for Evliya Çelebi; the national languages for tales.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions about religion, kin feuds and the insurgency years can be dangerous to ask; outsiders' curiosity has a long colonial frame; women's spaces are closed to men's questions.
- **Held for review:** none.
