# Dagestan: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Lak, Kumyk, Lezgian, Chechen, Azerbaijani; Arabic for religious teaching on speech. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small highland jamaats** against **Makhachkala, Kaspiysk and Khasavyurt,** where anonymity changes what talk can do.
- **Women and men:** the spring and the kitchen against the godekan and the mosque yard.
- **Avars, Dargins, Laks:** compare honor vocabularies and mocking songs.
- **Kumyks and Nogais,** with Turkic shame words and sung quatrains.
- **Lezgins and the Azerbaijanis of Derbent,** with ashug satire.
- **Akkin Chechens,** with Chechen honor terms.
- **Mountain Jews and Russians of Kizlyar:** smaller communities with their own reputation networks.
- **Young people online** against elders offline; **migrants** whose conduct in Moscow is reported home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Religious teaching and honor ethnography are plentiful; studies of gossip itself are few; online material is large but scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for classical ethics; the national languages for songs and nicknames; Azerbaijani for the south.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gossip about women's conduct can lead to violence; public shaming videos and forced apologies; denunciation archives touch living families; slander and libel law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
