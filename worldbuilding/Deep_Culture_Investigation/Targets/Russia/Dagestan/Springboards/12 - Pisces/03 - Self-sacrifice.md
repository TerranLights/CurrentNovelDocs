# Dagestan: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Lak, Lezgian, Dargin, Kumyk, Azerbaijani, Andi-group languages for Botlikh and Tsumada; Arabic and Hebrew for the religious terms. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars of Gimry, Akhulgo and Gunib:** the imamate's sacrifice is local family memory there.
- **Andi peoples of Botlikh and Tsumada:** the 1999 defense is their own recent sacrifice.
- **Kumyks of the plain:** the old Tarki shamkhalate sided with the empire at times, so their heroes may differ from the highlanders' (*check*).
- **Laks and Dargins:** the migrant craftsman and the waiting wife, plus a heavy Soviet war-memory culture.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans:** border heroes and Sharvili tales give a southern version.
- **Derbent's Shia Azerbaijanis:** Husayn's martyrdom frames suffering for the faith differently.
- **Mountain Jews:** Soviet war service and community sacrifice, read through their own memory books and diaspora writing.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Kizlyar:** Orthodox martyrs and the Cossack ethic of service.
- **Families of police and families of insurgents:** rival claims on the word "martyr".
- **Women of every people:** the self-denying mother against the woman who fights or protests.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war and imamate memory; mixed for everyday family sacrifice, which is mostly oral.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar and Lak for the heroic songs; Arabic for imamate sources; Azerbaijani for Ashura; Hebrew and Juhuri for Mountain Jewish memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the words *шахид* and *джихад* carry legal risk under Russian extremism law and deep grief on both sides; disappearances; Shamil's legacy is contested between peoples. Military duty as law goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
