# Dagestan: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Arabic (sin vocabulary); Kumyk, Avar, Lezgian, Dargin, Lak, Nogai, Azerbaijani. **Concept in one line:** what the culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is researched only in Libra and Capricorn; this sheet takes the idea of the wrong and the wrongdoer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland Avar and Dargin villages:** feud memory and village shame weigh a wrong differently from the state.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** land, livestock and pasture wrongs, from the plain and steppe side.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans:** cross-border trade, to test what the south condemns and what it shrugs off.
- **Makhachkala and Kaspiysk:** city crime, poaching and the business of protection.
- **Sufi and Salafi-leaning believers:** each side has called the other's acts wrongs; how each speaks of the insurgent.
- **Families of the disappeared and families of police:** opposite experiences of who the wrongdoer was.
- **Women:** wrongs done to them inside the family, and who names them crimes.
- **Mountain Jews and Russians:** minority views of safety and of who is to blame.
- **Officials and businessmen:** crimes of the powerful, seen from inside the patronage networks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political violence and corruption reporting; mixed for everyday wrongs; official statistics likely understate both.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Arabic for sin vocabulary; Avar, Kumyk, Dargin for feud and abrek folklore; Lezgian and Azerbaijani for the south.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counterinsurgency abuses and disappearances; harms to women and girls (consent and safety come first); named living people in corruption cases; insurgency talk can carry legal risk for sources. Every legal question goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
