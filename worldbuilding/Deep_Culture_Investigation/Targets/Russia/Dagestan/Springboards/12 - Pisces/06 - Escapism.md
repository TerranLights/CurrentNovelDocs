# Dagestan: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak, Azerbaijani; Arabic for the religious terms on intoxication and idle play; Turkish for emigrant and media links. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Soviet-era men:** drink at the workplace and the feast, to test against their sons' habits.
- **Young religious men:** to test whether hookah lounges and betting apps fill the place drink held for their fathers.
- **Women:** which escapes are open to them (television, phones, visits), and when flight from home becomes the only one.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Kizlyar:** a vine-growing district with Orthodox feasts.
- **Derbent's Azerbaijanis and Mountain Jews:** old wine country with its own feast customs.
- **Highland villages:** the pastures and the city as places to get away to.
- **Makhachkala:** the cafés, beaches and malls of the republic's one big city.
- **Emigrants:** 19th-century families in Turkey, Jordan and Syria, and the post-2022 leavers abroad.
- **Families of those who left for Syria:** a lost generation whose escape is a wound.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; drink and drug data exist but are distorted by stigma; tourism data good; emigration data scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Arabic first; Avar and Dargin for the song tradition; Turkish for emigrant descendants.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mobilization and leaving the country carry legal risk; Syria returnees and their families; women in shelters (consent, safety, no identifying details). Drug and alcohol law and the mobilization law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
