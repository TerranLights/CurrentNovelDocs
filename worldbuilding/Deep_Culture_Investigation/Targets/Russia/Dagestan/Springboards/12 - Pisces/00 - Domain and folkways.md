*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak, Tabasaran, Nogai, Chechen, Azerbaijani, Rutul, Agul, Tsakhur, Juhuri (the Mountain Jews' Iranian-language speech); Arabic for the religious and manuscript record. **Concept in one line:** how the culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape, with magic ways as the folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars and the Andi-Tsez peoples:** the largest people beside small highland neighbors whose spirit lore may diverge.
- **Dargins, Kubachi and Kaitag:** separate highland communities, each to test on its own.
- **Laks of Kumukh:** an old seat of Islamic learning, and a people moved to the lowlands in 1944.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** Turkic-speaking plain and steppe peoples with their own spirit lore; Nogais mostly Hanafi.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs:** southern peoples tied across the Azerbaijani border.
- **Azerbaijanis of Derbent:** Shia mourning rites give suffering another frame.
- **Mountain Jews (Juhuro):** Derbent's own unseen, and a diaspora in Israel and Moscow.
- **Russians, Terek Cossacks, Old Believers of Kizlyar:** Orthodox and older Russian folk belief.
- **Chechen-Akkins of Khasavyurt and Novolak:** the 1944 deportation as a memory of confinement.
- **Sufi disciples and Salafi-leaning believers:** opposed views of saints and charms.
- **City, mountain village, resettled lowland village; migrants and emigrant descendants abroad:** distance from the old shrines and healers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Islam, Sufism and manuscripts; mixed for folk healing; thin and stigma-laden for mental health.
- **Lands:** the tracker's pre-war lands are given only as "Russia", so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign is written for the core lands of the Republic of Dagestan.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Arabic, Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgian; Juhuri, Nogai and Azerbaijani for the minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Sufi-Salafi rift and the counterinsurgency (disappearances; the 2024 attacks on synagogues and churches in Derbent and Makhachkala); jinn-healing abuse cases; women's seclusion. Adat and sharia as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
