# Dagestan: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak and the other republic languages; Arabic for the religious vocabulary; Hebrew and Juhuri for Mountain Jewish charity. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland Avar and Dargin villages:** the village community's own collections against the newer city foundations.
- **Kumyk and Nogai lowland and steppe communities:** Turkic pity-words and Nogai Hanafi alms rules may draw the line differently.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans of the south:** cross-border kin in Azerbaijan, who may be the first called on.
- **Derbent's Shia Azerbaijanis:** Muharram charity meals as a distinct giving season.
- **Mountain Jews:** tzedakah and diaspora funds sending help back.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Kizlyar:** Orthodox parish charity and state social services.
- **Akkin Chechens of Khasavyurt:** hosts of war refugees, with deportation memory behind them.
- **Sufi and Salafi-leaning congregations:** each runs its own charity and may limit whom it helps.
- **Labor migrants in western Siberia:** money sent home for the sick and for funerals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in religious teaching and local NGO appeals, thin in measured giving for the republic alone; much of the practice is oral and village-level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Arabic first; Avar, Kumyk, Lezgian, Dargin for proverbs and sermons; Hebrew and Juhuri for the Mountain Jewish side.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charities linked by the state to extremism (some foundations closed); wartime captivity is contested across Russian, Georgian and Dagestani memory; families' privacy in appeals for sick children. Mercy and justice in law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
