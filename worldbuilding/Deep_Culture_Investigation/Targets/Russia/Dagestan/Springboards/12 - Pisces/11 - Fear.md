# Dagestan: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Arabic (fear of God, the grave); Avar, Kumyk, Lezgian, Dargin, Lak, Azerbaijani, Juhuri; Persian for the old Derbent chronicle tradition. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and the culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain Jews of Derbent and Makhachkala:** fear sharpened by the 2023 riot and the 2024 attacks.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Kizlyar:** the 1996 raid as a shared memory.
- **Kumyks and Avars of Buynaksk:** the 1999 bombing and its aftermath.
- **Salafi-leaning families:** fear of the register, of abduction and of being named.
- **Sufi communities:** fear of attacks on sheikhs and mosques, from the other side of the rift.
- **Highland villages:** mudflows, landslides, wolves and isolation in winter.
- **Makhachkala and Kaspiysk:** high-rises in a quake zone, and city crime.
- **Women and girls:** fear of gossip and shame, and of harm at home.
- **Children:** the *dev* and other tale monsters, and how adults use them.
- **Laks and Chechen-Akkins of Novolak:** fear around unsettled land.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for terror events and natural hazards; mixed for folk fear and fright cures; thin for measured anxiety (student samples only).
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic for sermons; Avar, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak and Dargin for tales and fright cures; Juhuri for Mountain Jewish accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** terror victims and families; the register and abductions; the 2023 riot (ongoing prosecutions, minority safety); women's fear of harm at home (consent and safety). Policing and anti-extremism law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
