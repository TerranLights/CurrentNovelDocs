# Dagestan: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgian, Lak, Tabasaran, Nogai, Azerbaijani, Juhuri. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Andi-Tsez and Archi villages:** small peoples who often spoke several neighbors' languages, a test of cross-group understanding.
- **Women and men:** who weeps, who sits in silence, who may voice another's pain at a funeral.
- **Soviet-educated elders and young religious believers:** different scripts for feeling (literature and theater against sermon and du'a).
- **Mixed Makhachkala neighborhoods and single-people mountain villages:** daily practice in reading strangers against kin.
- **Kumyks of the plain:** hosts of resettled highlanders, a site of friction and of learning the other.
- **Nogais of the northern steppe:** a small Turkic people, to hear how the larger peoples' feelings are read from the margin.
- **Derbent's Shia Azerbaijanis, Lezgins and Mountain Jews:** three communities sharing one old city.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Kizlyar:** how a shrinking Russian community reads its Muslim neighbors, and is read by them.
- **Chechen-Akkins and Laks of Novolak:** two peoples with competing claims to the same lands, where empathy is tested.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; linguistic and folklore pointers are good, psychological measurement is thin and mostly in student samples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Lezgian, Lak and Dargin for laments and heart idioms; Kumyk and Nogai for the Turkic side.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic land disputes (Novolak, the lowland resettlements); grief after counterinsurgency deaths and the 2024 attacks; mourners' privacy in condolence fieldwork. Feud reconciliation as law goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
