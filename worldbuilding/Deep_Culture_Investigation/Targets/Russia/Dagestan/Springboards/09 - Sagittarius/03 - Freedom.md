# Dagestan: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars of the old free communities** and **Avars of the former Khunzakh khanate**: self-rule against princely rule.
- **Dargins of the Akusha-Dargo union** and **Kaitag under its utsmi**: the same contrast in another people.
- **Kumyks under the shamkhals of Tarki**: an estate society where freedom ran by rank.
- **Laks of Kazikumukh**: a khanate with a strong learned class.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Rutuls, Aguls, Tsakhurs**: southern societies divided by a state border since 1991.
- **Akkin Chechens** and **the Laks resettled in Novolak**: deportation and its after-effects.
- **Nogais of the steppe**: a nomadic sense of open land, and a 1990s national movement.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Kizlyar**: frontier liberties under the tsar.
- **Women and young people in Makhachkala**: freedom of dress, marriage and movement as their own question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 19th-century wars and for village self-rule; mixed for present-day attitudes; politically shaped on both the Russian and the insurgent side.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Arabic, Avar first; Kumyk, Lezgin and Dargin for the folk songs and the 1990s movements.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the counterinsurgency era, rights reports and the killing of journalists are live matters; protect named private people. The legal side of rights goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
