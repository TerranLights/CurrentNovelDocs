## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kubachi and other craft villages**: honesty bound to a hallmark and a trade name.
- **Market traders in Makhachkala, Khasavyurt and Derbent**: bargaining norms of each.
- **Officials and public employees** against **private villagers**: honesty toward the state or toward kin.
- **Avars, Dargins and Laks**: highland codes of the given word, each in its own words.
- **Kumyks and Nogais**: plains and steppe codes, with Turkic terms.
- **Lezgins and Azerbaijanis of the south**: trade across the Azerbaijan border.
- **Akkin Chechens**: the honor-rivalry ideal of yakh'.
- **Juhuro and Russian merchants**: minority traders' standing and norms.
- **Students and teachers**: exam cheating seen from both sides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in press cases and folklore; mixed in surveys; thin in measured everyday honesty.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Arabic for the ethics; local languages for tales and proverbs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases name living people; Transparency's chapter is banned, so cite its archive with care; no claim that any people is more or less honest. Bribery law goes to Libra; what law is for goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
