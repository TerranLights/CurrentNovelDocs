# Dagestan: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Kumyk, Lezgin, Dargin, Lak, Tsez and Archi (for grammar), Azerbaijani, Chechen, Nogai; Arabic for the scholarly tradition. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it (scripture, elders, scientists, the press, one's own experience), and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Speakers of Tsez, Archi and other languages with evidential marking** against **Russian-only urban speakers**: to test whether grammar shapes how claims are framed.
- **Madrasa-trained scholars and their students**: truth through chains of teachers.
- **Soviet-trained historians and scientists**: truth through the academy and the archive.
- **Lezgins and Azerbaijanis of Derbent**: rival accounts of the same city.
- **Avars and Dargins**: their memories of the Imamate, to set side by side.
- **Kumyks and Nogais**: plains peoples with their own histories of land and origin.
- **Akkin Chechens and Laks of Novolak**: two truths about one resettled district.
- **Young Telegram and Instagram users**: news through channels, not newspapers.
- **Juhuro and Russian communities**: minority accounts of the republic's past.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for linguistics and for contested history; mixed for media trust; thin for survey measures of trust in science at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Arabic first; the local languages for proverbs and village histories; linguistic literature is partly in English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic history is politically live; the 2023 riot and repression files involve living people. Oaths in courts and disputes belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
