# Dagestan: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak, Azerbaijani, Chechen; Arabic for Sufi and jihad literature. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avar descendants of Shamil's supporters** and **families tied to his opponents**: memory of who held firm.
- **Murids of a shaykh** against **Muslims without a Sufi tie**: a binding vow or a personal stand.
- **Avars and Kumyks of the early Soviet years**: the two Bolshevik exemplars above (Dakhadaev, Buynaksky *check*), a secular line to follow.
- **Dargins and Laks**: scholarly lineages beside the Soviet intelligentsia, two kinds of principle to set side by side.
- **Kumyk plains villages**: land protests, where conviction meets property.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans**: cross-border causes since 1991.
- **Akkin Chechens**: deportation memory and its shaping of stands.
- **Russian converts** and **Russians who stayed Orthodox**: two kinds of firmness in a minority.
- **Juhuro who stayed** and **Juhuro who left**: staying as a stand.
- **Women** and **men**: who leads street protest, and who is expected to.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 19th-century exemplars; mixed for 20th-century biographies; thin and risky for present-day activism.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Arabic, Avar; Kumyk and Lak for the protests and poets.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyrdom vocabulary is used by the insurgency (see 10, Fundamentalism); protesters and rights campaigners face prosecution; anonymize living people. Prosecutions themselves go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
