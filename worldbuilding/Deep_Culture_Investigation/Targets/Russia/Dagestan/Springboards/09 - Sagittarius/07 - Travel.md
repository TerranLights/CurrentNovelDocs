## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Laks**: itinerant craftsmen and performers, the classic case to test against others.
- **Kubachi Dargins**: metalworkers who sold their craft across empires.
- **Avar and Dargin herders**: the mountain-to-plain round.
- **Nogais**: a steppe past of mobile herding.
- **Kumyks and Derbent's people**: plains and coastal hosts to traders and travelers.
- **Lezgins and Tsakhurs**: kin across the Azerbaijan border.
- **Shia Azerbaijanis of Derbent**: pilgrimage southward.
- **Juhuro**: emigration, and home as a place to visit, to trace by family.
- **Diaspora descendants in Turkey and the Middle East**: the journey seen from the far end.
- **Young urban migrants**: Moscow and Siberia as the new road.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and ethnography; mixed for migration statistics; thin for travel diaries in the local languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Arabic; Turkish for the diaspora; Hebrew for the Juhuro in Israel; Lak, Avar and Lezgin for songs of leaving.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** travel to Syria and Turkey in the 2010s is tied to insurgency files; border and migration status are private matters. Migration law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
