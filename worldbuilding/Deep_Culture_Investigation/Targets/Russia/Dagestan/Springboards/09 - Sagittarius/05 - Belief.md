# Dagestan: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Kumyk, Nogai, Lezgin, Dargin, Lak, Azerbaijani, Chechen, Judeo-Tat; Arabic for creeds and protective texts. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars and the Andi-Tsez peoples**: mountain beliefs tied to peaks, springs and weather.
- **Dargins, with Kubachi and Kaitag apart**: a craft town and an old principality, whose lore may differ from the Dargin villages.
- **Laks**: an early-Islamic center, to test how folk belief survived in a learned setting.
- **Kumyks and Nogais**: Turkic spirit lore and steppe beliefs.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**: spring festivals and shrine lore.
- **Shia Azerbaijanis and Tats of Derbent**: a different creed and calendar.
- **Juhuro (Mountain Jews)**: a minority belief system recorded early.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Kizlyar**: Christian and folk belief.
- **Akkin Chechens**: Chechen folk belief inside Dagestan.
- **Older Soviet-schooled people** against **younger believers**: belief without a creed against creed-first belief.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in older ethnography; mixed for present-day belief; thin for surveys at district level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Arabic for the protective texts and creeds; Kumyk, Nogai, Avar and Lezgin for folklore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** folk practices may be condemned by some clergy (see 10, Fundamentalism); healers' clients are private; do not treat older ethnographers' labels ("superstition") as neutral.
- **Held for review:** none.
