# Dagestan: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Arabic; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Azerbaijani, Chechen for local sermons and media. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change. Whether the word fits secular and political certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avar and Dargin Salafi-leaning villages** against **Sufi-led villages nearby**: the same people on both sides of the line.
- **Andi and Tsez valleys (Botlikh, Tsumada)**: the 1999 front line, with their own memory of it.
- **Kumyk and Chechen Khasavyurt**: a mixed city where currents cross.
- **Lezgins and Tabasarans of the south**: whether currents there run separately, with ties across the Azerbaijan border.
- **Laks**: a learned tradition, to test how it met restorationist claims.
- **Shia Azerbaijanis of Derbent**: their own view of Sunni polemic against Shia practice.
- **Juhuro and Orthodox Russians**: non-Muslim neighbors, before and after the 2024 attacks.
- **Young urban men and women**: online preaching's audience, to measure rather than assume.
- **Muftiate clergy**, **independent scholars** and **Salafi imams**: three claims to orthodoxy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in political science and news; mixed in ethnography; thin and partisan in surveys. Insider voices are scarce and often filtered.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Arabic first; Avar and Dargin for village preaching; Azerbaijani for Derbent.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** high risk. Some Salafi and other texts are on Russia's federal list of banned materials; possessing them can be prosecuted. Naming people as Salafi can endanger them; anonymize. Do not use insurgent media as a pointer. The republic's 1999 ban on "Wahhabi" activity and later counter-extremism law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit). No people is to be cast as fundamentalist: measure variation.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-origin revivalist movements in the nation's lands (category only).
