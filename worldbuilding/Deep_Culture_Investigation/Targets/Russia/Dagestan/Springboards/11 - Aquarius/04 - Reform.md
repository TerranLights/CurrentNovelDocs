# Dagestan: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Arabic (the language of the reform scholars and of the early press); Avar, Lak, Kumyk, Dargin, Lezgin, Azerbaijani, Nogai. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland scholarly villages (Sogratl, Kudutl, Akusha)**, to test how far reform by religious learning came from such centers.
- **Laks and Kumyks of Buynaksk (Temir-Khan-Shura)**, the town of the reform press.
- **Lezgins and Azerbaijanis of the south**, to test the pull of Baku's reform writing.
- **Avars and Dargins of the former imamate**, where reform once became war.
- **Sufi and Salafi congregations across peoples**, for rival claims to be renewers.
- **Nogais**, a steppe people reached by reform through other routes.
- **Terek Cossacks**, for Soviet measures done to them.
- **Akkin Chechens**, for deportation and return as "reform" from above.
- **Mountain Jews**, for Soviet changes to a religious minority.
- **Women's groups**, for campaigns that aimed at women's lives, heard from women.
- **Families of former militants**, for the reform of persons as they experienced it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history of religious reform and the Soviet program; mixed for the post-Soviet period; thin in surveys of support for reform.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic (essential for the scholars and the early press); Russian; Lak, Kumyk, Avar, Azerbaijani; Ottoman and Azerbaijani Turkish for links to Istanbul and Baku.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counter-extremism lists, militants and their families; the deaths of journalists; the Sufi-Salafi dispute. Reforms of law and courts go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
