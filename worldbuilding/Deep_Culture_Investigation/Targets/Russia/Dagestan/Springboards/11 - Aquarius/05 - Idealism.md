# Dagestan: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Arabic (Sufi and religious ideals); Avar, Lezgin, Kumyk, Lak, Dargin, Nogai; Juhuri and Hebrew for the Mountain Jewish side. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; it includes visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars**, to test how Shamil's imamate is kept as an ideal past.
- **Lezgins**, for a Soviet ideal poet and a later national ideal across the border.
- **Kumyks**, for lowland, Turkic-language ideals and the Mountain Republic era.
- **Laks**, to test the ideals of a town-based intelligentsia against the village ones.
- **Dargins of Kubachi**, where craft perfection may serve as an ideal of its own.
- **Nogais**, for a steppe people's heroes and their ideal of the good life.
- **Sufi murids and Salafi youth**, two rival ideals of the pure believer.
- **The Soviet-trained generation against those born after 1991**, for ideals kept or lost.
- **Mountain Jews**, for the ideal of return.
- **Russians and Cossacks**, for Orthodox and Soviet ideals in the north.
- **Diaspora descendants in Turkey and Jordan**, for an ideal homeland seen from afar.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literary and religious sources; mixed in memoirs; thin in measured values by people.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic; Avar, Lezgin, Kumyk, Lak; Hebrew and Juhuri; Turkish for émigrés.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** an ideal Islamic order is tied to the insurgency and to counter-extremism law; the Kadar villages; heroes revered by one people and resented by another. Present-day calls for a religious state are reported, never endorsed.
- **Held for review:** none.
