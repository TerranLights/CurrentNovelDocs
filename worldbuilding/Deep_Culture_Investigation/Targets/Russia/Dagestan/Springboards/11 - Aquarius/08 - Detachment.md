# Dagestan: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Arabic and Persian for the Sufi vocabulary; Avar, Dargin, Lak, Kumyk, Lezgin, Nogai, Azerbaijani. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes. Cultures value some forms of it (calm, objectivity, impartiality, equanimity) and condemn others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sufi murids**, for retreat and patience as spiritual training.
- **Salafi congregations**, for criticism of Sufi retreat and shrine visits.
- **Highland and Nogai herders**, for solitude built into pastoral work.
- **Avar and Dargin men and women**, to compare men's expected composure with women's laments.
- **Shia Azerbaijanis of Derbent**, to set public mourning rites beside any norm of restraint.
- **Laks and the urban intelligentsia**, for irony and ironic distance as a style.
- **Kumyk lowland towns**, for composure and public emotion in larger settlements.
- **Mountain Jews**, for their own mourning customs and distance kept as a minority.
- **Makhachkala's newcomers**, for urban anonymity after village closeness.
- **Labor migrants**, for long distance from home and family.
- **Elders left in emptying villages**, for solitude not chosen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Sufi and folklore sources; mixed on mourning; thin on measured emotional style.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic (essential for the Sufi texts); Persian terms; Russian; Avar, Lak, Dargin, Kumyk, Azerbaijani.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sufi-Salafi disputes over retreat and shrines; trauma after violence; mental illness and depression, which composure may hide. Impartiality as a duty of officials and judges goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
