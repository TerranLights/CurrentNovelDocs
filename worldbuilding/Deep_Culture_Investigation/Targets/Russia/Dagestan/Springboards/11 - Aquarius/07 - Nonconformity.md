# Dagestan: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (including youth slang); Arabic for religious dissent; Avar, Kumyk, Lezgin, Dargin, Lak, Azerbaijani. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avar highland villages**, where the abrek tradition and Salafi departures from the elders both have roots to test.
- **Makhachkala youth**, for art, music and street style against family expectation.
- **Kumyk towns (Khasavyurt, Buynaksk)**, for lowland youth and religious splits.
- **Derbent's Shia Azerbaijanis**, as a religious minority beside a Sunni majority.
- **Mountain Jews**, for a minority faith and the choices of those who stayed.
- **Christian converts and Russian Orthodox residents**, for faith outside the Muslim norm.
- **Women who live alone, divorce, or dress against family custom**, to see whether dress and marriage are the commonest public tests.
- **Laks and the urban intelligentsia**, to test whether secular professionals are allowed more room.
- **Nogais and the small southern peoples**, for how departure is judged in very small communities.
- **Returnees from study abroad**, for new religious norms brought home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in literary and religious-studies sources; thin and dangerous to gather on sexual and gender nonconformity.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic; Avar, Kumyk, Lezgin, Azerbaijani; English for recent anthropology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sexual and gender minorities face real danger (never name or locate people); apostasy; "extremism" listings; forced apologies; human-rights groups closed or under official designations. Any law on religion or "extremism" goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
