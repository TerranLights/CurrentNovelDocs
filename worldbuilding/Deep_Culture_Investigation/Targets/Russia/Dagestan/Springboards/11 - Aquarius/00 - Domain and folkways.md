# Dagestan: The domain: community and ideals (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak, Tabasaran, Nogai, Azerbaijani, Juhuri (Mountain Jews); Arabic (manuscripts, religion); Turkish (emigrant diaspora). **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups and what they hope for: friends, groups, hopes, humanitarian ideals, and the ways of settlement, migration, association and hospitality.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars of the highlands**, for village leagues set against the Khunzakh khanate's memory.
- **The Andi and Tsez (Dido) peoples**, where one village can be one language and one people.
- **Dargins, with Kubachi and Kaitag**, for craft villages beside a former ruler's domain.
- **Laks**, for bonds kept across long craft migration.
- **Lezgins**, for a people's ties split by the Azerbaijan border.
- **Tabasarans, Aghuls, Rutuls and Tsakhurs**, for small southern societies hidden behind larger neighbors.
- **Kumyks**, for lowland villages sharing land with resettled highlanders.
- **Nogais**, for steppe settlement with sparse neighbors.
- **Chechen-Akkins of Novolaksky and Khasavyurt**, for a community deported in 1944 and rebuilt on return.
- **Azerbaijanis of Derbent**, for an old urban community bound by Shia congregations.
- **Mountain Jews**, for a synagogue community shaped by emigration.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks around Kizlyar**, for stanitsa assemblies.
- **Makhachkala's mixed new districts**, for bonds formed among villagers of every people.
- **Diasporas in western Siberia, Turkey, Syria and Jordan**, for community kept abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Russian ethnography of the village; mixed for cities; thin in surveys by people.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands of the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic and the Arabic-script writing of local languages (*ajam*); Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak and the smaller languages; Azerbaijani; Juhuri; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "jamaat" also names armed cells in security usage; insurgency, the 1999 events, the "Wahhabi" label, land disputes, media under official designations. Customary law (adat) goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Library catalog: "Агларов сельская община" and "Хашаев общественный строй", then read their bibliographies.
2. Search scanned SMOMPK volumes for "гостеприимство", "годекан" and "помочь".
3. Journal search: "джамаат" and "тухум" in *История, археология и этнография Кавказа*.
4. Census tables: 2021 nationality of the Republic of Dagestan, then compare with 2010 to see category changes.
5. Ask the Tsadasa institute for a catalog of village chronicles and jamaat agreements.
