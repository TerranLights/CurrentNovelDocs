# Dagestan: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Kumyk, Lezgin, Dargin, Lak, Tabasaran, Nogai, Azerbaijani; Arabic for religious teaching on equal worth. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects. Which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Free highland communities against former khanate lands** (Khunzakh, Kazikumukh, the Tarki shamkhalate, the Kaitag utsmiate, the Derbent khanate), for equality among free men against rank under rulers.
- **Families once ranked as dependents or unfree**, to learn, from their own accounts, whether old labels still weigh on marriage and standing.
- **Kumyk princely and uzden families**, for lowland estate memory.
- **Tabasarans and Lezgins**, for southern free societies beside local rulers.
- **Women in highland villages, lowland towns and Makhachkala**, to see whether equality between the sexes is argued differently in each.
- **Aghuls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs and the Andi-Tsez peoples**, small peoples whose share in offices and census categories tests group equality.
- **Nogais**, for a steppe people's own view of its standing in the republic.
- **Russians and Cossacks**, for memories of Soviet leveling.
- **Mountain Jews**, for equal standing as a religious minority.
- **Labor migrants and their home villages**, for the gap between earnings abroad and at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in Soviet and post-Soviet writing on estates; official income data may miss informal earnings; gender data are argued over.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Arabic; Avar, Kumyk, Lezgin, Dargin, Lak, Tabasaran, Nogai, Azerbaijani.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent from unfree families (stigma; never name families); genital cutting and violence against women; the rights of sexual minorities; ethnic quota disputes. The estate rules in customary law and any law on equality go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
