# Dagestan: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak, Tabasaran, Chechen, Azerbaijani, Nogai; Arabic for religious teaching on upbringing. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **High Avar villages** and the **Andi, Tsez and Archi** communities: tiny-language settings where grandparents may teach a language the school does not.
- **Dargins**, including **Kubachi**, where a craft is taught from early age (*check*).
- **Laks**: whether men's work away from home changed who raised the children.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**: lullaby and child-rearing words in five Lezgic languages, and families split by the Azerbaijan border.
- **Kumyk** families, including former noble lines with fosterage customs (*check*).
- **Nogais** of the steppe: a Turkic, formerly nomadic setting for raising children, set against the mountain villages.
- **Akkin Chechens** of the Khasavyurt area: child-rearing in families marked by the 1944 deportation and return.
- **Derbent Azerbaijanis** and **Mountain Jews**: two urban traditions of raising children in one old city.
- **Russians and Cossacks** of the north (Kizlyar area): an Orthodox and Russian-language contrast in who raises the child.
- **Urban Makhachkala families** against **village families**; **children raised by grandparents** while parents work in Siberia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and ethnopedagogy (often idealized); mixed in surveys; observation studies thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the national languages for folklore originals; Arabic for manuscripts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** physical discipline and its limits; religious schooling under state scrutiny; boys' and girls' upbringing differing (a question to research, not to assume). Family-law questions go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
