# Dagestan: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Lak, Dargin, Lezgin, Kumyk, Azerbaijani, Chechen; Arabic for teaching on orphans. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland districts to test for emptying** (Tsumada, Tlyarata, Charoda, Kulin and others) against **plain suburbs**.
- **Avars** and the **Andi and Tsez** villages: small communities most exposed to emptying (*check*).
- **Laks**: men's work away from home as a test case for "left behind" (*check* its history).
- **Dargins** (Kubachi craftsmen working away, *check*; Kaitag villages).
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**: southern out-migration and the Azerbaijan border.
- **Akkin Chechens** and the **Laks of Novolak**: forced leaving and pending resettlement.
- **Kumyks** and **Nogais**.
- **Russians and Mountain Jews**: communities whose numbers in Dagestan fell after the 1990s (*check*), and those who stayed.
- **Divorced women** and **children of divorce** across peoples.
- **Families of migrants** in Western Siberia and Moscow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** official child-care statistics exist; depopulation measurable; feelings of abandonment found mostly in folklore, literature and the press.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Lak, Lezgin for laments and tales; Chechen for the Aukh memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child custody after divorce; babies given up; deportation memory; stigma on divorced women and on families who place elders. Name variation; do not cast any people as abandoning or steadfast.
- **Held for review:** none.
