# Dagestan: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Lezgin, Kumyk, Dargin, Lak, Chechen, Azerbaijani; Arabic and Persian loanwords for travelers' lodging. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khasavyurt** and the **Akkin Chechen** villages: the first place to check for families who took in the displaced of the Chechen wars.
- **Laks of Novolak**: a community whose own resettlement is still pending.
- **Avar, Dargin and Lak highland villages**: guest customs in places with few roads.
- **Shepherd families** on the kutans and summer pastures.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Rutuls, Aguls, Tsakhurs**: southern hospitality customs to separate, and kin across the Azerbaijan border.
- **Kumyks** and **Nogais** of the plain and steppe.
- **Derbent** (Azerbaijanis, Mountain Jews, Tats): urban charity and the old road.
- **Russians and Cossacks** of the north.
- **Makhachkala's poor and newly arrived**: the city's shelter gap, if there is one.
- **Women and LGBT people seeking refuge** outside the family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the displacement years in rights reports; thin for homelessness counts; hospitality well covered in idealized ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Chechen and Avar for the Khasavyurt area; Azerbaijani for Derbent; English for international reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** displacement and war; Memorial's legal status in Russia; people fleeing families; asylum law goes to Libra. Hospitality must not be written up as a national trait; it is a practice to research, varying by people and place.
- **Held for review:** none.
