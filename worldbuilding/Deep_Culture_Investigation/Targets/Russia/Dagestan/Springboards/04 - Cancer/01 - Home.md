# Dagestan: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak, Tabasaran, Chechen, Azerbaijani, Nogai, Juhuri. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stone villages of the high Avar, Dargin and Lak districts** against **villages rebuilt on the plain** after resettlement: same people, two kinds of home.
- **Kubachi** and **Balkhar** (craft villages): home as a workshop.
- **Makhachkala, Kaspiysk, Khasavyurt**: apartments against self-built suburbs.
- **Derbent's old quarters**: Azerbaijani, Mountain Jewish and Tat homes.
- **Lezgin, Tabasaran, Rutul, Agul and Tsakhur villages** of the south: separate building traditions to check.
- **Kumyk** lowland villages and the **Nogai** steppe: houses on flat land, once mobile dwellings.
- **Akkin Chechens**: homes lost in 1944 and the return.
- **Terek Cossack** settlements around Kizlyar.
- **Kutan dwellers** and seasonal herders.
- **Migrants in Western Siberia** who build a house "back home".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for architecture and migration in Russian; mixed for attitudes; thin in English beyond travel writing.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Lak and Lezgin for village memory; Azerbaijani and Juhuri for Derbent.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land seizures, demolitions and the Novolak question are live disputes; property law belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here. Women's household seclusion and the privacy of the home are questions to research, not assume.
- **Held for review:** none.
