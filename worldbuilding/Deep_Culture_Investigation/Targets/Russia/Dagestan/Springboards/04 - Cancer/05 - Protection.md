## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **High Avar and Dargin villages** with tower traditions against **lowland Kumyk** villages in open country.
- **Derbent**: an old walled city, with Azerbaijani, Mountain Jewish and Tat memories of protection and danger.
- **Laks** and **Lezgins**: different heroic traditions to set side by side.
- **Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**: border villages, some divided by the state line.
- **Akkin Chechens** and the **Laks of Novolak**: two peoples with a shared, disputed homeland.
- **Nogais** and **Cossacks** of the north: steppe and frontier settings.
- **Shepherds** on the kutans.
- **Women** across peoples: protected by kin, by NGOs, by the state; voices to separate.
- **Urban** families relying on the state against **village** families relying on kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and architecture; mixed in modern safety data; NGO material uneven and contested.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Lezgin, Lak for epics; Arabic for amulet texts; Azerbaijani for Derbent.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1999 incursion and later counterterrorism operations; feuds; harm to women and girls justified as honor; security services' records are closed. Customary law and feud law go to Libra and Capricorn. Never one voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
