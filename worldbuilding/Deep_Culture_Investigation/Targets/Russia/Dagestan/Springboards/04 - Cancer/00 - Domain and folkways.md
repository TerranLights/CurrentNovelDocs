# Dagestan: The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak, Tabasaran, Chechen, Nogai, Azerbaijani, Juhuri (the Mountain Jews' Iranian-language speech), Rutul, Agul, Tsakhur, the Andi and Tsez languages, Archi; Arabic for the pre-modern record. **Concept in one line:** what is home, and who is family? ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avar highlands** (Khunzakh, Gunib, Botlikh), with the **Andi and Tsez peoples and the Archi** kept apart: counted as Avars, possibly different households.
- **Dargins**, with **Kubachi** and **Kaitag** apart: strong local identities.
- **Laks** of Kumukh, and the Laks moved to the Novolak district in 1944: one people in two settings.
- **Lezgins** and the southern **Tabasaran, Agul, Rutul and Tsakhur**: each its own language.
- **Kumyk** plain and **Nogai** steppe: lowland and once-mobile ways.
- **Akkin Chechens** (Khasavyurt, Novolak): own kin system; deportation and return.
- **Derbent**: Azerbaijanis, Mountain Jews, Tats.
- **Russians and Terek Cossacks** (Kizlyar, Tarumovka): another house tradition.
- **Makhachkala's new suburbs** against **mountain villages** and **winter-pasture (kutan) settlements**.
- **Migrant and diaspora households**: Western Siberia, Moscow, Turkey, northern Azerbaijan (Avars, Lezgins).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Russian-language ethnography; mixed in statistics (republic figures easy, people-level ones harder); thin in English.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak; Arabic for manuscripts; Chechen, Azerbaijani, Nogai, Juhuri.
- **Lands:** the tracker carries "Russia" as the pre-war lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the Republic of Dagestan's lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 deportation and the Lak resettlement onto Chechen land (live disputes); village land quarrels; violence in the home; the census grouping of small peoples. Never one voice for "the Dagestanis".
- **Held for review:** none.
