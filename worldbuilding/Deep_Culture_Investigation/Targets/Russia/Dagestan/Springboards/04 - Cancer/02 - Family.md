*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Dagestan, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Avar, Dargin, Kumyk, Lezgin, Lak, Chechen, Azerbaijani, Nogai, Juhuri; the Andi, Tsez and Archi languages; Arabic for genealogies. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Avars** of the old free communities against the **former Avar Khanate** lands: kin under self-rule and under a ruling house.
- **Andi, Tsez and Archi** villages: small endogamous communities to check against the large peoples.
- **Dargins**, with **Kaitag** (a former ruler's domain) and **Kubachi** (a craft village) apart.
- **Laks**: check how men's seasonal craft migration shaped households.
- **Lezgins, Tabasarans, Aguls, Rutuls, Tsakhurs**: kin terms and groups to compare across the south; Lezgin and Tsakhur families split by the Azerbaijan border.
- **Kumyks** and **Nogais**: Turkic-language peoples; estate ranks and clan names to check.
- **Akkin Chechens**: *teip* terms to set beside the tukhum.
- **Derbent Azerbaijanis** and **Mountain Jews**.
- **Russians and Cossacks**.
- **Mixed urban families** in Makhachkala: marriages across peoples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich ethnography and linguistics; statistics mixed; attitude surveys thin and scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the main national languages; Arabic for genealogies; English and German for the linguistic grammars.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** kin networks and nepotism are politically charged; cousin marriage and endogamy carry stigma in outside media; family law (marriage, divorce, custody) goes to Libra and Capricorn. Mixed and interfaith marriages deserve care.
- **Held for review:** none.
