*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays:** on how worth was kept in exile, compare those who lived through exile, their children, and those born after 1957.
- **Circassians:** a written ethic of humaneness; compare princely and commoner lineages on what treatment each claims as due.
- **Abazins:** a small people's children learning conduct partly through Circassian and Russian; check whether their readers differ.
- **Nogais:** check how a former steppe people's norms of respect for elders took shape in settled villages.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** repression of Cossacks gives a second history of dignity lost and restored.
- **Labor migrants and the old:** dignity at work far away, and in old age at home or in care.
- **Disabled people and their families:** care in the family against state services.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Deportation testimony and conduct books are plentiful; statistics on treatment and care are thin and may not split by people.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess; Arabic for Qur'anic commentary; Kazakh or Kyrgyz archives for exile records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation testimony is personal and painful; Memorial and SOVA carry legal labels in Russia, so access and citation may be restricted; the legal side of rehabilitation goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
