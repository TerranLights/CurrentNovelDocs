## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Circassians and Abazins:** the expulsion and exile give a separate collective memory; compare homeland and Ottoman-descended families on how it is told.
- **Karachays:** the 1828 annexation, then the deportation; check which of these is spoken of as a lowering and which as a trial endured.
- **Young men:** army service, sport and street quarrels, where many of the pointers cluster.
- **Young women and their families:** reputation, abduction and gossip, a separate field with its own risks.
- **Nogais:** a people who lost a steppe polity; check whether that loss is framed in these terms at all.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** the circle's punishments and the de-Cossackization years.
- **Diaspora descendants in Turkey and Jordan:** exile memory told at a distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. History and folklore are rich; data on everyday shaming are thin and mostly from rights groups.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the four native languages for insult lore; Ottoman Turkish for refugee records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** women's reputation and abduction cases put informants at risk; never name private persons; blood feud and reconciliation have a legal side for Libra or Capricorn; Circassian genocide claims are contested in Russia.
- **Held for review:** none.
