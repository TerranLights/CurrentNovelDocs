## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Circassians:** the guest house and the *кунак* bond are well documented; compare old princely families and commoner villages on who hosted whom.
- **Karachays:** pastoral giving (livestock, meat) and post-return rebuilding; check how mutual aid in resettled villages is remembered.
- **Abazins:** shared village feasts in a small, often bilingual community.
- **Nogais:** steppe guest customs that may differ in form from mountain ones.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** Orthodox parish charity and stanitsa mutual aid.
- **Pious and secular households:** zakat and sadaqa against Soviet-era and civic forms of help.
- **Migrants in Moscow and abroad:** money sent home, and patrons who build in their villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on hospitality and wedding exchange in ethnography; thin on measured giving at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian-Cherkess and Karachay-Balkar; Arabic terms; English for Bell.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** money flows through religious bodies may draw official suspicion; bride-wealth sums are private family matters; patrons may be political figures.
- **Held for review:** none.
