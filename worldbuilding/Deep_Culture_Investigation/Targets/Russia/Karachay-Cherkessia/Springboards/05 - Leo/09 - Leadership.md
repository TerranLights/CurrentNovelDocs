## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays:** princely lineages abolished, then rebuilt communities after 1957; check which leaders the return produced.
- **Circassians:** the old princely and noble order and the *хасэ*; compare descendants of each rank today.
- **Abazins:** leaders of a small people who must work through larger coalitions.
- **Nogais:** clan and *мырза* memory in a single district.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** elected atamans and the *круг*, against appointed officials.
- **Women:** the *гуащэ* tradition and women in today's parliament and business.
- **Religious against secular leaders**, and older imams against younger preachers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. History of princely orders is rich; current leadership is reachable through press and office lists but sensitive.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian-Cherkess and Karachay-Balkar; Arabic for Jordan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic sharing of top posts and the 1999 and 2004 crises are politically charged; formal authority and command belong to Capricorn, the law of offices to Libra; avoid informants' names in clan matters.
- **Held for review:** none.
