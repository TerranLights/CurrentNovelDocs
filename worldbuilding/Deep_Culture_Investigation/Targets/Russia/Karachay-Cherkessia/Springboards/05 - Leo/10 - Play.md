## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Circassians:** ritual and seasonal games are well documented; compare those records with current village and urban play.
- **Karachays:** pastoral play (riding, knucklebones, mountain games); compare high villages with lowland settlements.
- **Abazins:** children's games in a bilingual setting; check what is shared with Circassian children and what is not.
- **Nogais:** steppe racing and wrestling forms.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** Cossack skill play and Russian children's games in the stanitsas.
- **Girls and boys:** map play spaces and permitted games separately for each, at each age.
- **Observant and secular families:** on music, games and dance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Circassian ritual games; mixed on Karachay; thin on Abaza and Nogai, and on adult play today.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the four native languages for game names and rhymes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling is legally restricted and partly hidden; the law side goes to Libra or Capricorn; filming children needs consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
