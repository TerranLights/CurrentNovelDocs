## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays:** old seasonal festivals and the cradle feast; compare villages that kept them with those resettled after 1957.
- **Circassians:** compare joy as staged by formal ensembles with joy at village weddings and dzhegu evenings.
- **Abazins:** festive song in a small language community; check what is shared with Circassian neighbors.
- **Nogais:** steppe-derived festivals and songs.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** the Orthodox festival calendar.
- **Observant and secular households:** compare joy at the Eids with joy at civic and family feasts with music.
- **The old and the young:** check whether elders and youth name different occasions as their happiest.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on festivals and music; thin on measured happiness at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the four native languages for songs and festival names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** music and dance at weddings are a live religious dispute in some families; pre-Islamic festivals can be politically and religiously charged; filming dancers needs consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
