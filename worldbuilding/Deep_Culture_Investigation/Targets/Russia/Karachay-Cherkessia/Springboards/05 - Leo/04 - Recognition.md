## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Circassians:** a strong feast-ranking tradition led by the *тхьэмадэ*; compare formal village feasts with urban banquets.
- **Karachays:** the *тамата*'s role and lineage seniority; check how a rehabilitated people frames its demands for acknowledgment.
- **Abazins and Nogais:** recognition as peoples, through districts, press and schools in their language.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** state and Cossack honor systems, distinct from feast-based standing.
- **Religious figures:** *хаджи* and *эфенди* as earned titles of address, against secular credentials.
- **Women:** check whose work is named at feasts and in obituaries, and whose is left unnamed.
- **Diaspora:** recognition campaigns abroad and their reception at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Honors lists and press are reachable; feast etiquette is well described in ethnography; survey data on feeling recognized is near absent.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian-Cherkess and Karachay-Balkar for songs and press; Abaza and Nogai press for the smaller peoples.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recognition of the deportation and of the Circassian expulsion is politically charged; Dissernet findings name living people; anything on the legal side of rehabilitation goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
