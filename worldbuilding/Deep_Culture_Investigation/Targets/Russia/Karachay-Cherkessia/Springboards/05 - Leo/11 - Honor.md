*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Circassians:** a written noble code and its spread to commoners; compare descendants of nobles and commoners today.
- **Karachays:** *намыс* set beside lineage (*тукъум*) ties; check how deportation-era stories shape talk of honor.
- **Abazins:** a code possibly shared with the Abkhaz rather than the Circassians; test the line.
- **Nogais:** steppe heroic ideals in a settled district.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** regimental and Orthodox honor.
- **Women:** honor rules applied to women and by women; study through women's own accounts, never assumed.
- **Young men in the city:** sport, street and online "respect", against village norms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in ethnography and folklore; thin and sensitive on current practice.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian-Cherkess and Karachay-Balkar; Abaza and Abkhaz sources; Turkish for diaspora handbooks.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blood feud, its settlement and violence tied to family honor are sensitive, and their legal side goes to Libra or Capricorn; family-honor cases put women at risk, so keep informants anonymous; the deportation of a people with soldiers at the front is painful memory.
- **Held for review:** none.
