## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: clan (тукъум) networks and uzden ethics as the frame for whom to rely on.
- **Circassians**: guest-friendship, fosterage and the Circassian ethical code.
- **Abazins**: a small community where everyone may be known; trust may run differently.
- **Nogais**: steppe kin divisions and the Edige tradition of loyalty to a lord.
- **Cossacks and Russians**: godparent kinship and stanitsa mutual aid.
- **Cherkessk residents of mixed origin**: trust across ethnic lines in an urban setting.
- **Returnees and their descendants**: whether trust in the state differs among families marked by deportation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on kunak and fosterage as historical forms; thin on measured trust at republic level; polling may not split by people.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for polls and scholarship; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for honor and guest vocabulary; Nogai for the Edige epic; Abaza for clan studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic trust is politically charged; election trust touches on fraud claims; informants may be wary of naming clans.
- **Held for review:** none.
