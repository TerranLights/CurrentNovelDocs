## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper-valley Karachay herders**: control of pasture and livestock against reserve rules and state farms.
- **Circassian villages**: etiquette of restraint as a form of self- and social control.
- **Abazins**: a small people's household rules; check whether they follow Circassian patterns or their own.
- **Nogais of the plain**: steppe water and grazing control, different from the mountain cases.
- **Cossack stanitsas**: Cossack self-policing and its relations with the state police.
- **Young women in Cherkessk and Karachayevsk**: family control over study, work and marriage.
- **Believers under security watch**: state control over religious life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Water, reserve and security control are documented; household and gender control rests on thin, often outside sources; little measured data at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for state, security and press sources; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for household etiquette and sayings on fate; Abaza and Nogai for the two smaller peoples' customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** control of women and of believers is sensitive and may endanger informants; security-regime data is partial.
- **Held for review:** none.
