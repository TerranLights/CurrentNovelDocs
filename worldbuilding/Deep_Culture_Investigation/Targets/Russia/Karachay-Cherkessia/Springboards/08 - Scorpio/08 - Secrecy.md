# Karachay-Cherkessia: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay households**: avoidance speech and what a bride may not say or ask.
- **Circassian hunters and families**: a secret speech of the hunt and an etiquette of reticence.
- **Abazins**: a small community where secrets may be harder to keep; check their own forms.
- **Nogais**: steppe kin and the gossip of a compact district.
- **Soviet-era believers** of all titular peoples: faith practiced in hiding.
- **Cossack and Russian families**: what was hidden about decossackized relatives.
- **Officials and journalists in Cherkessk**: what the state keeps closed and what leaks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Avoidance speech and hidden Soviet religion are studied; family secrets and privacy attitudes have little data; state archives are partly closed.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Arabic for manuscripts; French for older travel accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restricted rites and sacred places should be recorded only as communities allow; archive access may be blocked; anonymous channels may expose people.
- **Held for review:** none.
