# Karachay-Cherkessia: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza, Nogai. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong; forgiveness and letting go are its alternatives.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: feud and reconciliation within and between тукъумs; the role of маслагъат.
- **Circassians**: princely and noble feud traditions and the blood-price custom.
- **Abazins**: feud customs of a small people bordering Circassian and Karachay lands.
- **Nogais**: steppe revenge epics and blood-price words of Kypchak origin.
- **Kuban Cossacks**: frontier raiding memory, revenge seen from the other side of the line.
- **Young men in towns**: street retaliation and its links to older honor talk, as a question.
- **Families of insurgents and of police**: reprisal at group scale in the 2000s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Historical feud material is rich; current feuds are reported case by case and seldom counted; Abaza and Nogai sources are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for press and historiography; Kabardian-Cherkess for the Nart edition and feud terms; Karachay-Balkar for heroic songs; Nogai for the Karasay-Kazy epic; Abaza for village ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** live feuds endanger families; insurgency and reprisal are security topics; avoid naming private families from the press in public notes.
- **Held for review:** none. Private vengeance against the state's punishment goes to Capricorn; blood price as compensation goes to Libra.
