## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: the whole people removed in 1943; purging as collective punishment, not only of officials.
- **Circassians and Abazins**: expulsion memory from the 1860s rather than from Soviet deportation.
- **Kuban Cossacks**: a purged estate whose descendants now revive it.
- **Nogais**: steppe herders hit by collectivization; their purge record may be thin and should be searched apart.
- **Muslim believers under suspicion today**, across all titular peoples: screening and registration.
- **Officials and the republic's elite**: anti-corruption arrests as a modern housecleaning.
- **Russian settlers sent to the Klukhori lands**: those who moved in after the clearing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 1930s and 1943 (published documents, memory books); mixed for folk cleansing; current screening of believers is reported mainly by rights groups and independent media.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Georgian for Klukhori records; Turkish for Muhajir memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation and the 1860s expulsions are grievance topics; Memorial was liquidated in Russia, and its sites may be reachable only from abroad; counter-extremism data touches living people.
- **Held for review:** none.
