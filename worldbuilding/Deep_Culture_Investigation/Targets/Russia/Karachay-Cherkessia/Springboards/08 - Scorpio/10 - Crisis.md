## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: the deportation as the crisis that marks all later ones; 1999 as a contest for the top post.
- **Circassians and Abazins**: the 1999 standoff seen from the other side, and fears of being outnumbered.
- **Nogais**: steppe-district hazards (drought, floods on the plain) unlike the mountain ones.
- **Cossacks and Russians**: the 1990s calls for a separate Cossack unit, and the civil war of 1918-1920.
- **Mountain-resort workers**: avalanches, rescues and tourism shocks.
- **Labor migrants and their families**: economic crisis met by leaving.
- **Young men**: the insurgency years as a crisis of a generation, a question to research.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1999, 2004 and natural hazards; mixed for personal and economic crisis; thin for mental-health crisis by people.
