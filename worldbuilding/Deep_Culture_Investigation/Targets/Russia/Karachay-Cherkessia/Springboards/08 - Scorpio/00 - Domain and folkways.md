# Karachay-Cherkessia: Domain and folkways (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar (Karachay dialects); Kabardian-Cherkess (the republic's Circassian standard); Abaza; Nogai. **Concept in one line:** the domain of death and transformation: how death, power and the hidden are faced, plus the folkways of death, sex, inheritance and shared resources.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays** of the upper Kuban, Teberda and Mara valleys against those of the Malokarachayevsky foothills: herding commons and death memory shaped by the 1943-1957 exile.
- **Circassians** (Besleney and Kabardian-speaking villages, Khabezsky and Adyge-Khablsky districts): a distinct code of etiquette around mourning and marriage.
- **Abazins** (Abazinsky district, Inzhich-Chukun, Psyzh, Elburgan): a small Abkhaz-related people whose folkways risk being merged into the Circassian file.
- **Nogais** (Nogaysky district): Kypchak steppe heritage, different marriage and kin patterns.
- **Russians and Kuban Cossacks** (Zelenchukskaya, Storozhevaya, Pregradnaya, Urupsky): Orthodox funerals and stanitsa property customs.
- **Smaller groups** (Ossetians, Armenians, Greeks, Tatars; *check* which are present) and **Cherkessk** as an urban mixed setting.
- **Diasporas:** Karachays left in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan after exile; Muhajir descendants in Turkey and the Levant.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on wedding and funeral ethnography and on the deportation; thin on sex ways and household money, which are rarely written up; Abaza and Nogai material is scarce.
- **Lands:** the tracker header lists only "Russia" as pre-war lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet and the concept sheets are written for the core lands of the present republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza and Nogai for the press and folklore; Ottoman and modern Turkish for diaspora sources; Arabic for older epitaphs and religious texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1943 deportation and Caucasian War deaths are live grievances; sex and bride abduction are delicate; family honor limits talk of inheritance quarrels.
- **Held for review:** none.
