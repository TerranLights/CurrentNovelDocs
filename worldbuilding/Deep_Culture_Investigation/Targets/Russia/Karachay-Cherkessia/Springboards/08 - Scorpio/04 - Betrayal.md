## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: a people charged with betrayal as a whole; how the charge itself is felt as the state's betrayal.
- **Circassians**: Nart and Andemirkan stories of treachery by lords and kin.
- **Abazins**: Abaza Nart variants may carry their own betrayal episodes.
- **Kuban Cossacks**: Red-White civil war switches of side and the 1945 handover.
- **Nogais**: steppe loyalties between mirzas and khans; check what survives in Nogai song.
- **Families split by Soviet informing**, across all peoples.
- **Mixed marriages in Cherkessk**: infidelity and loyalty across ethnic lines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the collaboration charge and on Nart material; thin on infidelity and on present-day informing.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; German for occupation records; Turkish for the diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the collaboration charge is a deep grievance and still argued in the press; informer files are mostly closed; infidelity touches family honor.
- **Held for review:** none.
