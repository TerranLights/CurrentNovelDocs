## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay herding families:** merit shown with flocks and horses, honored in Soviet medals; compare with town careers.
- **Circassians:** whether valor (*лӀыгъэ*) still works as a measure, and how it carries into schooling and office today.
- **Abazins:** a people whose deeds are often filed under Circassian song; look for their own singers and contests.
- **Nogais:** how the Edige tradition is retold now, and what it is used to praise in village life.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** Cossack decorations and service records as a separate merit system.
- **State employees of each people:** how open competitions and kin ties are talked about in each group.
- **Diaspora descendants:** Mamluk and Ottoman careers told as family memory, and how the story is used.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in song and award records; mixed in sociology of patronage; national survey data only.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar, Kabardino-Cherkess, Nogai and Abaza for songs and epics; Arabic and Ottoman Turkish for Mamluk sources; German for Klaproth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption claims about named officials; ethnic shares of honors; descent claims tied to Mamluk lines.
- **Held for review:** none.
