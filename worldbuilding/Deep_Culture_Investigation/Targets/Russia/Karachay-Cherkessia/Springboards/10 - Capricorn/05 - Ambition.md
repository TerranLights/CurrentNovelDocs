## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay families in mountain auls and in Cherkessk and Moscow:** leaving to rise against staying to build at home.
- **Circassians of the villages and of Cherkessk:** education and state posts as the route up; compare women's and men's paths.
- **Abazins:** a small people where each rising career may be read as the people's own; check how that weighs on the person.
- **Nogais:** pastoral families and the road to schooling and the city.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** whether leaving the republic reads as ambition or as loss in their own accounts.
- **Young men in sport:** wrestling and martial arts as a career, with family and village stakes.
- **Diaspora returnees from Turkey, Syria and Jordan:** ambitions brought back, and what they meet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good official data on education and migration, rich epic and tale material; little on aspirations asked directly.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardino-Cherkess for epic and tale; Abaza; Arabic and Turkish for diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan and ethnic patronage in hiring and office; exam-score disputes; resort land conflicts.
- **Held for review:** none.
