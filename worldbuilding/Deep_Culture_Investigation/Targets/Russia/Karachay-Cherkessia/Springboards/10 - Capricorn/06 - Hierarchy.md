## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay lineages descended from princely, noble and dependent houses:** whether old rank still shapes marriage and standing.
- **Circassian villages of Kabardian and Besleney origin:** different noble systems that may have left different memories.
- **Abazin villages:** their own princely families and dependents, rarely studied apart from Circassian ones.
- **Nogai villages:** mirza descent against commoner descent after settling.
- **Cossack stanitsas:** the line between Cossacks and non-Cossack settlers (*иногородние*), to trace from records to memory.
- **The five titular peoples as groups:** the share of top posts each holds, a ladder between peoples.
- **Seniority inside families:** elder and younger siblings, brides and mothers-in-law; compare across all peoples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 19th-century estate records and ethnography; mixed for present practice; Gini and post lists are open.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar, Kabardino-Cherkess, Abaza and Nogai for the estate terms; Polish for Łapiński's original.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent from dependent estates may still be a sore subject; the ethnic balance of posts is contested; genealogies may be private.
- **Held for review:** none.
