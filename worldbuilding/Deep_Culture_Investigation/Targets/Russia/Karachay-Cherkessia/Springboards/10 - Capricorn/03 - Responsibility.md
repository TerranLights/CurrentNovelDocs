## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay lineages (*тукъум*):** whether kin still stand surety for a member's wrong, varying by valley and by city or village.
- **Circassians:** whether loss of face (*напэ*) works as the sanction for failure; compare urban Cherkessk families with Khabez villages.
- **Abazins:** guarantee and compensation customs recorded under Circassian names; separate their own terms.
- **Nogais:** which herding tasks children are trusted with, and at what age; compare with mountain shepherd camps.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** imperial and Soviet ideas of personal guilt before the court, to set beside kin answerability.
- **Generation of the exile:** people who grew up under collective blame, against their grandchildren.
- **Mountain-tourism workers (guides, rescuers, resort staff):** a new arena where blame is assigned to persons and firms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in customary-law sources and ethnography; mixed for modern practice; measured data mostly Russia-wide.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardino-Cherkess for oral accounts; Abaza; Ottoman and Arabic only for older religious texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blood feuds in progress, family punishment of insurgents' kin, deportation trauma and genocide claims; access to Memorial's materials may be limited in Russia.
- **Held for review:** none.
