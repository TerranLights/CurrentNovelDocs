# Karachay-Cherkessia: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardino-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it. Capricorn takes the spirit of the law; the letter goes to Libra.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay villages:** where *тёре* memory, Islamic arbitration and state courts meet; compare old auls with plains settlements.
- **Circassian villages:** whether *хабзэ* is spoken of as outranking or complementing the statute; check by generation.
- **Abazins:** customary rulings recorded under Circassian headings; recover Abaza terms and cases.
- **Nogais:** steppe custom and Islamic law in small communities far from the capital.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** state law as one's own, or Cossack custom beside it; patrols as a test case.
- **Observant young Muslims:** whether sharia is spoken of as a higher law, and how state "extremism" rules meet that.
- **Former deportees and their children:** how the commandant's regime is remembered, and what trust in law followed rehabilitation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in 19th-century legal ethnography; mixed for present practice; trust data national only.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardino-Cherkess for oral codes; Arabic for Islamic legal texts; Abaza and Nogai for village cases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "extremism" prosecutions of Muslims; police abuse claims; deportation-era records may be partly closed. Letter-of-the-law questions belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
