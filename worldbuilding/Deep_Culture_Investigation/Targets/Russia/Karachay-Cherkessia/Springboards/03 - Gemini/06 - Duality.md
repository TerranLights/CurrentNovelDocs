## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays and Circassians as the two named peoples:** the pairing in the republic's name sets them as halves; ask how each speaks of the other half.
- **Abazins and Nogais:** peoples left out of the name, for whom two-ness may read as exclusion.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** a large population that is neither of the titular pair, with its own Kuban and Stavropol tie.
- **Circassian repatriates:** two homelands held in one life.
- **Karachays with relatives in Central Asia:** an exile-born second home.
- **Muslim families living near Alan Christian monuments:** two religious pasts in one landscape.
- **Urban bilinguals and rural speakers:** who switches languages in a day and who rarely needs to.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; political splits and bilingualism likely well documented; folk ideas of paired opposites and twin beliefs thin and scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza, Nogai; Turkish and Arabic for repatriate sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inter-ethnic rivalry over the paired name; the wartime "collaboration" charge behind the 1943 deportation (record it as charged and contested); reformist and older Islamic layers, which security services monitor; customary and Islamic norms as rules belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
