## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay lineage elders against Circassian village elders:** whether a go-between is chosen by lineage or by village standing.
- **Abazins:** often bilingual, so they may serve as brokers between larger neighbors.
- **Nogais:** a steppe tradition of envoys and in-law ties.
- **Russians and Cossack atamans:** a Cossack society as its own channel to the authorities.
- **Religious figures and secular councils:** two kinds of third party.
- **Young people in schools:** formal mediation training against family-led settling.
- **Women:** whether and how they step between quarreling men or families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; historical envoys and matchmaking likely well described; living mediation practice and its outcomes thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza, Nogai; Greek or Latin editions for Byzantine letters; Ottoman Turkish for some envoy records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inter-ethnic disputes and election history; blood-feud settlement and customary or Islamic courts are rule questions for Libra (what the rules say) and Capricorn (legal culture); disputes in named families are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
