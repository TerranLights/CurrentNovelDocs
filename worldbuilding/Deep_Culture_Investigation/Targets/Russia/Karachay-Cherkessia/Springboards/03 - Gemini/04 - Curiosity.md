## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The SAO staff settlement at Nizhny Arkhyz:** a community of scientists, many from elsewhere, set beside Karachay and Russian neighbors.
- **Guides, drivers and guesthouse hosts in Dombay, Teberda and Arkhyz:** the people who meet outsiders' questions daily.
- **Circassian and Karachay readers of the Alan past:** rival origin claims may shape what each finds worth digging into.
- **Cossack stanitsa museums and local historians:** an inquiry into the stanitsas' own past.
- **Abaza and Nogai heritage seekers, including diaspora visitors:** small peoples searching for records of themselves.
- **Children in Cherkessk and children in the auls:** access to technoparks and museums against learning by roaming the valleys.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; institutions and tourism likely well documented; everyday norms on prying questions thin, a fieldwork matter.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza, Nogai; German and Arabic for older outside sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** origin and ethnogenesis debates (Alan heritage) are politically charged; DNA results can feed them; illegal digging; sacred sites, so ask before entering or photographing.
- **Held for review:** none.
