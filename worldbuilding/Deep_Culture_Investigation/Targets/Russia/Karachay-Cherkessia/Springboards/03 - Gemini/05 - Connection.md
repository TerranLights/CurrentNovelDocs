## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay lineage networks:** ties organized by тукъум, including families split between the republic and Central Asia.
- **Circassian villages tied to the diaspora:** relatives in Turkey, Jordan and Syria, and the ones who came back.
- **Abazins:** links across the ridge to Abkhazia as a second center of ties.
- **Nogais:** ties running east to Nogai lands in Dagestan and Stavropol Krai rather than to the mountains.
- **Cossacks and Russians:** host ties through Kuban and Stavropol, not through lineage.
- **Young men working away:** connection kept at a distance.
- **Elders living alone in emptied upland villages:** whether isolation is named, and who checks on them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; diaspora and lineage organizations likely visible online; network data and loneliness figures thin for the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza, Nogai; Turkish and Arabic for the diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lineage ties and public office (nepotism claims) are politically charged; diaspora bodies can draw security attention; wedding and funeral records are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
