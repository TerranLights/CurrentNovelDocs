## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cherkessk readers against upland villagers** (Uchkulan, Kart-Dzhurt, Teberda): phone feeds against news carried by kin, mosque and neighbors.
- **Circassians who follow Nalchik and Maykop media:** news flowing in from other Adyghe republics and the diaspora press in Turkey.
- **Karachays following Balkar outlets in Kabardino-Balkaria:** one language, two republics' news.
- **Nogais of Nogaysky district:** links to Nogai media in Dagestan and Stavropol Krai.
- **Abazins:** a very small media base, so whether news in their own language reaches them at all.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Zelenchuksky and Urupsky districts:** reliance on federal and Stavropol or Krasnodar regional channels.
- **Generations:** television-first elders against Telegram-first youth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; official statistics and historical documents likely rich; region-level trust and news-use data likely thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza, Nogai; Turkish for diaspora outlets.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "foreign agent" labels and blocked sites (note the label and how a researcher reaches the site, not as a verdict); security-service operations; deportation files; genealogies are family property, so ask before use. The rules on media themselves belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
