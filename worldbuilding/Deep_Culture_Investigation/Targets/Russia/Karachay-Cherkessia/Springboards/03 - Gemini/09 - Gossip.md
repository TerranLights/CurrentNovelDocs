## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small upland auls against Cherkessk:** where everyone is known by lineage, talk may travel and bind differently than in a city of strangers.
- **Men's and women's gathering places:** separate circuits of talk, and the topics each keeps.
- **Abaza villages:** a small people, where gossip may cross into Circassian and Karachay circles through marriage.
- **Nogai villages of the steppe district:** a different settlement pattern and its own sayings on talk.
- **Cossack stanitsas:** the Russian village tradition of neighbor talk.
- **Young people online:** anonymous pages and comment threads against elders' face-to-face talk.
- **Diaspora links:** gossip crossing between the republic and Turkey or Central Asia through family chats.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed; folklore and religious teaching likely available; everyday gossip itself is a fieldwork matter and rarely written down.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza, Nogai; Arabic terms in religious texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gossip about women's conduct can bring real harm, so never record names; online pages name real people; rumors around inter-ethnic incidents; "fakes" laws are for Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
