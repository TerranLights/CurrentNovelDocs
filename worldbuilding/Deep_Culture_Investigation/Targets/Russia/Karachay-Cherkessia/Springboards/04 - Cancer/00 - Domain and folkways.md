# Karachay-Cherkessia: Domain and folkways (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardino-Cherkess (the Besleney and Kabardian speech of the republic's Circassians); Abaza; Nogai. **Concept in one line:** the domain of home and family: what home is, and who counts as family (household forms, roots, family memory, hospitality at home, building ways, family ways, child-rearing ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays, gorge by gorge** (Teberda, Uchkulan and the upper Kuban, Khurzuk, Kardonik, Mara): household and lineage ways may differ between high auls and lowland resettlement villages.
- **Circassians** of the Khabez and Adyge-Khabl districts, Besleney and Kabardian villages kept apart.
- **Abazins**, separating Tapanta and Ashkharua speech communities.
- **Nogais** of the Nogai district, with a steppe-pastoral household history.
- **Russians** of Cherkessk and the Zelenchuksky and Urupsky districts, and **Kuban Cossack** stanitsas, as a separate family and building tradition.
- **Ossetians** of Kosta-Khetagurovo and other small minorities found in the census nationality tables.
- **Families shaped by exile:** Karachays born in Kazakhstan or Kyrgyzstan before 1957, and their descendants.
- **Diasporas:** Circassian, Abaza and Karachay émigrés abroad, and any repatriate families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich older Russian-language ethnography; recent statistics for a small republic; thinner print in Abaza and Nogai.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardino-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Turkish and Arabic for diaspora material; German for early travelers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1943 deportation and its losses; land and status quarrels between the titular peoples since the 1990s; family matters touching customary and religious law go to Libra (the letter) or Capricorn (the spirit), not here. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the core lands of the former Karachay-Cherkess Republic.
- **Held for review:** none.
