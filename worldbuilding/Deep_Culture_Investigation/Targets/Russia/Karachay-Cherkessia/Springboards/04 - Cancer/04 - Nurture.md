## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay mountain families**, where grandparents and herding may still carry a large part of rearing.
- **Circassian villages** of the Khabez district, to compare upbringing ideals named in Circassian with those reported in practice.
- **Abazin families**, often bilingual in Abaza and Circassian, to see which language and which customs reach infants first.
- **Nogai households**, where cradle and naming customs may draw on a steppe Turkic tradition.
- **Russian and Cossack families**, whose school programs and church life give a separate route of moral formation.
- **Cherkessk apartment families** with kindergartens nearby, against village families with kin at hand.
- **Fathers, uncles and coaches**, who may nurture in ways the mother-centered literature misses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; folk-pedagogy literature is large but often idealized; survey data on actual practice for this republic is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardino-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** physical discipline of children and its rules go to Capricorn; avoid treating ideal texts as practice.
- **Held for review:** none.
