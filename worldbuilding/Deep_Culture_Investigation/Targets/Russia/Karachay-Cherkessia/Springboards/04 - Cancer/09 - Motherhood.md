## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay women**, tracing the passage from келин to mother to senior woman of the house, with its own words.
- **Circassian women**, whose гуащэ title for a husband's mother may mark a separate rank to study.
- **Abaza mothers**, often married across into Circassian or Karachay families, for which customs a mixed household follows.
- **Nogai mothers**, whose birth and naming customs may come from a steppe Turkic line.
- **Russian and Cossack mothers**, with Orthodox and Soviet models of the mother.
- **Mothers of large families** against those with one or two children, since the state rewards the first group.
- **Mothers who raised children in exile**, a generation now largely gone, known through their children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; folklore and ethnography of birth and avoidance are plentiful; recent studies of mothers' own views in the republic are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardino-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; French for Dumézil.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reproductive health and abortion; bride abduction, whose rules go to Libra or Capricorn; women's private accounts of in-law relations.
- **Held for review:** none.
