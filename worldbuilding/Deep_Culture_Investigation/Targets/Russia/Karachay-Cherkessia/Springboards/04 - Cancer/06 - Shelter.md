*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay herders and their families**, for whom a hut on the pastures is a second roof through the summer.
- **Karachays who were children in exile**, whose memories of finding shelter in Central Asia form their own body of evidence.
- **Circassians**, whose old lodging for travelers can be set against present guesthouse work.
- **Abazins**, to see whether lodging and refuge pass through Circassian neighbors' customs or their own.
- **Nogais**, with a past of movable dwellings.
- **Russians in Cherkessk**, where state shelters and church charity may carry more of the load.
- **Refugee and migrant families** who arrived since the 1990s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; deportation memoirs and travel accounts are plentiful; present-day homelessness data for the republic is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardino-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Kazakh and Kyrgyz for exile-side archives; Arabic for Syrian arrivals; English for travelers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the conditions of the special settlements and deaths there; refugee status; asylum customs belong to Libra (the letter) or Capricorn (the spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
