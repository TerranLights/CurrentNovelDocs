## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay tukhums** of the upper Kuban against lowland villages: whether lineage still sets who helps whom.
- **Circassians**, comparing descendants of old noble houses with commoner lineages, since descent was ranked.
- **Abazins**, whose clan names often span both Abaza and Circassian villages, to see where kin lines cross language lines.
- **Nogais**, with a clan system of steppe origin.
- **Russians and Kuban Cossacks**, where the nuclear family and the stanitsa may count for more than lineage.
- **Mixed families** (Karachay-Circassian, Abaza-Circassian, Russian-titular marriages), to see whose kin claim the children.
- **Families split by the 1943 exile**, with branches left in Central Asia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Soviet and post-Soviet ethnography for the larger peoples; mixed for the Nogais; thin for current kin networks outside online groups.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardino-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Turkish for diaspora branches.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blood feud and family honor are delicate, and their customary rules belong to Libra or Capricorn; genealogies may hold private family matters.
- **Held for review:** none.
