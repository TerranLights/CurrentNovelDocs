## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays of the upper Kuban and Teberda gorges:** mountain sacred places and spirit lore may survive unevenly by gorge.
- **Circassians:** the old pantheon's survival in idioms and oaths against its absence from practice.
- **Abazins:** their beliefs are often folded into Circassian or Abkhaz accounts; keep them separate.
- **Nogais:** a steppe set of spirits and healers that differs from the mountain peoples'.
- **Cossack and Russian villagers:** Orthodox folk belief, with Soviet-era loss and later revival.
- **Urban professionals in Cherkessk against rural elders:** degree of trust in omens, healers and charms.
- **Women and men:** childbirth, healing and charm beliefs are often held and passed on by women; check who speaks in the sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich for folklore; thin for measured belief (no regional survey of folk beliefs is known to the agent).
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; German and French for early travelers; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clergy may call some folk practices forbidden; record them without endorsing either side. Healers may not wish to be named.
- **Held for review:** none.
