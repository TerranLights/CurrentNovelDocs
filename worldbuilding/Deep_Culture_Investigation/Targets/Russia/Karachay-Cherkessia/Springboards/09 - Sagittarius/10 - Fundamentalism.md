# Karachay-Cherkessia: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Arabic for doctrinal terms. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young urban Karachays against village elders:** whether the 1990s restorationist current followed a generational line is a question to test with both sides' own words.
- **Circassian and Abaza Muslims:** whether literalist currents reached them differently from Karachays.
- **Nogais:** often studied under Dagestan and Stavropol; check local Nogai religious life apart from them.
- **Sufi-leaning or custom-keeping families:** keepers of grave visits and memorial meals, the practices the charge of innovation may be aimed at.
- **Pre-Islamic revivalists:** Circassian and Karachay intellectuals who restore older faiths, a mirror image of Islamic restoration.
- **Orthodox and Cossack communities:** strict Orthodox or Old Believer currents, and anti-sect campaigns.
- **Women in strict households:** their own accounts, rarely heard in reporting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed and skewed. Security and media sources dominate; the movements' own words and believers' everyday lives are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for the security and court record; Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza and Nogai for local sermons and community voices; Arabic for the doctrinal texts; Turkish for the diaspora and study-abroad links.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** very high. "Wahhabi" and "extremist" are official labels with legal effect; naming individuals can endanger them; some sources are blocked or designated in Russia. Record who applies each label. Extremism law goes to Libra and Capricorn; armed conflict is not the concept's center.
- **Held for review:** none.
