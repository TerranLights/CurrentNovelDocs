# Karachay-Cherkessia: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shepherds and highland villagers against Cherkessk office workers:** purpose tied to land and herd against purpose tied to career.
- **Young labor migrants to Moscow and the oil regions:** whether leaving reads as losing meaning or as finding it.
- **The exile generation of Karachays and its grandchildren:** suffering given a meaning, or left without one.
- **Circassians:** how far the code of conduct, against Islam, is named as the source of purpose.
- **Abazins and Nogais:** small peoples for whom language and survival as a people may weigh in the answer.
- **Russians and Cossack descendants:** Orthodox, Soviet and post-Soviet sources of purpose side by side.
- **Women and men separately:** family, honor and work may be weighted differently, and the sources speak with different voices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Folklore and literature are rich; measured data on meaning is scarce and mostly student samples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide is underreported and stigmatized in Muslim communities; laments may belong to mourners and be shared only by consent.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library catalog for Karachay-Balkar and Circassian proverb collections and lament anthologies.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for «смысложизненные ориентации Карачаево-Черкесия».
3. Pull Rosstat КОУЖ life-satisfaction tables for the republic, every available wave.
4. Find Bgazhnokov's *Адыгская этика* and Khan-Girey's notes in library catalogs.
5. Search GTRK Karachay-Cherkessia's video archive for documentaries on the summer pastures.
