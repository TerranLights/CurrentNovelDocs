## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Livestock traders and herders:** handshake deals among kin and neighbors against dealings with outside buyers.
- **Officials and applicants:** honesty in offices, as each side describes it; the arrangement against the bribe.
- **Students and teachers:** cheating and its tolerance across the exam reforms.
- **Circassian aristocratic families and others:** whether the old code's truthfulness is claimed as a family ideal.
- **Karachay, Abaza and Nogai villages:** the conscience word in each language may carry different weight; compare idioms, not peoples.
- **Cossack stanitsas:** Russian village norms of keeping one's word.
- **Tourism workers in Dombay and Arkhyz:** honesty toward strangers who will not return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Corruption and exam data exist at the regional level; everyday honesty norms need folklore and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for the corruption and exam data; Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza and Nogai for the oath, proverb and folklore pointers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption claims can defame named people; report patterns from sources, never accusations. Some anti-corruption projects carry Russian legal designations. Bribery as crime goes to Pisces; its law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
