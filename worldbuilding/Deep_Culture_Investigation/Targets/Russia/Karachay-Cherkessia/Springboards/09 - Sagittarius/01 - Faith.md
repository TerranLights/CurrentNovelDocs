# Karachay-Cherkessia: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Arabic for liturgy. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what cannot be seen or proven (God, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay elders raised in Central Asian exile and their grandchildren:** faith kept in secret against faith learned openly after 1990.
- **Circassians of Khabez against Circassians of Cherkessk:** village mosque life and town life may hold faith differently.
- **Abazins:** few published sources on their piety; their own voice is the gap.
- **Nogais of Erken-Shakhar and nearby auls:** steppe Islam with its own saint and shrine traditions *check*.
- **Orthodox Russians:** Cossack descendants who rebuilt parishes against Cherkessk's Soviet-secular townspeople.
- **Converts** in both directions (to Orthodoxy, Protestantism or Islam): rare cases that show where the lines between faith and people are drawn.
- **Diaspora Karachays and Circassians in Turkey:** faith that was never under Soviet atheism, as a contrast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Archival and press material on Islam is rich; measured religiosity is thin and scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Arabic (sermons, manuscripts); Turkish (diaspora).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Muslims' faith is often reported through a security lens; read believers' own words first. Missionary activity among Muslims is a charged topic. Religion law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
