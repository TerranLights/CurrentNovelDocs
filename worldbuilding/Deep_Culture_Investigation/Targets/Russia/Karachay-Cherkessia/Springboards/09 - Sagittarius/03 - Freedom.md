## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay descendants of *ёзден* families against those of bonded estates:** release from bondage in the 1860s may be remembered differently.
- **Circassians of the Besleney villages:** a people that fought, lost and stayed, as against the exiled majority abroad.
- **Abazins:** caught between Circassian, Karachay and Russian power; freedom as survival of a small people.
- **Nogais:** former steppe nomads whose mobility was curbed by settlement.
- **Kuban Cossack descendants:** *воля* as a Cossack self-image, set beside their role in the conquest.
- **Returnees from Central Asia and those born after 1957:** freedom of movement as lived memory against inherited story.
- **Young Muslims and older imams:** freedom of worship read through very different experiences of the state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for the 19th-century conquest and the deportation; thin for opinion data on freedom today.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Turkish and Ottoman Turkish; Kazakh for exile archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1828 and 1864 anniversaries, Cossack claims and the 1990s republic declarations divide peoples; press and religious-freedom cases can touch people still under prosecution. Rights in law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
