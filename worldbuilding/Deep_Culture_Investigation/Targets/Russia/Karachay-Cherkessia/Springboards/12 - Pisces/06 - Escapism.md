# Karachay-Cherkessia: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men in Cherkessk** against **young men in highland auls**: drink, drugs, gyms and phones may be the escapes on offer in one setting and not the other.
- **Observant Muslim households** against **Soviet-secular ones**: drink may be shamed in one and tolerated at feasts in the other.
- **Russians and Kuban Cossacks**: Orthodox drinking customs and festive toasts sit alongside Muslim neighbors' rules.
- **Karachay herding families**: the high pasture as retreat or burden.
- **Abazins and Nogais**: small village networks may leave little room to escape unseen.
- **Women of each people**: approved escapes (visiting, music, television) may differ from men's; measure, do not assume.
- **Labor migrants and their families**: going away as escape, and the toll on those left at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; health statistics and resort data exist; local drinking and leisure customs need ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for health statistics and press; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for feast and song terms; Abaza and Nogai village sources where they exist.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug and alcohol use carries strong shame in Muslim communities, so self-report will be low; youth departures to armed groups in Syria (2013 onward) are sometimes framed as escape and are highly sensitive, handled only through published scholarship. Gambling is legally limited in Russia; any law angle goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
