**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: whether blood debt and oath shape the image of the wrongdoer in highland villages more than in lowland ones.
- **Circassians**: the shame-and-face idiom (напэ), and Besleney against Kabardian lineages' memory of feud.
- **Abazins**: small communities where one family's wrong may mark the whole lineage.
- **Nogais**: steppe herding life and the old weight of horse theft.
- **Russians and Kuban Cossacks**: stanitsa views of "crime" may cast highland neighbors as the danger, and the reverse; record both.
- **Young men**: the age group most likely to be pictured as wrongdoers and as avengers.
- **Women**: victims of abduction and those who judge it; their views are seldom recorded.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; folklore of outlaws and feud is rich; recorded statistics exist; what ordinary people condemn needs survey and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for statistics and news; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for outlaw ballads and shame idioms; Abaza and Nogai for small-community accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the insurgency of the 1990s and 2000s, terrorism charges against people from the republic and the 1999 election violence are highly sensitive; some sources carry Russian "foreign agent" or "extremist" labels; bride abduction and feud involve living victims. Law as rules goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
