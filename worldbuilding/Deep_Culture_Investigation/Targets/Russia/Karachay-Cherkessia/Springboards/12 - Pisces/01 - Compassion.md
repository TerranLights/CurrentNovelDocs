# Karachay-Cherkessia: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Arabic for alms vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays in highland auls** against **Cherkessk apartment dwellers**: the help a village owes its own may reach strangers in the city differently.
- **Circassians**, Besleney villages against Kabardian-speaking ones: pity's place among the Adyghe virtues may be recorded differently by subgroup.
- **Abazins**: a small people whose charity may run mainly through kin and village rather than public bodies.
- **Nogais**: steppe hospitality traditions toward the traveler in need.
- **Russians and Kuban Cossacks**: Orthodox almsgiving and parish charity against Muslim zakat and sadaqa.
- **Syrian Circassian returnees**: both recipients and observers of local compassion.
- **Observant Muslims and secular Soviet-trained families**: whether giving is framed as duty to God or as civic help.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; proverbs, ethics monographs and religious notices are likely good; republic-level giving statistics are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for proverbs and verse; Abaza and Nogai for their press; Arabic terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** appeals for sick children name real minors; handle with privacy. Mercy toward offenders touches law: point to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library catalog for "Бгажноков адыгская этика" and list chapters on pity and mercy.
2. Search "Нарт сёзле" with "джазыкъ" or "садакъа" to gather the proverbs.
3. Check the DUM KChR site for "закят" and "Курбан-байрам" notices, 2015 onward.
4. Search Russian press for "черкесы из Сирии Карачаево-Черкесия помощь".
5. Pull the ministry's annual reports for "социальная помощь" by district.
