## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay herders** of the high valleys: weather and animal signs learned on the pasture, where reading the land is a working skill.
- **Circassian elders and the хасэ tradition**: experience as public authority, apart from omens.
- **Abazins**: omen and dream customs that may follow Abkhaz parallels rather than Circassian ones.
- **Nogais**: steppe weather lore and dream customs.
- **Russian and Cossack households**: Russian folk omens (приметы) living beside Muslim neighbors' signs.
- **Reformist Muslims**: likely to reject omens while keeping istikhara, so the line between allowed and forbidden knowing shifts by group.
- **Mountain professionals** (rescuers, avalanche staff, guides): trained hunches against instrument data.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed; epic and herding lore are documented; current everyday hunches need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; Arabic for devotional terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination is condemned by many Muslim teachers, so people may hide it; frame questions around signs and experience, not sorcery.
- **Held for review:** none.
