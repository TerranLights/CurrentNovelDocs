## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: Islamic devotion alongside an old sky-and-mountain layer and a small Tengri revival; three strands to keep apart.
- **Circassians**: Islamic practice beside the Khabze revival; Besleney and Kabardian villages may differ in both.
- **Abazins**: shrine traditions with Abkhaz cognates, and a Christian memory that may outlast it elsewhere.
- **Nogais**: steppe folk Islam with holy graves, studied by an insider scholar.
- **Russians and Kuban Cossacks**: Orthodox practice centered on old Alanian churches and new pilgrim sites.
- **Ossetians**: possible traces of their own sacred places, to be checked locally.
- **Reformist young Muslims**: likely critics of saint visits and household rites; their view shapes what others will say about practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; old ethnography and pilgrim sites are documented; current practice is harder to reach, and some is private.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai; French for Dubois; Arabic terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tensions between reformist and traditional Muslims, and between Muslims and neo-traditional revivals, are sharp and touch security policy; ask about practice, not about loyalties. Doctrine and religious bodies belong to Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
