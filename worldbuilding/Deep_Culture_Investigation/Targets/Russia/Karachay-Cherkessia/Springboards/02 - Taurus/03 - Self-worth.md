## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: worth tied to a deported and restored people may differ by family exile history and by generation born in Central Asia.
- **Circassians of the republic**: the *adyğağe* ideal against everyday practice; compare older villages with Cherkessk.
- **Abazins**: a small people's sense of worth beside the larger Circassian and Karachay neighbors.
- **Nogais**: a former steppe people now a minority in the foothills; what standing rests on for them.
- **Russians and Cossacks**: the post-Soviet Cossack revival as a claim to standing; compare with non-Cossack Russians.
- **Descendants of princely and noble lines** against **descendants of dependents**: whether old rank still enters a person's worth.
- **Men away on migrant work** against **those at home**; **women** in each people, whose worth may rest on different grounds; check, never assume.
- **Muslim and Orthodox congregations**: worth from God as each teaches it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Circassian ethics is well written up; Karachay and Abaza material is thinner; survey data by people is scarce.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for codes, songs and literature; Abaza and Nogai for the small units; Ottoman Turkish or a translation for Evliya Çelebi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor codes are easily turned into stereotypes; report each as a source states it and name variation. Deportation memory and 1864 are painful and political. Suicide data needs care. Honor killings or customary penalties, if met, go to Libra or Capricorn and are not scouted here.
- **Held for review:** none.
