## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: deportation and return broke residence for a whole people; compare families who regained old houses with those resettled in new villages.
- **Circassians**: villages founded or moved by the imperial authorities in the 19th century against older Besleney settlements *check*.
- **Abazins**: medieval migration from the Black Sea coast to the north slope, then 19th-century emigration; continuity may be traced differently.
- **Nogais**: a steppe people now in fixed villages; settled life is comparatively recent.
- **Cossack stanitsas**: founded as frontier posts in the 19th century; Soviet decossackization (расказачивание) as their own break *check*.
- **Residents of Cherkessk**: a city that changed its name several times, with a mixed and mobile population.
- **Herding families on summer pastures** against **townspeople**: a yearly round that keeps going.
- **Young people leaving for Stavropol, Moscow or abroad** against **those who stay**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Migration, marriage and price statistics are good; continuity in specific families and villages needs archives, cemeteries and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for legends and epitaphs; Ottoman Turkish for the Istanbul archive; Arabic script for old epitaphs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1864 expulsion and deportation are painful; the 1999 and 2004 crises turned on rivalry between peoples, so report each side's account. Cemeteries and graves call for respect and permission.
- **Held for review:** none.
