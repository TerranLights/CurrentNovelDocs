# Karachay-Cherkessia: Domain and folkways (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess (Circassian); Abaza; Nogai. **Concept in one line:** the domain of possessions and value: what is worth having, and how it is held (goods, money, herds, land, food, dress, wealth ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays of the upper Kuban and Teberda valleys** against **Karachays resettled in the foothill villages after the 1957 return**: two distinct relations to mountain pasture and inherited houses.
- **Circassians (Besleney and Kabardian groups, Khabezsky and Adyge-Khablsky districts)**: a separate vocabulary of worth and of guest obligations to set beside the Turkic one.
- **Abazins (Tapanta and Ashkharywa dialect groups)** *check*: a small people whose household economy is rarely studied apart from the Circassians.
- **Nogais of the Nogaysky district**: steppe herding heritage against the mountain transhumance of their neighbors.
- **Russians and Kuban Cossacks of Zelenchuksky, Urupsky and Cherkessk**: stanitsa land allotment and Orthodox household customs.
- **Ossetians of Kosta-Khetagurovo and smaller groups (Armenians, Greeks, Ukrainians)** *check*: confirm presence in the census before treating them as units.
- **Urban Cherkessk against the auls**, and **diasporas** (Turkey, Jordan, Syria, Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan): remittances and things sent home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Official statistics and Soviet ethnography are rich; data broken down by people (not by district) is thin, and Abaza and Nogai material is thinnest.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for folklore and press; Abaza and Nogai for the smaller units; Turkish and Arabic for diaspora sources.
- **Lands:** this nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled (the tracker carries only "Russia"); this sheet is written for the core lands of the Karachay-Cherkess Republic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1943 deportation and lost property; land and pasture disputes between peoples; the 1864 Circassian expulsion; the historical slave trade; inheritance and customary law (adat) go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian national library catalogs (РГБ, РНБ) for "материальная культура карачаевцев" and the same phrase for черкесов, абазин, ногайцев.
2. Pull the ВСХП-2016 republic volume and the household budget survey tables for Karachay-Cherkessia.
3. Search eLibrary.ru for КЧИГИ proceedings with "хозяйство", "имущество" and "свадебные обряды".
4. Search the СМОМПК index for "карачай", "абазин" and "ногай".
5. Look up "мал" and "байлыкъ" in the 1989 Karachay-Balkar dictionary and "мылъку" in a Kabardian-Cherkess dictionary to fix the term list.
