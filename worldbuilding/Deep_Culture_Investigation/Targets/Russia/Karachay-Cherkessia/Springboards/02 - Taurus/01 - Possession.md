# Karachay-Cherkessia: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza, Nogai. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay families with deportation memory**: objects carried to Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan and back may carry a weight that families without that history do not share; separate the generations.
- **Upper-valley Karachays (Uchkulan, Khurzuk, Teberda)** against **foothill Karachays**: whether storage of goods differs between old stone houses and newer homes.
- **Circassians of Khabezsky district**: the handing on of weapons and gold-embroidered dress as family pieces, to be compared with the Turkic neighbors.
- **Abazins of Abazinsky district and Psyzh** *check*: how a small community shares tools and objects across villages.
- **Nogais of Erken-Shakhar and nearby auls**: the portable household of a former steppe people (felt, chests) against the mountain house.
- **Russians and Cossacks of Zelenchukskaya and Pregradnaya** *check*: icons, family chests and the 40th-day handling of a dead person's things.
- **Ossetians of Kosta-Khetagurovo** *check*: a small community whose keepsakes may follow Ossetian rather than local custom.
- **Cherkessk renters and students**: what young people living away from the aul keep for themselves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Museums and Soviet ethnography are rich for objects; personal attachment to things is thin and needs interviews and memoirs.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for tales and memoirs; Abaza and Nogai for the small units.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation losses; objects taken by 19th-century collectors and the Russian army; theft statistics can feed stereotypes about peoples, so report variation only; inheritance rules belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
