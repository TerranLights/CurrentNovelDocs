## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper-valley villages (Teberda, Uchkulan, Arkhyz)** against **plains settlements**: mountain hazards weigh on some households far more than others.
- **Pensioners and public-sector workers** against **traders, herders and the self-employed**: security from the state against security from one's own work.
- **Karachays with strong clan ties** against **urban mixed families in Cherkessk**: whether kin still act as insurance.
- **Circassian and Abaza villages**: lineage obligations may run differently in each; compare them, not assume one pattern.
- **Nogai villages of the steppe edge**: water and grazing security in drier land.
- **Cossack stanitsas**: Cossack patrols and self-defense groups as a claim to guard the community *check*.
- **Households with members working in Moscow or abroad**: remittances as a cushion.
- **Young men in districts touched by counterterrorism operations**: insecurity of the person, not only of income.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Official income, pension and hazard data is good; data on informal savings, kin aid and fear of crime is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for blessings and kin terms; Kazakh or Kyrgyz archives may hold Russian-language files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counterterrorism operations and the insurgency of the 2000s; deportation; human-rights sources may be banned or hard to reach in Russia; never frame any people as a danger. Policing and courts belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
