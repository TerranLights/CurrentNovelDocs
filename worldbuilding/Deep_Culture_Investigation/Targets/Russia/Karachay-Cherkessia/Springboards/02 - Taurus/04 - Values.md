## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Circassians**: *adyge xabze* as a secular code beside Islam; compare villages with a strong Khase presence and those without.
- **Karachays**: custom (*adet*) and Islam may be ranked differently by older and younger believers.
- **Abazins**: whether the Abaza community shares the Circassian code or keeps its own form.
- **Nogais**: steppe custom and Islam in a small minority, compared with Nogai communities studied in neighboring regions.
- **Russians and Cossacks**: Orthodox and Cossack revival against Soviet secular habits.
- **Soviet-raised elders against post-1990 youth**: the largest likely value gap.
- **Diaspora returnees and visitors (Turkey, Jordan, Syria)**: priorities brought back from abroad.
- **Urban Cherkessk against mountain auls**: tourism and migration alongside farming life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Ethnographic statements of codes are rich; measured values data for the republic itself is thin, and regional samples in national surveys are small.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian-Cherkess and Karachay-Balkar for codes and sermons; Arabic for religious terms; Abaza and Nogai for the smaller units.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious currents and the security services' labels for them; "traditional values" as federal policy language; generalizing codes into a single voice for a people. Customary rulings go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
