# Karachay-Cherkessia: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza, Nogai. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: bride abduction, staged or real, may be judged differently in upper valleys and in Cherkessk; record the range of views.
- **Circassians**: avoidance customs that limit public shows of attachment between spouses and toward children *check*; compare older and younger couples.
- **Abazins**: whether guarding customs follow Circassian forms or their own.
- **Nogais**: family control and sharing customs in a small Turkic minority.
- **Russians and Cossacks**: Orthodox marriage and jealousy norms, and how they differ from Muslim neighbors' or not.
- **Herders on summer pastures**: territoriality over grazing and water sources.
- **Resort-town residents**: guarding local jobs and places against outsiders.
- **Mixed marriages** between peoples: whose family claims the children and the couple's time.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Ethnography of marriage customs is rich but old; current data on control in couples is scarce and region-level surveys are rare.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for songs and terms; Abaza for the epic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bride abduction and violence against women are painful and easily stereotyped; some NGOs are labeled "foreign agents" and may be hard to reach; never cast any people as controlling. Marriage, custody and abduction as offenses go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
