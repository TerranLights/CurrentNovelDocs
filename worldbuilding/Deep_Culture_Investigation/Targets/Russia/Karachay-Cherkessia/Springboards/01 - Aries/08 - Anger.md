## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays and Circassians in the same towns:** test whether display of anger differs between them or mostly by family and class.
- **Men and women:** which anger may be shown, and where (home, street, gathering).
- **Elders and young men:** the elder as the one who cools a quarrel, and youths as the ones who flare.
- **Abaza and Nogai villages:** smaller communities' ways of settling grievance with larger neighbors.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** Orthodox and Soviet frames of anger; Cossack revival rhetoric.
- **Religious practice:** observant families, secular families and new Salafi-leaning circles may preach anger and restraint differently.
- **Cherkessk against mountain villages:** urban anonymity against village oversight of temper.
- **Diaspora returnees:** grievance tied to exile and return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for linguistic studies and political events; mixed for everyday anger; thin for family anger because it is under-reported.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for idiom; Abaza and Nogai for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic clashes; family violence; feuds; protest coverage blocked in Russia. Revenge belongs to Scorpio; feud law and reconciliation rules belong to Libra and Capricorn.
