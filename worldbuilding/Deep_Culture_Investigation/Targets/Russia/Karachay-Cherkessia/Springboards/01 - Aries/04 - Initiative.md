## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay highland villages with tourism (Teberda, Dombay, Arkhyz) against pastoral villages:** where a new market opened, who seized it first.
- **Circassian villages of the Khabez district:** expect different crafts and trades than the Karachay gorges; compare who starts them.
- **Abaza villages:** a small people's founders and the trades their villages are known for.
- **Nogais of Nogaysky district:** steppe farming and trade, nearer the Stavropol markets.
- **Russian stanitsas of Zelenchuk and Urup districts:** farming enterprise and Cossack revival projects.
- **Women against men:** home enterprise and craft against public business.
- **Young people against elders:** whether acting before an elder speaks is read as drive or as presumption, by family.
- **Kin networks in Moscow and other cities:** migrant businesses tied to home villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; business and volunteer statistics are rich and open; the folk side of initiative (communal work, who leads) is thinner and mainly in Russian ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for folk terms; Abaza and Nogai for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** informal economy and clan-based business may be touchy to ask about; land disputes. Rules on business registration go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
