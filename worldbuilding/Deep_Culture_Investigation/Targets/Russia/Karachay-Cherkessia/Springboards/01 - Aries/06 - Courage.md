## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays:** test whether the 1828 defeat, the 1942 front and the 1943 deportation are told as one sequence, by gorge and generation.
- **Circassians:** test how far the 19th-century war shapes the courage ideal; compare Besleney and Kabardian lines.
- **Abaza:** a small people's heroes, and whether they are claimed by neighbors.
- **Nogais:** steppe heroic tradition, and service in Russian wars.
- **Russians and Kuban Cossacks:** the Cossack warrior image; Cossack memory of the same 19th-century wars on the other side.
- **Women:** endurance in exile and war; whether it is named courage.
- **Mountain guides and rescuers:** a working, physical courage apart from war memory.
- **Families of soldiers in recent wars:** present-day service and its costs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heroic song and war memory; mixed for moral courage; thin for women's courage and for Abaza and Nogai materials.
