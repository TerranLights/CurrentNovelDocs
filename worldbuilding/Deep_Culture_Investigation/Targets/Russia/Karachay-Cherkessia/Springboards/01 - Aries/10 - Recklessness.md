## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men against everyone else:** expect the widest gap in road, mountain and weapon risk.
- **Karachay highland youth:** mountain life and herding with their own dangers, against Cherkessk youth with cars.
- **Circassian villages:** whether the abrek image is honored, mourned or rejected today.
- **Abaza and Nogai youth:** smaller communities with denser social oversight (to be tested).
- **Russians and Cossacks:** alcohol and удаль in the stanitsas; the Cossack image of the daring rider.
- **Observant and secular families:** religious restraint against secular risk norms on drink and gambling.
- **Resort workers and guides of Dombay and Arkhyz:** professional risk against tourists' risk.
- **Migrant workers on rotation:** risk taken far from home, outside family eyes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for measured risk (crashes, mortality, mountain accidents); mixed for folk judgment of rashness; thin for Abaza and Nogai.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for folklore and idiom; Nogai and Abaza for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** guns at weddings; drug use; outlaw memory that touches on recent armed groups; stereotypes of "Caucasian" young men in Russian media, to be reported as stereotypes and never repeated as findings. Traffic and weapons law go to Libra and Capricorn.
