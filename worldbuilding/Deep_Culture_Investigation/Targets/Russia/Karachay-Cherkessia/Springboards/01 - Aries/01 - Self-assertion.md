## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young brides and in-laws in Karachay and Circassian households:** avoidance rules may shape a young wife's voice most sharply.
- **Highland Karachay villages (Uchkulan, Khurzuk, Kart-Jurt) against Cherkessk:** village assembly norms against urban, mixed-ethnic workplaces.
- **Circassian Besleney and Kabardian-speaking villages:** two sub-traditions of etiquette worth testing separately.
- **Abaza villages of Abazinsky district:** a smaller people speaking up between two larger ones.
- **Nogais:** steppe verbal-contest traditions, if they persist, differ from the highland feast order.
- **Kuban Cossack stanitsas:** the circle as a forum for claims, against Muslim assembly forms.
- **Market traders of Cherkessk and Karachaevsk:** haggling as everyday self-assertion across ethnic lines.
- **Students and young professionals:** where federal workplace norms meet elders' expectations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on Circassian etiquette, uneven for Karachay speech norms, thin for Abaza and Nogai.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian-Cherkess and Karachay-Balkar for toasts and proverbs; Abaza and Nogai for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** public confrontations between ethnic groups and online conflict videos; women's voice inside families. Complaint to courts and legal claims go to Libra and Capricorn.
