## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay wrestling families and villages:** combat sport as village pride; compare gorges.
- **Circassian youth at a джэгу:** contest in dance and bearing rather than force.
- **Abaza and Nogai athletes:** whether smaller peoples' winners are claimed by them or absorbed into the republic's tally.
- **Russian and Cossack stanitsas:** team sports, Cossack riding and their own festival contests.
- **Girls and women:** which contests are open to them (dance, school olympiads, some sports) and which are not.
- **Herders against townspeople:** livestock fairs against school exams as fields of rivalry.
- **Resort-town youth (Dombay, Arkhyz, Teberda):** ski and guiding contests linked to tourism.
- **Families and lineages:** rivalry between clans over status and success, if people discuss it.
