## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cherkess villages:** bards, the dance master and avoidance customs give criticism set offices and limits.
- **Karachay villages:** mocking songs and elders' public rebuke; test how they differ from the Cherkess forms.
- **Abazins:** check whether they shared the Circassian bard and dance-master roles.
- **Ak-Nogai:** a verse genre of moral reproach from the steppe.
- **Ossetians:** a small community with a national poet of social criticism.
- **Cossacks and Russians:** the Cossack circle and the Soviet self-criticism meeting.
- **Activists and journalists against officials:** criticism upward, with real risks.
- **Women and young people:** who may speak a fault aloud at a gathering, and to whom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for traditional forms in folklore; thin and risky for modern dissent, which is shaped by the federal political climate.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian-Cherkess, Karachay-Balkar and Nogai for oral forms; Ossetian for Khetagurov in the original.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of authorities carries legal and personal risk; memory politics (1864, 1943) is contested; never name private individuals from complaint channels.
