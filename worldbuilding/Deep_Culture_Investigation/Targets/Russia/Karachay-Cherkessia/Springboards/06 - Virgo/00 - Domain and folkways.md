## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays by valley:** the upper Kuban and Uchkulan, Teberda and Mara communities, and the lowland villages settled after the 1957 return, since herding and farming split along altitude.
- **Cherkess:** Besleney-speaking and Kabardian-speaking villages (Khabez and Adyge-Khabl districts), to test whether the old estate order lingers differently.
- **Abazins:** Tapanta and Ashkharywa speakers, whose villages sit between Cherkess and Karachay neighbors.
- **Nogais:** the Ak-Nogai of Nogaysky district, with a steppe herding past unlike the mountain peoples'.
- **Russians and Kuban Cossacks:** stanitsas such as Zelenchukskaya and Pregradnaya, with Cossack service and farm work as a separate line.
- **Smaller groups:** Ossetians (Kosta Khetagurov village), Armenians and Ukrainians in Cherkessk and the plains.
- **Generations and places:** the deportation-and-return generation against later ones; Cherkessk against mountain villages and resort towns (Teberda, Dombay, Arkhyz).
- **Diasporas:** Circassian, Karachay, Abaza and Nogai communities in Turkey, Syria and Jordan; Karachays who stayed in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: older ethnography is rich on herding, crafts and household; modern surveys rarely go below federal-district level; minority-language press is uneven online.
- **Lands:** the tracker carries "Russia" as the pre-war lands, which is broad; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. This sign's sheets are written for the core lands of the former Karachay-Cherkess Republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for folklore and press; Abaza and Nogai for their own papers and folklore; Turkish and Arabic for diaspora and Islamic sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1943 deportation and the 1860s exile are living, contested memory; tensions between peoples in republic politics; religious practice under security pressure; the closed Memorial archives.
- **Neighbors:** clock, calendar and public order go to Capricorn; labor law to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
