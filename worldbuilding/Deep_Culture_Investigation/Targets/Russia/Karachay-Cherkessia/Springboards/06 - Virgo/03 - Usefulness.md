## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders in Karachay, Cherkess, Abaza and Nogai villages:** each people's council of elders gives a separate role to test.
- **Urban pensioners in Cherkessk:** apartment life without a plot, flock or garden.
- **Herding families' children:** tasks with animals from an early age, against town children.
- **Disabled people and their families:** where worth is argued apart from work.
- **Russian and Cossack stanitsa households:** garden plots and livestock on a different pattern.
- **Returnees from Central Asia and their descendants:** practical skills learned in exile.
- **Speakers of the smaller languages:** Abaza and Nogai, where the "useful language" debate cuts deepest.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Census, disability and pension data are open; elders' roles and the sense of being needed sit in folklore and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza and Nogai for the smaller peoples; Arabic for hadith sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disability and institutional care; language policy is politically charged; the Nart cliff motif must not be read as a claim about real practice.
- **Neighbors:** self-worth goes to Taurus; independence to Aries; dignity to Leo.
- **Held for review:** none.
