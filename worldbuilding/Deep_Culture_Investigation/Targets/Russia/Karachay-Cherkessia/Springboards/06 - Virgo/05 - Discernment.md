## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cherkess villages:** the Kazanoko tradition and Kabardian horse lines give judgment named models.
- **Karachay:** Satanay's counsel and tamga lore, to test whether the two Nart traditions trust the same judges.
- **Ak-Nogai:** the steppe sages' sayings, a third wisdom tradition.
- **Abazins:** caught between Circassian and Karachay models; check which they draw on.
- **Elders against youth online:** scam targets and rumor sharers are rarely the same group.
- **Orthodox Russians and Cossacks:** spiritual fathers as discerners.
- **Women matchmakers and senior women:** often the hidden judges of a match.
