## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Circassian women embroiderers and Karachay and Nogai felt makers:** three craft traditions, each with its own idea of a flawless piece.
- **Quran reciters and madrasa students:** exactness in sound and rule.
- **Dancers and riders:** Circassian and Karachay dance troupes; Karachay, Cherkess and Cossack horsemen.
- **Abazins:** check which craft and dance standards they share or hold apart.
- **Students and their families:** the medal and high-score race.
- **Brides' families across peoples:** the staged perfect wedding.
- **Factory and office workers in Cherkessk:** industrial quality standards against craft ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Craft and dress ethnography is good; perfectionism as a personal or clinical trait has almost no regional data.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian-Cherkess and Karachay-Balkar for craft terms; Arabic for religious sources; Nogai for felt.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental-health stigma; religious practice is watched by the state; family pressure on students.
- **Neighbors:** discipline and order go to Capricorn; pride and humiliation to Leo; anxiety to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** none.
