## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cherkess villages:** a recorded rite of community healing and a reputation for folk surgery.
- **Karachay villages:** dairy foods, mineral springs and mountain air as health practice.
- **Abazins and Ak-Nogai:** their own healers and remedies, thinly studied.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** healing charms and Orthodox healing customs.
- **Muslims who use ruqya and those who avoid it:** a live dispute within Islam in the republic.
- **Mountain districts against Cherkessk:** distance to hospitals and specialists.
- **Deportation survivors and their children:** illness and loss in exile shape family health memory.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Federal health statistics are good; folk medicine is uneven by people; healers' own accounts need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian-Cherkess and Karachay-Balkar for rites and terms; Abaza and Nogai for their healers; Arabic for ruqya.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' knowledge may be private; ruqya and other Islamic practice attract security attention; mental illness, addiction and suicide carry stigma; deportation mortality is painful history.
- **Neighbors:** care and nurture go to Cancer; death to Scorpio; addiction to Pisces; health law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
