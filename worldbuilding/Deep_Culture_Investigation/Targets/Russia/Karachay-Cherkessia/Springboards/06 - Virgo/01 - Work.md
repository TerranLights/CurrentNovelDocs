# Karachay-Cherkessia: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay mountain herders against lowland Karachay farmers:** altitude decides whether the year turns on flocks or fields.
- **Cherkess villages of the Khabez and Adyge-Khabl districts:** an old estate past in which certain work marked rank; test what it left.
- **Abazins in Abazinsky district (Inzhich-Chukun, Psyzh):** a small people with farming close to both neighbors.
- **Ak-Nogai of Nogaysky district:** steppe herding roots, now farm villages.
- **Russian and Cossack stanitsas:** grain and stock farming with a separate history of service.
- **Cherkessk's industrial and office workers:** wage work against village self-provisioning.
- **Labor migrants:** men on rotation work in Siberia or Moscow and the households they leave.
- **Women's work across all peoples:** felt-making, dairy work, the garden plot; not to be read as one pattern.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rosstat gives the formal economy well; informal work, household plots and herding are thinner and sit in ethnography and local press.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess; Nogai and Abaza for their own villages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation-era forced labor; comparisons of "industrious" and "idle" peoples are an old colonial trope and must be read as claims, never repeated.
- **Neighbors:** labor law goes to Libra or Capricorn; wealth and possessions to Taurus.
- **Held for review:** none.
