## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Observatory staff and Nizhny Arkhyz residents against Zelenchuk villages:** a research enclave beside farm and herding communities.
- **Madrasa-trained and secular-trained men:** two schooling traditions of reasoning.
- **Soviet-trained engineers and teachers against the post-1991 generation:** the change in what technical education leads to.
- **Karachay shepherds:** reading weather and animals on high pastures.
- **Cherkess and Abaza villages:** riddle and counting traditions in Northwest Caucasian languages.
- **Ak-Nogai:** a Turkic steppe tradition of reckoning distinct from the mountain Karachay one.
- **Russian and Cossack stanitsas:** schooling and farm accounting on a separate pattern.

## 4. Flags
