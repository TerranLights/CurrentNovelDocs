## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Resettled lowland villages against those still in the high valleys:** the clearest case of improvement done to a place and its people.
- **Cherkess and Karachay:** the earlier Jadid and later Soviet reform layers reached them differently; check.
- **Abazins and Ak-Nogai:** script changes and revitalization weigh most on the smallest languages.
- **Cossacks:** remade by de-Cossackization, then revived as a registered society.
- **Syrian Circassian repatriates:** betterment through return.
- **Zelenchuk and Teberda valley residents:** development projects on their doorstep.
- **Young people leaving to study elsewhere:** improvement through departure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. State development documents are abundant; how people judge "improvement" done to them needs oral history and local press.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; all four native languages for script history and revitalization; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** resettlement, de-Cossackization and the Caucasian War are contested history; land and water disputes are live and political; foreign-linked NGOs face legal limits.
- **Neighbors:** reform and hope go to Aquarius; ambition and merit to Capricorn; education to Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
