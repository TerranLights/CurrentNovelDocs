## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay lineages of the upper Kuban:** marriage as an alliance between *тукъумла*, with lineage elders as the parties who bargain.
- **Karachays of the towns and newer settlements:** whether choice of spouse has moved toward the couple, to set against the highland villages.
- **Circassians (Kabardians and Besleney):** a past of noble fosterage and guest-friendship between houses, and its echoes in present ties.
- **Abazins:** marriage ties with Circassian and Karachay neighbors in a small people, where marrying out may be common or rare.
- **Nogais:** steppe wedding forms and kin bargaining, documented apart from the mountain peoples.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** Orthodox marriage and civil registration with no *никах* layer, and mixed couples with the Muslim peoples.
- **Business owners in Cherkessk, Karachayevsk and the resort valleys:** partnership in firms and tourism, and who is trusted as a co-founder.
- **Diaspora couples:** marriages made with Karachays, Circassians and Abazins in Turkey, a link to watch.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; marriage statistics and ethnography are plentiful, business partnership and mixed marriage are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardino-Cherkess for wedding and love songs; Abaza and Nogai for their own rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered religious marriage and plural marriage; bride abduction; divorce and custody across religious and civil rules; land and resort deals can be politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Family Code's chapters on the marriage contract and on spouses' common property.
2. Ask the republic's ЗАГС or search its site for «браки и разводы» by year and district.
3. Search eLibrary.ru for «межнациональные браки Карачаево-Черкесия» and «куначество аталычество».
4. Find Batchaev's and Kosven's studies in the Russian State Library catalog.
5. Search nalog.ru's register for agricultural cooperatives in the Karachayevsky and Khabezsky districts and list their founders' surnames.
