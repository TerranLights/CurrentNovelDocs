## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland and lowland Karachays:** pasture rights and herd shares in the old highland villages against farming land shares in the plains.
- **Circassians of the Khabez district:** feast portions and seating by age and rank, a different ground for a "due share" than equal splits.
- **Abazins:** inheritance and land-share disputes in a small people where households are closely tied.
- **Nogais:** a steppe pattern of dividing herds and grazing, to check against both mountain peoples.
- **Cossack stanitsas of the Zelenchuksky and Urupsky districts:** equal male land shares as their historic yardstick.
- **Women heirs:** where civil-code equal shares meet religious half-shares, a likely seam within every Muslim people here.
- **Urban young people in Cherkessk:** exams, hiring and connections as the main tests of fair rules.
- **Families returned from deportation:** houses and land lost after 1943, and whether later division seemed fair to them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; statutes and statistics are easy to reach, household practice of division is thin on paper and needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardino-Cherkess for proverbs and games; Abaza and Nogai for tales; Arabic for inheritance texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inheritance quarrels and land disputes involve living families; exam fraud cases name individuals; land questions may run along ethnic lines.
- **Held for review:** none.
