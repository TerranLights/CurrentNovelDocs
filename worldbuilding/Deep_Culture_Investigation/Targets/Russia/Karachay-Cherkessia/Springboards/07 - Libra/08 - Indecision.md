# Karachay-Cherkessia: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (most psychology and press), the national languages for sayings and divination terms, Arabic for prayers of guidance. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay extended families:** whether a choice rests with the person or waits for the senior men and women of the lineage.
- **Circassian families:** the rules of deference to elders that etiquette studies describe, to check, so that a young person's silence before a choice is not misread as indecision.
- **Abazins and Nogais in their own districts:** local councils deciding with few members, and how long they deliberate.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** Russian sayings on delay, and how a Cossack assembly reaches its decision.
- **Muslims who pray *istikhāra*:** whether waiting before a choice is framed as a religious step, to set beside secular views of delay.
- **Students and young graduates:** choices about migration to big Russian cities and about marriage.
- **Political actors in 1991-1992:** groups that held off a final choice on the republic's shape.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin; indecision is rarely a named subject here; most leads come from family sociology, folklore and election data, and need careful framing.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the national languages for sayings and divination; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1991-1992 disputes are still political; divination can be disapproved of by religious authorities, so speakers may not discuss it openly.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries

2. Search eLibrary.ru for «принятие решений в семье Северный Кавказ» and «Мельбурнский опросник студенты».
3. Pull "against all" shares by district for the republic's 1999 and 2003 elections.
4. Search folklore catalogs for «Насреддин карачаевский фольклор» and «гадание на бараньей лопатке Кавказ».
5. Search Russian Muslim sites for «истихара» and note which are read in the republic.
