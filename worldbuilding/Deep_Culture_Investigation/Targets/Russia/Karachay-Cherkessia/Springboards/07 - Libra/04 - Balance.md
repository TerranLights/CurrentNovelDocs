## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays and Circassians:** the two largest peoples, whose shares in office are most often weighed in the press.
- **Abazins and Nogais:** whether their own districts, formed in the 2000s, changed their weight in the republic.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** their place in the arrangement, especially in the Zelenchuksky and Urupsky districts.
- **People of Teberda, Dombay and Arkhyz:** tourism income set against land and water.
- **Herders and farmers:** highland grazing against lowland crops as two uses to keep in proportion.
- **Bilingual young people:** Russian and a national language weighed in school and at home.
- **Muslims and Orthodox:** two religious calendars of fast and feast within one republic.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; election and legal data are rich, the informal sharing of posts is mostly in the press and must not be taken as a written rule.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the national languages for terms of moderation and for craft vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic shares of power touch live tensions; "extremism" labels; resort projects that are disputed.
- **Held for review:** none.
