## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay families deported in 1943 and returned after 1957:** the direct holders of the rehabilitation claim; their memoirs show what redress was sought.
- **Highland Karachay lineages of Uchkulan and Khurzuk:** where the old council and feud procedure is likeliest to survive in memory.
- **Circassians of the Khabez and Adyge-Khabl districts:** the 1864 memory and Adyghe Khabze's own procedure for judging a wrong.
- **Abazins (Tapanta and Shkaraua):** how its elders handled feud, checked apart from the Circassians.
- **Nogais of the Nogaysky district:** a minority's claims about land and language as questions of rightful order.
- **Russians and Cossacks of the stanitsas:** village courts and redress with no Islamic layer.
- **Muslims who use the Spiritual Directorate and those outside it:** where religious judgment sits beside or against state courts.
- **Women in feud, abduction and divorce cases:** whose due is decided in each of the three legal orders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for imperial records and the deportation, thin for present-day customary hearings, which leave few papers.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the four national languages for folklore and testimony; Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation and genocide claims are contested in public; counterterrorism trials; feud cases involve living families; Memorial's materials are restricted in Russia.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search sudact.ru for «Карачаево-Черкесской» with «кровная месть» and with «статья 76 УК РФ», to see how often reconciliation ends a case.
2. Search the State Archive of Krasnodar Krai's guide for «горский словесный суд» and «Баталпашинский отдел».
3. Read the 1991 rehabilitation law, then search the Russian State Library for memoir collections on the Karachay deportation.
4. Pull three years of the republic's human-rights commissioner's annual reports and list complaint categories.
5. Search folklore catalogs for «абрек» with «карачаевский фольклор» and «адыгский фольклор».
