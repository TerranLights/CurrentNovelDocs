# Karachay-Cherkessia: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian for law, courts and press; Karachay-Balkar, Kabardino-Cherkess, Abaza and Nogai for kin and folk terms. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland Karachay villages where most residents share a few lineages:** where an official or judge with no tie to a party may be hard to find.
- **Officials and staff in Cherkessk ministries:** the main stage for disclosure, procurement and hiring rules.
- **Circassian villages with a living *джэгу*:** a neutral role in ritual life, to set beside state ideas of neutrality.
- **Abazins and Nogais:** how members of smaller peoples meet officials and judges from larger ones, to be asked, not assumed.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** whether settlers' networks in the stanitsas work like the mountain peoples' lineage ties or differently.
- **Jurors and court users:** ordinary people choosing between kin loyalty and an oath.
- **Young graduates seeking state jobs:** where open competitions meet family connections.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; laws and disclosures are open, actual favoritism is hard to observe and is mostly reported in the press or court cases.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; national languages for kin vocabulary and folk roles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption allegations against named people; some outlets are labeled foreign agents or undesirable in Russia, which limits access and citation; ethnic favoritism is a charged subject.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read 273-FZ article 10 (conflict of interest) and the recusal articles of the two procedural codes.
2. Search the judges' qualification collegium's site for disciplinary decisions since 2015.
3. Search zakupki.gov.ru for the republic's largest contracts and note repeat winners.
4. Search eLibrary.ru for «клановость Карачаево-Черкесия» and «кумовство Северный Кавказ».
5. Search folklore and ethnography catalogs for «хьэтыякъуэ» and «джэгу».
