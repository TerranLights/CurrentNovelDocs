## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Circassian noble lineages and their descendants:** an inherited code of envoys and etiquette, recorded more than any other here.
- **Karachay elders and lineage heads:** the 1828 terms, the deportation petitions and present councils as three moments of their dealing with the state.
- **Abazins:** relations with Abkhazia and with Circassian and Karachay neighbors, a three-way diplomacy.
- **Nogais:** a national movement whose partners lie partly outside the republic, in Dagestan and Stavropol.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** dealings with the mountain villages and with Moscow through Cossack bodies.
- **Women who manage in-law relations:** the household's daily diplomacy, seldom in the published sources.
- **Diaspora associations in Turkey, Jordan and Syria:** envoys between the republic and communities abroad.
- **Repatriates from Syria and Turkey:** newcomers who must negotiate language and custom on arrival.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for Circassian etiquette and imperial records, thin for present-day back-channel talks, which leave little in print.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the four national languages; Turkish for diaspora bodies; Ottoman Turkish and Greek for older texts in translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Alan descent claims between peoples; the Abkhaz war; Circassian diaspora politics; some national movements have been under official pressure.
- **Held for review:** none.
