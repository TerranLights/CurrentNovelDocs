# Karachay-Cherkessia: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian with the republic's four national languages (Karachay-Balkar, Kabardino-Cherkess, Abaza, Nogai); Arabic for alms and mourning terms; German for one early travel source. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachay lineages:** funeral and wedding contributions tracked within and between *тукъумла*, for the account-keeping of a large kin group.
- **Circassian households:** a host's duty to a guest and the guest's return, recorded with unusual detail.
- **Abazins:** gift rules where a small people's kin ties overlap with Circassian and Karachay neighbors.
- **Nogais:** steppe hospitality and herd loans, to check against the mountain peoples.
- **Cossack and Russian stanitsas:** collective work repaid with a feast, a pattern with no Islamic alms layer.
- **Townspeople in Cherkessk:** whether neighbors in apartment blocks keep the village rules of return.
- **Officials and voters:** favors given and expected in politics.
- **Families abroad:** help between the republic and Turkey or Central Asia, and the obligations it creates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; ethnographies of hospitality and funerals are many, measured data on informal help is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the national languages for terms and folklore; Arabic for religious giving; German for Klaproth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patronage and vote-buying allegations; family accounts of gifts are private; religious charity can be caught up in extremism inquiries.
- **Held for review:** none.
