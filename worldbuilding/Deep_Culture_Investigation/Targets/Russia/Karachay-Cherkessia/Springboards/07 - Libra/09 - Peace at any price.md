# Karachay-Cherkessia: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian for press, law and rights reports; Karachay-Balkar, Kabardino-Cherkess, Abaza and Nogai for sayings and codes of restraint; Arabic for patience and settlement. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women in Karachay, Circassian, Abaza and Nogai families:** who keeps quiet about harm at home, and what the four peoples' own sources say.
- **Elders who settle feuds:** whether settlement asks the wronged side to give way.
- **Karachay and Circassian political leaders around 1999:** peace between the two largest peoples, and what each side conceded.
- **Abazins and Nogais:** whether smaller peoples avoid open disputes with larger neighbors, to ask, not assume.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** the Russian proverbs of quiet endurance, and village responses to disputes over land.
- **Religious communities under official pressure:** where silence may be caution rather than harmony.
- **Young people online:** self-censorship and its reasons.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; legal and press sources exist, the private side of silence is hard to reach and needs trusted interviewers.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the national languages for codes of restraint and songs; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence at home and honor crimes; named political crises; anti-extremism cases; several rights groups and outlets are restricted in Russia.
- **Held for review:** none.
