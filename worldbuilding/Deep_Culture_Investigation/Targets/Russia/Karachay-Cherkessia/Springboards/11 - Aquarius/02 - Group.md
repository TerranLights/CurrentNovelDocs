# Karachay-Cherkessia: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how the group is weighed against the person, where the line between "us" and "them" falls, and what is expected of members acting together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**, organized through lineages: whether the lineage or the person answers for an act.
- **Cherkess and Abazins**, often acting as one bloc in politics *check*: whether the bloc holds below the level of elites.
- **Abazins on their own**, a small people inside the bloc, with their own congress.
- **Nogais**, a small people whose wider group lives outside the republic, in Stavropol and Dagestan.
- **Russians and Cossack descendants**: the circle and the Cossack society as forms foreign to the mountain lineages.
- **Young religious groups** against the official Muslim board: generation and faith as a line of division.
- **Townspeople of Cherkessk** against villagers: where the group of origin weakens.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for politics and the deportation; mixed for everyday groups; thin for lineage life described from inside.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for movement documents and the press; Turkish for Ottoman sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective blame for 1943 and 1864; the share of posts among peoples; armed groups and prosecutions, where informants face risk; feud cases.
