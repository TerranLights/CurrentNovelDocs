## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays** of the upper Kuban and Teberda valleys against those of the Malokarachay district (Uchkeken): different valleys, different village networks.
- **Karachays born in Central Asian exile** against those born after return: two generations of a rebuilt community.
- **Cherkess** of the Khabez and Adyge-Khabl districts against Cherkessk townspeople; Besleney-speaking against Kabardian-speaking villages.
- **Abazins** of the Abaza and Ust-Dzheguta districts (Psyzh, Inzhich-Chukun), split by the Tapanta and Shkaraua dialects *check*: a small people living among larger ones.
- **Nogais** of the Nogai district (Erken-Shakhar), the Ak-Nogai *check*: steppe heritage, different from the mountain peoples.
- **Russians**, urban in Cherkessk, rural in the Zelenchuk and Urup stanitsas; **Kuban Cossack** descendants and their associations.
- **Smaller groups:** Ossetians, Greeks, Armenians *check* the villages.
- **Returnees**: Syrian Circassians and Turkish Karachays who settled in the republic *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Circassian and Karachay ethnography (mostly in Russian); thinner for Abaza and Nogai; thin for republic-level association statistics.
- **Lands:** the tracker gives the pre-war lands only as "Russia", so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet and the others in this sign are written for the core lands of the Karachay-Cherkess Republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Karachay-Balkar, Kabardian-Cherkess, Abaza and Nogai for press and folklore; Turkish and Arabic for diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1943 deportation and the 1864 Circassian expulsion; the balance of posts among peoples; the 1999 election crisis; religious policing; outlets designated in Russia (RFE/RL's Kavkaz.Realii) put informants at risk.
- **Held for review:** none.
