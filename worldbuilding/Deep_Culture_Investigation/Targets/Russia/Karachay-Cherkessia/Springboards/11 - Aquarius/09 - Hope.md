## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: the generation that waited in exile against those born after 1957, for whom return is a story told to them.
- **Cherkess**: whether hopes are tied to the diaspora's return and to political standing.
- **Abazins**: whether hope centers on a small language's survival.
- **Nogais**: hopes for their district and their steppe kin outside the republic.
- **Russians and Cossack descendants**: those who stay and those who leave the republic.
- **Young people**: leaving for Moscow or abroad against building a life at home.
- **Families with sick members**, whose hope rests on treatment and on strangers' help.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the exile-and-return story; mixed for present expectations (regional survey samples are small); thin for old wishing rites in print.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for songs and blessings; Nogai for spring customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the deportation; political hopes of smaller peoples, which touch borders and status; suicide data, where families may hide causes.
- **Held for review:** none.
