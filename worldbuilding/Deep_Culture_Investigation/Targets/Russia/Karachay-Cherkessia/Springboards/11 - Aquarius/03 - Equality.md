## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of princely and noble lineages** against those of commoner and bondsman lineages, among Karachays and Cherkess: whether old rank still matters in marriage or standing.
- **Men and women** in each people, and **elders and juniors**: equality across sex and age.
- **Karachays, Cherkess, Abazins, Nogais and Russians** as peoples with unequal shares of office and population.
- **Abazins and Nogais**, the two smallest titular groups: equality as a small people's demand.
- **Cossack descendants against descendants of settlers** in the stanitsas.
- **Cherkessk against the mountain districts**: incomes and services.
- **Returnees and repatriates** (from Central Asia, Syria, Turkey) against settled residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 19th-century estates and Soviet policy; mixed for gender; regional statistics exist but rarely split by people.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for terms of rank and address; English for traveler sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shares of office among peoples; women's rights, including marriage by abduction; descent from bondsmen, a private matter in families. The law of equal rights belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
