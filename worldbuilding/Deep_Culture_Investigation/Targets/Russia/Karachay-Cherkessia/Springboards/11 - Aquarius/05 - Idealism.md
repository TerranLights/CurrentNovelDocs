# Karachay-Cherkessia: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: the standard of *namys* as kept by elders, and as restated by young devout Muslims.
- **Cherkess**: *adyghaghe* among villagers, townspeople and diaspora returnees.
- **Abazins**: whether a separate named code exists, or the Circassian one is shared.
- **Nogais**: the steppe epic ideal against present village life.
- **Russians and Cossack descendants**: Cossack honor and Orthodox ideals.
- **The Soviet-educated generation**: communist ideals and the 1990s let-down.
- **Climbers, scientists and teachers**, whose callings carry their own ideals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for codes of conduct in Circassian studies; mixed for Karachay ethics; thin for Abaza and Nogai ideals in print.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Kabardian-Cherkess and Karachay-Balkar for songs and poetry; Nogai for the epic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious ideals touch "extremism" policing; ethnic codes can be used to rank peoples. Do not adopt outsiders' "noble mountaineer" image as a finding.
