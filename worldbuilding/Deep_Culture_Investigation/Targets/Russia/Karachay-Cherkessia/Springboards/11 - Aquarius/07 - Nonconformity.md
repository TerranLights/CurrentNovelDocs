## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karachays**: young men and women who break with a family's or a mosque's expectations, and what the shame words do to them.
- **Cherkess**: whether a person "without khabze" is talked about differently in villages and in town.
- **Abazins and Nogais**, small peoples in whom one departure is visible to all.
- **Religious camps** (official Muslims, Salafis, converts): to compare whom each camp names as the deviant.
- **Russians** of Cherkessk and the stanitsas: whether youth subcultures there met the same sanctions.
- **Gay and transgender people**, whose safety limits what can be asked at all.
- **Returnees** from Turkey and Syria: whether their habits are read as departures from local custom.
