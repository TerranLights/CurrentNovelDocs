# Karachay-Cherkessia: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Karachay-Cherkessia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar; Kabardian-Cherkess; Abaza; Nogai. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **High Karachay auls** (Uchkulan, Khurzuk, Kart-Dzhurt *check*) against Karachay villages on the plain settled after the 1957 return *check*: old and new place communities.
- **Cherkess villages** of the Khabez district against Cherkess living in Cherkessk apartment blocks: whether the village bond survives a move to town.
- **Abaza villages** (Psyzh, Kara-Pago, Inzhich-Chukun *check*), scattered among other peoples' villages.
- **Nogai auls** (Erken-Shakhar, Ikon-Khalk *check*), with roots in the steppe rather than the mountains.
- **Cossack stanitsas** (Zelenchukskaya, Ispravnaya, Pregradnaya *check*): a settler community with its own assembly tradition.
- **The observatory settlement** at Nizhny Arkhyz *check*: a scientists' community with no kin base.
- **Shepherds on the summer pastures**, a seasonal community, and **village people working in Moscow or abroad**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 19th-century and Soviet village description; mixed for the present; village-level material is often local and offline.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Karachay-Balkar and Kabardian-Cherkess for local press and family histories; Abaza and Nogai for those villages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation and exile testimony; land disputes between peoples over returned or resettled villages; Memorial's archives are politically restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the SMOMPK index for *карачаевцы* and *черкесы*, and read the village descriptions.
2. In the Russian State Library catalog: *тукъум*, *карачаевские аулы история*, *колхоз Карачаево-Черкесия*.
3. Pull village population series for the Karachayevsky and Khabez districts from Rosstat's municipal database.
4. Search VKontakte for groups named *Учкулан*, *Хабез*, *Псыж* and *Эркен-Шахар* and note what they post.
5. Search the Higher School of Economics site for *абазинская экспедиция*.
