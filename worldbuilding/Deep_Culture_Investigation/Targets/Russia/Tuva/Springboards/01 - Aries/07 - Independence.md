## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding households** in western, central and southeastern kozhuuns: herd size and self-provision differ by pasture.
- **Tozhu Tuvans:** taiga subsistence against supplies flown or trucked in.
- **Salaried and pensioner households** in Kyzyl, Shagonar and Ak-Dovurak.
- **Old Believers:** hermitage self-reliance and village farms.
- **Russian farming towns** (Turan, Piy-Khem): settler-style household farms.
- **Women heads of household and elders:** reliance on kin, pensions and benefits.
- **Labor migrants** and their home families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in statistics and pastoral ethnography; thin on attitudes to asking for help, which need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Manchu for tribute records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** independence movements and the 1990s tensions are politically sensitive; poverty and subsidy figures invite stereotype: present them as context, not character. Statutes on sovereignty, benefits and land go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
