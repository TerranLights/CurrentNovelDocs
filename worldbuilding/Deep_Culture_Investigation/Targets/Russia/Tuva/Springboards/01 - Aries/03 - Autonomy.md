## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** in western and southeastern kozhuuns against salaried families in Kyzyl and Ak-Dovurak: different pathways to a household of one's own.
- **Tozhu Tuvans:** small reindeer and hunting camps; clan communities as collective self-rule.
- **Women and men:** marriage choice, property and residence after marriage.
- **Generations:** those who went through Soviet boarding schools and collectivization against those raised after 1991.
- **Old Believers:** community self-rule and refusal of outside control.
- **Kyzyl Russians** and mixed families: where decisions sit.
- **Religious lines:** Buddhist, shamanist and Christian families on medical and life choices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. History of collective autonomy is rich; personal decision-making in families is thin and needs ethnography and social media.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for Qing records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** constitutional sovereignty, self-government statutes and land rights are law: route to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit); boarding-school memories may be painful; Old Believer communities may refuse study.
- **Held for review:** none.
