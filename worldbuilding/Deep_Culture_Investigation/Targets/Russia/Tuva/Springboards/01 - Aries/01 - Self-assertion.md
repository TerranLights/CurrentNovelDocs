## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and the young:** speaking order and the kin-term ladder may differ most here.
- **Rural herding kozhuuns against Kyzyl:** Tuvan-dominant speech settings against bilingual offices and schools.
- **Women:** herding households, urban professionals, women performers and athletes.
- **Western and southeastern Tuva:** dialect and border influence (Altai, Mongol) on speech etiquette.
- **Tozhu Tuvans:** small taiga camps, a different audience for a claim.
- **Russians:** Old Believer community meetings on the upper Yenisei; Kyzyl's Russian-speaking public life.
- **Clergy and shamans:** lamas and shamans as licensed public voices; separate them.
- **Returnees:** graduates back from Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk or Moscow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and epic are rich; everyday assertion is thin in print and needs observation and social media.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Manchu for the Qing archive.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic chants may be restricted in use; workplace disputes and anything touching law go to Libra or Capricorn; avoid a single "Tuvan temperament".
- **Held for review:** none.
