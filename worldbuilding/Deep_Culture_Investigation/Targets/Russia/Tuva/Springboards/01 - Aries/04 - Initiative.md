## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding households** (western and southeastern kozhuuns): self-started work on the camp against state-led agricultural programs.
- **Kyzyl's traders and small-business owners:** retail, services, cross-border trade.
- **Craft districts:** Bai-Taiga carvers; felt and leather makers.
- **Tozhu Tuvans:** hunting and reindeer work, and new tourism ventures in the taiga.
- **Russians:** Old Believer farms and trades on the upper Yenisei; Turan and Piy-Khem farming towns.
- **Women:** women-led small business and crafts; women's volunteer networks.
- **Generations:** Soviet-trained managers, 1990s traders, young freelancers and the self-employed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Business statistics are open; cultural attitudes to acting unasked are thin and need ethnography and folklore.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for border trade.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** none special; avoid reading poverty statistics as a verdict on initiative. Labor law and regulation go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
