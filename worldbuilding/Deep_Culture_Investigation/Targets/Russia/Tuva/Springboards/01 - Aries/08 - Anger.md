*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women; boys and girls:** different display rules are likely and need separate sampling.
- **Buddhist, shamanist and Christian households:** anger as affliction, as spirit harm, or as sin.
- **Kyzyl and rural kozhuuns:** crowding, unemployment and the cooling role of kin and elders.
- **Western and southeastern Tuva:** dialect vocabularies and Mongol-border usage.
- **Tozhu Tuvans:** small-camp life where quarrels cannot be escaped.
- **Russians and Tuvans** in Kyzyl: memories of 1990 and present tensions, from both sides.
- **Generations:** those who lived through 1990 against those born after it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Ritual and religious framings are rich; everyday display rules are thin; violence and crime figures exist but are a stereotype trap.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian; Tibetan for teaching texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and the 1990 events involve living victims; shamanic curse rites may be restricted; alcohol and crime figures and anything legal go to Libra or Capricorn; revenge and feuds go to Scorpio. Never frame a people as "hot-tempered".
- **Held for review:** none.
