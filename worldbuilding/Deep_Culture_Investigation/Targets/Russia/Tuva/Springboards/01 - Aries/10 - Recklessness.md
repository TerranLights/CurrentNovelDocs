## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men and young women:** driving, drink and daring may split sharply by gender and age.
- **Hunters and taiga workers** (Tozhu, western taiga, upper Yenisei): risk at work against risk for thrill.
- **Herding households:** ice crossings, winter travel and stock moves.
- **Kyzyl youth:** cars, motorcycles, nightlife.
- **Buddhist, shamanist and Christian families:** caution sought from lamas, diviners or prayer.
- **Russians:** Old Believer caution and Russian-speaking youth culture in Kyzyl.
- **Tourists and guides:** outsiders' risk-taking in Tuvan wilds.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in statistics (road deaths, injuries, fires); thin on local meanings of daring, which need folklore and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan; Mongolian; Old Turkic in transliteration.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol, drugs and crime figures are a stereotype trap and touch law (route to Libra or Capricorn); divination practice may be private; road and ice deaths touch grieving families.
- **Held for review:** none.
