*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western high-pasture herders** (Mongun-Taiga, Bai-Taiga): cold, altitude and loss of stock as endurance tests.
- **Tozhu Tuvans:** taiga hunting and reindeer work; danger in remote camps.
- **Southeastern steppe herders** (Erzin, Tes-Khem): Mongol-border hero vocabulary.
- **Families of soldiers and veterans:** wartime courage across generations.
- **Lamas and shamans:** keeping faith under repression as a separate form of courage.
- **Women:** endurance, protection of the household, women volunteers and rescuers.
- **Russians:** Old Believers' memory of persecution; Kyzyl's war-memorial culture.
- **Urban youth** in wrestling, boxing and mixed martial arts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for epic, war history and repression memory; thin for everyday moral courage.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Old Turkic in transliteration; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recent military deaths and their memorials are a live grief: approach with care and no recruitment material; repression testimony involves living families; hunters' rites toward animals may be restricted; avoid a single "warrior people" frame.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Vasilyev corpus and its translations for "ärdäm" and list the deeds it is tied to.
2. Search eLibrary for "Самдан героический эпос тувинцев" and note the hero-test motifs.
3. Search "тувинские добровольцы воспоминания" for published memoirs and letters.
4. Find the repression memory book for Tuva and note which testimonies speak of resistance.
5. Ask TIGPI's manuscript fund for its catalog of hunters' and hero tales by kozhuun.
