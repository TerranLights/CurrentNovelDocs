## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kozhuun teams:** western, central, southeastern and Tozhu districts as rival blocs at Naadym.
- **Herding families** that raise racehorses against city families in Olympic-style sport.
- **Women and girls:** women's archery, chess and khoomei contests, and women's khuresh where it is held; their standing.
- **Children:** jockeys and young wrestlers; school competition and exams.
- **Russians and other residents:** participation in Tuvan games against Russian-style sport.
- **Buddhist and shamanist settings:** contests held at or blessed by temples and offerings.
- **Diaspora athletes:** Tuvans competing for Russian teams abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport and festival; thin for attitudes to envy and losing, which need folklore and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child jockeys and children's welfare; exam rankings that place the Republic low invite stereotype: treat as context; betting rules and sport law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
