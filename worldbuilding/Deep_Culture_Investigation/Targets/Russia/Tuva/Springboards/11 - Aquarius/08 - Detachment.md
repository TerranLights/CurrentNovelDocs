# Tuva: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for contemplative teaching. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings or from outcomes; valued as calm or equanimity, condemned as coldness or withdrawal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Monastics** in retreat and **lay Buddhists** who practice calm in daily life.
- **Herding families** in remote winter camps against **Kyzyl's** apartment dwellers.
- **Tozhu hunters** who spend seasons alone.
- **Old Believers**, with withdrawal written into their faith.
- **Men and women**, and **elders and the young**, under avoidance and display customs.
- **Russians and Tuvans**, whose styles of reserve may be read wrongly by each other.
- **Elders left alone** in emptied sumons against those living with kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Ethnography of funerals and avoidance customs is solid; present-day emotional styles are thin and easily stereotyped.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Tibetan for contemplative texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims that a people is "reserved" or "cold" are stereotypes unless measured, and should be listed as claims; avoidance customs touch on family life and women's position; some retreat and funeral practices may be private.
- **Held for review:** none. (Impartiality of judges and officials is for Libra.)
