# Tuva: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for Buddhist ethics; English for outsiders' images. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Monastics and lay Buddhists**; **shamanic practitioners**; **Christian converts**: three sets of ideals of the good life.
- **The Soviet generation** of teachers, doctors and officials against those who came of age after 1991.
- **Old Believers**, whose settlement story is tied to a utopian legend.
- **Western herding districts and Kyzyl**: ideals of the herder's life against urban careers.
- **Tozhu**: ideals of taiga life as their own people state them.
- **Tuvans abroad** in Mongolia and Xinjiang, and students in Russian cities, for ideals of the homeland seen from far away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Epic, Buddhist and Soviet ideals are well documented; present-day idealism and its loss are thin, found mostly in interviews, press and online.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Tibetan for Buddhist texts; Church Slavonic for Old Believer writings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** outsiders' romantic images of Tuva (throat singing, shamans, "the last nomads") must be named as such and kept apart from local voices; some shamanic teaching is not for outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
