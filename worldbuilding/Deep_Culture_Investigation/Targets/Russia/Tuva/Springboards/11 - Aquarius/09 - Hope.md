# Tuva: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for Buddhist teaching; Old Turkic for the earliest layer. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** of the west and south, hoping for good grass and herds.
- **Kyzyl** families, whose hopes may center on schooling and state jobs.
- **Young people** deciding whether to leave for Siberian cities.
- **Tozhu**, whose hopes are tied to reindeer and the taiga.
- **Buddhists, shamanic clients and Christians**, who place hope in different powers.
- **Old Believers**, with their own expectation of the End.
- **Russians** in Kyzyl and Turan, many of whom have left or think of leaving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Ritual and blessing material is rich in Tuvan folklore; measured hope (polls, youth plans) is thin and must be found in regional studies.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Tibetan for Buddhist terms; Old Turkic for the comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and alcohol deaths are grave and easily turned into stereotype; use them only with local voices and care. Divination and some shamanic rites may be private.
- **Held for review:** none.
