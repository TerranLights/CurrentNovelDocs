# Tuva: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for steppe sworn friendship and the border; English for foreign friends of Tuva. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women**, by generation: boarding-school cohorts, Soviet workplaces, young urban friend groups.
- **Herding districts** of the west and south against **Kyzyl**, where friends may matter more than neighbors.
- **Tozhu** hunters and herders, where partners in work may be the key bond.
- **Tuvan-Russian friendships** in mixed towns (Kyzyl, Turan, Ak-Dovurak) against separate lives.
- **Old Believers**, who may keep friendship inside the faith.
- **Students and migrants** in Siberian cities.
- **Diaspora Tuvans** in Mongolia and Xinjiang.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Formal bonds (sworn friendship, in-laws) may appear in ethnography; everyday friendship is scarcely studied and will need interviews, songs and online material.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Mongolian for the steppe comparison; English for foreign friends' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "friendship of peoples" is an official frame for 1944 and should be read against other voices; foreign friends' accounts lean romantic and should not stand for Tuvans' own view.
- **Held for review:** none.
