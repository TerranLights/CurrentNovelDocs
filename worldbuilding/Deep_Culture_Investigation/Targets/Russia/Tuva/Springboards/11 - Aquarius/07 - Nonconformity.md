# Tuva: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for the holy-madman tradition; Hungarian and German for early fieldwork. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers**: dissent as the community's founding act.
- **Shamans** called against their will, against **Buddhist clergy** and lay believers who judge them.
- **Kyzyl youth** (rock, rap, online subcultures) against **young herders** in the west and south.
- **Tozhu**, where taiga life may itself be seen as the odd way by steppe Tuvans; check how each side speaks of the other.
- **Christian converts** in a Buddhist and shamanic setting.
- **Artists and musicians who left** for Moscow or Europe, against those who stayed.
- **Russians and Tuvans** in mixed towns, where each may be the other's "different".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Shamanic calling and Old Believer dissent are well studied; youth and artistic nonconformity are thin, mostly music press and online.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Tibetan for the Buddhist frame; Hungarian, German and French for some fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Russian federal restrictions on LGBT expression make gender and sexual nonconformity a risk to research and to sources; keep it to published and anonymous material (the rules themselves belong to Libra and Capricorn). Shamanic calling and mental illness must be handled with the practitioners' own words. Old Believers may not wish to be studied.
- **Held for review:** none.
