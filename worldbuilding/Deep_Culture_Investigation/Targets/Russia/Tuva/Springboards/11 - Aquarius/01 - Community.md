# Tuva: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Chinese for the cross-border Tuvan communities. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life, recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders' camps** in the Khemchik basin and Mongun-Taiga against **sumon villages** and their schools and clubs.
- **Kyzyl** apartment blocks and the self-built outskirts where rural migrants settled.
- **Old Believer villages** of the Upper Yenisei (Erzhei, Uzhep and others *check*): closed, faith-defined communities.
- **Russian farming villages** of Piy-Khem and Turan, from the settler period.
- **Tozhu** reindeer-herding and hunting camps, scattered across the taiga.
- **Southern border communities** of Erzin and Tes-Khem.
- **Monastery communities** at restored khurees, and the newer Christian congregations.
- **Cross-border Tuvan communities** in Mongolia and Xinjiang, as contrast cases.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Soviet and Russian ethnography covers camp life well; present-day community life is thin except in local press and online.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian first; Mongolian and Chinese for the diaspora; Old Mongolian script for Qing records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Old Believer communities often shun outsiders and cameras; cairn rites may involve knowledge kept within lineages; the earthquake and relief coverage involves recent losses.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search Russian scholarly databases (eLibrary, CyberLeninka) for "соседская община тувинцев" and "аал тувинцев".

3. Search the Ministry of Justice NPO registry for organizations registered in Tyva, sorted by type.
4. Search press archives for "землетрясение Тыва 2011 помощь" and "Каа-Хем землетрясение".
5. Search "старообрядцы Верхнего Енисея" for the Siberian Branch expedition publications.
