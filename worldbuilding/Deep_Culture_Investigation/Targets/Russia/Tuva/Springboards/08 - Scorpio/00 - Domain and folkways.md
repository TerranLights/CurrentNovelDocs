# Tuva: The domain, death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (тыва дыл, Cyrillic with ң, ө, ү), Russian; Mongolian for cross-border kin. **Concept in one line:** how the culture faces death, power and what is hidden, through its death ways, sex ways, shared resources, inheritance and secrets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Tuva** (Bai-Taiga, Barun-Khemchik, Mongun-Taiga, Ovyur, Sut-Khol): high-mountain herding; customs may differ from the center.
- **Central Tuva** (Ulug-Khem, Dzun-Khemchik, Chaa-Khol, Piy-Khem): kurgan country and the old princely seats.
- **Southern Tuva** (Erzin, Tes-Khem, Tere-Khol): steppe and the Ubsunur basin along the Mongolian border.
- **Todzha (Tozhu) Tuvans**: eastern taiga reindeer herders and hunters; a separate dialect and burial history.
- **Kyzyl and the towns** (Ak-Dovurak, Chadan, Shagonar, Turan): funeral homes, cemeteries and money against the herding camp.
- **Russians of Tuva**, including **Old Believers** of the upper Yenisei (Kaa-Khem): Orthodox and priestless death ways.
- **Faith lines**: Buddhist, shamanist, both at once, Orthodox, Protestant converts, the nonreligious.
- **Cross-border kin**: Tuvans of Mongolia (Tsengel; the Dukha reindeer herders) and of China's Altai.
- **Clans (söök)**: inheritance, marriage rules and naming; separate by clan where sources allow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Death ways, shamanism, archaeology and vital statistics are rich; sex ways, money in marriage and secrecy are thin and will lean on folklore, press and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Mongolian; German (two sources); Chinese and Manchu (Qing-era and Xinjiang material).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamans' knowledge as their keepers choose to share it; human remains and grave goods from the kurgans held in museums; suicide, alcohol deaths and war deaths; the repressions. **Scope:** the tracker carries "Russia" as pre-war lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this pass writes for the core lands of the Republic of Tuva.
- **Held for review:** none.
