# Tuva: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Manchu for the Qing-era nobility; Chinese for the mining firms' own reports. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how the culture judges who holds it and how (legitimate authority goes to Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Tuva (the Khemchik valley)** and **central Tuva**: old princely seats and present leaders' home districts.
- **Kyzyl's officials, managers and teachers** against **herding families** whose power is in livestock.
- **Clans (söök)** with many officeholders against those with few.
- **Women in office and in households**, from Khertek Anchimaa-Toka to now.
- **Shamans, lamas and their followers**: spiritual power and its claims on public life.
- **Mining towns and districts** beside the Kyzyl-Tashtyg and Elegest works.
- **Russians and Old Believers**: a minority's place in republic power.
- **Todzha Tuvans**: a small group dealing with firms and the state over taiga land.
- **Tuvans of Mongolia**, a minority within Bayan-Ölgii's Kazakh majority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal political history; mixed for present elites (press, official biographies); thin for hidden power and household power.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian, Manchu, Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan politics invites stereotype, so test it with records and name variation; reporting on federal figures and security bodies is politically sensitive; legitimate authority and rank go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
