# Tuva: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Oirat for older compensation customs. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing or deter the next wrong, and the alternatives to it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding kozhuuns where livestock theft is common** (the south and west) against **Kyzyl**: payback over herds against payback in the city.
- **Clans (söök)**: whether a wrong against one member is a wrong against the clan, and for how long.
- **Shamanist families** (spiritual payback, curses returned) against **Buddhist teaching** of letting go.
- **Todzha hunters**: retribution from spirit-masters of the taiga.
- **Young men in towns** against elders who mediate.
- **Tuvans and Russians** of the republic, with the memory of 1990.
- **Old Believers**: a separate teaching on forgiveness and enmity.
- **Tuvans of Mongolia**, for border feuds seen from the other side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and curse lore are rich; homicide data exist; modern feuds and vigilante acts are known only through the press and must be read carefully.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian, Oirat (for the 1640 code).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violent crime and vigilante acts; the 1990 clashes; shamans' curse work as they choose to share it. Private vengeance against the state's punishment is the spirit of the law and goes to Capricorn; compensation codes as written go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
