# Tuva: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for border kin and Qing-era terms. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so (self-control goes to Capricorn; freedom from control to Aries).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Steppe herders of the south and west** against **taiga herders and hunters of Todzha**: different land, different state controls.
- **Border-zone kozhuuns** (Erzin, Tes-Khem, Mongun-Taiga, Ovyur) against inland districts.
- **Families with boarding-school generations** against those whose children stayed home.
- **Kyzyl's salaried households** against camp households: whose clock and whose rules.
- **Women and men, elders and the young**: who decides in the household and at the camp.
- **Riverbank settlements** along the Yenisei and the reservoir's edge: control of water from outside.
- **Old Believers**: strict self-government against state control.
- **Tuvans of Mongolia**: a different state's controls over herding and movement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. State control of herders is well documented; personal and household control is thin and needs fieldwork; divination and weather lore are scattered across ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family violence; the security services and the border zone; any rule on alcohol or movement is law and goes to Capricorn, which takes its spirit; self-control also goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
