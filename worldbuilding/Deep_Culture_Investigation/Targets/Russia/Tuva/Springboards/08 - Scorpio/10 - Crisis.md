# Tuva: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for winter disasters shared across the border. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, a family, a community or a country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders of Erzin, Tes-Khem and Mongun-Taiga**: exposure to winter disaster and drought.
- **Todzha and Kaa-Khem taiga communities**: fire, isolation and the reach of rescue.
- **Kyzyl's residents**: earthquakes, floods, smoke and the 1990s collapse of jobs.
- **Russians who left in the 1990s** and those who stayed; their accounts of the same years.
- **Families in long hardship** against salaried households: the slow crisis against the sudden one.
- **Buddhist, shamanist and Christian households**: how each reads and answers a disaster.
- **Generations**: those who lived through the 1990s as adults, against those born after.
- **Tuvans of Mongolia**, hit by the same winters under another state's relief system.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official emergency and demographic records; mixed for household coping; thin for personal and mental-health crises outside hotline statistics.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1990s out-migration and its causes are disputed; epidemic counts are politically charged; mobilization and war losses are a present crisis and are handled through the Death sheet with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
