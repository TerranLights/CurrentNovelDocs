# Tuva: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for cross-border ties. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down; loyalty is researched here for now.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding camps** against **Kyzyl apartment blocks**: trust inside a camp group against trust among strangers.
- **Clans (söök) and in-laws**: whether trust follows the clan, the marriage or the neighborhood.
- **Tuvans and Russians of the republic**: interethnic trust after the 1990s, from both sides.
- **Old Believers of the upper Yenisei**: a narrow radius of trust by choice.
- **Todzha Tuvans**: a small community dealing with outside firms for land and game.
- **Border kozhuuns**: trust and distrust toward kin and herders across the Mongolian line.
- **Generations**: those who lived through the late Soviet collapse of savings, against the young.
- **Tuvans living outside the republic**, and returnees.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. National pollsters rarely publish a Tuva subsample; local surveys exist but are scattered; election and health data are rich and need careful reading.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** election analysis is politically charged; interethnic trust after 1990 is painful; trust in police and courts is the spirit of the law and goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
