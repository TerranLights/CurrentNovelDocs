# Tuva: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Classical Tibetan for the lamas' day-reckoning; Old Turkic for the oldest omen text. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders of the high west and the steppe:** weather and animal sense.
- **Tozhu hunters and reindeer herders:** forest knowledge.
- **Lamas and shamans:** two kinds of insight, two kinds of authority.
- **Doctors, teachers and officials trained in Russian institutions.**
- **Kyzyl's young adults:** online dream and omen talk.
- **Russian residents and Old Believers:** Orthodox and folk omens of their own.
- **Women and men:** whose intuition is trusted, in which domains.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for divination and folk knowledge; thin for everyday hunches and expert judgment outside healing.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian, Classical Tibetan; Old Turkic for *Irk Bitig*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some divination methods are a shaman's own, not for display; calendrical day-reckoning is a living religious service; record divination as belief and practice only, never as a source.
- **Held for review:** none.
