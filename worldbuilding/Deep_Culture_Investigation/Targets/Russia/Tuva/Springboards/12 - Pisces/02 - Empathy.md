# Tuva: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan and Russian (most people are bilingual, with uneven depth); Tozhu speech for the northeast; Mongolian for the border districts. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural herders against Kyzyl's office and student population:** different settings for reading others.
- **Tuvan-dominant speakers against Russian-dominant Tuvans:** language may shape who feels understood.
- **Russian residents and the Old Believer villages:** empathy across the ethnic line, and inside a closed faith.
- **Tozhu taiga people:** relations with animals and forest.
- **Western, Mongolian-facing districts:** song and kin customs may differ.
- **Men and women; young and old:** who may weep, who may sing out feeling.
- **Tuvans in Mongolia and China:** as contrast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in song, drama and ritual forms; thin in measured empathy; local psychology papers exist but samples are small.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Old Turkic readings for the epitaphs; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1990 events are painful on both sides; helpline data is confidential; shamans' spirit-voicing may be restricted knowledge.
- **Held for review:** none.
