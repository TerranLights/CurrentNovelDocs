# Tuva: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Manchu, Mongolian and Chinese for the Qing conquest; Classical Tibetan for rites of dying. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher; release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families of high pastures (Mongun-Taiga, Bai-Taiga)** facing hard winters.
- **Steppe farmers and townspeople** whose losses are of other kinds.
- **Buddhist and shamanist families:** different rites of release at death.
- **Orthodox Russians and Old Believers:** humility and endurance in Christian terms.
- **Tozhu taiga people:** acceptance of the forest's limits.
- **Elders who lived through the 1930s and their grandchildren.**
- **Men and women:** who may yield, and where (sport, family quarrels, illness).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; history and ritual are well recorded; personal letting go and end-of-life choices are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Manchu, Mongolian and Chinese for the Qing period; Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1944 and the 1930s are political; child abandonment touches living families; rites for the dead may be closed; outsiders' "fatalism" label must never be adopted.
- **Held for review:** none.
