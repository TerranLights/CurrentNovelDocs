# Tuva: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (most health and policy data), Tuvan (songs, talk, local press); Mongolian for the border districts. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kyzyl's young adults:** screens, music, nightlife.
- **Village herders and the unemployed of former industrial towns (Ak-Dovurak, Khovu-Aksy):** different reasons and means.
- **Tozhu taiga communities:** isolation, seasonal work.
- **Western districts:** milk-spirit traditions and Mongolian ties.
- **Buddhist and shamanist families and Orthodox Russians:** different abstinence routes.
- **Old Believers:** a faith that forbids drink and tobacco for many members.
- **Men and women; young and old:** who drinks and who is blamed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for alcohol statistics and policy; mixed for drugs; thin for other escapes (games, fantasy reading).
- **Languages needed:** Russian first, Tuvan for songs and talk, Mongolian for western comparisons.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drinking is the subject of an old outsiders' stereotype about the region's peoples, so the research names variation and the causes people give, never one image; addiction records are confidential; hemp harvesting is a criminal matter.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search Russian news for "Тыва ограничение продажи алкоголя" and gather the dates and debates.
2. Pull Rosstat "продажа алкогольной продукции на душу населения" for Республика Тыва.
3. Find the national narcology center's regional report and Tuva's rows.
4. Search the Tuvan press for "кодирование", "зарок" and "лама" with "арага".
5. Search eLibrary.ru for "хойтпак арага обряд".
