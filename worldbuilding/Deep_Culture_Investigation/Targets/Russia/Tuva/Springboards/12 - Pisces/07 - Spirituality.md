# Tuva: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Classical Tibetan and Mongolian for the Buddhist record; Chinese and Kazakh for the Tuvans of Xinjiang. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion (the experience and practice; institutions are Sagittarius's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buddhist-leaning western districts (Barun-Khemchik, Dzun-Khemchik, Mongun-Taiga)** against **central and eastern districts:** the balance of lama and shaman.
- **Tozhu taiga people:** forest spirits and reindeer.
- **Kyzyl's urban seekers:** neo-shamanic and New Age practice, including tourists' influence.
- **Tuvan Protestant converts:** a smaller, separate stream.
- **Russian Orthodox and Old Believers:** two Christian spiritualities with different rules.
- **The secular and Soviet-raised generation:** nonbelief and its forms.
- **Tuvans in Mongolia and China.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; a much-studied field with many native scholars; surveys exist but differ in method.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Classical Tibetan, Mongolian; Chinese for Kanas.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some rites are closed to outsiders; the 1930s destruction of temples and killing of clergy; rivalry among shamans' groups and between faiths; tourism can pressure sacred places.
- **Held for review:** none.
