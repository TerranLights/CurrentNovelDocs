# Tuva: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tozhu speech for taiga lore; Classical Tibetan for temple imagery. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families of the steppe and high pastures:** predators, hard winters, night travel.
- **Tozhu taiga people:** forest spirits, bears, isolation.
- **Kyzyl residents:** floods, crime fears, online scary tales.
- **Quake-affected districts (Kaa-Khem and nearby):** fear of the land.
- **Families with repression victims:** a political fear passed down.
- **Russian residents and Old Believers:** their own fears, including fear of being a minority.
- **Children, teens and elders:** whose fears are taken seriously.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for spirit lore and disasters; mixed for political fear; thin for measured anxiety and fear of crime at the republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for temple texts; Mongolian for western parallels.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some names of spirits and of the dead are not said aloud; youth suicide is a grave and private subject; the 1930s files touch living families; records of the repression may be hard to reach.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Find *Тувинские героические сказания* (Novosibirsk folklore series) in a library catalog.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "албыс", "кут тувинцы" and "испуг лечение тувинцы".
3. Pull the emergency ministry's Tuva reports on the 2011 and 2012 earthquakes.
4. Search the archived Memorial lists and the National Archive for Tuvan repression files.
5. Search VKontakte for "коргунчуг тоолдар".
