# Tuva: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Classical Tibetan for monastic vows; Old Turkic for the oldest inscriptions. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **War-memory families** across the districts that sent volunteers.
- **Herding households:** the eldest child's and the mother's share of the work.
- **Lamas and nuns against lay families:** the cost of vows.
- **Shamans and their kin:** the calling as a burden.
- **Kyzyl's urban families and the outward migrants:** sacrifice by moving away.
- **Russian Orthodox residents and Old Believers:** fasting and endurance in two forms of one faith.
- **Tozhu taiga families:** survival duties in a small community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war memory and ritual offerings; mixed for caregiving; thin for vows and the shaman's cost outside ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Mongolian and Tibetan for monastic material; German and English for Arzhaan reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recent war deaths and their families; state framing of sacrifice; burials and the dead at Arzhaan; healers' accounts of their sickness may be private.
- **Held for review:** none.
