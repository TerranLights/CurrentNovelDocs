# Tuva: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (statistics, courts, press), Tuvan (talk, shaming, tales); Mongolian for border livestock theft. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is researched in Libra and Capricorn; this sheet points only to the culture's idea of wrongdoing and the wrongdoer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **High-pasture herding districts:** livestock theft and cross-border rustling.
- **Kyzyl:** street crime, youth groups, the court and press center.
- **Former industrial towns (Ak-Dovurak, Khovu-Aksy):** joblessness and its wrongs.
- **Tozhu taiga and the mountain reserves:** poaching as livelihood and as offense.
- **Russian residents and Old Believers:** their own picture of the Tuvan majority and of wrongdoing, and the reverse.
- **Families of prisoners and of victims:** shame and blame on kin.
- **Young and old; men and women:** whose wrongs are seen, whose are hidden.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in statistics and press; mixed in ethnography; thin in voices of wrongdoers and victims.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Tuvan for talk and tales; Mongolian for the border.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional crime figures are often used to stereotype Tuvans; the research must name causes and variation people give, never one image; domestic violence and prison records concern living people; the 1990 interethnic violence is painful history.
- **Law:** Qing-era Mongol codes, Soviet and Russian criminal law and courts belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); cite those sheets.
- **Held for review:** none.
