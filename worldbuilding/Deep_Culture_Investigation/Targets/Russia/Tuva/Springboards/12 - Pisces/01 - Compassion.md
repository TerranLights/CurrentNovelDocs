# Tuva: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Classical Tibetan and Mongolian for Buddhist teaching; Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families of the western and southern districts:** help moves through kin and neighbors; animals are in daily care.
- **Tozhu reindeer herders and hunters:** sharing rules of a small taiga people, and treatment of game.
- **Kyzyl's townspeople:** charity through funds, social networks and the state.
- **Buddhist laity:** merit-making and gifts to temples.
- **Shamanist households:** help asked of spirits and healers.
- **Orthodox Russians and Old Believers:** church mercy and closed-community almsgiving.
- **Tuvans in Mongolia and China:** diaspora comparison cases.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and ethnography; mixed for charity and volunteering; thin for any survey of compassion itself.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Tibetan and Mongolian for Buddhist teaching; German for Taube.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fundraising pages name sick children; poverty coverage can shame recipients; mercy toward offenders is law, so it goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
