# Tuva: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Manchu or Chinese for the Qing-era borders; Church Slavonic and old Russian for Old Believer rules. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders of the western high pastures (Mongun-Taiga, Bai-Taiga)** against **steppe farmers of central Tuva:** land lines differ by use.
- **Tozhu reindeer herders and hunters:** taiga hunting grounds and game rules.
- **Southern border districts:** life inside the border zone.
- **Kyzyl apartment dwellers against yurt and house dwellers:** the house as boundary.
- **Old Believers and other Russians:** a purity line and an ethnic line.
- **Families across the Mongolian border:** kin divided by the state line.
- **Buddhist and shamanist users of sacred sites:** whose rules hold there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for material culture and border history; mixed for land use; thin for personal space and privacy.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian; Manchu or Chinese for Qing records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** border-zone material may be restricted; claims on land across the Mongolian line are political; sacred-site rules may exclude some people, a topic to approach through participants.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the National Library of Tuva for "Вайнштейн Мир кочевников".
2. Find Donahoe's dissertation in ProQuest or the university repository.
3. Pull 2016 agricultural census tables for Республика Тыва by kozhuun.
4. Search eLibrary.ru for "старообрядцы Тувы" and "граница Тувы и Монголии".
5. Ask the National Archive of Mongolia about Uriankhai banner files.
