*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Children at herding camps and in Kyzyl**: open land against apartment courtyards and screens.
- **Boys and girls**: which games each plays, without assuming fixed lines.
- **Adults and elders**: bone games, chess, cards and archery in later life.
- **Western, central and southern districts**: local game variants.
- **Tozhu children**: taiga and reindeer play.
- **Russian and Old Believer children**: their own games and holiday play.
- **Buddhist and shamanist households**: games tied to Shagaa and other festivals.
- **Cross-border Tuvans** in Mongolia: shared bone games.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; folk games are collected but scattered; digital and urban play are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some bone and dice games overlap with divining by casting, which practitioners may treat as serious, not play; gambling questions belong to the domain sheet.
- **Held for review:** none.
