# Tuva: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for cross-border terms. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women**: what honor asks of each, tested in each group's own words.
- **Lineages (сөөк)**: honor held by clan, and quarrels between them.
- **Herders and town dwellers**: word-keeping in livestock deals against salaried life.
- **Wrestlers and their home districts**: sport honor as district honor.
- **Tozhu hunters**: honor in taiga conduct.
- **Buddhists, shamanists and Christians**: different duties to the dead and to the word given.
- **Russians and Old Believers**: Orthodox and Old Believer ideas of honor and good name.
- **Veterans and their families**: military honor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for customary rules and sport ritual; mixed for clan quarrels; thin for measured honor attitudes.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan (key sources are in Tuvan only), Russian; Mongolian for comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor tied to women's conduct and to violence must be framed as a question, never a trait; recent wars are sensitive; any legal side of feuds belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
