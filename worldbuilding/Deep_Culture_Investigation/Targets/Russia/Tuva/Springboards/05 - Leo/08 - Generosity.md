# Tuva: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for cross-border comparison. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Steppe herders and taiga hunters** (central and western districts against Tozhu): different sharing rules.
- **Kyzyl's salaried families**: cash gifts, crowdfunding and formal charity.
- **Buddhist donors and shamanist households**: merit against offerings to spirits.
- **Christians and Old Believers**: alms and church giving.
- **The well-off and the poor**: giving for standing against giving from need.
- **Migrants in Russian cities**: giving home from afar.
- **Southern districts near Mongolia**: shared Mongolian forms to check.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hospitality and ritual offerings; mixed for gift exchange; thin for measured charity and remittances.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ritual offerings to spirits may involve practitioner knowledge; avoid portraying hospitality as a fixed trait of the people.
- **Held for review:** none.
