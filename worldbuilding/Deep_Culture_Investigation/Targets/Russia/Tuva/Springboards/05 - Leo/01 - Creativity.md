## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bai-Taiga and the western districts**: carving and music centers to check, not assume.
- **Kyzyl's trained artists and musicians**: conservatory and art-school training against learning by watching.
- **Tozhu reindeer herders**: taiga crafts and their own materials.
- **Southern steppe districts (Erzin, Tes-Khem)**: check felt and leather work shared with Mongolian neighbors.
- **Old Believer villages of the upper Yenisei**: their own crafts and any book-copying or icon tradition.
- **Town Russians and mixed families**: Soviet-style amateur art circles.
- **Buddhist monastery craft** (painting, sculpture, rebuilt temples): sacred making with fixed canons.
- **Younger urban musicians and producers**: fusion, recording and online release.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for music and carving; mixed for literature; thin for everyday improvising and invention.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; German for Tschinag; Mongolian for the cross-border comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamans' drums and ritual objects may carry restrictions on making and showing; craft sold to tourists and collectors raises questions of who profits.
- **Held for review:** none.
