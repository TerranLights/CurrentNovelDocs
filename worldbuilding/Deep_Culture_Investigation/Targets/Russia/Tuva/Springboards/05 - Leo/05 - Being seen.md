# Tuva: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; English for the outside gaze. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families at seasonal camps**: rarely seen by towns and offices.
- **Kyzyl's young and online**: seeking an audience.
- **Elders**: acknowledged first at Shagaa; possibly overlooked in town life.
- **Tozhu**: visibility as a distinct Indigenous people.
- **Old Believers**: chosen withdrawal from view.
- **Russians as a local minority**: whose language and history are visible in Kyzyl.
- **Women and girls**: dress, gaze and public presence, without assuming a single norm.
- **Tuvans in Russian cities and abroad**: being seen as Asian in Russia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; census and outsider images are rich; everyday gaze and greeting rules need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; English for the Western literature of Tuva as an exotic place.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some shamanic protective knowledge may be restricted; racism toward Tuvans elsewhere in Russia is sensitive and must be framed through documented cases only.
- **Held for review:** none.
