# Tuva: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Tibetan for didactic texts. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women**: what lowers each, and how it is answered.
- **Young conscripts and students** away from home: humiliation in Russian institutions.
- **Herders and town dwellers**: mockery across the rural-urban line.
- **Tuvans and local Russians**: memory of the 1990 tensions on both sides.
- **Tozhu**: a small people's experience of being looked down on, if any.
- **Buddhists, shamanists and Christians**: different teachings on humility and shame.
- **Old Believers**: their history of persecution and withdrawal.
- **Cross-border Tuvans** in Mongolia and China: being a minority there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; folklore and history are reachable, measured data on bullying and shaming are thin at the regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Tibetan for didactic and Buddhist texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic history, army hazing and imprisonment are sensitive; avoid tying humiliation or violence to the people as a trait; curse knowledge may be restricted by practitioners.
- **Held for review:** none.
