# Tuva: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for Qing-era and early-republic records. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineages and districts**: leadership by clan standing against leadership by office.
- **Herders and town officials**: practical leaders against appointed heads.
- **Shamans, lamas and pastors**: three religious kinds of leading.
- **Tozhu communities**: Indigenous self-organization.
- **Old Believers**: lay preceptors and elders.
- **Women in public and family life**: formal and informal leadership.
- **Russians of Kyzyl and the settler towns**: Soviet managerial traditions.
- **Cross-border Tuvans** under Mongolian and Chinese local governments.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Tuvan People's Republic's leaders and party records; mixed for clan and religious leadership; thin for everyday leadership in families and work.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Manchu for the Qing era.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** present-day regional politics and the 1930s purges are sensitive; formal authority and its legal side belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
