# Tuva: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and German for cross-border tale collections. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tuvan clans and districts** (surname-clans such as Mongush, Oorzhak, Ondar, Kuular, Saryglar, *check*): pride in lineage and home district.
- **Tozhu**: Indigenous status and taiga identity.
- **Western, central and southern Tuvans**: rival district prides at Naadym.
- **Russians of Kyzyl and the old settler villages; Old Believers**: their own pride in place and faith.
- **Urban professionals and rural herders**: different achievements on display.
- **Buddhists**: teaching on humility; shamanists and Christians, with other moral language on pride.
- **Tuvans of Mongolia and China**: pride in being Tuvan as a minority elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and war memory; mixed for folklore of pride; thin for everyday bragging norms.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; German and Mongolian for cross-border collections; Old Turkic for the runic texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** federal politics and the present war; the late-Soviet national movement and the 1990 interethnic tensions; avoid treating pride as a national trait.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the republic's official page on its symbols and the anthem's text and history.
2. Search the Russian State Library catalog for «История Тувы» and list the volumes covering 1921 to 1944.
3. Search eLibrary.ru for «тувинские пословицы гордость» and «этническая идентичность тувинцев».
4. Request Taube's *Tuwinische Volksmärchen* and note tales of arrogance and humbling.
5. Search the Vasilyev corpus for epitaphs listing deeds, with the find sites in Tuva.
