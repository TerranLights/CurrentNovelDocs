# Tuva: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Manchu-era Mongolian documents for the Qing period. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders and craftspeople against salaried professionals**: whose work reaches the honors lists.
- **Men and women**: titles for wrestlers and singers against recognition of mothers and teachers.
- **District communities** (western, central, southern, Tozhu): local heroes and district pride at festivals.
- **Clergy and practitioners**: lamas, shamans and pastors, each with their own ranks.
- **Russians and Old Believers**: Soviet and church forms of honor.
- **Families of repression victims**: recognition of wrongs.
- **Tuvans of Mongolia and China**: recognition as a minority people abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors and sport titles; mixed for hospitality honors; thin for workplace or everyday credit.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Manchu-era sources for the Qing period.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** repression records and Memorial's status in Russia (closed in 2021) make access uncertain; some shamanic titles are disputed between groups.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the republic's legal-information portal for «почетное звание» and list ten years of honorees by field.
2. Search *Новые исследования Тувы* for «хүреш звания» and «ужа».
3. Search the Memorial database export for «Тувинская» to gauge coverage.
4. Search philatelic catalogs for "Tannu Tuva" stamp issues and their catalog notes.
5. Ask the National Archive of the Republic of Tuva (*check*) for Soviet labor-award records by district.
