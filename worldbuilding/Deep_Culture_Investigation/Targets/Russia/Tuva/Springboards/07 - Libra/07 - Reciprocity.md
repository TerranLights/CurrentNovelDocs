# Tuva: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Manchu and Mongolian for tribute records. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding households** whose exchanges are in livestock, labor and meat, against **wage earners in Kyzyl** whose exchanges are in money and favors.
- **Todzha reindeer herders and hunters**: sharing meat and furs in a thin-population taiga.
- **Western and central districts**: offerings to land spirits and the size of shared rites.
- **Buddhist and shamanic practitioners**: different exchanges with the sacred.
- **Russian villagers and Old Believers**: the work-bee and church charity.
- **Women and men**: who keeps track of gifts, and whose labor is lent.
- **Tuvans living outside the republic**: what migrants send home and why.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich ethnography of offerings and gifts; thin data on money transfers; the livestock-loan program should have official reports.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Manchu, Mongolian and Chinese for tribute records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some offerings and rites are for participants only; payments to shamans and lamas are a delicate subject; describing kin obligation as a "burden" or a "virtue" imports a verdict.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Tyva government portal for "Кыштаг для молодой семьи" to find the program's rules and reports.
2. Search Google Scholar for "Purzycki Tyva cher eezi".
3. Search eLibrary.ru for "өнчү" and "тувинский похоронный обряд".
4. Find Rosstat's household-budget tables by region for transfers between households.
5. Search Tuvan folklore catalogs for grateful-animal tales.
