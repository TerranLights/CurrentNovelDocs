# Tuva: The domain, partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (тыва дыл, Cyrillic with ң, ө, ү), Russian; the Todzha dialect of the northeast; Classical (vertical-script) Mongolian, Manchu and Tibetan for the Qing-era and monastic papers. **Concept in one line:** what is a fair bargain and a good partnership? ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Tuvans** (Kyzyl, Ulug-Khem, Chaa-Khol): the urban core, where state law is densest.
- **Western Tuvans** (Barun-Khemchik, Dzun-Khemchik, Mongun-Taiga, Bai-Taiga, Ovyur, Sut-Khol): mountain herding districts with their own dialect and clan mix.
- **Southeastern Tuvans** (Erzin, Tes-Khem): steppe herders near the Mongolian border.
- **Todzha Tuvans** (Todzhinsky district, upper Tere-Khol): taiga reindeer herders and hunters, a distinct kin and exchange system.
- **Russians**: Old Believer settlements on the Kaa-Khem (upper Yenisei), and settler villages around Turan in Piy-Khem; separate marriage and church rules.
- **By faith**: Buddhists, shamanic practitioners, Orthodox, and the newer Protestant congregations *check*.
- **Kin across the border**: Tuvans of Mongolia (Tsengel; the Dukha of Khövsgöl *check*) and of Xinjiang's Altay (Kanas *check*), and the Tofa and Soyot of the Sayan, if the post-war lands reach them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Russian-language ethnography is rich; Tuvan-language work is scattered. The nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled (its tracker carries "Russia" as pre-war lands); this sign is written for the republic's core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian (both scripts), Manchu and Tibetan for older papers; German for Taube and Tschinag.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some shamanic and clan knowledge may be restricted by its keepers; violence and alcohol data invite stereotypes (name variation); the 1944 incorporation and the 1930s purges are charged; some pollsters carry "foreign agent" status.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library and eLibrary.ru for "обычное право тувинцев" and "тувинская свадьба".

3. Search *Новые исследования Тувы* for "чөптүг", "өглениишкин" and "куда" to find Tuvan-language authors.
4. Pull Tyvastat's marital-status tables by district from the 2010 and 2021 censuses.
5. Search Purzycki's publications for "Tyva" and "random allocation game" to find the experimental data.
