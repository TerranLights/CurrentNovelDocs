*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding households in an aal** against **town couples in Kyzyl**: partnership as shared labor against partnership as companionship.
- **Todzha households**: taiga partnerships for hunting and reindeer.
- **Western, central and southeastern districts**: courting customs and the weight of clan in a match.
- **Mixed Tuvan-Russian couples** in Kyzyl and Turan.
- **Old Believers of the Kaa-Khem**: marriage inside the community.
- **Buddhist, shamanic and Christian families**: what each teaches about the couple.
- **Tuvans of Mongolia and Xinjiang**: marriage across borders, if the post-war lands reach them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Registry and census data are good; customary partnership forms need ethnography and interviews; business data is open but says little about bonds.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence; the 1944 incorporation as "partnership" is politically charged; clan ties in business are a stereotype trap.
- **Held for review:** none.
