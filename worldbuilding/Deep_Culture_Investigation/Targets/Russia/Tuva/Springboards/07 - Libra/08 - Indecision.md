# Tuva: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for Buddhist teaching on doubt. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** where decisions turn on weather and stock, against **Kyzyl households** with jobs, schools and mortgages.
- **Families who consult lamas or shamans** before a choice, and those who do not.
- **Elders and the young**: the weight of parents' and elders' word in marriage, work and migration.
- **Students leaving for Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk or Moscow**: the decision to go and the decision to return.
- **Russian and Old Believer communities**: church and community guidance on choices.
- **Districts that voted differently in 1993 and since**: a lead to political division, not a label.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Divination and ritual timing are well documented in ethnography; indecision as a trait is barely studied; election records give only indirect measures.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination sessions are private and some practitioners will not discuss them; reading a vote as "indecision" can mislabel protest; avoid casting the culture as fatalistic.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "хуваанак гадание" and "гадание на лопатке тувинцы".
2. Pull Tuva's age at first marriage from Rosstat's demographic yearbooks for several decades.

4. Ask a Kyzyl temple office how families request a day for a wedding or a move.
5. Search Tuvan folklore catalogs for tales and proverbs on haste and delay.
