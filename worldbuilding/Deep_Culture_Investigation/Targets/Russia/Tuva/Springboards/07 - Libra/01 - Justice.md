# Tuva: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for Qing-era case files; Tibetan for Buddhist moral texts. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kyzyl and Ak-Dovurak** against the herding districts: courts, police and lawyers are close in town and far on the pasture.
- **Border districts** (Erzin, Mongun-Taiga, Tes-Khem, Ovyur): long histories of cross-border herds and raids; compare how theft is judged.
- **Todzha**: small, scattered taiga communities where a court may be a long journey away.
- **Old Believers of the Kaa-Khem**: their own church discipline beside the state's.
- **Buddhists, shamanic practitioners and Christians**: different teachings on merit, curse and forgiveness.
- **Descendants of the repressed**: memory of the 1930s as an injustice to be named.
- **Former prisoners and their families**: justice seen from the receiving end.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Court documents and Russian-language press are plentiful; customary practice survives mostly in ethnography and oral memory; Qing-era files are rich but hard to reach.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian (Cyrillic and vertical script) and Manchu for Qing files; Tibetan for monastic texts; Hungarian or English for Diószegi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** crime and homicide figures are a stereotype trap; report variation by district and year, never one picture of a people. Repression archives may be partly closed; Memorial's databases are now politically sensitive in Russia. Victims' names in court files need care.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. On sudrf.ru, search Tuvan district courts for "примирение сторон" and "скотокрадство" to sample how cases end.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "обычное право тувинцев наказание" and "урянхайский суд".
3. Ask the National Archive of Tyva for its finding aids on Tuvan People's Republic courts.
4. Search the Ombudsman of Tyva's site for its latest annual report.
5. Search Tuvan folklore catalogs for "Эрлик" and "буруу" in tales and proverbs.
