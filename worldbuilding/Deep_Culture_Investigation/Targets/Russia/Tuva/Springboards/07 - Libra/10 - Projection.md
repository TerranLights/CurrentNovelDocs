# Tuva: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for mind-training texts. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others what one disowns in oneself; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shamanic, Buddhist, Orthodox and Protestant practitioners**: each explains misfortune and blame differently.
- **Central and western districts**: compare beliefs about curses and envy, without assuming a gradient.
- **Russians and Tuvans in Kyzyl**: the images each holds of the other, and how those changed after 1990.
- **Tuvans living outside the republic**: being the outsider rather than naming one.
- **Families of the repressed**: being made the "enemy" in living memory.
- **Todzha and the taiga**: beliefs about dangerous spirits of remote places.
- **Generations**: young people online against elders' oral tradition.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and Soviet propaganda are well preserved; projection as a psychological idea is little studied for Tuva; outside media images are easy to collect.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian for folklore, press and archives; Tibetan for the mind-training text.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** curse accusations name real neighbors and can harm them; some cleansing rites are private; the purges and present war propaganda are politically sensitive; outside stereotypes of Tuva must be studied, not repeated.
- **Held for review:** none.
