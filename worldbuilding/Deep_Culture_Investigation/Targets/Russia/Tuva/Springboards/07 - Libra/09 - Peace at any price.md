# Tuva: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for Buddhist teaching on forbearance; Church Slavonic for Old Believer writings. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men**: who is expected to endure, and who to speak.
- **Kyzyl and the districts**: anonymity of town against the closeness of small sumons where speaking up has a cost.
- **Russians and Tuvans**: memory of 1990 from both sides.
- **Old Believers**: withdrawal as a way of keeping peace with the outside world.
- **Buddhist practitioners**: forbearance as taught against forbearance as practiced.
- **Families of the repressed and of soldiers**: silence about loss, imposed or chosen.
- **Young Tuvans online**: newer readiness to name harm publicly.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Official reports and law are open; the private side of this concept lives in interviews, theater and oral history.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan; Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence, the 1990 clashes, the purges and the current war are all sensitive; speaking about the war carries legal risk; protect informants and avoid casting any people as long-suffering by nature.
- **Held for review:** none.
