# Tuva: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for the medical canon. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others; and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Steppe herders of the south** (Erzin, Tes-Khem), **high-mountain herders** (Mongun-Taiga) and **taiga herders of Todzha**: three different seasonal rounds and land relations.
- **Users of Buddhist, shamanic and hospital medicine**: different ideas of a body out of balance.
- **Mining and industrial towns** (Ak-Dovurak's asbestos works; the coal fields) against pasture districts.
- **Kyzyl's office workers**: balance between work and kin duties in town.
- **Russian villagers and Old Believers**: farming calendars and church fasts.
- **Women and men**: the work of each season, as each describes it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Ecology and medicine have good literatures; the everyday idea of balance in Tuvan speech is thin in print and will need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for the medical canon; English for ethnomusicology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some shamanic and ovaa practice is restricted to participants; mining disputes are politically charged; avoid romanticizing herders as "in harmony with nature".
- **Held for review:** none.
