# Tuva: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for Qing-era tribute and the borderlands; Tozhu speech forms. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** in Ulug-Khem, Dzun-Khemchik and Ovyur: duties set by herds, camps and kin.
- **Kyzyl salaried families:** duties to employers and the state beside kin duties.
- **Tozhu** reindeer herders and hunters: duties to animals and taiga.
- **Old Believers:** duties to faith that have set them against state requirements.
- **Russian townspeople**, often with army or civil-service families.
- **Families of conscripts and contract soldiers**, by district.
- **Mongolian-speaking families** near the border and **Tuvans in Mongolia and Xinjiang**, under other states' service laws.
- **Men and women; eldest and youngest children:** who carries which duty, to research.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Ethnography of kin and ritual duty is good; survey data for Tuva alone are thin; war-duty material is plentiful but heavily managed.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Manchu for tribute records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war deaths and mobilization; families' grief; closed parts of shamanic and funeral rites; criminal liability for some war reporting.
- **Law:** duties set in law (conscription, taxes) are read here only for their spirit; the letter goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
