*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for monastic rule and practice names; Mongolian for borderland usage. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others or by oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** of the west and south: discipline learned with animals and seasons.
- **Kyzyl schoolchildren** and **boarding-school pupils** from remote kozhuuns: home against institution.
- **Tozhu** hunting families: training for the taiga.
- **Monks, lay Buddhists and shamans:** three disciplines of practice.
- **Wrestlers, archers, jockeys and throat singers:** craft and sport disciplines, by district schools.
- **Old Believers:** fasting and rule kept against outside ways.
- **Russian townspeople**, and **conscripts and cadets**.
- **Young men online:** what the self-discipline culture promises them, and how those who left it describe it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good on sport and music; boarding-school and hazing material is uneven and personal.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for monastic terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in boarding schools and the army; parts of monastic and shamanic training kept within the group; minors in sport.
- **Held for review:** none.
