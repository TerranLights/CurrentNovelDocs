# Tuva: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for shared Buddhist and festival terms. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders** in Ovyur, Mongun-Taiga and Erzin: merit shown in animals and contests.
- **Kyzyl's public-sector employees:** exams and competitions against connections.
- **Families of noble descent** against **people's-republic and Soviet-era promoted families**.
- **Buddhists** (merit by giving) against **shamanists** (gift by lineage).
- **Athletes and artists** from rural sports and music schools.
- **Tozhu hunters:** plan-based against custom-based shares.
- **Russians** in town careers; **Old Believers** apart from state ladders.
- **Men and women:** whose titles and prizes are counted, to research.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Honors lists and Soviet press are plentiful; recent data on how posts are actually filled is thin and contested.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims of clan favoritism touch named families and officials: treat as claims, not findings; shamanic lineage can be private.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the National Library of Tuva catalog for "Гребнев тувинский героический эпос".

4. Search the Tuvan government site for "конкурс на замещение вакантной должности".
5. Search temple sites for "буян" and "заслуги".
