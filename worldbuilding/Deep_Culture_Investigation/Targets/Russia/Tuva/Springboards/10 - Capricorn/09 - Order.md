# Tuva: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for shared ritual terms. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders** of the west, south and Tozhu taiga: order in camps, moves and pastures.
- **Kyzyl's old center** against its **newer suburbs and dacha settlements**.
- **Industrial and mixed towns** (Ak-Dovurak, Turan, Shagonar).
- **Buddhist, shamanist and Orthodox households:** different purification and ordering rites.
- **Old Believers:** strict household order apart.
- **Russian and Tuvan residents:** to test how each describes public order and the other.
- **Young people and elders:** at festivals, at night, under curfew rules.
- **Tuvans in Mongolia and Xinjiang:** shared rites under other public orders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on ritual order; official crime statistics plentiful but blunt; residents' own accounts scattered online.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** crime figures invite stereotype, so pair them with residents' voices; interethnic tension; closed parts of purification rites.
- **Law:** public-order offenses as written belong to Libra; crime to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** none.
