# Tuva: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** in the west and south: children made answerable for herds early; ask whether men are expected to answer for the household's fortunes in a way others are not, and how men in each group speak of it.
- **Kyzyl officials and public-sector employees:** accountability in office.
- **Industrial towns** (Ak-Dovurak, Khovu-Aksy area, Kaa-Khem coal villages): responsibility for health and land.
- **Tozhu** communes: collective stewardship.
- **Buddhist, shamanist and Christian** families: different accounts of why misfortune comes.
- **Old Believers** and **Russian townspeople:** separate moral traditions of guilt and confession.
- **Grandparents raising grandchildren** (to research, not assume) and **migrant parents** working outside Tuva.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to thin. Rich on ritual and cosmology; thin on attribution surveys for Tuva alone.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for legal custom; German for Taube.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol and violence are a stereotype trap: name variation, avoid one voice; occupational illness claims; officials' corruption cases under investigation.
- **Law:** the age and grounds of criminal responsibility belong to the letter (Libra) and crime (Pisces); here only the spirit.
- **Held for review:** none.
