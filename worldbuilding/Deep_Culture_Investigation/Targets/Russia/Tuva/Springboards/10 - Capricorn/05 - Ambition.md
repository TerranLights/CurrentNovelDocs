# Tuva: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for Tuvans of western Mongolia; Old Turkic for the epitaphs. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural herding families** in Mongun-Taiga, Bai-Taiga, Erzin and Ovyur: wealth in herds against wealth in degrees.
- **Kyzyl's educated middle class** and **public-sector careers**.
- **Young people who leave** for Siberian cities, and those who return.
- **Monks, shamans and artists:** ambition through a path of practice.
- **Tozhu youth:** staying with reindeer and taiga, or leaving.
- **Russians** of the towns, many oriented to careers outside Tuva.
- **Tuvans in Mongolia and Xinjiang.**
- **Men and women:** whether rising and providing is a man's measure in each group, how men who do not rise are seen and see themselves, and how this differs for women.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Education and migration data exist; Tuvan-voiced writing on ambition is scattered across press and social media.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for the diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** military contract service as a route to income since 2022; poverty framing that slides into stereotype.
- **Held for review:** none.
