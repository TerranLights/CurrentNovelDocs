## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western kozhuuns**, home of the 1883 rising, against the center and east.
- **Old Believers**, whose refusal is their faith.
- **Believers who hid practice** under Soviet rule against those raised secular.
- **Herders who stayed on the land** after 1991 against those who moved to Kyzyl.
- **Ethnic Russians and Tuvans**, each labeled by the other.
- **Tozhu Tuvans, Tofalars and Soyots**, if carried, and their claims to land and status.
- **Diaspora**: Tsengel Tuvans and Kanas Tuvans holding to name and language.
- **Men and women**: who is called stubborn and who determined *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; resistance history and language data are good; personal stubbornness is barely studied.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian, Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the repression of the 1930s; local protests; ethnic stereotypes (record labels, never adopt them); Memorial's records may be hard to reach.
- **Held for review:** none.
