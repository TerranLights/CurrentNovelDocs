# Tuva: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan and Russian; Old Turkic for the runic epitaphs; Mongolian for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding men in the kozhuuns** whose worth may rest on herds and providing, against **men in Kyzyl** in wage work or unemployment.
- **Women**, whose worth may rest on other grounds (family, schooling, office) *check*.
- **Wrestlers, singers and craftsmen** with public titles.
- **Tuvan speakers against Russian-speaking Tuvans** in Kyzyl.
- **Ethnic Russians and Old Believers**, a minority since the 1990s.
- **Tozhu Tuvans, Tofalars and Soyots**, whose Indigenous status may change how worth is claimed.
- **Buddhist, shamanic and Christian households.**
- **Diaspora** in Mongolia and China, minorities within minorities.
- **Generations:** Soviet-era award holders against post-1991 youth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good folklore and award records, scattered modern psychology; suicide data exist but need care.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Tuvan, Mongolian; Old Turkic corpus editions are in Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide, alcohol and unemployment; interethnic conflict of 1990; shamanic honors.
- **Held for review:** none.
