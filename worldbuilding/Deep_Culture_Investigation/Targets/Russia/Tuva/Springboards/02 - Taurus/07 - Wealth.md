# Tuva: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large-herd families** in western and southern kozhuuns against small holders.
- **Kyzyl's salaried class**: state employees, traders, mining staff.
- **Taiga hunters and prospectors in Todzha.**
- **Descendants of families labeled bai** in the 1930s, and how they speak of it.
- **Buddhist donors and monasteries.**
- **Ethnic Russian farmers and traders.**
- **Tofalars and Soyots**, if carried: furs and reindeer as their measures of wealth *check*.
- **Diaspora**: Tsengel and Dukha herders (Mongolia), Kanas villagers in a tourist economy (China).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good republic statistics and Soviet class histories; personal attitudes to wealth are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for statistics and Soviet histories; Tuvan for fiction and ritual; Mongolian for herd censuses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1930s dispossession and executions; envy beliefs and wealth rites; new household money from military contracts since 2022, to be handled with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
