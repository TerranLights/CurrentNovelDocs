*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western kozhuuns** (Barun-Khemchik, Dzun-Khemchik, Mongun-Taiga, Ovyur, Bai-Taiga): yak and sheep herding, western dialect, older Buddhist centers.
- **Central basin** (Ulug-Khem, Chaa-Khol, Piy-Khem, Tandy): herding and farming; Russian villages in the Turan and Uyuk valleys.
- **Southern borderland** (Erzin, Tes-Khem, Tere-Khol): sand steppe, other herd mixes.
- **Todzha (Tozhu Tuvans):** taiga hunting, reindeer.
- **Kyzyl:** urban, largely Russian-speaking, wages and credit.
- **Old Believers of the upper Yenisei** (Erzhei, Sizim *check*): their own rules of thrift, food and property.
- **Ethnic Russians and other settlers.**
- **Kin peoples**, if in the lands: Tofalars (Irkutsk region), Soyots (Buryatia).
- **Diaspora:** Tsengel Tuvans and Dukha reindeer herders in Mongolia; Tuvans of the Kanas area in Xinjiang, China.
- **By faith:** Buddhist, shamanic, Orthodox, Old Believer, Protestant converts, secular.
- **By clan (сөөк, söök):** Mongush, Oorzhak, Kuular, Ondar, Saryglar and others, if gift or inheritance customs differ *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich Soviet-era ethnography and republic statistics; thin on present-day personal values; Tozhu and diaspora data scattered across languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Tuvan; Mongolian (Qing archives, diaspora); German (Tschinag); Chinese (Kanas).
- **Lands:** the tracker lists the pre-war lands as "Russia", which is broad; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. These Taurus sheets are written for the Republic of Tuva's lands and the kin peoples named above.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic invocations and consecrated (ыдык) animals; the 1930s repression of monks, shamans and herd owners; debt and theft (legal side to Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru and CyberLeninka for «тувинцы скотоводство собственность» and «материальная культура тувинцев».

3. Open Tyvastat for the latest household budget survey and the 2016 agricultural census tables by kozhuun.
4. Look up мал, хөреңги, эт-сеп, ыдык and кежик in the Tuvan-Russian dictionary (ed. Tenishev, 1968 *check*) and the Tuvan Talking Dictionary.
5. Search *Новые исследования Тувы* for «Шагаа», «Наадым», «подарок».
