# Tuva: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan and Russian; Mongolian and Tibetan for Buddhist teaching texts; Mongolian for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buddhist-leaning western kozhuuns** against areas where shamanic practice is more open *check*.
- **Herding families against Kyzyl professionals**: what is ranked first when herd, school and job compete.
- **Generations:** those raised in the Tuvan People's Republic, the Soviet period and after 1991.
- **Ethnic Russians, Old Believers and Christian converts.**
- **Tozhu Tuvans**, whose taiga life may order values differently.
- **Tofalars and Soyots**, if their lands are carried.
- **Diaspora** in Mongolia and China.
- **Tuvan speakers against Russian speakers.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in written customs and taboos, thin in measured values; national surveys likely did not sample Tuva.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan (key sources are in Tuvan only), Russian, Mongolian; Tibetan helps for monastery texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred places and shamanic rules; the reassessment of Soviet figures; religious rivalry between Buddhists, shamans and Christians.
- **Held for review:** none.
