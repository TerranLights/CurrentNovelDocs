# Tuva: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan and Russian; Mongolian for dzud literature and the diaspora; the Tozhu dialect for taiga risks. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Steppe herders** most exposed to dzud and drought: western Mongun-Taiga yak herders against central-basin sheep herders.
- **Southern borderland (Erzin, Tes-Khem)**: cross-border theft and dry years *check*.
- **Tozhu taiga families**: wolves, fires and poor hunting years.
- **Kyzyl wage earners and pensioners**: state payments, credit and urban safety.
- **Dacha settlers on Kyzyl's edge**: housing and heating insecurity.
- **Russian villagers and Old Believers.**
- **Tofalars and Soyots**, if carried: mountain-taiga risks of their own.
- **Diaspora in Mongolia** under a different social system and the same dzud risk.
- **Women and elders**, since fear of crime and old-age security may differ by sex and age *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; disaster and benefit data exist at republic level; household savings and fear of crime are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for official data; Tuvan for local press; Mongolian for dzud studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** crime, theft and alcohol; protective shamanic objects, which some keepers may not want shown; legal aspects to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
