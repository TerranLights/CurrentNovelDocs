# Tuva: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Steppe herders** (milk foods, mutton, open air) against **Tozhu taiga families** (reindeer milk, game, berries).
- **Western kozhuuns** with yak-milk foods against the central basin.
- **Kyzyl**, with Russian, Chinese and fast-food tastes beside Tuvan home cooking.
- **Devout Buddhists and lamas**, who may fast, against secular households.
- **Ethnic Russians and Old Believers**: the banya, Russian cooking, Old Believer food rules.
- **Generations**: elders and the young on food, music and dress.
- **Tofalars and Soyots**, if carried: their own taiga foods.
- **Diaspora**: Tsengel and Dukha (Mongolia); Kanas (China).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; music and food are well covered; touch norms, rest and comfort are thin.
