# Tuva: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan and Russian; Mongolian for the diaspora and the shared Uvs basin. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families who still keep the four-camp round** against those settled in villages since the 1950s and 1960s.
- **Kyzyl's newer residents from the kozhuuns** against long-urban families.
- **Old Believer villages**, which may prize unchanged ways.
- **Ethnic Russian families** who stayed after 1990, against those who left.
- **Tozhu Tuvans**, whose taiga mobility follows other rhythms.
- **Tofalars and Soyots**, if carried: tiny groups whose continuity is itself in question.
- **Diaspora** in Mongolia and China, as long-settled minorities.
- **Generations:** those who lived through 1944, 1991 and the 1990s against those born after 2000.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; census and migration data are good; sense of stability and its feelings are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian (censuses, histories), Tuvan (songs, calendar), Mongolian (Uvs basin, diaspora).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 incorporation and its reading; interethnic events of 1990 and the Russian exodus; the destruction of monasteries.
- **Held for review:** none.
