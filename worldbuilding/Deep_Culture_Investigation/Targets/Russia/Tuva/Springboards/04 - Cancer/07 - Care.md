*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** far from clinics: home and spring care against flights and feldshers.
- **Kyzyl:** hospitals, paid carers, care homes.
- **Todzhu:** care in the taiga, and links with the Toora-Khem district hospital *check*.
- **Buddhist, shamanic and Orthodox households:** different healers and rites at the sickbed.
- **Russians and Old Believers:** Old Believer reluctance or openness toward state medicine, to check.
- **Generations:** elders cared for at home by children, against elders living alone in villages; how common each is is a question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: health-system reports and healing ethnography exist; unpaid family care is barely measured and needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for the medical classics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic and lama healing may be held by practitioners; tuberculosis, alcohol harm and suicide are heavy topics needing care; the treatment of elders in families is private.
- **Held for review:** none.
