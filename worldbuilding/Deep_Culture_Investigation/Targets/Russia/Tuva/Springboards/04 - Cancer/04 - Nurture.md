# Tuva: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; German for one tale collection. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** of the west and south: what children learn on the camp, and from whom.
- **Kyzyl families:** preschools, Russian-language schooling, apartment childhoods.
- **Todzhu:** children raised among reindeer and the hunt.
- **Russian families and Old Believers:** Orthodox or Old Believer rearing; the upper Yenisei's own schools.
- **Buddhist, shamanic and Christian households:** each may name and protect a child differently.
- **Outside comparison:** Tuvans of Mongolia (in Taube's tales) and of Xinjiang.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: ethnopedagogy and folklore are plentiful in Russian; present-day parenting is thin and needs interviews and social media.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; German for Taube.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the boarding-school past; child neglect and alcohol harm; some protective rites for children may be private.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "этнопедагогика тувинцев" and list the most cited authors.
2. Search the Tuvan folklore volumes and the TIGPI archive for "өпей ыры".
3. Search for "школы-интернаты Тувы" and "кочевая школа Тыва" and sort by decade.
4. Request Taube's tale collection and note tales framed for children.
5. Search Tuvan news for "Совет отцов" and "кижизидилге".
