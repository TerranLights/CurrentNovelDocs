# Tuva: Domain and folkways (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (тыва дыл, Cyrillic with ң, ө, ү), Russian; the Todzhu (Tozhu) dialect of the northeast; Mongolian for the southern borderlands and the Tuvan communities across the border. **Concept in one line:** the domain of home and family: what is home, and who is family; with Fischer's three Cancer folkways (building ways, family ways, child-rearing ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Tuvans** (Kyzyl, Ulug-Khem, Chaa-Khol, Tandy): the most urbanized; Kyzyl apartment and self-built suburbs against steppe camps.
- **Western Tuvans** (Barun-Khemchik, Dzun-Khemchik, Mongun-Taiga, Bai-Taiga, Ovyur, Sut-Khol): high-mountain herding; dialect differences.
- **Southeastern Tuvans** (Erzin, Tes-Khem, Tere-Khol): steppe and sand country on the Mongolian border.
- **Todzhu (Tozhu)** of the northeast taiga: reindeer herders and hunters with their own dwellings.
- **Russians** in Kyzyl and the Piy-Khem and Kaa-Khem valleys, and the **Old Believers** of the upper Yenisei.
- **Smaller groups:** Khakas, Mongols and mixed Tuvan-Russian families in Kyzyl.
- **Outside comparison only:** Tuvans of western Mongolia (Tsengel) and of the Altai in Xinjiang; Tuvan students and workers in Krasnoyarsk, Abakan, Novosibirsk and Moscow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: Soviet ethnography is rich on dwellings and rites; current family practice is thinner. **The nation's exact lands are still to be settled** (the tracker lists "Russia" as the pre-war lands); this sheet is written for the core lands of the Tuva Republic.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Mongolian and German for the diaspora; Old Turkic for the inscriptions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic household rites and clan knowledge may be held by families; collectivization, boarding schools and alcohol-related family harm are painful subjects; Soviet sources carry an official frame.
- **Held for review:** none.
