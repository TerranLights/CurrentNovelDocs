# Tuva: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Church Slavonic for Old Believer manuscripts. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families:** shelter given in camps to travelers, kin and herds.
- **Kyzyl:** shelters, crisis centers and the services for the homeless.
- **Todzhu hunters:** taiga cabins and hunting camps.
- **Old Believers:** a community that came seeking refuge.
- **Russian villagers** of the Piy-Khem and Kaa-Khem valleys: the Siberian village's customs of lodging.
- **Rural migrants in Kyzyl's outskirts:** the edge between a self-built roof and none.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed: travelers' accounts and ethnography are good for the old customs; present-day shelter data are scattered in news and social-service pages.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; English for Carruthers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and crisis centers; homelessness and alcohol; children in care.
- **Held for review:** none.
