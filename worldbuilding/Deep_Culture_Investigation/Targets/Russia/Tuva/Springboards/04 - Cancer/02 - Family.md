# Tuva: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for cross-border kin. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western clans** (the Oorzhak, Kuular, Ondar and others of Barun- and Dzun-Khemchik *check*): to test how clan memory differs by district.
- **Central and eastern Tuvans:** different clan sets and more mixing in Kyzyl.
- **Todzhu:** small taiga groups, with clan lines of their own *check*.
- **Russian families** of Kyzyl and the villages; **Old Believers**; mixed Tuvan-Russian families.
- **Kyzyl's urban families** against those still on herding camps: how far kin obligations travel.
- **Outside comparison:** the clans of Mongolian Tuvans (Tsengel) and of the Altai Tuvans of Xinjiang.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on clans and rites in Soviet ethnography; thinner on present-day obligations, which need Tuvan-language interviews and social media.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Mongolian for cross-border kin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan and genealogy knowledge can be family-held; mixed marriages and Tuvan-Russian tension in the 1990s are sore subjects; family law belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "термины родства тувинского языка" and collect the kin-term charts.
2. Request Mannai-ool (2004) and list every söök name with its home kozhuun.
3. Pull the 2021 census family-composition tables for Tyva.
4. Search the Tuvan folklore series volumes for epics and tales about kin quarrels.
5. Search VKontakte for "сөөктер Тыва" and note how people describe their clan.
