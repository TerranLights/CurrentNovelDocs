## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding mothers** in the west and south: birth far from clinics; work on the camp.
- **Kyzyl mothers:** wage work, preschools, smaller families, to test.
- **Todzhu mothers:** taiga births and the care of reindeer-camp children.
- **Buddhist, shamanic and Orthodox mothers:** different protectors and rites.
- **Russian and Old Believer mothers:** large families in the hamlets; Orthodox ideals.
- **Grandmothers as mothers:** kin who raise children while parents work away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: birth rites, songs and state programs are reachable; mothers' own accounts need interviews and Tuvan-language media.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Old Turkic for Umay.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth customs and rites to protect infants may be private; maternal health and alcohol harm; soldiers' mothers and the current war are censored subjects in Russia.
- **Held for review:** none.
