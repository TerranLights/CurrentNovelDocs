# Tuva: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Old Turkic for the inscriptions. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding districts:** children sent to town schools; elders left on camps or brought in.
- **Kyzyl:** where social orphanhood and kin guardianship are concentrated, to check.
- **Former industrial towns** (Ak-Dovurak, Khovu-Aksy): places left by employers.
- **Russian villages** of the Piy-Khem and Kaa-Khem valleys: families who left, and those who stayed.
- **Todzhu:** the loss of herds and of camps.
- **Old Believers:** leaving a faith community.
- **Families divided by the borders** with Mongolia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: official child-care data and folklore are reachable; felt abandonment needs memoirs and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Old Turkic for Malov.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children in care; alcohol harm; the purges; men away at the current war, which is a censored subject in Russia; Memorial was dissolved in 2021 and its archives may be hard to reach.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Request Malov (1952) and list the epitaphs found in Tuva that speak of parting.
2. Search Tuvan tale collections for "Өскүс-оол".
3. Find the Tyva Ministry of Education's latest figures on children without parental care.
4. Search for "репрессии в Туве" and archived Memorial lists for Tuvan victims.
5. Search eLibrary.ru for "социальное сиротство Тыва".
