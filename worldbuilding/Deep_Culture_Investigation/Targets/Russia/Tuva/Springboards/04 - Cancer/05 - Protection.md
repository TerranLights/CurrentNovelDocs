*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Todzhu dialect for the taiga. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Steppe herders** of the west and south: herds, dogs and wolves.
- **Todzhu** hunters and reindeer herders: forest dangers and the taiga's master.
- **Kyzyl residents:** locks, police, emergency services, private guards.
- **Buddhist, shamanic and Orthodox households:** different protectors and rites.
- **Old Believers:** protection through distance and faith.
- **Families of soldiers:** from 1943 to the present.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rites, amulets and epics are well recorded; household safety practice today is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some invocations and protective objects belong to shamans and families; domestic violence; the current war's toll on Tuvan families is a sensitive and censored subject in Russia; crime and policing belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
