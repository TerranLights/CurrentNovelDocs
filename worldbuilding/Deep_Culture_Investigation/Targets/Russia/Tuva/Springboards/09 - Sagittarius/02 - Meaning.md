# Tuva: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Chinese for kin abroad. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders** on summer and winter camps against **wage workers and civil servants** in Kyzyl, Ak-Dovurak and Chadan.
- **Former mining towns** (Ak-Dovurak's asbestos works) against farming kozhuuns: work's loss of point.
- **Young men** leaving for army service or work in other parts of Russia, and those who stay.
- **Todzhu** reindeer herders, a small people within the nation.
- **Buddhist-practicing families**, **shamans' clients**, **Christian converts**, and the **unaffiliated**: each may place meaning differently.
- **Russians** and **Old Believers**: a settler line with its own sense of calling.
- **Tuvans in Mongolia and China:** meaning across a border.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Literature and folklore are rich; psychology studies exist but small; numbers on despair are sensitive and must not be read as a portrait.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian; Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and addiction; avoid any framing that turns figures into a stereotype. Epic telling may have rules on time and place of performance.
- **Held for review:** none.
