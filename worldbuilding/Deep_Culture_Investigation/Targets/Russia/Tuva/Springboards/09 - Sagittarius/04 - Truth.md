*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and clan-history keepers** against **young people** who get news from phones.
- **Lamas** (scriptural and debate-trained truth) and **shamans** (truth through spirits and divination), and their lay clients.
- **Scientists and teachers** in Kyzyl, the bearers of school and academic truth.
- **Tuvan-language** and **Russian-language** audiences: two press worlds that may tell different stories.
- **Todzhu** communities, whose own history may be written mostly by outsiders.
- **Russians and Old Believers:** another set of trusted sources (priest, elder, scripture).
- **Tuvans in Mongolia and China**, who may hold other accounts of shared history.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Linguistic, folklore and historical sources are good; measured trust in news or science at republic level is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Classical Tibetan; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** purge memory, the 1944 framing and clan disputes; divination may be private. Press freedom is limited across Russia, so read official media with that in mind.
- **Held for review:** none.
