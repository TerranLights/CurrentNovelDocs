# Tuva: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian; Classical Tibetan for old pilgrim records. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** whose year is a round of camps, against **Kyzyl** residents who travel by road and air.
- **Todzhu** reindeer herders: taiga routes, boats and caravans, cut off by road in some seasons.
- **Southern and western border kozhuuns** (Erzin, Mongun-Taiga, Ovyur): kin and trade across the Mongolian line.
- **Students and labor migrants** in Siberian and Russian cities.
- **Old Believers**, who came as migrants seeking a refuge, and **Russians** who arrived by the tract.
- **Tuvans of Mongolia and China** who visit or move to Tuva.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Exploration and ethnography are rich; data on present-day travel habits are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian; Chinese; Classical Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some sacred places limit visitors; military service and work far from home are sensitive topics now. Border zones may need permits.
- **Held for review:** pilgrimage destinations in South Asia visited by Tuvan Buddhists (category only).
