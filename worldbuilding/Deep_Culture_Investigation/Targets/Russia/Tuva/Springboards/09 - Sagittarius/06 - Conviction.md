## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lama, shaman and convert lines:** three kinds of committed believer, each with a different claim to firmness.
- **Old Believers** of Kaa-Khem: a principled withdrawal from the world.
- **Soviet-era activists' descendants** against **families of the purged**: two memories of conviction.
- **Western and central kozhuuns**, where memories of resistance and collaboration may differ; check.
- **Todzhu** herders, who faced settlement campaigns.
- **Russians** in Kyzyl: a smaller group with its own civic and religious lines.
- **Tuvans abroad** (Mongolia, Xinjiang): holding to custom as a minority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Biography and history are good; present-day measures of conviction or protest are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian, Chinese; Classical Tibetan for vows; Church Slavonic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** public dissent carries real risk in Russia today, including stances on military service; never expose informants. Election data must not be read as a portrait. Conscientious objection as law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
