# Tuva: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Old Turkic for the older and cross-border layers. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders** whose work is movement, against **townspeople** in Kyzyl and the mining towns.
- **Western Tuva**, where resistance to collectivization is said to have been strongest: test this, then compare with the center.
- **Todzhu** taiga people: forced settlement and its memory.
- **Old Believers** of the upper Yenisei: freedom of faith as a founding reason.
- **Russians** who stayed after the early 1990s, and those who left: how each tells that time.
- **Generations:** those who remember the TNR's last years or their parents' accounts, Soviet-born adults, and the post-1991 young.
- **Tuvans in Mongolia and in Xinjiang:** the same people's freedoms under two other states.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. History is rich but politically framed; present-day opinion on freedom is thinly measured at regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian; Old Turkic and Manchu or Chinese for the oldest records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** independence, 1944 and the early 1990s interethnic tensions are politically delicate; protect informants. No law research here.
- **Held for review:** none.
