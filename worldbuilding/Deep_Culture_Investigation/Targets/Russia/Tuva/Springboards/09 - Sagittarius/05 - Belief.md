# Tuva: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian, Church Slavonic (Old Believer books). **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** who keep taboos tied to livestock and fire, against **urban families** where the same customs may fade or change.
- **Western kozhuuns** and **central Tuva**: Buddhist and shamanic belief may mix differently; check.
- **Todzhu** taiga people: spirit-masters of forest and reindeer.
- **Southern border kozhuuns** (Erzin, Tere-Khol): influences from Mongolia.
- **Russians** (Orthodox, secular, Soviet atheist heritage) and **Old Believers** (strict doctrine, own books).
- **Protestant Tuvans**, who may reject old beliefs outright; their reasons in their own words.
- **Generations:** Soviet-schooled skeptics, revival-era believers, and today's young.
- **Tuvans in Mongolia and China.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and ethnography of beliefs are rich; numbers on who holds what are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan (Cyrillic and the 1930s Latin script), Russian, Mongolian, Church Slavonic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some beliefs about spirits and sacred places are not shared with outsiders; the word "superstition" (суеверие) is a judgment, so avoid it in fieldwork.
- **Held for review:** none.
