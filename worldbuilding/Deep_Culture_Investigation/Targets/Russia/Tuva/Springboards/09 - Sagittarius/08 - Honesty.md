# Tuva: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and German (diaspora folklore). **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders** whose wealth is livestock, against **town wage earners** and **traders**.
- **Kozhuuns near the Mongolian border**, where cross-border trade and herds meet: check how honesty is negotiated there.
- **Todzhu** reindeer herders, in small, tightly knit communities.
- **Officials and teachers**, who meet bribery and exam rules from the inside.
- **Russian and Old Believer** communities, with their own trade ethics.
- **Generations:** elders taught by folk pedagogy, and the young online.
- **Tuvans in Mongolia** (Taube's tales) and **China**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and pedagogy are well covered; corruption data exist but are coarse and politically shaped.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian, Mongolian; German for Taube.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** livestock theft, bribery and exam cheating are easy to turn into stereotypes about a people; name variation and sources, never a verdict. Curses may be private.
- **Held for review:** none.
