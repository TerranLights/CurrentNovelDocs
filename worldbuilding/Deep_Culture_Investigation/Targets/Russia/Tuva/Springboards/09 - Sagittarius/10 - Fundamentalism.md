# Tuva: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Tuvan; Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts; Mongolian and Classical Tibetan for the Buddhist layer. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers** of the upper Yenisei, by concord and by village: degrees of separation.
- **Tuvan evangelical and Pentecostal** congregations, and the families of converts.
- **The Buddhist sangha** (strict and accommodating lamas) and **shamans' societies** (codifiers and independents).
- **Orthodox** parishes in Kyzyl and the Turan-Uyuk basin.
- **Restorationist circles** online (Tengrist, native-faith).
- **Newcomer Muslims** in Kyzyl.
- **The secular majority's** inherited Soviet atheism, as a counter-pole.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Old Believer scholarship is solid; data on Protestant and newer groups are scarce and often hostile in tone.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Tuvan, Church Slavonic; Mongolian and Classical Tibetan for Buddhist history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some groups are restricted or banned in Russia, and members face real risk; never name individuals. "Sect" is a loaded word; use members' own names for their groups. Hermit communities may refuse contact.
- **Held for review:** none.
