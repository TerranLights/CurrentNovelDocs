# Tuva: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; German and English for foreign archaeology and outsiders' accounts. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore; and how far a culture encourages, channels, limits or punishes it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders' children** against **town children**: what each may ask adults, and how answers are given.
- **Todzha hunters and reindeer herders**: tracking, reading signs, the curiosity of the taiga.
- **The Uyuk valley and Tere-Khol communities**, who live beside famous digs.
- **Local historians and schoolteachers** in the kozhuuns, against academic scholars from Kyzyl and Novosibirsk.
- **Lamas and shamans**, whose knowledge may not be open to every asker.
- **Old Believers**, who may set their own limits on outside knowledge (a question to test).
- **Russian incomers** and their curiosity about Tuvans, and the reverse.
- **Tourism hosts** in Kyzyl and the steppe camps, who meet foreign curiosity daily.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Archaeology and folklore are rich; everyday attitudes to asking and prying are thin and will need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; German for the Arzhan publications; English for outsider accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** graves and the dead; questions about livestock counts, names and the deceased; genetic research and consent; shamanic knowledge closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search a library catalog for «тувинские загадки» and Kurbatsky's book.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for «Аржан-2 восприятие местное население» and «Пор-Бажын экспедиция».
3. Check the Russian Geographical Society's Tuva branch for youth expedition programs.
4. Run «запреты тувинцев вопросы» in ethnographic databases.
5. Watch *Genghis Blues* and list which Tuvans speak, and about what.
