# Tuva: Learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for monastic teaching; Church Slavonic for Old Believer home teaching. **Concept in one line:** how people come to know and to do: in the family, by watching and pitching in, through play, in early school, at work and across a life (schooling as an institution: Sagittarius).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding camps** against **Kyzyl's schools**: learning by doing in the camp, by lesson in town.
- **Todzha reindeer herders and hunters**: taiga skills and any schooling that moves with the camp.
- **Mongun-Taiga and the western districts**: throat singing taught early within families.
- **Boarding-school generations** against children schooled at home or nearby.
- **Tuvan-medium village schools** against **Russian-medium urban schools**.
- **Buddhist novices** of the revived monasteries; **shamans' apprentices** where they exist.
- **Russian and Old Believer families**, with their own teachers and texts.
- **Girls and boys**: which tasks and crafts each is taught first.
- **Students who leave** for universities in Krasnoyarsk, Novosibirsk and beyond.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on Soviet schooling and folk pedagogy (often idealized); thin on how learning in the camp actually works today.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Tibetan for monastic teaching; Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school memories can be painful; shamanic apprenticeship may be restricted; exam results invite ranking, so use them only as one measure.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
