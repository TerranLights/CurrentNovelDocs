# Tuva: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small sumons** where everyone knows everyone, against **Kyzyl**, with anonymous city pages.
- **Herding camps**, where gossip may travel by visit and phone across long distances.
- **Generations**: elders' face-to-face talk against young people's group chats.
- **Women and men**: whether talk is named "gossip" for one and something else for the other; to test, not assume.
- **Buddhist, shamanic and Orthodox circles**, each with its own teaching on speech about others.
- **Russian and Old Believer villages**, with their own censure.
- **Todzha and the western districts**, where small populations make reputation hard to escape.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin. Folklore and the repression record exist; everyday gossip is hardly studied and will need observation or interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gossip concerns real, private people: quote nothing from social media; repression records name victims and informers; evil-eye accusations can harm.
- **Held for review:** none.
