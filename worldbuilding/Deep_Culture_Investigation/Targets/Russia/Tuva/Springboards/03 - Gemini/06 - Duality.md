# Tuva: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for Buddhist doctrine and the masked dance; English for foreign anthropology. **Concept in one line:** how a culture lives with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how it treats people who show two faces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kyzyl residents** who move between Russian work and Tuvan home, against **rural herders** who may live mostly in Tuvan.
- **Buddhist and shamanic households**, and those who use both.
- **Mixed Tuvan-Russian families** and their children.
- **Russians and Old Believers**, whose own two-ness (church and state, village and town) differs.
- **Soviet-era elders** who lived under two states (the People's Republic and the USSR).
- **Western districts** near Mongolia, with Mongolian ties beside Tuvan ones.
- **Tuvans living outside Tuva**, with a home self and an away self.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Religion and history are well covered; everyday double identity and code-switching are thin and recent.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Tibetan for the masked dance; English for foreign anthropology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 annexation is politically charged; shamanic cosmology may be restricted; "two-faced" labels touch ethnic stereotypes, so describe them, never adopt them.
- **Held for review:** none.
