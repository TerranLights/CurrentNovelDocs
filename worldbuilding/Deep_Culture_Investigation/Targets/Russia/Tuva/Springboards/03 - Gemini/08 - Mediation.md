# Tuva: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Manchu-era records for the Qing frontier. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and, more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors). Courts and legal procedure: Libra and Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding communities** in the kozhuuns, where elders and kin may settle quarrels, against **Kyzyl**, with offices and professionals.
- **Border districts** (Erzin, Ovyur, Mongun-Taiga), with cross-border disputes and go-betweens.
- **Todzha**, small and spread out: who mediates where officials are far away.
- **Buddhist, shamanic and Orthodox circles**, each with its own intercessor.
- **Russian and Old Believer villages**, which may turn to priests or village heads rather than clan elders.
- **Women and men** as go-betweens (matchmakers, kin-keepers, elders).
- **Young people** in schools with reconciliation services, against those without.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Matchmaking and religious intercession are described; everyday quarrel-settling is barely studied and will need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan and Russian; Mongolian and Manchu-era sources for the Qing frontier.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1990 clashes remain painful; family disputes are private; shamanic intercession may be guarded. Courts, arbitration and law are Libra's and Capricorn's, not this sheet's.
- **Held for review:** none.
