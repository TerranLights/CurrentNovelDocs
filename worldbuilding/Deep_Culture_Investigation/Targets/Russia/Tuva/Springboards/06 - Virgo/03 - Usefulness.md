# Tuva: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (Cyrillic), Russian; Mongolian for cognates and the cross-border communities. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding camps against Kyzyl:** children's and elders' tasks differ with the household economy.
- **Todzha reindeer and hunting households:** a separate inventory of useful animals and skills.
- **Western high-pasture families** (Mongun-Taiga, Bai-Taiga): yak-based thrift and tools.
- **Russian and Old Believer villages** of the upper Yenisei: a different household economy and family order.
- **Pensioners and grandparents** raising grandchildren while parents work away.
- **Disabled people** in villages and in Kyzyl.
- **Tuvan-dominant against Russian-dominant families:** which language and skills are called useful.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; statistics on pensions and disability are good; folk attitudes need Tuvan-language fieldwork and folklore.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for cognates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school memories can be painful; disability and joblessness are stigmatized topics; plant-healing knowledge may be held by healers who do not share it freely.
- **Held for review:** none.
