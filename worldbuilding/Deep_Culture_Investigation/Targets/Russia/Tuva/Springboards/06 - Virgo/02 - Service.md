# Tuva: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (Cyrillic), Russian; Mongolian and Classical Mongolian script for Qing-era records. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding camps against Kyzyl flats:** service to guests and elders under different housing and time pressures.
- **Buddhist laity, shamanist households and Orthodox or Old Believer families:** different religious frames for serving.
- **Veterans' and soldiers' families:** military service as family identity, from 1944 to the present.
- **Public servants in Kyzyl** against **private traders and market sellers**: state service against customer service.
- **Todzha communities:** hosting and help in small, remote settlements.
- **Women and men, elders and the young:** who is expected to serve at the hearth and the feast.
- **Tsengel and Kanas Tuvans:** hospitality and duty outside the Russian state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on military history and etiquette; mixed on volunteering; thin on customer service.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Classical Mongolian for Qing records; Manchu for some Qing documents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** present-day military service and casualties are politically sensitive; monastery serfdom and noyon privilege are charged Soviet-era themes, so read both Soviet and post-Soviet accounts. Duty and hierarchy as such go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. cyberleninka.ru: "гостеприимство тувинцев" and "этикет тувинцев аалчы"; note who serves whom.
2. TIGPI explanatory dictionary: *бараан бол-*, *дузала-*, *буян*; copy the definitions and examples.
3. Search "тувинские добровольцы 1944" for archives, memorial sites and anniversary coverage.
4. Dobro.рф: filter by "Республика Тыва" for volunteer projects and organizers.
5. Library: *История Тувы* vol. 2, chapters on Qing-era social structure and dues.
