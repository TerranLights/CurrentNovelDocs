*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (Tyva dyl, Cyrillic), Russian; Mongolian for the cross-border Tuvan communities; Old Turkic (transliterated) for the runic inscriptions. **Concept in one line:** what is good work and a well-kept life: where work sits in a life, the daily and yearly round, the kept house, chores, craft, rest, and the inherited work ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central valleys** (Ulug-Khem, Khemchik, Kyzyl kozhuun): mixed herding and farming, closest to the capital.
- **Western high pastures** (Mongun-Taiga, Bai-Taiga, Barun-Khemchik): yak and sheep herding at altitude, a distinct dialect.
- **Southern desert-steppe** (Erzin, Tes-Khem, Ovyur): camels, the Mongolian border.
- **Todzha** (Tozhu): taiga reindeer herding, hunting and fishing.
- **Upper Yenisei** (Kaa-Khem, Piy-Khem): Russian settler and Old Believer villages.
- **Kyzyl and the mining towns** (Ak-Dovurak, Khovu-Aksy, Turan): wage and industrial work.
- **Cross-border Tuvans:** Tsengel (Mongolia), the Kanas area (Xinjiang), and the related Dukha; students and workers in Krasnoyarsk, Novosibirsk and Moscow.
- **Religious and age lines:** Buddhist, shamanist, Orthodox and Old Believer households; Soviet-trained against post-Soviet generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: Russian ethnography and statistics rich; Tuvan-language material essential and mostly offline; English thin outside music and Vainshtein.
- **Lands:** the tracker carries "Russia" as pre-war lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; these sheets are written for the core lands of the Republic of Tuva.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for Tsengel; Old Turkic reading for the epitaphs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamans' and lamas' healing knowledge; deficit framing of unemployment and alcohol in press and official reports; military contract service as a job (politically sensitive). Labor law goes to Libra; the clock and public order to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
