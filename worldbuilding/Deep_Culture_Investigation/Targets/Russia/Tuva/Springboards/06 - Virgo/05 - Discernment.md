# Tuva: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (Cyrillic), Russian; Mongolian for comparison and the cross-border Tuvans. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Clans** (for example Oorzhak, Mongush, Ondar, Kuular, Saryglar, Salchak, Dongak): whether clan origin weighs in judging people, and where it does not.
- **Horse-breeding districts of the west and center** against **Todzha reindeer herders**: different animals to judge.
- **Buddhist, shamanist and Orthodox households:** whose spiritual counsel is trusted.
- **Elders in villages** against **young people online in Kyzyl**: experience against digital savvy.
- **Russian and Old Believer residents:** separate matchmaking and religious-discernment customs.
- **Tsengel and Kanas Tuvans:** judgment as a small minority among other peoples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for horse and clan vocabulary and shamanic texts; mixed for the present day; scams are documented mainly in police and press reports.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian and Kazakh for Tsengel.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamans' and diviners' methods may be partly closed; do not treat divination as gullibility. Clan judgments touch on prejudice; read critically. Judging truth in law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
