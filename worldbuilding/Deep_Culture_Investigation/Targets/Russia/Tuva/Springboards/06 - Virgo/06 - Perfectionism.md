*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western kozhuuns** (Bai-Taiga, Barun-Khemchik, Mongun-Taiga): carving and singing traditions with masters.
- **Central valleys and Kyzyl:** urban crafts, schools and contests.
- **Buddhist ritual specialists** against **shamans**: different ideas of correct performance.
- **Old Believer villages:** exact rite as religious duty.
- **Todzha:** reindeer-herding crafts (harness, birch-bark) with their own standards.
- **Soviet-trained workers** against **those who came of age after 1991**: quality marks against market branding.
- **Men and women:** crafts divided by sex (saddles, silver against felt, embroidery, sewing), *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for music and craft; mixed for ritual exactness; thin for perfectionism as a psychological trait.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan for sacred art.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ritual details may be withheld; ask what may be shared. The republic's suicide and mental-health figures are a sensitive area; never tie them to perfectionism without evidence, and leave them to specialists.
- **Held for review:** none.
