# Tuva: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (Cyrillic), Russian; Mongolian for the Tsengel and Kanas Tuvans. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Steppe herders of the central and southern kozhuuns** against **western high-pasture yak herders** (Mongun-Taiga): different herds, different labor loads.
- **Todzha reindeer herders and fur hunters**: taiga work and a separate seasonal calendar.
- **Kyzyl public-sector workers** (teachers, clerks, doctors): the largest wage employers.
- **Mining and company towns** (Ak-Dovurak, Khovu-Aksy, Kyzyl-Tashtyg area): industrial and shift work.
- **Russian and Old Believer villages of the upper Yenisei**: farming, logging and a religious work rule.
- **Workers who left**: rotational workers and students in Krasnoyarsk, Novosibirsk and Moscow; Tsengel Tuvans in Mongolia.
- **Generations**: those who worked in sovkhozes against those who came of age after 1991.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official statistics and Soviet labor history; mixed for folk attitudes; thin in English.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for the cross-border communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unemployment and poverty are often framed as a Tuvan failing in Russian media; read for that bias. Military contract service as paid work is politically sensitive. Labor law and sick-leave rules go to Libra; legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Tenishev's dictionary: every entry built on *ажыл*, plus *кежээ* and *чалгаа*; note compounds and set phrases.
2. elibrary.ru: "трудовые традиции тувинцев" and "отношение к труду Тува"; sort by citation.
3. Rosstat: labor force survey tables, "Республика Тыва", unemployment and informal employment, 2010 to the latest year.
4. TIGPI catalog: proverb and folk-tale volumes; search "үлегер домактар ажыл".
5. VK search: "Кадарчы Тыва" and "вахта тувинцы" for present-day talk about work.
