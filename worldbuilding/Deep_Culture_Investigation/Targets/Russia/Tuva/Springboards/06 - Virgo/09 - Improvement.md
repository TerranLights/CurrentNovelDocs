# Tuva: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (Cyrillic, and the 1930s Latin script for early sources), Russian; Mongolian for comparison. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

English keywords: Tuva sedentarization nomads; Sayano-Shushenskaya reservoir Shagonar; Tuvan Latin alphabet 1930; Tuvan language revitalization; Kyzyl-Kuragino railway; Tuva Buddhist revival.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders who stayed mobile** against **families settled in Soviet times**: improvement as gain or loss.
- **People displaced by the reservoir** (old Shagonar and the flooded valleys).
- **Todzha reindeer herders:** decline and revival of herding as renewal on their own terms.
- **Buddhist and shamanic revival communities:** renewal by return.
- **Mining districts** (Ak-Dovurak, Kyzyl-Tashtyg area): development's promises and costs.
- **Russian settlers and Old Believers:** progress welcomed or refused.
- **Young Tuvans leaving to study and work** against **those who stay**: getting ahead and its geography.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet development history and present programs; mixed for personal self-improvement; thin in English.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, including the Latin-script 1930s press; Russian; Mongolian for comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Soviet "civilizing" campaigns against shamans, lamas and nomads must be told first by those they were imposed on; displacement and repression memories are painful; Chinese-owned mining is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
