# Tuva: Analysis (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (Cyrillic), Russian; Mongolian and Tibetan for the monastic and calendrical learning. **Concept in one line:** taking a whole apart to understand it: reasoning from parts, evidence, measurement and detail.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders reckoning stock and pasture** against **trained zootechnicians and veterinarians**: experience against measured expertise.
- **Buddhist monastic learners** against **shamanic practitioners**: argument by definition against other ways of knowing, as each describes itself.
- **Kyzyl's university-educated** against **rural kozhuuns**: schooling, language of instruction and access.
- **Tuvan-medium and Russian-medium schools**: whether the language of reasoning differs.
- **Western, southern and Todzha districts**: test results and schooling access vary with distance.
- **Russian and Old Believer communities**: different stances toward secular science.
- **Tuvans studying in Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk and Moscow**: the out-migrating analytic professions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; exam and institutional data are easy; folk reckoning and monastic learning need specialist and Tuvan-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Tibetan and Mongolian for manuscripts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination (for example 41-stone casting, Tuvan *хуваанак*, *check*) is a living practice; treat it as its practitioners describe it, not as failed reasoning. Avoid reading low exam scores as a trait; they track schooling and language.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-located monastic colleges where some Tuvan monks train, as a category only.
