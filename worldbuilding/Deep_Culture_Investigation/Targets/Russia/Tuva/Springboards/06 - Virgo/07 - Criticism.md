# Tuva: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Tuva, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Tuvan (Cyrillic), Russian; Mongolian for cognates and cross-border Tuvans. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and the young:** whether and how the young may ever correct elders.
- **Kin categories:** joking kin against kin owed silence and respect.
- **Tuvan-speaking villages** (western and southern kozhuuns) against **Russian-speaking Kyzyl**: different channels of complaint.
- **Officials and public-sector workers** against **private traders and herders**: who can afford to criticize authority.
- **Buddhist, shamanist and Orthodox communities:** religious teaching on harsh speech and on judging others.
- **Todzha:** small settlements where criticism has nowhere to hide.
- **Soviet generation** against **post-Soviet generation:** party self-criticism against social-media complaint.
- **Russian and Old Believer villages:** community discipline under their own religious rule.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and Soviet political history; mixed for present-day feedback norms; press criticism is documented but constrained.
- **Languages needed:** Tuvan, Russian; Mongolian for cognates and Tsengel.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of the authorities and of the war is legally risky in present-day Russia; do not identify private critics. Curses and shamanic displeasure are serious matters to those involved. Free-speech law and censorship as law go to Libra; authority to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
