# Chechnya: Possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen (Нохчийн мотт), Russian; Nogai, Kumyk, Avar and Ingush for minorities; Arabic, Turkish, Georgian and Kazakh for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** the domain of possessions and value: what is worth having, and how is it held (money, goods, resources, land; the food, dress and wealth folkways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland communities** (Itum-Kale, Shatoy, Sharoy, Vedeno) against the **lowland plains** of the Sunzha and Terek: herding against farm and town wealth, and the forced move down from the mountains after the 1957 return.
- **Tukkhum and teip groupings** (Akki, Melkhi, Cheberloy, Sharoy, Shatoy, Terloy, Orstkhoy, Chanti, Nokhchmakhkoy): kin-held land and name prestige may differ.
- **Grozny** against **villages (*юрт*)**: wages and apartments against plots and herds.
- **Nadterechny (north of the Terek)**: a distinct Soviet and war-era history of land and loyalty.
- **Terek and Grebensky Cossack descendants** in Naursky and Shelkovskoy districts, including Old Believers: their own inheritance and household customs.
- **Nogai** of the Shelkovskoy steppe; **Kumyk and Avar** villages near the Dagestan line: herding and market traditions of their own.
- **Sufi *wird* communities** (Qadiriya, Naqshbandiya): almsgiving and feasting may vary.
- **Diaspora:** Akkin Chechens in Dagestan's Khasavyurt area, Kists in Georgia's Pankisi, Chechens in Jordan, Turkey, Syria, Kazakhstan and Western Europe.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich 19th-century ethnography and Soviet folklore; thin, politically shaped modern data; national surveys seldom break out Chechnya; many local archives were lost in the wars *check*.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Chechen Republic's lands.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen (Cyrillic with palochka Ӏ; older Arabic-script texts, and the Latin alphabets of 1925-1938 and the 1990s), Russian; Nogai, Kumyk, Avar; Arabic for religious texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fieldwork carries risk for informants under the present authorities; property ties to war losses, compensation and seizures; family wealth touches honor (*сий*) and shame (*эхь*); women's property and wedding-payment rules. The legal side of inheritance and *adat* belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
