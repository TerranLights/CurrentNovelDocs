# Chechnya: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Soviet-era oil and industrial families** of Grozny against **rural farming households**: two older routes to being well-off.
- **Households tied to public office and state contracts** against **traders and small business owners**.
- **Men working in Moscow, the Siberian oil fields or abroad** and the families living on what they send.
- **Teips with famous elders, scholars or saints** against those without: wealth in names and followers, not only money.
- **Nogai families** whose *бай* forebears were dekulakized.
- **Terek Cossack descendants**, whose stanitsa wealth rested on land shares.
- **Kists** of Pankisi; **old-diaspora families** in Jordan and Turkey.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good historical and statistical sources; little honest public talk about where large fortunes come from.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for religious teaching; Georgian for Pankisi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wealth near power is dangerous to research on the ground; display stories can turn into stereotypes, so record who displays and who does not; poverty brings shame in many families. Tax and property law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull Rosstat's regional series "коэффициент Джини" and "распределение населения по величине среднедушевых доходов" for the Chechen Republic.
2. Search the Accounts Chamber site for "Чеченская Республика проверка".
3. Search eLibrary.ru for "раскулачивание Чечено-Ингушетия" and "ногайцы раскулачивание".
4. Search library catalogs for "Чермоев" and "история грозненской нефти".
5. Search folklore editions for tales with *мисканиг* or "бедняк" in the title.
