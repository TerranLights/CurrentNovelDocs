# Chechnya: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Kumyk and Nogai for minorities; Georgian, German and Arabic for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women**: what worth rests on for each, and who judges it; ask, do not assume.
- **Elders (*воккха стаг*)** against **young men** formed by the wars and by social media.
- **Families with a member killed, jailed or exiled** in the conflicts: worth after loss and stigma.
- **Wird communities** (Kunta-Haji's Qadiri followers; Naqshbandi lines): humility before God as a source of worth.
- **The Kazakhstan-born generation** and those who stayed in Central Asia.
- **Russian, Cossack, Nogai and Kumyk residents**, whose own words (*казачья честь*, *намус*) carry different weight.
- **Diaspora**: Jordan and Turkey, settled for generations; Austria, France, Germany and Poland, settled since 2000; Kists in Pankisi.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on ideals in folklore and essays; thin on measured self-esteem; official health data may understate distress.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; German and French for the European diaspora; Georgian for Pankisi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor talk can feed stereotypes; record each voice as one voice. Gender, sexuality and public shaming are dangerous topics for informants; mental illness carries stigma.
- **Held for review:** none.
