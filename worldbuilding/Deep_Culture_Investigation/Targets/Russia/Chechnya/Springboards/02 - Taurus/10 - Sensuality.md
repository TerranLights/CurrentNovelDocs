## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Weddings and family feasts** against **religious gatherings**: two settings where pleasure is allowed in different measure.
- **Strictly observant households** against **more relaxed ones**, in Grozny and in villages.
- **Highland cooks** (dried meat, cheese, wild garlic) against **lowland cooks** (corn, pumpkin, garden produce).
- **Young people** who listen to Russian and global pop against **elders** who prefer *илли* and *назма*.
- **Cossack, Nogai and Kumyk** neighbors, with their own food, drink and music.
- **Kists** of Pankisi and **old-diaspora families** in Jordan and Turkey.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on food, music and dance as heritage; thin on private pleasures and how people talk about comfort; sex is Scorpio's and is not scouted here.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Nogai; Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** music, dress and gender mixing are policed, so informants may answer with the official line; some *zikr* forms are restricted to members; avoid treating one family's austerity or one wedding's display as typical.
- **Held for review:** none.
