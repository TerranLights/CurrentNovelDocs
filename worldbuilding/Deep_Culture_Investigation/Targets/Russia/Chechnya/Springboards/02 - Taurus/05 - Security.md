# Chechnya: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families who sheltered in Ingushetia or Dagestan** during the wars against **those who stayed in Grozny**: different lessons about what keeps a family safe.
- **Public-sector workers** (whose posts depend on the republic) against **traders and migrant laborers** working elsewhere in Russia.
- **Pensioners**, including the elderly Russians who stayed in Grozny.
- **Mountain districts** near former fighting zones (Vedeno, Shatoy, Itum-Kale) where mines and road closures lasted longest.
- **Nogai herders** of the steppe, facing drought rather than war.
- **Cossack descendants and Russians** who left in the 1990s: insecurity as the reason for leaving.
- **Kists** of Pankisi and **Chechens in Poland, Austria and France**, where status is the main risk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good federal statistics and rights archives; official figures and independent monitoring often disagree; little direct study of how people feel about security.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Polish, German, French and Georgian for the diaspora and neighbors.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions on fear of the authorities, disappearances or past fighting put informants at risk; Memorial was dissolved in Russia in 2021, so its archive must be used with care for those named; weapons and policing go to Libra and Capricorn where law is involved.
- **Held for review:** none.
