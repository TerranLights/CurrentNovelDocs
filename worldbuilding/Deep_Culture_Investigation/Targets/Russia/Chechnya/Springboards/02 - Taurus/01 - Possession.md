# Chechnya: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Nogai, Kumyk and Ingush for minorities and neighbors; Georgian, Arabic and Turkish for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men:** jewelry, cloth and the bride's goods against weapons, cars and tools; who keeps them after marriage or divorce is a question, not an answer.
- **Families who returned after the wars** against those who stayed in Ingushetia, Kazakhstan or Europe: what each kept and rebuilt.
- **Grozny apartment dwellers** against **owners of rebuilt private houses** in the suburbs and villages.
- **Highland households** (Itum-Kale, Sharoy, Cheberloy) where old storage towers and lofts once stood.
- **Grebensky and Terek Cossack descendants**, including Old Believers, whose icons and books are their own class of heirloom.
- **Nogai and Kumyk families** of the northern and eastern districts.
- **Kists of Pankisi** and **Akkin Chechens** of Khasavyurt, living beside Georgian and Dagestani neighbors.
- **Descendants of the 19th-century exiles** in Jordan, Turkey and Syria against the **post-2000 refugees** in Austria, France, Germany and Poland.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich museum and ethnographic material for the 19th century; good rights and humanitarian records of loss; little direct study of everyday belongings today.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Georgian and Arabic for the diaspora; Church Slavonic for Old Believer heirlooms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** looting and war loss name living people and units; theft and loss carry shame; relics at Sufi shrines (*зиярат*) may be restricted to the faithful; weapons ownership has a legal side for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
