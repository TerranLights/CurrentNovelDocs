# Chechnya: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sufi brotherhood followers** (the Kunta-Haji, Vis-Haji, Batal-Haji and other *wirds*; Naqshbandi lines) against **Salafi-leaning believers** and the **less observant**.
- **The Soviet-educated generation** in Grozny against **those raised in wartime or after 2009**.
- **Highland lineages** against **lowland and Nadterechny families**, whose 20th-century histories differ.
- **Women** in public work, at home and abroad: who speaks for "family values".
- **Russian Orthodox and Old Believer residents** of the northern stanitsas.
- **Nogai** of the north and **Kumyk** villages in the east.
- **Kists** of Pankisi, Muslim with a remembered Christian past.
- **Diaspora:** Jordan, Turkey and Syria (settled since the 1860s) against Western Europe (since 2000).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich normative sources (sermons, proverbs, official programs); measured values data is thin and rarely broken out by republic.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; French, German, Turkish for the diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "traditional values" is a state slogan, so what people say on record may follow it; Salafi belonging can bring arrest; religious disagreement is risky to voice. The letter and spirit of law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
