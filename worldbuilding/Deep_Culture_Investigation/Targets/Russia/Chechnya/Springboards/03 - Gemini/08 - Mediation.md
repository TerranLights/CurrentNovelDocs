# Chechnya: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for religious intercession and reconciliation; Ingush and Kumyk for neighbors and minority villages; English and German for international mediation. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and the go-between more broadly (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders of large teips** and **elders of small or broken lineages**: unequal standing as go-betweens.
- **Sufi sheikhs and their followers**, and **imams of the state Muftiate**: two religious sources of intercession.
- **Women**, as possible peacemakers within and between families.
- **Highland villages** (Itum-Kali, Vedeno, Sharoy) and **the plain**: possibly different customs for stepping in.
- **Kumyk, Nogai and Avar communities**, with Dagestani forms of mediation.
- **Russian residents** of the Terek left bank, who may turn to state bodies.
- **Ingush neighbors** in the border districts.
- **The diaspora**, where community figures go between families and foreign institutions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Ethnography of reconciliation and nineteenth-century history are well covered; present practice is reported mostly by journalists and human-rights groups.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Ingush and Kumyk; English and German for international and asylum sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reconciliation of feuds touches blood and honor, and present state involvement is politically charged; informants may be at risk. Customary and religious law as such go to Libra (the letter) and Capricorn (the spirit); this sheet covers only the go-between and the ritual of making peace.
- **Held for review:** none.
