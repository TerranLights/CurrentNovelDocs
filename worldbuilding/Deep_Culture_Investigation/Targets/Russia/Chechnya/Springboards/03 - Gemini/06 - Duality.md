# Chechnya: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for religious pairs; Ingush and Kumyk; Georgian for the Kists; Turkish and European languages for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** how the culture lives with two-ness: two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how it treats people who show two faces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian-dominant urban families** and **Chechen-dominant village families**: two-ness may be felt from opposite sides.
- **Religious traditionalists of the Sufi brotherhoods** and **Salafi-leaning youth**: different views on mixing custom and religion.
- **The Terek left bank**, with its Cossack and Nogai heritage and long frontier mixing.
- **Mixed Chechen-Russian or Chechen-Ingush families**.
- **Akkin Chechens** in Dagestan, Chechen and Dagestani at once.
- **Kists**, Chechen and Georgian.
- **Muhajir descendants** in Turkey, Jordan and Syria, and **recent emigrants** in Europe.
- **Veterans and families on different sides** of the wars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Literature, frontier history and diaspora studies exist; direct study of dualism as a worldview is likely thin.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic, Georgian, Turkish; German, French and Polish for diaspora studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** loyalties in the wars, accusations of hypocrisy or betrayal, and the Ingush border are highly charged; avoid putting informants in the position of choosing sides. Adat and sharia as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
