## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Plain and city Chechens** (Grozny, Argun, Gudermes, Urus-Martan): the base of the literary language and of Russian-medium schooling.
- **Highland communities** (Itum-Kali, Shatoy, Sharoy, Vedeno; Cheberloy and Melkhi): distinct dialects and older patterns of visiting along gorges.
- **Teip and tukkhum affiliations** (тайп, тукхум): kin networks that may route talk, news and help.
- **Sufi brotherhoods** (Qadiri vird groups after Kunta-Haji, Naqshbandi lines) and Salafi-leaning circles: different settings for religious learning.
- **Terek left-bank districts** (Naursky, Shelkovskoy): Terek Cossack and Russian heritage, plus Nogai villages.
- **Kumyk and Avar villages** (for example Braguny *check*): other first languages inside the republic.
- **Ingush in the western border districts**: a close Vainakh language, with a disputed border.
- **Akkin Chechens** (Khasavyurt area, Dagestan) and **Kists** (Pankisi Gorge, Georgia): kin with their own varieties.
- **Generations**: the deportation-born, the war-years youth, those schooled after 2009.
- **Diaspora**: Jordan, Turkey, Syria, Kazakhstan, and post-war Europe (Austria, France, Germany, Belgium, Poland, Norway).
