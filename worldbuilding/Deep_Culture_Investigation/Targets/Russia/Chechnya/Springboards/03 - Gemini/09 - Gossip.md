*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women's and men's circles**: different places and topics; whether the claim that one sex gossips more holds is a question, not a premise.
- **Villages** where everyone knows everyone, and **Grozny's apartment blocks**.
- **Young people online** and **elders in the mosque yard**.
- **Families with high standing** and **families already talked about**: unequal exposure.
- **Religious circles** that preach against backbiting, and how they practice it.
- **Kumyk, Nogai and Russian communities**, with their own networks of talk.
- **The European diaspora**, where talk crosses borders.
- **People returning from abroad** or from prison, whose past is retold.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Religious teaching and honor-and-shame ethnography exist; direct study of Chechen gossip is likely absent; online material is plentiful but ephemeral.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; German and French for diaspora research.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gossip about named people can endanger them, especially women; do not collect or repeat identifiable rumors. Public shaming by authorities is politically sensitive. Slander as a legal wrong goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
