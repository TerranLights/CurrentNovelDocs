## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men and young women:** different arenas of contest are open to each.
- **Wrestling villages and districts:** rivalry between local schools of sport; *check* which.
- **Akkin Chechens** of Khasavyurt, Dagestan (a wrestling center), and **Kists,** if in the lands.
- **Lineages:** contest for status between teips, without assuming any one pattern.
- **Students and religious learners:** exam and recitation rivalry.
- **Russians and Terek Cossack descendants; Nogais; Kumyks:** their own sports and games.
- **Diaspora athletes** in Sweden, Austria, France, Poland, Turkey.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport results; thin for attitudes; exam data are disputed.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Swedish, German, French; Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** combat sport is tied to the republic's leadership and to sanctions; teip rivalry is a delicate topic and must not be reduced to a stereotype. Ambition as a life goal is in Capricorn; play and recognition in Leo.
- **Held for review:** none.
