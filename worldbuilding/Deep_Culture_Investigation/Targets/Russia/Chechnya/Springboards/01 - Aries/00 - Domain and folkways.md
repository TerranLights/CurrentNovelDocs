## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highlanders and lowlanders** (ламанхой, аренхой): different lineage depth and memory of land.
- **The tukhums** (for example Ch'ebarloy, Sharoy, Shatoy, Terloy, Mälkhi, Akkiy, Nokhchmakhkoy *check* list): lineage blocks with their own dialects and stories.
- **Akkin (Aukh) Chechens** of western Dagestan, if they fall in the lands: a border identity.
- **Kists** of Pankisi (Georgia), if carried: Chechen speech, Georgian setting.
- **Ingush:** closest kin, a separate self-name; separate, not merged.
- **Terek (Grebensky) Cossacks and Russians** of the Naursky and Shelkovskoy districts: a different self entirely.
- **Nogais** of the northern steppe and **Kumyks** (for example Braguny *check*): Turkic-speaking minorities.
- **Sufi vird brotherhoods** (Qadiri, Naqshbandi) and younger scripturalist believers.
- **Generations** (born in Central Asian exile, in the wars, after); **Grozny and villages; men and women;** diasporas in Turkey, Jordan, Syria, Europe.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich Russian-language ethnography and war-era life-writing; thin Chechen-language scholarship online; self-construal survey data likely absent.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen (incl. older Arabic- and Latin-script texts), Russian; Arabic, Turkish, Georgian, Nogai, Kumyk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the deportation and both wars; informant safety under the present political climate (some outlets are banned or designated in Russia); avoid militant websites; family privacy. Customary law (Ӏадат) goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Scope:** the tracker carries "Russia" as pre-war lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the core lands of the present Chechen Republic.
- **Held for review:** none.
