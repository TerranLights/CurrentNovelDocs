# Chechnya: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic, Turkish, Kazakh-Russian and Georgian for the diasporas and the Kists; Nogai for the northern steppe. **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **By lineage:** teips and tukhums, including those of disputed or mixed origin.
- **Highland Itum-Kali and Sharoy districts** against the lowland plain and **Grozny:** depth of place-memory.
- **Akkin (Aukh) Chechens** of Dagestan and **Kists:** border identities.
- **Ingush:** shared Vainakh frame, distinct self-name and history.
- **Russians, Terek Cossack descendants and Nogais** in the north, **Kumyks:** other origin stories within the same lands.
- **Generations:** born in exile; born in the wars; born after.
- **Diasporas:** Turkey, Jordan, Syria, Kazakhstan, the European refugee communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on history and memory; thin on measured identity (few independent surveys).
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic, Turkish, Georgian, Nogai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national symbols, the deportation, both wars and secession are politically charged; origin claims are disputed with neighbors (the Ingush border included). Keep every claim sourced; do not adopt any side's story.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "этническое самосознание чеченцев" and "чеченская идентичность".
2. Search for published teip histories: "история тейпа" plus a teip name, then check who published them.
3. Find a collection of 1944 deportation documents and testimony; note the generational accounts.
4. Search Arabic sources for "الشيشان في الأردن" and English for "Chechen Jordanian identity".
5. Search Georgian catalogs for "ქისტები" with "იდენტობა" (identity).
