## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland herders and lowland farmers and traders:** different starting conditions and seasons.
- **Grozny's new professionals and the village:** who starts a firm, who starts a work party.
- **Women working from home** and **men in labor migration:** two different routes to "starting something".
- **Returnees from Europe** with outside capital or skills.
- **Russians, Terek Cossack descendants and Nogais** in the northern districts: other village economies.
- **Kists** (Pankisi) and **Akkin Chechens** (Dagestan), if in the lands.
- **Turkish and Jordanian Chechens:** long-settled trading communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Statistics exist but are federal in design; the informal economy is large and poorly measured; belkhi is described mainly in ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic, Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** business and charity are tied to state power; do not name private individuals; war-era self-help records hold personal data.
- **Held for review:** none.
