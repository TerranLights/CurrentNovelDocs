# Chechnya: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; German, French and Polish for the European refugee communities; Georgian for the Kists. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young women and young men:** the gap in what each may decide.
- **Divorced women and widows:** a different standing to choose; *check*.
- **Highland lineage villages and Grozny apartments:** how far "people" watch.
- **Sufi murids and scripturalist young believers:** obedience against personal reading.
- **Russians and Terek Cossack descendants; Nogais and Kumyks:** other family patterns in the same lands.
- **Kists** and **Akkin Chechens**, if in the lands.
- **Refugee families in Austria, France, Poland, Germany, Belgium:** choice reshaped by a second society.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Ethnography and rights reporting are plentiful; measured data on personal choice is thin; abuse cases dominate the record and may skew it.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; German, French, Polish for the diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced marriage, honor-related violence and LGBT persecution are dangerous topics for informants; anonymize everything. Custody and divorce rules are law: Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit). Collective sovereignty is in "Independence".
- **Held for review:** none.
