## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland families tied to tower villages** and **lowland villages founded or refounded in the 19th and 20th centuries.**
- **Returnees of 1957** and **those born in Kazakhstan or Kyrgyzstan:** two memories of a fresh start.
- **Akkin Chechens** (Dagestan), whose return met other settlers, if in the lands.
- **Russians and Terek Cossack descendants:** a settler founding story on the same ground.
- **Nogais** and **Kumyks:** older plains communities with their own origins.
- **Kists:** a 19th-century move into Georgia.
- **Turkish, Jordanian and Syrian Chechens;** recent refugees in Europe.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on deportation and return; mixed on calendar and birth rites; thin on everyday "fresh start" attitudes.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic, Turkish, Georgian; German and French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** founding stories are contested between peoples (Grozny's fort; land on return in 1957); war reconstruction is politically framed. Present each side's own account.
- **Held for review:** none.
