# Chechnya: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for Islamic teaching on anger; Ingush and Georgian for neighboring comparison. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women:** permitted displays differ; women's anger may be hidden in sources.
- **Elders and young men:** elders as coolers of anger, the young as its carriers; *check* both roles.
- **Feuding and reconciled lineages:** grudge against forgiveness.
- **Sufi brotherhoods and younger scripturalists:** two religious vocabularies of anger.
- **Ingush:** a shared feud vocabulary with separate institutions.
- **Russians and Terek Cossack descendants; Nogais; Kumyks:** their own norms of temper in the north.
- **Akkin Chechens and Kists:** whether insult and restraint are framed alike in these border communities, if they fall inside the lands.
- **Diaspora youth in Europe,** where outside stereotypes of temper follow them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Feud and war violence are heavily documented; everyday anger and its cooling are barely studied.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic; Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** outside writing is full of stereotypes about Chechen temper; never let it stand as evidence. Feud cases name living families. Customary procedure belongs to Libra and Capricorn. Vengeance as a practice may also be researched in Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
