# Chechnya: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen (including the 1990s Latin-script texts), Russian; Georgian for Pankisi; Turkish for the diaspora villages. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others; self-reliance and self-sufficiency.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland herding families** and **lowland plot farmers:** different self-provisioning.
- **Grozny wage earners and public employees:** reliance on salaries and the state.
- **Households of labor migrants** in Moscow and abroad.
- **War-disabled people and widows:** dependence by circumstance, and its standing.
- **Russians and Terek Cossack descendants** in the northern villages; **Nogais** (steppe herding) and **Kumyks**.
- **Kists** (Pankisi, Georgia) and **Akkin Chechens** (Dagestan side): how kin-group independence reads at the edges, if these lands are held.
- **Diasporas** in Turkey, Jordan, Austria, France and Germany.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Budget and farm data exist; attitudes to asking for help are barely measured; the independence movement is richly documented and fiercely contested.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen (Cyrillic and 1990s Latin script), Russian; Georgian, Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national independence and secession are prosecutable topics in Russia; never press informants on them. Statutes and constitutions go to Libra; obedience and legitimacy to Capricorn. Personal choice is in "Autonomy".
- **Held for review:** none.
