# Chechnya: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Kumyk and Nogai; Georgian for the Kist borderlands; German and French for diaspora youth. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how people in Chechnya's lands understand the chosen bond of liking, loyalty and help between non-kin, from the sworn guest-friend and the comrade to school friends, and how far it may reach across sex, people and faith.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men's friendship** and **women's friendship**: different settings, rules and visibility; cross-sex friendship as a question.
- **Village** friend groups against **Grozny** student and work friends.
- **Lowland families with old Cossack kunak ties** against **highland** families without them.
- **Chechens and the republic's Russians, Kumyks and Nogais**: friendship across peoples now, and the pre-1991 mixed city.
- **Wartime comrades** on opposing sides, and veterans.
- **The exile generation**, with friends among Kazakhs and other exiles.
- **Diaspora youth** in Europe; the **Kists** of Pankisi.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the old guest-friend institutions; thin on present-day friendship, especially women's, and with no regional survey numbers likely.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Kumyk, Nogai; Georgian; German, French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** friendships across the war's sides, and across sex, can expose people; ask in ways that protect them. Old kunak romance in Russian literature is an outsider's lens.
- **Held for review:** none.
