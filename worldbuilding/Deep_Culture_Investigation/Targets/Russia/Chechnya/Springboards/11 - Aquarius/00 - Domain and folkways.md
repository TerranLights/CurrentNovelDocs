## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lowland and urban Chechens** (Grozny, Argun, Gudermes, Urus-Martan) against **highland societies** of the Argun gorge and Sharoy-Itum-Kale (Maistoy, Melkhi, Terloy, Cheberloy, Shatoy): settlement and clan density differ.
- **Ichkerians of the southeast** (Vedeno, Nozhay-Yurt) and the **Orstkhoy of the west**: distinct regional traditions.
- **Terek left bank** (Naursky, Shelkovskoy): Cossack stanitsas, including Grebensky Old Believer descendants, and Nogai and Kumyk villages: different group forms entirely.
- **Sufi wird communities** (Qadiri branches, Naqshbandi) against **Salafi** circles: different "we".
- **The exile generation** born in Kazakhstan or Kyrgyzstan, against those born after the 1957 return and the **war-born** generations.
- **Ingush** across the border, and **Akkin (Aukh) Chechens** of Dagestan (a separate target): kin outside the lands.
- **Diasporas**: Turkey, Jordan, Syria (dispersed), Europe (Austria, France, Germany, Belgium, Poland), Kazakhstan; the Kists of Pankisi.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich Russian-language ethnography of clan and hospitality, often romanticized; thin independent survey data after 2009.
- **Lands:** the tracker carries the pre-war lands as "Russia", so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the Chechen Republic's core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Kumyk, Nogai; Turkish, Georgian, German and French for the diasporas.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** present-day political control and reprisals against relatives make contact with informants risky; war, deportation and disappearances are trauma subjects; clan names can be used politically. Law sits in Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
