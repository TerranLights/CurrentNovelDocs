# Chechnya: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for religious dispute terms; German and French for exiles in Europe. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how people in Chechnya's lands meet departures from the group's norms of belief, dress, conduct and way of life (the outlaw, the dissenting brotherhood, the person who will not fit), and how far each is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sufi brotherhoods** with distinctive dress or rite (the Vis-Hajji followers; loud-zikr Qadiri circles) against **Salafis**, each of whom the other may call nonconforming.
- **Secular and Soviet-minded** Chechens and **non-believers**, a group rarely heard.
- **Women** who defy marriage or dress rules, and **men** who refuse expected roles.
- **Sexual and gender minorities**, almost entirely hidden.
- **Old Believer descendants** and other **Russians** of the left bank; **Kumyks** and **Nogais**.
- **Rural** against **Grozny**: what counts as odd in each.
- **Exiles and the diaspora young** in Europe and Turkey.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on religious dissent and the outlaw in history; present-day nonconformity is documented mostly by human-rights reports and is otherwise thin, because it is dangerous.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; German, French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the most dangerous subject in this sign. Persecution of gay men, women fleeing families and dissidents abroad (including attacks on exiles) put real people at risk. Never identify a person; use published, anonymized material only; owning some banned texts carries legal risk in Russia.
- **Held for review:** none.
