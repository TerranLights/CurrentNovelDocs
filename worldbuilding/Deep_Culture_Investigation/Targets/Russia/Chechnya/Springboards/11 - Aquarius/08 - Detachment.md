# Chechnya: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for teaching on patience and renunciation; German and Norwegian or English for refugee mental-health studies. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: which kinds of distance (restraint of feeling, composure in grief and danger, respectful avoidance, renunciation, solitude, withdrawal from public life) people in Chechnya's lands count as right, and which as coldness or indifference.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men** and **women**: different display rules in grief and before elders.
- **Daughters-in-law** and **younger men** in the household, held to the strictest distance; **elders**, who set it.
- **Sufi practitioners** of seclusion, against **Salafi** teaching on mourning, against **secular** Grozny professionals (doctors, journalists).
- **War-exposed** generations, against those born after 2009.
- **Highland herders** and **lowland townspeople**.
- **Russians**, **Kumyks** and **Nogais** of the republic, with other mourning and avoidance customs.
- **Diaspora** families under host-country therapy norms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich ethnography of avoidance and mourning; thin and hard to get on present-day emotional life; official health and turnout figures are unreliable.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; German, Norwegian, Polish for the refugee studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trauma, suicide and mental illness carry stigma; never treat restraint as a national trait. Impartiality in courts goes to Libra; detachment of officials as a legal ideal to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
