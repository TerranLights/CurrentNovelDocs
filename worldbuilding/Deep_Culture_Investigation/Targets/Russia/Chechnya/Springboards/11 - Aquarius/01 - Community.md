# Chechnya: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for congregational terms; Nogai and Kumyk for the Terek left bank. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how people in Chechnya's lands name and feel the bond among those who share a place, a faith, an origin or a cause, what members owe one another, and how that bond is lost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland villages emptied after 1944** and never fully resettled, against the **plains villages** their people moved to: place community lost and rebuilt.
- **Grozny** after the wars, rebuilt and mostly Chechen, against the **pre-1991 mixed city**.
- **Shrine villages** of Sufi branches (around the Kunta-Hajji and Vis-Hajji communities *check*), against villages organized by **mosque** alone or by **Salafi** congregations.
- **Terek left-bank stanitsas** and **Nogai** settlements of the Shelkovskoy and Naursky districts.
- **Kumyk** villages near the Dagestan border.
- **Refugee and labor diasporas** in Europe and Russia's cities against the **old diasporas** of Turkey and Jordan, and the **Kists** of Pankisi.
- Men and women: their separate gatherings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on the funeral, the wedding and village customs; thin on felt "sense of community" or loneliness measures.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic; Nogai, Kumyk; German, French, Polish for Europe.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 deportation, Khaibakh and the wars are trauma subjects; testimony comes first; contact with residents can expose them. Community as pressure (shaming, control) is a hard subject to ask about inside the republic.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Find the *Чеченцы* volume ("Народы и культуры") and copy its chapters on village and social life into the reading list.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "тезет" + "чеченцы" and for "односельчане" + "Чечня".
3. Search the Ministry of Justice NGO register for the Chechen Republic and tally types.
4. Search Google Scholar for "Chechen refugees" + "Austria" OR "Poland" + "community".
