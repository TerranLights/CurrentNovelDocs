*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for Imamate-era documents; Turkish for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how people in Chechnya's lands weigh the group (lineage, village, brotherhood, people) against the person, where they draw "us" and "them", and what acting together, loyalty, desertion and shared blame mean to them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large lowland teips** against **small highland teips** whose members are few: how much a group can carry.
- **Teips of the old tukhums** (Ichkeri, Cheberloy, Sharoy, Shatoy, Akkiy, Melkhi, Orstkhoy *check* the list): different group histories.
- **Sufi wird members** (Qadiri and Naqshbandi branches) and **Salafis**: membership by faith cutting across clan.
- **Security-service men** and their families, against families of insurgents and émigrés.
- **Terek Cossack** descendants, **Nogais** and **Kumyks**: other group forms in the same lands.
- **Urban professionals and the young online**, against village elders.
- **Diaspora** groups in Europe, Turkey, Jordan and Kazakhstan, and the **Kists**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on clan structure, often repetitive; thin and dangerous to gather on present-day loyalty, desertion and shared blame.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for the Imamate; Turkish, German, French for diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective punishment, disowning of relatives and the war in Ukraine are live political matters; protect anyone quoted. Blood revenge and customary settlement as law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
