# Chechnya: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for religious ideals; Turkish and Arabic for diaspora writing. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how people in Chechnya's lands judge living by ideals held above present interest, which visions of a better order (a pious state, an independent homeland, a golden past) they have held, and how disillusion is spoken of.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sufi ideals** of patience and the perfect person, against **Salafi** ideals of a pure community, against **national-secular** ideals of 1991.
- **The 1990s generation**, who fought for or lived Ichkeria, against those raised after 2009.
- **Soviet-educated** Chechens, with Soviet ideals of progress and friendship of peoples.
- **Highland** families, who may idealize the mountains as origin, against **plains** families.
- **Russians**, **Cossack descendants**, **Kumyks** and **Nogais**, each with their own heroes.
- **Women's** ideals, as women state them, against the men's code.
- **Exiles and diasporas** in Europe, Turkey and Jordan; the **Kists**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, song and ethics writing; very thin in survey data for the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** independence and jihadist ideals touch Russian "extremism" law; some songs and texts are banned, and holding or sharing them may carry risk. Romantic outsider images (the noble highlander) are stereotypes to be named as such, never taken as the people's voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
