*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Qadiri vird communities (Kunta-Haji's lines) and Naqshbandi lines:** loud ecstatic zikr against silent remembrance.
- **Highland lineages whose villages were never resettled:** dissolution as lived history.
- **Grozny's Russian-speaking families against village Chechen speakers:** language edges.
- **Ingush neighbors:** the other half of the dissolved shared republic.
- **Terek Cossack stanitsas:** their numbers and the change since the 1990s are to be measured.
- **Kumyks of Braguny:** a Turkic village inside a Chechen-speaking district.
- **Diaspora in Jordan, Turkey and Europe; Kists of Pankisi:** outside the lands, for comparison of assimilation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on Sufi teaching and deportation; thin on how people themselves describe ego loss; no known measured data.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Ingush, Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the zikr is a living rite; filming or describing it may need the community's leave. The 1992 split and the Ingush border remain politically charged. Divorce and family breakup are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
