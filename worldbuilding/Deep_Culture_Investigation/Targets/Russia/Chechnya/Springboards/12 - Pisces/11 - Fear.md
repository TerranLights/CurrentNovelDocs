# Chechnya: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic (fear of God, protective prayer); Kumyk and Nogai for Turkic fear figures in the north; Ingush for border villages. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how fear of danger, real or imagined (war, the authorities, disaster, the unseen, God, shame), and its diffuse forms, worry and anxiety, are named, shown, managed and used in Chechnya's lands.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Children of the war years and children born after 2009:** different objects of fear.
- **Villages hit by sweep operations and those spared:** local memory of terror.
- **Men and women:** who may show fear, and before whom.
- **Grozny and the highlands:** bombing memory against forest and mountain legends.
- **Families with relatives abroad or in detention:** fear for absent kin.
- **Terek Cossacks and Russians:** their own fear figures and memories of the 1990s.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** Turkic fears around birth and the night.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on war fear from testimony; thin on everyday worry and on measured anxiety.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Kumyk, Nogai, Ingush.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fear of the authorities cannot be openly discussed by informants; trauma testimony needs care and consent. Folk fear figures may be sensitive for those who hold them. Courage belongs to Aries.
- **Held for review:** none.
