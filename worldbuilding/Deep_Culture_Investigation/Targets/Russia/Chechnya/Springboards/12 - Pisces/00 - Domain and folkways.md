# Chechnya: Domain and folkways (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen (нохчийн мотт, *noxçiyn mott*; Cyrillic since 1938, Arabic script before 1925, Latin 1925-1938), Russian; Kumyk, Nogai and Ingush for the minorities and border villages; Arabic for religious texts. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how people in Chechnya's lands meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape, including madness and distress, sleep and night, confinement, spirits, transcendence, and the "magic ways" of luck, healing and hidden harm.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lowland (*аре*) and highland (*лам*) Chechens:** whether sanctuary and tower-tomb customs lasted longer in the uplands is a question to test.
- **Qadiri and Naqshbandi vird communities:** loud and silent zikr imply different paths to the transcendent.
- **Newer scripturalist Muslims:** they may reject shrines and charms; a contested, sensitive line.
- **Russian and Terek Cossack residents of Naursky and Shelkovskoy districts:** Orthodox and Old Believer heritage, a separate vocabulary of saints and the dead.
- **Kumyks (Braguny) and Nogais of the northern steppe:** Turkic-language folk healing and spirit lore.
- **Deportation-generation elders versus post-war youth:** lived exile against media-learned memory.
- **Kists of Pankisi (Georgia), Akkin Chechens (Dagestan), diaspora in Jordan, Turkey and Europe:** outside the lands, useful as comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for Sufism, war and deportation; thin and politically shaped for mental-health statistics and suicide.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Kumyk and Nogai for the north; Georgian for Pankisi sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war trauma, torture and filtration testimony; criticism of authorities carries risk for informants; some shrine and family customs may be private. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core republic.
- **Held for review:** none.
