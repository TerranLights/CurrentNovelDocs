# Chechnya: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic (sin and injustice); Ingush for the border villages, Kumyk and Nogai for the northern minorities. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: what counts in Chechnya's lands as a serious wrong against the community, and how the wrongdoer, the victim and the outlaw are seen. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland and lowland communities:** older memories of raiding and outlawry against town life.
- **Families of victims and families of wrongdoers:** often the same networks; how each speaks.
- **Former Russian and Cossack residents of Grozny and the Terek districts:** a minority memory of the 1990s.
- **Kumyk and Nogai steppe villages:** livestock theft and land quarrels as local wrongs.
- **The young online:** state and opposition channels give rival crime stories.
- **Diaspora in Europe:** wrongs and stigma seen from abroad.
- **Ingush neighbors:** a shared vocabulary and separate cases.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed and politically shaped. Rich in human-rights and media material; official crime figures are hard to trust or compare.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Kumyk, Nogai, Ingush; French and German for diaspora coverage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** extremely high. Wrongs by officials, the disappeared, insurgency and extremism labels bring real danger to informants; many Telegram channels are banned. Never repeat the "criminal people" stereotype; record it as a claim only. Blood feud, courts and customary law are Capricorn's and Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
