## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sufi murids and scripturalist Muslims:** whether saints' insight and dream guidance are accepted.
- **Women elders and younger women:** household authority in reading signs and dreams.
- **Highland herders and lowland townspeople:** land knowledge against urban judgment.
- **Doctors, engineers and other professionals:** expert hunches against official protocol.
- **Former residents of wartime Grozny:** survival judgments about strangers.
- **Terek Cossacks and Nogais:** their own omen and weather traditions.
- **Kumyks of Braguny:** Turkic dream words and customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Religious guidance and folklore exist; there is no measured intuition data for the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; German for early travelers; Kumyk and Nogai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims to hidden knowledge may be judged as sorcery or as unorthodox, so practitioners may speak guardedly; saints' stories belong to their communities. Divination is studied as a practice only, never used as a source.
- **Held for review:** none.
