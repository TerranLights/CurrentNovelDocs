# Chechnya: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for religious terms on idle diversion and intoxicants; Kumyk, Nogai and Ingush for minority and border communities. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining is sought in Chechnya's lands (through play, sport, screens, travel, intoxicants, withdrawal or leaving), and which routes are blessed, tolerated or shamed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men and young women:** which escapes are open to each in public and at home.
- **Grozny and Argun against highland villages:** malls, gyms and screens against the hills.
- **Families of former fighters and families of officials:** different pressures and permitted outlets.
- **Russians and Cossacks of the Terek districts:** drinking, festivity and church calendars of their own.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** steppe and village leisure.
- **Returnees and deportees from Europe:** escape that failed or was reversed.
- **Diaspora in Austria, France, Germany and Poland:** escape as settlement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Drinking, drugs and gambling are hidden subjects; tourism and sport are well documented by the state.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for sermons; Kumyk and Nogai for the north.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** admitting drink or drug use can bring public shaming; asylum and emigration touch on persecution and on families still at home. Rules on alcohol sale belong to Libra; here, only how people speak of escape.
- **Held for review:** none.
