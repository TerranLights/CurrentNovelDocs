# Chechnya: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for the religious vocabulary of mercy; Kumyk and Ingush in border and northern villages. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how being moved by another's suffering, and mercy toward those in one's power, are named, taught, organized and limited in Chechnya's lands.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland villages (Itum-Kale, Sharoy, Vedeno uplands):** small communities where work parties and neighborly aid may survive in older forms.
- **Grozny's rebuilt apartment districts:** strangers as neighbors; whether help reaches non-kin.
- **Large teips against small or broken lineages:** who can call on a network and who is left to the state or charity.
- **Women heading households after the wars:** widows as givers and receivers of help.
- **Russian and Cossack residents of the Terek districts:** Orthodox charity, and how Chechen neighbors treat them.
- **Kumyk Braguny and the Nogai steppe:** Turkic almsgiving words and customs.
- **Returnees and refugees from Europe:** expectations shaped abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Plenty on official charity and war relief; thin on everyday giving and on mercy toward offenders.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Kumyk for the north.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state charity is politically charged, so informants may speak only of approved forms; mercy toward former fighters or their families is risky to discuss. Mercy against justice belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
