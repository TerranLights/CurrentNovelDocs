# Chechnya: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for the vocabulary of submission to God and for 19th-century chronicles; Ottoman Turkish for the 1860s emigration; Kumyk for the north. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how ceasing to resist, and handing one's will over to God, fate, an enemy, illness or death, is named and judged in Chechnya's lands, including letting go and release.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Qadiri followers of Kunta-Haji's line and households that honor armed resistance:** two models of yielding and refusing.
- **Elders and the young:** memories of 1944 and 1957 against post-war life.
- **Families of former fighters who accepted amnesty:** a very sensitive group, only through published scholarship.
- **Women:** acceptance in marriage and widowhood, in their own words.
- **Terek Cossacks and Russians:** their own history of defeat in the 1920s and the 1990s.
- **Descendants of the 1865 emigrants in Turkey and Jordan:** outside the lands, with a different ending to the same war.
- **Ingush neighbors:** a shared history of deportation and return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for historic defeats and religious teaching; thin and dangerous for recent surrender and amnesty.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Ottoman and modern Turkish; Kumyk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** amnesty, changing sides and the 1990s defeats are politically charged; informants face risk. Reconciliation as law belongs to Capricorn; end-of-life law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
