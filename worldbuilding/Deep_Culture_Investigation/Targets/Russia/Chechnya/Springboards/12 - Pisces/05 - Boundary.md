*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland lineages around Itum-Kale, Sharoy and Malkhista:** tower lands and border-zone life.
- **Lowland villages and Grozny:** fences, courtyards and apartment walls; neighbors who are not kin.
- **Women and men:** body, dress and space lines, as each describes them.
- **Brides and in-laws:** avoidance customs across generations.
- **Terek Cossacks and Russians north of the Terek:** a different house and fence tradition.
- **Nogais of the Shelkovskoy steppe and Kumyks of Braguny:** minority land edges.
- **Ingush neighbors and Kists across the Georgian ridge:** kin peoples on the far side of a line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good for architecture, kinship and border politics; thin on personal space and privacy as people describe them.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Ingush, Georgian, Kumyk, Nogai; Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** women's dress, private speech and the Ingush border are politically sensitive; informants face risk. Border zones may need permits. What a border or a dress rule says in law belongs to Libra; its spirit to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
