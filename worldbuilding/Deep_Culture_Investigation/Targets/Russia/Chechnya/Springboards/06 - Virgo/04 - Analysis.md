## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Grozny's engineers and doctors**, heirs of the Soviet oil-city intelligentsia: analysis as a profession.
- **Village ʿulama and madrasa students:** text-based reasoning in Arabic.
- **Young urban believers drawn to text-literal readings** against **wird elders:** different authorities for working things out.
- **Mountain herders and farmers:** practical inference from weather, land and animals.
- **Women in medicine, teaching and science:** who is allowed to be "the clever one".
- **Terek Cossack descendants:** an Orthodox, Russian-schooled tradition.
- **Nogai and Kumyk communities:** their own counting words and riddles.
- **Ingush residents:** cognate vocabulary to test.
- **Diaspora academics in Europe and Turkey:** analysis in exile.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Federal education and research statistics exist; manuscript catalogs are rich but scattered; studies of reasoning styles in the republic are likely absent.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Kumyk, Nogai; Turkish and German for diaspora work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exam-integrity disputes; independent analysis of the republic is politically sensitive, and so are religious polemics (Salafism carries legal risk for informants).
- **Held for review:** none.
