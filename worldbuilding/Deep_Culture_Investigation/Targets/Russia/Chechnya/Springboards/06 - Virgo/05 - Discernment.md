## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families that keep detailed teip genealogies** against **urban families that do not:** judging by lineage.
- **Mountain-origin and lowland families:** different weight on descent and on village reputation.
- **Wird elders, imams and the young who learn religion online:** whose spiritual judgment counts.
- **Elder women** who vet brides and in-laws: a judging role of their own.
- **Older and younger people online:** exposure to scams and rumors.
- **Terek Cossack descendants:** Orthodox and Russian-schooled frames for judgment.
- **Nogai and Kumyk communities:** their own trickster tales and choosing customs.
- **Ingush residents:** different exogamy and inquiry customs to test.
- **Kists of Pankisi (Georgia) and the Jordanian and European diaspora:** kin judgment at a distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on custom and genealogy in Russian; thin on survey measures; religious advice is plentiful online but varied in authority.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Kumyk, Nogai; Georgian for Pankisi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lineage status and old hierarchies; matchmaking and women's choice; dream and spiritual claims; rumor research in a tightly controlled media space.
- **Held for review:** none.
