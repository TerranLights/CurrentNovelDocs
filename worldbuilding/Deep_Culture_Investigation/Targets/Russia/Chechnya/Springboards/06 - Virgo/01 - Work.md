# Chechnya: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Kumyk, Nogai, Ingush; Arabic for religious texts. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how purposeful effort, paid or unpaid, is named, ranked and placed in a life in Chechnya's lands, and how idleness and overwork are judged.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain shepherds and summer-pasture families:** seasonal herding labor against lowland work.
- **Lowland grain and orchard farmers** of the Terek-Sunzha plain: the collective-farm heritage and its private successors.
- **Grozny's public-sector and professional workers:** salaried office work and its standing.
- **Bazaar traders and small-shop owners:** self-employment as a separate work world.
- **Men who work away** (construction crews, migrants to other Russian regions): absence as part of a working life.
- **Women working at home, in schools and in clinics:** whose work is named as work.
- **Grebensky and Terek Cossack descendants:** an Orthodox farming and vineyard tradition.
- **Nogai herders of the northern steppe** and **Kumyk villagers** in the northeast: Turkic-speaking work ways.
- **Ingush residents:** kin norms to test separately.
- **Diaspora in Europe and Kazakhstan:** work under other states' rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Soviet and federal statistics exist in Russian; attitude surveys rarely isolate Chechnya; oral and proverb sources are richer than print in Chechen.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for sermons; Nogai and Kumyk for the north; German, French or Polish for diaspora studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unemployment and dependence on state jobs are politically charged; jobs in security structures; Memorial's archives after its closure; forced labor in exile is a family trauma.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "трудовые ценности чеченцев", "Дзуцев этносоциология" and "шабашники Чечено-Ингушетия".
2. Pull Rosstat's regional labor-force tables for "Чеченская Республика" and the ChIASSR statistical yearbooks.
3. Find a Chechen proverb collection and list every proverb built on болх, къахьегар or мало.
4. Search the Muftiate's site and Telegram for sermons on "халяльный заработок" and "къахьегар".
5. Check GESIS for ISSP Work Orientations Russian files and their regional variable.
