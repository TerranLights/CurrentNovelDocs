# Chechnya: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for religious counsel; Kumyk, Nogai, Ingush. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how pointing out faults (counsel, reproach, complaint, satire, dissent, self-examination) is named and practiced in Chechnya's lands, who may criticize whom, and whether in private or in public.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and the young:** who may reproach whom, and in front of whom.
- **Mothers-in-law and daughters-in-law:** household criticism as a separate field.
- **Wird communities and text-literal critics** of Sufi practice: religious criticism in both directions.
- **Writers, journalists and state media workers:** professional criticism under constraint.
- **Urban youth online** against **village households.**
- **Terek Cossack descendants:** Russian proverbs and the Cossack circle as a model of open criticism.
- **Nogai and Kumyk communities:** their own shame and teasing vocabulary.
- **Ingush residents:** how reproof between kin is phrased in a closely related language.
- **Diaspora in Europe and Turkey:** criticism from outside the republic.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in NGO and press reporting on political criticism; thinner on everyday criticism in families and work; linguistic etiquette studies exist in Russian.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Kumyk, Nogai; German and French for diaspora media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** this is the most sensitive concept in the sign. Public criticism of authority carries documented risk; diaspora critics have been attacked; interviewees and their relatives must be protected; never name living private critics in public notes. Political criticism here sits close to law (point to Libra or Capricorn for that side).
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "речевой этикет чеченцев порицание" and "авторитет старейшин чеченцы".
2. Find a Chechen translation or commentary of al-Nawawi's Forty Hadith and the teaching on naṣīḥa.
3. Read the HRW 2016 report's method section to see what kinds of criticism it records.
4. Search *Вайнах* and *Орга* archives for book reviews to judge the range of literary criticism.
5. Search Radio Marsho for Chechen words used for criticism and complaint.
