## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dancers and musicians** of state ensembles: performance held to an exact standard.
- **Qur'an reciters and zikr circles:** devotional exactness, by wird.
- **Craftsmen and builders:** standards in stone, metal and wood.
- **Brides, mothers and households preparing feasts:** the perfect home and wedding.
- **Wrestlers and their coaches:** technique as discipline.
- **School pupils competing for medals and contests.**
- **Terek Cossack descendants:** choirs and their own standards.
- **Nogai epic singers** and **Kumyk** performers.
- **Ingush residents:** whether standards in dance and recitation differ from Chechen ones.
- **Diaspora in Europe:** whether standards shift abroad.
