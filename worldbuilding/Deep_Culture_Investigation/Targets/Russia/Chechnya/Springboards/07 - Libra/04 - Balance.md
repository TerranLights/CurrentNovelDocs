## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Teips and tukkhums:** whether a balance among lineages in public office is claimed, sought or denied.
- **Qadiri and Naqshbandi brotherhoods and Salafi-leaning believers:** each may claim the middle way for itself.
- **Highland herding villages and lowland oil, farming and town districts:** different pulls of land, work and rest.
- **Women carrying home and paid work,** and young men whose time goes to service, sport or migration work.
- **Nogai steppe pastoralists:** a seasonal round of grazing and rest of their own.
- **Russian and Cossack-descended residents** of the Terek bank: Orthodox fasting and feasting rhythms beside the Muslim calendar.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** written law and election data are rich; independent measures of checks on power are few; everyday balance is thinly studied; ecological data are scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Nogai and Kumyk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** any study of the concentration of power is politically risky; religious healing and exorcism are sensitive in families.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read 414-FZ (2021) and the Chechen constitution's articles on parliament and the head of the republic side by side.
2. Search news archives for «договор о разграничении полномочий Чечня» and date each draft.
3. Download Central Election Commission returns for the republic's head and parliament since 2003.
4. Search eLibrary.ru for «васатыя» (also «васатия») and «умеренный ислам Чечня».
5. Search museum catalogs for «чеченский войлочный ковер истанг» and record the ornament studies cited.
