## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and young adults:** who decides marriage, study and migration, and whether the young defer or decide alone.
- **Women and men:** whose decisions are expected to be referred to others.
- **Sufi disciples and Salafi-leaning believers:** guidance from a sheikh against direct reading of texts.
- **Officials and ordinary petitioners:** the time an office takes against the time the law allows.
- **Nogai, Kumyk and Russian families:** their own ways of reaching family decisions.
- **Diaspora families in Europe deciding whether to return:** an open, long-running decision, for comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin; most material is religious, folkloric or political-historical; no regional measures of indecision exist to the agent's knowledge; country-level Russian scores (uncertainty avoidance) say little about the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian first; Arabic for the devotional texts; the Nogai and Kumyk dictionaries for minority idioms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental-health data from war survivors; family decisions on marriage are private; the political deadlock of 1991-1994 is contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
