# Chechnya: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic (teaching on strife and patience); Nogai and Kumyk. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how peace or harmony is kept here by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, who bears that cost, and how it is told apart from principled nonviolence and from surrender.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kunta-Haji's Qadiri followers and other brotherhoods:** non-resistance as a remembered teaching against other paths.
- **Families of the Khaibakh and deportation victims:** a long silence and its breaking.
- **Former fighters who accepted amnesty, those who did not, and their families.**
- **Women in families where violence is hidden,** and women's crisis networks that reach them.
- **LGBT Chechens and their families:** the sharpest case of a family peace bought at one member's cost.
- **Nogai, Kumyk and remaining Russian residents:** accommodation as minorities, a question to test, not a finding.
- **Exiles and diaspora critics** in Europe, who speak about what residents may not.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 19th-century and wartime episodes; very thin and dangerous to collect for present-day silences; official statistics cannot measure what is not reported.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Nogai and Kumyk; English, French and German for exile reporting.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence in families, persecution of LGBT people and forced apologies concern living people; informants and their kin can be punished; do not seek direct testimony without strong protections.
- **Held for review:** none.
