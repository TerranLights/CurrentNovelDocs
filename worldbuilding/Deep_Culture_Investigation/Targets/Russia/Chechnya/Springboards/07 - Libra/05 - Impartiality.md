*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Teips with members in high office and teips without:** whether impartial rules are felt differently from each side.
- **Former separatist fighters amnestied into the security forces and families of those who were not:** loyalty against neutral duty.
- **Mediators for reconciliations** chosen from lineages outside a quarrel, if they are: a native form of neutrality to test.
- **Nogai and Kumyk communities** in district offices dominated by Chechen staff, and Russian residents in Naursky and Shelkovskoy.
- **Grozny's state employees against villagers** who meet officials only through kin.
- **Diaspora Chechens in Europe** in asylum hearings: impartiality as experienced from outside, for comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** written rules are rich; real practice is thinly and riskily documented; corruption statistics measure enforcement, not prevalence.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Nogai and Kumyk; English for the scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** naming kin networks of officials can endanger sources and researchers; rights reports concern living people.
- **Held for review:** none.
