## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former Ichkeria officials and exiles against the current government:** two traditions of talks with Moscow and the world.
- **Sufi brotherhood leaders and village imams:** mediators of a religious kind.
- **Elders of the lineages** who negotiate marriages and settlements, set apart from state officials.
- **Women** as go-betweens inside families and in public appeals.
- **Nogai, Kumyk and Russian communities:** their own channels to the Chechen majority and to Moscow.
- **Kists, Akkin Chechens and the diasporas** in Jordan, Turkey and Europe, as brokers across borders, for comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 1990s talks and for imperial and Muscovite embassy records; thin for everyday tact, which is mostly oral.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Georgian; Arabic and Turkish for the diaspora; English for OSCE and memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hostage negotiations involve living victims; separatist figures are banned or prosecuted in Russia; citing them may carry risk for local researchers.
- **Held for review:** none.
