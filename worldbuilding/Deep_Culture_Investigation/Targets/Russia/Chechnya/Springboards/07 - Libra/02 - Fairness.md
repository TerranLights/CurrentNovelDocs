## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brothers and sisters as heirs:** a separate question in every unit, since fiqh shares and the Civil Code differ.
- **Highland lineages that once held common meadows** (Itum-Kale, Sharoy) against lowland farmers on the Terek and Sunzha with private plots.
- **Public-sector workers** (whose pay and posts pass through patronage) against traders and the self-employed.
- **Families resettled after the wars** against those who never left, on aid and housing shares.
- **Nogai pastoral families** of the Naursky and Shelkovskoy steppe: Turkic rules for flocks and grazing.
- **Russian and Cossack-descended residents:** inheritance under Civil Code shares only, as a contrast case.
- **Young men in sport and the security services:** where merit, rank and connections compete as grounds for a fair reward.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good for written rules (fiqh, Civil Code, exam data), thin for everyday practice; little independent survey work; official figures may be shaped.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Nogai and Kumyk for the steppe and eastern villages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption allegations against named officials; complaints against the authorities carry risk; women's inheritance can be a private family matter.
- **Held for review:** none.
