## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sufi vird communities and Salafi-leaning believers:** each side's view of the other's certainty.
- **The official "traditional Islam" establishment:** a second claim to the one true faith, to test against the definition.
- **Former fighters and families of fighters:** where strictness and militancy did and did not meet.
- **Young urban believers and village elders:** online currents against inherited practice.
- **Women:** dress, marriage and seclusion under strict and official norms, in their own accounts.
- **Old Believer heritage among Terek Cossack descendants:** a Christian restoration-of-the-original tradition.
- **Kumyk and Avar Muslims near the Daghestani border:** different histories of Salafi growth.
- **Diaspora in Turkey, Austria, France and Georgia's Pankisi:** currents formed abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in security studies and journalism; thin and risky for believers' own accounts; much writing is by one side.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Chechen, Arabic; English and French for security literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** highly sensitive. "Wahhabi" is a label that can bring arrest or punishment of whole families; never apply it to identifiable people. Many insurgent materials are banned in Russia and should be read only through scholarly compilations. Violence is the rare edge, not the whole of strict practice. Sharia under Ichkeria and anti-extremism law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
