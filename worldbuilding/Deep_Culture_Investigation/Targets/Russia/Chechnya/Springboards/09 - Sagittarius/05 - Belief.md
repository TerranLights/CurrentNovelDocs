*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain districts (Itum-Kali, Sharoy, Galanchozh area) and the plain:** whether pre-Islamic belief survives more in one.
- **Older women and men:** who keeps folk beliefs and who keeps creed.
- **Sufi murids, Salafi-leaning believers and the Soviet-secular:** what each takes as warrant (sheikh, text, science).
- **Families from different virds:** beliefs about saints' powers and shrines.
- **Kumyks, Nogais, Avars and Russians:** separate belief worlds in their own languages.
- **Returnees from Kazakhstan exile and from Europe:** beliefs gained or dropped abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-Islamic mythology and folklore; mixed for present-day folk belief; thin for surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Arabic for creed; Kumyk, Avar and Nogai for minority lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some practices (pre-Islamic rites, certain healers) may be condemned by religious authorities, so practitioners may not speak openly; treat folk healing as belief, not as medical fact. Divination traditions are Pisces's magic ways; note them here only as belief.
- **Held for review:** none.
