## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sheikhs' followers and Salafi-leaning readers:** whose reading of scripture counts.
- **Elders who keep genealogies and the university-educated young:** oral against written proof.
- **Families who lived through the wars in Grozny and those in highland villages:** different witness positions.
- **Pro-government and opposition audiences, inside and abroad:** rival news worlds.
- **Russian-speaking residents:** federal media as the main source of truth.
- **Kumyk and Nogai villages:** news in their own languages, often from Dagestan or Stavropol.
- **Diaspora in Austria, France and Norway:** truth told from outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war and deportation documentation; thin for measured trust in sources inside the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Chechen; Arabic for doctrinal texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** contesting official accounts is risky in the republic; witnesses and monitors have been killed. Memorial's archives are partly restricted after its liquidation. Do not republish identifying details of people in apology videos.
- **Held for review:** none.
