# Chechnya: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for oaths of allegiance; Kumyk and Nogai; English and French for exile politics. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on (its strength, not its content) is named, honored, mocked or punished in Chechnya's lands, and where the line is drawn between steadfastness, compromise and turning one's coat. Guilt in court is not this concept.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vird communities:** whether commitment to a sheikh is held differently by Qadiri and Naqshbandi followers.
- **Ichkerian veterans, pro-federal fighters and those who switched:** three stances toward principle and compromise.
- **Women activists and mothers of the disappeared:** conviction outside armed politics.
- **Soviet-era party and intelligentsia families:** secular conviction.
- **Terek Cossack descendants and Old Believer lines:** a separate history of holding to an older faith.
- **Kumyk and Nogai communities:** their own histories of accommodation and refusal.
- **Diaspora in Europe:** exile politics, both loyal and opposition.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history and political documents; thin in surveys; much is advocacy and must be read as such.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Arabic for 19th-century oaths; English and French for exile politics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** extremely sensitive. Stating a political conviction can put people and families at risk; dissidents abroad have been attacked. Never record names or identifying details. Some Ichkerian materials may be banned in Russia. Violence is described, not endorsed.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries

4. Find the O'Loughlin, Toal and Bakke survey questionnaire and note items on loyalty and political action.
