# Chechnya: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for Sufi texts; Kumyk and Nogai for the minority villages; Turkish and German for the diaspora. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: what people in Chechnya's lands say makes life, work and suffering worth it, where they place life's point (cosmic and personal), and how loss of meaning is spoken of.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders who lived the deportation and their grandchildren:** two different reference points for suffering.
- **Highland villages of Sharoy, Itum-Kali and Vedeno against Grozny's new districts:** land and ancestors as a source of meaning versus city careers.
- **Religious and Soviet-secular families:** the hereafter against work and homeland.
- **Men and women:** whose lives laments praise, and for what.
- **War generations of 1994-2009:** veterans of all sides, widows, orphans.
- **Kumyk, Nogai and Russian residents:** their own sources of meaning, not folded into the Chechen frame.
- **Diaspora in Europe and Turkey:** purpose rebuilt abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, song and memoir; thin in measured data on meaning.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen for songs and laments; Russian for most scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war trauma; grief rites are private, and recording at a тезет needs family consent. Despair and suicide are hard to speak of publicly; approach through caregivers.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read *Еха буьйсанаш* (or its Russian edition "Долгие ночи") with a list of passages on purpose and suffering.
2. Search the National Electronic Library (НЭБ) for "илли" and "СМОМПК чеченские" to locate digitized song and tale texts.
3. Pull the КОУЖ tables for the Chechen Republic and note which life-satisfaction items exist.
4. Search eLibrary.ru for "смысл жизни чеченская молодежь".
5. Search for Iliyasov's articles and the sources they cite.
