# Chechnya: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian (including Cossack usage), Arabic for 18th- and 19th-century religious-political texts; Kumyk and Nogai; Turkish, German and French for émigrés. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint, and freedom as a political and spiritual ideal, are named and argued over in Chechnya's lands; autonomy and independence of the self stay in Aries.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland communities with an old self-governing past (Itum-Kali, Sharoy, Cheberloy) and lowland villages long under Russian forts:** different histories of rule.
- **Ichkerian veterans and émigrés, pro-federal Chechens, and Soviet-era elders:** three political meanings of freedom.
- **Women and men:** what freedom of movement, dress and marriage means for each.
- **Sufi brotherhoods and Salafi-leaning believers:** inner and spiritual freedom read differently.
- **Terek Cossack descendants:** the Cossack воля.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** steppe and plain peoples with their own past of khans, steppe liberty and Russian rule.
- **Diaspora in Turkey, Jordan and the European Union:** freedom as the reason to leave, or to stay away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history, myth and rights reporting; thin for surveys, which barely sample the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Arabic for imamate documents; French and German for some scholarship and émigré writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** freedom and independence are among the most dangerous topics to discuss inside the republic; never name living informants. Ichkerian materials can be restricted or labeled extremist in Russia, so access abroad may be needed. Law on speech and assembly goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
