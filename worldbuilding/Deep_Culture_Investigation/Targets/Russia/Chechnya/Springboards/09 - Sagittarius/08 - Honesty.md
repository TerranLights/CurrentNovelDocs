## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Traders and bazaar families against public-sector employees:** honest dealing as livelihood or as duty.
- **Rural highland villages and Grozny:** whether trust rests on knowing everyone or on rules.
- **Elders and the young:** honesty taught by custom or by school and media.
- **Religious teachers and secular professionals:** whose standard of truthfulness counts.
- **Kumyk, Nogai and Russian residents:** their own codes and proverbs.
- **Returnees from Europe:** contact with other expectations of candor toward the state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in proverbs and ethics writing; thin and politically fraught for corruption data.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Kumyk and Nogai for minority proverbs; Arabic for ethical terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** speaking about corruption or forced levies is risky inside the republic; never attribute statements to identifiable people. Treat outsider stereotypes of deceit as material to examine, not as findings. Legal sides of fraud and bribery go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
