## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Boys in wrestling, boxing and MMA schools** and those outside them: sport as the main public discipline path for young men.
- **Girls and women:** discipline imposed by dress and conduct rules; separate their own accounts from enforcers'.
- **Qadiri and Naqshbandi murids:** different bodily disciplines of worship.
- **Soviet-educated Grozny families:** school and Pioneer discipline remembered against the present.
- **Former fighters and security-force members:** military discipline of different armies.
- **Highland and lowland households:** herding and village routines against urban school timetables.
- **Nogai and Kumyk villages; remaining Russians:** their own child-rearing customs.
- **Diaspora** in Europe, Turkey, Jordan and Pankisi.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; sport, dance and imposed rules are documented; ordinary home discipline is thin and mostly oral.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Georgian, French and German for borderland and diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** coercive rules on women; physical punishment of children; abuse in detention; children in staged fights. Protect informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
