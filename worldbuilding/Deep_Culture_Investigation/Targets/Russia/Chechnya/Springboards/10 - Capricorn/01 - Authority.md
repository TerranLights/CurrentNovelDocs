# Chechnya: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic (religious authority); Ingush, Kumyk, Nogai and Georgian for kin and minority communities. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: whose right to direct, decide or speak for others is accepted in Chechnya's lands (parents, elders, sheikhs, imams, officials, commanders, the republic's head), how far that reach extends into daily life, and when refusal is held to be right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Qadiri virds and Naqshbandi lines:** murid obedience to different sheikhs, and their different stances toward the state.
- **Salafi-leaning believers** (repressed): a rival claim that only scripture commands, against sheikhs and elders.
- **Highland villages** (Itum-Kale, Sharoy): elders' authority under thinner state presence.
- **Grozny's Soviet-educated families:** office and expertise as the source of authority.
- **Security-service families** and former fighters who changed sides: authority by force and patronage.
- **Women heads of household** after the wars: authority taken by necessity, to be documented carefully.
- **Nogai and Kumyk villages:** a former nobility (biy, uzden) with its own memory of command.
- **Kists of Pankisi; Chechens in Jordan and Turkey:** authority under a foreign state that does not reach the community's inner life.
- **European refugee families:** parents' authority under host-country child-protection rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Imamate and the wars; for present-day authority, mostly human-rights documentation and official display, with little independent survey work.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Georgian and German for borderland and diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions about obeying or refusing the present rule are dangerous for informants inside the republic; use exiles' and published testimony first. Sufi lineage leadership is a living religious matter.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library catalog for "низам Шамиля" and "наибство Чечня" for the pre-modern layer.
2. Read Zelkina, then Akaev on Kunta-Haji, to separate spiritual from political command.
3. Search Novaya Gazeta's archive for "извинения на камеру Чечня" and "ответственность родственников".
4. Search EUAA's portal for "Chechnya" country-of-origin reports to start the permission share.
5. Search Chechen proverb collections for "ден", "воккха стаг" and "ладогӀа".
