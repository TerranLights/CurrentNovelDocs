# Chechnya: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Kumyk, Nogai and Kabardian for the old princely and steppe orders; Arabic for Sufi ranks; Georgian and Turkish for kin and diaspora communities. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how people in Chechnya's lands are ranked higher and lower (by lineage, origin, age, sex, office, religious standing, wealth and closeness to power), how those ranks are shown, and how far the often-repeated image of a society without princes holds up.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineages claiming senior or "original" status, and those described as of outside origin.**
- **Families stigmatized for unfree descent:** only through their own accounts, with consent.
- **Lowland villages once under princely rule** against **highland communities** that were not.
- **Sufi lines:** sheikhs' families, deputies and murids as a parallel ladder.
- **Kin of the ruling family and security-force officers** against ordinary families.
- **Women and men; elder and younger brothers:** rank by sex and birth order.
- **Nogai, Kumyk, Avar and remaining Russian and Cossack communities.**
- **Diaspora:** Turkey and Jordan (older ranks preserved or not); Europe; Kists of Pankisi.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the nineteenth century; present ranking by lineage and descent is spoken of more than written.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Kumyk, Arabic; Turkish and Georgian for diaspora and borderland sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unfree-descent labels can harm living families; lineage rank is contested; closeness to power is dangerous to discuss. Never label a named family.
- **Held for review:** none.
