# Chechnya: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic (discord and security in religious teaching); Kumyk, Nogai and Georgian for minority and kin communities; German and French for older ethnography and refugee communities. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: what counts as things and people being in their proper place in Chechnya's lands (in the cosmos, the village, the home, the street and the state), how the war years of disorder are remembered, and how the present "stability" is felt and judged.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland tower villages and lowland towns:** old settlement order against Soviet grids and post-war rebuilding.
- **Grozny residents who stayed through the wars** against those who returned.
- **Former fighters, police and security forces:** order kept by arms.
- **Women at home and in public:** domestic order and rules on public presence, in women's own words.
- **Sufi and Salafi-leaning believers:** different senses of religious order and discord.
- **Remaining Russians and Cossack-descended families** of Naursky and Shelkovskoy; **Nogai and Kumyk villages.**
- **Displaced people, Kists of Pankisi, and diaspora in Europe, Turkey and Jordan.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disorder (war reporting, human-rights work); thin and filtered for how the present order is felt; crime statistics are of uncertain reliability.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; German, French and Georgian for older ethnography and diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** memories of filtration camps and disappearances; criticism of "stability"; outsiders' stereotypes of Chechens as disorderly must be recorded only as claims.
- **Held for review:** none.
