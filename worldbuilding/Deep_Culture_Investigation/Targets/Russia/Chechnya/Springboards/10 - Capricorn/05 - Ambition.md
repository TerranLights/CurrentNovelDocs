*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Soviet-era oil, academic and party families:** state ladders now largely gone.
- **Security-service and administration careers:** rising by loyalty and patronage.
- **Young men in sport and business:** the most visible routes today.
- **Women professionals:** doctors, teachers, scholars; their own accounts of limits and openings.
- **Highland-origin and lowland families:** different access to schools and city jobs.
- **Nogai, Kumyk and remaining Russian communities:** different ladders and networks.
- **Moscow business diaspora; Turkish, Jordanian and Syrian communities; European refugees; Kists of Pankisi.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; official success stories are abundant, independent accounts thinner; exam and economic data are disputed.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic, Turkish; Georgian for Pankisi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wealth tied to the ruling circle; criticism of patronage carries risk; women's ambitions touch gender rules. Protect informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
