# Chechnya: Authority and duty, domain and folkways (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen (нохчийн мотт), Russian; Ingush and Kist for kin communities; Kumyk and Nogai for plains minorities; Arabic for religious texts; Georgian and Turkish for borderland and diaspora sources. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: who commands and who obeys in Chechnya's lands, what is owed each way, and how career, standing, a good name, age, time, rank, order and power are reckoned there.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lowland Chechens** (Grozny, Argun, Gudermes, Urus-Martan): Soviet urban careers layered on kin.
- **Highland Chechens** (Itum-Kale, Sharoy, Cheberloy, Galanchozh): tower settlements, late Russian rule.
- **Eastern districts** (Vedeno, Nozhai-Yurt, Kurchaloy): Imamate heartland, rebel memory.
- **Akkin Chechens** near Dagestan and **Kists of Pankisi** (Georgia): kin under other administrations.
- **Orstkhoy** of the Ingush border: contested belonging.
- **Terek Cossacks and other Russians** (Naursky, Shelkovskoy), now few: their own service ranks.
- **Nogai and Bragun Kumyks**: Turkic plains peoples.
- **Sufi virds**, Qadiri and Naqshbandi: parallel spiritual hierarchies.
- **Generations**: deportation-born, Soviet-educated, war-born.
- **Diaspora** in Turkey, Jordan, Syria, Kazakhstan and Europe.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and imperial and war-era sources; thin and filtered for the present.
- **The nation's exact lands are still to be settled** (the tracker carries "Russia"); this sheet is written for the Chechen core lands, kin borderlands and diaspora.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen (Cyrillic with the palochka Ӏ), Russian, Arabic; Georgian, Turkish, German for borderland and diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of authority puts informants at risk; collective punishment of families; gendered duties and so-called honor violence; persecution of gay men (2017). Never name living informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
