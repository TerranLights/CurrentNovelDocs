# Chechnya: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic (fate and repentance); Ingush and Georgian for kin communities; Turkish for the old diaspora. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: who in Chechnya's lands is held answerable for a deed (the doer, the household, the lineage, the whole people), how fault is set beside God's decree, and how blame is admitted, paid off or forgiven.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Feuding and reconciled lineages:** liability carried by kin; compare those who reconciled publicly with those who refused.
- **Families of insurgents and of exiled critics:** the present doctrine of relatives' liability, from their side.
- **Sufi and Salafi-leaning believers:** different readings of decree and personal choice.
- **Doctors, teachers and officials:** answerability in office and in a profession.
- **Widows and eldest sons after the wars:** household responsibility taken early.
- **Highland and lowland villages:** custom against state courts in assigning blame.
- **Nogai, Kumyk and remaining Russian communities:** other customs of compensation and confession.
- **Kists of Pankisi; diaspora in Turkey, Jordan and Europe:** answerability under states that reject kin liability.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in nineteenth-century adat collections and recent conflict studies; ordinary everyday responsibility (chores, children, work) is thinly documented.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Georgian and Turkish for borderland and diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** living blood feuds; families punished for relatives; deportation trauma. Do not name parties to feuds.
- **Held for review:** none.
