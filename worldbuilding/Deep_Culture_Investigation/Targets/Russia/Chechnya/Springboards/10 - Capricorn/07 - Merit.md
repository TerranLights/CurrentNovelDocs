# Chechnya: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former fighters, security-force members and amnestied men:** rewards for service and loyalty.
- **Doctors, teachers and engineers:** professional merit against appointment.
- **Religious scholars and reciters:** merit by learning, trained at home or abroad.
- **Soviet-educated families and the war-time generation** who lost schooling.
- **Women:** which merits are rewarded in them, from their own accounts.
- **Highland and lowland families:** different networks for posts.
- **Nogai, Kumyk and remaining Russian communities:** access to posts as minorities.
- **Diaspora:** Jordan and Turkey (wasta, torpil); Europe (credentials); Kists in Georgia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin on independent evidence of how posts are filled; official claims of competition and anecdotal claims of payment both need checking.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian, Arabic; Turkish, German and Georgian for diaspora and borderland sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption allegations; loyalty rewards in the security forces; informants risk losing jobs. Never name individuals as having bought posts.
- **Held for review:** none.
