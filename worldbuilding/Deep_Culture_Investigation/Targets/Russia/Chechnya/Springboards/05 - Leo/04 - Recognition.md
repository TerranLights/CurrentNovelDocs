# Chechnya: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for religious titles and pre-modern letters. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, deeds or standing (from a word of credit to a title, an honor, or an admission of a wrong) is given and withheld in Chechnya's lands.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Hajjis, ustazes and sheikhs' families against secular professionals: two ladders of standing.
- Veterans and families of the two sides in 1994–2009: one side honored, the other unacknowledged; handle with care.
- Families of the disappeared, whose request is acknowledgment itself.
- Akkin Chechens and the unrestored Aukh district *check*: recognition as a land claim across the Dagestan line.
- Russian and Cossack residents of the north, seeking acknowledgment of their own losses and departures.
- Women whose household and care work is praised in speeches but rarely titled.
- Kists in Georgia, recognized as citizens under a different state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in official honors and press; thin on how ordinary people weigh them; many archives lost.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for manuscripts; English, German and French for foreign acknowledgments.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recognition of the conflicts' victims is politically charged; public praise of officials may not be free; protect families of the disappeared.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the republic's official portal for "наградить орденом" and log a year of honorees by field.
2. Search Visaitov and other posthumous Heroes for the dates and campaigns behind their awards.
3. Search eLibrary.ru for "награды Шамиля" and "знаки отличия Шамиля" to find the insignia studies.
4. Search the Russian State Military Historical Archive guides for the Native Division's award lists.
5. Search eLibrary.ru for "чеченская антропонимия" for naming after benefactors.
