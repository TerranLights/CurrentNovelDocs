## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Boys and girls: separate games, rough play and ages at which play is expected to end.
- Highland children who help with herds against Grozny's apartment children.
- Cossack and Russian children in the north, with their own street games (lapta and others *check*).
- Nogai steppe villages, with Turkic games and horse play *check*.
- Families close to Sufi circles against Salafi-leaning households, which may judge games and music differently.
- Children raised in camps and in exile in 1999–2004, now parents.
- Diaspora children in European summer language camps.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore; thin on numbers (republic time-use data may not exist); modern play mostly online.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Kumyk and Nogai; Arabic for religious rulings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling is condemned in religious teaching and may be hidden; children's images online need care.
- **Held for review:** none.
