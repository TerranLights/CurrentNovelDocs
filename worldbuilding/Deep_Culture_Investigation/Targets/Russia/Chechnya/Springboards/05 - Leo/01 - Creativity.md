*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Highland tower-building societies (Maysta, Melkhi, the upper Argun) against the plain: a stone-craft heritage absent from the lowland villages.
- Women's felt and embroidery work against men's metal, wood and building trades: who is called a master.
- Soviet-trained painters and writers against the post-2000 generation: different patrons and rules.
- Terek Cossack woodcarving and song-making in the northern districts, set against Chechen forms.
- Nogai felt-making on the northern steppe edge *check*.
- Sufi brotherhoods that compose nazmash against circles that reject new devotional forms as bid'a.
- Artists in the European diaspora (Austria, France, Belgium) working outside home rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on towers and folklore; thin on numbers; many collections and manuscripts lost in 1994–2009.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for religious texts; Georgian for Kist and highland border material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official limits on music and art; artists' safety when speaking about rules; repression of writers in the 1930s and the 1944 deportation as gaps in the record.
- **Held for review:** none.
