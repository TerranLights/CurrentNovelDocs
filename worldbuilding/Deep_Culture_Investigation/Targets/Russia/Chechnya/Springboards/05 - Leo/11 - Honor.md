# Chechnya: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Ingush for border kin; Arabic for family-honor terms. **Concept in one line:** to be researched: how worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (the word given, courage, loyalty, propriety), lost by insult or failure, and defended, is understood in Chechnya's lands. Feud and revenge as such belong to Scorpio, and customary law to Libra and Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Men and women: the claim that men's honor rests on courage and women's on propriety is one to test.
- Teips with long feud histories against those without: whether honor weighs differently.
- Young admirers of abrek tales against elders charged with making peace.
- Sufi brotherhoods, for whom religious duty may override yakh, against secular honor talk.
- Ingush villages along the Sunzha, with ezdel as their own code.
- Terek Cossack families, with a frontier honor tradition of their own.
- Diaspora youth in Austria and France, between home codes and host laws.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and literature; mixed in present practice; the topic is often written about by outsiders in stereotyped terms.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen, Ingush, Russian; Arabic; German and French for diaspora press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence justified by honor, especially against women; feuds in living families; never name parties to a feud.
- **Held for review:** none.
