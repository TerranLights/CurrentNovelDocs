## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Highland societies with no hereditary princes against lowland areas long in contact with Kumyk and Kabardian nobility.
- Hereditary sheikh families of Qadiri lines against mosques led by appointed imams.
- Teips that choose spokesmen by age, against those where wealth or office now leads.
- Women who lead in practice (mothers of large households, heads of aid groups) without titles.
- Terek Cossack stanitsas, with elected atamans.
- Nogai villages with memories of murza lineages *check*.
- Diaspora community heads in Austria and France, chosen far from home rules.
