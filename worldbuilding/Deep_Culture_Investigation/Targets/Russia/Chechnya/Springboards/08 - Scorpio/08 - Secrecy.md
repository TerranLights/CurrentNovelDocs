*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Naqshbandi and Qadiri brotherhoods:** silent and loud worship; differing degrees of inner, initiates-only teaching.
- **Women and men:** what each must cover or keep unsaid; bodily modesty against public silence.
- **Brides and in-laws:** name avoidance and speech limits inside the household.
- **Families of the detained and disappeared:** silence as protection.
- **Second wives and their children:** households outside official record.
- **People who must hide sexual orientation or loss of faith:** concealment as survival.
- **Older Soviet-era believers:** memories of hidden prayer and teaching.
- **Russians, Cossacks, Kumyks and Nogais:** what minorities keep from the majority and the state.
- **Exiles in Europe and Pankisi Kists:** exposure from abroad, or a silence kept abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; much of the subject is by nature unrecorded. Historical and human rights sources are good; everyday privacy norms are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Arabic religious terms; Georgian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiates-only Sufi teaching (record only what members choose to share); hidden detainees; LGBT people; exiles; the researcher must not expose anyone. State-secret law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
