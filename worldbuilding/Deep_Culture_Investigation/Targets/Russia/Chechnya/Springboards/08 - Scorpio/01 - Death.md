# Chechnya: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families bereaved in the 1994-96 and 1999-2009 wars:** the largest group shaped by violent and missing death.
- **Survivors and children of the 1944 deportation:** dead buried in Central Asia, never brought home.
- **Highland villages around Itum-Kale and Shatoy:** older ideas of the dead near abandoned crypt sites.
- **Qadiri and Naqshbandi brotherhood members:** loud and silent zikr at deaths; saints' tombs as places of the holy dead.
- **Salafi-minded younger believers:** reject tomb visits and costly mourning; a live dispute.
- **Women and men:** who wails, who stays silent, who goes to the grave.
- **Orthodox and Old Believer Russians and Cossacks:** a Christian death calendar in the same lands.
- **Kumyks near Gudermes and Nogais in Shelkovskoy district:** Turkic Muslim neighbors to separate from Chechen practice.
- **Pankisi Kists and the Jordanian and Turkish Chechens:** older migrations with their own memory of the dead.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Religious texts and war documentation are rich; ethnographic work on grief itself is thinner; official death statistics need care.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Arabic for doctrine; Georgian for Kist sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war dead, the disappeared and extrajudicial killings; naming the dead can expose living kin; grief researchers should work through trusted local partners.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the muftiate's site and Chechen news for "тезет" with "фетва" or "ограничение" to find recent rulings on mourning.

3. Search eLibrary.ru for "горе" or "утрата" with "чеченцы" for grief studies.
4. Search MSF's report archive for "Chechnya mental health".
5. Find published илли collections and list songs whose heroes die.
