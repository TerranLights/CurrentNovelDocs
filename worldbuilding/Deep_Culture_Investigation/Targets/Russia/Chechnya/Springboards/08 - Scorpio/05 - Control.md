## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young women and their families:** dress, movement and marriage are the most watched.
- **Daughters-in-law and mothers-in-law:** control inside the household.
- **Young men:** checkpoints, security service, and family pressure to conform.
- **Village elders and imams:** the old form of social control, against the state's.
- **Highland and lowland communities:** different weight of teip and village control.
- **Sufi murids:** surrender of control to a sheikh as a religious good.
- **Russians and Cossacks:** living under the republic's moral codes as non-Muslims.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** minorities whose customs may sit differently with these codes.
- **Pankisi Kists and the European diaspora:** controlled from a distance, or free of it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. NGO and scholarly accounts of state and family control are rich; survey data on locus of control or household money are close to absent for the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Arabic terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** women's control, escape from families, surveillance; survivors in hiding; do not identify individuals. Adat and courts as law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
