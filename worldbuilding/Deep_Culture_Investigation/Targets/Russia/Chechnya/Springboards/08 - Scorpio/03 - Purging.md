## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of insurgents and of those labeled "Wahhabi":** the main target of collective expulsions and shaming.
- **Women:** dress and "virtue" campaigns and honor-based expulsions bear on them differently.
- **Men and women targeted for sexual orientation:** purged from families and the republic.
- **Sufi brotherhoods and Salafi believers:** each may treat the other's practice as impure.
- **Village elders and teip councils:** where expulsion from the kin group is decided.
- **Grozny's oil districts and the Terek lowlands:** places of physical pollution.
- **Russians and Cossacks who left in the 1990s:** an exodus described differently by each side.
- **Kumyks and Nogais:** their own purity customs and their place in purges.
- **Kists in Pankisi and refugees in Europe:** purged or fled, and looking back.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on wartime and post-war state purges (human rights record); mixed on purity customs; thin on everyday cleansing outside religion.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Chechen, Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sexual orientation, honor violence, insurgents' kin, deportation; real risk to sources in the republic and some abroad; never name private victims. ECHR rulings and criminal process go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
