*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for Islamic teaching on orphans; Kumyk and Nogai; French, German and English for exile and war reporting. **Concept in one line:** to research leaving someone one was bound to care for or stay with in Chechnya's lands (children, spouses, elders, villages), and the fear and experience of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **War orphans** taken in by kin against those placed in state care.
- **Mothers and fathers after divorce**: who is judged as leaving the children (a question to research).
- **Families of men who went to fight abroad**, and the children brought back.
- **Highland families** whose villages were left after 1944, against lowland families who stayed in place.
- **Russian and Cossack families** who left in the 1990s, and the elders who did not.
- **Kumyk and Nogai families**, whose customs for taking in orphans are to be set beside the Chechen ones.
- **Exiles in Europe** and the relatives left in the republic.
- **LGBT Chechens** rejected by family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. War orphans and the 1990s exodus are well reported; everyday abandonment (divorce, labor migration, elders alone) is scattered across press and NGO cases; official statistics need cross-checking against NGO and press accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Arabic for religious charity; French, German and English for reporting; Kumyk and Nogai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children of fighters abroad, LGBT people rejected by kin, and mothers cut off from children after divorce are high-risk topics; protect names. Custody and divorce law go to Libra; betrayal and grief to Scorpio; leaving home as independence to Aries.
- **Held for review:** none.
