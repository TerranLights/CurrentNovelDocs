# Chechnya: Home and family, the domain and its folkways (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen (Cyrillic with the palochka Ӏ), Russian; Ingush, Kumyk and Nogai for border and minority villages; Arabic and Turkish for the old diaspora. **Concept in one line:** to research what home is and who counts as family in Chechnya's lands: household forms, how far kinship reaches, building ways, family ways and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lowland Chechens** (Sunzha valley, Terek plain) against **highland Chechens** (Itum-Kale, Sharoi, Shatoi, Vedeno, Nozhai-Yurt): house types, plot sizes, distance from the ancestral village.
- **The old mountain societies** (Malkhi, Maisti, Terloi, Cheberloi, Sharoi): tower settlements and clan-land ties of their own.
- **Orstkhoy and Akkin (Aukh) Chechens**, border groups whose kin ties cross into Ingushetia and Dagestan.
- **Ingush**, if Ingush lands fall in the nation: a close kin system with its own terms and sources.
- **Grebensky and Terek Cossacks**, including Old Believer families, on the left bank of the Terek (Naursky, Shelkovsky districts).
- **Kumyks** (Braguny and nearby villages) and **Nogais** of the northern steppe: Turkic household and kin forms.
- **Grozny's urban families** against village households; and the **generation born in deportation** in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: ethnography and census rich, independent family surveys inside the republic thin. Check wartime archive and museum losses before relying on any holding.
- **Lands:** the pre-war lands are listed only as "Russia", so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the Chechen Republic's core lands and border groups.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian first; Ingush, Kumyk and Nogai for minorities; Arabic, Turkish, German and French for diaspora material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor-based violence, women's autonomy, persecution of LGBT people, the government's family campaigns; informants may be at risk, so anonymity matters; clan affiliation can be politically charged. Customary law (adat) and family law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
