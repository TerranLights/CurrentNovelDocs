# Chechnya: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Ingush for the old shared mythology; Kumyk and Nogai; Arabic for religious teaching; French and German for exile. **Concept in one line:** to research being a mother in Chechnya's lands and the standing and ideals attached to it: bearing and raising children, the mother-in-law and the bride, and the mother as a figure of faith and nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation

- **Mothers-in-law and daughters-in-law**, as two positions within one household.
- **Mothers who lost or are still searching for sons** in the wars.

- **Kumyk and Nogai mothers**, with Turkic kin terms and customs.
- **Ingush mothers**, since Akhriev's Tusholi material comes from Ingush villages, if Ingushetia is part of the nation.
- **Divorced mothers** and **mothers in exile** in Europe.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in symbols, holidays and statistics; thinner on mothers' own voices, which come mostly from memoir, poetry and human-rights testimony.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Ingush for mythology; Arabic for sermons; Kumyk and Nogai; French and German for exile.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official portraits of the ruling family's matriarch are political; women's reproductive choices and divorce are high-risk topics; protect interviewees.
