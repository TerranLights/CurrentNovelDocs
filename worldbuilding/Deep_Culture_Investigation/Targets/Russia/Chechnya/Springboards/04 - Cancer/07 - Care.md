# Chechnya: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Arabic for religious healing and charity; Kumyk and Nogai; German and French for care in exile. **Concept in one line:** to research how people in Chechnya's lands attend to those who cannot manage alone (the sick, the old, the disabled, the bereaved): the concern, the work, who does it, and who pays.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large village households** against **Grozny's smaller urban households**: who is at home to care.
- **Women and men** as carers: daughters, daughters-in-law, sons and grandsons (a question to research, not to assume).
- **Families of the war-disabled** and families caring for the mentally ill.
- **Russian and Cossack pensioners** with few kin left in the republic.
- **Kumyk and Nogai households**, whose healers and charities may run through Dagestani networks (a question).
- **Highland families** far from hospitals, against towns with clinics.
- **Diaspora families** in Europe and the Middle East.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Health-service and aid data exist; unpaid family care is almost unmeasured; religious and folk healing are documented mostly in the press and ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Arabic for healing and charity texts; Kumyk and Nogai; German and French for exile.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental illness and disability can carry stigma, so interviews need care; state charity is politically charged. Care of the dead body and mourning rites lean to Scorpio; service and health systems to Virgo; duty to parents to Capricorn; compassion to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** none.
