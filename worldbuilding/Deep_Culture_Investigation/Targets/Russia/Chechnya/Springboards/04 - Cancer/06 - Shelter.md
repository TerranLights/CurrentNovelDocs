*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland families**, heirs to tower and stone-house traditions, against **lowland families** in wattle-and-daub and brick houses.
- **Grozny residents of every nationality** who sheltered in basements together during the sieges.
- **Families displaced inside the republic** against **those who sheltered in Ingushetia** and elsewhere.
- **Deportation-era families** whose shelter in Central Asia shaped later memory.
- **Terek Cossacks and the remaining Russians** of the stanitsas, whose villages also took in or lost people in the wars.
- **Kumyk and Nogai villages** on the edge of the fighting.
- **Exiles in Poland, Austria, Germany and France**, and people who fled for their safety, LGBT people among them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wartime displacement (UN, NGO and press records); thin for everyday hospitality and for homelessness inside the republic now.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Polish, German and French for exile; Ingush; Arabic; Kumyk and Nogai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sheltering fighters or the persecuted was and may still be punished; displaced people's records hold personal data. Asylum and refugee law goes to Libra; hospitality's ethics and the duty to strangers may also touch Capricorn; property and housing as possessions belong to Taurus.
- **Held for review:** none.
