# Chechnya: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chechnya, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chechen, Russian; Kumyk and Nogai; Arabic for religious upbringing; German and Norwegian among others for diaspora schooling. **Concept in one line:** to research how children in Chechnya's lands are raised toward growth (fed, warmed, taught and shaped), who does the raising, and what upbringing is meant to produce.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland herding families** against **lowland farming families**: children's work, chores and time with animals.
- **Grozny's urban and professional families** against village households, including how much Russian and Chechen each household uses with small children.
- **Families who raised children in temporary camps and centers** during and after the wars.
- **Families centered on religious schooling** against families favoring secular state schooling (a careful line, not a split to assume).
- **Terek Cossacks and Old Believers**, with Russian Orthodox and Old Believer upbringing.
- **Kumyk and Nogai families**, with Turkic cradle and lullaby traditions.
- **Ingush households**, for cradle customs and lullabies to set beside the Chechen ones, if the nation includes Ingush lands.
- **Diaspora parents** in Europe and the Middle East, raising children far from kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folklore and pedagogy dissertations are plentiful but often normative (how upbringing should be, not how it is); family-level survey data from inside the republic is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Chechen and Russian; Arabic for religious material; Kumyk and Nogai; German, Norwegian and French for diaspora schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children's wartime trauma; the state's use of youth programs for loyalty; the line between upbringing and discipline. Physical discipline and parents' authority go to Capricorn; learning and schooling to Gemini and Sagittarius; children's play to Leo.
- **Held for review:** none.
