# Ingushetia: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for comparison of shared Nakh terms; Arabic for Islamic ethics. **Concept in one line:** the value a person is held to have, and what that value is said to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders** whose standing comes with age, against **young men** in a tight job market.
- **Women**, whose standing may be read through family reputation, against **men**; and educated professional women against those in the home.
- **Sufi vird members**, **Salafi-leaning youth** and secular professionals: three different grounds of worth.
- **Families of deportation survivors** and **Prigorodny displaced** households, where dignity after loss is a living theme.
- **Chechen-speaking villages**, whose shared Nakh words may carry different weight.
- **Diaspora youth in Western Europe**, measuring themselves against two societies.
- Public-sector employees, sports champions and entrepreneurs: different roads to respect.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in epic, poetry and ethics writing; thin in measured data; mental-health figures are likely incomplete.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for shared terms; Arabic for sermons.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor-based violence against women; youth radicalization and the counterinsurgency years; suicide is a taboo subject. Present variation, never one voice for men or women. The public enforcement of honor codes as law goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library catalog for "эздел" and read the earliest and the most recent book.
2. Read Dalgat's chapters on the *illi* heroes for the pre-modern baseline.

4. Search eLibrary.ru for "самооценка молодежи Ингушетия" and "ях понятие".
5. Search Kavkazsky Uzel for "честь женщины Ингушетия" to frame the sensitive end.
