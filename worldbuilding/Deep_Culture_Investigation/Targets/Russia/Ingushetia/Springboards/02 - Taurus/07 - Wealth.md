## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Business families in Nazran and Magas** against **public-sector households** and **benefit-dependent households**.
- **Malgobek**, built around oil, against farming and herding villages.
- **Mountain herders** in Dzheyrakh, where livestock may still be the main store of value.
- **Sufi brotherhoods** and **Salafi-leaning circles**, whose preaching on display and alms may diverge.
- **Women's wealth** (gold, trousseau, own earnings) against **men's** (cars, houses, firms).
- **Chechen-speaking villages** and the remaining **Russian residents** of the Sunzha valley.
- **Diaspora** households in Europe and Turkey, and their remittances and building at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Inequality statistics exist at region level; display is easy to see online; where large fortunes come from is poorly documented and touches on politics.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for sermons and zakat rules.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption allegations around public money; wealth of officials; bride-payment disputes inside families. Display at weddings is a public argument; give all sides. Rules on bride payment and inheritance as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Download Rosstat's regional Gini and income-quintile tables for Ingushetia.
2. Search the СМОМПК volumes' tables of contents for Ingush villages and read their economic sections.
3. Search news for "калым Ингушетия ограничение" and record the dates and bodies involved.
4. Search eLibrary.ru for "шабашники Чечено-Ингушская АССР".
5. Collect twenty wedding-procession videos and their comments, noting praise and criticism.
