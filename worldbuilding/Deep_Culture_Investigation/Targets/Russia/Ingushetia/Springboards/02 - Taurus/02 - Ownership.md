# Ingushetia: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for the border villages; Arabic for Islamic property terms; Ossetian for the Prigorodny literature. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, exclude others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain lineages** whose claims rest on ancestral towers and burial grounds, against **lowland families** whose land came through Soviet allotment or purchase.
- **The Sunzha district**: lands of former Cossack settlements, now held by Ingush, Chechen and some Russian families; the earlier holders are remembered differently by each.
- **Chechen-speaking villages near the 2018 border line**, where both republics' claims meet.
- **Prigorodny claimants and displaced families**, whose houses lie outside the republic.
- **Returnees of 1957** who found their homes occupied, against families settled in new villages.
- **Mosque and community holdings**, and **private businesses** in Nazran, Magas and Malgobek.
- Women's holding of houses and land in practice, as against men's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for the 19th-century land question and the Prigorodny dispute; thin for everyday ownership today. Registry data may undercount land held by custom.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Ossetian and Chechen for the other parties to disputes; Arabic for waqf.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Prigorodny and the 2018 border are live disputes; protest leaders were jailed after 2019 *check*; Cossack and Ossetian heirs see the history differently. Customary and sharia rules on inheritance and land go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
