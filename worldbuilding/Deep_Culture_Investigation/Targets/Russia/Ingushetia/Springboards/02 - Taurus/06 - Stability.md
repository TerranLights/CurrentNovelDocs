# Ingushetia: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families who returned to their own villages in 1957** against those **resettled into other places**.
- **Old lowland villages** (Nazran's surroundings, Ekazhevo, Surkhakhi *check*) against **Magas**, a city with no old families.
- **Mountain-origin lineages** who keep ties to empty high villages, against families with no mountain roots.
- **Malgobek**, a town shaped by oil work and wartime fighting, against farming villages.
- **The Sunzha valley**, where populations shifted after the 1990s, and its remaining **Russian residents**.
- **Prigorodny displaced**, still waiting to return.
- **Diaspora branches** in Kazakhstan, Turkey and Western Europe.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Federal statistics are plentiful but census figures for the republic are contested; the ruptures (1944, 1957, 1992) are richly documented, everyday continuity much less.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Kazakh and Turkish for diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation, Prigorodny and the border shifts are painful; political turnover touches living officials. The rules of village and land continuity as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
