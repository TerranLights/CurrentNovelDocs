# Ingushetia: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Public-sector families** (teachers, doctors, officials) with steady pay, against **families on benefits** or **seasonal and migrant work**.
- **Villages hit hardest in the counterinsurgency years** (around Nazran, Karabulak, Malgobek and the Sunzha forests *check*) against quieter places.
- **Prigorodny displaced households** and **Chechen war displaced people** who stayed in the republic.
- **Mountain hamlets** in Dzheyrakh, with few services, against Magas.
- **Pensioners**, especially deportation survivors, against young families.
- **Households that avoid interest and insurance on religious grounds**, where they exist, against those that use banks freely.
- **Diaspora** households in Europe sending money home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on poverty statistics and on conflict-era insecurity; thin on how people themselves plan for the future.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic; English for aid and rights reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counterinsurgency abuses and disappearances; arms; informants and fear of speaking. Memorial was dissolved by Russian courts *check*; its archives may be online only in mirrors. Policing and its rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
