# Ingushetia: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for Islamic ethics; Georgian for the medieval Christian layer. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a community counts as worth having, doing or being, how those things are ranked, and how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Followers of the Sufi virds**, **Salafi-leaning believers** and **secular, Soviet-educated families**: three accounts of what comes first.
- **Elders and mosque communities** against **students and young professionals** in Magas and Nazran.
- **Women's** own accounts against the accounts given about them.
- **Mountain-origin lineages**, which may keep older customs at family gatherings, against families long settled in the plain.
- **Chechen-speaking villages**, where adat and Islam may be balanced differently.
- **Small Russian Orthodox and Cossack-heritage communities** in the Sunzha valley.
- **Returnees from Kazakhstan** and **diaspora in Western Europe**, shaped by other societies' norms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Surveys exist, but mostly at the North Caucasus or Russia level; Ingush-specific numbers will be in small local studies.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic; Georgian for the chronicles; French for the diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** debates between Islamic movements, which the state polices; "traditional values" is also federal political language, so separate official rhetoric from what people say. Customary rules as a legal system go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
