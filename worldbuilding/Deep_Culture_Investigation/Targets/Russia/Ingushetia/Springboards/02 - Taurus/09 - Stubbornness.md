## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders** against **younger people**: whether firmness and compromise are weighed differently by age.
- **Protest supporters** of 2018–2019 against **those who backed the agreement or stayed out**; give both.
- **Sufi brotherhoods**, whose Soviet-era survival is a test case, against newer **Salafi-leaning circles** and **secular families**.
- **Prigorodny displaced** who hold out for return against those who resettled.
- **Ingush-speaking villages** against **Russian-dominant urban families**, on keeping the language.
- **Chechen-speaking villages** and **Russian residents** of the Sunzha valley, where outsiders' labels of local character also circulate.
- **Diaspora** in Turkey and Western Europe, on how long customs are held abroad.
