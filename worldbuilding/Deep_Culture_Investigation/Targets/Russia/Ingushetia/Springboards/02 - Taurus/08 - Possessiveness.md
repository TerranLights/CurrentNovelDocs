*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women who have spoken out or left** against **families and elders** who see guarding as protection; present both accounts.
- **Divorced mothers**, **fathers' families** and **mothers' families** in custody quarrels.
- **The Orstkhoy-descended families**, claimed by two peoples.
- **Villages along the Chechen line** (Arshty, Galashki, Muzhichi *check*) and their grazing claims.
- **Mountain lineages** guarding tower and grove sites against outsiders and tourists in Dzheyrakh.
- **Younger urban couples** in Magas, where norms may differ from the villages.
- **Diaspora families in Western Europe**, where host-country rules on custody and control differ.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Little survey data; rich in rights reporting and ethnography; reporting skews to the worst cases.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Ossetian and Chechen for the heritage disputes; English for regional media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** control and violence against women; custody; bride abduction; heritage disputes that feed ethnic tension. Protect sources and avoid one voice for Ingush men or families. Rules on custody, marriage and borders as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
