## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Devout households** where music and mixed dancing may be limited, against **families who hold full *lovzar* celebrations**.
- **Mountain villages**, with their herbs, dairy and spring water, against **lowland and city tables** in Nazran and Magas.
- **Sufi zikr circles**, where sound and movement are devotional, against **Salafi-leaning circles** that may reject them.
- **Women's** spaces of comfort and adornment against **men's** gatherings.
- **Chechen-speaking villages**, whose shared dishes and music may differ in detail.
- **Russian residents** of the Sunzha valley, with their own food and drink customs.
- **Diaspora** cooks in Europe, Turkey and Kazakhstan adapting the table to new ingredients.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for food, music and dance; thin on touch, comfort and private pleasure, which are rarely written about.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for devotional terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** debates over music, dancing and alcohol are religious and political; gender norms limit what outsiders may observe; zikr may be closed to outsiders. Sexuality goes to Scorpio. Bans as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
