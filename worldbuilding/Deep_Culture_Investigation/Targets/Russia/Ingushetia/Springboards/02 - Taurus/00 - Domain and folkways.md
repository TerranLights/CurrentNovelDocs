## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain districts (Dzheyrakh, the Assa and Armkhi valleys)** against **the Nazran plain and Magas**: tower-and-terrace holdings against lowland plots and city housing.
- **Malgobek and the hill villages**: an oil-field town's wage economy against farming villages.
- **The Sunzha valley**: Russian- and Cossack-heritage settlements and their changed populations, and Chechen-speaking villages such as Arshty and Galashki *check*.
- **Families displaced from the Prigorodny district in 1992** and their settlements: what loss of a house meant for holding goods.
- **Families returned from Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan** after the 1944 deportation, against those who stayed in Central Asia.
- **Sufi vird communities** (the Kunta-Haji and Batal-Haji brotherhoods) and Salafi-leaning circles, whose teaching on display and alms may differ.
- **Diasporas** in Turkey and Jordan (19th-century emigrants) and in Western Europe (post-2000 refugees).
- Teip networks, women and men, and generations raised before and after 1991.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on old ethnography, archaeology and towers; thin on surveys made for the republic itself. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (the tracker header carries "Russia" broadly); this sheet and the sheets after it are written for the core Ingush lands. Census figures for the republic have been disputed; use them with care *check*.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Chechen; Arabic for Islamic texts; Georgian, German and French for the older sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 deportation and lost property; the 1992 Prigorodny conflict; the 2018 border dispute and the arrests after it; teip and family reputations; counterinsurgency years. Customary inheritance and property law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
