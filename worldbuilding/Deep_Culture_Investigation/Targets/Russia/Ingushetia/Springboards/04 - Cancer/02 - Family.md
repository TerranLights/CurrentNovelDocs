# Ingushetia: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for shared Vainakh kinship literature. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large clans with many branches and small clans with one village seat:** the kin network a person can call on differs in size and reach.
- **Orstkhoy (Karabulak) families:** kin ties that run into Chechen lineages; how they reckon belonging.
- **Mountain societies of origin** (Dzheyrakh, Khamkhi, Tsori, Fyappi *check*): clans grouped by old society; whether that grouping still matters.
- **Mixed Ingush-Chechen and Ingush-Ossetian families:** where kin lines cross the region's sharpest boundaries.
- **Sufi vird communities:** a religious bond that may run alongside the clan.
- **Russian and other non-Ingush families in Sunzha and Karabulak:** family forms not built on clans.
- **Families split by the 1944 exile and by later migration:** branches in Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Moscow and the European Union.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for clan structure in ethnography; mixed for everyday kin duties; thin in surveys, which rarely separate the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for comparative Vainakh works; Kazakh for the exile-era branches.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan genealogies belong to the families and some are disputed; clan bodies have taken political roles; honor-related violence and custody fights after divorce appear in rights reporting and must not stand for all families. Customary or religious rules of marriage and inheritance go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
