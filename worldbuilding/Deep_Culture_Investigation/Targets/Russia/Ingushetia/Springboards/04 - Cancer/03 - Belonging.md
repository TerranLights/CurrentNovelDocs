*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ingush of the plain towns and Ingush of the mountain-origin societies:** whether ancestry from a tower seat marks a deeper insider.
- **Orstkhoy (Karabulak) descendants:** a group placed differently in Ingush and Chechen accounts *check*.
- **Chechen residents of the republic:** neighbors and close kin in language, and on the other side of the 2018 border dispute.
- **Russian residents of Cossack heritage in Sunzha:** a community with its own stanitsa and church history on the same land.
- **Ingush families from the Prigorodny villages:** belonging to a district they no longer live in, or live in under another republic.
- **Followers of different Sufi virds and of Salafi-leaning congregations:** belonging through a religious community that may cross or divide clans.
- **Kazakhstan- and Kyrgyzstan-born Ingush, and migrants in Moscow and the European Union:** how membership survives distance and a second language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in press and protest records on land and statehood; thin on everyday felt belonging, which needs Ingush-language media and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen; Georgian (medieval sources); Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Ingush-Chechen and Ingush-Ossetian territorial questions are live and have led to prosecutions; religious belonging is policed; naming who is "not a real member" can endanger people. Membership rules in law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
