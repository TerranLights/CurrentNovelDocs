# Ingushetia: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen where villages or displaced families are Chechen. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families with a named mountain seat and families settled on the plain for generations:** the ancestral hamlet and the lived-in house may be two different "homes".
- **Nazran's walled private compounds and Magas's apartment blocks:** two housing forms built in the same decades.
- **Households displaced from the Prigorodny district:** for many, home remains a place across the border.
- **Chechen families who sheltered in Ingushetia during the Chechen wars,** some of whom stayed: temporary home turned permanent.
- **Russian households of Cossack heritage in the Sunzha district stanitsas:** a different house tradition on the same land.
- **Older people born in Kazakhstan or Kyrgyzstan exile:** whether home names the place of birth or the place of return.
- **Migrants working in Moscow, other Russian cities or the European Union** who build or extend a house back home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for house forms, statistics and the exile and return; thin for home as a feeling, which needs Ingush-language memoirs, songs and talk.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen; Kazakh or Kyrgyz archive aids; Ossetian sources for the Prigorodny side, read with care.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Prigorodny homes are part of a live territorial dispute; deportation testimony is grief; photos inside homes and of women at home need consent. Property and land inheritance belong to Taurus and Scorpio; any rules on them go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
