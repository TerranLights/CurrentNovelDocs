# Ingushetia: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious upbringing texts; Chechen for shared children's folklore. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Households with grandparents at home and young couples in Magas apartments:** who actually feeds and teaches a small child.
- **Village households on the plain and families in the mountain Dzheyrakh district:** chores, animals and distance from schools.
- **Families following different religious currents:** what religious formation is expected of a child, and when.
- **Russian families in Sunzha and Chechen families in the republic:** neighboring upbringing traditions with their own languages and schools.
- **Displaced households from Prigorodny and from the Chechen wars:** nurture under disruption.
- **Sons and daughters:** whether the same words and tasks apply to both (gender ways sit in Libra; note only what touches upbringing).
- **Children raised in Moscow, other Russian cities or the European Union:** whether Ingush-language nurture survives, and who carries it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in local ethnopedagogy theses and folklore; thin in measured data on parenting practice, which federal surveys seldom break out for the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious material; Chechen.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** physical discipline, child marriage and custody after divorce are reported by rights groups and are not to be generalized; photos and videos of children need consent. Discipline as authority belongs to Capricorn; schooling to Gemini and Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
