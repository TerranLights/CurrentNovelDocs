# Ingushetia: The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush (ГӀалгӀай мотт), Russian; also Chechen for shared Vainakh material and for Chechen residents. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain, home and family: what counts as a home and who counts as family, with household forms, building ways, family ways and child-rearing ways as its folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The old mountain societies** (Dzheyrakh, Fyappi or Metskhal, Khamkhi, Tsori, Galgai *check*): each had its own tower seats, sanctuaries and clan clusters, so home and descent may differ by society.
- **Lowland Nazran and Karabulak villages:** settled from the mountains; compare the move from towers to farmsteads.
- **Orstkhoy (Karabulak) families:** a group between Ingush and Chechen identities; their kin reckoning is worth separating.
- **Malgobek:** an oil-industry town with Soviet housing; worker families beside village kin.
- **Sunzha district:** Russian residents of Cossack heritage and Chechen residents beside Ingush ones.
- **Families displaced from the Prigorodny district (1992):** home lost and resettled within living memory.
- **Families born in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan exile (1944-1957) and their children:** home remade twice.
- **Sufi brotherhood (vird) communities:** religious kin ties that may reach across clans.
- **Diaspora:** Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Moscow, Turkey, Jordan and the European Union; family kept at a distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for towers, clans and census households; thin for the home's inner life, which sits in oral memory, Ingush-language talk and closed family archives. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the republic's core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush and Russian; Chechen for shared Vainakh work; Georgian for pre-modern outside sources; Kazakh and Kyrgyz archives for the exile years.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 deportation and the 1992 Prigorodny conflict are live grief and territorial disputes; clan genealogies belong to the families; women's images and private family matters are guarded; organized clan bodies are political. Customary and religious rules on marriage and custody go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
