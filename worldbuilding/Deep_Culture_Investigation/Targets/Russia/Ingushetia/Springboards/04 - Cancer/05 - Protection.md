# Ingushetia: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for shared epic and abrek lore. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain-tower lineages and lowland clans:** whether defense memory is attached to a seat and a tower or to a village.
- **Families affected by security operations of the 2000s:** protection sought from courts, rights groups and kin.
- **Women heading households and women's groups:** who protects whom when men are absent through migration, prison or death.
- **Orstkhoy and Chechen residents:** shared epic defenders and separate heroes.
- **Russian residents of Sunzha's stanitsas:** a Cossack tradition of armed settlement on the same line.
- **Sufi vird communities and mosque congregations:** protection through prayer, saints and religious leaders.
- **Families of soldiers and police officers from the republic:** the state's protectors as kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in tower archaeology, epic and rights reporting; thin in measured household data on protection.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for the shared epic; Ossetian for the other side of the 1992 conflict.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abductions, security operations and the insurgency years are dangerous to research and to name sources from; honor-based violence and blood feud are not to be generalized. Feud, reconciliation and customary rules go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
