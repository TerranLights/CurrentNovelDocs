# Ingushetia: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for shared Vainakh material. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young mothers in their husband's parents' house and mothers in separate apartments:** where a mother's authority starts and whose it is.
- **Mothers of very large families and mothers with one or two children:** the standing each receives in public and among kin.
- **Mothers who are divorced or widowed:** whether they keep their children, and how they are spoken of.
- **Mothers in mountain villages and in the towns:** distance from maternity care and from mothers' own kin.
- **Russian mothers in Sunzha and Chechen mothers in the republic:** neighboring traditions with different religions or customs of birth.
- **Mothers of the deportation generation:** the women who bore and raised children in exile, as their children remember them.
- **Ingush mothers in Moscow and the European Union:** raising children far from the husband's kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in birth statistics and state honors; thin in mothers' own words, which sit in songs, women's media and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** women's privacy and images; divorce, custody and polygyny are sensitive and go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit) where rules are involved; questions about in-law relations must be asked carefully.
