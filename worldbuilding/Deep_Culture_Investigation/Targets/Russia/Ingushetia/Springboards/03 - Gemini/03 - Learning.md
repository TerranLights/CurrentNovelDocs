*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Households with livestock and land in the foothills and mountains:** children's learning by working beside adults.
- **Town families in Nazran, Magas and Karabulak:** more preschool and tutoring, less farm work.
- **Boys and girls:** which tasks and skills each is taught, and by whom.
- **Families with strong religious schooling against secular-schooled ones:** two learning tracks, sometimes in one household.
- **Grandparents schooled in exile and grandchildren schooled at home:** the language of learning across generations.
- **Children of Chechen and Prigorodny displacement in the 1990s and 2000s:** learning in camps and temporary schools.
- **Russian and other minority pupils in the Sunzha valley:** schooling in a mostly Ingush setting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Exam and census data are open at the republic level; studies of learning at home exist mostly as ethnopedagogy theses of uneven quality.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ingush; Arabic for religious materials; Kazakh possibly for exile school records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children's records; religious schooling is politically watched in Russia, and some study circles may be treated as suspect. The legal side of native-language schooling belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
