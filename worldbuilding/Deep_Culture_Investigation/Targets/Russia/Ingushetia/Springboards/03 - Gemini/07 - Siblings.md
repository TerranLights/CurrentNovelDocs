# Ingushetia: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious usage of "brother". **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters and the sibling bond: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eldest and youngest sons:** different expected duties and shares.
- **Sisters married into other lineages:** what ties and duties remain with their own brothers.
- **Half-siblings from second marriages:** how they are counted and treated in the household.
- **Families with many children in villages against smaller families in Magas or Moscow:** how sibling roles shift with family size.
- **Families separated in the deportation or in 1992:** siblings who grew up apart.
- **Orstkhoi and Chechen-linked families:** whether kin terms and duties differ.
- **Sufi brotherhoods and urban youth groups:** "brother" used for non-kin in each.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Kinship ethnography and fertility data are fairly rich; studies of sibling relationships as such (rivalry, care in old age) are probably thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions about brothers and family honor touch on violence against women; handle with care and protect informants. Inheritance under Islamic rules and the courts belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
