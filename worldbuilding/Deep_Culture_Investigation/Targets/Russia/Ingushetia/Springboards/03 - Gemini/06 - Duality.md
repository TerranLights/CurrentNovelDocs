# Ingushetia: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious terms; Georgian for the medieval Christian layer. **Concept in one line:** living with two-ness: two identities, languages, loyalties or views held at once; the world divided into pairs; and the treatment of people who show two faces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland-origin and lowland families:** a named pair inside the people itself.
- **Orstkhoi-descended families:** people who may count as Ingush or Chechen, or both.
- **Families in Sunzha with Chechen or Cossack neighbors:** two communities in daily contact.
- **Officials and soldiers in Russian state service:** carrying a federal and a national loyalty at once.
- **Pious Muslims and those who keep older customs:** how each treats the pre-Islamic layer.
- **Returnees raised in Kazakhstan or Kyrgyzstan:** a third culture beside the Ingush and Russian ones.
- **Children of mixed marriages:** where the single-choice census question fails.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. History of loyalty and separation is well documented in Russian; studies of personal dual identity are probably thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic; Georgian for medieval records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the deportation's "traitor" charge, the Ingush-Chechen border and the 1992 split are live political issues; talk of syncretism can offend believers. Avoid framing anyone as two-faced.
- **Held for review:** none.

5. Search for "ингушская антропонимика" and compare official and home name forms.
