# Ingushetia: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious reconciliation terms; Ossetian for cross-border peace work (terms to be gathered from Ossetian-language sources). **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders of prominent lineages against those of smaller ones:** whose word carries as go-between.
- **Imams and Sufi sheikhs' families:** religious mediation and intercession, possibly by different rules.
- **Women:** the kerchief custom and women's peace work against their absence from formal councils.
- **Young men and officials:** who is trusted to mediate with security forces.
- **Villages bordering North Ossetia:** cross-community mediation after 1992.
- **Chechen neighbors in Sunzha:** settlements between two related peoples.
- **Ingush communities in European asylum centers:** elders as go-betweens with host authorities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Feud reconciliation is well covered in Russian press and ethnography; everyday mediation (family, neighbors, school) is likely thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic; Ossetian for cross-border work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** feuds involve living families and violence; Beslan and the 1992 conflict are painful for several peoples; protest-era talks touch prosecuted people. The customary-law side of feud settlement (adat codes such as Tsarist-era compilations) belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
