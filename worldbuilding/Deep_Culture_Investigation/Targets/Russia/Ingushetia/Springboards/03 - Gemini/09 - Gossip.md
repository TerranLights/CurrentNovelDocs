# Ingushetia: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious teaching on speech about others. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor: unconfirmed reports passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women's and men's networks:** different places and occasions of talk, and different costs of being talked about.
- **Close-knit villages against Magas and Nazran:** the reach of talk where everyone knows everyone.
- **Families of high standing:** more public, possibly more exposed to talk.
- **Young people online against elders:** anonymous channels against face-to-face talk.
- **Sufi and Salafi-leaning circles:** rumors that cross religious lines, and the teaching each stresses.
- **Families abroad:** talk at a distance and what it does to those at home.
- **Russian and Chechen neighbors in Sunzha:** talk across community lines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Religious teaching and press reports of viral rumors are reachable; studies of everyday gossip as such are probably thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for classical texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gossip about women's conduct can lead to violence; never name individuals from shaming videos or rumor channels. Political rumor channels may be fronts for security services or rivals. Russia's laws on "fake news" and defamation belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
