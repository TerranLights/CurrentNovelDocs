# Ingushetia: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Kazakh for diaspora ties; Arabic for religious teaching on kin ties. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or cut off.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large, prominent lineages against small or newly settled families:** whose networks carry jobs and favors.
- **Orstkhoi-descended families:** ties that span the Ingush-Chechen line.
- **Married women:** links kept with natal kin after joining a husband's lineage.
- **Sufi brotherhood members against non-members:** a second network across lineages.
- **Ingush living in Moscow and other Russian cities:** hometown associations far from the republic.
- **Families with relatives who stayed in Kazakhstan:** cross-border ties from the exile years.
- **Elders without close kin nearby and young people on social networks:** the two ends of connection by need and by habit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Ethnography on kinship is rich; measured data on loneliness or networks at the republic level is likely thin, and federal surveys may have small regional samples.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Kazakh for diaspora bodies; Arabic for religious teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan politics and favoritism are touchy topics; naming specific families risks harm. Congress and protest gatherings since 2018 have led to prosecutions.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLIBRARY.ru for "куначество вайнахи" and "экзогамия ингушей".

3. Search Telegram and the open web for "совет тейпов Ингушетии" and note member lineages and activities.
4. Pull the Central Bank's cross-border transfer data for the Republic of Ingushetia.
5. Check which Rosstat living-conditions items are published at the republic level.
