## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young male drivers and their fathers:** who sets the rules for the first car, and how risk is excused or punished.
- **Families who forbid motorcades and gunfire at their weddings and those who allow them:** check how each choice is argued.
- **Highland and lowland communities:** mountain risks against road and town risks.
- **Construction workers abroad in Russia:** danger at work taken on for income.
- **Sufi elders and young Salafi-leaning men:** different religious arguments against rashness.
- **Chechen and Cossack-descended residents:** car, gun and drinking norms that may differ.
- **Diaspora youth in European cities:** risk in other legal and social settings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in official statistics (traffic, mortality, credit); thin in surveys of risk attitudes; qualitative material mostly in press and videos.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious terms.
