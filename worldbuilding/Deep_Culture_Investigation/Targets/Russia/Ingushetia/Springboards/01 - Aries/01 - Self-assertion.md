*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain-origin and lowland families:** claims made through lineage councils against urban and workplace settings.
- **Youngest and elder sons, daughters-in-law and daughters:** each holds a different license to speak at home.
- **Followers of the Sufi wirds (Kunta-Hajji, Batal-Hajji) and Salafi-leaning youth:** different rules on contradicting a teacher.
- **Elders on teip councils and young men online:** in-person and anonymous voices.
- **Chechen and Russian-speaking residents of Sunzha and Malgobek districts:** separate speech norms within the republic.
- **Diaspora in Austria, Germany and Moscow:** whether school and workplace rules abroad change what is said at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: strong ethnographic material on deference and avoidance; little survey data on assertiveness; online material is plentiful but scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush and Russian; Chechen for comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest participants faced prosecution; name no private person from social media; video appeals may have been made under pressure.
- **Law:** customary law, councils' rulings and reconciliation procedures go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
