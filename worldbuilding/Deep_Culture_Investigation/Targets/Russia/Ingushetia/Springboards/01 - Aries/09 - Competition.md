## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men and young women:** which arenas (combat sport, study, arts, dance) are open to each; check rather than assume.
- **Villages and districts:** rivalries between Malgobek, Nazran, Sunzha and highland communities at tournaments.
- **Teips in public life:** competition for posts and prestige.
- **Religious communities:** contests (recitation) that a congregation endorses, and those it frowns on.
- **Ingush athletes representing other countries** (for example Europeans of Ingush origin): divided loyalties in victory.
- **Chechen residents:** shared sports clubs and a sensitive wider rivalry.
- **Cossack-descended residents:** their own riding and sport traditions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport (results are public); mixed for traditional games; thin for attitudes toward competition.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for the epic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Chechen-Ingush and Ossetian-Ingush rivalries are politically charged; clan politics; exam-integrity questions; avoid naming private people.
- **Held for review:** none.
