*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women:** martial courage against endurance and moral courage in hardship.
- **Veterans of Afghanistan, the Chechen wars and police service:** decorated courage, and the silences around it.
- **Rights defenders and journalists:** courage in speaking, often against officials.
- **Athletes and coaches:** courage trained in the ring and on the mat.
- **Highland and lowland communities:** separate hero stories tied to their own valleys and villages.
- **Cossack-descended residents of Sunzha:** their own martial tradition and heroes.
- **Chechen residents:** shared Vainakh heroes claimed in two ways.
- **Diaspora in Europe:** whether courage is still told in the old frames abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore, military history and rights reporting; thin in surveys of what people call brave.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for shared songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counterinsurgency and armed groups; present-day military recruitment; the deaths of journalists and activists; avoid any pointer that celebrates violence; family grief.
- **Held for review:** none.
