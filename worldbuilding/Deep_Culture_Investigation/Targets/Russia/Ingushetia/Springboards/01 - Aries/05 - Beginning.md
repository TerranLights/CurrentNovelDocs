## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland clans tracing origins to named tower villages and lowland settlements founded later:** different first-settler stories.
- **Families with deportation-born elders:** beginnings remembered as starting from nothing in Central Asia.
- **Prigorodny-district families resettled after 1992:** a second forced start.
- **Cossack-descended Orthodox residents of Sunzha:** another calendar, with a winter New Year and Christmas.
- **Chechen residents:** shared Vainakh origin stories told from another side.
- **Families returning from Europe:** coming home as a fresh start.
- **Young couples in new housing in Magas and Nazran:** new households away from the parental home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: older ethnography is rich on origins and rites; statistics are thin on second chances; rehabilitation programs are politically framed.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Georgian for highland history; Arabic for Islamic calendar terms; Kazakh for archive work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the deportation archive; former fighters and their families; pre-Islamic rites can be a delicate topic for some believers.
- **Held for review:** none.
