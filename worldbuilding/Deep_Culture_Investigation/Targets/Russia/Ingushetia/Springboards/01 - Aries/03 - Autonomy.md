## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women by generation:** grandmothers married before the 1990s, mothers, and daughters in higher education; consent and choice of work differ.
- **Youngest sons and older brothers:** the son who stays and the son who leaves have different room to decide.
- **Highland-origin clans and lowland newcomer families:** how strongly a clan council still claims a say.
- **Sufi murids and Salafi-leaning believers:** obedience to a teacher against direct reading of texts.
- **Migrants in Moscow and other Russian cities:** autonomy gained, and how much is still steered from home.
- **Chechen and Cossack-descended Russian residents of the Sunzha district:** different family structures.
- **Diaspora families in Austria, Germany and France:** children raised under European school and youth rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: household statistics and press coverage exist; little direct survey data on felt freedom of choice at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ingush; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence against women and forced marriage; survivors must not be identified; family-honor topics; political autonomy questions are delicate under current federal rule.
- **Law:** marriage law, custody, Sharia-based rulings and customary law go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
