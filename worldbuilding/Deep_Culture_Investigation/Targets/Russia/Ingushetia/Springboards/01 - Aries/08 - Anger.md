# Ingushetia: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious terms. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women:** separate display rules; check whether women's anger is voiced through lament or through male kin.
- **Elders and young men:** who is expected to cool a quarrel and who to hold back; check by generation.
- **Families in feud and families mediating:** anger carried for years against anger closed by ceremony.
- **Sufi and Salafi-leaning believers:** different religious teachers on anger and its control.
- **Prigorodny-displaced families:** grievance tied to land.
- **Police and security staff and families of the disappeared:** anger on both sides of the counterinsurgency years.
- **Chechen and Russian residents:** other emotion vocabularies and norms.
- **Diaspora youth in Europe:** anger in a second language and setting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on feud and reconciliation as institutions; thin on everyday anger; emotional vocabulary needs a dictionary pass.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic; Ossetian sources for 1992.
