## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women running home-based businesses:** crafts, baking and online trade, where initiative meets family permission.
- **Families resettling the highlands:** private efforts to restore tower villages.
- **Business owners in Nazran and Magas against those in Moscow:** start-ups at home and in migration.
- **Sufi brotherhoods and mosque communities:** mutual aid organized by a congregation rather than by a founder.
- **Students and young professionals:** grant-funded youth projects against family-backed ventures.
- **Chechen and Russian residents of Sunzha and Malgobek districts:** separate business and volunteer networks.
- **Diaspora in Europe:** associations founded abroad, and money sent home for local projects.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich: registries and grant results are online; ethnography of communal work is older and patchy; little survey data on initiative as a trait.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ingush.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** humanitarian reports touch on the Chechen wars; some civic initiatives in 2018–19 led to prosecutions; keep private business owners unnamed.
- **Held for review:** none.
