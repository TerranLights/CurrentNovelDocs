## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Students at Ingush State University:** degrees pursued at home and what they hope a degree brings.
- **Madrasa and Islamic institute students:** religious learning as a full path, with its own titles.
- **Graduates of foreign Islamic universities:** standing earned abroad, judged at home.
- **Soviet-trained professionals:** an intelligentsia formed in Grozny, Vladikavkaz and Moscow.
- **Young women:** access to study away from home and the fields chosen, from women's own accounts.
- **Rural schools of Dzheyrakh, Malgobek and Sunzha districts:** distance, teacher shortages and the pull of the towns.
- **Kazakhstan-born elders:** schooling in exile and its gaps.
- **Russian-language schools in former Cossack stanitsas:** a different school history inside the same republic.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; attainment data and university records exist; qualitative standing of the educated is thin in print.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; Turkish for the study-abroad route.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** foreign religious study is viewed by security services as a risk factor; returnees may avoid speaking openly; native-language policy is politically charged. Early schooling and curiosity are Gemini's.
- **Held for review:** none.
