# Ingushetia: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for devotional texts; Chechen for shared Vainakh devotional vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what cannot be seen or proven (God, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, institutions, progress or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kunta-Hajji Qadiri circles:** loud zikr as the core act of faith; compare its places of worship and leadership.
- **The Batal-Hajji community of Surkhakhi:** a tightly bounded order, useful to test how an order's faith shapes kinship.
- **Revival-minded and Salafi-identifying believers:** trust placed in text over intercession; compare their view of ziyarats.
- **Women's circles:** devotions led by women, measured separately from men's mosque attendance.
- **Elders raised under Soviet atheism and their grandchildren:** faith kept quietly against faith displayed.
- **Mountain Dzheyrakh villages:** memory of shrine cults beside Islam.
- **Orthodox Russians and Cossack descendants in Sunzha:** a minority faith in a Muslim republic.
- **Ingush in Kazakhstan:** faith shaped by exile and by a different Muslim environment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in sermons, songs and ethnography, thin in measured religiosity at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; Kazakh and Russian for the Kazakhstan community.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** order membership and Salafi identity are politically charged after the insurgency years; private wird texts and family ziyarats need consent; women's gatherings need a woman researcher or invitation.
- **Held for review:** none.
