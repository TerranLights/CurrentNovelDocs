# Ingushetia: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ingush, Arabic; English for the comparative and security literature. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against change; whether the word fits a given group is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Nasyr-Kort congregation and other revival-leaning mosques:** the movement's own account of restoring pure practice.
- **Muftiate-aligned imams and Kunta-Hajji's followers:** the "traditional" side, which also claims fidelity to the original.
- **The Batal-Hajji community:** strict boundary-keeping inside Sufism, a test of whether strictness and literalism are the same thing.
- **Families of insurgency members and of their victims:** the violent edge and its toll, kept apart from the nonviolent majority of either current.
- **Returnees from Syria and Iraq:** the furthest end, and reintegration.
- **Soviet-raised secular Ingush:** how the word "Wahhabi" is used from outside belief.
- **Young converts formed online:** a revival reached through Russian-language media rather than a teacher.
- **Women of both currents:** dress, seclusion and study as boundaries, from their own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in news, analysis and security literature; thin and biased in the movement's own voice; surveys almost absent.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ingush, Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the highest-risk sheet. A "Wahhabi" label can lead to police registration, detention or worse; never record names of ordinary believers; insurgent propaganda sites are banned and unsafe, so reach that material only through scholarship. No stereotype of either current; the security literature has its own bias.
- **Held for review:** pointers to South Asian-origin revival movements and their literature in the republic, set aside under the standing rule.
