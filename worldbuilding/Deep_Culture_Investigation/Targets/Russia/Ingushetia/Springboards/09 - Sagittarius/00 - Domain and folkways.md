*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush (ГӀалгӀай мотт, Cyrillic with the palochka Ӏ; earlier Arabic and Latin scripts), Russian, Chechen, Arabic, Georgian. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain: religion, philosophy, higher learning and long journeys, framed by the question of what is held true and where meaning is located.

Law-shaped questions (adat, sharia, courts) go to Libra and Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain Ingush of Dzheyrakh and Assa** (Галгай, Фяппи, Цори, Хамхи societies *check*): the sanctuary landscape and the latest Islamization.
- **Lowland Nazran, Magas, Karabulak and Malgobek:** older mosque life, urban schooling, the republic's institutions.
- **Sunzha district:** former Cossack stanitsas, Orthodox Russian remnants and Chechen villages beside Ingush returnees.
- **Followers of Kunta-Hajji's wird and the Batal-Hajji community (батал-хаджинцы *check*):** distinct orders with their own leaders and customs.
- **Salafi-identifying believers:** the revival current most at odds with the orders.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district and Vladikavkaz:** life across the 1992 conflict line.
- **Diasporas:** Kazakhstan (left from deportation), Turkey and Jordan (1865 muhajir descendants *check*), Moscow and western Europe (recent).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for pre-Islamic ethnography and archaeology, Sufi history and conflict news; thin for measured belief (no census question, small survey samples). The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (the pre-war lands are listed only as Russia), so these sheets are written for the core Ingush lands.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Chechen, Arabic, Georgian; Turkish for diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the insurgency years and counterterror operations; the 1992 Ossetian-Ingush conflict; the 2018 border protests; disputes between Sufi orders and Salafis; deportation memory. Name variation, never one voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
