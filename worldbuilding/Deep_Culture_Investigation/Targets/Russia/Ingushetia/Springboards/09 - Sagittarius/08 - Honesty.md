## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Public-sector workers (teachers, doctors, clerks) and the people they serve:** two views of the same transaction.
- **Market traders of Nazran and Malgobek:** honesty with buyers who are kin, neighbors or strangers.
- **School students, parents and exam staff:** the exam-cheating question from each side.
- **Imams and religious teachers:** honesty preached against honesty practiced, with no assumption either way.
- **Lineage elders:** loyalty owed to kin set against candor owed to outsiders.
- **Returning labor migrants and small entrepreneurs:** trust in partners met away from home.
- **Russian and Chechen residents of Sunzha:** honesty across community lines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; official corruption surveys exist but are shaped by who runs them; folklore and teaching are richer.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** naming officials or families as dishonest can carry real danger and defamation risk; corruption claims must be sourced, never generalized to a people. Bribery as law is Libra's; legal culture is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
