*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Teip-council elders against younger protest organizers:** whether a stand is taken by consensus of lineages or by individuals.
- **Families of the jailed protest leaders:** conviction carried by kin who did not choose it.
- **Displaced families from the Prigorodny district:** a years-long stand on return.
- **Women campaigners:** public stands by women, judged by different rules.
- **Former fighters through the adaptation commission:** convictions renounced, held or hidden.
- **Sufi murids and converts to revivalism:** the steadfast born-in against the convert's zeal.
- **Officials and deputies:** loyalty to Moscow, to the republic and to kin, weighed against each other.
- **Diaspora activists in Europe:** stands taken safely from abroad, judged by those at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in news, NGO records and speeches; thin in surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current prosecutions, insurgency, the disappeared; quoting people can expose them; interviews need anonymity and consent. Court proceedings and verdicts belong to Libra, legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
