# Ingushetia: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Ottoman Turkish and Turkish for the emigrant record; Arabic for the religious idiom of freedom. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state, and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineages from the mountain free societies (Джейрах, Фяппи, Хамхи, Цори) against lowland Nazran families:** whether a heritage of self-rule shapes talk of freedom.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district and families displaced in 1992:** freedom as the ability to return to one's own house.
- **Participants and families of the 2018-2019 protests:** collective freedom of voice, after arrests.
- **Women of different generations:** freedom of movement, study and marriage choice, which may diverge sharply by family.
- **Salafi-identifying believers and Sufi murids:** each side's claim of freedom of conscience against the other or the state.
- **Journalists, bloggers and Instagram commentators:** the working limits of speech.
- **Descendants of the 1865 emigrants in Turkey and Jordan:** freedom of faith as the founding reason for their diaspora.
- **Recent asylum seekers in western Europe:** freedom as a reason to leave, told from outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history, monitoring and news; thin in surveys of attitudes at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Turkish (and Ottoman Turkish), Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current political prosecutions; insurgency-era detentions; speakers may self-censor or face risk, so interviews need anonymity; women's freedom must be studied through women's own accounts. Rights as written law go to Libra, legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
