# Ingushetia: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families displaced from the Prigorodny district:** their account of 1992 as the truth most at stake.
- **Historians at the republic institute and independent amateur historians:** which past each defends, and with what evidence.
- **Viewers of official broadcasting and readers of Telegram channels:** two news diets, to be compared rather than assumed.
- **Sufi-aligned and Salafi-identifying believers:** different routes to religious truth (teacher, text).
- **Mountain-descended lineages with tower claims:** physical proof of descent against written records.
- **Russian-speaking non-Ingush residents in Sunzha:** whose versions of local events they hear and trust.
- **Kazakhstan-born elders:** exile memory as a version of history official texts long left out.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in contested history and media, thin in surveys of trust.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Ossetian-side Russian sources for balance.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1992 conflict, the border dispute and casualty figures are bitterly contested; some outlets are blocked or labeled in Russia; source protection matters. How facts are settled in court is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
