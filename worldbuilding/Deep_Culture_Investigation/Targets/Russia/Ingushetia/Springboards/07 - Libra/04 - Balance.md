## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain Dzheyrakh communities and visitors:** tourism, grazing and conservation pull the land in different directions.
- **Sufi devotees and reform-minded Salafi-leaning Muslims:** what counts as the religious middle may be drawn differently.
- **The republic's officials and the federal center:** balance of powers takes a regional form here.
- **Teips in public appointments:** whether offices are expected to be balanced among clans is a question to test.
- **Seasonal labor migrants working outside the republic:** the split between home and distant work.
- **Working women:** paid work weighed against household and in-law duties.
- **Chechen and Russian residents of Sunzha district:** language and representation as a balance among groups.
- **Healers and doctors:** two ideas of restoring the body.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; built heritage and nature monitoring are well recorded; folk medicine and inner balance are thin and need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ingush for dance, healing and seasonal terms; Arabic terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "extremism" labeling has led to prosecutions, so handle religious sources with care; teip balance in government touches living officials.
- **Held for review:** none.
