*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young people choosing a spouse or career:** the weight of the family council on a personal decision.
- **Elders in teip and village gatherings:** deliberation may be expected to be long; test whether slowness is seen as respect.
- **Followers of a Sufi teacher:** decisions referred to a guide.
- **Reform-minded younger Muslims:** individual prayer for guidance without an intermediary.
- **Families of the deported who came back, and those who stayed in Kazakhstan:** a generational choice still discussed.
- **Prigorodny families:** living with a public decision deferred for decades.
- **Women:** whether their choices pass through male kin, and how they describe that.
- **Diaspora in Europe:** whether to stay abroad or return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely thin for personal indecision; better for deferred public decisions in the written law; personal practice needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ingush for tales and family talk; Arabic religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** marriage decisions under family pressure involve private persons; the border question is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
