# Ingushetia: the hidden and transcendence, with magic ways (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush (ГӀалгӀай мотт, Cyrillic with the palochka Ӏ), Russian, Chechen (Sunzha villages), Arabic (religious vocabulary). **Concept in one line:** how suffering, the unseen, confinement and the wish to escape are met; plus magic ways (the line between religion, magic and science; what is done about luck, harm and hidden forces).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain Ingushetia (Dzheyrakh, Assa, Armkhi) against the Nazran plain:** the sacred landscape is in the mountains; most people live on the plain.
- **The old mountain societies (Dzheyrakh, Fyappiy, Khamkhi, Tsori, Metskhal, Galashki; *check*):** cults and legends differed by gorge.
- **Orstkhoy-descended families:** a border society with its own place in the Nart-Orstkhoy cycle.
- **Sunzha district:** Chechen villages and the Cossack-descended Russian minority (Sleptsovskaya) add Orthodox and folk-Russian views of the unseen.
- **Qadiri vird communities (Kunta-Hajji's line, the Batal-Hajji line) against the official muftiate and Salafi-leaning youth:** different lines between piety and "magic".
- **Deportation-born elders against the generations born after the 1957 return.**
- **Families displaced from the Prigorodny district, and settlers from Chechnya after the 1990s wars.**
- **Diaspora:** Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan (where the exile happened), Moscow, and nineteenth-century muhajir descendants in Turkey and Jordan.
- **Women and men separately:** laments, healing and dream-telling may be gendered.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich older ethnography, archaeology and folklore; thin present-day surveys of belief or distress. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled (its pre-war lands are given only as "Russia"); this sheet is written for the core lands of the Republic of Ingushetia.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Chechen; Arabic (religious texts); Georgian (chronicles).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 deportation and 1992 Prigorodny conflict; counterinsurgency disappearances and the armed underground of the 2000s; internal vird matters; jinn-healing and sorcery accusations; women's privacy in mourning. Law (adat, sharia courts) is not researched here: see Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries

3. Ask ИнгНИИ and the Dzheyrakh-Assa reserve what recordings and site legends they hold.
4. Pull Rosstat's mortality and health tables for the Republic of Ingushetia.
5. Find the Memorial deportation-testimony collections for Ingush survivors' accounts.
