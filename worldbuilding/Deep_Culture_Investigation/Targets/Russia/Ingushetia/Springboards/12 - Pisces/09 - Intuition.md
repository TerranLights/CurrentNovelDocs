# Ingushetia: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian, Chechen, Arabic (guidance and dream terms); Ossetian for epic comparison. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women elders and men elders:** who is credited with knowing what is hidden in families.
- **Sufi brotherhood followers:** for whom a sheikh's insight carries weight.
- **Salafi-leaning believers:** their position on dreams, signs and divination is a contrast to check.
- **Mountain shepherds and plain farmers:** different terrain, different signs read.
- **Urban professionals in Magas:** data and credentials against the "nose".
- **The Sunzha Russian minority:** Russian omens and winter divination customs.
- **Chechen residents:** closely related sign-lore, with local variants.
- **Young people online:** the paid interpreter and the app.
- **Kazakhstan Ingush:** contact with Kazakh healers and seers in exile, to check.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Folklore and religious guidance exist; there is almost no measurement of how people trust intuition.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; Ossetian for epic comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination may be condemned locally, so practitioners may not speak openly; dream interpretation touches private family matters; gut suspicion at checkpoints belongs to the fear and crime sheets.
- **Held for review:** none.
