# Ingushetia: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian, Chechen, Arabic (sin vocabulary). **Concept in one line:** what counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how those who commit it are seen. Law itself is researched only in Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); this sheet cites that work rather than repeating it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families who keep abrek memory alive:** a living case of the outlaw as hero.
- **Families of the abducted and of police officers killed:** opposite experiences of the 2000s.
- **Prigorodny-displaced households:** where unpunished wrongs are the central grievance.
- **Mountain and plain:** older codes of feud against urban crime.
- **The Sunzha Russian minority:** its own account of wrongs suffered in the 1990s-2000s, to check against census change.
- **Chechen residents:** comparable abrek heroes (Zelimkhan) and their own memory of state violence.
- **Sufi and Salafi-leaning communities:** different lists of what counts as grave sin.
- **Young men and their families:** drug and theft cases as family shame.
- **Moscow and other diaspora:** living under the outsider's stereotype.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Plenty on state violence and official statistics; little on how ordinary people rank wrongs.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush and Russian first; Chechen for border villages; Arabic for the sin literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abductions, torture and killings by security forces; named victims and suspects; blood-feud families; the 1992 conflict; stereotypes are to be recorded as stereotypes, never adopted. All law questions go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
