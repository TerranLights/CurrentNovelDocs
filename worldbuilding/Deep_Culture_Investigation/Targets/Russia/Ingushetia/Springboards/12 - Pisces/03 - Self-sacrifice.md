# Ingushetia: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian, Chechen, Arabic (religious terms). **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Veterans' families by war:** WWII, Afghanistan, the Chechen wars, and those of recent service; each war's sacrifice is framed differently.
- **Families of police killed in the 2000s and families of the underground's dead:** opposite claims to the same word.
- **Qadiri vird communities against Salafi-leaning circles:** different meanings of martyrdom and of fasting's merit.
- **Mothers of large families against working urban women in Magas and Nazran:** who is expected to give up what.
- **The son who stays to care for parents, and his brothers who leave:** which son carries the duty, to check by family.
- **Sunzha's Cossack-descended Russians:** Orthodox Lent and Russian war-memorial culture.
- **Chechen villages within the republic:** a neighboring sacrifice memory (the 1944 deportation, the 1990s wars).
- **The mountain Dzheyrakh and Assa villages:** where the towers carry stories of defense.
- **Labor migrants in Siberia and Moscow:** remittances and absence as sacrifice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich official and commemorative material on military sacrifice; thin data on everyday sacrifice in families.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Chechen; Arabic for religious rulings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyrdom claims tied to the armed underground and to suicide attacks in the republic (2009 and later): handle as contested speech only; the 2019 protest prosecutions; current Russian military service and casualties, which are politically restricted to report.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search pamyat-naroda.ru by birthplace for Ingush districts (Nazran, Malgobek, Sunzha) to see the scope of WWII records.
