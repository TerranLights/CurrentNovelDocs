# Ingushetia: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian, Chechen, Arabic (eschatology); Persian for the medieval chronicle. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and the ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men:** the group most exposed to raids and detention in the 2000s.
- **Women:** fear of reputation and gossip may weigh differently.
- **Elders with deportation memory:** fear passed down as a family story.
- **Villages near the Ossetian line:** fear of renewed conflict.
- **Mountain Dzheyrakh and Assa residents:** mudflows, landslides and winter isolation.
- **The Sunzha Russian minority:** its own fears of the 1990s-2000s.
- **Chechen residents:** neighbors to a different security regime.
- **Children:** bogeymen and jinn stories, as they tell them.
- **Moscow diaspora:** fear of police stops and profiling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Human-rights reports on fear of violence are rich; measures of anxiety and worry at republic level are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; Persian for Yazdi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counterinsurgency victims and witnesses; Beslan and collective blame; psychiatric records; stories of jinn possession told privately.
- **Held for review:** none.
