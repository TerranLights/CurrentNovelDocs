# Ingushetia: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen; Arabic for religious titles of leading. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders of large and small teips:** a lineage's size may shape whether its elders can speak for others.
- **Sufi sheikhs' families (Kunta-Hajji and Batal-Hajji lines) and independent imams:** inherited spiritual leadership against learned or elected leadership.
- **Afghan and Chechen war veterans:** military experience as a ground for being followed.
- **Young urban activists of 2018–2019:** leaders who rose by protest, against appointed officials.
- **Women heads of NGOs and officials:** leadership in a field where formal and informal power may be gendered.
- **Mountain-society spokesmen (Dzheyrakh, Assa valley) and lowland town leaders:** local standing may rest on different grounds.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** fairly rich for political leaders and protest events; mixed for teip and village leadership; thin for workplace leadership.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic; English for Western political science.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest leaders were prosecuted; tensions between secular heads and the Muftiate; security-service involvement in politics; the Arabic word amir has militant associations, so read it in context.
- **Held for review:** none.
