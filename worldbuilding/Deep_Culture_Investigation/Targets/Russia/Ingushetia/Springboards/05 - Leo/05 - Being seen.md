# Ingushetia: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen; Arabic for religious terms on the gaze and on showing off. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young women, married women and widows:** rules about being photographed, posting and appearing in public may change through life stages.
- **Men from families of the disappeared:** visibility as a campaign for the missing, against the risk of being watched.
- **Students and workers who live in Moscow and St. Petersburg:** being seen as a "Caucasian" outsider, a different problem from visibility at home.
- **Sufi brotherhood members at public zikr and Salafi-leaning believers who avoid display:** opposite stances on being seen in worship.
- **The Chechen minority of Sunzha and the remaining Russian residents:** visibility in the republic's media and statistics as minorities.
- **Ingush in the Mozdok and Prigorodny districts of North Ossetia:** a population on the far side of a border, easily left out of the republic's own counts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Plenty on customs of display and on rights abuses; thin on everyday attention and loneliness.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic; German for early travel writing.
