# Ingushetia: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen; Arabic for the religious vocabulary of arrogance. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large, prominent teips against small or newer lineages:** pride in ancestry may be louder where a family has more names to cite.
- **Families with towers in the mountain societies against lowland families without them:** the tower as proof of origin may divide claims.
- **Orstkhoy (Karabulak) families:** a heritage claimed by both Ingush and Chechens, so group pride may be contested.
- **Families displaced from the Prigorodny district:** pride bound up with land lost and claimed.
- **The Kazakhstan-born generation:** pride in having endured exile may take a different form from pride in deeds.
- **Russian and Cossack residents of Sunzha:** pride in a stanitsa's own past, apart from Ingush narratives.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in published memory, lineage books and media; thin in measured data at republic level.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic; some Chechen for the shared epic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pride narratives touch the Prigorodny dispute and the Chechen border; lineage claims can start quarrels; keep rival claims side by side without judging.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search library catalogs for "ингушские пословицы" and for Dalgat 1972.
2. Search "история тейпа" with Ingush surnames such as Мальсаговы or Евлоевы to sample lineage books.
3. Search the FOM site for "Георейтинг Ингушетия гордость".
4. Search YouTube for "встреча чемпиона Назрань" to see a welcome ritual.
5. Search the Muftiate's channels for "кибр" or "высокомерие".
