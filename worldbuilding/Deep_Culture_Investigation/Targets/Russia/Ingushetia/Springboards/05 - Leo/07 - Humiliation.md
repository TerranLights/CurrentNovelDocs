# Ingushetia: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen; Arabic for religious terms of abasement. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families in active feud and families reconciled:** how humiliation is carried, settled or kept alive over generations.
- **Young men and young women:** insult may be read through violence for one and sexual reputation for the other, a question to test.
- **Conscripts and migrant workers outside the republic:** humiliation at the hands of outsiders, with no kin nearby to answer it.
- **Grandchildren of special settlers:** inherited memory of the komendatura.
- **Murids of Sufi brotherhoods:** voluntary abasement as a spiritual act.
- **Chechen and Russian residents of Sunzha:** whether insults across communities are handled by the same customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for feud, conflict and rights abuse; thin for everyday embarrassment and teasing.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush and Russian first; Chechen for feud and insult vocabulary shared across the border; Arabic for the religious terms of abasement.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** feuds involve living families; honor-related violence; detention and torture cases; the Prigorodny conflict remains disputed, so keep Ossetian and Ingush accounts side by side.
- **Held for review:** none.
