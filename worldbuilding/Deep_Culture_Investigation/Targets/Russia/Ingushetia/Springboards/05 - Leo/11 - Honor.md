# Ingushetia: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen; Arabic for religious terms of honor. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men of fighting age and elders past it:** the duty to answer an insult and the authority to settle one may sit with different ages.
- **Women in families of the old mountain lineages and women in professional town families:** whether family honor weighs on them the same way, to test.
- **Batal-Hajji and Kunta-Hajji followers:** brotherhoods whose teachings may differ on vengeance and restraint.
- **Veterans' families from the Second World War:** honor through service in a war that ended in exile for their kin.
- **Orstkhoy families:** a shared Chechen-Ingush heritage in which honor terms may be borrowed both ways.
- **Ingush in Europe:** honor norms under another state's courts and schools.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in ethnography and popular writing; mixed in measured research; the risk is idealized self-description, so seek critical and women's voices.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen; Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor-related violence against women; feuds between living families; customary law on insult and compensation belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
