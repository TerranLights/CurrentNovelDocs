# Ingushetia: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen; Arabic for the scriptural vocabulary of human worth. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; often held to be inherent rather than earned.

English keywords: Ingush funeral customs; Ingush elderly care; Ingushetia poverty; Ingush deportation testimony; North Caucasus xenophobia Russia; Ingush labor migration; Ingushetia ombudsman.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders kept at home against the few in institutions:** the setting of old age may shape how dignity is described.
- **Families of men working away in Siberian oil regions:** dignity tied to earning far from home, and its cost to the household.
- **Women in conservative and in urban professional families:** the meaning of bodily dignity and public bearing may differ.
- **Deportation survivors and their grandchildren:** testimony from the first generation against inherited family memory.
- **Displaced Prigorodny families in temporary settlements:** decades of insecure housing as a test of a "decent life".
- **Disabled people and their carers:** dignity as access and choice, a different axis from honor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Statistics and rights reports exist; first-person talk of dignity is mostly in testimony and online.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic; German, French or Dutch for diaspora material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** detention and custody abuse; women's cases; deportation testimony held by families; asylum seekers' safety.
- **Held for review:** none.
