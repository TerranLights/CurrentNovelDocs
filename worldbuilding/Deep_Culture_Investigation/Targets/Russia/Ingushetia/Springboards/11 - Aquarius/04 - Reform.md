# Ingushetia: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (most reform records) and Ingush; Arabic for Islamic renewal texts; Georgian for medieval church history. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole, and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sufi brotherhoods** and **Salafi reformists**: two opposed programs of religious change.
- **Elders' councils** against **younger urban activists**, on who may change custom.
- **Mountain-origin families** asked to return to the Dzheyrakh district, against **the plain's settled families**.
- **Teachers and parents in Russian-medium and Ingush-teaching schools**, on language reform.
- **Women**, as targets and authors of reforms of custom (weddings, divorce, dress).
- **Chechen and Russian residents** of Sunzha, who may view republic reforms from outside.
- **Prigorodny-displaced families**, for whom "reform" may mean a political return.
- **Émigrés and the Turkish and Jordanian diasporas**, who carried older reform debates abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich Soviet archives; post-1992 reform is documented mainly in the press; attitude surveys thin. Some Checheno-Ingush ASSR records may have been lost in the 1990s wars, *check*.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ingush, Arabic; Georgian for church history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counterinsurgency and militant rehabilitation; prosecuted activists; Sufi-Salafi tension. Reforms of law as written go to Libra; the spirit of law to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
