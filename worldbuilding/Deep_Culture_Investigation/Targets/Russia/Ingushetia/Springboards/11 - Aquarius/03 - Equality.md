*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large and prosperous teips against small ones**: standing in public life may differ.
- **Mountain-origin families and old lowland families** of Nazran: older status claims may diverge.
- **Women and men**, and **younger and elder sons**: precedence inside households.
- **Urban professionals in Magas and Nazran** against **rural households** dependent on public jobs or remittances.
- **Chechen residents of Sunzha villages**, and the **remaining Russian and Cossack** residents.
- **Families displaced from Prigorodny**, who lost land and homes.
- **Wird members and Salafi-leaning Muslims**, whose ideas of religious equality may diverge.
- **Diaspora in Kazakhstan and Jordan**, who lived as minorities and may judge equality differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in 19th-century and Soviet records of estates and land; official statistics exist but are disputed for this region; attitude surveys thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious texts; Kabardian and Ossetian sources for the comparison neighbors.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor-related violence against women; Prigorodny property; the claim of a classless past is itself contested, so read every source critically. Inheritance and divorce as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
