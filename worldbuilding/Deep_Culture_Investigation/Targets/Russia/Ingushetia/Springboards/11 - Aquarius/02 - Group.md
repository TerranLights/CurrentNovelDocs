*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large teips against small ones**: weight in councils and elections may differ.
- **The old mountain societies** (Fyappi, Galgai, Tsorin, Dzherakh, Metskhal, Khamkhi) against **Nazran-plain families**, whose grouping history differs.
- **Orstkhoi-descended families**, claimed in both Chechen and Ingush tradition: a contested boundary of "us".
- **Wird members** (Kunta-Hajji, Batal-Hajji and others) against **non-affiliated and Salafi-leaning Muslims**.
- **Chechen neighbors** in Sunzha villages, and the few **Russian and Cossack** residents left.
- **Prigorodny-displaced families** as a group defined by loss.
- **Kists of Pankisi** and the **Jordanian and Turkish diasporas**, whose grouping may have changed in exile.
- **Women**, who join a husband's household but keep their own lineage ties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in genealogy and history; mixed on how groups decide today; thin in surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush and Russian; Chechen and Ossetian archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan precedence disputes; the Orstkhoi question; 2019 prosecutions of council leaders; DNA claims of descent. Blood revenge and customary justice as law belong to Libra and Capricorn; here only the group bond.
- **Held for review:** none.
