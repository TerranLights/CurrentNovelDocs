# Ingushetia: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush and Russian; Arabic for the ethical vocabulary of Islam; Persian and Arabic geographers for medieval Alania. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders, who often carry the oral code,** against **young people restating it online**.
- **Sufi wird members**, **Salafi-leaning youth** and **secular professionals**: three sources of ideals.
- **Mountain-origin families** with tower and shrine memories against **plain families** with Soviet-era heroes.
- **Women**, whose ideal of conduct may be stated differently in the ezdel guides and in their own words.
- **Families displaced from Prigorodny** and **returnees from Central Asia**, for ideals shaped by loss.
- **Chechen and Russian residents**, whose heroes and ideals may differ.
- **Diaspora in Jordan and Turkey**, where older Muslim ideals may have been kept apart from Soviet ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore, poetry and moral guides; thin in measured values. Much of the material is advocacy, so separate the ideal stated from the practice observed.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush and Russian; Arabic; Persian and Arabic medieval sources in translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Alan heritage is disputed with Ossetian scholars; abrek and militant imagery can blur; wartime loyalty and the deportation remain charged.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the National Library of Ingushetia catalog and eLibrary.ru for "эздел" and "ингушский этикет".
2. Find the folklore volumes holding Ingush илли and list the virtues praised in five of them.
3. Read Yandiev's best-known poems and note the ideals they name.
4. Search Lebedeva's publications for "ингуши" or "Северный Кавказ ценности".
5. Pull the republic's registered projects on Dobro.rf and sort them by cause.
