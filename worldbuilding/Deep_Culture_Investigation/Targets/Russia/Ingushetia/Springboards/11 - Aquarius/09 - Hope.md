# Ingushetia: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush and Russian; Arabic for prayer and eschatology; Kazakh and Turkish for exile and diaspora sources. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders who returned in 1957** against **grandchildren born in the republic**: hope as lived history or as inherited story.
- **Families displaced from Prigorodny**, whose central hope may differ from those living in their home villages.
- **Mountain-origin families** hoping to rebuild ancestral towers, against **lowland families** focused on work and housing.
- **Young men migrating north for work** and **young women** whose prospects may hinge on schooling and marriage.
- **Sufi, Salafi-leaning and secular Ingush**, for religious against worldly hope.
- **Chechen and Russian residents** of Sunzha, who may hope for different futures for the same land.
- **Diaspora in Kazakhstan, Jordan and Turkey**, whose hopes may run toward or away from the republic.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Exile and return are richly documented; current aspirations thinly measured; national polls rarely isolate the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush and Russian; Arabic; Kazakh and Turkish for diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Prigorodny return hopes are politically charged; Kunta-Hajji lore is sacred to many followers; the rehabilitation of repressed peoples as law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Kunstkamera archive guide for "Шиллинг ингуши полевые материалы".
2. Search the РГАНИ catalog for "письма ингушей возвращение 1956".
3. Find anthologies with "песни о выселении" or "депортация 1944 песни" and list the homecoming texts.
4. Search Ingushstat for migration tables by age, then search for "ингуши Сургут" community pages.
5. Search Telegram and YouTube for Ingush-language sermons on "доӀа" and record the hopes they speak to.
