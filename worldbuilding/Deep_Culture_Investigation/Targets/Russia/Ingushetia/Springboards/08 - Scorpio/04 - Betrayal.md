# Ingushetia: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Supporters of the 2018-2019 protest movement** against **families close to the republic's leadership**: opposing views of who betrayed the land.
- **Families of WWII veterans** against **families who lost most kin in the deportation**: how wartime loyalty is used in memory.
- **Orstkhoy families**: belonging argued between Ingush and Chechen identities, open to charges of switching sides.
- **Sufi murids under an oath to a sheikh** against **unaffiliated or Salafi-leaning believers**: different objects of loyalty.
- **Families with a member accused of informing**, or of joining armed groups: betrayal charged from two directions.
- **Women and men** on infidelity and its consequences: the double standard as a question to research, not an assumption.
- **Sunzha Cossack descendants** and **Ingush villages near the old Cossack line**: older land-loss grievances on both sides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for political betrayal (land, deportation, protest); thin for infidelity and betrayal among friends.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for the border dispute; Arabic for religious vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the border dispute and the protest trials are live and dangerous; informer accusations have led to killings; honor-related violence needs survivor-centered handling. Treason as law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
