# Ingushetia: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic (amana and religious trust); Chechen for border villages. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down; loyalty is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Traders at Nazran's market** against **public-sector employees**: reliance on personal reputation versus on salaried institutions.
- **Ingush villages bordering Chechnya** (Sunzha district) against **those near North Ossetia**: different neighbors and histories of cross-group trust.
- **Families displaced from the Prigorodny district**: trust in neighbors and the state after 1992.
- **Families who returned from Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan** and kept friendships there.
- **Remaining Russian residents of the Sunzha stanitsas**: trust relations across faith and language.
- **Young people who buy and sell through Instagram and WhatsApp** against **older market-based traders**.
- **Diaspora asylum families in Western Europe**: trust in host states against home institutions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good for election distrust and guest customs; thin for measured interpersonal trust in the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious terms; Turkish or Kazakh for diaspora associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** election-integrity work is politically risky; interethnic trust questions touch the unresolved 1992 conflict. Trust in police and courts is the spirit of the law, researched under Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
