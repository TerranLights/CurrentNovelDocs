# Ingushetia: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic (forgiveness and retaliation in religious teaching); Georgian for mountain-frontier literature; Turkish for diaspora. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing or deter, together with its alternatives: forgiveness, reconciliation and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain-descended teips** against **lowland Nazran-area teips**: older and newer feud histories, and different mediators.
- **Families in reconciliations brokered by the republic** against **those settled by elders and imams alone**.
- **Families of men killed in the counterinsurgency**: revenge against the state as a separate question from feud between families.
- **Families displaced in 1992**: group reprisal and its memory, as distinct from feuds between lineages.
- **Sufi brotherhoods** against **Salafi-leaning circles**: different religious arguments around retaliation and pardon.
- **Women in feuding families**: their roles in starting, carrying and ending feuds, as they describe them.
- **Orstkhoy families and Sunzha-district Chechen neighbors**: feuds that cross the Ingush-Chechen line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good press and folklore on feud and reconciliation; thin for petty, workplace or online revenge.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; Georgian for frontier literature; Turkish for diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** live feuds endanger people named in research; insurgency and 1992 reprisal are politically sensitive. Payment for harm is Libra's compensation question; private vengeance against state punishment is the spirit of the law, researched under Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
