# Ingushetia: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; Georgian and Russian church archives for older conversion history. **Concept in one line:** a new life coming through an ending: conversion, repentance, recovery, return and rebuilding, revival of language and heritage, and beliefs about resurrection.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The generation born in Central Asian exile** against **their children born after the return**: two different "new lives".
- **Batal-Haji wird followers**, **Kunta-Haji Qadiri followers** and **Naqshbandi followers**: distinct initiation paths and stories of turning.
- **Salafi-leaning young men** who describe a break with family practice: rebirth as rupture.
- **Former fighters and their families** who went through the commission: return judged by kin and neighbors.
- **Prigorodny-displaced families** rebuilt in new settlements (Maiskoye and similar).
- **Russian residents of the Sunzha stanitsas** who converted on marriage, and those who did not.
- **Mountain lineages** rebuilding ancestral towers against **lowland lineages** with no tower sites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good on missions, Sufism and the return; thin on personal recovery, relapse and deconversion.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; Georgian and Ossetian-related archival Russian for the mission records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** leaving Islam is dangerous to discuss; former fighters and their kin face surveillance; drug use carries heavy shame. Sufi inner teachings belong to their members. Rehabilitation as law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
