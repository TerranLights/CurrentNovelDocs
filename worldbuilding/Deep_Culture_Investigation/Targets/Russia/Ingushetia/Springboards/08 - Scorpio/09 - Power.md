*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large, well-connected teips** against **small teips** and families without a strong lineage network: patronage reach.
- **Officials and security personnel (siloviki)** against **business families** and **religious authorities**: three different kinds of power.
- **Mountain-origin lineages** claiming older standing against **lowland Nazran families** central to modern politics.
- **Supporters of each republic head** (Aushev, Yevkurov, Kalimatov eras): different patron networks.
- **Women in public office and civil society** against **women in family-run businesses**: visible and less visible power.
- **Sufi sheikhs' circles** against **Salafi-leaning preachers**: spiritual authority and its political weight.
- **Sunzha stanitsa Russian residents** and **Sunzha-district Chechens**: minority access to power.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on elite politics and federal relations; thin on household and village power as lived.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for medieval and religious sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan politics and security-service power are risky topics; the Alania-Magas link and the Mekhk-Khel's antiquity are contested. Legitimate authority, hierarchy and power ways belong to Capricorn; corruption as law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
