# Ingushetia: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic (providence and reliance on God); German and French for early travelers. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so, from fate and planning to family oversight, state checkpoints and the management of land and water.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women working in Magas and Nazran** against **women in villages**: family oversight of movement and work.
- **Young men working away in Moscow or abroad** against **those living under elders at home**.
- **Families under security watch** (relatives of fighters, protest leaders) against **families who are not**.
- **Mountain herding families** near the Erzi reserve against **lowland irrigation farmers**: control of land and water.
- **Sufi murids** guided by a sheikh against **believers without a spiritual guide**.
- **Sunzha stanitsa residents**: Russian household norms of control within the same republic.
- **Diaspora families in Western Europe**: control of daughters and sons under different state rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for state security control; thin and risky for household control and money in families.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; German for Klaproth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** women and LGBT people fleeing family control can be endangered by exposure; never name individuals from crisis-group cases. Security sources may face pressure. Domestic-violence and guardianship law goes to Libra or Capricorn; self-discipline goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
