# Ingushetia: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic (ritual purity); Turkish and Ottoman Turkish for the 1860s emigration. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a land, from ritual washing and fasting to expulsions and political purges.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain-descended villages** with remembered sanctuary taboos against **plains villages** with older Islamic purity practice.
- **Followers of ruqya healers** against **those who reject them**, often split along Sufi and Salafi lines.
- **Families of those purged in 1937** and **families with a relative on a modern extremism list**: two layers of being cast out.
- **Descendants of the 1865 emigrants in Turkey**, and **families expelled from the Prigorodny district in 1992**: expulsion seen from outside the land.
- **Malgobek oil-district residents** against **mountain residents**: pollution as lived experience.
- **Sunzha stanitsa residents**: purity and fasting customs of the Orthodox calendar alongside Islamic ones.
- **Officials and public-sector families** under anti-corruption pressure against **private-sector families**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good archival trail for expulsions and Soviet purges; thin on bodily cleansing, healing and shunning as lived.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; Ottoman Turkish for the emigration.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 deportation and the 1992 conflict are framed differently by Ingush and Ossetian sources; extremism lists and security purges carry legal danger for sources; ruqya and jinn possession touch mental illness. Prosecutions as law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
