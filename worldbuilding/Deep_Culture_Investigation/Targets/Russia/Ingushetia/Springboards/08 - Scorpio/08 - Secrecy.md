# Ingushetia: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic (Sufi and ethical vocabulary of the hidden); French and English for Soviet-era studies of underground religion. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how what may, must or must not be hidden is judged; privacy is one facet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Naqshbandi silent-zikr circles** against **the loud Qadiri circles** (including the Batal-Haji community): two different relations to the hidden.
- **Brides and young wives** against **older women**: how in-law avoidance and silence are kept or relaxed by generation.
- **Families who kept religion hidden in Soviet times** against **those who first practiced openly after 1990**.
- **People living with stigmatized illness**, and their families: concealment inside kin networks.
- **LGBT Ingush at home and in the diaspora**: secrecy as survival; only with protective protocols.
- **Officials and security personnel** against **anonymous online critics**.
- **Mountain lineages with tower marks** against **lowland lineages**: what each keeps of its origins.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for private and family secrets; mixed for religious secrecy; restricted for state secrets.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; English and French for Soviet-era Sufi studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** LGBT and HIV topics can endanger people; any sacred or initiatory knowledge of Sufi orders, and the remembered lore of sanctuary lineages, is recorded only as far as members or published sources share it. Confidentiality as law goes to Libra; state secrecy as legal culture goes to Capricorn.
