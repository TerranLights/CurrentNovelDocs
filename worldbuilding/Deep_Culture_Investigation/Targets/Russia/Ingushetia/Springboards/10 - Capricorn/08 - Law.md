*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and imams who mediate** against **young lawyers and court users**: which disputes go to which forum, and who decides that.
- **Sufi-observing families**, often defending local custom, against **Salafi-leaning critics** who call adat un-Islamic, and both against **secular professionals**.
- **Women** seeking divorce or custody: how custom, Islamic law and state courts each treat them is to be researched.
- **Families of protest prisoners** and **families in the security forces**: two very different experiences of state law.
- **Prigorodny district Ingush**, whose land claims rest on a 1991 law never carried out, against **residents of the republic's own lands**.
- **Chechen residents of Sunzha district** under two neighboring republics' law cultures; **European diaspora** families using foreign courts and home mediation side by side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in rights reporting and legal-pluralism scholarship; thin on surveys of trust in courts for the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; English and French for ECHR and diaspora cases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the protest trials, disappearances and feud cases involve living people at real risk; no names beyond public court records. The permission share and statute reading go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search HUDOC for "Ingushetia" and sort judgments by subject.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "правовой плюрализм Ингушетия" and "адат шариат ингуши".
3. Find the 2018 Ingush Constitutional Court ruling and the federal Constitutional Court's response, and read press reactions in Ingush outlets.
4. Search for histories of "шариатские суды Ингушская автономная область".
5. Download the latest annual report of the republic's human-rights ombudsman.
