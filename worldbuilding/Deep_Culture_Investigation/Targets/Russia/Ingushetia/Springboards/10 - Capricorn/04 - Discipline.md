# Ingushetia: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for spiritual disciplines; German and French for diaspora child-welfare cases. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others or by oneself (self-discipline, self-control, willpower).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Boys in wrestling and fight clubs** against **girls**, whose training in bearing and conduct runs through the household; sports discipline and household discipline may differ sharply.
- **Sufi zikr participants** against **Salafi-leaning youth**: different spiritual disciplines and different views of the other's.
- **Families of mountain origin** against **Magas and Nazran professionals**: whether strict avoidance customs hold equally in town.
- **Children raised in exile in Kazakhstan** against those raised after return: memories of how they were corrected.
- **European diaspora families**, under foreign child-protection rules, against families in the republic.
- **Chechen and former Cossack families of Sunzha district**: neighboring ways of raising and correcting children on the same streets.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; ethnography and education theses are plentiful in Russian; measured data on child correction for the republic is likely thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic; German and French for diaspora cases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** physical correction of children and the policing of girls' and women's conduct are sensitive; keep families anonymous. Sufi ritual may be closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
