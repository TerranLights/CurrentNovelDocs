# Ingushetia: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Georgian and Kabardian for pre-modern outside sources. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lowland villages once claimed by Kabardian princes** against **mountain societies** that kept their own councils: two different histories of outside rank.
- **Lineages counted as old and founding** against **later arrivals or adopted families**: whether lineage seniority still carries weight is to be checked.
- **Women of the household by age and position** (mother-in-law, eldest daughter-in-law, new bride): rank inside the home.
- **Sheikh families** against ordinary believers, and against **Salafi-leaning circles** that reject sacred lineage.
- **Chechen families, the remaining Russians and the Cossack descendants** of Sunzha district: ranks between peoples on one ground.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny district and Vladikavkaz**, a minority under another people's administration.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; old documents and Soviet historiography are plentiful but partisan; current data on clan or gender rank is thin and sensitive.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Georgian, Kabardian; Kazakh for exile archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent from unfree status, clan ranking and women's position are personal and inflammatory; never name families. The egalitarian-or-ranked debate is political; record both sides.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "социальная структура ингушей" and "феодализм вайнахи" and list both camps.

3. Search the dissertation catalog for "речевой этикет ингушского языка".
4. Search Polian's book and Kazakh archive guides for "ингуши спецпоселенцы".
5. Watch official video of the June 4 Day of the Republic and note the order of speakers.
