# Ingushetia: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious reckoning; Ossetian for the Prigorodny side. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of insurgents** against **families of police and security officers**: kin answerability cuts across both and set them against each other.
- **Lineages in a feud** against **lineages acting as mediators**: who carries blame and who carries the work of ending it.
- **People displaced from the Prigorodny district** against families who were never displaced: whom each holds responsible for 1992 and for the lack of return.
- **Women managing households** against **men answering in public**: whether household and public responsibility split by sex is to be researched.
- **The deportation generation** against today's young: whether they frame responsibility for the past as the state's, the people's or God's.
- **Former Cossack families of Sunzha district** and **Chechen residents**: other communities' accounts of who answers for the 1990s departures and disputes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; old customary-law sources and conflict reporting are rich; surveys of responsibility attitudes are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush, Russian, Arabic, Ossetian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blame for 1992 and for the insurgency is contested and dangerous to discuss; feud cases involve living families, so no names. The letter of the law on criminal responsibility belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library for Leontovich's *Адаты кавказских горцев* and find the Ingush sections.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "ингуш* коллективная ответственность" and "примирение кровников Ингушетия".
3. Read the HRW 1996 Prigorodny report, then find an Ossetian-side account to set beside it.
4. Search news archives for "комиссия по примирению Ингушетия" and list the cases reported.
5. Search for "Souleimanov blood revenge insurgency" in journal databases.
