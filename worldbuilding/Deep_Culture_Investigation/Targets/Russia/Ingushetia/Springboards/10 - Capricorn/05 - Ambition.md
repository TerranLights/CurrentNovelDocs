# Ingushetia: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Kazakh and Turkish for diaspora careers. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men leaving for Moscow and other cities** against **those who stay**: ambition tied to leaving, and how it is judged at home.
- **Women in medicine, teaching and law** against **women at home**: which ambitions are open to women is to be researched, not assumed.
- **Clans with members in high office** against **clans without**: whether advancement is read as personal or as the lineage's.
- **Athletes and soldiers** against **graduates in white-collar fields**: rival ladders with different prestige.
- **Salafi-leaning and Sufi-observing young people**: how piety reframes worldly striving in each.
- **The Kazakhstan, Turkish and Jordanian descendants** of exiles and earlier migrants, and the **Ingush of the Prigorodny district**, whose prospects run through another republic.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; labor and education data exist, but regional figures are disputed; much is in business and sports press.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first, Ingush, Kazakh, Turkish, Arabic (Jordan).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wealth and corruption claims around named families are libel-prone; cite only published sources. Exam-cheating coverage can stigmatize a whole people; read with care.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Download Rosstat's regional labor-force tables for Ingushetia (latest year and 2010).
2. Search for *Северный Кавказ: модернизационный вызов* and its chapter on youth and migration.
3. Search eLibrary.ru for "ингушская молодежь жизненные стратегии".
4. Search the Ingush institute's folklore catalog for Nart tales of Botkiy Shirtka.
5. Search news for "ингушское землячество Москва" and list its activities.
