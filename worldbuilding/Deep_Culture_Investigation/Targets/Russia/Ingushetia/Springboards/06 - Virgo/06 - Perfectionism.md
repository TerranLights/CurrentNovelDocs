# Ingushetia: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious terms of excellence. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stonemasons, restorers and builders:** precision in the most visible craft.
- **Women who make festive dress and embroidery:** exactness in a revived tradition.
- **Dancers and musicians in state ensembles and at weddings:** staged perfection against social performance.
- **Pupils and parents chasing medals and top scores:** high standards set by family and school.
- **Quran students and hafiz candidates:** ritual exactness as a religious duty.
- **Brides and wedding families:** the flawless event and its costs.
- **Wrestlers and their coaches:** a sport culture built on drilled technique.
- **Urban professionals in Magas and rural households:** different standards for house and appearance.
- **Russian residents and Ingush abroad in Europe:** other standards of finish and comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Craft and ritual exactness are documented; perfectionism as a measured trait is almost unstudied in the republic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for scales and education data, Ingush for craft and song terms, Arabic for recitation rules.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental-health topics carry stigma; wedding customs and dress touch family reputation; heritage restoration has political sponsors.
- **Held for review:** none.
