# Ingushetia: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Chechen for the resident Chechen community. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort, paid or unpaid, that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nazran's traders and market workers:** commerce and self-employment as the main work form for many.
- **Magas and the public sector:** state jobs as the employer of choice, and what that does to the idea of a career.
- **Malgobek and Karabulak:** oil and factory work beside farming.
- **Sunzha district farmers and the remaining Russian residents of the stanitsas:** a different inherited farm calendar.
- **Mountain residents, rangers and guides of the Dzheyrakh district:** reserve and tourism jobs replacing herding.
- **Rotation workers and their families:** men away for weeks at a time; women running the household.
- **Ingush builders and entrepreneurs in Moscow:** work far from kin oversight.
- **Prigorodny displaced households:** work disrupted by losing land and homes.
- **Chechen families in the republic:** many shared terms, but separate job networks.
- **Women in teaching, health and home-based work; young people without work:** whose effort is counted.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: federal statistics may undercount informal and migrant work; ethnography of work is thin and mostly Soviet-era.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for nearly all data; Ingush for folklore and the press; Chechen for comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unemployment and corruption in hiring are political topics; labor migrants may be wary of questions about income; Soviet exile labor ties to deportation trauma.
- **Law:** labor codes and contracts go to Libra; the spirit of obligation to work goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
