# Ingushetia: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious self-refinement. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the idea that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families who rebuilt after 1957:** betterment as recovery.
- **Residents of Magas and of older Nazran:** a planned city against organic growth.
- **Families restoring ancestral mountain towers and villages:** improvement as return to origins.
- **Sunzha district households:** land long contested between Cossacks and Ingush.
- **Prigorodny families:** rebuilding on contested ground.
- **Students and graduates away in Russian cities:** mobility through schooling.
- **Sufi and reformist circles:** self-refinement through a shaykh or through scripture.
- **Farmers in new orchard projects:** development money and its winners.
- **Women entrepreneurs and course-takers:** new routes to getting ahead.
- **Ingush-language activists:** improvement as keeping what was lost.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Imperial and Soviet "improvement" programs are well documented from the state side; how families speak of getting ahead is mostly oral and online.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ingush; Arabic for religious texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** imperial resettlement and land loss, the Prigorodny question and development money are political; imposed improvement should be told first in the affected people's voices.
- **Held for review:** none.
