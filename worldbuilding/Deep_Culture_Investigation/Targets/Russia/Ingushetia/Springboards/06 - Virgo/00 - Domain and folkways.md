# Ingushetia: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush (ГӀалгӀай мотт, Cyrillic with the palochka Ӏ), Russian; Chechen for the neighboring and resident Chechen communities; Arabic for the religious register. **Concept in one line:** the domain of work, service, the daily round and health: what counts as good work and a well-kept life, and how those areas are grouped.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nazran and the plain villages** (Ekazhevo, Surkhakhi, Plievo): market and trade work; the baseline.
- **Malgobek district:** an oil town and grain steppe, so industrial shift work sits beside farming.
- **Sunzha district and the former Cossack stanitsas** (Sleptsovskaya, Troitskaya, Nesterovskaya): remaining Russian residents may keep a different work calendar.
- **Mountain Ingushetia and its old societies** (Dzheyrakh, Fyappi/Metskhal, Khamkhi, Tsori; Galashki): upland work remembered versus today's reserve and tourism jobs.
- **Ingush of the Prigorodny District and the displaced of 1992:** separate work and health histories.
- **Those born in Kazakh or Kyrgyz exile:** they may have brought other work habits back.
- **Labor migrants** in Moscow, Siberia and the northern oil regions: remittance work far from the household.
- **Chechens in the republic, including former refugees:** close kin in language, with their own networks.
- **Sufi wird communities** (Kunta-Haji, Batal-Haji, Naqshbandi) and reform-minded young Muslims: different religious framings of the day.
- **Women and men; elders and youth:** the task division runs through every unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich older Russian-language ethnography; thin republic-level surveys; much everyday practice lives in Ingush-language social media.
- **Languages needed:** Ingush and Russian first; Chechen for comparison; Arabic for religious texts; Georgian for medieval chronicles of the Dzurdzuk and Kist lands; Kazakh for exile-era archives.
- **Lands:** the tracker header carries "Russia" as the pre-war land, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet and the rest of the sign are written for the core republic and its people.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 deportation, the Prigorodny conflict, the counterinsurgency of the 2000s and the 2018 to 2019 border protests are live political topics; women's work touches family honor; some healing and sanctuary customs may be private.
- **Held for review:** none.
