# Ingushetia: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ingushetia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Ingush, Russian; Arabic for religious admonition. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders and the young:** who may voice a reproach, and to whom.
- **Mothers-in-law and daughters-in-law:** correction inside the household.
- **Religious leaders and the secular government:** criticism across two kinds of authority.
- **Opposition activists and protest leaders:** criticism at personal risk.
- **Ingush critics in exile in Europe:** speaking freely from outside.
- **State media staff and independent bloggers:** what each can say.
- **Sufi brotherhoods and Salafi-leaning preachers:** criticism of each other's practice.
- **Activists of the Prigorodny question:** a long-running grievance with its own rhetoric.
- **Lineage councils:** collective censure of a member.
- **Chechen residents and Russian residents:** neighbors with other habits of complaint.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Political criticism is well recorded by rights groups and the press; everyday reproach and teasing are mostly undocumented.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for rights monitoring and the press, Ingush for folklore and online satire, Arabic for sermons on admonition.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest participants face prosecution; naming critics could endanger them; criticism of religious figures and of the federal center is risky; family reproach is private.
- **Law:** speech and protest laws as written go to Libra; the spirit of obedience and legal culture goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
