# Russia (core state): Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian and Ukrainian first; Crimean Tatar, Karelian, Komi and pre-modern East Slavic for deeper layers. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men of call-up age and their families,** against those exempt; draft evaders and emigrants since 2022.
- **Village Russia, the North and Old Believer families,** where communal help and church duty may run differently.
- **Ukrainian regions:** western Ukraine with Greek Catholic parish life against the east and south.
- **Crimean Tatars:** kin and religious duties shaped by deportation and return.
- **Karelians, Veps, Komi, Sami, Nenets:** duties within herding brigades and kin.
- **Women as caregivers** across generations (the *babushka* role) against men; urban dual-earner couples.
- **Labor migrants** from the Caucasus and Central Asia, whose remittances run outward.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state duty, war memory and Soviet moral teaching; mixed for family care data; thin for minority-language terms.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; old East Slavic for oath and tyaglo records; Crimean Tatar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** military duty and evasion are legally risky topics now; desertion and refusal records are classified. War commemoration is a political battlefield between Russia and Ukraine: keep each state's practice apart.
- **Held for review:** none.
