## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families who keep the Orthodox fasts and Old Believers** against secular households.
- **Parents of different generations:** Soviet-reared grandparents, younger "gentle" parents in big cities.
- **Military families and cadet-school pupils.**
- **Elite craft and sport schools** (ballet, chess, skating) against ordinary schools.
- **Ukrainian regions:** Greek Catholic west, Orthodox center, the industrial east.
- **Crimean Tatars** and other Muslim communities; **Jewish** observant communities.
- **Karelians, Komi, Sami, Nenets:** training of herders and fishers, as each people describes it.
- **Prisoners and former prisoners:** the penal regime's discipline.
