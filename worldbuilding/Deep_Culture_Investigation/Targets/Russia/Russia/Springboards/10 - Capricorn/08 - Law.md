# Russia (core state): Law, the spirit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Old East Slavic and Church Slavonic; Crimean Tatar and Ottoman Turkish for the khanate's courts. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey, use, bend or avoid it. Capricorn takes the spirit; the letter is Libra's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former prisoners and their families,** and the underworld code they meet.
- **Small business owners** facing inspections and hostile takeovers (*reiderstvo*).
- **Ukrainian reform constituencies after 2014** against older networks; Galicia's Habsburg legal memory.
- **Crimean Tatars:** khanate, Soviet and Russian law layered on one people.
- **Old Believers,** including groups that historically refused state documents.
- **Sami and Nenets** customary rules; **Karelian and Komi** village practice.
- **Roma** communities with their own dispute bodies, as they describe them.
- **Lawyers and judges themselves.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial and Soviet legal history and court statistics; mixed for opinion data; thin for minority customary law.
