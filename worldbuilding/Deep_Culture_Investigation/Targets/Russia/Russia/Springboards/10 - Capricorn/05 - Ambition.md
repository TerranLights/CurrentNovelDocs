# Russia (core state): Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar and the Northern peoples' languages for the minority layer; 17th-c. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg against provincial towns:** the capital as the place to rise, and the resentment of newcomers.
- **Old Believer and merchant heritage families** against service and intelligentsia families.
- **Ukrainian regions:** western Ukraine with heavy labor migration; Kyiv's tech and service sector; the industrial east.
- **Women and men:** Soviet women's careers with the "double burden"; ambition judged by gender, to research.
- **Crimean Tatars** rebuilding after return; **Jewish** families and late-Soviet quotas on education.
- **Sami, Nenets, Komi, Karelian** youth moving for education.
- **Labor migrants** from the Caucasus and Central Asia in Russian cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet mobility, literature and portal surveys; mixed for current mobility statistics; thin for minority youth aspirations.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar; older Russian for 17th-c. tales.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wealth lists touch on oligarchs and sanctions; envy of the rich is easy to caricature; avoid one-voice claims about "Russian envy" or "laziness".
- **Held for review:** none.
