## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural households with large plots** against city flat-dwellers who buy everything.
- **Single mothers, pensioners and the young:** who may depend on whom without shame.
- **Old Believers and other religious communities:** self-sufficiency as faith.
- **The Russian North** (Pomors, Komi, Karelians): fishing, hunting and foraging households.
- **The carried Ukrainian lands:** homestead traditions; migrant-earner households of the west.
- **Nenets and Kola Sami:** herding collectives and state supply.
- **Crimean Tatars:** self-built homes and land after the return.
- **Disabled people and those in state care:** independence redefined.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household economy and polls; mixed for attitudes to help-seeking; thin for Arctic peoples' self-provisioning statistics.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Old Russian; Crimean Tatar, Nenets and Yiddish by group.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional and national independence is the most sensitive topic in this nation's lands, and secession talk is risky to research in person; keep to published sources. Autonomy is its own entry; community is Aquarius's, care is Cancer's, possessions Taurus's.
- **Held for review:** none.
