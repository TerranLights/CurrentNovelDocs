## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women:** who may shout, swear or show *обида*, and who is called hysterical.
- **Generations:** Soviet-era restraint at work against today's online outrage.
- **Industrial towns, villages and the capitals:** different outlets, from the street to the comment box.
- **Monastics and observant believers** against the secular majority: anger as a sin to confess.
- **The Russian North:** local protest traditions (the landfill disputes).
- **The carried Ukrainian lands:** a different swearing culture and different histories of revolt.
- **Jewish families (the *broygez* silence), Crimean Tatars, Karelians, Komi and Nenets:** check each group's own rules for showing anger; do not assume restraint or heat.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for language, history of revolt and psychology tools; mixed for everyday display; thin and sensitive for domestic violence.
