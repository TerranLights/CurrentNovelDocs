## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old noble and merchant families against Soviet-era workers:** two histories of striving for rank.
- **Schools in Moscow and St. Petersburg** against provincial schools: the pressure of admissions and olympiads.
- **Football and hockey fan cultures by city.**
- **Villages of Central Russia and the North:** fistfight and verse-duel traditions.
- **The carried Ukrainian lands:** their own sports rivalries and folk contests (wrestling, Cossack games).
- **Nenets, Komi and Kola Sami:** herders' races and Arctic games.
- **Crimean Tatars:** wrestling and horse contests at spring festivals.
- **Women and girls:** which contests are theirs (rhythmic gymnastics, figure skating, beauty pageants).
