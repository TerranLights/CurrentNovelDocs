## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former commune villages of Central Russia** against the carried Ukrainian lands, where household tenure was more common: two histories of who decides about land and labor.
- **Cities and countryside:** young adults' leaving age and housing costs differ sharply.
- **Women and men:** who decides about work, children and money.
- **Observant Orthodox families, Old Believers, Baptists and other Protestants:** obedience, conscience and congregational self-rule differ.
- **Nenets herders and Kola Sami:** collective decisions in camps and brigades.
- **Crimean Tatars:** collective self-rule claims.
- **Disabled people and residents of state care homes (*интернаты*):** autonomy at its lowest; check self-advocacy groups.
- **The elderly, and generations:** Soviet "latchkey" childhood against today's supervised childhood.
