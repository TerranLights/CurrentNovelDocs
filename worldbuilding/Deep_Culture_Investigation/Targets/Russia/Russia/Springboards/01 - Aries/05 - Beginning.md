## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox believers** (church year from September 1; Julian dates) against secular households keeping the civil calendar.
- **Russian villages of the North and Center** against the carried Ukrainian lands, where carol and masquerade cycles differ (Malanka, *щедрівки*).
- **Crimean Tatars:** spring new-year feasts and the "new start" of the return from exile.
- **Jewish communities:** Rosh Hashanah and their own arrival stories.
- **Karelians, Veps and Komi:** Finnic and Permic seasonal openings; *check* the Karelian autumn feast.
- **Nenets and Kola Sami:** the herding year and the sun's return after polar night.
- **Internal migrants, returnees and evacuees:** people who started over within the nation's lands.
