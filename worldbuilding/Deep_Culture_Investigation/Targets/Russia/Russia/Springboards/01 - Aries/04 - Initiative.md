## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers and other religious minorities (Molokans, Baptists):** historic merchant and craft initiative under restriction.
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg tech and service sectors** against company towns and depressed industrial regions.
- **The carried Ukrainian lands:** a volunteer and small-business culture shaped by its own recent history; the south's port economy; the west's labor migration.
- **The Russian North:** Pomor and Komi trading and fishing traditions; Izhma Komi herders.
- **Karelians and Veps:** communal work parties in Finnic villages.
- **Crimean Tatars:** self-built settlements and businesses after the return.
- **Jews of Odesa and Moscow; Armenian and Central Asian traders in city markets:** diaspora enterprise.
- **Women and men; young and old:** who is expected to step forward.

## 4. Flags
