## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Military families and garrison towns** against civilian city life: courage as a profession or as a rare test.
- **Men and women:** *мужество* and its gendering; women veterans and nurses.
- **Believers:** the passion-bearer and martyr model among Orthodox and Old Believers.
- **The Russian North and Pomor coast:** sea and ice endurance; the bogatyr songs' home ground.
- **The carried Ukrainian lands:** Cossack epic heritage; recent war memory on another side.
- **Crimean Tatars; Jewish communities:** courage remembered through deportation, pogroms and self-defense.
- **Nenets and Kola Sami:** hunting and herding endurance in their own terms.
- **Dissidents and rights activists** against official heroes: who is honored and by whom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for war and literature; mixed for everyday and moral courage; thin for minority traditions in print.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Old Russian, Church Slavonic; Crimean Tatar, Nenets and Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war memory is state-managed and war-charged; dissident and rights archives are closed or relabeled; current war heroism is propaganda on several sides. Fear goes to Pisces, honor to Leo, duty to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
