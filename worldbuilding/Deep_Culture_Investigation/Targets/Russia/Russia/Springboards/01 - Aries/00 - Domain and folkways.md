## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg** against provincial Central Russia: naming fashions and self-presentation may diverge.
- **The Russian North** (Arkhangelsk, Vologda, Pomor coast): old-believer and Pomor naming and dress traditions.
- **Black Earth and the southwest borderlands** (Kursk, Belgorod, Voronezh): mixed Russian-Ukrainian village cultures.
- **The carried Ukrainian lands,** west, center, east and south, kept apart.
- **Crimean Tatars:** a separate naming system, faith and memory of deportation.
- **Karelians, Veps, Izhorians and Votes; Komi; Nenets; Kola Sami:** Finno-Ugric and Arctic personhood, naming and dress.
- **Old Believers:** beard, dress and name rules held against the mainstream.
- **Jews, Roma, Russian Germans, Greeks of the Azov coast:** diaspora selves within the nation.
- **Labor migrants in the big cities:** renaming and self-presentation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich in Russian and for Soviet history; mixed for the carried Ukrainian lands (rich but politically charged); thin for small Finno-Ugric and Arctic peoples outside academic ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Ukrainian above all; Old Russian and Church Slavonic for the pre-modern layer; minority languages by unit.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the nation's exact lands, and where its line with Ukraine runs, are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands. Some pollsters, media and archives carry "foreign agent" or "undesirable" labels or are closed; war-affected lands need care. Indigenous naming and personhood knowledge may be restricted to the community.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search ruscorpora.ru for *душа*, *личность* and *я сам* in the spoken and main corpora, sorted by period.
2. Pull the latest ZAGS popular-name lists for Moscow, St. Petersburg and two provincial regions, and compare rare-name shares.
3. Search Prozhito for diaries by region and decade, filtering for self-description passages.
4. Search elibrary.ru for "тест двадцати утверждений" (Twenty Statements Test) to find Russian self-construal studies.
5. Check the Karelian and Komi institutes' publication lists for "имянаречение" (naming ritual) and "традиционный костюм".
