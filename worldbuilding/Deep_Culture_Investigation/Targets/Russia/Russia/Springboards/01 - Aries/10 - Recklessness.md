## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men** against women and older men: risk and its toll differ most by sex and age.
- **Capitals and provincial towns:** urban stunts against rural driving and drinking risks.
- **The North and big-lake regions:** ice, sea and cold as everyday dangers.
- **Industrial and mining towns:** dangerous work as background to private risk.
- **The carried Ukrainian lands:** the Chernobyl zone, and war-zone risk that is not chosen.
- **Religious communities** (Orthodox, Old Believer, Protestant) and their teaching on tempting God and on drink.
- **Minority peoples (Karelians, Komi, Nenets, Crimean Tatars):** check their own warning tales and drinking norms; avoid assumptions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mortality, road and drink data, and literature; mixed for attitude studies; thin for minority peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Church Slavonic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol and mortality talk invites stereotypes; this sheet names variation only. War-zone risk is not recklessness and should not be framed as such. Escape and fear go to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** none.
