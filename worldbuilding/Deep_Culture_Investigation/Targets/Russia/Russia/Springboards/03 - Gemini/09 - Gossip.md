*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages and small towns** against **big cities:** how far everyone knows everyone, and who escapes talk.
- **Courtyard and apartment-block life:** benches, entrances and shared flats as watching posts.
- **Ukrainian regions**, if carried: Carpathian mocking verse and village reputation in the west, against eastern industrial towns.
- **Jews:** shtetl memory, and rumors aimed at them.
- **Roma:** target of rumor, and talk inside their own communities, as they describe it.
- **Tatars and Crimean Tatars:** Muslim teaching on backbiting.
- **Old Believers and close religious groups:** reputation inside a small community.
- **Karelians, Veps and Sami:** very small communities where talk travels fast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore, literature and history of rumor and denunciation; mixed for present gossip habits.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Church Slavonic, Yiddish; Tatar, Crimean Tatar, Romani.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rumors about minorities (blood libel, Roma) must be studied as harm, never repeated as claims; denunciation and police archives raise access and privacy questions; slander and defamation law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
