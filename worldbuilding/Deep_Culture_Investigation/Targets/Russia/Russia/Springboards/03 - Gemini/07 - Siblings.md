# Russia: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Old Russian for chronicle and saints' lives; Tatar, Yiddish and Karelian for minority strands. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how a culture understands the sibling bond: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian villages and cities:** sibling roles in farm households against small urban families with one child.
- **Ukrainian regions**, if carried: western and central village customs of siblings at weddings and inheritance, against eastern towns.
- **Tatars:** age-marked sibling terms used as respectful address to non-kin.
- **Jews:** families scattered by emigration, with siblings in several countries.
- **Roma:** large sibling sets within wide kin groups.
- **Old Believers:** large families and close sibling networks.
- **Karelians, Veps, Sami, Nenets:** each people's own kin system.
- **Families divided by the Russian-Ukrainian line:** brothers and sisters on different sides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for kin terms, folklore and demography; mixed for adult sibling ties; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Old Russian; Tatar, Yiddish, Romani, Finnic, Sami and Nenets.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "fraternal peoples" and "elder brother" are loaded metaphors for Russian-Ukrainian relations; siblings separated by war and state care are painful subjects; inheritance law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "термины родства" with "сиблинги" and "восточные славяне".

3. Download the Russian GGS waves and look for contact and help between adult siblings.
