# Russia: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Church Slavonic for intercession; Yiddish, Tatar and Romani for minority strands. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and, more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Devout Orthodox families:** saints, priests and elders as intercessors and counselors.
- **Village households:** matchmakers, elders and kin as go-betweens; customs may differ north and south.
- **Ukrainian regions**, if carried: wedding go-betweens and Greek Catholic parish life in the west against Orthodox east and center.
- **Urban professionals:** courts, HR and new formal mediation against informal fixers.
- **Old Believers:** settling matters inside the community.
- **Jews:** the matchmaker and rabbinic counsel in the past.
- **Roma:** elders' assemblies.
- **Muslim Tatars and migrants from Central Asia and the Caucasus:** imams and elders as go-betweens.
- **Sami and Nenets:** their own ways of settling quarrels, as they describe them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for religion, wedding ritual and Soviet petitioning; mixed for present practice; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Church Slavonic; Yiddish, Tatar, Romani.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criminal arbitration and fixers touch crime; Roma customs must be described as they describe them, without stereotype; the federal mediation law, courts of arbitration, justices of the peace and comrades' courts belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
