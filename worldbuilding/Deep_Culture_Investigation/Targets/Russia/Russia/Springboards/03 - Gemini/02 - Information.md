*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **TV-reliant older people against Telegram- and YouTube-reliant younger people:** the generational split in channels.
- **Big cities against small towns and villages:** broadband, local papers and the district newspaper (районка).
- **Late-Soviet intelligentsia families:** samizdat and foreign radio as a family memory.
- **Ukrainian-speaking and Russian-speaking Ukrainian regions**, if carried: different media worlds and different trust.
- **Karelians and Veps**, with their own small press; **Crimean Tatars**, with a broadcaster that has moved.
- **Old Believers:** printed and manuscript books as authority, as they describe it.
- **Sami, Nenets and northern Pomor fishers:** knowledge of weather, ice and sea, as they share it.
- **Labor migrants from Central Asia and the Caucasus:** information carried in their own languages through messaging apps.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and surveys; mixed for present trust, because answers given under censorship and legal risk need careful reading.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Church Slavonic for early sources; Karelian, Veps, Crimean Tatar, Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some polling and rights bodies carry legal labels in Russia, Memorial was dissolved by court order, and many sites are blocked; state security archives are largely closed; respondents may self-censor; Sami and Nenets land knowledge is theirs to share; media law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search Levada Center's site for "источники информации" and "доверие СМИ", and download the yearly series.
2. Search the Russian State Library catalog for "Вести-Куранты" to find the published volumes.
3. Search the Blinken archive's catalog for "Radio Liberty audience research" on listening in the Soviet Union.
4. Search IMI's site for "свобода слова" reports by region.
5. Search the Petrozavodsk minority newspapers' sites for their archives and circulation notes.
