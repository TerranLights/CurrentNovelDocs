# Russia: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Old Russian for medieval brotherhoods; Yiddish and Tatar for minority strands. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected (or not).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg professionals** against **small-town residents**: networks for jobs, care and favors may run differently.
- **Older people living alone in villages:** isolation and the help of neighbors and kin.
- **Ukrainian regions**, if carried: godparent ties and village networks in the west and center, against eastern industrial towns.
- **Old Believers:** congregational ties across distant settlements.
- **Jews:** family ties across emigration.
- **Roma:** extended kin networks.
- **Karelians, Veps and Sami:** small communities where everyone is linked.
- **Migrants from Central Asia and the Caucasus:** home-region networks in the cities.
- **Families split by war and border changes**, whose ties run across new lines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for blat and Soviet networks; mixed for present loneliness; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Old Russian; Yiddish, Tatar, Romani and Finnic languages for their communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ties across the Russian-Ukrainian line are painful and political; corruption through connections touches law, which belongs to Libra and Capricorn; Roma research must avoid stereotypes and use their own voices.
- **Held for review:** none.
