## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Village families against city families:** children learning by working beside adults against schooled, tutored children.
- **Families of teachers and engineers (the intelligentsia):** home reading and hobby circles as learning.
- **Old Believers:** learning to read in church script at home.
- **Jewish families:** the kheyder past and later secular schooling.
- **Ukrainian regions**, if carried: Ukrainian-language schooling in the west and center against Russian-language schooling in the east and south.
- **Karelians, Veps and Komi:** language nests and native-language classes.
- **Sami and Nenets:** reindeer-herding knowledge and the boarding-school break.
- **Children of migrants:** learning Russian as a second language in city schools.
- **Deaf children:** schools for the deaf and oral against signed teaching.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (a deep pedagogical literature, textbook archives, assessments); thin for small peoples' home learning.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Church Slavonic, Yiddish, Tatar, Finnic and Sami languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the language of schooling is politically charged between Russian and Ukrainian; boarding-school histories are painful and told by those peoples first; Sami and Nenets land knowledge is theirs to share; education law belongs to Libra, and schooling as an institution to Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
