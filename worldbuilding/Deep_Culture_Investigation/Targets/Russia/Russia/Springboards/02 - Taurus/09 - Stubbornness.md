## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in religious and dissident history; mixed in surveys; thin for northern peoples' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Church Slavonic; Crimean Tatar; Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dissent and protest are politically dangerous topics in Russia now; Memorial was closed and archives may have moved; war resilience in Ukraine is charged. Treat refusal as variation, never as a national trait. Trials of dissenters as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
