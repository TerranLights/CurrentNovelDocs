## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow (Rublyovka) and St. Petersburg elites** against **provincial towns and villages**.
- **Ukraine:** Kyiv (Koncha-Zaspa), Dnipro and the Donbas (industrial fortunes), Odesa (port trade), Galicia and the west (remittance wealth).
- **Old Believer merchant heritage; Jewish merchants of the former Pale and Odesa; Greek merchant families of the south; Karaites.**
- **Crimean Tatars:** trade, gardens and craft before deportation; rebuilding after.
- **Nenets, Komi-Izhemtsy, Kola Sami:** herds as wealth.
- **Hungarians of Zakarpattia; Romanians of Bukovina; Bulgarians and Gagauz of the Budjak:** farm and migrant earnings.
- **Labor migrants** and the households living on their pay.
