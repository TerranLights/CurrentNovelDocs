# Russia (core state): Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Yiddish and Hungarian for minorities. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg professionals** against **monotown and village men** after the 1990s.
- **Russian North and Pomors**, long free of serfdom, against **the Black Earth serf villages**.
- **Ukrainian regions:** Galicia (Polish *honor* tradition), Kyiv, the Donbas mining towns (worth through hard labor), the south.
- **Crimean Tatars:** worth of a people after deportation.
- **Karelians, Veps, Komi, Erzya and Moksha:** small peoples' standing in Russian-speaking towns.
- **Nenets and Kola Sami:** herders' worth against settled norms.
- **Jews of the former Pale; Old Believers; veterans; the clergy and monastics.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and Soviet-era history; mixed for survey measures; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** army hazing, suicide, war trauma, prison culture; the Revolution of Dignity and the war are politically charged in both main languages. Honor in court (dishonor suits) goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
