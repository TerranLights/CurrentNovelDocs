## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban cafe and restaurant culture** (Moscow, St. Petersburg, Kyiv, Odesa, Lviv) against **village tables and bathhouses**.
- **Orthodox observers of the fasts** against **secular households**.
- **Ukraine by region:** Poltava (Gogol's table), Galicia (coffee and pastry), the Carpathians, Odesa (port and Jewish kitchens).
- **Karelians, Veps and Komi:** sauna and northern food.
- **Erzya and Moksha:** village feasts.
- **Crimean Tatars:** their cuisine, ornament and gardens.
- **Jews of the former Pale; Hungarians of Zakarpattia** (wine, paprika); **Nenets and Kola Sami.**
