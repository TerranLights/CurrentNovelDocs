*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg salaried workers** against **monotown and village households** relying on gardens and pensions.
- **Black Earth farm villages** against **Russian North fishers and Pomors**.
- **Komi Republic, Karelia, Mordovia:** northern and Volga minority regions after 1991.
- **Nenets and Kola Sami:** herd loss and climate risk.
- **Ukraine:** frontline east and south against the west; internally displaced families.
- **Crimean Tatars:** security after deportation and return.
- **Jews of the former Pale:** pogrom memory and emigration as security.
- **Pensioners, veterans and labor migrants in the big cities.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the post-1991 crises and household surveys; mixed for the pre-modern record; thin for small northern peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Church Slavonic for chronicles; Komi, Nenets for local terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine, war, displacement, organized crime; anything on current military or civil-defense matters must stay at the level of public, everyday practice. National security policy is out of scope; pension law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
