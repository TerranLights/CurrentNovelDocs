# Russia (core state): Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Komi, Nenets, Yiddish for minority angles. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muscovites and Petersburgers in former communal flats** against **village households**: shared space against farmstead storage.
- **The Russian North and Pomors** against **the Black Earth villages**: different household inventories and chest traditions.
- **Ukrainian village culture by region:** Polissia, Podillia, Slobozhanshchyna, Galicia; and **Carpathian Hutsuls**, whose carved and metal goods differ.
- **Crimean Tatars:** possession broken by deportation and return.
- **Nenets, Komi-Izhemtsy and Kola Sami herders:** mobile households, herd marks.
- **Old Believers:** rules on shared and separate vessels *check*.
- **Jews of the former Pale; Karelians and Veps; Erzya and Moksha.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and Soviet-era ethnography; mixed in surveys; thin for herding and small Finnic peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar and Yiddish for testimony and archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** confiscation, dekulakization and deportation; war losses since 2014 and 2022; theft and looting accounts. Property in the legal sense goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
