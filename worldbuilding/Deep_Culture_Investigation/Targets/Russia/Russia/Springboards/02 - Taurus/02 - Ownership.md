## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black Earth villages** (commune, repartition, then collective farm) against **the Russian North** (state peasants, household land).
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg flat-owners** against **rural *pai* holders** and the landless.
- **Right-Bank and Left-Bank Ukraine; Galicia and Volhynia**, which were outside the Russian Empire's commune; **Zakarpattia** under Hungarian law until the 20th century.
- **Hutsuls and Boykos**, with shared upland pasture.
- **Crimean Tatars**, whose claims run through deportation and return.
- **Nenets, Kola Sami, Komi-Izhemtsy:** herds and pasture rather than fields.
- **Old Believer merchant families; Jewish families of the former Pale**, for whom owning land was long restricted.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land history and polling; mixed for Indigenous holdings; thin for Krymchaks and Karaites.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Hungarian and Polish for the western archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dekulakization, collectivization, the Holodomor, seizures since 2014 and 2022, contested Crimean land. Property law (codes, courts, title rules) goes to Libra; the spirit of property law to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
