## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Capital professionals** against **small-town and village Russia**: survey gaps are wide.
- **Generations:** Soviet-born, 1990s-born, post-2010 youth.
- **Ukraine by region:** Galicia and Volhynia (Greek Catholic and Orthodox), the center, the Russian-speaking east and south.
- **Old Believers; Protestants (Baptists, Pentecostals); Greek Catholics of Galicia and Zakarpattia.**
- **Muslims:** Crimean Tatars; migrants in Moscow and St. Petersburg.
- **Jews of the former Pale; Karelians, Veps, Komi, Erzya and Moksha; Nenets and Sami herders**, whose teachings on land and animals need their own voices.
- **Hungarians of Zakarpattia; Romanians of Bukovina; Bulgarians and Gagauz of the Budjak.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in surveys and philosophy; mixed for minority teachings; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Church Slavonic for early texts; Crimean Tatar and Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "values" is a political battleground in both main lands since 2014 and 2022; read state documents and survey wording with care. Law on values goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
