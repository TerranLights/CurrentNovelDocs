## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Moscow and St. Petersburg families** against **newcomers from the regions and abroad**.
- **Russian North villages** (old-settled) against **Black Earth villages** reshaped by collectivization.
- **Monotowns** against diverse cities.
- **Ukraine:** Carpathian highlanders, Galicia, the steppe south (settled late, in the 18th-19th c.), the Donbas (industrial in-migration).
- **Old Believers; Seto, Veps, Karelians.**
- **Jews of the former Pale**, and the large emigrations since.
- **Crimean Tatars**, whose continuity was broken in 1944.
- **Internally displaced Ukrainians; labor migrants** in Russian cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and the late-Soviet period; mixed for village continuity; thin for Seto and Veps.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Yiddish for the Pale.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "stability" is a loaded political word; deportation and displacement; current war. Registration rules (*propiska*) as law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
