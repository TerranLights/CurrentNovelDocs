# Russia (core state): The domain, possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian; with Karelian, Veps, Komi, Erzya and Moksha, Nenets, Crimean Tatar, Yiddish, Hungarian and Romanian for the minorities. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how is it held: possessions, money, resources, food, dress and wealth.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Russia and the Golden Ring** against **the Black Earth provinces** (Voronezh, Kursk, Tambov): land-poor against grain-rich peasant pasts.
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg** against small towns and villages: the sharpest wealth gap.
- **The Russian North** (Arkhangelsk, Vologda; the Pomors): no serfdom for most peasants and a sea trade.
- **Karelians, Veps, Komi, Nenets and Kola Sami:** Finnic, Uralic and herding economies; reindeer as wealth.
- **Erzya and Moksha** (Mordovia): village household customs of their own.
- **Ukraine's regions:** the Left and Right Banks, Slobozhanshchyna, the Donbas, the south and Odesa, Galicia, Volhynia, Podillia, Polissia, Bukovina and Zakarpattia; Carpathian highlanders (Hutsuls, Boykos, Lemkos).
- **Crimean Tatars, Karaites and Krymchaks; Hungarians and Rusyns of Zakarpattia; Romanians of Bukovina; Bulgarians and Gagauz of the Budjak; Greeks of the Azov coast.**
- **Old Believers**; **Jews** of the former Pale; **Muslim migrants** in the big cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Russian and Ukrainian; mixed for Karelians, Veps, Komi and Crimean Tatars; thin for Kola Sami, Nenets, Krymchaks and Karaites. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (its pre-war lands are listed as Russia and Ukraine); this sign's sheets are written for the core lands of both.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Ukrainian above all; the minority languages for local terms; Yiddish and Polish for the archives of the western lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine (1921-1922, 1932-1933, 1946-1947), dekulakization and confiscation, war losses and looted property, deportation of the Crimean Tatars (1944) with loss of homes; current war damage and seized property. Polling since 2022 needs care.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
