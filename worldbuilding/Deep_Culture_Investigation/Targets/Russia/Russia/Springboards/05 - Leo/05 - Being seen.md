# Russia: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Romani, Yiddish, Kildin Sami and Nenets for minority strands. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Capitals against villages:** anonymity in Moscow, St. Petersburg and Kyiv against being known by everyone in a small place.
- **Communal-flat and dormitory dwellers** against those raised in private flats and dachas.
- **The old,** especially rural women living alone in emptying villages of the north and center.
- **Ukrainian regions;** Russian-speaking and Ukrainian-speaking households, whose visibility changed with language politics.
- **Roma:** watched as a group, unseen as persons, by their own accounts in the sources.
- **Crimean Tatars, Jews, Azov Greeks:** visibility as protection and as danger at different times.
- **Kola Sami, Nenets, Vepsians, Izhorians:** being counted at all.
- **Labor migrants and the unhoused** in the big cities; **LGBT people,** for whom visibility carries legal risk in Russia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, folk belief and Soviet surveillance history; mixed for present-day feeling seen; thin for the smallest Indigenous peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Romani and Sami sources where they exist.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state surveillance and wartime informing are live risks for informants; never name sources who could be harmed. Census categories are politically fought. Evil-eye charms may still be in private use; collect them as published, not from people unasked. The law on surveillance goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
