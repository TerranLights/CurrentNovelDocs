## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages and small towns** (work bees, shared harvests) against cities (formal charity, crowdfunding).
- **Orthodox parishes and Old Believer communities:** almsgiving and church giving.
- **Muslim Crimean Tatars:** sharing meat at Qurban Bayram and other Islamic giving.
- **Jewish communities:** tsdoke and community welfare.
- **Ukrainian regions;** the wartime volunteer and donation networks, organized differently in the west, center and east.
- **Kola Sami and Nenets:** sharing of catch and herd, as they describe it.
- **Roma:** giving within extended families.
- **The new rich and the old intelligentsia:** different models of patronage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history of charity and recent giving surveys; mixed for informal giving (gifts to doctors and teachers, family help), which surveys undercount; thin for northern Indigenous sharing.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish sources; Crimean Tatar for Muslim giving.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime fundraising, including for armed forces, is partisan and may expose donors; never name private donors. NGOs in Russia face "foreign agent" rules that affect what they publish. Gifts that shade into bribery go to Libra for the letter of the law.
- **Held for review:** none.
