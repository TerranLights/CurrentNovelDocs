# Russia: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Rusyn and Yiddish for minority strands. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg:** a long rivalry of civic pride, worth separating from provincial pride.
- **Single-industry towns (моногорода) and mining and factory districts:** pride in work and its loss when plants close.
- **Ukrainian regions:** Galicia, the Dnipro center, Zaporizhzhia (Cossack heritage), the Donbas and the Black Sea south; each tells a different story of pride.
- **Transcarpathian Rusyns:** a pride claimed apart from both larger nations.
- **Crimean Tatars:** collective pride shaped by deportation and return.
- **Pomors and Old Believers:** pride in old ways and in endurance.
- **Karelians, Vepsians, Komi:** language revival as pride.
- **Jews, Roma:** pride in a community's survival and in named achievers.
- **Veterans' families and the young:** pride in war memory against other sources of pride.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national-pride polling and church teaching; mixed for local and work pride; thin for the smaller minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar and Rusyn sources for those peoples.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national pride is at the heart of the war since 2014 and 2022; polls, textbooks and media on both sides are partisan, and wartime polling in Russia carries fear effects. Pride marches are banned or attacked in parts of the lands. Avoid any line that gives one people a single pride.
- **Held for review:** none.
