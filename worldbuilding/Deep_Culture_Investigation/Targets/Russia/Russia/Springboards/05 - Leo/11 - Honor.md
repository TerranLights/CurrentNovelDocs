# Russia: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar and Yiddish for minority codes; Romani sources where a Roma group's own terms are published. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nobility, merchants, clergy and peasants** in the historical record: different codes for each estate.
- **Cossack-heritage communities** in the Ukrainian lands: chivalric and comradely honor.
- **Military families and officers:** the uniform's honor.
- **The criminal world and prison-shaped neighborhoods:** a code with its own words.
- **Football supporters and street youth** in the big cities.
- **Crimean Tatars:** namus and family honor.
- **Roma:** honor and shame rules that differ between Roma groups.
- **Jewish communities:** koved through learning and lineage.
- **Old Believers:** a reputation for keeping one's word, to test against sources.
- **Women and men:** whether family honor rests on women's conduct, as a question for each group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for noble and Muscovite honor and for literature; mixed for village and minority codes; thin for present-day honor talk outside the military and the criminal world.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Yiddish and Romani for those communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence in families justified by honor is a research question to ask in each group, not a claim to make; use the words of those who lived it. Military honor is bound up with the war on both sides. Honor suits as written law, and courts of honor, go to Libra; loyalty and revenge to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
