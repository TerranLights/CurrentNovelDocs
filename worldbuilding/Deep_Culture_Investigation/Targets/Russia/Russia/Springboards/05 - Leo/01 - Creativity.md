# Russia: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Yiddish and the Finnic languages for minority crafts. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Named craft villages and towns** of central Russia (Gzhel, Fedoskino, Palekh, Mstera, Kholui, the Khokhloma and Gorodets districts): tradition passed down within a place.
- **The Russian North** (Mezen painting, Kargopol toys, Vologda lace): older ornament, less tourist pressure.
- **Science towns and tech hubs** (Moscow, St. Petersburg, Kyiv, Kharkiv, the naukogrady): creativity as invention and software.
- **Ukrainian regions:** Petrykivka (Dnipro region); Poltava pottery; Hutsul wood carving and pysanky in the Carpathians; Transcarpathian weaving; paper-cutting (vytynanky).
- **Crimean Tatars:** ornament (örnek) and embroidery as identity work.
- **Karelians, Vepsians, Komi, Kola Sami, Nenets:** embroidery, birch-bark, beadwork, fur mosaic.
- **Jewish** papercut and klezmer traditions; **Roma** smithing and music.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crafts, Soviet art and invention; mixed for present-day amateur making; thin for the smaller Finno-Ugric and Arctic crafts outside museum catalogs.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar and Finnic terms for minority crafts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** museum holdings moved or damaged in the war since 2022, and contested claims over Crimean and Ukrainian heritage; read every source for its side. Copyright and patent rules as written go to Libra; who owns designs to Taurus.
- **Held for review:** none.
