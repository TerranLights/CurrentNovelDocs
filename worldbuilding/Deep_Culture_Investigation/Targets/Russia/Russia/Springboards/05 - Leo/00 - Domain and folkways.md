## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg against provincial towns and villages:** display, celebrity and nightlife are capital-heavy.
- **The Russian North** (Arkhangelsk, Vologda, the White Sea Pomors, Old Believer villages): distinct festive and craft traditions.
- **Central and southern Russian villages:** the round-dance and mumming heartland of the folklore record.
- **Ukrainian lands by region:** the east and south; the center and the Dnipro; Galicia; the Carpathian Hutsuls, Boikos and Lemkos; Transcarpathian Rusyns and Hungarians; Bukovina's Romanians; Budjak's Bulgarians and Gagauz.
- **Crimea and the Azov shore:** Crimean Tatars, Karaites, Krymchaks and the Azov Greeks (Urum and Rumeic speakers).
- **Finno-Ugric and Arctic peoples:** Karelians, Vepsians, Izhorians, Komi, Kola Sami, Nenets.
- **Jews, Roma, Belarusians;** churchgoers against secular households; Soviet-born against post-1991 generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Russian and Ukrainian majorities; mixed for Crimea and the borderland minorities; thin for Vepsians, Izhorians and Kola Sami.
- **Lands:** the target carries the pre-war lands of Russia and Ukraine, and its exact lands, including where the line with Ukraine runs, are still to be settled. These sheets are written for the Russian and Ukrainian core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Ukrainian throughout; Crimean Tatar, Yiddish and the Finnic languages for minority sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** since 2014, and more since 2022, festivals, sport, celebrity and holidays are tied to the war; read every source for its side and date. Public display of LGBT identity is restricted in Russia; the letter of such rules, and of gambling and lottery rules, goes to Libra, their spirit to Capricorn. Some Sami and Nenets song and ritual knowledge is held by families; ask before using it.
- **Held for review:** none.
