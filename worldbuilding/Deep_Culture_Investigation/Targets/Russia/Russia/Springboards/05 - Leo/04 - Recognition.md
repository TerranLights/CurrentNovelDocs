# Russia: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Rusyn, Yiddish and the languages of the northern Indigenous peoples for their own forms. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **State-sector workers** (teachers, doctors, factory staff), where titles and boards of honor still matter, against private-sector staff.
- **Academics and artists,** whose standing hangs on degrees, titles and prizes.
- **Veterans and their families:** recognition of service, and its politics.
- **Ukrainian regions:** different state honors, memorials and renamings since 1991 and 2014.
- **Kola Sami, Nenets, Vepsians, Izhorians:** recognition as peoples, with practical stakes.
- **Rusyns, Crimean Tatars, Azov Greeks:** naming, census and memory claims.
- **Old Believers:** recognition of their rites after centuries of exclusion.
- **Labor migrants in the big cities:** credentials and work that go unrecognized.
- **Women in science and the arts:** credit given and withheld.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors and award lists; mixed for everyday workplace praise; thin for how northern Indigenous peoples honor their own members, which they may not share.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Sami, Nenets and Rusyn sources for those peoples.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recognition of past wrongs (repression, famine, deportation) is contested between states and within them; Memorial was dissolved in Russia, so check which copies of its data are current. Wartime awards and renamings are partisan. The legal status of peoples goes to Libra for its letter.
- **Held for review:** none.
