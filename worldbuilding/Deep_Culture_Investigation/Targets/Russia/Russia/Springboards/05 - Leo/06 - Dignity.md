# Russia: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Yiddish and Romani for minority strands. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pensioners,** above all rural women: dignity in poverty and old age.
- **Disabled people in institutions** against those living with family.
- **Workers in old industrial towns** of central Russia and the Donbas: dignity at work and after closures.
- **Ukrainian regions,** where the word carries recent political weight; compare the center and west with the east and south.
- **Crimean Tatars:** the dignity of a deported people.
- **Roma:** respect as their own communities define it, against how institutions treat them.
- **Former prisoners and their families;** the unhoused.
- **Old Believers and devout Orthodox:** dignity in religious teaching and bearing.
- **Kola Sami, Nenets, Vepsians:** the dignity of small peoples and their languages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, memoir and NGO reports; mixed in surveys; thin on how small minorities frame dignity in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Yiddish and Romani for those communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** camp, deportation and wartime testimony carries trauma and political weight; use published testimony and cite survivors' own words first. NGOs in Russia face "foreign agent" pressure, so their reports may be cut or moved. Rights as written (dignity in constitutions and codes) go to Libra; their spirit to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
