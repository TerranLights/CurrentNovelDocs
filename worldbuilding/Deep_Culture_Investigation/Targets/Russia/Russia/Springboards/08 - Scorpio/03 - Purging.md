## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Orthodox who keep the fasts and confess, against the unchurched; Old Believers (their own purity rules about vessels and outsiders, to check); Baptists and Pentecostals (sobriety as cleansing); Muslims (ablution, Ramadan) among Tatars, Crimean Tatars and migrants; Jews (kashrut, the mikveh); Karelians, Vepsians and Komi, for whom the bathhouse holds its own beliefs (to check); Nenets and Kola Sami, for cleansing by smoke or fire, as they share it; Ukrainian villages with *шептухи* (whisper-healers) in Polissia and the Carpathians; urban wellness and detox consumers; families of deported peoples; veterans of the Chernobyl cleanup. Why: what pollutes, and how it is removed, varies by faith, by trade and by memory.

## 4. Flags
