# Russia (core state): Domain and folkways (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian and Ukrainian (both Cyrillic); Church Slavonic and Old East Slavic for the pre-modern record; minority languages of the core lands (Karelian, Vepsian, Komi, Nenets, Tatar, Crimean Tatar; Yiddish for the historic Jewish record). **Concept in one line:** death and transformation: how the culture faces death, power and what is hidden (death and rebirth, sex, shared resources, inheritance, secrets).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Moscow and Central Russia (non-Black-Earth north against Black-Earth south); St. Petersburg and the Northwest; Novgorod and Pskov; the Russian North (Arkhangelsk, Vologda, Olonets, the Pomors); Old Believers; Finnic peoples (Karelians, Vepsians, Izhorians, Votes, Ingrian Finns); Komi and Komi-Izhemtsy; Nenets and Kola Sami (reindeer-herding death and inheritance ways); Tatar and other Muslim communities and Central Asian and Caucasus migrants in the big cities; Jews (Moscow; the former Pale in Ukraine); Ukrainians by region (Polissia, Podillia, Sloboda Ukraine, the steppe south, Galicia with its Greek Catholics, Hutsuls, Boikos and Lemkos in the Carpathians); Crimean Tatars; Gagauz and Bulgarians of the Budjak; Rusyns and Hungarians of Zakarpattia; Roma; Baptist and Pentecostal communities. Why: faith, lineage and land shape death, inheritance and sex ways differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for funeral ethnography and Soviet-era death; mixed for household money and inheritance; thinner and filtered for sex and power since the 2010s.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Church Slavonic, and the minority languages above.
- **Lands:** the header carries Russia and Ukraine, and the line between this row and Ukraine is open; this sign's sheets write for the core lands of European Russia and the Ukrainian lands carried, with other groups listed as candidates until the nation's exact lands are settled.
