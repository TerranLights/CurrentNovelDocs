Military families and veterans (oath, desertion, captivity); prisoners and former prisoners (the thieves' code); Orthodox, Old Believers and evangelicals (the Nikonian reforms as a betrayal of the faith, as Old Believers tell it); Ukrainians in the east, center and west, who may name different traitors; Crimean Tatars; Jews; descendants of Cossack hosts (Zaporozhian memory); Russian émigré families; Karelians and Ingrian Finns (wartime suspicion); Moscow and St. Petersburg against small towns, where everyone knows; women and men, on the double standard in infidelity, as a question.
