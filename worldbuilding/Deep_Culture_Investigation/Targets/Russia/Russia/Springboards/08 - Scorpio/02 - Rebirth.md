# Russia (core state): Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
The newly churched (*воцерковлённые*) against lifelong Orthodox and the nonreligious; Old Believers (their rites for receiving converts, to check); Baptists, Pentecostals and charismatics (regional strength to check); Greek Catholics in Galicia; Jews in Moscow, Kyiv and Odesa returning to practice; Tatar and other Muslims in the cities; Crimean Tatars after return; Karelians, Vepsians and Komi in language revival; neopagan Rodnovery groups (*Родноверие*, Ukrainian *Рідновірство*), as members describe themselves; former prisoners and people in recovery; veterans of the Afghan and later wars. Why: what counts as true change, and who must vouch for it, may differ by faith and by experience.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for religious history, Soviet remaking and literature; mixed for current conversion and recovery; thin for Finnic revival outside regional sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Church Slavonic; Crimean Tatar, Tatar, Karelian and Hebrew for minority cases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some evangelical and other groups face legal pressure in Russia, so members may not speak openly; the Crimean Tatar return and Ukrainian "revival" discourse are politically charged between the two states; neopagan groups range widely and some overlap with extremist milieus, so record each by its own account.
- **Held for review:** none.
