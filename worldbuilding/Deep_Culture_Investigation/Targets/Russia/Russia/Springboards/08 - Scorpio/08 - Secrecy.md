# Russia (core state): Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Karelian and Komi for holders of restricted knowledge; Tatar and Crimean Tatar. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden (privacy included).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Residents of former closed towns and defense-industry families; Moscow and St. Petersburg intelligentsia (kitchen culture) against villages where neighbors know everything; Old Believers, some of whom long hid their practice; Greek Catholics who worshiped underground (1946-1989); Baptists and Pentecostals with a history of hidden meetings; Jews and families of the repressed who concealed origins; LGBT people, by city and generation; Hutsuls and other Carpathian highlanders (*мольфари*); Karelians, Komi, Nenets and Kola Sami, whose sacred places and healers' knowledge may be restricted; Crimean Tatars; Roma; migrants without papers. Why: what must be hidden, and from whom, may follow faith, profession, origin and history of persecution.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state secrecy and the Soviet private sphere; mixed for current privacy attitudes; thin and restricted for minority sacred knowledge.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Karelian, Komi and Nenets for the North.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nenets, Sami, Komi and Karelian sacred sites and healers' knowledge are recorded only as the communities allow and never retold; LGBT topics are legally restricted in Russia, so informants may be at risk; state-secrecy research can expose researchers and sources. Laws on state secrets and data, as written, go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
