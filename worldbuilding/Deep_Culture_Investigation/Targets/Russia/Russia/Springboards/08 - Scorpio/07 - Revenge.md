## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
The old gentry dueling code against peasant and merchant ways of settling scores; Cossack-heritage communities (Zaporozhian memory); Carpathian highlanders (Hutsuls, Boikos) and Podillia, with their outlaw legends; prisoners and former prisoners; veterans of the Afghan, Chechen and later wars; Orthodox and evangelical teaching on forgiveness, against honor talk; Crimean Tatars (memory of deportation, justice against vengeance); Jews (pogrom memory); Roma, whose own dispute customs need their own account; North Caucasus migrants in the big cities, only as they describe themselves, never as a stereotype; urban youth online. Why: whether getting even is duty, sin or folly may vary by estate, faith, war memory and region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, film and song; mixed for measured attitudes (few direct surveys); sensitive and partisan for wartime and ethnic revenge.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Yiddish and Polish for the 1768 and pogrom records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pogroms, the 1768 Uman massacre and wartime reprisals carry rival national readings; leaked private material must not be reproduced; avoid framing any people as feud-prone. Blood vengeance in old law codes (for example *Русская правда*) is Libra's; private against state punishment is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
