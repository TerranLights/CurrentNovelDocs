# Russia (core state): Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian, Church Slavonic; Karelian and Tatar for craft sources. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Craft villages** (Palekh, Mstyora, Kholuy, Vologda, Gzhel) against industrial towns: hand perfection against mass production.
- **Elite schools, ballet and sport** against ordinary schooling: where perfection is demanded of children.
- **Defense and space engineers** against consumer factories: the Soviet split in quality, as workers recall it.
- **Orthodox monastic culture:** perfection as a spiritual path.
- **Ukrainian lands:** embroidery, *pysanky* and Petrykivka standards.
- **Karelians and the Northern carpenters:** wooden building traditions.
- **Tatar leatherworkers and jewelers:** their own standards of fine work.
- **Women:** the perfect home and appearance as expectations to examine, not assume.

## 4. Flags

## 5. First queries
1. Read «Левша», then search the Russian National Corpus for «подковать блоху» and «и так сойдёт».
2. Search eLibrary and CyberLeninka for «перфекционизм шкала адаптация» to find the Russian scale studies.
3. Search for the 1967 Quality Mark history and Soviet press complaints about *brak*.
4. Find a modern edition of an icon painters' *podlinnik* with an introduction on its rules.
5. Search Ukrainian sources for «петриківський розпис майстри стандарти» to start the Ukrainian craft line.
