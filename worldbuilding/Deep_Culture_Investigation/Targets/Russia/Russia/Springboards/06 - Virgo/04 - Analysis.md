## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Academic and science towns** (Dubna, Obninsk, Pushchino, Chernogolovka) against the rest: places built for analysis.
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg technical elites** against rural Russia, where practical wit (*smekalka*) is praised.
- **Ukrainian lands:** Kyiv and Kharkiv science traditions and their own schools.
- **Jewish heritage communities:** the learning tradition of the western lands and its later secular forms; to be checked, not assumed.
- **Old Believers:** close reading of sacred texts as a form of scrutiny.
- **Tatars in the capitals:** Muslim scholarly traditions and modern technical careers.
- **Women in science:** Soviet-era participation and its limits, as women scientists tell it.

## 4. Flags
