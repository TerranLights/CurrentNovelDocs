*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg professionals:** coaching, therapy and self-development culture.
- **Industrial towns:** lean programs and the rationalizer legacy on the shop floor.
- **Dacha and village households:** improving land and house by hand.
- **Old Believers:** a principled refusal of some changes.
- **Ukrainian lands:** *Prosvita* and a separate path of reform since 1991.
- **Crimean Tatars and Volga Tatars:** Jadid reform and its memory.
- **Karelians and Vepsians:** language and culture renewal.
- **Neighborhoods affected by urban renewal:** residents' own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Petrine and Soviet projects and on diaries; mixed on present self-help; thin for minorities beyond Tatar reform.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Tatar and Crimean Tatar (older texts in Arabic script); Karelian and Vepsian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "reforging" through forced labor; resettlement and demolition programs; Crimean Tatar history of deportation.
- **Held for review:** none.
