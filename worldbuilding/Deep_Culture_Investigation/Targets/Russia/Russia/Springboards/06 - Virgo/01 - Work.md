# Russia (core state): Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Karelian, Tatar, Uzbek and Tajik for minority and migrant sources. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow's office class against factory towns** (Ivanovo, Tula, Yaroslavl): desk work against shop-floor identity.
- **Village and dacha households:** subsistence gardening as work in its own right.
- **Ukrainian lands:** peasant, chumak (carter-trader) and Donbas mining traditions as separate work cultures.
- **Pomors and northern fishing villages:** sea work by season.
- **Karelians and Vepsians:** forest and fishing work in their own languages.
- **Old Believer communities:** reputations for trade and diligence to be tested, not assumed.
- **Central Asian and South Caucasus migrants:** construction, cleaning, markets.
- **Men and women:** the "double burden" of paid work and housework, a theme the scholarship names.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, Soviet labor history and literature; mixed for migrants; thin for northern minorities outside folklore archives.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Karelian and Finnish for runo-songs; Uzbek, Tajik and Kyrgyz for migrants' own voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced labor and its memory; migrants' legal status; the late Soviet anti-"parasite" campaigns touch law, so their legal side belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
