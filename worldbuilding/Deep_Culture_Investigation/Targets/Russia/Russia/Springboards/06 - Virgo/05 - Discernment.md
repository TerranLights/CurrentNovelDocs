## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Monastic and devout Orthodox circles** against secular urban professionals: spiritual against worldly discernment.
- **Older people and the young:** scam targets and their differing media habits.
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg tastemakers** against provincial taste: food, dress, art.
- **Ukrainian lands:** distinct courtship customs and a distinct recent history of judging news.
- **Village households of the North:** forest knowledge (mushrooms, berries, weather).
- **Karelians and Vepsians:** sages and counsel in Finnic tradition.
- **Tatars:** Muslim and Tatar proverb traditions on good sense.
- **Migrants:** how hosts judge them and how they read their hosts, as both tell it.
