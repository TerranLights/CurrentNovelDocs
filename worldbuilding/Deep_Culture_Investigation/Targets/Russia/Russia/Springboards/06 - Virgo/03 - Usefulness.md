## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Grandmothers and grandfathers:** the *babushka* who raises grandchildren against the retired man without a role.
- **Disabled people:** organizations of the blind and deaf with their own workplaces against those outside them.
- **Village households:** practical skill as everyday necessity, against city specialists.
- **Office workers in Moscow and St. Petersburg:** the "office plankton" jibe and how insiders answer it.
- **Ukrainian lands:** their own proverb stock and post-Soviet disability and pension histories.
- **Vepsians and Karelians:** household and forest skills in minority terms.
- **Labor migrants:** judged by their usefulness in the city, and how they speak of it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on literary debates and Soviet pedagogy; mixed on elders and disability; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Vepsian and Karelian dictionaries for minority terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** institutional care homes (psychoneurological *internaty*) and their residents; the pension-age protests; eugenic or "defectology" history needs care.
- **Held for review:** none.
