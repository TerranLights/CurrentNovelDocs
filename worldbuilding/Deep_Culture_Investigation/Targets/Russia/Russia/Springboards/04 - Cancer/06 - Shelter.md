## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Street homeless in the capitals** against **rural poor** taken in by kin.
- **Displaced Ukrainians and evacuees**, inside and outside the country, by the kind of shelter they found.
- **Chernobyl evacuees** and returnees.
- **Crimean Tatars:** exile in 1944, then return to land with no house.
- **Jewish communities:** communal shelters before 1917; refuge and its refusal in wartime.
- **Old Believer** wanderers and their hosts; **monasteries** and pilgrims.
- **Nenets herders:** the tent as shelter, and hospitality on the tundra.
- **Women and children fleeing violence**; children in state shelters (see Abandonment).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial charity and literature; mixed for present homelessness (counts disputed); fast-changing for wartime displacement.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Yiddish; Crimean Tatar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war displacement and shelters under attack; the stigma of the *bomzh* label; people in shelters as vulnerable informants.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
