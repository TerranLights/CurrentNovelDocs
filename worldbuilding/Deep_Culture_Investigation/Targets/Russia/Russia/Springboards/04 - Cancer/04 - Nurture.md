## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Grandmother-raised children** (the classic three-generation arrangement) against **families using nannies, daycare or neither**.
- **Rural North and central villages** against **big-city educated parents**.
- **Ukrainian regions:** Galicia (church-centered upbringing), the center, the Russian-speaking east; Hutsul and Boyko mountain families.
- **Crimean Tatar families** after return: keeping language and manners alive.
- **Nenets herding families** and children sent to boarding and nomadic schools.
- **Jewish families** before and after emigration.
- **Old Believer** and religious families.
- **Karelian, Veps and Seto families:** home language against school language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on pedagogy, Soviet upbringing and folklore; mixed on present-day practice; thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Yiddish; Crimean Tatar; Nenets for school-and-family sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding schools for Indigenous children; outsider "national character" theories; wartime evacuations and removals of children.
- **Held for review:** none.
