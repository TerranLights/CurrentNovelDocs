## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mothers who give up babies** and **fathers who leave**: judged the same or not.
- **Children in state care** against those raised by grandparents or kin.
- **Families of labor migrants:** western Ukrainian villages; Russian provincial towns sending men to work far away.
- **War orphans and evacuees**, past and present.
- **Elders left alone** in emptied villages of the North and center.
- **Crimean Tatars** (deportation), **Jewish families** (deserted wives; emigration), **Old Believers** (break-ups in closed families).
- **Nenets children** in boarding schools far from their families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history (foundlings, street children) and orphanage reform; mixed for present family desertion; thin for minority experience.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Yiddish; Crimean Tatar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** removal of Ukrainian children during the war (a live international issue); orphanage abuse; deportation; adoptees' privacy. Laws on adoption and support go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
