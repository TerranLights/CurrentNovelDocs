## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban professional mothers** against **village mothers** of the North and center.
- **Mothers of many children** (including Orthodox and Old Believer families) against one-child families.
- **Single mothers** and the grandmothers who co-raise.
- **Ukrainian regions:** Galicia, the center, the east; Hutsul and Boyko mountain mothers; the Berehynia revival.
- **Crimean Tatar mothers**, deportation and return; **Jewish mothers** in memory and humor; **Karaite**, **Azov Greek** and **Budjak Bulgarian** families.
- **Nenets mothers** with children in boarding schools.
- **Soldiers' mothers** as a group of their own.
