## 3. Who to compare inside the nation (freedom ways by founding population)
- **Novgorod and Pskov**: the memory of town self-rule.
- **The Russian North** (Pomors): peasants who were never serfs.
- **Central Russia**: the serfdom heartland and its post-1861 villages.
- **Cossack and steppe lands of Ukraine**: Zaporozhian liberty, and its later uses.
- **Galicia and western Ukraine**: freedom under Habsburg, Polish and Soviet rule, a different arc.
- **Old Believers**: freedom gained by flight to forests and frontiers.
- **Siberian settlers**: the frontier without landlords.
- **Crimean Tatars**: deportation and return.
- **Small Indigenous peoples of the North**: freedom of movement for herders, and self-determination.
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg intelligentsia**: freedom of speech and conscience.
