# Russia (core state): Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Old Russian for early sermons; Komi, Sakha, Buryat and Nenets terms for folk belief. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Churched believers** against **"Orthodox by culture"**, who belong without believing much of the creed.
- **Soviet-raised convinced atheists**, a shrinking generation with its own belief system.
- **Urban seekers**: psychics' clients, Roerich followers, esoteric circles.
- **Rodnovers**: Slavic neo-pagans building beliefs from scholarship.
- **Polissia and the Carpathians (Hutsuls)**: archaic folk belief recorded in depth.
- **Old Believers**: a creed held to the letter.
- **Komi, Karelians, Nenets**: spirits of forest, water and tundra.
- **Sakha and Buryats**: spirit-masters of place beside Christianity or Buddhism.
- **Crimean Tatars and Jews**: other creeds, other folk beliefs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Slavic folk belief and for surveys; mixed for new movements; thinner and sensitive for Indigenous beliefs.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Old Russian; Komi, Sakha, Buryat, Nenets for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Soviet ethnography framed beliefs as "survivals"; some groups are labeled "sects" or banned (law: Libra or Capricorn); Indigenous sacred knowledge may be restricted; "superstition" is a label to quote, not adopt.
- **Held for review:** none.
