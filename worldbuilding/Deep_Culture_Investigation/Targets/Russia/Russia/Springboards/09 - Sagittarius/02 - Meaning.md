# Russia (core state): Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Nenets and Komi for northern community sources (terms to be found). **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Generations**: Soviet-raised adults who lost a frame in 1991 against those born after it.
- **Single-industry towns** (*моногорода*): meaning tied to a plant that closed or may close.
- **Villages** of the North and center, with their emptying and their village-prose image.
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg professionals**: careers, consumer life, emigration talk.
- **Veterans** (of Afghanistan, Chechnya and later wars): purpose found and lost.
- **Ukrainian lands**: east and west, with their own crises after 1991 and after 2014.
- **Monastics and the churched**: meaning given by faith.
- **Nenets, Komi and Khanty herders and fishers**: meaning bound to land and animals.
- **Labor migrants from Central Asia** in the big cities: meaning in remittances and family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in philosophy, literature and oral history; mixed in surveys (life satisfaction more than meaning); thin for Indigenous peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Nenets for herders' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and alcohol data; war trauma on both sides of the pre-war border; Alexievich and Stus are politically charged in different ways.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Tolstoy's *Confession* and Frank's *Смысл жизни* to set the native philosophical frame.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "смысложизненные ориентации" plus region names for local test results.
3. Open RLMS-HSE documentation for well-being items.
4. Read Yurchak's chapters on "being outside" and Alexievich's opening voices.
5. Search for "Skovoroda srodna pratsia" scholarship in Ukrainian.
