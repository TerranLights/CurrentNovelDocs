# Russia (core state): Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Ukrainian; Old Russian for the pilgrimage genre; Crimean Tatar, Komi and Nenets for community sources; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish pilgrimage. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muscovites and Petersburgers**: foreign travel and emigration.
- **Provincial and rural Russians**: seasonal labor migration, pilgrimages on foot.
- **Ukrainian lands**: labor migration westward, chumak memory, Greek Catholic pilgrimages.
- **Crimean Tatars**: hajj, deportation and return as journeys.
- **Jews**: emigration waves and returns as pilgrims.
- **Komi-Izhemtsy and Nenets**: herders' migrations.
- **Old Believers**: flight and wandering sects (the *бегуны*).
- **Northerners and Siberians**: distance as daily life.
- **Labor migrants from Central Asia and the Caucasus** in the big cities: the arriving side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pilgrimage, literature and Soviet tourism; mixed for present migration data; thin for herders' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Old Russian; Crimean Tatar, Komi, Nenets; Yiddish and Hebrew.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exile, deportation and wartime displacement; recent emigration is politically charged; border rules and exit bans are law (Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.
