*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Priested and priestless Old Believers**: different distances from "the world"; neither should be assumed strict without evidence.
- **Monastic and lay Orthodox activists** (Moscow street groups, conservative monasteries) against liberal and mainstream parishes.
- **Unregistered Baptists and Pentecostals**, especially in Ukrainian lands.
- **Crimean Tatars and Moscow's Muslims**: traditional, Sufi-rooted and Salafi currents, kept apart.
- **Haredi and Chabad Jewish communities**: strict observance on its own terms.
- **New religious movements** and their leavers.
- **Rodnovers**: restoring an imagined original pagan faith.
- **Secular critics** who use the word as a charge.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on history and on official labeling; thin on members' own accounts of strict groups.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Church Slavonic; Crimean Tatar and Arabic terms for Muslim currents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "extremist" and "sect" designations, bans and prosecutions (including of Crimean Tatars and Jehovah's Witnesses) are law: Libra or Capricorn only; some monitors are closed or designated; the term is an insult, so quote who uses it. Never treat a people as one voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
