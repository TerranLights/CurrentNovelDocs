## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg** against provincial towns and villages: practice and new movements cluster differently.
- **The Russian North** (Arkhangelsk, Vologda, Pomors, Pechora Old Believers): no serfdom, strong dissenting traditions.
- **Central and Black Earth Russia**: the parish heartland and its shrines.
- **Ukrainian lands**: east, center and west differ by church (Greek Catholic Galicia, OCU and UOC, a Baptist and Pentecostal belt).
- **Crimean Tatars**: Sunni Muslims with a deportation history.
- **Karelians, Vepsians, Komi, Sami, Nenets**: Orthodox and older layers.
- **Siberian peoples** (Buryats, Sakha, Altaians, Tuvans, Khanty, Mansi): Buddhism and revived Indigenous faiths.
- **Jews**, **Protestants**, **Rodnovers** (Slavic neo-pagans), the **unaffiliated** urban young.
