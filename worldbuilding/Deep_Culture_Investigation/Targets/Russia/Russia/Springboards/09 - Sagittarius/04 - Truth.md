## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Television viewers against online and Telegram readers**: different sources of what counts as true.
- **Science towns** (Dubna, Obninsk, Novosibirsk's Akademgorodok): communities built on scientific authority.
- **Churched Orthodox and Old Believers**: revealed truth and the authority of old books.
- **Ukrainian lands**: separate media spheres, memory laws and fact-checkers; east and west.
- **Crimean Tatars**: a people whose account of deportation was long denied.
- **Nenets, Komi and other northern peoples**: oral records and their standing.
- **Families of repression victims** and memory activists.
- **Rural elders**, for proverbs and the trust given to eyewitnesses.
