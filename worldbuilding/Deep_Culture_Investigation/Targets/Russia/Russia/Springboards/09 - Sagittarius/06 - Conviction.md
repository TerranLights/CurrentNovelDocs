## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers**: steadfastness as the founding story.
- **Peace sectarians** (Dukhobors, Molokans, Tolstoyans, Baptists who refused arms): conscience against the state.
- **The intelligentsia** of Moscow and St. Petersburg: principle as identity.
- **Soviet loyalists and their heirs**: conviction for the state, not against it.
- **Ukrainian Sixtiers and western Ukraine**: national conviction in dissent.
- **Crimean Tatars**: a decades-long national movement.
- **Khanty and Nenets**: resistance memory in the North.
- **Workers and miners** (Kuzbass and Donbas strikes of 1989): conviction in labor.
- **The young**: protest generations of 2011-12 and after.
