## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Russian villages against Moscow and St. Petersburg.**
- **The Russian North** (Arkhangelsk, Vologda, Karelia, Pomors): lament, charm and Old Believer traditions.
- **Old Believers:** a separate devotional world with its own hermitages.
- **Ukrainian lands:** Polesie, the Carpathian Hutsuls, the Greek Catholic west, the Orthodox center and east.
- **Muslim peoples** (Crimean Tatars, Tatars, Bashkirs): Islamic and folk healing.
- **Finno-Ugric peoples** (Mari, Udmurt, Komi, Karelians, Veps, Erzya, Moksha): sacred groves and revived native faiths.
- **Siberian peoples** (Sakha, Buryats, Tuvans, Altai, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenki), as each shares its ways.
- **Jewish heritage of Ukraine** (Hasidic origins in Podolia).
- **Generations:** Soviet-schooled atheists against post-1991 seekers.
