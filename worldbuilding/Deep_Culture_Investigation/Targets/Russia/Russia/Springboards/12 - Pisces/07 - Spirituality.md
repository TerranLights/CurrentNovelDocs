# Russia: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian; Church Slavonic; Tatar, Mari, Sakha, Tuvan and Buryat for minority material. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion (the experience, the practice and the sacred outside the church; institutions belong to Sagittarius).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Churched Orthodox against cultural Orthodox and the unaffiliated "believers in my own way."**
- **Old Believers** (several concords): piety outside the state church.
- **Protestants** (Baptists, Pentecostals): strong in parts of Siberia and the Ukrainian lands.
- **Muslim peoples** (Tatars, Bashkirs; North Caucasus peoples where carried): mosque, saints' graves and folk Islam.
- **Buddhist peoples** (Buryats, Kalmyks, Tuvans).
- **Shamanic and native-faith revivals** (Sakha Aiyy faith, Tuvans, Altai, Mari, Udmurt, Erzya), each as it describes itself.
- **Rodnovers and New Age seekers** in the cities.
- **Jewish communities** and Hasidic pilgrimage in the Ukrainian lands.
- **Ukrainian Orthodox and Greek Catholics**, and the jurisdictions that split from each other.
- **Convinced atheists** of the Soviet-educated generation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Orthodoxy and for survey data since 1991; mixed for minority faiths; closed ceremonies are not for research.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Church Slavonic, Ukrainian; Tatar, Mari, Sakha, Tuvan, Buryat.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the state's use of "духовность"; groups banned or labeled "extremist" (Jehovah's Witnesses, 2017; some Muslim groups); the church split in Ukraine (2018-2019); Indigenous sacred knowledge; separate experience from institutions (Sagittarius).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities and movements in the nation's lands.
