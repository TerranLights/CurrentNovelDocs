# Russia: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian; Old Russian; Tatar and Buryat for minority material. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Village elders, hunters and fishers** (Russian North, Siberia) against city professionals: different trusted knowledge.
- **Women and men:** who is credited with "knowing".
- **Churched Orthodox:** discernment and the warning against delusion.
- **Muslim communities:** dreams and signs in Islamic tradition, as each community holds them.
- **Buryat, Sakha, Tuvan and Evenki peoples:** dream and sign reading, as each describes it.
- **Ukrainian lands:** their own calendar of signs and premonitions.
- **Scientists, engineers and chess players:** the trained intuition of experts.
- **Generations:** Soviet scientific-atheist schooling against post-1991 openness to psychics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk omens and for literary exemplars; mixed for measured intuition; few local samples of decision-research scales.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Old Russian, Church Slavonic, Ukrainian; Tatar, Buryat.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "smekalka" as a national boast is a stereotype to test; Indigenous knowledge of land and animals may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
