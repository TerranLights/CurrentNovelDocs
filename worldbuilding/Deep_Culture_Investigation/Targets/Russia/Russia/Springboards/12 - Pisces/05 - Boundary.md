# Russia: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian; Tatar and Nenets for minority material. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former communal-apartment dwellers against those raised in separate flats.**
- **Villages against cities:** open courtyards and kin visits against locked steel doors.
- **Dacha owners** against people without a plot.
- **Old Believers:** purity lines with the "worldly".
- **Muslim communities** (Tatars, Bashkirs): gender and food lines as each community describes them.
- **Nenets, Khanty, Komi and other northern peoples:** territorial and sacred boundaries.
- **Ukrainian lands:** the house and field boundary, and memory of collectivization erasing private plots.
- **Border towns** (Ivangorod; the line with Ukraine) and **closed-city residents.**
- **Generations:** grandparents raising grandchildren against young couples who want separate homes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich ethnography on the house and on communal living, few surveys on privacy as such.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Nenets, Khanty and Tatar as needed.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state borders and the line with Ukraine are acutely political; closed cities and border zones restrict entry; Indigenous sacred sites hold restricted knowledge; property and residence rules belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
