# Russia: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian; Church Slavonic and Old Russian; Tatar for minority material. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death); release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Churched Orthodox and Old Believers:** smirenie as practiced, against Old Believer refusal.
- **Muslim communities** (Tatars, Bashkirs): trust in God's decree.
- **Siberian Tatars and other conquered peoples:** memory of defeat and accommodation.
- **Veterans' and prisoners' families:** captivity as stigma or as endurance.
- **Ukrainian lands:** Доля, captives' laments, and present-day refusal to yield.
- **Gulag survivors' families:** the inner code of not yielding.
- **Men and women:** who may "drop their hands".
- **Hospice families in big cities against rural areas** with little palliative care.
- **Generations:** Soviet-era endurance against post-Soviet self-help language of "letting go" (отпустить ситуацию).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on religious and war material; thin on hospice and end-of-life practice outside big cities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Church Slavonic, Old Russian, Ukrainian; Tatar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "Russian fatalism" claim is a stereotype to test, not adopt; prisoners of war and surrender in the war from 2022 are acutely sensitive; euthanasia and advance directives touch law (Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.
