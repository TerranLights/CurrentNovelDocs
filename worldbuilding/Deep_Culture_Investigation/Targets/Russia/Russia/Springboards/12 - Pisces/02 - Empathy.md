# Russia: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian; Karelian and Tatar for minority material. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern Russians, Karelians and Veps:** lament traditions recorded late, where empathy is voiced for others.
- **Urban intelligentsia against working-class and rural speakers:** "разговор по душам" may mean different things.
- **Men and women:** who may weep with others, and where.
- **Ukrainian lands:** lament, literature and the post-2014 civic vocabulary.
- **Tatars, Bashkirs and other Muslim peoples:** religious framings of feeling for the other.
- **Sakha, Buryat and other Siberian peoples:** norms about naming another's feelings aloud, as each describes them.
- **Generations:** Soviet-schooled "responsiveness" against the post-2000 psychology of "empathy".
- **Neurodivergent people's own groups** in the large cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in folklore and literature, thinner for measured empathy outside school samples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Old Russian for pre-modern texts, Karelian and Veps for laments.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** empathy across the front line in the war from 2022 and dehumanizing wartime language are highly sensitive; sources are polarized and some outlets blocked.
- **Held for review:** none.
