# Russia: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian; Old Russian; Tatar and Sakha for minority material. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and the culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Generations:** those raised under the Terror, the Cold War, the 1990s and the 2000s each fear different things.
- **Big cities against small towns and villages:** crime and terror attacks against spirits and illness.
- **Women and men:** who may show fear.
- **Ukrainian lands:** fright illness, witches' places, and fear shaped by war since 2014.
- **Tatars and other Muslim peoples:** forest spirits, jinn and the fear of God.
- **Sakha, Evenki, Khanty and other northern peoples:** harmful spirits and dangerous places, as each shares them.
- **Labor migrants and ethnic minorities:** fear of the police and of hostility, against the majority's fear of outsiders.
- **Parents** of school-age children: online scares.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; long polling series, deep folklore, abundant literature.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Old Russian, Ukrainian; Tatar, Sakha.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Terror and its present memory work (Memorial was liquidated in 2021-2022); terror attacks of 1999-2004 and the war from 2022 are politically charged; fear of the state is hard to poll honestly; Indigenous spirit knowledge may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
