# Russia: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (including criminal argot); Ukrainian; Tatar for minority material. **Concept in one line** (named by the author): what the culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. **Law** is researched only in Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); this sheet takes the idea of wrongdoing and the wrongdoer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large cities against industrial and prison-camp towns** (where the camp culture shaped local speech and norms).
- **Generations:** those who lived the 1990s as young men, against those raised after.
- **Ukrainian lands:** the opryshky legend, Odesa's lore, and their own 1990s memory.
- **Roma communities:** stereotyped as criminal; their own voices first.
- **Labor migrants from Central Asia and the Caucasus:** the "migrant crime" belief, tested against data.
- **Muslim communities** (Tatars, Bashkirs): sin, shame and wrongdoing in religious terms.
- **Northern and Siberian Indigenous peoples:** how harm is spoken of and repaired, as each shares it.
- **Former prisoners and their families:** stigma and return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore, literature, film and organized-crime scholarship; official statistics are disputed; victimization data are recent and few.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, including criminal argot (феня); Ukrainian; Tatar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political prisoners and "crimes" defined by the state; the war from 2022; ethnic stereotyping of migrants and Roma; youth groups banned as extremist; all legal questions go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
