# Russia: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Ukrainian; Old Russian for pre-modern tales; Tatar and Sakha for minority material. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Industrial towns and depopulating villages against Moscow and St. Petersburg:** different escapes and different costs.
- **Men and women:** who drinks, who gambles, who reads or watches, who is blamed.
- **Old Believers and Orthodox monastics:** withdrawal from the world as a religious path, not a vice.
- **Muslim communities** (Tatars, Bashkirs, North Caucasus peoples where carried): abstinence norms and their practice.
- **Northern and Siberian Indigenous peoples** (Sakha, Evenki, Nenets, Khanty): alcohol harm and their own recovery movements.
- **Ukrainian lands:** the Chernobyl Zone subculture; different drinking and leisure customs by region.
- **Generations:** late-Soviet dacha, 1990s clubs, the gaming young.
- **Emigrants** against those who stayed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on alcohol and mortality; mixed on gaming and fandom; thin on gambling since casinos were confined to a few zones (2009).
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Old Russian; Sakha and Tatar for community material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drinking stereotypes (name variation, never a trait); coercive rehab; drug policy and emigration are charged; Indigenous alcohol harm from the peoples' own voices first.
- **Held for review:** none.
