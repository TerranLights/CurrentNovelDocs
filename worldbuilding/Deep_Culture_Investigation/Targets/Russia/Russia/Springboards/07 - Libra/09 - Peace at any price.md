## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mennonites, Doukhobors, Molokans and Tolstoyans:** historical pacifists, most of whom emigrated; trace descendants and records.
- **Baptists, Pentecostals and Jehovah's Witnesses:** today's objectors and their legal standing.
- **Orthodox, Greek Catholic and secular majorities:** meekness taught in church against civic norms.
- **Crimean Tatars:** a nonviolent national movement.
- **Soldiers' families:** silence and speaking up about the army.
- **Village and city women:** family silence about violence, by region and generation.
- **Small-business owners of the 1990s:** paying for protection.
- **Labor migrants and Roma:** paying officials to avoid trouble.
- **Older Soviet-born against post-2014 cohorts in Ukraine:** "so long as there's no war" against other priorities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pacifist-sect history, literature and survey items; mixed for domestic silence; current war polling is constrained.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Old East Slavic; Plautdietsch, German and Crimean Tatar for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the whole topic sits on the live war; concessions questions are politically explosive in both lands; Jehovah's Witnesses are banned in Russia; ANNA and similar groups carry "foreign agent" status; survivors' testimonies need consent and care.
- **Held for review:** none.
