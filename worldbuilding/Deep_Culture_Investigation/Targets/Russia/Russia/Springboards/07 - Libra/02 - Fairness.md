## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Russian villages with repartition against Ukrainian and Cossack hereditary farms:** different histories of dividing land.
- **Pomors and northern fishing and hunting crews:** share rules of their own.
- **Karelians, Vepsians and Komi:** northern Finno-Ugric sharing customs, likely thinly documented.
- **Old Believer communities:** strong internal rules of mutual aid and trade.
- **The Soviet privileged and the queue:** families with access against those without.
- **Labor migrants from Central Asia and the Caucasus:** fairness in pay and in dealings with officials, from the newcomer's side.
- **Roma communities in Ukraine and Russia:** their own rules of division and dispute, published by them where possible.
- **Kyiv and Moscow against the provinces; the young post-ЗНО and post-ЕГЭ cohorts against older ones.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet distribution and on survey items; mixed on folk division rules; thin on Finno-Ugric and Roma customs.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Karelian, Komi and Crimean Tatar for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anti-corruption organizations in Russia face "undesirable" designations; the doping story is politically charged; Roma topics need care against stereotype.
