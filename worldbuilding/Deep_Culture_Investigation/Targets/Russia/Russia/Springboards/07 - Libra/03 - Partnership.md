## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Russian peasant joint families against Ukrainian nuclear households:** a long-noted contrast in household form, to test.
- **Old Believers, including priestless branches:** some branches rejected or reshaped church marriage.
- **Greek Catholics of Galicia and Orthodox of the east and center:** different marriage rites and church rules.
- **Jews of the former Pale:** the matchmaker tradition and its afterlife.
- **Crimean Tatars:** Muslim marriage and family alliances, after deportation and return.
- **Nenets, Komi and Sami herders:** pairing in a camp economy.
- **Labor-migrant families of western Ukraine and the Russian provinces:** spouses apart for years.
- **Same-sex couples in big cities:** partnerships the law in both lands treats very differently.
- **Business founders of the 1990s:** partnerships made without trusted courts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on demography and family law; mixed on business partnership practice; thin on northern Indigenous pairing.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Old Novgorodian; Yiddish, Nenets and Crimean Tatar for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** same-sex partnership touches Russian laws on "propaganda" and "extremism"; Pereiaslav is politically charged in both lands; marriage-agency and "bride" industries need care against exploitation and stereotype.
- **Held for review:** none.
