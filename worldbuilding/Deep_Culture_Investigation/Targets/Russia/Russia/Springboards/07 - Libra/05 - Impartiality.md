## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Capitals against small towns:** where everyone knows the judge.
- **Regional political networks of Ukraine (the Donetsk and Dnipropetrovsk groupings of the 1990s-2000s):** loyalty against neutral office, to be traced in sources rather than assumed.
- **Soviet nomenklatura families and their heirs:** inherited access.
- **Old Believer merchant networks:** trust inside the faith against outsiders.
- **Jewish communities of the former Pale:** the historical kahal and community courts.
- **Crimean Tatars:** a people whose standing before officials changed with deportation and return.
- **Labor migrants and Roma:** facing officials as outsiders.
- **Russian North and Karelia against Moscow:** distance from the center, small-town office-holding.
- **Generations:** those who grew up with *blat* against the e-declaration generation in Ukraine.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on corruption surveys and investigative records; mixed on court impartiality studies; thin on minority community courts.
