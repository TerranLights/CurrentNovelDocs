## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern and Southern Russians, Pomors, Old Believers:** distinct wedding cycles and village self-rule.
- **Ukrainian regions** (Galicia, Volhynia, Podillia, Polissia, Sloboda Ukraine, the Zaporozhian south, Donbas, Transcarpathia, Bukovina): different legal histories (Polish-Lithuanian, Habsburg, Russian imperial) and rites.
- **Hutsuls, Boykos, Lemkos, Rusyns:** Carpathian customs.
- **Crimean Tatars, Karaites, Krymchaks:** Muslim and Turkic-Jewish family law and wedding ways.
- **Karelians, Vepsians, Komi, Sami, Nenets:** Finno-Ugric and Arctic bonds, bride price and dispute customs.
- **Jews of the former Pale (Ukraine), Russian Germans, Greeks of the Azov coast, Bulgarians and Gagauz of the Odesa region:** community courts and marriage law of their own.
- **City against village; Soviet-educated against post-1991 generations.**
