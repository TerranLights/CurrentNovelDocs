## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern Russian villages (Karelia, Arkhangelsk, Vologda) against central black-earth villages:** labor exchange and feasting rules.
- **Ukrainian villages and their толока:** regional differences across Polissia, Podillia and the steppe.
- **Karelians and Vepsians:** Finnic customs of collective work.
- **Hutsuls and Boykos:** mountain hospitality and the debt it creates.
- **Crimean Tatars:** hospitality customs and their return, as they describe them.
- **Jewish communities:** organized mutual aid.
- **Old Believers:** mutual help within the faith, limits toward outsiders.
- **Labor-migrant families:** remittance obligations.
- **Urban professionals and pensioners:** gifts to doctors and teachers, and the generational change in "thank you" gifts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for village ethnography and household surveys; mixed for gifts to professionals (gray zone); thin for Finnic and Crimean Tatar exchange in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian, Old East Slavic; Karelian, Vepsian, Yiddish, Crimean Tatar for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the line between gift and bribe touches the law; remittance flows between the two lands changed with the war; avoid casting either land as a "gift culture" or a "favor culture" in one voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
