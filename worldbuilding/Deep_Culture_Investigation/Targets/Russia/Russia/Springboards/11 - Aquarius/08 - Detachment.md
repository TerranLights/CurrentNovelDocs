## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Monastic and lay Orthodox** practice, with Greek Catholics in western Ukraine.
- **Men and women,** on what may be shown; elders raised in wartime against the young.
- **Medical, military and police professionals,** trained to keep distance.
- **Big-city anonymity** in Moscow, St. Petersburg and Kyiv against village familiarity.
- **Crimean Tatars,** with Muslim teaching on patience; Jewish and Roma communities.
- **Kola Sami, Nenets and Karelians,** where outsiders' notes of "reserve" need the people's own framing.
- **Since 2022:** those who look away, those who emigrated inwardly or abroad, and those who engage.
