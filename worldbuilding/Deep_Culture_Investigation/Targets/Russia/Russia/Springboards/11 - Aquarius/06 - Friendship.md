## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in language studies, literature and Soviet sociology; mixed in present survey data; thin on minority-language friendship terms.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Old Russian for the epics; Crimean Tatar and Karelian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** friendships broken by the war and politics are painful; record people's own accounts. *Druzhba narodov* doctrine is official ideology; read as such. Cronyism (*kumivstvo*) data is reported in politics; keep it apart from village godparent customs.
- **Held for review:** none.
