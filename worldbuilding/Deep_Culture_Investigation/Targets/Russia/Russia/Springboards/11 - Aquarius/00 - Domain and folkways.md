*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moscow and St. Petersburg against small towns and villages:** apartment life against the *dvor* and village street.
- **The Russian North and Old Believers:** Pomor fishing artels, *bespopovtsy* congregations without priests.
- **Ukrainian regions:** Galicia (Habsburg-era associations), central Dnipro lands (Cossack and *hromada* heritage), the industrial east, the steppe south and Odesa.
- **Crimean Tatars:** community rebuilt after the 1944 deportation and return.
- **Karelians, Veps, Komi, Kola Sami, Nenets:** village, *siida* and herding-camp ties.
- **Rusyns, Hutsuls, Boikos; Bulgarians and Gagauz of Budjak; Azov Greeks; Hungarians and Romanians on the western border.**
- **Jewish, Karaite, Krymchak and Roma communities;** Mennonite and German colony histories in the south.
- **Labor migrants** in big cities, with their own diaspora associations.
