## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Intelligentsia families** in Moscow, St. Petersburg, Kyiv, Kharkiv and Odesa against working-class and village cohorts.
- **Old Believers,** with their own utopian legends.
- **Soviet-educated idealists, the disillusioned 1990s cohort, and post-2000 volunteers.**
- **Ukrainian volunteers since 2014 and 2022** against Russian volunteer movements.
- **Crimean Tatars,** whose return movement was built on ideals held across exile.
- **Jewish communities, Doukhobor and Molokan heritage in the south, Tolstoyan descendants.**
- **Karelian, Sami and Nenets** teachings on right living, in their own voices where recorded.

## 4. Flags
