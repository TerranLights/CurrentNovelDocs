# Russia (core state): Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Russia (core state), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian and Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar, Nenets, Komi and Karelian for the minority layers; Church Slavonic and Old Russian for the oldest records. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life, recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Megacity apartment blocks against old-town wooden quarters** and garden-plot settlements.
- **Dying villages of the central and northwestern oblasts** against suburban growth belts around Moscow and Kyiv.
- **Old Believer villages and northern Pomor settlements,** with their own congregational life.
- **Ukrainian village quarters and farmstead hamlets** in Polissia, Podillia, Slobozhanshchyna, the Carpathians and the steppe.
- **Crimean Tatar neighborhoods** built on returned land since the late 1980s.
- **Nenets herding camps, Sami villages of the Kola, Komi and Veps villages.**
- **Internally displaced people** from Donbas and Crimea since 2014 and since 2022, and the towns that took them in.
- **Monastery and parish communities;** Protestant, Catholic and Jewish congregations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the commune, communal flat and dacha; mixed on present neighbor surveys; thin on Indigenous communities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Ukrainian; Crimean Tatar; Nenets and Komi for local voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war displacement and destroyed towns make "loss of community" a live wound; record people's own framing. The Anastasia movement is contested; describe it neutrally.
- **Held for review:** none.
