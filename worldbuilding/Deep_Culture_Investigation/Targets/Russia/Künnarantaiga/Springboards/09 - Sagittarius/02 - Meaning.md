# Künnarantaiga: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Sakha, Buryat, Chukchi and Nanai (through their literatures), Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Angara and Bratsk resettled families** (Irkutsk): meaning after the flooding of home villages.
- **Sakha villagers and Yakutsk professionals:** kinship, cattle, horses and land against careers and city life.
- **Eveny and Evenki herders and their schooled children:** whose purpose follows the herd and whose does not.
- **Chukchi and Yupik sea-mammal hunters:** meaning in the hunt and the coast, as they state it.
- **Nanai, Ulchi and Nivkh fishing villages on the Amur and Sakhalin.**
- **Kolyma and Chukotka newcomers and their children:** people who came for money, romance or by force, and stayed or left.
- **Naval Vladivostok; BAM towns (Tynda, Severobaykalsk); Birobidzhan; Sakhalin Koreans; Central Asian labor migrants.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in literature (village prose, Sakha classics, Gulag memoirs); thin in measured meaning for these units.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Sakha, Buryat; Chukchi and Nanai works mostly via Russian versions; Yiddish for Birobidzhan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data among Indigenous youth needs care in framing and community voice; recent war service and its meaning for families in Buryatia and Zabaykalsky is politically sensitive and risky for informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
