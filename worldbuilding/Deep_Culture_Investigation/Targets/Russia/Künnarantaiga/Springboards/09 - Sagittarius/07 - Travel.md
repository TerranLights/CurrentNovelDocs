# Künnarantaiga: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki and Even; Chukchi and Siberian Yupik; Japanese; Korean; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding peoples** (Evens, Evenki, Chukchi, Koryak, Anabar Dolgans): mobility as home.
- **Coastal Chukchi and Yupik:** cross-strait kin travel.
- **Sakha and Buryat villagers:** leaving for Yakutsk, Ulan-Ude, Irkutsk, Moscow; and return.
- **Old-settler Russians and Cossack descendants** against Soviet-era arrivals who now leave "for the mainland".
- **Shift workers** in the Mirny and Aldan diamond and gold fields, Magadan and Chukotka mines.
- **Vladivostok and Sakhalin:** East Asian travelers in and out; Chinese and Korean traders.
- **Birobidzhan's emigrants; Sakhalin Koreans who left for South Korea; former Japanese residents.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exploration and resettlement history; good migration statistics; thin for herders' own travel accounts in print.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Japanese for Kōdayū and the Kuril visits; Korean; Chinese; Chukchi and Yupik via specialists.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** border zones (Chukotka, the Kurils) limit access for researchers; the Japan exchanges and cross-strait links were suspended after 2022; some sacred routes and places are not for visitors.
- **Held for review:** none.
