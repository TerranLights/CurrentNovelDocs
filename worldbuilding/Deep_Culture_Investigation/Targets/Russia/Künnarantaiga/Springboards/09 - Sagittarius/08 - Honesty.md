## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fishing towns** (Kamchatka, Sakhalin, Kurils, Primorye coast): honesty toward the state against honesty within the crew.
- **Mining and gold towns** (Magadan, Chukotka, Aldan, Bodaybo): gold theft, company rules and miners' codes.
- **Border trade towns** (Blagoveshchensk, Pogranichny, Zabaykalsk) and Vladivostok's car market.
- **Sakha and Buryat villages:** kin trust against dealings with officials and outsiders.
- **Taiga hunters** (Evenki, Udege, Russian *promyslovik* hunters).
- **Kolyma towns** where camp speech persists.
- **Central Asian and Chinese workers:** honesty owed to migrants by employers, and the reverse.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on corruption nationally; thin and anecdotal by unit; much of the border-trade and poaching material is journalism.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, including camp and criminal slang; Sakha and Buryat; Chinese for the border trade.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and smuggling topics endanger informants; avoid any claim that a people is honest or dishonest. Bribery as law goes to Libra; legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
