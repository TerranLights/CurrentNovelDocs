## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** central uluses, the Vilyuy, the Arctic north; Orthodox, Aiyy-revival and unaffiliated Sakha.
- **Buryats:** Irkutsk (western) against Khori and Aga (eastern) communities, where Buddhist and shamanic weight is said to differ (a question to test).
- **Evenki and Evens,** spread over Sakha, Amur, Khabarovsk, Zabaykalsky, Buryatia, Magadan and Kamchatka: one people, many settings.
- **Northeast peoples:** Chukchi, Siberian Yupik, Koryak, Itelmen, Chuvan, Yukaghir, Aleuts of the Commander Islands.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples:** Nanai, Ulchi, Udege, Oroch, Negidal, Nivkh, Uilta; the Ainu layer.
- **Smaller peoples:** Tofalars (Irkutsk), Soyots (Buryatia), Hamnigans, Anabar Dolgans.
- **Russian old-settlers:** Lena settlers, Russkoye Ustye, Markovo, Kamchadals; Cossack hosts of Amur, Ussuri and Transbaikal; Semeiskie; Ukrainians of the Zeleny Klin.
- **Later layers:** Birobidzhan Jews; Sakhalin Koreans; Chinese; Tatars; Central Asian migrants; Polish exiles' descendants; Kolyma's Gulag-founded towns; shift workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for Orthodox missions, Buryat Buddhism and Sakha revival; thin for the small peoples' present practice. The tracker lists only "Russia" and Stage 0 has not been written, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; these sheets are written for the twelve core units from Baikal to the Bering Strait.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat (Cyrillic, and the old Mongolian script for pre-1930s texts); Classical Tibetan for datsan texts; Yiddish; Japanese for Karafuto-era Sakhalin; Korean; Chinese; Indigenous languages through local scholars.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic knowledge and sacred sites with access rules (Olkhon, Barkhan-Uula and others) as communities state them; groups listed as "extremist" (the 2017 Jehovah's Witnesses ban) put informants at risk; "foreign agent" labels on pollsters and on Memorial; Gulag memory.
- **Held for review:** classical Buddhist treatises of South Asian origin studied in the datsans (category only).
