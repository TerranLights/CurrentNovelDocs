## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers:** Semeiskie in Transbaikal; returnees in Primorye.
- **Buryat Buddhists and lamas:** monastic steadfastness then and now.
- **Sakha intelligentsia:** the 1920s-30s generation and today's activists.
- **Descendants of Polish exiles; Decembrist and narodnik memory in Irkutsk and Yakutsk.**
- **Baptist and Pentecostal congregations,** some with Soviet histories of refusal.
- **Environmental activists** in Irkutsk, Sakhalin and Kamchatka; **Indigenous land activists** among Udege (Bikin) and Evenki.
- **Birobidzhan;** **Kolyma towns** founded by the camps.
