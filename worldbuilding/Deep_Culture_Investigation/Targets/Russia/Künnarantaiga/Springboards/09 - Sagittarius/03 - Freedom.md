# Künnarantaiga: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Korean (Sakhalin); Yiddish; Chukchi and Even through local scholars. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of exiles and convicts** (Irkutsk, Nerchinsk, Sakhalin): freedom as release.
- **Cossack hosts** (Amur, Ussuri, Transbaikal): *vol'nitsa* and service at once.
- **Semeiskie Old Believers:** freedom of worship after forced resettlement.
- **Sakha and Buryats:** freedom as a people's sovereignty; compare the republics with Aga and Ust-Orda, which lost their own districts.
- **Chukchi, Koryak and Evens:** freedom of movement for herding and hunting, and border-zone limits in Chukotka.
- **Sakhalin Koreans; Birobidzhan Jews; Chinese traders; Central Asian migrants:** freedom of movement and residence.
- **Vladivostok's car and port culture against inland towns; Kolyma's Gulag-founded towns.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exile and political history; recent protest material is plentiful but partisan and hard to verify.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for national movements; Korean for Sakhalin; Yiddish for early Birobidzhan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** opposition and protest topics carry legal risk for informants; Memorial was liquidated (2021-2022) and its databases sit on mirrors; sovereignty claims are politically charged. No law research here: legal texts go to Libra, legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
