*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats of Aga and Khori against Irkutsk Buryats:** written chronicles and monastic learning against oral shamanic authority.
- **Sakha:** Sakha-language media audiences against Russian-language media audiences.
- **Chukchi, Yupik and Koryak hunters:** experience-based truth about ice, animals and weather.
- **Nanai, Ulchi and Nivkh communities:** older tellers as keepers of true accounts.
- **Sakhalin and Kuril residents:** Russian, Korean and Ainu accounts of the Japanese period and 1945.
- **Gulag survivors' descendants in Kolyma and Irkutsk:** truth restored or still hidden.
- **Scientists' towns (Irkutsk Akademgorodok, Yakutsk science center) against villages.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for press history and Buryat chronicles; thin and politically shaped for current trust in news.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat, with old Mongolian script; Japanese and Korean for Sakhalin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** truth about repression, war and current politics carries risk for informants; border history with Japan and China is officially contested; some shamanic and clan knowledge is not for outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
