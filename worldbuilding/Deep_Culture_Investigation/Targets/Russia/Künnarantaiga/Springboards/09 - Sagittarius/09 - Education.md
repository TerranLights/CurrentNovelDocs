## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** Sakha-medium and Russian-medium schools; village pupils against Yakutsk lyceums.
- **Buryats:** monastic learning against secular universities; Aga and Irkutsk Buryats.
- **Evens, Evenki, Chukchi, Koryak, Nanai, Nivkh:** boarding-school generations against nomadic-school pupils.
- **Russian old-settlers and Cossack villages** against university towns (Irkutsk, Vladivostok, Khabarovsk).
- **Sakhalin Koreans:** the closing of Korean-language schools and later revival.
- **Birobidzhan:** Yiddish schooling then and now.
- **Naval and military families** in Primorye and Kamchatka; **mining towns** with technical colleges.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institution histories and attainment statistics; mixed for Indigenous experiences of schooling.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Classical Tibetan for monastic curricula; Yiddish; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school memories can be painful and should be gathered through the communities; language policy in schools is politically charged. Early schooling and learning as such belong to Gemini.
- **Held for review:** classical Buddhist treatises of South Asian origin taught in datsan curricula (category only).
