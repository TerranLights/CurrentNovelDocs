# Künnarantaiga: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian, Sakha, Buryat; Evenki and the Northeast languages through local scholars; Yiddish; Korean. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aga and Khori Buryats against Ust-Orda (Ekhirit-Bulagat) Buryats:** Buddhist centers against communities known for shamanic revival; test, do not assume.
- **Yakutsk against Sakha villages:** Orthodox, Aiyy and unaffiliated faith may divide by town and countryside.
- **Eveny and Evenki herding brigades:** faith in remote camps, with and without missions.
- **Chukchi and Siberian Yupik coastal villages:** evangelical and Orthodox converts beside older practice.
- **Semeiskie and other Old Believers:** priestless faith against official Orthodoxy.
- **Polish-descended Catholics; Birobidzhan's small Jewish community; Sakhalin Koreans; Central Asian Muslims in Yakutsk, Irkutsk and Vladivostok.**
- **Kolyma and BAM towns:** places founded on secular faith (or coercion) rather than old belief.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for Buryat Buddhism and Orthodox missions; thinner for small peoples' faith today and for secular faith at the regional level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Sakha, Buryat; Classical Tibetan for liturgy; Yiddish and Korean for minority congregations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic practice and sacred knowledge as communities set the limits; the 2017 ban on Jehovah's Witnesses and pressure on some Protestant groups put believers at risk if named; distinguish faith from law (no law research here; see Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.
