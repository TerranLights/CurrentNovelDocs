## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** Aiyy-faith practitioners, Orthodox Sakha, unaffiliated; Yakutsk against ulus villages.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist east (Ivolginsk, Aga, Kizhinga) against shamanic west (Ust-Orda, Olkhon, Tunka); mixed families.
- **Evenks and Evens:** shamanic revival and Christian converts in remote settlements.
- **Northeast** (Chukchi, Koryak, Itelmen, Yupik): sea and herd festivals.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples** (Nanai, Ulchi, Nivkh, Udege): bear and river rites, as shared.
- **Settler Russians:** Orthodox, Old Believers, Molokans, Baptists, new movements.
- **Jewish AO and Sakhalin Koreans:** diaspora revival.
- **Cities against villages; generations** (Soviet atheist schooling against post-1991 seekers).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Sakha and Buryat revival; mixed for settler Russian popular piety; thin for Negidal, Oroch and Tofalars.
