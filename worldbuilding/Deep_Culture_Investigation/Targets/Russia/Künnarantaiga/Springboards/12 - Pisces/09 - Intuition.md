## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hunters and herders** (Evenks, Evens, Udege, Chukchi, Koryak): intuition as craft.
- **Sea hunters** (Yupik, coastal Chukchi, Koryak, Itelmen): ice and weather sense.
- **Sakha:** dream-telling in villages against Yakutsk's psychic trade.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist divination and shamanic signs; urban clients of both.
- **Settler villages** (Cossack, Old Believer, Ukrainian-rooted): Christmastide divination, omens.
- **Prospectors, miners, geologists, sailors and pilots:** professional hunches.
- **Sakhalin Koreans:** reading the room across languages.
- **Women and men separately:** "women's intuition" talk against hunters' and prospectors' instinct.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hunters' and herders' knowledge; mixed for divination (many studies, uneven by people); thin for measured intuition by region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki; Yupik through bilingual projects; German for 18th-c. sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamans' and lamas' divination (respect limits on what may be shared); TV portrayals of shamans (exoticism); prospecting areas (security-sensitive in some mines); divination is studied as a practice people use, never as a source.
- **Held for review:** none.
