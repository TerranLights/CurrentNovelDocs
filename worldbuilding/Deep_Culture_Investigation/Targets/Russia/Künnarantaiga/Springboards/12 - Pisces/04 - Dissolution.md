# Künnarantaiga: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryat Buddhists** (Ivolginsk, Aga, Tunka) against shamanic practitioners of the west.
- **Sakha:** urban Yakutsk festival crowds against ulus villages.
- **Evens, Evenks, Koryak, Chukchi:** herders after the state farms broke up.
- **Small Amur and Sakhalin peoples** (Udege, Oroch, Negidal, Nivkh, Uilta): language loss.
- **Kamchadals and other mixed old-settler groups:** fusion as a long process.
- **Kolyma and Chukotka towns:** those who left for the "mainland" and those who stayed.
- **Jewish AO, Sakhalin Koreans, Ainu heritage:** diaspora, return and fading.
- **Old Believers:** those who stayed and those who emigrated.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the post-Soviet collapse of northern towns and for language loss; mixed for ecstatic and trance experience (mostly older ethnography); thin for dementia and care.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Tibetan terms; Japanese; Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic trance and funeral customs (ask communities); Itigilov's body as a sacred object; fly-agaric use (present practice may be private); forced resettlement and boarding-school memory.
