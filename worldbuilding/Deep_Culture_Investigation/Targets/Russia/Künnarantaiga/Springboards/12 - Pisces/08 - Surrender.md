## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox settlers** and **Old Believers:** смирение taught against stubborn holding on.
- **Chukchi and Koryak:** memory of resistance to conquest.
- **Sakha:** fate decreed by the Aiyy against personal striving.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist letting go at death; shamanic families' duty to spirits.
- **Nanai, Ulchi, Udege:** the shaman's call.
- **Kolyma, Magadan and BAM-area families:** camp memory of giving up or enduring.
- **Sakhalin and the Kurils:** Russian and Japanese memories of 1945.
- **Patients and families:** hospice users in cities against rural districts without services.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war, conquest and camp memory; mixed for recovery and end-of-life care by unit; thin on present-day fate beliefs by region.
