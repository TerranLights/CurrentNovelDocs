## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kolyma, Chukotka and northern Sakha:** long winters, the "mainland" dream, seasonal workers.
- **Port cities** (Vladivostok, Nakhodka, Petropavlovsk): sailors and fishermen, shore leave and its customs.
- **Sakha and Buryat villages:** sober-village campaigns and their critics.
- **Chukchi, Koryak, Evens and other northern peoples:** alcohol, and their own recovery and revival movements.
- **Amur and Primorye farm districts:** wild hemp and rural boredom.
- **Old Believers:** abstinence and withdrawal as religious life.
- **Buddhist and Orthodox practice:** retreat and pilgrimage as approved escape.
- **Generations:** Soviet-era drinking against young people's screens, gyms and foreign travel.
- **Men and women separately:** the hunt, the garage and the binge against other escapes.
