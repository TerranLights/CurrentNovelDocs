# Künnarantaiga: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with thieves' argot); Sakha; Buryat; Japanese and Chinese for cross-border smuggling. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. The law itself is researched in Libra and Capricorn, and only cited here.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Port and border cities** (Vladivostok, Nakhodka, Khabarovsk, Blagoveshchensk): smuggling, gangs of the 1990s.
- **Kolyma and Magadan:** camp-world codes and their afterlife.
- **Gold districts** (Bodaibo, Aldan, Chukotka): illegal digging and its tolerance.
- **Coastal fishing towns** (Kamchatka, Sakhalin, Kurils): crab and salmon poaching.
- **Taiga logging districts** (Primorye, Khabarovsk, Amur, Zabaykalsky): timber trade to China.
- **Sakha and Buryat communities:** their own words for wrong and retribution.
- **Indigenous hunters and fishers:** subsistence rights against "poaching" labels.
- **Generations:** 1990s youths against those raised after 2000.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exile and Gulag crime culture; mixed for present-day crime perception by unit; thin for Indigenous peoples' own ideas of wrong, outside Sakha.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, with argot; Sakha; Buryat; Japanese; Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current political prosecutions (out of scope here; never label them crime); police abuse topics; organized crime (source safety); ethnic stereotyping of Chinese, Central Asian or Caucasian migrants (record claims, never adopt); law and punishment as written go to Libra and Capricorn.
