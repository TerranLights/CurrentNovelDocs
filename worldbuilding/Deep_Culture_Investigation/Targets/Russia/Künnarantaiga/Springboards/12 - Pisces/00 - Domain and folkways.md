## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** central alaas, Vilyui, Arctic uluses, Yakutsk.
- **Buryats:** western Ekhirit-Bulagat (Irkutsk, Ust-Orda; shamanic ties) against eastern Khori and Aga (Buddhist ties); Soyots; Tofalars.
- **Tungusic peoples:** Evenks, Evens, Dolgans, Nanai, Ulchi, Udege, Oroch, Negidal, Uilta (separate healing traditions).
- **Northeast:** Chukchi (reindeer and coastal), Siberian Yupik, Koryak, Itelmen, Kamchadals, Yukaghirs, Chuvans, Commander Aleuts.
- **Lower Amur and Sakhalin:** Nivkh; Ainu heritage; Sakhalin Koreans; Japanese-era Karafuto memory.
- **Settler Russians:** Semeiskie Old Believers; Cossack descendants; Molokans; Ukrainian-rooted Primorye villages; Gulag-descended Kolyma families; BAM towns.
- **Jewish AO:** Jewish and Yiddish heritage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for shamanism, the Gulag and Sakhalin's penal colony; mixed for regional mental health; thin for Negidal, Oroch, Chuvans, Tofalars.
- **Lands:** the tracker header names only "Russia"; these sheets work from the 12 units in the territory reference, and the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Yiddish; Korean; Japanese (Karafuto); Indigenous languages mostly via bilingual editions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamans' and clans' restricted knowledge, and sacred sites such as Olkhon's Shaman Rock (ask communities first); Soviet boarding schools (интернаты) for Indigenous children, in survivors' voices first; Gulag memory; "foreign agent" and "undesirable" labels on pollsters and NGOs (Levada, Memorial); "Arctic hysteria" as a colonial label.
- **Held for review:** none.
