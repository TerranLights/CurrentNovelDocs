# Künnarantaiga: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga (Күннарантайга), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki; Chukchi; Yiddish; Korean. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Locals born in the North and newcomers from "the mainland"**, in Magadan, Chukotka, Kamchatka and Sakhalin.
- **Sakha and Russians in Yakutsk**, where both are large.
- **Buryats** in Buryatia, Aga and Ust-Orda, and their ties with Mongolia and China.
- **Kamchadals and other mixed old-settler groups** whose Native status is argued.
- **Small peoples recognized and not**: Kereks, Chuvans, Taz, Soyots, Tofalars.
- **Sakhalin Koreans and Koryo-saram**, two Korean histories in one region.
- **Jews and non-Jews** in the Jewish Autonomous Oblast.
- **Central Asian and Chinese migrants** in Vladivostok, Khabarovsk and Irkutsk.
- **Old Believer returnees** and **Cossack revival** groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buryat, Sakha, Chukotka and Birobidzhan scholarship; mixed for regional identity polls; thin for Central Asian migrants' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Korean; Yiddish for older settler sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regionalism and Native rights are politically charged in present-day Russia; some Native organizations have faced official pressure. Community and group belong to Aquarius; identity to Aries; citizenship law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
