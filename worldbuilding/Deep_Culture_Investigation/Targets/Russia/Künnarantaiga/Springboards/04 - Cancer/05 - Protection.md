## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack descendants and revival groups** in Zabaykalsky, Amur and Primorye.
- **Hunting and taiga villages** (Evenks, Udege, Nanai, Russian old-settlers), where bears, fire and isolation shape guarding.
- **Coastal and island towns** of Kamchatka, the Kurils and Sakhalin, exposed to tsunamis and volcanoes.
- **Buryats**, Buddhist and shamanic, with different protectors.
- **Sakha**, with their own spirit and soul beliefs.
- **Old Believers**, with Orthodox-rooted charms and prayers of their own.
- **Military and naval families** in Vladivostok and Kamchatka.
- **Women in large cities** against small settlements, for protection from violence at home.
