# Künnarantaiga: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga (Күннарантайга), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi and Koryak (through Russian and English sources); Korean for Sakhalin. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Reindeer herders** (Chukchi, Koryaks, Evens, Evenks), whose tents and camps are shelter in motion.
- **Taiga hunters and trappers** (Russian old-settlers, Evenks, Udege, Nanai) who share open cabins.
- **Sakha villagers** along the Lena, exposed to spring ice floods.
- **BAM and mining towns**, and those still in trailers and barracks.
- **Gulag descendants and former exiles** in Magadan and Kolyma.
- **Homeless people in Irkutsk, Khabarovsk and Vladivostok**, and the Church and city groups that serve them.
- **Central Asian and Chinese labor migrants** in dormitories and shared flats.
- **Kuril and Kamchatka coastal residents** facing tsunami and volcano evacuations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dwelling ethnography and disaster coverage; mixed for homelessness, which is undercounted; thin for small peoples' own rules on taking in travelers.
- **Languages needed:** Russian above all; Sakha and Buryat; Chukchi and Koryak through bilingual editions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Gulag history is politically fraught, and the Memorial society that held much of it was closed in 2021; shelter addresses for women must not be published. Hospitality has no home sign; compassion and charity point to Pisces, housing as property to Taurus.
- **Held for review:** none.
