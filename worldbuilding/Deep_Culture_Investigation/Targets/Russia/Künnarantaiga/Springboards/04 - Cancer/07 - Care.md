*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Sakha, Buryat and Evenki villages**, where kin may carry most care.
- **Russian city families** in Irkutsk, Khabarovsk and Vladivostok, with more access to paid care and homes.
- **Herding camps** in Chukotka, Kamchatka and northern Sakha, far from clinics.
- **Buddhist Buryats**, with lama-physicians alongside state medicine.
- **Sakha and Evenki healers' clients**, who may use healers and doctors together.
- **Old Believer communities**, with church-centered care to check.
- **Older people whose children left for European Russia** (common after 1991), against those with kin nearby.
- **Central Asian women working as carers** in the cities.
- **Korean and Jewish families**, whose care duties are to be asked, not assumed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Rosstat and ministry figures; mixed for family care, which surveys may under-sample in these regions; thin for small peoples' own accounts of caring for elders.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Tibetan through Russian editions; Evenki through bilingual texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healing rites may be private; care-home conditions and COVID-19 deaths are touchy. Health and service point to Virgo, compassion to Pisces, duty to Capricorn, dying and inheritance to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
