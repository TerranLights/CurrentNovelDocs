*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** (Evens, Evenks, Chukchi, Koryaks, Dolgans of Anabar) choosing between camp life, nomadic schools and boarding schools.
- **Rural Sakha** in the central and Vilyuy districts against Yakutsk.
- **Buryat families**, Buddhist-leaning and shamanic-leaning, rural and urban.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples** (Nanai, Ulchi, Udege, Nivkh) where grandparents may hold the language.
- **Semeiskie and other Old Believers**; **Cossack revival** families.
- **Russian city families** in Vladivostok, Khabarovsk and Irkutsk; **military families** in Kamchatka.
- **Children raised by grandparents** while parents work on rotation or move away.
- **Korean and Jewish families**, with their own home-teaching traditions to check, not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet and post-Soviet pedagogy and folklore editions; mixed for current practice; thin for small peoples' own present-day voices.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Evenki and Chukchi through bilingual editions; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding schools and taking children from herder families are painful histories; children's data is protected. Schooling points to Gemini and Sagittarius, discipline to Capricorn, play to Leo.
- **Held for review:** none.
