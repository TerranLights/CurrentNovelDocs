# Künnarantaiga: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga (Күннарантайга), region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki and Even; Chukchi (through Russian, English and French sources); Yiddish; Korean. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture (the mother of the nation, the holy mother, Mother Earth).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha mothers**, rural and in Yakutsk.
- **Buryat mothers**, Buddhist-leaning and shamanic-leaning.
- **Herding mothers** among Evens, Evenks, Chukchi and Koryaks, and those who move to villages for children's schooling.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples' mothers** (Nanai, Ulchi, Nivkh, Udege), in clan-based families.
- **Old Believer mothers**, often of many children.
- **Russian city mothers**, married and single, in Vladivostok, Khabarovsk and Irkutsk.
- **Military wives and soldiers' mothers** in garrison towns.
- **Sakhalin Korean, Jewish and Central Asian migrant mothers**, each with their own ideals to ask about.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for fertility statistics and state honors; mixed for regional mothers' own voices; thin for small peoples' present-day birth customs.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Evenki and Even via dictionaries; French for some Chukotka work; Korean and Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some birth rites are women's knowledge and private; soldiers' mothers' groups face official pressure; abortion is Aries (autonomy). Fatherhood belongs in the domain sheet; marriage and gender to Libra; birth and sex to Scorpio; self-sacrifice to Pisces.
