# Künnarantaiga: Communication and learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga (Күннарантайга), region Russia: Sakha, Amur, Buryatia, Zabaykalsky, Chukotka, Kamchatka, Khabarovsk, Magadan, Primorye, Sakhalin with the Kurils, the Jewish Autonomous Oblast and Irkutsk. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; the Indigenous languages of the North and the Amur (Evenki, Even, Chukchi, Koryak, Nanai, Nivkh and others); Yiddish and Korean for heritage communities. **Concept in one line:** how people talk, learn and connect close to home: speech ways, learning ways, neighbors and short journeys.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian old-timers** (Kolyma, Russkoye Ustye, Markovo, the Lena) against **Soviet-era incomers** (BAM builders, Magadan mining towns): different speech histories.
- **Sakha** of the central, Vilyuy and northern districts; Yakutsk against villages.
- **Buryats**, western (Irkutsk, Ust-Orda) against eastern (Khori, Aga); Buddhist and shamanist families.
- **Evenki and Evens**, spread over many units; **Dolgans** of Anabar; **Yukaghirs** of the Kolyma.
- **Chukchi, Siberian Yupik, Koryaks, Itelmens and Kamchadals; Aleuts** of the Commanders.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples**: Nanai, Ulch, Udege, Negidal, Oroch, Nivkh, Uilta; Ainu heritage.
- **Soyots** (Buryatia) and **Tofalars** (Irkutsk).
- **Semeiskie** and **Cossack descendants** (Transbaikal, Amur, Ussuri); **Ukrainian-heritage** villages of Primorye.
- **Jewish-heritage** families of the JAO; **Sakhalin Koreans**; Chinese and Central Asian migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Russian, Sakha and Buryat; mixed for Evenki, Chukchi and the Amur peoples; thin for Kerek, Negidal, Uilta and other small groups, mostly recorded by outsiders.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat (classical Mongolian script for older texts); Yiddish and Korean for the heritage press; Japanese for some Ainu and Sakhalin work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamans' speech, blessings and some stories are restricted knowledge; ask first. Boarding-school memories, Kolyma's Gulag past and the Kurils' status need care. Language-status statutes go to Libra.
- **Scope:** the tracker header names only "Russia" and no scope file exists yet; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled at Stage 0. These sheets are written for the twelve units listed above.
- **Held for review:** none.
