# Künnarantaiga: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Korean; Yiddish; Chinese for rumors along the Amur. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small settlements and villages** ("everyone knows everyone") against **Vladivostok, Khabarovsk and Irkutsk**.
- **Former camp and mining towns** of Kolyma and Magadan, where informing has a long memory.
- **Sakha villages** and **Yakutsk**.
- **Buddhist and shamanist Buryats**: teachings on speech.
- **Mixed Indigenous-Russian villages** of Chukotka, Kamchatka and the Amur.
- **Semeiskie Old Believers**: talk as a check on observance.
- **Border towns** in Primorye, Amur and the JAO: rumors about neighbors across the river.
- **Sakhalin Koreans** and **JAO Jewish-heritage families**.
- **Men's spaces** (garages, hunting cabins, fishing camps) against **women's spaces** (markets, kitchens, salons): to test, not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet denunciation and Gulag history; mixed for rumor studies; thin for everyday gossip among small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Korean; Yiddish; Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** denunciation files name real families; security-service archives are mostly closed; slander and defamation as law go to Libra; rumors about ethnic groups need careful handling.
- **Held for review:** none.
