# Künnarantaiga: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Nivkh and Chukchi for older ethnography; Chinese and Latin for the border treaties; Korean and Japanese for the Sakhalin repatriation talks. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and, more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors). ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats**: lamas, shamans and matchmakers as three kinds of go-between.
- **Sakha**: elders, shamans and kin in quarrels and courtship.
- **Orthodox Russians** and **Semeiskie Old Believers**: priest, saint and lay leader.
- **Evenki and Evens**: clan elders between families in herding disputes.
- **Nivkh, Nanai, Ulch**: in-law clans as mediators.
- **Chukchi and Yupik**: trade partners and brokers on the coast.
- **Amur and Primorye borderlands**: Chinese-Russian interpreters and fixers.
- **Sakhalin Koreans**: associations dealing with three governments.
- **Labor-migrant communities**: diaspora leaders between migrants and officials.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on treaties and shamanism; mixed on matchmaking; thin on present-day informal peacemaking.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Latin and Manchu sources (through translation) for Nerchinsk; Chinese; Korean; Japanese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic practice; the cult of Itigelov; legal mediation and courts belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
