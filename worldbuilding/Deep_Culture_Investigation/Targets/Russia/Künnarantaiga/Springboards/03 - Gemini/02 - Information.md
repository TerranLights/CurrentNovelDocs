# Künnarantaiga: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chinese, Korean and Japanese for cross-border media; Yupik and Chukchi for land and ice knowledge. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how a culture gathers, passes on, trusts, withholds and uses it: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Large cities** (Vladivostok, Khabarovsk, Irkutsk, Yakutsk) against **settlements reached only by air or winter road**, where signal and print are scarce.
- **Sakha-language** and **Russian-language** audiences in Sakha.
- **Buryats** with access to Mongolian media; **Aga and Khori** against **Ust-Orda**.
- **Herders and sea-mammal hunters** (Even, Evenki, Chukchi, Yupik, Koryak) whose land and ice knowledge has its own channels.
- **Kolyma and Magadan families** with Gulag pasts.
- **Border populations** in Primorye, Amur and the JAO; **Kuril islanders**; **Sakhalin Koreans**; **labor migrants**.
- **Old and young**: television against Telegram and VK.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich at the national and federal-district level; thin at the settlement level; rich for Soviet propaganda history, thin for how it was received.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chinese and Korean for cross-border flows; Yupik and Chukchi terms for ice knowledge.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current press restrictions and "foreign agent" labels affect sources and informants; some Indigenous knowledge is not to be shared outside the community; Gulag archives are partly closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
