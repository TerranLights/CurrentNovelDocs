*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Academic towns** (Irkutsk's science district, Vladivostok's Russky Island campus) against **district kraevedy**.
- **Evenki, Nivkh, Nanai and Sakha hunters**: rules on asking and naming.
- **Buddhist Buryats**: open debate in philosophy against teachings kept for initiates.
- **Northern Sakha districts** (Ust-Yana, Allaikhovsky): finds of the deep past as trade and as wonder.
- **Kamchatka and Kuril volcano towns**: living with things that draw scientists.
- **Olkhon and Baikal shore communities** hosting visitors.
- **Semeiskie Old Believers**: guarded toward outsiders' questions.
- **Chukchi and Yupik** coastal villages long visited by Americans and researchers.
- **JAO Jewish-heritage families** and **Sakhalin Koreans**: genealogy and family-history searching.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the history of expeditions and learned societies; mixed for kraevedenie; thin for everyday nosiness and children's questions.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; German and Latin for 18th-century papers; Korean; Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred sites and shamanic knowledge; human remains and objects taken to museums; tusk trade legality is a Libra matter.
- **Held for review:** none.
