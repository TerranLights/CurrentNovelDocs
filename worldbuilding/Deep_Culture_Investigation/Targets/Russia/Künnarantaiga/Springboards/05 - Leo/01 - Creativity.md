## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha**: epic singers, smiths and jewelers, film-makers; central against Arctic districts.
- **Buryats**: Buddhist painters and makers of ritual objects against secular artists; Aga and Ust-Orda as their own cases.
- **Chukchi and Yupik** carvers of the coast against reindeer Chukchi of the interior.
- **Nanai, Ulch, Udege, Nivkh, Uilta**: ornament, fish-skin and birch-bark work, carving.
- **Evenki and Evens**: beadwork and reindeer-gear decoration; scattered communities across Sakha, Amur, Khabarovsk and Magadan.
- **Itelmen and Koryak** of Kamchatka; **Kurils and Sakhalin**, with an Ainu past.
- **Russian** city art worlds (Vladivostok, Irkutsk, Khabarovsk) against village crafts; **Old Believer** painters of Transbaikalia.
- **Jewish** artists of Birobidzhan; **Sakhalin Korean** writers and musicians; **Chinese** and **Central Asian** craftspeople in markets.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Sakha and Chukchi arts and for named writers; mixed for city art scenes; thin for invention and everyday making-do, which needs forums and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for literary and epic sources; Chukchi, Nanai and Nivkh terms from their dictionaries (Savelyeva and Taksami's Nivkh-Russian dictionary *check*).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ritual objects and shamanic designs may be restricted by their makers' families; carving walrus ivory touches trade rules (Libra for the letter). Copying of Indigenous ornament by souvenir firms is a live dispute.
- **Held for review:** none.
