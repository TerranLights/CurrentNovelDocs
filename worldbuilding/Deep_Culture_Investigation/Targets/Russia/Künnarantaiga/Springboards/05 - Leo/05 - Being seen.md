# Künnarantaiga: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia: Irkutsk and Buryatia east through Sakha and the Amur lands to Kamchatka, the Kurils and Chukotka. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi and Yupik; Korean; Yiddish; Ukrainian; Chinese for the border trade towns. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha**: city and village; customs of guarding children from notice.
- **Buryats**: Buddhist and shamanist families; Ulan-Ude against Aga and Ust-Orda.
- **Small peoples** with low counts (Chuvans, Kamchadals, Oroch, Negidal, Uilta, Tofalars, Soyots, Aleuts of the Commander Islands): risk of erasure in statistics.
- **Ainu descendants** of Sakhalin and the Kurils.
- **Chinese** traders and workers; **Central Asian** migrants; **Sakhalin Koreans**; **Jews** of Birobidzhan.
- **Russians**: residents of dying northern towns; Gulag descendants; port youth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; the census and museums are rich; feelings of being overlooked need interviews and forums; thin for Ainu and the smallest peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi and Yupik for Chukotka; Korean; Chinese for border towns; Japanese for Karafuto-era Ainu records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance, ethnic hostility toward migrants, and Ainu status are charged; keep individuals unnamed. Beliefs about the evil eye and name taboos may be private to families.
- **Held for review:** none.
