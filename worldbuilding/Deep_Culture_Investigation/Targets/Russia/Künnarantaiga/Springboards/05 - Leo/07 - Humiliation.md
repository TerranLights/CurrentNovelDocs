# Künnarantaiga: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia: from Lake Baikal and the Lena to the Amur, Sakhalin, the Kurils, Kamchatka and Chukotka. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Korean; Yiddish; Japanese and Chinese for neighbors' and captives' records. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha** and **Buryats**: experiences of insult in cities; rural joking.
- **Small peoples**: boarding-school and workplace slights; Chukchi as the butt of Soviet-era jokes (study the jokes as a source, never repeat them as a view).
- **Russians**: conscripts and fleet sailors; prisoners and former prisoners; Gulag descendants; Old Believers; Cossacks.
- **Japanese** captives and expellees (as memory); **Chinese** and **Central Asian** migrants; **Sakhalin Koreans**; **Jews** of Birobidzhan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in memoirs and literature; mixed for current bullying and hazing; thin for small peoples' own terms of shame.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Japanese; Chinese; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** army hazing, prison castes, ethnic clashes and sexual humiliation are dangerous topics in Russia and painful for victims; anonymize everyone; jokes about peoples are sources to analyze, not to reproduce.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search «челобитная якутов воевода Головин» for published 17th-century complaints.

3. Search «дедовщина Тихоокеанский флот» and «Восточный военный округ» for case reports.
4. Search «частушки Забайкалья семейские» for collections.
5. Sample «Подслушано Якутск» and «Подслушано Чита» for kinds of public shaming.
