# Künnarantaiga: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia: the twelve eastern units from Irkutsk and Buryatia to Chukotka. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Korean; Yiddish; Ukrainian; Indigenous languages through their dictionaries. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha** (Yakutsk elites, rural districts, Arctic districts): different objects of pride.
- **Buryats** of Buryatia, Aga and Ust-Orda; Buddhist and shamanist families.
- **Evenki, Evens, Dolgans, Yukaghirs** of Sakha: pride as small peoples beside the larger Sakha.
- **Chukchi, Koryak, Itelmen, Yupik**: herders against sea hunters.
- **Nanai, Ulch, Nivkh, Udege, Uilta, Oroch**: river and coastal peoples.
- **Russians**: Old Believers, Cossack descendants, fleet and port families, Magadan miners, newcomers from the west.
- **Jews**, **Sakhalin Koreans**, **Ukrainians**, **Chinese** and **Central Asian** migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official symbols and holidays; mixed for everyday bragging and its sanctions, which needs proverbs, forums and interviews; thin for small peoples' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for proverbs and epics; Korean; Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** group pride touches separatism and "extremism" charges in Russia today; LGBTQ Pride is a legal danger zone (Libra for the letter); keep individuals anonymous. Never cast any people as "proud" as a trait.
- **Held for review:** none.
