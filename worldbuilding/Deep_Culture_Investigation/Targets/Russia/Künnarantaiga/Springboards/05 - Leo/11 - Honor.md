## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha**: clan memory, wrestlers, rural honor of the family name.
- **Buryats**: wrestlers' titles, clan honor, Buddhist ethics of conduct.
- **Chukchi and Koryak**: warrior memory; **Evenki and Evens**: hunters' good name; **Nanai**, **Nivkh**, **Udege**: feud and settlement in old accounts.
- **Russians**: officers and fleet families, Cossacks, Old Believers, former prisoners and their codes, miners.
- **Sakhalin Koreans**, **Chinese** traders, **Jews** of Birobidzhan, **Central Asian** migrants (honor talk to study, never assume).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military honor and literature; mixed for family honor; thin for survey measures in these lands.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi via translations; Korean; Chinese; Japanese for the 1945 campaign.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criminal codes, family violence justified by honor and war memory are charged topics; the legal letter goes to Libra, the spirit of duty to Capricorn. Never ascribe an "honor culture" to a people as a trait.
- **Held for review:** none.
