## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha**: toyon heritage, republic leaders, rural heads.
- **Buryats**: clan titles, kolkhoz chairmen, Buddhist and shamanist leaders; Aga and Ust-Orda.
- **Chukchi, Koryak, Even, Evenki**: herd owners, brigade heads, community heads.
- **Nanai, Udege, Nivkh, Ulch**: village and community heads; ties to Khabarovsk and Primorye politics.
- **Russians**: Cossacks, fleet officers, mine and fishery bosses, governors from Moscow against local-born.
- **Sakhalin Koreans**, **Jews** of Birobidzhan, **Chinese** traders' networks.
- Men and women: women's leadership in villages, schools and cultural centers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal and historical leaders; mixed for informal leaders, needing interviews; thin for small peoples' own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi and Even terms; Chinese for Amur history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current politics and protests carry risk; elections and law go to Libra, the spirit of authority to Capricorn. Founder monuments are disputed by Indigenous groups and in China.
- **Held for review:** none.
