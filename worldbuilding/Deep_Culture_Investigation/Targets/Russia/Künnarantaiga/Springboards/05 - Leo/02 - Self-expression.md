# Künnarantaiga: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia (Sakha, Irkutsk, Buryatia, Zabaykalsky, the Amur basin, Primorye, Sakhalin and the Kurils, Magadan, Kamchatka, Chukotka). **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki, Even, Chukchi, Koryak; Yiddish; Korean. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha**: improvised singers, city youth of Yakutsk, rural districts.
- **Buryats**: Buddhist and shamanist families; Aga and Ust-Orda; city youth of Ulan-Ude.
- **Evens, Evenki, Koryak, Chukchi, Yupik, Itelmen**: personal songs, tattoo, dance, and their staging.
- **Nanai, Nivkh, Ulch, Udege**: storytelling and song, and staged ensembles.
- **Russians**: Vladivostok and Khabarovsk youth scenes; Old Believers' rules on dress and speech; Irkutsk intelligentsia with an exile past; military towns.
- **Jews** of Birobidzhan; **Sakhalin Koreans** across three generations; **Ukrainians** of Primorye.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for staged song and dance; mixed for everyday speech, which needs forums and interviews; thin for some small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha for toyuk; Buryat; Even and Chukchi song texts with translations; Yiddish; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political and gender expression carry legal risk in Russia today (the letter goes to Libra); never expose individuals. Personal songs may belong to their owners and families: ask before use.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search «тойук конкурс» and «оһуокай» with Sakha district names for contest results and recordings.
2. Search «личная песня» with «эвены», «коряки», «чукчи» in Russian ethnomusicology journals.
3. Search the Piłsudski cylinder project for Sakhalin song catalogs.
4. Sample ykt.ru and vl.ru forums for «высказаться» and «душа нараспашку».
5. Search «Биробиджан идиш поэзия» for poets and anthologies.
