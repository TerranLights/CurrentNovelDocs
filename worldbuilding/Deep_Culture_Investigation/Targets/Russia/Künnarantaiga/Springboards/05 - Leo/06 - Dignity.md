# Künnarantaiga: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia: twelve eastern units, Irkutsk and Buryatia to Kamchatka and Chukotka. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Even and Evenki; Yiddish; Korean; Ukrainian. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha**: city and rural ethics of the decent person.
- **Buryats**: Buddhist, shamanist and Orthodox Buryats; elders' etiquette.
- **Evens and Evenki** herders; **Chukchi**, **Koryak** and **Yupik** villagers; **Nanai**, **Nivkh**, **Udege** in river villages.
- **Russians**: Gulag descendants and former camp staff families in Magadan; shift workers; Old Believers; the old in dying towns.
- **Migrants** from Central Asia and China; **Sakhalin Koreans**; **Jews** of Birobidzhan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Gulag and for literature; mixed for care and work; thin for small peoples' own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Even and Chukchi for oral histories; Korean; Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prison, camp and care-home treatment are politically sensitive; survivors' and families' testimony must be anonymized. Burial archaeology involves human remains: follow the descendants' wishes.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search «достоинство» with «Шаламов» and «Колыма» in literary studies for the debate on dignity in the camps.
2. Search «киһи буол» and «сиэр-туом» in the Big Explanatory Dictionary of Sakha and in Sakha ethics essays.
3. Open each unit's ombudsman annual report and list complaint categories on treatment.
4. Search «вахтовики» forums by mining region (Neryungri, Mirny, Chukotka).
5. Search «Mission archéologique française en Sibérie orientale» for Sakha burial reports.
