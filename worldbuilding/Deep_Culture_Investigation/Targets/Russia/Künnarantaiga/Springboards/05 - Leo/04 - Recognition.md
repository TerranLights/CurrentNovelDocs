# Künnarantaiga: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia: the lands from Lake Baikal to the Bering Strait. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Korean; Yiddish; Indigenous languages through dictionaries; Japanese for Karafuto-era records. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha**: republic honors, olonkho singers' titles, athletes.
- **Buryats**: Buddhist titles against secular and Soviet honors; Aga and Ust-Orda.
- **Small peoples** (Evenki, Evens, Yukaghirs, Dolgans, Chukchi, Koryak, Itelmen, Kamchadals, Soyots, Tofalars, Nanai, Nivkh, Udege, Ulch, Uilta, Oroch, Negidal, Yupik, Aleuts of the Commander Islands): official listing, and recognition of elders.
- **Russians**: BAM builders, fleet families, miners of Kolyma, Gulag descendants, Old Believers.
- **Sakhalin Koreans**, **Japanese**-era Sakhalin memory, **Jews** of Birobidzhan, **Chinese** and **Central Asian** migrants whose work is rarely credited.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors and titles; mixed for workplace praise; thin for how small peoples grant recognition among themselves.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Korean; Japanese for Karafuto memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Gulag memory and Memorial's materials are politically charged and access may be limited; Indigenous status is a legal matter (Libra for the letter, Capricorn for its spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
