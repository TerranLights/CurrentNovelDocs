*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Classical Mongolian for Buryat clan records. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha** from different districts (*uluses*), whose home loyalties may matter in Yakutsk.
- **Buryats** of different clans (Khori, Ekhirit, Bulagat, Khongodor) and of Aga against Ulan-Ude.
- **Small Indigenous communities**, where every official is kin to someone.
- **Port and fishing towns** (Vladivostok, Nakhodka, Petropavlovsk, Korsakov) where quotas and customs offices meet business.
- **Chukotka** under patronage against other northern regions.
- **Old Believer villages** with their own elders; **Birobidzhan** and the Jewish community.
- **Migrants**, who meet officials from outside every network.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on corruption cases and national frames; mixed for regional surveys; thin for Indigenous peoples' own rules on judging kin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Classical Mongolian for older clan records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and election-monitoring sources may be labeled "foreign agent" or "undesirable"; naming clans or districts in hiring can stir feeling; handle living persons carefully.
- **Held for review:** none.
