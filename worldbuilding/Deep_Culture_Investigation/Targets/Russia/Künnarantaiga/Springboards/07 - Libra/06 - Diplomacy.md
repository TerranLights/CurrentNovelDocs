## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Primorye and Sakhalin**, facing Japan, Korea and China daily.
- **Kuril islanders**, on the disputed line.
- **Buryats and Zabaykalsky** people, tied to Mongolia and the Chinese border at Zabaykalsk.
- **Chukotka Yupik and Chukchi**, with kin in Alaska.
- **Amur and Khabarovsk** border towns opposite Heihe and Fuyuan.
- **Sakha**, distant from foreign borders, whose diplomacy is mostly with Moscow.
- **Sakhalin Koreans**, between Russia and both Koreas.
- **Nivkh, Ulchi and Nanai**, heirs to the old river trade.
