# Künnarantaiga: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi and Koryak through translated oral literature; Japanese for the Kurils. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of the repressed** in Magadan, Kolyma and Irkutsk, and those of Gulag guards.
- **Sakha** and **Buryat** families, on keeping quarrels inside the home.
- **Molokans, Old Believers** and other dissenting communities with peace teachings.
- **Port and border business people** (Vladivostok, Nakhodka, Blagoveshchensk) on paying protection.
- **Kuril and Sakhalin** residents, on the islands question.
- **Chukchi, Koryaks, Itelmens**, through their own stories of war and peace.
- **Migrant workers**, for whom speaking up carries extra risk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, the Gulag past and protest surveys; mixed for family silence; thin for Indigenous peacemaking in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Japanese for the Kuril side; Chukchi and Koryak through translations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence in the home; the war since 2022 and calls for peace, which carry legal risk for speakers; some crisis-center and survey bodies are labeled "foreign agent"; protect informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
