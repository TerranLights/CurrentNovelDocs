# Künnarantaiga: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chinese and Korean for migrant and deported communities; Yiddish for Birobidzhan. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russians and Sakha in Yakutsk**, each side's image of the other.
- **Primorye and Amur Russians** and **Chinese traders and workers**.
- **Sakhalin Koreans** and the memory of the deported **Koryo-saram**.
- **Central Asian and Caucasus migrants** in the cities.
- **Chukchi**, as joke targets and as writers who answered.
- **Birobidzhan's Jewish community**; **Kolyma descendants** of prisoners and guards.
- **Buryats**, Cisbaikal and Transbaikal, on curses, gossip and blame.
- **Far Easterners** as a whole, in their image of Moscow and the west of Russia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for deportations, purges and migrant fear; mixed for folk theories of harm; thin for Indigenous accounts of blame.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Chinese and Korean; Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic slurs and jokes must be recorded as material, not repeated as findings; spirit sickness and curses touch living families; migrant cases and repression archives carry risk for informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
