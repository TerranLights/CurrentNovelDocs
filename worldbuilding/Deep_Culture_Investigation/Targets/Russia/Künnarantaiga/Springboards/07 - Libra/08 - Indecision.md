## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern towns** (Magadan, Chukotka, northern Sakha), where staying or leaving is a lifelong question.
- **Sakha**, family councils in villages against young people in Yakutsk.
- **Buryats**, who may consult lamas, shamans or both.
- **Evens and Evenki**, clan talk and herding decisions.
- **Semeiskie** and other Old Believers.
- **Kuril islanders** and **Sakhalin** residents waiting on larger decisions.
- **Young people in Vladivostok, Khabarovsk and Irkutsk** weighing a move west or abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, public projects and migration; mixed for divination and family decision-making; thin for psychological measures by region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Japanese for the Kurils; Tibetan and Mongolian for calendar texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination and lamaist practice involve living specialists and their clients; migration choices touch draft and war-related departures since 2022, which informants may not discuss.
- **Held for review:** none.
