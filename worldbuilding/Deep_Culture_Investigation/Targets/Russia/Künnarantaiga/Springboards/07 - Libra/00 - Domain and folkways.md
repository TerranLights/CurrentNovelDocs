**Target:** Künnarantaiga (Күннарантайга), region Russia: Sakha (Yakutia), Irkutsk, Buryatia, Zabaykalsky, Amur, the Jewish Autonomous Oblast, Khabarovsk, Primorsky, Sakhalin (with the Kurils), Magadan, Kamchatka, Chukotka. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki, Even, Chukchi, Koryak, Nivkh, Nanai, Yukaghir; Yiddish, Korean, Chinese and Japanese for heritage and border records. **Concept in one line:** partnership and justice: what is a fair bargain and a good partnership, including marriage, contracts, open enemies, gender ways and the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha**, central herding districts against the northern reindeer and fishing districts.
- **Buryats**: Cisbaikal (Ekhirit-Bulagat) against Transbaikal (Khori, Aga, Selenga); shamanist and Buddhist strands.
- **Evenki and Evens** across Sakha, Amur, Khabarovsk, Zabaykalsky and Magadan; **Yukaghirs**, **Dolgans** of Anabar.
- **Chukchi, Siberian Yupik, Koryaks, Itelmens, Kamchadals** of the northeast.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples**: Nanai, Ulchi, Udege, Oroch, Negidal, Nivkh, Uilta (Orok); the **Ainu** heritage of Sakhalin and the Kurils; **Soyots** and **Tofalars** of the Sayan.
- **Russian old settlers**: Semeiskie Old Believers, Russkoye Ustye, Markovo, the Transbaikal, Amur and Ussuri Cossacks, Amur Molokans.
- **Heritage communities**: Birobidzhan Jews; Sakhalin Koreans; Zeleny Klin Ukrainians; Irkutsk Poles; Chinese and Central Asian migrants.
