# Künnarantaiga: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Tibetan for Buryat medical texts; Nanai, Ulchi and Nivkh for ornament terms. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others; and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha**, central herding districts against the Arctic north and Yakutsk.
- **Buryats**, Buddhist Transbaikal against shamanist Cisbaikal and Olkhon Island.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples** (Nanai, Ulchi, Nivkh, Udege), through craft and river life.
- **Evens and Evenki**, nomadic against settled.
- **Baikal shore communities** against Irkutsk and Angara industrial towns.
- **Shift-work families** in Magadan, Chukotka and western Sakha.
- **Semeiskie** and other Old Believers; **Primorye and Khabarovsk** voters.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Sakha and Buryat cosmology and for Baikal; mixed for everyday work-life balance; thin for regional time-use data.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Tibetan (with Mongolian) for medical texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic and lamaist healing knowledge is partly restricted; some Baikal groups have been dissolved or labeled; election data is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
