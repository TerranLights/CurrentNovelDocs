# Künnarantaiga: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia (the twelve eastern units). **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi and Siberian Yupik for marine-hunting shares; Chinese and Japanese for border trade. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shift workers (*vakhtoviki*)** in mines and oil fields against settled residents of the same districts.
- **Diamond towns** of western Sakha (Mirny, Udachny) against Sakha farming districts.
- **Coastal Chukotka villages** (Yupik and Chukchi) against reindeer-herding Chukchi inland.
- **Evens and Evenki** in herding brigades across Sakha, Magadan and Khabarovsk.
- **Fishing settlements** of Kamchatka, the Okhotsk coast and Sakhalin; **Kuril islanders**.
- **Angara and Bratsk resettled villagers** (Irkutsk).
- **Buryats**, Aga and Cisbaikal; **Primorye's** car and cross-border traders; **Chinese and Central Asian migrant workers**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pay law and resource politics; mixed for everyday fairness in the regions; thin for national surveys split below country level.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat; Chukchi and Yupik terms through Krupnik and local dictionaries; Japanese for the car trade.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** quota and diamond deals touch powerful firms and officials; whaling shares involve Indigenous hunting knowledge shared on the hunters' terms.
- **Held for review:** none.
