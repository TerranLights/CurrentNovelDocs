## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Magadan and Kolyma towns:** Gulag survivors' descendants and incomers' families, against people who arrived later.
- **Irkutsk Oblast:** dam-displaced villages (Angara) against the cities built by the dams.
- **Sakha:** diamond and mining towns (Mirny, Udachny) against farming and herding villages.
- **Amur peoples:** Udege, Nanai and Ulch stewardship against commercial loggers and fishers.
- **Sakhalin:** Nivkh and Uilta against oil-town incomers.
- **Koreans:** deported families' descendants and Sakhalin Koreans, separately.
- **Buryats:** Baikal-shore communities against Ulan-Ude.
- **Chinese residents and traders** on the Amur border.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters and the Gulag; thin for everyday family responsibility outside Sakha and Buryatia. The age of criminal responsibility and legal liability belong under Law (spirit only) and Libra (letter).
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Korean; Chinese for the Amur border; Polish for Sieroszewski's original.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Gulag memory work faces pressure since Memorial's dissolution; environmental NGOs may carry "foreign agent" labels; the 1900 Blagoveshchensk killings remain a painful subject.
- **Held for review:** none.
