*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** village families and sports schools against urban Yakutsk; Arctic herding families against settled ones.
- **Buryats:** lay families against monastic novices; eastern Buddhist against western shamanic upbringing.
- **Even, Evenki, Chukchi and Koryak:** children raised in camps against those raised in boarding schools.
- **Semeiskie Old Believers:** fasting and household rule.
- **Naval and Cossack families:** Vladivostok, Kamchatka, Transbaikal.
- **Shift workers, prospectors and miners:** discipline set by the job.
- **Gulag-heritage families** in Magadan and Kolyma.
- **Sakhalin Koreans and Birobidzhan's families:** upbringing across migration.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, the military and the camps; mixed for family discipline; thin for the smaller peoples beyond boarding-school accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Tibetan and classical Mongolian for monastic rules; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school harms and child punishment need survivors' accounts first; hazing in the forces is sensitive; monastic rules may be partly restricted to the ordained.
- **Held for review:** none.
