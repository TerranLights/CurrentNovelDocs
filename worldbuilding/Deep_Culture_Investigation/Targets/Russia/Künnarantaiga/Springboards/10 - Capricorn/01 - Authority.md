*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Republic against oblast and krai:** Sakha and Buryatia have their own constitutions and parliaments; the others do not; the source of local authority may differ.
- **Eastern and western Buryats:** lama authority among Khori and Aga against shamanic and clan authority in Ust-Orda and around Baikal.
- **Sakha:** clan elders and village administration against republic officials.
- **Chukotka:** reindeer camps against coastal Chukchi and Yupik villages.
- **The Amur peoples:** Nanai, Ulch and Udege clans that once dealt with the Qing, against later Soviet councils.
- **Semeiskie Old Believers:** priestless authority of elders and books.
- **Closed military and naval towns** (Kamchatka, Primorye): command as daily life.
- **Birobidzhan and Sakhalin Korean families:** household authority across migration.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Sakha and Buryat; mixed for the Amur and Chukotka; national surveys rarely break out the region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat and classical Mongolian; Japanese for Mamiya; Chukchi and Nanai terms through specialists.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest and opposition records may be legally risky to hold or cite inside Russia; shamanic authority is partly restricted knowledge; Buddhist leadership disputes are live.
- **Held for review:** none.
