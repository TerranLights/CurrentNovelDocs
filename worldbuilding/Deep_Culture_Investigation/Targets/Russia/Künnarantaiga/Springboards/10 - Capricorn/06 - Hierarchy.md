## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** descendants of old toyon lines against commoner families; Yakutsk elites against villages.
- **Buryats:** Khori and Aga clan nobility memory against western Buryat clans.
- **Chukchi:** reindeer and coastal communities; Siberian Yupik beside them.
- **Koryak, Even and Evenki:** herd-owner and herder ranks.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples:** clan ranking, and Karafuto-era colonial rank on Sakhalin.
- **Cossack families** against peasant settlers; **old settlers** against Soviet newcomers.
- **Garrison and naval towns:** military rank as social rank.
- **Gulag-heritage towns:** camp ranks remembered in speech.
- **Migrants:** Chinese and Central Asian workers at the bottom of labor markets; Sakhalin Koreans and their past status.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical rank in Sakha and Buryat; mixed for present class; thin for small peoples' internal ranking.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat and classical Mongolian; Chukchi terms through specialists; Japanese for Karafuto; Korean; Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan genealogies can be private family knowledge; criminal-world ranks and army hazing are sensitive; ranking of peoples and migrants must be recorded as prejudice to study, never adopted.
- **Held for review:** none.
