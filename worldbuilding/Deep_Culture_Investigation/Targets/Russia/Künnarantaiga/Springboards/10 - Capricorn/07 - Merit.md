## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** selective-school graduates against village youth; *olonkhosut* and athletes as earned standing.
- **Buryats:** monastic learning against secular schooling; Aga and Khori against western Buryats.
- **Chukchi, Koryak, Even and Evenki:** skill in herding and hunting against school credentials; quota students and their home communities.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples:** fishing and craft skill against wage work.
- **Incomer specialists** (engineers, geologists, doctors) whose northern careers rested on credentials.
- **Sakhalin Koreans:** education as the route upward.
- **Jewish Autonomous Oblast:** professionals who stayed or left.
- **Migrant workers:** credentials not recognized.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams and honors; mixed for attitudes; thin for how small peoples define earned standing.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Tibetan for monastic degrees; Koryak and Chukchi terms through specialists; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims of clan favoritism are politically charged and must be weighed, not repeated; some monastic examinations are closed to outsiders; new veterans' fast-track programs into office are a live, sensitive topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
