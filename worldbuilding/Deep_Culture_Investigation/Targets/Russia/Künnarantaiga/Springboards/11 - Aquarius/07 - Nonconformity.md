## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers:** Semeiskie (Buryatia, Zabaykalsky); Primorye and Amur communities, including returnees from abroad.
- **Political exiles' descendants:** Polish, Decembrist and other exile families in Irkutsk and Transbaikal.
- **Shamans and their clients:** Sakha, Buryat, Evenki, Nanai, Chukchi; revivalists against Orthodox and Buddhist neighbors.
- **Port and border cities:** Vladivostok's Asia-facing youth culture; Khabarovsk; Blagoveshchensk.
- **Sakha creative scene** in Yakutsk against village norms.
- **People who live outside the usual roles:** taiga hermits and lone hunters; unmarried or childless adults; gender-nonconforming people (see Flags).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Old Believers and political exiles; mixed on youth scenes; thin and dangerous to gather on gender and sexual nonconformity.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Church Slavonic for older Old Believer texts; Polish; Sakha and Buryat for scene media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "LGBT movement" designated extremist in Russia (2023): online material is scarce and contact can endanger people; forced psychiatry; shamanic knowledge; Old Believer communities' wish to stay apart.
- **Held for review:** none.
