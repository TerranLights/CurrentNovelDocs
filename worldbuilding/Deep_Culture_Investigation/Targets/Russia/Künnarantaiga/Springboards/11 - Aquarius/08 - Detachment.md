# Künnarantaiga: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga (Күннарантайга), region Russia, Baikal to the Bering Strait. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat (with Tibetan Buddhist terms); Sakha; Evenki. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; valued as calm or objectivity, condemned as coldness or indifference.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryat Buddhists** (lamas, laity) against **western Buryat shamanist** families; **Orthodox** monastics and laity.
- **Evenki and Even hunters and herders** (autonomy, solitude in the taiga) against their village relatives.
- **Sakha** in Yakutsk and in villages; display of feeling at funerals and gatherings.
- **Newcomers and their children** in Magadan, Chukotka and mining towns; **rotation workers** in Sakha and Chukotka.
- **Gulag survivors' families** (Kolyma); military and naval towns (composure as a trade).
- **City dwellers** in Vladivostok, Irkutsk, Khabarovsk: anonymity against small-town closeness.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Buddhist and Orthodox teaching; mixed on everyday reserve; thin on Indigenous display rules in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Tibetan for Buddhist sources; Sakha; Evenki terms via Russian ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious practice held by vows; Gulag trauma; mental-health records; avoid any "cold northerner" or "stoic native" stereotype.
- **Held for review:** none.
