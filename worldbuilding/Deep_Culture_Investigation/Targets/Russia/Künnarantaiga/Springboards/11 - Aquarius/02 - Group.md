# Künnarantaiga: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Clan-organized peoples:** Buryats (western clans of Irkutsk and Ust-Orda; eastern Khori, Aga), Sakha (central and Vilyui uluses), Evenks, Nanai, Ulchi, Nivkh.
- **Camp-based northeast:** reindeer Chukchi and Koryak (Chavchu) against coastal villages (Nymylan Koryak, Yupik).
- **Russian groups:** Cossack descendants; Old Believer communities; Gulag and Dalstroy-descended Magadan; BAM-era cohorts.
- **Work crews:** fishing collectives on Kamchatka and Sakhalin; gold-mining artels; rotation (*vakhta*) workers in Sakha and Chukotka.
- **City and youth groups:** Vladivostok, Khabarovsk, Irkutsk; youth gangs in Zabaykalsky and Buryatia (see Flags).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on clans (Soviet and pre-Soviet ethnography); mixed on present-day groups; thin on Uilta, Taz and Soyot groupings.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat (classical Mongolian script for the old chronicles).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan genealogies and sacrifices; youth-gang culture (the "AUE" label is banned as extremist, 2020); protest participation and 2022 mobilization, where ethnic groups were discussed as wholes (avoid blaming any people as a whole); hazing.
- **Held for review:** none.
