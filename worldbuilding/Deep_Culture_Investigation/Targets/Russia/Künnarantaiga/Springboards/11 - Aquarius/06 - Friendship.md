# Künnarantaiga: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga (Күннарантайга), region Russia, Baikal to the Bering Strait. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki; Chukchi; Korean; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats** (the *anda* bond and its present use; city and village); **Sakha** (friendship in alaas villages against Yakutsk; ulus fellowship).
- **Chukchi and Yupik** (old partner bonds; relatives in Alaska); **Koryak and Itelmen** villages.
- **Russian men in field and shift work:** geologists, miners, fishers, rotation workers; army and navy veterans.
- **Border towns:** Blagoveshchensk, Pogranichny, Zabaykalsk; Chinese traders and Russian partners.
- **Sakhalin Koreans** and their Russian classmates; Jews in Birobidzhan.
- **Students** in Vladivostok, Irkutsk and Yakutsk, including students from Asia.
- **Women's friendships** in each group, less recorded and worth separating.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Soviet and post-Soviet literature and film; mixed in surveys; thin on women's friendship and on smaller Indigenous peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi and Yupik for partner terms; Chinese and Korean for border and Sakhalin sides; English for Alaska accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** old exchange customs between partners recorded by outside ethnographers (read critically, do not sensationalize); lapsed ties with Western sister cities; personal data on reunion and forum pages.
- **Held for review:** none.
