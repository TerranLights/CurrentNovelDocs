**Target:** Künnarantaiga (Күннарантайга), region Russia, Baikal to the Bering Strait. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki; Korean; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Indigenous peoples on the official list** (Evenks, Evens, Chukchi, Koryaks, Nanai, Nivkh and others) against old-settler mixed groups whose listing is debated (*check* Kamchadals' status), and against Sakha and Buryats, who are titular peoples, not "small-numbered".
- **Titular peoples:** Sakha in Sakha; Buryats in Buryatia and in Irkutsk Oblast and Zabaykalsky, where they are minorities.
- **Herders against villagers:** reindeer families against settled Chukchi, Koryak and Even.
- **Migrants:** Chinese traders and workers; Central Asian workers; Sakhalin Koreans; North Korean contract workers (Primorye, Khabarovsk).
- **Class and place:** resource towns (Mirny, Neryungri, Bodaybo) against villages; Vladivostok against Primorye's rural districts; newcomers against old settlers.
- **Women and men** in each people; urban Sakha and Buryat women's roles against village roles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Rosstat numbers; mixed on attitudes; thin on present-day Indigenous views of status in their own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for local media; Chinese and Korean for migrants' side.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic slurs in online material (describe, never repeat); 1900 Blagoveshchensk and 1937 deportation memory; migrant raids; colonial-era estate terms.
- **Held for review:** none.
