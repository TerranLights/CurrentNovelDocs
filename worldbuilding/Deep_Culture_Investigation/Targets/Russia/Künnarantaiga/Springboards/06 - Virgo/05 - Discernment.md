# Künnarantaiga: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia; lands from the Angara and Baikal through Transbaikalia, Yakutia and the Amur to the Pacific coast, Sakhalin, Kamchatka and Chukotka. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with Church Slavonic); Sakha; Buryat; Yiddish; Korean; Koryak and Itelmen through folklore editions. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vladivostok and Khabarovsk traders** in cars, fish and Chinese goods, whose street savvy is a regional byword in Russian media (to be tested, not assumed).
- **Orthodox parishes** against **Old Believers** of Zabaykalye and the Primorye taiga, with their lay judges of faith.
- **Buddhist Buryats,** who may consult lamas, against **shamanist Buryats,** who may consult a böö; urban Buryats who do neither.
- **Sakha** in Yakutsk and in the central villages, and their trades in horses, ivory and diamonds.
- **Koryak, Itelmen and Chukchi** communities, through their own trickster and judgment tales.
- **Birobidzhan's remaining Jewish community** and **Sakhalin Koreans**, as smaller worlds of counsel and matchmaking.
- **Irkutsk's intelligentsia** and its writers of village prose, against new urban professionals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on literature, folklore and trade lore; little survey data on judgment itself.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Church Slavonic; Sakha and Buryat; Yiddish and Korean for the two minority archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination may be private practice; keep to published accounts. Fraud data can be read as a slur on a city; use with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
