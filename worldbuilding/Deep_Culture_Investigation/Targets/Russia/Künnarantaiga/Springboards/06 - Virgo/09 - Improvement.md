# Künnarantaiga: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia; the Angara and Baikal country, Transbaikalia, Yakutia, the Amur and Ussuri, the Okhotsk coast, Sakhalin, Kamchatka and Chukotka. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat (with Tibetan for Buddhist self-cultivation); Chukchi and Even through Soviet-era and revival sources. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Reindeer herders** (Even, Evenki, Chukchi, Koryak) settled under Soviet plans, against those who kept nomadic camps.
- **Sakha** villages flooded or changed by dams and mines, against Yakutsk's rising professional class.
- **Buryats** in Buddhist practice, in shamanist revival and in secular urban careers.
- **Angara and Amur dam towns** (Bratsk, Ust-Ilimsk, Zeya), built as improvement, and the old villages they replaced.
- **New settlers** under the Far Eastern Hectare and the 2010s development drive, against old-settler families.
- **Southern Sakhalin,** with its Japanese-built past, and Sakhalin Koreans.
- **Udege of the Bikin,** whose forest was contested between logging plans and protection.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet development and its critics; mixed on present-day self-improvement culture by unit.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Tibetan for Buddhist teaching texts; Japanese for Karafuto-era records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** settlement of nomads, boarding schools, flooded villages and graves are painful histories; tell them in the affected peoples' voices. Land law is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
