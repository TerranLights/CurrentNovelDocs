# Künnarantaiga: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia; from Lake Baikal and the Sayan foothills through Yakutia and the Amur to the Pacific islands and the Bering Strait. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat (with Tibetan for monastic medicine); Nanai and other Amur languages through ethnography; Korean. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats** who use lama physicians, those who use shamans (böö), and those who use neither.
- **Sakha** urban and village families; healers (ojuun, udagan, herbalists) and their clients.
- **Even, Evenki, Chukchi, Koryak, Yupik** and other Northern peoples, with their own diets and healing ways, and their health gaps.
- **Nanai, Ulch, Nivkh, Udege** on the Amur and Sakhalin, where fish-based diets and shamanic tradition meet clinic medicine.
- **Russian old settlers and Old Believers,** with folk healers, herbals and the bathhouse.
- **Sakhalin Koreans** and Primorye residents who seek Korean or Chinese medicine.
- **Northern mining towns** (Mirny, Neryungri, Pevek, Bilibino) against southern farm districts of Amur and Primorye.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Health statistics by unit are good; ethnography of healing is strong for Sakha, Buryats and Amur peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat and Tibetan for monastic medicine; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic healing knowledge may be restricted by healers; alcohol and Indigenous health need great care to avoid stereotype; abortion and end-of-life law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
