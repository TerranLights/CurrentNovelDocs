# Künnarantaiga: Analysis (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia: the twelve units from Irkutsk and Buryatia to Chukotka and the Kurils. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat (with Tibetan for monastic learning); Chukchi and Siberian Yupik through ice and weather projects. **Concept in one line:** taking a whole apart to understand it: reasoning from parts, evidence, measurement and detail.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Science towns** (Irkutsk's Akademgorodok, Yakutsk's institute district, Vladivostok's FEB RAS campus) against **mining, logging and fishing towns**.
- **Buryat monastic learning** against **Buryat shamanic tradition** and secular Soviet schooling.
- **Sakha** urban schooling and olympiad culture, against rural Sakha in the Arctic districts.
- **Chukchi, Yupik and Koryak sea hunters and herders,** whose knowledge of ice, weather and animals rests on long observation.
- **Evenki and Even herders,** with detailed reindeer classifications.
- **Engineers of diamond, gold and coal mining** (Mirny, Aldan, Neryungri, Bodaybo) and the Pacific Fleet's technical staff.
- **Old Believer communities,** where book learning is religious and secular science may be held at a distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institutional science and exam statistics; mixed for everyday attitudes to reasoning; Indigenous knowledge is documented but must be read in the holders' own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat and Tibetan for monastic learning; English for international polar projects.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous knowledge may be restricted by its holders; divination and shamanic knowing are other ways of knowing, not "failed analysis"; describe, never rank.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. FIPI or Rosobrnadzor: download ЕГЭ mathematics results by subject of the federation and pull the twelve units.
