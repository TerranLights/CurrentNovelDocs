# Künnarantaiga: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia; its twelve units reach from Irkutsk and Ulan-Ude to Khabarovsk, Vladivostok, Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk and Anadyr. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Yiddish; Korean. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khabarovsk and Vladivostok,** with recent mass protest, against capitals where public criticism is quieter (a research question, not a finding).
- **Sakha and Buryat** cultural elites, who criticize in both Russian and their own languages, and village communities.
- **Old Believer and Cossack** communities, with folk satire and strong in-group discipline.
- **Birobidzhan's Jewish community,** whose history includes criticism turned into purge.
- **Chukchi, Koryak, Even and Evenki** communities, where elders' reproach and teasing may carry the weight that formal complaint carries elsewhere.
- **Environmental activists** of Baikal and the Amur against industrial towns that depend on the plants they criticize.
- **Military towns** of Primorye and Kamchatka, where criticism upward has its own limits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet meeting culture and recent protest coverage; thin on everyday criticism styles by group.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for national press and theater; Yiddish for Birobidzhan; Korean for Sakhalin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political criticism and protest are sensitive now; use published sources only and avoid exposing individuals. Laws on speech and protest belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
