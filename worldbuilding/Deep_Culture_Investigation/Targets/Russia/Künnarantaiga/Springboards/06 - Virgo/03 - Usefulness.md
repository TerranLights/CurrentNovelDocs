# Künnarantaiga: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia; its lands run from Irkutsk and Buryatia through Sakha and the Amur basin to Kamchatka, Sakhalin and Chukotka. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi and Nanai through their literatures; Evenki. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding and hunting camps** (Even, Evenki, Chukchi, Koryak), where every hand and object has a task, against **city households** in Vladivostok, Irkutsk or Khabarovsk.
- **Sakha and Buryat village families** with cattle and hay, where children's help is part of the year, against their urban kin.
- **Northern pensioners** who leave Magadan, Chukotka or the Sakha North when their working years end, against those who stay: a research question on whether the North is seen as a place for working years only.
- **Old Believer households** (Semeiskie; Primorye taiga communities), where thrift and handwork carry religious weight.
- **Amur peoples** (Nanai, Ulch, Nivkh) and their craft revival, set beside souvenir trade.
- **Disabled people and their organizations** in each capital.
- **Japanese-era and Korean households on Sakhalin**, whose objects and homes were reused by later settlers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Literature and ethnography are good; direct attitude data on "feeling useful" by unit is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Chukchi and Yukaghir authors are mostly read in Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elders, disability and the old; abandoned Northern settlements carry family grief. Avoid writing any people as idle or industrious.
- **Held for review:** none.
