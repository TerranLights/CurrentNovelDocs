## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers** (Semeiskie; Primorye and Amur taiga communities), where exact rite is a matter of salvation.
- **Buryat Buddhists,** with monastic exactness in ritual and art, against secular Buryat urban life.
- **Sakha** craft masters (smiths, horsehair and birch-bark workers) and epic performers.
- **Chukchi and Yupik carvers** of Uelen and Lorino; **Koryak** craft and dance ensembles.
- **Nanai, Ulch, Udege and Nivkh** women's craft and its revival.
- **Engineers and workers** in aviation, shipbuilding and space (Komsomolsk, Irkutsk, Ulan-Ude, Bolshoy Kamen, Tsiolkovsky).
- **School pupils and students,** whose "straight-A" pressure may differ between the republics' lyceums and ordinary schools.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Craft and religious exactness are well documented; psychological data on perfectionism by unit is thin and scattered in student studies.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred craft and ritual rules may be restricted; eating disorders, anxiety and student suicide need care. Corruption reporting is a fact base only; criminal law is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
