*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia: Baikal, the Lena, the Amur and the Pacific coast to Chukotka. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Nivkh and other Indigenous languages through translated ethnography; Korean; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** spirit beliefs (ichchi, abaasy) in uluses against Yakutsk city life; Aar Aiyy followers and Orthodox.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist households against shamanist Ekhirit-Bulagat; city and steppe.
- **Nivkh, Ulchi, Nanai, Udege** on the Amur and Sakhalin; **Chukchi, Koryak, Yupik** in the Northeast; **Evens and Evenks** in herding camps.
- **Russians:** old-timers, Cossack descendants, port and naval cities (Vladivostok, Petropavlovsk), mining and Gulag towns (Magadan, Bodaibo), Irkutsk intelligentsia.
- **Sakhalin Koreans** (han); **Birobidzhan Jews**; Chinese and Central Asian migrants as targets of anger.
- **Men and women; generations** raised in the 1990s collapse against those raised after 2000.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Spirit anger and curse traditions are well documented for the Sakha; Russian emotion semantics is rich but not regional; present-day anger at home and in public is thin and scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for belief texts; Korean for the Sakhalin diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** curses and spirit rites may be held as dangerous or private; avoid printing working formulas. Anger at police and the state, domestic violence and ethnic tension (toward migrants or between peoples) are sensitive. Stereotypes about drinking and anger in Indigenous communities must not be reproduced; feuds and revenge as justice go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
