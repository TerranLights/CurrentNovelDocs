## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** strongman and hunter ideals in uluses against city soldiers' and rescuers' memory.
- **Buryats:** Geser and the three manly games against Buddhist teaching on fearlessness.
- **Evenks and Evens:** hunters and herders alone in taiga and mountains; snipers from hunting families.
- **Chukchi and Yupik** sea hunters; **Koryak** and **Itelmen**; **Aleuts of the Commander Islands**.
- **Udege, Nanai, Ulchi, Oroch:** tiger and bear country on the Amur and Ussuri.
- **Russians:** Cossack descendants (Amur, Ussuri, Transbaikal), Old Believers, Gulag survivors and their children, border and naval families of Primorye and Kamchatka.
- **Women's courage:** Decembrists' wives, war widows, women hunters and herders.
- **Sakhalin Koreans; Birobidzhan Jews** (war memory); **Ukrainians of the Green Wedge**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war and hero memory and for Soviet literature; mixed for Indigenous courage, which is often told through outsiders; thin for moral courage in everyday life.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for epic and hero legends; Udege and Nanai mainly through Russian translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the current war has heavy losses among Buryat, Sakha and other eastern families, and courage talk is part of state messaging; treat with great care and keep living people unnamed. Gulag and dissident courage is a risky topic inside Russia. Some hunting and bear rites may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
