*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia: ice rivers, volcanoes, cold seas and long roads from Irkutsk to Chukotka and Sakhalin. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Itelmen and Koryak folklore in translation; Korean; Chinese for casino and border sources. **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men in port and border cities** (Vladivostok, Nakhodka, Khabarovsk, Blagoveshchensk) against rural youth.
- **Sakha:** winter driving on the Lena and long ice roads; hunters and fishers in the far north.
- **Buryats and Irkutsk Russians** on the Baikal ice and in the fire-prone steppe and forest.
- **Chukchi, Yupik, Koryak, Itelmen:** sea hunting and travel on sea ice, under their own safety rules.
- **Evens, Evenks, Yukaghirs:** herders and hunters in mountain and taiga; **Nivkh, Nanai, Ulchi** on the Amur ice.
- **Fishing crews, prospectors and shift workers;** poachers and those who inform on them.
- **Tourists and newcomers** against long-term residents; **Sakhalin and Primorye gamblers and casino workers.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for deaths, crashes and fires (official regional data); mixed for attitudes; thin for the line between brave and reckless in Indigenous terms outside hunting ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for proverbs; Chinese and Korean for gambling tourism.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drinking statistics are often misused to stereotype Indigenous peoples; use them only with care and with the communities' own framing. Poaching and illegal mining implicate living people. Hunting safety rules may sit inside restricted knowledge.
- **Held for review:** none.
