## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** national sports in uluses and in Yakutsk; olonkho contests; strong school-olympiad culture to test.
- **Buryats:** Aga and Khori steppe contests against Irkutsk Buryats; Buddhist teaching on envy and pride.
- **Herding peoples:** Chukchi, Koryak, Even, Evenk and Dolgan reindeer races and wrestling.
- **Coastal and river peoples:** Yupik, Itelmen, Nivkh, Nanai, Ulchi (boat, kayak and sled contests, *check*).
- **Russians:** port-city fan cultures in Vladivostok and Khabarovsk; Irkutsk bandy; Cossack riding contests revived in Amur and Zabaykalsky.
- **Sakhalin Koreans** (school striving, to test against data, not stereotype); **Birobidzhan** youth.
- **Women's contests** (pageants, wrestling, archery) against men's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festival sports and pro sport; mixed for school and business rivalry; thin for envy and its sanctions outside old ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for event names and folklore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pageants and "national" sports can be used to rank peoples; avoid such framing. Evil-eye remedies may be private. Some reindeer-herder contests double as ritual.
- **Held for review:** none.
