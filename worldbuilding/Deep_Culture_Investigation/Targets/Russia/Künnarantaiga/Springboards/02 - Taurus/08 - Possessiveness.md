# Künnarantaiga: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia (taiga, tundra and coast from Baikal to the Bering Strait, with its port cities). **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki, Chukchi, Yupik; Yiddish; Korean. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hunters and gatherers in the taiga** (Udege, Nanai, Evenk, Russian hunters): territory and the duty to share.
- **Coastal sea-mammal hunters** (Yupik, coastal Chukchi, Koryak): shares of a catch.
- **Reindeer Chukchi and Evens:** marriage alliances and herd claims.
- **Sakha and Buryat families,** where kin claims on children and in-laws may run differently.
- **Semeiskie Old Believers and Cossack descendants:** guarding daughters and households.
- **Port and fishing towns** with long absences at sea.
- **Shift-work families** living apart for weeks.
- **Sakhalin Koreans; Jews of Birobidzhan; migrant workers.**
- **Young city couples** against village elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; ethnography of sharing is rich; partner control by region is thin and sensitive.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; the Chukchi and Yupik terms for shares.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence by partners; outsider accounts of Chukchi marriage, which must be read critically; some hunting lore is kept within families. Rules and courts on family violence go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
