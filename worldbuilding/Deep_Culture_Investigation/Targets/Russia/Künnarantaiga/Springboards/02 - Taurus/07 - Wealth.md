## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Merchant cities:** Irkutsk and Kyakhta against the port cities of Vladivostok and Blagoveshchensk.
- **Gold and diamond towns:** Bodaybo, Susuman, Bilibino, Mirny, Udachny.
- **Fishing coasts:** Kamchatka, Sakhalin, the Kurils, Okhotsk shore.
- **Sakha cattle households** against **reindeer herders** (Even, Evenk, Chukchi, Koryak), where herd size marks wealth.
- **Buryat herders** of Aga and the steppe; Buddhist and shamanic families.
- **Coastal Yupik and Chukchi hunters,** whose wealth is shared catch.
- **Amur peoples** (Nanai, Ulchi, Nivkh) and the fish and fur trade.
- **Chinese traders, Korean farmers, Jews of Birobidzhan, Old Believers.**
- **The 1990s new rich** against pensioners and public-sector workers.
