## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** dairy and horse meat, kymys at festivals; summer pleasures against winter cold.
- **Buryats:** Cis- and Trans-Baikal; buuza, dairy and healing springs; Buddhist restraint.
- **Russian old settlers and Cossack descendants:** bath, berries, the dacha.
- **Semeiskie Old Believers:** fasts and strict rules on drink and dress.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples** (Nanai, Ulchi, Nivkh, Udege, Uilta): fish, fat and berry cuisines.
- **Chukchi, Yupik, Koryak and Itelmen:** sea-mammal and reindeer foods.
- **Sakhalin Koreans and Koryo-saram; Chinese traders; Jews of Birobidzhan; Muslim Tatars and migrants.**
- **Port cities** (Vladivostok, Nakhodka) against interior villages.
- **Sober villages** against drinking towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on food, music and dance; thin on touch and private pleasure.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Korean; the Amur languages for food words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol must not be framed through stereotypes about Indigenous peoples; some songs and dances belong to ceremonies; the Millionka's past carries ethnic stigma. Joy and play belong to Leo; sex to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
