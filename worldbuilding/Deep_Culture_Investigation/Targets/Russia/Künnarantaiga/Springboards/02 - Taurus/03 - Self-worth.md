# Künnarantaiga: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia (from Irkutsk and Buryatia across Sakha and the Amur to Kamchatka and Chukotka). **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi, Even, Evenki, Nanai; Yiddish; Korean. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha** in Yakutsk and villages: revival-era pride against language loss in towns.
- **Buryats** of Ulan-Ude, Aga and the Irkutsk side: Buddhist, shamanic and Orthodox families may ground worth differently.
- **Small peoples** (Even, Evenk, Chukchi, Koryak, Nivkh, Nanai, Udege, Yukaghir, Itelmen): boarding-school generations against their grandchildren.
- **Kolyma and Chukotka settlers:** "Northerners" by pride, and those left behind when the mines closed.
- **Port and fleet towns** (Nakhodka, Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, Korsakov): sailors' standing, the bich.
- **Semeiskie Old Believers:** worth through faith and household.
- **Jews of Birobidzhan; Sakhalin Koreans; Chinese and Central Asian migrant workers.**
- **Men and women** within each: provider roles in shift-work and herding economies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; literature and memoir rich, measured self-esteem by region thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Korean for Sakhalin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide; boarding-school harm; ethnic slurs (describe, never quote); Gulag memory.
- **Held for review:** none.
