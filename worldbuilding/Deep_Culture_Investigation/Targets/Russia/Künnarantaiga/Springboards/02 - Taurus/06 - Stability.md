# Künnarantaiga: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia (old settler valleys, nomad routes, closed ports and outflow towns between Baikal and the Bering Strait). **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki, Even, Nivkh, Udege; Japanese; Korean. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-settler Russians** (chaldony, Russkoye Ustye, Lena villagers) against Soviet-era newcomers.
- **Settled Sakha** in the central districts against **nomadic Evens, Evenks and reindeer Chukchi.**
- **Resettled peoples:** Nivkh, Udege, Oroch, Uilta, and coastal Yupik moved from closed villages.
- **Cossack descendants** along the Amur and Ussuri.
- **Semeiskie Old Believers,** settled since the 18th century.
- **Closed and military towns** (Vladivostok before 1992, Vilyuchinsk, Fokino).
- **Outflow towns** in Kolyma and Chukotka against growing Yakutsk.
- **Sakhalin Koreans,** who stayed after 1945, and **Japanese** who left.
- **Shift workers** and their home towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on migration and Soviet resettlement; thin on everyday routine by people.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Japanese for Karafuto memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced resettlement and deportation; military towns; grave sites. Order and tradition as duty belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
