# Künnarantaiga: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia (twelve eastern units, Irkutsk to Chukotka). **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Evenki, Even, Chukchi, Nivkh, Itelmen, Udege; Chinese and Japanese for the Amur and Sakhalin past; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Reindeer herders:** state-farm successors against family and clan communities (Even, Evenk, Chukchi, Koryak, Dolgan of Anabar).
- **Sakha alaas villages** against Yakutsk flat owners.
- **Buryats:** Cis-Baikal, whose pastures settlers took from the 1890s *check*, against Trans-Baikal Khori and Aga.
- **Cossack descendants** of the Amur, Ussuri and Transbaikal hosts, once holders of granted land.
- **Semeiskie Old Believers** and their farmsteads.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples:** clan places among the Nivkh, Nanai, Udege, Ulchi.
- **Kuril and South Sakhalin residents,** living on land once Japanese and Ainu.
- **Chinese tenants and their Russian landlords; JAO collective-farm heirs.**
- **Fishers of Kamchatka and Sakhalin;** Commander Islands Aleuts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet and post-Soviet tenure in ethnography; budget and census data good; attitudes thin by region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Chinese for 1900; Japanese for Karafuto.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Kuril dispute with Japan; Blagoveshchensk 1900; the 1937 Korean deportation; Indigenous land claims. Statutes on land, quotas and clan communities belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); here only practice and attitude.
- **Held for review:** none.
