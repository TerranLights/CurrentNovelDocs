## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Semeiskie Old Believers** of Buryatia and Zabaykalsky.
- **Descendants of political exiles** in Irkutsk, Chita and Yakutsk.
- **Chukchi,** with their memory of resisting conquest; **Koryaks** and **Yukaghirs** with their own histories.
- **Reindeer herders** who held out against collectivization.
- **Udege, Nanai and other Amur peoples** defending forest and rivers.
- **Vladivostok motorists** and port workers.
- **Khabarovsk townspeople** in 2020.
- **Northern holdouts** in Kolyma and Chukotka.
- **Sakhalin Koreans; Kuril residents; Cossack descendants.**
- **Sakha and Buryats,** where firmness may be framed through clan, faith or revival.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on exile, schism and protest history; thin on everyday stubbornness and its words in the Indigenous languages.
