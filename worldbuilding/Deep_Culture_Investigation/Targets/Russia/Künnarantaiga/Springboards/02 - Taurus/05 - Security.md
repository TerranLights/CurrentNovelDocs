# Künnarantaiga: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia (the permafrost interior, the Amur valley, the earthquake coast of Kamchatka, Sakhalin and the Kurils, and the Arctic Northeast). **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; Chukchi, Siberian Yupik, Itelmen, Even; Korean. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Arctic Sakha, Even and Evenk villages,** dependent on the supply run and on herds.
- **Chukchi, Yupik and Koryak coastal hunters,** whose security rests on the hunt and on sharing.
- **Amur valley farmers and towns** (Amur, JAO, Khabarovsk) living with floods.
- **Kamchatka, Sakhalin and Kuril residents** living with earthquakes and tsunamis.
- **Monotown and shift workers** (Mirny, Bilibino, Bodaybo, Kolyma).
- **Pensioners** who stayed in the North against those who left for the "mainland".
- **Buryat herders** of Aga and the steppe districts.
- **Port and fishing towns** with seasonal work.
- **Migrants** (Chinese, Central Asian, Korean) without the same safety net.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on disasters and the northern economy; household security by people thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Korean; the Chukchi and Yupik terms need checking.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Soviet-era secrecy around disasters; sacred sites (oboo) and their rites; insecurity talk can touch politics.
- **Held for review:** none.
