# Künnarantaiga: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat; Korean, Japanese and Yiddish for specific communities. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean again. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** fire-and-smoke cleansing in Aiyy practice against Orthodox confession.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist (lamas, juniper, Sagaalgan cleansing) against shamanic purification.
- **Evenki, Evens, Chukchi:** purification after a death or a hunt, where published by the peoples themselves.
- **Old Believers (Semeiskie, Primorye returnees):** purity of household and dishes.
- **Orthodox Russians and Cossack families:** Lent, confession and the bathhouse.
- **Sakhalin Koreans and Birobidzhan Jews:** expelled or resettled peoples, and their own cleansing rites.
- **Irkutsk and Baikal-shore communities:** environmental purity as identity.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on repression and Baikal; mixed on folk cleansing; thin on the smaller peoples' rites.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Japanese and Korean for the expulsions; Tibetan for the medical canon.
