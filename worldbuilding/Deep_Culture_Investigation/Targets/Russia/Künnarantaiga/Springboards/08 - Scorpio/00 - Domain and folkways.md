# Künnarantaiga: Domain and folkways (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga (Күннарантайга), region Russia: Sakha (Yakutia), Irkutsk, Buryatia, Zabaykalsky, Amur, the Jewish Autonomous Oblast, Khabarovsk, Primorsky, Sakhalin with the Kurils, Magadan, Kamchatka and Chukotka. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Evenki, Even, Chukchi, Koryak, Nanai, Nivkh, Yukaghir; Yiddish and Korean for two settler communities. **Concept in one line:** death and transformation: how the culture faces death, power and what is hidden, with its death ways and sex ways, shared resources and inheritance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** Orthodox, Aiyy revival and mixed practice to be separated.
- **Buryats:** eastern (Buddhist-leaning) against western, Irkutsk-side (shamanic-leaning) groups.
- **Evenki and Evens:** dispersed across Sakha, Amur, Khabarovsk, Buryatia and Magadan; herding against settled lives.
- **Chukchi, Koryaks, Itelmens, Kamchadals, Yupik and Kereks:** the northeast, coastal against reindeer peoples.
- **Yukaghirs and Chuvans:** small peoples of the Kolyma basin.
- **Amur and Sakhalin peoples:** Nanai, Ulchi, Udege, Orochi, Negidals, Nivkhs, Uilta (Oroks); the Ainu past of Sakhalin and the Kurils.
- **Soyots and Tofalars:** small Sayan peoples.
- **Russian settler layers:** Cossacks, Old Believers, Gulag survivors' descendants, Soviet-era northern migrants.
- **Green Wedge Ukrainians, Sakhalin Koreans, Birobidzhan Jews, Tatars, Irkutsk Poles:** each with its own rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Sakha, Buryats and the Gulag; mixed for the Amur peoples; thin for Kereks, Chuvans, Tofalars and Soyots.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Sakha and Buryat; Yiddish, Korean and Japanese (Karafuto period) for specific communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic knowledge, burial sites, human remains in museums; Gulag mass graves; Indigenous suicide. The tracker lists only "Russia" as pre-war lands; these twelve units come from the territory reference, and the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** none.
