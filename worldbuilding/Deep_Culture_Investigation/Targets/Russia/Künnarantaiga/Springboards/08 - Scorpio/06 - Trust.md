*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** kin and district ties against trust in Yakutsk's institutions.
- **Buryats:** clan networks; Buddhist and shamanic communities.
- **Evenki, Evens, Chukchi, Yupik:** exchange partners and hospitality on the land.
- **Russian settlers:** old-settler villages against Soviet-era migrant towns (BAM, Magadan, Kolyma).
- **Border cities:** Blagoveshchensk, Khabarovsk, Vladivostok, with Chinese traders and workers.
- **Sakhalin Koreans, Birobidzhan Jews:** minority networks and the state's promises.
- **Old Believers:** trust inside a closed faith community.
- **Military and naval towns:** trust in command, closed towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: regional survey items exist but are scattered; ethnography rich for border trade and the northeast.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Chinese and Korean for cross-border and Sakhalin sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** distrust of Chinese neighbors is a charged topic, to be framed as a question; trust in the state is politically sensitive; RGADA records carry a colonial frame.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search FOM's site for "Георейтинг доверие" and list the Far East region tables.

4. Read the chapters on trade in *On the Edge*, then search "Благовещенск Хэйхэ доверие".
5. Search regional hunting forums for "зимовье правила" and note what is expected of strangers.
