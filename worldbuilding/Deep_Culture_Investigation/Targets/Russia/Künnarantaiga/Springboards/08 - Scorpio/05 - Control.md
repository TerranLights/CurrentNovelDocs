# Künnarantaiga: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so (self-control is Capricorn's; freedom from control is Aries's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** cattle and horse breeders of the central alaas villages against Arctic hunters and fishers.
- **Evenki and Evens:** herders and hunters whose ideas of luck and risk differ from settled people's.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist teaching on letting go against shamanic bargaining with spirits.
- **Irkutsk and Angara resettled villagers:** descendants of those moved for the dams.
- **BAM towns (Tynda, Severobaikalsk, Neryungri):** Soviet migrants' sense of mastery over the land.
- **Chukchi, Yupik, Koryaks:** life under border-zone and quota controls.
- **Amur-flood communities in the Jewish Autonomous Oblast and Khabarovsk:** after the 2013 flood.
- **Old Believers and Cossack families:** household authority and church control.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on dams, BAM and fires; mixed on couples and households; thin on locus-of-control measures for the region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Evenki for luck and hunting terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic abuse; NGO status (some centers are on official registers that restrict them); border-zone rules; Indigenous hunting rights. Law on these points is Libra's and Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
