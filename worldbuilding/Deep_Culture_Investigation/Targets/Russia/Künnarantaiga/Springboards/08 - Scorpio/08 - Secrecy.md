# Künnarantaiga: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Evenki; Japanese, Korean and Chinese for specific communities. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be hidden; privacy is a facet. (The law of state secrets is Libra's and Capricorn's.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha and Evenki hunters:** avoidance speech and kept knowledge of the land.
- **Buryats:** shamanic lineages and Buddhist initiation as two kinds of restriction.
- **Old Believers:** a faith kept apart from outsiders.
- **Closed-town and military families:** Kamchatka, Primorye, Amur.
- **Gulag and exile descendants (Magadan, Kolyma, Irkutsk):** family silences.
- **Sakhalin Koreans and stay-behind Japanese:** hidden or doubled identities.
- **Birobidzhan Jews:** identity shown or hidden over time.
- **Chinese residents past and present:** the port cities' hidden quarters.
- **LGBT people in the region's cities:** privacy under pressure, approached only through safe published sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet secret projects now revealed; mixed on family secrets; thin, by design, on restricted Indigenous knowledge.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Evenki; Japanese, Korean, Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic and Buddhist restricted teachings must not be sought beyond what holders publish; military sites; LGBT people face legal risk, so no contact that could expose anyone; several outlets carry official labels that restrict access.
- **Held for review:** none.
