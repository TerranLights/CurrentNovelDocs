*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat; Korean and Japanese for Sakhalin and the Kurils. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause owed loyalty. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats:** clan loyalty and the Mongol heritage of judging traitors.
- **Sakha:** stories of the conquest and of toyons who chose sides.
- **Cossack descendants (Transbaikal, Amur, Ussuri):** the Civil War and emigration as loyalty and desertion.
- **Military and naval towns:** oaths, deserters, defectors.
- **Gulag-descended and criminal subcultures (Magadan, Komsomolsk-on-Amur):** the code against informing.
- **Chukchi, Even and other northern peoples:** promises of the state, as they describe them.
- **Sakhalin Koreans, Kuril and Sakhalin Russians, Japanese former residents:** three sides of 1945.
- **Old Believers:** the schism itself as a story of apostasy.
