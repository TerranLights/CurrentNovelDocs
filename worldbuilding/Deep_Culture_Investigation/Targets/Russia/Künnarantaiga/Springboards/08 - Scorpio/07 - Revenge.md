# Künnarantaiga: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Chukchi, Nivkh, Udege; Japanese for the Civil War reprisals; Korean for Sakhalin. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing or deter the next wrong. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sakha:** clan-war legends against Orthodox and Aiyy teaching on retribution.
- **Chukchi and Koryaks:** war tales and reprisal memory.
- **Nivkhs, Nanai, Udege:** feud payment and animal revenge beliefs.
- **Buryats:** clan feuds; Buddhist anger teaching.
- **Russian Cossack and partisan descendants:** Civil War reprisals.
- **Criminal subcultures of the port and camp cities:** codes of payback.
- **Rural Primorye and Khabarovsk towns:** sympathy for avengers of police abuse, as a question.
- **Sakhalin Koreans and Japanese former residents:** 1945 reprisal fears.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the Civil War and the 1990s; mixed on folklore; thin on measured attitudes.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Chukchi and Nivkh for folklore; Japanese and Korean for 1920 and 1945.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** school attacks and recent violent cases; the Primorye case is political; feud stories may name living families.
- **Held for review:** none.
