# Künnarantaiga: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Künnarantaiga, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Chukchi, Itelmen; Chinese for the border epidemics. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, a family, a community or a country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kamchatka and Kuril residents:** volcanoes, quakes, tsunami.
- **Sakha river towns and alaas villages:** floods, permafrost, wildfire smoke.
- **Chukotka and Magadan:** 1990s collapse, supply failures, depopulation.
- **Amur and Primorye border cities:** epidemics and cross-border fears.
- **Buryats:** drought rites and mutual help in the steppe districts of Aga and Khori.
- **Itelmens, Koryaks, Chukchi, Evens:** crisis on the land, far from services.
- **Old Believers:** the end-times frame for hard times, as a question.
- **Soviet-era migrant towns (BAM, mining towns):** crisis when the employer closes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on natural hazards and the 1990s; mixed on mutual help; thin on how small peoples frame crisis in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Chukchi, Koryak and Itelmen for oral histories; Chinese for the plague years.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide in northern communities; blame on Chinese migrants in epidemics; current emergencies may be reported only in part.
- **Held for review:** none.
