## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats**: cross-border kin in Mongolia, Buddhist protocol, a history of envoys.
- **Russians of the Chinese border** (Zabaykalsky Krai) against **interior cities**: daily dealing with Chinese traders.
- **Altaians** (zaisans), **Khakas** and **Shors**: clan spokesmen before officials.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets**: Indigenous associations and company talks; **Evenks, Dolgans, Kets, Selkups**: smaller voices with fewer bodies.
- **Kazakhs** of the Omsk and Altai borderlands; **Germans** (ties to Germany); **Central Asian migrants** and their associations.
- **Labor towns** of the Kuzbass and Norilsk: unions as negotiators.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the conquest era and Russian-Qing relations; mixed for present Indigenous negotiations (association sites, press); thin on everyday tact in Siberian speech specifically.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Mongolian; Chinese for border sources; German; Khanty and Nenets for association materials in those languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Indigenous activists and associations have been labeled "extremist" or "foreign agent" since 2022; Russia's Arctic Council role is suspended or reduced *check*; China-related land deals are a heated political topic.
