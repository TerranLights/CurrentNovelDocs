# Siberia: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat; Altai; Khakas; Siberian Tatar; Khanty and Mansi; Nenets; Evenki. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old settler villages** against **towns built by camps** (Norilsk, Igarka, the Taishet and Kraslag camp zones): commune justice against the camp's.
- **Descendants of deportees** (Volga Germans, Balts, Poles, western Ukrainians, Kalmyks who stayed) and of exiles: memories of injustice the majority may not share.
- **Buryats** (Buddhist and shamanist), **Altaians** and **Khakas**: clan ways of answering wrongs and their revival.
- **Ob-Ugric peoples, Nenets, Evenks, Selkups, Kets**: oaths, payment for harm, and their meeting with state courts.
- **Siberian Tatars** (Muslim ethics of justice); **Old Believers** (avoidance of state courts *check*); **Cossack** communities.
- **Prison-colony towns**, their guards' families and prisoners' families; **oil and mining workers** in Yugra, Yamal and the Kuzbass (the 1989 strikes).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tsarist exile and the Gulag; mixed for today's courts (federal-level data abound, regional splits take digging); thin for Indigenous justice outside Soviet ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian above all; Buryat and Khakas for clan sources; Khanty and Mansi for oath and bear-feast material; German for the Volga German memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** repression archives are being closed; Memorial is liquidated; several outlets and NGOs are labeled "foreign agents" or "undesirable"; prisoners and their families can be at risk; bear-related oaths touch restricted Ob-Ugric knowledge.
- **Held for review:** none.
