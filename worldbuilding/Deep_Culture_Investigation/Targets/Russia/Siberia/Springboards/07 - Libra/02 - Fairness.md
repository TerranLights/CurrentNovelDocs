# Siberia: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with Siberian dialect forms); Buryat; Altai; Khakas; Evenki; Nenets; Khanty. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: one's due share, taking turns, and the same rules for everyone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old settler villages** (first-claim land) against **later settlers** of the Stolypin era (allotted land) and **Soviet new towns**: three histories of who got what.
- **Northern shift workers** (вахтовики) and residents on Northern pay against **southern Siberian** workers without it.
- **Evenks, Dolgans, Nganasans, Nenets, Khanty**: hunting and herding shares, each with its own rule.
- **Buryats**, **Altaians**, **Khakas**, **Shors**, **Teleuts**: inheritance and the shares of the feast.
- **Siberian Tatars** (Islamic inheritance shares *check*); **German and Mennonite villages** (cooperative traditions); **Old Believers**.
- **Resource regions** (Yamal, Yugra, Norilsk, the Kuzbass) against **budget-receiving regions** (Altai Republic, Buryatia, Zabaykalsky Krai): fairness between regions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Pay, budget and survey data are rich; Indigenous sharing rules are well documented in Soviet ethnography but need reading against the herders' and hunters' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, with Siberian dialect; Evenki and Nenets for sharing terms; Buryat for inheritance.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** none major; the regional-grievance theme ("Siberia feeds Moscow") can touch separatism laws, so handle political material with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
