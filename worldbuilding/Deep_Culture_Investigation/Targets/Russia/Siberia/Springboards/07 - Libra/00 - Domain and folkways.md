# Siberia: The domain: partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Shor, Siberian Tatar; Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Selkup, Ket, Evenki, Dolgan; German (Siberian German and Mennonite villages). **Concept in one line:** what a fair bargain and a good partnership are (marriage, contracts, open enemies, disputes, the letter of the law).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian old settlers (старожилы) against later migrants** (Stolypin-era peasants, Soviet builders, oil and gas shift workers): different bargains with land and state.
- **Old Believers** (Semeiskie, Kerzhaks) and **Siberian Cossacks**: their own marriage and dispute customs.
- **Buryats** of the republic, Aga and Ust-Orda: Buddhist and shamanist, eastern and western clans.
- **Altaians**, northern against southern; **Khakas, Shors, Teleuts, Chulyms, Tofalars**; **Siberian Tatars** (Muslim marriage).
- **Khanty and Mansi**, **Nenets** (tundra and forest), **Selkups, Kets, Evenks, Dolgans, Nganasans, Enets**.
- **Germans and Mennonites**, **Kazakhs** of the southern border, exile descendants, Central Asian labor migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Scope:** the tracker gives the pre-war lands only as "Russia", so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for western and eastern Siberia, from Tyumen and Yamal to Transbaikalia.
- **Data:** rich in Russian for law and statistics; mixed for Buryats and Altaians; thin, and filtered through Soviet ethnography, for the small peoples of the North.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat; Altai; Khakas; Tatar; Khanty and Mansi; Nenets; Evenki; German.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ob-Ugric bear-feast songs and some clan knowledge are restricted; Memorial was ordered closed (2021-2022); some outlets carry "foreign agent" labels; понятия and penal colonies need care.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library catalog for «обычное право бурят» and «Устав об управлении инородцев».
2. Pull Rosstat's ЕМИСС series «число браков» and «число разводов» for each Siberian region, 2000 to the latest year.
3. Search sudact.ru for «родовые угодья» with «соглашение» (land-agreement cases in Yugra and Yamal).
4. Search the Max Planck Institute for Social Anthropology site for "Siberia" with "reciprocity" and "marriage".
