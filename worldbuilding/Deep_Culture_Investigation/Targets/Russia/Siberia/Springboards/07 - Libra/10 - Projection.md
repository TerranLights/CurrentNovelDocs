## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-settler Russians** and the **сибиряк** identity against **Moscow**, and against **later newcomers**.
- **Volga Germans**, **Poles**, **Balts** and others deported as "enemies", and their neighbors.
- **Central Asian migrants** and **Chinese traders and workers**: the current targets of blame, in their own words.
- **Buryats**, **Altaians**, **Khakas**: envy and gossip as named causes of harm.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenks**: how they were projected onto, and how they mirror outsiders.
- **City rivalries**: Novosibirsk, Omsk, Krasnoyarsk, Tomsk, Irkutsk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Russian images of Siberia and its peoples and for Soviet enemy-making; mixed for current xenophobia (national polls, NGO data); thin for Indigenous peoples' own views of outsiders.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat; Khanty and Mansi; Chinese, Kyrgyz, Uzbek and Tajik for the views of those blamed.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs for migrants are recorded only as data; SOVA has faced official pressure and Memorial is closed; Siberian regionalism can be treated as separatism; the bear-ceremony skits belong to restricted ritual.
- **Held for review:** none.
