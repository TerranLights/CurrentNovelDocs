# Siberia: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryat Buddhists** (Tibetan medicine, the middle way) against **Buryat shamanists** (offerings to place spirits).
- **Altaians** of the White Faith (Ак Јаҥ) and of other paths; **Khakas** and **Shors**: calendar rites and sacred mountains.
- **Evenks, Kets, Selkups**: cosmologies of tiers and the shaman's role.
- **Khanty and Mansi**, **Nenets**: sacred places and limits on land use, against oil and gas.
- **Baikal and Angara communities**, including villages moved by the dams.
- **Akademgorodok and other science towns**; **oil and mining towns**; **Orthodox, Old Believer and Muslim (Siberian Tatar)** keepers of fasting calendars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the environmental side (Baikal, dams, spills); mixed on political balance (federal data dominant); mixed on health as balance (Tibetan medicine well studied, Russian folk medicine scattered).
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and some Tibetan terms; Altai and Khakas for calendar rites; Evenki for cosmology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic rites and sacred-site knowledge may be restricted; the Ukok burial is a live grievance; environmental NGOs face closure and "foreign agent" status.
- **Held for review:** none.
