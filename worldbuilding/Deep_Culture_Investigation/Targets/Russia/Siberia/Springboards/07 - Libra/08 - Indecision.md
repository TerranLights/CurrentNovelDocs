# Siberia: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat; Evenki; Khanty and Mansi; Altai. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northerners** of Norilsk, Yamal and Yugra (the choice to stay or leave) against **southern Siberian** city dwellers.
- **Buryats**: Buddhist and shamanist advisers in personal choices; clan assemblies.
- **Evenks, Kets, Selkups, Dolgans**: assemblies and the pace of collective decisions.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets**: consultation with kin and spirits before a move.
- **Altaians, Khakas, Shors**: clan councils and seasonal decisions.
- **Old Believers**, **Mennonites** and **Siberian Tatars**: religious ways of deciding; **young professionals** of Novosibirsk and Tomsk facing the choice to emigrate.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Language and folklore sources are rich; civic delay is well covered in the press; direct studies of personal indecision in Siberia are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat; Evenki; Khanty and Mansi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** decisions to leave the country or avoid the 2022 mobilization (felt strongly in Buryatia and other republics) are politically dangerous to ask about; divination practice can be restricted to practitioners.
- **Held for review:** none.
