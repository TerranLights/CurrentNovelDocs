# Siberia: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat; Siberian Tatar; German (Mennonite sources); Khanty and Nenets. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even at the price of one's own or another's rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers** (withdrawal), **Tolstoyan** descendants and **Mennonites** (pacifism): three different refusals of conflict.
- **Women in villages** against **women in large cities**: silence about violence at home, and the help within reach.
- **Mining and metal towns** (Kuzbass, Norilsk): endurance of danger and pollution for wages.
- **Buryats** (Buddhist forbearance) and **Siberian Tatars** (Islamic patience).
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenks**: accommodation with industry and officials, in their own accounts.
- **Central Asian migrants**: silence enforced by fear of losing status.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on the Gulag, Old Believers and industrial disasters; mixed on domestic violence (NGO data, little official breakdown); thin on present Indigenous accommodation in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; German (Mennonite archives); Buryat; Tatar; Khanty and Nenets.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors of violence must not be exposed; anti-war speech since 2022 is criminalized, so silence about the war is not a free choice and must not be read as consent; some women's NGOs are labeled "foreign agents"; Mennonite and Old Believer communities may be closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Russian State Library catalog for «Воспоминания крестьян-толстовцев» and «коммуна Жизнь и труд Кузнецк».

3. Search eLibrary.ru for «пословицы о терпении семья насилие» and «сабыр сибирские татары».
4. Search Russian news archives for «Листвяжная предупреждения шахтёров» and «Распадская протест 2010».
5. Search Mennonite archives for "Slavgorod" and "Kulunda" with "Forstei" or "alternative service".
