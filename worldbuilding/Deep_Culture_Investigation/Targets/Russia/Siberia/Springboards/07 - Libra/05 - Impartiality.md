# Siberia: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat; Altai; Siberian Tatar; Khanty; Evenki. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Big-city professionals** (Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk, Tomsk) against **small towns and villages**, where an official is a neighbor or a relative.
- **Buryats**: home-place (нютаг) networks against republic-wide merit rules.
- **Evenks, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Dolgans** in small settlements: kin among officials and the state's distance.
- **Siberian Tatars** and **Central Asian migrants**: dealings with police and offices as outsiders.
- **Residents of closed towns** and **company towns** (Norilsk, oil towns of Yugra): privilege and loyalty attached to place and employer.
- **Old Believers** and **Mennonites**: communities that choose their own leaders and avoid outside offices *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in national-level corruption and network studies; mixed for regional breakdowns; thin for Indigenous communities' own rules on kin and judging.
- **Languages needed:** Russian above all; Buryat for home-place networks; Khanty and Evenki for settlement studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Golos, RFE/RL and Transparency International are labeled "foreign agent" or "undesirable" in Russia; sources inside the region can be at risk; corruption cases can be politically driven, so read each with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
