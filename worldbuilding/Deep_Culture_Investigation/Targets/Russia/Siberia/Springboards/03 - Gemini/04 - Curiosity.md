# Siberia: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Siberian Tatar, Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Khanty, Evenki; German for 18th-century expedition writing. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore; and how far a culture encourages, channels, limits or punishes it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Science and university towns** (Akademgorodok, Tomsk, Koltsovo) against **one-industry and closed towns** (Seversk, Zheleznogorsk), where some questions were forbidden.
- **Russian old settlers and Old Believers**, who may treat outside novelty with caution; to check, not assume.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Selkups**: rules on asking and on sacred places by clan.
- **Kets**, much studied by outsiders: how they see researchers' curiosity.
- **Altaians** (south and north), **Khakas, Shors and Teleuts**: archaeology on their lands.
- **Evenks and Dolgans** of the Yenisei and Taymyr: wide nomadic routes, so a separate case for curiosity about new ground; to check.
- **Siberian Tatars** and **Siberian Germans**.
- **Exiles' descendants** (Decembrists, Poles, political exiles), a line of amateur naturalists.
- **Western Buryats**, if inside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for expeditions, museums and science history; mixed for local-lore and amateur traditions; thin for everyday attitudes to nosy questions.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; German and Latin for 18th-century expedition writing; the minority languages for tales.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** burials, sacred sites and the Ukok case are live grievances; sacred places of Khanty and Mansi are restricted; closed towns and military sites may still limit what can be asked.
- **Held for review:** none.
