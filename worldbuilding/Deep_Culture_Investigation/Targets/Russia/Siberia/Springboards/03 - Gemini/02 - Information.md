# Siberia: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Altai, Khakas, Siberian Tatar, Buryat, Nenets, Khanty, Selkup, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how a culture gathers, passes on, trusts, withholds and uses it: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Big cities (Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk, Omsk, Irkutsk) against villages and closed or one-industry towns** (Seversk, Zheleznogorsk, Norilsk): access to news and permission to know differ.
- **Tundra herders on Yamal and Taymyr** (Nenets, Dolgans, Nganasans) against settled fishers and workers.
- **Khanty and Mansi near oil fields**: information from companies and the state against kin channels.
- **Evenks and Kets on the Yenisei**: small, scattered communities with few channels.
- **Altai and Khakas republics**: their own-language media and republic-level press.
- **Siberian Tatar villages**, where mosques may be a news place.
- **Old Believer communities**, who may avoid television and the internet; to check, not to assume.
- **Shift workers on вахта** and **migrants from Central Asia**, whose news comes from far away.
- **Older and younger generations**: TV against Telegram and VK.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press history and archives; mixed for present-day media use by region; thin for minority-language channels and herders' information networks.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; German for Müller and the 18th-century academic expeditions; the minority languages for local media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some independent outlets and pollsters carry the "foreign agent" label or are blocked; repression records are sensitive; some land and sacred-site knowledge is restricted by its peoples. Press law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Open the GPNTB SB RAS catalog and search «периодическая печать Сибири XIX век» for digitized regional newspapers.
2. Find the latest Rosstat «использование информационных технологий домашними хозяйствами» tables and list the Siberian regions.
3. Search eLibrary.ru for «Красный чум» with «Ямал» or «Таймыр».
4. Search for «аудитив селькупский» and «эвиденциальность хантыйский» to find the grammar studies.
5. Search Open List for a Siberian town to test how far the repression records reach.
