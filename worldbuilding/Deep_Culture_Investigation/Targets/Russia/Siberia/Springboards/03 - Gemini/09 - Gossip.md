# Siberia: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Siberian Tatar, Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Evenki, Khanty, Nenets. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small old-settler villages** against **large cities**: where everyone knows everyone, and where no one does.
- **Closed towns and one-industry towns** (Seversk, Norilsk): gossip where speech about the plant or the base is limited.
- **Shift-work camps** in Khanty-Mansi and Yamal: talk among men far from home, and about families left behind.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets herding camps** and **Evenk and Ket settlements**: very small worlds of talk.
- **Altai, Khakas, Shor and Teleut villages**: clan talk and teasing songs, if any.
- **Siberian Tatar communities**: Islamic teaching on backbiting.
- **Old Believers**: closed congregations and talk about those who leave.
- **Migrant communities**, where rumor about police checks may travel fast.
- **Schoolchildren and teenagers online**, against **pensioners** on the bench and in the shop queue.
- **Western Buryats**, if inside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for denunciation archives and folklore; mixed for urban legends and online gossip; thin for everyday gossip in small peoples' own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar and Buryat for religious and everyday terms; the northern languages for camp talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Kemerovo fire is a live grief; some scholars and outlets carry the "foreign agent" label; sorcery accusations and denunciations touch living families. Slander and defamation as law go to Libra and Capricorn. The bench-gossip "babushka" is a stereotype to test, not adopt.
- **Held for review:** none.
