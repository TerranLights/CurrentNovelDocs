# Siberia: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Siberian Tatar, Buryat, Evenki, Altai, Khakas, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Ket. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how a culture understands the sibling bond: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian old-settler families** against **Stolypin-era and Soviet-era migrant families**: different family sizes and inheritance habits.
- **Old Believers**: large households of married brothers.
- **Siberian Tatars, Altaians, Khakas, Shors, Teleuts**: Turkic age-ranked sibling terms and youngest-child customs.
- **Khanty and Mansi**, by moiety: who counts as a sibling across clan lines.
- **Nenets, Selkups, Enets, Nganasans**: herding brothers sharing herds.
- **Evenks and Dolgans**; **Kets**.
- **Western Buryats**, if inside.
- **Siberian Germans and Mennonites**: large families, emigration splitting siblings between countries.
- **Central Asian and Caucasus migrants**: siblings in chain migration.
- **Urban one- and two-child families** against rural large families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for kinship terms and Soviet household ethnography; mixed for sibling care of parents; thin for present-day sibling relations among small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; each minority language's kin terms; English for the cross-cultural databases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children in care, boarding-school separations and sibling violence are private and painful; levirate and in-law avoidance belong to living families. Inheritance as law goes to Libra and Capricorn; here only family custom.
- **Held for review:** none.
