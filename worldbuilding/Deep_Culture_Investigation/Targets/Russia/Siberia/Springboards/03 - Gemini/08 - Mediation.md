# Siberia: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Altai, Khakas, Siberian Tatar, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenki, Buryat. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and, more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian old-settler villages** (commune and church as settlement venues) against **cities** with professional mediators.
- **Khanty and Mansi** near oil fields, where companies and communities need brokers.
- **Yamal and Taymyr herders** (Nenets, Dolgans, Nganasans): brigade leaders and traders as go-betweens.
- **Altaians**: clan heads; south against north.
- **Khakas, Shors, Teleuts**: congresses and elders' councils.
- **Siberian Tatars**: imams and elders.
- **Evenks, Kets, Selkups**: very small communities, where a go-between may be a relative.
- **Kuzbass and other mining towns**: labor go-betweens.
- **Central Asian and Caucasus migrants**: diaspora leaders.
- **Western Buryats**, if inside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial-era go-betweens and labor history; mixed for company-community brokering; thin for everyday family and neighbor peacemaking.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the minority languages for courtship and religious terms; English for the Western ethnographies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** oil-field disputes and criminal arbitration are sensitive; spirit go-betweens belong to their peoples. Customary law, courts and arbitration as law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
