# Siberia: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Altai, Khakas, Teleut, Siberian Tatar, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenki, Ket, Buryat; German and Polish for diaspora ties. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected (or not).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-settler villages** with dense kin and godparent ties against **new industrial cities** built by people from everywhere.
- **Vakhta workers and their families** in Tyumen oblast, Khanty-Mansi and Yamal.
- **Khanty and Mansi** by moiety and river; **Nenets** by clan; **Selkups, Kets, Enets, Nganasans**: very small populations.
- **Altai clans** (south and north), **Khakas, Shors, Teleuts**.
- **Siberian Tatars**, whose ties may run through mosques and village networks.
- **Evenks and Dolgans** spread across wide districts.
- **Siberian Germans, Poles, Ukrainians**: ties to distant homelands.
- **Central Asian and Caucasus migrants** and their hometown societies.
- **Old Believers**; **Western Buryats**, if inside.
- **Elderly people living alone** in shrinking villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for *blat* and Soviet-era kinship ethnography; mixed for loneliness and social contact by region; thin for present-day ties in small peoples' communities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; the minority languages for kin and friendship terms; German and Polish for diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ties with the dead and clan rites are private; migrants' networks can draw police attention; favors and cronyism shade into corruption, which is law: Libra and Capricorn. Belonging is Cancer's and community Aquarius's; this sheet keeps to the ties themselves.
- **Held for review:** none.
