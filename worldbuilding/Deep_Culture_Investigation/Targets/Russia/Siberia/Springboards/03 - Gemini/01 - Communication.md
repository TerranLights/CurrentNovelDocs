# Siberia: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenki, Altai, Khakas, Shor, Siberian Tatar, Buryat. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people, by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media; and what a culture believes good communication is and what it is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-settler villages against new industrial cities** (Norilsk, Surgut, Bratsk): mixed-origin cities may have no shared local etiquette.
- **Khanty and Mansi** by river basin: avoidance and respect speech may vary with clan and dialect.
- **Tundra Nenets against forest Nenets and Selkups**: camp life and taiga life shape talk differently.
- **Evenks of Evenkia against Evenks in Buryat or Sakha borderlands**: different neighbors, different contact languages.
- **Altai-kizhi, Telengit and the northern Altaians** (Tubalar, Chelkan, Kumandin): epic speech and blessing customs may not be shared.
- **Khakas, Shors and Teleuts**: Turkic, but with different Christian, shamanic and Russian layers.
- **Siberian Tatars against Volga Tatar migrants**: same written standard, different home speech.
- **Western Buryats**, if inside: respect speech to elders and in-laws.
- **Deaf communities**, and **Central Asian migrants** speaking Russian as a second language at work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and Soviet ethnography; mixed for present-day conversation; thin for everyday talk among small peoples in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; each minority language for its genres; some Finnish and Hungarian Ob-Ugric scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** speech to spirits and shamanic texts can be restricted; in-law avoidance belongs to living families. Free-speech law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
