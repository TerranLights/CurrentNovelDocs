# Siberia: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Sakha, Siberian Tatar; German. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers:** priestly and priestless communities, wandering "runners", and Old Believers who live in towns.
- **Descendants of exiles:** Decembrist, Polish and populist exiles in Irkutsk, Tomsk and Yakutsk.
- **Protestant minorities:** Baptists, Pentecostals, Mennonites, Lutherans.
- **Shamans and their families:** Sakha, Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Evenki.
- **Urban scenes:** Omsk and Novosibirsk punk and rock, Akademgorodok intellectuals, Tomsk students.
- **Village eccentrics and town eccentrics,** Shukshin's Altai and present-day districts compared.
- **People who are taken to be different without choosing it:** Central Asian migrants, people with disabilities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Old Believers and exile (archives, histories); mixed for subcultures; thin and now hard to get for gender and sexual minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts; Sakha and Buryat for shamans' own accounts; German for Mennonite memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "LGBT movement" is banned as extremist in Russia since 2023, so contact and sourcing are dangerous; some Protestant groups are under pressure; shamans' knowledge may be restricted; protest art invites prosecution.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Dauria chapters of Avvakum's *Житие*, then search eLibrary.ru for "старообрядцы Сибири XVIII век протест".
2. Search the SB RAS State Public Scientific-Technical Library for its Old Believer manuscript collection.
3. Search "Летов Омск сибирский панк история" for interviews and fan archives.
4. Search press archives for "Монстрация Новосибирск" by year.
5. Search "Сибирская семёрка пятидесятники Черногорск" for the Pentecostal case.
