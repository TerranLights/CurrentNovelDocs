# Siberia: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Altai. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; it includes visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers** of Altai and Transbaikalia, keepers of the Belovodye tradition.
- **Soviet-era arrivals** who came to BAM, Bratsk, Norilsk and the oil fields for an ideal, and their children.
- **Akademgorodok and the Tomsk university milieu.**
- **Sakha and Buryat national intellectuals,** who hold up their own epic heroes.
- **Altaians,** whose valleys outsiders made into a spiritual ideal (compare their own view with the visitors').
- **New religious and eco-settlers:** Vissarionites, family-homestead settlers.
- **Village Prose readers** in Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk, who set the old village against the new town.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet construction ideals and Old Believer legends; mixed for epic ideals (editions exist, analysis mostly in Russian); thin and partisan for new religious movements.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha for the Olonkho; Buryat for Geser; Altai for local views of the Uimon legends.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Vissarion's community has been the subject of criminal proceedings, so sources are partisan; new movements may be labeled "sects" in Russian sources; Altai sacred sites are claimed by several groups.
- **Held for review:** none.
