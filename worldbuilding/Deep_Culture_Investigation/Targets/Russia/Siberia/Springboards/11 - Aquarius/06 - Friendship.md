# Siberia: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Sakha, Altai, Siberian Tatar, Kazakh; Evenki. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian villagers and townspeople:** the godparent bond, in old settler and in migrant villages.
- **Men in taiga trades:** hunters, trappers, geologists, rotational oil workers.
- **Buryats:** the anda tradition and present-day usage.
- **Sakha:** central villages against Arctic districts.
- **Evenki and other reindeer peoples:** trading partners and herding friends.
- **Gulag and exile descendants** (Norilsk, Tomsk, Krasnoyarsk): friendships formed in camps and exile.
- **Kazakhs and Siberian Tatars** in mixed villages: friendships across faith.
- **Students** in Tomsk and Novosibirsk, from across Siberia and Central Asia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and memoirs; mixed for survey data (national, rarely regional); thin for formal friendship among Northern peoples outside older ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Mongolian for anda; Sakha; Evenki for trading-partner terms; Polish for Sieroszewski's original.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** veterans' groups are entangled with present wars; Gulag memoirs are painful; drinking as friendship needs neutral handling, with no stereotype of one people.
- **Held for review:** none.
