# Siberia: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Khakas, Siberian Tatar, Kazakh; Evenki, Dolgan, Nenets. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hunting and herding peoples** (Dolgans, Nganasans, Evenki, Nenets): sharing customs differ by people and by herd size.
- **Sakha:** central districts, where historical records name toyon elites, against the Arctic districts.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist monastic ranks against lay village life; east and west separately.
- **Russian old settlers** against Stolypin migrants (land and standing in a village).
- **Oil and gas districts** (Khanty-Mansi, Yamal) against farm districts of Altai and Omsk (regional wealth).
- **Central Asian and Caucasus migrants** in Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk and Irkutsk (unequal treatment).
- **Women and men** in Northern camps and in mining towns.
- **German and Polish descendants of deportees** (standing regained or not).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for income statistics and Soviet debates; mixed for sharing customs (good fieldwork for a few peoples); thin for minority women's own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Dolgan and Evenki for fieldwork; Kazakh and Tatar for those communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic standing and migrant discrimination are touchy topics; the legal standing of Indigenous peoples belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here; SOVA carries a state designation.
- **Held for review:** none.
