# Siberia: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Sakha, Evenki, Khanty and Mansi; German. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how a culture weighs the group against the person, draws the line between "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats:** clan-organized Khori and Aga against western Buryats, where clan memory is kept differently.
- **Evenki, Evens and Dolgans:** clans spread across vast hunting lands.
- **Khanty and Mansi:** moiety and clan organization.
- **Nenets:** herding brigades and the camps under them.
- **Altai and Khakas:** clan membership still named.
- **Sakha:** clans folded into rural districts.
- **Kuzbass miners and Norilsk metal workers:** class groups with strike memory.
- **Old Believer congregations and Mennonites:** faith groups that held together under pressure.
- **Cossack revival societies:** Siberian and Transbaikal Cossack hosts.
- **Youth:** prison-culture groups; fan groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet collectives and strikes; mixed for clan organization (much Soviet ethnography, little in the peoples' own voices); thin and risky for criminal and prison-culture groups.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat for chronicles and clan genealogies; Sakha; Khanty and Mansi for moiety terms; German for Mennonite records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** AUE is banned as extremist in Russia, so research on it carries risk; strike memory and the Siberian divisions are politically charged; clan genealogies may be family property.
- **Held for review:** none.
