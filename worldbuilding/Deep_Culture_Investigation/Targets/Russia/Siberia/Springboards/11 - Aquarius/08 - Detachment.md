# Siberia: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat (with Tibetan and Mongolian Buddhist terms); Sakha; German. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; cultures value some forms of it (calm, objectivity, equanimity) and condemn others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryat Buddhist monks and laity,** and Buryats who follow shamanist ways.
- **Old Believer hermits and village Old Believers.**
- **Taiga hunters and fishers** (Russian, Evenki, Khanty, Ket).
- **Sakha** villagers against Yakutsk residents.
- **Rotational workers and their families.**
- **Urban professionals** (doctors, scientists in Akademgorodok) for professional detachment.
- **Mennonites and Lutherans** in German villages.
- **Elders living alone** in emptying villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buddhist texts and Old Believer hermits; mixed for emotion research; thin for display rules among Northern peoples outside funeral ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tibetan, Mongolian and Buryat for Buddhist texts; Sakha; German.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grief customs and the dead are sensitive; some Buddhist teachings are given only to initiates; isolation and suicide in Northern villages must be handled with care and without stereotype.
- **Held for review:** none.
