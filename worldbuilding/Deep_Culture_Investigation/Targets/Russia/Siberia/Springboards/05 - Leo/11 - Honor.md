*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Siberian Tatar, Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Shor, Khanty, Mansi. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old settlers and Cossack descendants**: the word given, insult and fistfight.
- **Old Believers**: honor bound to faith and strict conduct.
- **Siberian Tatars, Bukharans and Kazakhs**: *namus* and family name.
- **Altai, Khakas, Shors, Teleuts**: epic codes and clan reputation.
- **Buryats**: honoring elders, the Geser code, Buddhist conduct.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenks**: oaths, hunting codes, honor among herders.
- **Prisoners, former prisoners and prison towns**: the criminal code.
- **Officers and military families** of garrison towns.
- **Men and women**: what counts as a man's honor and a woman's, a pattern to test, not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history, epic and criminal-code studies; mixed for current family honor; thin for survey work in Siberia.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tatar, Buryat and Altai for terms and epics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bride capture and violence justified by honor are sensitive and need survivors' own words; war memory is now state-managed; prison-code words carry stigma.
