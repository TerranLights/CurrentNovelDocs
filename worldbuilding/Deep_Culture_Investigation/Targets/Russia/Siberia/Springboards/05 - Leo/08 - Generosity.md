## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Evenk, Dolgan and Ket hunters**, and **Nenets and Khanty herders**: sharing rules of the kill and the herd.
- **Buryats**: Buddhist and shamanic offerings, guest customs.
- **Altai, Khakas, Teleuts and Shors**: feasts and bride-wealth.
- **Siberian Tatars, Bukharans, Kazakhs**: Muslim almsgiving.
- **Old-settler Russian villages and Old Believers**: work bees, alms, hospitality to strangers.
- **City philanthropists** (merchant past, oil and metals present) against small donors.
- **Germans and Mennonites** of Omsk and Altai Krai: church-based mutual aid.
- **Migrant communities**: remittances and help among fellow migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Indigenous sharing ethnography and merchant history; mixed for surveys; thin for informal giving among kin, which is rarely counted.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Evenki, Nenets and Buryat terms for sharing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** offerings to spirits at sacred places may be restricted; corporate "gifts" to Indigenous communities are politically charged; NGO funding from abroad is legally fraught.
- **Held for review:** none.
