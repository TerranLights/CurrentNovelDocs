# Siberia: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Khanty, Mansi, Buryat, Evenki, Ket, Selkup. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The smallest peoples** (Kets, Enets, Nganasans, Chulyms, Tofalars, Soyots): near the edge of being counted at all.
- **Larger Indigenous peoples** (Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenks, Altai, Khakas, Buryats): media and schooling in their own languages.
- **Women in households with avoidance customs**, against urban women.
- **Siberian Tatars**, often counted inside "Tatars".
- **Old people in shrinking villages** of Omsk, Novosibirsk and Krasnoyarsk regions.
- **Oil and gas shift workers**, present for weeks and absent for weeks.
- **Central Asian and Chinese migrants**, watched by police more than seen.
- **Descendants of the repressed**, and regions that feel unseen by Moscow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ethnography and census categories; mixed for media; thin for everyday gaze and greeting norms, which need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Khanty, Mansi, Buryat and Evenki for the press and for avoidance terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mass graves and the families of victims; LGBT people now forced out of public view by law; surveillance of migrants and activists; sacred places where women or outsiders must not look.
- **Held for review:** none.
