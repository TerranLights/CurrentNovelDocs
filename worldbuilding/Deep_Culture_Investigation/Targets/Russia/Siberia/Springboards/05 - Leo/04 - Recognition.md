# Siberia: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Khanty, Nenets, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Industrial towns** (Kuzbass mining, Norilsk, Bratsk, the oil cities) against farming districts of Altai Krai and Omsk.
- **Republics' own honors** (Buryatia, Altai Republic, Khakassia) against federal ones.
- **Recognized small peoples** (Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Selkup, Kets, Evenks, Dolgans, Nganasans, Shors, Teleuts, Kumandins, Chelkans, Tofalars, Soyots) against groups denied the status.
- **Siberian Tatars** and self-declared "Siberians", whose recognition as groups is disputed.
- **Descendants of the repressed and deported** (Germans, Poles, Balts, Kalmyks).
- **Scientists** of Akademgorodok and Tomsk, recognized by academic rank.
- **Women's work** at home and on farms, and honors such as "Mother Heroine".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors and Soviet recognition; mixed for living Indigenous practice; thin for private praise in families.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and other languages for forms of address and thanks.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Memorial was liquidated (2021 to 2022) and its records are under pressure; victims' families may not wish to be named; Indigenous status disputes are live.
- **Law:** the rules of rehabilitation and of Indigenous status as written go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
