# Siberia: Domain and folkways (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Siberian Tatar, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenki, Selkup, Ket, Shor, Dolgan; Sakha (Yakut) if those lands are carried. **Concept in one line:** creativity and self-expression: how people shine, play and take pleasure (play, pleasure, romance, children, fame, celebration, games of chance, sport, display).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian old settlers (*starozhily*) against later settlers** (Stolypin-era, Soviet, industrial).
- **Old Believers:** Kerzhaks of Altai; Semeiskie of Transbaikalia (UNESCO-listed *check*).
- **Ob-Ugrians (Khanty, Mansi) and Samoyedic peoples** (Nenets, Selkup, Enets, Nganasan).
- **Evenks, Dolgans and Kets** of Krasnoyarsk Krai's north.
- **South Siberian Turkic peoples:** Altai (Telengits, Tubalars, Chelkans, Kumandins), Teleuts, Shors, Khakas, Chulyms, Siberian Tatars: separate festivals and epics.
- **Buryats** (Ust-Orda; the Republic and Aga), Soyots and Tofalars: Buddhist against shamanic festival forms.
- **Sakha**; **Germans** (Azovo, Omsk; Altai Mennonites); **Poles** (Vershina, Irkutsk); **Kazakhs** of Omsk and Altai Krai; Central Asian migrants in the cities.
- **Oil and gas shift workers** and Akademgorodok's scientists: occupational leisure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Indigenous ethnography and state statistics; thin for lived pleasure and romance outside cities; much is Soviet-staged folk display, so separate stage from home.
- **Lands:** the tracker header lists the pre-war lands only as "Russia"; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. These sheets cover the core lands, Tyumen and Yamal east to Lake Baikal, plus Buryatia, Transbaikalia and Sakha where carried.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; minority languages mostly via bilingual editions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ob-Ugric bear-feast songs and some shamanic performances are restricted; ask first. Access from abroad has narrowed since 2022, and some outlets carry "foreign agent" labels.
- **Law:** gambling and festival rules as written go to Libra; legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the catalog of the Russian State Library and eLibrary.ru for "праздник" plus each people's name ("ханты", "алтайцы", "хакасы") and "этнография".
2. Find the volumes of "Памятники фольклора народов Сибири и Дальнего Востока" for Khanty, Altai, Khakas, Buryat and Evenki, and list their game and festival sections.
3. Pull Rosstat's time-use tables for the Siberian federal subjects and RLMS-HSE's leisure items for its Siberian sites.
4. Search CyberLeninka for "народные игры Сибири" and "этноспорт" to map the Indigenous-sport literature.
5. Pull festival calendars from the houses of folk creativity in Tyumen, Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk and Irkutsk.
