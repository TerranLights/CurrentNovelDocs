## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ob-Ugric and Samoyedic craftswomen** (Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Selkup): pattern, family marks, sewing.
- **South Siberian Turkic makers** (Altai, Khakas, Shor, Teleut): felt, wood, metal, and epic-singers as verbal makers.
- **Evenk, Dolgan, Ket and Nganasan** craft traditions of the Krasnoyarsk north.
- **Buryat** Buddhist artists against shamanic and secular makers.
- **Russian village craft** (carving, icon painting among Old Believers, embroidery) against city art schools.
- **Scientists and engineers** of Novosibirsk and Tomsk; **rural improvisers** of the taiga.
- **Sakha** filmmakers and carvers, where those lands are carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for museum and ethnographic craft; mixed for living Indigenous art; thin for everyday improvising, which lives online and in talk.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; minority terms through bilingual dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Ukok mummy's removal and the calls for reburial are a live dispute; sacred figures (Khanty family spirits) held in museums; the purges of Indigenous artists.
- **Held for review:** none.
