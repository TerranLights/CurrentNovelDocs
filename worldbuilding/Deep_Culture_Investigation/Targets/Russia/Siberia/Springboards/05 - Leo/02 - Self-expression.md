# Siberia: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Nenets, Khanty, Altai, Khakas. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **City subcultures** of Omsk, Novosibirsk, Tomsk and Tyumen (rock, punk, rap) against small towns.
- **Village Russians and old settlers**, where reserve and candor may be read differently.
- **Old Believers**, with strict dress and speech norms.
- **Buryats** (Buddhist and shamanic), **Altai and Khakas** singers, **Khanty, Mansi and Nenets** herders and town-dwellers.
- **Siberian Tatars** and **Kazakhs**, Muslim norms of modesty.
- **Central Asian migrants** in the cities; **Germans** and **Poles** of old settlements.
- **Generations:** late-Soviet youth against today's.
- **Men and women:** who may show feeling, at the table and at the funeral.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for music, literature and folklore; mixed for surveys; thin and fragile for political expression, since 2022 laws on speech have chilled answers.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; minority folklore through bilingual editions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** LGBT expression is legally banned as "extremist" (2023); naming living people who posted dissent can harm them; personal songs may be owned, so ask before reuse.
- **Law:** speech laws as written go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
