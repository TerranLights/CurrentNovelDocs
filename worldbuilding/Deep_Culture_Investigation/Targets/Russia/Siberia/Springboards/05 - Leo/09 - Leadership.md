# Siberia: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Siberian Tatar, Khanty, Nenets, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack descendants** of Omsk and Altai Krai: elected leaders and revived hosts.
- **Old Believer communities** with lay preceptors.
- **Buryat clans**, Buddhist and shamanic.
- **Altai, Khakas, Shor and Teleut** clan heads and modern movement leaders.
- **Siberian Tatars**: memories of the khanate and its nobles.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenks, Dolgans, Kets**: clan elders, herding brigades, association leaders.
- **Industrial bosses**: mine and plant directors of Kuzbass, Norilsk, Bratsk.
- **Scientists** of Akademgorodok and Tomsk; **women** in village and regional leadership.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history of chiefs, atamans and governors; mixed for Indigenous leadership today; thin for everyday leadership in families and work crews.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Tatar for clan and khanate sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** opposition leaders in the regions face prosecution; Indigenous activists have been pressured; current governors are appointed, so public talk of leadership is cautious.
- **Law:** formal authority and the right to command go to Capricorn; election rules as written to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
