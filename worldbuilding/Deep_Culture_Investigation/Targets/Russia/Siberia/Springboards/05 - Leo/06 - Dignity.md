# Siberia: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Shor, Nenets, Sakha where carried. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-settler and Old Believer villages** against Soviet new towns.
- **Miners of Kuzbass** and **metal workers of Norilsk**, against office and service workers.
- **Buryat, Altai, Khakas and Evenk** teachings on respect for elders and places.
- **Shors, Teleuts, Khanty and Nenets** facing mining and drilling on their lands.
- **Prisoners, former prisoners and prison-town families** (Krasnoyarsk, Irkutsk, Kemerovo colonies).
- **The old in care homes** and in emptying villages.
- **Migrants from Central Asia**, and the dignity of document checks and housing.
- **Descendants of exiles** (Decembrists, Poles, Germans).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature of exile and prison, and in labor history; mixed for official reports; thin for everyday courtesy norms.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; minority languages for teachings about land and elders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prison and care-home abuse; living protesters; teachings about spirits are partly restricted, so use what the peoples publish.
- **Law:** rights law as written goes to Libra; legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
