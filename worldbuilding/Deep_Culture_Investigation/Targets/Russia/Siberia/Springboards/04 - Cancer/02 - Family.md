# Siberia: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Sakha, Altai, Khakas; Evenki, Nenets, Khanty, Ket; German for Russian German families. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryat tribal groups:** Khori (Transbaikalia) against Ekhirit and Bulagat (west of Baikal, Ust-Orda): different clan histories and Buddhist or shamanic weight.
- **Sakha by district:** central uluses against Vilyuy and northern Sakha.
- **Northern and southern Altaians:** Tubalar, Chelkan and Kumandin against Altai-kizhi, Telengit and Teleut.
- **Khakas subgroups** (Kachin, Sagai, Kyzyl, Koibal) and the **Shors**.
- **Khanty groups:** northern, eastern (Vakh, Vasyugan) and southern; tundra against forest **Nenets**.
- **Evenki, Ket, Selkup, Dolgan, Nganasan:** small peoples with clan systems under pressure from boarding and resettlement.
- **Old Believer families and Orthodox settler villages;** **Siberian Tatars** (Tobol-Irtysh, Baraba, Tomsk).
- **Russian Germans, Kazakhs and Ukrainian-descended villages;** **Central Asian migrant families** split between Siberia and home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for demography and Russian kin terms; mixed for clan systems (older ethnography, newer native scholarship); thin for the smallest peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat, Sakha and Altai for genealogies and clan media; German for Russian German sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan genealogies and sacred family objects may be closed to outsiders; family histories of repression and deportation are painful and can be politically delicate.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull marriage and divorce rates by Siberian region from the demographic yearbook, 2000 to the latest year.
2. Register with RLMS-HSE and check which Siberian sites and which kin-transfer items exist.

4. Search the National Library of Buryatia's catalog for "угай бэшэг" and "Хоринские летописи".
5. Search VKontakte for "родовой сход" and "сӧӧк" to find living clan gatherings and their organizers.
