## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Altai, Khakas, Shor and Teleut families:** Umay rites alongside Orthodox, Burkhanist or secular practice.
- **Sakha:** village against Yakutsk mothers; districts with higher and lower birth rates.
- **Buryat:** Buddhist, shamanic and Orthodox Buryat families.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets and Evenki:** birth in the camp or the far hospital; mothers parted from boarding-school children.
- **Old Believer families:** family size and birth at home or in hospital, to test by community.
- **Russian settler villages** against **big-city mothers** in Novosibirsk, Omsk and Krasnoyarsk.
- **Siberian Tatar, Kazakh and Central Asian migrant mothers:** Muslim family customs in Siberian cities.
- **Shift-work and military families:** mothers raising children alone for long spells.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, benefits and settler folklore; mixed for Indigenous birth customs (older ethnography, some native scholarship); thin for mothers' own voices outside cities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Altai and Khakas for terms and rites; Khanty and Nenets for birth customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth rites may be women's knowledge, not for men or outsiders; mothers of soldiers are a politically charged topic now; state family policy is tied to current politics. Reproductive and family law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "Умай" with "алтайцы", "хакасы", "шорцы" and for "Айыысыт".
2. Pull women-by-children-born tables from the 2010 and 2021 censuses for each Siberian region and people.
3. Search the Social Fund of Russia and regional sites for "региональный материнский капитал" in each Siberian region.
4. Search Tomsk and Barnaul folklore holdings for "родильная обрядность" and "повитуха".
5. Search VKontakte for city mothers' groups and note their main topics over a year.
