# Siberia: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Evenki; Central Asian languages (Uzbek, Tajik, Kyrgyz) for migrant housing. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taiga hunters and herders** (Evenki, Ket, Selkup, Russian *промысловики*): the cabin and camp customs of open shelter.
- **Sakha cattle-keepers:** people and animals under one roof.
- **Descendants of special settlers and deportees** in Tomsk, Narym, Krasnoyarsk and the Altai.
- **Old Believer communities,** some still keeping remote refuges.
- **Flood- and fire-prone villages** along the Angara, Iya, Ob and Lena.
- **Big-city homeless people** and the services and churches that reach them.
- **Central Asian migrant workers** in crowded flats and site trailers.
- **Northern industrial towns** (Norilsk; oil towns of the Ob) where shelter itself is the struggle against cold.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical exile and for building in the cold; mixed for homelessness and disasters; thin for shelter for women and for migrant housing outside press reports.
- **Languages needed:** Russian above all; Sakha and Evenki for dwelling terms; Uzbek, Tajik and Kyrgyz for migrants' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** repression history (the special settlements; Nazino) is painful, and access to its archives may have narrowed (*check*); the "БОМЖ" label is a slur; women's shelters protect their addresses. Shelter law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Avvakum's *Житие* and Chekhov's *Из Сибири* together for the old meaning of lodging and cover in Siberia.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "спецпоселенцы Нарым землянки" and "Назинская трагедия".
3. Search Irkutsk Oblast news archives for "Тулун наводнение пункт временного размещения" and residents' testimony.
4. Search city sites and VKontakte for "пункт обогрева" and "ночлежка" in Novosibirsk, Omsk, Krasnoyarsk and Irkutsk.
5. Search the Permafrost Institute's publications for "жилище" and "деформация зданий".
