# Siberia: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Altai; Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Shor, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryat Buddhists and Buryat shamanists:** protection through lamas, through ancestors, or both.
- **Sakha** households keeping the hearth and the spirit-masters; **Altai** Burkhanist and shamanic families.
- **Nenets, Khanty and Mansi** herders and fishers: family guardians carried with the camp; sacred sites off limits.
- **Shor, Teleut and Kumandin villages** near mines: protection of land and graves.
- **Orthodox and Old Believer settlers:** icons, prayers and charms; Old Believers' rules on contact with outsiders, which vary by community.
- **Cossack communities** against civic volunteers.
- **Oil and mining towns:** company security and closed sites.
- **City women's self-defense and safety groups** against village expectations of who protects whom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and ethnography; mixed on present-day practice; activist material is plentiful but partisan.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat and Nenets for terms and rites; Khanty and Shor for land cases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred family images and sites are often closed to outsiders, and women or strangers may be barred from some; land-defense activism carries legal and personal risk for those involved, so quote public statements only; current war memory is politically charged. Weapon and self-defense law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search Pekarsky's dictionary (digitized) for "иччи", "харыстаа" and related headwords.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "онгоны бурят" and "семейные святыни ханты".
3. Search Tomsk State University's folklore holdings for "обереги Сибирь" and "заговоры старожилов".
4. Search Лиза Алерт's site for its Siberian detachments and their reports on searches for children and elders.
5. Search news archives for "родовые угодья ханты нефть" and "шорцы разрез" for land-protection cases.
