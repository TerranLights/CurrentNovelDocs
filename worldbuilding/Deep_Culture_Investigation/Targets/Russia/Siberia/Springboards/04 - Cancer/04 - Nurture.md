# Siberia: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Siberian Tatar; Eveny, Evenki, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Ket. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families** (Eveny, Evenki, Nenets, Dolgan): children between camp and boarding school.
- **Fishing and hunting peoples** (Khanty, Mansi, Ket, Selkup): teaching skills on the river and in the forest.
- **Sakha and Buryat villages** against their capitals (Yakutsk, Ulan-Ude): native-language rearing against Russian-language schooling.
- **Old Believer families** (*семейские*, *кержаки*): strict religious upbringing.
- **Settler villages** with grandmother-centered rearing against **city families** with nurseries and enrichment classes.
- **Shift-work families** of the oil north: one parent away for weeks.
- **Siberian Tatar, Kazakh and Russian German families:** Muslim, Lutheran, Catholic or Mennonite heritage in how children are raised.
- **Migrant families from Central Asia:** children raised across two countries, sometimes by kin left behind.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and Soviet-era ethnography; mixed for present-day Indigenous child-rearing; thin for the smallest peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for ethnopedagogy and children's media; Eveny, Evenki, Khanty and Nenets for lullabies and terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school histories; children's sacred and protective objects may be shown or not by choice; studies of children need consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
