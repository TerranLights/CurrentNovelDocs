# Siberia: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Altai, Siberian Tatar, Kazakh; Nenets, Evenki, Dolgan; Lithuanian and German for deportee and diaspora sources. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old settlers against later waves:** Stolypin-era migrants, Soviet builders, oil and gas newcomers.
- **Republic peoples** (Sakha, Buryat, Altai, Khakas) against **small Indigenous peoples** (Evenki, Ket, Selkup, Shor, Tofalar, Teleut, Chulym, Dolgan, Nganasan): belonging set by numbers and status.
- **Siberian Tatars** against Volga Tatar newcomers in the same towns.
- **Descendants of the exiled and deported** (Poles, Lithuanians, Germans, Kalmyks, dekulakized peasants).
- **Russian Germans** who stayed, left for Germany, or returned.
- **Central Asian and Caucasian labor migrants** and the **Chinese** traders and workers of the eastern cities.
- **City against village;** the **closed towns,** where residence needs a permit.
- **Siberians who left** for Moscow and abroad, and what ties them back.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for regionalism and census self-naming; mixed for felt belonging; thin and scattered for small peoples and migrants.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for festival and community media; Lithuanian, Polish and German for exile and diaspora memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Siberian regionalism and the regionalist flag are politically charged, and online material on it may be blocked or risky to its authors (*check*); migrant belonging is a heated topic. The legal status of Indigenous peoples goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
