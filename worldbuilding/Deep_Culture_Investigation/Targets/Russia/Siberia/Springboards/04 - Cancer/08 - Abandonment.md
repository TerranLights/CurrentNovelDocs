# Siberia: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Nenets; Polish, Lithuanian and German for deportee memory. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small villages of the Ob, Angara, Yenisey and Altai foothills:** the old left behind as the young leave.
- **Arctic and industrial towns** (Norilsk, Dikson, Taimyr settlements): whole populations leaving.
- **Herding and fishing peoples** (Nenets, Evenki, Dolgan, Khanty): children sent to boarding school; parents left in camp.
- **Descendants of special settlers and deportees:** children placed in children's homes.
- **Families of shift workers and of long-distance migrants:** a parent gone for weeks, months or years.
- **Children's homes** and their graduates, city and village.
- **Sakha and Buryat villages** against the cities that draw their young.
- **Families split by emigration** (Russian Germans to Germany; Siberians to Moscow and abroad).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for settlement counts, migration and orphan statistics; mixed for lived experience; thin for Indigenous accounts outside fiction.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat and Nenets for folklore and memory; Lithuanian, Polish and German for deportee memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** repression and deportation are politically charged and painful; children's records are private; framing out-migration as "Siberia abandoned by Moscow" is political. Parental duty and child-welfare law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the settlement tables of the 2010 and 2021 censuses and list districts with the most empty villages.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for "неперспективные деревни Сибирь" and "сельская депопуляция Томская область".
3. Read *Кража* and *Анико из рода Ного* for the inside views of the children's home and the boarding school.
4. Search usynovite.ru and regional ministries for the number of children without parental care by Siberian region.
5. Search Lithuanian and Polish memory archives for Siberian deportee childhood memoirs.
