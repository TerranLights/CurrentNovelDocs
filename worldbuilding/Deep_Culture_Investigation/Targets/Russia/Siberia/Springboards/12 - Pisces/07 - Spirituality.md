# Siberia: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with Church Slavonic); Buryat and Tibetan; Altai; Khakas; Shor; Sakha (if carried); Old Turkic for inscriptions. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion (experience, practice and the sacred outside the church; institutions are Sagittarius's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eastern (Transbaikal) Buryats,** mainly Buddhist, against **western (Cisbaikal) Buryats,** shamanist and Orthodox; urban Ulan-Ude seekers against village households.
- **Southern Altaians** (Burkhanist heritage, Tengrists, Orthodox) against **northern Altaians** (Tubalars, Chelkans, Kumandins) with their own forest and mountain spirits.
- **Khakas subgroups** (Sagai, Kacha, Kyzyl, Koibal) and **Shors:** revivals of different strengths.
- **Russian Orthodox Siberians:** churched minority against "believers in the soul"; Old Believers apart.
- **Siberian Tatars:** Muslim saints' shrines near Tobolsk; Baraba Tatars.
- **Evenks, Kets, Selkups, Nenets, Dolgans:** small-community practice under pressure.
- **Newcomer seekers and eco-settlers** in the Altai and around Baikal.
- **Siberian Germans:** Lutheran, Catholic and Mennonite remnants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buryat Buddhism and Altai religion in Russian and English; Orthodox lay devotion is well documented nationally but thin for Siberia; small peoples' practice thin and partly closed.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, Church Slavonic; Buryat, Tibetan, classical Mongolian script for Buryat texts; Altai, Khakas, Old Turkic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** closed ceremonies and shamanic knowledge (respected, not researched); sacred sites and tourism; *dukhovnost'* as a political slogan; registration disputes between official and unregistered groups.
- **Held for review:** a New Age pilgrimage site in Omsk Oblast, set aside under the South Asian rule (category only).
