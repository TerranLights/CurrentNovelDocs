# Siberia: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Altai, Khakas, Buryat, Siberian Tatar, Khanty, Evenki, Sakha (if carried). **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taiga villagers and hunters** (Russian old settlers, Evenks, Kets): bears, cold and getting lost.
- **Ob-Ugric and Evenki communities:** naming taboos and spirits of the forest and underworld.
- **Altai herders:** fear from the sky (rocket debris) and of sacred-site violation.
- **Industrial cities** (Krasnoyarsk, Norilsk, Kemerovo, Bratsk): pollution, dams, mines, fires.
- **Families with repression history** (Tomsk, Kolpashevo, deportee villages): fear of the state handed down.
- **Siberian Tatars, Buryats, Khakas:** fright illness and soul-calling, each in its own form.
- **Children and parents:** ticks, strangers and the *babaika*.
- **Border-near and Far-East-facing districts:** fear of outsiders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, folklore and the Soviet terror; polling on fears is national with thin regional cuts; anxiety epidemiology for Siberia is likely thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Altai, Buryat, Khakas, Siberian Tatar; Khanty, Evenki and Sakha via specialists.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disaster grief; mass graves and repression; xenophobic fears must be recorded, not echoed; spirit names some speakers avoid saying aloud.
- **Held for review:** none.
