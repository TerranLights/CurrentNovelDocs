# Siberia: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Sakha, Evenki, Khanty; Tibetan for Buryat Buddhist texts. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian village Siberians against city dwellers:** customs of alms at the door against NGO giving in Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk, Irkutsk.
- **Old Believers** (*kerzhaki*, *semeiskie*): charity kept inside the community, or open to outsiders.
- **Buddhist eastern Buryats against shamanist-Orthodox western Buryats:** two languages of mercy in one people.
- **Evenks, Kets, Selkups and other hunters:** sharing and pity toward animals and the needy in small communities.
- **Khanty and Mansi** (if carried): the bear ceremony's ethics.
- **Altaians and Khakas:** compassion in kin networks (*söök*, clan) *check*.
- **Descendants of deportees and of the villagers who received them:** memory of help given and refused.
- **Prison and camp towns:** families of prisoners and staff.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for Orthodox charity and 19th-c. exile writing; survey data mostly national with thin regional cuts; Indigenous terms for pity are poorly documented in English.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Tibetan for Buddhist texts; Sakha, Altai, Khakas dictionaries; Lithuanian, Latvian, German for memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prisoners and ex-prisoners; deportation memory; NGOs labeled "foreign agents" may have closed or renamed; Ob-Ugric ritual details are partly restricted to participants.
- **Held for review:** none.
