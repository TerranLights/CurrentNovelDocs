# Siberia: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Sakha, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **City dwellers** (Novosibirsk, Omsk, Krasnoyarsk, Irkutsk): dacha, tourism, music scenes and screens.
- **Men in village and rotation work:** hunting, fishing and the binge.
- **Old Believers:** the legend of escape to a free land, and the wanderers who refused settled life.
- **Indigenous communities** (Altaians, Buryats, Khakas, Evenks, Sakha if carried) with sober-village campaigns against those without.
- **Young people in punk and climbing subcultures** against their parents' generation.
- **Siberian Germans and Mennonites:** emigration as escape, and religious abstinence norms *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for drinking statistics and for literature and songs; thin for gaming and media use with regional cuts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Sakha for community campaigns.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous drinking is a stereotype trap: research must name variation and use community-led sources, never one voice; drug use and HIV in some Siberian cities; coercive rehab centers.
- **Held for review:** none.
