# Siberia: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Altai, Buryat, Sakha, Khakas, Evenki, Yukaghir (if carried). **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Russian | сопереживание (soperezhivanie) | feeling-with, "co-living" another's experience | older native word, beside the loan эмпатия (empatiya) |
| Russian | переживание (perezhivanie) | lived emotional experience | also a psychology term (Vygotsky) |
| Russian | чуткость (chutkost'); отзывчивость (otzyvchivost') | sensitivity, tact; responsiveness | praise words for a person who "hears" others |
| Russian | войти в положение (voiti v polozhenie); побывать в чужой шкуре (pobyvat' v chuzhoi shkure) | to enter another's situation; to be in another's skin | everyday perspective-taking idioms |
| Russian | причитания (prichitaniya); плач (plach) | laments; keening | shared mourning as felt-with grief |
| Buryat | сэдьхэл (sedkhel) | mind, heart, feeling | *check* spelling |
| Buryat; Sakha | ёхор (yokhor); оһуохай (ohuokhai) | circle dances sung in unison | attunement in a body of dancers |
| Altai | кайчы (kaichy); кай (kai) | epic singer; throat-sung epic performance | *check* |

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hunters and herders** (Evenks, Dolgans, Nganasans, Kets, Selkups) against farm villages: reading animals' minds as daily work.
- **Southern Altaians, Telengits and Khakas:** epic audiences and shamanic séances as schools of perspective-taking.
- **Buryats** (eastern and western): circle dance and Buddhist teaching on others' minds.
- **Russian villages with laments** (old-settler and Old Believer communities) against cities where keening has faded.
- **Big-city students and professionals** (Novosibirsk, Tomsk, Krasnoyarsk): the loan word *empatiya* and therapy talk.
- **Men in mining, oil and rotation work** (*vakhta* camps): what feeling-with looks like where it is mocked.
- **Siberian Germans, Kazakhs and Tatars:** cross-ethnic perspective-taking in mixed villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good psychology literature in Russian with student samples; the Indigenous material is mostly about humans and animals, not "empathy" by name, so researchers must translate the concept carefully.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Altai and Sakha for epic and dance texts; Evenki and Dolgan glossaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic séance details and recordings; repression-memory projects under official pressure; helpline data is confidential.
- **Held for review:** none.
