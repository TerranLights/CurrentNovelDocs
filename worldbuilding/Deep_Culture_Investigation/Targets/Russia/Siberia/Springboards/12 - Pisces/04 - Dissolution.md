# Siberia: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Siberian Tatar, Altai (with Teleut, Kumandin), Buryat, Evenki, Ket, Selkup, Chulym, Tofa. **Concept in one line:** the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities (proposed definition).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Angara and Yenisei villages resettled by dams** against villages never moved.
- **Small peoples near language loss:** Kets, Chulyms, Tofalars, Kumandins, Selkups, Shors, Soyots.
- **Teleuts, Tubalars, Chelkans** within the Altaian category: being merged and unmerged by census.
- **Siberian Tatars** (Tobol-Irtysh, Baraba, Tomsk) and their merging into "Tatars" in statistics.
- **Old-settler Russians who call themselves Siberians** against those who say "Russian".
- **Evenks and Buryats in trance traditions** against Orthodox and Buddhist contemplative forms.
- **Mining towns in decline** (Kuzbass) against growing cities (Novosibirsk, Tyumen if carried).
- **Mixed families** (Russian with Buryat, Khakas, Altaian): what merges and what is kept.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for reservoir villages, census categories and literature; thin for first-person accounts of trance and of language loss in the smallest peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Ket, Selkup, Chulym, Tofa via specialists; Siberian Tatar; Altai dialects.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** census categorization is politically live; shamanic and fly-agaric use touch both restricted knowledge and current drug law; resettlement grievances; dementia stigma.
- **Held for review:** none.
