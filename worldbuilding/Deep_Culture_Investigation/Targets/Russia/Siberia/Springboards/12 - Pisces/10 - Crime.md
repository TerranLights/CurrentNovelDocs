# Siberia: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with criminal argot, *fenya*); Buryat; Altai; Sakha; Polish and German for exiles' memoirs. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is not researched here: see Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-settler villages** with exile history against newer towns: the convict as "unfortunate" or as threat.
- **Prison and camp towns** (Krasnoyarsk Krai, Irkutsk Oblast, Norilsk) and families of staff and prisoners.
- **Small-town youth in Transbaikal and Buryatia** drawn to prison-code culture against their parents.
- **Altaian, Buryat and Khakas herding villages:** livestock theft as the grave crime.
- **Logging and poaching districts** (Angara, Baikal shore): tolerated wrongs.
- **Big-city middle classes** (Novosibirsk, Tomsk): white-collar crime and corruption seen differently.
- **Polish, Baltic and German exiles' descendants:** crime and punishment as political memory.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exile and prison history and for criminal subculture; regional victimization data is thin; Indigenous ideas of grave wrong are poorly documented outside Soviet frames.
- **Languages needed:** Russian including argot; Buryat, Altai; Polish and German for exile memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** AUE is a banned group; research must not reproduce its material; serial-killer victims' families; organized-crime names carry legal risk; no stereotype of any people as criminal. Law research stays in Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
