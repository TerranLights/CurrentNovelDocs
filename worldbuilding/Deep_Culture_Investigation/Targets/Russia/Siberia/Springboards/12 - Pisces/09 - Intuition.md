# Siberia: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Khakas; Altai; Buryat with Tibetan and Mongolian; Siberian Tatar; Evenki, Dolgan, Nganasan. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Trappers, fishers and herders** (Russian old settlers, Evenks, Kets, Dolgans, Nganasans): hard-won intuition about land and animals.
- **Buryat and Altai-Khakas traditions:** learned divination and the healer's pulse against family omen reading.
- **Old Believer villages:** manuscript omen books and their keepers.
- **Siberian Tatars:** omens read inside Islam.
- **Akademgorodok and university towns:** scientific suspicion of the hunch against private omen habits.
- **Industrial crews** (mines, fire crews, rotation workers): the gut against the rulebook.
- **Women healers and grandmothers** in villages: "the one who knows", as each family describes her.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich folklore on omens and dreams; good documentary material on hunters; little measured data on intuition in Siberia.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, including old manuscript Russian; Khakas, Altai; Tibetan and classical Mongolian for Buryat texts; Siberian Tatar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic and lamaic divination may be closed; charm books are records of belief only; avoid framing any people as "intuitive" by nature.
- **Held for review:** none.
