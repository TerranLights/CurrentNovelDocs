# Siberia: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (and its old-settler dialects); Buryat; Altai and Khakas; Siberian Tatar; Kazakh; Khanty, Nenets, Evenki and Dolgan through bilingual media and Russian ethnography. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-settler villages** (Ob, Angara, Lena valleys) against Soviet-built industrial towns.
- **Kuzbass miners; Norilsk smelter workers; West Siberian oil and gas crews,** each with its own holidays and pride.
- **Gold prospectors' towns** (Bodaibo and the Yenisei goldfields).
- **Herders and fishers:** Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Selkups; Evenks and Dolgans; Buryat livestock families; Altai herders.
- **Taiga hunters and gatherers:** northern Altaians, Shors, Kets, Tofalars.
- **Akademgorodok and the university towns:** mental work as a calling.
- **Farm districts of the southern steppe:** Germans, Kazakhs, Ukrainians, Russians.
- **Rotation workers and labor migrants** from other parts of the former USSR: work far from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet and industrial labor and for official statistics; good ethnography for herders; thin for unpaid household work outside time-use data.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Khanty and Buryat for local-language press; Nenets and Evenki through Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor migrants' status; strike memories in Kuzbass; the old anti-parasite measures and labor law, which belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
