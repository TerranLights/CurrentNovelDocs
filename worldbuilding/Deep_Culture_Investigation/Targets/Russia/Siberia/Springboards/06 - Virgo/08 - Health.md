## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats:** Buddhist households using lama-physicians against shamanic households west of Baikal; city against steppe.
- **Altaians and Khakas:** shamanic, Burkhanist and Orthodox families; resort districts as hosts of visitors.
- **Old-settler Russians and Old Believers:** charms, herbals and bathhouse healing; Old Believer views of state medicine.
- **Northern peoples** (Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Evenks, Dolgans, Nganasans, Selkups, Kets): camp life against settlement life; distance from care.
- **Industrial towns** (Kuzbass, Norilsk, Bratsk): work and pollution.
- **Siberian Tatars, Kazakhs, Germans:** religious and family healing practices; mosque and church charities.
- **Rotation workers:** health on and off shift.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official statistics and Soviet medicine; good for Buryat medicine and Altai shamanism; thin and medicalized for small northern peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Tibetan; Altai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic and lama healing knowledge that practitioners do not share; alcohol and suicide data, which must never be turned into a stereotype of any people; Norilsk and Kuzbass pollution, which are politically sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
