# Siberia: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Siberian Tatar, Altai and Khakas; Kazakh and German in settler districts; northern languages mainly through Russian. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cossack descendants** (Omsk, Transbaikalia) against old-settler peasants, who served differently.
- **Buryat Buddhist households** against shamanic ones west of Baikal: service to lamas and to spirits.
- **Orthodox parishes** against **priestless Old Believers** (the *chasovennye* of the Yenisei, check), who keep service lay.
- **Altaians**, as targets of mission service and as hosts in their own feast order.
- **Northern herders and fishers** (Nenets, Khanty, Evenks): the guest served in the tent; service owed to elders.
- **German Lutherans and Mennonites** of Altai Krai and Omsk; **Kazakhs**; **Siberian Tatars** with their mosque communities.
- **Cities against villages and rotation camps:** paid service work against kin help.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state, church and Soviet service in Russian; mixed for Buddhist and festival service; thin for domestic service and for small northern peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat for datsan materials; Church Slavonic and older Russian for 17th-century records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** military service and war deaths, now politically charged (handle with care, no claims); shamanic and monastic knowledge that is not shared openly. Public and civil service as defined in law: point to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
