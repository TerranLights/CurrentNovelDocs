# Siberia: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (and older Russian for Old Believer writing); Buryat; Altai; Siberian Tatar; Kazakh; Khanty and Nenets terms through craft studies in Russian. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers** (*Semeiskie*, Altai *Kerzhaki*, Yenisei hermitage communities), for whom exactness of rite is a core issue.
- **Buryat Buddhist craftsmen and lamas** against Cisbaikal Buryat households with clan rites.
- **Northern women makers** (Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Dolgan beadwork and fur), and **men's crafts** (sleds, boats, knives).
- **Southern Turkic makers** (Altai and Khakas felt, saddles, wood); **Tatar and Kazakh** masters.
- **Industrial towns** (aircraft, defense, metallurgy) against farm districts: factory quality norms.
- **Academic towns** (Novosibirsk, Tomsk) and their schools: grades and olympiad pressure.
- **Performers and athletes** in the big cities' academies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet quality, sport and the arts; mixed for craft; thin for measured perfectionism outside student samples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; older Russian and Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts; Buryat and Tibetan terms for datsan art.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ritual exactness and its breaches may be private to a clan or a congregation; student distress and suicide must be handled with care and never tied to one group.
- **Held for review:** none.
