## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers** (the *chasovennye* of the Yenisei and Tomsk taiga; the *Semeiskie*): judgment kept by elders and books.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist households who ask lamas against shamanic households who ask shamans; Ulan-Ude against villages.
- **Altaians:** Burkhanist (*Ак Јаҥ*, check) families against Orthodox and shamanic ones.
- **Khanty and Mansi** (moieties, sacred places); **Nenets** herders; **Evenks**; **Kets** and **Selkups**.
- **Hunters, trappers and fishers** against office workers: judging land and animals.
- **Older people and town newcomers,** often the targets of scams; **students** in Tomsk and Novosibirsk.
- **Germans, Kazakhs, Siberian Tatars:** marriage judgment inside and across groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Old Believer texts and Soviet-era ethnography of ritual; mixed for present-day trust and scams; thin for taste and connoisseurship.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Church Slavonic; Buryat; Altai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic and lama practices that clients or practitioners keep private; sacred places of the Khanty and Mansi; *Сибирь.Реалии* is labeled a "foreign agent" in Russia and access is blocked there; describe divination as practice, not as a source of claims.
- **Held for review:** none.
