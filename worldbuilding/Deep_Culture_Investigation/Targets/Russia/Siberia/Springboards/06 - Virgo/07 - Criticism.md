# Siberia: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat; Siberian Tatar; Kazakh; Shor and Khanty materials mostly through Russian. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages and small towns** against **university cities** (Tomsk, Novosibirsk, Irkutsk): blunt talk, teasing and "cutting down".
- **Miners and industrial workers** (Kuzbass, Norilsk) as a protest tradition.
- **Shors and Teleuts** in coal country; **Khanty and Mansi** against oil firms.
- **Buryats:** shame and in-law teasing; Ulan-Ude intelligentsia against villages.
- **Old Believers**, with their own ways of reproach and expulsion from the community.
- **Altaians, Khakas, Siberian Tatars, Kazakhs and Germans:** check each for teasing and joking relations.
- **Generations:** Soviet meeting culture against online review and comment culture.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet and literary criticism; mixed for village satire; present-day political criticism is well recorded by monitors but risky to research.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat; Shor material through Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of the state now carries legal risk for speakers and possibly for researchers; Indigenous protests; sacred parts of the bear festival. Law on speech belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
