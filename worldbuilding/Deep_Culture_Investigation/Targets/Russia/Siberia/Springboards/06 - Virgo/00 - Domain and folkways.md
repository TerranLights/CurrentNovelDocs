# Siberia: Work and service, the domain and its folkways (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (including the old-settler dialects); Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Shor and Siberian Tatar in the south; Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Selkup, Ket, Evenki, Dolgan and Nganasan in the north; Kazakh, German and Ukrainian in settler districts. **Concept in one line:** the domain of work and service: what is good work and a well-kept life?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-settler Russians** (*старожилы*, *чалдоны*) against Stolypin-era settlers, Soviet project workers and rotation migrants: different work histories.
- **Old Believers:** the *Semeiskie* of Transbaikalia, the *Kerzhaki* of Altai, the taiga hermitages of the Yenisei and Tomsk lands.
- **Buryats:** west of Baikal (Ust-Orda) against Transbaikal and Aga; shamanic against Buddhist households.
- **Altaians:** southern Altai-kizhi and Telengits against the northern taiga Tubalars, Chelkans and Kumandins.
- **Khakas; Shors and Teleuts** of Kuzbass (coal country); **Siberian Tatars** (Tobol-Irtysh, Baraba).
- **Northern peoples:** Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Selkups, Kets, Evenks, Dolgans, Nganasans; the Sayan Soyots and Tofalars.
- **Settler minorities:** Germans (Azovo in Omsk, the German district of Altai Krai), Kazakhs, Ukrainians, the Polish village of Vershina.
- **Town types:** Akademgorodok, Kuzbass and Norilsk, the oil towns (Surgut), farm districts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Russian for peasant, Soviet and industrial work; mixed for Buryat, Altai and Khakas; thin for Kets, Enets, Tofalars and other small peoples.
- **Lands:** the tracker carries "Russia" as its pre-war lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. This sign's sheets are written for the core Western and Eastern Siberian lands, from Tyumen to Transbaikalia.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Buryat, Altai, Khakas; northern languages mostly through Russian translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Gulag memory work (Memorial was dissolved in 2021–22; check its Siberian archives); Indigenous rights groups under pressure; foreign researchers' access; healers' restricted knowledge.
- **Held for review:** none.
