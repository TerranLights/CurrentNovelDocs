## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Science towns** (Akademgorodok; Tomsk's university quarter; Krasnoyarsk's campus) against **resource towns** and farm districts: whose reasoning is trusted.
- **Buryat lamas and laity:** formal debate learning against village common sense; Cisbaikal (more shamanic) Buryats as a contrast.
- **Northern herders, hunters and fishers** (Nenets, Evenks, Kets, Selkups, Dolgans): observation-based knowledge.
- **Old Believers:** book learning kept by copying and close reading of texts.
- **Southern Turkic peoples** (Altaians, Khakas, Shors) and **Siberian Tatars**; **German and Kazakh** villages.
- **Generations:** the Soviet engineering cohort against the IT generation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet and present-day science and schooling; mixed on Buddhist learning (the 1930s purges broke the line); thin on small peoples' reasoning systems.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Tibetan for datsan texts; Old Mongolian script for older Buryat material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monastic teaching may be closed to outsiders; Indigenous knowledge should be presented as its holders present it; no ranking of peoples by test scores.
- **Held for review:** none.
