## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern peoples** (Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Evenks, Dolgans, Nganasans, Selkups, Kets): targets of imposed improvement, now running their own schools and revival.
- **Shors, Teleuts, Chulyms, Soyots and Tofalars:** small groups facing language loss, with revival projects.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist revival since 1990 against Soviet-era secular betterment.
- **Old-settler Russians and Old Believers**, who often resisted outside improvement; **Stolypin-era and Virgin Lands settlers**, who embodied it.
- **Germans and Kazakhs** of the southern steppe; **Altaians and Khakas** with their own revival movements.
- **Science towns and new oil cities** (Akademgorodok; Surgut; Novy Urengoy) as built "improvements".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial and Soviet improvement schemes (all in Russian, all from the improvers' side); thinner for how the improved saw it; growing for revival efforts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat; Shor and Khakas for revival media; older Russian for 18th-century texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school harms and forced settlement; camp "reforging"; flooded villages; Indigenous activists under legal pressure. Always tell imposed improvement first in the affected peoples' voices.
- **Held for review:** none.
