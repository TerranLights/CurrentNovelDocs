## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats and Tuvans:** wrestling and archery as national contests; Buddhist festival settings.
- **Altaians, Khakas, Shors:** their own festival games, to set beside the Buryat and Tuvan forms.
- **Siberian Tatars:** Sabantuy contests.
- **Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Evenki, Dolgans:** herders' and hunters' contests and the line between contest and cooperation.
- **Russian city sports cultures** (bandy, hockey, football) and their fan groups.
- **Academic families** around Akademgorodok and Tomsk against rural schools.
- **Industrial towns** with a memory of Soviet emulation.
- **Women and girls:** which contests are open to them in each group, and which are not.
