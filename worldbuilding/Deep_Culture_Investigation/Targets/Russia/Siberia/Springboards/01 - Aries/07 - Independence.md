*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Trappers and fishers** of the Yenisei, Ob and Angara against town residents.
- **Reindeer herders** (Nenets, Khanty, Evenki, Dolgans): provisioning from the herd, against settled kin who depend on wages and transfers.
- **Tuvan and Buryat pastoral families** against city households in Kyzyl and Ulan-Ude.
- **Old Believers** in the Altai and Transbaikalia, with self-sufficiency as faith.
- **Dacha and garden households** in Novosibirsk, Omsk and Krasnoyarsk.
- **Remote northern settlements** that live on the supply run.
- **Communes and homesteaders** who came from the cities by choice.
- **Elders** living alone in emptying villages, against those taken in by children.
- **Migrant workers** sending money home, and families who live on it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household-plot statistics and regional budgets; mixed for everyday self-reliance; thin for help-seeking and emotional independence.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tuvan and Buryat for pastoral families; Nenets and Evenki for herding communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** closed religious communities (Vissarion) have been prosecuted; some Ringing Cedars books have faced bans; poaching talk touches crime. Regional autonomy in law goes to Libra; the spirit of loyalty to the center to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
