# Siberia: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (including Siberian dialects); Buryat, Tuvan, Altai, Khakas, Siberian Tatar; Nenets, Khanty, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Village and city:** Altai and Krasnoyarsk villages against Novosibirsk, Omsk, Irkutsk; Akademgorodok's academic culture of argument.
- **Old settlers, newcomers and shift workers** (*vakhtoviki*) in the oil and gas north: whose claims count locally.
- **Workers in mining towns** of the Kuzbass and Norilsk: a tradition of collective claim against individual quiet.
- **Buryats:** elders and juniors, Cisbaikal and Transbaikal; assembly speech order.
- **Tuvans, Altaians, Khakas:** clan elders against younger, urban speakers.
- **Nenets, Khanty, Mansi:** tundra and taiga families against town-based activists.
- **Old Believers** (*Semeiskie*, *Kerzhaki*): a culture of refusal toward outside authority.
- **Labor migrants** from Central Asia and Siberia's Kazakhs and Germans: assertion from a weak position.
- **Gender:** what is praised in men and sanctioned in women, in each group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pan-Russian speech and politeness; thin for Siberia-specific measures; Indigenous speech etiquette sits mostly in ethnographies and folklore series.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat, Tuvan, Altai, Khakas; Nenets for Yamal.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest and strike participation can be politically sensitive; some Indigenous activists are in exile or carry official labels. Rights claims in court go to Libra; the spirit of obeying authority to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
