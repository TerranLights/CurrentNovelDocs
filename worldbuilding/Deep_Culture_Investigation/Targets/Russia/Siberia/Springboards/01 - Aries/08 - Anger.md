# Siberia: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Tuvan, Buryat, Altai, Khakas; Khanty, Nenets, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian Siberians,** city and village; men and women; generations raised in the 1990s against the young.
- **Tuvans and Buryats:** Buddhist, shamanist and secular frames for anger.
- **Altaians and Khakas:** shamanist and Burkhanist views on curses and harm.
- **Northern peoples** (Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenki, Ket): avoidance, restraint and spirit sanctions to check, without assuming one style.
- **Old Believers:** anger as sin.
- **Prison and post-prison communities** in a region with many colonies: codes of insult and response.
- **Mining and industrial towns** (Kuzbass, Norilsk): collective anger at employers.
- **Migrants from Central Asia,** often the targets of others' anger.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Russian emotion words and protest history; mixed for everyday anger display; thin for Indigenous emotion concepts outside shamanism studies.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tuvan and Buryat for religious and folk frames.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence and alcohol in Indigenous communities are easily turned into stereotypes; treat data with care and context. Protest and regionalism are politically sensitive. Curses and shamans' practice may be restricted knowledge. Revenge goes to Scorpio, justice to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
