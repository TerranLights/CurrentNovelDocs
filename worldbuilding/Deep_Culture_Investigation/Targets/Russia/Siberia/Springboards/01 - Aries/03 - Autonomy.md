# Siberia: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Tuvan, Buryat, Altai, Khakas; Nenets, Khanty, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled.

English keywords: autonomy Siberia, Siberian peasant commune, Old Believers Belovodye, Kazym rebellion, Mandalada Nenets, Tuvan People's Republic, Indigenous self-government Russia, boarding schools Siberia nomads.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers** (Altai *Kerzhaki*, *Semeiskie*, taiga hermits): autonomy through withdrawal.
- **Old-settler peasants** without a serf past against later settlers and collective-farm villages.
- **Reindeer herders** (Nenets, Khanty, Evenki, Dolgans) against settled kin in towns: control over movement and children.
- **Tuvans,** with a memory of a separate state; **Buryats,** with clan self-rule under the empire; **Altaians** with Burkhanist and clan institutions.
- **Shift workers** in oil and gas against permanent residents.
- **Cities with student cultures** (Tomsk, Novosibirsk) against villages where family decides more.
- **Women and men:** who chooses a spouse, a job, a move, in each group.
- **Older generations** shaped by collective farms against post-Soviet youth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history and ethnography of collective autonomy; mixed for personal choice; household surveys exist but Siberian subsamples are small.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Tuvan, Buryat, Altai; Nenets and Khanty for oral histories of the uprisings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** uprisings, deportations and regional self-rule are politically sensitive; family memory of the 1930s may be private. The legal status of Indigenous communities and territories goes to Libra; obedience and the spirit of state law go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
