# Siberia: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Tuvan, Khakas, Altai; Evenki, Khanty, Mansi; German and Finnish for early Ob-Ugrian fieldwork. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats, Tuvans, Altaians, Khakas:** epic hero traditions and their modern uses.
- **Hunting peoples** (Evenki, Khanty, Mansi, Ket, Selkup): courage toward animals and the taiga, framed by respect and taboo.
- **Nenets and Dolgan herders:** endurance in the tundra.
- **Cossack descendants** with a valor-centered estate memory.
- **Siberian Tatars,** whose view of the conquest's "heroes" may differ sharply.
- **Miners, loggers, river and oil workers:** courage of dangerous trades.
- **Old Believers:** courage as steadfast faith under persecution.
- **Women:** endurance at home and in war work, and whether it is called courage.
- **Urban activists and journalists:** moral courage at personal cost.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war and award records and for epic texts; mixed for everyday and moral courage; thin for Indigenous courage concepts outside folklore.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat, Tuvan, Khakas; Finnish and German for early fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war service and casualties from the current war, especially in Buryatia and Tuva, are highly sensitive and the independent counts are restricted inside Russia; activists and closed groups carry official labels; bear ceremonies hold restricted songs.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLibrary.ru for "Абай Гэсэр героизм" and "алыптыг нымах герой".
2. Search pamyat-naroda.ru for award records by Siberian draft offices (e.g. Krasnoyarsk, Novosibirsk).
3. Search "Авиалесоохрана парашютисты-пожарные воспоминания".
4. Search the Finno-Ugrian Society's catalog for *Wogulische Volksdichtung* and its bear-song volume.
5. Search "Байкальская экологическая волна история" for activists' accounts.
