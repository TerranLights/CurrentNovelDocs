# Siberia: Recklessness (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Tuvan, Altai. **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men** in villages and cities: driving, drinking, fighting; and older men's judgments of them.
- **Hunters, trappers and fishers** on ice and rivers, against townspeople.
- **Climbers, rafters and tourists** in the Altai and Sayan, against the local peoples there.
- **Altaians and Tuvans:** risk to the spirit world as well as the body.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist and shamanist frames of fate and luck.
- **Northern herders** (Nenets, Evenki, Dolgans): judgment in weather and on ice.
- **Mining and logging towns:** risk accepted as part of work.
- **Prison and post-prison culture:** daring as reputation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crash, fire and rescue data; mixed for attitudes to risk; thin for Indigenous risk concepts outside fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Altai and Tuvan for sacred-site debates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol harm is easily turned into stereotypes of particular peoples; present it with regional and social context. Sacred-site knowledge may be restricted. Courage is the neighbor entry; fear and escape go to Pisces.
