# Siberia: Beginning (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Tuvan, Altai, Khakas; Ket, Khanty, Mansi, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** how a culture treats starting: origins, firsts, new starts, and starting over after failure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buddhist Buryats and Tuvans** (lunar new year) against **shamanist western Buryats** and **Russian Orthodox** Siberians (January calendar).
- **Altaians** and **Khakas,** with their own spring and white-month new years.
- **Northern peoples** (Ket, Khanty, Mansi, Evenki, Nenets, Selkup): seasonal beginnings tied to ice, fish and herds.
- **Siberian Tatars:** Muslim feasts and the start of a new year in the Hijri calendar.
- **Old settlers against newcomers,** and Stolypin settlers' descendants.
- **Deportees and special settlers** (Germans, Balts, Poles, Kalmyks and others) whose beginnings were forced.
- **Soviet construction towns** (Bratsk, Ust-Ilimsk, Severobaikalsk) where the town's own birth is recent.
- **People starting over** after prison, addiction or divorce, in cities and villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for founding history, resettlement and festival calendars; mixed for personal fresh starts; thin for the smallest northern peoples' birth rites.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat, Tuvan, Altai, Khakas; German for Ket and Selkup fieldwork (Kai Donner).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the conquest as a "beginning" is contested by the peoples already there; deportation and Gulag memory can be private; some birth and naming rites are not for outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
