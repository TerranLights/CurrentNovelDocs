# Siberia: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Evenki, Altai, Khakas, Siberian Tatar, Khanty, Selkup. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how a culture understands dying, the dead and what death means, with grief and mourning.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian villagers against city people**: home dying and village cemeteries against hospital deaths.
- **Old Believers**: their own rules for the dying and for burial apart.
- **Sakha**: Aiyy faith, Orthodox and secular ideas of the dead side by side.
- **Transbaikal Buryats (Buddhist) and western Buryats (shamanist and Orthodox)**: rebirth against ancestral spirits.
- **Evenki, Evens, Yukaghirs, Dolgans**: hunting peoples with clan lands of the dead.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Selkup, Ket**: images of the dead and mourning periods to check.
- **Altai (including Burkhanist families), Khakas, Shor**: Turkic south.
- **Siberian Tatars**: Muslim burial custom and shrine graves.
- **Mining and industrial towns** (Kuzbass, Norilsk): work deaths and disasters.
- **Families of the repressed and deported**: graves unknown, deaths learned decades later.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mortality numbers rich; the meaning of death rich for Sakha, Buryats and Russian folklore, thin for Ket, Selkup, Nganasan and Tofalar.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Finnish and German for early Ob-Ugric ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide in small communities; speaking the names of the dead where avoided; shamans' accounts of death; repression and Gulag graves. Assisted dying and the law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Download regional deaths by cause for Novosibirsk, Irkutsk, Kemerovo oblasts, Sakha, Buryatia and Khanty-Mansiysk from Demoscope or the regional mortality database.
2. Library search: «похоронные причитания» Сибирь; «заложные покойники» Сибирь.
3. Read Anisimov and Karjalainen side by side for Evenki and Ob-Ugric souls of the dead.
4. Search «суицид» with «Якутия», «эвенки», «ханты» in Russian medical journals.
5. Search Sakha-language media for «үөр» and Buryat-language media for «үхэл» to find present-day talk of the dead.
