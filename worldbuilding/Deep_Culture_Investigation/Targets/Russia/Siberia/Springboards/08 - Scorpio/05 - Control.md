## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian village families**: household headship and the commune's eye.
- **Old Believer communities**: control by elders and community rules.
- **Industrial and company towns** (Kuzbass, Norilsk, Bratsk): employers' and the state's hold on life.
- **Closed and scientific towns** (Seversk, Zheleznogorsk, Akademgorodok): guarded entry.
- **Sakha**: fate and weather charms; village self-rule on drinking.
- **Buryats**: Buddhist and shamanist ideas of fate.
- **Evenki and Evens**: boarding schools and state farms.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets**: oil companies on herding lands; collectivization risings.
- **Altai, Khakas, Shor**: Ülgen and fate; mining on their lands.
- **Prisoners and ex-prisoners** in colony towns.
- **Women in rural and Indigenous communities**: control in couples, to ask about carefully.

## 4. Flags
