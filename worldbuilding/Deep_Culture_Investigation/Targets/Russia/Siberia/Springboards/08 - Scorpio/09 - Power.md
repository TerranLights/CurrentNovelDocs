# Siberia: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Altai, Khanty, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it, how it is held and how it is used.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian old settlers and Cossack descendants**: village elders, the commune, the atamans.
- **Company towns and mining regions** (Kuzbass, Norilsk, Bratsk): power of employers.
- **Regional capitals** (Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk, Irkutsk, Omsk, Tomsk): governors, mayors, opposition.
- **Science and university towns** (Akademgorodok, Tomsk): expertise as a kind of power.
- **Sakha**: sovereignty politics, diamonds, lineage leaders.
- **Buryats**: clan chiefs in history; lamas and shamans today.
- **Altai, Khakas, Shor**: clan chiefs (*zaisan*), *kams*, and mining interests.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets**: oil companies and herders' associations.
- **Evenki, Evens, Dolgans**: clan communities and state farms.
- **Prison and camp towns**: official and underworld hierarchies.
- **Labor migrants**: power of brokers and employers.
