# Siberia: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian old-settler villages**: mob justice and its memory.
- **Cossack descendants** in Transbaikal, Altai and Omsk: memory of reprisals, given and suffered.
- **Former prison and camp towns**: the thieves' code of payback.
- **Old Believers**: what their teaching says of vengeance.
- **Sakha**: clan legends of feud.
- **Buryats**: karma against ancestral duty.
- **Altai, Khakas, Shor, Teleut**: epics of avenging the father.
- **Evenki, Evens, Ket**: clan feud and settlement in older ethnography.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets**: harm sent through sacred powers, to check.
- **Partisan and White families** of the Civil War.
- **Urban youth** in Transbaikal and Irkutsk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in epics, Civil War history and 1990s crime reporting; thin on everyday revenge and on forgiveness practice in small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Altai, Khakas, Sakha and Buryat for epic texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the AUE ban; ethnic framing of revenge stories (Rasputin's novel); shamans' harm. Private vengeance against state punishment is researched under Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Library search: «месть» «эпос» with алтайцы, хакасы, якуты.
2. Read *Эллэйада* for Sakha feud tales, then Dolgikh for the clans named.
3. Search: «самосуд» «конокрад» in 19th-century Siberian newspapers (for example the digitized *Сибирская газета*, Tomsk, *check*).
4. Pull homicide counts by region from Rosstat and the Ministry of Internal Affairs.
5. Search regional press: «АУЕ» with «Забайкалье», «Бурятия», «Иркутск», 2015-2021.
