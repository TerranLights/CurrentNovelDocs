# Siberia: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Khanty, Nenets, Altai. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one facet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers**: wanderers' hidden refuges against settled communities.
- **Closed-town residents and nuclear workers** and their families.
- **Families of the repressed and deported**: silence across generations.
- **Russian Germans, Poles, Lithuanians**: identities hidden and later revealed.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Selkup**: sacred sites and restricted songs.
- **Sakha and Evenki**: word taboos, shamans' knowledge.
- **Buryats**: Buddhist initiations and shamanist family spirits.
- **Altai**: Burkhanist and shamanist knowledge held by families.
- **Sexual minorities**, urban and rural.
- **Labor migrants without full papers**.
- **Small villages against large cities**: where nothing stays hidden against anonymity.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state secrecy and repression; mixed for family secrets; Indigenous restricted knowledge should not be researched beyond what keepers choose to share.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Khanty, Nenets, Sakha and Buryat with native scholars' help.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred sites and closed ceremonies; FSB files; sexual minorities; foreign-agent and "undesirable" media; respondents may keep answers private on purpose.
- **Held for review:** none.
