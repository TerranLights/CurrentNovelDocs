## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian old settlers** (*starozhily*, *chaldony*) against later and Soviet-era migrants: different depth of local custom.
- **Old Believers** (semeiskie, kerzhaks, taiga hermitages): own death rites, closed to outsiders.
- **Sakha**: Aiyy faith revival over Orthodox and Soviet layers.
- **Buryats**: separate Transbaikal (Buddhist) and Cis-Baikal communities.
- **Evenki, Evens, Dolgans, Yukaghirs**: dispersed hunters and herders.
- **Ob-Ugrians (Khanty, Mansi)** and **Samoyedic peoples (Nenets, Selkup, Nganasan, Enets)**: oil-region lands.
- **Ket**: a language isolate on the Yenisei.
- **Altai, Teleut, Khakas, Shor, Chulym, Tofalar**: Turkic south; Burkhanism in Altai.
- **Siberian Tatars**: Muslim, with roots in the Sibir khanate.
- **Deportees' descendants** (Germans, Poles, Balts, Ukrainians) and **recent labor migrants**.
- **Towns apart**: closed nuclear towns (Seversk, Zheleznogorsk), Akademgorodok, oil towns, Kuzbass mining towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet ethnography, the Gulag and Sakha and Buryat culture; thin for small peoples (Ket, Enets, Tofalar, Chulym) and for sex ways. Read Soviet sources for ideology.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Sakha and Buryat for the large groups; field notes in the small languages often exist only through Russian.
- **Lands:** the pre-war lands carried are given only as "Russia"; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. Tuva is its own target. This sign's sheets are written for the core Siberian lands.
- **Law:** inheritance law and property statutes belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); here only custom and feeling.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamans' knowledge, sacred places and the dead's names in several peoples; excavated human remains (Ukok); repression archives now hard to reach after Memorial's liquidation; sexual minorities under current Russian restrictions.
- **Held for review:** none.
