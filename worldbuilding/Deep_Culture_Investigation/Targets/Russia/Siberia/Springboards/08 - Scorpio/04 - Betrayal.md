*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian villagers against city dwellers**: what counts as infidelity and who forgives it.
- **Old Believers**: what counts as leaving the faith, and how leavers are treated.
- **Families of informers and of the informed-on**, often in the same village.
- **Ex-prisoners and the camp underworld**: the thieves' code.
- **Sakha**: kin loyalty and the 17th-century Sakha-Russian conflicts.
- **Buryats**: clan loyalty, and the Geser tradition.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets**: the Kazym and Yamal risings and their memory.
- **Evenki, Dolgans, Altai, Khakas**: kin and clan obligations to check.
- **Siberian Tatars**: the Sibir khanate's fall and its memory.
- **Deported peoples' descendants** (Germans, Balts, Poles, Kalmyks): branded traitors as peoples.
- **Soldiers' and veterans' families** across the wars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet informing, the Civil War and camp codes; mixed on infidelity; thin on how small peoples name betrayal.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Khanty terms through Russian dictionaries first.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** NKVD files; present-day denunciations; the label "traitor" on whole deported peoples. Treason law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
