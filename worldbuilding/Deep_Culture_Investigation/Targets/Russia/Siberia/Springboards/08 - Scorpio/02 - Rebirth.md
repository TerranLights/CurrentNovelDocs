# Siberia: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Altai, Evenki, Yukaghir, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and the return of the dead.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox Russians**: church revival after 1991, Epiphany plunges, rehab run by parishes.
- **Old Believers**: their own ideas of purity, flight and starting over (Belovodye).
- **Russian Baptists, Pentecostals and other Protestants**: sudden conversion and testimony.
- **Sakha**: Aiyy faith, Orthodox and secular revival side by side.
- **Transbaikal and western Buryats**: Buddhist rebirth against shamanist return of ancestors.
- **Evenki, Yukaghirs, Ket**: souls that return in children.
- **Altai**: Burkhanism and its present heirs.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets**: revival programs, evangelical converts and those who refuse both.
- **Released prisoners and ex-convict towns** around former camps.
- **Disaster towns** (Tulun after the 2019 flood; Sayanogorsk after the 2009 dam accident): rebuilding.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Buddhist and Sakha revival and on Russian literary redemption; thin on individual recovery and on reborn-soul beliefs in the small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha; Buryat; German and English for early shamanism studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shaman initiations; who is said to be reborn in a child; state favor and disfavor for some religious groups.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Library search: «шаманская болезнь» and «посвящение шамана» with якуты, эвенки, буряты.
2. Read Bernstein and Willerslev for Buryat and Yukaghir rebirth, then follow their Russian citations.
3. Search Sakha media for «Аар Айыы» and «ыһыах»; Buryat media for «хубилгаан».
4. Search regional news: «новая жизнь» «реабилитационный центр» plus the city name (Новосибирск, Иркутск, Красноярск).
5. Search the Returned Names database by oblast for families' later rehabilitation requests.
