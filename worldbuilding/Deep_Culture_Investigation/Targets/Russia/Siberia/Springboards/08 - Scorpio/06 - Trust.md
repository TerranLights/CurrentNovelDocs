*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian villagers against city dwellers**: tab credit and unlocked doors against locks and checks, as questions.
- **Taiga hunters and fishermen**: trust among absent strangers.
- **Old Believers**: how far trust reaches beyond the community.
- **Sakha**: lineage and village self-help.
- **Buryats**: clan and sworn-brother bonds; Buddhist and shamanist congregations.
- **Nenets, Khanty, Mansi**: herders' dealings with oil companies and officials.
- **Evenki, Dolgans, Evens**: reciprocity after the state farms.
- **Siberian Tatars** and **Muslim newcomers**: mosque communities and trust across faiths.
- **Labor migrants from Central Asia**: trust in brokers and employers.
- **Science towns and closed towns**: trust in the state's word and in each other.
- **Kuzbass miners**: trust in employers after disasters.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national and regional trust polls; mixed for regional media; thin for trust in small Indigenous communities beyond a few ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat; Nenets terms through Stammler's glossaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** polling under political pressure (answers may be guarded); Levada is listed as a "foreign agent." Trust in police and courts is researched under Capricorn.
