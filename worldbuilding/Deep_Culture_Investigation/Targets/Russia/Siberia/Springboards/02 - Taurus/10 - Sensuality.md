## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banya culture** of old-settler Russians against **urban saunas** and resort spas.
- **Sakha:** horse-milk drinks, khomus and summer circle dances.
- **Buryats:** sacred springs, Tibetan medicine, the round dance.
- **Khakas, Altaians, Shors:** throat-sung epics, fermented milk drinks.
- **Evenks, Evens, Nenets, Khanty:** raw and frozen fish and meat, smoke rites, fur against the cold.
- **Old Believers:** restraint in food and drink, fasting calendars.
- **Muslim Siberian Tatars:** feast foods and fasting.
- **City youth:** cafés, clubs, food delivery.
