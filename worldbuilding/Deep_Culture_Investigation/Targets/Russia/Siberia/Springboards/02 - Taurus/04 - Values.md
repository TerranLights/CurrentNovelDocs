# Siberia: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (including Church Slavonic for Old Believer writing); Buryat, Sakha, Altai; Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenk literatures. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, how those things are ranked, and how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox, Old Believer, Buddhist, shamanist and Muslim** communities (Siberian Tatars), and evangelical converts among northern peoples: each ranks things differently.
- **Generations:** Soviet-educated parents against post-1991 children.
- **University cities** (Novosibirsk, Tomsk, Krasnoyarsk, Irkutsk) against **resource towns** (Surgut, Norilsk, Bratsk) and **villages**.
- **Sakha, Buryats, Altaians, Khakas:** republics with their own value revivals.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenks:** writers who speak for land and kin.
- **German, Polish and Ukrainian** descendants: imported church and family values.
- **Regionalists** (областники past and present) who claim distinct Siberian values.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national surveys; mixed for regional cuts; strong in literature and in Old Believer manuscripts.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Church Slavonic; Buryat and Sakha; Khanty, Mansi and Nenets originals where translations differ.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "traditional values" is a state political program; separate official statements from what people say; regionalism is politically sensitive; mission records carry an outsider's bias.
- **Held for review:** none.
