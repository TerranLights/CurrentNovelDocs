# Siberia: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat, Khakas, Altai; Nenets, Khanty and Evenk for boarding-school and youth sources. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mining and factory towns** (Kuzbass, Norilsk, Bratsk): men's worth after closures.
- **Rotation workers** and their families in the home towns.
- **Indigenous generations:** boarding-school pupils, their parents, and today's youth reviving language and dress.
- **Sakha and Buryats:** epic and Buddhist models of the person, urban and rural.
- **Descendants of exiles and special settlers** (Germans, Poles, Balts, kulak families): inherited stigma and pride.
- **Old Believers:** worth through faithfulness and separation.
- **Akademgorodok and university cities** against the village: education as worth.
- **Siberian Tatars, Khakas, Altaians:** standing as titular or non-titular peoples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Russian psychology is rich but rarely regional; Indigenous testimony exists but is scattered; literature is strong.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for the press and epic; Nenets and Khanty for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school harm, youth suicide and alcohol are painful subjects; ask communities how they wish them told; exile and camp memory; avoid treating any people as one voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
