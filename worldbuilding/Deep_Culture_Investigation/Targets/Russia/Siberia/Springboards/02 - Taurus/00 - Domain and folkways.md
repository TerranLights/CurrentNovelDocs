# Siberia: Possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Sakha (Yakut), Altai, Khakas, Shor, Siberian Tatar; Evenk, Even, Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Selkup, Ket, Dolgan; settler German, Ukrainian, Polish and Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** the domain of possessions and value: what is worth having, and how is it held (money, resources, food, dress, wealth).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-settler Russians** (старожилы, чалдоны) against later waves (Stolypin-era переселенцы, Soviet builders, вахта workers): different depths of attachment to land and goods.
- **Old Believers:** the Semeiskie of Transbaikalia and the Altai kerzhaks: thrift, plainness and inherited goods worth separating.
- **Buryats** (Khori, Ekhirit-Bulagat, Aga, Selenga): Buddhist and shamanist households, livestock wealth.
- **Sakha:** horse-and-cattle economy, the alaas homestead.
- **Altaians, Khakas, Shors, Teleuts, Kumandins:** South Siberian Turkic herders and hunters, each its own case.
- **Siberian Tatars** (Tobol-Irtysh, Baraba): Muslim household and inheritance customs.
- **Reindeer and fishing peoples:** Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Selkups, Evenks, Evens, Dolgans, Nganasans, Kets, Tofalars: herds, catches and clan grounds.
- **Settler diasporas:** Germans of the German National District (Altai Krai), the Polish village of Vershina (Irkutsk Oblast), Ukrainians and Kazakhs of the Omsk steppe.
- **City against village:** Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk, Omsk, Irkutsk against taiga and tundra settlements.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Russian and in Soviet ethnography; mixed for Turkic and Mongolic peoples; thin for the smallest peoples (Kets, Tofalars, Chulyms, Enets), where much sits in unpublished field notes.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Buryat, Sakha, Altai and Khakas for the republics' own press and folklore; German for 18th-19th-century expedition accounts.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled (the header carries "Russia"); this sign is written for the core from the Irtysh to Transbaikalia, with the Yamal-Yugra north and Sakha as border cases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dispossession, deportation (раскулачивание, special settlers) and Indigenous land loss; sacred and burial objects (the Ukok burial, Ob-Ugric sacred sites) that peoples ask outsiders not to display or handle.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Rosstat: search "бюджеты домашних хозяйств" with each region's name (Новосибирская область, Республика Бурятия, Республика Саха).
2. Library catalog: "Памятники фольклора народов Сибири" to list the volumes by people.
3. Goskatalog: search "Бурятия украшения", "ханты одежда", "старообрядцы утварь".
4. eLibrary.ru and CyberLeninka: "материальная культура" + each people's name, then "ясак мягкая рухлядь".
5. Read Stammler, Crate and Humphrey's bibliographies for regional field studies.
