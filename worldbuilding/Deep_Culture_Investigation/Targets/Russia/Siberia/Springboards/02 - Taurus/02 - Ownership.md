# Siberia: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Sakha, Altai, Shor; Evenk, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khanty, Mansi and Forest Nenets** of the oil districts: clan grounds inside licensed fields.
- **Yamal Nenets:** pasture and gas.
- **Evenks** of Krasnoyarsk, Irkutsk and Transbaikalia: three histories of land reform.
- **Buryats** of Aga and Ust-Orda: their districts merged into larger regions in 2008.
- **Sakha:** alaas and hay plots, horse herds on shared ground.
- **Altaians and Shors:** sacred mountains; mined homelands.
- **Old-settler and Cossack Russians** (Transbaikal and Siberian Cossacks): service land and its loss.
- **Dacha and garage holders** in the big cities: everyday informal ownership.
- **Foreign leaseholders** (Chinese timber and farm leases in Transbaikalia, as locals and lessees each describe them).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land disputes in press and NGO reports; rich in archives for the 17th-19th centuries; thin for Indigenous terms of ownership in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Old Mongolian script for steppe-duma papers; Khanty and Mansi dialect terms for grounds; Chinese for the lessees' side.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous land conflicts with energy firms; some Indigenous organizations have been closed or labeled, so sources may vanish; anti-Chinese feeling around leases; law on land and property goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
