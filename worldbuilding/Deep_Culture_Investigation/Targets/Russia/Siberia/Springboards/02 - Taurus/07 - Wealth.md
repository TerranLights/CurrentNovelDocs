## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Resource north** (Yugra, Yamal, Norilsk, Mirny) against **farming south** (Altai Krai, Omsk steppe): wealth from extraction against wealth from land.
- **Merchant towns** (Irkutsk, Kyakhta, Tobolsk, Tomsk) against **Soviet science cities** (Akademgorodok).
- **Reindeer herders:** Nenets, Evens, Evenks, Dolgans: herd wealth.
- **Buryat and Sakha** livestock households, and Sakha urban elites tied to diamonds.
- **Siberian Tatars and Bukharans:** trade wealth.
- **Old Believers:** wealth held quietly by hard work, in their own view (to be checked).
- **Dekulakized and exiled families** and their descendants.
- **Kets, Selkups, Shors:** small peoples living beside large extraction.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant and gold history and for official income data; mixed for how herders reckon wealth in their own terms; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha, Buryat and Altai for folklore and press; Even and Nenets for herd talk; Chinese and Mongolian sources for the Kyakhta trade.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** oligarch wealth and the 1990s violence; dekulakization; Indigenous views of extraction wealth; questions of herd size may be private.
