# Siberia: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for household and ritual terms; Evenk, Khanty and Nenets for food stores and camp life. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Arctic settlements** on the supply run (Taimyr, Evenkia, northern Sakha) against **southern cities** on rail lines.
- **Herders and hunters** (Nenets, Evenks, Evens, Dolgans): security in herd size and cached stores.
- **Sakha cattle households:** hay and permafrost cellars.
- **Mining and one-industry towns** (Kuzbass, Norilsk): security tied to a single employer.
- **Pensioners and northern veterans** who stayed or left.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist and shamanist rites of protection.
- **Old Believers:** self-sufficient taiga households.
- **Recent migrants from Central Asia** in Siberian cities: security without local kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1990s household studies and current polls; mixed for northern food security; thin for Indigenous terms and rites in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Sakha and Buryat for rites and blessings; Evenk and Nenets for camp storage words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** environmental organizations have been closed or labeled, so some fire and spill data may be gone or risky to cite; protest sources; some rites are clan-only; law on pensions and northern benefits goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
