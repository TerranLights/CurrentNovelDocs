# Siberia: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Sakha; Evenk, Khanty, Nenets for seasonal movement. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old settlers** (старожилы, чалдоны) against **Soviet-era arrivals** and their children.
- **Settled and mobile households** among Evenks, Nenets, Dolgans and Khanty: settled villages against herding camps.
- **Monotown residents** (Kuzbass, Norilsk, Bratsk) against **diversified cities** (Novosibirsk, Tomsk).
- **Rotation workers' families**: a stable home with a moving earner.
- **Old Believers:** continuity held as a duty.
- **Buryats and Sakha:** birthplace and homestead ties across city migration.
- **People displaced by dams** on the Angara and Yenisei.
- **Northerners who left** for the south or western Russia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official migration and vital statistics; rich for Old Believers and old settlers in history; mixed for how people themselves speak of stability.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Sakha for homeland words; Evenk and Nenets for seasonal-cycle terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sedentarization and village closures are painful histories; "stability" is part of state rhetoric, so separate slogan from lived sense; displaced communities.
- **Held for review:** none.
