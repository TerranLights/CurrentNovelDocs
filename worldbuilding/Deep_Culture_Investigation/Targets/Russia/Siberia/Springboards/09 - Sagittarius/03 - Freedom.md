# Siberia: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat; Altai; Siberian Tatar; Khanty and Nenets; Polish for exiles' records. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers,** especially the Altai Kerzhaki and the Semeiskie: a history of flight from the state church.
- **Descendants of exiles:** Decembrists, Polish insurgents of 1863, Volga Germans deported in 1941, the "special settlers".
- **Siberian Cossacks** of Omsk and Transbaikalia: test how service and privilege shaped their idea of freedom.
- **Old-settler villagers** (*starozhily*, *chaldony*) against later Stolypin-era migrants.
- **Buryats and Altaians:** national self-rule.
- **Khanty, Nenets and Evenks:** freedom of movement on the land and from boarding schools.
- **Urban liberals and artists** of Novosibirsk, Tomsk and Irkutsk.
- **Rural and small-town conservatives:** freedom as order and family autonomy (to be tested, not assumed).
- **Central Asian labor migrants:** freedom of movement and its limits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exile history and the Decembrists; mixed for regionalism; thin and politically sensitive for present-day opinion.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat; Altai; Khanty; Polish and German for exiles' archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some media and organizations named here are labeled "foreign agents" or banned in Russia; present-day contacts may face risk; talk of Siberian autonomy can be prosecuted. Laws on freedom go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
