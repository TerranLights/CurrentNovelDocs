# Siberia: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian (with Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts); Buryat; Khanty; Altai; Polish for the exiles of 1863. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Priestless Old Believers** of the Yenisei and Abakan taiga against **priestly Semeiskie** of Transbaikalia.
- **Pentecostals and unregistered Baptists** of Khakassia, Krasnoyarsk and the Altai.
- **Descendants of political exiles** (Decembrists, Poles, Narodniks) in Irkutsk, Chita and Tobolsk.
- **Buryat Buddhists** whose families kept practice through the purges.
- **Khanty, Mansi and Nenets land defenders** in the oil and gas districts.
- **Altaians** defending the Ukok and other sacred sites.
- **Environmental activists** of Irkutsk and Baikal.
- **Soviet-era believers in the revolutionary cause,** and their children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Old Believers, exiles and the Lykovs; mixed for Protestants and Indigenous activists; thin for present-day measures by region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Church Slavonic; Buryat; Khanty; Altai; Polish.
