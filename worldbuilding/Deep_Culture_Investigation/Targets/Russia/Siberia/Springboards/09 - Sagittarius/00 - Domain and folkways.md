## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian Orthodox majority of the southern cities** (Novosibirsk, Omsk, Krasnoyarsk, Irkutsk, Tomsk, Barnaul): cities to set against rural parishes.
- **Old Believers** (Semeiskie of Transbaikalia, Kerzhaki of Altai, priestless communities of the Yenisei): their own religious ways and a freedom-from-the-state thread.
- **Buryats** (Republic of Buryatia, Aga and Ust-Orda districts): Buddhism and shamanism side by side; east and west differ.
- **Altai peoples** (Altai-Kizhi, Telengits, Tubalars, Chelkans, Kumandins): Burkhanism, shamanism, Orthodoxy.
- **Khakas, Shors, Teleuts** of the Sayan-Altai: distinct mission histories.
- **Northern Indigenous peoples** (Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Selkups, Kets, Evenks, Dolgans, Nganasans, Enets): reindeer and taiga worldviews, small populations.
- **Siberian Tatars and Bukharans** (Tobolsk, Tyumen, Omsk, Baraba): the oldest Muslim layer.
- **Settler minorities** (Germans of the Altai and Omsk national districts, Ukrainians, Poles, Estonians, Mennonites and Lutherans): imported faiths.
- **Newer migrants** from Central Asia and the Caucasus in the big cities: mosques and prayer rooms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buryat Buddhism, Altai religion and Old Believers; mixed for urban Orthodoxy; thin for small northern peoples today. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (its tracker carries the pre-war lands of Russia); these sheets cover the core lands from the Urals to Transbaikalia.
- **Languages needed:** Russian first; Buryat and classical Mongolian script; Altai and Khakas; Tibetan for monastery texts; German for settler archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamans' and elders' knowledge, sacred sites (Baikal's Olkhon, Altai's Ukok and Belukha) and burial remains are the peoples' to control; Jehovah's Witnesses and some other groups face bans, so contact with members needs care; the legal side of religion goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
