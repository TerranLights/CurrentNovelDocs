## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-settler villages** against **big cities:** whether small-community trust and urban caution differ (to be tested).
- **Merchant and Old Believer trading families:** claims of commercial honesty to check against records.
- **Indigenous tribute payers and their descendants** (Khanty, Evenks, Buryats, Khakas): the history of being cheated in trade, as they tell it.
- **Gold prospectors, loggers and fishers** in gray economies.
- **Border traders** of Transbaikalia and Altai with China, Mongolia and Kazakhstan.
- **University communities** of Tomsk, Novosibirsk and Krasnoyarsk: academic honesty.
- **Officials and the public:** the bribe seen from both sides.
- **Central Asian migrant workers:** honesty owed and not paid (wage theft, papers).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical tribute records and merchant history; mixed for corruption surveys (often national); thin for folk honesty norms by people.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, including old chancery hand; Buryat; Siberian Tatar; Khakas and Altai; Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption reporting carries risk for sources; stereotypes about honest Siberians or dishonest outsiders must be treated as claims, never findings; bribery law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
