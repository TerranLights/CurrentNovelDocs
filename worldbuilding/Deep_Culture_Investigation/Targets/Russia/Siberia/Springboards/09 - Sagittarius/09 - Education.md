## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **University cities** (Tomsk, Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk, Irkutsk, Omsk) against **small towns and villages**.
- **Akademgorodok families:** inherited scholarly standing.
- **Buryats, Khakas, Altaians:** national universities and language departments.
- **Evenks, Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Dolgans:** boarding and nomadic schooling, and the Indigenous educated elite.
- **Buryat lamas and lay students** of monastic schools.
- **Old Believers:** distance from state schooling (to be checked community by community).
- **International students** from Mongolia, China, Kazakhstan and Central Asia.
- **Young people who leave** for Moscow, St. Petersburg or abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for university and Academy history; mixed for Indigenous schooling; thin for present-day attitudes to the educated by region.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Tibetan; Altai; Khakas; Evenki, Khanty and Nenets.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school memories are painful for many families; academic freedom has narrowed and scholars may speak with caution; education law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Josephson's *New Atlantis Revisited* and the Tomsk university histories for the founding ideals.
