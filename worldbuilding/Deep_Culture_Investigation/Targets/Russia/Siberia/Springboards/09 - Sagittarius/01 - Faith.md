# Siberia: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat; Altai; Khakas; Khanty and Mansi; Evenki; Siberian Tatar; German and Polish for settler parishes. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Churched and nominal Orthodox:** big cities against old villages on the Tobol, Irtysh and Angara.
- **Old Believers:** priestly and priestless accords, each with its own way of keeping faith apart from the state church.
- **Western and eastern Buryats:** whether shamanic and Buddhist faith weigh differently on the Irkutsk and Transbaikal sides is a question to test; Aga Buryats separately.
- **Altai Burkhanists, shamanic traditionalists and Orthodox Tubalars and Kumandins.**
- **Khakas and Shors:** largely baptized peoples with a shamanic revival.
- **Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenks:** Orthodox and older faiths held together.
- **Siberian Tatars** against **Central Asian migrant Muslims** in the same cities.
- **German Lutherans, Mennonites and Catholics; Polish Catholics** (for example the village of Vershina, Irkutsk Oblast, *check*).
- **Baptists and Pentecostals,** with congregations across the region's towns.
- **Jewish communities** of Novosibirsk, Irkutsk and Omsk.
- **The unaffiliated** of Akademgorodok and the student cities: ask about faith in science.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Orthodoxy and Buryat Buddhism; mixed for Protestants and Muslims; thin and partly restricted for Indigenous traditional faith.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat (Cyrillic and classical Mongolian script); Altai; Khakas; Khanty and Mansi; German and Polish for parish archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic and Ob-Ugric sacred knowledge is the peoples' to share; banned groups (Jehovah's Witnesses and others) need care with members; laws on religion go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
