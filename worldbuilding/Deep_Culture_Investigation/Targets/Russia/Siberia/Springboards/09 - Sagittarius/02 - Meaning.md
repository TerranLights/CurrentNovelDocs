# Siberia: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat; Khakas; Khanty; Nenets; Altai. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Angara and Yenisei villagers** moved for dams (Bratsk, Ust-Ilimsk, Boguchany): meaning tied to flooded places.
- **Kuzbass miners and monotown families:** work as meaning, and its collapse.
- **Rotational (*vakhta*) oil and gas workers** of Khanty-Mansi and Yamal: meaning split between the field and a home far away.
- **Scientists of Akademgorodok and Tomsk:** the calling of research.
- **Nenets, Khanty and Evenk herders and hunters:** meaning in land, reindeer and kin, as they describe it.
- **Buryat and Altai families:** epic and ancestral traditions of purpose.
- **Old Believers:** how a strict faith frames a life's point.
- **Young people leaving for Moscow or abroad:** whether Siberia is a place with a future.
- **Descendants of exiles and deportees:** meaning made from a family's suffering.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and folklore; mixed in psychology (many small student samples); thin in regional polling on meaning as such.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat; Khakas; Khanty; Nenets; Altai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide among small northern peoples is a painful subject and must be handled with community consent; some epic singing is tied to ritual settings.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search eLIBRARY.ru for "смысложизненные ориентации" with "Сибирь", "студенты Томска", "коренные народы Севера" to list measured groups.
2. Read *Farewell to Matyora* and *Tsar-Fish* together, noting where each locates the point of a life.
3. Open the *Monuments of Folklore* series catalog and pick the Buryat, Khakas and Khanty volumes.
4. Pull Rosstat mortality and suicide tables for each Siberian region and northern district.
5. Search press archives for "моногорода Кузбасса" and "шахтёрские забастовки 1989".
