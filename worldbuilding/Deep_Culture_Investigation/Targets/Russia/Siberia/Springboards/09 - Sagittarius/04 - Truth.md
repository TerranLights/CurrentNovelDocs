# Siberia: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat (with classical Mongolian script for chronicles); Khanty, Mansi and Evenki; Khakas and Altai; Siberian Tatar. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it (scripture, elders, scientists, the press, one's own experience), and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers:** the weight given to old books and right ritual.
- **Scientists and engineers** of Akademgorodok, Tomsk and Krasnoyarsk: empirical truth and its public standing.
- **Buryat lamas and lay Buddhists:** argued doctrine against popular devotion.
- **Shamanic practitioners and Indigenous elders** (Buryat, Khakas, Altai, Khanty, Evenk): oral record and revelation, as they describe them.
- **Families of repression victims** against those who defend the Soviet record.
- **Older television viewers and younger internet users.**
- **Kuzbass and Norilsk residents,** where industrial accidents meet official versions of events.
- **Siberian Tatars:** Islamic learning and local memory.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science history and Old Believer textual scholarship; mixed for repression memory; thin and risky for present-day media trust.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and classical Mongolian; Tibetan for monastic debate; Khanty, Mansi, Evenki; Khakas and Altai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Memorial was dissolved by court order (2021-2022); repression research and independent media carry legal risk; oaths and sacred records belong to the communities. Disputes settled in court go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
