# Siberia: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Akademgorodok, Tomsk and Krasnoyarsk university milieus:** merit through science and tests.
- **Industrial towns:** honors for labor, seniority against skill.
- **Officials and the Party-era elite's heirs:** appointment against competition.
- **Buryats:** Buddhist scholastic degrees and secular schooling; urban and rural.
- **Khakas and Altai:** epic performers, wrestlers and the value placed on mastery.
- **Nenets, Khanty, Evenki:** herding and hunting skill against school credentials; quota students.
- **Sakha,** if in the lands: olonkho performers and Yakutsk's selective schools.
- **Migrant workers from Central Asia,** whose credentials are often not recognized.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for schools, science and Soviet honors; mixed for attitudes; thin for traditional measures of skill outside festival reports.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Khakas and Sakha for epic traditions; Buryat and Tibetan terms for Buddhist degrees.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption in exams and posts is sensitive; quota debates can turn into ethnic resentment, so record each side's words; some epic repertoires are passed on only within families.
- **Held for review:** none.
