# Siberia: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Khakas, Sakha; Classical Chinese for early sources. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old settlers (*chaldony*, *kerzhaki*)** against 19th-century peasant migrants, Soviet-era arrivals and exiles' descendants.
- **Buryats:** clan precedence (Khori, Ekhirit, Bulagat) and lama ranks; Cisbaikal and Transbaikal.
- **Khanty and Mansi:** phratry and clan order; river groups (Kazym, Vakh, Ob, Northern Sos'va).
- **Nenets:** wealthy and poor herders; tundra and forest Nenets.
- **Khakas, Altai, Shor:** clan (*seok*) rank; southern and northern Altai groups.
- **Sakha,** if in the lands: toyon descent and ulus.
- **Cossack descendants, military towns, penal-colony towns.**
- **Central Asian and Caucasus migrants** and how they are placed in city hierarchies; Kazakh and German villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet and Russian institutions; mixed for older native ranks (much is colonial or Soviet ethnography); thin for present everyday rank among small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Classical Chinese and Old Turkic for early sources; Buryat, Khanty and Mansi for clan terms; Polish for Sieroszewski's other writings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prison caste terms are degrading and should only be described; racism and ethnic rank are touchy, and some activists have been pressured; women's taboos should be studied through women's own voices.
- **Held for review:** none.
