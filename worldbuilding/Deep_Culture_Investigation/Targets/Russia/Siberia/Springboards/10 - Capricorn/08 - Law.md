# Siberia: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Evenki, Shor, Sakha; Mongolian for early codes. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it. Capricorn takes the spirit of the law only; the letter goes to Libra.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats and Evenki:** memories of customary law and assemblies; Buddhist and shamanist views of right conduct.
- **Altai, Khakas and Shor:** clan custom, land conflicts with mines and resorts.
- **Khanty, Mansi and Nenets:** family hunting grounds against licenses and oil leases.
- **Old Believers:** rejection of, or caution toward, state law.
- **Fishing and hunting villages** (Baikal, the Yenisei, the Ob) where poaching laws bite.
- **Former camp towns and penal-colony districts:** the convict code beside state law.
- **City professionals** (Novosibirsk, Tomsk) against rural districts.
- **Sakha,** if in the lands; Central Asian migrants facing police checks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Russian for legal sociology and history; mixed for regional attitudes; thin and colonial-filtered for customary law.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Mongolian and Oirat for the 1640 code; Buryat and Evenki for assembly records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** courts, police and corruption are sensitive subjects; interviewees may face risk; 1990s crime touches living people; Indigenous land disputes are often in active litigation.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Find the 1841 *Свод степных законов* in a library catalog and any modern edition with commentary.
2. Search eLibrary.ru for «правосознание сибиряков» and «обычное право бурят».
3. Search regional press for «запрет на вылов омуля» reactions, 2017 onward.
4. Read Hendley's methods chapter for regions covered, then compare with Siberian polls.
5. Search «Казас шорцы уголь» for residents' statements.
