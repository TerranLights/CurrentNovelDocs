*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers:** household and prayer discipline kept for centuries apart from the state church.
- **Buryat Buddhists and shamanists:** two training paths with different teachers and tests.
- **Sakha, Buryat, Khakas and Altai** sports traditions, each with its own festival and rules.
- **Nenets, Evenki and Ket** families: hunting and herding apprenticeship against boarding-school discipline.
- **Industrial and shift workers** (Kuzbass mines, Yamal and Khanty-Mansi oil fields).
- **Military and Cossack families,** cadet schools.
- **Akademgorodok and the arts:** discipline of study and stage.
- **Former camp towns** (Norilsk, the Krasnoyarsk and Irkutsk penal zones).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Russian on sport, army, camps and religion; mixed on child-rearing; thin on Indigenous training ways outside Soviet ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Tibetan terms for monastic training; Sakha and Nenets for memoir and folklore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shaman initiations and some monastic teaching are closed to outsiders; drinking and sobriety talk easily slides into stereotype, so use communities' own words; Gulag and prison memory touches families.
- **Held for review:** none.
