# Siberia: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Khakas, Sakha, Khanty, Mansi, Nenets, Evenki, Siberian Tatar. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats:** Buddhist and shamanist families; Cisbaikal (Irkutsk Oblast) against Transbaikal (Buryatia, Aga).
- **Altai:** southern Altai-Kizhi and Telengit against northern Tubalar, Chelkan and Kumandin, with different mission histories.
- **Khakas and Shor:** Turkic peoples of the Minusinsk Basin and Mountain Shoria, beside Kuzbass industrial authority.
- **Ob-Ugric (Khanty, Mansi) and Nenets:** clan and spirit authority against oil and gas districts.
- **Evenki, Ket, Selkup, Dolgan, Nganasan:** small peoples dealing with distant administrations.
- **Russian Siberians:** Old Believers, Cossack descendants, Akademgorodok scientists (expert authority), mining and factory towns, rural districts.
- **Siberian Tatars** (Tobol, Baraba, Tom groups) and the German and Polish villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Russian for religion, labor and prisons; mixed for regional survey splits (small samples); thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat, Altai, Khanty and Nenets for folklore and the press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of authority is legally risky in Russia now, so interviews and forums need care and anonymity; shamanic authority can involve knowledge restricted to practitioners; prison sources touch living people.
- **Held for review:** none.
