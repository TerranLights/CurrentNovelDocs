# Siberia: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Industrial towns** (Kuzbass, Norilsk, Bratsk, Angarsk): workers' views of firm and state responsibility for safety and pollution.
- **Khanty, Mansi and Nenets** families in oil and gas districts, against herders far from them.
- **Dolgan and Nganasan** in Taimyr, after the 2020 spill.
- **Buryats:** clan-law memory and Buddhist teaching on deeds.
- **Khakas,** around the Sayano-Shushenskaya dam and its lost lands.
- **Lake Baikal communities:** Irkutsk activists, Baikalsk mill workers, Ol'khon residents.
- **Russian villages** in wildfire zones against city dwellers who breathe the smoke.
- **Old Believer communities,** with their own rules for answering wrongs inside the group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters and environmental disputes; mixed for everyday blame; thin for Indigenous terms.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat for Buddhist and customary texts; Khanty and Nenets through dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Memorial and several environmental groups were closed or labeled "foreign agents", so their materials survive in archives and abroad; families of disaster victims deserve care.
- **Held for review:** none.
