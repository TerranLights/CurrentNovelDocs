# Siberia: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Evenki, Nenets, Altai, Sakha. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats:** shamanist and Buddhist ideas of cosmic order; yurt space in herding districts against city flats.
- **Evenki, Nenets, Khanty, Mansi:** camp and tent order; order with the spirit-owners of land.
- **Altai and Khakas:** ritual places on passes and springs; clan order.
- **Sakha,** if in the lands: the three worlds of the olonkho.
- **Russian old settlers and Old Believers:** household order and religious ritual.
- **Planned Soviet cities and science towns** against old merchant towns.
- **Industrial and penal-colony districts** with memories of 1990s disorder.
- **Younger urban groups** for whom orderly cities and protest both matter.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history, cosmology and urban policy; mixed for present attitudes; thin and Soviet-filtered for some small peoples' cosmologies.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Mongolian for religious terms; Evenki and Nenets through dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some cosmological knowledge belongs to shamans and is not for outsiders; protest and federalism topics are legally risky; civil-war memory is still argued over.
- **Held for review:** none.
