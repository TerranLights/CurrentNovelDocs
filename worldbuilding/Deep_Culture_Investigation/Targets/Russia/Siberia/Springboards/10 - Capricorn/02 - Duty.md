# Siberia: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Siberia, region Russia. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian; Buryat, Sakha, Altai, Khakas, Evenki, Khanty, Nenets. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buryats:** clan duties of the *tailagan*; Buddhist duties of merit; families hit by mobilization.
- **Evenki and Nenets:** sharing duties among hunters and herders, nomadic against settled families.
- **Khanty and Mansi:** duties to sacred sites now crossed by oil infrastructure.
- **Altai, Khakas, Shor:** clan (*seok*) obligations and their urban fate.
- **Sakha,** if in the lands: kin duties in the *ulus* and the city of Yakutsk.
- **Russian old settlers** against Soviet-era migrants who came on a "Komsomol ticket".
- **Old Believers:** religious duties of fasting and prayer kept apart from the state.
- **Military towns and families,** veterans' groups, and Siberian Cossack societies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet and war memory; mixed for regional surveys; thin and mostly ethnographic for the small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Russian; Buryat and Sakha for epics and press; Evenki and Khanty terms through dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mobilization and war are dangerous topics in Russia now; protect sources and avoid naming informants. Clan rites may close to outsiders, and some are for men only.
- **Held for review:** none.
