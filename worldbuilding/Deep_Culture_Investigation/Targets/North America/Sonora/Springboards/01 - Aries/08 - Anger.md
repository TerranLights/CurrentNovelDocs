## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for victimization surveys and folk-healing studies; mixed for everyday display rules; thin for anger among the smaller Indigenous peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous languages for folk concepts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence, disappearances and cartel killings are live; healers' knowledge may be restricted; do not use survey figures as a portrait of a people. Riot and protest accounts are partisan. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet follows the core lands.
