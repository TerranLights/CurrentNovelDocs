# Sonora: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, plus Western Apache, O'odham, Yoeme and Comcaac for Indigenous speech and silence. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Spanish-speaking Sonorans against English-speaking Arizonans; border-crossers who move between both norms.
- Western Apache, O'odham, Yoeme, Yoreme, Comcaac and Cucapá: each with its own speaking and silence rules.
- Women and men; elders and the young; workers and managers.
- Market traders against office workers; ranch families against city dwellers.
- Newcomers against old families; migrants against residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey data and for Western Apache silence; mixed for Spanish everyday assertion; thin for Comcaac and Cucapá speech norms.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous languages for speech norms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** consumer and labor complaint systems touch law; point to Libra and Capricorn for legal sides. Elders' protocols for speaking may be restricted. Do not read one voice for a people. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet follows the core lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
