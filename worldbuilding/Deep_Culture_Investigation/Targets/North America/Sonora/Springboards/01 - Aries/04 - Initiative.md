## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Border-industry workers and managers (assembly plants, aerospace, auto) against farm owners of the irrigated valleys and against ranch and mining families.
- Women's cooperatives and savings circles against male-led firms.
- Recent migrants who start businesses against old founding families.
- Indigenous communities' work parties and enterprises (Yoeme, O'odham, Yoreme, Comcaac) taken separately.
- Mormon and Mennonite agricultural colonies; Anglo ranch and retiree communities on the Arizona side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business formation and self-employment counts; mixed for volunteering; thin for informal and migrant enterprise.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** cartel-linked fortune stories sit beside founder lore and are dangerous and contested; popular songs of self-made men need care. Cooperative and ejido rules are law: point to Libra and Capricorn. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet follows the core lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
