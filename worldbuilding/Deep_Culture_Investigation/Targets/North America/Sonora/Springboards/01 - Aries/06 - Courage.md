*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, plus Yoeme, O'odham and Apache-language sources for peoples' own accounts. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Yoeme, Mayo, O'odham, Apache (Chiricahua and Western Apache lands in Arizona) and Comcaac: separate warrior and survival histories, told by each people.
- Soldiers' families, firefighters, miners and ranchers: dangerous-trade cultures.
- Men and women as told about: women's endurance and migration stories.
- Migrants, aid volunteers and the border patrol, each describing their own bravery.
- Mexican-side and U.S.-side veterans, who serve different states.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for award records, labor and Yaqui war history; mixed for everyday moral courage; thin for women's and children's accounts in print.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous languages for peoples' own war and endurance songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** narcocorridos praise contested nerve and are dangerous to cite; migrant deaths and cartel violence are live; warrior and ceremonial knowledge may be restricted. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet follows the core lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
