# Sonora: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, and the nations' own languages through their programs. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, mind, group, institution or place, so the whole can be clean again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Curandero-using families of Sonoran and Mexican American descent; O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo and Comcaac communities, each with its own cleansing and restricted ceremony; Chinese Mexican descendants; Latter-day Saint colonists; evangelical converts who reject folk cleansing; mining towns (Bisbee, Cananea) and farm towns; unhoused and encampment communities; recent migrants. Compare them because each defines pollution, and who may cleanse, differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on pollution and expulsion history; thin on household cleansing beyond folk-medicine studies. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' and ceremonial cleansing is restricted by many peoples; use only what keepers publish. "Limpieza social" and cartel purges are dangerous topics; use public journalism and families' accounts.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
