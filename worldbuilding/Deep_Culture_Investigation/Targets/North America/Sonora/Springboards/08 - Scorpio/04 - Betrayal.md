# Sonora: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, and the nations' own languages through their programs. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Sonoran and border Mexican families; Anglo Arizonans; Tohono O'odham and Akimel O'odham; Yaqui and Mayo; the Apache nations; Latter-day Saint colonists; miners' and farm workers' communities; migrant and deportee communities; families with kin on both sides of the line. Compare them because the betrayal that matters (treaty, family, class, kin across the border) is different for each.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich. Rich on song, history and press; thin on private infidelity and informing at community level. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** informing and cartel retaliation are dangerous topics; use published songs and journalism, never contacts. Peoples' own accounts of treaty betrayal come first.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
