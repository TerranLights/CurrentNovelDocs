# Sonora: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, and the nations' own languages through their programs. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or institution, accepting the risk of being let down; loyalty is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Kin-based border Mexican American families; Anglo retiree communities; ranching and farm communities; O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo, Comcaac and Apache nations, whose trust in state researchers differs; Latter-day Saint colonists with church-centered trust; migrants and recent arrivals; miners' and city workers' communities; members of mutual-aid and fraternal societies. Compare them because the radius of trust (kin, congregation, nation, stranger) differs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on survey trust; thin on how trust is earned in daily life. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** distrust of researchers is itself a finding to respect; Native research requires each nation's consent. Smuggling and armed-group material is dangerous.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
