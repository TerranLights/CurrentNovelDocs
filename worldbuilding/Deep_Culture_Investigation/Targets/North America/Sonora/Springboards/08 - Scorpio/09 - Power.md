## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Hermosillo and Phoenix elites; mining and agribusiness owners and their workers; ranching families; Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham, Comcaac, Apache and other nations governing themselves; Latter-day Saint communities; farmworker unions; border-city politicians; small-town *caciques*; women and men. Compare them because power is held through land, water, company, church, kin and tradition in different places.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on political history; mixed on hidden and informal power. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** armed-group power is dangerous; use public reporting only. Native political and ceremonial authority is theirs to describe. Law and authority belong to Capricorn; this sheet takes only power.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
