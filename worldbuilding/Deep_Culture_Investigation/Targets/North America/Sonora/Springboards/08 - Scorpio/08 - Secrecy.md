*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Undocumented and mixed-status families; Native nations with restricted ceremony (Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham, Comcaac, Apache, Hopi); Latter-day Saint colonists; Catholic and evangelical families; border-town and small-town residents against Phoenix and Hermosillo; sexual minorities by region and generation; journalists and activists; families with converted-Jewish heritage. Compare them because what is hidden, and from whom, differs by status and community.

## 4. Flags
