*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Hermosillo and Phoenix suburb households; irrigated-valley farm communities; farmworker (*jornalero*) families; Yaqui and Mayo peoples, whose river is contested; Tohono O'odham, whose land the border crosses; the Comcaac and their coast; Latter-day Saint colony families; ranching communities; retirees and gated-community residents; migrant shelter users. Compare them because control means water, land, household or movement depending on who is asked.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on water and border control; mixed on household control (ENDIREH is strong, ethnographic work thin). The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** control of territory by armed groups is dangerous to research; stay with published journalism. Land and water knowledge of Native nations is theirs to share.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
