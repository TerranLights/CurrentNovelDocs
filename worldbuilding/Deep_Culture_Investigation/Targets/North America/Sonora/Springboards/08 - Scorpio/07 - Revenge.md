## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Ranching and mining communities; border Mexican American families; Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham, Apache and Comcaac peoples, each with its own account of reprisal and peacemaking; farm valleys and cities; congregations that teach forgiveness against those with honor codes; gang and prison subcultures; women and men; recent migrants. Compare them because the duty to avenge and the duty to forgive are weighted differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on feuds and historical legends; thin on quiet, everyday revenge. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current armed-group retaliation is dangerous to ask about; use published journalism. School-violence research is delicate.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
