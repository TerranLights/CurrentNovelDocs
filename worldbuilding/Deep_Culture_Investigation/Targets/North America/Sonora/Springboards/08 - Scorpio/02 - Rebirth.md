## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Catholic and evangelical Sonoran families; Latter-day Saint communities; Yaqui and Mayo communities with their Lenten and Easter forms; O'odham; the Comcaac, whose language work is separate; deportees and returning migrants; recovery-house residents; Arizona retirees who moved to start over; wildfire-affected towns. Compare them because renewal is told through different institutions: sacrament, testimony, ceremony, program or move.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on recovery programs and language revival; thin on ordinary Sonoran conversion accounts. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Native languages through the nations' own programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Native ceremony and its meanings are often restricted; take only what each nation publishes. Forced conversion and mission history are painful; read them in the nations' voices.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
