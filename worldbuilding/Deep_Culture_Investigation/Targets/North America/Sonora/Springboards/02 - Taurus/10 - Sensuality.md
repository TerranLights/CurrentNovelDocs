# Sonora: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish and English; O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo and Seri for Indigenous sources. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound, sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Indigenous nations:** O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo, Comcaac, Apache: foods, music and bathing practices, each as the nation shares them.
- **Coastal towns against inland desert against sierra:** fish, beef, produce and heat.
- **Border cities against ranch country:** different leisure, nightlife and music.
- **Religious groups:** Catholic festival worship against Latter-day Saint and evangelical restraint.
- **Retirees and tourists** against residents; **generations** in food and drink habits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, music and festival documentation; mixed for touch and comfort; thin for private pleasure. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial music and dance (Yaqui and Mayo ritual), sweat-bath and healing practices, and sexuality (belongs to Scorpio); use only what nations publish.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- **Food ways proper** are covered in the domain sheet.
