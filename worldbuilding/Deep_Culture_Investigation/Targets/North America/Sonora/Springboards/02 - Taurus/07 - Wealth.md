# Sonora: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish and English. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mining, ranching, agribusiness and border-trade families**: old fortunes and new ones.
- **Indigenous nations**: O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo, Comcaac, Apache: wealth from gaming, fisheries, land or none; what each counts as wealth, from its own sources.
- **Retirees and second-home owners** against long-term residents.
- **Maquiladora and farm laborers** against professionals; **migrant households** receiving remittances.
- **Gender and generation:** who inherits, who displays.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for income and inequality on each side of the border; mixed for what wealth means in talk; thin for hidden wealth and for Indigenous accounts outside what nations publish. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** narco-linked wealth (published reporting only; risk to sources); nations' gaming finances; living families named in rich lists.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- **Law:** taxation and property law go to Libra and Capricorn.
