## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Indigenous nations:** Tohono O'odham, Akimel O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo, Comcaac, Apache: each with its own published teachings; do not merge them.
- **Religious communities:** Catholic parishes, Latter-day Saint colonists in the sierra, evangelical churches, Jewish and Muslim families, the nonreligious.
- **Ranch, farm, mine and border-city households:** different working worlds, different priorities.
- **Generations:** the grandparents' homeland against the teens' online world; Spanish-dominant against English-dominant families.
- **Elites and ordinary people:** do they name the same values?

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national-level surveys, but state or valley samples are small; mixed for church and school documents; thin for Indigenous values outside what nations publish. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous-language sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred teachings and ceremonial instruction held by Indigenous communities; religious dissent; political polarization.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- **Scholars' labels** such as *familismo* are hypotheses to test, not findings.
