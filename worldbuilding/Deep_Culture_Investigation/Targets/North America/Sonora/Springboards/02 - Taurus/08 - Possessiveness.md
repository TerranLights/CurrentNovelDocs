# Sonora: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish and English. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, people included, beyond what the bond or thing warrants, and keeping others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gender and generation:** older and younger women and men; whether jealousy is still read as love.
- **Rural ranch and farm households against city and border households.**
- **Indigenous nations:** O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo, Comcaac: sharing teachings and rules on family claims, as each publishes them.
- **Religious communities:** Catholic, Latter-day Saint and evangelical teaching on marriage and covetousness.
- **Migrant and cross-border families:** parents who hold on to children across the line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for violence surveys, thin for everyday possessiveness and for sharing practices; mixed for song and media. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and femicide data (handle with care; protect sources); family privacy; Indigenous kinship rules.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
