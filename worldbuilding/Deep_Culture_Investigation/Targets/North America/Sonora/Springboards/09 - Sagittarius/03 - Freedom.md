*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham (Tohono and Akimel), Comcaac and Yuman peoples (freedom as sovereignty); ranchers and farmers; miners and union families; libertarian and conservative Arizona against border-city Mexican American communities; migrants and the citizens who aid or oppose them; journalists; retirees on open land. Compare them because freedom may mean land, speech, movement or no master.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on press-freedom and Native sovereignty news; mixed on folk meanings of the word; thin on Comcaac and Mayo views in print. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Native terms only via the nations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** journalist threats, land and water disputes and migrant deaths are dangerous; use public reports. Law is researched only in Libra and Capricorn; the sources above are for ideas and politics, not statutes.
