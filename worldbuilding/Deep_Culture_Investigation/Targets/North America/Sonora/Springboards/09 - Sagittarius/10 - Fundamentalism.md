# Sonora: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, and the Native languages of the Sonoran Desert lands (O'odham, Yaqui/Yoeme, Mayo/Yoreme, Comcaac). **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; the word is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Catholic conservatives (integralist currents) against Catholic liberals; evangelical and Pentecostal congregations; Latter-day Saint mainstream against fundamentalist Mormon communities; Jehovah's Witnesses and other small high-boundary churches; megachurches against village chapels; homeschooling families; leavers; secular certainty as a contested case; Native Christians on their own terms. Compare them because strictness differs by denomination and generation, and the word is often an insult.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Arizona congregations and on scholarship about Mexico's Protestant growth; mixed on Mormon colony life; thin on Sonoran case studies and on leavers. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** high-control groups and abuse cases need care for victims; use groups' own names and statements beside critics'. Never apply the word to Native religion. Violence cases are rare edges, not the norm.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
