# Sonora: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, and the Native languages of the Sonoran Desert lands (O'odham, Yaqui/Yoeme, Mayo/Yoreme, Comcaac). **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Mining, farm (Yaqui and Mayo valleys) and ranch workers; maquiladora workers; students; Phoenix and Tucson professionals; retirees in planned communities; migrants and the people who aid them; Catholics, Pentecostals and the unaffiliated; O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo and Comcaac peoples, each on its own terms; young people called *ninis*. Compare them because work, kin and faith carry meaning differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on well-being and mortality statistics; mixed on purpose in people's own words; thin outside the news. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Native-language terms only via the nations' programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and despair data need care; Native teachings on purpose are restricted in part.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
