## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Church-based activists (Catholic, Presbyterian and others) against religious conservatives; farm-worker and union families; border-wall opponents against border-vigilance supporters; Native defenders of land and water (Yaqui, O'odham, Comcaac); Mormon colony families; students; party loyalists against switchers. Compare them because conviction flows from faith, class, land or party, and each calls the other zeal.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on sanctuary, farm-worker and Yaqui-water stories; mixed on everyday conviction; thin on private conscience. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence, vigilantism and cartel-linked cases are dangerous; use published accounts only. Law is researched only in Libra and Capricorn.
