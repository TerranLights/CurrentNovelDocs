## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Sonoran Catholic parishes and border-city Mexican American congregations; Pentecostal and evangelical churches; Latter-day Saint communities in Arizona and the Sonoran colonies; Mennonite farm settlements; the unaffiliated and humanist circles of Tucson and Phoenix; O'odham (Tohono and Akimel), Yaqui, Mayo, Comcaac, Yuman peoples and Apache; ranchers and the libertarian West; college towns against mining and farm towns; migrants. Compare them because sources of truth differ by group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on census religion, mission history and border migration; mixed on philosophy and Sonoran thought; thin on small congregations and on freedom as a folk concept. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Native-language work only through the nations' programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Native ceremony, shrines and sacred places are restricted by many nations (Yaqui Holy Week, O'odham pilgrimages); use only what each nation publishes. Migrant and folk-saint material can touch on danger.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Law is researched only in Libra and Capricorn.
