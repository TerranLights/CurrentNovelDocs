# Sonora: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, and the Native languages of the Sonoran Desert lands (O'odham, Yaqui/Yoeme, Mayo/Yoreme, Comcaac). **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Catholic parishes against Pentecostal and evangelical churches; Latter-day Saint communities; the unaffiliated; folk-devotion circles; O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo and Comcaac peoples, each on its own terms, including Native Christians and traditionalists; migrants in transit and their hosts; ejido towns against Hermosillo, Tucson and Phoenix; generations. Compare them because the object of faith differs by group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on surveys and Catholic history; mixed on Pentecostal and folk faith; thin on faith in science, the market or the nation at the local level. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Native-language terms only via the nations' programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Yaqui, Mayo and O'odham ceremony is restricted in part; use only what each nation publishes. Abuse cases and cult devotions need care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
