## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Indigenous peoples (Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham, Comcaac, Cocopah), each in its own account.
- Ranch and farm elites against agricultural workers, many of them migrants from southern states.
- Border factory workers against professionals in Hermosillo and Tucson.
- Mexican-heritage and Anglo-American residents on the Arizona side.
- Men and women; younger and older generations; lighter- and darker-skinned families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in official statistics; mixed on attitudes; thin on Indigenous views of equality.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for first-hand accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs and racial sorting recur in sources; quote none beyond a word. Histories of removal and violence against Indigenous peoples call for the nations' own accounts first.
- **Law:** no law here; see Libra and Capricorn. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
