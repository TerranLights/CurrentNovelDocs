## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Farmers and agribusiness in the Yaqui and Mayo valleys against smallholders and ejidatarios.
- Indigenous peoples as targets and authors of reform, each in its own account.
- Mining towns, border cities and port cities.
- Church-based reformers (Catholic, Protestant, Latter-day Saints) against secular ones.
- Generations that remember the revolution's heirs against those of the 4T.
- The Arizona side's Anglo-American reformers and Mexican-heritage organizers.

- **Data:** rich on the revolution and agrarian eras; mixed for the present; thin on small towns.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for first-hand accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anticlerical campaigns and agrarian conflicts are still contested in memory; reforms imposed on Indigenous peoples call for the nations' own accounts first. Present-day political partisanship in sources.
