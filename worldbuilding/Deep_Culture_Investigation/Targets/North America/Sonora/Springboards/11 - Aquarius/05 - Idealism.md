## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Young volunteers and students against retirees and long-settled families.
- Faith-based idealists (Catholic, Protestant, Latter-day Saints) against secular ones.
- Border humanitarians, farm-modernizers, and artists and architects who came to build something new.
- Indigenous nations' own ideals of right living, in their own voices.
- Political camps: those who inherit the revolution's ideals and those of the 4T.
