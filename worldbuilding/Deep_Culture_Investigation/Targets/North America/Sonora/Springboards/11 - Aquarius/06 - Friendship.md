# Sonora: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for the nations' own forms of friendship and alliance. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Border-city residents with friends on both sides against inland ranch and mining towns.
- Men's and women's friendship; young and old; city and countryside.
- The Indigenous nations' own ritual and ceremonial friendships, with published sources only.
- Migrant families from the south and families long in place.
- Retirees and winter visitors against year-round residents.
- Church circles (Catholic, Protestant, Latter-day Saints) against workplace and sports circles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good survey data from INEGI and U.S. sources, thin on informal friendship.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for first-hand sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ritual sponsorship tied to Indigenous ceremonies is restricted in part; use what the nations publish. Favors between friends can shade into corruption, so sources may be political.
- **Law:** no law here; see Libra and Capricorn. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
