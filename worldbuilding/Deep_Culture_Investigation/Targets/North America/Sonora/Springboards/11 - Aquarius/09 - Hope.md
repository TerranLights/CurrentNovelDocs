# Sonora: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for the nations' own hopes and prayers. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Migrants on the move and their families at home against families long in place.
- Youth, including DACA recipients and students, against elders.
- Farm, mining, border-factory and professional households by class.
- Indigenous nations' own hopes for survival and land, in their own words.
- Faith communities: Catholic pilgrims, Protestants, Latter-day Saints, the unaffiliated.
- Families of the missing and families of the dead on the migrant trails.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on expectations and remittances; mixed on religious practice; thin on private hope.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for first-hand sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrant deaths, the disappeared and suicide are sensitive; Yaqui and other ceremonies are restricted outside what the nations publish.
- **Law:** no law here; see Libra and Capricorn. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
