## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Sonoran Desert Indigenous peoples (Yaqui, Mayo, Tohono O'odham, Akimel O'odham, Comcaac, Cocopah), each separately: their own governments and societies.
- Border cities against ranch and ejido towns, and the Colorado River and Yaqui valley farm districts.
- Mexican-heritage, Anglo-American, Mormon-colony, and Chinese-descent communities (Mexicali) as lines of settlement.
- Retirees and winter visitors (planned communities) against long-settled families.
- Recent migrants against families long in place.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the Arizona side and for INEGI's national surveys; mixed at state level; thin for the smallest peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous languages for first-hand sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial societies and some Indigenous knowledge are restricted; follow the peoples' own publications and permissions. Migrant-aid work can be politically charged; read both sides.
- **Law:** no law here; see Libra and Capricorn. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
