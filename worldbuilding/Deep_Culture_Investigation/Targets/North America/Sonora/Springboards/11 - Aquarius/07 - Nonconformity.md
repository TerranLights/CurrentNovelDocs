## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Border-city and Tucson youth scenes against inland ranch and farm towns.
- Faith minorities (evangelical, Latter-day Saints, the unaffiliated) against the Catholic majority.
- Indigenous nations: their own accounts of people who differ, with published sources only.
- Artists and retirees who came to alternative towns against families long settled.
- Men and women; sexual and gender minorities; generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on the U.S. side's surveys, thin on Sonora's subcultures.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for first-hand sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence against sexual and gender minorities; organized-crime culture and its songs are dangerous ground; Indigenous teachings on gender and difference are the nations' to share.
- **Law:** no law here; see Libra and Capricorn. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran core.
