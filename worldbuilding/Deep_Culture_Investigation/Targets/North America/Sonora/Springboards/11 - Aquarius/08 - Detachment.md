# Sonora: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for the nations' own teachings on composure and solitude. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance, emotional, personal or social, from people, from one's own feelings or from outcomes; which forms a culture values and which it condemns.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Ranch and desert-edge households against border-city and big-city apartment life.
- Men and women; elders living alone against young adults.
- Workers in service and care jobs, where distance is trained.
- Indigenous nations' own norms of composure and non-interference.
- Border commuters and migrants away from kin against families long in place.
- Retirees in planned communities against year-round residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good statistics on living alone, thin on cultural norms of reserve.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for first-hand sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mourning and healing practices of the nations are restricted in part. Loneliness, suicide and violence-linked trauma call for care.
- **Law:** no law here; see Libra and Capricorn. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
