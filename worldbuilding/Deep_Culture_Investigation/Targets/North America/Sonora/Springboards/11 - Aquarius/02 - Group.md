*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation

- Indigenous nations: their own clans, societies and governments, separately.
- Union members against non-members; border commuters against settled residents.
- Winter retirees against year-round residents.
- Youth groups and neighborhood gangs against church and sports groups.
- Mexican-heritage, Anglo-American and Mormon-colony settlements.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong on labor history and election returns, thin on informal groups.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for first-hand accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial societies of the Yaqui, Mayo and O'odham are restricted; use only what the nations publish. Organized-crime groups are dangerous to study first-hand; use court-free journalism and scholarship only. Collectives of searching families should be approached with care.
- **Law:** no law here; see Libra and Capricorn. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
