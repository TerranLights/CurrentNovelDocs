## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
University and research-city people (Tucson, Hermosillo) against ranching and farming families; miners and assayers in copper towns; fishers, whose reading of sea and weather is practical analysis; Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Seri knowledge-holders and their own ways of reasoning; Mexican American and Anglo school communities in Arizona; Latter-day Saint schools; people working for border manufacturers in quality control.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for test scores and research institutions, thin for how ordinary people name and judge "analysis."
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for reckoning and observation terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous ecological and calendrical knowledge is held by its communities; do not extract it without their consent. Test-score comparisons invite stereotypes, so read as variation only. Law excluded. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
