## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Elders and children among Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Seri families; ranching and farm households with hired hands against city households; Mexican American multigenerational homes in Tucson; retirees who moved from elsewhere; migrants working abroad and the families they left; disabled people and their carers; Latter-day Saint colonies with strong church welfare; people in border towns whose jobs are tied to factories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for aging and disability surveys, thin for how people themselves judge "useless" or "burden."
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for elder roles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elders' knowledge of plants, healing and songs may be restricted. End-of-life talk and disability need care. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
