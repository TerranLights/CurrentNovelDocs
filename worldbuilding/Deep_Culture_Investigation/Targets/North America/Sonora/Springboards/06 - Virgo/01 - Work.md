# Sonora: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Mexico). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham, Seri. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Copper miners (Cananea, Nacozari, Bisbee, Morenci); ranching families; irrigated-valley farmers and the migrant farmworkers who pick for them; fishing communities of the Gulf coast; Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham, Seri and Cucapá workers in wage and subsistence work; border factory workers; service and resort workers; Latter-day Saint colonists; technicians in Tucson's defense and optics industries; people in the informal economy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mining and farm labor, statistics and history; mixed for informal and household work; thin for Indigenous subsistence work.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for work terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** undocumented workers and wage theft need care; Indigenous subsistence and ceremonial work belongs to the communities. Labor law belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
