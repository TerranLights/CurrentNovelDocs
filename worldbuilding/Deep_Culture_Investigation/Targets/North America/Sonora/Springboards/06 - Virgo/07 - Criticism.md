## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Journalists and activists against officials; workers and supervisors in border factories; parents and children in Mexican American households against Anglo households; Yaqui, Mayo and O'odham communities, where councils and elders correct members in ways of their own; church communities (Catholic, Pentecostal, Latter-day Saint) with their own forms of correction; students and teachers; online communities against face-to-face ones; older and younger generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for press and survey data, thin for private criticism and family correction.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages for scolding and advice terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** danger to journalists and critics; names of living critics are not pointers. Indigenous correction and council practice may be closed. Political satire needs context. Law excluded. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
