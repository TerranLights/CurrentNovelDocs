# Sonora: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Native languages through each nation's own programs. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how a culture understands the sibling bond: who counts, what siblings owe, how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Large rural and ranch families against small urban ones; Mexican-heritage households with extended exchange against Anglo nuclear ones; Mennonite and Mormon colonists (very large families); O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo, Seri and Apache peoples, each with its own kin terms and cousin rules; migrant families where one sibling went north first; older siblings who interpret or work for the family; twins and only children; half- and step-siblings in blended families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for family size and households; mixed for care and rivalry; thin for private feeling, except in memoirs.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** kin systems and clan relationships belong to the nations; use only what they share. Inheritance rules are held for Libra and Capricorn. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
