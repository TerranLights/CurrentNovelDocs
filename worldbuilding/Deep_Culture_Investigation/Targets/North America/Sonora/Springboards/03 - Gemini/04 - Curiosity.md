# Sonora: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Native languages through each nation's own programs. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, find out and explore, and how far a culture encourages, channels, limits or punishes it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Children asked to question and children taught to watch first (family by family); university and observatory towns (Tucson, Flagstaff) against ranch and mining towns; Mexican-heritage, Anglo and Native households; tourists, retirees and snowbirds against residents; the nations that set research rules for outsiders (O'odham, Yaqui, Seri, Havasupai, Apache); border residents asked constant questions by officials; migrants who may not be asked about status.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for museums, surveys and research-ethics cases; thin for everyday rules about whom one may ask what.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions about status, origins and religion; outsiders' research on Native peoples has a painful record, so read it critically. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Havasupai case and one Seri or O'odham research-protocol page for the consent frame.
