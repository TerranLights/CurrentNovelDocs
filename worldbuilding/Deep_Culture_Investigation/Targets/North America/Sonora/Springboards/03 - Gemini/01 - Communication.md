# Sonora: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, Western Apache, O'odham, Yoeme, Comcaac. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and what people believe good communication is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Hermosillo and Tucson city speakers against ranch, mining and farm towns; Spanish-dominant, English-dominant and bilingual households; the O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo, Seri, Western Apache and Kumeyaay peoples, each with its own speech norms (silence, who speaks first); older and younger generations; Deaf communities; recent migrants who speak Mixtec or Zapotec.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Spanish and English media and surveys; mixed for Native communication norms (some published, some held by the nations); thin for everyday private talk.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Native languages only as each nation shares them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial and council speech belongs to its people; do not extract it. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
