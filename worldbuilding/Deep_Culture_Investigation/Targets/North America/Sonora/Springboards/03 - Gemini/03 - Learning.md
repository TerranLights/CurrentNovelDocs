## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Urban Hermosillo, Tucson and Phoenix families against ranch, desert and mining communities; Mexican-heritage, Anglo and mixed families; O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo, Seri, Apache and Kumeyaay peoples, whose elders, language programs and schools differ; Mennonite and Mormon colonists; migrant farmworker children moving between schools; homeschoolers and private-school families; rural-teacher-college graduates against university graduates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for test scores, attainment and school systems; mixed for family and community learning ways; thin for informal learning outside published studies.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Native languages through each nation's materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** residential and boarding school histories are painful; tell them in the nations' words first. Some teachings are not for outsiders. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
