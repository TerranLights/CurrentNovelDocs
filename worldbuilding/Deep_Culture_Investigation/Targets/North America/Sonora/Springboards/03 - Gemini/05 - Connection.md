## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Transborder families and those who stay on one side; Tucson and Phoenix retirees and snowbirds against long-rooted ranch families; Mexican-heritage households with compadrazgo ties and Anglo households without; O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo, Seri and Apache peoples, each with its own kin and pilgrimage ties; Mennonite and Mormon colonies, tight within and thin without; recent migrants separated from kin; men and women (friendship studies); young people on apps against elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for remittances, migration and loneliness surveys; mixed for ritual kinship in Sonora; thin for private networks and favors.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family separation and deportation are live and painful; migrants' names stay private. Ceremony-linked gatherings belong to their communities. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
