# Sonora: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Native languages through each nation's own programs. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, and rumor: unconfirmed reports passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Small ranch, mining and desert towns (where everyone knows) against Phoenix, Tucson and Hermosillo; Spanish and English speakers; tightly bound communities (Mennonite and Mormon colonies, Native nations, church parishes) against loosely bound ones; women, men and teenagers; workplace and online gossip; O'odham, Yaqui, Mayo, Seri and Apache peoples, each with its own speaking rules, as shared; border towns where rumors cross the line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for direct gossip, since talk is private; mixed for rumors, legends and media; rich for harassment and bullying surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** real people's scandals and unproven claims; log patterns, not names. Slander law goes to Libra and Capricorn. Some communities treat speech about the absent or the dead as restricted. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
