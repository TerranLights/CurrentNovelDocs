## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Public-school teachers, with their union, against private-sector professionals.
- Mining and factory workers, with seniority systems, against farm and fishing owners.
- First-generation university students against old professional families.
- Native peoples and communities whose earned roles may follow different rules.
- Migrants and daily cross-border workers whose credentials may not transfer.
- Arizona long-settled families against newcomers, if Arizona lands are carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. National survey items exist; state cuts are rare; employment and teacher-post material is mostly press and specialist studies; Native earned-role material is thin and held by communities. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** admissions disputes involving named individuals; ritual roles and their selection within Native communities.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
