# Sonora: Law, spirit of the law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*. Spirit only (what law is for, obedience, discretion, legal culture); the letter of the law belongs to Libra.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish; English; Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham, Seri for community sources. **Concept in one line:** how people relate to the binding rules of their community: what law is for, whose it is, and whether to obey, use, bend or avoid it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Yaqui, Mayo, Seri, O'odham and Navajo communities with their own justice.
- Ranch and farm communities that settle matters among themselves.
- Border residents on both sides, including daily crossers and long-settled families.
- Migrants, citizens and people without papers, whose dealings with law differ.
- Mining towns and port towns with different police histories.
- Retirees and newcomers in Arizona; Latter-day Saint colonies with church discipline.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich. Survey items by state and rich border history; Native justice sources are held by communities. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence and disappearances (families first); undocumented status; customary justice shared only on community terms; named living officials.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands. Statute-level work is left to Libra.
