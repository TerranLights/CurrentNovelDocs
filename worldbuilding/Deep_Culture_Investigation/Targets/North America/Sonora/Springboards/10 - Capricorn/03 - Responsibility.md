## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Yaqui, Mayo, Seri and O'odham communities: stewardship of land and sea as stated by each.
- Families of migrants, where children often interpret for parents.
- Mining-town, farm and fishing households.
- Hermosillo urban middle class against rural ranch families.
- Workers in the border maquiladora towns against farm workers.
- Latter-day Saint colonies, with their own church structure of accountability.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Case-based material is rich (the daycare fire, the spill, the fire deaths); survey items on personal against collective responsibility are national and rarely split by state. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** families of the daycare and fire victims; community accounts of land loss are told on the community's terms.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
