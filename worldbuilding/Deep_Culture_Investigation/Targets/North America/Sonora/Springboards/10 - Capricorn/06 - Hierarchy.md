## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Old landed and ranch families against wage workers on farms and in mines.
- Mestizo majority and Native peoples, each separated: Yaqui, Mayo, Seri, Guarijío, Pima Bajo, Cucapá.
- Migrants from southern Mexico living in the north against long-settled Sonorans.
- Tucson's old Mexican families, Anglo newcomers and retirees, if Arizona lands are carried.
- Chinese-Mexican descendants and other settled minorities.
- Domestic workers and their employers; maquiladora workers and managers.
- O'odham nations with their own offices and kin groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich. Class and color data are national with state cuts; Sonoran labor and ethnic history is well documented; Seri and O'odham ranking structures are in specialist ethnography. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Yaqui and O'odham for office terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** racial and color terms and slurs are described, not used; village office lists and ceremonial ranks are shared only on community terms.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
