## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Yaqui and Mayo communities, whose village offices run beside municipal government.
- Seri, Guarijío and Pima Bajo communities, where the data differ in kind.
- Ejido members against private landholders.
- Mining-town workers against valley farmers.
- Latter-day Saint colonies against Catholic towns.
- Arizona county voters (rural sheriff counties against Tucson and Phoenix).
- O'odham nations, if Arizona lands are carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. State-level survey items exist; community authority data are mostly ethnography and are older. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Yaqui for governance terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the inner workings of Yaqui governance and ceremony are shared on the community's terms; living disputes between tribal officials and state agencies.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
