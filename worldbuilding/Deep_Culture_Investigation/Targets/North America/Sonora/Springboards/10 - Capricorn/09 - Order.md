## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Capital Hermosillo against border towns (Nogales, Agua Prieta, San Luis Río Colorado) and port towns (Guaymas, Puerto Peñasco).
- Mining and company towns against farm and ranch towns.
- Yaqui, Mayo, Seri and O'odham communities, with order that includes ritual and kin.
- Arizona planned communities against inner-city neighborhoods and rural counties, if Arizona lands are carried.
- Households in the settled middle class against households of migrants or workers on shifts.
- Church-run communities such as Latter-day Saint colonies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich for crime and perceived safety by state or city; household and cosmic order are thin and ethnographic. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the rest is written for the Sonoran core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Yaqui and Seri for ritual order.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial sequence and sacred sites are shared only as communities allow; violence and disappearances (families first); people living outside, named in news.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
