# Sonora: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (northern Mexican usage), English, plus Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Comcaac for community sources. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Catholic, Latter-day Saint, evangelical and nonreligious households.
- Rural ranching families against city professionals.
- Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Comcaac communities, each with its own marriage and cooperation customs.
- Cross-border families living on both sides of the line.
- Mining and maquiladora families, where work shapes the household.
- Same-sex couples, whose recognition differs by where they live.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; counts of couples are rich; the meaning of partnership is thin and lives in songs, fiction and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; community languages for Indigenous practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous kinship and ceremonial ties may be restricted. Marriage law is outside this sheet's scope and belongs in section 0 of Libra, in letter-of-the-law form. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
