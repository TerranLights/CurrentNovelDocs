# Sonora: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (northern Mexican usage), English; Indigenous languages for community councils. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Ranchers and farmers, who decide fast by season, against office and public workers.
- Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Comcaac councils, with their own methods.
- Catholic, Latter-day Saint and evangelical households that route choices through clergy or prayer.
- Younger adults and older generations.
- Business owners and wage workers, who face different risks in deciding.
- Border residents choosing which side to live on.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin; undecided-voter and turnout figures exist, while private habits of deciding must come from fieldwork, sayings and fiction. Words for it are the best first handle.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clinical indecision overlaps with depression and anxiety, so use aggregate data only. Tribal council processes may be closed. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
