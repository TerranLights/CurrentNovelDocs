# Sonora: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (northern Mexican usage) and English; Indigenous languages for community sources. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others what one disowns in oneself, and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Long-settled families and newcomers, including snowbirds and retirees.
- Residents of border towns against those far from the line.
- Mexican-heritage and Anglo-heritage Arizonans, in each direction.
- Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Comcaac communities, who have been targets of images and who have their own views of outsiders.
- Households that use healers against those that use clinics, for how they explain misfortune.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; hate-crime and poll data are rich; private projection and folk explanations of misfortune are thin and need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hostile images of living groups are charged; treat them as terms to study. Yaqui and other ceremonies may be restricted. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

5. Ask tribal offices what is public about Lenten ceremony.
