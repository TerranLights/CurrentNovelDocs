# Sonora: Domain and folkways, partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (northern Mexican usage), English, plus Yaqui/Yoeme, Mayo/Yoreme, O'odham and Comcaac for community sources. **Domain question:** what is a fair bargain and a good partnership? Traditional areas: partnership, marriage, contracts, open enemies, balance, justice. Libra takes the letter of the law only: what statutes, codes, treaties and courts say as written.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Catholic, Latter-day Saint, evangelical and nonreligious households.
- Rural ranching and farming Sonora against Hermosillo, Tucson and Phoenix.
- Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham, Comcaac and Navajo communities, each with its own marriage and dispute customs.
- Long-settled families, newcomers and cross-border households.
- Mining, maquiladora and farm-labor communities.
- Mexican-heritage and Anglo-heritage Arizonans.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on written codes, treaties and court structure; mixed on handshake norms and marriage customs; thin on tribal practice in print.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous languages for community material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tribal marriage and dispute customs, and some Yaqui and Comcaac knowledge, may be closed. Spirit-of-law material belongs in Capricorn. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
