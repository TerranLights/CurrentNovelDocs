# Sonora: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (northern Mexican usage), English; Yaqui/Yoeme, Mayo/Yoreme, O'odham and Comcaac for community sources. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Comcaac communities, each with its own giving customs.
- Catholic parishes against Latter-day Saint wards and evangelical congregations.
- Remittance-sending families against households that receive none.
- Mining, ranching and farm-labor households.
- Cross-border families who send goods and money back and forth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; remittances and charity are rich; everyday favor-trading is thin and ethnographic.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous languages for ceremonial exchange.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Yaqui, Mayo and O'odham ceremonies, and their sponsorship and offering customs, may be partly closed. Revenge is Scorpio's, not here. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
