# Sonora: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (northern Mexican usage) and English; Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Comcaac for community sources. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Border-town residents and officials against interior residents.
- Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Comcaac nations, which have conducted their own negotiations with outsiders.
- Ranchers, miners, farmers and officials: different bargaining styles.
- Children who translate for parents against fluent bilingual professionals.
- Newcomers against long-settled families.
- Men and women in family mediation roles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; treaty and governors' documents are rich; tact in daily life is thin and must come from fiction, interviews and psychology studies.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous languages for tribal protocol.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current diplomatic and security matters are politically charged. Tribal protocol and ceremony may be restricted. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
