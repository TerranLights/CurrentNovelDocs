*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Hermosillo and Tucson households against ranch, mining and valley-farm towns; Spanish-descended ranch families, mestizo townspeople and Mexican migrants; the O'odham, Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac, Cucapá and Western Apache peoples, each with its own house and kin order; border-straddling families; retirees and seasonal residents; Mennonite or other settler colonies if present in the lands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census and housing; mixed for kinship (older outsider accounts, newer community-led work); thin for private emotional climate.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Native languages only as each nation shares them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan, lineage and burial knowledge belongs to its people; use only what they publish. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
