# Sonora: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, O'odham, Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Women, men and children who stay while a parent migrates; single mothers and fathers; foster and adopted children; elders in care homes and those who live alone; Native families touched by boarding schools and removals; deported and repatriated families; mining-town and ranch communities that emptied; faith communities that treat leaving differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household counts and agency data; mixed for felt abandonment; thin and painful for private cases.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-welfare cases, the missing and the dead are not to be extracted; removal history belongs to the nations' own accounts. Surrender laws belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
