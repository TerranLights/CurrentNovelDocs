# Sonora: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, O'odham, Yoeme, Comcaac. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Unhoused people against people doubling up with kin; migrants in transit against settled residents; shelter providers (church, civic, tribal, neighbors); women and children fleeing violence; elders in care homes; Native nations' own practices for guests and emergencies; ranch and farm workers in employer housing; retirees in seasonal homes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. counts and agency reports; mixed for Mexican homeless data (no national census of it *check*); thin for informal sheltering by kin.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrants' safety and shelter locations, and abuse survivors' refuges, must not be published; Native emergency practice only as the nations share it. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
