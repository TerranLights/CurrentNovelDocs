# Sonora: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, O'odham, Yoeme, Comcaac. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
City dwellers in gated or barred homes against rural households; women, men, parents and elders on who guards whom; migrants and the people who aid them; ranchers, farm workers and border-town residents; the Native nations and their own guardians (such as the Yoeme traditional guard, *check*); retirees and seasonal residents; Deaf and disabled residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for security surveys and emergency guidance; mixed for informal protection; thin for protection by kin and community.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Native languages as each nation shares them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence, extortion and disappearances are live issues; arms and self-defense statutes belong to Libra and Capricorn, so do not research them here. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
