## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Natives of the state against migrants from elsewhere in Mexico; Mexican Americans and Anglo residents in southern Arizona; seasonal retirees; the Native nations, whose belonging rests on kin and enrollment rules each sets itself; the Chinese-descended and other long-settled minorities; Deaf and LGBTQ communities; the young against the old.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys on discrimination and identity; mixed for felt belonging; thin for small groups.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Native terms as each nation shares them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** enrollment disputes and descent claims are the nations' own business; cite only public documents. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

1. Pull ENADIS and BIARE tables for Sonora.
