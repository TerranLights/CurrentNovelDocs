# Sonora: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English, O'odham, Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Catholic ranch and town families against Protestant, Latter-day Saint and nonreligious ones; Spanish-descended landowning families against mestizo townspeople and recent migrants; each Native nation's own kin order (O'odham, Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac, Western Apache, Cucapá); mixed-status and transborder families; retirees living far from kin; single-parent, adoptive and foster households.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household structure and values scales; mixed for kin systems (older outsider accounts); thin for estrangement and private conflict.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, English; Native languages as each nation shares them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan and lineage knowledge belongs to its people; family violence data (INEGI's ENDIREH *check*) needs careful handling. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
