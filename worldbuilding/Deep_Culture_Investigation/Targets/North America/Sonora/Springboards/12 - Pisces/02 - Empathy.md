# Sonora: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (Sonoran and Arizona border varieties), English; Indigenous-language poetry and teaching materials. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spanish | *empatía*, *ponerse en los zapatos del otro* | empathy, put yourself in another's shoes | Check whether it is a school or therapy word |
| Spanish | *ponerse en su lugar*, *entender al otro* | take someone's place, understand | Older idioms |
| Spanish | *simpatía* | a script of warmth and smooth relations | Not English "sympathy" |
| Spanish | *buena onda*, *sensible*, *delicado* | good vibe, sensitive, touchy | Praise or mockery |
| Spanish | *frío*, *insensible*, *sin sentimientos* | cold, insensitive | The absence of it |
| English | "empath", "emotional intelligence", "tone-deaf", "read the room" | | Online and workplace |

English keywords: emotional labor, perspective-taking, bedside manner, social-emotional learning, interpreters, testimony, documentary photography.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Spanish-dominant and English-dominant speakers: *simpatía* may sit differently across them.
- Healthcare, call-center and service workers, who are paid for empathy, against kin and friends who give it free.
- Border residents and people from elsewhere in Arizona or Mexico.
- Indigenous communities that publish their own accounts of knowing another's mind.
- Men and women, young and old: check who may show feeling.
- Religious groups with differing views of empathy as virtue or trap.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on scales, school programs and border journalism; mixed on everyday talk; thin on local Spanish idioms in print. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous-language sources through the peoples' own presses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrant and survivor testimony should be taken in their own voices and with consent; do not treat scale scores as a national trait.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search "simpatía" with "Hispanic" in psychology databases, then read for caveats.
2. Look up Mexican and U.S. samples in Chopik et al. (2017).
3. Collect two or three border writers' own statements on understanding others.
4. Find SEP and Arizona SEL curricula and note which empathy words they use.
5. Interview-style search: "emotional labor" with "call center Hermosillo".
