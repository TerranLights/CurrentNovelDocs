# Sonora: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Yaqui and O'odham for ceremonial vows (published sources only). **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Mothers, eldest daughters and sons, men who left as braceros or migrants.
- Families of migrants who stay against those who leave; those who crossed and those who aided.
- Military and first-responder families (Arizona and Sonoran).
- Latter-day Saint families, with missions and tithing.
- Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Comcaac communities, each with its own teaching about giving for the people.
- Retirees and caregivers in Arizona against Sonoran households with live-in elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on remittances, time use and firefighter records; mixed on mothers' ideals in Sonoran Spanish; thin on Indigenous teachings in print. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Yaqui and O'odham ceremonial vows are restricted knowledge; the Tillman and fire-death material involves grieving families; suicide framed as sacrifice needs care.
- **Held for review:** none.
