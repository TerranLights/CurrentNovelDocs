*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Indigenous communities by degree of language retention and revival: Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham, Comcaac, Pima Bajo.
- Long-settled mestizo Sonorans against recent arrivals from the south of Mexico.
- Mexican American families in Arizona, across generations of assimilation.
- Ecstatic against restrained congregations (Pentecostal, Catholic, Latter-day Saint).
- Mining and ranching towns that closed against growing metro areas.
- Psychedelic and New Age communities against religious users bound by their own rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on language counts, marriage endings and town histories; mixed on congregational ecstasy; thin on private experiences of self-loss, and on psychedelic use in Sonora. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous sources only as the peoples publish them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous ceremonial trance and any sacred plant or toad practice are restricted or legally charged; deportation and assimilation history needs the peoples' own accounts first; dementia and divorce are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
