# Sonora: the domain and folkways (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages of the Sonoran Desert (O'odham, Yaqui/Yoeme, Mayo/Yoreme, Comcaac/Seri). **Concept in one line:** how the culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape (the hidden, confinement, transcendence, magic ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Sonorense mestizo communities, rural against Hermosillo and the border cities.
- Tucson and Phoenix Mexican American families (long-settled against recent arrivals).
- Yaqui (Yoeme) in Sonora and in Arizona; Mayo (Yoreme); Tohono and Akimel O'odham; Comcaac; Apache peoples: each has its own religion and its own limits on what is told.
- Anglo Arizonans, retirees, Latter-day Saint colonies, Black, Chinese and other long-settled communities.
- Migrants and the people who aid them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on healing, shrines and border death; thin on mental-health talk in Spanish at the substate level. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous-language material usually only through the peoples' own publications.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial knowledge of the Yaqui, O'odham and Comcaac is restricted; boarding-school and migrant-death material needs survivors' and families' voices first.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
