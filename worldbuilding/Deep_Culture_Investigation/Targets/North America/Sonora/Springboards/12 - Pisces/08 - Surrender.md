# Sonora: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Apache, Yaqui and O'odham for war-memory sources. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher; release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Yaqui, Apache, Comcaac and O'odham communities, each with its own war memory and its own language for survival.
- Catholic believers using *si Dios quiere* against Protestant and Latter-day Saint teaching on will and agency.
- Men and women, young and old, on giving up and holding on.
- People in recovery (Spanish and English meetings) against those outside it.
- Hospice and nursing staff against families deciding about care.
- Farm and ranch families facing loss of land or water.
- Migrants at the border, for whom giving up and going on may be one act.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on recovery programs, place of death and war history; mixed on everyday idioms; thin on Indigenous statements about surrender in accessible print. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous sources through tribal publications.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deathbed, suicide and assisted-dying material is private; do not treat "fatalism" as a trait of a people; war memories are tribal property and need the nations' own voices first. No law research here.
- **Held for review:** none.
