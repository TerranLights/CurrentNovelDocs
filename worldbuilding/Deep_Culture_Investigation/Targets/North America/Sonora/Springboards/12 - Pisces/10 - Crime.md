# Sonora: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (Sonoran and border slang), English. **Concept in one line:** what the culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving condemnation, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is not researched here: see Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Border-city residents (Nogales, Caborca, San Luis) against inland towns and Phoenix and Tucson suburbs.
- Rural communities with long histories of smuggling against those with none.
- Families of the disappeared and of victims, apart from families of offenders.
- Indigenous communities with their own handling of wrongdoing (see Capricorn).
- Young men near cartel recruitment against older neighbors.
- Churches that minister to prisoners and gang members.
- Retirees and newcomers, whose fear of crime may rest on different news.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official counts and victimization surveys; mixed on fear and attitudes; thin on the offenders' own accounts. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** cartel material is dangerous for sources; do not collect names of informants or of victims' families without consent; migrant death records involve grief; no law research here.
- **Held for review:** none.
