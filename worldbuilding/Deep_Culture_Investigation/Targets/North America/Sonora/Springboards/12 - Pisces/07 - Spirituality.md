# Sonora: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Yaqui, O'odham, Mayo, Comcaac in published sources. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, outside as well as inside organized religion (practice, experience, popular devotion).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Catholic folk devotion in Sonoran towns against Arizona Mexican American parishes.
- Evangelical and Pentecostal congregations, and recent converts.
- Latter-day Saint communities in both Sonora and Arizona.
- Yaqui, Mayo, O'odham and Comcaac religious life, each separate and each with its own limits on telling.
- Nones and seekers in Phoenix and Tucson; New Age practitioners in Sedona.
- Desert monastics and retreatants.
- Migrants carrying devotions from the south.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on census and survey counts and on older ethnographies; mixed on lived spirituality outside churches; thin on contemporary Indigenous religion in print by design. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous-language material only through the peoples' own publications.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** closed ceremonies are respected, not researched; older ethnographies may have published what the peoples now consider restricted, so read critically and ask the communities. Faith as institution belongs to Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
