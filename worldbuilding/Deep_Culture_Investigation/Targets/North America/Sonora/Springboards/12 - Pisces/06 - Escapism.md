## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Resort-coast workers and visitors against desert-town residents.
- Retirees and winter visitors against year-round Sonorans.
- Young adults in Phoenix and Hermosillo: screens, nightlife, gaming.
- Indigenous communities with recovery movements against those with casino economies.
- Workers in maquiladoras and agriculture, for whom escape is time-limited.
- Border residents who cross for leisure.
- Latter-day Saint communities, with their own limits on drink and drug use.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on addiction statistics and tourism; mixed on everyday leisure; thin on informal rehab houses and on Sonoran drinking customs in print. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** addiction and recovery are private; informal rehab houses may involve coercion and abuse; cartel-linked drug-trade material belongs under Crime; ceremonial drinks of the O'odham and Yaqui are restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
