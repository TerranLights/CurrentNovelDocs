# Sonora: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Indigenous-language tale collections where published. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, including worry and anxiety.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Border-city residents against Phoenix and Tucson suburbs.
- Mixed-status families against citizens and long-settled residents.
- Rural and ranch households facing drought and heat, against urban residents.
- Men and women, older and younger people: fear of crime and its display.
- Indigenous communities with their own stories of danger and the unseen.
- Mining-region communities after the contamination.
- Churches preaching *temor de Dios* against secular households.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on perception surveys and hazard records; mixed on fear as a cultural idiom; thin on local variants of frightening tales. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fear of deportation is a safety matter for sources; do not collect names or addresses; Indigenous stories of the unseen may be seasonal or restricted (some are told only in set months); do not treat fear as a national trait.
- **Held for review:** none.
