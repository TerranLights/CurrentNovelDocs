*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Indigenous-language terms in published dictionaries. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Ranch and farm families with weather and cattle knowledge against urban residents.
- Trackers and desert guides, Indigenous and not, against ordinary hikers.
- Healers and their clients against clinicians and patients.
- Women and men on "women's intuition", by generation.
- Catholic discernment traditions against Pentecostal "leadings".
- Technical workers (mining, nursing, firefighting, aviation) with learned intuition.
- Gamblers and prospectors, with luck beliefs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: strong scales and U.S. polls, thin local ethnography of hunches; Castaneda is contested and should not be used as evidence about any people. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous dream and sign knowledge is restricted; do not extract trackers' names; divination as a practice is studied, never used as a source.
- **Held for review:** none.
