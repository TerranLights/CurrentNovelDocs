## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Border cities (Tijuana, Nogales, Mexicali) against inland ranch towns and desert communities.
- Generations: elders, teens, and those raised on social media.
- Men and women; sexual and gender minorities in cities against small towns.
- Indigenous peoples (Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac, O'odham, Cucapá, Kumeyaay, Apache) with their own norms of speech and silence.
- Churches: Catholic, Pentecostal and evangelical, Latter-day Saint colonies in Sonora and Chihuahua.
- Newcomers and long-settled families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Dress, music and public art are well documented; private disclosure is thin and often inferred from surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous-language material needs community guidance.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial regalia and personal songs held by Indigenous peoples; identity data on gender and sexuality; migrants' accounts.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
