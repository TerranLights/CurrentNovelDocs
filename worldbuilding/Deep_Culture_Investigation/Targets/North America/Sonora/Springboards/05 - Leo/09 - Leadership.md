## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Elected officials against traditional and ejido authorities; party-linked bosses against union and church leaders.
- Indigenous nations (Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac, O'odham, Cucapá, Kumeyaay, Apache), each with its own way of choosing and removing leaders.
- Ranching, mining, farming and maquiladora settings; border cities against interior villages.
- Women and men; older and younger generations; Catholic, Pentecostal and Latter-day Saint congregations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Elected leadership is well documented; informal and traditional leadership is thin and best sought through community-approved sources.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous titles via community guidance.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** internal selection of traditional authorities; party and cartel-linked power, which is dangerous to document by name.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
