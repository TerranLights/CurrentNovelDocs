*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages of the desert borderlands. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Formally recognized peoples against communities denied recognition (Indigenous nations on both sides of the line; Mexican Americans; Chinese Mexican families in Mexicali and Baja).
- Employers against workers: who gets credit in mines, fields, ranches and assembly plants.
- Men and women; migrants and long-settled residents; credentialed professionals against the self-taught.
- Church and civic groups that run their own honors.

## 4. Flags

- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tribal enrollment and recognition disputes; migrants' status.
