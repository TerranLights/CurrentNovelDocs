# Sonora: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages of the desert borderlands. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Migrants and long-settled residents; those with and without papers.
- Farmworkers, maquiladora workers, domestic workers, and the unhoused against professionals.
- Indigenous peoples (Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac, O'odham, Cucapá, Kumeyaay, Apache), whose visibility to the state and to media differs from nation to nation.
- Women, sexual and gender minorities; the old; disabled people.
- Border-city residents against rural ranch households; families of the disappeared.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Census, media and public-health data are rich; felt visibility is thin and best found in interviews and testimony.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disappearance, migrants' deaths, identifying people at risk; belief about the evil eye held in families and by healers.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
