## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Border cities against ranch towns and desert communities; Mexican American, Mexico-born and Anglo residents on the U.S. side.
- Indigenous peoples (Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac, O'odham, Cucapá, Kumeyaay, Apache), each with a different history of collective humiliation.
- Boys and girls; sexual minorities; migrants and deportees.
- Church-based and secular milieus; schools, teams and workplaces as separate settings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Bullying and violence surveys are rich; felt humiliation, joking and hazing are thin and context-bound.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous accounts need community guidance.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivor accounts of abuse and deportation; identify no victims; sexual humiliation.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
