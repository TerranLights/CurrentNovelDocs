## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Households receiving remittances against those sending them; migrants against stayers.
- Catholic, Pentecostal, Latter-day Saint and secular givers; tithing communities against others.
- Indigenous peoples (Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac, O'odham, Cucapá, Kumeyaay, Apache), with distinct traditions of sharing and sponsorship.
- Ranchers, miners, maquiladora workers and professionals; urban against rural.
- Retirees and winter visitors in Arizona against long-term residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Formal giving is measured; informal giving, hospitality and family sharing are thin and undercounted.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous terms need community guidance.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial sponsorship and what is given in ritual belong to each community; aid to migrants may expose helpers and recipients.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
