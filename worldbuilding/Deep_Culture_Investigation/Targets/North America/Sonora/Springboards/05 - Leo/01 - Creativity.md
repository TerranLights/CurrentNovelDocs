## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Fine-art circles against craft and folk-art makers; urban arts districts against rural and desert workshops.
- Indigenous peoples (Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac, O'odham, Cucapá, Kumeyaay, Paipai, Apache), each with its own craft and its own rules on who may make and use designs.
- Border artists crossing between Tijuana and San Diego, and between Nogales and Tucson.
- Migrants and long-settled families; men and women makers; younger digital creators.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Arts economy data are good at state level; everyday making-do and craft knowledge are thin and held by makers.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous terms through each community's teachers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** designs, songs and techniques some peoples treat as owned or ceremonial; disputes with fashion brands over copied designs.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
