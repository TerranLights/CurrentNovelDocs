# Sonora: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Sonora, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish, English; Indigenous languages of the desert borderlands. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the Sonoran Desert core.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Ranching, mining, farming and fishing families against city professionals and maquiladora workers.
- Men and women; boys and girls; army and police families.
- Indigenous peoples (Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac, O'odham, Cucapá, Kumeyaay, Apache), each with its own warrior, veteran and elder honors.
- Mexican American and Anglo residents of Arizona against Mexico-born residents; churches and secular settings.
- Gangs and other groups with their own codes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Honor-culture research is mostly from the American South and the Mediterranean; Sonoran evidence is sparse and must be tested, not assumed.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence in families and organized crime; ceremonial honors held by Indigenous peoples; veterans' moral injury.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
