*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Border cities (Tijuana, Mexicali, Nogales) against interior ranch and mining towns, and Arizona retiree communities.
- Indigenous peoples (Yoeme, Mayo, Comcaac, O'odham, Cucapá, Kumeyaay, Apache), whose joy and humor in ceremony are their own.
- Catholic, Pentecostal and Latter-day Saint settings; secular and church-based celebration.
- Age groups: children, teens, elders; men and women; migrants away from home against those at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Well-being surveys give numbers by state; what joy is and how it is shown is thin and best found in festival coverage and testimony.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and English; Indigenous accounts need community guidance.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial dances and songs and the sacred meaning of some festivals belong to communities; do not record or interpret without permission.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
