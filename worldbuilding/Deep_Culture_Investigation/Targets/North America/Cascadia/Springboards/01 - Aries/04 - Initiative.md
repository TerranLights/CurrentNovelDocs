# Cascadia: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (post-war: Washington, Oregon, California, Southeast Alaska, coastal and interior British Columbia, Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Spanish (the Bajas, El Norte); Indigenous languages by unit (see flags). **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Left Coast and BC coast against Far West interior: metro startup culture against ranch, orchard and mining-town self-reliance.
- El Norte, Baja California and Sudcalifornia: border industry, migrant enterprise and need-driven business.
- Indigenous units (Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin edge, Dene): own enterprise and revival initiatives.
- Within units: immigrants, gender and class (need against opportunity).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. business and civic measures; mixed for BC and the Bajas; thin for Indigenous communities and for folk meanings.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Indigenous-language terms need a community or language-program source (query, not guess).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous communities' own knowledge and language programs may carry restrictions; ask before use.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Statistics offices: new-business and self-employment data for each unit's counties and regions.
2. Secretary of State ballot-initiative histories for Oregon, Washington and California.
3. Library catalog: "founder" and "pioneer" biographies by city, plus Woodard's *American Nations* on the Left Coast and Far West (*check*).
4. Spanish: "acomedido" and "echarle ganas" in Baja newspapers and dictionaries of Mexican Spanish.
5. Indigenous enterprise and language-revival programs, by nation.
