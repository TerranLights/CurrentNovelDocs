# Cascadia: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia (post-war nation: Washington, Oregon, California, Southeast Alaska Panhandle, British Columbia, Baja California and Baja California Sur), region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, plus Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), Tagalog, Vietnamese, and the Indigenous languages of its lands. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Left Coast and Bay Area settlers; British Columbia coast; Far West interior (Central Valley, Okie-descended, ranching); El Norte (Southern California, Baja California); Baja California Sur; Panhandle settlers; the Indigenous units (Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin, Dene); and immigrant generations (Chinese, Filipino, Vietnamese, Mexican, Mixtec and Triqui farmworker communities).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in English for U.S. states; mixed for British Columbia; mixed to thin for the Bajas below national level; thin for Indigenous peoples.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Cantonese and Mandarin helpful; Indigenous languages for any direct work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous communities' own knowledge and protocols; ask the nation before using it. Domestic-violence data is sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
