# Cascadia: Beginning (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America. Lands: Washington, Oregon and California; British Columbia; the Southeast Alaska Panhandle; Baja California and Baja California Sur; with the Indigenous peoples of all of them. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin) and Tagalog for large immigrant communities; Indigenous languages by unit (Lushootseed, Haida, Tlingit, Kumeyaay/Kumiai, others). **Concept in one line:** how a culture treats starting: origins, firsts, new starts, and starting over after failure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Left Coast and Far West settlers (newcomer against old family); British Columbia coast; El Norte and Sudcalifornia (arrival and migrant-city beginnings); the Indigenous units (origin stories against the settler "new land" frame); recent arrivals, such as Asian, Latin American and tech migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the U.S. and Canadian settler side; mixed for the Bajas; Indigenous material mixed and held by the nations.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Chinese; Indigenous languages by unit.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** creation stories and first-salmon or first-foods ceremonies may be restricted; use only what a nation publishes. Founding days and plaques are disputed between settler and Indigenous accounts.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. ACS and Statistics Canada birthplace and migration tables for the target's states and province, then INEGI for the Bajas.
2. Search "fresh start" and "starting over" with each state or province's historical society and newspaper archives.
3. Search each Indigenous nation's own site for "origin story", "first salmon" and "language program".
4. Search the Spanish terms with Tijuana and La Paz newspapers and with the Colegio de la Frontera Norte.
