## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **S1 Left Coast** (Puget Sound to the Bay Area): technology booms and outdoor sport.
- **S2 BC coast:** a Canadian safety and drug-policy setting.
- **S3 Far West:** interior, mining, railroad and fire country.
- **S4 El Norte and S5 Sudcalifornia:** Spanish-language and Baja road and sea culture.
- **Indigenous units:** the Northwest Coast peoples (sea and whale-hunting risk), the Plateau and Great Basin peoples, and the Kumeyaay and other Baja Indigenous communities, each on its own terms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in English for health, traffic and rescue; mixed in Spanish for Baja; thin for Indigenous concepts of risk.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Indigenous languages through the nations' own programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** overdose and suicide deaths; Indigenous knowledge may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
