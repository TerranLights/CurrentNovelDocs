# Cascadia: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, Canadian French (small), and the main Indigenous languages of its lands. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered in a society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast and British Columbia coast:** restorative and reform politics against the Canadian and U.S. court systems.
- **Far West interior:** rural and ranching views of law and force; federal-land disputes.
- **El Norte (southern California) and Sudcalifornia (the Bajas):** Mexican civil-law ideas, and U.S. ideas, side by side.
- **Indigenous units:** Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Subarctic Dene peoples, separated because each nation holds its own tradition and courts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for measured attitudes and institutions in the U.S., Canada and Mexico; thin and uneven for Indigenous nations' own ideas, which are published by the nations at their discretion.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; French and Indigenous languages for specific units.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** each Indigenous nation's justice practice and restricted knowledge, to be used only as the nation shares it; victims of violence; ongoing cases; the disappeared.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull the World Justice Project index and Mexico States index for Washington, Oregon, California, British Columbia and the Bajas.

3. Search each Indigenous nation's own site for "justice", "peacemaking" and "court", and note which publish.
4. Search the Spanish terms in Baja California newspapers.
