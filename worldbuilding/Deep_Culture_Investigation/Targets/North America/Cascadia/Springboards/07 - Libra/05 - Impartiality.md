# Cascadia: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, French (some Canadian texts), main immigrant languages, Indigenous languages. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **S1 Left Coast and S2 British Columbia coast:** merit traditions with different roots (U.S. reform politics against Westminster civil service).
- **S3 Far West:** interior towns where officials and neighbors overlap.
- **S4 El Norte and S5 Sudcalifornia:** Mexican institutions beside California's; the language of connections.
- **S1a Panhandle settlers:** very small towns where everyone knows the judge.
- **I1 to I6 Indigenous units:** where hereditary rank, clan and elected councils both bear on office; each people separately.
- **Later layers:** immigrant communities with their own words for connections (Chinese, Filipino, Vietnamese, Korean, Armenian, Iranian and others).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institutions and perception (surveys, regulators, press); thin for everyday folk understanding; mixed across the border (three separate series). Little online for Indigenous units.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; French for some federal texts; Chinese for diaspora media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous governance, kinship and rank may be restricted; ask first. Corruption allegations name living people: cite rulings, not rumor.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
