# Cascadia: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California, the Alaska Panhandle, British Columbia, Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, Chinuk Wawa (Chinook Jargon), plus the main Indigenous, Chinese and Filipino-language communities. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force, between nations and peoples and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast settlers and the BC coast**: Yankee-founded civic habits against British-colonial ones.
- **Far West interior (Central Valley, Columbia plateau, BC interior)**: ranching and mining places, direct or indirect speech as an open question.
- **El Norte and the Bajas**: Spanish-speaking border communities, where cross-border life is daily.
- **Northwest Coast peoples (Coast Salish, Haida, Tlingit)**: own protocols, feasts and treaty traditions.
- **Plateau peoples**: the Celilo trading tradition and their own councils.
- **California and Yuman peoples (including Kumeyaay)**: border-straddling nations with separate protocols.
- **Treaty and non-treaty nations in BC**: different negotiating histories.
- **Immigrant communities**: Chinese, Filipino, Japanese, Mexican; compare by generation and by language brokering.
- **Labor and business**: profession-level styles, not one voice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on institutions and histories (treaties, labor, boundaries); mixed on everyday tact; thin on tact within each Indigenous nation unless the nation publishes it.
- **Languages needed:** English and Spanish; Chinese and Tagalog for immigrant communities; Indigenous languages for protocol terms; some French for BC archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** each nation's protocols and ceremonial knowledge belong to that nation; seek what it publishes itself. Treaty disputes are live.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
