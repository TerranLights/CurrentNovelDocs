# Cascadia: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California, the Alaska Panhandle, British Columbia, Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, some French (Canadian federal sources), and the Indigenous languages of its lands. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

English keywords: undecided voters, blank ballots, voto nulo, referendum, public consultation, permitting delay, shared decision-making, advance directive, consensus decision-making, choice overload.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **S1 Left Coast:** consultation-heavy civic politics; technology and outdoor-sport choice culture.
- **S2 BC coast:** a Canadian setting of hearings, referendums and treaty talks.
- **S3 Far West:** interior, agricultural and mining communities; different pace and institutions.
- **S4 El Norte and S5 Sudcalifornia:** Spanish-language families and Mexican consultation law; Baja against Southern California.
- **Indigenous units:** each nation's own way of deciding (councils, consensus, elders), on its own terms; Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman and Dene peoples separated.
- **Variation to test, not assume:** generation, class, gender, profession, and family-centered against individual decisions in immigrant families (Mexican-American, Chinese-American, Filipino and others).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for voting, polling, permitting and end-of-life records in English; mixed in Spanish for Baja; thin for Indigenous concepts, and for everyday indecision outside surveys. Law appears only as the letter of rules (procedure, referendum law, permitting) per Libra.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish, some French; Indigenous languages through the nations' own programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** end-of-life and family decisions; mental-health data; Indigenous governance knowledge may be restricted or held by the community.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull blank-ballot and late-decider data for each state, BC and the two Bajas, by county or district.
2. Search city and council minutes for "tabled", "continued" and "consultation" in Seattle, Portland, Vancouver and Tijuana.
3. Search "Seattle process" and its local equivalents in press and city records.
4. Search Spanish: "indecisión", "nadar entre dos aguas" with Tijuana, Mexicali and La Paz.
5. Ask Indigenous governments and language programs how they wish decision-making to be documented, with consent.
