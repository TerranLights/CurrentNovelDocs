## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast settlers (S1) and Panhandle settlers (S1a):** the core settler culture; the Panhandle's Native share differs.
- **British Columbia coast (S2):** Westminster and common-law tradition, different from U.S. states.
- **Far West interior (S3):** environment-shaped, many farm-labor and Dust Bowl strands.
- **El Norte (S4) and Sudcalifornia (S5):** Mexican civil-law tradition (Spanish-language sources).
- **Indigenous units I1 to I6, and tribal groups T1 to T8:** each has its own marriage, kin and settlement rules; separate them by nation.
- Also separate by religion, immigrant community and urban against rural.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Mexican statistics; mixed for Canada below the province; thin for Indigenous units and for Panhandle-only data.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish, French; Indigenous languages and Chinese for specific units.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous legal knowledge and ceremony may be restricted; use only what each nation publishes. Intimate-partner violence data is sensitive.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

1. ACS, Statistics Canada and INEGI tables on marital status and age at first marriage for the three jurisdictions' target lands.

5. WVS and GSS items on marriage and gender, read by region.
