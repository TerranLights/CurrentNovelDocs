## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast** (Puget Sound to the Bay Area) against **Far West** interior (Central Valley, Sierra, Columbia interior): metro, tech and wellness settings against ranching, farming and mining ones.
- **British Columbia coast** against **Panhandle settlers**: different founding layers; check for local differences in how work-trades describe a feel for weather and sea.
- **El Norte** (southern California) against **Sudcalifornia** (Baja California Sur): Mexican and Catholic folk practice, border life.
- **Chinese Canadian, Chinese American, Mexican and Mexican American, Filipino and Pacific Islander communities**: separate on language and practice.
- **Indigenous units:** Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Subarctic Dene peoples; separate each, with the languages' own words for dreams and knowing.
- **By trade and setting:** nurses, pilots, fishers, loggers, firefighters, farmers, tech workers, investors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in English on surveys, scholarship and popular culture, though mostly national; thin for state or province cuts. Mixed in Spanish and Chinese. Thin and community-held for Indigenous peoples.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish, Chinese; Indigenous languages with community partners.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous knowledge of dreams, vision and spiritual seeing is often restricted or private; ask first and cite only what a community publishes. Folk healers' practice is personal. Gut judgment of strangers touches bias and race.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
