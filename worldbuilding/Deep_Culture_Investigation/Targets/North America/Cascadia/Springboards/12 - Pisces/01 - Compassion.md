# Cascadia: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Mexican and Californian Spanish, Cantonese and Mandarin, Tagalog, Indigenous languages. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Indigenous languages | none given | ask each nation's language program for words and for how kindness is described | do not guess terms |

English keywords: compassion fatigue, charity, almsgiving, mutual aid, harm reduction, hospitality, volunteering, giving, helping strangers, deserving poor, encampments.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast** (urban and coastal U.S.): secular and church charity, encampment politics.
- **British Columbia coast:** Canadian public-program habits and the Downtown Eastside's harm-reduction history.
- **Far West and interior:** rural and small-town giving, church pantries, Dust Bowl descendants.
- **El Norte** (Southern California) and **Baja California and Baja California Sur:** Catholic *misericordia*, border and migrant aid.
- **Panhandle:** small-town and fishing communities.
- **Chinese, Filipino and Japanese communities; Mexican and Central American immigrants; migrant farm workers:** remittances, hometown and clan associations.
- **Indigenous units** (Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin, Subarctic Dene): separate each; do not merge.
- **Also by:** religion versus secular; class; gender; politics; rural versus urban.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian giving and volunteering; mixed for Mexico (state breakouts uncertain); thin for Indigenous communities and most Asian-language communities.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Cantonese, Mandarin and Tagalog for specific communities; Indigenous languages through the nations' own programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Indigenous knowledge is restricted; ask each nation before using anything beyond published sources. Homelessness, addiction and migrant status call for care with named individuals.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search section 1 terms in the Seattle Times, Oregonian, SF Chronicle, LA Times and Vancouver Sun archives.
