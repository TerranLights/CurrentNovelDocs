# Cascadia: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Indigenous languages by nation. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion (faith and religion as institutions belong to Sagittarius).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast settler culture (Puget Sound, Willamette, Bay Area):** the "None Zone" claim to test, and a long record of retreat and counterculture centers.
- **British Columbia coast:** a separate census and survey system; test against the Left Coast.
- **Far West interior:** often more churched; test against the coast.
- **El Norte and Sudcalifornia:** Catholic mission history and popular devotion; Baja against California on the same question.
- **Indigenous units (Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin edge, northern Dene):** separate by culture area, then by nation; their own words, their own limits on sharing.
- **Cross-cutting groups:** generation, immigrant generation, Pentecostal and Latter-day Saint congregations, converts to contemplative practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys of the U.S. and Canadian parts; mixed for Mexico (religion in the census, little on spirituality); thin and uneven for Indigenous nations, by their choice.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Indigenous languages by nation; Chinese and other diaspora languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** closed ceremonies and restricted knowledge are respected, not researched; sacred-site disputes; abuse inside spiritual groups; the line between mental illness and spiritual experience; suppression of ceremonies in the past.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
