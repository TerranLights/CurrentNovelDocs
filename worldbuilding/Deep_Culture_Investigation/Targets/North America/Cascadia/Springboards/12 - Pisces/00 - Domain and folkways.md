# Cascadia: The hidden and transcendence, domain and folkways (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. Own knowledge only; nothing verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, plus Chinese, Tagalog and Vietnamese for large immigrant communities, and Indigenous languages by unit. **Domain in one line:** how the culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape: the hidden and unconscious, confinement, sacrifice, compassion, transcendence, and magic ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Settler units:** Left Coast, British Columbia coast, Far West, El Norte (southern California, northern Baja), Sudcalifornia; the Panhandle's settlers. Each has a different relation to church, wilderness and the unseen.
- **Indigenous units:** Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Subarctic Dene peoples; separate by nation, not by culture area.
- **Immigrant communities:** East and Southeast Asian, Latin American (including Mixtec, Triqui and Zapotec in San Quintín), Pacific Islander, Black, Slavic and Russian Old Believer communities.
- **Other cuts:** rural against urban; the religiously unaffiliated; recovery fellowships.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian health surveys, institutional history and New Age scenes; mixed for Baja; thin for Indigenous belief in public sources, and thin for immigrant communities' private practice.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Chinese, Tagalog and Vietnamese for immigrant sources; each Indigenous language by unit.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous spiritual and healing knowledge, and burial and sacred sites, may be restricted; use only what each nation publishes. Boarding school and asylum material involves survivors and unmarked graves. Suicide and overdose data need careful handling.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull CDC WONDER and PLACES, the BC Coroners Service and INEGI series for suicide, overdose and short sleep, by county or region.
2. Read the Pew and PRRI state-level religion and paranormal items, by state and province.
3. Search the Online Archive of California, Densho and the Oregon asylum sources for confinement history.
4. Survey botánicas, psychic shops and haunted-site tourism in Seattle, Portland, the Bay Area, Los Angeles, Vancouver and Tijuana.
5. Ask each Indigenous nation's own cultural office or language program what it publishes on healing and spirit.
