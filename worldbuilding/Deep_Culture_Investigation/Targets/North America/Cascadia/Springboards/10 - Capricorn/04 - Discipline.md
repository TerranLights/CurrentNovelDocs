## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian schools; mixed for family practice; thin for Baja and Indigenous teaching unless nations share it.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Chinese and Tagalog for large communities; Indigenous-language programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors' testimony; teachings that a nation restricts; abuse called discipline. Ask permission and follow each nation's protocols.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search CRDC and DataQuest for suspension and expulsion by school, then compare Washington, Oregon and Baja California.
2. Read the TRC report and the Interior boarding-school reports for Cascadia's schools, survivors first.
3. Search regional press for "willful defiance", "gentle parenting", "mano dura", "chancla".
4. Query the Spanish and Chinese press and radio for parenting and school-discipline columns.
5. Contact each nation's education or language department for what it shares publicly.
