# Cascadia: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur; the Alaska Panhandle). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, French (British Columbia federal materials); Indigenous languages by nation. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community and how people relate to them; Capricorn takes the spirit (what law is for, obeying, bending, avoiding), Libra the letter.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast, Far West, B.C. coast, El Norte, Sudcalifornia:** different legal cultures and trust in police; city against rural county.
- **Common-law U.S. and British Columbia against civil-law Baja:** two legal traditions in one nation.
- **Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Subarctic Dene peoples:** each its own legal order; do not merge.
- **Immigrant generations and race:** differing contact with police and courts.
- **Rural sheriffs against urban departments; religious communities (Catholic, Latter-day Saint, others):** different sources of authority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian attitudes and measures; mixed for Baja (state-level surveys exist, ethnography thinner); thin and uneven for Indigenous nations, held by each.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish, French; Indigenous languages for customary law.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** each Indigenous nation's own law and restricted knowledge, shared only as the nation shares it; police violence, undocumented life and corruption are sensitive. Law only in Capricorn and Libra: keep to the spirit here.
- **Held for review:** none, except any South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
