## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast (Bay Area, Puget Sound, Willamette):** outdoor recreation, creative economies, supporters' culture.
- **British Columbia coast:** hockey and Canadian sport institutions beside Pacific outdoor life.
- **Far West (interior, Central Valley, Okanagan):** rodeo, fairs, high-school-game towns.
- **El Norte (Los Angeles, San Diego, Tijuana, Mexicali):** entertainment industry, charrería, fiestas, lucha libre, border crossing for pleasure.
- **Sudcalifornia (La Paz, Los Cabos):** tourism economy as a pleasure industry; ranch festivals.
- **Panhandle settlers:** fishing towns, Fourth of July and local fairs.
- **Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin edge and Dene peoples:** feasting, games, canoe and horse traditions, as each people shares them.
- **Immigrant communities:** festivals and children's display.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the U.S. and Canadian lobes (time use, participation, gaming, press); mixed for the Bajas (INEGI strong on sport and time use, thin by municipality); thin and community-held for Indigenous play and feasting; thin for fandom and romance below the national level.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Indigenous languages for terms (with permission); community languages for immigrant festivals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** potlatch, songs, dances, crests and names belong to their families and nations; some are not for outsiders. Gambling, sex-adjacent vice tourism and drug laws are sensitive; sex belongs to Scorpio, food to Taurus, escape to Pisces. No law outside Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
