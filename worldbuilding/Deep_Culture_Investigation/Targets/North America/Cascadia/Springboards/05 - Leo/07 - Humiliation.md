# Cascadia: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, plus Indigenous and immigrant-community languages (below). **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast settler culture and the British Columbia coast:** do school and workplace norms of correction differ across the border?
- **Far West interior:** rural, ranching and farm-labor towns; hazing and teasing in small schools.
- **El Norte and Sudcalifornia (Southern California and the Bajas):** Spanish-language usage; border cultures.
- **Panhandle settlers:** small-town fishing communities, likely thin data.
- **Indigenous units:** Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin, Subarctic Dene; separate by people, not by "Native".
- **Immigrant communities:** Chinese, Japanese, Filipino, Mexican and Central American, Southeast Asian, Pacific Islander; whether shame is also framed in heritage languages.
- **Within each:** gender, age, class, LGBTQ youth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on school bullying, online shaming and hazing in English; mixed for Spanish and the Bajas; thin for Indigenous peoples' own concepts and for the Panhandle.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; for Indigenous and immigrant communities, community language programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous knowledge may be restricted; residential school and incarceration testimony needs care; do not quote survivors beyond a few words; sexual violence and abuse material.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
