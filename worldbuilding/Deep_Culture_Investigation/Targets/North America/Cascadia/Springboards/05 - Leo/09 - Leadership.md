*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast core** (western Washington and Oregon, coastal California): settler civic and tech-founder leadership.
- **British Columbia coast:** British colonial founding, Canadian political forms.
- **Far West interior** (Central Valley, interior Washington, Oregon, British Columbia): ranching, farming, extraction.
- **Southern California and northern Baja California:** Mexican-heritage settlement, border life.
- **Baja California Sur:** small-town and ranching setting.
- **Panhandle settlers:** Russian-American, Norwegian, fishing roots.
- **Indigenous units:** Northwest Coast peoples (hereditary and elected leadership side by side), California peoples and Kumeyaay bands, modern-treaty nations, Baja peoples (Kumiai, Pa ipai, Kiliwa, Cucapá).
- **Asian-heritage and Mexican-heritage communities** of the cities and farm valleys.
- **Cross-cuts:** gender, generation, union against management.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the U.S. and Canadian settler sides; mixed for the Bajas; mixed to thin for Indigenous leadership, where the nations' own publications are the best route.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Chinese and Tagalog; Indigenous languages for terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hereditary names, privileges and ceremonies belong to each nation; use only what is shared publicly. Hereditary-against-elected disputes are current politics.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
