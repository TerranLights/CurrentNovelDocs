# Cascadia: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California, the Southeast Alaska Panhandle, British Columbia, Baja California and Baja California Sur, with their Indigenous nations). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Chinese (Cantonese and Mandarin), Tagalog, Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese and Russian for immigrant communities; Indigenous languages as each nation publishes its own. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore; and how far a culture encourages, channels, limits or punishes it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Left Coast, British Columbia coast, Far West and El Norte settler cultures: tech, university and port cities against farm, mining and desert communities.
- Baja California and Baja California Sur: a Mexican public-science setting and a border.
- Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and northern Dene peoples: each nation's own rules on asking, and on knowledge not open to every asker.
- Immigrant communities (Chinese, Filipino, Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese, Russian, Mexican and Central American): school and family habits about children's questions.
- Age, gender, religious tradition, urban and rural.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for museum, citizen-science and survey data on the U.S. side; mixed for British Columbia; thin for Baja California and for everyday question-asking norms. Indigenous material is mostly the nations' own statements.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; community languages for the immigrant press; Indigenous languages only through the nations' own materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restricted Indigenous knowledge, and research and collecting without consent; follow the nations' own protocols and read their statements first. Questions about immigration status, illness and ancestry are sensitive for interviews.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
