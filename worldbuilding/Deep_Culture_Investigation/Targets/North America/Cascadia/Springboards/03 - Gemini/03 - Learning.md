# Cascadia: Learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America. Pre-war lands: Washington, Oregon, California, Southeast Alaska (the Panhandle), British Columbia, Baja California and Baja California Sur, plus the Indigenous nations inside them. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, French (British Columbia), and Indigenous languages by unit. **Concept in one line:** how people come to know and to do: in the family, by watching and pitching in, through play, in early school, at work and across a life (schooling as an institution is Sagittarius).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **S1 Left Coast and S3 Far West:** the settler cultures of the U.S. coast and interior; compare homeschooling, alternative schooling, and the rural-urban split.
- **S2 British Columbia coast:** a Canadian provincial school system, French immersion, and Aboriginal education policy.
- **S4 El Norte and S5 Sudcalifornia:** Spanish-speaking, Mexican-heritage learning ways, *educación*, and different school systems north and south of the line.
- **S1a Panhandle settlers:** small, remote; thin data likely.
- **I1 to I6 Indigenous units:** separate each (coast, plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin edge, Dene); their own learning ways and language programs differ.
- **Later layers:** Mexican Indigenous migrants and Asian-heritage communities, by generation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. schooling measures; mixed for B.C. and the Bajas; thin and community-held for learning from elders and land. Informal learning (pitching in, play) is mostly in ethnography, not surveys.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish, some French; Indigenous languages for primary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous knowledge held by elders may be restricted; ask each nation what may be described. Residential-school testimony is the survivors' to tell, and the nations first. Disability and failure are silent subjects.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
