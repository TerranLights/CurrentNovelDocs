# Cascadia: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California, the Southeast Alaska Panhandle, British Columbia, Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), Tagalog, Chinuk Wawa, and the Indigenous languages of the lands. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between (broker, interpreter, intercessor).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Left Coast settler culture (Puget Sound, Willamette, Bay Area): institutional and professional mediation versus informal practice.
- British Columbia coast: Canadian "honest broker" idiom and Hong Kong and Chinese diaspora go-betweens.
- Far West interior and Central Valley: farmworker go-betweens (labor contractors, *comadres*, parish priests).
- El Norte (Southern California, Baja California) and Baja California Sur: Mexican family and parish mediation, *gestores* and *coyotes*.
- Peace-church communities (Quaker, Mennonite) against Catholic intercession.
- Indigenous units: Northwest Coast peoples, Plateau peoples, California peoples, Yuman peoples (Kumiai, Cucapá), Great Basin peoples; each nation's own practice, separately.
- Immigrant communities: Chinese, Filipino, Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese, Oaxacan (Mixtec, Triqui, Zapotec) with children as brokers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institutional mediation (centers, labor, schools) in English; mixed for Spanish and Baja; thin for informal go-betweens, which live in oral and family settings.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Chinese and Tagalog for diasporas; Indigenous languages for peacemaking terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous peacemaking and ceremony; use only what the nations publish. Domestic-violence cases in mediation. Sealed family disputes.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
