## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Left Coast against rural and inland areas: trades skill, tech work.
- British Columbia against the U.S. states: different support systems.
- Baja California: Spanish-speaking, border work; compare Mexican-heritage households in California.
- Immigrant and refugee communities: expectations of children's and elders' help (a research question).
- Indigenous peoples (Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin, Subarctic Dene), each separately.
- By age, class and ability: retirees, the unemployed, disabled workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian surveys; mixed for Baja; thin for Indigenous concepts except as nations share them.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; each Indigenous nation's own materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elders' teachings and restricted knowledge; sterilization and asylum records naming living families; read suicide and end-of-life data with care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
