# Cascadia: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast (Puget Sound to Monterey):** the Yankee heritage of moral and civic judgment; heavy tasting and media-literacy culture.
- **British Columbia coast:** British colonial and Canadian consumer institutions, with a large Chinese-Canadian population.
- **Far West (interior Washington, Oregon, California, BC):** rural trust against urban savvy; Dust Bowl-descended and Mexican-heritage farm communities.
- **El Norte (southern California, Baja California):** Mexican Spanish vocabulary and institutions; border scam and *notario* fraud.
- **Sudcalifornia (Baja California Sur):** a small, separate Mexican setting; Jesuit mission history.
- **Panhandle settlers:** small communities with fishing and Russian-American roots.
- **Indigenous units:** Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Subarctic Dene peoples, each separately.
- **Also:** religious groups, age cohorts, newcomers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian fraud and survey data, mixed for Mexico by state, thin for Indigenous and immigrant-language views of judgment.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; others for immigrant communities and Indigenous nations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors of cults and scams (underreporting, shame); Indigenous knowledge about reading signs and people may be restricted; prejudice presented as judgment.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
