# Cascadia: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; also Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), Tagalog, Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese; Tlingit, Haida, Chinuk Wawa, the Coast Salish languages, Kumiai and other Indigenous languages of the lands. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- S1 Left Coast and S2 British Columbia coast: self-help and optimization culture against the British colonial tradition of civic improvement.
- S3 Far West: improvement as land and water works, farm and Dust Bowl families.
- S4 El Norte and S5 Sudcalifornia: *superación* and *autoconstrucción*; border-city industrial work; compare Baja with the U.S. side.
- S1a Panhandle settlers: fishing and resource towns.
- Immigrant and first-generation households (Chinese, Filipino, Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese, Mexican): improvement as family project or pressure; separate by decade of arrival.
- Indigenous units I1 to I5, as the nations name them: imposed "improvement" and their own renewal.
- Class and politics: markets against the state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for English self-help, mobility and housing; rich for assimilation schooling in the official reports; mixed for Spanish self-help at the regional level; thin for Chinese and Tagalog usage and for Indigenous words.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Chinese and Tagalog for immigrant sources; Indigenous languages through the nations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding and residential school testimony (handle with care, survivors' voices first); the phrase *mejorar la raza* is a historical racial reproach, to be treated as a cited term only; some Indigenous knowledge on renewal may be restricted; ask each nation.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
