# Cascadia: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Mandarin and Cantonese, Korean, Vietnamese, Tagalog, Canadian French for community sources; Indigenous languages by nation. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | perfectionist, nitpicky, fussy, meticulous, "good enough", "done is better than perfect", "fail fast" | the concept and its neighbors | also "attention to detail", "control freak" |
| Spanish (Bajas, Latino Californians) | perfeccionista, exigente, quisquilloso, detallista, meticuloso | perfectionist, demanding, fussy, detail-minded | *detallista* may be praise |
| Spanish | ahí se va, al aventón, chambón, chambonada | "that'll do", done hastily, sloppy worker, botched job | the low end of the range |
| Mandarin | 完美主義 (wánměi zhǔyì), 追求完美 | perfectionism, pursuing perfection | traditional script for Hong Kong-origin sources |
| Indigenous languages | unknown | words for exactness, care and imperfection in making | ask the nations; see Flags |

English keywords: perfectionism, academic pressure, quality culture, craftsmanship, tightness, scrupulosity, good-enough.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Silicon Valley and tech workers against Seattle's aerospace and medical-device engineers:** "fail fast" against "zero defects".
- **Immigrant and second-generation families (East and Southeast Asian, Latino, Slavic, others):** standards and pressure, as a question to ask.
- **Baja California border cities (Tijuana, Mexicali) against Baja California Sur:** assembly plants against tourism and fishing.
- **Settler regions (Left Coast, British Columbia coast, Far West, El Norte):** craft, school and church traditions differ.
- **Indigenous peoples (Northwest Coast, California, Plateau, Yuman, Great Basin, Subarctic Dene):** exacting and deliberately imperfect making, if the nations choose to share it.
- **Religious communities:** Catholic, Mainline and Evangelical Protestant, Latter-day Saint, Buddhist, secular.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian psychology and schools; mixed for Baja California (fewer studies, border industry data); thin for Indigenous understandings and for small immigrant communities.
- **Languages needed:** English; Spanish for the Bajas; Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese, Tagalog for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous knowledge of making and ritual exactness may be restricted; use only what nations publish. Suicide, eating disorders and clinical material need care. No national stereotypes (no single "tiger parent" voice).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search Google Scholar for "perfectionism" with "British Columbia", "California" and "Washington" students.
2. Pull the school health surveys' stress items by state and province.
3. Search Spanish: "ahí se va", "chambón", "perfeccionismo" with "Tijuana" or "Mexicali", in Zeta Tijuana and university theses.
4. Look up the Palo Alto coverage and CDC report for the academic-pressure debate.
5. Ask the major museums and tribal cultural centers what they publish about makers' standards.
