# Cascadia: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English (U.S. and Canadian), Spanish (Baja), French (small Canadian presence), Cantonese and Mandarin, Tagalog, Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese; Indigenous languages through each nation's own language program. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast (Puget Sound, Willamette, Bay Area):** a claimed indirect style, to test, not adopt.
- **British Columbia coast:** Canadian norms and the Canadian press.
- **Far West interior:** a possible bluntness contrast.
- **Southern California and the Bajas (El Norte):** Spanish-speaking family and border norms.
- **Southeast Alaska settlers:** small-town criticism.
- **Indigenous units** (Coast Salish, Tlingit and Haida, Californian peoples, Bajas peoples): separate each; correction by teasing may differ by nation.
- **Immigrant communities:** East and Southeast Asian, Mexican and Central American, Pacific Islander, Russian-speaking and others: different feedback norms and press.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian workplaces, press freedom and online reviews; mixed for families and Spanish-language Baja; thin for Indigenous practice (usually oral, community-held).
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; heritage languages for community media; Indigenous languages for terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous teaching on correction and shame may be restricted; ask the nation's own program before use. Violence against journalists in the Bajas: use data without naming living sources.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
