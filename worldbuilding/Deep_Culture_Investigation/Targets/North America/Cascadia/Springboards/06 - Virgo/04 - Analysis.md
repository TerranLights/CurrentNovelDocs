*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; French and Chinese-language sources for British Columbia and California; Indigenous languages of the lands (see Flags). **Concept in one line:** taking a whole apart to understand it: reasoning from parts, evidence, measurement and detail.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tech and research hubs against resource and farm towns** (Bay Area, Seattle, Vancouver against interior and valley communities): different daily uses and views of analysis.
- **Baja California Spanish-speaking communities against U.S. and Canadian settler lobes:** a different school system and different words.
- **Chinese-heritage, Filipino, Vietnamese, Japanese and Korean communities** in California and British Columbia: separate schooling traditions and sources.
- **Indigenous nations of the Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Dene lands:** each has its own ways of observing and testing; separate, never merged.
- **Religious groups** (Catholic, evangelical, secular, Latter-day Saint pockets): views of reason and science.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the U.S. and Canadian lobes (surveys, press, scholarship); mixed for Baja California (national surveys, thin regional cuts); thin and uneven for Indigenous framings of analysis.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; French and Chinese for some sources; Indigenous languages for community material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous knowledge held by community; some is restricted, and data sovereignty principles apply to Indigenous data. Ask the nation before using any. Missing-data work on violence against Indigenous women is sensitive.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. NCES Skills Map and NAEP: pull numeracy and math results for WA, OR, CA; PCAP for BC.

3. Search each major paper for "data journalism" and "fact check" with "science" and "common sense" to map public quarrels.
4. Read the frames in Hofstadter and Nisbett against the regional units as questions, not answers.
5. Contact Indigenous education and language programs about their own terms and protocols before any research.
