# Cascadia: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (pre-war lands: Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, Indigenous languages of the lands, and the larger immigrant languages. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for the U.S. West Coast and British Columbia; mixed for Baja (INEGI is coarse); thin for Indigenous peoples, where kin may frame the bond.
- **Languages needed:** English; Spanish; immigrant languages; Indigenous languages through each nation's program.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous protocols on kin, ceremony and alliance may be restricted; seek permission and prefer tribally approved sources. Oral-history subjects may be living.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
