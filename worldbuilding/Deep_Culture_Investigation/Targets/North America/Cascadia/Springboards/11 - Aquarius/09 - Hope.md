# Cascadia: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, Chinuk Wawa, the Indigenous languages (see Flags), and the main immigrant languages (Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese). **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast, British Columbia coast, Far West, El Norte and Sudcalifornia** (the settler units): founder hopes differ (godly commonwealth, gold, land, the border), as do who now holds power.
- **Religious and immigrant groups** (Catholic saints and *mandas* against secular "manifesting"; Chinese, Filipino, Vietnamese, Mexican and Central American): migration is itself a wager on hope.
- **Migrants in the Bajas** (Mixtec, Triqui, Zapotec farm labor; people waiting at the border): hope in hardship.
- **Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Dene peoples:** hope for survival, land and language, told in each people's own voice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian attitudes and despair deaths (state and county); mixed for Baja (INEGI covers it, thinner on hope itself); thin for the Indigenous units' own hope, and most of it is oral or held by the nations.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese for immigrant units; Indigenous languages through the nations' programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and despair data; Indigenous ceremonies (renewal rites) are shared only as each nation chooses; migrants' accounts may carry legal risk; the missing and their families.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Gallup and Pew: how each cuts hope, expectation and "next generation" questions by state, province or country, and whether the West or British Columbia is separable.
2. INEGI: well-being and expectations tables for Baja California and Baja California Sur.
3. CDC WONDER, county level: despair deaths for the three U.S. states and the Panhandle, with BC's coroners' data alongside.
4. Library and archive search: "hope" with Terry Fox, Harvey Milk, Chávez and Huerta; then the border shrines (Juan Soldado, Santo Toribio Romo).
5. Write to the language and culture programs of the Indigenous nations for what they will share, before any outside source.
