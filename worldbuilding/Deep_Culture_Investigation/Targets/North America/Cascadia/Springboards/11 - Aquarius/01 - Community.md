# Cascadia: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Spanish; French (British Columbia, minor); Chinese, Tagalog, Korean, Vietnamese and Japanese (immigrant communities); Indigenous languages (Nuu-chah-nulth, Tlingit, Haida, Kumeyaay and others, by unit). **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast and British Columbia coast:** city, suburb and small town; renters and owners (the "Seattle Freeze" is a local idea to test).
- **Far West (interior, Central Valley):** towns tied to farm, ranch and mining work; Dust Bowl and farm-labor communities.
- **El Norte and Sudcalifornia (Southern California, the Bajas):** barrio, colonia and ejido; Oaxacan migrants against older border families.
- **Immigrant and heritage communities:** Chinese, Japanese, Filipino, Korean, Vietnamese, Latino; native-place associations and churches.
- **Indigenous units** (Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin, Subarctic Dene): each its own voice, on and off reserve.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the U.S. states and British Columbia (surveys, archives, local press); mixed for the Bajas (INEGI strong, small-area detail thin); thin to uneven for Indigenous communities in outside sources.
- **Languages needed:** English and Spanish at minimum; Chinese for Chinatown and Vancouver sources; Indigenous languages by unit.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous ceremonial and restricted knowledge (use only what the nations publish or share); undocumented migrants; unhoused people; high-control groups' members.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
