# Cascadia: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California, Southeast Alaska, British Columbia, Baja California, Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Indigenous and immigrant languages for specific units. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The five settler units** (Left Coast, Far West, El Norte, British Columbia coast, Sudcalifornia): different founding norms.
- **The six Indigenous units:** sharing and owning may be framed in their own terms; compare unit by unit.
- **Both sides of the Southern California and Baja California border:** Spanish and English in play.
- **Migrant Mixtec, Triqui and Zapotec farmworkers; Dust Bowl descendants:** separate family and land habits to check.
- **Chinese, Japanese, Filipino, Korean and Vietnamese communities:** later layers with their own words.
- **Generation, gender, religion** inside each unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for partner control and online territory; mixed for sharing and children; thin for Indigenous units, Sudcalifornia and Southeast Alaska settlers.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Indigenous and immigrant languages for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous teachings on sharing and owning may be the nations' to state; ask first. Abuse data and killings by partners need care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
