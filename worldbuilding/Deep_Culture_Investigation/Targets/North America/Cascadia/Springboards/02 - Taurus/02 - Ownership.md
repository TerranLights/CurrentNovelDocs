# Cascadia: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California, the Alaska Panhandle, British Columbia, the Bajas). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Spanish; Chinese (Vancouver, Bay Area); Indigenous languages by unit. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Left Coast against Far West settlers (corporate and federal land against family holdings); the British Columbia coast against the Panhandle's settlers; El Norte against Sudcalifornia (*ejido* and coast); owners against renters; ranchers against farmers and federal agencies; *ejidatarios* against *avecindados*; recent immigrant owners against older holders; the Indigenous units, each with its own system of holding (Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin, Subarctic Dene).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian housing and land; mixed for the Bajas; thin and uneven for Indigenous systems of holding, much of it written by outsiders.
- **Languages needed:** English; Spanish; Chinese; each Indigenous language through its own program.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** owned crests, names, songs and stories belong to named houses and clans: use only what a nation shares, and ask first. Land-back and title disputes are live: record each side as it tells it. The "Chief Seattle" speech is an outside-authored text *check*.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
