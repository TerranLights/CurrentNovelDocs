# Cascadia: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America. Lands carried: Washington, Oregon, California, British Columbia, Baja California, Baja California Sur, and the Southeast Alaska Panhandle, plus the Indigenous peoples of those lands. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Cantonese and Mandarin, Tagalog, Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese (large immigrant communities); Indigenous languages by unit. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Tech hubs (Bay Area, Seattle, Vancouver) against rural counties and the Far West interior: different founders and fortunes.
- Old fortunes (gold rush, railroad, timber, land, oil) against new (tech, film, real estate, crypto).
- Los Cabos and resort Baja California Sur against the sierra ranches and the border cities: tourism wealth against maquiladora and remittance wealth.
- Immigrant generations (Mexican, Chinese, Japanese, Filipino, Vietnamese, Korean, Armenian, Iranian): wealth brought, built or sent home.
- Indigenous peoples by nation: Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Subarctic Dene each reckon wealth differently; gaming and non-gaming nations; corporation-based Alaska Natives (Sealaska) against reservation-based nations.
- The Panhandle's fishing and tourism towns against Juneau's government economy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian income and wealth numbers and for attitude surveys; mixed for Baja California Sur and the Panhandle; thin and community-held for how Indigenous peoples reckon wealth, with outsiders' accounts often older and contested.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Chinese for the Bay Area and Vancouver; Indigenous languages for terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** feasts, regalia, crests, names and titles belong to families and nations to explain; gaming and distribution figures are the nations' own; a question to research, not assume: whether wealthy families hide or show what they have, in their own telling; wealth and violence (narco display) needs careful sourcing.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
