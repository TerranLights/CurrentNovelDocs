# Cascadia: Domain and folkways, possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America. Lands: Washington, Oregon, California, the Southeast Alaska Panhandle, British Columbia, Baja California and Baja California Sur. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Chinuk Wawa (Chinook Jargon); the Indigenous languages of the Northwest Coast, Plateau, California and the Yuman Bajas; Cantonese, Mandarin, Japanese, Tagalog, Korean and Vietnamese for the large immigrant communities. **Domain in one line:** what is worth having, and how is it held (possessions, money, resources, values, self-worth), with Fischer's folkways of food, dress and wealth.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast (Washington, western Oregon, northern California)** against the **Far West interior** and **El Norte (Southern California, Baja California)**: Woodard's lines; they differ in land and money history.
- **Sudcalifornia (Baja California Sur)**: oldest settlement, ranch life, tourism economy.
- **British Columbia coast** against interior BC.
- **Indigenous peoples by area:** Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman Bajas; each has its own foods and giving customs.
- **Immigrant communities:** Chinese-, Japanese-, Filipino-, Mexican- and Central American-heritage communities, and Mixtec, Triqui and Zapotec farm workers in Baja California.
- **Latter-day Saints, Adventists, Muslims, Jews:** food rules and tithing.
- **Urban tech and port cities** against rural farm, timber and fishing towns; rich and poor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for spending, wealth, diet and the United States and Canada; mixed for Mexico by state; thin for Baja California Sur; thin and uneven for Indigenous views outside what nations publish themselves.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Indigenous languages and Chinese for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regalia, ceremony, feast protocol and some foods belong to the nations; use only what a nation publishes, and say regalia, not costume. Mind the holdings of seized objects.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
