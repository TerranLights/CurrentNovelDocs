## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast metros against Far West interior:** rural against urban practice.
- **British Columbia coast against the U.S. lobes:** different public-safety history.
- **El Norte and the Bajas:** *amparo*, *cuidar*, community self-defense.
- **San Quintín migrant communities (Mixtec, Triqui, Zapotec):** their own practices.
- **Indigenous units, nation by nation:** the nations' own guardian and land-defense ways.
- **Immigrant-heritage communities (Chinese, Filipino and others):** across generations.
- **Gender and race:** who feels protected, and by whom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for safety surveys and preparedness (U.S., Canada); mixed for the Bajas below state level; thin for Indigenous concepts unless the nations publish them.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Indigenous languages with the nations' permission.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** extortion and cartel violence; abuse by protectors; child removal from Indigenous families; restricted protective ceremony; missing and murdered women.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

5. Search local newspaper archives for "rescue" and "evacuation" per unit.
