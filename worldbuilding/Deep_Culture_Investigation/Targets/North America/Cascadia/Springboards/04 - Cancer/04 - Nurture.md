# Cascadia: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, Indigenous languages, larger immigrant languages. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast and Far West settlers:** the scope file's founding stocks; interior against coast.
- **El Norte, Baja California, Sudcalifornia:** Spanish-language *crianza*; border cities and ranch country; mainland migrants.
- **British Columbia coast:** British colonial founding; Chinese and Japanese Canadian families.
- **Panhandle:** Tlingit, Haida and Tsimshian households; settler fishing families.
- **Indigenous units:** separate by nation, never one voice; residential and boarding schools bear differently on each.
- **Cross-cutting:** class, immigrant generation, mothers and fathers, grandparent-headed homes, religion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. and Canadian parts; mixed for the Bajas; thin and uneven for Indigenous teachings.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; later Cantonese, Mandarin, Tagalog, Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous child-rearing teachings may be ceremonial or restricted; use only what each nation publishes. Child-protection records are confidential. The boarding-school history is live.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

1. GSS and WVS child-values items, by region.
