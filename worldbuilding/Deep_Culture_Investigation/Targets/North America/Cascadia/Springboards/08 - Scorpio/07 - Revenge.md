# Cascadia: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Indigenous languages by unit; immigrant-heritage languages as a later layer. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast and the BC coast:** settler regional cultures; compare how each frames getting even and forgiving.
- **Far West:** ranching and mining interior, the likeliest place to test honor-culture ideas.
- **El Norte and Sudcalifornia:** Mexican-heritage and border traditions; Sur is a separate founding.
- **Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman and Dene peoples:** each people's own accounts of feud, payment and peacemaking; do not merge them.
- **Later layers:** immigrant-heritage communities named in the scope; gang and prison subcultures; congregations teaching forgiveness.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for English media, homicide counts and history; mixed for Baja and Spanish; thin and uneven for Indigenous accounts; very thin on revenge fantasies and quiet revenge.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; for the Indigenous units, the nations' own sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Indigenous knowledge is restricted; the ethnography is colonial-era; school violence, gang material and revenge porn must be studied without reproducing it; living victims and families.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
