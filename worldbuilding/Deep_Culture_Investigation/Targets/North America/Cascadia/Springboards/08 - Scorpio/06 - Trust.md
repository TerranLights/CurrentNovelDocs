# Cascadia: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Indigenous languages of the coast, plateau and the Bajas; French and the main immigrant languages as needed. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The settler regional cultures** (Left Coast, the BC coast, the Far West, El Norte, Sudcalifornia, the Panhandle's settlers): different founders and different habits of credit, church and neighborliness.
- **Indigenous nations by culture area** (Northwest Coast, Coast Salish, Plateau, California, Yuman peoples of the Bajas): their own teachings on relationship and reciprocity, stated by the nations themselves.
- **Migrant Indigenous communities in San Quintín and California** (Mixtec, Zapotec, Triqui): trust networks formed on the road.
- **Urban against rural; border against interior; newcomers against old families:** the radius of trust may differ.
- **Immigrant communities** (Chinese, Filipino, Vietnamese, Korean and others): kin and community trust.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the U.S. and Canadian parts (surveys, polls, scholarship); mixed for the Bajas; thin and mostly unpublished for Indigenous understandings.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Indigenous languages with the nations' consent; Chinese and others for immigrant sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** a nation's ceremonial and legal knowledge (feast witnessing, treaty protocols) belongs to the nation: use only what it publishes. Medical, racial and police mistrust are questions to ask each group, not conclusions. Trust in police and courts is held for Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
