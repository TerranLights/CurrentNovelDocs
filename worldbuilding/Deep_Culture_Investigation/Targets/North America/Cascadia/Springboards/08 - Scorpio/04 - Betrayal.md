# Cascadia: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, plus the large immigrant languages of the coast and the region's Indigenous languages. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Settler coast** (Left Coast, British Columbia coast): infidelity and whistleblower attitudes in a tech and media setting; employer loyalty.
- **El Norte and Sudcalifornia (Baja):** Mexican-lineage households; *traición* vocabulary and corridos from the source tradition.
- **Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Dene peoples:** broken promises between peoples and the nations' own terms; separate each nation.
- **Immigrant communities** by language (Chinese, Filipino, Japanese and others): their own words and, where they exist, tales of betrayal.
- **Occupational groups:** police, military, unions, former gang members, clergy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for infidelity attitudes, institutional betrayal and corridos; mixed for whistleblowing; thin for Indigenous words and tales, and for informing inside close communities.
- **Languages needed:** English and Spanish at minimum; Chinese, Tagalog and Japanese for the immigrant units; Indigenous languages only through each nation's own program.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Indigenous knowledge is restricted; take only what a nation has published or approved. Abuse and survivor accounts need care; gang and immigration-status informing: published and former-member accounts only.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull the General Social Survey infidelity item for the Pacific region, and Gallup's moral acceptability series.
