# Cascadia: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California, the Alaska Panhandle, British Columbia, the Bajas). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, French, Cantonese and Mandarin, Korean, Tagalog, Vietnamese, and the Indigenous languages of the lands. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and beliefs that the dead return.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast:** recovery-retreat and "spiritual but not religious" scenes against church traditions.
- **British Columbia coast:** Chinese, Korean and Filipino congregations differ from British-founded churches.
- **Far West:** evangelical and Pentecostal areas; fire-recovery towns.
- **El Norte and Sudcalifornia:** Catholic and evangelical contrast; *anexo* houses; migrants.
- **Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Subarctic Dene peoples:** each with its own revival and language work.
- Within each: generation, class, immigrant against native-born.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on surveys and recovery; mixed on the Bajas and on Asian-language congregations; thin and restricted on Indigenous ceremony.
- **Languages needed:** English and Spanish first; Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese and Tagalog for congregations; Indigenous languages through their programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial knowledge belongs to its nation; use only what it publishes or grants. Forced-conversion and residential school testimony needs care. Recovery fellowships guard anonymity.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
