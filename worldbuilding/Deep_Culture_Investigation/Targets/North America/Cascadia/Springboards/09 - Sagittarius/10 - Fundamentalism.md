# Cascadia: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Russian, Mandarin, Korean for immigrant congregations; Indigenous-language terms through the nations' own language programs. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Denominations:** Baptist, Pentecostal, Calvary Chapel-style, Reformed, Anabaptist, Latter-day Saint splinters, traditionalist Catholic.
- **Regions:** Central Valley, northern interior and the Fraser Valley against the coastal cities (measured, not assumed); border cities against Baja California Sur.
- **Immigrant churches:** Latino Pentecostal, Slavic evangelical, Korean and Chinese-language.
- **Separatist communities:** Doukhobor lines, Bountiful, Mormon colonies in northern Mexico.
- **Indigenous Christians and traditionalists:** faith varies within each people.
- **Generations:** insiders, leavers, the second generation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the United States; mixed for British Columbia; thin for the Bajas, immigrant and Indigenous congregations.
- **Languages needed:** English and Spanish; Russian, Mandarin, Korean for minority congregations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in high-control groups; leavers naming living people; Indigenous religious knowledge, sought only through the nations' own channels; "religious trauma" is contested.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
