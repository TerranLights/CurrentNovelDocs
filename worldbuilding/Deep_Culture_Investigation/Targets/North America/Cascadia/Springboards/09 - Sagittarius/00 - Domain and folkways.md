## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast against Far West:** the unaffiliated coast against the interior's churches.
- **British Columbia coast:** its own colonial history, and the Pacific Rim outlook.
- **El Norte and Sudcalifornia (the Bajas):** Mexican Catholic and *laicidad* inheritance, with growing Pentecostal and evangelical life.
- **Panhandle:** Russian Orthodox and Tlingit communities, as they describe themselves.
- **Indigenous units:** Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin, Subarctic Dene; each states its own religion.
- **Migrant and diaspora congregations:** Chinese, Japanese, Filipino, Vietnamese, Korean, Mexican and Central American, Slavic evangelical, Molokan, Doukhobor, Mennonite, Latter-day Saint.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census and survey counts in the three countries (different questions, so shares do not line up); mixed for philosophy and worldview; thin for Indigenous belief outside the nations' own publications.
- **Languages needed:** English and Spanish; Chinese and Russian for diaspora sources; the Indigenous languages for ceremony and knowledge.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restricted ceremonial knowledge and sacred places; the suppressed religions' own accounts; congregations that guard their records. Seek the community's own published account and consent.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
