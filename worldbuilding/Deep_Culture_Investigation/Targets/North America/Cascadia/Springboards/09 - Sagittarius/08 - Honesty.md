# Cascadia: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon and California whole; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; Baja California and Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish, Chinook Jargon (historical), Cantonese and Mandarin, and the Indigenous languages of the lands. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast and the Pacific Northwest** against **the Far West interior** and **El Norte** (southern California): different founding populations and business cultures.
- **British Columbia** against the U.S. states: separate national institutions and survey series.
- **The Bajas** against Alta California: Mexican institutions and slang; the border's gray economy.
- **Immigrant communities**: Chinese, Filipino, Mexican and Central American; first against later generations.
- **Indigenous peoples**: Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin and Subarctic Dene peoples, and the Indigenous communities of the Bajas.
- **Occupation and class**: tech, agriculture, fishing, timber, border trade.
- **Religious communities**: for example, Latter-day Saint, Catholic, evangelical, Buddhist and secular.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for U.S. national and by-state measures; mixed for BC (national Canadian series, thin below province level); mixed for the Bajas (INEGI by state; thin on culture). Very thin for Indigenous units.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Cantonese and Mandarin for Chinese communities; Indigenous languages for terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** a people's teachings on truth, oaths or speech may be restricted; ask nations and language programs (for example, Sealaska Heritage Institute; First Peoples' Cultural Council *check*) before using anything. Corruption and bribery material, and migrants' fear of speaking to officials, can distort surveys.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
