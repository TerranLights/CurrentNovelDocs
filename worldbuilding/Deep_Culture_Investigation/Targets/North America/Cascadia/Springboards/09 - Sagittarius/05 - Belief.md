# Cascadia: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California; the Alaska Panhandle; British Columbia; the Bajas). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; Chinese and Tagalog for immigrant communities; Indigenous languages by unit. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true (about God, the world, people, right and wrong) and what warrant people want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast, Far West, El Norte, Sudcalifornia:** settler regions that may differ in affiliation and in what may be asked.
- **British Columbia versus the U.S. coast:** separate survey and census systems.
- **Panhandle settlers versus Tlingit, Haida and Tsimshian communities,** including those with Russian Orthodox or Protestant histories.
- **Indigenous peoples** (Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin, Dene; Baja Kumiai, Pa ipai, Kiliwa, Cucapá), each on its own terms, and migrant Mixtec, Triqui and Zapotec communities.
- **Immigrant faiths:** Chinese, Filipino, Vietnamese, Korean, Armenian, Mexican; Muslim, Jewish, Buddhist, Latter-day Saint.
- **Nones, evangelicals, Catholics, mainline Protestants,** by generation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey measures; mixed for folk belief; thin for Indigenous belief and for the Bajas beyond the census.
- **Languages needed:** English, Spanish; Chinese and Tagalog for some sources; Indigenous languages for community-held material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous religious knowledge is often restricted; use only what a nation has published or approved. Hidden belief or unbelief within families.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
