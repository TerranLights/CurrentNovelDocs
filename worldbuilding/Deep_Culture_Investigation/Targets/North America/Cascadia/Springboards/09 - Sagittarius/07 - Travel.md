# Cascadia: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Cascadia, region North America (Washington, Oregon, California, the Alaska Panhandle, British Columbia, Baja California, Baja California Sur). **Language(s) for searching:** English, Spanish; also Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), Vietnamese, French (British Columbia), Chinook Jargon, and the Indigenous languages of each unit. **Concept in one line:** going far from home (journeys, exploration, pilgrimage, migration) and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left Coast and British Columbia coast:** leisure travel, outdoor culture, ports and cruise trade.
- **Far West interior:** road, rail and seasonal labor; long distances.
- **El Norte and Sudcalifornia:** the border, return journeys, migrants and crossers.
- **Panhandle settlers:** ferry and sea travel, seasonal work.
- **Immigrant communities (Chinese, Vietnamese, Filipino, Mexican, Central American):** journeys of arrival and visits home.
- **Indigenous units** (Northwest Coast, Plateau, California, Yuman, Great Basin, Dene): each people's own travel and seasonal rounds, as they describe them; borders cut several of these lands.
- **Class, generation, documented vs. undocumented:** who can leave and return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tourism, migration counts and travel writing; mixed for migrants' own accounts; thin and uneven for Indigenous views, where the data is held by the nations.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish essential; Chinese and Vietnamese for diaspora sources; Indigenous languages for any original-language material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deaths at the border; trafficking; the stories and travel knowledge that peoples hold as their own (ask the nations, do not extract from outsiders' summaries); undocumented migrants' privacy.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
