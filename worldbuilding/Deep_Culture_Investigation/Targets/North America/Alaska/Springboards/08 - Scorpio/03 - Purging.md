# Alaska: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America; pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Russian; Iñupiaq, Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Dena'ina and other Athabascan languages, Tlingit, Haida, Tsimshian; Spanish. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Native peoples with a steam bath or smoke cleansing tradition and those without (ask each; do not assume): Yup'ik and Cup'ik, Iñupiat, Athabascan peoples, Tlingit, Haida.
- Russian Orthodox communities (confession, fasting) and Old Believer villages (communal discipline).
- Villages with small-community means of removing a person against the cities' courts and police.
- Communities affected by contamination (spill-affected Prince William Sound and Cook Inlet towns; military sites).
- Anchorage's unhoused population and the city that clears camps.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on contamination and cleanup, thin on bodily and spiritual cleansing except in oral history and health writing.
- **Languages needed:** English; Russian; Native languages for the best terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healing and cleansing rites some peoples keep within the community; banishment cases involving young people; boarding-school testimony; wartime removal; addiction.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the language archives and Project Jukebox for "steam bath", "bath house", "cleansing" and "sickness from".
2. Open the Contaminated Sites Program's site list and note which are on Native or village lands.
3. Read the Trustee Council's pages on community effects of the Exxon Valdez spill beside local news accounts.
4. Search news archives for "banished" and "village court" in Alaska and sort cases by who describes them.
5. Pull the commission report on the Unangax̂ removal and the survivors' accounts of it.
