# Alaska: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America; pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Russian; Iñupiaq, Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Dena'ina and other Athabascan languages, Tlingit, Haida, Tsimshian; Spanish. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing, or deter the next wrong; with its alternatives: forgiveness, payment, apology, letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Each Native people separately, since older records of redress differ by people and by region: Iñupiat, Yup'ik and Cup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangax̂, Athabascan peoples, Tlingit, Haida, Tsimshian.
- Village life, where a feud lasts among neighbors who cannot avoid each other, against urban life.
- Frontier-descended communities (mining, homesteading) against recent arrivals.
- Orthodox, Catholic, evangelical and other congregations that teach forgiveness differently.
- Occupational rivalries: fishing fleets, guides, cannery and crew networks.
- Veterans and military communities with their own rules of retaliation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Older ethnography is rich but outsider-written; present-day data on revenge are indirect (violence reports, news).
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for the peoples' own terms; Russian for church and Russian-era sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** accounts of past violence between peoples; clan obligations and payments; survivors' and families' privacy in recent cases; avoid presenting any people as vengeful: record variation and each people's own account.
- **Law:** no law research here; the letter of the law on retaliation belongs in Libra, legal culture in Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
