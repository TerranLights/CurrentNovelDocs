# Alaska: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America; carries pre-war USA and Canada lands. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Iñupiaq, Central Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Tlingit, Haida, Tsimshian, Dene languages; Russian for Orthodox and colonial records. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Peoples whose values are published by their own bodies (Iñupiat, Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Dene, Tlingit, Haida); village against city; Orthodox, Presbyterian, Catholic, Pentecostal and Latter-day Saint residents; miners, loggers, fishers and oil workers; conservation and development camps; old Alaska families against recent arrivals and the military; newcomer communities (Filipino, Korean, Samoan, Hmong, Hispanic); Yukon communities if carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Native value statements; mixed for general public attitudes (national surveys seldom split Alaska out); thin on newcomers.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for the key terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** value lists are published by the nations for teaching; cite with their framing. Politics of development and subsistence are heated; record disagreement, not one voice. Law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
