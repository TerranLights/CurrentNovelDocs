# Alaska: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America; carries pre-war USA and Canada lands. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Iñupiaq, Central Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Tlingit, Haida, Dena'ina, Koyukon, Gwich'in; Russian for colonial records. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Iñupiat, Yup'ik and Cup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangax̂, Interior Dene peoples, and Tlingit, Haida and Tsimshian; each marks having and belonging differently. Also compare Bush villages with Anchorage and the road system; Russian Orthodox villages; gold-rush descendants; military families; newer immigrant households; and people with and without a house.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on objects and museums; thin on everyday household attitudes.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for possessive grammar; Russian for pre-1867 material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** objects of the dead, clan property and repatriation cases belong to the nations concerned; ask before use. Law on objects goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
