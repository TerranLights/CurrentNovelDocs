## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Long-resident Native communities against recent arrivals; Bush villages against Anchorage and the road system; Panhandle towns; mining and oil towns that rose and fell; fishing communities with fixed seasons; military households that rotate; relocating coastal villages; Orthodox parishes; gold-rush descendants; seasonal workers; Yukon towns if carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on population and economic cycles; mixed on attitudes toward staying or going; thin on family firms.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for place and season terms; Russian for pre-1867 records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relocation, forced moves and school-driven migration are painful; use communities' own framings. Place names and camp sites are held by families and peoples; ask before publishing.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
