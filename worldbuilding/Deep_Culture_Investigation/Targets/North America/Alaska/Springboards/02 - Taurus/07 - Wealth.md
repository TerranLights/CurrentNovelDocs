# Alaska: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Peoples whose wealth was reckoned in rank, names and giving (Tlingit, Haida, Tsimshian) against peoples whose standing runs through hunting, sharing and kin (Iñupiat, Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Dene); shareholders and non-shareholders; fishing permit holders and crew; oil and mining workers; Anchorage professionals; Bush households on cash plus subsistence; military families; very recent immigrants; Yukon communities if carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on income, corporations and the dividend; mixed on private wealth; thin on attitudes toward the rich.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for feast and giving terms; Russian for fur-trade records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** potlatch protocols and house wealth belong to the peoples; use their own published accounts. Tax and property law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the dividend history and read how the fund describes itself to residents.
2. Pull ACS income, poverty and Gini tables for a few census areas, urban and Bush.
3. Read two corporation reports for how "value" and "shareholder" are used.
4. Search Alaska Native Language Center dictionaries for "rich", "poor", "give away".
5. Read the feast chapters in Kan and Burch for how peoples explain giving.
