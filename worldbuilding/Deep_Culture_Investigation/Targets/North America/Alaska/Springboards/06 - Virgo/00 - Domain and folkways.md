*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada; the exact Canadian lands are in the territory file, not read here, so Yukon and adjoining northwestern lands are assumed). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Alaska Native languages (Iñupiaq, Yup'ik and Cup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Dena languages such as Dena'ina, Koyukon and Gwich'in, Tlingit, Haida); Canadian Yukon languages (Southern and Northern Tutchone, Kaska, Han, Tagish); Russian for the Orthodox and colonial record. **Concept in one line:** what is good work and a well-kept life; how the culture files work, service, daily routine, cleanliness, craft, rest and health together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Alaska Native peoples by language family: Iñupiat, Yup'ik and Cup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangax̂, Dena peoples, Eyak, Tlingit, Haida, Tsimshian; each has its own livelihood and seasonal round.
- Roadless rural "bush" communities against the road system (Anchorage, Fairbanks, the Kenai) and the Panhandle's fishing towns.
- Oilfield and pipeline rotation workers against fishers, miners, homesteaders and military families.
- Russian Orthodox communities; later arrivals (Filipino cannery workers, Korean, Samoan, Hmong, Latin American and Pacific Islander communities, check which are large).
- Canadian side: Yukon First Nations by language; Whitehorse and the Dawson gold-rush towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on subsistence harvest and oral history; thin on routine and cleanliness; Alaska-only cells often missing in national surveys.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native-language terms need elder or dictionary support; some Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Native knowledge is restricted (ceremony, clan property, healing); oral histories carry access and consent terms; respect each people's own protocols. Boarding-school and epidemic memory is sensitive.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
