## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Rich on Elders' counsel in published texts; thin on anything measured about judgment and gullibility for Alaska.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for counsel terms; Russian for the church record.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Elders' counsel is shared on the speaker's terms; tales of tricksters and of spirits' signs may be seasonal or restricted; fraud victims and cult histories are painful, read with care; prejudice treated as "judgment" is a hazard in colonial sources.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
