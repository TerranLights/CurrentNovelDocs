# Alaska: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada; Yukon and adjoining northwestern lands assumed). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Alaska Native languages and Yukon First Nations languages. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or knowledge, practical value.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | useful, handy, "pull your weight", "make do", "a burden", "pitch in" | usefulness and its lack | check how Alaskans phrase it, for example "an Alaska fix" |
| English | Elder (capitalized in Native usage) | an honored knowledge-bearer | how the word is used by Native organizations; *check* |
| English | culture camp, fish camp, "learning by doing" | where children are taught useful skills | program names vary |

| Russian | pol'za | benefit, use | for Orthodox and colonial documents, *check* |

English keywords: self-reliance, resourcefulness, repair, reuse, bush ingenuity, hand-me-downs, caring for Elders, aging in place, Pioneer Homes, disability, retirement, "ANSEP".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Alaska Native peoples, by how each frames Elders' roles and children's training.
- Roadless villages (where each person's handiness may matter) against the road system and Anchorage.
- Homesteaders and cabin-dwellers against salaried urban residents.
- Veterans and military families against civilians.
- Pioneer-era "old timers" and their descendants against recent arrivals.
- Yukon First Nations against Whitehorse residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Little is asked outright about usefulness; look in Elders' statements, school and culture-camp materials, and obituaries.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for any role words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Elders' teachings are shared under the speaker's terms; boarding-school, relocation and forced-sterilization or similar histories bear on who was judged useful, and need care; elder suicide and burden talk need respectful handling.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read two years of the Elders and Youth Conference proceedings for statements on a person's role.
2. Search the Alaska Native Knowledge Network for "Elders" with "teach" and "role".
3. Search the Pioneer Homes and the aging commission for their stated aims.
4. Search the Alaska oral histories for "retired" and "needed".
5. Browse two regional swap groups for the commonest "wanted" posts, and describe the genre only.
