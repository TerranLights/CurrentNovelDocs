# Alaska: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada; Yukon and adjoining northwestern lands assumed). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Alaska Native languages and Yukon First Nations languages; Russian for Orthodox parish records. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, household, customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Alaska Native peoples, each with its own hosting and obligation institutions (the Tlingit and Haida clan system; Iñupiat whaling crews; Yup'ik men's house and village gatherings; Interior Dena funeral and memorial feasts).
- Military communities (Anchorage, Fairbanks, Delta Junction) against civilians.
- Tourism and lodge workers (seasonal, many from outside) against year-round residents.
- Russian Orthodox parishes (Aleutians, Kodiak, Interior) against other denominations.
- Road-system towns against roadless villages, where volunteers fill public roles.
- Yukon First Nations governments and communities against Whitehorse.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on hosting, sharing and memory in Native ethnography; thin on volunteering below the state level; hospitality and customer service almost undocumented for Alaska.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for the terms; Russian for parish material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** potlatch, clan hosting and funeral practice are ceremonial; use only what peoples publish themselves, and respect consent terms on recordings. Military-service memory touches survivors; read with care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
