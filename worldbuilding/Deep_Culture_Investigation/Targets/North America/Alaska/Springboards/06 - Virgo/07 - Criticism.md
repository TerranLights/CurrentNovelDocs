# Alaska: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada; Yukon and adjoining northwestern lands assumed). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Alaska Native languages and Yukon First Nations languages; Russian for Orthodox and colonial records. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Native peoples by their forms of correction: Elders' counsel, ceremonial speech, teasing; Athabaskan, Yup'ik, Iñupiaq and Tlingit conversational styles are not the same.
- Rural villages, where every critic is also a neighbor, against Anchorage and Fairbanks.
- Native corporation shareholders and boards; tribal councils; borough assemblies.
- Press, bloggers and call-in listeners; partisans of each major camp.
- Military and civilian workplaces (feedback cultures).
- Yukon First Nations governments against Whitehorse's territorial politics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on interethnic communication and on political commentary; thin on private or workplace criticism.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for correction and teasing words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial oratory and Elders' counsel carry protocols; critical speech inside small villages can have social cost, so identify no one in notes; some sources treat Native communication styles as deficits, to be read critically.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
