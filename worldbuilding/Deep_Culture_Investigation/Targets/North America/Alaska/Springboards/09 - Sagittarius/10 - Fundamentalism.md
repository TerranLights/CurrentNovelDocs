# Alaska: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Russian (Old Believer and Orthodox usage); Central Yup'ik, Iñupiaq, Alutiiq, Gwich'in, Tlingit and other Native languages for local terms; French for Canadian sources. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

- **Military chaplaincy** circles.

- **Yukon**: Mennonite, evangelical and Anglican communities, and First Nations revival movements.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Denominational counts are good; the line between conservative faith and "fundamentalism" is the researcher's, so use communities' own self-descriptions first.
- **Languages needed:** English; Russian for Old Believer and Orthodox texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** closed religious communities and former members are sensitive; do not use "fundamentalist" or "cult" as a label for a people. Native ceremonial knowledge, including what missionaries banned, is restricted to what communities choose to publish.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
