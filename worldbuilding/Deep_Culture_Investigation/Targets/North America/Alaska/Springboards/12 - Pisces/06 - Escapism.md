# Alaska: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Indigenous languages of its lands (Yup'ik, Iñupiaq, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Dena'ina, Gwich'in, Tlingit and others); Russian; Tagalog; Spanish; Korean; French and Inuktitut on the Canadian side. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Native villages against Anchorage and Fairbanks; "dry", "damp" and "wet" communities; military families and rotating workers; commercial fishing crews and oil-field rotations, where time away is structured; recent arrivals who came to start over, against long-term residents; tourism workers; seasonal residents who winter "Outside"; Canadian-side northern towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for substance use and overdose; thin for non-drug escape (games, reading, wilderness retreat), which sits mainly in media and memoir.
- **Languages needed:** English; Russian and Tagalog for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** substance-use data is sensitive, and stigma toward Native communities is a risk: prefer Native-authored sources. Do not quote individual clients or patients.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
