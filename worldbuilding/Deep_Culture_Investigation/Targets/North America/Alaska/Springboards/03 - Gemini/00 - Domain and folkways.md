## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Peoples by language family (Iñupiat, Yup'ik and Cup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangax, Tlingit, Haida, Tsimshian, Dene); village against regional hub against Anchorage and the road system; Southeast ferry towns against the interior; Ninilchik and other old Russian-descended communities; military families; fishing and cannery workforces; newcomer communities (Filipino, Korean, Samoan, Hmong, Hispanic); Yukon towns if carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Native languages and schooling; mixed on neighborliness and everyday talk; thin on newcomers' speech.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for key terms; Russian for church records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school and residential-school testimony belongs to survivors; cite the nations' own statements first. Some recordings and stories carry owner or clan restrictions; check archive access terms. Law and language policy go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
