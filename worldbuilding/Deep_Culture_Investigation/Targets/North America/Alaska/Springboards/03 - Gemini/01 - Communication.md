# Alaska: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, a North American target built from lands of the pre-war USA and Canada. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Iñupiaq, Central Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Tlingit, Haida, Dene languages (Koyukon, Gwich'in, Ahtna, Dena'ina); Russian. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and what people believe good communication is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Athabaskan (Dene) speakers against Iñupiat and Yup'ik speakers, since published studies already separate their styles; Tlingit and Haida oratory against Interior talk; elders against youth; village residents against Anchorage; Southeast ferry-town talk; Russian-descended communities; military and oil-industry workers; newcomers who use Spanish, Tagalog, Korean, Hmong or Samoan; Deaf residents; Yukon communities if carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Native languages and Dene-English talk; mixed for ordinary Alaskan English speech; thin for newcomer households.
- **Languages needed:** English; the Native languages for core terms; Russian for old letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some speech belongs to families or clans (songs, stories, names); use only what the nations publish or archive for public access. Silence and eye contact are community-specific questions, not generalizations. Law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Scollons' book for the questions it asks about turn-taking and silence.
2. Query the Alaska Native Language Archive for each language's words for "speak", "listen" and "message".
3. Search public radio archives for "message to the bush" programs.
4. Check ACS broadband and home-language tables for remote boroughs.
5. Read the Alaska Native Knowledge Network's cultural guidelines on communication.
