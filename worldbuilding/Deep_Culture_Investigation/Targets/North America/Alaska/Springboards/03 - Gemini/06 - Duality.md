# Alaska: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, North America, with pre-war USA and Canada lands; its borders with Canada and the Bering Strait make two-ness a place question as well as a people question. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Central Yup'ik, Siberian Yupik, Iñupiaq, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Tlingit, Haida, Dene languages; Russian. **Concept in one line:** how people live with two-ness: two identities, languages or loyalties at once, paired views of the world, and the person who shows two faces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Creole and Russian-descended families (Ninilchik, Kodiak, Sitka) against Native communities that kept the Orthodox faith in their own way and those that did not; peoples split by a national line (Gwich'in, Tlingit, Han, Tagish, Iñupiat) against those who sit wholly inside; people of mixed Native and settler ancestry; village residents who move to cities and back; Skagway-Whitehorse and Hyder-Stewart border towns; military families with a second home elsewhere; newcomers who hold two languages and two countries; Two-Spirit and other Native LGBTQ communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on dual-faith and Creole history and on Native educators' two-worlds writing; thin on border-town daily life and on mixed-heritage people's own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** English; Russian for church and company records; Native languages for terms of "outsider" and "between".
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Two-Spirit identity is for Two-Spirit people and their nations to define; do not read it as simple duality. Blood-quantum, enrollment and "who counts as Native" are painful and contested: record their own accounts. Syncretic ritual may be private. Law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
