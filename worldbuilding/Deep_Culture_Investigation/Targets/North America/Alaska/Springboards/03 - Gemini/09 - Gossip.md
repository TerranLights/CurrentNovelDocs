# Alaska: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America; the nation's lands came from the pre-war USA and Canada, and most of its places are small, close communities. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Central Yup'ik, Iñupiaq, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Tlingit, Haida, Dene languages; Russian. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people and their private lives, and the rumor that passes from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Small villages (a few hundred people) against hubs such as Bethel, Nome and Utqiagvik, and against Anchorage; peoples whose elders have published rules about speaking of others; Orthodox parishes against other congregations; cannery and fishing-season camps; military and oil-field work camps; online-only communities; newcomers new to a village; men's against women's talk, to be measured not assumed; Yukon communities if carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin. Little published on gossip itself in Alaska; the lead is ethnography, local media and public online pages.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for dictionary terms; Russian for older church papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gossip records name living people; use only aggregated or published material. Do not collect private groups or messages. Talk about missing and murdered Indigenous women and about abuse is painful; use the nations' and women's organizations' own statements first. Slander as law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Gluckman and Allport and Postman for method, then Briggs for an Arctic model.
2. Search Fienup-Riordan and Nelson for elders' statements on talking about others.
3. Search *Hometown, Alaska* archives for "rumor" and "gossip".
4. Look at blotter and letters columns in two small-town papers for a month.
5. Check each Native language dictionary for words glossed "gossip".
