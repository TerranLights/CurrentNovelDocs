# Alaska: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska (North America), assembled from pre-war USA and Canada lands, so both American and Canadian records may apply. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Central Yup'ik, Iñupiaq, Unangam Tunuu, Alutiiq, Tlingit, Dene languages; Russian. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how people gather, pass on, trust, withhold and use it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Road-system Alaskans against off-road villages; Native peoples (Yup'ik and Cup'ik, Iñupiat, Athabaskan, Tlingit and Haida, Aleut and Alutiiq), since several run their own data bodies; elders and youth; Orthodox and other church communities as news channels; fishers, hunters and oil workers; the military; newcomers who rely on messaging apps in other languages; people on the Canadian side if carried.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on Native knowledge ownership debates and on disaster alerts; thin on how ordinary households judge news; national surveys seldom split out Alaska.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for terms; Russian for colonial archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Native knowledge and stories may be restricted by the nations; read their published protocols before using any data. Individual health or tribal enrollment data is private. Politics of media trust is heated: record variation, not one voice. Law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Native bodies' published data and knowledge protocols before any collection.
2. Pull ACS internet-subscription and FCC data for every borough and census area.
3. Search *Tundra Times* and Alaska newspapers for "news", "rumor" and "radio".
4. Look up the Alaska Earthquake Center's alert history for a case of information in crisis.
5. Check Medill for Alaska news-desert counties.
