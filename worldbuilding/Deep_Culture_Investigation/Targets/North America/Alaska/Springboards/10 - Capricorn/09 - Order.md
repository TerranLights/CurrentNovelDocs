# Alaska: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Tlingit, Haida, Iñupiaq, Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Gwich'in, Koyukon, Dena'ina; Russian for the colonial and church record. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Villages that are dry, damp or wet and their different stances; road-system towns against off-road villages; Anchorage neighborhoods and their homeless and housed residents; Southeast, Iñupiat, Yup'ik and Dene peoples with their own senses of right order; Orthodox and Old Believer communities with their own ordered life; oil, fishing and tourism seasonal workers; military base communities; survivors of the 1964 earthquake and the 1989 spill; newcomers who came for the "wild" and those who came for work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public safety, alcohol policy and disaster history; mixed for ideas of cosmic and social order in Native thought; thin for everyday orderliness at home.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for terms of order and balance; Russian for church records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence, sexual assault and suicide data; missing and murdered persons; clan and ceremonial knowledge; elders' interviews with access terms.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- **Note:** statutes on public order are Libra's work; here look at the culture's ideas of order.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Nelson and Kawagley for the community's own account of how the world is ordered.
2. Search Project Jukebox for "safe", "wild", "settled down", "quiet".
3. Find the Alaska Victimization Survey summary and the local-option alcohol list.
4. Search the Alaska Native Language Center dictionaries for words for order, balance and proper way.
5. Pull records of the 1964 earthquake and boomtown policing for how order was restored.
