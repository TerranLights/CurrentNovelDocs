# Alaska: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Iñupiaq, Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Tlingit, Haida, Gwich'in, Koyukon, Dena'ina; Russian for church sources. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Native communities that stress the skilled provider (Iñupiat, Yup'ik, Dene, Alutiiq, Unangax); Southeast clan societies, where names and rights are inherited and then validated in public; Native corporation shareholders, descendants and nonshareholders; Permanent Fund dividend recipients across income levels; urban and rural job seekers; military and federal households; fishing and oil workers; immigrant groups; old-timers against newcomers who claim standing by length of residence.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Permanent Fund debate, scholarship programs and hunting skill; mixed for hiring practices and for how ordinary Alaskans explain others' success; thin for non-Native views on preference in villages.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for terms about skill and provider.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hiring-preference disputes are sensitive and often litigated; clan names and rights belong to their holders; private shareholder matters.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- **Note:** preference law (as written) belongs to Libra; here look at what the culture thinks desert is.
