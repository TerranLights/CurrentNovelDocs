# Alaska: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Iñupiaq, Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Tlingit, Haida, Gwich'in, Koyukon, Dena'ina; Russian for parish sources. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Peoples whose teachings frame duty through kin, clan or harvest sharing (Tlingit and Haida, Yup'ik and Cup'ig, Iñupiat, Dene peoples), set side by side rather than merged; Alutiiq and Unangax communities with Orthodox godparent ties; veterans and military families; fishing and oil households; urban Anchorage residents and rural villagers, since distance changes what can be done for kin; caregivers of aging parents; recent immigrant communities sending money abroad; newcomers who did not grow up with local obligations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for harvest sharing and Native teachings; mixed for formal civic duty by Alaskans specifically; thin for family-duty surveys at the state level.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for key terms; Russian for parish sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan and ceremonial obligations are the owners' to tell; elders' interviews may carry restrictions; wartime and boarding-school memories; harvest locations should not be published.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- **Note:** law-related duties (the Crown's, the federal trust, the state's duties to tribes) are handled in Libra, Capricorn Law and Order, not here.
