# Alaska: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Yup'ik, Iñupiaq, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Tlingit, Haida, Gwich'in, Koyukon, Dena'ina; Russian for church sources. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Alutiiq and Native communities of Prince William Sound, who lived through the 1989 spill; Southeast clan peoples, where answerability is tied to house and clan; Yup'ik, Iñupiat and Dene villages, where answerability to kin and harvest groups is local; Anchorage and Fairbanks residents; oil, fishing, mining and tourism workers; state and federal employees; Native corporation shareholders and non-shareholders; recent immigrants; veterans.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for spill litigation, Native stewardship statements and corporate accountability; mixed for family-level responsibility; thin for how blame is assigned within households.
- **Languages needed:** English; Native languages for core terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school and child-welfare cases; suicide and abuse; named individuals in lawsuits; elders' interviews with access terms.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- **Note:** liability and tort law belong to Libra's letter-of-the-law work; here look at the spirit (what answerability means), not the statutes.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Trustee Council's public history of the spill and the arguments over blame.
2. Search Alaska Native corporation reports for "stewardship" and "responsibility".
3. Search Project Jukebox and the Native Knowledge Network for "responsible" and "taking care".
4. Search regional news for resignations and ethics cases involving state officials.
5. Search the language centers' dictionaries for words for answerable, caretaker, look after.
