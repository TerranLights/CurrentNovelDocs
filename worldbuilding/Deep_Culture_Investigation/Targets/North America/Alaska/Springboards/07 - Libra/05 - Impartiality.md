## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Small off-road villages, where officials, judges and parties are kin or neighbors, against Anchorage, Fairbanks and Juneau.
- Native nations where hereditary or clan standing shapes office, against those with elected councils only; ask each nation for its own account.
- Tribal courts and state magistrates serving the same people.
- Corporate shareholders and nonshareholders in hiring and benefits.
- Military installations with their own rule systems.
- Russian-descended parishes with church-appointed arbiters; newer immigrant communities with their own networks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, ethics decisions and corruption cases; mixed for daily favoritism, which is rarely recorded; thin for ethnic networks.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous dictionaries; Russian for church sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disciplinary files and tribal proceedings may be closed; named living officials appear in news cases.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
