# Alaska: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Indigenous languages by dictionary (Iñupiaq, Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Dene languages, Tlingit, Haida, Tsimshian); Russian; Spanish; Tagalog. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even at the price of one's own or another's rights, truth or justice. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Small villages where reporting a neighbor or kin carries heavy social cost, against cities with anonymous services.
- Native nations with councils or elders who mediate, against those whose disputes go to state courts; each nation's account is its own.
- Survivors of boarding schools and of church-run institutions, and the communities around them.
- Quaker and other peace-church communities; Russian Orthodox parishes.
- Rural residents far from police, who may avoid conflict because help is hours away.
- Immigrant and military families, whose silence may be shaped by status or rank.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for victimization surveys, statutes and boarding-school records; mixed for everyday avoidance; thin for private family silence.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous dictionaries; Russian for church sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse and disappearance cases involve living victims and families; boarding-school testimony is survivors' own; some ethnographies of "restraint" risk stereotyping, so read them critically and compare nations separately.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull the Alaska Victimization Survey and ANDVSA reporting data.
2. Read the Judicial Council's account of the plea-bargaining ban and Alaska's statutes on mediation and nondisclosure.
3. Search the Federal Indian Boarding School Initiative reports for Alaska schools.
4. Search the Language Center and Native Knowledge Network for published accounts of mediation and elders' counsel.
5. Search Alaska news archives for investigations of long-kept silences.
