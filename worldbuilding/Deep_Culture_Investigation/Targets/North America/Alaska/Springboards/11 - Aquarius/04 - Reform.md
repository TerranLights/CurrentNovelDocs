# Alaska: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Tlingit, Haida, Iñupiaq, Yup'ik, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Dene languages; Russian. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
The Native rights generation (Brotherhood, Sisterhood, land claims) against later regional and village leaders; Native communities with sobriety and wellness movements against those without; church traditions (Orthodox, Presbyterian, Episcopal, Moravian, Catholic, Old Believer); fishing communities in the limited-entry and Bristol Bay debates; North Slope Iñupiat communities (development and its limits) against Bristol Bay and Gulf communities; coastal villages seeking relocation from erosion; Anchorage and Juneau progressives against Mat-Su and Interior libertarians; capital-move supporters and opponents; and people who describe reforms as done to them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on land claims, statehood, resource politics and language revitalization; mixed on sobriety and healing movements; thin on everyday attitudes toward reform as such.
- **Languages needed:** English; Russian for Old Believer and Orthodox sources; Native-language terms for renewal.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school and missionary harms; addiction and recovery stories are personal; testimony in village inquiries may carry access conditions.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
