# Alaska: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Iñupiaq, Yup'ik and Cup'ig, Alutiiq, Unangam Tunuu, Tlingit, Haida, Dene languages; Russian (church Slavonic for Orthodox services); Tagalog; Spanish. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |

English keywords: hope, optimism, despair, future, survival, relocation, climate, salmon run, land, youth, resilience, boom and bust.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Native peoples with differing hopes for language, land and youth (Iñupiat, Yup'ik, Dene, Tlingit, Haida, Alutiiq, Unangax); Orthodox villages, especially those that keep the starring custom; coastal villages facing erosion; commercial fishing families, season to season; oil-dependent households and the boom-and-bust cycle; seasonal cannery workers and immigrants seeking a better wage; gold-era and homestead settler descendants; elders in care and hospice; urban Native youth; searching families of missing relatives; and any exile or newcomer cohort the scope names.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on ritual hope (Orthodox, Native, fish runs) and on climate relocation; mixed on measured outlook; thin on Alaska-specific hope scales.
- **Languages needed:** English; Russian and Church Slavonic for services; Native-language terms with Native guidance; Tagalog.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and despair data; searches for missing and murdered relatives; accounts of loss and relocation; some church rites and Native ceremonies are not for outsiders' recording.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
