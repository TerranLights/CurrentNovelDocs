# Alaska: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Alaska, region North America (pre-war lands carried: USA, Canada). **Language(s) for searching:** English; Russian; Yup'ik, Iñupiaq, Alutiiq, Tlingit, Dena'ina, Gwich'in and other Native languages; Tagalog; Spanish. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Village households against Anchorage, Fairbanks and Juneau households: different meanings of "home" are likely to sit here.
- Native peoples whose home is a seasonal round (camp, village, fish camp) against settled-town residents.
- Southeast clan houses (Tlingit, Haida, Tsimshian) against interior Athabaskan and Arctic Iñupiat households.
- Old Believer villages and Russian Orthodox communities.
- Homesteaders, cabin dwellers and "off the grid" households.
- Military families in transit; seasonal and cannery workers; recent immigrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on housing counts and conditions; mixed on what home means; the meaning is mostly in oral and published elders' sources.
- **Languages needed:** English; the Native languages for home and land words; Russian for older records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** place names and family land are tied to holders; use what the nations publish. Housing-condition data can stigmatize; read it beside the people's own accounts.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the ANLC dictionaries for the words for house, home and land in Yup'ik, Iñupiaq and Tlingit.
2. Pull ACS and Alaska housing assessment tables on tenure, rooms and fuel by region.
3. Search Project Jukebox for "my house", "where I grew up" and "cabin".
4. Look in the Alaska Native Knowledge Network for place-based teaching material.
5. Search Alaska Public Media for "housing" call-in shows and read the callers' words.
