# Australia: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian), Aboriginal English, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, main migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples:** frontier-era defenders of Country, desert communities, southeastern families under the protection era; ranger groups in the north.
- **Torres Strait Islanders:** island guardianship of sea and kin.
- **Rural and remote:** station and bush families, volunteer fire communities, against suburbs.
- **Migrant communities:** Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese, Pacific: protective customs for children.
- **Coastal and inland:** surf culture, cyclone and flood regions, bushfire zones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emergency services, surveys and inquiries; mixed for family and Indigenous practice; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal languages for guardian terms; Greek, Italian, Arabic and Chinese for amulet and evil-eye sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** frontier massacre records and sites; names and images of the dead; domestic violence and child abuse testimony; sacred and gender-restricted knowledge. Firearm and child-protection law are held for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
