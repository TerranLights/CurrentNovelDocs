# Australia: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian), Aboriginal English, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, main migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples:** belonging to Country (Yolŋu, Anangu, Noongar, Warlpiri), against city-raised Koori and Murri people reconnecting through Link-Up.
- **Torres Strait Islanders** in the islands and on the mainland.
- **Migrant waves:** postwar Greek and Italian, later Vietnamese, Lebanese, Chinese, Pacific and Māori; refugees against skilled arrivals.
- **Place:** capital cities against country towns, and "blow-ins" against old families.
- **Older settler stock** and Anzac-era memory bearers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and media; mixed for Indigenous belonging, where much is held in communities; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal languages for "people" and "outsider" terms; Greek, Chinese, Vietnamese and Arabic for diaspora voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs appear in sources, so quote none beyond the term itself; removed-child records; community protocols on who may speak for Country.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
