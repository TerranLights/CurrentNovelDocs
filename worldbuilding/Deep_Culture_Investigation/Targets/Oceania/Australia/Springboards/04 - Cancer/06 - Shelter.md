# Australia: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian), Aboriginal English, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, main migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples:** town-camp communities (Arrernte and neighboring peoples), Darwin's long-grass sleepers (Larrakia country), southeastern urban families, peoples with historic shelter traditions (Gunditjmara).
- **Torres Strait Islanders:** island and mainland housing.
- **Refugees and asylum seekers:** by arrival era, from Vietnamese boat arrivals to later groups, and by place of detention or settlement.
- **Postwar migrants:** hostel generation against later arrivals.
- **Place:** capital-city homelessness, caravan-park towns, fire and flood zones, remote communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for counts, charities and media; mixed for informal shelter and for Indigenous camps; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal languages for shelter terms; Vietnamese, Arabic and Chinese for refugee voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** detention accounts, domestic violence refuge locations (kept confidential), names and images of the dead, people's addresses. Tenancy and refugee law are held for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
