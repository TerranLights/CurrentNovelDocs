# Australia: Domain and folkways, home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian), Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages and Kriol, plus the main migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** what is home, and who is family? Household forms, fathering, roots, family memory, hospitality, the home's walls, the family table, house types, kin ordering and child-rearing.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples by region:** Yolŋu (Arnhem Land), Anangu (central desert), Warlpiri, Arrernte, Noongar (southwest), Koori (NSW and Victoria), Murri (Queensland), Nunga (South Australia), palawa (Tasmania): different kin systems and land ties.
- **Torres Strait Islanders:** island households with their own adoption and feasting customs.
- **Migrant families:** Anglo-Celtic, southern European, Chinese, Vietnamese, Lebanese, Pacific Islander and Māori households differ in who lives together.
- **Place of living:** capital-city suburbs, regional towns, farms and stations, remote communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census, housing and media; mixed for Indigenous families, where the best material is in community and AIATSIS holdings; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled (header records no change).
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal languages and Kriol for kin terms; migrant languages for communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names of the deceased in Aboriginal communities, gender-restricted and sacred knowledge, kin charts held by communities; follow access rules.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
