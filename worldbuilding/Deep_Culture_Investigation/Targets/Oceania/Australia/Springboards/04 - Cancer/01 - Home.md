# Australia: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian); Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages and Kriol; main migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples:** Yolŋu homelands (Arnhem Land), Anangu, Noongar, Koori in southeastern cities, palawa: home as Country and as a city address differ.
- **Torres Strait Islanders:** island homes against mainland households.
- **Migrant groups:** Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Lebanese, Turkish, Pacific and Māori communities, first and second generation.
- **Tenure and place:** owners, renters, public-housing tenants; city suburbs, small towns, stations and remote communities; coast shacks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing, media and heritage; mixed for Indigenous homes, where meaning sits in community-held records; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for place terms; migrant languages for homeland vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deceased persons' names and images in Indigenous communities; sacred sites; community oral histories with access conditions.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

1. ABS census: dwelling structure by state and by Indigenous status.
