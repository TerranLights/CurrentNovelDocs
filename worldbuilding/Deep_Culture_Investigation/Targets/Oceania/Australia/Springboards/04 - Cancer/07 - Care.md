# Australia: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian), Aboriginal English, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, main migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who cannot meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples:** desert (Anangu, Warlpiri), Arnhem Land (Yolŋu), southeastern urban families, kinship carers; each with its own healing and mourning custom.
- **Torres Strait Islanders:** island elder care and child-giving.
- **Migrant communities:** Chinese, Greek, Italian, Vietnamese, Lebanese, Pacific and Māori; first generation against daughters and sons who are carers.
- **Rural and remote:** bush nursing and Flying Doctor communities against capital-city services.
- **Care workers:** migrant aged-care workers, nurses, nannies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, inquiries and media; mixed for Indigenous and migrant family care, which sits in community sources; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal languages for healer and mourning terms; Chinese, Greek, Italian, Vietnamese and Arabic for aged-care sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' knowledge and bush-medicine details; names and images of the dead; testimony on abuse in care; do not publish community-restricted knowledge.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
