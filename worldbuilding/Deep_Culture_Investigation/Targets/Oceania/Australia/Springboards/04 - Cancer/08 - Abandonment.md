# Australia: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian), Aboriginal English, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, main migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal families:** removal histories differ by state and peoples (southeast, Western Australia, Northern Territory); communities with Link-Up and healing programs.
- **Torres Strait Islanders:** island custom against mainland removal.
- **Child migrants and Forgotten Australians:** British and Maltese child migrants, and those placed in institutions.
- **Forced-adoption mothers and adoptees.**
- **Migrant families split by distance:** Ten Pound Poms, Chinese, Greek, Italian, Vietnamese, Pacific seasonal workers.
- **Rural elders and emptied towns.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in official inquiries and testimony; mixed for desertion within ordinary families; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal languages for removal terms; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese and Arabic for migration accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** testimony of removal, institutional abuse and forced adoption; closed adoption and removal records; names and images of the dead. Child-welfare and adoption law is held for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. NLA catalog: "Bringing Them Home Oral History Project", then read three transcripts' summaries.
2. Senate reports "Lost Innocents" and "Forgotten Australians": contents and recommendations lists.
3. Trove: "Female Orphan School".
4. Library search: Pierce, *The Country of Lost Children*; Hammerton and Thomson.
5. AIFS: survey summaries on parents after separation.
