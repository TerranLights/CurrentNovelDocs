# Australia: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian), Aboriginal English, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, main migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples:** Yolŋu, Arrernte, Pintupi and other desert peoples, Noongar, Koori and Murri families in cities; children raised by kin outside the parents' home.
- **Torres Strait Islanders:** child-giving between families.
- **Migrant communities:** Chinese, Greek, Italian, Vietnamese, Arabic-speaking, Samoan, Tongan and Māori families, by generation.
- **Setting:** inner-city, suburb, rural and remote; stations and small towns, where distance shapes schooling.
- **Parents' background:** newcomers against older settler families; refugees.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on public advice, early-childhood data and media; mixed on Indigenous and migrant practice, which is better held in community sources; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal languages for nurture terms; Chinese, Greek, Italian, Vietnamese and Arabic for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children's records and images; removed-child histories; women's knowledge about birth and infant care held in some communities.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

1. AEDC site: the domain definitions, then community data for one remote and one city community.
