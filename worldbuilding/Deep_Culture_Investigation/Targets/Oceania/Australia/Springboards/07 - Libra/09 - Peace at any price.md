# Australia: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian usage); Aboriginal English and Aboriginal languages (Dyirbal); Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Arabic, Italian. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even at the price of one's own or another's rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples with avoidance customs and with makarrata-type rites (Yolŋu, Dyirbal, desert peoples); Torres Strait Islanders.
- Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Arab and Italian families; women seeking help in each community.
- Defense, police and public-service workplaces against private firms and trades.
- Church and parish communities (Catholic, Anglican, Orthodox, Pentecostal); Quakers and other peace churches.
- Rural towns where everyone knows everyone against large cities.
- Young adults against the old on speaking out.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries, statutes and statistics; mixed for family and workplace silence; thin for how any group describes giving way, in its own words.
- **Languages needed:** English; Dyirbal and other Aboriginal language terms; Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Arabic and Italian for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors' accounts of abuse and violence; live whistleblower cases; kin avoidance rules held within peoples. Do not assume any group's silence means consent; the sheet gives no verdict. Follow the AIATSIS code (*check*).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
