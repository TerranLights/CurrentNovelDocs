# Australia: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian usage); Aboriginal languages and Aboriginal English; Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Italian, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples (Yolŋu, Warlpiri, Noongar and others), who name outsiders in their own terms; Torres Strait Islanders.
- Targeted groups: Chinese (past), Arab and Lebanese Australians, Sudanese Australians, Muslim communities, asylum seekers held offshore.
- Western Sydney and the North Shore; coast and inland; Queensland and the southern states; Perth and the east.
- Anglo-Celtic, Greek, Italian, Vietnamese communities, with their own evil-eye and envy beliefs.
- The parties and their supporters, each accusing the other.
- Fire-prone towns after Black Summer; unionists and employers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries, statutes and media; mixed for personal and family blame; thin for migrant and Aboriginal pictures of others in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** English; Yolŋu Matha, Warlpiri, Noongar terms; Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Italian, Vietnamese for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs recorded for study only, live cases, and Aboriginal accounts of sorcery or harm (held within peoples); follow the AIATSIS code (*check*). No group is named as a projector or a target without a source.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
