# Australia: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian usage); Yolŋu Matha, Noongar; Arabic, Chinese, Greek, Italian, Samoan, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Integrity regimes differ by state: New South Wales, Queensland, Victoria, Western Australia, South Australia, Tasmania.
- Aboriginal communities and organizations where kin ties shape office, against bodies that bar such ties; Torres Strait Islanders.
- Samoan, Tongan, Arab, Chinese, Greek, Italian and Vietnamese communities with strong connection customs.
- Small country towns against big cities; the public service against private firms and unions.
- Old-school and club networks (private schools, clubs) against merit-hiring workplaces (*check* how to measure).
- Fly-in mining camps against suburbs.
