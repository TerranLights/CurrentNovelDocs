# Australia: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian usage); Aboriginal languages (Woiwurrung, Noongar, Pitjantjatjara); Pacific, Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Italian, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between states and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal nations with different protocols: Kulin (Victoria), Noongar (southwest), Anangu (desert), Yolŋu (Arnhem Land), Eora (Sydney).
- Torres Strait Islanders; Pacific Islander communities (Samoan, Tongan, Fijian) with *talanoa* and *fa'aaloalo*.
- Chinese, Vietnamese, Arab, Greek and Italian communities with their own rules of face and courtesy.
- Union and employer sides; the public service and the foreign service; parliament and the press.
- City and rural talk styles (*check* how to measure); recent migrants against the long settled.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for foreign policy, treaties and labor; mixed for everyday tact; thin for migrant and Aboriginal diplomacy in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** English; Woiwurrung, Noongar, Pitjantjatjara; Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Italian, Tongan, Samoan, Vietnamese for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protocols tied to ceremony and named elders; live treaty talks. Use what peoples publish; follow the AIATSIS code (*check*). No stereotype: test "no worries" and "mickey-taking" per group.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
