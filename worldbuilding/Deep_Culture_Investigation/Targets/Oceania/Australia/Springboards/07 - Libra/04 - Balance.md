# Australia: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Yolŋu Matha, Pitjantjatjara and other Aboriginal languages; Chinese, Greek, Arabic, Vietnamese, Italian. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others, and the steadiness that follows, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Ngarrindjeri, Barkandji and other river peoples; Gunditjmara; Yolŋu; Anangu: each with its own idea of balance with Country.
- Irrigators, upstream and downstream states, towns and environment groups on the Murray-Darling.
- Fly-in workers and their families; shift and care workers; office workers; farm households.
- Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian and Arab communities: other systems of bodily and moral balance.
- Buddhist, Muslim and Christian practitioners; the young and the old on rest and work.
- Tasmania and the Top End against the southeastern cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on work time, water, fire and public records; mixed on health and religion; thin on migrant balance ideas.
- **Languages needed:** English; Yolŋu Matha, Pitjantjatjara; Chinese, Greek, Arabic and Vietnamese for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' and ceremonial knowledge is restricted; use only what peoples and healers publish, with the AIATSIS code (*check*). Dark Emu is contested: record both sides.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
