# Australia: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian usage); Aboriginal English, Wiradjuri and other Aboriginal languages; Torres Strait; Samoan, Tongan; Chinese, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples of the desert, Top End, southeast and cities; Torres Strait Islanders.
- Samoan, Tongan and Fijian households and churches; Chinese, Vietnamese, Arab, Greek and Italian communities.
- Bush and farm towns with bush-fire brigades against city apartments.
- Welfare recipients and employers under mutual-obligation rules.
- Clubs: RSL, CWA, surf clubs, football clubs; unions and friendly societies.
- Remote and city Aboriginal organizations that carry family claims on money.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for volunteering, surveys, welfare policy and disaster records; mixed for migrant obligation; thin for Aboriginal and Island reciprocity in people's own words.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English, Wiradjuri and Torres Strait terms; Samoan, Tongan, Chinese, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mourning duties, names of the dead and ceremonial exchange are restricted; "humbug" is easily turned into a stereotype, so test it per group and follow the AIATSIS code (*check*).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
