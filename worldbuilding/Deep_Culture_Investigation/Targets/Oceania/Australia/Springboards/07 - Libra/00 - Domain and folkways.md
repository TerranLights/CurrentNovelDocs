## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal nations apart: Yolŋu (Arnhem Land), desert peoples (Warlpiri, Pitjantjatjara, Arrernte), Noongar (southwest), Kulin and Gunditjmara (Victoria), Wiradjuri and Gamilaraay (New South Wales), palawa (Tasmania): separate laws of marriage and dispute.
- Torres Strait Islanders (Meriam, Kalaw Lagaw Ya speakers): their own custom, apart from the mainland.
- Sistergirls and Brotherboys: gender ways named by Aboriginal people themselves.
- Settler streams: Anglo-Celtic, Irish Catholic, Protestant; convict-origin colonies against free-settled ones (South Australia, Victoria).
- Migrant communities: Greek, Italian, Lebanese and Arab, Chinese, Vietnamese, Pacific (Samoan, Tongan, Maori): different bonds between unrelated people.
- Outback and cities; mining towns; the Christian and Muslim, Jewish and Buddhist communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, statutes and cases; mixed for migrant communities; thin and uneven for Aboriginal law as the peoples hold it.
- **Languages needed:** English first; Yolŋu Matha, Pitjantjatjara, Warlpiri and Kriol for terms; Chinese, Greek and Arabic for migrant sources. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet and the others follow the core lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** women's and men's business, sacred law and names or images of the dead are restricted; follow the AIATSIS code of ethics (*check*) and use what peoples publish.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. ABS "Marriages and Divorces, Australia", latest tables by state, and the 2017 postal survey results by electorate.

4. Yoorrook Justice Commission and First Peoples' Assembly of Victoria: published reports and treaty documents.
