# Australia: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Yolŋu Matha, Arrernte, Warlpiri, Kriol; Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Arabic, Italian. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as more or less equals: in love, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples with moiety, section or subsection systems (Yolŋu, Warlpiri, Arrernte) against those without them; Torres Strait Islanders.
- Bass Strait Islander and Broome communities from mixed unions.
- Settler streams: Anglo-Celtic, Irish Catholic, Lutheran German (South Australia).
- Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian and Arab communities: credit clubs, family firms, godparent ties.
- Cities, farm towns and the outback; same-sex couples after 2017; unmarried against married couples.
- Treaty and joint-management partners in the Northern Territory, Victoria and South Australia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, statutes, treaties and business law; mixed for couples' own accounts; thin for migrant credit clubs and Aboriginal partnership in peoples' own words.
- **Languages needed:** English; Yolŋu Matha, Arrernte, Warlpiri and Kriol for terms; Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Arabic for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** marriage and moiety knowledge tied to ceremony, and Dreaming narratives, are restricted in part; use only what peoples publish.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
