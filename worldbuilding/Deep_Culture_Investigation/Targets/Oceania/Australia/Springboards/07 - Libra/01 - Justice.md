# Australia: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages and Kriol; Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Italian, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are ordered; the letter of the law is the legal side here.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Yolŋu, Warlpiri, Pitjantjatjara and Noongar: living justice practices apart from the state's.
- Bwgcolman (Palm Island) and Torres Strait Islanders: separate histories with police and courts, and Meriam land justice.
- Stolen Generations survivors and families: redress as lived.
- Penal-origin colonies (New South Wales, Tasmania, Western Australia, Queensland's Moreton Bay) against free-settled South Australia.
- Irish Catholic and Anglican settler lines; Chinese, Italian, Arab and Vietnamese communities, with different histories of the law.
- Remote Northern Territory and the cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, cases, inquiries and statistics; thin for Aboriginal justice as peoples hold it; mixed for migrant views.
- **Languages needed:** English; Yolŋu Matha, Warlpiri, Pitjantjatjara and Kriol for terms; Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Italian for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names and images of the dead, sacred law, massacre sites and live cases; take only what peoples publish and follow the AIATSIS code (*check*).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
