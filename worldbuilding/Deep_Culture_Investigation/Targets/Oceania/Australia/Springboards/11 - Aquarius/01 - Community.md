# Australia: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian); Aboriginal languages and Kriol; Torres Strait languages; migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (place, faith, trade, origin, interest, cause), who recognize one another as members and owe one another something, and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Remote Aboriginal settlements and homeland outstations (Warlpiri, Arrernte, Yolŋu, Aṉangu) against urban Aboriginal communities (Redfern, Fitzroy, Inala) and Palawa in Tasmania; Torres Strait Islanders on the islands and the mainland; country towns against capital-city suburbs; fire and flood-prone regions against other places; long-settled Anglo-Celtic suburbs against Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Vietnamese, Chinese, Filipino and Pacific neighborhoods; Norfolk Island as a small separate case.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, disaster inquiries and suburb studies; mixed for remote Aboriginal communities (policy-driven sources outweigh insiders' own); thin for the smallest island communities.
- **Languages needed:** English; Warlpiri, Arrernte, Kriol and others for remote materials; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese and Chinese for diaspora press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Stolen Generations testimony; deceased persons' names and images; community-held records.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
