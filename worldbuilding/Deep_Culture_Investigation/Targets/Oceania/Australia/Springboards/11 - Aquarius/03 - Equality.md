# Australia: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian); Aboriginal English, Kriol and Aboriginal languages; migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples (remote Central Desert, Arnhem Land, urban east-coast Koori and Murri communities, Palawa) and Torres Strait Islanders, whose stated equalities differ from the state's; South Sea Islander communities in Queensland; Chinese and later Asian Australians; European post-war migrants and Lebanese-born people; women and men (rural and urban); city and country, west Sydney against the north shore, Perth against the rest; Tasmania; the long-settled working-class suburbs against new fringe estates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pay, income and attitudes; mixed for Aboriginal equality (policy data outweighs Aboriginal voices); thin for small island groups.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and Kriol terms; community-language press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** women's and men's restricted Aboriginal knowledge; deceased persons' names and images; slurs recorded in sources (describe, do not repeat); racism inquiry material.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
