# Australia: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian); Aboriginal English, Kriol and Aboriginal languages; migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Men and women, and generations; Aboriginal peoples whose kin systems make "friend" a different thing (Yolŋu, Warlpiri, Aṉangu, Koori and Murri communities); Torres Strait Islanders; city and country (men's sheds, pubs, clubs); Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese and Filipino communities with ritual kin or hometown ties; Pacific communities (church and village ties); newcomers; people in remote mining and cattle regions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys on loneliness and social ties, and for popular culture; mixed for Aboriginal friendship (kin systems differ from the term "friend"); thin for ritual-kin practices after the first generation.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and community languages; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese for diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deceased persons' names and images; some men's and women's knowledge in Aboriginal settings.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
