# Australia: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian); Aboriginal English and Tiwi and other Aboriginal languages; migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature, and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Capital cities (Fitzroy, Newtown, Fortitude Valley) against country towns; Nimbin and Byron Bay, Tasmania and Lightning Ridge as refuges; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander gender-diverse communities (sistergirls, brotherboys) and their regions; religious dissenters (Quakers, Exclusive Brethren, new movements) and the "No religion" group; Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Vietnamese and Chinese communities where young people balance community and the wider society; punk and music scenes; conscientious objectors in past wars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for subcultures, arts and LGBTIQ research; mixed for religious dissent and Aboriginal gender-diverse communities; thin for older eccentrics outside cities.
- **Languages needed:** English; Tiwi and other Aboriginal languages; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese for diaspora voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** outing and identity of living persons; cult and abuse cases; slurs recorded in sources; Aboriginal community protocols on gender and knowledge.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
