# Australia: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian); Warlpiri, Yolŋu Matha and other Aboriginal languages; migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples' own ideals of right living by region (Warlpiri, Yolŋu, Aṉangu, Noongar, Wiradjuri) against outsiders' images; Torres Strait Islanders; the northern New South Wales rainforest communities and Tasmania (counterculture); inland missionary and bush-service communities; Catholic and Anglican religious communities; Chinese, Greek, Italian, Lebanese and Vietnamese diaspora ideals (homeland politics and faith); youth against older cohorts; city and regional Australia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for utopian colonies, youth surveys and volunteers; mixed for Aboriginal ideals (community-authored materials, but also outsiders' romantic images); thin for migrant idealism.
- **Languages needed:** English; Warlpiri and Yolŋu Matha for community texts; Chinese for Datong sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** colony and mission records involving Aboriginal peoples; cult material (Sydney and Melbourne groups); deceased persons' names and images.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
