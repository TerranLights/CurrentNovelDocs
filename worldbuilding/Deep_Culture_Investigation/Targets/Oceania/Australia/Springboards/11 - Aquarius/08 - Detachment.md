# Australia: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian); Aboriginal English, Ngan'gikurunggurr, Pitjantjatjara and other Aboriginal languages; migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; which forms are named as admired (calm, objectivity, equanimity) and which as faulted (coldness, indifference), to be checked.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Remote and outback communities against capital cities; Aboriginal peoples with different communicative styles (Central Desert, Arnhem Land, Torres Strait, urban Aboriginal); men and women, and generations; farm and mining workers; Buddhist, Christian and secular groups; migrants who moved from warmer or cooler social contexts; first responders, medical and journalism professionals who train for distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mental health, loneliness and politics; mixed for cultural reserve (outsider claims are common and should be read as claims); thin for Aboriginal communicative norms outside linguistics.
- **Languages needed:** English; Ngan'gikurunggurr, Pitjantjatjara and Aboriginal English for community writing; Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic and Greek for religious and classical sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data and grief; avoidance customs and kin restrictions; deceased persons' names and images.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
