# Australia: Domain and folkways, community and ideals (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian), Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages and Kriol, plus migrant languages (Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese, Filipino languages, Pacific languages). **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups, and what they hope for (friends, groups, hopes, humanitarian ideals), with the customs of settlement, association and hospitality.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples by region (Yolŋu, Aṉangu, Noongar, Wiradjuri, Murri, Koori, Palawa), because kin systems and gatherings differ by country; Torres Strait Islanders (Ailan Kastom); Anglo-Celtic settler towns against cities; post-war European (Greek, Italian) and later Lebanese, Vietnamese, Chinese, Filipino and Pacific communities, whose associations are well documented; Norfolk Island and the Cocos and Christmas Island communities as small distinct cases; remote, regional and capital-city settings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for volunteering, surveys and associations; mixed for Aboriginal community life (much is restricted or held by communities); thin for the smallest island communities.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal and Torres Strait languages and Kriol; the migrant languages above for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aboriginal restricted knowledge, initiation and sorry-business material; follow the AIATSIS ethics code; some names or images of the deceased should not be reproduced; refugee-policy material is contested. The nation's exact lands and units are still to be settled at Stage 0; this and the other Aquarius sheets are written for the core lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
