# Australia: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian); Kriol, Pitjantjatjara and other Aboriginal languages; migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples by region (remote Arnhem Land, Central Desert, Kimberley, urban east coast, Tasmania), whose hopes concern Country, children and recognition; refugee and recent migrant groups (Vietnamese, Lebanese, Sudanese and Syrian arrivals) against the earlier British, Irish, Greek and Italian waves; Pacific labor migrants; farmers in drought and flood regions; young people and older people; Pentecostal, Catholic, Orthodox, Muslim, Buddhist and secular communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, official data and disaster follow-up; mixed for Aboriginal hope (policy indicators more than community voices); thin for non-English religious hope.
- **Languages needed:** English; Kriol, Pitjantjatjara and other Aboriginal languages; Arabic, Greek, Chinese, Vietnamese, Italian for diaspora sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and despair data; Stolen Generations testimony; deceased persons' names and images; refugee cases under review; gambling harm.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
