# Australia: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, Samoan and Tongan, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, household, customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples** (Anangu, Yolngu, Wiradjuri, Noongar and others): reciprocity and kin duty against wage service.
- **Torres Strait Islanders** and **Pacific communities** (Samoan, Tongan): service to family, chief and church.
- **Bush and coast**: volunteer fire and surf clubs against the city.
- **Veterans and public servants** (Canberra) against hospitality, retail and domestic workers.
- **Migrant communities** (Italian, Greek, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese) and church congregations, each with its own welfare society.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for volunteering, honors and military memory; thinner and community-held for Indigenous obligation. No scope file exists; post-war lands are unrecorded, so the core mainland and Tasmania are assumed.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous and Pacific terms with community guidance; migrant languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Stolen Generations material and unpaid or withheld work are painful and carry protocols; deceased persons' names and images; cultural terms should not be extracted without community leave. Pre-war sources need a post-war check. No law research here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull the ABS volunteering counts and the Giving Australia results, by state.

4. Search Haskins and Saunders for domestic service, then AIATSIS for Indigenous terms of obligation.
