# Australia: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal makers** (desert weavers, Ngarrindjeri, Tiwi, Torres Strait artists): standards set by community and ancestors; ask makers rather than assume.
- **Students**: selective and private schools against public, and migrant families (Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek and others), where pressure varies by family; do not assume.
- **Trades**: tradies, builders, chefs, surgeons and pilots, against "near enough" attitudes.
- **Gender and age**: girls and women under appearance standards; young adults against older.
- **City and bush**: Melbourne and Sydney against rural and mining towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mental-health and youth surveys, sport and building quality; mixed for private attitudes. No scope file exists; post-war lands are unrecorded, so the core mainland and Tasmania are assumed.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal terms with community help; Greek, Arabic and Chinese for family-pressure studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eating disorder and suicide material needs care; ceremonial design standards are community-held; names of deceased makers carry protocols. Pre-war sources need a post-war check.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
