# Australia: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples** (Noongar, Kaurna, Koori, Murri, Yolngu, Anangu): "improvement" imposed through protection and assimilation against self-led renewal.
- **Torres Strait Islanders:** island-led development, church and custom.
- **Migrant waves** (British, Southern European, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese): hopes for the children; check each group, do not assume.
- **Farmers and irrigators** against **urban renovators**; **reformers** (temperance, Landcare) against those they reform.
- **Class and state:** home-owning suburbs against remote communities and renters.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for policy, education and housing; thin for private self-improvement habits. No scope file exists; post-war lands are unrecorded, so the core mainland and Tasmania are assumed.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal terms with community guidance; migrant languages for aspiration studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** removal records and mission files are controlled and painful; names of the deceased carry protocols; the legal side of land "improvement" belongs to Libra and Capricorn. Pre-war sources need a post-war check.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
