# Australia: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Kriol, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, main migrant languages. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples**, desert, Top End, Noongar, Koori and Murri: land work, station work, ranger work, welfare-linked work.
- **Torres Strait Islanders** and **Australian South Sea Islander** descendants: sea, pearl and cane work.
- **Settler regions**: Queensland sugar and pastoral, Victoria's goldfields and trades, South Australia, Western Australia's mining, Tasmania.
- **Mining and FIFO workers** against city office workers against tradies against farmers.
- **Migrant waves**: Chinese, Italian, Greek, Lebanese, Vietnamese, Pacific; and women, casual workers and the unemployed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, labor history and media; thinner and community-held for Aboriginal and Islander meanings of work. No scope file exists; post-war lands are unrecorded, so the core mainland and Tasmania are assumed.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal and Islander language terms with community guidance; migrant languages for oral histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "Dark Emu" debate is contested; wages withheld from Aboriginal workers and images or names of the deceased carry protocols; pre-war sources need a post-war check.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
