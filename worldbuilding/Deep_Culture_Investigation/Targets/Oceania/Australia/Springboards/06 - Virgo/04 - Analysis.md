## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples** (Yolngu, Anangu, Noongar, Koori): their own systems of observation and counting beside Western analysis.
- **Torres Strait Islanders:** navigation, seasonal and sea knowledge.
- **Scientific and professional centers** (Canberra, Sydney, Melbourne) against the outback and resource towns.
- **Migrant students and professionals** (Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian, Lebanese): schooling cultures vary; check, do not assume.
- **Gender and class**, and farmers and tradies against universities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, schooling and science institutions; mixed for Indigenous knowledge, which is community-held. No scope file exists; post-war lands are unrecorded, so the core mainland and Tasmania are assumed.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous language terms with community help; migrant languages for schooling studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aboriginal knowledge (star, seasonal and ceremonial) may be restricted; early anthropological sources carry racist claims, so read them critically; deceased persons' names and images carry protocols. Pre-war sources need a post-war check.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
