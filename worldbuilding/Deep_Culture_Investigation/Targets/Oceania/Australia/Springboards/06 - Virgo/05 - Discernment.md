# Australia: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English, Kriol), Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples** (central desert, Top End, Noongar, Koori, Murri): judging people through kin and sign, and the mistrust built by history.
- **Torres Strait Islanders** and **Pacific communities:** elders' counsel and church.
- **Metropolitan and rural**: Melbourne's café and wine culture, agricultural shows, the outback.
- **Migrant communities** (Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese): scam targeting, religious discernment, community trust.
- **Church traditions**: Orthodox, Catholic, Pentecostal, Quaker, secular.
- **Age and class**: young adults online against older Australians, "bogans" against "latte sippers" (*check* terms).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for scams, news trust and wine and show judging; thinner for private judgment of character. No scope file exists; post-war lands are unrecorded, so the core mainland and Tasmania are assumed.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Greek and Arabic for the religious and classical terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aboriginal knowledge of "clever" people, signs and ceremony is restricted and community-held, not for extraction; cult and abuse survivors' accounts need care; names of the deceased carry protocols. Pre-war sources need a post-war check.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
