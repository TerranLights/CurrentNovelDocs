# Australia: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples** (central desert, Top End, Koori, Murri, Noongar): shaming, joking and avoidance relations, and protest against the state.
- **Torres Strait Islanders:** correction within families, church and island custom.
- **Public against private sector**, and sport, where sledging is part of play.
- **Migrant communities** (Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese): face and direct against indirect criticism; variation inside each.
- **City and bush**; **young adults online against older Australians**; satirists, critics and whistleblowers against the officials they name.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media, satire, sport and public-service surveys; thin for private family criticism. No scope file exists; post-war lands are unrecorded, so the core mainland and Tasmania are assumed.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English with community guidance; migrant languages for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shaming and avoidance customs are community-held; protest texts name living people; whistleblower and press-freedom cases involve legal questions held for Libra and Capricorn; names of the deceased carry protocols. Pre-war sources need a post-war check.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
