# Australia: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aboriginal peoples** (Anangu, Yolngu, Noongar, Koori, Murri, Palawa): wellbeing as country, kin and culture; gaps and community-controlled care.
- **Torres Strait Islanders** and **Pacific communities**: island custom, church and extended family.
- **Remote and rural** against **capital cities**: access and self-reliance.
- **Migrant communities** (Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese): folk and herbal systems beside biomedicine.
- **Workers**: miners and asbestos communities; farmers; men and women; older Australians.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, public-health history and campaigns; thinner for folk and private practice. No scope file exists; post-war lands are unrecorded, so the core mainland and Tasmania are assumed.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal terms with community help; migrant languages for beliefs about illness.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' knowledge and ceremony are restricted; mourning customs may bar naming the dead; disease, trauma and mental-health material needs care. Pre-war sources need a post-war check. No law research; public-health law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
