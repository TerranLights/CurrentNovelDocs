# Australia: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Pitjantjatjara, Warlpiri, Yolŋu Matha, Kriol, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** what moves people in Australia to relieve another's suffering, and how far does mercy reach (stranger, offender, enemy, animal, one's own self)?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Desert, Arnhem Land and urban Aboriginal groups (kin-based care against institutional care); Torres Strait Islanders; Irish Catholic, Anglican and secular settler traditions; Greek, Italian, Lebanese and Vietnamese communities with their own giving customs; rural towns against capital cities; veterans' networks (Legacy); wealthy and low-income suburbs; Tasmanians and Western Australians as separate regional publics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for giving, volunteering and attitude surveys; mixed for Indigenous care norms, which rest on ethnography and community voices; thin for private compassion.
- **Languages needed:** English; Pitjantjatjara or Warlpiri through community sources; immigrant languages for local charities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "demand sharing" and "humbug" are easily turned into stereotype, so keep community voices first; stories of the destitute and the dead have protocols.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
