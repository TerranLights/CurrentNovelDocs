# Australia: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Indigenous languages through dictionaries and community sources, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** when the gut and the data disagree, whose hunch do people in Australia trust (bush hand, tracker, nurse, mother, elder, expert), and is it read as skill, gift or prejudice?

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong for hospital, aviation, hiring and public-opinion records; thin and sometimes restricted for Indigenous knowledge.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** "clever man" and "clever woman" knowledge and sorcery (restricted); the Chamberlain case (family and public sensitivities); police tracking and frontier violence.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
