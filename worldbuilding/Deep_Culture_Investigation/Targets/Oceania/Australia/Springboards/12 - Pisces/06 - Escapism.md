# Australia: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese, with Indigenous sources through community bodies. **Concept in one line:** which reliefs from a harsh, dull or confining reality (drink, gambling, travel, screens, the bush, dropping out) do people in Australia take, bless or shame, and how do those who take them describe it?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Remote Aboriginal communities and urban Aboriginal people (their own programs and voices); Torres Strait and Pacific Islander kava drinkers; club and pub communities in towns against capitals; Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Vietnamese and Chinese leisure venues; retirees on the road; Year-12 leavers; gamblers and recovery fellowships; city-leavers in coastal and hinterland towns; bush workers and fly-in fly-out mine workforces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for drug, alcohol and gambling statistics and tourism; mixed for private escape; media and slang give wide evidence.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages through community services; migrant languages for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** addiction accounts and overdose data; remote-community material (stereotype risk; use community-led sources); cannabis and injecting-site topics are contested.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
