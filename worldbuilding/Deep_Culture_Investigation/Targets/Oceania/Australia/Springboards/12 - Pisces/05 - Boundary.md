# Australia: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Indigenous languages (Warlpiri, Dyirbal, Yolŋu Matha), Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** where do people in Australia draw lines between self and others (body, house, fence, work, Country, state, nation, group), and how firmly are they held or opened?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples by Country, with their gendered and ceremonial protocols; Torres Strait Islanders on a border zone; suburban Anglo-Celtic households; Greek, Italian, Lebanese and Vietnamese families with their own norms of hospitality and reserve; remote pastoral and mining communities; border-state residents (Western Australia, Queensland, Tasmania); church and school staff under conduct codes; recent arrivals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for privacy, workplace and border material; mixed for household norms; thin openly for Indigenous avoidance and secret-sacred boundaries.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for kin-protocol terms; migrant languages for household norms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** men's and women's business and sacred-site detail; Stolen Generations and detention material; survivors of institutional abuse; names and images of the dead.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
