# Australia: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Indigenous languages through language-centre dictionaries, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** how do people in Australia handle ceasing to resist (to God, fate, illness, death, an enemy, a quarrel, the land), and when is yielding read as wisdom, shame or defeat?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples with different frontier histories (Tasmania, Kimberley, Central Desert, Arnhem Land); Stolen Generations families and mothers of forced adoption; Anzac-minded towns against communities skeptical of the legend; rural farmers in drought against coastal city dwellers; Muslim, Orthodox and Buddhist communities with their own languages of accepting fate; recovery fellowships; hospice and palliative-care staff; people in states with and without voluntary assisted dying access.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health, disaster and war history; mixed for private acceptance; thin for Indigenous accounts of yielding outside published testimony.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for dictionary terms; migrant languages for religious phrases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and assisted-dying material; massacre and removal histories; forced-adoption testimony; names and images of the dead; fatalism is an outsider's label, to be tested against community voices.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
