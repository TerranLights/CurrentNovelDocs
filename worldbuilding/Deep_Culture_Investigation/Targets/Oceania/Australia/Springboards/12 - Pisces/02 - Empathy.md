# Australia: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Pitjantjatjara, Yolŋu Matha, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** whose feelings and minds are people in Australia expected to read or share, by what practices is that learned (listening, yarning, role-play), and where does it stop?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal groups with their own listening practices (Daly River, Western Desert, Arnhem Land, south-east "yarning" cultures); Torres Strait Islanders; bush and rural men against urban professionals; Greek, Italian, Lebanese and Vietnamese families; people in health and teaching roles; autistic and neurodivergent Australians writing for themselves; veterans' groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; clinical and survey data on empathy exist, but everyday empathy lives in memoir, media and community voices.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for community sources; immigrant languages for family accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** yarning and deep-listening materials are shared under community protocols; Stolen Generations testimony; some recordings of the deceased.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
