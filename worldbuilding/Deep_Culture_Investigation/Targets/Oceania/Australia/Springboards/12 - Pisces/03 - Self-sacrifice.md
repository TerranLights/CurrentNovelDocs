# Australia: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese, with Indigenous-language sources through community language centres. **Concept in one line:** who in Australia is expected to give up comfort, health or life for whom (child, mate, country, community, God), and how is that giving honored, repaid or resented?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Returned service families and Anzac-observant towns against communities more skeptical of Anzac; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander veterans and Stolen Generations families; grandmothers raising grandchildren in remote communities; post-war Greek, Italian and later Vietnamese and Lebanese migrant parents; volunteer rescuers in coastal and bushfire country; sole carers in rural areas; labor and union communities; Muslim and Orthodox observers of fasting and sacrifice feasts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war memory, carers and bravery records; mixed for family sacrifice, which sits in oral history; thin for Indigenous ceremonial sacrifice in the open.
- **Languages needed:** English; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese or Chinese for migrant testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiation and mourning ceremony details are restricted in many communities; suicide in veterans' accounts; detention protest and self-harm records; images and names of the dead.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Australian War Memorial: Last Post Ceremony archive, a sample of stories and wording.
2. Honours search: Star of Courage and Cross of Valour citations, by decade.
3. ABS Survey of Disability, Ageing and Carers: primary-carer tables by age and sex.
4. Trove: "Self Denial" Salvation Army appeal, 1920-1960.
5. National Library oral histories: search "sacrifices" with "migrant" and "parents".
