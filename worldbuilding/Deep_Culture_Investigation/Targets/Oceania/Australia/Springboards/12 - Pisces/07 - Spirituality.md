Desert Aboriginal groups (Anangu), Arnhem Land communities (Yolŋu, Kunwinjku), south-east and urban Aboriginal people, Torres Strait Islanders; Anglican, Catholic and Uniting congregations against the "no religion" majority; Greek and Italian Catholic and Orthodox families; Chinese and Vietnamese Buddhists; Muslim communities; Pentecostal networks; New Age belts (northern New South Wales); coastal sea-and-surf people against inland bush people; young adults against older.
