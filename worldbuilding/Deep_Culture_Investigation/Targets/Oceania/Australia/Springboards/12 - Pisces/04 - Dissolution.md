# Australia: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Indigenous languages (Pitjantjatjara, Warlpiri, Yolŋu Matha, Kriol), Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** how does Australia treat the loss of the self's edges in something larger (crowd, Country, God, love) and the ending of bonds, groups and identities (divorce, assimilation, closed towns and congregations): as gift, danger or loss?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples whose Country is central, and Stolen Generations families; Torres Strait Islanders; first- and second-generation Greek, Italian, Vietnamese, Lebanese and Chinese migrants; Pentecostal and festival communities; coastal surfers; northern New South Wales alternative communities; former factory and mining towns (Elizabeth, Broadmeadows, Whyalla); former members of high-control groups; families of people with dementia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and policy history; mixed for lived merging, which sits in memoir, film and fieldwork; thin openly for ceremony.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for community sources; migrant languages for family histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial detail (restricted in part); assimilation-era records name living families; survivors first for Stolen Generations; cult-survivor accounts.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
