# Australia: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Indigenous languages through community sources, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** what do people in Australia learn to fear (fire, sea, bush, creatures, outsiders, authorities, shame), what do they learn not to fear, and is fear shown, hidden, laughed at or treated?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Fire-zone and flood-zone residents against city residents; Top End and Queensland cyclone communities; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities (fear of services and of harm, and their own accounts); refugees and asylum seekers; Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Vietnamese and Chinese families; young people and the aged; coastal swimmers and surfers; bush workers; people in Melbourne after the long lockdown.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, polls and mental-health surveys; rich in horror film and folklore; thin for private fear and for Indigenous accounts outside published sources.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages with community permission; migrant languages for clinic and community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors of fires and attacks; refugee trauma; Aboriginal fear-figure stories (some restricted); suicide and anxiety data; fear of outsiders to be recorded as a claim, never adopted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
