# Australia: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese, with Indigenous sources through community bodies. **Concept in one line:** what do people in Australia count as a real wrong against the community, whose wrongs are overlooked, how is the wrongdoer pictured (convict, outlaw, crook, sick, enemy), and can he come back?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities (victims, those imprisoned, and healing groups); descendants of convicts against later migrants; rural and urban publics; Italian-, Greek-, Lebanese-, Vietnamese- and Chinese-Australian communities, each a target of stereotype; bikie, gangland and white-collar worlds; victims' families and survivors of institutions; online communities of true-crime fans.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, inquiries and true-crime media; mixed for attitudes to wrongdoing; thin for community-level repair, which sits with Aboriginal organizations.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages through community bodies; migrant languages for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivor and victim testimony; names of the dead; frontier massacre records; ethnic stereotypes in crime coverage, to be recorded as claims, never adopted. Law is researched in Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
