# Australia: Domain and folkways, the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian and Aboriginal English), Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages (Pitjantjatjara, Warlpiri, Arrernte, Yolŋu Matha, Noongar, Kriol, Meriam Mir, Kalaw Lagaw Ya), and the main immigrant languages (Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese). **Concept in one line:** how does the nation meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape (the hidden and transcendence)?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples by Country (Central Desert, Arnhem Land, Kimberley, Noongar, south-east); Torres Strait Islanders; Anglo-Celtic and Irish Catholic settlers; Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Vietnamese and Chinese communities; Pacific Islander communities; Pentecostal and charismatic networks; alternative-lifestyle belts (northern New South Wales); remote bush against coastal cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health surveys and institutional inquiries; mixed for belief; Indigenous spiritual knowledge is held in communities, so thin in the open.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages and interpreters for community sources; immigrant languages for the communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** secret-sacred and gendered knowledge ("men's" and "women's business"); images and names of the deceased in many Aboriginal communities (check AIATSIS protocols); survivor testimony; suicide data (reporting guidelines).
- **Scope:** the nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled (no scope file yet); this sheet and the sheets that follow are written for the core lands of mainland Australia and Tasmania.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
