# Australia: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English and Kriol); Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities by region, whose speech norms differ from English-language readings of honesty.
- Sport, politics, banking, mining and university cultures, each with documented integrity cases.
- Rural honesty-box economies against city commerce; small-town and big-city norms.
- Migrant business communities (Greek, Italian, Chinese, Lebanese, Vietnamese) and newcomers learning local norms.
- Convict-descended against postwar and recent arrivals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for integrity inquiries, scams and attitudes surveys; mixed for everyday honesty; thin for private candor and for community-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and Kriol via linguistic studies; community languages for migrant sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names in ongoing cases; Aboriginal testimony; do not treat the sources as findings on any group's character.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
