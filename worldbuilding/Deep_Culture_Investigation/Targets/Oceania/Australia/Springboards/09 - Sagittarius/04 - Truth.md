# Australia: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English); Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese; Aboriginal languages such as Yolngu Matha and Pitjantjatjara. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it, and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal knowledge holders by region (Yolngu, Anangu, Gunditjmara, Wiradjuri, Kulin, Noongar) and Torres Strait Islanders; separate from settler historians.
- Scientists and skeptics against creationist and alternative-health communities.
- Readers by outlet: ABC, News Corp papers, Guardian Australia, foreign-language press, social platforms.
- Migrant communities with their own news sources (Chinese, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Vietnamese); regional and rural against city readers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for trust surveys and the frontier-history dispute; mixed for everyday truth and rumor; thin for rumor in community languages.
- **Languages needed:** English; community-language press; Aboriginal-language sources through AIATSIS.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** massacre records and testimony; restricted ceremonial knowledge; partisan media disputes; the Dark Emu debate is heated, so read both sides.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
