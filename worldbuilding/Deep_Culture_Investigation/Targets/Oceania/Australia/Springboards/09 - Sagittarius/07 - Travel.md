# Australia: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese; Aboriginal languages and Kriol; Pacific languages. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples by region: travel on and between Country, trading, and visitors' access to sacred places.
- Convict-descended, free-settler and postwar migrant families; refugees by decade (Europe, Indochina, Middle East).
- City against remote communities; mining-state workers (WA, Queensland) against southern cities.
- Chinese, Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Vietnamese and Pacific Islander communities: arrival routes and hometown links.
- Retirees on the road, backpackers, and young Australians going overseas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official flows, explorers and immigration history; mixed for ordinary travel attitudes; thin for community-language migrant memory.
- **Languages needed:** English; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese and Vietnamese for diaspora sources; Aboriginal languages for Country.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child migrants and the forgotten Australians; deaths on the sea voyage; names of the deceased on exploration records; sacred places closed to visitors.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

4. Search Tourism Australia's campaign archive for slogans from 1980 to now.
5. Search NAA RecordSearch for "assisted passage" and "migrant hostel".
