*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples by region (Yolngu, Anangu, Arrernte, Warlpiri, Noongar, Wiradjuri, Kulin, Murri, palawa) and Torres Strait Islanders: different Law, language and church history.
- States and cities: Sydney (Anglican and Pentecostal), Melbourne, Adelaide's dissenting founding, Perth, Brisbane, Hobart, Darwin and the Territory, the bush against the coast.
- Migrant faiths: Greek and Italian, Lebanese and Turkish, Chinese, Vietnamese, Jewish, Pacific Islander and Maori, Macedonian and Serbian Orthodox.
- Convict, free-settler, Irish Catholic, Scottish Presbyterian and German Lutheran descent lines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for counts, surveys and church history; mixed for philosophy; thin for lay worldview. No scope file exists, and the tracker records no change to pre-war lands; the exact post-war lands (mainland, Tasmania, external territories) are still to be settled. Written for the mainland and Tasmania.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages for migrant faiths; Aboriginal languages and Kriol via AIATSIS.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restricted men's and women's knowledge and images and names of the deceased; read collections under AIATSIS protocols and ask permission. Abuse inquiry material is painful.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
