## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities (Warlpiri, Yolngu, Anangu, Murri, Koori, Noongar, Torres Strait): bilingual and community-run schooling against mainstream.
- Government, Catholic and independent school families; the old "sandstone" universities against newer ones; TAFE and apprenticeship paths.
- Migrant communities and their language schools; international students.
- States (Victoria, NSW, Queensland, WA, SA, Tasmania, the Territory) and city against remote.
- First-in-family students against families with a degree tradition.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official statistics, histories and university archives; mixed for attitudes to the educated; thin for community-run education in Aboriginal languages and for migrant Saturday schools.
- **Languages needed:** English; Warlpiri, Yolngu Matha and others for community programs; Greek, Chinese, Arabic, Italian for community-school material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mission and dormitory school histories and the Stolen Generations; restricted knowledge not for classroom use; student-debt and cheating cases.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
