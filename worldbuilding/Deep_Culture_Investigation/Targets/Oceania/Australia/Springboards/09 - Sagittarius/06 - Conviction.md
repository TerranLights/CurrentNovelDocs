*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Unionized workers (shearers, waterside, building) against employers; party members against independents.
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander political movements by region (Yolngu, Gurindji, Murri, Koori, Noongar, Torres Strait).
- Peace and church communities (Quakers, Catholic, Uniting, Pentecostal) against secular activists.
- Migrants with homeland causes: Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Lebanese.
- Tasmania and New South Wales environmental movements against mining and farming communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for protest history, labor and Aboriginal activism; mixed for everyday conviction; thin for private conscience and non-English community sources.
- **Languages needed:** English; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Arabic and Vietnamese for community press; Aboriginal languages for petitions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names of the dead; protest violence; refugee detention cases; veteran and conscription memories.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
