# Australia: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese; Aboriginal languages and Kriol, Torres Strait languages. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Anglican (Sydney evangelical against Melbourne), Catholic (Irish and later Italian, Maltese, Vietnamese), Presbyterian and Uniting, Lutheran (Barossa and Central Australia).
- Pentecostal and charismatic churches against the older denominations; migrant-majority congregations.
- Aboriginal Christians and traditional-law holders, by region (Arrernte, Anangu, Yolngu, Murri, Noongar); Torres Strait Islanders; Pacific Islander and South Sea Islander communities.
- Orthodox, Muslim, Jewish, Buddhist and Chinese-popular religion communities; the unaffiliated; city against outback.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for counts, church history and trust surveys; mixed for lived faith; thin for private faith and for non-Christian communities outside the censuses.
- **Languages needed:** English; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese and Vietnamese for migrant sources; Aboriginal-language mission texts, Kriol.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse inquiry material; stolen-generation mission histories; ceremonial knowledge, which only the holders may share.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Open Trove and search "day of humiliation and prayer" and "revival meeting" by decade, 1850 to 1914.
2. Read the Christian Research Association's latest survey summary for the categories of faith it uses.
3. Search the Lutheran Archives catalog for "Hermannsburg" and "Ntaria" collections.
4. Search the Uniting Aboriginal and Islander Christian Congress site for founding statements.
5. Compare Roy Morgan's ratings of clergy with other trust surveys, by year.
