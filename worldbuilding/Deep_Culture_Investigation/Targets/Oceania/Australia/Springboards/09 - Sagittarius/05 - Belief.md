# Australia: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese; Aboriginal languages (Pitjantjatjara, Kunwinjku, Noongar) and Torres Strait languages. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Denominations: Catholic, Anglican, Uniting, Pentecostal, Orthodox; Muslim, Jewish, Buddhist; the unaffiliated.
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities by region, with Christian and traditional belief each stated on its own terms.
- Migrant families: Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese and Vietnamese folk belief, first against second generation.
- Rural and outback against city; Nimbin-style alternative communities; older against younger.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey measures and folklore; mixed for folk belief in community languages; thin for private belief.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages for migrant folk belief; Aboriginal languages through AIATSIS and community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** secret-sacred Aboriginal beliefs and rites; the deceased's names and images; do not treat "pointing the bone" as public lore.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
