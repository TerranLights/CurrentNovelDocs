# Australia: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English and Kriol); Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese; Yolngu Matha and other Aboriginal languages. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal nations and Torres Strait Islanders by region (Gurindji, Yolngu, Kulin, Wiradjuri, Noongar, Murri), with sovereignty as a separate question.
- Convict-descended against free-settler families; Tasmania, with its convict past, against South Australia, founded unconvicted; Western Australia's separatist stream.
- Irish, Italian, Greek, Chinese, Vietnamese and Pacific Island arrivals; wartime internees.
- Urban against bush and station communities; farmers and miners against inner-city residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Aboriginal rights history, convicts and internment; mixed for private ideas of freedom; recent protest material is contested and politically charged.
- **Languages needed:** English; Kriol and Aboriginal languages via AIATSIS; Chinese, Greek, Italian, Arabic and Vietnamese press for migrant voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Stolen Generations testimony; names and images of the deceased; detention accounts; protest material that is partisan.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
