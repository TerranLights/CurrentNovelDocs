# Australia: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal English; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Public servants and political-party members; selective and non-selective school families; regional and remote students; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander applicants and professionals; migrants with overseas qualifications, by origin; women and men in leadership; sport pathways against arts pathways; family firms and farms against corporations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries, audits and surveys; mixed for community understandings of connections and of earned roles.
- **Languages needed:** English; Mandarin, Arabic, Greek, Italian and Vietnamese for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial standing is not for outsiders to rank; ICAC and audit findings name living people, so read them as records.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Parliament of Australia: Coombs Royal Commission report, chapters on merit.
2. ANAO: sports grants audit, summary and recommendations.
3. Australian Data Archive: ISSP Social Inequality, Australian file.
4. Trove (Canberra Times runs to 1995) or a press database: "jobs for the boys".
5. APS Commission site: "merit" in the APS Values and guidance.
