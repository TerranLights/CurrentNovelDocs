# Australia: Law, its spirit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal English, Arrernte, Warlpiri; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them (what law is for, whose it is, whether to obey, use, bend or avoid it). ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples with their own legal orders (desert, Arnhem Land, southeast) and Torres Strait Islanders; remote against urban policing; rural and fire-prone areas; state differences (Queensland, Victoria, Western Australia); Muslim, Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek and Italian communities; legal-aid users against lawyers' clients; police, prison and court workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, inquiries and cases; mixed for community customs; thin for private settlement.
- **Languages needed:** English; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian for community sources; Indigenous terms through language centers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** customary law and payback are community-held, and some accounts are restricted; deaths in custody; named people in inquiries; religious-law debate in public life.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
