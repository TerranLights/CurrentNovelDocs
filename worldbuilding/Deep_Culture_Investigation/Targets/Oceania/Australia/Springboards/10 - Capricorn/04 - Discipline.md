## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Government against independent and Catholic schools; boarding and country schools against city day schools; military and police families; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities with differing initiation and child-rearing traditions; Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian and Arabic-speaking households by generation; Orthodox, Muslim and Buddhist fasting communities; footy and cricket clubs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school, military and sport reviews; thin for private household practice; restricted for initiation.
- **Languages needed:** English; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiation and ceremonial discipline; abuse in schools, defense and children's homes; deceased persons' names.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
