# Australia: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Yolŋu Matha and Pitjantjatjara; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal nations with Elder-based and council-based government (Anangu, Yolŋu, Noongar, Koori); Torres Strait Islanders under Ailan Kastom; state police and defense families against civilian ones; Irish Catholic and Protestant lines; rural volunteers against city professionals; post-war European, Chinese, Vietnamese and Arabic-speaking migrants and their Australian-born children; generations around 1970 and 2000.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries and polling; thin for authority inside migrant families and Aboriginal communities in writing communities control.
- **Languages needed:** English; community help for Yolŋu Matha and Pitjantjatjara.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-abuse and Stolen Generations testimony; restricted ceremonial authority; deceased persons' names.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
