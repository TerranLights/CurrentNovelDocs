# Australia: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal English and Yolŋu Matha; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities, including those who leave country for study and work; mainland states (Western Australia's mining economy against the southeast); city against regional towns; Greek and Italian post-war migrants and their children; Chinese, Vietnamese and Arabic-speaking families by wave; selective-school against local-school families; farm families; generations around 1990 and 2010.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on mobility, education and the press; mixed on migrant and Aboriginal ambition in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages for migrant sources; Yolŋu Matha and others through language centers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** none special; avoid reading one voice into any community.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
