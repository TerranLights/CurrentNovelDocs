# Australia: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal English; Pitjantjatjara and Torres Strait languages; Tongan, Samoan; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples with kin-based sharing (desert, Arnhem Land, southeast); Torres Strait Islanders; Pacific communities (Tongan, Samoan); Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic-speaking, Greek and Italian families, by migration wave; Anzac-tradition families against others; rural volunteers against city households; carers by gender and age.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and inquiries; mixed for migrant and Aboriginal family duty (mostly small studies and community sources).
- **Languages needed:** English; Tongan, Samoan, Mandarin and others for community sources; Aboriginal language terms need community advice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** veteran suicide and abuse testimony; deceased persons' names; restricted ceremonial obligations.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Australian War Memorial site: "Last Post Ceremony", then the Ode text.
2. ABS: Survey of Disability, Ageing and Carers, unpaid carers tables.
3. Royal commissions: Black Saturday final report, section on household preparation.
4. Journal search: "Peterson demand sharing 1993" and "humbugging".
5. AIATSIS or a land council: "ranger program", "caring for country".
