*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples with skin or subsection systems and those with other forms; Torres Strait Islanders; pastoral and mining regions against cities; Sydney's North Shore, Eastern Suburbs and Western Sydney; Melbourne's east and west; remote communities against major cities; state against independent and Catholic school families; convict-descended against later settlers; post-war European, Chinese, Vietnamese and Arabic-speaking migrants; women and men at work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for class measures, pay and honors; thin for informal rank in migrant and Aboriginal settings in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages for migrant sources; Warlpiri and Yolŋu Matha through language centers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** kin and skin-name rules in some communities; race and ancestry statistics.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

1. ABS: SEIFA index of relative advantage, by suburb or local government area.
