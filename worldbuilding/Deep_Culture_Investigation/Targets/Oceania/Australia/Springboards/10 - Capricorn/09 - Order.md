# Australia: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal English, Warlpiri, Torres Strait languages; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples whose Law orders land, kin and season; Torres Strait Islanders; remote communities against capital cities; Sydney's beachside, western and southwestern suburbs; Melbourne's inner and outer suburbs; Perth and Darwin; farm and fire-prone districts; migrant communities by wave; youth and festival crowds.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crime, safety and disaster records; mixed for household and neighborhood order; thin for community-held Aboriginal accounts in public sources.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages for migrant sources; Warlpiri and Torres Strait terms through language centers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deaths in custody and policing of Indigenous people; riots with ethnic framing, to be read as contested; some Dreaming and sky knowledge is restricted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
