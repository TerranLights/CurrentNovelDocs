# Australia: Domain and folkways, authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian English); Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages (Yolŋu Matha, Warlpiri, Pitjantjatjara, Noongar, Torres Strait Creole, Kala Lagaw Ya, Meriam Mir, Kriol); migrant-heritage languages (Mandarin, Cantonese, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian). **Concept in one line:** who commands, who obeys, and what is owed; with the folkways of career, status, reputation, age, time, rank, order and power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal nations by region (Yolŋu of Arnhem Land; Western Desert peoples; Noongar of the southwest; Koori, Murri, Nunga and palawa in the southeast, north and south); Torres Strait Islanders (Ailan Kastom); convict-descended and free-settler lines, Irish Catholic and Protestant traditions; states and territories (frontier against old settled colonies); cities against bush and outback; post-war European migrants; later Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic-speaking and Pasifika communities; military, farming and union families; generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, inquiries and press; mixed for Indigenous governance (community-held); thin for migrant communities' own terms.
- **Languages needed:** English first; Indigenous languages need community or AIATSIS help; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian for migrant pointers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred and gender-restricted knowledge; names and images of deceased Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled, so this sheet is written for the mainland and Tasmania and does not repeat the hedge. Law is taken for its spirit only; the letter belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
