# Australia: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal English; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal communities on welfare and self-determination, with differing views (Cape York, desert, urban southeast); Torres Strait Islanders; farming and fire-prone regions against cities; migrants by wave (Greek and Italian, Vietnamese, Chinese, Arabic-speaking); public servants and ministers' staff; generations around the 2008 Apology.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries, apologies and polls; mixed for household practice.
- **Languages needed:** English; Mandarin, Arabic, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian for community sources; community advice for Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Stolen Generations and detention testimony; deaths in custody; restricted narratives; deceased persons' names.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
