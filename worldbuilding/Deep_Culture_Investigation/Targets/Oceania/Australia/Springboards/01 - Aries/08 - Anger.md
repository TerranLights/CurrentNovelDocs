# Australia: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Pitjantjatjara, Yolŋu Matha and other Aboriginal languages; Mandarin, Greek, Arabic, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal communities by region and language (the Western Desert, Arnhem Land, urban south-east), and Torres Strait Islanders.
- Men and women; young and older people; workplaces (service work, trades, health care).
- Pub, sports and surf cultures against home and office settings.
- Migrant communities: Chinese, Greek, Lebanese and other Arabic-speaking, Vietnamese; first and second generations.
- Capital cities against regional and outback towns; Queensland, Western Australia, New South Wales and Victoria as places with different public-violence records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for protest, crime and workplace data; mixed for Aboriginal and migrant words for anger; thin for private family anger beyond domestic-violence reporting.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal languages through community centers; Chinese, Greek, Arabic and Vietnamese for diaspora emotion words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deaths in custody and riot records; domestic-violence data; deceased persons' names; anger-related ceremonial matters (a people's own).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
