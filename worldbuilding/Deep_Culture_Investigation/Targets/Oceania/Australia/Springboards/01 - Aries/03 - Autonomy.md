# Australia: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Aboriginal English; Pitjantjatjara, Yolŋu Matha and other Aboriginal languages; Mandarin, Greek, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled, for persons and for groups.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal communities by region: Western Desert lands, Top End, Kimberley, south-east cities; and Torres Strait Islanders.
- Pastoral and rural families against capital-city households; selector-descended farm families.
- Families of migrant origin by generation: Chinese, Greek, Vietnamese, Lebanese, Italian, Pacific Islander.
- Teenagers, working-age adults and older people in care; people with disability using self-directed funding.
- Casual and gig workers against salaried and unionized workers.
- Western Australia, Queensland, Tasmania and the Territories, where states or territories press separate claims.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household, youth and time-use statistics; mixed for Aboriginal autonomy (rich in policy history, uneven on daily life); thin for family decision-making among small migrant groups.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal-language sources via community centers; Chinese, Greek and Vietnamese for family-obligation terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** records of removed children and exemption certificates; community decision-making that is not for outsiders; bodily-autonomy topics (end of life, reproduction): record attitudes only, and send statute questions to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
