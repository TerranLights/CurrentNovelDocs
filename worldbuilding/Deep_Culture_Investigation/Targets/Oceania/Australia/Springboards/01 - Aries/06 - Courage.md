# Australia: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Aboriginal and Torres Strait languages; Mandarin, Greek, Arabic, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, with ordeals, frontier resistance and military service; remote and urban communities.
- Rural fire, farm and fishing communities against city emergency workers.
- Veterans of each war, serving members and families; women in service and nursing.
- Refugee and migrant communities (Vietnamese, Lebanese, Chinese, Greek, Pacific Islander), whose journeys are named as courage.
- Men and women; young and old; people from the beaches (lifesavers), the bush (stockmen) and the cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official honors, military archives and disaster inquiries; mixed for Aboriginal frontier courage (strong histories, contested memory); thin for everyday social courage.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal-language sources through community centers; Chinese, Greek, Arabic and Vietnamese for diaspora accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** secret initiation material in early ethnographies; deceased persons; reports on veteran suicide and alleged war crimes; refugee trauma.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
