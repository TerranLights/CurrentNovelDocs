## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Victoria (Australian rules), New South Wales and Queensland (rugby league and union), Western Australia, South Australia and Tasmania, each with its own codes and rivalries.
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities, with traditional and modern games, rural and urban.
- Migrant communities with club cultures: Greek, Italian, Croatian, Lebanese, Vietnamese, Chinese, Pacific Islander.
- Public schools, Catholic schools and private schools; selective schools against local ones.
- City and country; men and women; amateur and professional players.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport participation, club history and school testing; mixed for Aboriginal traditional games (partial, contested); thin for competition in private family life.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal languages via community centers; Chinese, Greek, Arabic and Vietnamese for diaspora and club-community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** racial abuse in sport; the Marn Grook origin dispute; doping cases that name individuals; gambling harm.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
