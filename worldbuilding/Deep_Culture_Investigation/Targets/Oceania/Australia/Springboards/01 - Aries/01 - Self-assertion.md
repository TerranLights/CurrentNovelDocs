# Australia: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Aboriginal languages (Pitjantjatjara, Yolŋu Matha and others); Mandarin, Greek, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal communities by language region, and Torres Strait Islanders, where who may speak and when is set by kin and country.
- Rural and bush communities against Sydney and Melbourne; Perth and Tasmania as places with their own self-image.
- Men and women; workplace against home; trade unionists against managers.
- Migrant communities (Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Lebanese, Pacific Islander) by generation, since parents and children may differ.
- Older and younger speakers, and the families who arrived early against those who arrived late.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dictionaries, workplace and school sources; mixed for Aboriginal speaking protocols (rich in print, restricted in practice); thin for small migrant groups.
- **Languages needed:** English first; Aboriginal-language material through community language centers; Chinese, Greek and Vietnamese for diaspora comparisons.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protocols of who may speak for country; deceased people named in petitions and archives; Torres Strait feasts and unveilings.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
