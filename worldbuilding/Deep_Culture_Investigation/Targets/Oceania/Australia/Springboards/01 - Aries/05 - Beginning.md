# Australia: Beginning (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Aboriginal languages (Warlpiri, Yolŋu Matha and others); Torres Strait languages; Mandarin, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** how a culture treats starting: origins, firsts, new years and seasons, and starting over after failure or migration.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples by region and language, with their own creation accounts and seasons; Torres Strait Islanders.
- Descendants of convicts and free settlers against postwar assisted migrants and recent refugees.
- Gold-rush-era Chinese families, Greek and Italian post-1945 migrants, Vietnamese refugees, and recent arrivals.
- Capital cities against rural towns; the north (cyclone and wet-season) against the south.
- Canberra, a planned city begun in 1913, against older settlements.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration, disaster and settler records; mixed for Aboriginal origin accounts (many published, many restricted); thin for family-level "starting over" after bankruptcy or divorce.
- **Languages needed:** English; Warlpiri, Yolŋu Matha and other sources via community centers; Chinese and Vietnamese for diaspora memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** creation narratives that are sacred or gender-restricted; deceased persons' names and images; refugee and removed-children records.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
