# Australia: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia (mainland, Tasmania, offshore islands), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English, Aboriginal English, Kriol, Yolŋu Matha, Greek, Italian, Mandarin and Cantonese, Vietnamese, Arabic. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Men and women; generations; Aboriginal communities (Yolŋu, Anangu, Noongar, Koori, Murri) and Torres Strait Islanders, each with its own speech and mourning rules; remote and regional against capital cities; Anglo-Celtic working-class, Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese and Arabic-speaking families; Pacific and Māori communities; LGBTQ+ people in city and country; religious communities (Catholic, Anglican, Orthodox, Muslim, Buddhist, Pentecostal).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mental health, youth and campaigns; mixed for Aboriginal speech styles and migrant homes; thin for private feeling.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Greek, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Italian for community voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mourning customs, names and images of the dead; suicide material; community-held ceremonial knowledge.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
