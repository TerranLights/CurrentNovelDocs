## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Desert, Kimberley, Arnhem Land and Cape York art centres against urban Aboriginal artists in Sydney and Melbourne; Torres Strait Islanders; palawa and Victorian revivalists; bush and backyard improvisers against city art worlds; Anglo-Celtic, Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese and Pacific makers; men's and women's craft spaces; remote and regional artists against capital-city galleries.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Indigenous art, markets and artists' incomes; mixed for everyday improvising; thin for individual feeling about creativity.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous-language terms for design and law; Greek, Chinese, Italian, Vietnamese for migrant craft.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred designs, men's and women's business, images of deceased artists (use AIATSIS protocols); some art is held under community permission.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
