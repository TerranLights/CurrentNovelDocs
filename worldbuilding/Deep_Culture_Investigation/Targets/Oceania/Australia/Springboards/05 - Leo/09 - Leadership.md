*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples (Warlpiri, Yolŋu, Anangu, Noongar, Wurundjeri and other Koori, Murri, Nunga) and Torres Strait Islanders, each with its own Elders and ceremonial roles; Labor, Liberal and National parties; bush, union and city business worlds; Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese and Samoan community leaders; school, sport and military leaders; women and men.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for politics, business and sport; mixed for Aboriginal, Islander and migrant leadership; thin for informal and family leadership.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and local languages; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese, Samoan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial roles and some leaders' names after death; party-political memoirs are partisan.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search Trove and Parliamentary Library for "spill", "faceless men" and "captain's call".
2. Pull Australian Election Study trust items and Lowy Poll questions on leaders.
3. Search Indigenous Governance Awards for criteria and winners by region.
4. Search AWM for Dunlop and Monash papers.
5. Find community-federation histories for Greek, Italian, Lebanese and Vietnamese communities.
