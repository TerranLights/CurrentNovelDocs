# Australia: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia (mainland, Tasmania, offshore islands), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English, Aboriginal English and Kriol, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Mandarin and Cantonese, Vietnamese, Māori, Samoan. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples (Tiwi, Yolŋu, Murri, Koori, Noongar, Anangu) and Torres Strait Islanders with their own festivals; city and bush; coastal and inland; Anglo-Celtic, Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese, Pacific and Māori communities; Pentecostal against Anglican and Catholic worshippers; young and old; people in remote communities against capital cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wellbeing surveys, festivals and music media; mixed for Aboriginal and migrant joy; thin for private, daily joy.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Samoan, Māori, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremony gatherings tied to mourning or initiation; festival footage may show restricted performances; outsider accounts of "cheerful natives" are colonial tropes.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull Australian Unity Wellbeing Index and HILDA life-satisfaction tables by age and region.
