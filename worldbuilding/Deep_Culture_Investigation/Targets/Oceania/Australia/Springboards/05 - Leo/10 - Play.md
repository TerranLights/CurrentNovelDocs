## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples (Gunditjmara, Wiradjuri, Warlpiri, Yolŋu, Murri, Noongar, palawa) and Torres Strait Islanders, with different games and joking rules; city and bush children; coastal and inland; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Lebanese and Vietnamese families; Pacific and Māori communities; girls and boys; adults in clubs, pubs and gaming; the old at bowls.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for children's lore, time use and video games; mixed for Aboriginal games (some contested and some written by outsiders); thin for adult everyday play.
- **Languages needed:** English; Kriol and Aboriginal English; Wiradjuri, Djab wurrung; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Arabic for community games.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Aboriginal play is tied to ceremony; Marn Grook's origin is debated and politicized.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
