# Australia: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia (mainland, Tasmania, offshore islands), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English, Aboriginal English, Kriol, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Mandarin and Cantonese, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples (Yolŋu, Anangu, Noongar, Koori, Murri) and Torres Strait Islanders, with rules on seeing the dead and on avoidance; Stolen Generations survivors; remote, regional and capital-city residents; the old, living alone; Anglo-Celtic, Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese and Vietnamese communities; recent arrivals; LGBTQ+ people; people with disabilities; night-shift and service workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media representation, census and surveys of loneliness and LGBTQ+ life; mixed for Aboriginal gaze and avoidance customs; thin for everyday noticing.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and local languages; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese for folklore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names and images of deceased Aboriginal people; avoidance customs; Stolen Generations accounts; outsider portraits of Aboriginal people need critical reading.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
