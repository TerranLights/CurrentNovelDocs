# Australia: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia (mainland, Tasmania, offshore islands), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English, Aboriginal English, Italian, Greek, Mandarin and Cantonese, Vietnamese, Arabic. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievement or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor. Law on recognition goes to Libra and Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples and Torres Strait Islanders by region (Gurindji, Yolŋu, Noongar, Koori, Murri, Nunga, palawa); Anglo-Celtic and Irish-Catholic descendants; Italian, Greek, Chinese, Vietnamese and Lebanese communities; Pacific and Māori peoples; women and men; skilled migrants against locally trained workers; remote against capital-city institutions; unions, trades and professions; war veterans and Indigenous servicemen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors, awards and Reconciliation; mixed for migrant honors and workplace praise; thin for everyday thanks.
- **Languages needed:** English; Italian, Greek, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic for community honors.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names and images of deceased Aboriginal people; Stolen Generations testimony; recognition in law is Libra's and Capricorn's, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Query the Australian Honours search for the Order of Australia by decade, field and sex.
2. Search Trove for "knights and dames" and "gong" to trace how titles were received.
3. Pull the latest Reconciliation Barometer and note what it asks about acknowledgment.
4. Search the Australian Women's Register for "recognition" and "overlooked".
5. Search *Il Globo* and *Neos Kosmos* for community honors lists.
