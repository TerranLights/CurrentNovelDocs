## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples (Yolŋu, Warlpiri, Anangu, Noongar, Koori, Murri) with distinct sharing and exchange; Torres Strait Islanders; Anglo-Celtic working-class and rural communities; church, club and union members; Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese and Vietnamese families; Pacific and Māori communities with strong gift obligations; wealthy donors against small donors; remote against urban; disaster-hit regions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal giving, tax, volunteering and appeals; mixed for Aboriginal and Pacific informal giving; thin for hospitality inside homes.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Greek, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese, Italian, Māori, Samoan for community practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonial exchange and funeral obligations; gifts tied to kinship records; explorer accounts misread Aboriginal giving.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
