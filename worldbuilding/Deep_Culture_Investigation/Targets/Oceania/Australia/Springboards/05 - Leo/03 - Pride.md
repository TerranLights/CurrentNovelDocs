# Australia: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia (mainland, Tasmania, offshore islands), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English, Aboriginal English, Greek, Italian, Mandarin and Cantonese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Māori. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples (Wiradjuri, Noongar, Yolŋu, Koori, Murri, Nunga, palawa) and Torres Strait Islanders, each with their own pride in Country; Anglo-Celtic and Irish-Catholic descendants; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese and Lebanese communities; Pacific and Māori peoples; state against state (Victoria, Queensland, Western Australia, Tasmania); city against country town; private-school against public-school graduates; trades and farming against professions; LGBTQ+ communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national pride, Anzac and sport; mixed for Aboriginal and migrant pride; thin for private, everyday pride.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Australia Day and flag disputes are contested; Aboriginal names of the dead and some Country-related knowledge need permission.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
