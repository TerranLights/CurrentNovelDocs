# Australia: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia (mainland, Tasmania, offshore islands), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English (official spelling "honour"), Aboriginal English, Greek, Italian, Arabic, Mandarin, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (word, courage, loyalty) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended. Customary retribution is Scorpio's; law goes to Libra and Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples (Yarralin and other Victoria River peoples, Yolŋu, Noongar, Koori) and Torres Strait Islanders; military and veteran families; rural and bush communities; city business and professional circles; Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese and Vietnamese families (variation within each, not one voice); Irish-Catholic and Anglo-Protestant lines; sport, union and club members; women and men; generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war honor, sport and public records; mixed for Aboriginal moral life and migrant family honor; thin for private oaths and trade trust.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal languages and Aboriginal English; Greek, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Vietnamese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war-crime findings; honor-related harm in families (treat as variation to research, never a stereotype); Aboriginal Law and some moral teaching are restricted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
