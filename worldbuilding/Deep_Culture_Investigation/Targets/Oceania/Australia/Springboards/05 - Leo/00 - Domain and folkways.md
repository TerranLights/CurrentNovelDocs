## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Yolŋu (Arnhem Land), Anangu and Warlpiri (desert), Noongar, Koori, Murri, Nunga and palawa (Tasmania) peoples, and Torres Strait Islanders, whose dance and art traditions differ; the footy line (Australian rules states against rugby league states); city against bush and mining towns; Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Hobart; Anglo-Celtic, Irish-Catholic, post-war Greek, Italian, and Lebanese communities; Chinese and Vietnamese Australians; Pacific and Māori communities; LGBTQ+ festivals; generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, gambling, arts participation and time use; mixed for Indigenous creativity (much held in archives, some restricted); thin for private romance and courtship.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal and Torres Strait languages and Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic for community material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** men's and women's ceremonial knowledge, images and names of the dead (consult AIATSIS protocols); colonial sources are written by outsiders.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
