# Australia: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia (mainland, Tasmania, offshore islands), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English, Aboriginal English, Wiradjuri, Yolŋu Matha, Yumplatok (Torres Strait Creole), Greek, Italian, Arabic, Mandarin and Cantonese, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned. Law goes to Libra and Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
Aboriginal peoples (Wiradjuri, Yolŋu, Noongar, Koori, Murri, Nunga, palawa) and Torres Strait Islanders; Stolen Generations survivors; the old in care and the disabled; working-class and rural Anglo-Celtic families; Greek and Italian migrant families; Lebanese and Vietnamese refugee communities; Chinese Australians; asylum seekers; welfare recipients; city against bush.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries, poverty and discrimination surveys; mixed for Aboriginal concepts; thin for everyday dignity.
- **Languages needed:** English; Wiradjuri, Yolŋu Matha, Yumplatok for concepts; Greek, Arabic, Vietnamese, Chinese, Italian for community voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** testimony of abuse, detention and deaths; names and images of the dead; ancestral remains.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
