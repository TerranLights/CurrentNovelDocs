# Australia: Domain and folkways (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English); Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages (Warlpiri, Yolngu Matha, Tiwi, Pitjantjatjara, Kala Lagaw Ya, Meriam Mir); the main community languages (Greek, Italian, Chinese, Arabic, Vietnamese, Maori, Samoan, Tongan). **Concept in one line:** the domain of death and transformation: how the culture faces death, power and what is hidden, with its shared resources, inheritance, sex ways and death ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples by region (Yolngu, Tiwi, Western Desert, Arrernte, Noongar, Koori, Murri, Nunga, Palawa): death and name rules differ.
- Torres Strait Islanders: tombstone ceremonies.
- Anglo-Celtic settler regions: Irish Catholic against Protestant; bush against city.
- Greek, Italian and Lebanese communities: mutual aid, memorial rites.
- Chinese Australians: goldfields heritage, Qingming.
- Pacific peoples and Maori: funeral exchange.
- Muslim, Jewish and Buddhist communities: burial and mourning practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, newspapers and surveys; mixed for Aboriginal practice (much is restricted); thin for private sex and money habits outside surveys.
- **Languages needed:** English for most; the Aboriginal and Torres Strait languages and the community languages for primary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names of the dead and images of the deceased can be restricted in many Aboriginal communities; secret-sacred men's and women's business must not be sought; follow AIATSIS protocols. No `00 - Scope.md` exists for this target yet and its exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
