# Australia: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English); Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples by region: betrayal by settlers, missions and governments is told differently in each.
- Settler lines: Irish Catholic, English Protestant, convict-descended families.
- Unionized and non-unionized workers; rural against urban.
- Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese and Lebanese communities: homeland politics shape who counts as a traitor.
- Armed forces and veterans against civilians.
- Political machines (Labor, Liberal, National) and their factions.
- Men and women: the double standard on infidelity, as a question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public inquiries, press and political history; mixed for family betrayal (survey and memoir only); thin for informing inside Aboriginal and migrant communities.
- **Languages needed:** English; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese and Arabic for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Stolen Generations and abuse survivors' testimony; organized-crime and club material; living persons named in inquiries.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
