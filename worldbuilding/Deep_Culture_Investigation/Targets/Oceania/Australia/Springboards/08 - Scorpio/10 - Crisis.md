# Australia: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English); Noongar, Yolngu and other Aboriginal seasonal and hazard terms; Torres Strait Creole; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, a family, a community or a country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples by region: fire knowledge, seasonal calendars, and trauma legacies differ.
- Torres Strait Islanders: sea-level and storm threat to home islands.
- Bushfire and cyclone regions (Victoria, New South Wales, Tasmania; north Queensland and Northern Territory) against flood regions.
- Farmers and graziers in drought against city dwellers.
- Newly arrived refugees and long-settled migrant families.
- Renters, homeowners and the unhoused.
- Men and women in mental-health crisis; young and old.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, public-health and economic crises; mixed for personal and family crisis (surveys and memoir); thin for how migrants and Aboriginal communities name and meet crisis in their own terms.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages and Aboriginal languages for primary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors' testimony; suicide and mental-health crises; Aboriginal trauma accounts; living persons named in inquiries. Law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
