# Australia: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples by region: smoking, cultural burning and healing differ across Anangu, Yolngu, Koori, Noongar and others.
- Torres Strait Islanders and Pacific peoples: cleansing and healing practice.
- Irish Catholic, Protestant, Orthodox, Muslim and Jewish communities: confession, fasting and purity.
- Closed or high-control groups (Exclusive Brethren, Jehovah's Witnesses): shunning.
- Greek, Italian and Vietnamese families: evil eye, coining, folk cures.
- Politically organized groups: unions, parties, the Labor and Liberal machines.
- Town against city: the small town's gossip and exile.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public inquiries and the press; mixed for folk cleansing and Aboriginal practice (often private); thin for how ordinary people think of purging.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages and Aboriginal languages for primary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aboriginal healing and ceremonial smoke practices; massacre and removal records; shunned members' testimony; eating disorders.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
