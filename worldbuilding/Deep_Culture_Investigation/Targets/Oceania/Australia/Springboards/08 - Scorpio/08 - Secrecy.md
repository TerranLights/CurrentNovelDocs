# Australia: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English); Yolngu Matha, Pitjantjatjara and Arrernte terms; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Maori. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden (privacy included).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples by region and by gender: men's and women's business differ.
- Torres Strait Islanders: family and clan knowledge.
- Migrant families (Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Lebanese, Jewish): shame and reticence.
- Small rural towns against big-city anonymity.
- Closeted and out LGBTQ Australians by generation; Sistergirls and Brotherboys.
- Public servants, soldiers and spy agencies.
- Church and lodge members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state secrecy, privacy surveys and inquiries; mixed for family secrets (memoir and support-service sources); sparse by design for restricted Aboriginal knowledge.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages and Aboriginal languages for primary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** secret-sacred men's and women's knowledge must not be sought, copied or retold; adoption and abuse survivors' testimony; living persons in whistleblower cases. Follow AIATSIS protocols.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
