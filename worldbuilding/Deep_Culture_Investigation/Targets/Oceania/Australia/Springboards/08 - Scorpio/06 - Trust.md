# Australia: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English); Yolngu Matha and other Aboriginal languages; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Maori. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down (loyalty is researched here until it has a home).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples by region: kin-based trust against distrust of institutions.
- Torres Strait Islanders and Pacific peoples: obligation and exchange networks.
- Anglo-Celtic rural, small-town and urban Australians: trust in strangers.
- Migrant communities (Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Lebanese): ritual kin, credit groups.
- Newer refugee groups: trust in state and agencies.
- Remote outback and city dwellers: distance, small-town knowing.
- Old against young: trust in news, banks, government.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national surveys and public inquiries; mixed for everyday and migrant trust; thin for trust inside Aboriginal communities, which is better learned from community sources.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages and Aboriginal languages for primary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trust in police and child-removal agencies for Aboriginal families; refugees' distrust of authorities; survivors' accounts. Trust in police and courts is the spirit of the law (Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
