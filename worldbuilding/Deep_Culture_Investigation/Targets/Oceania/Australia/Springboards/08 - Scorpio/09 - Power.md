# Australia: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English); Warlpiri, Yolngu Matha and other Aboriginal terms; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Maori. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it, how it is held and how it is used (legitimate authority is Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples: traditional governance against land councils and state structures; different regions.
- Pastoral and mining families and companies against small farmers and towns.
- Union members against employers; party factions.
- Anglo-Celtic establishment, Irish Catholic and migrant communities: who is inside.
- Women, men and LGBTQ people: who holds office.
- Remote and urban Australia: distance from decisions.
- Church, lodge and clubs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for politics, inquiries, media and wealth; mixed for hidden power and elite networks; thin for power inside families and communities, which memoir and ethnography must supply.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages and Aboriginal languages for primary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aboriginal leadership matters tied to restricted knowledge; living persons in corruption and political cases; abuse survivors' testimony. Legitimate authority and obedience belong to Capricorn; law to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
