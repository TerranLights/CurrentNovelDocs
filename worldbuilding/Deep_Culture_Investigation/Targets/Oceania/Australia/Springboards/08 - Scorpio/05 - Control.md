# Australia: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English); Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Maori. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so (self-control belongs to Capricorn; freedom from control is Aries).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities by region: control by governments against community control; the Northern Territory against other states.
- Anglo-Celtic families, Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese and Lebanese families: parental authority.
- Rural fire and water regions against cities.
- Men against women: who holds the household purse and who decides.
- Young against old: curfews, caring for aging parents.
- Remote, rural and urban Australians: distance from services.
- Veterans and others used to command structures.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys on safety and attitudes, fire, water and public inquiries; mixed for parenting and household money; thin for private control, beyond what shelters and surveys disclose.
- **Languages needed:** English; community languages and Aboriginal languages for primary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic-violence survivors' accounts; Aboriginal children's removal and Stolen Wages testimony; living persons named in inquiries.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
