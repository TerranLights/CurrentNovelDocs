# Australia: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English); Yolngu Matha and Western Desert languages; Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic, Maori. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples by region: Yolngu, Western Desert, Noongar, Koori; payback and settlement differ.
- Torres Strait Islanders and Pacific peoples: exchange and obligation.
- Irish Catholic and Protestant settler lines; sectarian memory.
- Rural, outback and city Australians; sport communities.
- Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese and Vietnamese families: homeland feud memory.
- Gang and club members; prisoners.
- Men and women: retaliation after infidelity or insult.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, sport, political memoirs and some homicide data; mixed for Aboriginal practice and migrant feud memory; thin for private revenge.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal and community languages for primary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aboriginal retaliation and sorcery matters; family-violence homicide; living persons in political memoirs; gang material. The law of private vengeance belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
