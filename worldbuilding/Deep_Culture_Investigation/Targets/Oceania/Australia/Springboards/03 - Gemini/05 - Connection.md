# Australia: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages (Yolŋu Matha, Pitjantjatjara, Warlpiri, Noongar); Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Vietnamese, Italian. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples with different kin systems: Warlpiri, Yolŋu, Anangu, Noongar, Koori, Murri, Palawa; Torres Strait Islanders.
- Australian South Sea Islander descendants in Queensland, and other Pacific-heritage communities.
- Migrants by wave and place: Greek, Italian, Chinese, Lebanese, Vietnamese; and their children.
- Men in rural and outer-suburban areas against women's networks; older people living alone.
- Veterans and service families; volunteers in fire and surf bodies.
- Remote station and mining towns (fly-in workers) against capital cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loneliness and wellbeing surveys; mixed for kinship and sharing (older ethnography, outsiders); thin for informal networks and nepotism, which are rarely recorded.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages for kin terms; Chinese and Arabic for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** kin terms and avoidance rules, names of the dead, mourning protocols, and private information on isolated people; "guānxi" and "wāsṭa" are labels to test, not explanations of a group.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
