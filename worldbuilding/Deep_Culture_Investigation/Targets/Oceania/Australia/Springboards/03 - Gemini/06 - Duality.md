# Australia: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Yolŋu Matha, Pitjantjatjara, Kulin-nation languages and other Aboriginal languages; Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Vietnamese, Italian. **Concept in one line:** how a culture lives with two-ness: two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; the world divided into pairs; and how it treats people who show two faces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples with moiety systems (Yolŋu, Kulin) against those without; Aboriginal people in cities, country towns and remote communities, in their own terms.
- Torres Strait Islanders on the islands and on the mainland.
- Tasmanian Aboriginal communities.
- Children of migrants: Greek, Italian, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese; British-born arrivals.
- Sydney rugby league and Melbourne AFL cultures; city and bush.
- Deaf people; performers with stage personas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for memoir, fiction, comedy and place names; mixed for Indigenous cosmology (older, outsider writing); thin for private double lives.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages through community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** identity is contested in Aboriginal communities (who may speak, and who may not be questioned); avoid outsiders deciding who belongs; moiety lore can be restricted; "wog" and similar words are slurs, used only as published titles.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. *My Place* and *Benang*: note how each author names the two sides, then find Aboriginal critics' replies.
2. Geographical-names board lists: dual-named places by state and the dates.
3. ABS ancestry tables: top ten multi-origin combinations.
4. Search "moiety" with Dhuwa, Yirritja and Bunjil, Waa in AIATSIS.
5. Dictionary entries for "cultural cringe" and "currency lad".
