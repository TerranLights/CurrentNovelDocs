# Australia: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages (Wiradjuri, Pitjantjatjara, Wajarri and others); Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Vietnamese, Italian. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, find out and explore, and how far a culture encourages, channels, limits or punishes it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples with different rules about asking and who may know: Anangu, Wiradjuri, Wajarri Yamaji, Boorong country in Victoria, Palawa, Yolŋu; Torres Strait Islanders (Haddon's expedition field).
- Children, and teenagers, against adults and retirees.
- Remote and regional people against capital-city museum audiences.
- Migrants and descendants searching for origins, against settler families with long records.
- Scientists against community researchers and Elders on the same questions.
- Tasmanian museum and festival publics (MONA) against mainland ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science, museum and survey measures; mixed for everyday nosiness and its limits; thin for Indigenous norms of asking beyond a few communities.
- **Languages needed:** English; Wiradjuri and other language-center materials; Chinese for migrant interest.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** secret and sacred material, burial and ancestral remains, names of the dead; a request not to climb or to photograph is a rule, not a source; many records need permission.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
