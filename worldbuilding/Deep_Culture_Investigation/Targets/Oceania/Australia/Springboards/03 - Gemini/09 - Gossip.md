# Australia: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including Aboriginal English); Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages through language centers; Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Vietnamese, Italian. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal communities by place (Warlpiri, Anangu, Yolŋu, Noongar, Koori), with attention to "shame" and to how rumor moves on social media.
- Torres Strait Islanders, with "coconut wireless" ties across islands.
- Small country and outback towns against suburbs and apartment blocks.
- Migrant communities with tight kin networks: Greek, Lebanese, Chinese, Vietnamese.
- Women's and men's talk (the galah session against the pub; to check, not assume).
- Workplaces, schools and online groups.
- Politicians and public figures against private people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for scandal press, celebrity gossip and online abuse; mixed for private and small-town talk (memoir, fiction, oral history); thin for Indigenous gossip norms (little published).
- **Languages needed:** English; Arabic, Chinese, Greek and Vietnamese for community sources; Indigenous languages via language centers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names of the dead; victims and survivors in whisper-network material; rumor about living people in archives; defamation and privacy law belong to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
