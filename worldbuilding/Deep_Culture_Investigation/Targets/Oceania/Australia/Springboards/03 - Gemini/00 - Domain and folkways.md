# Australia: The domain: communication and learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Australian spellings in sources: "neighbourhood", "organisation"), Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages and Kriol, Auslan, and the main immigrant languages. **Concept in one line:** how people talk, learn and connect close to home: speech, languages, siblings and neighbors, short journeys, early learning.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples by region, in their own terms: Koori, Murri, Nunga, Noongar, Palawa, Yolŋu, Anangu, Warlpiri, Arrernte, Yamatji; also Torres Strait Islanders (Kala Lagaw Ya, Meriam Mir), a distinct people.
- Remote and very remote against metropolitan: School of the Air, community schools, long travel.
- Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Hobart and the country towns.
- Migrant communities by wave: British and Irish, Greek, Italian, German (Barossa, Lutheran), Chinese, Lebanese and Arabic-speaking, Vietnamese, Pacific Islander, New Zealander.
- The Deaf community and Indigenous sign users.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census language, schooling, media and Australian English; mixed for Indigenous talk norms (many are in researcher accounts, written by outsiders); thin for neighbor norms outside surveys. Exact post-war lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the continent and Tasmania.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages as available; Chinese and Arabic for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Aboriginal knowledge is restricted by gender, age or initiation; names of deceased persons; read AIATSIS ethical guidance first.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
