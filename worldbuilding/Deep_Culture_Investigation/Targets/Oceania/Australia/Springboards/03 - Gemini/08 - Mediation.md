# Australia: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Yolŋu Matha, Pitjantjatjara, Warlpiri and other Aboriginal languages; Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Vietnamese, Italian. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision; the go-between who stands between parties.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal communities with night patrols, elders' groups and women's councils (Warlpiri, Anangu, Yolŋu, Noongar, Koori), against towns without.
- Torres Strait Islanders, with their own Island Councils and customs.
- Migrant communities by faith and origin: Lebanese (Muslim and Christian), Greek Orthodox, Chinese, Vietnamese, Pacific Islander.
- Outback, bush and station settings (clergy, flying doctors) against capitals.
- Schools, workplaces and unions as three arenas.
- Women's and men's go-between roles (check).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal services, church and peace-mission reports; mixed for Indigenous peacemaking (varies by place, partly written by outsiders); thin for informal go-betweens and family mediation.
- **Languages needed:** English; Yolŋu Matha, Pitjantjatjara and others through community organizations; Arabic and Chinese for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aboriginal law and ceremony are restricted by custom; payback is sensitive; family disputes involve private lives and, at times, violence (check how services treat it); courts, tribunals, statutory mediation, industrial arbitration and native-title mediation belong to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
