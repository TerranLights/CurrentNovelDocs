## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples (Yolŋu, Anangu, Warlpiri, Noongar, Koori, Murri, Palawa, Kaurna) and Torres Strait Islanders: learning from elders and on country, and different school experiences.
- Remote and very remote communities against capitals: distance education and boarding.
- State systems (New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland, others): government, Catholic and independent schools.
- Migrant families by origin: Chinese, Greek, Lebanese, Vietnamese, Italian, Pacific Islander.
- Rural trades and farm families against city professionals.
- Deaf learners and learners with disability.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school tests, longitudinal studies and policy; mixed for family and community learning outside school; thin for informal learning.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous languages through language centers; Chinese and Arabic for heritage-school sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiation and gendered knowledge is restricted; removal-era school records are painful and access-controlled; get community permission before using images of children or deceased people.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
