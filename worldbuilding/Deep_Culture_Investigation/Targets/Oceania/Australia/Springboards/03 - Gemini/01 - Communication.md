# Australia: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages, Kriol and Yumplatok; Chinese, Arabic, Greek, Vietnamese, Italian. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and what a culture believes good communication is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal peoples by region (Yolŋu, Anangu, Noongar, Koori, Murri, Palawa) and Torres Strait Islanders: different languages, registers and rules about speaking.
- Remote communities against capital cities; bush and outback against coast.
- Migrants by wave and language: Greek, Italian, Chinese, Lebanese, Vietnamese, Pacific Islander.
- Men's and women's speech settings, the old and the young, Deaf Auslan users.
- English speakers by state, class and school type.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for English speech studies, media and surveys; mixed for Indigenous norms (often written by outsiders, from a few communities); thin for private talk.
- **Languages needed:** English; Indigenous-language and community-media access through translation, or a language center.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** avoidance speech, names of the deceased and restricted men's and women's knowledge; check AIATSIS guidance and community permission.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
