# Australia: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Remote Aboriginal communities with local-language radio, against Aboriginal people in the capitals.
- Stolen Generations families searching records, against those who never needed to.
- Regional towns with no local paper, against Sydney and Melbourne.
- Migrant communities that rely on non-English media and on WeChat or other apps.
- Fire- and flood-affected places (Queensland, New South Wales, Victoria, Tasmania) for crisis information.
- Older and younger people, in news sources and in trust.
- Torres Strait Islanders, with their own media and island-to-mainland links.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for news use, trust and access; mixed for Indigenous knowledge systems and archives; thin for rumor and word of mouth.
- **Languages needed:** English; Chinese and Arabic for community media; Indigenous languages through the media organizations themselves.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restricted knowledge, images and names of the dead (many archives warn users), and closed records on removed children; follow each archive's protocols and the AIATSIS code.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
