# Australia: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Kimberley and Western Desert languages through community-led sources; Māori; Cantonese and Mandarin, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian and Arabic. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it. (The left-right spending measure is read under Ownership.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities by region, between remote art-center economies, town and city businesses, and mining-agreement communities.
- Mining states (Western Australia, Queensland) against the southeast cities, and the old pastoral districts.
- Wealthy harbor and bay suburbs, outer mortgage-belt suburbs, and country towns.
- Old Anglo-Celtic landed families, post-war European business families, and recent skilled arrivals from Asia and the Middle East.
- Homeowners against renters, and older against younger households.
- People who gamble heavily against those who do not.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wealth statistics and attitudes; mixed for display and discretion, which is thinly measured; thin for informal giving and Aboriginal economies, where community sources lead.
- **Languages needed:** English; Kriol and Aboriginal English; community languages for migrant economies; Aboriginal and Māori terms through community-led sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** art-center and mining-royalty details held by communities; gambling harm and debt; restricted images and names in historical collections.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
