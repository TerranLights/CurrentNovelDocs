# Australia: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Kriol and Aboriginal English; Cantonese and Mandarin, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian and Arabic; Aboriginal languages only through community-led sources. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities by region, with remote, town and city settings treated apart.
- Surf coasts and beach towns (Sydney's south, the Gold Coast, Margaret River) against inland towns.
- Men and women, and young adults against older couples, for how jealousy is read.
- Migrant communities by language and arrival period, for family guarding and courtship customs, researched as questions.
- Country towns and suburbs, for neighborhood territorial claims.
- Separated and blended families, for children and shared homes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for family-violence research and service reports; mixed for everyday possessiveness; thin for non-violent clinging, stinginess and surf localism beyond press and film.
- **Languages needed:** English; Kriol and Aboriginal English; community languages for migrant services and press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family violence and killing, handled with care; Aboriginal family and community matters; contested racial territory claims at beaches; coercive-control law belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
