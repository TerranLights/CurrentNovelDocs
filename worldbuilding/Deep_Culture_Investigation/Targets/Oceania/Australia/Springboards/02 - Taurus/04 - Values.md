# Australia: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Pitjantjatjara, Ngangiwumirr, Yolŋu Matha and other Aboriginal languages through community-led sources; Torres Strait Creole; Māori; Mandarin, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian and Arabic. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal groups by nation and by region, with attention to each people's teaching and to urban and remote differences.
- Torres Strait Islanders, where *Ailan Kastom* and church life are intertwined.
- Religious groups: Catholics, Anglicans, Pentecostals, Orthodox, Muslims, Buddhists, Jews and those with no religion.
- Generations (older settlers, post-war migrants, recent migrants and young Australians).
- Rural and city residents; mining towns; the states.
- Migrant communities by language and arrival period (Greek, Italian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Lebanese and other Arabic-speaking, Māori and Pacific).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and political speech; mixed for Aboriginal teaching, which is best read in Aboriginal authors' own words; thin for private household values.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; community languages for migrant sources; Aboriginal and Māori terms only via community-led sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restricted men's and women's knowledge; sacred stories not for public use; contested histories behind national symbols (Anzac, Australia Day).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
