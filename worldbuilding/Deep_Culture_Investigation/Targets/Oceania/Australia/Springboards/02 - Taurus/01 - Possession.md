# Australia: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Pitjantjatjara, Warlpiri, Yolŋu Matha, Noongar and Torres Strait languages through community-led sources; Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian and Arabic for community press. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal communities by country: the rules about a dead person's things, names and images differ, so separate desert, Arnhem Land, southeast and urban groups.
- Torres Strait Islanders, where the tombstone opening and gift exchange are community-run events.
- Itinerant workers' tradition (drovers, shearers) against farm, town and city households.
- Post-war European arrivals and Vietnamese refugee families, for what was carried and kept.
- Chinese-Australian families of the goldfield era and of recent decades, for the heirloom and the ancestral shrine.
- Older people downsizing against younger renters, for what is kept and what is let go.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for museums, op shops and migrant collections; mixed for Aboriginal practice, where sources are community-held; thin for lending, borrowing and clutter as lived habits.
- **Languages needed:** English first; Aboriginal English and Kriol; community languages for migrant press and museums; Aboriginal languages only through community-led sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names and images of the dead; sacred-secret objects, which are not to be sought out; objects taken and removed; the history of children's removal and what was lost with it, handled through Link-Up services and the people's own accounts.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
