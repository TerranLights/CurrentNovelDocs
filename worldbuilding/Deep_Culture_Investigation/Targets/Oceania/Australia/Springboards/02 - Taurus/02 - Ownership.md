# Australia: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Torres Strait Creole (Yumplatok); Warlpiri, Pitjantjatjara, Yolŋu Matha and Noongar through community-led sources; Chinese, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian and Arabic for community press. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on (here also the left-right reading: how much of what is produced is spent by government).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal groups by tenure system: desert owners and managers (Warlpiri, Pitjantjatjara), Arnhem Land clans (Yolŋu), the southwest (Noongar), the southeast and Victoria, Tasmania, and urban communities.
- Torres Strait Islanders by island group, where Island custom governs land and adoption.
- Pastoralists and farmers against mining-region residents (Western Australia's Pilbara, Queensland's coal districts).
- Homeowners, renters and strata residents, and public housing tenants.
- Post-war European and later Asian and Middle Eastern arrivals, for the first-home goal.
- City and state differences in tenure (Sydney and Melbourne against Perth, Adelaide, Hobart and Darwin).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing and government finance; mixed for Aboriginal tenure, where community sources lead; thin for collective, co-operative and digital ownership.
- **Languages needed:** English; Kriol and Aboriginal English; Aboriginal languages only through community-led sources; community languages for migrant press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims to country and restricted knowledge of sites; family disputes over claims; the legal side of title, native title and tenure acts belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
