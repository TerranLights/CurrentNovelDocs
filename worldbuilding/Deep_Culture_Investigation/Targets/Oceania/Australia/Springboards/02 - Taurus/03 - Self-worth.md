# Australia: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Australia, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** Australian English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; Pitjantjatjara, Yolŋu Matha and other Aboriginal languages through community-led sources; Mandarin, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian, Arabic and Māori for the communities that use them. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people by region and community, including urban Aboriginal people, whose accounts of worth and shame may differ from remote ones.
- Men and women, young and old, for the places where each is said to lose or keep standing.
- Rural, mining-town and city residents, and people out of work.
- Post-war migrants and refugees, and their children, for face, honor and *philótimo* ideas as the communities word them.
- Anglo-Celtic settler lines against recent arrivals from Asia, the Middle East and Africa.
- Veterans and their families; people on welfare payments.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and public debate; mixed for Aboriginal worth, which is best taken from Indigenous-led sources; thin for private habits of praise and self-talk.
- **Languages needed:** English; Aboriginal English and Kriol; community languages for migrant sources; Aboriginal languages only through community-led sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and self-harm data and testimony; Stolen Generations testimony; names and images of the dead; shame and trauma accounts that people may not want quoted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
