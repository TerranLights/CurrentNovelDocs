# Tepenian National Saints

## What a Saint is in Tepenia (the author's ruling, 2026-11-01)
- **Not religious.** Tepenians, robots and humans alike, do not think of their Saints the way saints are thought of today. A Saint is not someone anointed by God in Heaven, or anything to that effect.
- **Cultural and historical reverence.** Tepenians hold these figures in very high esteem: the men who gave up huge portions of their lives, and sometimes their lives themselves, for the sake of Antarctica's future.
- **The nearest real-world equivalent** is the way Americans regard the Founding Fathers, but held higher still. It is very high esteem and reverence, short of anything divine.
- **Religious buildings can still carry a Saint's name.** A religious building or establishment may be named for a Saint even though the Saints themselves are not religious figures. Whether one is (for example, the "St. Roald's Chapel" in Troll's megasheet) is decided case by case.

Canon context: the DerivationMechanisms Glossary (`T.md`, "Tepenian Saints", updated with this ruling on 2026-11-01) covers pre-2083 Antarctic explorers who unknowingly prepared the home the exiles would later need. The honorific uses the first name: St. Robert (Scott), St. Ernest (Shackleton), St. Roald (Amundsen), St. Douglas (Mawson) and St. Richard (Byrd) on the national roster; St. Carsten (Borchgrevink) and St. Jules (Dumont d'Urville) in their city files.
