# Research Unions — Pre-War Scientific Institution Naming

Author-specified 2026-09-26, while developing the Sovereign Republic of Hawaii's National Character Profile.
This is the naming convention behind **U.R.U.K.** (Underwater Research Union: Kappa), the pre-war facility
where the first sentient robot, En.Ki.Du, was created in 2083 — U.R.U.K. turns out to be one instance of a
broader, deliberate naming system, not a one-off name. **The title is a slight simplification**: the naming
system itself outlives the pre-war period it started in — see Orbital, below, a domain that can only exist
once orbital access does, centuries after 2083.

**Already established in Hana Jinn's existing character files, found after the author pointed to them** —
`reference/Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Past_History_-_Known_to_Tepenians/Hana Jinn/README.md` and
`Personal_Background/PreWar_History.md` — which confirm a **second** Research Union, A.R.U.Ta.G., and correct
this file's own first-pass assumption about how the Greek-letter part works (see below). This file's job is
to state the *pattern* explicitly, since nowhere else does that — the individual facilities themselves were
already partly on the record.

---

## The naming pattern

**`[Domain] Research Union: [Greek letter or letters]`**, abbreviated
**`[Domain initial].R.U.[Greek-letter abbreviation(s)]`**.

- **Domain** — the operating environment the facility is built for. **Four confirmed, author-specified
  2026-09-26: Underwater** (U.R.U.K.), **Aerial** (A.R.U.Ta.G.), **Terrestrial**, and **Subterranean**. The
  domain list is open-ended by design, not a closed set — plausible further domains, not yet confirmed: Polar,
  and (later, see below) Orbital.
  - **⚠️ Orbital is explicitly NOT a pre-war/original-era domain, author-specified 2026-09-26.** Research
    Unions as a naming system predate any society actually being able to *reach* orbit — orbit only becomes
    reachable as a consequence of Hana Jinn's own A.R.U.Ta.G. research (metamaterials for mass drivers) being
    built out into real infrastructure, decades to centuries later (Mallory Dufay's work, mid-2300s). So an
    Orbital Research Union, if one is ever named, belongs to a **later era** of the naming system, not the
    original pre-war set alongside U.R.U.K./A.R.U.Ta.G. — the author confirmed this "does eventually end up
    being a thing later on," making Research Unions a naming convention that outlived the pre-war period it
    started in, not a strictly pre-war-only institution. Worth remembering when placing any future Orbital
    Research Union on the timeline: it cannot predate the orbital infrastructure Hana's own research enabled.
- **Research Union** — the fixed middle term. Every facility in this system is a "Research Union," regardless
  of domain — this is the institutional type, not a location-specific name.
- **Greek letter(s)** — **designates one specific, individual establishment**, not the domain as a whole.
  **Corrected from this file's first draft**: the designation is not always a single letter. U.R.U.K. uses one
  (Kappa → "K"); A.R.U.Ta.G. uses **two** (Tau Gamma → "Ta.G.") — so the abbreviation scheme takes as many
  Greek-letter syllables as the facility's own designation needs, not a fixed count. A domain can plausibly
  have more than one Research Union facility, each with its own letter-combination — an Underwater Research
  Union: Alpha would be a *different* facility from U.R.U.K., not a synonym for it.

## What's confirmed vs. open

**Confirmed:**
- **Four domains named: Underwater, Aerial, Terrestrial, Subterranean** (author-specified 2026-09-26). Only
  Underwater and Aerial have a named specific facility so far (below) — Terrestrial and Subterranean are
  confirmed as domain *types* that exist in this naming system, with no individual facility named under
  either yet.
- **Orbital is a later-era domain, not part of the original pre-war set** — see above. Confirmed to eventually
  exist, but only once orbital access itself exists, which postdates 2083 by decades to centuries.
- **U.R.U.K. = Underwater Research Union: Kappa.** Off the coast of Hawaii. Pre-war. Where En.Ki.Du, the first
  sentient robot, was created — **September 26, 2083** (per `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Timeline Eras/1 The
  First Interwar Period/Timeline.md`; the year alone, 2083, was this project's prior level of precision). Was
  under United States dominion at the time — predates the Sovereign Republic of Hawaii, which didn't exist yet
  (`nations/World-Nations-Roster.md`, author-corrected 2026-09-18).
  - **En.Ki.Du's own name is itself an acronym**, per `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/World_History_Reference.md`:
    **Entelechian** (Aristotle's concept of entelechy), **Kinetic** (moves under his own power), **Duumvir**
    (he is one half of a two-person team, not a "Triumvir"). Also an Epic of Gilgamesh reference (Enkidu;
    Uruk, the city Gilgamesh ruled). His human counterpart is named **Gil(bert) Gamesh** — the Gilgamesh to
    his Enkidu — "not yet otherwise documented anywhere in the wider setting" per that same source. Not part
    of this file's own Research Union research, but directly relevant to U.R.U.K. and worth carrying forward
    with it.
- **A.R.U.Ta.G. = Aerial Research Union: Tau Gamma.** Where Hana Jinn (a Gynoid, Data Analytics and Research
  Expert, SP 4w3, originally of U.R.U.K.) was relocated to — requested by name for her exceptional ability,
  per the author. At A.R.U.Ta.G. she researched metamaterials for mass drivers to launch reusable rockets
  into low-Earth orbit — early engineering groundwork for Tepenia's orbital infrastructure, later built out
  by Mallory Dufay (a structural/safety inspector from the CSA, active mid-2300s). Whether Hana survived to
  the Falkland Treaty is unestablished.
- **Robots are Research Union personnel, not just their creations.** Hana's own case shows the pattern isn't
  only "robots get built at a Research Union" (En.Ki.Du at U.R.U.K.) — a robot can also *work* at one, get
  requested by name, and transfer between facilities across domains (underwater → aerial). Worth keeping in
  mind for any future Research Union character.
- The naming system itself: Domain + "Research Union" + one-or-more Greek letters, unique per facility.

**Open, not yet decided:**
- How many Research Unions exist in total, across all domains, and who operates/operated them (a single
  global pre-war body? multiple national programs using a shared naming convention? something else?).
- Whether the Greek-letter choice is meaningful (founding order, security tier, something else) or arbitrary
  per facility — and, now that a two-letter case exists (Tau Gamma), whether letter *count* itself means
  anything (e.g., a facility's scale, importance, or generation) or is just cosmetic.
- Whether any specific Terrestrial or Subterranean Research Union has a name, location, or role yet — the
  domain types are confirmed, individual facilities are not.
- Whether any Research Unions still exist/matter post-2083, or whether this is purely pre-war institutional
  history at this point (Hana's own post-A.R.U.Ta.G. fate is itself unestablished).
