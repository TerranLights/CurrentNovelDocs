## *Electric Dust*: the current premise (AUTHOR, 2026-10-11; the author is **not fully convinced** it is the best option and wants suggestions)
- **Carlos Agustín Rodríguez** (the neuroscientist; the "owner" in the older song concept: confirmed) was **originally trying to prove that robots have souls**. That was **not** why he bought Estefanía: he knew he tended to "retreat into his own head", so her **original purpose** was to be a **"realistic perspective"** to keep him in check. **His buying her is not shown in the book.**
- **Later Carlos came to believe in a fundamental "robot humanity"**, that **the soul is the result of physical processes and not metaphysical ones**, and that **robots generate souls of their own, as humans do**.
- **The incident (the author's first idea):** tests he ran on her were inconclusive, so he subjected himself to the experiment and had Estefanía run the tests on him. **Through a misunderstanding she made a perfectly innocent but critical mistake, and it killed Carlos.** Under normal circumstances she would be arrested for manslaughter; because robots were not legally persons then, she would be **nearly guaranteed to be destroyed.**
- **The escape:** **Alonso Laurentino Reyes**, Carlos's close friend and **mentor figure** and a firm believer in robot rights and robot humanity, **helped her escape and told her to flee to a collective of robots and humans in the desert**; he would stay and handle the situation and **meet her later.**
- **Author's further answers (2026-10-11):** the book is **essentially Estefanía's origin story: where she comes from, who she is, what she's about.** A pursuer would be **HyperHedral only if there is a pursuer at all.** The author is **still not sure that "her mistake kills a friend" is the best reason for her being forced to leave her original home city** and is open to other reasons.
- **Fixed by the rest of the collection:** the novel is set in about June 2131; the next work (a movie, early July) has her meeting her human boyfriend and finding her tribe; robots are property; robot personhood does not arrive for centuries and the Timeline's Act 2 trigger (Feray, ~2180) is where "undeniable humanity" is first argued publicly, so *Electric Dust* must **not** leave the world with public proof that robots have souls.
