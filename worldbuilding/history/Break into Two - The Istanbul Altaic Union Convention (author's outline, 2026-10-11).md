# Break into Two (the "Doorway to Act 2"): the Istanbul Altaic Union convention

**Staging file, 2026-10-11.** The author's outline of the trigger for Act 2 of the First Interwar Period, recorded as stated, with a first consistency check against the files. **Nothing here is canon until the author says so, and nothing is written to the `TepenianUniverseTimeline` repo** (never without an explicit instruction). Flags: **AUTHOR** = stated by the author; **CHECK** = my cross-reference against project files; **OPEN** = needs the author; **PROPOSED** = my suggestion; (search) / ⚠️M = web / memory.

## 1. The outline (AUTHOR, 2026-10-11)
- **Where it sits.** The First Interwar Period's "Doorway of No Return #1" (Snyder's Break into Two), the trigger for Act 2. The author's idea for it is "pretty reasonably clear-ish".
- **Who is not directly involved.** The three consignatories at the International Court of Diplomacy at Jeju-do (Japan, Korea, the Sinian Federation).
- **Who is.** A country the three consignatories would **listen to: Kazakhstan.**
- **The Altaic Union.** In this universe the Altaic Union has become a stronger organization. In the real world Hungary is (currently) an *observer* member of the corresponding organization; in the Tepenian timeline **Hungary is a "semi-participant" (or equivalent role) member.**
- **The story.**
  1. A **Hungarian politician, widely known as strongly anti-robot-rights**, is sent on assignment to **Turkey** in preparation for an **Altaic Union convention in Istanbul**.
  2. He is **paired with a Turkish robot, Feray Yazıcı** (the main character).
  3. At the start he believes robot personhood is an **inversion of nature and a threat to humans**.
  4. Through a series of struggles, hardships and crises, **Feray sacrifices herself to save him**, in a way that makes her **fundamental humanity undeniable to everyone who witnessed it**.
  5. At the very end he delivers a speech to the **Altaic Congress arguing for robot rights of legal personhood**; **the Kazakh diplomacy team is particularly persuaded** to side with him and the robots.

## 4. The "Altaic Union" and Hungary (CHECK / OPEN)

- **OPEN: why Kazakhstan.** The files do not say. Kazakhstan is the largest Central Asian nation in the West Asia data; what makes it a country the three ICD consignatories listen to (trade, the steppe corridor, a mediator role, language) is open.
